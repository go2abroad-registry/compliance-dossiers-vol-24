Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of Samoa (Inland Revenue Department and Samoa Customs Service), electronic services portal: Talamo e-Filing (www.revenue.gov.ws); overseas entity registry and International Company supervisor: Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA, www.sifa.ws) acting under the International Companies Act 1987 and the International Companies Regulation.

E-government filing portal

International Companies do not require a Ministry for Revenue TIN for offshore business. Procedure: (1) engage a SIFA-licensed registered agent and file the Articles of Incorporation; (2) SIFA issues the Certificate of Incorporation, the IC registration number and the statutory tax-exemption status certificate; this certificate constitutes the entity's tax identity for banks and investors; (3) obtain a TIN and/or GST registration from the Ministry for Revenue only if the IC opens a local Samoa bank account, becomes a GST registrant for domestic taxable supplies, or must act as a Samoa withholding agent — application is made by the registered agent on the prescribed MfR form, attaching the SIFA certificate, constitution, directors, and registered-office confirmation; (4) turnaround: SIFA certificate within 1 business day; supplementary MfR TIN within 3–5 business days; (5) no minimum capital and no non-resident ownership restriction apply.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Samoa IC is statutorily exempt non-resident: no Samoa corporate income tax, dividend withholding tax, GST, capital gains tax, customs import duty or stamp duty on qualifying offshore business; exemption is automatic and not elective, conditional on the statutory restrictions (no business with Samoan residents, no Samoan land interests, no management and control in Samoa, no Samoa-source income, licensed registered agent maintained).

Available election forms

No Samoan check-the-box election: the IC is an opaque corporation by law. (1) Domestic conversion: an IC may elect, by special resolution with SIFA consent, to register as an ordinary Samoa domestic company and thereby become subject to entity-level taxation under the Income Tax Act 2015 — an opt-in into the taxable regime. (2) Foreign-company downgrade: the IC may elect to be struck from the IC register and re-licensed as a foreign company branch, giving rise to Samoan tax only on Samoa-source income. (3) Hybrid-mismatch rules: Samoa does not provide a hybrid election; the IC's tax status is determined by the investor jurisdiction's classification rules (e.g., US check-the-box, CFC regimes, OECD Pillar Two income-inclusion rule), and the 2015 Income Tax Act contains CFC and general anti-avoidance rules relevant only to Samoan-resident shareholders.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 50,000 (Samoan Tala) of annual taxable supplies measured on a 12-month (rolling or prospective) basis; mandatory GST registration applies when the threshold is met or exceeded; voluntary registration is open to suppliers of taxable supplies; a Samoa International Company whose supplies are zero-rated offshore international services is outside the registration net but may optionally register to recover GST on Samoan inputs.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: zero-rated when actually exported from Samoa and supported by customs documentation and foreign payment. Services: zero-rated as 'international services' when the recipient is a non-resident located outside Samoa at the time of supply, the service is not physically performed in Samoa to a material extent, and it does not relate to Samoan land or goods in Samoa. The Consumer Panel Survey Platform's electronic market-research SaaS qualifies for zero-rating on the offshore platform fee; any on-the-ground consumer polling fieldwork actually performed in Samoa is a local taxable supply (15% GST) and should be separately invoiced. An IC operating wholly offshore does not charge GST; if voluntarily GST-registered, it may zero-rate the SaaS export and obtain refunds of input GST on Samoan overheads.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged via ASYCUDA World (Samoa Customs Service)
  • Commercial invoice bearing the supplier's GST registration number and zero-rated endorsement
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of foreign exchange within the statutory repatriation window
  • For pure digital/international services: recipient tax-residence certificate, service agreement and online-delivery records replace the customs documents

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate applies: Samoa does not operate an e-invoicing clearance/API system, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. GST-registered suppliers may issue paper or PDF tax invoices with the prescribed GST particulars; GST returns are filed electronically on the Ministry for Revenue's Talamo e-Services portal, and customs export declarations are transmitted through ASYCUDA World. Cross-border SaaS invoices issued by the Consumer Panel Survey Platform to overseas clients in PDF/email format, without XML clearance or fiscal codes, are legally sufficient.

Audit scrutiny is dual: the Ministry for Revenue (with Samoa Customs) audits GST, income and export substantiation; SIFA audits IC exemption compliance under the International Companies Act 1987. Enforced corrective measures for false zero-rating claims and misuse of IC exemptions: (1) GST shortfall assessment reversed to 15% plus additional tax (25% late-payment tax and 2% per month/marginal penalty tax under the GST Act), monetary fines and imprisonment on conviction; (2) shortfall penalties up to 150% for deliberate tax evasion under the Tax Administration Act 2012, with the Commissioner able to disregard anti-avoidance arrangements and to assess for up to 8 years in fraud cases; (3) an IC that breaches its statutory exemption conditions becomes retrospectively assessable on Samoan-source income at ordinary rates, may be struck off by SIFA, and incurs SIFA administrative fines; (4) zero-rated export claims not substantiated within the documentation window are clawed back with interest running from the original GST due date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa operates under the Exchange Control Act and CBS Exchange Control Regulations, preserving current account convertibility for trade and services, while capital account transactions remain subject to approval thresholds. Non-resident controlled International Companies (ICs) may maintain foreign-currency and WST tala accounts through licensed commercial banks, and profits or proceeds derived from offshore business may be remitted freely provided source documentation and bank compliance validation are satisfactory. There are no domestic tax-clearance hurdles for ICs because the entity enjoys zero corporate income tax in Samoa, but transaction reason codes and beneficial ownership disclosures must be furnished.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa's exchange control framework requires Samoan resident exporters to repatriate export proceeds to Samoa through a licensed CBS bank within the statutory ninety-day period and to convert export receipts to WST tala except where CBS has granted an exemption. For a non-resident-owned International Company relying on the International Companies Act exemptions, mandatory surrender does not apply automatically; clean offshore collections into foreign-currency accounts are permitted, but any inward payment intended for local WST use must be supported by an exchange control application and, where required, a written exemption obtained by the licensed bank before conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Apia, Samoa

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. PayPal and Wise are not licensed banks in Samoa and cannot independently provide Samoa IBANs or clear WST tala. They are treated as cross-border payment aggregators and can be used for consumer-panel mass payouts only if paired with a licensed Samoan bank or with duly authorised money remittance operators under the Money Remittance Act 2016. Use of offshore EMIs is legally permissible for settling commercial obligations outside Samoa, but they do not satisfy CBS prudential banking or AML/CFT onboarding for treasury operations.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File/Master File threshold applies to International Companies because they are exempt from income tax; for taxable resident companies, contemporaneous documentation is required where related-party transactions exceed WST 1,000,000 (Local File) or WST 5,000,000 (Master File) per annum.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies Act 1988, as amended by the Economic Substance Regulations 2020, requires every International Company carrying on relevant activities including intellectual property, headquarters, distribution, and service functions to demonstrate economic substance in Samoa or a comparable OECD jurisdiction. A consumer panel survey platform cannot be a mere shell; it must maintain local physical presence, employees, expenditure, and management decision-making for the exempt status to hold.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Sham Offshore Structure

Statutory sanction: Under the Samoa Tax Administration Act 2015, penalties of up to WST 1,000,000 or 150% of the evaded tax, and imprisonment for up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, interest, and penalties; courts may pierce the corporate veil, impose travel bans, and freeze personal assets.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the International Companies Register, retroactive cancellation of exempt status, fines up to WST 250,000 for false declarations, and possible deemed incorporation as a taxable resident company.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for all debts of the company, including tax liabilities, and can face civil restitution orders and disqualification from acting as directors for up to 10 years.
AML/CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Samoa Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018, entities face fines up to WST 1,000,000, cancellation of money services provider approval, and mandatory compliance audits; individuals are subject to imprisonment up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to criminal prosecution, travel bans, confiscation of illegal proceeds, and bans on managing any financial or commercial entity in Samoa.
