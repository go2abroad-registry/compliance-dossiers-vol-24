Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (Samoa) - official tax administration portal: www.revenue.gov.ws; corporate registry for International Companies: Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) at www.sifa.ws

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate an International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 (ICA 1987) through SIFA. Step 2: No Samoan tax identification number (TIN) is automatically required because a Samoan International Company (IC) is an exempt non-resident vehicle; it is not subject to domestic income tax or VAGST on foreign-source digital content sales. Step 3: If the IC nevertheless carries on a domestic taxable activity or is required to charge VAGST, apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a TIN and VAGST registration on the prescribed form. Supporting documentation includes: original certificate of incorporation issued by SIFA; certificate of registered office and registered agent; notarised passport copies of directors/members and ultimate beneficial owners; board resolution appointing a local tax representative if needed; and details of the Samoan registered office. Step 4: The Ministry for Revenue issues the TIN / VAGST registration number. Step 5: For purely offshore distribution of micro-dramas (licensing, streaming, app-store sales), no Samoan TIN is required unless the company chooses to register voluntarily for input VAT recovery.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - A Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, gift/estate duty, foreign exchange controls, and all other taxes and duties (except annual government fees paid to SIFA). It is treated as a non-resident corporation not liable in Samoa on its foreign-source income. It cannot make taxable supplies to Samoan residents without a local trading licence; its global digital transactions are therefore outside the Samoan territorial tax net. No pass-through classification exists for ICs.

Available election forms

None - Samoa has no check-the-box entity classification election and no domestic hybrid-mismatch notification regime. The IC's tax-exempt status is mandatory and arises by operation of ICA 1987. An IC may voluntarily forfeit exempt status only by obtaining a domestic business/trading licence and becoming a resident taxpayer for tax purposes, which is a de facto tax election rather than a statutory check-the-box. There is no OECD GLOBE or hybrid mismatch election available to a Samoa IC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

SAT$100,000 (100,000 Samoan tālā) of taxable supplies per 12-month period; foreign/IC providers of digital content are not required to register unless they make domestic taxable supplies, which the International Companies Act prohibits without a local trading licence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A zero rate of VAGST applies to goods exported physically from Samoa and to services supplied to non-residents in respect of services performed outside Samoa, including international digital content licensing and streaming of vertical micro-dramas by a non-resident IC. To claim zero rating for a registered company: the customer/end-user must be a non-resident; the streaming or download must be supplied from outside Samoa or via a foreign platform; the risk, title, and benefit of the content must pass outside Samoa; and commercial evidence must show receipt of consideration from foreign sources. An IC without local trading activities already sits outside the VAGST charge; the zero-rating provision is the applicable rule if the IC voluntarily registers or is a mixed local/offshore supplier.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoan Customs export document covering goods exiting Samoa; for physical media only, digital exports are not customs-controlled)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (original transport document evidencing export from Samoa; for digital transactions, replace with app-store royalty reports and platform settlement statements)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit advice evidencing foreign currency payment received from outside Samoa within the contractual payment cycle)

Samoa does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls (CTC), or digital stamping. The Ministry for Revenue operates an electronic tax-filing portal for VAGST returns and taxpayer registration; VAT invoices may be issued electronically or in paper form and must contain the seller's name, TIN, date, description, quantity, price, and VAGST amount where applicable.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit any refund claim or zero-rating treatment under the Tax Administration Act 2016 and the VAGST Act 1992. A false export or zero-rating claim is treated as tax evasion: the tax is clawed back retroactively with statutory interest and penalties (up to 100% of the tax avoided), and officers/beneficial owners face criminal liability including monetary penalties and imprisonment. For an International Company that loses its exemption or improperly claims refunds, SIFA may also strike the company off the register and directors may be disqualified. Substantiation for digital export refunds requires platform settlement statements, bank remittance advices, IP licensing agreements, and app-store payment reports evidencing non-resident suppliers and non-resident end-users.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% for resident companies under the Income Tax Act 2015 (Samoa)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a duly exempt International Company because the IC is outside the Income Tax Act withholding machinery; the domestic statutory dividend withholding rate for resident non-IC companies is 15% payable by the corporate payer on distributions to shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File or Master File filing threshold is prescribed in Samoa for exempt ICs; best-practice compliance benchmark for the vertical micro-drama group is to maintain OECD-compliant transfer pricing documentation from the first related-party transaction and to hold a full Local File and Master File when related-party annual turnover exceeds WST 1,000,000; intra-group licensing of the micro-drama content library must be priced at arm's length.

Economic substance scope: The Samoa International Companies Economic Substance regime applies OECD and EU-style substance rules to International Companies carrying on relevant activities. Professing to be tax-exempt will not protect the IC if it holds and exploits micro-drama intellectual property: the licensing, acquisition, and global distribution of the content library fall within the Intellectual Property and Distribution and Service Centre categories. The IC must be managed and directed in Samoa, maintain qualified staff and adequate premises in Samoa at which core income-generating activities occur, and incur adequate operating expenditure; pure equity holding companies satisfy only a reduced substance test. Non-compliance is reportable to the competent authority and triggers penalties and deregistration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations (Income Tax Act 2015)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty equal to 100% of the tax avoided; administrative fines up to WST 500,000; loss of IC exemption with full 27% tax and interest recomputed from inception

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors face criminal prosecution, imprisonment up to 5 years, civil restitution for the tax loss, and a claw-back of fraudulent distributions; travel bans are available to secure the tax debt.
Shell Company and Sham Economic Substance (International Companies Economic Substance Regime)

Statutory sanction: Economic substance notification penalty of up to USD 500 per return; substance-test failure penalty up to WST 1,000,000; compulsory exchange of information with treaty partners; strike-off with retrospective dissolution

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: directors are jointly and severally liable for the IC's debts and reinstatement costs; the Registrar may impose travel non-objection conditions on directors exiting Samoa pending compliance, and directors face disqualification.
Money Laundering and AML/CFT Breaches (AML/CFT Act 2017)

Statutory sanction: Civil monetary penalties per infringement of up to WST 7,000,000; forfeiture of laundered property; suspension of the IC's registration by the Financial Intelligence Unit

Director personal liability: Directors, company secretaries, and the licensed registered agent face imprisonment up to 5 years for knowingly permitting predicate offending, personal fines, orders for civil restitution to defrauded parties, and the Samoa travel ban regime for absconding directors.
Nominee Governance and Fraudulent Misrepresentation (International Companies Act 1987)

Statutory sanction: Per-statutory fine of up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; compulsory deregistration of the IC; disqualification of the Samoa registered agent

Director personal liability: Each nominee and beneficial owner is personally liable for restitution of diverted profits; the Court may issue freezing orders over foreign bank accounts, and international arrest warrant channels apply where the evasion exceeds WST 100,000.
Non-Payment of Annual Government Fee and Statutory Filings

Statutory sanction: Suspension of the IC's tax-exempt status, late filing charges, automatic strike-off, and dissolution of the company at the Registrar's discretion

Director personal liability: Directors who trade through a struck-off IC lose limited liability and are jointly liable for all contract obligations of the micro-drama streaming business; reinstatement is discretionary and subject to Revenue clearance and penalty payment.
