Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA); online taxpayer portal available at https://www.sra.gov.ws (SRA e-Services / Taxpayer Access Point for TIN application, VAGST registration, and monthly filing).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) must obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the Samoa Revenue Authority via the licensed registered agent. Application forms (Company TIN Application) must be accompanied by: (1) Certificate of Incorporation issued by the Registrar of International Companies; (2) Memorandum and Articles of Association; (3) Certificate of Registered Office and Resident Agent in Samoa; and (4) directors/shareholders particulars and beneficial ownership declaration. TIN issuance typically occurs within 3-5 business days. Where the IC has Samoa-connected taxable supplies it must separately register for VAGST (VAGST registration number) using the same portal; offshore-only ICs are otherwise not required to file income tax returns due to statutory exemption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – International Companies incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 enjoy statutory exemption from Samoa income tax and stamp duty on offshore activities, provided no business is carried on in Samoa and no Samoa-source income is derived. Such ICs remain subject to VAGST on taxable supplies connected with Samoa (including certain digital services) and to annual government fees payable to the Registrar.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under Samoa law. Available elections include: (1) voluntary VAGST registration for non-resident remote/digital suppliers (simplified remote supplier registration); (2) conversion of an IC into a local (domestic) company under the International Companies Act 1987, which terminates the tax-exempt status and subjects the entity to standard Samoa income tax at 27%, VAGST, and general tax administration rules; and (3) redomiciliation of a foreign company into Samoa as an International Company without re-incorporation, preserving the tax-exempt classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 50,000 annual taxable turnover of supplies connected with Samoa. A remote/non-resident supplier of electronic, digital, and telecommunication services to Samoan consumers must register for VAGST once the value of Samoa-connected supplies exceeds WST 50,000 in any 12-month period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAGST) is available for: (1) goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply; (2) services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Samoa at the time of supply, provided the services are performed wholly outside Samoa or the contract is negotiated and effectively performed offshore; (3) international transportation of goods or passengers; and (4) supplies connected with foreign aid or diplomatic arrangements. A health/fitness app tracker IC may zero-rate subscription and licensing fees only where the direct recipient is a non-resident, the contract is executed offshore, the service benefits are consumed outside Samoa, and consideration is settled in foreign currency. B2C downloads through Apple IAP or Google Play Billing are generally treated as imported digital services subject to Samoa VAGST when consumed in Samoa.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA/Samoa Customs electronic declaration or equivalent export lodgement)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure of goods from Samoa (for goods exports)
  • Commercial Invoice and VAGST Tax Invoice issued to the non-resident buyer showing 0%
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Foreign Exchange Bank Credit Advice from a licensed Samoa commercial bank evidencing receipt of foreign currency funds
  • Service Contract, License, or Subscription Agreement with the offshore customer (for digital services)
  • Supporting evidence of offshore performance and consumption (e.g., server logs, geo-location/IP records, user residency data)

Samoa does not operate a mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC), or API-based digital stamping regime as of 2024. Registered suppliers must issue compliant VAGST tax invoices containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and TIN/VAGST number, recipient details, description, date, amount, and tax charged), retain records for 7 years, and file monthly VAGST returns electronically through the SRA online portal. The SRA may require additional data on cross-border digital transactions but no government pre-clearance of invoices is mandated.

The Samoa Revenue Authority may conduct audit review within the 7-year assessment period and claw back any incorrectly zero-rated supply by reassessing the full 15% VAGST plus statutory interest and late payment penalties. Fraudulent or deliberate false zero-rating claims attract penalty tax of up to 200% of the tax evaded and criminal prosecution under the Tax Administration Act 2014 and the VAGST Act 1997, with potential fines and imprisonment up to 5 years for directors and responsible officers. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days constitutes an exchange control offence under the Exchange Control Act 1976, punishable by fine, imprisonment, and forfeiture of the unrepatriated foreign currency proceeds, in addition to SRA tax adjustments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a partially convertible currency. Current-account payments, including trade in goods and services, are in practice freely processed through authorized dealers. Capital-account transactions, WST-denominated borrowings by foreign-owned entities, and any arrangement that creates a WST liability to a non-resident require prior exchange control approval. An International Company (IC) organized under Samoa's International Companies Act 2012 is treated as offshore and non-resident; where the Health Fitness App Tracker entity keeps its operating treasury in USD/EUR via non-resident settlement accounts, profit remittance and shareholder distributions can occur without WST conversion or CBS approval. Tajikistan? No. There is no freely floating retail offshore WST market; all WST quotations must be made by CBS-licensed authorized dealers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control framework, exporters of physical goods must repatriate and sell export proceeds to an authorized dealer in Samoa within the period specified by CBS (generally 30 days after receipt). Digital invisible exports, including app-store subscriptions, in-app purchases, and biometric telemetry services, are not captured by the physical goods surrender rule. Where the App Tracker IC receives USD/AUD/EUR directly from Apple and Google treasury intermediaries and neither routes the funds through a WST account nor lands them to a Samoa domestic bank, no statutory surrender/conversion obligation arises. If the IC voluntarily repatriates overseas funds to its Samoa authorized-dealer account, the foreign currency must be either maintained in an approved foreign-currency account or sold to the dealer within the prescribed surrender timeline, unless the IC has obtained an offshore company exemption.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) - the sole monetary authority, banking supervisor, and exchange control administrator under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984 and the Exchange Control Act 1976.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low. Samoa does not maintain a domestic electronic money institution (EMI) or payment-institution licensing regime, so fully digital offshore EMIs are not authorized deposit-takers in Samoa. However, a Samoa International Company may pragmatically use an EU/UK/FATF-jurisdiction EMI (e.g., an EMI licensed under PSD2 with a sponsor bank) to collect USD mounts? To be corrected. The platform is best used as a secondary collection and disbursement rail for Apple/Google IAP payouts and low-risk service-provider payments. Because the IC earns active fintech software revenue and presents a low AML inherent risk, mainstream international EMIs can open accounts remotely for the corporate entity; still, the primary statutory and banking relationship should remain with a CBS-licensed authorized dealer to ensure exchange-control compliance and documentary credibility for any future Samoan court or tax authority inquiry.

Late or incorrect filing: administrative shortfall penalty of 20% of the understated tax plus 1.5% monthly late-payment interest on outstanding amounts; criminal prosecution with a fine of up to 200 penalty units (SAT 40,000) and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly false returns.

Late or incorrect filing: administrative shortfall penalty of 20% of the understated tax plus 1.5% monthly late-payment interest on outstanding amounts; criminal prosecution with a fine of up to 200 penalty units (SAT 40,000) and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly false returns.
Box 6 Income tax payable at the 27% standard corporate rate. Computed as '0' on the exempt position; any corrected assessment must be appealed through the registered agent within 30 days. Late or incorrect filing: administrative shortfall penalty of 20% of the understated tax plus 1.5% monthly late-payment interest on outstanding amounts; criminal prosecution with a fine of up to 200 penalty units (SAT 40,000) and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly false returns.
Box 7 Credits (foreign tax credits, WHT credits) and net tax payable or refund. Enter '0'; where a foreign jurisdiction withheld on the IC's app revenue, credit claims are pursued in that jurisdiction, not in Samoa. Late or incorrect filing: administrative shortfall penalty of 20% of the understated tax plus 1.5% monthly late-payment interest on outstanding amounts; criminal prosecution with a fine of up to 200 penalty units (SAT 40,000) and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly false returns.
Box 8 Declaration by a director and certification by the registered agent. A non-resident director may sign by scanned signature; the registered agent must countersign to attest authority and accuracy. Late or incorrect filing: administrative shortfall penalty of 20% of the understated tax plus 1.5% monthly late-payment interest on outstanding amounts; criminal prosecution with a fine of up to 200 penalty units (SAT 40,000) and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly false returns.
Part A IC name, registration number, TEC number, and incorporation date. Match the exact registered name; non-resident shareholders' legal identities are disclosed only to the Ministry, never placed on the public register. Failure to renew: immediate loss of the exempt regime, retroactive exposure to 27% income tax on the period from expiry, and a late-renewal fee; material misdeclaration allows the Commissioner to revoke the TEC, triggering assessment and director personal liability.
Part B Declaration of continued compliance with ICA 1987 s.101: no business with Samoan residents, no Samoan domestic market activity, no Samoan real property. Offering paid fitness subscriptions to Samoan-resident consumers would breach this declaration and void the certificate. Failure to renew: immediate loss of the exempt regime, retroactive exposure to 27% income tax on the period from expiry, and a late-renewal fee; material misdeclaration allows the Commissioner to revoke the TEC, triggering assessment and director personal liability.
Part C Business activity description covering ISIC 6201 software publishing, revenue channels (Apple IAP, Google Play Billing), and expected annual turnover. State that all end-users are outside Samoa; the registered agent may attach an app distribution and jurisdiction report. Failure to renew: immediate loss of the exempt regime, retroactive exposure to 27% income tax on the period from expiry, and a late-renewal fee; material misdeclaration allows the Commissioner to revoke the TEC, triggering assessment and director personal liability.
Part D Beneficial ownership particulars (individuals exercising ultimate control), compliant with the AML/CFT Act 2019. A trust owning the IC must attach the trustee's contact details and the settled beneficiaries' categories, without exposing the trust deed publicly. Failure to renew: immediate loss of the exempt regime, retroactive exposure to 27% income tax on the period from expiry, and a late-renewal fee; material misdeclaration allows the Commissioner to revoke the TEC, triggering assessment and director personal liability.
Part E Registered agent certification of records location, board decision-making, and economic substance. Board meetings may be conducted online; minutes and accounting records must be physically available in Samoa or via the agent for production on demand. Failure to renew: immediate loss of the exempt regime, retroactive exposure to 27% income tax on the period from expiry, and a late-renewal fee; material misdeclaration allows the Commissioner to revoke the TEC, triggering assessment and director personal liability.
