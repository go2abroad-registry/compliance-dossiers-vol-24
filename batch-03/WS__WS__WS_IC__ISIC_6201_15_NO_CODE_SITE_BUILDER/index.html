Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of the Independent State of Samoa (MfR) — official government tax administration; online taxpayer portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws and https://tafao.revenue.gov.ws (Revenue Online Services)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 must first obtain a Certificate of Incorporation from the Samoa Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour. For national tax identity, the IC must then submit the prescribed TIN Registration Application (Form TIN-01) to the Ministry for Revenue, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered office consent, certified copy of the charter, and director/shareholder identification. Non-resident entities may file the application through a licensed registered agent (registered office provider) in Apia. The Ministry for Revenue issues the Tax Identification Number (TIN) upon verification; an International Company may simultaneously file the Application for Tax Exemption Confirmation to obtain a formal certificate confirming exemption from Samoa income tax, VAGST and withholding tax on foreign-source offshore activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available under Samoan law. The Income Tax Act 2014 and the International Companies Act 1987 provide an automatic territorial exemption regime for International Companies: a Samoa IC is exempt from Samoa income tax, VAGST and stamp duty on all income, assets, transactions and activities conducted beyond Samoa (offshore). The IC may electively apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a written Tax Exemption Certificate to evidence its exempt status to foreign banking and payment intermediaries; no hybrid mismatch, US check-the-box, or entity re-classification election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 30,000 (SAT 30,000) of taxable supplies in the preceding 12 months; a person making supplies below the threshold may voluntarily register; non-resident suppliers of imported digital services to Samoan consumers are subject to the VAGST and must register at any level once their supplies to Samoa exceed nil threshold under the reverse-charge/imported-services rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under the VAGST Act 2007 for (i) goods physically exported from Samoa within 60 days of supply and evidenced by customs documentation; and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply and where the services are not performed in connection with land or goods situated in Samoa and are not used in Samoa. A Samoa International Company providing no-code website builder / SaaS services to foreign customers may zero-rate the export of those services where the recipient belongs in a foreign country and the supply is not directly used in Samoa. Offshore service income of an IC remains exempt from Samoan income tax; input VAT credits and refunds may be claimed for VAT incurred on local purchases attributable exclusively to exported supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (lodged electronically with the Samoa Customs Service and duly assessed/accepted, with customs acknowledgment)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or original bank credit advice showing receipt of foreign exchange proceeds from the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice, purchase order or service contract, and for services a statement of use outside Samoa
  • Certificate of Origin (if required by destination country, for goods exports)

No mandatory real-time electronic invoicing, continuous transaction control (CTC) clearance, fiscal API, or digital stamping regime is currently in force for Samoa. Taxpayers must issue compliant tax invoices (within 28 days of supply) and retain electronic records; VAGST returns are filed electronically or on paper with the Ministry for Revenue, and the Ministry conducts risk-based post-clearance audits rather than pre-clearance invoice certification.

The Commissioner for Revenue has broad audit and reassessment powers. Zero-rated export claims that fail audit substantiation are clawed back with VAGST assessed at the standard rate of 15% plus interest from the date the refund was paid. Fraudulent export zero-rating or refund claims constitute tax evasion under the Tax Administration Act 2012 and the VAGST Act 2007, attracting penalties of up to 150% of the tax evaded, additional fines up to WST 100,000 per indictment, imprisonment for up to 7 years, and cancellation of the IC tax-exempt status. Directors and registered agents are jointly and severally liable where the IC fails to maintain proper export records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa Tala (WST) is convertible on the current account for trade in goods and services, remittances of profits, dividends, interest, and intra-group contract payments, all routed through authorised dealers. Capital-account transactions are comparatively restricted and require case-by-case Central Bank of Samoa approval. The WS_IC, being an International Company with a 100% offshore revenue model and no Samoan source of income, is permitted to hold foreign-currency (USD, EUR, CNH, AUD) accounts and to remit profits and capital to non-resident shareholders without forced conversion into WST. Convertibility hurdles arise only where the IC books local Samoan revenue or receives cash directly in Samoa without a documented underlying software subscription contract, in which case CBS/FIU attribution scrutiny and WST conversion requirements could apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters, the Exchange Control regime requires export proceeds to be collected within 180 days from the date of shipment and surrendered to an authorised dealer for conversion into Samoa Tala at the prevailing market rate. Licensed multi-currency retention accounts may be approved by the Central Bank of Samoa for exporters with recurring offshore receipts. For the WS_IC operating as a no-code website builder and SaaS platform, export receipts constitute offshore-source service income derived through Stripe and Paddle; the Central Bank of Samoa does not impose a mandatory surrender ratio (0% conversion obligation) where the funds are credited to an approved foreign-currency account of an International Company and supported by invoice and merchant settlement evidence for cross-border digital services, provided intra-group remittances and retained balances remain traceable for AML/CFT purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), which administers exchange control under the Exchange Control Ordinance 1978 and supervises all licensed commercial banks in Samoa as authorised dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI providers are neither licensed nor supervised by the Central Bank of Samoa; consequently, the WS_IC cannot use an unregulated EMI as a registered bank of record for CBS reporting. Feasibility is conditionally viable: Wise, Airwallex, and similar regulated EMI/PSP rails may be used in parallel for Stripe/Paddle payout aggregation and foreign contractor disbursements, provided the EMI is properly licensed in an FATF-compliant jurisdiction and all underlying commercial documentation is mirrored to the Samoan bank. Legal constraint: the Samoa FIU and the sponsoring bank will treat the EMI as a third-party payment intermediary only, never as a substitute for an in-Samoa corporate account, and the IC must ensure that EMI activities do not create an unintended Samoa tax-residency or money-services-business exposure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital is injected via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own account at a reputable bank and credited to the WS_IC's USD operational account at ANZ Bank Samoa Limited. The remittance instruction must state the IC registration number and a descriptive purpose such as 'Equity Contribution — Subscription to Shares' with SWIFT purpose category SUBS; the receiving bank maps the credit to the Central Bank of Samoa exchange-control category for offshore equity capital. Verification documents include the certified board resolution authorising the share issuance, a shareholder subscription agreement, recent account statements evidencing source of funds, and a confirmation from the Samoa registered agent that no minimum paid-in capital requirement applies to the International Company. There is no CBS pre-approval for inbound foreign-currency equity where the funded entity is an International Company and the credit remains in a foreign-currency account.Trade and operating disbursements settle primarily by SWIFT MT103 for software licences, cloud infrastructure, contractor payroll, and distributor commissions. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for International Companies: dividends and other profit distributions paid by a Samoan IC are statutorily exempt from withholding tax under s.198 of the International Companies Act 1987. For taxed resident companies, the domestic final withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents is 15%; applicable DTA rates may reduce this to 5% or 15% depending on shareholding thresholds.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within three months after each anniversary date of incorporation; the annual fee payment is due on the incorporation anniversary date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within three months after the end of the income year, which is the calendar year unless a substituted year is approved; therefore generally due by 31 March. Provisional tax installments are paid quarterly.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A transfer pricing documentation obligation in the form of a Local File, and a Master File where the group exceeds the reporting threshold, arises when the aggregate value of cross-border related-party transactions exceeds SAT 250,000 (approximately USD 94,000) in an income year. Country-by-Country reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Samoa applies the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices substance standard: an IC claiming the statutory tax exemption must demonstrate management and control in Samoa, adequate physical presence, employee capacity, and local expenditure proportionate to its core income-generating activities (CIGA), or must establish genuine foreign tax residence. Pure intellectual-property holding and conduit licensing arrangements are rebutted under the modified nexus approach; accordingly, the target no-code website builder monetizing via Stripe and Paddle must maintain either real Samoan substance or a verifiable foreign tax residence to remain defensible.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Sham Structure

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of undeclared Samoan-source income on a permanent-establishment basis; 100% evasion penalty; late payment interest; strike-off of the company; criminal fine up to SAT 100,000; confiscation of misapplied subscription funds.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and civil restitution; the Samoa Revenue Authority may issue departure prohibition orders (travel bans) against directors pending settlement, and convicted individuals face imprisonment of up to three years.
Shell Company and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Accrual of the 10% monthly late-fee surcharge; administrative strike-off and dissolution of the IC without a court restoration order; fines for beneficial-ownership register violations; refusal of certificate of good standing.

Director personal liability: Directors attract personal fines, disqualification from managing any Samoan company, and joint and several liability to creditors; nominee directors who conceal the principal are personally liable for resultant damages and may be criminally prosecuted.
AML and Sanctions Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Fines up to SAT 1,000,000; revocation of the licensed registered agent's authorization; freezing of balances held in Samoan banks; seizure of platform assets; exclusion from international payment rails.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal liability and imprisonment of up to seven years for money-laundering facilitation; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed, with extradition exposure to foreign enforcement authorities.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Repatriation Abuse

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length recharacterization; disallowance of excessive deductions; additional tax at 27%; penalty equal to 50% to 100% of the understated tax; statutory interest from the original due date.

Director personal liability: Directors approving non-commercial intercompany arrangements bear civil restitution liability for the shortfall; artificial pricing schemes expose them to fraudulent-evasion prosecution and personal asset seizure.
Economic Substance Protocol Violation

Statutory sanction: Retroactive revocation of the s.198 exemption; reassessment of all income at 27%; penalties up to SAT 500,000; mandatory removal from the International Companies Register with loss of legal personality.

Director personal liability: Directors incur joint and several liability for the reassessed tax and penalties; disqualification orders and departure bans are available to compel settlement of the company's tax debts.
