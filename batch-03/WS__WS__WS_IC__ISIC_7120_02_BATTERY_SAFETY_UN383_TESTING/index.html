Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Government of Samoa (Inland Revenue and Samoa Customs Service); official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) obtains its statutory identifier as follows: (1) incorporate under the International Companies Act 1987 via the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) as registrar; (2) automatically acquire a Certificate of Incorporation, which carves out the statutory tax-exempt status; (3) where the IC nevertheless undertakes local economic activity, apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) using the prescribed registration form and supporting constitutional documents; (4) register for VAGST separately if annual taxable turnover reaches or exceeds the statutory threshold. For purely exempt offshore holding/testing-service entities, a TIN is required only for filing or voluntary compliance purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Samoan law. International Companies are automatically and perpetually exempt from income tax and stamp duty under the International Companies Act 1987. A voluntary VAGST registration election is available for businesses below the compulsory threshold. No hybrid-mismatch or deemed-partnership elective regimes have been enacted that would apply to an IC.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (a) goods physically exported from Samoa, and (b) services supplied to a non-resident recipient for use entirely outside Samoa, including laboratory testing services such as UN38.3 battery safety testing and CE-RED electromagnetic testing, provided the supplier retains contemporaneous documentation proving the export or the offshore delivery of the service.

  • Samoa Customs Export Declaration (CusDec)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Commercial invoice and VAGST zero-rated tax invoice
  • Signed service contract / testing work order showing the overseas recipient and offshore enjoyment where services are zero-rated
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Samoan licensed bank evidencing receipt of the foreign consideration
  • Test certificates / laboratory reports confirming performance of the testing services in Samoa

None - Samoa does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, government e-invoicing APIs, or digital stamping. Taxpayers must issue VAGST tax invoices containing prescribed particulars, retain full accounting and source documentation, and file periodic returns through the Ministry for Revenue's tax portal or prescribed filing channels.

The Tax Administration Act 2012 and the Goods and Services Tax Act 2008 empower the Ministry for Revenue to audit zero-rating claims retrospectively; failure to substantiate export or offshore-service status within the statutory record-keeping period exposes the taxpayer to clawback of the zero-rated input tax credits, shortfall penalties of up to 150% of the tax shortfall, statutory interest, and criminal prosecution with fines up to WS$500,000 and/or imprisonment for fraudulent or reckless conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is materially convertible for current international transactions under Samoa's IMF Article VIII commitments. Capital account transactions, including non-resident borrowing, portfolio investment, and physical currency movement, require prior CBS approval or an exemption certificate. Non-resident International Companies (ICs) holding approved foreign-currency accounts may transfer capital and dividends freely, provided the funds do not enter the local WST banking system except through an authorized dealer. Profit remittance from a Samoan-source business requires a tax clearance certificate from the Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), audited financial statements, and evidence of settlement of annual international company fees.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export receipts from goods and services must be repatriated to Samoa within 30 days from the invoice date, unless CBS grants an exemption on justified commercial grounds. Receipts into a resident operating account are subject to a statutory 25% mandatory conversion to WST at an authorized dealer, with the balance retainable in foreign currency. An International Company licensed as non-resident and maintaining a designated offshore foreign-currency account is exempt from the mandatory conversion ratio and may retain 100% of export proceeds in USD, EUR, or AUD, subject to quarterly submission of export proceeds declarations to CBS.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) — The sole monetary, financial, and exchange-control authority for Samoa, administering the Exchange Control Act, the Central Bank of Samoa Act, and the Money Laundering Prevention Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW as a primary clearing rail; MEDIUM as a supplementary disbursement channel. Samoa does not maintain a fintech e-money institution (EMI) or digital-bank licensing framework; therefore Wise, Revolut, and similar offshore EMIs cannot hold a Central Bank settlement account in Samoa or clear WST independently. Such institutions must route through a licensed Samoan commercial bank under an agency or white-label arrangement, and any money service activity directed at Samoan residents triggers CBS registration, AML/CFT obligations, and Financial Intelligence Unit reporting. Consequently, the WS_IC may use Wise for low-value operational disbursements via visa-Mastercard virtual cards or remittance to supplier banks, but it must maintain a conventional correspondent banking tier for all regulated treasury transactions.

The remitting bank must be an LEI-registered financial institution; the remitter's source of funds must be pre-verified by the Samoan receiving bank. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory monetary threshold in Samoan Tala (WST) is prescribed for Local File or Master File; the Income Tax Act 2012 applies the arm's length principle to all related-party dealings of resident taxpayers, and the SRA may demand contemporaneous documentation on audit. Exempt ICs are outside the CIT net but must still substantiate cross-border intercompany pricing; a practical internal threshold of WST 1,000,000 of related-party turnover is recommended for maintaining a full TP file

Economic substance scope: Samoa does not maintain OECD/EU-style Economic Substance Regulations for International Companies; the ICA 1987 exemption is preserved only if the IC (i) does not derive Samoan-source income, (ii) maintains a registered office and licensed registered agent in Samoa, (iii) holds current registers of members, directors, and beneficial owners at the registered office, and (iv) pays the annual IC fee. Foreign shareholders remain subject to home-jurisdiction CFC, controlled-company, and Pillar Two rules; Samoa was removed from the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions in October 2023 and maintains TIEAs for information exchange

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Misuse of International Company Tax Exemption (de facto Shell Company with Samoan-source activity)

Statutory sanction: Loss of exempt status retrospectively; assessment at 27% CIT on all Samoan-source income; shortfall penalty of up to 200% of the tax avoided; monthly late-payment surcharge of 1.5%; cancellation of the IC by the Registrar of International Companies

Director personal liability: Directors who certified false exempt status are jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid tax and penalties, may be disqualified from acting as directors, subjected to travel bans, and prosecuted for tax evasion carrying imprisonment up to 5 years
Tax Evasion / False or Misleading Declarations

Statutory sanction: Shortfall penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded plus interest at 1.5% per month; criminal fines up to 200 penalty units (WST 20,000) and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; asset seizure and bank account freezing under the Tax Administration Act 2012

Director personal liability: Corporate veil may be pierced for fraud: directors and the registered agent are jointly and severally liable for evaded tax and penalties; professional disqualification, international travel bans, and custodial sentences apply
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 (as amended): fines up to WST 1,000,000 for bodies corporate and WST 500,000 for individuals; forfeiture orders; freezing of bank accounts; deregistration of the IC and revocation of the agent's license

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly involved in money laundering face imprisonment up to 14 years, travel bans, confiscation of personal assets, and personal liability for the laundered amounts
Director Governance Failure / Trading While Struck Off

Statutory sanction: Registrar strike-off; corporate bank account frozen; contracts entered after strike-off are void; restoration requires payment of all arrears, reinstatement fees, and SRA compliance clearances

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for all debts incurred while the company was struck off; disqualification orders prevent acting as a director of any Samoa company; continued trading after strike-off is a criminal offence punishable by fines and imprisonment
