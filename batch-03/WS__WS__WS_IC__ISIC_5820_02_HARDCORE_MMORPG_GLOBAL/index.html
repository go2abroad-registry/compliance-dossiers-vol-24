Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue – Samoa, located at Level 2, FMFM II Building, Sogi, Apia; official tax portal: www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

For a Samoa International Company (IC) that is tax-exempt, no standard TIN or VAT registration is required automatically. However, the IC must apply to the Ministry of Revenue for a Tax Exemption Certificate (TEC) using Form TEC-1. This requires submission of the Certificate of Incorporation, the IC's registered office address, and a sworn declaration that no Samoa‐source revenue will be derived. Upon approval, the Ministry issues a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) in a zero‐rated 'exempt' category, which must be quoted in any cross‐border contractual documentation. For ICs that later revoke the exemption, the standard corporate tax registration procedure applies via the Ministry of Revenue's online portal with submission of the Companies Registry extract and VAT registration application if applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended), an IC may irrevocably elect to be treated as a resident company under the Companies Act 2001. This election is filed with the Ministry of Revenue and the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) and prospectively subjects the company to Samoa corporate income tax (27% effective headline rate) and VAT, thereby terminating the exempt status. No 'check-the-box' or hybrid mismatch elections are available within Samoa's domestic tax law, but standard anti-hybrid rules under the International Companies (Tax) Amendment Act 2021 apply to prevent double non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (annual taxable turnover) for goods/services supplied in Samoa; a non-resident IC is generally outside the scope of VAT for offshore digital services, but if it derives any Samoa-sourced income (e.g., local app store sales) it would be required to register and charge VAT on those supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are zero-rated under section 12 of the VAT Act, including digital services supplied to non-residents (MMORPG gameplay subscriptions, in-app purchases, virtual items). The IC must demonstrate that the recipient is a non-Samoan resident, that the service is performed and consumed outside Samoa, and that the IC retains conclusive evidence of the supply being made in the ordinary course of business. For payments processed by Apple App Store and Google Play, the 'agent/aggregator' model applies: the platform is the supplier to the end user, and the IC's settlement receipts from the platform are treated as consideration for zero-rated supply to the platform (a non-resident).

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form SDF-7) endorsed by the Samoa Customs Service for physical exports (if any)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for physical goods) or digital delivery log (server-side records, IP logs, platform settlement reports)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or App Store/Google Pay settlement statement showing USD/WST transfer from the platform provider to the IC's bank account
  • Commercial contract between IC and Apple/Google, plus an annual zero-rated supply declaration (VAT Form ZRD-1)

Samoa does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance or continuous transaction control system. VAT invoices must be sequentially numbered and contain mandatory fields (seller name/TIN, buyer TIN, description, VAT rate). The Ministry of Revenue accepts electronic invoices (PDF/XMl) for audit purposes, but no government API or digital stamping is mandated. Approval of batch e-invoices is required for large exporters under the VAT Compliance Program.

The Ministry of Revenue may retrospectively assess VAT and impose penalties for improper zero-rating. Penalties include a late payment surcharge of 25% of the unpaid tax per annum, an additional negligence penalty of up to 200% of the evaded VAT, and criminal sanction for intentional misdeclaration (fine not exceeding WST 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 3 years). In cases of fraudulent export schemes, the IC's tax-exempt certificate may be revoked retroactively, and all previously zero-rated supplies will be reclassified as taxable, with interest from the original supply date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a managed-float currency with shallow offshore liquidity and no active offshore deliverable market. Resident enterprises are subject to comprehensive exchange control: current-account payments in foreign currency must be executed through an authorized dealer, individual remittances above the CBS declaration/approval threshold (typically WST 200,000 or equivalent) require regulatory declaration or prior approval, and all capital-account transactions require prior CBS consent. A licensed International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 is statutorily carved out of the exchange-control perimeter and is treated as non-resident: it may invoice, hold, borrow, and distribute in freely usable foreign currency (USD, EUR, HKD, SGD) without any CBS conversion mandate, and profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted. The predominant practical hurdle is therefore not legal convertibility but correspondent-bank risk appetite toward Pacific offshore vehicles, mitigable by maintaining SIFA good-standing certificates, audited substantive gaming operations, and live Apple App Store / Google Play publishing contracts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For ordinary resident exporters, the statutory surrender rules require all export proceeds to be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days of shipment or earnings, offered for conversion into WST through an authorized dealer, and held in a CBS-approved Foreign Currency Retention Account where applicable; set-off against offshore obligations without CBS approval is prohibited. For the WS_IC licensed International Company, no surrender rule and no mandatory conversion ratio apply: the entity may retain 100% of Apple App Store and Google Play USD payout proceeds in offshore or on-island USD accounts, entirely outside the Exchange Control Act 2015 surrender regime.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) as administrator of the Exchange Control Act 2015, the Exchange Control Regulations 2016, and the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2021, with delegated enforcement authority vested in licensed authorized dealer banks.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The IC annual return must be filed within 6 months after the end of the fiscal year. The income tax return follows the same deadline: by 31 December for a 30 June year-end, or by 30 June for a 31 December year-end. A tax-exempt IC must still file an annual return to evidence continued exemption.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WS$1,000,000 (approx USD $400,000) annual related-party turnover for the Local File and Master File; any cross-border related-party transaction above WS$100,000 also triggers simplified documentation. In practice, the Samoa MOR adopts OECD TP Guidelines; the IC must prepare contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation if the total value of related-party transactions exceeds WS$1,000,000 per annum.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Rules apply to International Companies under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2018 and the International Companies (Economic Substance) Rules 2021. An IC engaged in geographically mobile business (including digital and software/gaming publishing) must demonstrably exercise economic substance in Samoa: substantive management and control (board meetings in Samoa), adequate physical presence (registered office and local employees), and core income-generating activities performed in Samoa. Failure to do so results in denial of tax-exempt status, imposition of 27% tax on worldwide income from the mobile business, and deregistration penalties. For a hardcore MMORPG global operator, the royalty/software publishing income must be supported by a substance report showing actual decision-making, local IT staff, and active exploitation of the IP; a mere mailbox office is fatal.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Offshore Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Denial of the tax exemption certificate; retroactive taxation of all worldwide mobile-business income at 27%; penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded; criminal fine up to WS$100,000 for the company.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly sign false declarations are personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; they may be subject to civil restitution orders, travel bans by the Supreme Court of Samoa, and imprisonment up to 5 years for tax evasion.
Transfer Pricing Violation

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment to arm's length, imposition of 27% tax on the adjusted profit, a TP penalty of 20%–50% of the adjusted amount, and late payment interest at 2% per month.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or sign TP returns knowing they are not arm's length may be jointly and severally liable for adjusted taxes and penalties; flagrant cases attract criminal liability and disqualification from acting as a director of any Samoan IC.
Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Removal from the International Companies Register; revocation of the tax exemption certificate; a non-compliance penalty of WS$100,000; and possible court-ordered dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined WS$50,000 each, subjected to travel bans, and ordered to compensate the revenue loss by the Supreme Court. The corporate veil is pierced under the International Companies Act 2017 when the company is used for fraud.
AML / Financial Sanctions (Beneficial Ownership Disclosure)

Statutory sanction: Corporate fine up to WS$250,000; disqualification of the registered agent; severe breaches lead to asset freezing by the FIU and prohibition from receiving Apple/Google payouts because payment intermediaries require compliance certifications.

Director personal liability: Individual directors and ultimate beneficial owners face imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering and failing to report beneficial ownership; personal asset seizure under the Proceeds of Crime Act; travel bans are routinely imposed.
