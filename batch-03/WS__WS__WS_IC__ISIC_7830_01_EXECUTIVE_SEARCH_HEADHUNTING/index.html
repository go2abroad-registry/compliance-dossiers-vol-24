Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue (Matagaluega o Tupe Maua), Apia, Samoa; official online portal and e-Services platform at https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned Samoa International Company (IC) engaged in executive search and headhunting: (1) appoint a licensed registered agent in Samoa pursuant to the International Companies Act 2007; (2) file the memorandum and articles of association with the Registrar of International Companies, pay the applicable incorporation fee, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (3) upon incorporation, the registered agent submits the IC particulars via the Ministry of Revenue e-Services portal to obtain the company Tax Identification Number (TIN), which is the single statutory identifier for tax correspondence and, if required, VAGST registration; (4) because the IC's offshore business falls within the tax-exempt zone, no income tax registration return is required and the IC instead maintains its annual government licence fee; (5) if the IC makes any taxable supply in Samoa, the same TIN is used for the VAGST application and separate VAT ID is not issued. A board resolution authorising the agent and a certified copy of the certificate of incorporation are retained in the statutory records.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a non-resident-owned Samoa International Company (IC) engaged in executive search and headhunting: (1) appoint a licensed registered agent in Samoa pursuant to the International Companies Act 2007; (2) file the memorandum and articles of association with the Registrar of International Companies, pay the applicable incorporation fee, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (3) upon incorporation, the registered agent submits the IC particulars via the Ministry of Revenue e-Services portal to obtain the company Tax Identification Number (TIN), which is the single statutory identifier for tax correspondence and, if required, VAGST registration; (4) because the IC's offshore business falls within the tax-exempt zone, no income tax registration return is required and the IC instead maintains its annual government licence fee; (5) if the IC makes any taxable supply in Samoa, the same TIN is used for the VAGST application and separate VAT ID is not issued. A board resolution authorising the agent and a certified copy of the certificate of incorporation are retained in the statutory records.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a US-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election. The IC is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax by operation of the International Companies Act 2007; therefore no entity classification election is necessary. A registered agent may elect voluntary VAGST registration under the VAGST Act 2005 for an otherwise exempt offshore IC in order to recover input VAGST on local administrative expenses, subject to Ministry of Revenue approval. An IC may also elect to redomicile, continue, or convert to an ordinary Samoan company where it ceases to meet the offshore exemption conditions; such an election triggers ordinary resident company taxation and must be filed through the Ministry of Revenue.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 75,000 (Samoan Tala) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available below the threshold. Currency ISO code: WST

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and qualifying supplies of services are zero-rated under the VAGST Act 2005. For executive search and headhunting services rendered by a Samoa IC to offshore corporate clients, the IC may apply the 0% rate only where: (a) the recipient of the supply is a non-resident not physically present in Samoa at the time of supply; (b) the headhunting service is not performed by way of on-shore secondment or executive placement within Samoa's domestic labour market; (c) the CV sourcing, candidate interviewing, market research, and negotiation support are conducted offshore or through offshore communication channels; (d) the rights and economic benefits of the supply are enjoyed outside Samoa; and (e) the IC holds contemporaneous documentary evidence of the offshore supply chain. Because the IC operates within Samoa's offshore tax-exempt zone and is excluded from Samoan exchange control rules, retention and success-fee payments may be received in any freely convertible currency into the IC's offshore account without Central Bank of Samoa approval; no mandatory conversion of foreign currency into Samoan Tala is required.

  • Signed executive search or headhunting retainer agreement naming the non-resident corporate client and the offshore roles covered
  • Detailed VAGST tax invoice and statement of account showing the supply as an export of services and applying the 0% rate
  • Evidence of cross-border fee settlement, including SWIFT MT103 payment message or Wise B2B transaction record, matching the invoiced retainer and success fee
  • Substantiation of offshore place of supply and place of use, including interview locations, video-meeting records, candidate database access logs, and offshore email or correspondence files
  • Internal zero-rated supplies schedule reconciling the VAGST return to underlying contracts and remittance evidence; a physical customs export declaration is not applicable to intangible headhunting services

Samoa has no mandatory real-time e-invoicing, no government fiscal clearance platform, and no digital stamping requirement. VAGST-registered suppliers may issue paper or electronic tax invoices; the Ministry of Revenue requires only that invoices contain the prescribed particulars under the VAGST Act 2005 and that returns and TIN applications are filed electronically through the Ministry's online e-Services portal. API integration with the tax authority is optional and not a precondition for claiming output tax credit or zero-rating.

The Ministry of Revenue may audit any zero-rated export supply and require the entire zero-rating substantiation bundle to be produced on demand. Where export zero-rating is disallowed due to missing non-resident evidence, the VAT output tax becomes immediately recoverable with the zero-rated input credits clawed back plus default interest. Fraudulent zero-rating, false invoices, or deliberate mis-statement of the place of supply attract civil shortfall penalties of up to 100% of the evaded tax, increased to 200% for a second or deliberate offence, monetary fines of up to WST 50,000 per offence, and imprisonment for up to three years under the Tax Administration Act 2015 and the VAGST Act 2005. The Commissioner may also issue retrospective assessments outside the ordinary limitation period where evasion is established and may issue a direction freezing retainer, success-fee, or commission assets held on behalf of the IC to secure the clawback.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a CBS-managed float; current-account transactions (cross-border service fees, executive-search retainers) are freely convertible subject to documentary verification by the instructing local bank. Capital-account transactions (share capital injections, dividend repatriations) by an International Company are exempt from prior CBS approval under the ICA 1987, provided the transaction is recorded on CBS Form ECM/ICA disclosures; profit remittance is reportedly tax-free given the IC zero-tax status, but an onshore correspondent bank may impose an enhanced EDD review on high-value WST/foreign-currency conversions. Repatriation of non-resident IC earnings is structurally liberalized, yet banks routinely require an IC certificate of status plus a certificate from the registered agent confirming the beneficial owners are non-residents of Samoa.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa does not impose a statutorily binding surrender requirement on service exporters; the Exchange Control Regulations require repatriation of export proceeds to a licensed Samoan bank within 180 days from invoice date, failing which the accumulated shortfall must be surrendered to a commercial bank at the prevailing exchange rate. For International Companies, the Central Bank exempts proceeds routed to an offshore IC account held in a foreign currency; when such proceeds are brought onshore, the bank will enforce a 100% monetization upon conversion consistent with general surrender practice. Executive-search retainers and placement fees should be evidenced via signed client engagement agreements and dated invoices to avoid adverse classification as undeclared capital receipts.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984; exchange control powers delegated under the Exchange Control Act 1978 and Exchange Control Regulations 1984 (Samoa). The International Companies Act 1987 (as amended) expressly disapplies most exchange-control approval requirements for International Companies, substituting a lighter compliance reporting regime where funds are channelled through onshore Samoan banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: CONDITIONAL / LOW for primary treasury rails. Wise (TransferWise) B2B is legally usable only as a supplementary collection/disbursement rail for low-value retainer receipts (e.g., sub-USD 10,000 from headhunted clients) and employee expense reimbursements; it is not a licensed depository institution in Samoa and cannot settle payroll into Samoan bank accounts directly. Samoan FX control does not recognize EMIs as acceptance-parties for mandatory export-proceeds repatriation; therefore Wise balances must be regularly swept to a licensed Samoan commercial bank to preserve IC compliance status. The Central Bank of Samoa has no EMI license class; any persistent routing of large retainers through offshore EMIs without a local bank intermediary would be deemed a capital-account circumvention.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire routing: originating bank transmits SWIFT MT103 to the Samoan beneficiary bank; beneficiary bank credits the IC operational account. The remitter must state in Field 70 the purpose code 'Capital Injection / Shareholder Loan' with a supporting purpose narrative; CBS regulation requires the beneficiary bank to obtain a certified copy of the IC shareholder resolution and register of members before effecting credit. Where funds originate from greater-China family trusts or the mainland, the remitter should add a remittance remark matching the China SAFE 'other investment/capital' category; no separate CBS pre-approval is required for the IC, but the bank will file a CBS exchange-control report. For retained service-fee revenue (retainers), Field 70 must read 'Service fees – executive search retainer' plus the client engagement number; clean same-day clearing is achieved if funds arrive before the bank's wire cut-off (12:00 noon Apia time) and are not at-risk of OFAC/sanctions screening.Executive search disbursements (post-placement bonuses, contractor payroll, referral incentives) settle via SWIFT MT103 from the Samoan IC's account to the beneficiary's bank; Field 71A 'OUR' cost arrangement is recommended for executive candidates' cross-border tips; internet-banking-initiated wires over WST 50,000 require a redundant FM-check by the bank's foreign-exchange desk. For disbursements to subcontractor headhunters in tier-1 global hubs, a SWIFT MT202 COV for interbank cover may be triggered when the beneficiary bank instructs an intermediary; the IC must store the invoice plus placement agreement for five years. Commercial letters of credit (documentary LCs) are not practical for professional service payments, but the bank can issue a standby LC to a client-side procurement portal on request; the cost for a WST 250,000 standby LC is approximately 0.75–1.2% p.a. payable in advance.Deliverable outright forwards (up to 12 months tenor) with licensed Samoan commercial banks for USD-WST conversion of retainer pipelines based on invoiced and un-invoiced committed receivables; CBS notification required above WST 1 million notional.FX options (European-style, medium-tenor) purchased outright (no premium-selling) for USD-WST protecting against sharp tala depreciation in periods of fiscal stress; requires a signed risk-acknowledgement letter with the bank.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) only to swap USD fixed-rate receivables into WST-linked exposures if the IC maintains debt in Samoan banks; requires prior CBS regulatory consent; the IC's zero-tax status means hedging gains are not taxable.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in WST are not commonly supported by local Samoan banks; if the bank's offshore parent operator offers NDFs, they are permissible only when the ultimate settlement is in USD against the WST fix, subject to a bona-fide commercial hedge test and CBS reporting.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% (ordinary resident Samoa companies); effective 0% for International Companies exempt under the International Companies Act 1987

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% for dividends paid by ordinary resident Samoa companies to non-residents; 0% for dividends and distributions paid by an exempt International Company

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Resident companies: filing due within 6 months after the accounting year-end, with provisional tax installments due quarterly; exempt International Companies: annual government fee and substance declaration due within 1 month of the incorporation anniversary

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No codified Samoa Local File/Master File statutory threshold; revenue practice benchmark is SAT 2,000,000 of cross-border related-party turnover for resident taxpayers; tax-exempt International Companies have no transfer pricing documentation obligation

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations require International Companies to demonstrate economic substance proportionate to their activities: a physical office or registered agent in Samoa, adequate employees and qualifying expenditure in Samoa, board meetings held with quorum in Samoa, and annual substance reporting; failure to meet the doctrine results in financial penalties, strike-off, and potential retrospective taxation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Arrangements

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 200% of the understated tax plus default interest; criminal prosecution under the Samoa Tax Administration Act exposing fines up to SAT 200,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years; the exemption is void and tax is assessed at 27% with penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors may have the corporate veil pierced for fraudulent conduct; personal civil restitution of evaded tax, travel bans for unpaid assessments, asset restraint orders, and imprisonment in aggravated cases.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Fines for non-compliance with the Economic Substance Regulations, late fee penalties, deregistration and strike-off of the International Company, plus disqualification of the registered agent; reinstatement may be refused indefinitely.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid government fees and penalties; they face a bar from acting as directors in Samoa-controlled vehicles and adverse international reputational listings.
AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to SAT 1,000,000 per breach under the Samoa AML/CFT framework; suspension or revocation of the trust and company service provider license; seizure and civil forfeiture of the fee proceeds.

Director personal liability: Senior directors and compliance officers are individually prosecutable; money laundering convictions carry imprisonment up to 14 years, personal fines, and mandatory permanent travel bans when the offense involves cross-border wire transfers.
Unlicensed Employment Placement Business

Statutory sanction: Immediate business license revocation, cease-and-desist orders from the Labour and Commerce authorities, administrative fines per unlicensed transaction, and forced refund of client retainers.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for restitution of all collected fees, exclusion from re-licensing, criminal fraud conviction with custodial sentence, and asset freezing pending investigation.
