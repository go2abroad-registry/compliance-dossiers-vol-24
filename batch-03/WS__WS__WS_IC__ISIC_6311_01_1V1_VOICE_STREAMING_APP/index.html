Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR; Matagaluega o Tupe Maua) of the Independent State of Samoa, the consolidated tax and customs administration; taxpayer e-services portal: Samoa Revenue Online Services (SROS), www.revenue.gov.ws. The Samoa International Company Registry (Registrar of International and Foreign Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour) issues the IC registration number used for tax-exemption administration.

E-government filing portal

A pure offshore Samoa International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax and VAGST and is not required to obtain a TIN or VAGST ID; the IC registration number and annual Government fee receipt serve as the tax-identity bundle. If the IC creates a Samoan nexus (local branch, Samoan-source income, or taxable supplies to Samoan consumers), it must file the Ministry for Revenue business-registration application (Form T/R-1) via SROS or in person, attaching the IC certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles (or charter), registered-agent and registered-office confirmation, identification of directors and ultimate beneficial owners, and, if applicable, the resident agent undertaking. The Commissioner issues a TIN within 14-30 days, and VAGST registration is mandatory on the same application once a rolling 12-month taxable turnover reaches or is expected to reach WST 100,000. No minimum capital requirement applies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa has no check-the-box election. The IC's Tax_Exempt_Zone status is automatic under the International Companies Act 1987: exemption from income tax on offshore income, no capital gains tax, no stamp duty on offshore instruments, and no VAGST on non-Samoan supplies; the IC pays a fixed annual Government fee instead of income tax. The IC may elect (irrevocably) to re-domesticate to a company under the Companies Act 2001 or to obtain a licence to carry on business in Samoa, thereby entering the domestic income-tax and VAGST net for Samoan-source activities. No hybrid-mismatch election is recognised; the Tax Administration Act 2015 general anti-avoidance rule re-characterises hybrid or conduit arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (SAT 100,000) of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period; registration is compulsory once the threshold is met or expected to be met, with voluntary registration at the Commissioner's discretion. For the offshore voice-streaming IC, no threshold is triggered because the supplies of digital audio and companion services through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing are supplied for consumption entirely outside Samoa and no VAGST is chargeable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAGST) applies to: (i) goods removed physically from Samoa under an accepted Customs export entry; and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident recipient where the services are performed outside Samoa and not enjoyed in Samoa. The voice-streaming social app supplies remote digital services (interactive audio chatrooms and companion features) to non-resident end users through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing; these qualify as exported services if the supplier evidences non-residence of the recipient, consumption outside Samoa, settlement in foreign currency via the platform, and consistent commercial paperwork. The supplier must not recover input VAGST attributable to exempt supplies and must separately identify zero-rated exports in its VAGST return.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs Service export entry) for physical goods
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch of goods from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advices evidencing receipt of export proceeds in convertible foreign currency within the CBS repatriation period
  • Zero-rated tax invoice, commercial contract, and sales order
  • For digital services: Apple IAP and Google Play Billing settlement statements, monthly sales reports, and non-resident status evidence of the counterparty or end users (foreign business registration, foreign VAT or equivalent ID, or passport)

None: Samoa does not operate a real-time electronic-invoicing clearance, continuous transaction control, fiscal API, or digital stamping regime. Registered persons may issue paper or electronic tax invoices that satisfy the content rules (supplier name and TIN, recipient TIN, date, description, quantity, consideration, VAGST rate and amount), and commercial records must be retained for at least 7 years.

MfR audits of zero-rated digital-service exporters concentrate on commercial substance: platform settlement reports, subscriber and usage geo-location data, and foreign-currency receipts must independently corroborate each zero-rated supply. A disallowed zero-rating claim gives rise to an assessed VAGST clawback plus a shortfall penalty of 100% of the shortfall for deliberate evasion, 40% for gross carelessness, or 20% for failure to take reasonable care, with monthly late-payment interest. Fraudulent export zero-rating is a criminal offence under the Tax Administration Act 2015 (fines up to WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years), the Commissioner may raise a jeopardy assessment, the four-year reassessment window applies, and directors and beneficial owners of the IC may be jointly and severally assessed where the IC is a mere facade. Non-repatriation of export proceeds within 180 days additionally exposes the exporter to Central Bank of Samoa exchange-control penalties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

WST is non-convertible on capital account; current-account payments may be processed through authorized dealers with documentary support. The WS_IC earns all revenue offshore in USD (Apple IAP and Google Play Billing proceeds), keeps a foreign-currency account, and pays no Samoan taxes; therefore WST convertibility and profit-remittance controls are structurally avoided. If the IC were a resident company, distributions to non-residents would attract dividend withholding tax under the Income Tax Act 2012 and CBS capital-transfer approval; the International Company exemption eliminates both hurdles. Practical rule: ring-fence revenue in USD, never settle in WST, and maintain no Samoan-resident counterparties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation applies to the WS_IC. Samoa's exchange-control surrender rules target resident exporters of physical goods, who must repatriate export proceeds within the periods prescribed in CBS exchange-control guidelines for the relevant export class and offer conversion into WST at the applicable ratio. Digital-services income of a non-resident International Company is not 'Samoa export proceeds'; Apple/Google remit net USD directly to the IC's foreign-currency account with zero conversion requirement. To preserve this exemption, the IC must earn no Samoan-source income, hold no WST revenue, and direct all app-store payouts to its USD corporate account.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) is the sole exchange-control authority, acting under the Exchange Control Ordinance 1961 and CBS Exchange Control Notices/Manual issued to authorized dealers. The Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) separately administers the International Companies Act 1987, under which the WS_IC is incorporated as a non-resident, tax-exempt International Company. Transactions touching Samoan Tala (WST) must flow through CBS-authorized dealers; non-resident IC transactions in USD conducted entirely offshore are exempt from CBS approval.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW-MEDIUM — viable only as a secondary settlement rail, not as the primary payout account for Apple/Google. Samoa has no domestic EMI licensing regime; the IC must use offshore EMIs (e.g., Payoneer, Airwallex, PingPong, Wise Business). Most offshore EMIs classify Samoa International Companies as high-risk offshore vehicles and reject consumer social/voice-chat apps due to digital-goods AML exposure; where accepted, EMIs impose transaction caps, settlement holds, and UBO EDD equal to bank standards. Apple IAP and Google Play Billing payout engines prefer commercial-bank SWIFT beneficiaries, and an EMI account may be rejected at payout registration because EMIs cannot issue a conventional SWIFT BIC/IBAN in the entity's name. Recommendation: maintain a BSP Samoa USD account as the primary rail and use an EMI only for gateway settlement or regional contractor payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial capital should arrive as a single named USD wire from the UBO or a documented group financing entity, using SWIFT MT103 with purpose code for equity contribution/share capital. Protocol: (1) board resolution authorizing share issuance and designating the receiving account; (2) UBO completes lawful outbound filings in the home jurisdiction (for PRC residents, SAFE 37号文 registration or a permitted individual remittance within the annual quota — never split wires into structuring tranches); (3) loan/shareholder agreement evidencing arm's-length terms if debt; (4) the receiving bank obtains a Capital Introduction Letter, Certificate of Incorporation, certificate of incumbency, and source-of-funds file; (5) CBS reporting of the inward non-resident transfer is discharged by the bank at crediting, with funds posted in USD without WST conversion. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa's Income Tax Act 2015 requires documentation for cross-border related-party transactions exceeding WST 10 million per annum (approximately USD 3.6 million). In practice, this threshold rarely applies to a tax-exempt International Company unless it has claimed tax residency or operates a taxable presence in Samoa.

Economic substance scope: Samoa Economic Substance Regulations require International Companies that generate relevant core income to demonstrate adequate physical presence, employees, expenditure, and management in Samoa if they claim tax residency or treaty benefits. For a voice streaming and interactive social application, the relevant category is likely 'Provision of Other Core Income-Generating Activities' or telecommunication-type services; a purely non-resident-operated exempt IC may have no substance requirement but must not claim treaty residency.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Assessment of unpaid tax, penalties up to 150% of the tax shortfall, and daily late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: Knowing directors may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; criminal conviction may carry imprisonment and a travel ban.
Shell Company / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Striking-off of the International Company, revocation of registration, and monetary penalties imposed by SIFA.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from acting as officers in Samoa and personally fined for signing false declarations.
AML and Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Fines up to USD 100,000 per violation, asset freezing and forfeiture, account detention, and suspicious transaction reporting orders.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may face criminal prosecution with imprisonment of 3 to 7 years, travel restrictions, and personal civil asset recovery.
Annual Return and License Fee Default

Statutory sanction: Automatic striking-off from the International Companies Register; late restoration requires settlement of all arrears and penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable for outstanding license fees and penalties; administrative disqualification may also apply.
