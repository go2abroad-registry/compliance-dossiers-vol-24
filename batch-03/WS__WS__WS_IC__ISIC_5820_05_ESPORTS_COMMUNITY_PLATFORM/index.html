Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue, Government of Samoa (formerly Samoa Tax Office) – online portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is statutorily non-resident and exempt from Samoa income tax; it is not required to register for income tax purposes and does not require a general TIN. If the IC engages in import/export of physical goods or voluntarily seeks VAGST registration, it must submit a TIN/VAGST application (Form VAT 01) to the Ministry of Revenue accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered agent certificate, registered office particulars, and director/beneficial owner declarations. The Ministry then issues a 9-digit Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) typically within 3–5 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is statutorily non-resident and exempt from Samoa income tax; it is not required to register for income tax purposes and does not require a general TIN. If the IC engages in import/export of physical goods or voluntarily seeks VAGST registration, it must submit a TIN/VAGST application (Form VAT 01) to the Ministry of Revenue accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered agent certificate, registered office particulars, and director/beneficial owner declarations. The Ministry then issues a 9-digit Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) typically within 3–5 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Samoa. The IC is by law a non-resident company wholly exempt from Samoa income tax, stamp duty, and withholding tax on dividends, interest, and royalties derived outside Samoa. Optional elections available under the International Companies Act 1987 include: (1) election to register as a Limited Life Company; (2) election for continuation/redomiciliation into or out of Samoa; (3) election to convert to a domestic company (which would then become taxable). No hybrid mismatch or branch cash-opt elections apply because an IC is excluded entirely from the Samoa tax net.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WS$100,000 annual taxable turnover for mandatory VAGST registration (VAGST Act 2003). Voluntary registration is available at the Commissioner's discretion for businesses with turnover below WS$100,000; an IC conducting only offshore business is not normally required to register unless it makes taxable supplies in Samoa.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to: (1) goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply or such extended period approved by the Commissioner; (2) services supplied to a non-resident recipient where the services are performed wholly outside Samoa; (3) supplies to a licensed free-zone enterprise; and (4) supplies of certain internationally connected services. For the esports community platform, subscription, matchmaking, tournament entry, and advertising services supplied to non-resident users on offshore servers qualify for zero-rating if the IC is VAGST-registered and maintains adequate documentary evidence of non-residency and offshore consumption. An IC is otherwise not subject to Samoa tax on such offshore income.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Entry for Export / Electronic SADashba form as accepted by Samoa Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Commercial Invoice showing supplier TIN and VAGST zero-rated wording
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency
  • Copy of the underlying contract or service agreement for cross-border digital services
  • Esports platform transaction records including user jurisdiction, payment rails, tournament settlement logs, and server-location evidence

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based invoice clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Samoa. Registered VAGST suppliers must issue tax invoices containing supplier name and TIN, recipient name and address, invoice date, description of supply, quantity, unit price, and VAGST amount. Returns are filed electronically through the Ministry of Revenue Tax Administration System (TAS); no government pre-approval of individual invoices is required.

The Ministry of Revenue may audit zero-rated export claims for up to 7 years after the tax period. Where zero-rating is fraudulently or incorrectly claimed, the Commissioner may issue a default assessment for unpaid VAGST plus an additional penalty up to 150% of the tax evaded, monthly late-payment interest at 1.5%, and criminal fines not exceeding WS$100,000 (companies) and WS$50,000 (individuals) or up to 3 years' imprisonment under the Tax Administration Act 2012. Samoa Customs may also seize goods, suspend exporter approvals, and apply additional penalties under the Customs Act 2014 for false export declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is convertible for bona fide current-account transactions (trade, services, dividends, royalties) when routed through authorised dealers; capital-account transfers generally require Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) approval. A Samoa International Company (IC) is statutorily exempted from exchange control, permitting unrestricted maintenance and conversion of USD/WST external accounts without CBS consent. The principal operative hurdles are bank-level: documentary proof of source of funds, audited financials, UBO identification, CBS Balance-of-Payments reporting by the dealing bank, and FATCA/CRS classification of the IC as a non-financial foreign entity. Profit remittance from an IC to non-resident shareholders is free of exchange control and Samoan dividend withholding tax, subject to corporate good standing and bank due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must repatriate proceeds within 180 days of shipment and surrender 100% of export earnings to an authorised dealer within 30 days of receipt, unless a specific CBS exemption is obtained. Samoa International Companies are outside this regime because the International Companies Act 1987 disapplies the Exchange Control Act 1976; export or platform revenue can be retained 100% in USD and held indefinitely on external accounts. Any mislabelled resident trade payment will trigger mandatory surrender, penalties, and CBS compliance review.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), administering the Exchange Control Act 1976, Exchange Control Regulations 1976, and the Financial Institutions Act 2000 (as amended); the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) supervises international company registrations and licensing under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low for a Samoa-incorporated IC. Stripe does not support merchant settlement to Samoan entities; PayPal permits only limited personal cross-border transfers for Samoa and no full merchant settlement. Major global EMIs (Airwallex, Currenxie, Mercury, Wise, Revolut) consistently decline WS-incorporated companies under jurisdiction origination policy. Recommended structural solution: a US or HK/SG subsidiary owns the Stripe/PayPal merchant accounts and sweeps net settlement to the Samoa IC treasury via SWIFT MT103 intercompany loan or dividend flows, supported by transfer-pricing documentation and CRS/FATCA reporting.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% for resident domestic companies under the Income Tax Act 2012; International Companies (ICs) are wholly exempt and therefore subject to a 0% effective rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Domestic statutory rate under the Income Tax Act 2012 is 15% on dividends paid by a resident Samoan company. Dividends and other distributions paid by an International Company are statutorily exempt from Samoan withholding tax (0%), because IC distributions are outside the Samoan income tax net by force of the International Companies Act 1987.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the anniversary date of the certificate of incorporation in each calendar year; no statutory extension is available and the annual government fee is payable simultaneously.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months after the end of the IC's financial year (by 30 June for a 31 December year-end); the declaration is mandatory even for ICs that carry no relevant activity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 0 - no statutory threshold is prescribed in Samoa requiring contemporaneous Local File or Master File for International Companies. Domestic transfer pricing rules exist in the Income Tax Act 2012 but apply only to resident taxable companies. For the exempt IC, transfer pricing documentation is not filed; however, the ESR framework requires records demonstrating that related-party transactions uphold arm's length outcomes and substance, and such records must be produced on demand within the Samoan statutory inspection window.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Requirements apply under the International Companies (Amendment) Act 2018 and the International Companies (Economic Substance) Rules 2020. An IC carrying on any of the nine relevant activities - banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, distribution and service center, or intellectual property business - must be managed and controlled in Samoa, must maintain adequate employees, physical premises, and annual expenditure proportionate to its income, and must file Form IC-ESD1 within 6 months of the financial year end. A pure equity holding company enjoys a reduced substance test. The esports tournament platform is not itself a relevant activity when it serves independent third-party gamers; it becomes an 'intellectual property business' if the platform software is licensed to affiliated group entities, and a 'distribution and service center' if it provides group services. Failure to satisfy the ESR test exposes the IC to penalties, deregistration, and loss of the statutory tax exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - False Economic Substance Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fine up to WST 500,000 per infraction, daily default penalties up to WST 10,000, cancellation of the IC's registration, retroactive revocation of the International Companies Act tax exemption, and reassessment of the IC as a resident company at the 27% corporate tax rate on its worldwide income; referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit for money-laundering predicate offence investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors, the company secretary, and the licensed resident agent may be prosecuted individually; penalties include disqualification from holding office in any Samoan company, departure prohibition orders under the Tax Administration Act, personal fines, and imprisonment for wilful false declaration; the resident agent is jointly and severally liable for unpaid penalties arising from negligent certifications.
Shell Company Without Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The IC is declared non-compliant with the Economic Substance Requirements; the Registrar imposes the ESR penalty, may strike the company off the register, and notifies tax treaty partners and the Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information; the Samoan Revenue Authority may disregard the exemption and assess tax at 27% plus penalty tax of up to 100% of the underpaid amount.

Director personal liability: Strike-off causes directors to become personally liable for debts and contractual obligations of the IC contracted after dissolution, including prize-payout obligations to esports participants; directors may face restoration applications, travel bans pending investigation, and criminal liability for continuing to trade under a deregistered entity.
Anti-Money Laundering - Beneficial Ownership and Fund Flow Violation

Statutory sanction: Offence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter Financing of Terrorism Act 2018: fines up to WST 500,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years upon conviction; asset restraint and freezing orders; mandatory suspicious transaction reporting to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit; the IC's registration may be voided and its bank/payment processor relationships closed.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer are personally criminally liable for grossly negligent AML failures; penalties include imprisonment, disqualification from managing companies, property forfeiture, and international travel restrictions; foreign beneficial owners may face equivalent AML prosecution in their state of residence and extradition exposure.
Unauthorized Domestic Business and Foreign Place of Effective Management

Statutory sanction: Under Samoan law, the IC breaches the offshore-licence prohibition against domestic business; the exemption is revoked and the IC is taxed at 27% on all income, with penalty interest accruing from the date of the breach. In the foreign POEM jurisdiction, the IC may be resident and taxable on worldwide income, and CFC rules may attribute its retained profits to controlling shareholders, with understatement penalties of up to 200% of the tax avoided.

Director personal liability: Non-resident directors who knowingly direct the entity from a high-tax state may incur personal liability for the foreign entity's unpaid taxes under director penalty provisions; shareholders may face CFC inclusions; travel bans and director disqualification orders may be issued in both Samoa and the foreign enforcement jurisdiction.
Repeated Late Filing and Trading After Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: Automatic dissolution with immediate loss of legal personality; all assets of the IC vest in the Registrar; contracts purportedly made after dissolution are void; restoration is possible only by court order upon payment of arrears, penalties, and legal costs; the dissipated assets may be subject to creditor recovery proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for all obligations incurred in the IC's name after the date of dissolution, including tournament prize-pool distributions owed to gamers and contractual damages to sponsors; personal civil liability may extend to the full value of transactions during the unlawful trading window, alongside criminal fines for continued fraudulent trading.
