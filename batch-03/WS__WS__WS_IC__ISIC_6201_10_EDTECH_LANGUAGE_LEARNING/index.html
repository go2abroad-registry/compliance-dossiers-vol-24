Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue, Samoa (formerly Samoa International Financial Authority; online filing through the Ministry of Revenue Taxpayer Access Portal: https://tap.revenue.gov.ws)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 2016 must apply to the Ministry of Revenue for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a GST registration number (if required). The application must be filed by a licensed Samoa registered agent using Form IRD-002, including certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office certificate, particulars of directors and shareholders, contact information of beneficial owners, and expected activity declaration. The Ministry will issue a TIN within 10 business days; the IC is also required to obtain a Business License if any physical presence is established, but offshore-only ICs use the registered agent as the official local address for tax correspondence.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 2016 must apply to the Ministry of Revenue for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a GST registration number (if required). The application must be filed by a licensed Samoa registered agent using Form IRD-002, including certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office certificate, particulars of directors and shareholders, contact information of beneficial owners, and expected activity declaration. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An International Company is by default a tax-exempt entity under the International Companies Act 2016 and the Income Tax Act 2015. No check-the-box election exists; the exemption is mandatory provided the company derives only offshore income (non-domestic-source income). The IC may elect to be treated as a taxable domestic company by giving written notice to the Ministry of Revenue or by carrying on business within Samoa through a permanent establishment, thereby forgoing the tax exemption. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available; Samoa has no hybrid mismatch rules, and ICs cannot be treated as partnerships or disregarded entities. An IC can, however, elect in its Articles to become a 'Foreign Company' registered under the Companies Act 2006, but that terminates the IC tax-exempt regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 200,000 annual taxable turnover (compulsory registration); voluntary registration permitted with approval from the Ministry of Revenue

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are zero-rated under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1996 when provided to a person outside Samoa, or services that are performed outside Samoa and for direct use or enjoyment outside Samoa. An EdTech language-learning subscription supplied through Apple App Store or Google Play to non-resident consumers qualifies as an export of services, provided the customer's registered address, IP address, and bank/payment card location indicate non-Samoan residence. Digital services that are delivered to end-users in Samoa (including tourists temporarily in Samoa) are standard-rated at 15%. The IC may zero-rate all subscription sales if it contracts with the app store platform operator (Apple/Google) as the customer, treating the platform as a non-resident recipient; evidence must show the platform accepts the digital content offshore. No special digital export license is needed.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for physical goods or any bundled tangible components)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (where applicable to physical goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit statement showing foreign currency receipt for the exported supply
  • Non-resident customer declaration or export service contract
  • IP geolocation and platform sales reports from Apple/Google listing each transaction by country
  • GST zero-rated schedule reconciling aggregate subscription revenue to platform payout reports
  • Registered agent's declaration of export substantiation and any e-invoice file for delayed invoicing jurisdictions

Samoa has no mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping regime at this stage. VAT-registered suppliers must issue tax invoices containing the words 'tax invoice', seller's name and GST registration number, invoice date, description of supply, GST amount, and total consideration. Tax invoices may be issued electronically in PDF or machine-readable format. For exports, the supplier must issue a zero-rated tax invoice and retain evidence of export within 28 days after the supply; all GST filings are submitted via the TAP portal. The Ministry has adopted OECD global digital economy guidance, but no separate digital services tax or real-time transaction clearance applies to online sales by International Companies.

The Ministry of Revenue may apply a 150% shortfall penalty on any falsely zero-rated supply, plus interest at the prescribed rate from the date of the original refund, and may claw back refunds paid within the prior five years. For intentional evasion, penalties increase to 300%, with fines up to WST 25,000 and imprisonment up to seven years. Directors and the registered agent may be jointly and severally liable for GST clawbacks and penalties if they knowingly assisted in false export documentation. The Ministry may deregister a GST-registered IC and impose a 100% late filing penalty if evidence of export is not maintained for seven years.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa maintains a managed-float currency (Samoan Tala, WST) that is fully convertible for current-account transactions through authorized dealers, but residents remain subject to CBS exchange-control declarations and prior-approval gates for capital-account movements. A Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt from exchange-control restrictions under section 77 of the International Companies Act 1987, allowing it to hold, borrow, lend, and transact freely in any foreign currency without CBS consent. Dividend and profit remittances from the IC bear no Samoan withholding tax and require no repatriation clearance. Practical friction is therefore confined to resident operating entities and WST-denominated balances; the IC should adopt USD as its functional treasury currency so that Apple IAP/Google Play Billing settlements (USD/EUR) bypass the WST conversion layer entirely.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Samoan-resident trading companies, export proceeds must generally be repatriated to Samoa and surrendered through an authorized dealer within six months of export unless a CBS exemption is granted; this statutory surrender regime does not apply to a non-resident International Company because section 77 of the International Companies Act 1987 disapplies the Exchange Control Act 1976 to ICs. Accordingly, EdTech subscription revenue collected via app-store rails may remain offshore in USD/EUR wallets and is subject to neither mandatory WST conversion nor forced repatriation. Any service fees subsequently paid by the IC to a Samoan-resident operational parent would enter Samoa as current-account inflows carrying the standard authorized-dealer documentation trail and CBS reporting obligation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) — administering the Exchange Control Act 1976 and Exchange Control Regulations 1977 on behalf of the Minister of Finance; the international-company regulatory overlay is administered by the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) pursuant to the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-to-Low. Samoa has no domestic digital-bank or electronic-money-institution licensing regime, and offshore EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Mercury, Wise Business) legally may service a Samoa IC but routinely decline Pacific offshore entities due to jurisdiction risk and limited compliance appetite. Where an EMI is obtained, the IC must present its SIFA-licensed registered-agent introduction, certified certificate of incorporation, UBO certificate, and source-of-funds dossier through the provider's enterprise program. The resilient structure is anchor bank accounts at ANZ/BSP Samoa paired with a USD-linked EMI wallet used solely for high-frequency, small-ticket app-store fee disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital should be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from the settlor, trustee, or shareholder account to the IC's USD account at ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa. The wire narrative and remitter BOP purpose code must map to direct investment, using the narrative 'Subscription of share capital in Samoa International Company' for equity or 'Shareholder loan advanced to Samoa International Company' for debt. The receiving bank requires a pre-agreed onboarding transaction pack: certified certificate of incorporation and constitution; board resolution authorizing the share issue or loan; UBO certificate and source-of-funds declaration; CBS exchange-control declaration (where the bank's compliance requires it) endorsed with the IC's section 77 exemption; executed subscription acknowledgment or loan agreement; and beneficial-ownership register extract. Funds should remain USD-denominated and be credited to the capital escrow or operating account without WST conversion. Apple/Google settlement batches arrive as a separate inbound rail — consolidated payout remittances from global app-store treasury nodes with remittance advices reconciled by the bank against the developer-program contracts.Outbound disbursements for content licensing, subcontractor engineering, localized marketing, and group management fees clear exclusively through SWIFT-mediated rails: customer credit transfers via MT103 supported by underlying agreements, invoices, and applicable withholding certifications, and interbank MT202 where the beneficiary operates through a correspondent overlay. Commercial documentary letters of credit (SWIFT MT700) are not applicable to pure software-as-a-service licensing but become permissible if the IC expands into hardware-enabled language-device sales requiring collateralized settlement. Samoan statutory payables — registered-agent fees, SIFA registry fees, licensed director fees — are discharged in WST through the licensed bank's EFTPOS/VDS channel or the CBS RTGS system, funded by a minimal USD-to-WST conversion. The account bank will require a standing transaction-justification framework identifying each counterparty, underlying agreement, and economic purpose to prevent shell-indicia or circular-flow flags.Onshore USD/WST FX forwards — available through ANZ Samoa and BSP Samoa as authorized-dealer products, conditional on documentary proof of underlying commercial exposure and CBS exchange-control reporting; speculative outrights are prohibited.Offshore USD/WST non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) — legally accessible in principle through global counterparty banks, but the WST offshore NDF market is exceptionally thin; execution is practical only in small notional sizes through regional desks.Cross-currency swaps (USD/WST) with a Samoan licensed authorized dealer — permissible in theory for balance-sheet hedging of WST liabilities, but an active onshore swap curve does not exist; given the IC's USD-functional-currency status, actual execution is rarely required.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2002, the Samoa Revenue Corporation may raise default assessments for understated income, impose penalties up to 200% of the tax evaded, add incremental late-payment surcharges, and prosecute officers; conviction carries a fine and imprisonment of up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false compliance declarations face criminal prosecution, civil restitution orders, travel bans issued by the Supreme Court of Samoa, asset freezing, mandatory attendance for examination, and disqualification from holding directorships; the corporate veil is pierced where the IC is used as a sham to defeat tax claims.
Shell Company / Sham Incorporation

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike off and dissolve the company, charge monthly default penalties (WST 100 per month plus 10% per annum on overdue fees), vest residual assets in the Registrar pending creditor claims, refuse restoration, and render contracts concluded during the struck-off period void.

Director personal liability: Directors incur strict personal liability for all debts incurred while the company is struck off; creditors and tax authorities may proceed directly against personal assets; a court may order restitution and impose a travel ban pending settlement.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and supporting AML/CFT regulations: fines up to WST 1,000,000 for corporate entities, confiscation and freezing of tainted proceeds on FIU application, and up to 10 years' imprisonment for individuals convicted of money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be personally prosecuted for willful blindness; personal freeze and forfeiture orders may be made against their assets, and the court may impose personal travel restrictions and disqualification from financial-services roles.
Unauthorized Domestic Business / Breach of IC Ring-Fence

Statutory sanction: The IC loses its exemption: the Income Tax Act 2002 applies retroactively at the 27% corporate rate, with interest on unpaid tax, late-filing penalties, and default-assessed taxes on all Samoa-source revenue; the Registrar may also strike off the company for breach of the International Companies Act.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved local business are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax; enforcement may include personal bank levies, asset freezing, travel bans, and criminal fines, overriding limited-liability protection.
