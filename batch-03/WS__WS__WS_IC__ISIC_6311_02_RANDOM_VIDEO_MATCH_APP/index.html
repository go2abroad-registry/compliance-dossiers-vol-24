Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Samoa (MaF) — official portal: Samoa Revenue Online Services (SROS) at https://eservice.revenue.gov.ws; primary website: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 2012 through the Registrar of International Companies in the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour, using a licensed offshore service provider/registered agent in Samoa. Step 2: Submit a Tax Identification Number (TIN) application (Form IR-01 'Application for Registration for Tax Purposes') to the Ministry for Revenue, either physically at the VAGST/Income Tax Unit or via MRS e-services at https://eservice.revenue.gov.ws. Step 3: Attach certified copies of: Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association (constitutional documents), registered agent address certificate, directors/shareholders name, address, nationality details, and a declaration that no business is carried on within Samoa. Step 4: The Ministry for Revenue issues a 9-digit TIN within 3–5 working days. If the IC elects to claim VAGST refunds or to employ local staff, it must in addition register for VAGST and PAYE through the same portal. Foreign-incorporated entities seeking to do business in Samoa would instead register an external branch with the Registrar of Companies and obtain a TIN on the same Form IR-01.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tala (WST) is not freely convertible outside Samoa and the offshore WST market is illiquid. Current-account transactions are generally handled through authorized dealer banks subject to documentary support and exchange-control limits; capital-account transactions, including shareholder capital injections and repatriation of funds, require central-bank approval or delegated approval. A tax-exempt Samoa International Company may maintain foreign-currency accounts and pay profits/dividends only after verification of source of funds, updated statutory records, and, where applicable, tax and registry clearance are filed with the bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory default: export proceeds must be repatriated through a Samoan licensed bank and surrendered/converted into WST no later than 90 days from shipment or invoice date. The mandatory conversion ratio is 100% unless the Central Bank of Samoa grants a foreign-currency retention account approval or an explicit exemption for a service exporter such as the ISIC 6311 video-matching application where offshore platform receipts are channeled through aggregators. Any conversion exemption should be documented as a CBS retention direction to avoid penalty.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), Apia, Samoa, acting under the Exchange Control Act and delegated authorization to licensed commercial banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for local WST clearing because offshore electronic-money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed by the Central Bank of Samoa and cannot access the domestic WST clearing system. An offshore EMI may serve only as an auxiliary USD/EUR payout channel for platform receipts, provided the Samoa entity retains a licensed commercial bank for all WST conversion, exchange-control reporting, and repatriation flows. There is no Samoa EMI license class, and use of an unlicensed overseas EMI as primary settlement rails will create an AML/fintech compliance blocker.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound injections must be routed by wire (SWIFT MT103 with gpi) to the Samoa IC's licensed commercial bank account. Beneficiary field must show the Samoa IC name exactly as registered; remittance information should identify 'capital contribution / shareholder subscription' and the ISIC 6311 development purpose. The receiving bank will require the corporate KYC pack, source of funds, capitalization resolution, updated register of members, and a written explanation of the non-resident status. If the remittance is in USD and WST conversion is required, the bank will quote against WST and, depending on amount, obtain delegated exchange-control approval. No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies for the International Company, but the bank may set a minimum opening deposit.Outbound vendor, cloud infrastructure, marketing, and licensing payments are settled through the licensed bank's SWIFT network using MT103 for direct remittances or MT700 documentary credit for larger structured trade. Samoa does not operate a local USD real-time gross settlement; Samoan bank USD is drawn through foreign correspondent banks in the United States, Australia, New Zealand, and Europe. Export invoices, service contracts, platform cost statements, and exchange-control forms must be retained to support the purpose of every outward transfer. For app-related disbursements to payment aggregators, net settlement statements from Apple IAP and Paymentwall are the governing clearing documents.Deliverable forward foreign-exchange contract (USD/WST) with a licensed Samoan commercial bank, available up to 12 months and supported by documented platform revenue and cost exposureCross-currency swap transaction with a licensed bank under delegated exchange-control approval, where a genuine capital and operational currency mismatch existsNon-deliverable-forward contract for WST/USD arranged offshore only with prior written approval of the Central Bank of Samoa; speculative trading in futures or options is not permissible

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 1,000,000 aggregate related-party cross-border transactions (recommended documentation threshold and standard commercial benchmark; Samoa's Income Tax Act does not prescribe a separate numeric local-file threshold in the public regulations). For a tax-exempt IC no Master File or Local File is statutorily due; nevertheless, any Samoan tax audit or economic substance review will require documentation demonstrating arm's length pricing of app IP, marketing, and intra-group service fees.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), administered by SIFSA, apply to International Companies conducting 'relevant activities' (including IP holding, service centre, distribution/headquarters, financing, and related licensing businesses). A video-match social app that owns the proprietary platform, user data, or branding and receives the corresponding revenue streams through Samoa is expected to satisfy management and control, employee, and physical-asset substance tests in Samoa, or to prove tax residence/exclusion in a reputable foreign jurisdiction with equivalent substance. Submission of the annual economic substance declaration/return is mandatory; zero revenue or blank declarations are not accepted as compliance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and deliberate misreporting

Statutory sanction: Additional assessment of the tax shortfall, shortfall penalty of up to 150% of the unpaid tax, late payment interest, and criminal conviction. Samoa criminal law provides for fines up to WST 1,000,000 for corporate offenders and imprisonment up to 7 years for knowing tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors and beneficial owners who knowingly sign false declarations or divert revenue are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and civil penalties; the court may impose travel bans, asset freezing orders and confiscation, and individual imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Shell company and sham corporate substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration by the Registrar; disqualification of the directors and agent; monetary penalty under the International Companies Act and ESR for false substance declarations; transactions may be recharacterised by the Inland Revenue as taxable in Samoa and in the jurisdiction of effective management.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of assets transferred out of the insolvent structure, personal liability for company debts incurred under a struck-off or sham entity, travel bans pending investigation, and disqualification from acting as director for up to 10 years.
AML/CFT failure

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's Money Laundering Prevention Act and AML/CFT amendments, fines for licensed agents and reporting entities up to WST 1,000,000, confiscation of proceeds, suspension or revocation of the financial services licence, and criminal prosecution of responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face individual criminal liability, civil penalties, asset confiscation, travel bans and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering or wilful blindness.
Transfer pricing manipulation and false economic substance certification

Statutory sanction: Reassessment on an arm's length basis; disallowance of the inflated expense; shortfall penalty up to 150%; ESR administrative penalty per relevant activity; strike-off of the IC; in severe cases criminal charges for false documents under Samoa's Crimes Act.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable for false ESR/TCR certifications; where a director causes or permits the mispricing, courts may order civil restitution, impose a travel ban, disallow the corporate veil, and impose imprisonment of up to 7 years for fraudulent documents.
