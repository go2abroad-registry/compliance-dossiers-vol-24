Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Government of Samoa — national online portal: https://mfr.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned entities must first incorporate as an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987 with the Registrar of International Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL). The IC then applies to the Ministry for Revenue for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the prescribed TIN application form (Form MfR 1), accompanied by the certified certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, register of directors and shareholders, and a declaration of offshore-only business. TINs are issued within one to two working days. If the IC later obtains a domestic business licence, it must also register for VAGST under the Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 2008 and for PAYE employer obligations before remunerating local staff.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — a non-resident International Company carrying on exclusively offshore business is exempt from Samoan income tax (initial 20-year exemption, renewable by Ministerial order), exempt from stamp duty, and outside Samoa's territorial tax net; it is not liable to VAGST on offshore turnover. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — a non-resident International Company carrying on exclusively offshore business is exempt from Samoan income tax (initial 20-year exemption, renewable by Ministerial order), exempt from stamp duty, and outside Samoa's territorial tax net; it is not liable to VAGST on offshore turnover. Breach of the offshore-only restriction voids the exemption and reclassifies the entity as a fully taxable Samoan resident.

Available election forms

Samoa does not provide a US-style check-the-box or LLC pass-through election. Available statutory elections are: (i) an IC may elect to be treated as tax-resident in Samoa if central management and control is exercised there, thereby opting into the normal Income Tax Act 2019 regime; (ii) application to the Minister for Finance to extend or confirm the 20-year tax exemption certificate; (iii) voluntary VAGST registration election; (iv) no hybrid mismatch election is recognised for ICs — the Income Tax Act 2019 general anti-avoidance rules and arm's length principles apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

SAT$ 100,000 annual taxable turnover (compulsory registration); an offshore-only International Company is not carrying on a taxable activity in Samoa and is therefore not registrable, unless it elects voluntary registration or holds a domestic business licence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated where the goods are exported in accordance with the VAGST Act 2008 and evidence is furnished within the time prescribed by the Commissioner of Revenue. Offshore services qualify for zero-rating where the recipient is a non-resident, the services are not supplied in connection with land or goods in Samoa, and the services are performed wholly outside Samoa. An International Company supplying 3D game art sculpting and character rigging BPO services to overseas game studios satisfies the export-of-services test; where the IC remains wholly offshore, such turnover is outside the scope of VAGST entirely rather than zero-rated, and any input VAT on local overheads can be recovered only if the IC has elected VAGST registration and makes zero-rated supplies.

  • Samoa Customs Service Export Declaration / customs clearance documents
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for physical exports; for digital 3D art deliverables, electronic delivery evidence, service contract, and milestone acceptance records are substituted
  • VAGST export certificate / zero-rating claim form endorsed by the Ministry for Revenue
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • International Company certificate of incorporation and tax exemption certificate proving offshore-only status

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no digital stamping requirement, and no government API pre-validation of invoices. VAGST-registered persons must issue tax invoices containing the prescribed particulars under the VAGST Act 2008 and retain duplicate copies, in paper or electronic form, for the seven-year audit window. Electronic invoices generated by MfR-approved accounting systems are acceptable as tax invoices.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit for a seven-year period. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims or undeclared domestic activity expose the IC to retrospective VAGST and income tax assessments, penalty tax of 100% to 200% of the tax shortfall, late-payment interest and penalties under the Tax Administration Act (including a 20% per annum penalty rate), criminal fines up to SAT$ 100,000, imprisonment of up to five years for directors, revocation of the International Company tax exemption certificate, and striking-off by the Registrar of International Companies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa is an International Monetary Fund Article VIII member, undertaking current account convertibility for the Samoan tala (WST). Current account payments, including trade in services and profit remittances, are generally permitted through authorised dealers without prior CBS approval, provided the transaction is supported by valid contracts, invoices, and tax clearance where applicable. Capital account transactions, particularly inbound equity injections to local resident companies and offshore lending, remain subject to CBS approval or notification under the Exchange Control Act 1979 and the CBS Exchange Control Guidelines. For an International Company (IC) licensed under the International Companies Act 1988, the exchange control regime is substantially relaxed: the IC is treated as a non-resident offshore vehicle and may maintain foreign currency accounts with authorised dealers without WST conversion requirements. Dividend and profit distributions by an IC to non-resident shareholders are not subject to Samoan tax or exchange control restrictions, provided the IC does not carry on business with Samoan residents and does not hold Samoan-situs property. Practical remittance hurdles arise only where bank compliance requires proof that the IC is not engaged in domestic prohibited activities and that its underlying revenue is sourced from bona fide offshore clients.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident trading entities, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa through an authorised dealer within 90 days from the date of export, and any proceeds not required for approved foreign exchange obligations must be converted into WST at the prevailing market rate. Mandatory conversion rates for resident exporters are assessed by CBS on a case-by-case basis, generally requiring conversion of 100% of export receipts within the repatriation window unless prior CBS approval has been obtained for retention in a foreign currency account or for direct settlement of offshore liabilities. International Companies are exempt from these surrender rules provided they are not engaged in 'ordinary business' with Samoan residents and do not operate a Samoan domestic business; their export or service receipts may be retained abroad or held in authorised dealer foreign currency accounts in Samoa without mandatory conversion. All exceptions must be supported by a CBS Exchange Control form, the IC's certificate of incorporation, and an annual declaration of non-resident status.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low. Samoa does not maintain a standalone e-money institution (EMI) or digital payment institution licence. Offshore digital EMIs and payment service providers cannot open bank accounts in Samoa or obtain CBS licensing without establishing a physical branch and complying with the Financial Institutions Act 1996 and the CBS Fit and Proper Guideline. A Samoa International Company may use an offshore EMI for collection of client payments as an auxiliary channel, but this creates significant AML risk because EMI transaction trails are frequently rejected by Samoa banks as insufficient evidence of 'clear source of funds'. The IC is advised to route the majority of inbound revenue through a correspondent-capable commercial bank in Samoa or through a reputable international bank in a low-risk jurisdiction, with the Samoa bank account maintained for formal settlement and operating expenses. PayPal and other digital wallets are permitted as marginal collection tools only where the wallet is held in the IC's name, the wallet history is fully reconcilable to invoices, and the aggregate monthly volume through such channels is kept below USD 5,000 to preserve the Low_Risk_Standard AML rating.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% — the statutory dividend withholding tax rate imposed under the Income Tax Act 2015 on dividends distributed by a Samoan company to a non-resident shareholder. Dividends paid by a tax-exempt International Company solely out of foreign-source income to a non-resident shareholder are outside the Samoan income tax base and therefore carry an effective 0% withholding tax rate; no formal WHT clearance is required for such distributions.

Filing is due within 6 months of the end of the IC's financial year (by 30 June for the default calendar-year basis). The prescribed annual government license fee must be settled on the anniversary of incorporation; arrears accrue monthly.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The IR2 is due no later than 6 months after the close of the company's year of income (the default year of income is the calendar year; a substituted balance date may be adopted with MoR approval). Any assessed corporate tax is payable on the same due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 10,000,000 — Samoa's transfer pricing rules follow the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines. A Local File is administratively required where the value of controlled transactions with a single related enterprise exceeds WST 1,000,000, and a Master File where aggregate cross-border related-party turnover exceeds WST 10,000,000 in a fiscal year. Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is recommended for all IC invoicing to related foreign gaming studios because the MoR may benchmark the service margin under TNMM and impute Samoan-source profits if the IC is re-characterized as tax-resident.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations, operative from 1 January 2020, apply to every IC carrying on a relevant activity. A 3D art outsourcing studio classified under ISIC 7490 may fall within the 'service center', 'headquarters', or 'intellectual property business' categories. Such ICs must demonstrate: (i) core income-generating activities directed toward foreign game clients at least supervised from Samoa; (ii) adequate physical premises, full-time employees, and operating expenditure proportionate to the scale of offshore activity; and (iii) an annual economic substance notification filed through SIFSA. Failure to satisfy the substance test results in loss of the tax exemption and application of the penalties detailed below.
