Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

South African Revenue Service (SARS); mandatory eFiling portal at https://www.sarsefiling.co.za; Customs operations via SARS eCustoms (SAD500 electronic declaration platform).

E-government filing portal

Foreign-resident entities must register for income tax using SARS Form IT77 (Registration of Non-Residents), obtain a unique income tax reference number, and appoint an appointed local representative / public officer as required by s 246 of the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 (TAA). VAT registration is effected via Form VAT101 (VAT101E for foreign suppliers) and becomes compulsory once taxable supplies exceed ZAR 1,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period. Non-residents with a South African business establishment, fixed address, local bank account, and fiscal representative are practically required before SARS will issue a VAT registration number. All registrations and tax returns must be filed electronically via eFiling.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; a (Pty) Ltd incorporated in South Africa is a resident company taxed on worldwide income under the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962 at the corporate rate of 27% (for years of assessment ending on or after 31 March 2023). No pass-through treatment is available. Territorial scope: worldwide income with foreign tax credits; capital gains are included at an effective 80% inclusion rate (resulting in a 21.6% maximum effective capital gains tax rate). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a (Pty) Ltd incorporated in South Africa is a resident company taxed on worldwide income under the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962 at the corporate rate of 27% (for years of assessment ending on or after 31 March 2023). No pass-through treatment is available. Territorial scope: worldwide income with foreign tax credits; capital gains are included at an effective 80% inclusion rate (resulting in a 21.6% maximum effective capital gains tax rate). No tax-exempt zone status applies to the distribution of craft beer within South Africa, although exports attract zero-rating under the VAT Act.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box regime exists; the Pty Ltd is per se a corporate taxpayer. Available elections relevant to the structure include: (i) Headquarter Company election under s 9I of the Income Tax Act (useful for holding and financing African distribution structures); (ii) voluntary VAT registration election where taxable supplies exceed ZAR 50,000 but are below the ZAR 1,000,000 compulsory threshold; (iii) CFC high-tax exemption election under s 9D(9B) where controlled foreign company income is subject to at least 90% of the South African tax that would have applied; (iv) hybrid mismatch rules contained in ss 23N–23U (no general election to alter hybrid character, but factual alignment of payments and withholding deductions is required); and (v) deemed dividend elections under s 64H for loan provisions to shareholders, which may affect dividend tax treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

ZAR 1,000,000 compulsory registration threshold (any consecutive 12-month period); voluntary registration permitted where taxable supplies exceed ZAR 50,000 in a 12-month period; foreign suppliers may register on a voluntary basis with SARS approval.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of craft beer and related goods are zero-rated under s 11(1)(a) of the VAT Act where the supplier transfers ownership under a sale agreement and exports the goods within 90 days from the date of the tax invoice or delivery (whichever is later); the supplier must be the holder of the goods and must physically remove them from South Africa. Services (e.g., distribution logistics services) supplied to non-residents may be zero-rated under s 11(2)(l) provided the services are not physically rendered to a resident in South Africa and are not in direct connection with movable property situated in South Africa at the time the services are rendered. SARS Interpretation Note 30 (Exports: Proof of Export) and VAT Guide for Exporters set out a closed list of acceptable proof-of-export documentation; zero-rating for exports to SACU member states (Botswana, Lesotho, Namibia, Eswatini) is only available where goods are exported beyond the SACU common customs area and the supplier holds evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD500) submitted via SARS eCustoms, stamped or tracked as 'Export Release'
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing physical removal from South Africa
  • Commercial Invoice reflecting the zero-rated value and basis for zero-rating
  • Packing List
  • Supplier's Export Certificate / Transfer Certificate issued by the port authority (where applicable)
  • Proof of Payment / Inward Remittance Certificate received from the foreign buyer (linking the funds to the specific export shipment)
  • SARB Currency & Exchanges Manual Form BLR or Foreign Currency Exchange Confirmation (for export proceeds)
  • Insurance certificate (when required by SARS as additional evidence under Interpretation Note 30)

No real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory digital stamping is currently in force. SARS has published a draft e-invoicing framework based on the PEPPOL BIS-4 standard and requires VAT-registered vendors to use a SARS-approved Managed Electronic Invoicing (MeI) solution if they voluntarily adopt e-invoicing. All VAT invoices must be issued within 21 days from the date of supply (s 20(6) VAT Act) and must contain all 15 mandatory fields prescribed by s 20(4). VAT returns (VAT201) must be filed electronically via eFiling, and payments must be made via EFT or approved banking channels. SARS conducts risk-based e-audits of VAT refunds using invoice-level data where available.

SARS cross-references SAD500 customs records, eFiling VAT returns, bill of lading data, and bank foreign currency receipts to detect improper zero-rating. Export proceeds must be received within 180 days from the date of export under the SARB Currency & Exchanges Manual; late receipt without SARB approval triggers penalty interest, possible forfeiture of the export VAT refund, and monetary administrative penalties under SARS's duty schedule. Incorrect VAT returns and zero-rating claims attract understatement penalties under ss 222–223 of the TAA, graded at 10% (substantial understatement), 25% (reasonable care not taken), 50% (gross negligence), and 75% (intentional tax evasion), plus interest determined under s 187 of the TAA. Where SARS determines that a refund was claimed fraudulently or without reasonable grounds, an additional tax of 100% of the tax shortfall may be imposed under s 234 of the VAT Act, and the VAT refund may be retrospectively clawed back with interest. Criminal prosecution for fraudulent export zero-rating carries a fine of up to ZAR 90,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 5 years per s 234 VAT Act, with directors and officers held personally liable under s 159 TAA for company tax debts and penalties arising from willful non-compliance or fraudulent claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

South Africa operates a structurally open current account; trade payments, service fees, royalties, and dividends are remittable without pre-approval when supported by prescribed commercial documentation and SARS tax clearance where applicable. Capital account convertibility is conditional and administered: non-resident equity subscriptions and qualifying shareholder loans require completion of Exchange Control Manual procedures, issue of a Transfer Certificate (TC) or Letter of Confirmation (LoC) upon realization, and routing exclusively through an Authorized Dealer. Profit remittance is permitted only from after-tax distributable reserves evidenced by audited financial statements and a formal dividend declaration; no standing surplus or dividend block exists beyond exchange-control documentary formality. The ZAR is fully convertible for current transactions via the Authorized Dealer market at freely negotiated spot rates; capital inflows onshore do not require FinSurv pre-approval at the point of receipt for ordinary share capital, while certain capital outflows, disinvestment proceeds, and non-resident loan repayments require TC issuance, tax clearance, and Form A2/FED declarations. Practical hurdles for a craft-beer exporter are limited to documentary integrity: invoices, shipping documents, and supporting export dockets must match the SARB Customs and Excise export declaration to avoid proceeds-shorting or transfer-pricing reconstruction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export payment for goods must be received in a freely convertible currency within 90 days from the date of shipment (30 days for designated primary agricultural and mineral commodities under the SARB Exchange Control Manual). Upon receipt, proceeds must be surrendered to an Authorized Dealer, i.e., converted to ZAR, unless an approved Foreign Currency Account (FCA) facility is held. Under the amended Exchange Control Manual effective from 2021, the historical forced-conversion percentage has been replaced by a risk-based dispensation: an exporter may credit the entirety of verified export receipts to a corporate FCA in support of future import commitments, hedging, and working capital, subject to the Authorized Dealer's FCA mandate and FinSurv oversight; however, funds not earmarked for foreign obligations are expected to be repatriated into ZAR on a timely basis. The statutory floor is unchanged: full receipt of foreign currency by day 90, confirmed by the bank's Export Verification System (EVS) and the SARS Customs export records; failure to timeously receive and surrender proceeds triggers a FinSurv non-compliance report, potential suspension of FCA privileges, and deemed-income tax adjustment.

Central bank supervision: South African Reserve Bank (SARB), acting corporately through the Financial Surveillance Department (FinSurv) under the Currency and Exchanges Act No. 9 of 1933 and the Exchange Control Regulations, 1961, with day-to-day administration delegated to Authorized Dealers (the registered commercial banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as a primary legal banking rail; MEDIUM as an ancillary collection aggregation layer. Offshore digital EMI platforms (multi-currency wallet providers and virtual-account issuers) are not Authorized Dealers under SARB exchange control and cannot legally hold surrendered export proceeds, issue Transfer Certificates, or settle statutory trade payments on behalf of a South African resident entity. They may be used solely as non-regulated collection portals for small-ticket EU/UK craft-retail receivables, subject to an immediate same-day sweep back to the South African Authorized Dealer account; any material balance held offshore creates an unlawful exchange-control exposure and a FICA compliance gap. EMI onboarding remains unattractive because South African banks price cross-border inward MT103s competitively and the FCA structure already provides fully compliant USD/EUR pooling.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders subscribe for ordinary or redeemable preference shares by wiring via SWIFT MT103 to the (Pty) Ltd's capital-proceeds ledger at a designated Authorized Dealer bank using the proper CATS capital-account purpose code (Non-Resident Share Capital / Foreign Direct Investment Equity). The Authorized Dealer requires the underlying share subscription agreement, board acceptance resolution, and investor bank details; funds are credited into a ring-fenced capital account, converted to ZAR at the dealing-desk spot rate, and formally recorded with FinSurv on issuance of the Share Certificates. The bank then issues a Transfer Certificate (TC) evidencing the non-resident holding value for future dividend remittance and disinvestment; no SARS clearance is required at entry, but the subscription price must not be below fair market value to avoid thin-capitalization and transfer-pricing challenges. Shareholder loans above the delegated threshold of R 3 million require prior FinSurv approval; below that threshold, the dealer may approve a loan agreement with a fixed maturity and arm's-length coupon, with repayment routed through Form A2 and TC referencing the original importation.Settlement of imported brewing ingredients, packaging, and cold-chain equipment is executed through two primary rails: (1) banker's commercial Letter of Credit (MT700 issuance, documents-against-payment using Incoterms-verified B/L and commercial invoice) for large/strategic purchases, or (2) direct SWIFT MT103 outward payment against invoice for established low-risk suppliers, quoting SARB CATS purpose codes for General Merchandise (Goods). The Authorized Dealer will verify the SARS Customs IIR filing for goods already entered or the proforma invoice for advance payments, and will report the transaction under the exchange-control return framework. Domestic distribution settlements between the Pty Ltd and South African wholesalers use the local real-time clearing rails: EFT via SAMOS (South African Multiple Option Settlement) and PayShap instant EFT, governed by the National Payment System Act, with direct-debit instructions used for recurring retail distributor collections. They may be used solely as non-regulated collection portals for small-ticket EU/UK craft-retail receivables, subject to an immediate same-day sweep back to the South African Authorized Dealer account; any material balance held offshore creates an unlawful exchange-control exposure and a FICA compliance gap. EMI onboarding remains unattractive because South African banks price cross-border inward MT103s competitively and the FCA structure already provides fully compliant USD/EUR pooling.
