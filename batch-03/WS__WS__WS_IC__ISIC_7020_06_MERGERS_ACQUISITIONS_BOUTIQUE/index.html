Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR) of the Government of Samoa, established 2023 by merging the Inland Revenue Department and Samoa Customs Division; operational as the single national tax and customs administration. Official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws; customs formalities administered through the ASYCUDA World single window.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) with the Registrar of International Companies and Trusts at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL) under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended). No minimum paid-up capital is required; non-resident beneficial ownership is permitted. Step 2: Appoint a licensed offshore service provider in Samoa to maintain the registered office and file statutory documents. Step 3: Submit TIN application Form TIN-01 to the MfR via the online e-services portal or in person, attaching the certificate of incorporation, charter or articles, certificate of good standing, beneficial ownership declaration, board resolution appointing directors, passport copies and proof of residential address of shareholders, directors and ultimate beneficial owners, and the MCIL annual licence fee receipt. Step 4: MfR issues a Tax Identification Number within 5–10 business days and records the company in the taxpayer register with the status 'Exempt International Company (Offshore)'. Step 5: No Samoa income tax, VAGST or payroll registration is triggered if the IC derives no Samoan-source income; the only recurring compliance obligation is the annual MCIL renewal licence fee.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the IC is a non-resident exempt entity under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended) enjoying a 20-year statutory exemption from incorporation (renewable on application) in respect of all Samoan income tax on income derived outside Samoa, VAGST on offshore supplies, stamp duty on instruments relating to the IC, and exchange control approvals on offshore transactions. The exemption is absolute for the moratorium period and does not require a fiscal ruling; it coexists with Samoa's source-based territorial income tax system under which even resident companies are generally taxed only on Samoan-source income.

Available election forms

Check-the-box elections are not a feature of Samoan tax law. Available elections and boundary mechanisms: (a) 'Opt-in' conversion under the International Companies Act 1987, whereby the IC re-registers as a domestic company under the Companies Act 2006 and thereby forfeits the exemption, becoming taxable on Samoan-source income; (b) voluntary VAGST registration election if the IC wishes to recover input tax on local overheads despite having no taxable Samoan supplies — practically inadvisable because it imposes return filing and invoicing obligations; (c) branch/permanent establishment election if the IC establishes a fixed place of business in Samoa, bringing profits into the corporate income tax net at 27% plus final withholding taxes; (d) no hybrid mismatch or fiscal unity elections are available; the IC cannot be grouped with domestic taxpayers for tax purposes; (e) an IC may apply to MfR for an advance tax ruling confirming the scope of its exemption, but such ruling is optional and non-precedential.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 200,000 per annum in taxable turnover for domestic suppliers. Non-resident suppliers of taxable goods delivered in Samoa or services performed in Samoa are registrable with no threshold and may be required to appoint a resident agent for VAGST purposes. An exempt IC that supplies only offshore advisory services to non-resident clients from outside Samoa has no Samoan VAGST nexus and no registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% VAGST applies under the VAGST Act to: (i) goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply under customs control; (ii) services supplied to a non-resident recipient where the benefit is wholly consumed outside Samoa, including professional services such as cross-border M&A advisory, financial consulting and due diligence; and (iii) services directly connected with exported goods. For the M&A advisory boutique (ISIC 7020), advisory mandates executed outside Samoa for non-resident clients are extra-territorial supplies, thus outside Samoan VAGST entirely, and the IC is treated as making no taxable supply. Export proceeds for both goods and services must be repatriated to a Samoa-licensed bank within 180 days of export or invoice date, and supported by inward remittance evidence. A VAGST-registered exporter claiming input credits must file monthly returns within 28 days of month-end and substantiate each zero-rated supply with the documentation bundle below.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged via ASYCUDA World with customs endorsement of physical exit (export goods only)
  • Commercial invoice and signed advisory/services agreement identifying the non-resident counterparty and place of supply
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for goods; for services, time-stamped engagement deliverables, offshore workpapers and board minutes evidencing performance outside Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds into a Samoa-licensed bank account
  • VAGST zero-rating declaration (MfR Form VAT-02) with supporting evidence of the client's non-resident status and beneficial ownership

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime exists in Samoa. MfR requires registered suppliers to issue a valid tax invoice within 21 days of supply and to retain accounting and invoicing records for six years. Customs export and import declarations are processed through the ASYCUDA World electronic single-window environment. MfR is progressively digitising taxpayer services through its online e-services portal; electronic invoicing is voluntary, and system-to-system integration is permitted but not mandated. Digital fiscal stamps are not used in the VAGST system.

MfR may audit zero-rated and exempt supplies within a 3-year assessment period, extended to 6 years where fraud or wilful misstatement is alleged. Unsubstantiated zero-rating is clawed back at 15% VAGST plus a late-payment penalty of 2% per month, compounded, and late-payment interest at the Central Bank of Samoa benchmark rate. Shortfall penalties escalate to 50% of the tax shortfall for failure to take reasonable care, 100% for intentional disregard, and 150% for fraudulent conduct. Criminal liability under the Tax Administration Act 2012 includes fines up to WST 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to five years for knowingly issuing false export certificates or falsified remittance records; directors and officers are jointly and severally liable, and an adverse finding triggers deregistration and forfeiture of any tax exemption certificate held by an International Company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

WST is a semi-convertible currency subject to exchange control administered by the CBS. Current-account payments, including dividends and professional fees, are permitted if supported by valid documentary evidence and settled through an authorized dealer. Capital-account transactions, including equity injections, intercompany loans, and outward capital repatriation, require prior CBS approval or filing of the applicable exchange control form. Profit remittance for International Companies is not blocked by the IC regime but is subject to ongoing payment instructions, availability of underlying audited financial statements, tax clearance where applicable, and CBS compliance review. No unconditional legal right to unrestricted conversion exists; CBS reserves the power to refuse or condition remittances in cases of inconsistent declarations or unresolved compliance issues.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days from the date of shipment (or the contractually due date if shorter) and received into an account with an authorized dealer. Unless a CBS-approved export retention facility is granted, 100% of repatriated export receipts must be surrendered to the authorized dealer in exchange for WST. For services-driven export revenue derived from cross-border M&A advisory fees, the same surrender obligation applies to the inbound success-fee wire, unless the fee is retained offshore with the prior written approval of the CBS under a specific retention exemption. Application for retention requires form CBS-ER/01, evidence of invoicing, and the underlying advisory agreement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low (restricted). Samoa has no dedicated electronic-money institution (EMI) licensing regime, and no offshore digital EMI may act as a bank account holder for a Samoan International Company unless separately licensed as a bank or authorized dealer. Global fintech rails such as Wise, Payoneer, and Mercury may be used only as invoicing and collection facilitators that eventually settle into a licensed Samoan bank account. The CBS will not recognize an offshore EMI as an authorized dealer for exchange control purposes, and any attempt to use an unlicensed EMI as the primary treasury conduit will fail AML due diligence and may trigger a statutory compliance filing obligation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity or capital injection wires from foreign shareholders must be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 using an authorized dealer bank in Samoa. The transfer should reference the CBS exchange-control purpose code appropriate to the transaction (typically 'equity capital' for non-resident direct investment). The receiving bank will require the capital remitter to provide a telefax or SWIFT confirmation of the underlying corporate resolution authorizing the injection, identification of the ultimate source of funds, and the bank statement of the remitting entity. Once credited, the capital must be booked to a designated Capital Escrow Account if it is intended for subsequent deal-disbursement under an M&A escrow arrangement. In all cases, the instruction must contain a clear narrative field identifying the IC company name, the nature of the transaction, and the corresponding contractual reference.Outbound trade disbursements for M&A transaction milestones, seller consideration, or cross-border settlement fees must be initiated via SWIFT MT103 for each direct beneficiary. For larger escrow-motivated payments conditioned on closing conditions, a Commercial Letter of Credit subject to UCP600 may be issued by the Samoan authorized dealer bank or confirmed through a corresponding bank in the target jurisdiction. Interbank treasury movements should use SWIFT MT202. All outbound instructions must be supported by the advisory agreement, board resolution, CBS exchange-control forms where required, and an FX instruction indicating whether conversion to a designated foreign currency is needed. Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% standard corporate income tax rate (International Companies are exempt from this rate on offshore income under s.168 International Companies Act 1987; the rate applies only if prohibited local business is carried on)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for distributions by an International Company to any shareholder, because s.168(2)(b) of the International Companies Act 1987 exempts dividends, interest and other sums paid by an IC from every Samoan tax and withholding tax. The ordinary domestic rate for dividends paid by a resident company to a non-resident shareholder is 15%, reduced to 5% where the recipient is a non-resident company holding at least 10% of the voting power.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 30 calendar days of the anniversary of incorporation; standard SIFA annual fee payment by no later than that date or, if the IC aligns to a calendar-year cycle, by 30 June.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six months after the fiscal year end (standard fiscal year is 31 December, so due by 30 June). An extension of up to 60 days may be approved by the Commissioner on written request.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No monetary Local File/Master File threshold is codified in Samoa. The Tax Administration Act 2012 empowers the Commissioner of Revenue to require contemporaneous pricing documentation for any related-party transaction where a Samoan taxable taxpayer is involved. A tax-exempt International Company is outside the scope of Samoan TP filing unless it is stripped of exemption; in that event the Commissioner may call for full documentation without any de-minimis threshold.

Economic substance scope: Samoa applies economic substance scrutiny through SIFA guidance issued under the International Companies Act 1987 in alignment with OECD BEPS Action 5. An International Company undertaking M&A advisory work must demonstrate (i) direction and management exercised in Samoa through resident-agent meetings and board resolutions, (ii) an active physical office in Apia, (iii) adequate local staff and operating expenditure commensurate with success-fee revenue, and (iv) an annual substance declaration to SIFA. A zero-substance or 'letterbox' IC risks withdrawal of IC status, and the entity can be re-characterised as a domestic taxable company liable at 27% plus 100% penalty tax.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Declaration

Statutory sanction: Penalty tax equal to 100% of the tax evaded plus a further evader penalty of 50% of the evaded tax, and monthly interest at 1.5% until paid; upon conviction in court, a fine up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years under Income Tax Act 2012 ss.126-127.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or caused the false declaration are jointly and severally liable for the assessed tax and penalties, thereby piercing the corporate veil. The court may also issue a travel ban, a freezing order on personal bank accounts, a civil restitution order for the full amount of missing tax, and a custodial sentence of up to 7 years.
Unauthorised Domestic Business / License Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act 1987 s.184, fines up to WST 10,000 per day for continuing breach and mandatory strike-off/deregistration. All fees earned in the unlawful period become taxable Samoan-source income paid before tax clearance, and may be subject to a non-availability of exemption and a 100% denial of deductions.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the daily fines and for the corporate income tax assessed on the illegal local trade, because they directed the IC to act beyond its lawful objects. The Registrar may apply for disqualification orders (up to 5 years) and a civil restitution order against the directors' personal assets.
AML/CFT Failure and Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018, a body corporate is liable to a fine not exceeding WST 1,000,000, confiscation of the funds and ancillary property, revocation of the IC license, and publication of the penalty.

Director personal liability: AML/CFT obligations apply to all officers personally. A negligent officer is liable to a fine of WST 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; a knowing breach carries imprisonment up to 10 years, a lifetime travel ban, and a restitution order for the laundered amount, with the corporate veil fully pierced to recover proceeds.
Shell Company Misrepresentation / CRS-AEOI Infringement

Statutory sanction: Under International Companies Act 1987 ss.196-198, a false declaration is an offence carrying a fine of up to WST 5,000 and deregistration; for tax purposes, the IC is re-characterised as a domestic resident company and is taxed at 27% with a 100% penalty tax and interest.

Director personal liability: The misrepresentation is imputed to every director who certified it. Directors may be sued in civil restitution for all taxes evaded, barred from leaving Samoa, disqualified as directors of any Samoan company for up to 10 years, and, if convicted of perjury under the Crimes Act 2013, imprisoned for up to 7 years.
