Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) - official portal: https://www.sra.gov.ws/

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by applying to the Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) either online through the SRA e-Services portal or by lodged Form TIN-01, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered office certificate, and proof of identity and address of directors and shareholders. International Companies are registered under the International Companies Act 2008 and enjoy income tax exemption; the TIN is issued for administrative and VAT purposes only if the entity makes taxable supplies. A separate VAGST registration (Form VAT-01) is required once the annual turnover from local taxable supplies exceeds WST 47,000.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a check-the-box classification election for tax purposes. An International Company may elect to convert to a local company under the Companies Act 2001, thereby subjecting itself to ordinary income tax; may voluntarily elect for VAGST registration if below threshold; and may elect to claim zero-rating for exported supplies through the return. Hybrid mismatch election mechanisms are not available under Samoa law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 47,000 annual taxable turnover (voluntary registration below threshold)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available on exports of goods where physical export from Samoa is proven by customs documentation. For services, including vertical ERP/CRM cloud software, a supply is zero-rated when provided to a non-resident who is outside Samoa and the services are used wholly outside Samoa. The supplier must retain evidence of the non-resident status, place of consumption, and export contract.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SRA/GST Export Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank foreign exchange record
  • External service agreement or cloud licensing/use-right contract

No real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory API-based e-invoicing system is in force. Suppliers must issue a valid VAGST tax invoice for taxable supplies, including electronically generated invoices, but no digital stamping approval is required. The Ministry of Revenue operates a receipted cash-register mandate only for prescribed retail/hospitality activities; vertical cloud software B2B exports are outside that scope.

SRA conducts risk-based audits of zero-rated claims; failure to substantiate eligibility results in VAGST clawback plus interest and a shortfall penalty of up to 100% of the tax; intentional evasion carries a further 100% civil penalty and criminal liability with fines up to WST 500,000 and/or imprisonment for up to five years. Directors of International Companies are jointly and severally liable for fraudulent claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa maintains a managed-float exchange control regime; the Samoan tala (WST) is not fully freely convertible outside Samoa. Current account transactions for trade and services are generally permitted through authorized dealer banks with duly presented commercial documentation. Capital account items — including inbound equity subscriptions, shareholder loans, dividend distributions, liquidation proceeds, and other profit repatriation — require prior CBS exchange control approval or a valid standing exemption granted to International Companies under the Exchange Control (International Companies) Exemption Notice. For an offshore SaaS/vertical CRM operator, profit remittance is navigable through audited financial statements and board resolutions; the CBS scrutinises substance and source-of-income documentation to prevent circumvention of domestic taxation and laundering of overseas earnings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters of goods and services from Samoa must obtain a valid exchange control approval for each export transaction. Export proceeds must be repatriated and offered for sale to an authorized dealer within 90 days from the date of export unless a CBS standing consent permits retention in a foreign currency account abroad. For an International Company exporting software-as-a-service subscriptions, the CBS generally grants standing permission to maintain offshore USD/AUD/NZD collection accounts where the underlying customer contracts are wholly non-resident and no conversion into WST is needed; if any proceeds are brought into Samoa, the mandatory conversion ratio is 100% into WST via an authorized dealer unless the foreign exchange approval explicitly permits a different split.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) acting under the Exchange Control Act 1970, the International Companies Act 1987, and the National Payments System Act 2016; the CBS is the sole foreign exchange administration authority in Samoa.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Samoa does not maintain a standalone electronic-money institution licensing regime for non-resident digital banks; offshore EMIs such as Stripe, Airwallex, and Mercury cannot directly settle into WST bank accounts without a domestic authorized dealer intermediary. These EMIs are viable for collecting subscription revenue in USD/EUR/AUD via card rails and for processing customer payouts, with periodic SWIFT sweeps to the Samoa IC bank account. Legal constraints include the foreign exchange approval obligation, the requirement to route any WST conversions through an authorized dealer, and CBS expectations regarding the traceability of cross-border payment flows. Therefore the recommended architecture is a hybrid: local Samoa bank account as the treasury anchor, with Stripe Billing as the front-end payment gateway and an accepted EMI aggregator only for short-dated settlement settlement corridors.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for inbound share capital into the Samoa International Company: (i) execute a directors' board resolution resolving to issue shares to the overseas investor; (ii) remit the contribution by SWIFT from the investor's bank using MT103/MT202, with the beneficiary field identifying the Samoan bank and the account reference 'WS IC Capital Escrow Account'; (iii) include in the remittance narrative the inward investment purpose code, e.g. 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — INTERNATIONAL COMPANY INCORPORATION NO. [...]', plus the unique reference of the exchange control approval; (iv) deliver to the Samoan bank a completed CBS foreign exchange declaration, certified board resolution, share application, and COP of the investor's board sanction; (v) the receiving bank will verify the reconciliation with the capital escrow credit and issue an inward foreign exchange certificate before share certificates are issued. For shareholder loans, the loan agreement, interest-rate schedule, and repayment plan must accompany the remittance to ensure the credit is classified as external financial liability rather than equity.Outbound settlements for software license fees, partner commissions, infrastructure purchases, and vendor payments are executed through the Samoa bank's SWIFT network. Routine operational disbursements use MT103 with full invoice referencing and must be supported by a current corporate resolution listing authorised signatories. Large-value infrastructure procurement may be effected through a Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C) advised via the Samoan bank's correspondent in New York or Sydney, with SWIFT MT700 issuance and MT754 acknowledgements; funds are released against presentation of complying shipping and intellectual-property delivery documents. The underlying clearing rails for USD and AUD are correspondent accounts maintained with tier-1 clearing banks overseas, as Samoa does not operate real-time foreign-currency gross settlement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (International Company fully exempt under the International Companies Act 1987; standard Samoa resident company headline rate is 27%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by an International Company; profits of an IC are exempt from Samoa tax and distributions to non-resident shareholders are free of withholding tax. A statutory non-resident dividend withholding rate of 15% applies only to resident companies paying dividends to non-residents, subject to treaty reduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months after the fiscal year-end; Samoa's fiscal year is the calendar year (1 January to 31 December), so the annual return deadline is 30 June of the following year. Provisional tax instalments, where applicable, are payable quarterly on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa does not publish a specific WST-denominated Local File threshold for International Companies; the Commissioner of Revenue may require a transfer pricing study where related-party revenue exceeds WST 1,000,000 in a tax year or where the IC conducts controlled transactions with a Samoa-resident connected person regardless of amount. Prudent compliance practice: prepare a Local File when annual related-party turnover reaches WST 1,000,000 and a Master File when consolidated group turnover exceeds WST 5,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), adopted in 2019 under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Rules, apply to International Companies carrying on relevant activities, expressly including distribution and service businesses, holding companies, and intellectual property holding, which covers vertical ERP/CRM software licensing. The IC must demonstrate in Samoa: board meetings and minutes, a physical office or premises, full-time employees appropriate to the ERP/CRM activity, and active decision-making from Samoa. Non-compliant ICs are struck off, their annual certificate is refused, and directors and the registered agent are liable to fines up to WST 100,000 plus disqualification orders.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Tax Evasion / False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Shortfall penalty equal to 100% of the tax avoided, plus a late payment penalty of 10% and an incremental late payment penalty of 2% per month thereafter; criminal fine up to WST 300,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for the entity and its controlling officers.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution for taxes, penalties and interest jointly and severally; the court may pierce the IC's veil and order directors to reimburse the company for the entire loss; directors on a non-resident board face travel bans, refusal of entry into Samoa, and extradition risk from treaty-partner states for tax fraud.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of the IC tax exemption with assessment at 27% on deemed Samoa-source income; ESR non-compliance penalty of WST 20,000 per failure; strike-off of the company and forfeiture of the IP assets registered in its name.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent are jointly liable for unpaid corporate tax up to WST 500,000; a director who signs a false economic substance declaration commits an offence carrying up to 5 years' imprisonment and automatic disqualification from acting as a director of any Samoa company.
AML / Anti-Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018, fines up to WST 1,000,000 for the entity; the registered agent's licence may be revoked; the IC becomes subject to a mandatory AML compliance audit and its bank accounts may be frozen by the Central Bank of Samoa.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit the laundering of criminal proceeds are liable to imprisonment up to 10 years and personal asset confiscation orders; directors also face international banking blacklists, travel bans in Samoa and treaty jurisdictions, and disqualification from professional office.
Unlawful Dividend Distribution / Insolvent Winding Up

Statutory sanction: The distribution is voidable and recoverable from shareholders; the company is liable to a civil penalty of three times the unlawful distribution amount; the Registrar may place the IC into compulsory winding up and assets may be seized.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorised the distribution are personally liable to compensate creditors without limitation; the court may order repatriation of funds from foreign accounts, impose a 3-year travel ban, and commit disobedient directors to imprisonment for contempt.
