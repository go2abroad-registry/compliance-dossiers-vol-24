Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (Mafoe mo Tupe Maua), Government of Samoa — official tax authority and portal: https://www.mfr.gov.ws. The Ministry administers the Income Tax Act 2015, the Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) Act 2014, and excise/stamp duties, and operates the Tax Online e-filing facility. Incorporation of an International Company (IC) is administered separately by the International Companies Office under the Registrar of International Companies within the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour.

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor incorporates a Samoa International Company under the International Companies Act 2017 through a licensed registered agent or Samoan corporate services provider. The Registrar of International Companies issues a Certificate of Incorporation bearing a unique IC registration number. Because the IC enjoys a statutory income tax and VAGST exemption, the Ministry for Revenue does not require a separate income tax TIN; the IC registration number is the standard government identifier. If the IC nevertheless makes registrable taxable supplies in Samoa, it must complete Form VAGST-001 through the Tax Online portal, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered agent confirmation, and an estimate of 12-month Samoa-sourced turnover; a VAGST registration number is then issued within the statutory processing period.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Samoa IC is a non-resident limited company statutorily exempt from income tax, VAGST, stamp duty, dividend withholding, and national provident fund levies on all offshore business income, assets, and transactions regardless of beneficial ownership; only an annual government fee (based on authorised capital) is payable to the International Companies Office. — A foreign investor incorporates a Samoa International Company under the International Companies Act 2017 through a licensed registered agent or Samoan corporate services provider. The Registrar of International Companies issues a Certificate of Incorporation bearing a unique IC registration number. Because the IC enjoys a statutory income tax and VAGST exemption, the Ministry for Revenue does not require a separate income tax TIN; the IC registration number is the standard government identifier. If the IC nevertheless makes registrable taxable supplies in Samoa, it must complete Form VAGST-001 through the Tax Online portal, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered agent confirmation, and an estimate of 12-month Samoa-sourced turnover; a VAGST registration number is then issued within the statutory processing period.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Samoa IC is a non-resident limited company statutorily exempt from income tax, VAGST, stamp duty, dividend withholding, and national provident fund levies on all offshore business income, assets, and transactions regardless of beneficial ownership; only an annual government fee (based on authorised capital) is payable to the International Companies Office.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election, hybrid entity election, or classification opt-in exists under Samoan law. The IC tax-exempt regime is automatic and mandatory for companies incorporated under the International Companies Act 2017 that do not carry on domestic business in Samoa. An IC may voluntarily relinquish its exempt status by filing an election with the Registrar to be converted into a domestic company under the Companies Act 2006, after which it becomes taxable at entity level under the Income Tax Act 2015 on worldwide income. Samoa does not recognise US-style hybrid mismatch elections; the Income Tax Act 2015 contains general anti-avoidance and income-apportionment rules, and no OECD Pillar Two or CFC registration election is available at this stage.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 — annual taxable turnover from supplies made in Samoa triggers mandatory VAGST registration, with voluntary registration permitted below the threshold. Offshore digital subscription revenue from non-resident end-users (including collections through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing) is outside Samoan territorial supplies and does not count toward the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For a VAGST-registered Samoan entity, a 0% VAGST rate applies to (i) goods exported within 28 days of supply with documentary proof of export and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Samoa at the time of performance where the direct and primary benefit is wholly or substantially offshore, including digital subscription platforms and serialised web-fiction content monetised through app stores. The Samoa IC qualifies as a Tax_Exempt_Zone non-resident entity and is not required to charge VAGST on foreign-consumer sales; conversely, it may not claim input VAT credits unless it voluntarily registers. If the IC elects voluntary registration, it must substantiate each zero-rated supply with platform payout statements and non-resident user evidence to preserve 0% treatment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs Service electronic export declaration) identifying the digital/electronic dispatches as intangible exports
  • Commercial invoice and sales reconciliation statement for the platform subscription transactions
  • App Store Connect (Apple IAP) and Google Play Console financial and payout reports evidencing gross revenue and applicable platform commission deductions
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or corresponding SWIFT/bank credit evidence and platform payout ledger proving offshore funds received in Samoa
  • Non-resident customer tax-location evidence (IP-address geolocation, billing address, and storefront region settings)
  • Retained VAGST invoice (if issued) and digital delivery log evidencing offshore supply

Samoa does not impose real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, SAF-T reporting, or digital stamping/QR e-invoicing. A VAGST-registered person must issue serially numbered VAGST invoices (full invoices above the invoicing threshold and simplified invoices below it), retain records for at least five years, file monthly or bi-monthly VAGST returns through the Ministry for Revenue's Tax Online portal, and submit annual reconciliation schedules. Apple and Google are not treated as Samoan fiscal clearance agents, and no API integration with the Ministry for Revenue is required for the exempt IC at this stage.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating is sanctioned under the VAGST Act 2014 and the Crimes Act 2013: the Ministry for Revenue may reassess the uncollected 15% VAGST retrospectively, add a late-payment penalty of up to 25% of the outstanding tax plus statutory monthly interest, claw back previously allowed input credits with compound interest, and impose fines up to WST 10,000 with imprisonment not exceeding three years for false invoices or knowingly false export declarations. Directors, beneficial owners, and the licensed registered agent may be jointly assessed under anti-avoidance provisions of the Income Tax Act 2015 if the IC attracts on-island economic substance. For the exempt IC itself, the principal Samoan enforcement risk is loss of exempt status, causing all taxes, penalties, and interest to become due plus deregistration; no export VAT refund is payable to an entity that never collected VAGST.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is convertible for current international trade and service transactions, but capital account movements remain subject to central bank documentation and prior approval requirements. Samoa maintains functional convertibility for dividends, interest, and profit remittances of licensed/offshore activities, provided the repatriation is supported by audited financial statements and tax clearance. International Companies incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 and operating wholly outside Samoa are treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes; their foreign-currency bank accounts may be freely credited and debited without WST conversion hurdles. Profit remittances to shareholders are generally permissible through the authorised banking system upon production of the annual return, beneficial ownership register, and board resolution confirming dividend availability.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters of physical goods, export proceeds must be collected within 180 days from date of shipment and surrendered to an authorised dealer in WST at the prevailing market rate; 100% conversion applies unless a specific exemption is granted by the Central Bank. For the International Company generating digital webnovel subscription revenue, no physical export surrender obligation applies; receivables may be held in foreign currency offshore without mandatory conversion. If funds are repatriated to Samoa and converted to WST, the transaction is recorded under the purpose code for software/information technology services and no surrender percentage is prescribed beyond standard current-account conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Falefa Sāmoa) acting as the Foreign Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Central Bank Act 1984

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium. Samoa currently has no statutory sandbox or dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime. Foreign digital banks, virtual IBAN providers, and non-bank payment service providers cannot obtain a direct clearing membership or a Samoa national clearing code. Such providers may only function through a licensed commercial bank as a supporting payment gateway for Apple IAP and Google Play settlements, but they are not permitted to hold the core corporate treasury account or perform fiduciary settlement. Use of offshore EMIs is permissible only as collection intermediaries that sweep funds daily into the licensed Samoan/offshore bank account; no account-holding or collateral authority may be delegated to the EMI.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital is wired via SWIFT MT103 from the parent or subscriber investment vehicle to the Samoan licensed bank (or the company's offshore clearing bank if using the non-resident IC credit line). The payment must use purpose code for equity injection (e.g., 'Capital Contribution - ISIC 9000 Digital Content Platform') and be supported by board minutes, share allotment resolution, source of funds declaration, and beneficial ownership schedule. The Samoan bank will record the injection as a non-resident direct investment and will verify that the payer/payee names match the KYC records. For loan capital, an executed facility letter, interest and repayment schedule, and external borrowing approval from the Central Bank (when exceeding exemption thresholds) must be provided. Upon credit confirmation, the funds are immediately available for platform operations without custody restriction.Outbound settlement for software licensing, content author royalties, and digital advertising fees is routed through the same licensed bank using SWIFT MT103. For high-value content acquisition (e.g., exclusive Chinese-language novel licensing exceeding USD 50,000), the bank will request either a proforma invoice, governing service agreement, and signed acceptance certificate; a Commercial Letter of Credit is available only for physical goods and is not required for digital services. Merchant disbursements to foreign authors may be batched through the payment gateway provider, but the clearing bank must provide a monthly settlement report for AML reconciliation.
