Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MFR), Government of Samoa, Apia (statutory successor to the Samoa Tax Administration); taxpayer e-services and registration portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws.

E-government filing portal

Step 2: the licensed trustee files with the MFR the SIFA certificate, Memorandum and Articles of Association, directors/shareholders/beneficial-ownership KYC pack, passports, banker's references, and registered-office confirmation. Step 4: because the IC elects tax-exempt status, the MFR designates the account 'International Company - Tax Exempt Zone - nil income tax and nil VAGST', and no VAGST registration is effected. Step 5: SIFA/MFR issue the tax-exemption/resident certificate for the SPV's bankers, ceding reinsurers, and foreign withholding-relief purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The International Company is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax, VAGST, stamp duty, and all dividend/interest/royalty/capital-gains withholding taxes; it is treated as non-resident for Samoan tax purposes, with the SIFA annual licence and government fees as the only fiscal charges; reinsurance premium income and investment earnings fall wholly outside the Samoan tax base.

Available election forms

On incorporation the IC elects exempt-company, non-resident, or ordinary-resident status in its constitutional documents; changes of classification are made by special resolution with SIFA/MFR consent. No domestic check-the-box regime exists; a US beneficial owner may separately file Form 8832 for US federal tax classification purposes. Samoa has not enacted an elective hybrid-mismatch regime; BEPS Action 2 outcomes are applied through negotiated tax treaties and the Inclusive Framework commitment rather than a shareholder election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 60,000 of taxable supplies in any 12-month period (or a reasonable expectation of exceeding WST 60,000 in the next 12 months) under the Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 2008, as amended; tax-exempt International Companies are outside the registrable VAGST net and must not register for VAGST even if offshore premium flows exceed the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supplier may claim the 0% VAGST rate on: (a) goods physically exported within 30 days of supply; (b) international transport and directly incidental services; (c) services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply, with the benefit consumed wholly offshore; and (d) prescribed insurance/reinsurance services where the underlying risk is located outside Samoa. Premiums, recoveries, and investment income of the captive reinsurance SPV qualify as zero-rated exported reinsurance services for any VAGST-registered company, whereas a tax-exempt International Company simply incurs no VAGST liability; in both cases the risk must be documented as offshore and the counterparty as non-resident.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDEC) or electronic export entry lodged with the Samoa Customs Service evidencing the exported goods
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing actual export within the statutory 30-day window
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank SWIFT/MT103 proof of foreign-currency receipt from the non-resident counterparty
  • Reinsurance policy, slip, bordereaux, or underwriting agreement evidencing offshore risk location, non-resident ceding insurer, and premium/settlement terms
  • Certified copy of the SIFA tax-exemption certificate/captive licence for the International Company

Samoa does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing digital stamping, or API transaction-clearance mandate. VAGST-registered suppliers must issue sequential, MFR-compliant tax invoices (paper or electronic) showing the supplier TIN, buyer TIN/name, invoice date, serial number, supply description, GST-exclusive amount, 15% VAGST amount, and the VAGST registration status; all invoices and bordereaux must be lodged with the MFR on demand and reconciled via the MFR eServices portal. The captive reinsurance IC, being VAGST-exempt, issues underwriter bordereaux, premium statements, and settlement advices instead of Samoan VAGST tax invoices.

False, fabricated, or inadequately documented zero-rating and refund claims trigger: (i) reopening of up to five prior tax years, extendable on evidence of fraud; (ii) retrospective clawback of refunds, input-VAT credits, and zero-rate concessions with statutory interest and late-payment penalties; (iii) shortfall penalties under the Tax Administration Act 2015 of 20% for failure to take reasonable care, 75% for recklessness, and 150% for intentional evasion; (iv) administrative monetary penalties and criminal prosecution with fines and imprisonment for responsible directors/officers; and (v) for licensed ICs, suspension or revocation of the captive insurance licence, loss of tax-exempt status, cancellation of IC registration, and disqualification of the licensed trustee/agent. If FX repatriation is not evidenced within 180 days, the MFR may recharacterise the reinsurance premium flow as a domestic Samoan supply and impose 15% VAGST retroactively, together with the penalties described above.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tālā (WST) is a managed-float currency with limited offshore liquidity. Current-account payments, including insurance premium, reinsurance claims, dividends and interest, are generally permitted without prior exchange control approval, but must be routed through an authorised dealer bank and supported by Form A2 documentation. Capital-account transactions, including capital injections by non-resident group parents, subordinated loan advances, and capital repatriation or dividend distribution, require prior written approval of the Central Bank under the Exchange Control Act. Profit remittance is permitted after lodgement of audited financial statements and an exchange control certificate; in practice processing of a capital-account approval takes four to six weeks, and remittances are not restricted by any dividend withholding tax for Samoa International Companies, but the bank must be given a clear disposal instruction referencing the approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All proceeds from exports of goods or services, including reinsurance services supplied to non-residents, must be repatriated to and received in Samoa within 30 days of the dates the proceeds are deposited offshore or, if received directly, within 30 days of receipt. Unless a foreign-currency retention exemption is granted by the Central Bank of Samoa, 100% of each receipt must be surrendered to and converted into Samoan Tālā on the same business day through an authorised dealer bank. The exporter, including a licensed captive reinsurance SPV, must present the underlying contract and an export declaration; conversion is at the prevailing market rate.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa) acting as the sole foreign exchange control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1977, the Central Bank of Samoa Act 2005, and the Financial Institutions Act 1996 (as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Samoa does not have a domestic digital EMI (electronic money institution) licensing category. Only licensed commercial banks and money-transfer operators under the Financial Institutions Act 1996 and Central Bank AMLCFT Rules may hold client funds and execute foreign-exchange conversions. Foreign EMIs cannot obtain a Samoan lending or deposit license and cannot open a nostro account for the SPV in WST or route SWIFT payments from the captive's premium flows. A non-bank EMI may be acceptable only for incidental expense payments at negligible volume, and never for underwriting reserves, capital escrow, or premium settlement obligations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inward equity, regulatory capital, or underwriting collateral must be injected by SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the overseas parent or ceding insurer to the SPV's held USD or WST account at an authorised dealer bank in Samoa. The sending bank must prominently include an appropriate purpose code — e.g., 1100 for equity capital injection, 2110 for insurance premium underwriting, or 2199 for reinsurance claims — in the SWIFT field 70. The Samoan bank will require a copy of the signed reinsurance contract, a board resolution of the SPV and a confirmation letter from the parent stating the value of the premium as calculated by an actuary. On receipt, if the transaction exceeds the bank's threshold, the bank will trigger an exchange-control declaration to the Central Bank and may require the repatriation/surrender of US dollars for any proportion needed to pay local taxes and fees.Reinsurance claims, profit commission payments and return-premium disbursements to group cedants are settled by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for correspondent-dependent flows), with GPI tracking and a beneficiary-account pre-validation. A claim file must include a board-approved claims settlement release, the loss reserve certificate, adjuster report, and the ceding company bordereaux. For collateralised reinsurance collateral release, the SPV will use a clean payment instruction through the bank rather than a commercial letter of credit; should an LC be unavoidable in a finance side-collateral agreement, the Samoan dealer bank will issue a fully cash-covered letter of credit (MT700/MT707) with maturity matching the reinsurance loss reserve. All outbound payments above a set threshold are checked by the Exchange Control Department to verify prior approval and tax clearance; no onward remittance is allowed until the bank records the purpose code (e.g., 3610 – reinsurance premium, 3410 – claims settlement).Deliverable plain-vanilla forward contracts in USD/WST, AUD/WST, JPY/WST with tenors up to 12 months, transacted through an authorised dealer bank and reserved strictly against documented reinsurance receivable or ceded claim exposure.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps between WST and USD/AUD/JPY, of one-year minimum notional, permitted on a hedge-only basis and subject to individual pre-approval from the Central Bank of Samoa.Cash-collateralised foreign-exchange options used as protective collars (zero-cost or cost-reducing) in deliverable currencies, prohibited from naked or speculative trading and executed under a bilateral ISDA/CSA with the dealer bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not statutorily codified for International Companies; prudent controlled transaction threshold for captive reinsurance SPV: SAT 1,500,000 (approx USD 550,000) in aggregate annual related-party reinsurance or service transactions above which contemporaneous Master File and Local File documentation is advised

Economic substance scope: Samoa has no general Economic Substance Regulations equivalent to other offshore jurisdictions; however, FISA-licensed captive reinsurance companies must satisfy a de facto economic substance condition: a licensed registered agent with a registered office in Samoa, local resident director (at least one), adequate administrative staff to maintain books and board minutes, local management oversight of reinsurance risk, and performance of key decisions in Samoa; lack of substance may be challenged by foreign Revenue authorities as 'sham entity' or 'conduit SPV'

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Domestic Source Business

Statutory sanction: Loss of IC tax-exempt status; assessment to all unpaid income tax at 27% for a period of up to seven years; penalty of 200% of underpaid tax plus monthly default interest of 2%; criminal fine up to SAT 1,000,000

Director personal liability: Directors, including non-resident directors, are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax if they knowingly signed an incorrect exemption attestation; criminal conviction for tax evasion carries imprisonment up to seven years; travel ban may issue pending collection of tax debt
Shell Company Devoid of Substance

Statutory sanction: FISA refusal to renew the insurance licence; revocation or suspension of licence with immediate cessation of reinsurance business; fines per Insurance Act 2007 up to SAT 250,000 per non-compliance, and an order for winding-up of the company

Director personal liability: Directors of an SPV found to be a sham may be personally liable for outstanding debts of the SPV under the Credit of Unregistered Business doctrine; local director may be disqualified by Samoa Registrar for a maximum of fifteen years; non-resident directors subject to contempt orders and assets-tracing proceedings carried out by theOfficial Administrator
Money Laundering / AML-ATF Breach

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa AML/CFT Act 2009 and FISA directives: civil penalty of up to USD 1,000,000; offence of money laundering carries imprisonment up to 25 years and unlimited fines; licence revocation; forfeiture of all related assets

Director personal liability: Sanctioned directors face personal criminal liability, confiscation orders against their personal assets, travel bans, and disqualification from holding directorships in Samoa or any FATF-compliant jurisdiction; a successful conviction results in imprisonment
Unlicensed Insurance / Unauthorized Reinsurance

Statutory sanction: FISA order to cease business and refund premiums; administrative penalty up to USD 500,000; each reinsurance transaction void as against public policy; the entity may not enforce rights under reinsurance agreements

Director personal liability: Any director or officer who knowingly allows unlicensed insurance business may incur personal liability for all claims made by ceding insurers; upon insolvency, a court may 'pierce the corporate veil' if the company is a mere façade of the director or parent; directors face prosecution under the Insurance Act with imprisonment up to ten years
False filings and Misrepresentation to Registrar/FISA

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine or imprisonment up to fourteen years under Samoa Crimes Act; mandatory deregistration; FISA may void the insurance licence; the entity and officers are barred from re-licensure for a period of five years

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the false return are personally accountable for restitution to any creditor who relied; the Director of Public Prosecutions may seek an asset freeze order, travel ban, and surrender of passport pending trial; imprisonment sentence is mandatory upon conviction
Terrorism Financing and Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Penalty of imprisonment up to 30 years and fine of SAT 2,000,000; freezing order on all assets held by the SPV; termination of the Samoa International Company registration as a matter of public interest

Director personal liability: Directors with knowledge or imperfect oversight may be prosecuted individually regardless of corporate form; travel bans and extradition may be initiated under Samoa international cooperation legislation; property in Samoa may be confiscated irrespective of legal title held
