>=25% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: International Companies Act 1987 (Act No. 11 of 1987-88), as amended, including the International Companies Amendment Act 2015 which mandatorily requires beneficial ownership records for every Samoa International Company; read with the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 (as amended) and the AML/CFT Guidelines of the Central Bank of Samoa / Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit (SFIU); beneficial ownership and CDD records are filed with the licensed Samoan registered agent and the MCIL International Companies Registry, with onward lawful disclosure to SFIU, Samoa Police, Customs, Revenue and foreign regulators on exchange-of-information requests.
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

The statutory registered office of a Samoa International Company must be a physical address in Samoa maintained by a licensed registered agent; a virtual mailbox or shell address cannot satisfy the statutory registered-office obligation, and the IC must be able to receive service of process and government correspondence at that physical address. Because International Companies are prohibited from carrying on business with Samoan residents and from owning Samoan real estate, the operating team of the App Growth Agency may work fully remotely outside Samoa; a virtual office for the SEO/ASO delivery teams in China, Southeast Asia or elsewhere is permitted and does not create Samoan corporate tax residency, while the Samoan registered agent remains the legal service address.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), Ministry of Finance, Government of Samoa; official portal: https://sra.gov.ws with SRA Online e-Services for TIN, VAGST and e-filing.

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned International Company must obtain a Samoa Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by submitting the prescribed TIN application form to SRA, accompanied by certified copy of the International Companies Act 1987 certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, certified passport copies of directors/shareholders, proof of registered agent or office address in Samoa, and a written authorisation to the resident registered agent. Applications may be filed online via SRA e-Services or at SRA Apia head office; SRA issues the TIN within 3-10 business days, subject to verification of beneficial ownership and AML/KYC clearance.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Foreign-owned International Company must obtain a Samoa Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by submitting the prescribed TIN application form to SRA, accompanied by certified copy of the International Companies Act 1987 certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, certified passport copies of directors/shareholders, proof of registered agent or office address in Samoa, and a written authorisation to the resident registered agent. Applications may be filed online via SRA e-Services or at SRA Apia head office; SRA issues the TIN within 3-10 business days, subject to verification of beneficial ownership and AML/KYC clearance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists in Samoa. An International Company may elect under the International Companies Act 1987 to register as a domestic (resident) company, irrevocably converting to full Samoa tax residency and losing the statutory tax-exempt status; otherwise the IC remains a tax-exempt non-resident entity. A separate VAGST registration election is available only if the IC makes taxable supplies within Samoa, which is generally prohibited for a licensed IC; no passthrough or flow-through entity election is recognised under the Income Tax Act 2007.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 per annum of taxable turnover; compulsory registration applies once annual taxable supplies exceed this threshold, and voluntary registration is available for persons making taxable supplies in Samoa below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under VAGST applies to (i) exported goods physically removed from Samoa within the prescribed timeframe and supported by customs export records, and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident recipient outside Samoa where the benefit of the services is received offshore and the supply does not relate directly to land or goods situated in Samoa. The International Company's offshore App Store Optimization, multilingual SEO and localization agency services supplied solely to non-residents, invoiced in foreign currency and paid from abroad, are eligible for 0% VAGST as exported services or are otherwise outside the scope of Samoa VAGST, provided no taxable supply occurs within Samoa.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic CusDEC lodged with Samoa Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods (where applicable)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, bank credit advice, or SWIFT MT103 evidencing foreign-currency payment received from the non-resident client
  • Executed service agreement or detailed sales invoice demonstrating the non-resident status of the client and offshore delivery of services
  • Samoa Certificate of Origin or VAT/GST exemption certificate where required by the destination jurisdiction

No real-time fiscal clearance, API connectivity, or digital stamping requirement is presently mandated by SRA. Registered VAGST persons must issue compliant VAGST tax invoices and credit notes in the prescribed format containing all statutory fields; electronic filing and payment of VAGST returns is mandatory through SRA Online e-Services, but there is no government pre-clearance or real-time invoice reporting system.

SRA auditors scrutinise export zero-rating claims against the full documentation bundle; failure to maintain adequate export evidence triggers retrospective clawback of unlawfully claimed VAGST input credits and repayment of missed output tax, plus penalty tax of 100% to 150% of the shortfall and an additional interest charge under the Tax Administration Act 2012. Knowingly false or fraudulent export zero-rating documentation constitutes an offence punishable by criminal fines and imprisonment, with directors, beneficial owners, and the resident registered agent jointly and severally liable for assessed amounts.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current account transactions are generally convertible for bona fide trade and service payments upon documentation. Capital account transactions, including inbound equity injections and foreign share transfers, require prior CBS Exchange Control approval or must be effected through an Authorized Dealer. Profit remittance is permitted subject to submission of audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and a remittance application evidencing foreign investment registration. Practical hurdles include documentary back-log, limited local correspondent banking relationships, and CBS scrutiny of intra-group payments for which transfer-pricing documentation may be mandated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters must repatriate full export proceeds through an Authorized Dealer within 90 days from the date of shipment. mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of the WST-equivalent export proceeds, unless a written exemption is granted by the CBS for proceeds retained in an approved foreign currency account. The exporter is required to provide a bill of lading, commercial invoice, customs export declaration, and bank credit advice confirming inward receipt prior to release of conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), Exchange Control Division

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low feasibility. Wise B2B and Payoneer are not licensed as banks or electronic money institutions in Samoa and cannot serve as the primary regulated clearing rail for settlement in Samoan tala or as local receivables accounts. They may function only as ancillary client collection or vendor payout channels, provided underlying transactions are recorded in the primary Samoan bank account and identified purpose codes are supplied. CBS exchange control rules require all Samoa-sourced and export-related flows to route through an Authorized Dealer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity subscription or shareholder loan proceeds must be remitted via SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the remitter’s bank to a CBS Authorized Dealer in Samoa using purpose code 'FDI – Equity Capital Contribution' or 'Intercompany Debt – Shareholder Subordinated Loan'. The bank requires a Board Resolution, updated register of shareholders, signed subscription agreement, and a completed CBS Exchange Control Form EX-1. For a Samoa International Company, capital contributions may be held in a designated Capital Escrow Account pending business use; the contribution must match the exact remitter name and the stated corporate purpose, and the bank will verify that no third-party payment instruction is hidden within the transfer narrative.Outbound trade settlements are cleared through SWIFT MT103 for direct vendor payments and through Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP600) for contract-backed import or service obligations. Local currency (WST) disbursements require settlement through the Samoa automated clearing house; foreign-currency disbursements are processed by the Authorized Dealer with supporting invoice, contract, and CBS form T/I for trade and non-trade payments. Vendor payments routed to Wise B2B or Payoneer should be structured as separate MT103 wires with client-specific remittance info, ensuring the Samoa bank’s beneficiary account is named as the same legal entity for AML traceability. The agency must ensure that outbound disbursements are not used for settlements with sanctioned entities and that CBS reporting thresholds for transactions above WST 100,000 are observed.Bank-issued WST/USD, WST/AUD, and WST/NZD deliverable forward contracts up to 12 months, available only through ANZ Bank Samoa or BSP with a CBS-approved underlying exposure declaration.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for exotic or offshore currency pairs, confined to USD and AUD referencing sources, structured through the bank’s offshore treasury desk and requiring hedging-scheme approval when in excess of USD 500,000.Cross-currency swaps for WST-linked principal flows, permissible solely with an Authorized Dealer under a CBS Exchange Control exemption and only where a matched commercial exposure is demonstrated.Interest-rate and FX options are generally restricted; plain-vanilla European FX options may be granted for hedging service receipts where the premium is funded from operating cash flow and settled through the Samoan banking channel.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 1,000,000 (administrative de minimis for cross-border related-party transactions; Samoa TP rules under the Income Tax Act 2012 and Tax Administration Act 2012 apply OECD ALP principles, but no express Local File/Master File monetary threshold is gazetted; ICs are outside the TP regime because they have no taxable income)

Economic substance scope: Applicable: Samoa's International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 requires every IC engaged in a 'relevant activity' — including holding of intellectual property, distribution and service center businesses, and pure holding companies — to demonstrate substantial economic presence in Samoa. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Offshore Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Under the Tax Administration Act 2012 and Crimes Act 2013: civil penalty up to 150% of the tax evaded plus interest at 10% per annum; criminal fines up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years; automatic disclosure to foreign tax authorities under the Common Reporting Standard and Exchange of Information framework

Director personal liability: Piercing of corporate veil: directors and ultimate beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid tax; imposition of travel bans, asset freezing orders, and extradition proceedings; personal imprisonment for up to 7 years
Shell Company / Zero Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2019 and International Companies Act 1987: fixed penalty up to WST 10,000 per violation; daily default penalty up to WST 1,000; maximum aggregate fines up to USD 500,000 or WST 10,000,000 for aggravated non-compliance; automatic exchange of non-compliant entity data with foreign tax authorities; administrative strike-off of the company

Director personal liability: Directors face disqualification orders from serving as directors of any Samoan company for up to 5 years; personal fines of up to USD 250,000 where willful non-compliance is proven; directors' personal assets may be reached for fines unpaid by the company
AML/CFT Violation (Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure)

Statutory sanction: Under the Financial Intelligence Unit Act 2000 (as amended) and Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act: fines up to WST 200,000; imprisonment up to 7 years; freezing and forfeiture of funds held in any payment rail; referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal prosecution

Director personal liability: Directors named in criminal proceedings, subject to personal travel restrictions and passport surrender while under investigation; personal liability for the value of laundered assets; disqualification from holding financial accounts in Samoa; imprisonment of up to 7 years
Fraudulent Trading and Asset Stripping

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act 1987, sections 157 and 179: court-ordered compensation to creditors; mandatory winding-up; voidable disposition of assets; statutory strike-off with forfeiture of corporate assets to the Crown

Director personal liability: Civil liability for the full amount of the company's debts incurred during fraudulent trading; personal civil restitution orders against director estates; global asset freeze; imprisonment up to 5 years for fraudulent trading under Samoa's Companies Winding-Up Act and International Companies Act
