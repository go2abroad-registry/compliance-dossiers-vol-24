Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Government of Samoa — online eServices portal at www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A non-resident-owned Samoa International Company (IC) is first incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 through a licensed registered agent (TCSP). The agent files Form MfR-TIN-01 with the Ministry for Revenue together with the Certificate of Incorporation, the IC charter, proof of the registered agent relationship, and identification evidence of the ultimate beneficial owner; a TIN is issued within 5–10 business days via the eServices portal. For an IC wholly engaged in international business with no Samoan-source income and no transactions with Samoa residents, no active income tax registration arises; VAGST registration is required only where the IC makes taxable supplies in Samoa, or is voluntarily registered to claim input credits.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident-owned Samoa International Company (IC) is first incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 through a licensed registered agent (TCSP). The agent files Form MfR-TIN-01 with the Ministry for Revenue together with the Certificate of Incorporation, the IC charter, proof of the registered agent relationship, and identification evidence of the ultimate beneficial owner; a TIN is issued within 5–10 business days via the eServices portal. For an IC wholly engaged in international business with no Samoan-source income and no transactions with Samoa residents, no active income tax registration arises; VAGST registration is required only where the IC makes taxable supplies in Samoa, or is voluntarily registered to claim input credits.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa law provides no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. Under the International Companies Act 1987, the IC automatically enjoys exemption from Samoan income tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and estate duty on international business, subject to an annual renewal fee. The IC may elect (by special resolution filed with the Samoa Companies Office) to re-domicile or convert to an ordinary resident company under the Companies Act 2006, thereby entering the territorial taxation regime of the Income Tax Act 2012; the IC may also elect to register for VAGST under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2018 if it makes taxable supplies in Samoa.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WS$100,000 (WST 100,000) annual taxable turnover for taxable supplies made in Samoa; mandatory registration at or above threshold, voluntary registration below threshold. Non-resident suppliers of imported digital services to Samoan consumers must separately register once annual digital turnover exceeds WS$30,000 under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2018, as amended.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Digital webtoon publishing and multilingual chapter-unlocking revenues qualify for the 0% VAGST rate as exported services under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2018 where: (i) the recipient is non-resident and physically outside Samoa at the time of supply (including purchases via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing by overseas readers); (ii) the services are not enjoyed in Samoa and do not relate to land or goods in Samoa; (iii) the IC retains Merchant-of-Record agreements, platform settlement reports, and payout advices evidencing offshore consumption; and (iv) the IC, if VAGST-registered, substantiates input credits only for expenses attributable to the export activity. Because the IC operates as a Tax_Exempt_Zone entity, the exemption is typically enjoyed outside the net-invoicing mechanism, with refund claims arising only in the voluntary-registration scenario.

  • Customs Export Declaration — not applicable to electronically delivered services; substituted by platform settlement reports showing per-country gross sales
  • Bill of Lading — not applicable to digital delivery; substituted by Apple IAP / Google Play Merchant-of-Record payout and remittance statements
  • Merchant of Record agreements between WS_IC, Apple Inc. (iTunes Store), and Google LLC (Play Store)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) from the IC's licensed bank in Samoa, or equivalent SWIFT/ACH payout advices from the platforms to the IC's bank account
  • End-user licensing and multilingual chapter-unlocking logs demonstrating delivery solely to non-resident customers outside Samoa

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or government digital stamping applies in Samoa. Registered businesses must issue sequentially numbered tax invoices containing prescribed supplier/customer details and the VAGST amount, file monthly or two-monthly VAGST returns, and pay via the MfR eServices portal; supporting electronic records must be retained for at least 5 years. MfR conducts risk-based automated cross-matching of declared export turnover against platform settlement and banking data, but does not pre-clear individual invoices.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit any 0% export zero-rating claim under the Tax Administration Act 2015, issuing a default assessment that claws back up to 3 years of uncharged VAGST at 15% (extended to 8 years for evasion and without time limit for fraud), plus a shortfall penalty of up to 150% of the tax avoided, 2% monthly interest, and late-payment penalties. Criminal prosecution for intentional evasion under Section 85 of the Tax Administration Act 2015 carries a fine of up to WS$1,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 7 years per count. Additionally, the Central Bank of Samoa may impose exchange-control sanctions on failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day deadline, and the IC faces deregistration and loss of its tax-exemption certificate under the International Companies Act 1987 for fraudulent or falsely maintained export documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is convertible for current-account international transactions, including software and digital content purchases, royalty remittances, webtoon license payments, and profit remittances from bona fide invested capital. Capital-account transfers, non-resident intercompany loans, intra-family trust injections, and offshore treasury placements remain subject to Central Bank prior authorization. For an International Company (WS_IC) deriving digital webtoon licensing revenue from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing, the ordinary remittance of platform royalties is treated as a current-account service fee once evidenced by the platform settlement statement and license agreement. However, any capital transaction structured as a subscription to equity, an interest-free shareholder loan, or a hybrid convertible note will trigger exchange-control review with documentary proof of economic substance in Samoa.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa maintains a repatriation-based export proceeds rule rather than a blanket mandatory percentage for digital exports. All export receipts, including online webtoon syndication income and advertising revenue, must be repatriated to a licensed Samoan bank account within 90 days of receipt by the offshore collection agent or payment gateway. There is no fixed statutory surrender ratio for digital IP exports; the Central Bank may apply a temporary surrender directive of up to 25% of realised foreign currency proceeds during a balance-of-payments emergency. At all events, the exporter must retain, for a minimum of five years, a documented trail showing net proceeds after deduction of Apple and Google commission fees, currency conversion costs, and downstream artist royalties. Failure to repatriate on time or launching a false online submission will be treated as a breach of the Exchange Control Regulation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984), acting in conjunction with the Samoan Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) and the Ministry of Revenue. The Central Bank administers the Exchange Control Regulation 1974, as amended, and is the sole licensing and supervisory authority for all bank and non-bank clearing channels.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% for resident companies under the Income Tax Act 2017; 0% effective rate for International Companies (International Companies Act 1987, s.198) on all income derived outside Samoa, provided the IC does not carry on business in Samoa and derives no Samoan-source income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for International Companies under the statutory ICA exemption; the general Samoan dividend withholding tax rate for resident companies paying non-residents is 15%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Local File or Master File obligation for International Companies because they are outside the Samoan income tax base; resident companies must prepare contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation when related-party transactions exceed WST 1,000,000 per tax year under the Income Tax Act 2017.

Economic substance scope: Samoa is a member of the OECD/G20 Inclusive Framework on BEPS. An IC is non-resident for Samoan tax purposes and must demonstrate that management, content licensing decisions, and commercial operations occur in the beneficial owner's Taiwan establishment. A shell IC without substance in any jurisdiction attracts BEPS minimum-standard, anti-money-laundering (AML/CFT Act 2019), and cross-border information-exchange scrutiny.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Prohibited Domestic Carrying-On of Business

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of the ICA tax exemption, assessment as a resident company at 27%, penalties up to 100% of tax avoided, and deregistration under the International Companies Act 1987.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for restitution of tax and penalties; the corporate veil is pierced for wilful misconduct, exposing directors to personal monetary judgments and disqualification.
Tax Evasion and Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: General anti-avoidance reassessment, denial of deductions, penalties of 100% of avoided tax, voidance of treaty benefits under the principal-purpose test, and potential nullification of the IC's exemption.

Director personal liability: Shadow-director and promoter liability attaches: civil restitution, financial penalties, travel bans, and disgorgement of professional fees for architects of the sham structure.
AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 14 years and/or fines up to WST 2,000,000 under the AML/CFT Act 2019, asset freezing and forfeiture, and compulsory strike-off of the IC.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign, authorize, or facilitate such transactions incur personal criminal prosecution, travel bans, asset-freezing orders, and lifetime disqualification from managing any Samoan company.
False Declaration in Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Summary criminal conviction, monetary fines, retroactive termination of the tax exemption, and the Registrar's discretionary deregistration.

Director personal liability: Statutory personal liability for fines and restoration costs, director disqualification, and imprisonment for knowingly falsifying the compliance declaration.
