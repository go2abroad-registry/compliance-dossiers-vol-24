Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Government of Samoa (successor to the Samoa Revenue Authority under the Ministry for Revenue Act 2022); statutory regulator for income tax, VAGST, and TIN issuance under the Tax Administration Act 2012; official e-services portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws (TIN registration, return filing, and Revenue Information Technology Application).

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company must obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by lodging Form TIN-01 with the Ministry for Revenue, accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation under the International Companies Act 2014, registered office address in Apia, certified passport copies of directors and ultimate beneficial owners, a licensed Samoa resident agent engagement letter, and beneficial ownership declarations; a foreign entity without a Samoa fixed establishment registers through a licensed resident agent and typically receives the TIN within 3–5 business days; a separate VAGST registration number must be obtained if the entity makes taxable supplies exceeding the threshold or elects voluntary registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: A Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt from all Samoan national and municipal taxation on income, capital gains, VAGST, stamp duty, and estate/gift duties under section 202 of the International Companies Act 2014, conditioned upon maintaining offshore-only activity, satisfying annual license fee and economic substance filing obligations, and not carrying on local business through a fixed place of business in Samoa. — A Samoa International Company must obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by lodging Form TIN-01 with the Ministry for Revenue, accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation under the International Companies Act 2014, registered office address in Apia, certified passport copies of directors and ultimate beneficial owners, a licensed Samoa resident agent engagement letter, and beneficial ownership declarations; a foreign entity without a Samoa fixed establishment registers through a licensed resident agent and typically receives the TIN within 3–5 business days; a separate VAGST registration number must be obtained if the entity makes taxable supplies exceeding the threshold or elects voluntary registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: A Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt from all Samoan national and municipal taxation on income, capital gains, VAGST, stamp duty, and estate/gift duties under section 202 of the International Companies Act 2014, conditioned upon maintaining offshore-only activity, satisfying annual license fee and economic substance filing obligations, and not carrying on local business through a fixed place of business in Samoa.

Available election forms

An IC may elect by special resolution or constitutional notice to waive the section 202 tax exemption and be assessed as a resident Samoa company under the Income Tax Act 2015; alternatively it may redomicile and continue as a foreign company under the Companies Act 2001. There is no US-style check-the-box election; Samoa's BEPS-aligned controlled-foreign-company, transfer-pricing, and anti-hybrid rules may reclassify or deny the exemption in abuse cases. For VAGST purposes, an IC may voluntarily register even below the WST 100,000 threshold, and related entities may elect consolidated group VAGST registration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoan Tala) of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period (threshold raised from WST 60,000 to WST 100,000 effective 2016); voluntary registration is permitted; a foreign supplier with no Samoa fixed establishment triggering reverse-charge obligations must register if supplying digital or electronically supplied services to Samoa-resident consumers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (1) goods physically exported from Samoa by the supplier before first supply to a Samoa resident; (2) services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Samoa at the time of supply and who derives no direct or indirect benefit from the service in Samoa; and (3) services supplied in connection with offshore goods or to foreign-going ships or aircraft. A TikTok MCN agency may zero-rate global creator-commission, talent-management, and offshore digital marketing services supplied to overseas creator affiliates and non-resident TikTok commercial counterparties, provided the service is performed wholly outside Samoa, no part is consumed by a Samoa-resident office, and contemporaneous documentation establishes the offshore supply chain. An IC enjoying the section 202 exemption remains wholly outside the VAGST net; zero-rating becomes relevant where the operating entity is a local Samoa company or an electing IC.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic submission via Samoa Department of Customs and Excise)
  • Commercial Invoice or VAGST Tax Invoice marked 'Zero-rated supply' with all TIN references
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 from a Samoa-licensed commercial bank evidencing foreign-currency receipt
  • Packing List and Certificate of Origin (where cargo is exported)
  • Service Contract, performance log, and reconciliation statement evidencing offshore service delivery (for zero-rated services)

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous audit transaction controls, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping mandate applies in Samoa (in contrast to Latin American and Middle Eastern regimes). Taxpayers must issue a prescribed VAGST tax invoice showing supplier name and TIN, recipient name and TIN, description of supply, invoice date, and separately stated 15% VAGST, and file VAGST returns electronically through the Ministry for Revenue e-services portal on a bimonthly basis; source records must be retained for 7 years and may be required in XML or system-generated format during audit.

The Ministry for Revenue may reassess and claw back zero-rated supplies within a 6-year reassessment window where documentation is incomplete, false, or fails to establish physical export or offshore recipient status; civil shortfall penalties of up to 100% of the unpaid VAGST may be imposed together with continuing monthly late-payment interest of 1.5%, cancellation of zero-rating approval, and disqualification of the exporter; fraudulent zero-rating claims constitute tax evasion under the Tax Administration Act 2012, punishable by criminal fines and/or imprisonment, public disclosure of the delinquent taxpayer, and loss of the IC exemption for the period concerned.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is a managed-float currency that is not freely tradable offshore; all onshore WST conversions must be executed through CBS-licensed authorised dealers. Current-account payments, including creator management fees and royalties, are generally permitted on submission of supporting commercial documentation. Capital-account transactions, such as equity injections, dividends, and profit repatriation, require prior exchange-control approval or coverage under a gazetted general permission. For a tax-exempt International Company, profit remittance hurdles are moderate but manageable: audited financial statements evidencing accumulated surplus, a registered-agent confirmation that the company has not transacted with Samoa residents, and a CBS remittance approval for the specific outflow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Samoa's Exchange Control Regulations, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days of export and surrendered to an authorised dealer in WST at the prevailing market rate, unless the CBS grants a foreign-currency retention licence. The default statutory conversion ratio is 100% of export receipts. For an offshore MCN agency receiving digital advertising and creator management revenue in USD, the CBS routinely grants an annual foreign-currency retention approval to allow the company to keep USD for subsequent offshore disbursements, subject to quarterly balance-of-payments and exchange-control reporting.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS); the national monetary authority and exchange control regulator, exercising delegated powers under the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, and the financial integrity framework overseen by the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium feasibility with defined legal constraints. Samoa does not license electronic money institutions, and offshore digital EMIs such as Wise B2B are not authorised dealers under Samoa's exchange-control regime. They may be used lawfully as disbursement rails for creator payouts, but the primary treasury account must remain with a CBS-licensed commercial bank for foreign-currency conversion and regulatory reporting. The EMI engagement should be structured as a non-deposit, contractual payment-agent service located outside Samoa, with explicit wording that it is not conducting banking business in Samoa.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use a single SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder or operating parent to the Samoa IC's USD operational account. Field 70 must state 'CAPITAL INJECTION — WS_IC' and the receiving bank must file an Inward Remittance Application using balance-of-payments code E2 for direct investment equity. CBS prior approval is not required for fresh equity in a non-resident International Company, but all inward credits at or above USD 50,000 must be reported to CBS. Supporting documents include the Certificate of Good Standing, Register of Members, board resolution authorising the capital call, shareholder source-of-funds declaration, and, for amounts exceeding USD 1 million, audited financial statements. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable to a tax-exempt International Company deriving only foreign-source international income. If an IC becomes subject to Samoan tax, transfer pricing documentation is required when the aggregate annual related-party transaction value exceeds WST 2 million or the entity enters into transactions with non-resident connected persons exceeding WST 500,000 in any single recordable transaction.

Economic substance scope: The Samoa Economic Substance Regulations apply to International Companies carrying on relevant activities such as distribution and service provision, financing, headquarters, and intellectual property holding. A global TikTok MCN agency providing creator management, brand representation, licensing, and marketing services is treated as a service-provision business and therefore must demonstrate economic substance in Samoa: a physical office or premises, at least one locally engaged officer or employee capable of making strategic decisions, board meetings held and minuted in Samoa, and core income-generating activities conducted in Samoa. Pure shell companies without Samoan administrative presence or board control are subject to ESR penalties, substantive recharacterization, and possible loss of exempt status.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Misrepresentation of Foreign-Source Income

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 150% of the tax shortfall plus a fine of up to WST 300,000 under the Income Tax Ordinance 2012; criminal prosecution for tax evasion carrying imprisonment of up to 7 years; mandatory reassessment at the 27% corporate rate; freezing of bank accounts and attachment of digital payment rails such as Wise B2B and TikTok Creator payouts.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax, penalties, and interest. Directors may be subjected to travel bans, prohibition orders, disqualification from acting as directors for up to 10 years, civil restitution to the Revenue Authority, and personal imprisonment.
Shell Company with No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: SIFSA may impose administrative penalties of up to WST 250,000, order strike-off from the International Companies Register, cancel the tax exemption, and reassess income at the standard 27% corporate rate with late payment surcharges; repeated violations result in mandatory liquidation of the IC and barring of the registered agent from representing new companies.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent may be held personally liable for withdrawal of the company's license, restoration costs, and unpaid taxes. Directors face civil liability for fraudulent trading, personal asset freezes, disqualification for up to 10 years, and imprisonment if the sham structure is used to defraud creditors or tax authorities.
Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Act 2017 as amended, a body corporate may be fined up to WST 1 million per offense; business operations may be suspended; funds and virtual assets may be frozen or confiscated; and criminal prosecution may lead to imprisonment of responsible individuals for up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined up to WST 100,000, subjected to travel bans, and disqualified from holding financial services licenses. Where directors knowingly participate in money laundering or fail to report suspicious transactions, they face criminal conviction, confiscation of personal and corporate assets, and imprisonment.
False Annual Return and Fraudulent Trading

Statutory sanction: Monetary fines of up to WST 50,000 per false statement, automatic late-filing surcharges of USD 20 per day, court-ordered strike-off, revocation of the IC certificate, and liability in civil proceedings for damages to aggrieved parties.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause the filing of false returns are personally liable for all penalties, damages, and costs. The court may impose personal contribution orders, travel bans, disqualification from managing any Samoan company for up to 10 years, and imprisonment of up to 2 years for fraudulent trading.
