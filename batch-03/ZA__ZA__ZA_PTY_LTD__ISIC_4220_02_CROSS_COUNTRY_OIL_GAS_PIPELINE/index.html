Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

South African Revenue Service (SARS) — eFiling Portal: https://www.sarsefiling.co.za; head office at SARS Taxpayer Services, Pretoria, Republic of South Africa

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity carrying on enterprise in South Africa must first obtain an income tax reference number via SARS eFiling by submitting the entity's certificate of incorporation, memorandum of incorporation, proof of foreign registration, and a completed SARS RA01 registration form naming a South African representative taxpayer. For a foreign branch operating under a South African (Pty) Ltd structure, the company must apply for a taxpayer reference number and simultaneously apply for VAT vendor registration once taxable supplies exceed ZAR 1,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period, or voluntarily from ZAR 50,000. Non-resident suppliers of electronic services must register separately with SARS for VAT and appoint a representative vendor. The eFiling platform issues the statutory income tax TIN and VAT registration number, which must be quoted on all invoices, customs declarations, and VAT201 returns.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a South African Proprietary Limited Company is a resident company subject to income tax on its worldwide income at the standard corporate rate of 27% under the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962. No pass-through treatment is available. Cross-border oil and gas pipeline EPC revenue is includible in gross income unless a specific s10(1) exemption applies.

Available election forms

South Africa does not administer a check-the-box election comparable to the United States. Relevant statutory elections include the controlled foreign company net-income inclusion elections under s9D, the hybrid mismatch adjustment elections under s9JA and s23M, the thin-capitalisation funding allowance election under s24C, the group loss-transfer election under s45 applicable to intra-group transactions, and the s12E(4) small business corporation election which is unavailable to corporate-owned entities. The cross-country pipeline contractor may also elect the s11D research and development allowance where eligible engineering process improvements are deployed.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration threshold: ZAR 1,000,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period. Voluntary registration threshold: ZAR 50,000. Non-resident electronic services threshold: ZAR 50,000, requiring appointment of a SARS representative vendor.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for zero-rating under s11(1)(a) of the VAT Act 89 of 1991 where movable goods are supplied to a non-resident and are removed from South Africa within 90 days of the time of supply. Services qualifying for zero-rating under s11(2)(k) include engineering, earthworks, trenching, welding, and pipeline construction services physically rendered outside South African territory. For the cross-country gas pipeline EPC contract, zero-rating also applies to the export of capital equipment and project materials that are cleared by SARS Customs and shipped to the non-resident project site; the contractor must retain the written contract, the customs export declaration, transportation documents, and bank records evidencing foreign-currency settlement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD500 / DA185) accepted and stamped by SARS Customs
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR road consignment note evidencing physical export from South Africa
  • Inward Foreign Exchange Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/telegraphic transfer record proving receipt of export proceeds
  • Signed international supply contract (EPC agreement) containing jurisdiction, payment, and delivery terms
  • VAT201 reconciliation schedule declaring the zero-rated exports and the s11 statutory justification
  • Certificate of Origin (SACU form) where preferential tariff treatment is claimed at the destination

South Africa currently operates no compulsory real-time fiscal clearance stamp; however, SARS has issued a Digital Compliance Framework requiring structured, machine-readable VAT invoices and phased migration to a centralised e-invoicing clearance platform integrated with SARS eFiling through API and MARC/EDI connectivity. Full electronic VAT invoices issued by pipeline contractors must display the SARS vendor number, description of the supply, quantity and value, VAT amount, and the mandatory certificate wording for zero-rated supplies. SARS performs real-time validation on VAT201 electronic submissions and automatically suspends refunds where the export documentation pack is incomplete.

SARS subjects zero-rated export claims to rigorous VAT refund integrity review. Failure to produce the s11 documentary evidence within 90 days, or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day deadline, triggers a retrospective VAT assessment at 15% on the full contract value with statutory interest. Under the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011, false or negligent zero-rating claims attract understatement penalties of up to 200% of the shortfall (s223), and refunds may be clawed back together with unlawful-avoidance penalties. In cases of fraud or deliberate misdeclaration, criminal prosecution may be instituted under s234 of the Tax Administration Act and the Customs and Excise Act 91 of 1964, exposing the entity and its responsible officers to financial penalties of up to 100% of the value of the exported goods and imprisonment without the option of a fine.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

ZAR is a fully deliverable and generally convertible currency for current-account purposes; dividends, interest, royalties, and trade payments are freely remittable through an Authorised Dealer without prior SARB approval provided the underlying non-resident shareholding has been recorded with the AD and the company holds a valid SARS tax-clearance status. Capital-account flows are permitted but subject to exchange-control oversight: non-resident equity disinvestment requires documented evidence of the original inward capital flow, external shareholder loans require prior SARB FinSurv approval above delegated AD limits, and profit remittance attracts Dividends Tax at 20% in the absence of treaty relief (reduced to 5-15% under most DTAs). There are no current-account quotas, no queuing mechanisms, no Tobin taxes, and no surrender ratios on a going-forward basis under the 2021 regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The historical mandatory surrender/conversion ratio was abolished under the Financial Surveillance Regulations, 2021; no percentage of export proceeds must be delivered to SARB or compulsorily converted into ZAR. However, all proceeds from exported goods or services under the cross-country pipeline EPC scope must be repatriated into South Africa within 180 days from the date of shipment or service completion, extendable to 12 months by application to the AD on bona fide commercial grounds. Repatriated amounts must either be credited to an SARB-approved Foreign Currency Account (FCA) held in the name of the Cape Town/Pretoria entity or sold into the domestic market immediately; each transaction is recorded on the AD's electronic Form A reporting system with supporting SARS customs documentation (SAD500) and the underlying commercial contract.

Central bank supervision: South African Reserve Bank (SARB), acting through the Financial Surveillance Department (FinSurv) under delegated authority of the Minister of Finance, and exercising statutory powers under the Exchange Control Regulations, 1961, the Financial Surveillance Regulations, 2021 (GN 386 of 30 April 2021), and the Banks Act, 1990. All operational foreign-exchange activity must be executed through commercial banks licensed as Authorised Dealers (ADs) and supervised by the SARB Prudential Authority, with the Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC) enforcing the FICA anti-money-laundering framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: LOW for core treasury and CONDITIONAL only for auxiliary ancillary flows. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, and similar) are not licensed under the Banks Act, 1990, and are not registered as Authorised Dealers with SARB; they cannot open ZAR settlement accounts, cannot clear Form A exchange-control declarations, and cannot report inward/outward flows to SARB FinSurv. Routing pipeline EPC revenues or capital injections through an unregistered EMI would break the 180-day repatriation trail and expose directors to criminal liability under the Exchange Control Regulations. An EMI may be used only for de-minimis operating disbursements (SaaS, subscription fees) where funding first clears through a local AD, and the local bank remains the sole reporting and settlement counterparty.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident equity injection must be routed by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the shareholder's bank to the ZA company's designated 'Capital - Non-Resident' ZAR or Foreign Currency Account held at the chosen Authorised Dealer. Immediately on receipt, the AD files the inward direct-investment return under the BPM6 financial-account classification (Share Capital / Direct Investment Equity) and issues a non-resident marker on the shareholding register, which is the legal predicate for all future dividend and disinvestment remittances. The wire purpose field must state: 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION - [COMPANY NAME] - DIRECT INVESTMENT - NON-RESIDENT SHAREHOLDER'. Within 30 days the AD requires the certified board resolution approving the issue, the signed subscription agreement, the remitter's source-of-funds statement, and the official share register. Where funding is structured as a shareholder loan rather than equity, prior SARB FinSurv approval is mandatory through an external-loan application, making plain equity the fastest and most reliable routing rail; optional conversion to ZAR is executed at the prevailing market rate through the AD.Outbound EPC disbursements clear through three rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 customer transfer for working capital, subcontractor payments, demurrage, insurance, and services, each preceded by an electronic Form A declaration to the AD and closed within 30 days by the service invoice or customs SAD500 for goods; (2) commercial documentary letters of credit governed by UCP 600 issued by the AD for imported line pipe, welding materials, and rotating equipment, settled at sight or deferred tenor against compliant shipping documents, representing the primary rail for high-value procurement; and (3) standby letters of credit and advance-payment guarantees under URDG 758 issued to the project owner and offtaker for performance, advanced-payment, and retention security. Related-party cross-border payments require contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation and confirmation of SARS tax-compliant status to avoid AD suspension or rejection of the Form A.Onshore deliverable Forward Exchange Contracts (FECs) executed through the AD for USD/EUR-denominated EPC milestone receivables and imported procurement payables, with tenors of 12-24 months acceptable where the underlying exposure is contractually confirmed.Plain-vanilla European-style FX options purchased from the AD for contingent tender-stage, bid-bond, and pricing-risk exposure, with premiums settled upfront from the operational account.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) for long-dated project financing in which USD or EUR debt is swapped into ZAR, subject to SARB FinSurv or AD approval and registration of the underlying external-loan agreement.Foreign-Currency Accounts (FCA/FFC) approved for retention of export proceeds up to the entity's operational limits, enabling natural hedging of USD-denominated procurement costs without drawing on the onshore interbank market.Non-deliverable forwards on ZAR are not permissible as an onshore rail because ZAR is fully deliverable; an offshore NDF may be used only by an offshore affiliate hedging an offshore-sourced exposure and may not be booked in the South African treasury.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: LOW for core treasury and CONDITIONAL only for auxiliary ancillary flows. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, and similar) are not licensed under the Banks Act, 1990, and are not registered as Authorised Dealers with SARB; they cannot open ZAR settlement accounts, cannot clear Form A exchange-control declarations, and cannot report inward/outward flows to SARB FinSurv. Routing pipeline EPC revenues or capital injections through an unregistered EMI would break the 180-day repatriation trail and expose directors to criminal liability under the Exchange Control Regulations. An EMI may be used only for de-minimis operating disbursements (SaaS, subscription fees) where funding first clears through a local AD, and the local bank remains the sole reporting and settlement counterparty.
