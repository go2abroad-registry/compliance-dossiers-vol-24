E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is automatically exempt from all Samoan national taxes, including income tax and VAGST, under section 193 of that Act. No TIN or VAGST registration number is required for the IC's offshore business operations (e.g., AI Avatar Video Generation SaaS). Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tālā (WST) is not an internationally deliverable offshore currency; convertibility exists only through CBS-licensed authorized dealers onshore. Current account transactions (software export receipts, royalties, professional fees) are freely convertible through authorized dealers under delegated CBS authority without prior central bank approval. Capital account transactions (equity injection, foreign loans, share redemption, dividend streaming, liquidation distributions) remain subject to the Exchange Control Act 1976: transactions within a dealer's delegated limit are processed immediately, while transactions above that bandwidth require a CBS capital-account application with a typical 5–10 business day turnaround. Profit remittance for an International Company (IC) is practically feasible but requires (i) proof of genuine distributable profits via examined/audited financials or a registered-agent solvency certificate, (ii) certification that the underlying inward funds entered Samoa through the banking system, and (iii) absence of delinquent Samoan liabilities. For this AI Avatar Video SaaS business, the convertibility profile is low-risk: customers are non-resident, revenue is export service income, and only procurement payments and profit distributions cross the capital account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1976, all export proceeds — including SaaS and software service receipts — must be repatriated into the Samoan banking system within 90 days from invoice/export date and must be offered for sale to an authorized dealer in exchange for WST unless CBS approves a foreign currency retention limit. Samoa imposes no fixed percentage conversion ratio (e.g., 50% or 80% surrender); the operative rule is effectively full repatriation and offer-and-sale (100%) unless a formal retention approval is granted to fund future foreign-currency procurement. The IC benefits from statutory exchange-control exemptions under the International Companies Act 1987 for dealings exclusively with non-residents, but once SaaS revenue is credited to a Samoan account, the authorized dealer records the inflow as export proceeds and applies the surrender/retention regime.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Apia, Samoa — statutory administrator of the Exchange Control Act 1976 and operator of the national real-time gross settlement system (Samoa Interbank Payment System, SIPS).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

LOW as an onshore clearing rail; MEDIUM as an offshore collection adjunct. Samoa has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution licensing class; the Financial Institutions Act 1996 requires a banking license for deposit-taking or domestic payment-system operation, and CBS would not permit an unlicensed offshore EMI (e.g., Wise, Mercury, Airwallex) to hold settlement accounts with CBoS or clearing privileges in SIPS. Offshore EMIs may legally sit upstream of the Samoan banking system: Stripe/Paddle collect client card/ACH receipts abroad, and the EMI issues a SWIFT USD payment to the IC's authorized-dealer account in Samoa. That layered structure is permissible because the EMI has no Samoan nexus, but it does not displace the obligation to bring the SaaS export proceeds into Samoa and report them as export receipts. Stripe and Paddle remain the sanctioned customer-facing rails; neither substitutes for a Samoan authorized-dealer bank account for FX-control and treasury purposes.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

No mandatory upfront equity injection exists because Samoan IC law imposes zero minimum capital; founder funding should be characterized as share capital and remitted via SWIFT MT103 from each shareholder's own account using the originating jurisdiction's capital-account purpose code (e.g., 'S0101 – Capital subscription' or equivalent BOPS guidance code), with the wire narrative 'IC share subscription – [shareholder name] – WS_IC'. The Samoan authorized dealer will verify (i) the IC board resolution allotting shares, (ii) the updated share register reflecting the subscriber, (iii) the registered agent's written acknowledgement, and (iv) absence of local liabilities; the credit is typically available within 1–2 banking days, rising to 5–10 business days where CBS prior approval is triggered by transaction size. All inward capital credits should be escrowed in the USD operational account until compliance release; conversion into WST operating float is limited to lawful domestic administrative expenditure permitted for an IC under the International Companies Act 1987.SWIFT MT103 is the principal clearing rail for outbound SaaS disbursements (cloud hosting, GPU subcontracting, licensing fees, contractor payments). For larger procurement of physical IP or infrastructure, a documentary commercial letter of credit under UCP 600 (sight or usance) is available from BSP or ANZ, advised via SWIFT MT700 in USD, although it is atypical for a pure software venture. Domestic WST transfers clear through the CBS-operated Samoa Interbank Payment System (SIPS), while all non-resident payments above the authorized dealer's reporting threshold are captured for CBS balance-of-payments statistics. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Company (IC) under the Samoa International Companies Act; general Samoa resident corporate income tax rate is 27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on distributions paid by a Samoa International Company to non-resident shareholders. Samoa's general non-resident dividend withholding tax rate for taxable resident companies is 15%, but the IC is outside the income tax net.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The International Company Annual Return is due within 30 days after the anniversary of incorporation. A domestic Samoa taxpayer's income tax return is generally due within three months after year-end; a calendar-year company would file by 31 March.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No formal Samoa transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to International Companies. Samoa's tax law contains general anti-avoidance powers to recharacterise non-arm's length transactions. A prudent internal threshold is WST 500,000 in aggregate annual controlled transactions; above this level the IC should maintain simplified Local File and Master File documentation even though Samoan income tax is not levied.

Economic substance scope: Samoa has not enacted BVI/Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the International Companies Act requires a Samoa registered office and licensed resident agent. Foreign tax authorities will still apply CFC, managed-and-controlled, place-of-effective-management, and substance-over-form rules to the AI Avatar Video SaaS business. Samoa anti-avoidance law can also disregard an IC facade if the entity engages in actual Samoa-sourced business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Tax Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may disregard the IC under Samoa's general anti-avoidance provisions, issue a default assessment on true economic profit, impose a shortfall penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded, add late-payment interest, and pursue criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false exemption declarations or orchestrate evasion are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution and fraud penalties. The corporate veil may be pierced when the IC is a mere instrumentality; directors may face travel bans, extradition, disqualification, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Lack of Commercial Substance

Statutory sanction: The Samoa Registrar may reject the annual return, impose Registry arrears and, after continued non-compliance, strike the IC off and dissolve it. Source-country tax authorities may reclassify the AI software business as resident elsewhere, impose CFC taxation, or disregard the Samoa IC entirely.

Director personal liability: Nominee and de facto directors can be disqualified from acting as directors. Where limited liability is used to evade contractual or regulatory debts, directors may be personally exposed to civil claims and restoration costs, and travel bans may be imposed in cross-border tax collection.
Anti-Money Laundering / AML-CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Samoa's Money Laundering Prevention Act and Financial Intelligence Unit regulations authorise civil penalties, confiscation of proceeds, revocation of the registered agent's license, interruption of banking and merchant-account relationships, and criminal prosecution of the IC and its officers.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers with knowledge or suspicion of unlawful fund flows can be personally charged with money laundering or financing of terrorism. Penalties include imprisonment up to 14 years, asset forfeiture, fines, travel bans, and permanent disqualification from managing regulated entities.
False Filing / Fraudulent Misrepresentation in Corporate Registry

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's International Companies Act and general companies legislation, false statements are punishable by fines and imprisonment; the entity may be struck off, and third parties may recover damages based on the false registry information.

Director personal liability: Signing officers are personally liable for false declaration offences. Courts may impose civil restitution beyond corporate assets, order travel bans, disqualify the individual from serving as a director of any Samoa company, and impose custodial sentences.
