Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue, Government of Samoa (formerly Inland Revenue Department); online portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws/

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must complete the Samoa Tax Identification Number (TIN) application through the Ministry of Revenue's online registration facility, submitting a certified copy of the incorporation certificate, passport of directors, and proof of registered office. Inbound entities that will engage in taxable supplies must also complete a VAT/VAGST registration application on the same portal, providing the TIN, business details, and turnover projections. International Companies are exempt from income tax and are not ordinarily registered for income tax; they may, however, apply for a non-resident or exempt status certificate from the Ministry of Revenue where required by counterparties or banks.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration for any business making taxable supplies exceeding WST 100,000 in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available below threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered supplier may zero-rate a supply where it is a direct export of tangible goods (i.e., goods delivered outside Samoa and exported within 28 days of supply) or an international service supplied to a non-resident who consumes the services wholly outside Samoa, or where the supply is made in connection with goods for export. The supplier must hold documentary evidence proving export, receipt by the foreign customer, and actual receipt of payment from a source outside Samoa. Zero-rating is denied where the goods or services are consumed in Samoa.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Entry / Export Entry Number)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (signed by carrier)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing foreign exchange receipt from the overseas buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate in Samoa. Registered suppliers must issue an invoice (in paper or electronic form) containing prescribed content under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2003 and retain it for audit; no government clearance platform is required.

The Ministry of Revenue may scrutinize zero-rated export claims retrospectively. An erroneous or fraudulent zero-rating claim triggers full clawback of the refund, plus interest at the late-payment rate, and a civil shortfall penalty of up to 200% of the undeclared tax. Wilful evasion carries criminal prosecution with fines up to WST 200,000 and imprisonment up to 12 months for individuals, and directors may be personally liable for penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa's national currency, the WST (Tala), is partially convertible. Current account transactions (e.g., software licensing royalties, developer payments, app store disbursements, ongoing operational expenses) are generally permitted for authorized dealers. Capital account transactions, including inbound equity injections, shareholder loans, loan repayments, and repatriation of invested capital, require Exchange Control approval from the Central Bank of Samoa. Non-resident holders of an International Company (IC) may freely remit current-account profits and dividends after submission of audited financial statements and payment of applicable Samoa income tax; however, capital repatriation is subject to a documented trail of approved capital importation and CBS prior approval to ensure historical currency purchase validation. While the WST is not freely deliverable offshore, commercial banks quote onshore rates within CBS statutory margins; all FX transactions must be executed through an authorized dealer unless an explicit exemption has been granted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds derived from goods and services, including mobile app gross revenue remitted to Samoa, must be collected within 6 months of shipment or the date the obligation arises, and must be offered for sale to an authorized dealer in Samoa within 30 days of receipt in foreign currency. The mandatory conversion ratio is 100%, meaning all foreign currency received from export sales must be converted to WST at the applicable bank selling rate, unless the IC has secured a CBS foreign currency retention allowance (typically 25%–50% of eligible foreign currency inflows) for operational liquidity or an approved ring-fenced foreign currency account for trade settlement. Receipts supporting the Samoa IC's digital application business must be evidenced by the App Store OR Google Play settlement statements and reported to the Central Bank through the commercial bank on a monthly basis. Unconverted retention balances require quarterly justification requests.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1975 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1985; supervises all authorized dealers in foreign exchange, approves all capital account transactions, and administers Samoa's national payments and settlement infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility of using offshore digital financial institutions (EMI) or digital banks as treasury side-rails is rated LOW for the core Samoa IC. The Central Bank of Samoa restricts maintained licenses to authorized banking institutions; no recognized licensing pathway exists for an EMI domiciled in the EEA or Hong Kong to act as the IC's primary settlement bank. Offshore EMIs may be used only as complementary billing corridors for utility-application in-app purchases through Stripe/Adyen acquiring partners that route via Apple Pay and Google Pay; they cannot legally be used to hold WST balances, must not conduct FX conversion on behalf of a Samoa IC, and cannot clear any Samoa Export Proceeds surrender obligations. Physical correspondent banking relationships at ANZ or BSP will remain the sole regulated conduit for both WST and USD current account activity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections to the Samoa International Company must be routed as SWIFT MT103 transfers from the parent treasury account to the designated ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa foreign currency account, with the full legal entity name WS_IC as beneficiary. Prior to the first wire, the IC must file a Capital Importation Application with the Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) through its account officer and obtain an Exchange Control approval certificate, which must be quoted in SWIFT field 70 (Remittance Information). To support conversion, the incoming wire should indicate a documented purpose code consistent with the Samoa Exchange Control Manual, such as 'C.1. Inward Investment – Equity Capital' or 'C.2. Intercompany Loan Facility'. For amounts exceeding USD 50,000, the bank will require a board resolution, a source-of-wealth declaration from final UBO, evidence that the sender is compliant with the ISIC 6201 app software development scope, and a copy of the approved CBS inbound application. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% standard corporate income tax rate applied to resident companies under the Samoa Income Tax Act 2012; International Companies (ICs) registered under the International Companies Act 1987-88 are statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax on all international business income, resulting in an effective rate of 0% for the subject mobile App Store revenue.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends resolved and paid by an International Company, because IC-distributed dividends are outside the scope of Samoa's 15% withholding tax imposed on resident companies under the Income Tax Act 2012 (s. 111). The general statutory rate of 15% applies only to dividends paid by non-exempt resident companies to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa does not prescribe formal Local File/Master File thresholds expressed in Samoan Tala (WST), and exempt International Companies have no mandatory TP documentation obligations unless they lose exempt status. The Income Tax Act 2012 incorporates the arm's length principle for cross-border related-party transactions of taxable entities, and the Commissioner of the Ministry of Revenue may require contemporaneous TP documentation on audit where aggregate related-party turnover is material. The OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines are used as interpretative guidance. For the subject IC, the practical benchmark is to maintain internal pricing records for the App Store royalty split between the IP owner and the platform operator (Apple/Google) and for any management fee paid to an affiliate.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 and accompanying Regulations apply to Samoa International Companies conducting 'relevant activities'. If the mobile utility app business is structured such that the IC owns the software IP and licenses it to app stores, the activity may fall under the 'intellectual property holding' relevant activity, which requires a high-threshold economic substance test: demonstrable active management at Samoan board level, decisions on development, maintenance, and enhancement of the IP made in Samoa, adequate qualified local employees, physical premises, and expenditure proportionate to the IP income. If structured purely as a digital distribution business, the activity is less likely to be a listed relevant activity; however, the IC must in all cases avoid trading with Samoan residents and preserve documentary evidence of commercial reality. Maintenance of a Samoan registered agent alone does not constitute substance; the IC must have a physical Apia office, a local bank account, a Samoan-resident director or manager with decision-making authority, and board minutes executed in Samoa.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Prohibited Domestic Trading

Statutory sanction: Loss of the full income tax exemption with immediate reassessment of all assessable income at 27%; addition of a 100% evaded-tax penalty under the Tax Administration Act 2012; interest accrual at the statutory rate; publication of the IC in the 'Defaulting International Companies' gazette; criminal conviction carrying a fine of up to WST 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years for the company's officers.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent trading: the Court may order directors jointly and severally liable for all unpaid tax, penalties, and creditor debts. Directors who signed a false declaration face personal fines, asset forfeiture orders, travel bans enforced at Samoa's ports of entry, and terms of imprisonment for each false statutory declaration made.
Shell Company / Inadequate Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of up to WST 20,000 for the first contravention and WST 50,000 for repeated contravention, plus a daily default penalty; the SIFA may strike the IC off the register and will exchange information with the beneficial owner's tax residence authority under Samoa's international tax information exchange agreements. The IC's bank account may be blocked by SIFA order pending compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly consenting to the false substance declaration are deemed personally liable for the penalty, may be disqualified from acting as directors of any Samoan international company for up to 10 years, and are subject to travel bans and criminal fines up to WST 50,000. Restitution to the government for fees improperly retained or repatriated may be ordered against their personal assets.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to WST 500,000 against the IC; confiscation of the account balance and connected assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2018; revocation of the registered agent's licence (resulting in automatic strike-off of the IC if no replacement agent is appointed within 30 days).

Director personal liability: Directors and senior managers knowingly involved in laundering proceeds face criminal liability: imprisonment of up to 10 years and fines of up to WST 1,000,000. Personal civil restitution is ordered for funds traced through the company; travel bans are automatic upon the FIU's issuance of a notification to the Immigration Division, and forfeiture of personal assets is available where the company is a facade for the director's benefit.
Annual Return and Government Fee Default

Statutory sanction: Accrual of the 10% monthly late-payment penalty (capped at 100% of the fee); issuance of a peremptory strike-off notice; deregistration after six months' continued default, at which point all contracts and assets of the IC vest in the Registrar for realisation. A struck-off IC cannot appear in app store agreements, and Apple/Google may terminate the developer account for loss of corporate existence.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally and jointly liable for all unpaid annual fees, late penalties, and restoration costs incurred to reinstate the IC; the Registrar may issue a certificate of debt enforceable against the directors' personal property. Repeated default subjects directors to a travel ban, disqualification from managing any Samoa company for 5 years, and civil restitution for assets dissipated after the company became insolvent.
