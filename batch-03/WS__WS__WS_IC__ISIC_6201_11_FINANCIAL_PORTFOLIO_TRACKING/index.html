Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Independent State of Samoa (Samoa Revenue Department), Apia; official administration portal: www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

Foreign investors incorporating a Samoa International Company obtain a Certificate of Incorporation from the Samoa International Companies and Foreign Companies Registry (SICR). For administrative identification, the Ministry for Revenue automatically assigns a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) upon registration of the company with the SICR. A separate VAGST identification number is required only where the International Company makes taxable supplies in Samoa and exceeds the WST 100,000 annual turnover threshold, in which case the foreign entity must apply for VAGST registration with the Ministry for Revenue, submitting the certificate of incorporation, statutory forms, and certified copies of identification of directors and shareholders. Where the International Company conducts wholly offshore activities, it generally receives a Certificate of Tax Exempt Status and is not required to file Samoan income tax returns, though it must maintain records of its offshore nature.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa International Companies are automatically classified as tax-exempt offshore entities and are excluded from Samoan income tax, stamp duty, exchange control, and VAGST on offshore transactions. There is no check-the-box election comparable to US tax classification. An International Company may elect under the International Companies Act 1987 to cancel its international status and re-domicile as a domestic company, thereby becoming subject to ordinary Samoan corporate taxation. Samoa has not enacted hybrid mismatch or transparent entity election rules; no similar statutory election mechanism exists for International Companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration permitted for WST 60,000 or more

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Samoa are zero-rated where the exporter retains evidence of export within the statutory time limit, ordinarily 28 days from the date of supply, and the goods leave Samoa to a destination outside Samoa. Services are zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time the services are performed and the benefit of the services is wholly outside Samoa. International Companies engaged exclusively in offshore financial portfolio tracking and similar fintech services are not within the Samoan VAGST net; consequently they are not required to charge output VAT and no formal zero-rating claim is necessary.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoan Customs Service CUSEX Form)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill issued to the overseas buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence in foreign currency
  • Commercial invoice and packing list evidencing Samoa origin
  • Certificate of Origin where preferential tariff treatment is claimed

No real-time electronic fiscal clearance, API reporting, or digital stamping mandate exists under Samoan law. Registered suppliers must issue a valid VAGST tax invoice containing statutory particulars (supplier name, VAGST registration number, date of supply, description, tax-exclusive value, and VAGST charged). E-invoicing is permissible in PDF or XML format but does not require pre-clearance by the Ministry for Revenue.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit zero-rated export claims retrospectively for up to 10 years where there is fraud or willful evasion, and 5 years in other cases. Where documentary substantiation is inadequate, the entire zero-rated output tax is clawed back as VAGST due, together with a late-payment penalty of 2% per month and an automatic penalty of 100% of the tax avoided for fraudulent misdeclaration. Directors and persons involved in facilitating a false zero-rating claim may be personally liable and subject to criminal prosecution under the VAGST Act 2008 and the Samoa Crimes Act 2013, resulting in fines up to WST 100,000 and imprisonment up to seven years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is not a freely convertible offshore currency; current-account payments for services, software subscriptions, and trade are processed by licensed authorised dealers on presentation of underlying contracts and invoices. Capital-account transactions, including non-resident equity injections and shareholder loan repayments, require Central Bank of Samoa approval if settled in WST. An International Company operating under the Tax-Exempt Zone may lawfully hold and transact foreign-currency accounts in USD, EUR, AUD, and NZD through licensed commercial banks without conversion to WST, provided it conducts no domestic Samoan business. Profit remittance and dividend distribution to non-resident shareholders are permissible after submission of audited financial statements, a valid board dividend resolution, and comprehensive source-of-funds documentation; no withholding tax applies to offshore International Company dividends, but the paying bank will require enhanced beneficial-owner clearance for trust structures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Samoa's Exchange Control Act and Central Bank directives, exporters of goods and services, including cross-border software and digital fintech services, must repatriate proceeds to Samoa within 30 days of receipt. The mandatory surrender ratio is 100% for proceeds intended to support domestic operating costs; however, authorised dealers may approve foreign-currency retention accounts in USD, EUR, AUD, or NZD allowing an exporter to retain up to 100% of export receipts for offshore treasury deployment and trade settlement, subject to monthly reconciliation with the Central Bank. For a non-resident International Company, the surrender obligation is administratively relaxed but not formally waived; all inward remittances must be tagged with the correct software/technology export purpose code and supported by service contracts and itemised invoices retained for CBS inspection.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), Exchange Control and AML/CFT Supervision Division, with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) acting as the statutory registry and licensing authority for International Companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for primary treasury reliance. Samoa does not issue standalone e-money institution licences without a commercial banking licence, and offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, or Mercury operate under foreign licences that are not reciprocally recognised by the Central Bank of Samoa. Such EMIs may be used as supplementary collection gateways for consumer payments, but the principal clearing account, regulatory nexus, and liquidity buffer must remain with a CBS-licensed commercial bank. Legal constraints prohibit routing Samoan-source WST conversions through unlicensed EMIs and require that all merchant settlement flows from Stripe and Apple IAP be consolidated into a licensed bank within the permitted end-of-day reconciliation window.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires a single SWIFT MT103 wire from the external shareholder's bank through a corresponding international bank to the beneficiary's foreign-currency account at ANZ or BSP in Samoa. The remittance must carry a transparent purpose code of 'Equity Injection', 'Direct Investment Capital Subscription', or the CBS-recognised capital transaction equivalent, with the beneficiary field showing the International Company legal name and account number. The supporting pack must include the board resolution approving the capital call, the shares subscription agreement, the certificate of incorporation, audited or management financials, and source-of-funds statement from the remitting shareholder. Funds should never be split into smaller transfers, routed through third-party wallets, or settled in cash; any deviation will trigger automatic AML suspicion and potential freezing by the correspondent bank.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 USD wire for non-documentary settlements such as software licensing fees, cloud infrastructure payments, legal retainers, and intercompany management fees. For physical goods or high-value trade arrangements, a commercial letter of credit issued by the Samoan authorised dealer bank is used, with documents including commercial invoice, bill of lading or air waybill, certificates of origin, and insurance certificate. Local Samoan Tala operating expenditures are cleared via the national RTGS and the bank's local transfer network. All outbound disbursements must be matched to supporting contracts and invoices, screened against UN, OFAC, EU, and Samoan sanctions lists at both the ordering and receiving banks, and recorded in the treasury ledger with the corresponding SWIFT reference.Deliverable FX forward contracts of up to 12 months against USD/WST, EUR/WST, AUD/WST, and NZD/WST with licensed Samoa authorised dealer banks, executed only to hedge receivables or payables arising from the software subscription business.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing WST, permitted only for hedging verifiable cross-border exposure and prohibited for speculative trading, with counterparty being a CBS-approved international bank and all profits or losses settled in convertible foreign currency.Plain vanilla cross-currency swaps between USD, EUR, AUD, and NZD for treasury cash pooling and liquidity rebalancing, with a maturity not exceeding 5 years and with Central Bank notification required if any settlement leg involves the Samoan Tala.Vanilla FX call and put options purchased from authorised dealer banks to protect highly probable forecasted foreign-currency inflows and outflows, where the notional amount is capped at 125% of the documented treasury exposure and no exotic or leveraged structures are permitted.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for International Companies; ordinary Samoa resident companies are taxed at 27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders; the general Samoa non-resident dividend withholding tax rate is 15% under the Income Tax Act 2014 for ordinary resident companies.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than the last day of the month following the anniversary of incorporation; annual registry fees are due on 31 March of each year for calendar-year registered companies.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable. Samoa International Companies are exempt from Samoa income tax and therefore outside the transfer pricing documentation regime. The Samoa Income Tax Act 2014 contains a general arm's length rule for connected-party cross-border transactions but prescribes no codified monetary threshold for IC compliance.

Economic substance scope: Samoa does not apply Economic Substance Regulations. International Companies are not required to evidence substantial activity or local management in Samoa. The International Companies Act 1987 only requires a registered office and resident agent; passive financial portfolio holding and tracking activities are expressly permissible as offshore business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abusive Offshore Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act 1987 and the Income Tax Act 2014: fines up to WST 100,000 for false statements; assessed tax plus a penalty of up to 50% of the unpaid tax; strike-off and cancellation of the IC's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for fines, assessed taxes, and civil restitution. Culpable directors face imprisonment of up to 7 years, travel bans pending investigation, and disqualification from acting as directors of any Samoa-registered company.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike the IC off the register, impose restoration fees, and revoke the license of any inbound trust or corporate service provider that facilitated the sham arrangement.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for the debts and liabilities of the struck-off company under the International Companies Act. Civil courts may pierce the corporate veil, order restitution, impose travel bans, and, in egregious cases, direct custodial sentences for contempt or fraud.
AML / Financial Crime Violation

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act: corporate fines up to WST 200,000 and imprisonment of officers up to 5 years. The licensed registered agent may also be subject to regulatory suspension or permanent revocation of its license.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal liability where culpable knowledge is proven. Courts may impose personal asset freezes, travel bans, and civil restitution orders. Directors of the registered agent and the IC can be jointly and severally liable for AML penalties if they authorized or failed to prevent the breach.
