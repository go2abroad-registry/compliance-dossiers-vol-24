Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Government of Samoa (integrated customs and inland revenue administration); e-services are accessed through the MfR Tax Online Portal.

E-government filing portal

An offshore International Company (IC) is first incorporated under the International Companies Act 1988 (as amended) through a licensed registered agent, with the Registrar of International Companies operating under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour. The IC is issued a Certificate of Exemption and is not required to register for income tax or VAGST with the Ministry for Revenue (MfR) so long as it does not transact business with Samoan residents or derive Samoan-source income. If the IC subsequently hires Samoan-resident employees, owns land in Samoa, or undertakes any other domestic taxable activity, it must apply to the MfR for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) within 14 days after becoming liable, using the prescribed application form, corporate constitutional documents, directors' and registered agent's identification, and evidence of incorporation; the TIN is issued by the MfR and is also a prerequisite for customs clearance if import transactions arise. An exempt IC must, in all cases, maintain its registered office and registered agent in Samoa and discharge the annual economic substance notification obligation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the International Company is treated as a Samoan offshore exempt entity: it is not resident for income tax on foreign-source business receipts, is not liable to VAGST on offshore financial supplies, and is exempt from stamp duty and exchange control, subject to the statutory limitations on carrying on business in or with Samoa and to the OECD-compliant economic substance requirements.

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a US-style check-the-box entity election. The exempt classification is mandatory for International Companies and cannot be waived or selectively opted out of; the IC may, however, request a binding tax ruling from the MfR confirming that a specified activity remains offshore-source and exempt. The Income Tax Act 2012 and the Tax Administration Act 2016 contain general anti-avoidance rules (GAAR), and Samoa applies internationally aligned hybrid mismatch and CFC ordering rules for resident companies; no elective hybrid-mismatch regime is available to exempt ICs, and double tax treaty relief does not apply to the IC's offshore income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory VAGST registration applies when taxable supplies, excluding exempt financial supplies, exceed or are reasonably expected to exceed WST 75,000 in any 12-month period; a person making only exempt supplies is not registrable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: a supply of goods is zero-rated where the goods are exported from Samoa within 28 days after the supply, title passes to a non-resident customer, and the supplier retains documentary evidence of physical export. Services: an offshore supply of services is zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident acting outside Samoa and the services are not used or enjoyed in Samoa. For the P2P micro-lending platform, loan interest and loan-origination or intermediation services are exempt financial supplies under the VAGST Act 2008, not taxable supplies, and therefore remain outside VAGST whether characterized as exempt or zero-rated; income derived by the IC from non-resident borrowers is foreign-source and is not subject to Samoan income tax, with zero-rating substantiation relevant only to ancillary fee recovery or cross-border goods collateral, if any.

  • Samoa Customs Export Declaration (electronic departure/cargo declaration lodged with the Ministry for Revenue)
  • Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Samoa
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing repatriation of export proceeds

Samoa has not introduced mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal digital stamping, or government API clearance. VAGST-registered businesses must issue a VAT invoice containing all prescribed particulars (supplier name and TIN, recipient details, description of supply, consideration, and VAGST amount) and retain original records for at least 7 years; returns and schedules are filed electronically through the MfR portal. Electronically generated invoices and optional QR codes are accepted, but there is no statutory fiscal clearance or digital sealing mandate.

Export zero-rating and refund claims are subject to audit by the Ministry for Revenue and refunds may be suspended pending verification. A false or fraudulent claim exposes the IC to: (i) retrospective clawback of the net VAGST refunded, together with late-payment interest; (ii) administrative shortfall penalties of up to 150% of the tax shortfall where recklessness or intentional disregard is established; (iii) monetary fines and imprisonment for tax evasion offences under the VAGST Act 2008 and the Tax Administration Act 2016; and (iv) cancellation or suspension of the zero-rating and refund facility. Directors, registered agents, and beneficial owners may be jointly pursued in civil recovery, and the exempt status of the International Company may be withdrawn for non-compliance with the economic substance regime.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa operates a managed float for the Samoan Tala (WST). Current account payments (trade, services, profit remittance) are freely permitted with underlying documentation. Capital account inflows and outflows require CBS approval unless executed strictly in non-resident foreign currency (usually USD) within the IC framework. There are no repatriation taxes or guaranteed conversion rights; profit remittance from domestic operations requires CBS approval and a tax clearance certificate. For ICs dealing only in foreign currency, convertibility is high and capital mobility is unrestricted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For domestic commercial exporters, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 180 days of shipment and surrendered to a licensed bank within 30 days of receipt, converting 100% of the foreign currency into WST. International Companies that do not export physical goods are exempt. For any minimal service exports from the IC (e.g., licensing fees), the sale is invoiced in foreign currency and no surrender is required as long as funds remain outside the WST banking system.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), through the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, administers all foreign exchange operations and capital account transactions. For International Companies (ICs) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987, direct foreign currency dealings are generally exempt from CBS approval provided no local currency (WST) is raised or used domestically.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low. Samoa does not have an electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework. Third-party digital wallets (PayPal, Skrill) generally do not support Samoa-based high-risk commercial onboarding. Established fintech payment processers such as Adyen or Checkout.com rarely accept merchant accounts for P2P lending platforms domiciled in Samoa. Therefore, cross-border settlement must rely on correspondent banking via local commercial banks or offshore banking partners. The Central Bank of Samoa has no sandbox for fintech lending; a Money Lenders License under the Money Lenders Act 2014 is required for any credit activity, and only class A banks may issue payment instruments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity or shareholder loan capital from the parent or investors must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 with clear purpose codes: for equity injection use 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital / IS3' under BOP; for intercompany loans use 'Other Investment – Loans – Non-Repayable / IS7'. Supporting documents shall include the IC's board resolution approving the capital injection, capital contribution agreement, source-of-funds evidence from the remitting bank, and a valid security/escrow account instruction at the receiving Samoan bank. No CBS approvall is required for foreign currency remittances to a non-resident IC account held entirely in USD, provided no WST is exchanged.For loan disbursements to micro-borrowers across the Pacific and global markets, the primary clearing rail is a SWIFT MT103 direct debit instruction from the IC's USD operational account to the beneficiary's local bank. For domestic Samoan borrowers, dollar amounts are converted to WST at prevailing rates and settled through the Samoa Automated Clearing House (ACH) operated by the Central Bank of Samoa. For wallet-based payouts (mobile money, e-wallets), a licensed payment aggregator in the beneficiary country is used, channeling funds through a pooling account at a correspondent bank. Trade-related payments (e.g., loan principal repayments) are credited via MT103 with purpose code 'Payment for Service / Interest on Loan' (ISIC/BoP code 'External Income on Loans' or 'Services'). Settlement of inbound loan repayments from foreign borrowers shall be matched daily against the IC's loan ledger to ensure no surplus is trapped in non-resident accounts.Forward FX contracts (USD/WST/EUR) transacted with ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa, with a maximum tenors of 12 months and delivery-backed against a physical lending pipeline.Cross-currency swaps (limited counterparties; only via offshore investment banks when underlying exposure exceeds USD 5 million, subject to CBS notification if WST leg is involved).Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on WST via CBS-approved offshore correspondents, permitted only for hedging anticipated loan repayments and not for speculative positions; settled in USD.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% (general rate imposed on dividends paid by a resident Samoa company to a non-resident shareholder); International Companies are statutorily exempt, and dividends paid by an IC to non-resident shareholders are payable gross with 0% Samoa withholding tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 1,000,000 (Samoa tala) aggregate related-party transactions per income year for mandatory Local File; Master File required when the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds WST 5,000,000. Transactions below WST 1,000,000 must still be documented and priced at arm's length.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to International Companies carrying on a relevant activity, including lending/banking, financing and leasing, fund management, and holding company operations. A P2P micro-lending platform must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Samoa: a physical local office, qualified local employees, board meetings held in Samoa, and operating expenditure proportionate to the income generated. An annual economic substance declaration is mandatory with SICO; failure to satisfy the test nullifies the tax exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Lending Structure

Statutory sanction: Samoa Income Tax Act (as amended): shortfall penalty of up to 200% of the evaded tax, plus interest at 1.5% per month; criminal conviction may lead to a fine of WST 20,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years; the tax authority may reopen assessments for up to ten years in fraud cases.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false tax return or cause the company to participate in a sham structure are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall; Samoa courts may issue civil restoration orders, asset forfeiture, travel bans, and impose imprisonment upon criminal conviction.
Unlicensed Micro-Lending / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Central Bank Act and AML Act: administrative fines of up to WST 100,000 per violation and criminal fines on the company of up to WST 1,000,000; the Central Bank may suspend or revoke the licence and the company may be ordered to cease operations with immediate effect.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior managers involved in such unlicensed activity face criminal prosecution, personal fines up to WST 50,000, director disqualification, travel restrictions, and imprisonment for up to five years if the AML breach is shown to be wilful or reckless.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations (Samoa): first-default penalty of WST 10,000; continuing default penalty of WST 5,000 per month; the Registrar may strike off the company and refer the case to the MfR for reversal of tax-exempt status and retrospective taxation.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for inaccurate or false economic substance declarations; the court may impose restitution of the entire tax liability, disqualify the director for up to ten years, issue a travel ban, and in fraud cases sentence imprisonment for up to five years.
Nominee Director Fraud / Misfeasance

Statutory sanction: International Companies Act 2006 and general Samoa companies law: penalty of up to WST 20,000 for misfeasance; the Registrar may strike off the IC for failing to maintain the registered agent or for providing materially false information in the annual return.

Director personal liability: Directors may be ordered to restore company assets and account for all secret profits; Samoan courts may impose financial restitution, issue travel bans, disqualify the individual from holding directorship, and on evidence of deception imprison for up to ten years under the Samoa Crimes Act.
