Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), Apia, Samoa; online portal: https://www.sra.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company (IC) is exempt from income tax and VAGST on offshore business and is not ordinarily required to hold a local TIN. Where a TIN is nonetheless needed (e.g., customs clearance, opening a domestic bank account, or engaging in local transactions), the foreign entity must file SRA Form TIN-01 (Taxpayer Identification Number Registration) with the Samoa Revenue Authority, accompanied by: (i) certified copy of the International Company certificate of incorporation; (ii) memorandum and articles of association; (iii) current certificate of good standing from the Samoa International Financial Services Authority (SIFSA); (iv) certified passport copies of directors/shareholders; (v) proof of the licensed registered agent in Samoa; and (vi) a copy of the registered office address. Applications are submitted in person or through the licensed registered agent. A TIN is issued within 5–10 business days upon complete filing and verification. Approval is at the discretion of the Commissioner of the SRA; non-resident entities are treated as non-residents for domestic tax purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Samoa International Company (IC) is exempt from income tax and VAGST on offshore business and is not ordinarily required to hold a local TIN. Where a TIN is nonetheless needed (e.g., customs clearance, opening a domestic bank account, or engaging in local transactions), the foreign entity must file SRA Form TIN-01 (Taxpayer Identification Number Registration) with the Samoa Revenue Authority, accompanied by: (i) certified copy of the International Company certificate of incorporation; (ii) memorandum and articles of association; (iii) current certificate of good standing from the Samoa International Financial Services Authority (SIFSA); (iv) certified passport copies of directors/shareholders; (v) proof of the licensed registered agent in Samoa; and (vi) a copy of the registered office address. Applications are submitted in person or through the licensed registered agent. A TIN is issued within 5–10 business days upon complete filing and verification. Approval is at the discretion of the Commissioner of the SRA; non-resident entities are treated as non-residents for domestic tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists for a Samoa International Company. Tax status is statutory: an IC that complies with the International Companies Act 2017 and receives only offshore income is automatically exempt from income tax, stamp duty, and VAGST on its offshore business (exemption available without election). An IC that elects to carry on business in Samoa with Samoan persons or devolves into domestic trading loses the exemption and becomes subject to ordinary Samoan income tax and the Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) Act 2008. No CFC-payment or conduit election is available to ICs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Samoa International Company may zero-rate its VAGST supplies if it is registered (or voluntarily registered) and the services qualify as exported services: (i) the services are supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply; (ii) the services are not supplied for use or consumption in Samoa; and (iii) the services are directly connected with movable property or intangible property situated or used outside Samoa, or relate to a business carried on entirely outside Samoa. Fractional CFO advisory, remote bookkeeping, and multicurrency ledger services delivered by a Samoa IC to overseas clients fall within the zero-rating category if no part of the service is consumed in Samoa and the recipient is a non-resident outside Samoa. The IC must hold the standard export substantiation pack as set out below.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SRA/Customs Form Cus-Decl) for goods; not applicable to pure services — for services, an SRA-issued Export Services Declaration or service export notification is required
  • Commercial invoice and detailed service contract (describing the offshore nature of the service)
  • Evidence of recipient non-residence: certificate of incorporation of the foreign client or utility bill/lease outside Samoa
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical exports only)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing full payment of the invoice in foreign currency from outside Samoa
  • Proof of delivery/use outside Samoa: signed service completion report or counterparty acknowledgment
  • Foreign currency purchase/sale confirmation from a licensed Samoan bank where applicable

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate is in force in Samoa. The SRA does not require API-based continuous clearance of e-invoices. Taxpayers may issue ordinary commercial invoices manually or electronically. Mandatory content for VAGST invoices: the seller's name and TIN, buyer's name, invoice date, description of goods/services, quantity and price, amount of VAGST charged, and total amount (for exempt supplies, a statement that VAGST is not charged). All VAT returns and payments must be filed electronically through the SRA's tax administration portal, but invoice clearance is not automated.

The Samoa Revenue Authority may audit any zero-rated export claim within the 4-year assessment window and review exchange control documentation to verify that export proceeds were received within 180 days. A failed or fraudulent zero-rating claim triggers full clawback of the uncharged VAGST at 15% plus late payment penalty of 1.5% per month (or part thereof) and default interest at the statutory rate; where fraud or gross negligence is established, a civil penalty of up to 300% of the tax evaded can be imposed, in addition to criminal liability of up to SAT 50,000 fine and 7 years' imprisonment per the Tax Administration Act 2016 and VAGST Act 2008. For International Companies, loss of the IC tax exemption is also possible if the activity is deemed to be domestic trading in Samoa.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (ISO 4217: WST) is a partially convertible Pacific currency: current-account remittances are approved by authorized dealers against documented trade or services invoices, while capital-account transfers and current payments above CBS-published thresholds require prior Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) approval. Profit remittance for an onshore licensed enterprise requires a Ministry-of-Revenue tax-clearance certificate and CBS filing; the International Company, by contrast, is tax-exempt and, under the International Companies Act 1987, lies outside CBS exchange control for all offshore business so long as it does not deal with Samoan residents. The residual convertibility hurdle is market depth: WST has no offshore interbank liquidity, conversion spreads widen materially above WST 1 million equivalent, and Samoan banks apply Enhanced Due Diligence to offshore IC treasury flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Regulations 1980 require goods-export proceeds to be fully repatriated within 180 days from shipment and surrendered to an authorized dealer within 28 days of receipt; the mandatory conversion ratio is a CBS policy parameter (current practice permits retention in a foreign-currency deposit account, with a continuing notification duty). Services receipts (ISIC 6920 fractional CFO fees) are invisible earnings: an onshore entity must route them through an authorized dealer against invoice evidence, but they are outside the 28-day goods-surrender regime. The Samoan International Company is wholly excluded where it has no resident trade: virtual-CFO fees may be collected offshore with no surrender or conversion requirement, and funds brought into Samoa may be held in foreign-currency deposit accounts free of mandatory conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS; Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa) — statutory exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976, with delegated authorized-dealer powers exercised by licensed commercial banks.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (Income Tax Act 2015 (Samoa), s.73(1)). A company incorporated in Samoa is tax resident and nominally taxable on worldwide income, but this entity is an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended) and, while holding a valid Tax Exemption Certificate, is taxable at 0% on qualified income derived from outside Samoa.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% withholding tax on dividends paid by a resident company (Income Tax Act 2015). A qualifying exempt International Company distributing profits accumulated entirely from offshore sources pays 0% Samoan dividend WHT because the distribution is sourced from the exempt offshore fund. Where the IC's profits have been re-characterised as Samoan-source, the 15% rate applies to dividend payments to non-resident shareholders; under an applicable comprehensive DTA (e.g., the Samoa-China DTA), the rate may be reduced to 5% where the beneficial owner is a company holding directly at least 25% of the voting capital, otherwise 10%.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The tax year is the calendar year (1 January to 31 December); the return must be lodged on or before 30 June of the year following the tax year (i.e., within 6 months after year-end) and any resulting tax paid by the same date. A tax-exempt IC with no Samoa-source income is not required to make a provisional payment but should file the exemption declaration section annually upon SRA request.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly installments are due on 30 September, 31 December, 31 March and 30 June of the tax year. An IC deriving no Samoa-source income and holding a valid exemption certificate is not obliged to pay installments but should lodge a nil declaration to maintain its taxpayer compliance record.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 500,000: aggregate cross-border related-party transaction value per annum above which the International Company must maintain contemporaneous Transfer Pricing Documentation (Local File) covering its Virtual CFO and bookkeeping service lines, including functional analysis, benchmarking study, and arm's length pricing justification. A group-wide Master File should be prepared where the MNE group's consolidated revenue exceeds WST 5,000,000 or where the ultimate parent is resident in a jurisdiction applying the OECD EUR 750 million filing threshold. Documentation must be delivered to SRA within 30 days of a written request.

Economic substance scope: Samoa has not enacted a BVI-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but the IC tax exemption is substance-conditioned. The International Companies Act 1987 (as amended in 2015) requires a registered office, a licensed Samoa registered agent, a directors' register, and accessible corporate records in Samoa. The Income Tax Act 2015 General Anti-Avoidance Rule (ss.92-93) empowers SRA to disregard an IC lacking economic substance and to tax its profits at 25% where control, management, staff and value creation occur in Samoa. Samoa's OECD Global Forum and BEPS Inclusive Framework commitments require genuine economic activity and exchange of information on request. A credible Virtual CFO substance profile must include board-approved service contracts, professional fee records, segregated multicurrency ledgers, documentary evidence of the client engagement and delivery jurisdiction, and arm's length charging for any intercompany software, payment-rail, or subcontracting services.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Offshore-Source Characterisation

Statutory sanction: Shortfall penalty of 100% of the avoided tax plus monthly deferral interest of 2% under the Tax Administration Act 2015; administrative fine up to WST 1,000,000; criminal prosecution with fines up to WST 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years; immediate cancellation of the IC Tax Exemption Certificate; recovery of all previously claimed exempt taxes; compulsory deregistration.

Director personal liability: Directors, virtual CFOs and tax agents who caused or acquiesced in the false characterisation are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax through piercing of the corporate veil; courts may order civil restitution of diverted profits; travel bans and departure prohibitions may be imposed; director disqualification for up to 5 years; imprisonment is additional and not dischargeable by corporate liquidation.
Shell Company / Absent Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration under the International Companies Act 1987; revocation of the Tax Exemption Certificate; SRA re-assessment of all profits at 25% plus the 100% evasion penalty; registration of enforcement charges against assets held by nominees; fines up to WST 100,000 per contravention; prohibition from opening or maintaining bank accounts in Samoa.

Director personal liability: Directors may be publicly named and disqualified for up to 5 years; liquidators may set aside sham arrangements as void against the company; directors are liable for company debts where the IC traded while insolvent and failed to remit tax; de facto responsible persons are personally assessed for unpaid tax; travel bans and contempt proceedings apply where corporate books are not produced.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism Act 2007 (as amended): fines up to WST 1,000,000 for the company and WST 500,000 for each officer per breach; freezing and confiscation of the IC's bank and payment-rail accounts; suspension or revocation of any required financial service registration; additional SRA penalties for failure to obtain or renew a TIN.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering or facilitation, personal fines, travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and permanent disqualification from managing regulated entities in Samoa and DTA/counterparty jurisdictions.
Withholding Tax Defaults on Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: WHT shortfall penalty of 20% of the unremitted amount plus interest at 2% per month; the grossed-up distribution is denied as a deduction and may be re-characterised as a deemed dividend taxable in the hands of the Samoan entity; criminal fines up to WST 1,000,000; imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion of withholding obligations.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly failed to withhold or remit are personally liable as persons deemed to have collected the tax; civil restitution proceedings may be brought by affected shareholders; travel restriction orders are routinely issued; personal liability is not discharged by liquidation or by resignation after the default date.
