Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of the Independent State of Samoa (MfR); official e-portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must file the prescribed TIN/GST registration application with MfR, submitting apostilled certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, certificate of good standing from the home registry, registered agent confirmation, Samoan office address and service of process details, ultimate beneficial ownership declaration, and notarized passport copies of all directors. MfR issues a Samoa Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where a taxable activity in Samoa exists, a separate GST registration number. An International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 is, unless it has a Samoan taxable supply nexus, outside the income tax registration net and receives only an International Company certificate file number.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity must file the prescribed TIN/GST registration application with MfR, submitting apostilled certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, certificate of good standing from the home registry, registered agent confirmation, Samoan office address and service of process details, ultimate beneficial ownership declaration, and notarized passport copies of all directors. MfR issues a Samoa Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where a taxable activity in Samoa exists, a separate GST registration number. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists in Samoa. An International Company obtains its tax-exempt status by valid incorporation and licensing under the International Companies Act 1987. It may voluntarily elect to register for GST if it makes taxable supplies with a Samoan nexus, but it cannot elect into Samoan income tax or alter its statutory offshore tax exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive twelve-month period

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to tangible goods exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply, where the supplier retains verified proof of export, and to services supplied to a non-resident for use or consumption entirely outside Samoa where the recipient is not engaged in a Samoan taxable activity. Offshore software development and maintenance services supplied by an International Company to a foreign client, invoiced in foreign currency, and performed outside Samoa satisfy this criterion.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through Samoan Customs systems
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or equivalent transport document evidencing physical departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a licensed Samoan bank proving foreign exchange receipt
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the exporting entity
  • Offshore service agreement and delivery or acceptance milestone records
  • Certificate of origin or export permit where required by the destination market

Samoa does not impose real-time fiscal clearance, API-based pre-approval, or digital stamping of invoices. GST-registered suppliers must issue prescribed tax invoices showing the TIN, GST amount, supplier and recipient details, and date of supply. Electronic invoices may be filed through the MfR e-services portal, are subject to post-filing audit, and must be retained for at least seven years.

MfR may retrospectively reassess any refunded GST within four years of the relevant tax period where zero-rating substantiation is defective. A false or misleading export claim attracts a tax shortfall penalty of up to 50% of the GST overclaimed, monthly late payment penalties, and interest. Intentional tax evasion is a criminal offence punishable by a fine not exceeding WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment not exceeding seven years, with full clawback of the refunded amount plus penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa tala operates under a managed-float regime with partial convertibility: current-account payments (trade invoices, software and IT services fees, insurance, interest, and normal operational remittances) are generally permitted through an authorized dealer upon production of genuine contract and invoice evidence; capital-account transactions (equity subscriptions, loan capital, liquidation distributions, and profit repatriation by non-resident International Companies) require CBS-specific exchange-control approval, but for a qualifying International Company such approvals are available on a non-prejudicial basis. Profit and dividend remittances are not subject to Samoan income or withholding tax for a tax-exempt IC, provided the paying bank receives a Certificate of Exemption/registered agent confirmation and the underlying revenue arises from qualifying offshore activities rather than domestic Samoan-source transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The statutory default requires all export and service receipts to be collected through a licensed authorized dealer in Samoa and repatriated no later than 90 days from shipment/invoice date. The mandatory conversion standard is 100% of the gross foreign-currency proceeds converted to Samoa tala unless the CBS grants a specific exemption. For an Offshore Development Center structured as an International Company, digital/software services may be treated as invisible current-account receipts, and a standing exemption may be requested in writing to retain USD/WST bank balances for working-capital purposes rather than forced conversion on every receipt.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1975, the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, and the Financial Institutions Act 1996; the CBS designates authorized foreign-exchange dealers (commercial banks) and grants exchange-control permissions for capital and current account transactions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

The viability rating is Medium for ancillary settlement and Low as a primary banking rail. Fintech electronic money institutions (e.g. Wise B2B, Payoneer) are not licensed by the Central Bank of Samoa, hold no Samoan banking licence, and cannot provide WST-denominated accounts or CBS-supervised liquidity. They may lawfully be used as collection corridors for global software development fees, but all funds must ultimately be moved through a licensed authorized dealer for Samoan statutory/tax payments, payroll, or capital repatriation. Additional legal constraints include a required board resolution designating the EMI, custody limitations applicable to client funds held overseas, and enhanced CRS/FATCA classification risk unless the IC obtains and provides a Samoa Tax Identification Number and exemption certificate.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 using the shareholder's own bank account in the shareholder's jurisdiction. The instructed beneficiary is the International Company's capital-escrow account at a CBS-licensed authorized dealer. Field 70/Remittance Information should carry purpose code 'EQUITY-INJECTION-IC' (or the dealer's required exchange-control purpose description) plus the share allotment reference. The bank will seek CBS approval on an application form for capital account transfers; concurrently the IC must submit a board allotment resolution, amended register of members, source-of-funds evidence (balance sheet/tax returns/bank statements), and a registered-agent certificate confirming solvency and legal existence. Wire splitting below reporting thresholds is expressly prohibited; funds should be received in full, recorded in the capital account, and swept to the operational account only after CBS confirmation and bank compliance release.Operational outflows are cleared using (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct transfer of software subcontract fees, vendor licences, consultancy invoices, and contractor salaries where the beneficiary has a bank account; (ii) SWIFT MT700 commercial letter of credit issued by the Samoan authorized dealer for physical equipment/hardware acquisitions, usually sight or 60-day usance, with supporting purchase order and transport documents; and (iii) SWIFT MT202 for interbank correspondent funding where a separate cover payment is required. All outbound remittance instructions must include the underlying contract/invoice reference in field 70, be accompanied by an approved purchase invoice or service certificate, and pass CBS current-account/anti-money-laundering screening. Ongoing digitised vendor payments may be batched through Wise B2B/Payoneer only for non-capital, low-value operating expenses and must never exceed the exchange-control documentary threshold without dealer confirmation.Deliverable Samoa Tala/USD forward contracts with a licensed authorized dealer, for maturities up to 12 months, backed by a written underlying trade or service exposure.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/WST available from global banks, economically hedging conversion risk without requiring Samoan settlement if the IC books in USD or an offshore vehicle.Cross-currency swaps (CCS/CCIRS) under an ISDA master agreement or Samoan bank treasury line, converting USD floating/floating flows to WST fixed/floating flows with CBS notification.FX swaps combining an immediate spot transaction with an agreed forward reversal, used for short-term liquidity bridging between the capital account and operational account.Option collars and participating forwards with a licensed dealer, documented as hedge-only instruments directly linked to forecast sale cycles of the offshore development center.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% statutory rate for International Companies (International Companies Act 1987, s.198); domestic headline corporate income tax rate for non-exempt companies is 27%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for International Companies and their non-resident shareholders (International Companies Act 1987, s.198; the distribution is exempt from all Samoan taxes). The domestic non-exempt withholding tax rate on dividends is 15%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not Applicable - Samoa has not enacted Local File, Master File, or CbCR documentation thresholds in law. The Commissioner of Inland Revenue may nonetheless adjust non-arm's length arrangements under general anti-avoidance provisions (Income Tax Ordinance 1974, s.99). Prudent ODC practice: maintain contemporaneous cost-plus or TNMM benchmarks for intercompany service fees even though no Samoan statutory threshold is triggered.

Economic substance scope: Samoa has no Economic Substance Regulations filing of the BVI or UAE style. Substance requirements are indirect: the IC must be administered by a licensed registered agent, maintain a registered office, keep accounting records and a beneficial ownership register (Beneficial Ownership Act 2020), and must not carry on business with Samoan residents. An ODC structure purely booking offshore income with no people or offices invites CFC attribution in the shareholder's home country.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / False Offshore Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under the International Companies Act 1987; assets are forfeited to the Registrar; the IC loses the s.198 tax exemption retroactively for the period of abuse.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally assessed for the re-imposed corporate income tax and penalties; the courts may impose a travel ban, restitution order for the diverted amounts, and criminal fines of up to USD 20,000.
Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Revocation of exempt status; reassessment of undeclared income at the 27% corporate rate plus a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded; confiscation of proceeds under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Criminal prosecution of directors for aiding tax evasion, with imprisonment up to 5 years and personal civil liability for the full tax debt.
AML / Financial Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007: fines up to WST 1,000,000 plus forfeiture of the transaction proceeds; Beneficial Ownership Act 2020: continuing daily default penalties against the company.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal fines up to WST 500,000, imprisonment up to 3 years, and professional disqualification from acting as company officers.
Fraudulent Trading

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the International Companies Act 1987; the directors become personally and severally liable for all debts of the company.

Director personal liability: Imprisonment up to 3 years, civil restitution of every diverted sum, and a supervisory travel ban pending repayment.
Annual Return and Fee Default

Statutory sanction: Monetary surcharge for late filing; strike-off after prolonged default; any bank balances and intellectual property held by the company escheat to the Government of Samoa.

Director personal liability: Directors are disqualified from managing any Samoan international company for 5 years and must fund the restoration costs personally before the company can be reinstated.
