Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue of the Independent State of Samoa (MOR); official e-services portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company ('IC') is not required to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number for its statutorily exempt offshore business. If the foreign-owned IC nevertheless becomes subject to a Samoa VAGST registration or withholding obligation, it must lodge a written registration application with the Ministry of Revenue, attaching: (i) a certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation; (ii) the IC Constitution or Memorandum of Association; (iii) written confirmation of the registered agent in Samoa; (iv) beneficial ownership details and passport copies of directors/shareholders; and (v) a statement of the taxable activities intended to be carried on in Samoa. MOR will issue a Samoa Taxpayer Identification Number and, where appropriate, a VAGST registration number upon complete lodgement. No TIN is required solely for the offshore CRO management business of an exempt International Company.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity election. The Samoa International Company is automatically classified as a tax-exempt offshore entity under the International Companies Act 1987. There is no domestic income-tax equivalent to a CFC election, branch tax election, or partnership pass-through election for the IC; this exemption is mandatory and cannot be waived. VAGST election is limited to compulsory or voluntary registration where a taxable supply in Samoa is being made.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 annual taxable turnover; a non-resident supplier carrying on a taxable activity in Samoa is registrable when this threshold is met or exceeded. A Samoa International Company supplying only offshore CRO and clinical-trial management services does not create a Samoa VAGST registration nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAGST-registered CRO may zero-rate the supply of clinical trial management, biostatistics, data management, regulatory affairs, and related consultancy services when those services are supplied to a non-resident sponsor for use and enjoyment entirely outside Samoa. Tangible exportations from Samoa — including clinical trial kits, biological samples, and investigational medical devices shipped to a foreign laboratory — also qualify for zero-rating where the exporter maintains documentary proof of physical exportation within the prescribed period. The claim is allowed only if the supply is not made in connection with a taxable activity carried on in Samoa by the recipient and the documentation is held before the relevant taxable period return is filed.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Samoa does not currently require real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, electronic invoice stamping, or mandatory API connectivity. A VAGST-registered supplier must issue a tax invoice containing the seller's name, tax identification number, invoice serial number and date, description of supply, taxable amount, rate, and VAGST charged. The Ministry of Revenue accepts standard PDF/physical invoices and does not mandate an approved digital clearance system for export zero-rated claims.

The Ministry of Revenue may audit zero-rated export transactions and require the taxpayer to produce the full customs, shipping, bank remittance, and contractual evidence trail. A fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claim will be reversed by assessment of a VAGST shortfall, with penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded, interest for late payment, and recovery of any refund as a debt due to the State. Criminal prosecution for tax evasion under the Tax Administration Act 2012 may be brought against the IC, its directors, and its tax agent. Claimants should retain all export and remittance records for a minimum of seven years to substantiate every 180-day outward payment cycle.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a managed currency that is not fully convertible. Current-account transactions, including cross-border CRO service fees, pass-through clinical-site reimbursements, and disbursements under a documented clinical trial master service agreement, may be settled through an authorized dealer bank without case-by-case CBS approval provided the dealer receives supporting contracts, invoices, and the standard exchange-control declaration. Capital-account transactions, including equity injections into the Samoa International Company, loan top-ups, and inbound acquisition funding, require prior CBS approval via the authorized dealer. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are lawful but subject to documentary hurdles: audited Samoan financial statements evidencing distributable profits, a tax clearance from the Samoan tax authority (Ministry for Revenue), withholding tax compliance on dividend distributions where the IC's exempt status does not apply, confirmation from the Registrar of International and Foreign Companies that the entity has no outstanding annual fees, and CBS exchange-control application processing. Because the IC enjoys an exempt tax status, the practical convertibility obstacle is documentary rather than fiscal, but any inbound USD that is not accompanied by a dealer-filed purpose narrative will be quarantined in a suspense capital account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All proceeds from exports of goods and services, expressly including Clinical Trial CRO milestone fees invoiced in USD, must be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days of the due date and 100% of the foreign currency receipts must be offered for surrender and conversion into WST through an authorized dealer bank. CBS retention approval may be obtained where demonstrable contractual need exists to retain USD operating balances, such as committed payments to offshore central laboratories; without retention approval, the CRO treasury must negotiate each inbound wire into WST at the dealer's telegraphic-transfer buying rate and maintain a contemporaneous surrender schedule reconciled with each MT103 credit. Failure to surrender is an exchange-control offence under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and can trigger penalties, forced conversion, and suspension of the dealer relationship; the CRO should therefore never allow sponsor funds to accumulate in an unapproved offshore EMI account.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), also styled Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa, operating under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984 and administering the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations 1978, and the Financial Institutions Act 1996 (as amended); CBS licenses all authorized dealer banks and supervises Samoa's foreign exchange, AML/CFT, and payments infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary custody treasury; MEDIUM for auxiliary disbursement only. Samoa has no statutory EMI or fintech licensing category; any deposit-taking or money-transfer business requires a CBS banking or financial-institution license under the Financial Institutions Act 1996. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Payoneer, or Airwallex may be used to receive sponsor payments and disburse international site payments, but they cannot operate as the CRO's authorized dealer, do not satisfy CBS surrender and reporting obligations, and create reportable fund movements outside authorized channels; the International Company should maintain its principal treasury at ANZ or BSP and use EMIs, if at all, only as a named-invoice collection conduit with automatic sweep to the Samoan dealer account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory Local File or Master File threshold applies to International Companies because they are tax-exempt; however, SIFA economic substance guidance and prudential practice require an arm's-length transfer pricing documentation package for cross-border related-party service transactions above SAT 10 million (approximately EUR 500,000), including CRO service agreements, fee benchmarks, cost allocation models, and functional risk profiles

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) Act 2018 applies to SIFA-licensed International Companies. Contract research organization and clinical trial management activities that derive income from intra-group services, service centre functions, or intellectual property holding are classified as relevant activities. The IC must satisfy: (a) direction and management exercised in Samoa, (b) adequate number of qualified employees, (c) adequate operating expenditure in Samoa, (d) physical presence through the registered agent, and (e) filing of an annual ESR return with SIFA. Failure to meet the economic substance test and annual ESR compliance (Form ESR-1) constitutes a serious offence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abuse of IC Exemption

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of tax-exempt status; assessment of all back taxes at the 25% corporate rate plus capital gains and withholding taxes; interest at 10% per annum and a penalty of 100% to 150% of the tax evaded under the Income Tax Act 2012; monetary fines up to SAT 1,000,000

Director personal liability: Directors and officers jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax where the evasion was deliberate or reckless; civil restitution of diverted funds; disqualification from acting as a director for up to 10 years; travel bans and imprisonment up to 5 years
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty under the ESR Act 2018 of up to SAT 500,000 for an initial breach and up to SAT 1,000,000 with imprisonment of directors for repeated breach; SIFA may strike off the company, revoke the international company licence, and assess deemed income at the full corporate rate

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for all penalties and outstanding government fees; cease-and-desist orders; travel restrictions; obstruction of company divestiture; custodial sentence up to 10 years if false declarations are certified
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Fines up to SAT 5,000,000 for the corporate entity; confiscation of funds under proceeds of crime provisions; suspension or revocation of the SIFA licence and banking relationship; freezing of the IC's assets; statutory penalty per transaction for unreported suspicious activity

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the registered agent face criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to 14 years; personal confiscation orders; travel bans and 10-year prohibition on holding financial services positions; civil fiduciary claims for loss suffered by third-party investors
Fraudulent Transfer Pricing and Sham Arrangements

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization and taxing of diverted profits at 25%; transfer pricing adjustments with interest; penalty of 50% of the under-reported gross revenue; reciprocal disclosures under the Multilateral Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance and exchange of information with treaty partners

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrated the sham are personally liable for the recharacterized tax and penalties; criminal sanctions for aiding tax evasion; civil clawback actions in the jurisdiction of the parent sponsor; travel bans and extradition risk under mutual legal assistance treaties; imprisonment up to 7 years
