Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila, Vanuatu (tax administration portal: customsinlandrevue.gov.vu); offshore entity licensing and registration are administered separately by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) via its e-registry portal (vfsc.vu).

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, personal income tax, capital gains tax, or VAT, and therefore does not issue a TIN or VAT ID to non-resident International Companies (ICs). The IC's statutory fiscal identifier is the VFSC registration number obtained under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] through a licensed agent; this number is used as the de facto taxpayer/entity identifier for bank onboarding, payment rails (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy), and foreign tax-residence attestation. No Inland Revenue registration, tax-return lodgement, VAT enrollment, or annual domestic tax filing is required for an IC that conducts business outside Vanuatu.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, transparent-entity election, or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Vanuatu law. A foreign-owned IC may elect its permitted activity scope at incorporation by confirming (via VFSC Form) that all business is transacted outside Vanuatu; this statutory offshore election preserves the zero-tax treatment while avoiding the domestic business-licence tax. There is no other tax classification election available, and no hybrid instrument or PE election is recognized by the domestic legislation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) operates under a managed-float regime referenced to a composite basket of AUD, USD, EUR, and JPY. Current-account convertibility is liberalized: cross-border service fees, software subscription receipts, and profit/dividend remittances may be executed through authorized dealers without prior central bank pre-approval when within delegated thresholds. Capital-account transactions, including inbound equity injections, non-resident borrowings, and treasury placements, require authorized-dealer approval or prior RBV permission above stipulated limits. The Vanuatu International Company (IC) benefits from statutory exemption from Vanuatu corporate income tax and withholding tax on dividends under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], but profit remittance to non-resident shareholders still requires exchange-control reporting, an authenticated board resolution, audited or accountant-certified financial statements, and AML/KYC evidence of the ultimate beneficial owners. Convertibility risk is moderate given the small depth of the onshore VUV market; full conversion of USD receipts to VUV is freely available for operational funding, while USD retention in onshore foreign-currency accounts is permitted under authorized-dealer standing rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 65] and subsidiary regulations, all export proceeds, including cross-border receipts from digital services and SaaS subscription sales, must be repatriated to Vanuatu through an authorized dealer within six (6) months of payment receipt. The foreign currency must be offered for sale to the authorized dealer, with the standard statutory conversion ratio being 100% of export proceeds, unless an express exemption or retention permission is granted by the RBV. In current banking practice, authorized dealers permit an IC to retain USD in a designated foreign-currency clearing account for subsequent outbound trade disbursements where a monitorable export revenue schedule and standing surrender election are filed; conversion to VUV is then limited to amounts required for local expenditure, statutory fees, and regulatory obligations. The micro-SaaS subscription collections from Paddle and Lemon Squeezy must therefore be swept via SWIFT MT103 into the IC's onshore Vanuatu USD account, with the retention election documented with the bank and reconciled quarterly to satisfy the six-month surrender timeline.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the central bank and delegated exchange control administrator under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 65], jointly administered with the Vanuatu Ministry of Finance; licensed commercial banks in Vanuatu operate as authorized dealers for all foreign exchange transactions.

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore digital EMI platforms, including Wise Platform, Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer, and similar payment-facilitation entities, generally decline entity KYB for jurisdiction code VU because of Vanuatu's FATF grey-list status, thin correspondent-bank appetite, and the exchange-control prohibition on holding foreign receipts indefinitely outside Vanuatu. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy, as Merchant of Record, do not provide the IC with a bank account but remit settlement proceeds via SWIFT MT103 to the IC's onshore account. Any offshore EMI collection arrangement would require an express RBV authorized-dealer exemption and would still attract enhanced due diligence under the AML/CFT Act; therefore the recommended compliant architecture is direct SWIFT settlement from the Merchant of Record to a Vanuatu licensed commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The promoter or investing shareholder remits initial subscribed capital from a home-country bank account via SWIFT MT103 to the IC's designated USD capital deposit account at BSP Vanuatu, ANZ Vanuatu, or BRED Vanuatu, with payment field 70 carrying a clear purpose code such as 'IC SUBSCRIPTION CAPITAL — ISSUE OF SHARES' and the VFSC IC registration number. The authorized dealer simultaneously obtains the board resolution authorizing the share issuance, the share allotment register, the source-of-funds bank statements, and a UBO declaration. Inbound amounts exceeding the authorized-dealer de minimis threshold are reported under the Exchange Control Act via the bank's RBV declaration form; no statutory minimum paid-in capital applies to a Vanuatu IC, but maintaining declared issued capital of at least USD 10,000 is the prudent banking standard to avoid empty-shell classification. The IC must preserve SWIFT confirmation messages, exchange-control deposit receipts, and the VFSC annual return filings as a full audit trail.Outbound treasury payments for trade settlement are executed exclusively via SWIFT MT103 from the IC's VUV/USD operational account at a Vanuatu authorized-dealer bank. Payments to global SaaS vendors, cloud infrastructure providers (AWS, Google Cloud, Cloudflare), software licensors, and affiliate marketing networks use UN/EDIFACT purpose codes such as '131' (other services) or '842' (subscription/licensing fees) as directed by the bank's compliance team. Merchant-of-record settlements from Paddle and Lemon Squeezy flow inbound as MT103 credits from the MoR settlement bank and are matched against the export revenue schedule. Dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are remitted via MT103 supported by audited or accountant-certified IC financial statements, the VFSC solvency and annual return filings, and the exchange-control remittance form. Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (nil) - Vanuatu does not levy corporate income tax on International Companies.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (nil). Vanuatu does not enact a dividend withholding tax, and an International Company may distribute all profits to a non-resident shareholder free of any Vanuatu tax, exchange control or balance-of-payments restriction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within six months after the annual registration anniversary date; the first Annual Return is due no later than 18 months after incorporation. There is no tax assessment, estimation, prepayment or final-return cycle because no corporate income tax is imposed.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the last day of the month immediately following the end of the tax period, roughly 30 days after the close of each quarter.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - Vanuatu enacts no transfer pricing statute and therefore imposes no Local File or Master File obligation measured in VUV. For defensibility in the beneficial owner's home jurisdiction, where the SaaS IP and commercial management may reside, a prudent minimum threshold is to retain a benchmarking or transfer pricing study once intercompany transactions exceed 30,000,000 VUV (approximately USD 250,000) per annum, even though no Vanuatu law requires it.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's economic substance legislation imposes a local substance test only on companies carrying on defined relevant activities, including banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centre, pure equity holding and intellectual property holding. A consumer Micro SaaS operator that derives its revenue from SaaS subscriptions, rather than from royalties on a separately held IP portfolio, falls outside the IP-holding head, and no Vanuatu economic substance test applies. The IC remains tax-exempt under the International Companies Act, and its only local presence is the registered office and agent. If the IC were later converted into a pure IP holding vehicle, or if it received passive royalties from related entities, the IP-holder substance rules would require locally staffed core income-generating activities, physical premises and adequate local expenditure, which are practically difficult for a letter-box IC. In addition, EU and OECD anti-haven screening, FATF monitoring and the beneficial owner's own CFC, economic substance and general anti-abuse rules must be assessed independently because they operate on top of the Vanuatu domestic exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Foreign Tax Evasion and Sham Corporation Abuse

Statutory sanction: The home tax authority disregards the IC as a sham, assesses the beneficial owner on the worldwide SaaS profits, applies negligence or intentional evasion penalties commonly ranging from 25% to 200% of the evaded tax, imposes interest, and may commence criminal tax-fraud proceedings. Under exchange-of-information arrangements, Vanuatu corporate and registry records of the IC and the beneficial owner are obtainable on lawful request.

Director personal liability: Shareholders and de facto directors face joint and several liability for the underlying corporate tax under foreign general anti-abuse rules. Travel bans, asset-restraint orders and extradition may be issued; punishment for deliberate fraud typically ranges from 12 months to 14 years imprisonment, alongside personal restitution orders.
Strike-Off and Loss of Corporate Shield / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act and the VFSC fee schedule, non-payment and non-filing attract late fees and daily surcharges. The Registrar may strike the IC off the register; the company is dissolved on default, its assets vest in the Registrar, and continued trading after strike-off is a statutory offence. The IC's merchant and bank accounts are frozen in practice when VFSC notifies payment processors and correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally liable for debts incurred after dissolution or where the company traded while insolvent. Foreign payment platforms and creditor jurisdictions apply quasi-veil-piercing: the beneficial owner and nominee directors are jointly sued for refunds, chargebacks and fraud where the IC is found to be a mere cloak.
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing (AML/CTF)

Statutory sanction: A breach of the Vanuatu AML/CTF legal framework can attract criminal fines up to 50,000,000 VUV and imprisonment up to 15 years for aggravated offences; enforcement agencies may seize the IC's accounts, grant recovery orders and initiate disqualification proceedings. Internationally, OFAC or FATF measures can list the entity, freeze correspondent banking relationships, and cause payment processors to terminate the merchant account with indefinite reserve withholding.

Director personal liability: Directors who operate an unlicensed money-service business, sign sham loan agreements or conceal the ultimate beneficial owner are personally prosecutable. Personal bankruptcy petitions, cross-border asset freezes and travel bans are customary; the Vanuatu corporate veil is pierced where the IC is the instrumentality of fraud or money laundering.
Fraudulent Invoice Scheme / Trade-Based Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: The foreign tax administration disallows the related-company deductions, reattributes the IC margin under CFC, GILTI, transfer pricing or general anti-abuse rules, and levies gross-up penalties commonly from 30% to 150% of the additional tax, plus interest. The IC is also exposed to VFSC scrutiny because the invoices contradict the registered-object clause and the record-keeping requirements of the International Companies Act.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve false invoices or intercompany agreements are exposed to tax-fraud charges in the payer jurisdiction. Personal civil restitution is ordered for disgorgement of inflated margins; in cross-border criminal proceedings, directors may be extradited, subjected to travel bans and sentenced to imprisonment applicable to false accounting, commonly from 1 to 14 years.
VAT Evasion and Merchant-of-Record Complicity

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu VAT Act, deliberate non-registration and under-remittance attract a civil penalty of up to 200% of the VAT due plus monthly interest; foreign digital-services tax and VAT/GST regimes impose comparable failure-to-register and accuracy penalties on both the platform and the IC. Pursuit of shadow-economy software vendors includes deregistration and criminal referral.

Director personal liability: VAT statutes in Vanuatu and major consumer markets contain director-liability clauses: a responsible director who authorised the evasion is jointly and severally liable from personal assets for the unpaid VAT, and where fraud is proved, the applicable custodial sentence is up to 12 months in Vanuatu and up to 7 years in some OECD jurisdictions.
