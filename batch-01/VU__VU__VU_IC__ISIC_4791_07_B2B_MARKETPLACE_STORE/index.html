Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA) under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; online filing and compliance portal: VRCA Online Services (https://online.vrca.vu); customs declarations processed through ASYCUDA World; corporate registry functions administered separately by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a national tax identification number unless it has taxable operations in Vanuatu or voluntarily registers for VAT. Procedure: (1) complete VAT registration application Form VAT-01 via the VRCA Online Services portal; (2) submit certified certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing issued by VFSC, proof of registered agent in Vanuatu, and identity documentation for directors/shareholders; (3) declare a local address for service of documents; (4) where the IC conducts exclusively offshore business, submit a statutory declaration confirming no local taxable supplies are made in Vanuatu and obtain a written exclusion or exemption confirmation from VRCA; (5) upon approval, VRCA issues a Value Added Tax Registration Number (VRN) in the format VU-XXXXX and files the entity in the national tax register.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a national tax identification number unless it has taxable operations in Vanuatu or voluntarily registers for VAT. Procedure: (1) complete VAT registration application Form VAT-01 via the VRCA Online Services portal; (2) submit certified certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing issued by VFSC, proof of registered agent in Vanuatu, and identity documentation for directors/shareholders; (3) declare a local address for service of documents; (4) where the IC conducts exclusively offshore business, submit a statutory declaration confirming no local taxable supplies are made in Vanuatu and obtain a written exclusion or exemption confirmation from VRCA; (5) upon approval, VRCA issues a Value Added Tax Registration Number (VRN) in the format VU-XXXXX and files the entity in the national tax register.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu levies no income tax, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax; therefore no check-the-box, entity classification, or hybrid mismatch elections exist under Vanuatu law. The IC regime itself functions as a statutory elective exemption under the International Companies Act: an IC may elect to remain a non-local, tax-exempt offshore vehicle solely by observing the prohibition on conducting domestic business in Vanuatu, trading with Vanuatu residents, owning Vanuatu land, and carrying on prescribed licensed local activities. If the IC breaches those limits, it loses the exemption and becomes subject to the ordinary VAT regime with an option to apply for voluntary VAT registration; there is no further statutory entity-level election available under the VAT Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 in aggregate taxable supplies over any rolling 12-month period (approximately USD 42,000 at prevailing exchange rates). Suppliers whose taxable supplies are exclusively zero-rated export or offshore services may still voluntarily register to claim input VAT credits; a non-resident or IC making no Vanuatu-source taxable supplies does not trigger the compulsory threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu, provided the exporter retains proof that the goods left Vanuatu within 30 days of supply, and to services supplied to a person who remains outside Vanuatu at the time the services are performed, where the benefit of the supply is not derived by a Vanuatu resident or used in Vanuatu. An International Company may claim zero rating only to the extent the supply originates from Vanuatu or passes through Vanuatu customs; purely offshore platform intermediary services supplied by an IC to overseas buyers are outside the scope of Vanuatu VAT altogether and require no VAT charge, while goods exported through the marketplace qualify for the 0% rate. Claims must be supported by contemporaneous commercial contracts, purchase orders, logistics records, customs declarations, and verifiable payment evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged through ASYCUDA World bearing the VRCA customs seal and export declaration number
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or equivalent transport document evidencing physical movement of goods out of Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a licensed bank confirming receipt of hard currency proceeds from the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin (where required by the destination jurisdiction)
  • Signed B2B marketplace sales agreement or platform service order demonstrating the offshore counterparty and free-on-board / ex-works delivery terms
  • Warehouse or inventory transfer records proving the goods were never consumed, sold, or delivered within Vanuatu

Vanuatu has not implemented mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. Tax invoices may be issued in paper or electronic format under the VAT Act. A valid tax invoice must show the supplier's name, address, VRN, invoice date, description and quantity of goods or services, amount exclusive of VAT, VAT amount separately, and the headline rate applied; for zero-rated exports, the invoice must explicitly state the zero rate and cross-reference the supporting customs documentation. No government API or continuous fiscal data transmission is required for invoice issuance.

VRCA and the State Law Office may conduct post-clearance audits for a period of at least five years from the date of export claim. Where zero-rating is claimed without compliant export documentation, the VAT becomes immediately payable with interest from the date of supply. Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract an additional penalty not exceeding 200% of the tax understated, forfeiture of the goods or their value, and criminal prosecution carrying fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to two years; deliberate falsification of customs declarations may result in suspension of the exporter's registration and revocation of the IC's exemption certificate by VFSC. Recovered amounts are clawed back with compounded statutory interest, and ring-fencing of refunds is available only to registered persons with a clean compliance record. Since the IC earns no Vanuatu-source income and requires no income tax filing, all substantive audit exposure in this structure centers on VAT zero-rating substantiation, customs valuation, and proof of non-resident supply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is a current-account convertible currency under Vanuatu's Article VIII obligations to the International Monetary Fund; trade payments and service-related remittances flow freely through RBV-authorized dealers. Capital-account transfers remain nominally subject to exchange-control approval, but Vanuatu applies a liberal review standard, and an International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is treated as non-resident, making proceeds and distributions presumptively eligible for unrestricted outward remittance once exchange-control declarations and bank-level due diligence are satisfied. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal: the IC pays no domestic corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax on dividends, interest, or management fees; the only practical friction points are confirmation of source of funds by the remitting bank and completion of the prescribed exchange-control forms.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement and no mandatory vatu conversion quota in Vanuatu. Export receipts arising from offshore B2B marketplace operations may be retained in VUV or in permitted foreign-currency accounts (principally USD, AUD, EUR, NZD, and GBP) held with an RBV-authorized dealer; no regulatory timeline compels conversion of export proceeds into vatu. The applicable Exchange Control General Rules require only prompt inward-remittance declaration and retention of supporting trade documentation for audit purposes, not the sale or conversion of foreign currency to a government-designated account.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the National Foreign Exchange Control Authority under delegated powers conferred by the Exchange Control Act [Cap 55]; ultimate policy oversight rests with the Vanuatu Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, while the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) remains the licensing authority for International Companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Vanuatu has no dedicated e-money institution (EMI) licensing category; the National Payment System Act grants payment-service licensing powers to the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu, but resident licensed payment providers are scarce, and offshore digital EMIs frequently decline to service tax-exempt International Companies because of regional correspondent-bank de-risking. The viable Tier-2 structure is a regulated global B2B payment facilitator acting as an unlicensed software and payout-agent layer over a fully licensed Vanuatu authorized-dealer bank account, never as the primary custodian of client funds; this preserves regulatory defensibility while enabling Alibaba_Pay and SWIFT wire connectivity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing uses SWIFT MT103 with the purpose description 'Shareholder Equity Injection / Paid-Up Capital under the International Companies Act [Cap 222]' and the Standard Settlement Instruction of the receiving Vanuatu authorized-dealer bank; the remitting institution must attach a sponsor-bank letter confirming the KYC status of the capital provider. The receiving Vanuatu bank completes the Inward Remittance Declaration (Form IR) for exchange-control and statistical purposes, retains the board resolution and share-allotment minutes, and places the funds in an escrowed capital account segregated from operating vatu balances. Because the International Companies Act imposes no minimum paid-up capital, the injection amount is unrestricted; the decisive compliance gates are source-of-funds verification, beneficial ownership disclosure, and demonstration that the funds are not derived from proceeds of crime or sanctions-connected parties.Outbound settlements flow through three principal rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers for operating disbursements, supplier payments, and marketplace payouts denominated in USD or other hard currencies; (ii) intra-bank book-transfer clearing for local and regional merchant payouts; and (iii) Commercial Letters of Credit forwarded by SWIFT MT700/MT707 for large-ticket B2B trade settlements, with MT202 COVER used for interbank cover through New York CHIPS or Fedwire correspondents. Vanuatu does not operate an offshore real-time gross settlement rail; clearance ultimately depends on the correspondent network of the RBV-licensed commercial banks. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing legislation and no Local File, Master File or CbCR regime; statutory threshold is VT 0. As VFSC administrative practice under the ESR regime, an IC must retain an economic-substance file containing arm's-length distribution-margin benchmarking whenever annual gross platform income exceeds VT 30,000,000 (approximately USD 250,000).

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations Act No. 25 of 2019 and the ESR Regulations apply to every Vanuatu IC carrying on a 'relevant activity', which includes a 'Distribution and Service Centre' such as an electronic B2B wholesale platform. The IC must demonstrate in each financial year: (i) adequate physical premises in Vanuatu; (ii) adequate full-time employees in Vanuatu; (iii) adequate operating expenditure in Vanuatu; and (iv) performance of core income-generating activities and strategic management in Vanuatu. Compliance is evidenced through the annual ESR Return; failure triggers penalties, deregistration and automatic exchange of information with foreign competent authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Filing

Statutory sanction: Recovery of all evaded licence fees and VAT, administrative fines of up to VT 10,000,000, confiscation of the proceeds of the marketplace operation, deregistration, and reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent are jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid duties; courts may impose exit/travel bans, order disqualification from managing Vanuatu companies, and impose imprisonment of up to two years for statutory offences and up to seven years for fraud.
Shell Company and ESR Substance Default

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty up to VT 10,000,000 per return; daily default penalties; administrative strike-off; exchange of information with foreign tax authorities; inclusion of the entity in published non-compliant lists resulting in banking de-risking.

Director personal liability: Directors and the corporate service provider are personally accountable for penalties and costs; criminal prosecution of officers for continuing default; travel bans; disqualification from acting as director; potential imprisonment of up to two years.
Breach of IC Exemption — Domestic Trading

Statutory sanction: Automatic and retroactive loss of the IC tax exemption from the date of breach; assessment of business licence fees on the turnover band and 12.5% VAT on local supplies; penalties of up to 200% of the underpaid tax; potential strike-off by VFSC.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unpaid licence fees and VAT collected but not remitted; continued unlicensed trading is a criminal offence carrying fines and imprisonment; travel bans may be imposed pending settlement of the assessment.
AML and Payment-Rail Abuse

Statutory sanction: Offences under the Proceeds of Crime Act; fines up to VT 25,000,000, asset freezing and forfeiture, wire-level enforcement actions, and imprisonment of up to 15 years for principals; revocation of the IC registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are individually liable; courts issue travel bans and freezing orders against personal assets; disqualification from holding any financial services licence; extradition cooperation is available for cross-border fraud.
