Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Agency for Business Registration of the Ministry of Planning and Investment operating the National Business Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn); the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) is the locally competent registry authority issuing the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) in the province where the registered office is situated.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 dated 17 June 2020, effective 1 January 2021 (Articles 27-31 on Enterprise Registration; Articles 74-88 on Single-Member Limited Liability Companies); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 (Articles 37-39 on Investment Registration Certificate procedure for foreign investors); Decree No. 01/2021/ND-CP on Enterprise Registration (Forms I-1, I-2, I-5); Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP on investment procedures; and Circular No. 05/2014/TT-NHNN as amended on Indirect Investment Capital Accounts.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Enterprise Registration (Giay de nghi dang ky doanh nghiep) submitted together with the Investment Project Proposal (De xuat du an dau tu), leading to the issuance of the Investment Registration Certificate (Giay chung nhan dang ky dau tu) and the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Giay chung nhan dang ky doanh nghiep); the ERC is the constitutive incorporation instrument of the limited liability company.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 46 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed under the Law on Enterprises 2020 for a single-member limited liability company engaged in passive family investment holding. The foreign investor must demonstrate financial capability equivalent to the committed charter capital in the IRC dossier under Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP. The charter capital must be fully contributed within ninety (90) days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate. No statutory escrow account or mandatory capital deposit account is required by incorporation law, but the contributed capital must be remitted into a legally licensed commercial bank account in Viet Nam; a separate Indirect Investment Capital Account (IICA) is required for inward remittances destined for securities and custody transactions.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification: the registered office must be covered by a Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate (Van ban chap nhan ket qua nghiem thu ve phong chay va chua chay) issued by the Fire Prevention and Fighting and Rescue Police Department (PC07) under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP; where the company leases space in an already-certified office building, it must obtain a certified copy of the building's acceptance certificate and, for any internal fit-out affecting escape routes or fire systems, a supplementary appraisal and acceptance. Structural load clearance: the physical handling and custody of assets are limited to quiet-office and safe-deposit functions; the lessor must provide the structural conformity certificate of the building with the intended use as offices, and the company must not alter the structural load-bearing elements without a civil engineer's certification. Workplace/occupational clearance: the General Director must adopt an internal labor regulation under Article 118 of the Labour Code No. 45/2019/QH13, submit a labor roster to the Department of Labour, Invalids and Social Affairs, complete the occupational safety and health risk assessment, appoint an occupational safety officer where there are five or more employees, register the employees with the provincial Social Insurance Agency within 30 days of commencement of employment, and, for employees handling securities custody or private banking wire operations, provide anti-fraud and AML/KYC operational protocols consistent with the State Bank of Viet Nam's AML regulations under the Anti-Money Laundering Law No. 14/2022/QH14.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed under the Law on Enterprises 2020 for a single-member limited liability company engaged in passive family investment holding. The foreign investor must demonstrate financial capability equivalent to the committed charter capital in the IRC dossier under Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP. The charter capital must be fully contributed within ninety (90) days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate. No statutory escrow account or mandatory capital deposit account is required by incorporation law, but the contributed capital must be remitted into a legally licensed commercial bank account in Viet Nam; a separate Indirect Investment Capital Account (IICA) is required for inward remittances destined for securities and custody transactions.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance – National e-Tax Portal: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn; National Business Registration Portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested limited liability company must first obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI). It then registers its enterprise through the National Business Registration Portal, which issues the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC). The Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, TIN) is automatically granted by the local tax authority upon ERC approval; the same TIN serves as the VAT registration code. The LLC must file an initial tax registration dossier with supporting corporate legal documents, passports of owners/legal representative, and invested capital charts. TIN issuance generally occurs within 1–3 working days after ERC approval and is delivered through the e-tax portal.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (flat 20% CIT; residents taxed on worldwide income; an LLC cannot be a pass-through entity under Vietnam law). Territorial_Scope: tax-resident LLCs are taxed on worldwide income; non-resident owners are taxed only on Vietnamese-source income. Tax_Exempt_Zone: no automatic tax-exempt zone status by registration alone; only licensed Export Processing Enterprises (EPEs) in export-processing, industrial or high-tech zones receive special 0% VAT and preferential CIT regimes. — A foreign-invested limited liability company must first obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (flat 20% CIT; residents taxed on worldwide income; an LLC cannot be a pass-through entity under Vietnam law). Territorial_Scope: tax-resident LLCs are taxed on worldwide income; non-resident owners are taxed only on Vietnamese-source income. Tax_Exempt_Zone: no automatic tax-exempt zone status by registration alone; only licensed Export Processing Enterprises (EPEs) in export-processing, industrial or high-tech zones receive special 0% VAT and preferential CIT regimes.

Available election forms

Vietnam does not operate check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch entity elections. Available statutory choices include: (i) election for non-resident contractors to pay withholding tax on gross turnover or on deemed profit rates; (ii) election to apply small- and medium-enterprise CIT incentives if statutory size and revenue thresholds are met; (iii) election to determine deductible expenses under the actual accounting method vs deemed accounting basis for certain foreign contractors; (iv) election between one-off or periodic VAT filing for imported services. No tax-transparency election exists for a domestic LLC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 1 billion in annual taxable turnover – VAT registration is mandatory once annual revenue from taxable activities exceeds VND 1 billion; for foreign-invested LLCs, VAT registration is issued together with the TIN at the outset irrespective of the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to exports of goods outside Vietnam, sale of goods to export-processing enterprises (EPEs) or into non-tariff zones, offshore services consumed outside Vietnam, and certain international transport and related services. Conditions require the LLC to be VAT-registered, hold a valid export sales contract, issue a compliant electronic invoice, obtain customs export declaration for goods, provide transportation documents evidencing export, and submit proof that the export payment was received from a non-resident by bank wire or L/C into a Vietnamese bank account within 180 days from the customs export clearance date.

  • Automated Customs Export Declaration – electronic VNACCS/VCIS clearance record
  • Commercial invoice issued under the electronic invoice system
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill evidencing export
  • Export sales contract (with Incoterms and payment terms)
  • Packing list and certificate of origin (if applicable)
  • Inward remittance certificate / bank SWIFT MT103 / bank credit advice showing receipt of foreign exchange from abroad

All taxpayers must use electronic invoices under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular No. 78/2021/TT-BTC. Invoices must be transmitted to the General Department of Taxation's e-invoice system in real time (within 12 hours after issuance) using the e-tax portal API or a service provider's XML/API integration. Invoices require a Tax Authority Code for enterprises; no physical stamping applies. For exports, customs electronic clearance is handled through the Vietnam Automated Cargo and Port Consolidated System (VNACCS/VCIS).

Tax authorities systematically reconcile export declarations, e-invoices, bank receipts, FX repatriation records and customs manifest data. Improper zero-rating claims result in retrospective clawback of input VAT credits, re-imposition of 10% VAT on the denied supply, late-payment interest at 0.03% per day, administrative penalties of 1–3 times the evaded amount for tax evasion, customs fines and freezing of export refunds. Criminal liability applies under the Penal Code (No. 100/2015/QH13, as amended) for large-scale tax fraud, smuggling or counterfeiting export documentation, with sentences up to 20 years' imprisonment and vessels/individual asset confiscation.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam operates a managed partial-convertibility regime for the Vietnamese Dong (VND). Current account transactions (goods trade, services, operational remittances, dividends on approved profits) are freely convertible through SBV-licensed banks upon presentation of underlying documentation (sales contracts, customs declarations, invoices, tax clearance). Capital account transactions are subject to strict control: inward direct investment capital requires an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC), registration of the capital contribution schedule, and the opening of a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at a single licensed commercial bank; intra-group loans require SBV-registered loan agreements; all outbound investments require separate registration. Profit and dividend remittance is permitted annually after audited financial statements, tax finalization, and completion of an annual profit-remittance registration, then executed solely through the DICA; practical hurdles include mandatory risk-based enhanced due diligence, documentary proof of source of funds, and the absence of a fully deliverable offshore VND market for round-tripping.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory surrender ratio for export proceeds in Viet Nam; exporters may retain 100% of export receipts in foreign currency accounts at SBV-licensed credit institutions. However, export proceeds must be repatriated to a licensed bank account in Viet Nam within 90 days from the date of customs clearance/exportation (extendable to 180 days under permitted long-rotation projects and with bank concurrence), and the exporter must submit transport/customs/sale documentation to evidence collection. Any conversion to VND is executed voluntarily at the bank's prevailing interbank rate, subject to the SBV's daily reference rate band; there is no statutory obligation to sell a fixed percentage to the central bank.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (Ngân Hàng Nhà Nước Việt Nam / SBV), exercising dual authority as the national central bank and the Foreign Exchange Administration through its Foreign Exchange Management Department. Regulatory framework: Foreign Exchange Ordinance No. 06/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended, Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP, and Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN as amended by Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI platforms (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, Revolut Business) are not licensed by the State Bank of Vietnam and are legally incapable of holding VND, opening corporate accounts on behalf of a Vietnamese-resident LLC, or functioning as the primary clearing rail for a DICA. The only permissible payment-technology channels are SBV-licensed non-bank payment intermediaries (e.g., MoMo, VNPT Epay, Payoo), which are limited to domestic e-wallet scenarios and cannot accept or disburse FDI capital. Using an offshore EMI as a treasury intermediary for the Vietnamese entity would constitute a direct breach of Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN and Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP (administrative sanctions in banking and foreign exchange), carrying fines, suspension of account usage, and potential criminal exposure for the legal representative.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The sole compliant route for inbound direct capital is the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained at the selected SBV-licensed commercial bank. Sequence: (1) issuance of the IRC and ERC with registered charter capital and contribution schedule; (2) corporate account opening (DICA plus operational VND/FX accounts); (3) the non-resident family/SPO wires via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from an account in the identical legal name, quoting the correct Vietnamese transaction-purpose code for direct equity investment/loan disbursement, the DICA number, the LLC corporate name, and tax identification number; (4) the bank verifies the remittance against the registered schedule, performs the SBV foreign-exchange reporting, and confirms no third-party offsetting; (5) conversion into VND is executed at the bank's interbank rate within the SBV reference-rate band, with a formal FX-conversion confirmation. No capital contribution may bypass the DICA, and any early or excess contribution requires prior approval and an amended IRC before remittance.For trade settlements the LLC may use (a) direct irrevocable SWIFT MT103 transfers through the onshore bank for service disbursements, intra-group current items, and operating expenses; (b) documentary collections with full shipping documents; or (c) commercial letters of credit under UCP600 issued by the Vietnamese bank and, where required, confirmed by an international correspondent bank. All outbound disbursements into foreign currency must be routed through the DICA with statutory purpose codes (e.g., dividend/profit remittance, repayment of foreign loans, outbound investment) and accompanied by audited financial statements, tax clearance, board resolutions, and supporting contracts. Securities purchases and custody trade settlements are executed through the Vietnamese Securities Depository (VSD) sub-custody network using VND settlement at the licensed custodian bank in accordance with Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN.Onshore deliverable FX forwards between the LLC and an SBV-licensed credit institution, with maximum tenor of 365 days and valid only against a genuine underlying current or capital transaction exposure; settled net in VND.FX swaps executed onshore with the licensed bank, providing simultaneous spot-and-forward conversion for treasury repositioning against qualified DICA-linked inflows/outflows.Currency swaps and cross-currency interest rate swaps transacted onshore for long-dated foreign-currency assets, intra-group loans, and investment positions duly registered with the SBV.FX options purchased from SBV-licensed banks to hedge committed trade or financial exposure, with premium payable in VND and documented underlying exposure.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible or legally enforceable as a hedging product for an onshore Vietnamese-resident entity; any offshore NDF exposure constitutes a foreign-exchange-control violation. Acceptable hedging is available only through deliverable onshore products executed via the licensed Vietnamese bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a Vietnamese LLC to its foreign corporate shareholder if the dividend is distributed from after-tax retained earnings and no deemed profit remittance applies; however, a foreign investor that transfers shares realizes capital gains taxed at 0.1% on gross transfer price (or 20% on net capital gains at the seller's election). For branch structures, a remittance of after-tax profits abroad is subject to a 0% remittance tax rate (no separate branch remittance tax), provided the branch has fulfilled CIT obligations; in practice official approval and tax clearance certification are required before remittance, and a 0.1% foreign contractor tax (FCT) may apply to certain cross-border services or deemed income streams. A Vietnamese LLC's distribution of dividends is exempt from WHT, but dividends received from a Vietnamese subsidiary by the LLC are CIT-exempt at the recipient if the subsidiary is also a Vietnamese enterprise and statutory shareholding conditions are met.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Final CIT return must be submitted no later than the 90th day after the end of the fiscal year (i.e., by March 31 for a calendar-year taxpayer). Quarterly provisional payments are due on the 30th day of the first month of the next quarter; total provisional payments during the year must not be less than 80% of the final annual CIT liability to avoid under-payment penalties and interest.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A taxpayer must prepare a Local File (and, where applicable, a Master File and Country-by-Country Report) if the entity has related-party transactions and the total related-party revenue exceeds VND 50 billion OR total related-party transaction value exceeds VND 30 billion during the tax period. Additionally, a Country-by-Country Report is required for ultimate parent groups with consolidated revenue of VND 18,000 billion or more. Even when the entity does not exceed these thresholds and has no related-party transactions, the annual CIT finalization return must include a declaration of related-party relationships and transactions; Decree 132/2020/ND-CP applies a transaction-by-transaction arm's length analysis and requires contemporaneous documentation within 30 days of a tax authority's request. A single family office with no intercompany transactions still must submit the related-party declaration form (Phụ lục III-B for transactions, or the 'GCNT' annex) and may be required to prove economic substance if it holds assets or receives income from Vietnamese sources.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam has not enacted full OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) like the UAE or BVI, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under the Law on Tax Administration (Articles 19 and 33), Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC, and the 'substance-over-form' principle allow tax authorities to disregard transactions lacking genuine business purpose. A Vietnamese single-family office LLC must maintain demonstrable substance: a physical office, local management, investment committee meeting minutes, discretion over investment decisions, local employees or properly documented outsourced management agreements, and accounting records explaining the flow of funds. Lack of substance can trigger: (1) recharacterization of intra-group financing as equity or hidden distribution; (2) disallowance of interest and management fee deductions; (3) imposition of FCT on deemed services; (4) CIT reassessment, late-payment interest, and penalties of up to 100% of the additional tax amount for fraud or concealment (under Article 16 of Decree 125/2020/ND-CP) or 10-20% for non-fraud errors and filing mistakes; (5) application of the Controlled Foreign Company rules if applicable in the shareholder's residence jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to 100% of the evaded tax amount under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP Article 16 (for concealment/fraud); an additional fine of VND 3,000,000 to VND 6,000,000 for form violations; late-payment interest of 0.03% per day; criminal prosecution possible under Penal Code 2015 (amended 2017) Article 200 for tax evasion: for amounts from VND 100,000,000 or from VND 300,000,000 if the taxpayer has prior tax-sanction convictions, the penalty is a fine of VND 100,000,000 to VND 4,500,000,000 or imprisonment from 6 months to 7 years; severe cases involving particularly large amounts and organized schemes can lead to up to 12 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives, directors, and actual managers who sign false returns or instruct concealment can be jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, fines, and interest; criminal liability attaches to the legal representative and financial controller; Vietnamese courts apply the 'legal representative presumption' and may impose travel bans, exit visa denials, seizure of personal assets, and imprisonment under Article 200/202 Penal Code; external market-entry restrictions and reputational debarment from serving as legal representative of any Vietnamese company for up to 5 years can follow.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authorities may disallow all related-party expenses and management fees, recharacterize the entity as a foreign-owned permanent establishment, reclassify all income as taxable at the 20% CIT rate, impose a 100% fraud penalty, and charge 0.03% daily late-payment interest; the Ministry of Planning and Investment and tax authorities may cooperate to suspend the enterprise's tax code or revoke the Investment Registration Certificate; under anti-money-laundering law, the State Bank of Vietnam may impose administrative fines on the financial institutions servicing the shell entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders who control the shell may be ordered by a court to pay the corporate tax liabilities out of personal assets under the audited 'enterprise legal personality abuse' doctrine; the director may face a travel ban pending tax debt collection; if the shell is used for money laundering or cross-border financial crime under Penal Code Article 324, directors face imprisonment from 5 to 10 years (or 10 to 15 years in organized/great-value cases) and fines up to VND 100,000,000,000; senior managers of the licensed bank or securities firm serving the family office can also face administrative sanctions for deficient AML/KYC due diligence.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and High-Risk Investment Holding Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines for individuals/institutions: up to VND 300,000,000 for reporting violations and up to VND 2,000,000,000 for repeating serious breaches of AML obligations; credit institutions and securities companies may be fined and have their licenses suspended; criminal prosecution for money laundering under Penal Code Article 324: fines up to VND 200,000,000 and imprisonment from 5 to 10 years, or life imprisonment if the offense involves organized crime, international networks, or enormous value (over VND 1,000,000,000,000); violative assets are confiscated.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the beneficial owner of the single-family office may be held criminally liable as principals or accomplices; evidence that a director ignored warnings from a compliance officer intensifies liability by 50% under the Law on AML; travel bans, seizure of passports, and prohibitions on leaving Vietnam are standard investigative measures; civil liability extends to restitution of laundered funds and damages to third-party investors.
Undercase Registration or Unlicensed Investment Activities (Cross-Border Regulatory Violation)

Statutory sanction: If the activity is deemed a securities/investment service, the enterprise can be subject to administrative fines from VND 100,000,000 up to VND 500,000,000 under Decree 128/2021/ND-CP and orders to cease operations; income from such unlicensed activity is fully taxable and may be deemed a source of criminal tax fraud; the SSC may report the violation to the Minister of Finance for criminal prosecution if thresholds apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who engage in unlicensed regulated activity face criminal liability for operating a securities firm without a license under Penal Code Article 209 (illegal business) with fines or imprisonment up to 5 years; they are jointly responsible for refunding investor losses; the family's reputational exposure and the legal representative's suspension from company management functions are consequential.
Failure to File Annual CIT Finalization and Related-Party Tax Disclosure (Procedural Failure)

Statutory sanction: Fixed fines graduated by severity: VND 4,000,000-8,000,000 for late filing up to 90 days (depending on number of articles), over 90 days: VND 15,000,000-25,000,000 plus 0.03% daily interest on the unpaid tax; failure to provide documentation upon request can trigger a re-assessment based on the tax authority's unilateral recharacterization, plus a 20% penalty on the under-declared amount; repeated violations may result in enterprise code suspension, cancellation of the tax registration, and forced revenue deregistration.

Director personal liability: The legal representative is personally liable for administrative fines that remain unpaid after the corporate assets are exhausted; Vietnamese writ-of-execution law allows the enforcement agency to place the legal representative on a no-exit list, temporarily suspend business activities, and confiscate personal motor vehicles, bank accounts, and real property to satisfy the debt.
