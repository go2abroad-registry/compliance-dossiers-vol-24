Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for current-account transactions, and Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, meaning no trade-related or invisible-payment restrictions apply to the ISIC 5320 express courier fee stream. Capital-account movements remain subject to procedural exchange control documentation through authorized dealers, but profit remittance and capital repatriation by a tax-exempt International Company are legally permissible without approval bottlenecks; practical friction arises from VUV market thinness, limited correspondent bank routing, and bank-level KYC de-risking, rather than from statutory convertibility barriers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 55], receipts for goods exports must be received through an authorized dealer and repatriated within six months of shipment; for service exports, including cross-border express courier billing, proceeds are treated as invisible transactions and must be repatriated through an authorized dealer without a mandatory statutory conversion ratio. Retained VUV and foreign currency earnings may be held in designated foreign-currency accounts at market rates, subject to the authorized bank's internal documentary review.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the monetary authority and administrator of the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 55], in coordination with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for the licensing of securities, exchange, and money transfer dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium onshore viability: Vanuatu does not recognize passporting of foreign e-money or EMI licenses; a foreign digital EMI (for example Wise or Airwallex) may be utilized only as an ancillary collection/settlement tool, while primary corporate settlement must flow through a locally licensed authorized dealer. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing statute, no Local File, no Master File, and no country-by-country reporting obligation; a VUV threshold is not defined. Prudent foreign parents must nonetheless maintain an OECD-aligned TP benchmarking study for the courier fee flows (typically a TNMM on delivery and sorting functions), because the home tax administration will reattribute profits of the Vanuatu IC under CFC and anti-avoidance rules and will expect arm's-length contemporaneous documentation.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) equivalent to those of BVI, Cayman or Jersey, and a standard IC is subject to no local ESR filing or substance test. Notwithstanding, EU and OECD monitoring of Vanuatu requires demonstrable offshore substance: a real registered office, minute books, signed courier service contracts, actual invoicing and matching bank flows, and board decisions undertaken by non-resident directors. A 'shell' IC with no bank account, no contracts and no transaction records is treated as a conduit or sham by foreign anti-BEPS rules and by Vanuatu AML supervisors, exposing the structure to recharacterisation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Beneficial Owner Home-Country Offence)

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu: criminal fraud liability under the Penal Code [Cap 138] with imprisonment exposure; the home tax administration imposes evasion penalties of 50–200% of understated tax plus interest, and courier bank records are obtainable through mutual legal assistance and Global Forum exchange channels.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false invoices or control the Vanuatu IC's bank account face fraudulent-trading liability; civil restitution of all evaded taxes, freezing orders over personal and corporate assets, travel bans, extradition requests and imprisonment in the home jurisdiction.
Prohibited Local Trading (Shell Company Misuse)

Statutory sanction: The IC forfeits its statutory tax exemption retrospectively; the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority assesses 15% VAT on all local supplies together with customs duties; VFSC strikes the company off the register and fines apply under the International Companies Act for each prohibited transaction.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the corporation's unpaid VAT, duties and penalties; VFSC disbars the offending directors and agent from forming any future Vanuatu entity, and the directors' personal assets answer for the tax shortfall.
AML/CFT and Unlicensed Payment Processing

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 5 of 2006: criminal fines and imprisonment of up to 10 years; the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit freezes the IC's accounts; VFSC revokes the IC registration without the usual strike-off cure.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers, directors and beneficial owners face criminal conviction with automatic confiscation of courier assets; directors of an IC used as a payment façade are personally liable in tort to defrauded third parties and are placed on the national financial exclusion list with travel bans and visa denials.
False Beneficial Ownership and Nominee Concealment

Statutory sanction: False statements under the International Companies Act subject the IC to strike-off, regulatory fines and forfeiture of the registered agent's licence; under the AML Act, false CDD information attracts imprisonment and fines in addition to the revocation of the company's exempt certificate.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors are criminally liable for the acts of the true principal, and the true beneficial owner, once identified, is personally liable for full restitution; fraudulent nominee structures cause automatic revocation of the exemption certificate and confiscation of dividend flows.
Fraudulent Tax Residency or Exemption Certificate Claim

Statutory sanction: VFSC withdraws the certificate and strikes off the IC; the foreign jurisdiction applies CFC, PFIC or exit-tax anti-abuse regimes, reattributes 100% of the courier profits to the beneficial owner and adds civil fraud penalties of up to 100% of the understated tax; professional advisers incur promoter-penalty exposure.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify offshore management in a tax residency or exemption filing are civilly liable for tax shortfalls, interest and penalties; travel restrictions are routinely imposed in such files and custodial sentences for organised tax fraud reach 7 years in the home jurisdiction.
