Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA) — the merged national authority administering the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 249], the Customs Act, and all indirect taxes; official portal: www.vrca.gov.vu. Corporate registration and licensing: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), portal: www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) with the VFSC under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation and unique VFSC Company Number. Step 2: Apply to the VFSC for the annual International Company license; no separate tax registration is required because a standard IC is statutorily exempt from all national taxes (including income, capital gains, withholding and stamp duties) and the VFSC license fee substitutes for tax filing obligations. Step 3: If and only if the IC will make taxable supplies in Vanuatu or import goods (e.g., local bunker supply), file Form VRCA-VAT-REG with the VRCA attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office certificate, director passport copies, and business license, to obtain a TIN/VAT registration certificate within approximately 5 to 10 business days. Step 4: For pure offshore brokerage of crude oil/refined products (title, risk, and delivery outside Vanuatu), no VAT registration is required and the IC operates solely on its VFSC number plus any separately obtained energy trading or marine fuel supply license.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or consolidated filing elections exist under Vanuatu law. The IC's tax-exempt classification is mandatory and automatic under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]. An IC may elect to convert into a resident ordinary company under the Companies Act [Cap. 191], thereby relinquishing the exemption and becoming subject to VAT, customs, and all resident obligations; no other elective regimes are available to a non-resident-owned IC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 aggregate taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months (compulsory registration threshold in Vanuatu Vatu); a purely offshore IC trading crude oil/refined products without local supplies or imports falls below the territorial nexus and is not registrable. Persons below the threshold may apply for voluntary registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods, including marine fuel oil and distillates supplied to international-going vessels, are zero-rated under Schedule 2 of the VAT Act, provided the supplier holds valid VAT registration (or the supply is entirely outside the territorial scope for offshore ICs) and proves removal of the goods from Vanuatu by customs export entry. The supplier must issue a tax invoice marked as an export/zero-rated supply, retain evidence that title and risk passed to a non-resident buyer, and demonstrate settlement in foreign currency. Crude oil and refined product trades brokered by an International Company with delivery, title transfer, and payment occurring entirely offshore are outside the charge to VAT and attract neither 12.5% nor 0% VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry) stating HS 2709 (crude petroleum oils) or HS 2710 (petroleum oils, not crude) and the local loading or storage facility
  • Bill of Lading or Bunker Delivery Note (BDN) evidencing delivery to the international vessel or foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/MT202 cable confirming receipt of foreign-currency proceeds in the IC's nominated bank account within the statutory period
  • Original VAT 'zero-rated export supply' tax invoice matched to the underlying purchase invoices, tank storage receipts, and the charter or sale confirmation to substantiate the exported quantity and value

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous clearance, digital stamping, or API-based e-invoicing mandate exists in Vanuatu. VAT invoices may be issued on paper or in PDF and must state the supplier VAT number, 'tax invoice' designation, date, description, quantity, price, and VAT charged (or 'zero-rated export supply' indication). Customs declarations are filed electronically through the ASYCUDA World system, which assigns an electronic customs entry reference, but this does not constitute tax invoice clearance.

A false zero-rating claim triggers reassessment clawing back the 12.5% VAT that should have been charged on the supply together with the reversal of any credited input VAT, plus a penalty of up to 100% of the under-assessed tax and interest at 10% per annum. Issuing fictitious tax invoices or knowingly false declarations is an offence under the VAT Act and Customs Act, carrying fines of up to VT 1,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 2 years for directors and officers; deliberate misdeclaration in customs export entries may render the fuel consignment forfeit and expose the IC to civil penalties of up to twice the duty evaded. The VRCA may cancel the VAT registration and the VFSC may strike off the International Company or revoke its energy-trading license for serious fraudulent conduct, with liabilities following the officers and any shadow principals.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu accepts IMF Article VIII obligations, so current-account payments and transfers are generally unrestricted. The Vatu (VUV) is not a globally tradable currency; spot liquidity is shallow and offshore accounts are normally maintained in USD for petroleum settlements. Capital-account convertibility is de facto available to non-resident International Companies because the IC regime prohibits carrying on business with Vanuatu residents, effectively ring-fencing profits offshore. Profit remittance via dividend or capital redemption is not subject to exchange control approval for a validly licensed IC, but must be executed through an authorised commercial bank with supporting corporate documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu currently imposes no mandatory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no minimum VUV conversion ratio. A licensed International Company engaged in ISIC 4661 wholesale crude oil and refined products may retain export receipts in USD or any other freely usable foreign currency. If a bank receives a VUV payment on behalf of the IC as an exception, the proceeds can be converted to USD under the bank's normal FX capacity without statutory surrender. The operative obligation is documentary: the bank will expect the underlying sales contract, invoice, bill of lading, and SWIFT payment reference to demonstrate a genuine trade transaction.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) acting under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 62], with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as the licensing authority for International Companies and foreign exchange dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for the primary treasury route. Vanuatu does not maintain a dedicated e-money institution framework for foreign-currency clearing, and offshore digital EMIs are not VFSC-regulated as banks. Such EMIs cannot issue or clear VUV-denominated instruments, do not provide documentary credit services, and are generally unacceptable for high-value petroleum cargo settlements. They may be used only as auxiliary non-VUV payment wallets where the trade counterparty accepts lower documentary standards, but the principal corporate banking relationship must remain with a licensed commercial bank or a reputable foreign correspondent bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0.00% - zero headline rate. The Republic of Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax, and no alternative minimum tax. An International Company registered under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) is entirely exempt from domestic income taxation.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0.00% - no dividend withholding tax is imposed in Vanuatu. The International Companies Act 1992 prohibits any withholding on dividends, redemption proceeds, or capital distributions of an International Company; non-resident shareholders are paid gross without declaration or prior ruling.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the anniversary date of incorporation each year. A late fee equal to 20% of the annual licence fee is levied after a 30-day grace period, and VFSC may suspend the company and initiate strike-off proceedings after continued default of three months.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the last day of the month immediately following the end of the taxable period, e.g., first quarter due on 30 April. No extension is permitted for offshore entities unless a written deferral is granted by the Commissioner.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing threshold is codified in Vanuatu in VUV, and no Local File or Master File obligation is imposed by the Income Tax regime. However, the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 requires an annual economic substance filing for any relevant activity; for ISIC 4661 wholesale fuel brokerage, the IC must evidence arm's-length pricing and real operational expenditure against controlled turnover of any size, including single-transaction SBLC routing. In practical VFSC administration, intensive substance review is triggered where annual controlled turnover exceeds VUV 150 million (approximately USD 1.25 million) or where intercompany commissions dominate the profit and loss statement.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019, as amended 2021, extends to International Companies conducting 'distribution and service centre business', which captures commodity wholesale brokerage when goods are procured from or sold to a foreign group member or connected non-resident. The IC must demonstrate central management and control in Vanuatu: board meetings at the registered office, local physical premises, qualified employees proportionate to the trade, a local bank account at a licensed Vanuatu bank for trade flows, and annual local operating expenditure reflective of the business. Failure renders the company subject to VFSC information requests, penalties up to VUV 5,000,000, daily default fines, licence suspension, and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by False Offshore Declaration (VAT and Customs Fraud)

Statutory sanction: VAT Act 1998: fraud penalty of 150% of the tax evaded plus fines up to VUV 50,000 per false return. Customs Act: forfeiture of goods and excise-equivalent penalties for smuggling concealment. Penal Code: imprisonment up to 7 years and criminal fines up to VUV 100,000 upon conviction.

Director personal liability: Non-resident directors who sign or instruct the false declaration are treated as principals; courts may pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution of evaded VAT, issue travel bans and departure prohibition orders pending payment, and impose personal fines with imprisonment up to 7 years where fraud is proven.
Shell Company / Sham Booking Vehicle (Denial of Economic Substance)

Statutory sanction: International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019: penalty up to VUV 5,000,000 plus VUV 50,000 per day of continued default. VFSC may refuse licence renewal, strike the IC off the register without limitation of liability, issue an adverse substance finding, and exchange the finding with treaty partners under the TIEA network.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent may be jointly ordered to pay penal sums as civil restitution, face prohibition from acting as a director for up to 5 years, be subjected to travel bans as enforcement measures, and, where false substance notices were filed, imprisonment not exceeding 2 years.
AML/CFT and Financial Sanctions Failure (High-Risk Energy Commodity Trading)

Statutory sanction: AML/CFT Act 2017 and Financial Intelligence Unit regulations: civil monetary penalties up to VUV 10,000,000 per breach, licence suspension or revocation of operational authorisation; money laundering is a predicate offence punishable by up to 15 years imprisonment and criminal fines up to VUV 15,000,000; asset freeze orders and confiscation of trade proceeds are mandatory on conviction.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and ultimate beneficial owners carry personal criminal liability; courts can order civil forfeiture of the SBLC proceeds, impose travel bans, revoke Vanuatu work or residence permits, and imprison the directing mind for up to 15 years where laundering is established.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Camouflaged Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu imposes no direct transfer pricing penalty; however, VFSC will treat the entity as lacking economic substance and impose ESR fines up to VUV 5,000,000. The residence state of the shareholder will commonly make a BEPS secondary adjustment with penalties of 20% to 40% of the under-reported amount, and TIEA exchange exposes the full pricing documentation and banking trail.

Director personal liability: Directors approving the mispricing face fiduciary claims for the lost value, civil restitution orders enforceable against offshore assets, travel bans during asset tracing, and, for deliberate misrepresentation to VFSC on the annual return, imprisonment up to 2 years.
