Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Vanuatu International Company. The Memorandum must state an authorized share capital (commonly USD 100,000 divided into shares of no par value). Paid-up capital is determined solely by the subscribers' allotment; no escrow account or capital deposit is required at incorporation under the International Companies Act 1993.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Vanuatu Fire and Rescue Service covering emergency exits, extinguisher placement, and alarm systems; Building Occupancy / structural-load inspection certificate from the Public Works Department under the Vanuatu Building Code; and workplace clearance from the Occupational Health and Safety Unit of the Department of Labour confirming dust extraction, ventilation, machine guarding, and worker safety protocols.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC) under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; operational customs portal via ASYCUDA World; company registration and annual maintenance through Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate International Company (IC) under International Companies Act Cap 222 via licensed VFSC agent; Certificate of Incorporation with company number constitutes the statutory identifier. 2) Register with VRC/ASYCUDA to obtain Importer/Exporter Code for customs declarations (no TIN/VAT ID exists because the IC is outside the income-tax and any VAT net). 3) If a physical office or factory is occupied, obtain municipal business license (Port Vila/Luganville); this is a regulatory fee separate from tax exemption. 4) File annual return and pay VFSC annual license fee; no tax filings required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — non-resident International Company wholly exempt from Vanuatu income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty and any indirect tax under International Companies Act Cap 222 s.209 (exemption) and s.7(4) (prohibition on domestic business with residents). — 1) Incorporate International Company (IC) under International Companies Act Cap 222 via licensed VFSC agent; Certificate of Incorporation with company number constitutes the statutory identifier. 2) Register with VRC/ASYCUDA to obtain Importer/Exporter Code for customs declarations (no TIN/VAT ID exists because the IC is outside the income-tax and any VAT net). 3) If a physical office or factory is occupied, obtain municipal business license (Port Vila/Luganville); this is a regulatory fee separate from tax exemption. 4) File annual return and pay VFSC annual license fee; no tax filings required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — non-resident International Company wholly exempt from Vanuatu income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty and any indirect tax under International Companies Act Cap 222 s.209 (exemption) and s.7(4) (prohibition on domestic business with residents).

Available election forms

None available under domestic law — Vanuatu has no check-the-box, CFM or hybrid-mismatch election. The only conceivable 'election' is re-registration as an ordinary company under Companies Act Cap 191, which would surrender exempt status and is therefore disfavoured; alternatively the IC may be redomiciled to/from another jurisdiction under the International Companies Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/SUT in force — operative indirect tax is the Vanuatu Customs Import Duty (tariff) under the Customs Act Cap 90; Value Added Tax Act 2023 has been enacted but implementation is deferred and not in effect.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable (no VAT effective); the deferred VAT Act provides a VUV 5,000,000 registration threshold with compulsory registration for cross-border service suppliers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Inapplicable in form because there is no VAT; Vanuatu exports are inherently tax-neutral (zero-rated by absence of any levy). The IC may lawfully export 100% of production free of duty and indirect tax. To avoid import duty on beechwood raw material, secure a temporary-inward-processing/immediate-export concession from VRC with a 12-month export completion deadline; otherwise the applicable tariff (generally 15% ad valorem for HS 9503 toys) becomes payable.

  • ASYCUDA World Electronic Customs Export Declaration (VAN-142)
  • Bill of Lading (or House Air Waybill for airfreight)
  • Commercial Invoice in English with HS 9503.00.00 and ISIC 3240 descriptors
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank credit advice (only where counterparty financing or foreign-exchange control checks are invoked; no statutory export repatriation deadline exists in Vanuatu)
  • Certificate of Origin (optional but recommended for tariff preferences in Australia, NZ and the EU)

No e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate. Export/import declarations are mandatory through ASYCUDA World single-window; each declaration receives an electronic customs declaration number (MRN-equivalent), which functions as the only fiscal clearance artefact.

Customs Act Cap 90 penalties — false export declarations attract fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or 12 months imprisonment, forfeiture of goods and demand of 30% of the value; abuse of temporary importation relief triggers full payment of duty on the value of the diverted goods; breach of the IC's non-trading-with-residents covenant may cause VFSC to revoke exempt status and expose historical transactions to taxation (retroactive clawback), although no income-tax base currently exists.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is not a freely convertible hard currency; the RBV operates a managed exchange-rate regime with the VUV linked to a basket of major trading-partner currencies. Current-account transactions (goods, services, trade disbursements, profit and royalty remittances) are routinely approved by authorised dealer banks under delegated powers, subject to documentary verification of the underlying commercial transaction. Capital-account items, including external borrowings and cross-currency principal swaps, require prior RBV approval or authorised-dealer approval under standing Exchange Control Notices. An International Company (IC) is, as a general rule, tax-exempt on all offshore income and faces no Vanuatu withholding, income, capital gains, or estate taxes, so dividend and profit remittance hurdles are fiscal-nil; the operative constraint is the thin VUV spot market and settlement timing. The entity should therefore maintain dual VUV/USD accounts, denominate e-commerce settlement in USD, and convert to VUV only for permitted local expenditures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 64] and applicable delegated Exchange Control Notice issued by the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu, export proceeds must be repatriated to a Vanuatu-authorized dealer bank and received within six (6) months from the date of export. Supporting evidence per shipment comprises the commercial invoice, bill of lading or airway bill, packing list, and the bank's inward remittance advice. Vanuatu imposes no mandatory statutory ratio compelling conversion of export receipts into VUV; exporters may retain foreign currency in approved Foreign Currency Accounts (USD/EUR/AUD) held with an authorized dealer. Voluntary conversion to VUV is optional and executed at the daily RBV reference rate. Failure to repatriate within the statutory timeline may expose the IC to enforcement directions or penalties under the Exchange Control Act; therefore, monthly reconciliation of Amazon Pay and Stripe payout reports to incoming MT103 credits is required.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) under the Banking Act [Cap. 129] and the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 64]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) registers and supervises International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Vanuatu has no domestic e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime, and the RBV does not regulate foreign EMIs operating into Vanuatu; therefore, an off-shore EMI cannot serve as the IC's sole treasury anchor. Offshore digital cross-border EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, and similar regulated payment firms) will accept a Vanuatu International Company only under their internal risk appetite, which frequently classifies offshore ICs as heightened-risk due to opaque substance and tax-haven associations. Amazon Pay and Stripe payout rails cannot directly settle into a VUV domestic account in all cases; a practical architecture is a Stripe/Amazon settlement node held in a supported market (US, UK, or HK) linked to a treasury gateway, with daily sweeping via SWIFT MT103 into ANZ/BSP Vanuatu. The domestic operational account must remain the compliance anchor, with the EMI used as a collection-and-payout utility only, never as a repository of capital or export proceeds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing is an SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's home-country bank to the IC's USD-denominated account at ANZ Bank (Vanuatu) Limited or BSP Vanuatu Limited. The wire must carry a purpose code consistent with 'equity subscription' or 'shareholder loan' (e.g., code GF for equity injection under the originating bank's taxonomy, or a narrative coded 'IC Capital Contribution, International Companies Act Cap. 222'). The bank file must contain: (1) board resolution authorising issuance of shares or the shareholder-advance agreement; (2) subscription agreement with per-share price and number of shares; (3) the VFSC registration certificate and registered-agent letter; (4) UBO and source-of-funds evidence as escalated in the AML/KYC dossier; and (5) an instruction to hold the funds in USD rather than convert to VUV, ensuring preservation of the e-commerce working-capital base. Where the capital is received in VUV, the authorised dealer executes conversion at the daily RBV reference rate and records the trade for balance-of-payments reporting.For raw beechwood supply, non-toxic finishing materials, third-party cutting and sanding, and fulfilment logistics: (i) payments under USD 250,000 per transaction are executed as USD SWIFT MT103 out of the ANZ/BSP account, with the beneficiary bank, BIC, and account number validated against the supplier's bank confirmation; (ii) first-time or high-value supplier relationships are settled by an irrevocable, sight commercial letter of credit issued by BSP or ANZ, payable against invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, packing list, certificate of non-toxic paint compliance, and inspection certificate if applicable; (iii) recurring small-ticket disbursements, including Amazon FBA warehousing fees and Stripe processing fees, may be swept via a corporate card or the approved EMI gateway with monthly reconciliation back to the core account. Each outbound transfer requires the underlying purchase order and invoice, the FX deal confirmation if VUV/USD conversion is involved, and the dealer-bank trade-data capture record required by the RBV; the six-month export-proceeds rule must be monitored so that any prepayment or delayed-payment manufacturing advance is declared and matched.Deliverable outright VUV/USD and VUV/AUD forward contracts with an RBV-licensed authorised dealer (ANZ, BSP, BRED), available only for confirmed commercial exposure and typically up to 12-month tenors under an ISDA master or bank foreign-exchange agreement.Offshore Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) in USD/VUV executed with an international bank or broker, permissible to hedge balance-sheet receivables and committed e-commerce payouts where the domestic deliverable market is illiquid; documentation must evidence the underlying Amazon/Stripe settlement flow.Plain-vanilla cross-currency swaps (USD/VUV or USD/AUD) between authorised dealers and the IC, used to hedge intercompany loan balances and multi-currency capital structures; principal exchange is subject to RBV capital-transaction approval and board authorisation.Spot FX conversions executed at the RBV daily reference rate as the operational conversion rail for receipts and disbursements, typically executed within a 1-to-5-business-day window to balance rate risk and settlement urgency.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - no corporate income tax and no transfer pricing legislation is in force; no Vatu-denominated threshold triggers Local File or Master File preparation for International Companies.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not impose a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute applicable to ICs. However, the International Companies Act requires a substantive registered office and a licensed registered agent within Vanuatu, and the VFSC conducts AML/CFT and beneficial-ownership oversight under the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act No. 5 of 2022. The IC must maintain local governance records, hold board minutes reflecting real decision-making, and avoid conducting business with Vanuatu residents. ICs engaged in genuine foreign-facing manufacturing-trading models (such as sale of baby Montessori wooden blocks to overseas markets) satisfy the ordinary substance expectations of the VFSC and Global Forum on Transparency.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: VFSC refusal of annual renewal, strike-off and deregistration under the International Companies Act; adverse reporting to the Global Forum and FATF-style peer review mechanisms; freezing of settlement proceeds by payment processors upon adverse finding.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly allow the entity to function as an empty shell may face compliance orders, civil restitution actions, disqualification from managing any VFSC-licensed entity, and travel bans imposed by the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit pending investigation.
Prohibited Local Business Dealing

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the international company status, revocation of the tax exemption, monetary penalties per licence offence, and forfeiture of trading profits derived from the unauthorised local activity.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unlicensed trading profits and may be prosecuted criminally, with fines and imprisonment of up to 12 months for continuing contravention.
AML/CFT Breach and Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Fines and penal sanctions under the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act No. 5 of 2022; VFSC may impose monetary penalties, suspend the registered agent, and place the IC under regulatory supervision.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent face personal criminal liability, civil restitution of laundered value, travel restrictions, and custodial sentences for wilful breach of reporting obligations.
False Declaration / Fraudulent Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Fraudulent declaration offences under the International Companies Act and general criminal law; VFSC may pursue strike-off, monetary penalties, and referral to the Public Prosecutor.

Director personal liability: Piercing of the corporate veil; directors who sign false returns are personally liable for civil damages to creditors and shareholders, may be required to make restitution, face travel bans, and risk imprisonment for perjury or fraud.
Tax Evasion in Home Jurisdiction of Foreign Owner

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu will exchange beneficial ownership and accounting information under the Global Forum peer-review mechanism upon lawful request; the foreign jurisdiction imposes its own evasion penalties, interest, and criminal prosecution independent of Vanuatu's zero-rate regime.

Director personal liability: Foreign directors remain subject to personal criminal liability in the beneficial owner's residence state; travel bans, extradition requests, civil restitution, and imprisonment apply under foreign revenue law without any protection from Vanuatu tax exemption.
