Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Department (VRCD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; online taxpayer portal: https://www.customs.gov.vu and Tax Administration System (TAS)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company obtains its statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) by filing the VRCD taxpayer registration application (Form VAT 001) together with the Certificate of Incorporation issued under the International Companies Act 1992, the registered agent's certificate, and certified identity documents of each beneficial owner; the application is submitted electronically through the VRCD Tax Administration System and the TIN is issued within 5-10 business days; no minimum capital or physical presence is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company obtains its statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) by filing the VRCD taxpayer registration application (Form VAT 001) together with the Certificate of Incorporation issued under the International Companies Act 1992, the registered agent's certificate, and certified identity documents of each beneficial owner; the application is submitted electronically through the VRCD Tax Administration System and the TIN is issued within 5-10 business days; no minimum capital or physical presence is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

International Companies (ICs) may elect under the International Companies Act 1992 to operate as a 'Compliant IC' for transparency and AML purposes, may elect to convert to a local exempt company, and may voluntarily revoke their tax-exempt status; no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections are available because Vanuatu imposes no income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on ICs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 annual taxable turnover; voluntary registration is permitted below this threshold but no IC may supply goods or services inside Vanuatu, so domestic VAT registration is generally unavailable or irrelevant for an IC.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods, including portable phased-array medical diagnostic scanners and color Doppler ultrasound consoles, are zero-rated under the Value Added Tax Act 1998 where the goods are physically exported from Vanuatu within the statutory period and the recipient is located outside Vanuatu; international services and services physically performed outside Vanuatu are also zero-rated; an International Company is wholly exempt from Vanuatu VAT and is not obligated to charge VAT on offshore export turnover.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Vanuatu Customs Form V78)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based e-invoicing mandate exists in Vanuatu; VAT-registered businesses must issue serially numbered VAT invoices and submit periodic VAT returns through the VRCD online tax administration system; exports are evidenced by standard customs declarations rather than government-cleared e-invoices.

Fraudulent zero-rating or export claims trigger retrospective VAT assessments, full clawback of zero-rated input tax credits, interest at 20% per annum, monetary penalties up to 200% of tax evaded, and criminal prosecution of directors and beneficial owners under the VAT Act 1998 and Customs Act 1986; failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days is treated as an unsubstantiated export and may result in imposition of VAT liability plus civil penalties, cancellation of the exporter's registration, and personal liability of company officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalized exchange-control regime. The Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible for both current account and capital account transactions. There are no restrictions, quotas, or approval gates on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or capital repatriation for International Companies. Funds can be freely held and transferred in VUV or any foreign currency, subject only to general AML/CFT reporting thresholds and balance-of-payments statistical disclosure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation deadline. Export receipts for medical equipment sales may be retained in USD, EUR, or any freely usable currency in a licensed VFSC-approved bank outside or inside the jurisdiction. A transaction-level record is required for RVB statistical returns but no form of exchange control enforcement is applied.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) in conjunction with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the overall monetary and exchange-monitoring authority. The RBV supervises banking and payment systems while the VFSC administers international corporate licensing and AML/CFT compliance for International Companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Vanuatu allows licensing of fintech and electronic money institutions under the Financial Dealers Act and the National Payments System Regulation. Offshore digital EMIs can technically open remote accounts for VU International Companies, but they are not suitable for holding statutory trust funds or capital escrow deposits required under VFSC licensing rules. A licensed offshore EMI may only serve as a supplemental clearing channel for operational disbursements, and the main bank account must remain with a domestic commercial bank.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (nil). Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax, and no undistributed profits tax. The Income Tax Act was repealed in 1987 and no direct taxation statute is currently in force.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (nil). Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, whether on cash dividends, deemed dividends, or distributions in kind, and no stamp duty or remittance tax attaches to shareholder distributions made by an International Company.

Continued non-payment beyond 90 days authorizes the Registrar to strike the company off the International Companies Register without further notice. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0 (nil statutory threshold). Vanuatu has no domestic transfer pricing, thin capitalization, controlled foreign company, or transfer-pricing documentation legislation, so no Local File or Master File is legally triggered. Notwithstanding the absence of a codified threshold, cross-border transactions (Swift_LC and TT_Wire sales of medical devices to foreign distributors) should be supported by an arm's-length benchmark analysis so that the trading counterparty's revenue authority does not recharacterize the IC's pricing on audit.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The International Companies (Economic Substance) (Amendment) Act 2019 and the Economic Substance Regulations 2019 require every International Company that carries on a 'relevant activity' to satisfy economic substance tests in Vanuatu unless it proves tax residence in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction by lodging a valid foreign TRC with the VFSC. For this scope (ISIC 3250 – manufacture and export distribution of color Doppler ultrasound consoles), the activity is analyzed as 'Distribution and Service Centre Business'. The IC must (a) file the prescribed Economic Substance Report with the annual return; (b) hold board meetings and make strategic decisions in Vanuatu; (c) maintain a permanent physical office with adequate full-time employees proportionate to the medical-equipment workload; (d) incur adequate operating expenditure locally; and (e) perform the core income-generating activities in Vanuatu, including contract negotiation, inventory and logistics risk oversight, and supervision of the SWIFT LC and TT wire export channels. Reliance solely on a registered agent or virtual-office packaging is fatal to the substance defense.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Prohibited Local Business / Unlicensed Vanuatu Trade

Statutory sanction: The prohibited transaction is void ab initio. The IC and every participating officer are exposed to an offence fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 (approximately USD 8,500) per contravention under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], and the VFSC may cancel the IC's registration and refuse re-registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent who knowingly procured the local trade are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution to the counterparty, subject to Supreme Court proceedings; the Registrar may issue a disqualification order barring each director from acting as an officer of any company registered in Vanuatu.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Evasion

Statutory sanction: False or omitted ESR filings attract VFSC financial penalties of up to VUV 5,000,000 (approximately USD 43,000), compulsory deregistration of the IC, and regulatory action against the licensed registered agent involved in the filing.

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally exposed to foreign tax-attack reattribution of the trading profits (CFC and offshore anti-abuse rules in the shareholder's home state), the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit may impose international travel-restriction notices, and the VFSC may disqualify the directors from office in any Vanuatu company for up to 10 years.
Money Laundering / AML/CFT Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 13 of 2014, deficient AML programs attract civil penalties of up to VUV 10,000,000 (approximately USD 86,000), the Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze all affected wire proceeds, and attributed money laundering is punishable by imprisonment of up to 15 years.

Director personal liability: The FIU may apply to the Supreme Court for pecuniary penalty orders and asset-freezing orders over the directors' personal property; the directors face imprisonment terms of up to 15 years, and the FIU may issue an international travel-restriction notice preventing departure from Vanuatu pending the investigation.
Tax Evasion and Material False Declaration

Statutory sanction: The false-declaration offence under the VFSC Act 1999 and the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] carries a fine of up to VUV 5,000,000 (approximately USD 43,000) and/or imprisonment of up to 7 years; the Registrar may deregister the IC and declare contracts executed during the period of deception void.

Director personal liability: Directors and the signatory officer are treated as primary offenders rather than mere agents of the company; criminal conviction triggers automatic civil restitution of all concealed profits, disqualification from corporate office, and, given the foreign-controlled character of an IC, provisional seizure of the directors' personal assets by order of the Supreme Court.
Fraudulent Trading / Post-Strike-Off Trading

Statutory sanction: Contracts made by a struck-off company are void; every person acting in the name of the struck-off company commits an offence under the International Companies Act punishable by fine up to VUV 1,000,000 (approximately USD 8,500) and imprisonment, and the VFSC will reject any reinstatement application until all government fees, penalties, and a corrective statutory declaration are submitted.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: directors are personally liable for the aggregate unpaid invoices, supply-contract damages, and the costs of any foreign winding-up petition; civil restitution orders may be registered against the directors' personal real property in Vanuatu; and persistent offenders face travel bans and a statutory bar on re-entry to any licensed corporate role in the Republic.
