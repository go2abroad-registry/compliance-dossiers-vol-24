Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of the Republic of Vanuatu; official portal: https://www.customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu and national government gateway www.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate as an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act No. 8 of 1992 (Cap. 222) through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles, and annual licence status; registration and annual fees are paid to VFSC. Step 2: If the IC intends to make taxable supplies within Vanuatu, or elects voluntary registration to recover input VAT, file the prescribed VAT Registration Application (Form VAT-1) with VCIR, attaching certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation, proof of registered office in Vanuatu, particulars of directors/shareholders, description of intended activities, and turnover forecast. Step 3: VCIR issues the VAT registration certificate and a unique VAT identification number; no general income-tax TIN is issued because Vanuatu does not impose corporate or personal income tax. Step 4: For pure offshore retail activity, no VAT ID is required unless there is a domestic supply nexus; the IC operates using its VFSC company number. Step 5: Foreign entities without domestic economic nexus are not required to obtain any Vanuatu tax number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch regime exists because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax and has not adopted BEPS hybrid mismatch rules. The operative statutory election is the tax-exempt offshore status of an International Company: an IC is statutorily exempt on all income and profits and on offshore transactions provided it conducts no domestic business with Vanuatu residents, owns no Vanuatu real estate, and is not licensed for domestic banking, insurance, or trust business. The IC may elect voluntary VAT registration (with input tax credit recovery) if it makes domestic taxable supplies, and may elect continuance/redomiciliation under the International Companies Act. No other entity-level tax classification election is available in Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million vatu) annual taxable turnover from supplies made in Vanuatu for compulsory VAT registration, approximately USD 41,000 at current exchange rates; turnover consisting solely of exported goods or supplies made outside Vanuatu is disregarded for the threshold; voluntary registration is available; the threshold is assessed on a rolling 12-month actual or projected basis.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Vanuatu are zero-rated under section 10 of the VAT Act where the supplier retains documentary evidence that the goods left Vanuatu within 28 days of the date of supply and applicable export conditions are met; services supplied to non-residents are zero-rated where the benefit is not enjoyed in Vanuatu. For an International Company selling smart-home IoT hardware to non-resident end-users through Stripe/Shopify payment rails, offshore retail sales are outside the scope of Vanuatu VAT, and qualifying export supplies are taxable at 0%, with full recovery of attributable input tax credits subject to export proof.

  • Customs export declaration (approved C-series form) endorsed by the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue
  • Original or certified true copy of the Bill of Lading (sea) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing physical departure from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply
  • Commercial invoice and packing list reconciled to the customs declaration
  • Bank inward remittance certificate or foreign exchange settlement proof for the exported goods
  • VAT export certificate / customs clearance endorsement confirming zero-rated export status

No real-time e-invoicing, API fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate is currently in force in Vanuatu. Taxable suppliers must issue a valid VAT invoice in paper or electronic form showing supplier VAT ID, invoice date, supplier name and address, description of the supply, VAT-exclusive value, applicable rate (15% standard or 0% export), and total VAT; export invoices must be supported by customs endorsement. VCIR accepts electronic filing of VAT returns and schedules through its e-services portal, but no mandatory clearance/TAXE software is imposed. VCIR has announced future customs and tax digitalization initiatives; statutory records must be retained for at least five years for audit purposes.

Export zero-rating claims are provisional and subject to documentary audit by VCIR; failure to produce export evidence within the audit window triggers full clawback of input tax credits plus statutory late-payment penalties and interest, and a civil penalty of up to 200% of the over-claimed amount. Fraudulent zero-rating or false customs declarations expose the IC and its officers to fines of up to VT 1,000,000 per offence and imprisonment for up to two years, cancellation of VAT registration, and strike-off or revocation of the International Company licence by VFSC; directors are jointly and severally liable for the resulting penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The vatu (VUV) is fully convertible for current-account transactions without prior RBV approval, and capital-account liberalization is broad: an International Company is statutorily treated as non-resident and exempt from the Exchange Control Act in all foreign-currency transactions. The company may hold unlimited USD, AUD, NZD, and EUR accounts with licensed banks and may remit dividends, interest, or liquidation proceeds offshore free of withholding tax and without exchange-control permits. The RBV operates a managed float of the VUV against a currency basket led by the USD, AUD, and NZD; practical convertibility constraints arise only from correspondent-bank de-risking and the thin deliverable depth of the vatu offshore, not from statutory restrictions. Profit remittance is unimpeded by exchange control; the sole procedural hurdle is the bank's internal confirmation of solvency plus filing of the statutory distribution declaration required for International Companies. The IC undertakes no domestic Vanuatu-facing trade, so no domestic VAT (15%) or customs duty exposure arises on its offshore e-commerce flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion rule applies to Vanuatu International Companies. Electronic retail export receipts denominated in USD may be retained indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts and applied directly to offshore supplier payments without conversion to vatu. Where a licensed Vanuatu bank intermediates a cross-border wire, the transfer is reportable to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (VFIU) under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act when it exceeds the statutory threshold, and RBV balance-of-payments reporting classifies the receipt as merchandise e-commerce export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) is the statutory monetary authority and delegated exchange-control administrator under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 90] and the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 322]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the licensing and AML/CFT supervisor of banks, offshore banking activities, trust and company service providers, and International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] and the Companies and Trust Service Providers Act (Act No. 24 of 2009).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Vanuatu does not maintain an electronic-money-institution licensing category; the Banking Act [Cap 22] confines deposit-taking to RBV-licensed banks, so unregulated offshore digital banks (Wise Business, Airwallex, Mercury, Stripe Treasury) cannot lawfully serve as the primary custody rail for IC balances. Global EMIs generally reject Vanuatu-registered entities under client-eligibility matrices, and critically neither Stripe nor Shopify Payments on-boards Vanuatu legal persons. The compliant two-tier settlement architecture is therefore: (i) a US or Hong Kong settlement company captures card settlements from the designated retail rails; and (ii) the VU_IC funds its treasury from that company exclusively by documented SWIFT MT103 intercompany transfers. Payment-neutral local alternatives are limited to VFSC-approved payment service providers; holding material deposits at unregulated EMIs is not permissible.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections route by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV where an intermediary bank is interposed) from the ordering shareholder's bank to the IC's ANZ or Westpac USD account. Field 50K must identify the true ordering shareholder, Field 57A the receiving bank BIC and IC account number, and Field 70 the narrative 'EQUITY INJECTION - INTERCOMPANY CAPITAL' together with the RBV statistical purpose code for direct-investment equity capital advised by the receiving bank. No exchange-control permit is required; the bank's verification pack comprises the board resolution approving the capital call, an updated register of members, a source-of-wealth declaration for inflows above USD 100,000 equivalent, and the bank's internal AML file. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt under s.54 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an IC to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - Vanuatu has no transfer pricing statute and no Local File or Master File threshold. Groups should nevertheless prepare OECD-aligned TP documentation where intercompany sales, IP licensing, or cost-sharing arrangements exceed VUV 10 million per annum to defend against foreign CFC, permanent establishment, and general anti-avoidance challenges.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations equivalent to BVI or Cayman statutes. However, genuine substance (physical office, employees, local bank account, board meetings) is indirectly required to avoid foreign CFC/GAAR recharacterization. Vanuatu remains on the EU Annex I non-cooperative list; therefore the IC's commercial purpose, substance, and transaction flows must be defensible in the shareholder's residence jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Offshore Concealment (Foreign Jurisdiction)

Statutory sanction: Foreign jurisdictions: back taxes, interest, civil fraud penalties (e.g., up to 75% in the US; up to 100% of tax in the UK), and criminal imprisonment. Vanuatu cooperates through the Global Forum exchange of information network, exposing the structure.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the IC veil as a sham: directors and controlling shareholders are jointly and severally liable for restitution; freeze orders, travel bans, and extradition proceedings may attach to personal assets.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Foreign recharacterization: all IC income is treated as income of the ultimate shareholder; denial of deductions; transfer pricing adjustments and penalties; EU listing countermeasures may trigger additional withholding taxes in EU counterparty jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false certificates of compliance are liable under the International Companies Act to fines and disqualification; foreign GAAR rules may impose personal liability on shadow directors.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Regime Violation

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu Proceeds of Crime Act and Financial Transactions Reporting Act: fines up to VUV 150 million and imprisonment up to 20 years; VFSC and FIU may freeze accounts, strike off the IC, and publicly name the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are criminally liable; the Vanuatu FIU may impose travel bans and asset freezing orders; disqualification from holding a directorship of any Vanuatu company.
Prohibited Local Business / Unlicensed Activity

Statutory sanction: Loss of the statutory tax exemption; fines under the International Companies Act; mandatory strike-off; unlicensed financial services carry separate criminal penalties under the Financial Dealers Act.

Director personal liability: Director fines and imprisonment for unlicensed financial activity; assets of directors may be reached in restitution; regional travel restrictions may be applied.
VAT Evasion (if local taxable activity occurs)

Statutory sanction: VAT Act: penalty up to 200% of the tax shortfall; daily surcharges; criminal fines and imprisonment for intentional evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors as 'controllers' are personally liable for the company's VAT debt and penalties; foreign parent shareholders may be pursued for unpaid VAT upon liquidation.
