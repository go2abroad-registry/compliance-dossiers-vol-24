Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Pure territorial/exemption model. An International Company is statutorily treated as a non-resident tax-exempt entity: no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no stamp duty under the International Companies Act. Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax on any entity. The IC is exposed only to indirect taxation: 12.5% VAT applies solely to taxable supplies made within Vanuatu above the registration threshold; export sales of polyresin statue garage kits to non-resident customers are zero-rated and outside the domestic taxable base.

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no US-style check-the-box election; IC tax-exempt status is statutory and automatic. Available elections include: (1) election in the Articles of Association to be limited by shares or by guarantee; (2) voluntary VAT registration under the VAT Act 2022 even below the VT 10 million threshold to recover input VAT on export production inputs; (3) election to maintain non-resident status for VAT zero-rating purposes; and (4) no hybrid mismatch election exists because Vanuatu has not adopted OECD BEPS Action 2 rules. All elections are documented through the Registered Agent and filed with VFSC/VRCD as applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (approximately USD 84,000) gross taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period, or as soon as the person has reasonable grounds to expect the threshold will be exceeded; separate non-resident supplier registration applies to foreign suppliers of electronic/digital services to consumers in Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods (including polyresin statue garage kits) are zero-rated under the VAT Act where: (i) the goods are exported by the supplier within 30 days of the invoice date or within a VRCD-approved extended period; (ii) the goods are consigned to a destination outside Vanuatu; and (iii) documentary proof of physical export is obtained and retained. Offshore services supplied to non-resident customers (e.g., custom sculpture design, licensing of artwork, and remote assembly instructions) are zero-rated where the benefit is enjoyed outside Vanuatu. Preorder deposits collected via Stripe or PayPal for goods subsequently exported qualify as zero-rated export supplies provided each payment record is matched to the corresponding export shipping documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (lodged through ASYCUDA World) showing HS/ISIC-aligned commodity description of resin statue kits
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Vanuatu
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds
  • VAT return with export schedule (VRCD supplementary export listing) identifying each zero-rated shipment
  • Preorder and payment rail records from Stripe/PayPal matching the export invoice

No real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate is currently operative in Vanuatu. Registered suppliers must issue VAT tax invoices containing the statutory particulars (supplier name and VAT registration number, invoice date, description of goods, taxable value, VAT amount) and retain invoices digitally for 7 years. VAT returns are filed electronically through the TAS portal on a monthly basis, with quarterly filing permitted for qualifying small taxpayers. Export clearances are processed through the ASYCUDA World customs system.

VRCD may audit export zero-rating claims with a minimum assessment window of 3 years, extended up to 7 years where fraud is alleged, and may compel production of bank, payment-rail (Stripe/PayPal), and customs records. Penalties for false zero-rating or forged export documentation include: (i) civil assessment of the VAT evaded at 12.5% plus a monetary penalty of up to double the tax shortfall and monthly default interest; (ii) criminal prosecution with fines up to VT 5,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; (iii) retrospective clawback of all input VAT credits claimed on production inputs (resin, pigment, mold materials) attributable to the disqualified export supplies; (iv) reclassification of the supply as taxable at 12.5% with backdated effect; and (v) referral to VFSC for deregistration of the International Company for conduct inconsistent with the tax-exempt regime. Where Vanuatu law imposes no statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline, the 180-day benchmark is retained here as the documentary and anti-money-laundering prudential standard applied by VFSC-authorized banks and export credit counterparties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is a managed-basket currency without deep international trading depth; Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, therefore current-account payments and profit remittances are not subject to exchange restrictions, but the RBV retains permission-based control over specified capital-account transactions. For the non-resident International Company (VU_IC), convertibility is effectively unrestricted: the International Companies Act No. 33 of 2022 deems the IC non-resident for exchange-control purposes and exempts it from RBV exchange-control permissions, enabling free retention and disbursement of USD, EUR and AUD accounts, unrestricted export pricing, and full profit/dividend distribution to non-resident shareholders without any Vanuatu withholdings, corporate income tax, capital gains tax or stamp duty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporting traders, the statutory baseline under RBV exchange-control practice is that export proceeds must be repatriated to Vanuatu through an authorized dealer and converted into VUV within 180 days of shipment where directed by RBV notice. The VU_IC is statutorily deemed non-resident, so no mandatory repatriation deadline, no surrender ratio and no conversion requirement applies to its USD/EUR pre-order and settlement proceeds for polyresin statue garage kits; the only binding duties are (i) retention of a documentary export spine (sales order, invoice, packing list, dispatch proof) and (ii) routing of any incidental USD-to-VUV conversion exclusively through an RBV-authorized dealer.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), Port Vila, the statutory central bank, monetary authority and exchange-control administrator under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125] and the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 69]; the RBV licenses and supervises all commercial banks in Vanuatu and designates authorized dealers for foreign-exchange conversion, while the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) registers and supervises the International Company (VU_IC); money-laundering and terrorism-financing examinations are conducted jointly through the RBV and the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit under the AML/CFT Act No. 18 of 2016.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW to CONDITIONAL. Vanuatu has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution licensing regime, and the AML/CFT Act No. 18 of 2016 together with RBV banking supervision requires any entity carrying on payment or money-transmission business in/into Vanuatu to hold a banking or authorized-dealer licence. Consequently, offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Stripe Treasury) generally refuse onboarding of VU-domiciled International Companies, and local banks will not clear VUV through unlicensed EMI accounts. The shareholder effects the equity injection by SWIFT MT103 from an onshore bank (Hong Kong, Singapore, Australia or USA) to the VU_IC's ANZ or BSP capital-escrow account in Port Vila, cleared through the bank's New York/Sydney correspondent network; the wire narrative (Field 70) must read 'Share capital injection under International Companies Act 2022 - subscription for ordinary shares' and must avoid ambiguous wording such as 'investment', 'loan', 'gift' or 'family transfer'. The verification dossier comprises a certified copy of the share register, board resolution approving the allotment, receipt confirmation of the VFSC annual return, and a source-of-wealth/net-worth questionnaire executed by the injecting shareholder. Where the IC subsequently funds a controlled overseas manufacturer by way of intra-group advance, the payment purpose code must be re-characterized as intercompany loan and evidenced by a written shareholder-loan agreement carrying commercial interest.Primary clearing rail for raw-material and casting-workshop payments: SWIFT MT103 in USD from the VU_IC's BSP/ANZ operational account, with full supplier remittance advice, purchase-order reference and trade-description in Field 70, funded by daily pre-sweep of the Stripe/PayPal settlement sub-account to eliminate unexplained float balances. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: LOW to CONDITIONAL. Vanuatu has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution licensing regime, and the AML/CFT Act No. 18 of 2016 together with RBV banking supervision requires any entity carrying on payment or money-transmission business in/into Vanuatu to hold a banking or authorized-dealer licence. Consequently, offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Stripe Treasury) generally refuse onboarding of VU-domiciled International Companies, and local banks will not clear VUV through unlicensed EMI accounts. Workable architecture: PayPal and Stripe merchant accounts remain feasible only as inbound pre-order collection rails, with payouts auto-swept directly into the VU_IC's ANZ or BSP USD account; the EMI must never function as the primary settlement bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no national corporate income tax on any company, and International Companies are expressly exempt from all Vanuatu income and profits taxes (s. 47(1) of the International Companies Act [Cap 222]).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — no dividend withholding tax and no deemed-dividend rules exist in Vanuatu; s. 47(1)(d) ICA exempts all distributions made by an International Company to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 3 months after each incorporation anniversary; the annual government fee is payable on the anniversary date itself. There is no CIT return deadline.

Deadline: · Frequency:

VAT returns are due by the 15th day after the end of each calendar month for registered persons; a pure offshore IC has no obligation to register or file.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and no transfer pricing legislation, hence no Local File or Master File threshold is prescribed locally. As prudent foreign-audit practice, when related-party inventory flows in the non-resident supply chain exceed USD 2,000,000 per annum, the group should maintain OECD-compliant documentation in the consumer market country to prevent profit recharacterisation; no VUV-denominated statutory threshold exists.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu currently imposes no Economic Substance Regulations filing regime comparable to BVI or Cayman, but the VFSC annual confirmation requires an exempt-status declaration and a beneficial ownership disclosure. Genuine substance must still be demonstrable: board meetings and minutes in Port Vila, an exclusive licensed registered agent, and the physical polyresin vacuum-casting, painting and shipping operations held in the foreign production site. A mailbox-only IC will trigger BEPS Action 7 permanent establishment and CFC recharacterisation in the consumer jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion & Sham Exemption Abuse

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu Revenue Administration Act 2017: penalties up to 200% of evaded VAT or import duty where any domestic element exists, criminal prosecution for fraudulent exemption use, and VFSC strike-off; foreign jurisdictions additionally recharacterise income under GAAR and CFC rules and impose their own penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil restitution of mischaracterised profits, VFSC director-disqualification orders, travel bans during active investigations under the Vanuatu Immigration Act, and Penal Code fraud terms of up to 12 years.
Prohibited Domestic Business (s. 6 International Companies Act)

Statutory sanction: Fine on conviction of up to VUV 1,000,000 per offence under the ICA, immediate forfeiture of exempt status, forced deregistration, and recovery of unpaid VAT at 12.5% plus late-payment penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise domestic trading are jointly and severally liable for the company's fines and VAT; non-citizen directors may be subjected to removal and travel-restriction procedures pending settlement, and imprisonment applies for contempt of VFSC orders.
AML/CFT & Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: AML and CFT Act 2011 (as amended): fines up to VUV 2,000,000, freezing and forfeiture of settlement accounts, Stripe and PayPal merchant termination, and VFSC refusal to accept the annual return.

Director personal liability: Directors who receive or authorise laundering of customer deposits are personally liable for restitution, subject to travel bans pending FIU investigation, and to money-laundering imprisonment of up to 10 years under the Vanuatu Penal Code.
Foreign DTA / Withholding Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Source-country penalties reach 30% to 40% of the grossed-up payments plus default interest; aggravated criminal-fraud sanctions apply and refunds to customers may be ordered; a Vanuatu exemption certificate does not cure foreign withholding obligations.

Director personal liability: Directors who endorse false withholding or preferential-rate certifications face disgorgement of fees, civil restitution to affected licensees, travel restrictions imposed through mutual legal assistance, and imprisonment for foreign tax fraud in the consumer state.
