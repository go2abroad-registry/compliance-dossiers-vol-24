Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: https://www.customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu; company and International Company registration is administered separately by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) at https://vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a corporate tax identification number because it is treated as fiscally exempt. If the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu or voluntarily registers for VAT, the foreign shareholder(s) must submit a VAT registration application to DCIR through the local registered agent, attaching the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, the IC tax-exemption certificate, director/shareholder details, registered office and registered agent details, bank account information, app store/operator agreements, and estimated 12-month taxable turnover. DCIR then issues the VAT identification number and VAT registration certificate. For purely offshore mobile app exports with no Vanuatu nexus, no national tax ID is required under standard practice.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a corporate tax identification number because it is treated as fiscally exempt. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Not applicable. Vanuatu has no check-the-box election and no hybrid mismatch election regime. The International Company may elect to obtain and maintain an exemption certificate under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] and remains non-resident and fiscally exempt provided it does not carry on domestic Vanuatu business.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 in annual taxable supplies made in Vanuatu during the preceding 12 months, or expected taxable supplies in the next 12 months; registration is compulsory once the threshold is met.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for goods physically exported from Vanuatu and for services supplied directly to non-resident recipients who are outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and where the service is not used or consumed in Vanuatu. A mobile utility app sold through Apple App Store or Google Play to non-resident end users or to Apple/Google as non-resident platform resellers qualifies as an export service where the offshore delivery, foreign payment, and non-resident user evidence is fully documented. Domestic sales to Vanuatu residents are taxable at the standard 12.5% VAT rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for physical goods only; not required for pure digital app supplies)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / electronic export manifest (for physical goods only)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or payment processor settlement report evidencing foreign currency receipt
  • Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console territory sales reports and transaction logs
  • App distribution agreement with Apple/Google or platform reseller agreement showing offshore recipient status
  • VFSC International Company registration certificate and tax exemption certificate

Vanuatu has not implemented a real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoice stamping, or government API clearance mandate. Standard VAT tax invoices are required under the VAT Act for taxable supplies; digital app sales discharged through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing may rely on platform sales receipts and settlement reports, but the IC must retain sufficient VAT invoice-level records when claiming zero-rating or input VAT credits.

DCIR may scrutinize zero-rating claims, require proof of foreign supply and settlement, and retrospectively claw back VAT refunded in respect of ineligible exports. Fraudulent zero-rating or false refund claims may result in statutory late-payment interest, civil penalties, recovery of unpaid VAT, and criminal prosecution with imprisonment or monetary fines. The IC must retain platform sales evidence, recipient location data, settlement records, and corresponding agreements for the full statutory record-keeping period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberal, principally convertible exchange-control regime. For licensed International Companies (ICs), both current-account and capital-account transactions are broadly unrestricted: the IC may hold, borrow, and remit foreign currency (USD, EUR, AUD) through domestic licensed banks or offshore accounts without prior RBV approval. The Vatu (VUV) is a small, managed currency with a shallow non-resident market; onshore VUV convertibility is interbank-supported but offshore exchange in VUV is limited, so all international treasury flows should be structurally denominated in USD/EUR/AUD. Profit, dividend, and capital repatriation from an IC is free of exchange-control restriction and, because the IC is tax-exempt under VFSC, there is no withholding tax or profit remittance hurdle.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. A VU_IC may retain 100% of export and SaaS subscription receipts earned from Apple App Store and Google Play in foreign currency, in either on-island foreign-currency accounts or approved offshore accounts, subject only to the standard RBV reporting threshold through the servicing licensed bank. No timeline-based repatriation requirement applies to IC export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) is the competent monetary authority and administrator of the Exchange Control Act (Cap 142); Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the licensing and AML/CFT supervisor for International Companies under the International Companies Act (Cap 222).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility for a Vanuatu IC. Vanuatu has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime; offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) may be used as downstream payment/collection conduits for Apple/Google payout aggregation but must not be represented as licensed banking in Vanuatu. The rigorously correct structure is: (a) a full-service licensed commercial bank in Vanuatu as the primary settlement/treasury account, and (b) a read-only EMI mirror only for converting small inbound app-store residuals where the EMI has passed the VU_IC CDD on a pass-through basis. Legal caution: EMIs that accept deposits without a Vanuatu banking licence could be construed as carrying on deposit-taking business under the Banking Act (Cap 54) if they market to Vanuatu residents; keep all EMI activity offshore, non-advertised, and non-resident-facing.

Shareholder capital injection must be routed via SWIFT MT103 to the VU_IC's licensed-bank account in Port Vila using purpose code 'IC capital contribution / equity subscription' on the RBV Exchange Control declaration; the bank will tag the credit as capital and hold it in the Capital Escrow Account pending verification of the corporate registry excerpt and the UBO declaration. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax; International Companies are expressly exempt from income tax on all profits under the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, no tax on distributions by International Companies, and no branch remittance tax or exit charge on profit repatriation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Return and the annual license fee are due on or within one month after each anniversary of the company's registration date. Non-payment generates a late penalty of 10% of the annual fee; the company becomes liable to strike-off if the fee remains unpaid for three months after the due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation regime exists in Vanuatu. International Companies are not required to prepare Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country reporting in Vatu or any other currency, and no monetary threshold triggers such obligations. Arm's length principles are not codified for ICs under domestic tax law.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) impose substance and annual reporting obligations only on undertakings performing relevant activities: banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, intellectual property holding, and distribution and service centers. A mobile utility software developer earning fee-based revenue from app-store sales is not a relevant activity and is therefore outside ESR filing scope. However, if the legal structure segregates the app's intellectual property into a separate IP-holding company or derives royalty income from the IP, an ESR return becomes mandatory, requiring local board meetings, local management decisions, adequate staff, and expenditure proportionate to the IP activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorized Domestic Business Activity

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of the tax-exempt status; the IC is legally prohibited from engaging in local business; VFSC may strike the company off the Register and impose monetary penalties on the company and every officer in default.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the company's regulatory defaults, subject to fines, restitution orders, and disqualification from acting as directors of any Vanuatu company for a prescribed period.
Tax Evasion and VAT Abuse

Statutory sanction: Civil assessment of unpaid tax, interest accrual, and statutory penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded; criminal prosecution for tax evasion; compulsory deregistration of the company and its exclusion from the IC regime.

Director personal liability: Criminal conviction of directors and officers who knowingly assist evasion, carrying terms of imprisonment, personal financial penalties, travel bans pending prosecution, and direct civil liability for remitted tax, notwithstanding the corporate veil.
Shell Company and Sham Substance Structure

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and refusal of restoration by VFSC; adverse regulatory findings impeding any future Vanuatu corporate registration; activation of international exchange of information mechanisms (tax information exchange agreements and the Global Forum standard) that expose the structure to foreign revenue authorities.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of diverted assets, disqualification orders against directors, personal exposure to foreign tax assessments where the sham is treated as the taxpayer's alter ego, and criminal liability if the structure is used to commit fraud or money laundering.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Default

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fines imposed under the VFSC supervisory framework, suspension or resignation of the registered agent, and eventual compulsory strike-off for non-compliance with beneficial ownership information requirements.

Director personal liability: Personal fines and imprisonment for knowingly furnishing false beneficial ownership information; directors face disqualification, personal liability for the company's regulatory costs, and cannot rely on corporate legal form to defeat AML enforcement.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation in Foreign Group Structures

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu imposes no domestic transfer pricing penalty, but foreign revenue authorities may recharacterize the arrangement, impose transfer pricing adjustments, apply withholding tax gross-ups, and request information from Vanuatu through international exchange mechanisms, triggering follow-on civil penalties in the foreign jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors of the foreign parent company face personal liability for fraudulent transfer pricing adjustments in the residence state; directors of the Vanuatu IC face regulatory reprimand, cooperation obligations under information exchange requests, and possible disqualification for facilitating an economic substance sham.
