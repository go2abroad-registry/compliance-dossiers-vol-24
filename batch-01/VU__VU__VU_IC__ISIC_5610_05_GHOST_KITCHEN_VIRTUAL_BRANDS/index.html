Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila, Vanuatu; official portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu; corporate registration and licensing authority: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned ghost kitchen operator incorporates an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] through a licensed VFSC registered agent. The agent files the Memorandum and Articles of Association; upon issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation, the IC receives a unique VFSC company registration number. Because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax, the IC does not obtain a domestic tax TIN. The operative tax identity comprises the Certificate of Incorporation, the VFSC registration certificate, and, where physical imports of kitchen equipment or packaged food occur, a DCIR customs client code. Non-resident ownership is permitted, no minimum capital is required, and no local tax registration filing is triggered by pure offshore delivery-platform revenue.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu has no check-the-box regime, no tax classification elections, and no hybrid mismatch election mechanism. An International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax and stamp duty by operation of the International Companies Act; no affirmative election is available or required to activate the exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax. Vanuatu indirect taxation is limited to customs import duties, selective export duties on a narrow statutory list of commodities, stamp duties (expressly exempt for ICs), tourist departure tax, and municipal rates, all administered under the Customs Act [Cap 66] and the Stamp Duties Act.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Vanuatu. A person importing goods must obtain a DCIR customs client code and lodge electronic import entries via ASYCUDA World; no economic turnover threshold triggers registration for indirect tax purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in VAT terms because Vanuatu levies no VAT or equivalent consumption tax on supplies. Exports of goods are generally free of export duty except for a narrow statutory list (principally sandalwood and certain marine products). Offshore services of a ghost kitchen and virtual brand operator, including delivery-platform settlement inflows, brand licensing fees, and management fees sourced from outside Vanuatu, fall outside the scope of Vanuatu indirect taxation and therefore carry an effective 0% rate without any refund mechanism or zero-rating claim process.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic entry) or equivalent import entry for inward-bound kitchen equipment
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (when preferential origin treatment is claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank settlement evidence (for trade settlement reconciliation and IC record-keeping)

None. Vanuatu does not mandate e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API-based tax reporting, or digital stamping. Standard electronic customs declaration and clearance operates solely through the ASYCUDA World border management system.

Vanuatu has no VAT zero-rating refund mechanism to defraud, so DCIR audit focus is directed at customs valuation, tariff classification, and the statutory list of dutiable exported commodities. Misdeclaration of exports or imports attracts penalties under the Customs Act [Cap 66], including forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties scaled to the duty evaded, retrospective assessment and collection of unpaid duty plus interest by the Commissioner of Customs, and imprisonment on conviction. In addition, International Companies engaged in sham or artificial transactions face VFSC administrative sanctions, deregistration, and money-laundering or terrorist-financing charges under the Vanuatu AML/CFT legislative regime.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for both current and capital transactions. Vanuatu accepts IMF Article VIII obligations, meaning there are no restrictions on payments and transfers for current international transactions and no multiple currency practices. The International Company (IC) may remit profits, dividends, liquidation proceeds, and intercompany payments in any foreign currency without RBV approval, withholding, or remittance tax. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level KYC/AML documentary checks and correspondent-bank risk appetite, not legal convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender regime: no mandatory conversion ratio, no prescribed repatriation timeline, and no requirement to hold export receipts in a VUV-denominated account. For the VU_IC ghost-kitchen and virtual-brand model, platform receivables (UberEats and DoorDash payouts) may be retained freely in USD, EUR, or VUV, converted at a licenced FX dealer's prevailing commercial rate, and used offshore or domestically without surrender, reporting, or administrative penalty.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 173]; the RBV is the sole monetary authority responsible for issuing the vatu (VUV), maintaining exchange-rate and financial-system stability, administering the interbank clearing system, and prudentially regulating all licensed banks. Exchange-control powers historically exist but are not activated; no separate foreign-exchange administration licence or prior-approval authority is required for current or capital account operations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Conditional Medium. Vanuatu has no domestic e-money-institution licensing regime, and the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) does not license digital EMI light-touch entrants. Reputable cross-border fintech rails (Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer) may be used as collector accounts for UberEats and DoorDash USD/EUR payouts and for low-value supplier sweeps, but the fintech provider will apply its own de-risking filters and substance checks. The fintech route is legally viable as a payout-only collection rail, but it cannot settle VUV obligations in-country, cannot issue trade finance instruments, and should never be the sole treasury bank; all final liquidity must be consolidated into a tier-1 Vanuatu bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity subscriptions and shareholder loans must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 through a correspondent bank into the designated USD or VUV account at BSP or BRED, with the payment field quoting the IC's Vanuatu International Companies Act registration number and a purpose narrative of either 'Direct Equity Investment into International Company' or 'Shareholder Loan – Working Capital'. The receipt must be pre-shadowed by a bank-letter notification at least one business day before value date. Supporting verification comprises the certified certificate of incorporation, resolution of directors approving the capital increase, executed subscription or loan agreement, bank reference for the remitting shareholder, and source-of-funds evidence for any amount exceeding USD 25,000. The receiving bank should freeze no funds if the files are uploaded before credit; any third-party unrelated remittance without matching paperwork must be returned or placed in suspense.Outbound trade settlement runs over the SWIFT rails: MT103 customer credit transfers are used for recurring supplier invoices, ingredient purchases, payroll outsourcing, and platform-related fees; MT202 COV supports correspondent USD clearing for second-tier beneficiary banks; and irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit, preferably confirmed by an international bank, are mandated for high-value kitchen-equipment imports. Domestic VUV obligations are cleared through the RBV-supervised interbank clearing and settlement system, using internal transfer instructions issued from the VUV sub-account. Payouts from UberEats and DoorDash are collected in the US/EU payout account, swept each settlement cycle by MT103 into the Vanuatu USD account with narrative 'Platform Payout Sweep – Food Delivery Receivables', then converted into VUV under a standing FX dealing instruction for local disbursements. Every outbound MT103 must carry an HS/UNSPSC-aligned purpose narrative, such as 'Trade Payment – Food Service Supplies' or 'Restaurant Equipment LC Settlement', and reference the corresponding invoice or PO number to avoid correspondent-bank rejection.Outright deliverable FX forwards (VUV/USD and VUV/EUR), permitted through RBV-authorised dealing banks for hedging genuine commercial exposures arising from platform payables and supplier invoices, subject to underlying transaction documentation and bank credit lines.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs), permissible only as bespoke offshore OTC arrangements for VUV because no standardised onshore NDF market exists; pricing and tenors are limited by offshore liquidity and all NDFs must reference a verifiable commercial cash-flow exposure.Cross-currency swaps and FX swaps (USD versus VUV), permissible for balance-sheet hedging of the IC's intercompany funding and for aligning the currency risk profile of the equity injection and platform receivables with VUV operational expenses, requiring a board-approved treasury policy and documented commercial rationale.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) 0 — Vanuatu has no domestic transfer-pricing legislation. International Companies are not subject to arm's-length documentation or CbC reporting requirements under Vanuatu law. However, if the IC transacts with grouped entities in jurisdictions that enforce OECD TP rules (e.g., Australia, Singapore, EU, US), the group may prepare consolidated Local File / Master File even though Vanuatu would not request it. The triggering threshold in practice is governed by the foreign parent jurisdiction's rules (e.g., ATO documentation threshold of AUD 1,000 turnover; EU and OECD CbC threshold of EUR 750M group revenue). For purposes of this filing, the threshold is the functional equivalent of zero because Vanuatu has no prescribed enforcement.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not currently apply Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) analogous to the UAE, Mauritius, or BVI ESR rules. The International Companies Act, however, contains a real-nexus requirement: every IC must maintain a registered office and registered agent in Vanuatu, and VFSC can require evidence of lawful conduct. The foreign tax authorities, e.g., the IRS, HMRC, or ATO, may apply their own offshore substance or controlled-foreign-corporation rules. For ghost kitchen operations conducted exclusively through delivery platforms in foreign cities, the Vanuatu IC must avoid creating a PE by ensuring: (1) contractual counter-party for orders is a separate foreign operating subsidiary; (2) the Vanuatu IC does not own delivery chattels or refrigerated assets abroad; (3) the virtual brand's trademark is licensed out, not generated by sales activity in Vanuatu; and (4) the board meetings, bank signing rights, and strategic decisions occur in Port Vila whenever possible. Lack of physical substance in Vanuatu can trigger anti-avoidance in foreign CFC regimes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (foreign indirect liability)

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu domestic sanction: VFSC has authority to strike off the offending IC and impose a penalty of up to VT 200,000. More critically, the foreign jurisdiction (e.g., IRS Code Sections 7201, 7206; UK Finance Act 2009, Schedule 17; Australian Criminal Code Act 1995 s 135.1) can impose criminal tax evasion penalties of up to 75% of the unreported tax, civil fraud penalties of 200% of the tax due, and extradition risk because Vanuatu lacks a general income tax base and cannot issue 'assessments' to shield the owners.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced by foreign courts where the Vanuatu IC is a mere instrumentality controlled by a single beneficial owner. Directors personally can face: (1) joint and several liability for all unpaid taxes, penalties, and interest; (2) travel bans imposed by the foreign tax authorities if they enter the country with an arrestable offence; (3) imprisonment up to 5 years for tax evasion conspiracy, wire fraud, or money service license violations; and (4) in the UK/AU, disqualification orders from acting as a director for up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse in Vanuatu

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act, section 117, any director or registered agent who signs a statement knowing it to be false is guilty of an offence punishable by a fine of not exceeding VT 200,000 and/or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years. Further, the company may be struck off and its assets escheat to the Vanuatu Government.

Director personal liability: Directors are liable to criminal penalties (VT 100,000 to VT 200,000 plus imprisonment). If the shell is used as part of a transnational tax arrangement, foreign civil courts impose restitution equal to the entire distribution received by the shareholder, plus punitive damages, and the director who receives dividends can be personally sued by creditors of the ghost kitchen trade creditors for fraudulent conveyance.
AML / Financial Sanctions Violation (Delivery Payout Railing)

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu financial AML penalties: a person convicted of money laundering under the AML/CFT Act faces a maximum fine of VT 50 million and/or 10 years imprisonment. Unlicensed financial services activity attracts a penalty of up to VT 200 million under the Financial Services Commission Act 2017. The VFSC may also revoke the registered agent's license, instantly killing all compliance compliance readiness.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and jailed for up to 10 years if they were willfully blind. In addition, foreign freezing orders (e.g., OFAC sanctions or UK HM Treasury designations) would block the Vanuatu bank accounts and the director's personal assets abroad; ignoring such orders constitutes a separate criminal offence in the user's country.
Fraudulent Misrepresentation in Food Safety and Brand Licensing

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no statutory franchising or food safety directive against this; but foreign food safety laws impose strict liability fines of up to USD 250,000 per violation in federal food safety cases (FDA), and the foreign registration/licensing authority can shut down the delivery storefronts. VFSC may strike off the IC for any act deemed injurious to the reputation of Vanuatu as an international financial centre.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal liability for felony offenses of reckless negligence in food safety laws (potential 3 to 5 years imprisonment). More practically, third party personal injury judgments obtained by consumers will be enforced against the Vanuatu IC's bank assets and the parent guarantor — if the director personally signed the delivery platform operating agreement, the court pierces the veil to the director's personal address.
Misrepresenative Declaration to VFSC / Failure of Registered Agent

Statutory sanction: The International Companies Act imposes a penalty of up to VT 200,000 on each director and a default fine of VT 10,000 per day while the failure continues. The company is subject to automatic dissolution after 6 months non-filing. If the UBO is a politically exposed person (PEP) or is sanctioned, VFSC will automatically require formal 'tax information exchange' inquiry

Director personal liability: Directors receive a direct criminal record: up to VT 200,000 fine and imprisonment for up to 2 years. If the UBO nondisclosure was used to evade foreign taxes, the foreign tax authority will treat the nominee director as a co-conspirator and can extradite that director under the applicable extradition treaty. Restitution to the foreign tax authority is unlimited and based on the whole tax avoided, not mere fees.
