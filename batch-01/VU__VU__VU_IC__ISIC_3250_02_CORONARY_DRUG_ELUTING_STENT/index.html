Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC) under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (portal: vrc.gov.vu); International Company incorporation and registry: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) (portal: vfsc.vu).

E-government filing portal

No TIN or VAT ID currently exists. The foreign-owned International Company is incorporated through a VFSC-licensed Registered Agent and receives a unique VFSC company registration number. Obtaining a customs exporter code from VRC requires the certificate of incorporation, registered agent certification, and bank/company details. No further tax registration is required because Vanuatu does not impose income tax, VAT, or withholding tax on qualifying ICs.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Vanuatu law. The offshore status election is the statutory International Company (IC) form under the International Companies Act; an IC is a non-resident exempt entity that may not conduct local business, hold land, or derive Vanuatu-source income. A domestic company incorporated under the Companies Act is also income tax exempt as Vanuatu has no income tax, but remains subject to municipal business licensing and import/customs charges.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Vanuatu has no VAT, GST, or Sales and Use Tax; indirect revenue arises narrowly from customs import duty, tobacco/alcohol excise, environment levy, and municipal licence fees.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – There is no VAT/GST registration threshold. Draft VAT proposals have not been enacted; until enactment, no threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT/GST zero-rating is available or necessary because exported goods and offshore services are outside any Vanuatu consumption tax. An IC exporting Class III coronary stents does not require a zero-rating certificate, VAT number, or VAT refund claim. If a future VAT is introduced, zero-rating would require proof of physical export: a customs export declaration, transport documents, and payment evidence, plus registration as a taxable person.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Electronic Export Entry in ASYCUDA World)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Ocean Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Certificate of Origin (if preferential duty treatment claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • SWIFT/Letter of Credit payment documentation
  • VFSC IC Certificate and Registered Agent Declaration

None. No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control obligation exists. Electronic customs declarations are lodged through ASYCUDA World for clearance, but that is not a tax e-invoicing mandate.

No export VAT refund currently exists, so no export zero-rating clawback can arise. Misrepresentations and false declarations to VRC/VFSC are penalised: goods may be forfeited, customs fines and imprisonment may be imposed under the Customs Act, and the IC may be deregistered for breaching exempt-status conditions. Any future VAT zero-rating fraud would be subject to retrospective assessment, late payment interest, and statutory penalties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates without any exchange control legislation; the Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible across both current and capital accounts. The exchange rate regime is a managed float against an undisclosed trade-weighted basket comprising the USD, AUD, EUR and JPY, with the official mid-rate published daily by RBV. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, royalty and interest payments, and capital repatriation are unrestricted for an International Company under the Tax Exempt Zone classification. The only practical condition is compliance with the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 13 of 2017 (AML/CTF Act) and the RBV/VFSC customer due diligence rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export receipts. A Vanuatu IC may retain 100 percent of foreign currency export proceeds in any authorised VUV or foreign currency account. Documentary evidence of the underlying export transaction (commercial invoice, transport document, and customs export declaration) must nonetheless be retained for AML/CTF and bank CDD inspection upon request.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], is the sole central bank, monetary authority and banking supervisor; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) regulates non-bank financial services, including financial dealers and securities, and registers International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]. Commercial banking is licensed by the RBV under the Banking Act [Cap. 42].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Vanuatu has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licence category. The Financial Dealers Act [Cap. 70] authorises only non-deposit-taking financial dealers for FX and securities dealing, while the Banking Act [Cap. 42] reserves deposit-taking and payment-clearing functions for licensed banks. Offshore digital EMIs (EU, UK, Singapore) cannot obtain a VUV settlement account, cannot access the RBV wholesale payment system, and may operate only in foreign currency corridors outside the domestic clearing network. A VFSC Financial Dealer licence provides no clearing utility; any EMI wishing to settle in VUV must engage a licensed domestic bank as settlement agent.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Standard compliant routing is a SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the IC's USD or VUV denominated account at a Port Vila licensed bank, with Field 23B set to CRED and Field 70 stating: Capital injection / share subscription of Vanuatu International Company [name], IC No. [VFSC registration number]. Vanuatu has no exchange control, so no RBV or VFSC prior approval is required, but the receiving bank applies standard CDD and may provisionally restrict withdrawals until the verification pack is delivered. Required verification pack: certified board resolution; signed share subscription agreement; updated register of members; notice of share allotment filed with the VFSC through the registered agent; and identity documentation of the remitting shareholder. Inbound funds in AUD or EUR are converted into VUV or USD at the RBV daily mid-rate plus bank margin.Trade disbursements use two principal clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct wires for open-account settlements and working capital payments, cleared through the bank's correspondent network (USD via New York correspondents such as JPMorgan or Citibank; AUD via ANZ Sydney; EUR via Bred Paris); and (2) documentary letters of credit issued under UCP 600 via SWIFT MT700, with amendments via MT707, for import of bioresorbable polymer substrates and precision tubing, confirmed by a prime bank in the supplier's jurisdiction where needed. Sight and usance tenors are permissible. For controlled MedTech shipments, bank documentary collections under URC 522 with MT400 series messages are also accepted. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax under the Income Tax Act or any other statute; International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes pursuant to the International Companies Act [Cap 222], s.116.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu law imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders. There is no income tax act levying WHT, and the IC Act s.116 exempts distributions from all Vanuatu taxation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 11 months after the anniversary of incorporation (or re-registration) and annually thereafter, aligned with payment of the VFSC annual levy. The registered agent is obligated to procure lodgement of the Annual Return and the beneficial ownership declaration.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the 21st day of the month following the end of the tax period; payment of output VAT net of input credits is due with the return.

Audit is not mandatory for the IC unless it holds a VFSC financial-services licence; a director-certified summary is acceptable for filing. Late lodgement triggers a VFSC late fee (approximately VUV 20,000 per return plus VUV 10,000 per month or part-month, subject to the current published VFSC fee schedule). Failure for 12 consecutive months exposes the IC to administrative strike-off, asset vesting in the Government of Vanuatu (recoverable only by court restoration), disqualification of the registered agent, and a fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 plus imprisonment of up to 2 years for a knowingly false declaration.
Box 7 – Declaration and Signature Signed declaration of accuracy and compliance with the IC Act, the AML/CFT Act, and the UBO record-keeping requirements. A non-resident director may sign, but the registered agent remains statutorily liable for content accuracy under the Companies and Trust Services Providers Act. Late lodgement triggers a VFSC late fee (approximately VUV 20,000 per return plus VUV 10,000 per month or part-month, subject to the current published VFSC fee schedule). Failure for 12 consecutive months exposes the IC to administrative strike-off, asset vesting in the Government of Vanuatu (recoverable only by court restoration), disqualification of the registered agent, and a fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 plus imprisonment of up to 2 years for a knowingly false declaration.
Box 1 – Taxable Supplies (Local Sales) Gross value of standard-rated supplies made in Vanuatu at 15% VAT, excluding zero-rated exports. Export of Class III coronary stents to foreign distributors is zero-rated; provide bills of lading, customs declarations, and proof of export from Vanuatu. Late return: fixed penalty of VUV 5,000–50,000. Under-declaration: 25% penalty plus 2% monthly interest. Intentional evasion: 200% penalty, seizure of movable property, and imprisonment of up to 2 years; directors may incur secondary liability when the company's non-payment results from recklessness.
Box 2 – Zero-Rated Supplies Value of exports and internationally supplied services. Retain full shipping documentation and foreign-currency invoices for at least 7 years; zero-rating is denied where goods remain in Vanuatu for local consumption. Late return: fixed penalty of VUV 5,000–50,000. Under-declaration: 25% penalty plus 2% monthly interest. Intentional evasion: 200% penalty, seizure of movable property, and imprisonment of up to 2 years; directors may incur secondary liability when the company's non-payment results from recklessness.
Box 3 – Input Tax Credits VAT paid on eligible business inputs attributable to taxable or zero-rated supplies. Non-resident ICs with no Vanuatu establishment cannot claim input credits; claim only on invoices properly issued by Vanuatu VAT registrants. Late return: fixed penalty of VUV 5,000–50,000. Under-declaration: 25% penalty plus 2% monthly interest. Intentional evasion: 200% penalty, seizure of movable property, and imprisonment of up to 2 years; directors may incur secondary liability when the company's non-payment results from recklessness.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold — Vanuatu has no domestic transfer-pricing law, no Local File, no Master File, and no country-by-country reporting obligation under VFSC rules. For practical compliance, an IC that transacts with related parties (stent component procurement, royalty flows, intercompany debt) should prepare voluntary OECD-aligned TP documentation when the annual value of controlled transactions exceeds VUV 20,000,000 (approximately USD 160,000), because such documentation will be demanded by the counterparty tax authority and supports economic substance assertions.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) comparable to BVI or Cayman. However, economic substance is de facto enforced through three channels: (1) the IC Act requires an in-Vanuatu licensed registered agent and registered office and prohibits domestic business activity; (2) the AML/CFT Act requires current UBO records to be physically maintained at the registered office; and (3) VFSC licensing standards require real activity for financial-service licence holders. For a coronary drug-eluting stent manufacturer, substantive functions (laser micro-machining, quality control, distribution contracts, board decisions) should be visibly performed outside Vanuatu, in the beneficial owner's operating jurisdiction, to avoid the VU IC being characterized as a 'managed from abroad' shell by OECD/EU listing criteria and foreign CFC regimes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion / Sham Offshore Exemption

Statutory sanction: In Vanuatu, no income tax can be assessed, but a knowingly false Annual Return is an offence under the IC Act punishable by a fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 2 years; VFSC may strike off the IC and prohibit the registered agent from acting. In the beneficial owner's home jurisdiction, invocation of CFC/ATAD/GILTI rules produces back taxes, interest, and accuracy-related penalties commonly exceeding 100% of the tax understatement; criminal tax evasion charges may follow where foreign returns falsely claim a Vanuatu 0% rate without substance.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of unpaid foreign taxes under shadow-director theories; travel bans and border alerts under mutual assistance treaty requests; extradition risk for tax-evasion indictments; in Vanuatu, individual directors who sign false declarations face personal fines, imprisonment, and disqualification from acting as directors of any VFSC-regulated company.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC administrative strike-off of the IC after persistent non-compliance; upon strike-off, assets vest in the Government of Vanuatu and require court restoration at significant cost (VUV 100,000+ plus legal fees); third-party creditors may pierce the exemption; the entity may be placed on EU/OECD non-cooperative lists, disrupting SWIFT wire and letter-of-credit processing by correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for restoration costs and for claims by creditors who transacted under a misleadingly opaque structure; disqualification orders issued by VFSC; personal exposure in the home jurisdiction under controlled-foreign-corporation attribution, causing the shareholder's worldwide stent-manufacturing income to be taxed currently at full marginal rates.
AML/CFT Breach and Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2018 (as amended), civil penalties of up to VUV 50,000,000 per contravention; criminal money-laundering offences expose the IC to unlimited fines and forfeiture of the value of the proceeds (including all stent-export revenue) plus imprisonment of the responsible individuals for up to 15 years; VFSC may suspend or revoke the IC's registration and the registered agent's licence.

Director personal liability: Direct criminal liability of directors and compliance officers for money laundering; asset freezes and restraint orders under mutual-assistance requests; statutory offence of 'tipping off' a client about a suspicious-transaction report resulting in 2 years' imprisonment; travel bans and post-conviction confiscation of personal residences; director disqualification for financial-crime conduct.
VAT Evasion and Local Medical-Distribution Mischaracterization

Statutory sanction: Under the VAT Act 2024 (effective 1 January 2025): penalty of 100% of the evaded VAT plus 2% monthly interest; seizure and disposal of the stent inventory present in Vanuatu; a fine of up to VUV 20,000,000 for directors who knowingly cause evasion; imprisonment of up to 2 years; cancellation of the IC's right to conduct any local supply activity.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for the IC's VAT debt where the failure is attributable to their recklessness or wilful blindness; personal liability extends to civil recovery orders and disqualification from managing any Vanuatu business; non-resident directors may be arrested upon entering Vanuatu under warrant for VAT-fraud prosecution.
