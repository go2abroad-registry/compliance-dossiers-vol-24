Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum charter capital applies to the non-conditional business line of registered agent/corporate secretarial services (ISIC 8299). Foreign investors subscribe charter capital in Vietnamese Dong or freely convertible currency and must fully contribute the committed capital within ninety (90) days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate, by wire transfer to the company's capital contribution account at a licensed bank. No escrow bank deposit is statutorily mandated, but the contribution schedule in the Investment Registration Certificate and Enterprise Registration Certificate must be strictly observed to avoid penalties, forced dissolution, or revocation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Design Appraisal and Acceptance Certificate by the Provincial Fire Prevention and Rescue Police under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP; the premises must comply with QCVN 06:2021/BXD for fire safety in buildings, including means of escape, fire alarm, extinguishers, and structural integrity. Civil defense requirements under Law on Civil Defense No. 02/2023/QH14 require the business to prepare an evacuation plan, assembly point, and emergency coordination with local authorities. Occupational clearance under Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH14 and Decree 44/2016/ND-CP requires registration of the occupational safety and health statement with the Department of Labour, Invalids and Social Affairs, and compliance with workplace hygiene standards, including lighting (QCVN 22:2016/BYT), microclimate (QCVN 26:2016/BYT), noise and vibration (QCVN 27:2016/BYT), and structural load certification by a licensed engineering unit for multi-storey premises.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum charter capital applies to the non-conditional business line of registered agent/corporate secretarial services (ISIC 8299). Foreign investors subscribe charter capital in Vietnamese Dong or freely convertible currency and must fully contribute the committed capital within ninety (90) days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate, by wire transfer to the company's capital contribution account at a licensed bank. No escrow bank deposit is statutorily mandated, but the contribution schedule in the Investment Registration Certificate and Enterprise Registration Certificate must be strictly observed to avoid penalties, forced dissolution, or revocation.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-invested Limited Liability Company: Step 1 - Obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) under the Law on Investment 2020; Step 2 - Apply for an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) via the National Business Registration Portal; the DPI electronically transmits enterprise registration data to the tax authority; Step 3 - The General Department of Taxation automatically issues the statutory 10-digit Tax Identification Number (Ma so thue, MST) contemporaneously with ERC issuance, requiring no separate paper application from the foreign entity; Step 4 - Activate the e-tax account at https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn and register electronic invoice templates with the tax authority; Step 5 - Submit the initial registration dossier for taxes (Form 07-MST on the e-tax portal) covering VAT, CIT, and license fees; Step 6 - File the first provisional VAT/CIT declarations in accordance with Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC within the statutory filing calendar; foreign contractors without a Vietnamese establishment that derive Vietnam-source income must separately obtain an MST under the Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) regime through withholding procedures.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (resident Vietnamese enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income at the headline 20% rate; no pass-through eligibility under Vietnamese law); global territorial scope for resident LLCs contrasted with territorial sourcing rules applicable to non-resident foreign contractors under the Foreign Contractor Tax regime; the registered agent and offshore secretarial business model is taxable as ordinary service income at CIT rates with no special tax-exempt-zone status unless physically located in an approved special economic zone or high-tech park under Decree 82/2018/ND-CP and applicable incentive schedules.

Available election forms

Viet Nam has no US-style check-the-box or general hybrid mismatch election. Available statutory elections include: (a) election of VAT calculation method - credit (deduction) method versus direct method on added value - permitted for enterprises with annual revenue below VND 1 billion or with incomplete accounting records; (b) election of CIT incentive statuses under tax incentive roadmaps for eligible sectors and regions, including software production and high-technology industries subject to reduced 10% or 15% rates with tax holidays; (c) election of the small- and medium-sized enterprise accounting regime under Circular 132/2018/TT-BTC; (d) election of consolidated VAT offsetting (bu tru) among dependent branches; (e) election to create an offshore stable establishment or contract structure triggering Foreign Contractor Tax withholding obligations rather than full VAT registration in certain cross-border service supply scenarios; (f) anti-hybrid and substance rules under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP disallow deductions for payments designed to achieve double non-taxation or mismatches lacking economic substance, and no domestic election may override transfer-pricing documentation requirements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese Dong (VND) is a managed-float currency and is not freely convertible. Current-account transactions, including cross-border service fees receivable from offshore clients for registered-agent and secretarial work, are generally convertible through SBV-licensed credit institutions, subject to production of the underlying service agreement, tax invoice, and corporate income tax compliance. Capital-account transactions (charter capital contribution, shareholder loans, equity transfers, and foreign borrowings) require prior investment registration with the Ministry of Planning and Investment and must be effected through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened at an authorized bank. Profit remittance to foreign shareholders is a current-account-like flow but is permitted only after the annual CIT finalization, audited financial statements, and satisfaction of the profit-remittance documentation requirements under Circular No. 186/2010/TT-BTC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 06/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 and its implementing regulations, the VN LLC, as an exporter of services, must repatriate all export proceeds to Vietnam within 180 days from the completion of service delivery and invoice issuance, crediting the amounts to a foreign-currency account at an SBV-licensed bank. The current mandatory conversion ratio is 0% for lawful export receipts; the SBV, however, retains the statutory power to require temporary mandatory sale of foreign currency to authorized banks during periods of exchange-rate stress. Retained foreign currency must remain in the licensed foreign-currency account and cannot be held abroad without SBV permission.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV - Ngan Hang Nha Nuoc Viet Nam), specifically the Foreign Exchange Administration Department and the Banking Supervision Agency.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium. Vietnam does not recognize offshore digital EMIs under Decree No. 101/2012/ND-CP (as amended) governing non-cash payments; payment-intermediary services require an SBV license, which offshore EMIs do not hold. Consequently, an offshore EMI cannot open a VND settlement account, issue domestic IBANs, or clear payments into Vietnam as a principal. Such platforms may be used only as a supplementary inbound collection channel for small-ticket client fees, with the final leg landing in the VN LLC's licensed-bank account through the EMI's local partner bank. Their use does not reduce statutory AML/KYC obligations, and the authoritative treasury ledger, tax invoice trail, and FX records must always be maintained within Vietnam.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

After the issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate and Investment Registration Certificate, the VN LLC opens a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at the chosen licensed bank. Charter-capital and shareholder-loan remittances are made via SWIFT MT103 from the foreign investor's own bank account (name and legal identity must match the investment registration), using the SBV-prescribed purpose code for direct investment capital contribution and a narrative referencing the ERC and IRC numbers. The receiving bank verifies the wire against the capital-contribution schedule, credits the DICA in foreign currency, and files the transaction with the SBV Foreign Exchange Administration. Conversion into VND is allowed only for lawful operating expenses within the registered business scope, supported by underlying invoices and payment requests.Operational cross-border payments (service imports, software subscriptions, subcontractor fees, royalties) are executed via SWIFT MT103 priority transfers from the VN LLC's USD or VND account through the licensed bank; the bank requires the underlying commercial contract, tax invoice, and Foreign Contractor Tax confirmation. For physical goods procurements, Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP600) and documentary collections are available through the bank's trade desk subject to import declarations and customs documentation. Profit and dividend remittances follow Circular No. 186/2010/TT-BTC: audited financial statements, CIT finalization proof, board resolution, and bank confirmation, then executed via MT103 with the SBV purpose code for profit remittance. All outbound wires must carry the correct purpose code and be supported by a fully documented commercial rationale.Outright deliverable USD/VND forward contracts with an SBV-licensed credit institution (typical tenor up to 365 days, requiring genuine underlying commercial exposure).Cross-currency swaps (USD/VND and USD/other major currencies) executed with a licensed bank for hedging committed cash flows.Vanilla FX options (call/put on USD/VND) purchased from a licensed bank for hedging real transaction exposures; option writing by non-bank entities is not permissible.Natural hedging through simultaneous USD-denominated receivables and payables, using the authorized foreign-currency current account without a derivative instrument.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for domestic residents; residents may not participate in the offshore VND NDF market without SBV authorization.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (no dividend withholding tax on corporate shareholders). Under Vietnamese domestic law, after-tax profits remitted to a foreign corporate shareholder are not subject to an additional dividend WHT, provided the company has fully satisfied its CIT obligations. For a foreign individual shareholder, a final personal income tax withholding of 5% applies to cash dividends under the PIT law.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

No later than the last day of the first month following the end of each calendar quarter (for a standard calendar-year company: Q1 – 30 April, Q2 – 31 July, Q3 – 31 October, Q4 – 31 January of the following year). A company with an approved different fiscal year uses the equivalent quarter-end deadlines.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 90 days from the end of the fiscal year. For a calendar-year company, no later than 31 March of the following year. A foreign-invested LLC with an approved different fiscal year must file within 90 days after that fiscal year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, a taxpayer is exempt from preparing the TP documentation (Local File and Master File) if: (i) the enterprise's total revenue in the fiscal year is less than VND 50 billion and the total value of all related-party transactions (including loans) is less than VND 30 billion; or (ii) the enterprise operates under a 'low-risk' status and meets the simplified declaration conditions. If total revenue equals or exceeds VND 50 billion, or related-party transactions equal or exceed VND 30 billion, the Vietnamese entity must prepare a Local File. A Master File is required when its ultimate parent company has consolidated global revenue of at least VND 20 trillion in the prior fiscal year. Even when documentation is exempt, the enterprise must still declare related-party transactions on the annual CIT finalisation form (Form 03/TNDN, Appendix) and maintain contemporaneous evidence of arm's length pricing.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not operate a statutory Economic Substance Regulations regime (ESR) akin to the UAE or Cayman Islands. However, the Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP incorporate a thorough substance-over-form doctrine and general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR): the tax authority may recharacterise transactions, disregard any arrangement lacking real economic substance, and reassess tax based on the actual commercial substance. A Vietnamese LLC providing registered agent/offshore secretarial services without maintaining local employees, office equipment, management decisions or commercial functions in Vietnam – or which simply routes fees to an offshore shell – risks being treated as a sham entity; its income may be deemed attributable to the foreign shareholder or a permanent establishment, and the CIT reassessment will be accompanied by penalties and interest.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (understating revenue / false accounting)

Statutory sanction: Civil: repayment of evaded tax plus interest of 0.03% per day; an administrative fine of up to 4 times the understated tax amount under the Law on Tax Administration 2019. Criminal: if the amount evaded is VND 100 million or more, prosecution under Article 200 of the Penal Code (ss. 1-4) with fines up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment of 3 to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The legal representative or director who signs the CIT return is personally liable alongside the company for the evaded tax and penalty; is subject to a travel ban (no exit visa) and a management ban; and in criminal cases may be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 7 years, with confiscation of assets and civil restitution.
Unlicensed / unregistered operation of corporate secretarial and registered agent services

Statutory sanction: Administrative sanction under Decree 122/2021/ND-CP and the Law on Enterprises 2020: fine ranging from VND 80,000,000 to VND 100,000,000 for operating without a licence or after licence revocation; additional fine possible for undeclared foreign ownership; compulsory suspension of the business and forced dissolution of the Vietnamese entity; any contracts entered into during the unauthorised period may be declared void.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly liable for any civil loss suffered by clients or third parties from the invalid contracts; they may be subject to administrative fines in their personal capacity (up to VND 50,000,000); in cases involving fraud or misrepresentation, criminal liability under Articles 217 or 221 of the Penal Code (false trading / wrongful business) with imprisonment of up to 10 years.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and KYC failures

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering Law 2022, Decree 125/2020 and SBV Circular 11/2023/TT-NHNN: administrative fine up to VND 500,000,000 for serious non-compliance; revocation of operating licence; permanent suspension of the business; if the conduct is linked to money laundering, criminal sanctions under Article 324 of the Penal Code with fines and imprisonment of up to 10 years for repeat offenders.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable for AML compliance deficiencies; they can be fined personally, subject to travel bans and a prohibition from holding management positions; where they knew or should have known of the laundering scheme, imprisonment for money laundering ranges from 3 to 10 years, and they are jointly liable civilly for clients' and the State's recoveries.
Transfer pricing manipulation / lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, the tax authority will recompute the taxpayer's income using the arm's length standard, disallow the excess related-party deductions, and impose additional CIT at 20%; late payment interest accrues at 0.03% per day; an administrative penalty of 20% of the underpaid tax applies for incorrect transfer pricing declarations, escalating to criminal tax evasion if the mispricing is fraudulent and exceeds the criminal threshold.

Director personal liability: The director who certified the TP documentation and final CIT return may be jointly liable for the additional tax and interest; a serious, deliberate transfer pricing fraud can expose directors to criminal liability under Article 200 of the Penal Code including imprisonment of up to 7 years and a travel ban; the company may be disqualified from obtaining certain tax and customs incentives in the future.
