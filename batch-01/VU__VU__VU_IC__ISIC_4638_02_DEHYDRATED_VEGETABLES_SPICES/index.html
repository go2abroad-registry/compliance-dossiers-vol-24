Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Service (VCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; taxpayer portal and VAT registration administered through VCIR head office in Port Vila.

E-government filing portal

A foreign International Company (IC) must first be incorporated under the Vanuatu International Companies Act through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No national income tax TIN is issued or required for an IC that confines its activities to offshore business. If the IC voluntarily registers for Vanuatu VAT (e.g., to zero-rate exported goods or claim refunds on local inputs), it must submit Form VAT-01 to VCIR together with the VFSC certificate of incorporation, registered agent certificate, certified passport copies of beneficial owners/directors, proof of local registered office address, and a letter of engagement from a Vanuatu-resident tax agent. VCIR issues a VAT registration number intended for all customs and indirect tax filings.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign International Company (IC) must first be incorporated under the Vanuatu International Companies Act through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No national income tax TIN is issued or required for an IC that confines its activities to offshore business. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election in its domestic tax legislation. An IC may voluntarily elect to register for VAT in respect of Vanuatu-source taxable supplies, thereby creating an island within the tax-exempt structure for domestic purchases and export zero-rating; all other offshore business receipts remain fully outside the territory of Vanuatu taxation. There is no available election to be treated as a taxable person for income tax purposes, as Vanuatu imposes no national income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover threshold for mandatory registration of persons making taxable supplies in Vanuatu; exports and offshore supplies are zero-rated and do not count towards the threshold unless the supplier elects to register voluntarily. Foreign and non-resident suppliers with no physical presence in Vanuatu are generally outside the VAT net except for specifically enumerated imported services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu by the supplier or the supplier's customs agent, provided the goods are removed from Vanuatu within 90 days of supply and are supported by customs outward movement records. Supplies of offshore services are zero-rated when made for the direct benefit of a recipient who is outside Vanuatu or when the services relate to movable property exported from Vanuatu. The exporter must hold, before the VAT return due date, documentary evidence proving export and the identity of the foreign buyer; perishable dehydrated garlic and chili consignments must be accompanied by phytosanitary certificates and consistent weight/packing records.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form C1/Export Entry) endorsed by Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Service
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment from a Vanuatu port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by an authorised bank in Vanuatu or foreign-exchange evidence traceable to the foreign buyer
  • Commercial tax invoice with zero-rated indicator and VCIR VAT registration number
  • Phytosanitary certificate and export permit for dehydrated vegetables, spices, garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper (issued by Vanuatu Biosecurity)
  • Packing list, certificate of origin and container tracking documentation aligning to the HS classification of ISIC 4638 goods

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government-API e-invoicing mandate currently applies in Vanuatu. Taxpayers may issue paper or electronic VAT invoices, but must include prescribed particulars (supplier name, address, VAT number, invoice date and number, description and value, VAT rate and amount). VAT returns are filed periodically through the VCIR electronic filing system, and all supporting invoice and customs records must be retained for 6 years.

VCIR audit examination focuses on the completeness of the export evidence chain, the genuineness of the foreign buyer, and timely repatriation of export proceeds. If zero-rating is claimed without lawful export, or documentation is forged or materially inconsistent, VCIR may issue a retrospective assessment recovering VAT at 13% on the full invoice value, plus a penalty tax of up to 200% of the tax, monthly late-payment interest at 5%, and criminal sanctions including a fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment for up to 12 months. Audit assessments may reach back 6 years from the date of the offending VAT return, and a cancelled VAT registration will nullify future zero-rating privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is a non-globally traded currency but is freely convertible for current and capital account purposes through RBV-licensed authorized dealers (ADs) in Vanuatu. A Vanuatu International Company (IC) organized under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is treated as a non-resident person and is exempt from exchange control restrictions applicable to residents; there is no statutory RBV approval requirement for receiving foreign currency, holding export proceeds in USD, or remitting dividends and profits to non-resident shareholders. Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on ICs, so profit remittance is unrestricted and requires only a valid board resolution and the standard wire verification file. The material constraint is operational liquidity: VUV/USD interbank quotes are obtainable only through ADs and may carry material spreads and minimum ticket sizes. Accordingly, the treasury blueprint denominates all trade settlements, documentary credit operations, and liquidity balances in USD, EUR, AUD, NZD, or CNH, converting into VUV only to meet incidental domestic operating expenses.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods physically exported from Vanuatu, the exchange control regime requires that export proceeds be repatriated to an authorized dealer in Vanuatu within six months of exportation, with the exporter obliged to offer the foreign currency for conversion into VUV if directed by the RBV; in current administrative practice the RBV does not impose mandatory conversion ratios on export receipts. The covered business model (ISIC 4638, bulk dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper export) is structured as a drop-shipment international trading conduit: merchandise moves directly from third-country origin suppliers to third-country destination buyers without transiting Vanuatu customs territory. The statutory export surrender regime therefore does not attach to the underlying sale proceeds. The IC will nevertheless maintain a comprehensive audit trailer for each shipment (signed supply agreements, commercial invoices, packing lists, full-set Bills of Lading, certificates of origin, phytosanitary and quality certificates, and SWIFT MT103/MT202 payment evidence) to demonstrate that no goods physically enter Vanuatu and that no export-proceeds surrender obligation arises. If any incidental consignment is ever shipped ex-Vanuatu, proceeds must be received within 180 days and may be retained in USD in a Vanuatu AD account without mandatory conversion, absent an express RBV direction.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) as the designated exchange control authority under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 88] as amended by the Exchange Control (Amendment) Act 2008, together with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as the statutory regulator of International Companies, authorized dealers, and licensed financial institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary rail and Medium only as a supplementary collection rail. Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and similar platforms do not hold an RBV authorized-dealer license, do not offer VUV-denominated accounts, and cannot issue Vanuatu international bank account numbers. Any foreign-exchange or payment transaction performed in Vanuatu through an entity that is not an authorized dealer would contravene the Exchange Control Act [Cap 88] where the transaction involves a resident person; a VU International Company is non-resident for exchange control purposes, so offshore EMI processing of its own foreign-currency flows does not of itself breach the statute. However, Vanuatu ADs will not fund or settle commercial letter of credit operations through an EMI account, and beneficiary banks in source and destination jurisdictions frequently reject TT or LC settlements sourced from EMI multi-lateral rails owing to correspondent banking de-risking. The recommended architecture is therefore to anchor all treasury activity in a Vanuatu-licensed bank account and to use an EMI account, if at all, only for low-value recurring receivables collections from identified buyers, with weekly sweeps of balances to the Vanuatu operating account and full recording in the AML surveillance file.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Paid-up share capital from a non-resident beneficial owner is injected via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 where the shareholder is a corporate treasury) to the IC's USD operational account held with a Vanuatu-licensed authorized dealer such as ANZ Bank (Vanuatu) Limited or BRED Bank (Vanuatu) Limited, routed through the AD's USD correspondent network commonly located in New York, Sydney, or Hong Kong. The remittance instruction must carry the following compliant data fields: beneficiary account title equal to the IC's registered legal name; the VFSC registration number; the transaction narrative 'Share capital contribution / subscription for shares in [IC legal name]'; and a cross-reference to the board resolution approving the capital call. On receipt, the AD will require a certified copy of the share register, the allotment resolution, and the completed UBO/KYC file before crediting, and will issue an inward remittance advice with the balance-of-payments classification 'equity capital'. There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement and no RBV approval is needed for non-resident equity inflow, but the AD will apply source-of-funds verification on each material transfer. The funds must be recorded in the IC's statutory books as issued share capital, and any later return of capital or dividend distribution must be supported by a board resolution and, where directed by the AD, a certificate of solvency.Supplier settlements for bulk dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper are cleared through two primary rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers from the Vanuatu operating account for open-account and cash-against-document trades; and (ii) commercial letters of credit issued and advised via SWIFT MT700 / MT710 / MT707 messages through the Vanuatu AD's correspondent banking arm in Sydney, Auckland, Singapore, or Hong Kong for documentary trade. As the VUV is not an internationally traded settlement currency, all LC obligations and TT payments are denominated in USD (or, on request, in EUR, AUD, or CNH) and debited against the IC's USD facility. For sight letters of credit issued in favor of origin suppliers, the negotiating bank presents the full documentary set (commercial invoice, full-set clean-on-board Bill of Lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, packing list, weight list, and insurance certificate) under UCP 600; the Vanuatu AD releases funds on compliant presentation and sends settlement advice via SWIFT MT103 or MT202. Where the IC sells under a documentary credit confirmed by a prime buyer-side bank, the IC presents documents through the Vanuatu AD and proceeds are credited to its USD account after negotiation or acceptance. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low as a primary rail and Medium only as a supplementary collection rail. Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and similar platforms do not hold an RBV authorized-dealer license, do not offer VUV-denominated accounts, and cannot issue Vanuatu international bank account numbers. Any foreign-exchange or payment transaction performed in Vanuatu through an entity that is not an authorized dealer would contravene the Exchange Control Act [Cap 88] where the transaction involves a resident person; a VU International Company is non-resident for exchange control purposes, so offshore EMI processing of its own foreign-currency flows does not of itself breach the statute. However, Vanuatu ADs will not fund or settle commercial letter of credit operations through an EMI account, and beneficiary banks in source and destination jurisdictions frequently reject TT or LC settlements sourced from EMI multi-lateral rails owing to correspondent banking de-risking. The recommended architecture is therefore to anchor all treasury activity in a Vanuatu-licensed bank account and to use an EMI account, if at all, only for low-value recurring receivables collections from identified buyers, with weekly sweeps of balances to the Vanuatu operating account and full recording in the AML surveillance file.

0% (Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or branch profit tax on any resident or non-resident company; the former Income Tax Act has been fully repealed and no income tax legislation is in force).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing, thin-capitalization, or CFC legislation; no Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country report is required or accepted by VFSC. The IC nonetheless retains a commercial pricing file for each export transaction.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) are not separately enacted for Vanuatu ICs; substance is enforced through (i) IC Act s. 6 prohibition on carrying on business with Vanuatu residents, (ii) the statutory prohibition on holding or leasing Vanuatu land, and (iii) the obligation to maintain a registered office and registered agent in Port Vila. To prevent foreign tax re-characterization as a shell, the IC must evidence genuine operational substance: negotiated supply contracts, inspection/quality certificates for the garlic and chili, title transfer documents, settlement through the IC's own bank account, and active board oversight of pricing and buyer credit risk.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under IC Act s. 252 and fines up to VUV 1,000,000 for false statements to VFSC; foreign financial intelligence units receive the transaction reports, triggering downstream criminal investigation in the buyer jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors face 3-year disqualification orders, travel bans, and asset restraint orders; courts pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution of all funds moved through the sham entity plus the beneficiary's profit.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act and the Proceeds of Crime Act; imprisonment up to 20 years and/or fines up to VUV 50,000,000; the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit may issue asset-freeze orders.

Director personal liability: Personal custodial sentences, international travel bans, confiscation of personal assets, and permanent disqualification from holding any corporate office in Vanuatu.
Tax Evasion (Customs / VAT Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Under the Customs Act [Cap. 246], penalty of 100% of evaded duty plus fines up to VUV 1,000,000; VAT Act fraud penalty of 25% of the shortfall; foreign tax authorities deny treaty benefits and impose gross-up withholding on the full margin.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false declarations are prosecuted personally; civil recovery orders follow proceeds into personal bank accounts; travel bans and imprisonment of up to 7 years apply.
IC Act Breach / Unlicensed Domestic Trade

Statutory sanction: Offence under IC Act s. 6: fine up to VUV 1,000,000 plus VUV 50,000 per day during continuous breach; revocation of IC status and compulsory re-registration as a domestic company, retroactively exposing the entity to 12.5% VAT, customs duty, and the full domestic tax regime.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized local trading face personal fines, imprisonment up to 2 years, and joint and several liability for VAT and customs duties assessed on the IC.
