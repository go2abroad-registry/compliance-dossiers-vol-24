Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (VIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; online portal: https://vrevenue.gov.vu (domestic taxes) and https://vfsc.vu (International Company registration)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC) is exempt from income tax and does not receive a national TIN solely for incorporation. If the IC conducts taxable local supplies, it must apply to VIRD for VAT registration using Form VAT-01, submitting the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, registered agent certificate, passport copies of directors, local registered address, and estimated annual taxable turnover. VIRD verifies the application and issues a VAT identification number. If the IC remains wholly offshore and does not make taxable supplies in Vanuatu, no TIN or VAT ID is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC) is exempt from income tax and does not receive a national TIN solely for incorporation. If the IC conducts taxable local supplies, it must apply to VIRD for VAT registration using Form VAT-01, submitting the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, registered agent certificate, passport copies of directors, local registered address, and estimated annual taxable turnover. VIRD verifies the application and issues a VAT identification number. If the IC remains wholly offshore and does not make taxable supplies in Vanuatu, no TIN or VAT ID is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No income tax law exists in Vanuatu, therefore no check-the-box, CFC, hybrid mismatch, or partnership tax election is applicable. Under the Value Added Tax Act, a VAT-registered person may elect the annual accounting scheme instead of the standard bi-monthly return cycle, and affiliated taxable persons may apply to VIRD for VAT grouping. An International Company may elect to remain non-resident and offshore by transacting exclusively outside Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 4,000,000 (approximately USD 35,000) estimated or actual taxable turnover in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted below this threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of physical goods, including medical devices and dialysis consumables, where the supplier exports the goods outside Vanuatu in the course of business and retains proof of export. Services supplied to a non-resident relating to exported goods or performed wholly outside Vanuatu may also qualify. The exporter must make the supply to an overseas customer, remove the goods physically from Vanuatu, and hold contemporaneous documentary evidence of export and foreign payment.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Original VAT invoice issued by the exporter
  • Contract or purchase order evidencing the export sale

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, digital stamping, or invoice clearance mandate is currently implemented in Vanuatu. Taxpayers may use any standard commercial invoicing system, but must issue prescribed two-copy VAT invoices for taxable supplies exceeding VT 2,000 and retain all records, including supplier invoices and export evidence, for at least 5 years.

VIRD may audit export zero-rating and input VAT refund claims. If the required export documentation is not produced within 180 days, or if the declaration is false, the zero-rating is clawed back and VAT is assessed at the standard 15% rate. Supplementary penalties range from 100% of the tax for non-fraudulent errors to 150% for fraud; monetary fines up to VT 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years apply to deliberate fraudulent export claims. Directors and responsible officers may be personally liable under the Value Added Tax Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

De jure and de facto convertibility of the vatu on both current and capital account: Vanuatu operates no exchange-control legislation, no central-bank approval or licensing requirement for capital inflows or outflows, and no restriction on profit, dividend, interest, or royalty remittances by tax-exempt International Companies; the VUV floats freely. Practical friction is limited to commercial-bank CDD and source-of-funds verification, and to the thin offshore depth of VUV; the treasury blueprint therefore designates USD as the functional currency and uses EUR only for procurement-linked hedging.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable: Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio into VUV, and no export-invoice matching or currency-verification regime. A Vanuatu International Company may retain 100% of dialysis-filter trade proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency in a designated RBV-licensed account; the only continuing obligation is to retain underlying invoices, transport documents, and MT103 payment records for five years under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — the central bank under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act, with sole monetary authority and prudential supervision of all licensed banks and domestic clearing infrastructure; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) administers the International Company registry and non-bank licensing, whereas FX and capital-movement policy is executed through the RBV.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability: mainstream global EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer) generally exclude Vanuatu-incorporated companies by jurisdiction policy, or impose severe velocity caps and whitelisted-counterparty rules; legally, an unlicensed offshore EMI may render foreign-currency payment-initiation services to a Vanuatu IC only as an ancillary technology service, and may not hold VUV deposits or solicit the Vanuatu public without RBV authorization. The settlement anchor must therefore remain an RBV-licensed bank, and any EMI may be used solely as a low-balance collection conduit for sub-threshold distributor receipts.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax on International Company (IC) income or gains derived from outside Vanuatu; ICs operate under a statutory tax-exempt-zone classification.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, no branch remittance tax, and no distribution tax on an International Company paying dividends to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within one calendar month (28 days) after the anniversary of the date of incorporation, which is the statutory filing anchor equivalent to a fiscal year-end for an IC. The IC may adopt any fiscal year for internal financial reporting, but the mandatory VFSC cycle is fixed to the incorporation anniversary.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — Vanuatu has no codified transfer-pricing legislation, no Local File or Master File obligation, and no vatu-denominated documentation threshold for International Companies. Notwithstanding the absence of domestic TP law, the IC should maintain contemporaneous arm's-length documentation as a defensive record because related-party transactions with the offshore manufacturing affiliate will be scrutinised under foreign CFC, PFIC, and secondary-adjustment rules in the shareholder's residence jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), administered through VFSC, apply to entities carrying on specified geographically mobile relevant activities such as IP holding, headquarters, distribution and service centres, financing and leasing, banking, insurance, shipping, and pure-equity holding. The extrusion and potting of hollow-fibre polyethersulfone dialyzers (ISIC 3250 / NAICS 339112) is a physical manufacturing activity and is not on the ESR relevant-activity list. However, the IC must still demonstrate genuine operational substance in its operating jurisdiction — physical plant, qualified employees, board control, and bank accounts — and must not be structured as a passive IP shell receiving royalties without any real manufacturing function, which would expose it to recharacterisation as a sham in foreign tax disputes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off and revocation of the IC's registration; revocation of the Registered Agent's licence; fines up to VT 5,000,000 for regulatory fraud; additional VAT and customs penalties of up to three times the dutiable or taxable value; forfeiture of goods and freezing of funds by the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and controlling shareholders face personal civil restitution for unpaid duties and taxes, director disqualification orders, travel bans pending investigation, and imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent tax evasion and false-declaration offences.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) and loss of good standing; foreign tax authorities may recharacterise the IC as a sham, attribute its profits to the ultimate parent, and impose full tax, interest, and negligence penalties in the shareholder's residence jurisdiction; Vanuatu itself imposes no penalty tax, but the VFSC may suspend the Registered Agent and the FIU may freeze the IC's accounts pending a beneficial-ownership audit.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who sign substance declarations that are knowingly false are personally liable for reckless misrepresentation; they face disqualification from acting as directors or agents in Vanuatu, monetary fines, and civil asset-tracing claims by creditors and foreign tax authorities seeking to pierce the corporate veil.
AML/CFT and Beneficial-Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to VT 10,000,000 under the AML/CFT regime; freezing and confiscation of the IC's bank accounts; revocation of IC registration; prohibition from conducting financial transactions in or through Vanuatu; cancellation of any health or export permits for medical devices.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal liability including imprisonment up to 15 years for money laundering, travel bans, personal confiscation orders, and disqualification; the corporate veil is routinely pierced in AML proceedings, and directors who concealed the beneficial owner are jointly and severally liable for the full value of laundered funds.
Unlicensed Medical-Device Activity and Trade Misinvoicing

Statutory sanction: Business licence revocation; customs penalties of up to three times the shipment value; seizure and destruction of non-compliant medical devices; prohibition on future importation into Vanuatu; criminal fines for trade misinvoicing plus confiscation of the proceeds of the unlawful sales.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign falsified bills of lading, certificates of origin, or LC documents are personally liable for defrauded creditors and third-party purchasers, face travel bans and asset freezes, and may be sentenced to imprisonment up to 12 years under Vanuatu fraud and customs provisions; the corporate veil is pierced where the IC is a mere instrumentality of the controlling shareholder.
