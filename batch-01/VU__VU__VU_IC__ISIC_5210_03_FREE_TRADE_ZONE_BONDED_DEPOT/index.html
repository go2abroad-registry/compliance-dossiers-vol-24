Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC) – Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila. Principal platforms: ASYCUDA World customs single window and VRC online inland-revenue registration/return portal (gov.vu domain).

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) under the International Companies Act 1992 (Cap 222); no minimum paid-in capital is required and 100% foreign ownership is permitted. 2) Through the licensed registered agent, obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, registered-office consent, and current good-standing certificate. 3) Apply to VRC for VAT registration once taxable turnover reaches or is likely to exceed VUV 10,000,000 in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available below the threshold; the application file includes the incorporation certificate, VFSC good-standing certificate, director and shareholder register, VIPA foreign-investment approval, any required business licence, proof of a physical Vanuatu place of business, and bank confirmation. 4) Register as importer/exporter in ASYCUDA World and obtain a Customs Bonded Warehouse Licence under the Customs Act (Cap 231), which requires premises approval, a fidelity bond or bank guarantee, and an approved electronic stock-control system. 5) If hiring local staff, register as an employer with the Vanuatu National Provident Fund (VNPF) for statutory contributions; note that Vanuatu currently imposes no income tax and no PAYE withholding registration. 6) Maintain the IC's non-resident conduct rules, including the prohibition on trading with Vanuatu residents and on holding Vanuatu land (outside narrow statutory concessions), to preserve the statutory exemption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – the IC is a statutory exempt international entity: Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, inheritance tax, or gift tax, and the bonded-depot IC therefore operates outside the domestic income-tax net. For indirect tax, only supplies made in Vanuatu for domestic consumption are in scope; bonded-warehouse storage and export-related supplies are zero-rated and goods remain suspended from customs duty and VAT until entered for home consumption.

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity classification and no hybrid-mismatch elections exist because Vanuatu has no income-tax legislation. The available elections are indirect-tax mechanisms: (a) voluntary VAT registration for sub-threshold taxable activities under the Value Added Tax Act (Cap 249); (b) customs bond suspension election, whereby goods brought into the approved bonded depot or free-trade-zone area are treated as not entered for home consumption and import duty plus 15% VAT are deferred until removal; (c) documented election to apply 0% VAT to export supplies and services connected with exported goods; and (d) appointment of a tax representative or licensed customs agent for filing and declaration purposes where the operator is non-resident.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 10,000,000 annual taxable turnover (effective 1 July 2022, raised from VUV 3,500,000). Registration is compulsory once the 12-month forecast or actual threshold is reached; voluntary registration is available below the threshold. A separate Customs Bonded Warehouse Licence is required for bonded-d depot operations irrespective of VAT turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to: (1) goods physically exported from Vanuatu within the statutory period, including goods removed from a bonded depot or designated free-trade-zone area for outward shipment; (2) services supplied directly in connection with exported goods or foreign-going ships and aircraft, such as bonded storage, handling, and international freight; (3) international carriage of passengers or goods; and (4) services supplied to non-residents for use wholly outside Vanuatu. If bonded goods are released into the domestic market instead, 15% VAT plus applicable customs duty is payable on the value at release.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged and validated in ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing carriage to a foreign port or airport
  • Port or airport outturn certificate / customs departure record proving physical exit from Vanuatu
  • Zero-rated commercial invoice identifying the non-resident buyer and INCOTERMS
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank foreign-currency receipt evidencing payment from abroad
  • Bonded warehouse entry/exit stock register in approved format and any applicable re-export permit

Vanuatu does not currently mandate real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance APIs, or digital stamping. Customs processing is digital through ASYCUDA World, with electronic declaration lodgement, selectivity, and risk-based inspection. VAT returns are filed monthly through the VRC online portal. Bonded-depot operators must maintain ASYCUDA-linked electronic stock and entry/exit records and lodge all entries through licensed customs agents; invoices must satisfy the content requirements of the VAT Act but no government clearance stamp or integrity token is imposed.

VRC may audit and reopen assessments for up to three years, extended to six years where fraud is alleged. If the mandatory documentation bundle is incomplete or inconsistent, the zero-rating is reversed and output VAT is assessed at 15% on the full consideration, with statutory interest and late-payment or shortfall penalties; wilful evasion attracts additional fines and criminal prosecution. The Commissioner may suspend or revoke the bonded warehouse licence and recommend VFSC or VIPA licence action against the operator. Knowingly falsifying export documentation constitutes an offence under the VAT Act and Customs Act, with criminal fines and imprisonment for responsible officers or directors, and may expose the enterprise to adverse findings through Vanuatu's exchange-of-information commitments with tax treaty and FATF partners.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Vanuatu maintains an open, notification-based exchange-control regime rather than a prior-approval regime, and International Companies operating in the free trade zone/bonded depot space face no local restriction on profit repatriation, dividend distribution, capital withdrawal, or settlement of trade obligations. Practical convertibility frictions are limited to the shallowness of the offshore VUV market and to correspondent-bank due diligence, not to Vanuatu regulatory prohibitions. For current account items, authorised commercial banks are permitted to sell and purchase foreign exchange against VUV; for capital account items, no standing RBV licence is required, although statistical reporting is expected. There is no capital gains tax, withholding tax, or income tax on an International Company, which further simplifies remittance mechanics.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or repatriation timeline applies to export receipts in Vanuatu. The Exchange Control Act [Cap. 62] does not impose conversion ratios, minimum hold periods, or prefixed surrender rates for goods or services exported from the customs free trade zone. Exporters, including bonded depot operators with International Company status, may retain foreign currency proceeds in offshore accounts or in foreign-currency accounts held onshore, at their election. The only administrative obligation is the accurate reporting of cross-border transactions through an authorised dealer for RBV balance-of-payments statistics and, separately, customs declaration of the physical movement of goods to the Vanuatu Customs and Border Compliance Service.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) – the national central bank and designated authority under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 62], responsible for foreign exchange policy, payment system oversight, and bank supervision; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately regulates International Companies and the financial services sector.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Vanuatu does not maintain a separate e-money institution (EMI) licence class, and offshore digital EMIs are not passportable into Vanuatu. Only RBV-licensed commercial banks may take deposits or provide payment/clearing services to an International Company in the ordinary course. A non-resident digital EMI cannot open a local settlement account or provide hosted payment services holding client funds without a local RBV banking licence, which in practice requires a physical presence in Port Vila, prudential capital, and full AML/CFT infrastructure. Digital EMIs may be used only as an ancillary payment-information layer, provided all actual clearing is effected through a local authorised bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant routing for inbound capital is an international SWIFT credit transfer (MT103 or MT202) from the investor’s bank to the International Company’s operating or capital-escrow account at a RBV-licensed commercial bank in Port Vila. The SWIFT message must bear the exact legal name of the International Company, the purpose code or narrative agreed with the receiving bank under RBV balance-of-payments statistical instructions, and a clear reference to ‘equity contribution – bonded depot capital injection’. The remitter should be the registered shareholder or the named intra-group lender; if a third-party remitter is unavoidable, a fully executed assignment, loan agreement, or written declaration must be pre-approved by the bank to prevent rejection. The receiving bank should receive an advance remittance advice containing the board resolution, share subscription/allocation document, source-of-funds declaration, customs free-zone licence, and evidence that the funds are not connected to a known money-laundering or sanctions predicate.Outbound trade disbursements are cleared primarily through SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier and trade payments. For documentary trade settlement, the bank issues commercial letters of credit through the SWIFT MT700 series (issue, amendment, reimbursement authorization), and documentary collections are processed under the SWIFT MT4xx series as advised by the receiving bank. Inbound VUV-denominated settlement from domestic trade is cleared through the local interbank clearing and RBV settlement system, while foreign-currency outflows are settled through the bank’s correspondent network. Bonded depot operations should segregate disbursement categories — customs duties and bond maintenance, freight and logistics, supplier payments, and intra-group fees — so that AML monitoring matches each outgoing wire to an underlying warehouse transaction and the AXIL/export documentation set.Spot VUV/USD and VUV/AUD purchase and sale through an RBV-licensed authorised dealer, physically deliverable onshore.Outright forward contracts for documented trade settlements and inventory replenishment, tenor normally up to 12 months and subject to dealer limits.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed offshore with a reputable counterparty, settling in USD, because the offshore VUV market is not fully deliverable.Cross-currency interest-rate and cross-currency principal swaps between VUV and USD/AUD where the authorised bank can source liquidity and the hedge is tied to real trade or funding exposure.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; International Companies (ICs) are absolutely exempt from all income, profits, and withholding taxes under Section 137 of the International Companies Act (Cap. 222).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, no branch remittance tax, and no secondary withholding tax on distributions from an International Company to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The VFSC Annual Return and the annual government licence fee are due on the anniversary of incorporation (treated as the fiscal year-end); a one-month statutory grace period applies before late filing surcharges accrue.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File or Master File threshold exists because the IC pays no corporate income tax; transfer pricing documentation is not a compliance trigger in Vanuatu. However, every IC conducting a relevant activity must file the annual Economic Substance Return without any minimum revenue threshold. If the IC belongs to an MNE group, Country-by-Country Reporting applies only when group consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million (approximately VUV 95.2 billion) under the CbC/MCAA framework.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations Act 2022 (ESR Act) and VFSC guidelines apply to all Vanuatu ICs. The bonded depot falls within 'distribution and service centre' as a relevant activity. Substance is demonstrated by: (a) meetings of directors and management held in Vanuatu; (b) adequate physical premises, including the customs-bonded warehousing facility; (c) local employees proportionate to activity; and (d) local operating expenditure. The registered agent files the confidential ESR return annually. Failure results in penalties and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Illegal Domestic Market Diversion

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the Certificate of Tax Exemption and loss of IC status; under the Customs Act (Cap. 149), forfeiture of duty-unpaid goods and conveyances, penal assessment of 100%-300% of evaded duty, and fines up to VUV 2,000,000 per unlawful removal; revocation of the bonded warehouse licence and FTZ approval.

Director personal liability: The court may pierce the corporate veil: directors and controlling beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for evaded customs duty and VAT; criminal penalties reach VUV 2,000,000 fines and imprisonment up to 7 years; directors may be disqualified from managing any Vanuatu company for 5 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance (ESR) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the ESR Act 2022: penalty up to VUV 1,000,000 plus VUV 100,000 per month of continuing breach; the VFSC may strike off the IC, report the non-resident parent to its home tax authority, and disqualify the registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly file false substance declarations are personally liable to a maximum fine of VUV 5,000,000 and 2 years' imprisonment; a foreign tax authority may attribute all bonded-warehouse profits to the foreign parent, exposing directors to foreign civil restitution and travel bans.
AML / CFT Violation and Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu's AML/CFT and Proceeds of Crime legislation imposes corporate fines up to VUV 10,000,000, freezing orders, confiscation of bonded goods, and cancellation of the FTZ operating licence; the IC may be placed into compulsory liquidation.

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners and directors who authorise trade-based money laundering face indictable offences with fines up to VUV 5,000,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; courts may order asset confiscation, passport surrender, and unconditional travel bans, defeating the corporate veil.
Customs / Free Trade Zone Bonded Depot Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Assessment of arrears duty and 12.5% VAT on every unreconciled removal, plus a surcharge of 20%-100% of the goods' value; Customs may seize and auction the inventory, revoke the bonded warehouse licence, and prosecute false declarations with corporate fines up to VUV 250,000.

Director personal liability: Operational directors and the licensed warehouse custodian are personally liable for duties on short-shipped goods; each responsible officer faces fines up to VUV 750,000 or imprisonment up to 3 years, and courts may order civil restitution equal to the full revenue loss to the State.
