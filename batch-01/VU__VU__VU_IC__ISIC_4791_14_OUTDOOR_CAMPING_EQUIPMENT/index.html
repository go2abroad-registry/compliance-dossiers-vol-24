Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) - Companies Registry Division, Port Vila, Republic of Vanuatu.

Governing statute

International Companies Act No. 32 of 1992 [Cap 222] (Republic of Vanuatu), as amended by the International Companies (Amendment) Act 2014; applicable sections include s. 10 (manner of incorporation), s. 12 (certificate of incorporation), s. 14 (registered office and registered agent), s. 34 (one or more directors) and the statutory tax-exemption provisions exempting International Companies from Vanuatu income tax, capital gains tax and withholding tax.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form 1 - Application for Registration of an International Company, submitted with the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association and the registered agent's consent; the final instrument issued is the Certificate of Incorporation under s. 12 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital and no escrow rule; an International Company may issue no-par-value shares and defer payment of consideration; the VFSC registration fee is approx. USD 300 plus a Commission-set annual renewal fee.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable - no employees, office, warehouse or shopfront in Vanuatu, so no fire-safety certification, structural-load inspection or workplace registration is triggered; if the company later establishes a physical administrative office, a Port Vila Municipality trade licence, fire service clearance and occupancy inspection would become mandatory.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Department (VRCD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official customs and revenue portal: https://www.customs.gov.vu; foreign investment licensing portal: https://www.vipa.gov.vu.

E-government filing portal

No TIN or VAT ID exists for Vanuatu International Companies (ICs). An IC incorporated under the International Companies Act Cap. 222 is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes and is not registered with VRCD for income tax. The sole administrative ancillary registration is the annual Vanuatu Business Licence (VBL) under the Business Licence Act Cap. 250, applicable only if the IC establishes a physical presence or trades domestically in Vanuatu; a pure offshore e-commerce retailer using Stripe/PayPal with no local establishment requires no tax registration and no taxpayer identification number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None available. Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no VAT/GST, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax; consequently no check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or fiscal-transparency elections exist in Vanuatu law. The IC may voluntarily elect in its constitutional documents to confine its activities to offshore trading, or by special resolution convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act Cap. 191, which would trigger only the nominal annual business licence fee and no tax liability.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax is in force in Vanuatu. Indirect levies are limited to customs import duties (Customs Act Cap. 251), excise duties on alcohol, tobacco and hydrocarbon fuels, and the 15% tourism levy (12.5% tourism levy plus 2.5% eco-tourism levy) which applies solely to accommodation and designated tourism services and does not extend to internet retailing of goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Vanuatu has no VAT/GST regime, therefore no economic turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration. The only monetary threshold in force is the annual VBL fee band, which is activity-based and generally ranges between VUV 1,000 and VUV 50,000 for retail-type businesses with a physical Vanuatu presence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the absence of any VAT/GST system. Exported goods and offshore e-commerce sales of outdoor camping equipment fall wholly outside the scope of Vanuatu taxation; no zero-rating claim, input tax credit, or export refund mechanism exists. Outward-bound cargo is automatically free of export duty, and digital sales by the IC to non-resident consumers via Stripe/PayPal incur zero Vanuatu tax liability.

  • None required for purely digital/offshore sales without physical movement of goods through Vanuatu territory
  • If goods are physically exported via Vanuatu ports: Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World), Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading or Air Waybill

None. Vanuatu operates no real-time fiscal clearance, no continuous transaction controls (CTC), no e-invoicing API mandate, and no digital stamping requirement. Ordinary commercial invoices are accepted; for physical cross-border cargo conveyance only, the ASYCUDA World customs declaration platform is employed.

No VAT clawback exposure exists because no VAT system is in force. Adjacent statutory penalties: false customs declarations under the Customs Act Cap. 251 attract fines and/or imprisonment as prescribed by that Act; an IC engaging in prohibited domestic trading without a business licence may be subject to penalty and forfeiture of profits under the International Companies Act Cap. 222. Offshore export earnings of the IC are exempt from all Vanuatu taxation with no retrospective clawback risk.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has no exchange control legislation and imposes no restrictions on current-account or capital-account transactions. The Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible; the IC may hold, convert, and remit USD, AUD, EUR, NZD, and other freely usable currencies without RBV prior approval. Capital injections, dividend distributions, profit repatriation, and cross-border trade settlements are unrestricted. The VUV operates on a managed float against a basket of currencies with no dual-rate mechanism and no resident/non-resident market segmentation. Profit remittance hurdles are nil, provided the entity holds a valid VFSC Certificate of Good Standing and the bank has current beneficial-ownership documentation. No exchange-control approval is required for the target payment rails (Stripe and PayPal settlement in USD).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion rules exist in Vanuatu. An exporting International Company may retain 100% of export receipts in VUV or in a foreign-currency account (FCA) at a licensed bank; there is no time limit for repatriation and no conversion ratio. The only obligations are statistical: the bank must report balance-of-payments (ITRS) data to the RBV, and the entity must provide trade-in-services data to the Vanuatu National Statistics Office when required. This accommodates an e-commerce retail model where USD proceeds from Stripe/PayPal are settled as FX to VUV or USD trade accounts.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Central Bank Act [Cap. 125], which also prudentially supervises licensed commercial banks and financial dealers under the Banking Act [Cap. 63]. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) regulates International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], but holds no FX control mandate.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: Low-to-Medium. Vanuatu has no domestic EMI licensing regime, and major offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Mercury, Revolut Business, Currenxie) generally decline Vanuatu-domiciled International Companies due to jurisdiction risk and the absence of a local SWIFT BIC relationship. A viable modality is to use Payoneer or Airwallex as payment aggregators for Shopify/Amazon settlements, then sweep balances via SWIFT to the Tier-1 Vanuatu bank account. A separately established US LLC may hold the Stripe merchant account, but this does not constitute EMI banking for the VU-IC. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing uses SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank with purpose code 'Equity capital - contribution to foreign incorporated enterprise' (IMF BPM6/ITRS category) and remittance reference 'VU-IC share capital injection'. Beneficiary name must exactly match the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation; the registered agent's Port Vila address and the IC registration number must appear in the beneficiary field. Bank-side verification comprises: (1) matching remitter to Certificate of Incumbency, (2) source-of-wealth forms for transfers above VUV 1 million (approx. USD 8,500), (3) CRS self-certification, and (4) RBV BOP reporting by the bank. No RBV approval, no excise, stamp duty, or capital duty is levied. For USD amounts above USD 100,000, pre-notification to the bank's ODD unit is advisable to avoid OFAC false positives on the correspondent chain (ANZ Australia / Westpac New Zealand / BSP PNG).For trade settlements (inventory purchases of outdoor camping equipment from Asian suppliers), preferred rails are: (1) SWIFT MT103 for purchase-order payments with purpose code 'Import of goods for resale' and the supplier invoice number; (2) commercial letters of credit issued by the Vanuatu bank via MT700 for bulk orders above USD 200,000, confirmed by the supplier's Chinese or Vietnamese bank; (3) MT202 COV for correspondent-bank cover when executing same-day USD clearing; (4) domestic beneficiary payments settled via the Vanuatu Interbank Payment System (VIPS) where local vendors are used; and (5) SWIFT GPI enabled on all outbound wires for end-to-end tracking. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0%. Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no payroll tax, and no estate or inheritance tax. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is wholly outside the Vanuatu income tax net.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Vanuatu has no withholding tax legislation. Dividends distributed by an International Company to non-resident shareholders are entirely outside the Vanuatu tax net; no dividend tax, distribution tax, or dividend withholding tax is chargeable.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None. Vanuatu has not enacted transfer-pricing, Master File, Local File, or Country-by-Country reporting legislation. No VUV threshold triggers a mandatory Local File or Master File, and no advance pricing agreement mechanism exists for an IC. If the IC belongs to a multinational group exceeding EUR 750 million consolidated revenue, any CbCR and transfer-pricing documentation obligation is discharged by the foreign ultimate parent under its own jurisdiction's rules; no Vanuatu filing is thereby generated.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted explicit Economic Substance Regulations comparable to the EU or UK ESR codes. Instead, the International Companies Act imposes a statutory offshore-substance boundary: an IC may not carry on business in Vanuatu, may not derive income from Vanuatu sources, and may not hold Vanuatu land. The registered office, licensed registered agent, and corporate records maintained in Port Vila constitute the only substance required by domestic law. However, under Vanuatu's participation in the OECD Inclusive Framework on BEPS, CRS/FATCA, and the domestic AML/CFT regime, a genuine online retailing business must exhibit substantive commercial activity in its target end-markets, including fulfilment contracts, payment-processing agreements, customer records, and invoicing history. A corporate envelope with no activity beyond nominee registration is a recognised shell indicator for FATF, EU listing, and correspondent-bank due diligence irrespective of Vanuatu domestic law.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Unlawful Local Business Activity

Statutory sanction: Automatic loss of the IC exemption; the entity becomes assessable to 12.5% VAT on Vanuatu supplies and may be exposed under any future corporate income tax legislation; VFSC fines under the International Companies Act and revocation of the IC certificate.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced; directors and ultimate beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for unpaid VAT, penalties and costs. Criminal prosecution under the Penal Code [Cap. 17] for tax fraud may result in imprisonment, court-ordered restitution, and disqualification from acting as company officers.
Artificial Shell Arrangement and Misuse of Corporate Form

Statutory sanction: Strike-off by the Registrar of International Companies; forfeiture of limited liability protection; withdrawal of the statutory tax exemption; classification as a shell or passive vehicle in FATF/OECD/EU assessments; and termination of correspondent banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Courts may lift the corporate veil and hold directors personally liable for creditor losses; the Vanuatu Supreme Court may impose travel bans and asset freezes; directors convicted of fraudulent trading face imprisonment, disqualification, and personal civil restitution.
Anti-Money Laundering and Financial Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Enforcement under the Vanuatu AML/CFT legal framework, including the AML/CFT Act 2021 and Financial Intelligence Unit powers: monetary fines, freezing of bank and payment accounts, forfeiture of funds, and termination of the registered-agent relationship.

Director personal liability: Directors and the ultimate beneficial owner face criminal money-laundering charges punishable by imprisonment, confiscation of property, loss of travel documents, and re-entry bans into Vanuatu; extraterritorial sanctions liability may also arise from the payment processors' own compliance obligations.
Tax-Residency Misrepresentation and Treaty-Abuse Fraud

Statutory sanction: Prosecution for fraud under the Penal Code [Cap. 17]; circulation of false official-looking certificates is a criminal offence; VFSC cancellation of the IC registration; rejection of the claim by the foreign tax authority with interest and penalties imposed.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability with imprisonment; extradition exposure under Vanuatu's Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters framework; permanent travel bans and disqualification orders; civil fraud claims by shareholders and counterparties.
