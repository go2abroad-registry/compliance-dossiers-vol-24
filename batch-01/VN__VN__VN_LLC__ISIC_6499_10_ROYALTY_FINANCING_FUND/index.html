Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Business Registration Office and Investment Registration Authority, Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) of the province or centrally-governed city where the registered office is located; national processing through the National Business Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) and the National Public Service Portal (dichvucong.gov.vn). The DPI accepts and issues both the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) for foreign-invested LLCs.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 (Articles 21-22, 31, 42, 44, 46-48, 54, 58, 63-67, 77-97); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 (Articles 22-24, 36-39, 60); Decree No. 01/2021/ND-CP on enterprise registration (Articles 21-33, Forms I-1 through III-17); Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP detailing the Law on Investment and implementing investment registration (Articles 30-32); Circular No. 02/2019/TT-BTC on registration fees; Law on Foreign Exchange No. 06/2005/QH12 and Circular No. 19/2014/TT-NHNN and Circular No. 06/2019/TT-NHNN on foreign-invested enterprise deposit accounts and direct investment capital (DICA).

Formation / constitutional filings

Joint Dossier for Issuance of Investment Registration Certificate and Enterprise Registration Certificate (Bộ Hồ sơ đăng ký đầu tư và đăng ký doanh nghiệp), consisting of: (1) Document on request for IRC issuance (Form I-1 of Decree 31/2021/ND-CP); (2) Written application for enterprise registration (Giấy đề nghị đăng ký doanh nghiệp, Form No. 1 of Decree 01/2021/ND-CP); (3) Draft Company Charter; (4) Member List (Form III-3) and capital contribution dossiers; (5) Notarized lease or certificate of location use right for the registered office; (6) Power of attorney and copies of investor identity documents.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum charter capital is imposed on a limited liability company in Vietnam for ISIC 6499 (non-dilutive revenue-based financing), nor is a mandatory escrow account required under the Enterprise Law. The charter capital is declared by the members and is freely agreed, but must be fully contributed within 90 days from the ERC issuance under Article 75 of Law on Enterprises 2020. Since the company is a foreign-invested entity conducting revenue-based financing, it must open a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at a licensed bank in Vietnam and contribute capital in convertible foreign currency or VND in accordance with Circular 06/2019/TT-NHNN. For counterparty credibility and compliance with institutional fund agreements, a prudential declared charter capital of at least USD 300,000 (or VND equivalent) is recommended; the declared capital must be wired from the investor's offshore account into the Company's DICA and be evidenced by a capital contribution certificate signed by the General Director. No State escrow or bank-guarantee deposit is mandated, but the Company must ensure the capital remitted is consistent with the IRC-registered investment capital to avoid exchange-control sanctions and future profit-repatriation blockage.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate (Giấy chứng nhận thẩm duyệt thiết kế PCCC) and Fire Safety Acceptance Decision (Văn bản nghiệm thu PCCC) under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP, covering the building and the Company's leased office area, with an evacuation plan, fire alarm system, portable extinguishers and an assigned self-defense firefighting team; the FPPC must be obtained before bringing the office into use. Structural clearance: obtain a copy of the building's Construction Completion Acceptance (Nghiệm thu hoàn thành công trình) from the landlord to demonstrate that the floor load and use classification are suitable for office/finance operations; any interior partitions, vault installations, or raised floors must be covered by a certified structural calculation if they exceed free-load limits. Occupational safety: register the workplace with the provincial DOLISA under the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13; conduct hazard identification and annual safety training; arrange pre-employment and periodic health examinations; provide required personal protective equipment for low-risk office work. Civil defense: if the premises is a multi-storey building, the Company's lease must confirm compliance with 'conditions for use of buildings for office services' (Nghị định 06/2021/NĐ-CP on quality management and maintenance of construction works); if the building is within a security protection area, obtain the Public Security clearance before lease execution.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (GDT / Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance; primary national electronic tax portal at https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice management portal at https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; business registration integrated with the National Business Registration Portal (https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn).

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor must first obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment / industrial zone authority. Upon issuance of the ERC, the LLC automatically receives a permanent Vietnamese tax identification number (MST / mã số thuế). The enterprise must then file Form 01-DK-TCT (Tax Registration Declaration) with the local Tax Sub-Department or via the GDT electronic portal within 10 working days of ERC issuance. Supporting documents include the IRC, ERC, notarized legal representative passport, the LLC's charter, and a designated office lease agreement. The MST is used for all CIT, VAT, employer-side PIT withholding, and foreign contractor tax obligations. Where the LLC is established inside an Export Processing Zone (EPE), additional registration at the one-stop customs / zone management authority is required, but the MST process is identical.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer subject to national Corporate Income Tax (CIT) at the standard 20% rate on its worldwide Vietnamese-source income; offshore passive income (including royalty and financing income sourced from abroad) is generally taxable if it is attributable to a Vietnam-resident entity, subject to treaty relief. There is no pass-through eligibility for LLCs under Vietnamese law. A wholly foreign-invested LLC is not a transparent entity for Vietnamese tax purposes; dividends repatriated to the parent are separately subject to a 0% withholding rate (standard regime) if all Vietnam CIT obligations have been settled. Revenue-based financing income may be characterized for foreign contractor tax purposes only if payments are made by a Vietnamese payer to a non-resident; in the present structure the funded digital brands are overseas, so the LLC is the local holding/financing vehicle and the FCT position is neutralized.

Available election forms

Vietnam does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch passthrough election. Available statutory choices include: (i) election between the credit-invoice VAT method and the direct method (only for small atypical taxpayers; a foreign-invested LLC is generally mandated to use the credit-invoice method); (ii) election to apply CIT incentive codes (e.g., 10-year preferential 20% for eligible new investment projects, 15% for EPE high-tech undertakings, 4-year CIT exemption and 50% reduction for certain socialized sectors) as filed on the tax declaration; (iii) election to issue e-invoices either via a GDT-coded approval channel or via a registered self-issuance solution subject to API submission; and (iv) for cross-border royalty/financing arrangements, the enterprise may elect to apply a Double Tax Agreement (DTA) limitation on benefits if the ultimate investor is resident in a treaty jurisdiction. No hybrid mismatch election exists, and the LLC cannot elect to be treated as a disregarded branch.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese Dong (VND) operates under a managed float with daily SBV reference-rate bands. Current-account transactions are broadly convertible in substance: cross-border trade in goods and services, royalty remittances, revenue-share payments, and profit distributions may be executed through licensed banks upon submission of underlying commercial documentation and SBV purpose codes. Capital-account transactions remain restricted: inbound direct investment capital must be routed through a licensed Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) registered with the SBV, with capital contribution completed within 90 days of the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) issuance; offshore borrowing by the enterprise requires prior registration under SBV regulations on foreign borrowings and repayments by enterprises (as amended). Profit remittances to foreign shareholders are treated as current transfers and are permitted after the annual audited financial statements are finalized and tax obligations are settled; the remitting bank verifies the profit-distribution resolution, the audit report, and the tax-payment confirmation. Liquidation proceeds and non-resident capital redemptions require SBV approval or a certificate of completion of tax/liquidation obligations. In practice, banks apply a transaction-by-transaction authenticity-and-legality review under the prevailing SBV foreign-exchange circulars for trade and service transactions, thereby rendering convertibility high for substantiated current transactions but conditional and documentation-intensive for capital-account events.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SBV Circular 02/2021/TT-NHNN (as amended) and Circular 05/2021/TT-NHNN, all export and current-account service receipts—including royalty income and revenue-share payments remitted to the Viet Nam LLC by offshore licensees—must be repatriated into a foreign-currency account held at a licensed credit institution in Viet Nam within 180 days of the contractual payment due date or, if earlier, the date of service performance. The mandatory surrender ratio is 100% of the export/service proceeds: the full amount must be brought onshore and cannot be retained abroad without prior SBV authorization. If the receiving licensed bank has a foreign-currency purchase need, the enterprise must sell to that bank such amount of the repatriated proceeds as the bank requests; if no purchase demand is raised, the proceeds may be retained in the foreign-currency account indefinitely. The enterprise must submit the underlying sale/service contract, invoice, customs or service-completion evidence, and the beneficiary identification when the proceeds are credited.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV, Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam) — the exclusive central bank, foreign exchange regulator, and licensing authority for all credit institutions and payment intermediaries in Viet Nam.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-to-MEDIUM for offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Wise, Decta, or Currenxie. Viet Nam does not recognize foreign electronic-money-institution (EMI) passports and does not permit offshore EMIs to hold VND settlement accounts or to function as the primary treasury bank for an FDI enterprise. Their legally permissible role is confined to (i) operating as an auxiliary foreign collection or expense vehicle outside Viet Nam, and (ii) originating SWIFT/SEPA credit transfers into the LLC's onshore licensed-bank account. Onshore payout and NAPAS ACH/Direct Debit access require a Vietnamese licensed payment-service intermediary under Decree 52/2024/ND-CP on non-cash payments; such EMIs cannot provide local clearing directly. The core legal constraint is that all current-account and capital-account transactions of a Viet Nam-domiciled enterprise must be settled through accounts at licensed credit institutions; using an offshore EMI as the final treasury repository creates FX-repatriation risk, documentation gaps, and SBV exposure, and is therefore not a compliant primary clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound FDI capital injection: the offshore fund wires USD via SWIFT MT103 with the SBV purpose code for foreign direct investment equity contribution (mapped to 'Góp vốn đầu tư trực tiếp nước ngoài' / FDI equity contribution). The credit must land in the LLC's USD DICA at the licensed Vietnamese bank within the 90-day capital-contribution window from the date of the IRC. The bank verifies (i) the IRC and the registered investment amount, (ii) the LLC's prior DICA registration with the SBV, and (iii) that the incoming amount does not exceed the remaining registered contribution schedule. The LLC then converts the USD to VND through the licensed bank at the interbank rate within the SBV daily trading band; the conversion ticket and DICA credit advice are retained as statutory dockets. Any excess over the registered amount must be returned or reclassified, and any shortfall requires an amended IRC reflecting the revised contribution schedule.Onshore disbursements to Vietnamese merchants and operating vendors are executed via NAPAS ACH Direct Debit/Credit and domestic interbank transfers from the VND operational account, keyed to the merchant's beneficiary bank code and purpose code for domestic commerce. Cross-border outbound disbursements—including royalty/licensor payments, SaaS and brand-asset fees, offshore merchant advances, and operating expenses—are executed by SWIFT MT103 with the appropriate SBV purpose code (trade-in-services, royalty payment, or financing operating costs) and substantiated by invoice, contract, and delivery evidence. Physical-goods trade settlements use SWIFT MT700 documentary credits, with a confirming bank for offshore beneficiaries; standby letters of credit under MT760 are available for financial undertakings. Every outbound payment passes the bank's FX, AML, and sanctions release control, including a check of the OFAC, UN, EU, UK, and Vietnamese sanctions lists.Onshore USD/VND deliverable forwards with licensed Vietnamese credit institutions, tenor up to 365 days, contracted under SBV Circular 02/2021/TT-NHNN for hedging the LLC's committed royalty/revenue-share exposure.Offshore USD/VND non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) with international counterparty banks, used to hedge the economic VND net exposure arising from the revenue-financing book without requiring onshore delivery.Onshore USD/VND cross-currency swaps and linked single-currency interest-rate swaps transacted with licensed Vietnamese banks for long-dated treasury hedging of capital, funding, and revenue-repatriation mismatches.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-to-MEDIUM for offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Wise, Decta, or Currenxie. Viet Nam does not recognize foreign electronic-money-institution (EMI) passports and does not permit offshore EMIs to hold VND settlement accounts or to function as the primary treasury bank for an FDI enterprise. Their legally permissible role is confined to (i) operating as an auxiliary foreign collection or expense vehicle outside Viet Nam, and (ii) originating SWIFT/SEPA credit transfers into the LLC's onshore licensed-bank account. Onshore payout and NAPAS ACH/Direct Debit access require a Vietnamese licensed payment-service intermediary under Decree 52/2024/ND-CP on non-cash payments; such EMIs cannot provide local clearing directly. The core legal constraint is that all current-account and capital-account transactions of a Viet Nam-domiciled enterprise must be settled through accounts at licensed credit institutions; using an offshore EMI as the final treasury repository creates FX-repatriation risk, documentation gaps, and SBV exposure, and is therefore not a compliant primary clearing rail.
