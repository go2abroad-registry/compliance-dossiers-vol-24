Not permitted as a standalone statutory head office. Article 43 of Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 requires the LLC to maintain its registered head office in Viet Nam at a physical address where official legal correspondence is served and records are accessible to regulatory inspection. Competent corporate registry

Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) of the province or centrally-run city where the registered office is located, operating the National Enterprise Registration Portal and the National Foreign Investment Information System under the supervision of the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI).

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a non-conditional foreign-invested limited liability company (Law on Enterprises 2020, Article 37(1)); for an ISIC 6420 pure equity holding SPV no sector-specific capital threshold is triggered under Law on Investment 2020 Article 27, hence the Charter may declare a commercially reasonable charter capital. The declared charter capital must be fully contributed by wire transfer into the Company's direct investment capital account (DICA) at a State Bank of Vietnam licensed bank within 90 days from the Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance date (Law on Enterprises 2020, Article 75(1)); foreign exchange and DICA opening procedures are governed by State Bank of Vietnam Circular No. 06/2019/TT-NHNN and Law on Foreign Exchange No. 03/1997/QH9. Any unpaid portion after the 90-day period must be rectified by a Members' Council resolution adjusting the contribution schedule, or by reduction of charter capital, with the resulting changes registered with the DPI.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the Company must verify that its leased premises are covered by the building owner's fire-safety certificate issued under the Fire Prevention and Fighting and Rescue Law No. 27/2001/QH10 (as amended by Law No. 40/2013/QH14) and Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP prior to occupancy; if the future office exceeds the threshold for high-risk premises under Annex III of Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP, the Company must obtain a police fire-safety appraisal and an operational fire-safety certificate from the Fire Police (C07). Occupational safety and health: the Company, if it hires employees, must carry out workplace risk assessments, provide safety training, and file labor-usage declarations with the provincial Department of Labour, Invalids and Social Affairs under the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13 and Decree No. 39/2016/ND-CP; structural load and worker-density clearance for the office floor is the responsibility of the building owner as evidenced by the building's operational certificate; no additional civil-defense clearance is required for a non-industrial office-funded holding entity.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a non-conditional foreign-invested limited liability company (Law on Enterprises 2020, Article 37(1)); for an ISIC 6420 pure equity holding SPV no sector-specific capital threshold is triggered under Law on Investment 2020 Article 27, hence the Charter may declare a commercially reasonable charter capital. The declared charter capital must be fully contributed by wire transfer into the Company's direct investment capital account (DICA) at a State Bank of Vietnam licensed bank within 90 days from the Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance date (Law on Enterprises 2020, Article 75(1)); foreign exchange and DICA opening procedures are governed by State Bank of Vietnam Circular No. 06/2019/TT-NHNN and Law on Foreign Exchange No. 03/1997/QH9. Any unpaid portion after the 90-day period must be rectified by a Members' Council resolution adjusting the contribution schedule, or by reduction of charter capital, with the resulting changes registered with the DPI.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Vietnam; official electronic tax portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-tax declaration, e-invoice data transmission, and online tax payment); enterprise registration integrated portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn; national public administrative service portal: https://dichvucong.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned LLC must obtain, in sequential order: (i) the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC – Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư) from the provincial Management Board / Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) under the Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14; and (ii) the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC – Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký doanh nghiệp) under the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14. Upon ERC issuance, the National Business Registration Database automatically generates the enterprise's 10-digit Tax Identification Number (TIN – Mã số thuế, MST) without a separate application; the TIN is simultaneously synchronized to the General Department of Taxation's master taxpayer database. Post-registration, the LLC must complete the supplementary tax registration declaration (Form 01-ĐK-TCT) electronically on the GDT e-tax portal, file a bank account notification (Form 09-ĐK-TCT), register the electronic signature of the legal representative, and open a direct investment capital account (DICA) at a licensed commercial bank for inward foreign equity contributions and outward repatriation. There is no separate VAT ID in Vietnam: the single MST is the universal tax identifier governing CIT, VAT, foreign contractor tax obligations, import/export customs codes, and social insurance declarations. For LLCs in export processing zones (EPE) the same TIN registration applies, with additional customs-related registration on the VNACCS/VCIS customs platform.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; a resident Vietnamese LLC is an independent taxpayer wholly subject to the Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12 (as amended by Laws 32/2013/QH13 and 71/2014/QH13), with a standard CIT rate of 20%; no pass-through, transparent, or flow-through fiscal status is recognized for Vietnamese LLCs. The LLC is taxed on worldwide income with a foreign tax credit mechanism; domestic inter-corporate dividends (dividends received from Vietnamese resident operating companies) are exempt from CIT (shareholding participation exemption), but interest income from loan receivables and capital gains on transfer of equity interests in non-publicly-traded targets are taxable at 20%; for portfolio/index-listed securities the deemed tax rate is 0.1% of gross proceeds. Foreign-source dividends received from an overseas subsidiary are taxable at the standard 20% rate subject to any applicable Vietnam double-taxation treaty relief and the foreign tax credit regime. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a resident Vietnamese LLC is an independent taxpayer wholly subject to the Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12 (as amended by Laws 32/2013/QH13 and 71/2014/QH13), with a standard CIT rate of 20%; no pass-through, transparent, or flow-through fiscal status is recognized for Vietnamese LLCs. The LLC is taxed on worldwide income with a foreign tax credit mechanism; domestic inter-corporate dividends (dividends received from Vietnamese resident operating companies) are exempt from CIT (shareholding participation exemption), but interest income from loan receivables and capital gains on transfer of equity interests in non-publicly-traded targets are taxable at 20%; for portfolio/index-listed securities the deemed tax rate is 0.1% of gross proceeds. Foreign-source dividends received from an overseas subsidiary are taxable at the standard 20% rate subject to any applicable Vietnam double-taxation treaty relief and the foreign tax credit regime. A pure equity-holding SPV (ISIC 6420) does not automatically qualify for any tax-exempt-zone concession; EPE/high-tech-zone concessions apply only to manufacturing or qualifying high-tech activities, not to passive holding income.

Available election forms

Vietnam's fiscal framework contains no check-the-box, consolidated-group filing, fiscal unity, or hybrid-mismatch elections under domestic CIT law. The legally available elections are: (1) loss carrying-forward and timing election — tax losses may be carried forward up to 5 consecutive years, with the enterprise electing which profitable year to offset first (Circular 78/2014/TT-BTC, Article 11); (2) depreciation method election among straight-line, declining balance, and units-of-production, subject to circular 45/2013/TT-BTC; (3) transfer-pricing election to apply the transactional net margin method, CUP, or TNMM with corresponding Director of Taxation approval; (4) unilateral/bilateral/multilateral Advance Pricing Agreement application with GDT and competent-tax-authority mutual agreement procedure access; and (5) a one-time irrevocable election by successor entities in qualifying cross-border asset reorganisation deals to adopt deemed asset/tax-basis rollover, pursuant to Circular 78/2014 read with Decree 218/2013. Foreign tax credits are mandatory (credit method) — no deduction election exists; the LLC can elect between the credit amount computed under the foreign jurisdiction's final liability or an equivalent preferential-low-tax regime, without entitling to excessive foreign tax credit beyond Vietnamese CIT at 20%.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 1,000,000,000 (one billion Vietnamese Dong) of annual taxable revenue; nevertheless, a foreign-invested LLC acting as a pure equity-holding SPV is compulsorily registered as a VAT taxpayer regardless of turnover, since only households and individuals with annual revenue not exceeding VND 100,000,000 may elect VAT-exempt status; voluntary VAT registration is permitted downwards to business assurance; registration is effectuated via the integrated TIN/ERC process and will encompass all Vietnamese taxable supplies, making the HoldCo liable to maintain VAT books and e-invoices from inception (Circular 105/2021/TT-BTC, Decree 209/2013)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For a pure-equity holding SPV (ISIC 6420), the 0% export VAT rate is inherently inapplicable to its typical revenue streams: dividends, loan interest, and equity-transfer capital gains fall outside the VAT taxable scope and are thus neither 0% nor refundable under Article 9 of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC. The SPV may claim 0% export VAT only if it separately undertakes qualifying export transactions, namely: (i) export of tangible goods with a valid electronic customs export declaration on VNACCS/VCIS and actual physical export of goods from Vietnam; (ii) sale of goods to Vietnamese export processing enterprises (EPE) which are classified as exports under Vietnam's 'no free-circulation zone' customs treatment; or (iii) supply of services to a foreign entity where the services are entirely consumed outside Vietnam (offshore services), including services to foreign contractors performing overseas construction or installation; to sustain 0% on offshore services the LLC must evidence the non-resident contracting counterparty, the place of performance outside Vietnamese customs territory, and negligible domestic consumption. Reinvoicing of intra-group management fees or cost-plus service resilience without an offshore consumption chain cannot be re-characterized as 0% supplies. The input VAT attributable to the SPV's overheads (that is not allocated to exempt dividend income) is deductible only under the credit-method, with output-side zero-rating claims reviewed strictly.

Mandatory and comprehensive e-invoicing regime under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC applies to every registered business, including holding SPVs. Each e-invoice must contain a standardized invoice code, a secure 2-to-6 digit verification code, a QR code, and a valid digital signature (hardware token, HSM, or certified remote signing). Two issuance modes exist: (i) invoices with tax-authority-issued invoice code (VAT code) generated through the GDT e-invoice portal and (ii) self-generated invoices without pre-issued authorisation on the basis of a registered invoice template. The issuer must transmit the XML invoice data to the GDT's consolidated e-invoice database no later than 12 hours from issuance, with large enterprises required to implement API-based real-time or near-real-time data synchronization under the Phase-2 e-invoice roadmap; there is no physical seal or stamp — fiscal clearance is wholly electronic. All e-invoice data is conservatively used by GDT for automated cross-matching of input VAT deductions and output VAT declarations; any mismatch, delayed transmission, or non-issuance triggers an automatic VAT-refund blockage and administrative warnings, and can suspend the LLC's VAT refund eligibility until rectification and penalties are settled.

Export zero-rating and VAT-refund dossiers of holding SPVs are referred to enhanced desk and field audit by the General Department of Taxation (tax risk management unit) and General Department of Customs (post-clearance audit), given passive holding entities' natural non-export character. Fraudulent or abusive zero-rating claims attract: (i) administrative tax penalties of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount, plus late-payment interest at 0.03% per day for every overdue day and a freeze of any pending refund setoff (Law on Tax Administration 38/2019, Articles 33, 34, 58 and 59; Decree 125/2020/ND-CP, Article 17); (ii) retrospective clawback of refunds and input-credit disallowance for an audit period, with the statute of limitation reaching 5 years for ordinary tax administration and extending to 10 years where fraudulent intent is established; (iii) criminal prosecution for tax evasion per Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 (as amended) — imprisonment of up to 7 years where the evaded amount exceeds VND 100 million or the total false-invoice amount exceeds VND 7.5 billion, plus fines up to 4.5 times the evaded amount, and seizure/confiscation of property; (iv) cross-administrative sanctions including revocation of the LLC's VAT-invoice issuance status, suspension of the ERC/IRC certificate registration, publication of the taxpayer's non-compliance status under Decree 125/2020; and (v) blacklisting of the legal representative and chief accountant as tax-delinquent individuals, thereby blocking issuance of travel-exit visas for repeat offenders. E-invoice data that is not transmitted, or invoices with unverified counterparties in the GDT database, are automatically de-matched, causing the input VAT shown on zero-rated exports to be voided and recoverable only after a full document-verification pre-refund audit, which generally precedes around 70% of all refund applications; the LLC's bank mandate verification through the IRC must evidence funds physically repatriated within 180 days before refund clearance is granted.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% (standard rate from 1 January 2016; Small and Medium Enterprises may qualify for a preferential 15% rate under certain conditions, which is irrelevant for a passive holding SPV)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Vietnam does not impose a separate dividend withholding tax on distributions from a Vietnamese LLC to foreign corporate shareholders. Dividends are paid out of after-tax CIT profits and are not subject to an additional statutory WHT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (TP documentation) is mandatory where (i) total taxpayer revenue in the year is ≥ VND 50 billion and total related-party transaction value is ≥ VND 1 billion, or (ii) total related-party transaction value is ≥ VND 30 billion regardless of revenue. Master File is mandatory where the ultimate parent’s consolidated annual revenue is ≥ VND 18,000 billion (approximately USD 774 million) or the local taxpayer’s total revenue is ≥ VND 1,000 billion. These thresholds are stated in VND under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP. For a pure equity holding SPV, related-party transactions typically include loans or financing, management fees, share transfer proceeds, and any IP usage; dividends received are not part of the TP transaction base.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not operate a dedicated Economic Substance Regulation (ESR) regime comparable to the Cayman Islands or BVI. However, Decree 132/2020 and the Law on Tax Administration (Article 19) impose a substance-over-form principle and a general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR). A pure equity holding SPV is required to demonstrate real management and commercial substance in Vietnam: active board meetings in Vietnam, preparation of minutes, strategic decision-making by Vietnamese-based directors, maintenance of local books and records, and appropriate staff/local office arrangements. If a Vietnamese entity is characterized as a low-risk, low-function entity, the tax authority may re-characterize profit allocation, disallow the dividend exclusion, and impose additional CIT plus penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Non-Filing

Statutory sanction: Under the Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, fines for tax evasion may reach 3 times the evaded amount. Under the Penal Code (Article 200), tax evasion is a criminal offence: if the evaded amount exceeds VND 100 million, the individual offender can be fined up to VND 4.5 billion or imprisoned for up to 7 years. The company may additionally incur revocation of the business registration certificate and a prohibition on operations.

Director personal liability: The legal representative (Director/Chairman) and compliance officers of the LLC are personally liable for the company's tax debts if they fail to comply with filing or payment duties. Tax authorities may issue travel bans (exit suspension) when a director has a personal or corporate tax arrears above VND 100 million. Criminal prosecution for evasion exposes the director to personal fines, civil restitution, and imprisonment up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the GAAR, the tax authority may re-characterize the entire corporate structure, deny the CIT non-taxable treatment of dividends, re-allocate income to the actual revenue earner, and impose additional CIT (20%) plus late payment interest at 0.03% per day. Administrative penalties for failure to provide substance documentation are up to VND 50 million; in serious cases the entity may be deemed to be conducting an illicit financial arrangement and the LLC’s tax registration may be revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held jointly and severally liable for the additional tax and penalties. If the shell arrangement is used to facilitate tax evasion or money laundering, personal criminal liability arises. Travel bans can be applied against directors of the shell entity until the tax debt is cleared.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Under Decree 132/2020, the tax authority may make a TP adjustment, recompute the taxable income, and impose a 20% penalty on the under-declared CIT amount, in addition to late payment interest at 0.03% per day. Failure to submit TP documentation (Local File/Master File) carries a penalty of VND 10–50 million. If intentional TP manipulation constitutes evasion, the criminal penalties prescribed for tax evasion apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or execute TP agreements without supporting economic analysis can be personally assessed for the additional tax debt if their actions were malicious or grossly negligent. They may be barred from leaving Vietnam while the case is pending, and may face criminal charges for false certification of transfer pricing documents.
Anti-Money Laundering / Officer Liability

Statutory sanction: Under the Law on Anti-Money Laundering (No. 12/2022/QH15) and Penal Code Article 324, money laundering is punishable by up to 15 years' imprisonment for individuals and fines up to VND 5 billion (procedure). Administrative penalties for AML violations may reach VND 200 million. The SPV may have its transaction accounts frozen and its business license suspended.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, beneficial owners, and compliance officers are subject to criminal prosecution for money laundering if they knew or should have known the source of assets. The corporate veil does not protect directors; they face personal criminal fines, imprisonment, travel bans, and asset forfeiture. Concurrent civil restitution to the victims or the state may be imposed.
