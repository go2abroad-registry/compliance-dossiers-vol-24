Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế, GDT), Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam; national e-tax portal: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; one-stop business registration gateway: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn; governing instruments: Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, Decree 126/2020/ND-CP, Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC.

E-government filing portal

A 100% foreign-invested LLC automatically obtains its 13-digit Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST) through the integrated one-stop registration procedure: the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) issues the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) and transfers the enterprise record electronically to the local tax office, which issues the MST without a separate tax application; the LLC then activates its e-tax account using a digital certificate/PKI token and files its VAT and CIT registrations from the first taxable period. Foreign contractors without a Vietnamese establishment that earn Vietnam-source income must instead register under the Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) regime and obtain a separate MST from the GDT; offshore software suppliers without local establishment must register, declare, and pay through the GDT foreign-supplier e-portal. No minimum paid-in capital or equity threshold is a precondition for tax ID issuance, and the LLC's foreign-invested ownership does not alter its taxpayer registration pathway.

Available election forms

Vietnam does not statutorily provide check-the-box entity elections or hybrid-mismatch elections comparable to other jurisdictions; available statutory elections and operational regimes include: (i) under the FCT regime, an offshore contractor may elect the deduction method (tax on assessed net income) instead of the direct gross-take method where full Vietnamese accounting and transfer-pricing documentation is maintained; (ii) the LLC may apply the VAT credit-invoice method rather than the direct deduction method where statutory eligibility exists; (iii) the LLC may register for and apply CIT incentive statuses (e.g., software enterprise, high-technology park, EPZ/export-processing enterprise) by reflecting qualifying investment in its tax determination files; (iv) an offshore supplier without local establishment may elect the simplified GDT e-portal filing regime for VAT plus FCT; no entity reclassification or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Vietnamese law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations and maintains current-account convertibility, so the ODC may make and receive payments for software services, royalties, intercompany charges and lawfully declared dividends without prior SBV approval on the underlying current transaction, subject to documentary verification by the servicing bank. The VND is nevertheless a managed, non-freely-convertible currency on the capital account: all charter-capital equity, shareholder loan injections, capital repatriation, loan repayments and liquidation proceeds must enter or leave Viet Nam exclusively through the mandatory Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained at an SBV-licensed credit institution. Profit remittance hurdles in practice are: completion of annual statutory audit by an approved auditing firm; final corporate income tax settlement with no outstanding tax, social insurance or customs liabilities; evidence of distributable profits per audited financial statements; a board resolution declaring the dividend; and a DICA balance sufficient to settle the remitted foreign currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the post-2015 regime there is no mandatory minimum surrender or conversion ratio for export proceeds of a foreign-invested enterprise; the former 30% forced-conversion requirement was abrogated by Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN. The binding statutory disciplines for the ISIC 6202 offshore development center are: (i) full repatriation: all foreign-currency receipts from current transactions, including software-services invoices to non-resident clients, must be transferred into the ODC's Foreign Currency Account (FCA) at an authorised credit institution within 15 days of receipt; (ii) timing: export-of-services proceeds must be collected within the contractual payment period, with a prudential banking baseline of 90 days from completion of the exported service where documentary collection or open-account settlement is used, and longer contractual tenors accepted on proof of the underlying agreement; (iii) conversion: the enterprise retains freedom to hold USD in the FCA or sell it to the authorised bank, but any conversion must be executed through the licensed bank at the prevailing rate and unlicensed retail conversion or off-shore netting arrangements are unlawful.

Central bank supervision: The State Bank of Vietnam (SBV; Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam) is the sole monetary, banking and foreign-exchange control authority, administering the exchange-control framework pursuant to the Law on the State Bank of Vietnam, the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 05/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended by Ordinance No. 07/2013/UBTVQH13, and Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN on foreign-exchange management for foreign direct investment. The Ministry of Finance and its General Department of Taxation operate the complementary tax-clearance gate on profit, dividend and other current-account remittance outflows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: LOW as a primary or statutory banking rail; CONDITIONAL as an ancillary collection concentrator only. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business/B2B, Payoneer, Airwallex and similar) do not hold a Vietnamese banking licence or foreign-bank branch licence under the Law on Credit Institutions and cannot lawfully open the mandatory DICA, maintain a Vietnamese-locale IBAN, settle VND, or perform treasury clearing inside Viet Nam. The permissible architecture for this ODC is: non-Vietnamese clients may remit USD/EUR into the offshore EMI wallet as a collection convenience, the EMI sweeps the consolidated balance to the onshore licensed FCA via SWIFT MT103 within the statutory repatriation discipline, and all invoicing, revenue recognition, tax filings and statutory books remain in the Vietnamese LLC. The EMI must never be used to invoice Vietnamese-resident clients or to pay local payroll in VND, as that would constitute authorised-payment-services and foreign-exchange evasion; the servicing bank must be disclosed of the EMI relationship during onboarding to avoid an undisclosed-channel AML negative.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections must be routed through the DICA. The foreign parent shall remit charter capital by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the LLC's DICA opened with the mandated relationship bank, with field 70 clearly stating: 'Contribution of charter capital by [Investor Name] under Investment Registration Certificate No. XXX' or 'Shareholder loan drawdown under Facility Agreement dated XXX and SBV foreign-borrowing registration No. XXX'. The bank will verify the remitter's identity, the amount against the IRC/approved capital schedule, and the loan registration or notification requirement under the SBV foreign-borrowing regulations; under the Law on Enterprises, charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days from the Enterprise Registration Certificate date if no longer schedule is approved. Upon verification, the funds are credited to the DICA and may be converted to VND for opex only against the LLC's written conversion instruction supported by underlying payment obligations. Capital receipts may never pass through third-party or EMI accounts, because the DICA is the exclusive statutory inbound rail.Outbound clearing rails comprise: (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for current-account disbursements, including fees to offshore subcontractors, software royalties, intercompany management charges, loan interest and principal, and profit/dividend remittances, each payment requiring an underlying contract, invoice, tax-withholding evidence (foreign-contractor tax certificates, WHT payment vouchers or FTCKR/CIT clearance) and a lawful purpose code on the bank remittance form; (ii) Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700 issuance and subsequent negotiation/payment) where the ODC procures hardware, software licenses or delivers turn-key IT projects requiring documentary control against shipping documents, proforma invoices and acceptance certificates; (iii) in-country operating expenses must be settled in VND after authorised-bank conversion, because inter-resident foreign-currency settlement outside licensed banks is prohibited; (iv) dividend and statutory capital-reduction remittances require DICA routing plus tax clearance and audited distributable-profit confirmation.Deliverable outright USD/VND FX forwards offered by SBV-licensed credit institutions, with tenor generally capped at 365 days and supported by underlying current-account exposure evidenced by signed software service contracts or invoices.USD/VND FX swaps (spot-plus-forward or forward flips) executed with licensed domestic banks to align VND operating liquidity with USD export revenue and to square DICA balances during capital repatriation.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (USD/VND CCIRS combined with interest-rate hedging) entered into by the LLC with a licensed bank in connection with a registered foreign-currency shareholder facility, documented under an ISDA/IAF 2002 suite with Vietnamese-law ancillary standby provisions.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in USD/VND are generally not permissible for onshore resident corporates, as the SBV restricts derivative dealing to authorised credit institutions and requires deliverable underlying exposure; the offshore NDF market serves only non-resident counterparties, and the ODC treasury policy should expressly exclude NDF usage to eliminate enforcement risk.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: LOW as a primary or statutory banking rail; CONDITIONAL as an ancillary collection concentrator only. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business/B2B, Payoneer, Airwallex and similar) do not hold a Vietnamese banking licence or foreign-bank branch licence under the Law on Credit Institutions and cannot lawfully open the mandatory DICA, maintain a Vietnamese-locale IBAN, settle VND, or perform treasury clearing inside Viet Nam. The permissible architecture for this ODC is: non-Vietnamese clients may remit USD/EUR into the offshore EMI wallet as a collection convenience, the EMI sweeps the consolidated balance to the onshore licensed FCA via SWIFT MT103 within the statutory repatriation discipline, and all invoicing, revenue recognition, tax filings and statutory books remain in the Vietnamese LLC. The EMI must never be used to invoice Vietnamese-resident clients or to pay local payroll in VND, as that would constitute authorised-payment-services and foreign-exchange evasion; the servicing bank must be disclosed of the EMI relationship during onboarding to avoid an undisclosed-channel AML negative.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Full Local File and Master File required when total related party transactions exceed VND 30 billion annually, or where the enterprise's total related party revenue exceeds VND 50 billion; a Country-by-Country Report applies to ultimate parent groups with consolidated revenue of at least VND 18,000 billion.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam applies economic substance principles through transfer pricing rules under Decree No. 132/2020/ND-CP and Circular No. 45/2021/TT-BTC. Enterprises must demonstrate actual business substance, relevant personnel, and risk management capabilities supporting intercompany arrangements. The economic substance requirement applies specifically to offshore development centers: the Vietnamese entity must perform software development functions with adequate qualified employees, control over development activities, and assume economic risks commensurate with its reported income. Pure shell structures with minimal staff and no operational substance are subject to recharacterization of profits by the tax authority under taxpayer anti-avoidance provisions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may re-determine taxable income, apply deemed profits, disallow excess interest deductions, impose fines up to 20% of underpaid tax, and charge late payment interest at 0.03% per day. Penalties for failing to maintain documentation include fines from VND 25,000,000 to VND 50,000,000 plus potential profit adjustment.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives and tax administration directors may be held personally liable for failure to file correct tax returns; criminal tax evasion penalties under the Penal Code range from VND 100,000,000 to VND 4,500,000,000 in fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years for deliberate underpayment exceeding thresholds.
Tax Evasion and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Fines up to 20 times the illegal invoice value; tax underpayment penalties of 20% of evaded tax; business license suspension up to 3 months; criminal prosecution under Penal Code Article 200 with fines up to VND 4.5 billion and imprisonment from 6 months to 7 years depending on scale.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false invoices or intentionally direct evasion face joint and several civil liability for unpaid tax, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and imprisonment; courts may pierce the corporate veil when fraud is proven.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may recharacterize transactions, reallocate profits to the ultimate parent, deny treaty benefits, impose anti-avoidance adjustments, and levy fines up to 20% of re-determined tax plus daily interest. Registration may be revoked under enterprise law for inactive companies.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal restitution orders for tax arrears where corporate assets are insufficient following fraudulent restructuring; entry bans and imprisonment may apply under anti-money laundering and tax evasion provisions.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Financial Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines under the AML Law and banking regulations for non-reporting, unauthorized payment services, and violations of foreign exchange regulations; fines range from VND 100,000,000 to VND 3,000,000,000; corporate bank accounts may be frozen and licenses suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners failing AML obligations face personal fines and criminal charges for money laundering under Penal Code Articles 251-252, with imprisonment up to 15 years and personal seizure of assets.
