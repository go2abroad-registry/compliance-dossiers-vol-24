Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (VCIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; taxpayer portal: VCIRD Revenue Online Services (VROLS).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must first appoint a registered Vanuatu agent and obtain an International Company (IC) registration certificate from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). The IC then files TIN application Form TIN-01 with VCIRD, attaching the VFSC certificate, certified passport copies of directors/ultimate beneficial owners, a local agent appointment affidavit, and proof of registered office. VCIRD issues the TIN within five business days. VAT registration is captured on the same form where projected annual taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold; no income tax registration is required because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. An International Company may voluntarily elect to be converted to a resident company under the Companies Act, which does not create income tax exposure given the absence of direct taxation; however, conversion subjects the entity to Vanuatu VAT, customs duties, and local business compliance obligations. No standalone election to be taxed as a partnership or disregarded entity exists in Vanuatu domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover (approx. USD 43,000)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically removed from Vanuatu by the IC for supply to an overseas customer within six months of the invoice date qualify for VAT zero-rating. Die-cast and tampo-printed scale models sold FOB or under CIF Incoterms to non-resident buyers are zero-rated provided the supplier retains credible evidence of export and foreign counterpart settlement. Commissionaire/external sales-agent arrangements remain zero-rated when title and risk pass outside Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Vanuatu Customs Form VDE/7 as per Customs Act Cap. 250)
  • Bill of Lading or House Airway Bill evidencing despatch from Vanuatu
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT credit advice or bank statement showing non-resident payment settlement

Vanuatu does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or a government API electronic invoicing gateway. VAT-registered suppliers issue an invoice containing the statutory VAT invoice particulars; no digital stamping or pre-clearance approval is required by VCIRD at present.

VCIRD may audit zero-rating claims within five years of assessment. Where the export substantiation bundle is incomplete or the payment is not received within the six-month benchmark, the zero-rated treatment is disallowed and output VAT becomes payable together with interest at 12% per annum and a late-payment penalty of up to 75% of the VAT undercharged. Deliberate fraudulent claims are subject to clawback of refunds, civil fines up to 200% of the amount involved, and imprisonment in aggravated cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations and operates a managed floating currency regime with the VUV anchored to a trade-weighted basket dominated by AUD, USD, NZD and EUR. Current-account international payments are effectively unrestricted under the general permissions granted to authorised dealers (ADs), covering import settlement, trade finance, service fees and legitimate profit remittance. Capital-account movements, including equity injections, intercompany lending and structured hedging, are permitted but attract documentary verification by the AD and, for significant VUV conversions, may require RBV capital-transfer endorsement against the bank's open-position limits. A Vanuatu International Company pays no income tax, capital gains tax or withholding tax under the International Companies Act; therefore the only operative remittance barrier is commercial-bank documentary gatekeeping: audited or compiled financial statements, board minutes approving the remittance, up-to-date beneficial ownership declarations and, where applicable, confirmation from the Vanuatu Revenue Authority of nil taxable status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Act (Cap 69) requires export proceeds to be repatriated within six months from the date of exportation and either offered for sale to an authorised dealer or credited to an approved foreign-currency External Account. Vanuatu imposes no fixed percentage surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. A Vanuatu International Company acting as a cross-border trading and treasury vehicle may retain 100% of USD export sales proceeds in a bank-designated External Account, provided the AD holds on file: the export customs declarations, bills of lading, commercial invoices, underlying procurement contracts with the die-casting foundries, and a board resolution authorising foreign-currency retention. For non-resident ICs with no Vanuatu-source activities, the retention approval is routinely granted without RBV referral.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act (Cap 125), acting as the sole monetary authority, issuer of the Vatu, and the administrator of the Exchange Control Act (Cap 69). The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) regulates the International Company form under the International Companies Act 1992, but holds no FX regulatory authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM and strictly ancillary. Offshore electronic-money institutions (EMIs) such as Airwallex, Wise, Payoneer and Revolut Business are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in Vanuatu, cannot hold VUV, and do not issue Vanuatu-based account identifiers. They may be used as secondary collection and FX-conversion rails for B2C card flows and for payer-side USD remittances, but every EMI transaction must ultimately settle through the primary SWIFT-enabled corporate account at ANZ or BSP. PayPal Business serves only as a B2C card-acquirer rail, not a bank; Vanuatu ICs may onboard PayPal where supported, yet PayPal will typically apply rolling 60-day reserve logic to high-value 'die-cast collectibles' sales and will require a verified linked bank account, product evidence and brand-licensing documentation. To protect the treasury, PayPal and EMI volumes should be capped below 15% of monthly collections and never used for foundry supplier disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity and intercompany capital injections must be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from the investor's banking relationship to the IC's designated USD External Account at ANZ or BSP. The wire narrative must expressly state 'Paid-up capital contribution – Vanuatu International Company – working capital for zinc alloy die-casting operations'. The AD will require the corporate documents, board resolution approving the capital increase, shareholder register extract, incumbency certificate, CRS self-certification and a source-of-funds statement. Inbound funds are held in the Capital Escrow Account for a compliance clearance period of up to five business days before being released for supplier disbursements. VUV-denominated capital injections above the AD's delegated limit require prior RBV clearance and are converted at the daily RBV reference rate; therefore the treasury should route all material injections in USD or AUD to preserve flexibility.The primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for pre-production deposits, milestone payments against purchase orders, and final settlement against shipping documents to Chinese die-casting and tampo-printing suppliers. For large monthly production batches the recommended rail is a Commercial Letter of Credit issued by ANZ/BSP Vanuatu in favour of the supplier, at sight or up to 90 days, with documents comprising commercial invoice, packing list, full set of bills of lading, certificate of origin and independent pre-shipment inspection certificate. USD clearing is executed through the AD's London or Sydney Nostro accounts; VUV clearing is restricted to the Vanuatu domestic interbank settlement system and should not be used for trade payables. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax on any company; International Companies (ICs) under the International Companies Act Cap. 222 are statutorily exempt from all income, profits, capital gains and distribution taxes.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax; an International Company may distribute profits to non-resident shareholders free of domestic tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold exists in Vanuatu because no transfer-pricing, Local File or Master File legislation has been enacted. Practical compliance benchmark recommended for international tax audits: prepare OECD-compliant documentation where the IC's annual related-party transactions (e.g., purchases of zinc alloy castings, tampo printing services and packaging from affiliated producers) exceed VUV 50,000,000 (approximately USD 420,000), and align with the parent group's Country-by-Country Reporting threshold of consolidated revenue equal to or greater than EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted formal Economic Substance Regulations in the style of BVI/Cayman. Nevertheless, VFSC practice and OECD Global Forum evaluations require the IC to prove real economic presence proportionate to its die-cast trading activity: (i) a physical office in Vanuatu (a PO Box address is insufficient); (ii) a licensed resident agent maintaining client records; (iii) board meetings held or recorded in Vanuatu; (iv) local employees and expenditure commensurate with turnover; (v) full accounting records retained in Vanuatu. Failure to substantiate these elements creates a presumption of a shell company, exposes the IC to strike-off, loss of the Certificate of Tax Exemption and inclusion on adverse foreign jurisdiction lists.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: No formal ESR penalty exists in Vanuatu law, but VFSC scrutiny leads to denial or revocation of the Certificate of Tax Exemption, compulsory conversion to domestic company status, administrative strike-off and publication on adverse jurisdiction registers; foreign counterparts simultaneously deny treaty or credit benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors cannot rely on limited liability where they knowingly file the annual offshore declaration falsely; they face personal fines, director disqualification orders and criminal liability for false statutory declarations.
AML / CFT Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu AML/CFT legislation: administrative fines up to VT 50,000,000 or a percentage of turnover for systemic breaches; VFSC may suspend or revoke the registered agent's licence; correspondent banks, SWIFT intermediaries and PayPal may terminate the IC's accounts.

Director personal liability: Individuals convicted of money laundering or terrorist financing offences face substantial criminal fines and imprisonment of up to 15 years; travel bans, asset-freezing orders and extradition cooperation apply.
VFSC Registration and Annual Return Default

Statutory sanction: Automatic late-filing surcharge, interest on unpaid annual duty, administrative strike-off from the International Companies Register; assets held in the company's name may be administratively dealt with after dissolution; restoration requires a court order and payment of all arrears.

Director personal liability: A director who continues to trade knowing the company is in default and liable to be struck off is personally liable for debts incurred after that knowledge; VFSC may also ban directors from managing any Vanuatu company.
Fraudulent Trading and Piercing the Corporate Veil

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act and Vanuatu Companies Act: civil restitution orders, director disqualification, winding-up on just and equitable grounds, fines for fraudulent accounting, and compulsory asset repatriation.

Director personal liability: Courts may impose full personal liability for company debts, issue travel bans and arrest warrants, and prosecute fraud and false accounting offences carrying imprisonment; banking and PayPal accounts may be frozen under court order.
Unregistered Cross-Border Indirect Taxes (EU IOSS / AU GST / US Sales Tax)

Statutory sanction: Source-country assessments: EU VAT at 20–27% with penalties up to 50% of tax due; Australian GST assessments and penalties; US state sales tax back assessments; customs detention and destruction of goods; PayPal, eBay and Amazon marketplace payment holds and deemed-supplier liability on marketplaces.

Director personal liability: Customs offences are criminalized in destination countries; directors who knowingly sign false commercial invoices face personal imprisonment for customs fraud and personal liability for import VAT and penalties.
