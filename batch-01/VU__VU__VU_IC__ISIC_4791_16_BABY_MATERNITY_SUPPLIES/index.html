Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; corporate registry and tax-exemption certificates: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); foreign investment and business licensing: Vanuatu Investment Promotion Authority (VIPA). Public portals: dcir.gov.vu, vfsc.vu, vipa.vu.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act Cap. 222 through a licensed VFSC registered agent; 100% non-resident ownership is permitted, no minimum paid-up capital is required. Step 2: Obtain a VIPA Foreign Investment Certificate and pay the annual External Business Licence fee (e-commerce classified as Low_Risk_Standard). Step 3: Register with DCIR in ASYCUDA World to obtain a Customs Trader Code / Importer-Exporter Code for any physical import or export movements. Step 4: Apply to VFSC for the statutory 20-year tax-exemption certificate confirming immunity from all present and future Vanuatu direct and indirect taxes. Step 5: No TIN or VAT number is required for the IC because the entity is exempt from income tax, withholding tax, stamp duty and, once commenced, VAT; the IC remains non-resident for income-tax purposes provided central management and control is exercised outside Vanuatu.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None applicable. Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax or withholding tax; therefore no check-the-box, pass-through, group-relief or hybrid mismatch elections exist, and no OECD Pillar Two qualified domestic minimum top-up tax has been enacted. The only statutory election is the IC's right to request a written 20-year tax-exemption certificate from VFSC under the International Companies Act, and the IC may elect to maintain non-resident status by keeping board meetings, accounting records and management functions outside Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Value Added Tax (VAT) - the Value Added Tax Act has been enacted but its commencement order has not been issued, so no VAT or GST is in operational force in Vanuatu. The currently effective indirect taxes are customs and excise duties under the Customs Act Cap. 91, municipal turnover and business licence taxes under the Port Vila and Luganville municipal acts, tourism-related levies, and stamp duty under the Stamp Duties Act Cap. 70.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable while the VAT commencement order is pending. The dormant VAT legislation prescribes registration for businesses with annual taxable turnover above a threshold to be fixed by Ministerial Order (draft threshold VUV 10,000,000, approximately USD 84,000), with zero-rating for exports once activated. No registration threshold applies to the current customs duty or municipal turnover tax regimes, which operate transactionally rather than through a periodic turnover registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Currently not applicable because no VAT/GST is in force; exported maternity and infant care commodities are outside the indirect tax base and no refund mechanism exists. If the dormant VAT is commenced, exports of goods and services to non-residents will be zero-rated, requiring evidence that title and risk passed outside Vanuatu, goods departed within 60 days of invoicing, and consideration was received within 180 days; input tax credits attributable to zero-rated supplies would be refundable on a quarterly basis.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World, VU-EXP form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT payment receipt
  • Certificate of Origin where required by destination customs

No electronic invoicing clearance, real-time fiscal reporting, API transmission or digital stamping mandate is in force. ASYCUDA World is used only for customs declarations and duty assessment. The IC may issue ordinary commercial invoices through Stripe and PayPal settlement rails without government clearance or fiscal device requirements.

Vanuatu imposes no income tax audit on International Companies; administrative scrutiny is limited to customs valuation, VIPA business licence fee returns and AML/CFT reviews of payment rail settlements. Under the Customs Act, a negligent false declaration attracts penalty duty of up to 200% of the duty evaded plus potential forfeiture of goods; under-declared licence fees trigger reassessment, a 100% surcharge and licence revocation by VIPA; breach of International Companies Act conditions may cause loss of the 20-year tax-exemption certificate, although no historical income tax is clawed back because the applicable rate is 0%. Should VAT be activated, fraudulent export zero-rating claims would attract a 150% tax-shortfall penalty, fines up to VUV 10,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years, with retrospective clawback of refunded input tax and monthly compounding interest at 1.5%.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a fully convertible unified exchange system for both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange control approvals, capital authorization requirements, or restrictions on the repatriation of profits, dividends, liquidation proceeds, or intercompany loan repayments from an International Company. The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) floats freely and can be exchanged through licensed banks; major currencies such as USD, AUD, EUR, and GBP may be held in domestic or offshore accounts without RBV permission. International Companies benefit from the absence of corporate income tax, withholding tax, and capital gains tax, so profit remittance has no tax barrier. The only practical frictions are AML/CFT source-of-funds verification, sanctions screening by correspondent banks, and each bank's internal risk appetite for offshore Vanuatu IC structures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed timeline for repatriation of export receipts in Vanuatu. Proceeds from online sales, marketplace settlements, Stripe/PayPal payouts, and direct SWIFT customer transfers may be retained in the original foreign currency or converted to VUV at the account holder's discretion. The bank record must identify the underlying export transaction through the sales invoice, payment method, and shipping evidence; the Financial Intelligence Unit reporting obligation applies only when a transaction appears suspicious under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing framework.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV; Banque de la Réserve de Vanuatu). The RBV regulates licensed banks and monetary policy; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the licensing and registry authority for International Companies, trust service providers, and other financial services intermediaries.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, but only with a small set of offshore digital EMI/neobank providers. Most EU/UK-licensed EMIs refuse Vanuatu International Companies due to perceived offshore risk, the absence of a Vanuatu EMI passporting regime, and sanctions/reputational concerns at their sponsor banks. Stripe and PayPal are available only as merchant payment acquirers, not as primary treasury accounts. For multi-currency liquidity, the safer legal route is a Vanuatu-licensed commercial bank account combined with an EMI's foreign-currency payout wallet, provided the EMI accepts the VU IC's corporate KYC and the EMI itself is licensed in its home jurisdiction.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or intercompany funding from shareholders should be sent via SWIFT MT103 from an established banking jurisdiction. The ordering field must disclose the true remitter/UBO, and the beneficiary line must match the International Company's legal name and bank mandate exactly. In field 70, use a clear purpose such as 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION / VU_IC' or 'INTERCOMPANY SHAREHOLDER LOAN / ISIC 4791'. Because Vanuatu has no exchange control permit, the only prerequisite is the bank's own internal pre-clearance: provide a board resolution authorizing the account, a subscription or loan agreement, source-of-funds documentation, and confirmation that proceeds will be used for working capital and supplier payments. For larger inbound amounts, notify the bank in advance to enable enhanced due diligence and to avoid holds caused by correspondent bank review.Trade payments to overseas suppliers of maternity and infant goods should be settled by SWIFT MT103 for ordinary purchase orders, using the beneficiary bank's SWIFT BIC and the supplier's IBAN or account number in field 59 and invoice/PO references in field 70. For large or high-value bulk shipments, issue an irrevocable documentary letter of credit subject to ICC UCP 600 through BRED Bank (Vanuatu) or Bank of South Pacific (Vanuatu), with the supplier's bank acting as advising or confirming bank; typical documents include commercial invoice, full set of bills of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, and inspection certificate. Direct card payments or digital wallet disbursements should be avoided for supplier settlement because of VUV/USD conversion costs, low clearing limits, and weaker provenance documentation. All outward wires are subject to OFAC and UN/EU sanctions screening at the Vanuatu bank and at any US/EU correspondent bank; a clean compliance record materially reduces settlement friction.Onshore VUV/USD and VUV/AUD plain-vanilla OTC forward contracts with an RBV-licensed commercial bank, supported by documented export/import invoices.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing VUV/USD used by international market-makers to hedge VUV receivables without settling in VUV.Cross-currency swaps between VUV and USD for medium-term balance-sheet hedging of intercompany funding.FX swaps for short-term working capital bridging between VUV and foreign currency accounts.Zero-premium collars or barrier options if specifically approved by the counterparty bank and supported by board authorization and an underlying treasury policy.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable in Vatu (VUV). Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation, no country-by-country reporting obligation, and no Local File or Master File requirement for International Companies. As the company is tax-exempt, transfer pricing could not affect Vanuatu tax liability.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not adopted Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) in the form used in BVI, Cayman, or Bermuda. An International Company is not legally required to demonstrate substantive economic activity in Vanuatu. However, banks and payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) routinely require a genuine commercial purpose, operational substance, and clear transaction records as part of their AML/KYC policies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Shell Company Abuse / Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act and Vanuatu Penal Code, conviction can lead to fines and, for directors/officers, imprisonment for up to 5 years. The VFSC may strike off the company and freeze its registered agent relationship.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where the company is found to be a sham or fraud. Directors and beneficial owners become personally liable for Civil restitution of diverted profits, bank charges, and third-party losses. Travel bans may be issued by the Vanuatu Supreme Court in enforcement proceedings.
Money Laundering (AML)

Statutory sanction: The Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act (Vanuatu) criminalises money laundering with penalties up to VUV 75 million or 15 years imprisonment. The Financial Intelligence Unit (VFIU) can freeze assets and report to the Public Prosecutor.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners face personal criminal liability. Civil forfeiture orders can attach personal assets. Travel bans under mutual assistance requests are routinely issued, and extradition may follow.
Fraudulent Trading / Insolvency

Statutory sanction: Under the Companies Regulation and general common law, fraudulent trading carries unlimited fines and imprisonment. The Court may wind up the company and appoint a liquidator with retrospective investigation powers.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the company's debts if they knowingly carried on business with intent to defraud creditors. The veil of incorporation is removed and personal bankruptcy proceedings may follow.
