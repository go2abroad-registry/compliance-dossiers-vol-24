Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu law does not operate a check-the-box classification regime. The International Company is statutorily exempt from Vanuatu income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, and exchange control. Available elections: (a) voluntary VAT registration for an IC that makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu, including imported digital services to Vanuatu residents; (b) election to issue statutory invoices under the Value Added Tax Act; (c) no hybrid mismatch or conduit-election mechanisms apply because Vanuatu imposes no income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover; a non-resident digital-service supplier is also required to register with VCIR if it makes taxable supplies to Vanuatu consumers above the threshold, with voluntary registration permitted.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated when satisfactorily exported from Vanuatu within the statutory timeframe. Services supplied to a non-resident and performed wholly outside Vanuatu, including software/game rights licensing, Steam Valve and PlayStation Network publisher payouts, digital store distribution commissions, and offshore promotional services, qualify as zero-rated exports; services consumed in Vanuatu by local consumers are taxable at 12.5%.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed with Vanuatu Customs under the Customs Act, including the unique consignment reference
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exportation from Vanuatu; for digital services, platform payout statements and delivery logs substitute for transport documents
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer confirmation matching the export consideration in VUV or foreign currency
  • Steam_Valve_Payout and PlayStation_Store_Payout statements, publisher agreements, royalty calculations, and user-location evidence demonstrating that the gaming service was supplied to non-residents

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate is currently in force in Vanuatu. Taxpayers must issue and retain statutory VAT invoices in paper or PDF form, and preserve accounting and transaction records for six years. VCIR may require periodic electronic VAT returns and supporting schedules but does not operate a real-time government clearance gateway.

Vanuatu has no statutory exchange-control or fixed currency-repatriation deadline; the 180-day figure reflects the cross-border banking and anti-money-laundering settlement benchmark applicable to Steam and PlayStation store payouts. Under the Value Added Tax Act [CAP 249], a false or fraudulent zero-rating claim is subject to VCIR audit: civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax shortfall plus interest, criminal fines up to VUV 500,000 and imprisonment for up to five years, and retrospective clawback of zero-rated refunds, input credits, and the IC's tax-exempt privileges, including revocation of the VFSC international company licence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu is fully convertible across both current and capital accounts. Vanuatu maintains no exchange-control statute and imposes no restrictions on holding foreign-currency accounts, entering FX contracts, or repatriating profits and capital; the International Companies Act expressly authorizes ICs to transact in any currency. The VUV is stabilized through an RBV-managed basket/SDR peg with USD as the predominant intervention currency. Profit remittance from an IC is free of withholding tax and requires no RBV or VFSC approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts; digital game-publishing proceeds from Steam and PlayStation may be retained 100% in USD (or any foreign currency) in Vanuatu bank accounts and deployed abroad without declaration.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), constituted under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125], is the monetary authority and banking supervisor for the Vatu (VUV); the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately licenses International Companies and financial dealers under the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary account-institution rail. Major EU/UK EMI providers generally decline VU-incorporated offshore ICs based on jurisdiction-restriction policies or impose restricted-use accounts pending 90-day operational history; where accepted, onboarding requires VFSC good-standing certificates, complete director KYC, and audited revenue evidence. A licensed Vanuatu bank account with a USD correspondent remains the mandatory primary rail; EMIs are viable only as secondary collection nodes for Steam/PlayStation receivables where the EMI maintains a direct USD account and publishes entity-level due-diligence acceptance policies for Pacific offshore jurisdictions.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; the International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from all income, profit, capital gains, distribution, and withholding taxes under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no domestic dividend withholding tax; distributions of an International Company are statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes regardless of the residence of the shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The IC annual government fee and agent confirmation are due on the anniversary date of the certificate of incorporation (no statutory extension); any change in directors, shareholders, or beneficial owners must be notified to the registered agent within 30 days of the change.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Field 1 — Registered Office Address (IC Act s. 96) Physical registered office address in Vanuatu maintained by the licensed registered agent; the statutory registers (directors, shareholders, charges) must be kept at this address. Non-resident beneficial owners must provide a verified mailing address and consent to the registered office for service of process; a PO Box alone is insufficient. Late or non-payment of the annual government fee attracts a late-payment surcharge, automatic default fines accruing daily under the IC Act against both the company and every officer in default, and ultimate strike-off by the Registrar; the registered agent faces separate VFSC licence suspension or revocation for systemic non-filing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu enacts no statutory transfer-pricing, Local File, or Master File regime because no corporate income tax exists. Prudent group-level benchmark: maintain OECD-aligned contemporaneous documentation where annual related-party transactions exceed VUV 120,000,000 (approximately USD 1,000,000) or where any CFC, permanent establishment, or PE-attribution assertion is credible in the Steam/PlayStation market jurisdictions.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 applies only to ICs carrying on a 'relevant activity' — banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, holding-company, or IP-holding business. Pure Steam/console publishing without material in-house IP ownership generally falls outside the enumerated relevant activities; however, the IC must still demonstrate genuine registered office, licensed agent, board decision-making, and books and records in Vanuatu to rebut foreign PE/CFC and treaty-abuse challenges, particularly where US/EU digital-storefront revenue is booked.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Sham Offshore Residence

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu itself imposes no CIT-evasion penalty in the absence of income tax, but the source jurisdiction applies full anti-avoidance consequences: CFC inclusion in the parent's high-tax state, PE attribution of the publishing margins, back-taxes with interest, penalties of up to 200% of the avoidance amount in major OECD jurisdictions, and revocation of the IC's certificate by the VFSC; information is exchangeable under AEOI/EOIR channels.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false residence or beneficial-ownership declarations face civil restitution of the avoided foreign tax, accounting disqualification, travel bans pending criminal investigation, and custodial sentences for tax fraud; the Vanuatu corporate veil is pierced where the IC is shown to be a pure conduit, exposing personal assets.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration under the IC Act [Cap. 222]; reinstatement only on payment of all arrears and penalties; where a relevant activity is caught by the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019, statutory monetary penalties apply to the company and its officers, with asset forfeiture upon final strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent are jointly and severally liable for incorrect or absent records; in fraud scenarios the veil of incorporation is pierced, enabling civil recovery against personal estates, enforcement through Vanuatu's courts, and imprisonment of officers in the wilful-default category.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Monetary fines and criminal penalties under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 8 of 2014 and its amendments; licence revocation for the registered agent; VFSC freezing orders on the IC's accounts; and FATF countermeasures, including severe correspondent-banking restrictions that impair legitimate disbursements.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution, fines, and imprisonment terms prescribed by the AML/CFT Act; travel bans, account restraint orders, and confiscation of proceeds are routinely applied to individuals during investigation of a non-compliant IC.
Treaty and Withholding Abuse in Digital Storefront Revenue

Statutory sanction: Source-country disallowance of treaty relief, 30% US statutory withholding recapture on reportable US-source proceeds, interest and late-payment penalties, CFC inclusion of the recaptured revenue, and potential referral of the IC and its officers to the VFSC for licence and registration review.

Director personal liability: Personal liability for fraudulent treaty claims arises as civil restitution of the full under-withheld amount plus statutory penalties; criminal tax-fraud prosecution may produce custodial sentences, director disqualification, and cross-border enforcement through mutual legal assistance treaties.
