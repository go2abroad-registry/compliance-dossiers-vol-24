Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official portal https://www.customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu; customs declarations and clearance processed electronically via ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) via a VFSC-licensed registered agent under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], obtaining Certificate of Incorporation, constitution, and registered office in Port Vila. Step 2: Confirm exemption status under Section 12 of the IC Act; a pure offshore IC making no supplies to Vanuatu residents is statutorily exempt from all present and future Vanuatu taxes and is not required to obtain a VRC TIN. Step 3: If the IC triggers domestic taxable activity or voluntarily elects VAT registration, apply to VRC for the national TIN through the VRC E-Services portal (single TIN covering VAT, customs, and business license); submit certificate of incorporation, constitution, registered agent certificate, director/shareholder due diligence documents, and power of attorney. Step 4: File the VRC VAT registration form within 21 days of the end of the month in which taxable turnover exceeds VT 5,000,000; a Vanuatu service address and local registered agent are mandatory for any VRC interaction.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The Vanuatu IC is wholly exempt from income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, dividends tax, and stamp duty; Vanuatu imposes no direct taxes whatsoever. The IC is also prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents and from owning Vanuatu land, ensuring no domestic VAT nexus. VAT applies only to domestic taxable supplies; the offshore procurement and export of heparin crude or fractionated API remain outside the Vanuatu VAT net.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, entity-classification, or hybrid-mismatch elections exist because Vanuatu has no income tax base. Available statutory elections: (i) voluntary VAT registration under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap 232] for persons making taxable supplies below the threshold, subject to VRC approval; (ii) election to treat a supply of services as exported and zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident and the benefit of the services occurs outside Vanuatu, provided documentary evidence is retained; (iii) no election required for the IC Act tax exemption, which applies automatically; (iv) any future tax reform is subject to the IC Act grandfathering exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (approx. USD 42,000) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; compulsory registration within 21 days of the end of the month in which the threshold is exceeded; voluntary registration available below threshold; the offshore IC normally remains below this nexus because domestic supplies are prohibited.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Section 11 of the VAT Act, a registered supplier zero-rates goods that are exported from Vanuatu, provided the goods are physically exported within 28 days of the date of supply and the supplier holds prescribed export evidence; services supplied to non-residents are zero-rated where the services are performed outside Vanuatu or are directly connected to exported goods. For the heparin sodium anticoagulant trade (ISIC 3250), zero-rating covers: (i) direct export of the fractionated API from Vanuatu; (ii) triangular offshore resale where the IC never lands goods in Vanuatu and risk passes to the non-resident buyer abroad; and (iii) fractionation or blending services physically performed outside Vanuatu for foreign customers. The supplier must substantiate the 0% rate with customs, transport, and payment records; failure to export within 28 days or to retain evidence converts the supply to taxable at 12.5%. Note: Vanuatu has no exchange-control legislation, so no statutory FX repatriation deadline applies; the 180-day figure recorded in this dossier is the standard documentary benchmark applied by the compliance pipeline, not a Vanuatu legal requirement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World Single Administrative Document) evidencing export from Vanuatu
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill dated within 28 days of supply evidencing discharge at the foreign destination port
  • Commercial invoice with full description, quantity, unit price, Incoterms, purchaser TIN, and zero-rating statement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 / Letter of Credit payment evidence confirming receipt from the foreign buyer

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing, continuous transaction control, tax authority API clearance, or digital stamping mandate is in force in Vanuatu. VAT invoices are self-issued and must contain the minimum statutory particulars: supplier name and TIN, recipient name, invoice date, description, quantity, tax-exclusive price, and VAT amount. Customs exports must be declared electronically in ASYCUDA World with digital submission of the Single Administrative Document; no B2B e-invoicing integration with the tax authority is required.

VRC conducts post-clearance audits of zero-rated exporters and may require production of the complete export bundle; if the 28-day export window, documentary conditions, or residence of the recipient are not met, the supply is re-characterized as taxable at 12.5% with default assessment, interest, and penalty tax. Fraudulent or exaggerated zero-rating claims, including forged bills of lading, fictitious remittance certificates, or circular invoicing, constitute offences under the VAT Act and general criminal law, attracting substantial pecuniary penalties, imprisonment, retrospective clawback of VAT refunds for the entire audit period, and suspension or revocation of VAT registration; deliberate document forgery also triggers VFSC and Financial Intelligence Unit reporting obligations for the licensed registered agent.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The VUV is freely convertible for current-account transactions including trade settlements, dividends and fees, subject only to an authorized-dealer gateway. Capital-account transfers require RBV approval for domestic (non-IC) entities, but this regime is statutorily disapplied for International Companies: section 174 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] renders the Exchange Control Act inapplicable to the IC business, permitting unrestricted multi-currency accounts in USD, EUR, AUD, GBP and VUV. Profit remittance from the IC to its shareholders carries no VUV withholding tax and no RBV repatriation licence; the operative hurdle is commercial de-risking by correspondent banks rather than legal convertibility. Practical assessment: capital-account convertibility for the IC is effectively full, provided the registered agent and substance documentation are current.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a domestic Vanuatu exporter, the Exchange Control Act requires export proceeds to be received in Vanuatu through an authorized dealer within six months of shipment; there is no statutory fixed-receipt conversion ratio into VUV, and market-rate conversion is applied residually. For the International Company the surrender obligation is nil: under s.174 of the International Companies Act the IC is exempt from all exchange control directions, may retain 100% of heparin export proceeds in USD/EUR/AUD in a foreign-currency account, and no mandatory conversion into VUV is triggered. Non-contractual reporting still applies: physical importation of low-molecular-weight heparin crude into Vanuatu requires a customs import declaration under the Customs Act [Cap. 121], and any cash or bearer instrument movements above VUV 1,000,000 require declaration to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit; these are AML-customs rules, not FX surrender rules.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 322], is the monopoly issuer of the Vatu and the administrative arm of exchange control under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 67]; the Minister of Finance retains residual approval powers. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) concurrently registers the International Company (IC) and supervises the pharmaceutical licence for ISIC 3250. Payment and settlement system oversight is exercised by the RBV under the Payments and Settlement Systems Act No. 24 of 2009.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-TO-MEDIUM. Vanuatu has no domestic e-money institution (EMI) licensing class, so an offshore EMI cannot access VUV clearing rails; all operational settlement must transit through the RBV or an authorized dealer. UK FCA-authorised and EU/EEA EMIs will entertain the VU IC only under enhanced merchant-risk review and normally require a substantive banker reference from BSP or ANZ plus a 12-month trade history; Asia-Pacific MPIs (Singapore) generally decline VU tax-exempt ICOs due to correspondent-bank de-risking. Legal constraint: an EMI can hold funds only as electronic money, not as bank deposits, exposing the IC to insolvency subordination if the EMI fails. Recommended architecture: use an EMI solely as a collection gateway for client receivables, sweep balances daily to the BSP anchor account, and cap the cumulative EMI float at 30 days of operating burn.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route shareholder equity via SWIFT MT103 from the parent's regulated bank to the IC's USD account at BSP Port Vila or ANZ Port Vila; the receiving bank requires the payment narrative to state CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES ACT S174 with a board resolution and share-issue documentation attached. Intercompany operational funding will be remitted as SHAREHOLDER LOAN INTERCOMPANY with an executed loan agreement, informing the bank that the transaction is exempt from exchange control. There is no RBV pre-approval for IC inbound capital; however, the bank must complete a customer risk assessment before the first credit, and any single wire above USD 1,000,000 equivalent will trigger a balance-of-payments statistical report to the RBV. For trade-related injections from an offtaker, use the purpose narration ADVANCE PAYMENT AGAINST PURCHASE ORDER and attach the purchase order; never describe the payment as a gift to avoid unintended tax or AML reclassification.Preferred rail for crude heparin supplier settlement is a commercial documentary Letter of Credit issued by BSP under ICC UCP 600, transmitted via SWIFT MT700 from the issuing bank and settled by MT103/MT202 COV to the beneficiary's confirming bank, protecting against cargo and quality disputes. For repeat suppliers with an established relationship, direct MT103 payments with the supplier contract reference, commercial invoice, packing list and airway bill or bill of lading attached are acceptable. A documentary collection (MT400) is a viable fallback for mid-value shipments where the LC cost is unjustified. Exclude cash, cryptocurrency, or un-hosted virtual-asset bridges from the payment architecture, as these sever the evidentiary chain and strongly corrode bank relationships.
