Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Finance and Treasury, Value Added Tax (VAT) Unit; online portal: https://vata.gov.vu (VAT electronic filing portal); company registry administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) via https://www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity forming an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act (Cap. 222) must engage a licensed registered agent in Vanuatu. The agent files the incorporation application with the VFSC, obtains the Certificate of Incorporation, and then applies for a VAT identification number with the Vanuatu VAT Unit if taxable supplies are made or if the entity elects voluntary registration. For non-resident ICs with no local VAT-taxable activities, no VAT registration is mandatory, but the IC must maintain a registered office and agent in Vanuatu. The statutory TIN for tax administration purposes is the VFSC company registration number; a separate VAT ID is issued by the VAT Unit upon registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity forming an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act (Cap. 222) must engage a licensed registered agent in Vanuatu. The agent files the incorporation application with the VFSC, obtains the Certificate of Incorporation, and then applies for a VAT identification number with the Vanuatu VAT Unit if taxable supplies are made or if the entity elects voluntary registration. For non-resident ICs with no local VAT-taxable activities, no VAT registration is mandatory, but the IC must maintain a registered office and agent in Vanuatu. The statutory TIN for tax administration purposes is the VFSC company registration number; a separate VAT ID is issued by the VAT Unit upon registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An International Company is by default exempt from Vanuatu income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and stamp duty on international transactions; however, it may elect to register for VAT under the Value Added Tax Act No. 3 of 1998 if it wishes to claim input VAT credits or engage in local taxable supplies. No check-the-box pass-through election exists under Vanuatu law, as the IC is a separate legal entity; hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized because Vanuatu generally imposes no direct corporate income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

A person making taxable supplies in Vanuatu of more than VT 5,000,000 (five million Vatu) in any 12-month period is required to register for VAT; registration is voluntary below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating is available under Section 11 of the Value Added Tax Act No. 3 of 1998 for the export of goods supplied to a non-resident, provided the goods are exported within 90 days of the supply, and for the supply of certain internationally rendered services (including ship construction, repair, and maintenance for non-resident clients) where the recipient is outside Vanuatu and the benefit of the service occurs outside Vanuatu. The shipyard must hold valid VAT registration and submit documentary evidence proving physical export from Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Vanuatu Customs Form C4)
  • Bill of Lading or Certificate of Shipment for ocean-going vessel delivery
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence confirming receipt of payment from the non-resident buyer
  • Original tax invoice marked as zero-rated export
  • Copy of the executed commercial contract and delivery acceptance certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate is currently implemented in Vanuatu. VAT-registered persons must issue a standard tax invoice within 28 days of a taxable supply, retain records for at least 10 years, and file VAT returns electronically through the Vanuatu VAT online portal by the last day of the month following the end of the tax period. API-based real-time clearance and digital stamping are not yet statutory requirements.

The VAT Commissioner may audit any zero-rated export claim and require proof of export and foreign exchange repatriation. If the exporter fails to substantiate zero-rating or the foreign currency proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days without approved exception, the zero rate is reversed and output VAT is assessed at 12.5%, with interest at 12% per annum. Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, imprisonment of up to 5 years, cancellation of VAT registration, and retrospective clawback of any input VAT credits claimed. Under Vanuatu's mutual administrative assistance commitments, failure to substantiate exports also constitutes tax evasion and may trigger exchange of information with the recipient jurisdiction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is broadly convertible on both current and capital account. Capital-account liberalization means no prior RBV approval is required for non-resident equity injections, dividend distributions, interest remittances, loan repayments, or liquidation proceeds of an IC. Practical hurdles are not statutory but institutional: (i) licensed commercial banks maintain strict internal USD-liquidity and correspondent-bank quotas; (ii) global correspondent banks apply enhanced due diligence on Vanuatu-flagged entities because of historic FATF grey-listing (2015-2018) and periodic EU tax-listing scrutiny; (iii) large USD flows must be channelled through the offshore nostro accounts of the clearing bank to avoid VUV liquidity constraints. Shipbuilding-related payments may lawfully be denominated and settled wholly in USD or EUR outside the VUV system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds. There is no mandatory conversion timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no requirement that shipbuilding milestone receipts be repatriated into VUV. Exporters and ICs may retain foreign currency indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts at an authorized dealer. For the VU_IC engaged in commercial vessel construction, all USD/EUR export proceeds under maritime installment contracts may be held offshore or in onshore nostro-linked foreign-currency accounts without RBV approval, provided the underlying transactions are documented and the IC maintains its offshore-status compliance.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125], acting as the monetary authority, bank supervisor, and administrator of exchange-control policy under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 90]. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the corporate registrar and licensing authority for International Companies (ICs). For a VU_IC, the International Companies Act [Cap 222] expressly disapplies exchange-control restrictions on transactions with non-residents, subject to the IC's statutory prohibition on carrying on domestic business with Vanuatu residents without VFSC consent.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low as a primary clearing rail; Medium only as a supplementary disbursement conduit. Vanuatu has no domestic EMI licensing category; offshore-authorized digital EMIs (e.g., UK FCA, MAS-Singapore, or Lithuania ECB-licensed) may service the IC for operational payments but cannot settle in VUV, cannot issue VUV-denominated instruments, and are frequently refused correspondent clearing for Vanuatu-registered ICs. Correspondent policy requires full beneficial-ownership disclosure and sanctions pre-screening before any EMI transaction is executed. Therefore the statutory maritime installment LC and escrow rails must be hosted at an RBV-licensed authorized dealer; EMIs should be restricted to petty operational spend.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu levies no general corporate income tax; International Companies are expressly exempt from all taxes on income, profits, dividends, capital gains, and interest pursuant to Section 47 of the International Companies Act 1992.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu does not impose any withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to resident or non-resident shareholders. Dividend distributions from the shipyard's tax-exempt profits are free from domestic WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual license fee and Annual Return are due on or before 31 December of each calendar year, calculated pro-rata for the registration year; late payment beyond the statutory grace period attracts automatic penalties.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The VAT Return and payment must be lodged within 30 days after the end of each calendar month; non-resident shipyard entities making local taxable supplies in Vanuatu must register if turnover exceeds the VAT registration threshold.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - Vanuatu has no statutory transfer pricing documentation regime and no Vatu-denominated Local File or Master File threshold. No arm's length documentation is required for the tax-exempt International Company (which is outside the CIT net). Nonetheless, under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019, any entity engaged in a 'relevant activity' must maintain contemporaneous economic substance records in Vanuatu; prudent shipyard entities should maintain benchmarked contracts for intra-group charter, IP, and financing transactions to protect foreign tax positions.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 (as amended) applies to tax-exempt ICs earning income from relevant activities, including Shipping, Financing and Leasing, Headquarters, Holding Company, IP Business, Distribution and Service Centre. Commercial shipbuilding of ocean container vessels under ISIC 3011 is a manufacturing activity and is not a statutorily designated relevant activity per se; however, if the VU_IC also earns income from vessel ownership, chartering, or ship management (beyond pure construction), the Shipping relevant activity is triggered. Triggered entities must demonstrate in Vanuatu: (i) local management control in the jurisdiction, (ii) commensurate physical premises and employees, and (iii) core income-generating activities conducted in Vanuatu. Failure to meet the economic substance test exposes the entity to exchange of information with foreign tax authorities, financial penalties of up to VT 10,000,000 for severe non-compliance, and risk of strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Abuse of Tax-Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties equal to the omitted tax plus a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded under the Vanuatu VAT Act 1998 for VAT fraud; cancellation of the International Company's exempt status by VFSC; disqualification of the registered agent; monetary penalties of up to VT 10,000,000 under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act for false substance declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors sign annual compliance declarations; knowing false declarations constitute an offense under the International Companies Act 1992 and the Economic Substance Act, carrying fines and imprisonment of up to 12 months. In flagrant schemes, courts may pierce the corporate veil and order directors to personally restore diverted shipyard funds or pay civil restitution to defrauded foreign revenue authorities.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 including financial penalties up to VT 10,000,000 and mandatory exchange of information with foreign competent authorities; VFSC may strike off the company for failure to file Annual Returns; the entity loses legal capacity and escrow/LC operations are frozen.

Director personal liability: Under the Economic Substance Act, officers who knowingly cause or permit the company to fail the substance test are personally liable for penalties; fines of up to VT 750,000 per officer; a court may impose a travel ban and disqualification from acting as director of any VFSC-licensed entity in Vanuatu.
Anti-Money Laundering / AML and CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Sanctions under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2014: monetary penalties up to VT 50,000,000 for corporate entities for serious AML failures; suspension or revocation of the International Company's registration and the agent's license; forfeiture of vessel-related funds held in escrow; inclusion of the entity on the Vanuatu AML high-risk advisory list, obstructing future LC and bank correspondent arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML officers face imprisonment of up to 25 years for money laundering offenses, up to 15 years for financing terrorism, and daily penalties for failure to file suspicious transaction reports; directors who knowingly sign false beneficial ownership declarations may face criminal conviction, restitution orders, and travel restrictions.
Civil Fraud / Misuse of Corporate Form (Piercing the Corporate Veil)

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu courts may pierce the veil under common law principles, rendering the International Company's separate legal personality ineffective for the fraudulent transaction; directors and shareholders may be jointly liable for all unpaid debts of the shipyard, including refunds due under LCs and escrow agreements, plus interest and legal costs; the VFSC may order forensic audit and liquidation.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution for the entire loss suffered by shipowner-customers and financiers; punitive damages in cases of deliberate deceit; disqualification orders under the International Companies Act barring management of any Vanuatu entity for up to 10 years; judicial travel bans and asset-freezing orders pending satisfaction of the judgment.
