Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; official government and tax portal: Vanuatu Government Portal (www.gov.vu).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity intending to operate as a Vanuatu International Company is registered with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No income-tax TIN is required because Vanuatu imposes no income tax; for VAT and customs purposes, the entity files the prescribed VAT and customs registration forms with the DCIR, attaching the IC certificate of incorporation, constitutive documents, registered office and agent confirmation, director and shareholder identification, and evidence of anticipated taxable turnover. The DCIR then issues the VAT identification number (VAT ID) and, where applicable, the customs importer/exporter code.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity intending to operate as a Vanuatu International Company is registered with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No income-tax TIN is required because Vanuatu imposes no income tax; for VAT and customs purposes, the entity files the prescribed VAT and customs registration forms with the DCIR, attaching the IC certificate of incorporation, constitutive documents, registered office and agent confirmation, director and shareholder identification, and evidence of anticipated taxable turnover. The DCIR then issues the VAT identification number (VAT ID) and, where applicable, the customs importer/exporter code.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Not applicable. Vanuatu has no check-the-box election and no hybrid-mismatch tax classification regime. An International Company is automatically exempt from income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, and stamp duty; it may voluntarily elect VAT registration only when making taxable supplies in Vanuatu, but no income-tax classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 of taxable supplies made in Vanuatu in any 12-month period

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu by a VAT-registered supplier, provided export takes place within the contractual and statutory timeframe and the goods are evidenced as leaving Vanuatu. Services supplied to a non-resident are zero-rated when the benefit is enjoyed outside Vanuatu and not directly connected with land or goods situated in Vanuatu. The exporter must substantiate each zero-rated supply with the prescribed export and remittance documentation before claiming input VAT refunds on the VAT return.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory e-invoicing API is currently implemented. VAT-registered suppliers must issue valid tax invoices, and customs export declarations are transmitted through ASYCUDA World. There is no government digital stamping requirement in effect.

DCIR and Customs conduct document-matching audits, verifying the customs export declaration, transport evidence, payment, and inward remittance within the statutory repatriation period. False or unsupported zero-rating claims result in retrospective VAT assessments at 12.5%, denial of input VAT refunds, late-payment interest, and monetary penalties which may reach double the tax in cases of fraud; deliberate false customs declarations are criminal offences and may result in fines, imprisonment, and disqualification from export zero-rating and customs processing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible across both current and capital account transactions; Vanuatu imposes no exchange control legislation restricting foreign-currency denominated accounts, profit repatriation, dividend distribution, or capital liquidation by non-residents. The operative constraint is liquidity depth rather than regulation: VUV is a minor non-deliverable currency with no active offshore NDF interbank market, so cross-border trade settlements are practically executed through USD, AUD, and EUR nostro corridors at authorized dealers. No prior central bank approval or capital account license is required for the IC to receive inbound equity, remit royalties, or disburse trade payments; the bank merely files standardized transaction reports under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act No. 13 of 2000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion percentages, or repatriation timelines exist under Vanuatu law. Foreign-currency export receipts may be retained indefinitely in USD/AUD/EUR accounts at authorized dealers; conversion to VUV is voluntary and driven solely by working capital needs. The only obligations are administrative: the authorized bank records the underlying commercial invoice and the IC must preserve shipping and customs documentation to evidence the trade on audit.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; monetary supervision is co-administered with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), which licenses authorized dealers and payment service providers under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act No. 31 of 2021.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low (conditional). Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Platform, Airwallex, Payoneer, Currencycloud) cannot hold a VFSC banking license and cannot issue VUV-denominated virtual IBANs; they are legally permissible only as non-resident USD settlement wallets that receive B2C marketplace collections (PayPal, Shopify Payments, Stripe) and then route inward MT103 remittances to the approved Vanuatu operational account. The controlling legal constraint is Section 6 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act No. 31 of 2021, which requires any person carrying on payment services in or from Vanuatu to hold a VFSC license; therefore the EMI arrangement must be structured as a passive payable-through corridor with no account held in the name of the IC at the EMI, no VUV conversion offshore, and complete transaction-level reporting into the Vanuatu accounting system.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold applies in Vanuatu because there is no corporate income tax or transfer-pricing filing law. In VUV terms, the threshold is effectively N/A. Prudent practice for a cross-border manufacturing group is to prepare OECD-compliant Local File/Master File documentation when required by a foreign affiliated entity's tax jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not impose an ongoing economic-substance test on a standard International Company operating exclusively offshore. However, the VFSC requires a registered agent, a registered office in Vanuatu, adequate accounting records, and compliance with AML/CFT obligations. If the IC holds assets or conducts manufacturing functions in another jurisdiction, that foreign jurisdiction's economic-substance, CFC, or permanent-establishment rules will apply to the actual activities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Offshore Tax Structure

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu law, false statements to the Registrar and fraudulent conduct may lead to monetary penalties, compulsory deregistration, confiscation of assets, and mutual legal assistance to foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in or facilitate tax fraud face personal civil liability for restitution and disgorgement. Criminal conviction may result in imprisonment, travel restrictions, and directorial disqualification.
Shell Company / Abusive Lack of Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC may impose regulatory fines, suspend or revoke the IC's registration, strike it off, and refer the matter to law enforcement. If the IC engages in domestic Vanuatu business without a valid licence, it loses the statutory tax-exempt character and may be assessed under Vanuatu's general tax and licensing rules.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil can be pierced where the IC is a facade for illicit arrangements. Directors may be held personally liable for debts, ordered to make restitution, subjected to travel bans, and disqualified from acting as directors in Vanuatu.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Violation

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu's AML/CFT legislation and VFSC enforcement powers, the responsible entity faces supervisory fines, suspension, deregistration, asset forfeiture, and criminal prosecution for serious offences.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers can be prosecuted individually. Personal liability includes large criminal fines, imprisonment, asset confiscation, travel restrictions, and civil restitution; the IC corporate veil is not a shield for deliberate money laundering or financial crime.
