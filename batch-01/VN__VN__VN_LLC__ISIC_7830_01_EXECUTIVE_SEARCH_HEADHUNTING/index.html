Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not acceptable as a standalone registered office. The registered address must be a real, physical premises in Viet Nam over which the LLC has a lawful right of use (ownership, lease, or consent of the owner), and the address must be accurately stated in the Enterprise Registration Certificate. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Viet Nam imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Limited Liability Company; the charter capital determined by the members must be fully contributed within 90 days from the Enterprise Registration Certificate. For the regulated headhunting activity, an additional security escrow deposit of VND 300,000,000 (approximately USD 12,800) must be placed in and blocked at a licensed commercial bank in the Company's name, maintained for the duration of the employment service license and withdrawable only after re-placement or lawful termination of the license.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tong Cuc Thue) under the Ministry of Finance of Viet Nam; official e-filing portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; national e-invoice portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; integrated business registration portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested Limited Liability Company automatically obtains a single statutory tax identification number (Ma So Thue, 'MST', 13-digit format) through the one-stop enterprise registration procedure at the provincial Department of Planning and Investment. The Certificate of Enterprise Registration functions as the tax registration certificate, with the MST simultaneously valid for CIT, VAT, personal income tax withholding, and social insurance contributions. Non-resident foreign contractors without a Vietnamese permanent establishment must register separately under the Foreign Contractor Tax regime using Form 01/GTGT (deduction method) or the direct/hybrid method forms at the local tax office where the Vietnamese paying entity is located.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Viet Nam has no check-the-box pass-through election and no general hybrid-mismatch election. A resident Vietnamese LLC is mandatorily taxed at entity level on worldwide income under the Corporate Income Tax Law 14/2008/QH12 and confirming Law 32/2013/QH13. Available statutory elections include: (i) selection among the three Foreign Contractor Tax computation methods (deduction, direct, hybrid); (ii) election to apply preferential CIT regimes (10% for 15 years, tax holidays, or reduced tax on the first four profitable years) for qualifying new high-technology or large-scale investment projects; and (iii) election by SMEs to pay land-use related taxes under alternative accounting bases. Any arrangement structured to exploit divergent tax classification between jurisdictions remains subject to the general anti-avoidance rule and substance-over-form review under Article 32 of Law 38/2019/QH14 on Tax Administration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 1,000,000,000 (one billion Vietnamese Dong) of annual revenue from taxable supplies is the compulsory VAT registration threshold for household and individual businesses; incorporated enterprises, including LLCs, are automatically VAT-registered upon obtaining their MST regardless of revenue level, with mandatory e-invoicing applicable from constitution.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Executive search and headhunting services qualify for the 0% VAT export rate under Article 9 of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC where: (i) the service is supplied to a foreign organization or individual under a written service contract; (ii) the service is consumed entirely outside Viet Nam; (iii) the foreign client has no permanent establishment in Viet Nam to which the service is attributable; and (iv) payment is remitted to the Vietnamese LLC in foreign currency through a licensed bank evidenced by an inward remittance certificate. Placement fees, retainer fees pre-paid by offshore clients, and success fees receivable from foreign clients for identifying C-suite talent outside Viet Nam are zero-rated; concurrent services rendered to the Vietnamese branch or subsidiary of an overseas client remain subject to 10% VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (To Khai Hai Quan Dien Tu) where goods are exported in connection with the supply (not typical for pure executive search)
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Airway Bill (AWB) evidencing cross-border movement, where applicable
  • Signed executive search service contract or engagement letter in Vietnamese and English between the VN_LLC and the overseas client
  • 0% VAT electronic invoice (XML format) transmitted to the GDT portal
  • Bank Inward Remittance Certificate (Xac Nhan Thu Tien Chuyen Nam) or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of foreign currency from the offshore client
  • Foreign client evidence of non-resident status (halal) such as corporate registration certificate, tax domicile certificate, or VAT/company-code extract of the overseas jurisdiction
  • Service delivery report / placement memorandum documenting that the service was consumed outside Viet Nam
  • KYC and AML standard onboarding records of the foreign client (low-risk standard privacy-tier for executive search)

Mandatory e-invoicing applies in full force from 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. Every VAT invoice must be issued as a structured XML file signed with a digital signature and transmitted to the General Department of Taxation portal immediately upon issuance; sales in certain high-risk sectors must obtain a pre-generated tax authority invoice code (Ma Cua Co Quan Thue) embedded in the invoice before the document is delivered to the customer. There is no per-transaction real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping requirement for standard executive-search supplies; instead a batch transmission protocol applies. For retained executive-search services retained on an international export basis, the LLC must maintain the 0% tax schedule e-invoice alongside the customs-free service export evidence.

The General Department of Taxation exercises heightened audit scrutiny over export-oriented zero-rating claims. A retroactive assessment period of up to 10 years applies to fraudulently claimed VAT refunds under Article 12 of Law 38/2019/QH14. On an adverse finding, the tax authority will claw back every input VAT credit previously refunded, plus interest calculated at the State Bank refinancing rate, and impose administrative fines equal to one to three times the evaded tax amount under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP. Repeated or aggravated falsification of export documentation further exposes legal representatives to criminal liability under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 (as amended) with imprisonment terms of up to 20 years, loss of e-invoice usage status, and permanent suspension from the e-invoice refund system. The LLC must therefore maintain immutable archive records for each zero-rated retainer, including end-to-end retained evidence of offshore consumption and foreign-currency realization.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

VND is not freely convertible offshore. Current-account transactions are largely convertible: foreign-invested enterprises may buy and sell foreign currency at licensed banks for trade and service payments without separate SBV approval, subject to underlying documentation. Capital-account transactions are controlled: charter capital injection, capital increases, shareholder loans, equity transfers, and liquidation/profit remittance require the registered IRC/ERC, an SBV-licensed direct-investment capital account, and prior registration or approval where required. Profit remittance to the non-resident shareholder is permitted annually following the completion of audited financial statements, CIT finalization, and filing of the post-audit profit remittance form (under Circular No. 186/2010/TT-BTC); the enterprise must prove the accumulated statutory reserve has been constituted, all tax obligations are settled, and there are no outstanding tax liabilities confirmed by the tax officer. VND convertibility for speculative or working-capital purposes unrelated to underlying commercial transactions is not permitted; all FX payments must be matched to genuine trade, investment, or loan exposure. The side effects for an executive-search LLC are that retainer invoices to foreign clients create service-export receivables collected in USD/EUR, which must either be held in a foreign-currency account or converted to VND, but the enterprise cannot freely transfer VND offshore to fund overseas group operations without an underlying permitted transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Article 12 of Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP as amended by Decree No. 15/2018/ND-CP, export proceeds received in foreign currency must be repatriated into Vietnam through a licensed bank within 90 calendar days from the date of service completion (for services) or customs clearance (for goods). There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio: the LLC may retain repatriated export proceeds in its foreign-currency account at a licensed bank and is permitted to sell them to the bank at market rates, but conversion outside licensed banks is prohibited. For ISIC 7830 executive search, each inbound retainer from a non-resident client is a service export; the underlying mandate letter, signed retainer agreement, placement invoice, delivery evidence, and bank SWIFT credit advice must be date-stamped and archived to demonstrate compliance with the 90-day repatriation window. Late repatriation or failure to repatriate is an administrative violation penalized under Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP as amended by Decree No. 143/2021/ND-CP with fines up to VND 100,000,000 and, for repeat or material non-compliance, suspension of foreign-currency accounts and adverse SBV inspection findings. The rule does not apply to lawfully retained balances required to pay approved foreign debts or amounts exempted in writing by the SBV.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam), acting through the Foreign Exchange Management Department, the Banking Supervision and Inspection Agency, and provincial branches; foreign exchange and payment-system regulations are issued under the Law on the State Bank of Vietnam 2010, the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange 2005 (as amended by Ordinance No. 06/2013/UBTVQH13), Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP, Decree No. 15/2018/ND-CP, Circular No. 28/2016/TT-NHNN, Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN, and Circular No. 09/2023/TT-NHNN. The SBV exclusively licenses foreign exchange activity and cross-border payment intermediation, while the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI) and provincial Departments of Planning and Investment (DPIs) retain authority over the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) that underpins capital-account flows for the foreign-invested LLC.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: CONDITIONAL with LOW reliance for primary clearing. Wise (Wise Asia-Pacific Pte. Ltd. and its local partner arrangements), Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar offshore digital EMIs are not licensed by the SBV as credit institutions or intermediate payment service providers and therefore cannot lawfully open VND-denominated accounts or independently settle payments into the domestic payment system. For the VN_LLC executive-search treasury, Wise B2B is viable only as an ancillary collection rail for small inbound retainer wires from Western clients where the client lacks SWIFT capabilities; funds must be routed to the licensee with an underlying service contract, and the collected foreign currency must be repatriated to the LLC's SBV-licensed VND or foreign-currency account within the 90-day window. Outbound uses of Wise for vendor or payroll payments from within Vietnam are not viable because the SBV requires cross-border outbound payment orders to be executed by licensed banks after know-your-transaction screening; a non-licensed EMI cannot provide the locally admissible FX invoice evidence and would create an unlawful channel. Airwallex is rated Medium for collection convenience but remains legally constrained to bank-partner rails. Legal risks include: operating an unauthorized payment service (administrative penalties under Decree 88/2019/ND-CP), inability to satisfy Vietnamese tax e-invoice mapping, and SBV foreign-currency control violations. Therefore the treasury design places Wise B2B strictly in a vendor-collection overflow layer, never as the statutory capital, tax payment, or primary payroll rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The VN LLC must open a direct investment capital account (a foreign-currency capital account) at an SBV-licensed bank before receiving charter capital. The process: submit the IRC, ERC, and the shareholders' resolution on capital contribution to the chosen bank; the bank registers the capital account and may require SBV confirmation where the contribution schedule is phased. Each inbound wire must be executed via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's overseas account with the purpose narrative clearly stating that it is a contribution to charter capital, citing the IRC number, ERC number and the legal entity name; use of the investment purpose code corresponding to foreign direct investment as required by the bank's reporting code list. The full subscribed capital must be contributed within 90 days from the certificate of enterprise registration per Article 47 of the Enterprise Law 2020; failure to contribute the remaining amount by the deadline renders the un-contributed portion non-transferable and is sanctionable under Decree No. 50/2021/ND-CP with fines from VND 20,000,000 to VND 30,000,000, and the bank will not credit late funds as lawful share capital. The bank issues the capital contribution confirmation (Giấy xác nhận góp vốn) used for the updated IRC and for later profit remittance verification. The LLC must never allow charter capital to be received into a personal account or an ordinary operating account; such inflows are classified as unidentifiable and may be refused or reported as suspicious. For subsequent capital increases, the capital account operating limit must first be increased by a DPI-approved adjusted IRC before the next MT103; for share transfers, the purchase price must be routed through the capital account after the DPI registers the transfer, with a Foreign Exchange Registration Letter to the SBV. For the executive-search LLC, the charter capital can be as low as VND 100,000 market-rate equivalent because no minimum capital applies to headhunting services, but the bank may impose a practical minimum commensurate with first-year payroll and the proposed physical office.The service-export treasury of the LLC will generate outbound flows for: overseas subcontractors and background verification vendors; candidate relocation payments; software and job-board subscriptions; payroll to expatriates; management fees to the foreign parent; and annual profit remittance to shareholders. All outbound disbursements must be executed through the licensed Vietnamese bank using SWIFT MT103 for standard vendor invoices below USD 100,000; Commercial Letters of Credit (documentary credit) are appropriate only where the transaction has a goods or equipment component, which is not typical for executive search, so the primary rails are bank e-banking transfers with full underlying documentation. Every wire must be tagged with the SBV statistical purpose code set under Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN; relevant codes include code 16 for professional, management and consulting services, code 22 for other business services, code 09 for remittance of profits and dividends, and code 02 for royalties and license fees. The LLC must apply Foreign Contractor Tax withholding (1 percent VAT plus 5 percent CIT on gross consideration, unless a reduced rate or exemption sits in the applicable Double Tax Agreement) when paying non-resident service providers, and must obtain the foreign vendor's tax identification number or residence certificate where DTA relief is claimed; the payment file must combine the commercial contract, foreign invoice, FCT declaration, and proof of filing with the tax office. Wires from USD 50,000 and above will be detained by the bank's transaction monitoring unit until the full set is produced; therefore, the treasury should schedule vendor payments weekly and pre-upload supporting PDFs to the e-banking portal. Management fees to the offshore parent must satisfy the arm's length condition and be included in the country-by-country transfer pricing documentation; undocumented management fees are frequently reclassified as dividends, triggering withholding tax and penalties. Profit remittance follows the post-audit procedure: audited annual financial statements, tax finalization, constitution of statutory reserves, submission of the profit-remittance declaration, and a single MT103 to the shareholder account after the bank's legal review.Outright deliverable VND/USD and VND/EUR currency forwards for a tenor of up to 365 days, executed with an SBV-licensed bank under Circular No. 28/2016/TT-NHNN as amended by Circular No. 15/2023/TT-NHNN, subject to a genuine and documented underlying trade, service, investment or loan exposure; non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not an authorized onshore hedging instrument for Vietnamese corporate clients.VND/USD and VND/EUR currency swaps permitted under the same SBV circular framework, enabling conversion of a retained foreign-currency receivable into VND while preserving future conversion rights, provided the notional does not exceed the verified underlying receivables or payables.FX options (put and call structures on VND against USD/EUR) offered by licensed banks as over-the-counter contracts, only with a partially or fully drawn underlying transaction and with option premium and strike values recorded in the bank's derivatives ledger; the exercise must be settled on a deliverable basis.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps for medium-term offshore shareholder loans that have been properly registered with the SBV under Circular No. 12/2022/TT-NHNN, enabling the LLC to swap foreign-currency debt service into VND cash-flow exposure while remaining within the registered facility ceiling.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: CONDITIONAL with LOW reliance for primary clearing. Wise (Wise Asia-Pacific Pte. Ltd. and its local partner arrangements), Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar offshore digital EMIs are not licensed by the SBV as credit institutions or intermediate payment service providers and therefore cannot lawfully open VND-denominated accounts or independently settle payments into the domestic payment system. For the VN_LLC executive-search treasury, Wise B2B is viable only as an ancillary collection rail for small inbound retainer wires from Western clients where the client lacks SWIFT capabilities; funds must be routed to the licensee with an underlying service contract, and the collected foreign currency must be repatriated to the LLC's SBV-licensed VND or foreign-currency account within the 90-day window. Outbound uses of Wise for vendor or payroll payments from within Vietnam are not viable because the SBV requires cross-border outbound payment orders to be executed by licensed banks after know-your-transaction screening; a non-licensed EMI cannot provide the locally admissible FX invoice evidence and would create an unlawful channel. Airwallex is rated Medium for collection convenience but remains legally constrained to bank-partner rails. Legal risks include: operating an unauthorized payment service (administrative penalties under Decree 88/2019/ND-CP), inability to satisfy Vietnamese tax e-invoice mapping, and SBV foreign-currency control violations. Therefore the treasury design places Wise B2B strictly in a vendor-collection overflow layer, never as the statutory capital, tax payment, or primary payroll rail.
