Formation / constitutional filings

The IRC authorizes the foreign-invested project and the ERC constitutes the legal incorporation instrument of the LLC.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tong Cuc Thue) under the Ministry of Finance of Viet Nam; national e-tax portal: https://etax.gdt.gov.vn; enterprise registration and tax code issuance are integrated through the National Business Registration Portal: https://www.dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested limited liability company must obtain a Vietnamese tax identification number (Ma So Thue) automatically upon enterprise registration. Procedure: (1) obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment or an industrial-zone/export-processing-zone authority if the SPAC holds a conditional investment license; (2) file the enterprise registration dossier through the National Business Registration Portal to obtain the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC); (3) the tax code is issued simultaneously with the ERC and is also the enterprise code; (4) if the LLC also has foreign lender accounts, escrow/trust accounts or cross-border financing activities, a separate withholding tax registration for the account may be completed with the local tax office through e-tax; (5) register the authorized legal representative's electronic signature and bank account for e-tax filing/payment; (6) for VAT, the entity must register in the credit method and declare the tax period through the Tax Management System (TMS) within the statutory timelines.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The Vietnamese LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer taxed on worldwide income at the standard CIT rate of 20%. Territorial scope: resident entities are subject to CIT on Vietnam-source income and foreign-source income, with a foreign tax credit for taxes paid abroad. Tax-exempt zone eligibility: preferential zones include export processing zones (EPZs), industrial zones, hi-tech zones, and economically disadvantaged regions; eligibility for tax holidays, reduced rates (10% for 15 years, etc.), and import-duty exemptions may apply if the SPAC obtains an investment incentive certificate, but a pure SOAC holding entity generally has no manufacturing activity and must verify its incentive eligibility with the licensing authority.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not offer a U.S.-style check-the-box pass-through election; all LLCs are automatically entity-level CIT taxpayers. Available statutory elections include: (1) VAT calculation method election between the credit (deduction) method and the direct method, subject to conditions on revenue and accounting systems; (2) CIT incentive election via the investment registration process, selecting the applicable tax holiday/reduction regime; (3) five-year CIT loss carryforward election for offset against future profits; (4) foreign tax credit election for foreign-source income; (5) election to apply the Vietnamese transfer-pricing documentation of Decree 132/2020/ND-CP on an arm's-length basis; and (6) related-party interest expense limitation elections subject to thin-capitalization ratio. Hybrid mismatch elections generally are not recognized; a foreign investor's home-country classification of the LLC does not change its Vietnamese tax status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 1 billion annual turnover for VAT registration of domestic production/trading activities. However, a foreign-invested LLC must register for VAT immediately upon enterprise registration, regardless of the threshold. In addition, cross-border e-commerce/digital service providers and foreign contractors performing services in Viet Nam are subject to VAT declaration through the foreign contractor tax regime (FCT), often without the VND 1 billion threshold. An SPAC LLC with no Vietnamese turnover is still required to maintain VAT registration while tax-exempt/zero-rated transactions are declared.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods are eligible for 0% VAT if they are physically exported outside Viet Nam and the export contract/customs declaration is consistent. Exported services also qualify when the service is consumed outside Viet Nam by a foreign customer, including offshore advisory, financial, or SPAC-related professional services, provided the service recipient has no permanent establishment in Viet Nam for the consumed service. Additional criteria: the export must be supported by a written contract, customs export declaration (for goods), foreign-currency remittance proof, and invoices; goods sold to duty-free shops or into export processing enterprises (EPEs) are also treated as export transactions for 0% VAT. The SPAC must avoid supplying domestic services to Vietnamese residential or local operating companies, as those services would be subject to 10% VAT and not zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Goods Declaration) with cancellation/clearance status
  • Bill of Lading / Airwaybill evidencing physical export
  • Commercial invoice and signed sales/export contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit statement evidencing foreign-currency payment into a Vietnamese account
  • Electronic VAT invoice (export invoice) containing the 0% VAT rate
  • Packing list and certificate of origin (if required by the transaction)
  • Customs authority written confirmation of actual export, including EPE export liquidation reports where applicable

Viet Nam mandates 100% electronic invoicing since July 2022 for all enterprises, including foreign-invested LLCs. Invoices must be in XML format through the General Department of Taxation's e-invoice system. Real-time clearance applies mainly to sales to consumers at retail/POS: the invoice data must be transmitted to the tax authority's customer-facing system (TCTViet) via API before delivery of goods or completion of service, and a verification code (digital stamp) is required. Business-to-business transactions are subject to e-invoice transmission after issuance within 24 hours unless an offline transmission code is granted. The SPAC LLC must use an approved electronic invoice provider or the tax authority's free system; no paper invoices are permissible.

False zero-rating or fictitious export claims are treated as VAT fraud and tax evasion. The tax authority may retrospectively claw back the 0% rate, impose 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount under the Tax Administration Law, charge overdue interest at 0.03% per day, and apply a fraud penalty up to VND 200 million for administrative violation. For criminal fraud, the Penal Code imposes imprisonment up to 3 years for evading VND 100 million or more, extending to 10 years for large-scale cross-border schemes. Exported goods not actually remitted within the FX deadline trigger a forced exchange-rate conversion and potential foreign-exchange administrative fine, and the VAT refund is withdrawn if the inward remittance certificate is later found invalid.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vietnam maintains current-account convertibility for genuine trade in goods and services, including profit and dividend remittances, subject to documentation, tax clearance, and payment processing through an authorized foreign-exchange bank. Capital-account transactions are strictly controlled: an SPAC's inbound subscribed capital, escrowed proceeds, intra-company loans, and any downstream M&A disbursements must be routed through a registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened at a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam. Profit remittance requires an audited annual financial statement, completion of corporate income tax finalization, and issuance by the tax authority of a confirmation of completed tax obligations. Convertibility of Vietnamese dong on the capital account is not free; offshore derivative transactions referencing VND, including offshore NDFs, are prohibited outside SBV-regulated onshore channels. For a blank-check SPAC with no operating revenue, the operative hurdle is not profit remittance but the documented registration and filing requirements under the investment registration certificate (IRC) and the foreign-exchange regulations governing direct investment capital flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the current SBV regime, there is no mandatory conversion ratio for genuine export proceeds; an exporting entity may lawfully retain all export receipts in a foreign-currency account at an authorized bank in Vietnam. Export proceeds must be received into Vietnam in accordance with the payment terms of the underlying export contract, and where no shorter term is contractually fixed, are expected to be repatriated no later than 90 days from the date of shipment or from the due date established by commercial documents, as applicable. The exporter remains subject to customary documentary verification, SBV reporting, and prompt conversion at eligible market rates if it chooses to transfer to VND. Non-repatriation beyond the permitted period triggers administrative penalties, customs-consignment disputes, and foreign-exchange compliance sanctions under SBV guidelines.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) - Foreign Exchange Management Department (FEMD), operating pursuant to the Law on the State Bank of Vietnam 2010, the Foreign Exchange Ordinance No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 (as amended by Ordinance No. 06/2013/UBTVQH13), and Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN, as amended.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low - legally constrained. Offshore digital EMIs, electronic banking platforms, and neobanks are not licensed as credit institutions in Vietnam, cannot hold VND-denominated accounts on behalf of a Vietnamese entity, and cannot act as custodians of a regulated Direct Investment Capital Account. Such platforms may be used only as ancillary treasury-tracking or multi-currency aggregation tools outside of Vietnam, while all lawful clearing and escrow must be executed through a locally licensed commercial bank or foreign bank branch authorized by the SBV. Use of an offshore EMI as the named settlement bank for inbound SPAC subscription proceeds is not compliant with Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN and will be rejected by Vietnamese banking counterparties.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

SPAC/blank-check corporate nature with no tangible operating business, no revenue, and no identified acquisition target at onboarding

Root cause: SPAC/blank-check corporate nature with no tangible operating business, no revenue, and no identified acquisition target at onboarding

Defense evidence: Executive board resolution establishing the SPAC investment mandate; duly executed escrow agreement; approved private-placement memorandum or investment term sheet; formal letter from counsel describing the legal structure and the purpose of the bank relationship; detailed cash-flow forecast for the escrow account; corporate bylaws and certificate of incorporation with Vietnamese notarized translation.
Opaque, multi-layered overseas ownership and beneficial ownership chain

Root cause: Opaque, multi-layered overseas ownership and beneficial ownership chain

Defense evidence: SBV-compliant UBO declaration identifying all natural-person beneficial owners of 25% or greater; certified and legalized passport extracts and address proofs of all UBOs; full corporate-ownership chart through every intermediate holding entity, supported by current registry extracts and articles of incorporation from each jurisdiction; external legal opinion confirming no bearer shares or nominee ambiguity.
Large inbound wires from diverse offshore investors with inconsistent sources of wealth and no auditable trail

Root cause: Large inbound wires from diverse offshore investors with inconsistent sources of wealth and no auditable trail

Defense evidence: Subscription agreements signed by each investor; investor bank statements or letter of commitment issued by the remitting financial institution; source-of-wealth certification for each investor meeting the VN $100,000/USD screening threshold; sanction-screening reports; tax identification numbers of all natural or legal subscribers; anti-money laundering declarations in the bank's prescribed form.
Proposed inbound transfer from a high-risk jurisdiction, shell-bank arrangement, or FATF-monitored country

Root cause: Proposed inbound transfer from a high-risk jurisdiction, shell-bank arrangement, or FATF-monitored country

Defense evidence: Written rationale for the jurisdiction's role in the corporate structure; negative sanctions and PEP-screening results; evidence of commercial purpose from the acquisition timeline; correspondent-bank reference from the originating regulated institution; legal analysis confirming that no designated person is a direct or indirect beneficial owner; enhanced due diligence undertaking to disclose all subsequent material changes.
Unclear future deployment of escrowed public-market proceeds; heightened exposure to latent money-laundering through future acquisition payments

Root cause: Unclear future deployment of escrowed public-market proceeds; heightened exposure to latent money-laundering through future acquisition payments

Defense evidence: IPO/SPAC placement agreement and prospectus summary; final escrow release conditions; board-approved acquisition policy including geographic and sector exclusions; AML/CFT compliance manual, sanctions policy, and client-risk classification policy of the SPAC; updated financial crime risk assessment; commitment to provide transaction-level reverse lookbacks and UBO refresh on counterparties at each drawdown.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound SPAC subscription and escrow proceeds must be routed by SWIFT MT103 (or equivalent electronic transfer) to the legal entity's registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank. The beneficiary must be the VN_LLC's full registered name and DICA number; the remittance instruction must contain, in field 70/72, the investment registration certificate reference, the direct-investment capital contribution purpose code, and the subscribing investor's legal name. The originating bank must pass the source of funds through to the Vietnamese bank's AML/CFT screening. The DICA is automatically segregated for capital accounting; proceeds must be matched against the approved capital contribution schedule and the foreign exchange registration granted by the SBV in connection with the IRC. No transfer may be netted or commingled with operational VND balances unless the bank has issued a formal conversion receipt.For lawful operating expenditures and eventual acquisition target payments, the SPAC may instruct disbursements from its operational or DICA-linked account through SWIFT MT103 for standard cross-border transfers, MT202 COV for onward clearing, or a documentary commercial letter of credit (LC) where the bank issues a sight or usance LC against a generated commercial invoice. The bank will require underlying contracts, target-company purchase agreements, professional-fee invoices, or due-diligence support before each outbound wire. Payment to overseas target accounts must state the correspondent relationship in field 72, and the overall treasury rule is to route all outgoing trade and M&A payments through the same onshore authorized bank to preserve audit completeness and SBV reporting consistency.SBV-regulated onshore FX forward transaction with an authorized commercial bank, tenor matched to a documented commercial trade obligation or acquisition payment dateFX swap (spot sale/purchase of foreign currency combined with simultaneous forward purchase/sale) executed with a licensed onshore credit institutionCross-currency swap (FX swap over a longer tenor) used only where there is a lawful underlying transaction such as cross-border acquisition financing or escrowed debtFX option (vanilla European or American option) purchased from an authorized bank where the underlying exposure is evidenced by invoice, LC, or signed share-purchase agreementOnshore permitted forward participation contracts and rate-smoothing arrangements under SBV Circular No. 02/2021/TT-NHNN and related guidance

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low - legally constrained. Offshore digital EMIs, electronic banking platforms, and neobanks are not licensed as credit institutions in Vietnam, cannot hold VND-denominated accounts on behalf of a Vietnamese entity, and cannot act as custodians of a regulated Direct Investment Capital Account. Such platforms may be used only as ancillary treasury-tracking or multi-currency aggregation tools outside of Vietnam, while all lawful clearing and escrow must be executed through a locally licensed commercial bank or foreign bank branch authorized by the SBV. Use of an offshore EMI as the named settlement bank for inbound SPAC subscription proceeds is not compliant with Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN and will be rejected by Vietnamese banking counterparties.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a Vietnamese limited liability company or joint-stock company to its corporate shareholder (resident or non-resident). However, a deemed profit distribution or interest paid to a foreign shareholder in a PIT treatment may be taxed at 5% – however, for corporate recipients under the CIT regime, dividends from Vietnamese entities are exempt from CIT and no dividend WHT applies under the Law on Corporate Income Tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual CIT finalization return is due no later than the 90th day from the end of the fiscal year (i.e., by March 31 for a calendar-year taxpayer). Quarterly provisional CIT declarations are due by the 30th day of the first month following the end of each quarterly tax period; advance quarterly payments must not be less than 80% of the annual CIT liability calculated at year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File thresholds: enterprises with related-party transactions exceeding 30 billion VND in value and gross revenue exceeding 1 trillion VND in the fiscal year, or total related-party transactions above 30 billion VND, must prepare TP documentation (Form 01, 02, 03 under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP). SPAC entities engaged in transactions with foreign shareholders, management fees, or intragroup financing must prepare documentation if thresholds are met; even below thresholds, a Country-by-Country Report is mandatory for groups with consolidated revenue of at least 6.3 trillion VND.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam applies economic substance principles through the beneficial ownership test in DTA claims and through General Anti-Avoidance Rules (GAAR) under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP and Article 5 of the Law on Tax Administration. For SPACs, substance is critical to avoid treaty shopping, to maintain tax residence, and to prevent recharacterization of service fees as dividends. Vietnam does not currently apply sector-specific Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) in the manner of UAE, but the GAAR and DTA abuse provisions achieve a substance requirement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Under-declaration of Income (Trốn thuế)

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty equal to 20% of the under-declared tax amount for ordinary errors; for intentional evasion, a penalty of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount plus late-payment interest. Violations above 100 million VND in evaded tax can trigger criminal prosecution under the Penal Code 2015 Article 200, with fines up to 4.5 billion VND or imprisonment up to 3 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives may be personally liable for tax debts of the enterprise if they intentionally fail to declare, debit accounts of the company, or dispose of assets; courts may order civil restitution of evaded taxes; criminal penalties extend to imprisonment for individuals; travel bans and exit suspensions are routinely imposed by Vietnamese tax authorities on legal representatives with outstanding tax debts.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation (Quản lý giá chuyển nhượng)

Statutory sanction: Re-determination of taxable income using the arm's length principle; penalty of 20% of the additional CIT assessed plus late-payment interest at 0.03%/day; administrative penalties for failure to file the TP form range from 4 million to 24 million VND; failure to provide documentation may lead the tax authority to apply the default profit mark-up method (2.5% to 5% mark-up on costs for distribution, 10% for manufacturing, 10% for services).

Director personal liability: Legal representatives are required to sign and certify TP documentation; if false documentation is proven, the tax authority may pursue responsible individuals with restitution claims and penalize them under tax administration sanctions; guarantees or personal liability may be sought for unpaid liabilities on dissolution or transfer of the business.
Shell Company / Non-operational SPAC without Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may reassess the entity as a sham, impose CIT on worldwide income without treaty protection, deny treaty benefits, impose fines for late filing, and revoke the Enterprise Registration Certificate; the Law on Enterprises permits forced dissolution and administrative fines up to 100 million VND for failure to report operations. Criminal liability for operating under a shell to commit financial fraud may apply under Penal Code Articles 174 (fraud) and 214.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for debts and tax liabilities if the shell is used to defraud creditors or tax authorities; the courts may pierce the corporate veil under Civil Code Article 90 and Enterprise Law; personal asset freeze, travel bans, and prohibition from leaving Vietnam apply to legal representatives of enterprises that fail to complete tax obligations before dissolution.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Violations in Escrow / Trust Fund Handling

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines under the Anti-Money Laundering Law 2022: from 30 million to 200 million VND for failure to report suspicious transactions; from 100 million to 300 million VND for serious license violations; the State Bank of Vietnam may suspend operations, revoke licenses, and institute criminal prosecution for money laundering under Penal Code Article 324, carrying imprisonment up to 15 years and fines up to 20 billion VND.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for AML compliance; they may be subject to criminal prosecution as accomplices if they knowingly allow the SPAC to launder proceeds; civil restitution of laundered funds and forfeiture of assets; travel bans and restrictions on departing the country may be imposed during investigation.
Withholding Tax and DTA Abuse / Dividend Payments to Tax-Haven Lenders

Statutory sanction: Sanction: liability for unpaid withholding tax, penalty of 20% of withheld tax not remitted, plus late-payment interest at 0.03%/day; administrative fine for failure to withhold from 1-2 times the tax amount; disallowance of interest expense exceeding the thin capitalization cap of 30% EBITDA; penalty for violating the conditions for investment loans.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding tax as withholding agents; criminal penalties up to 3 years' imprisonment for deliberate failure to collect or remit tax; travel bans and exit visas may be denied for the legal representative.
