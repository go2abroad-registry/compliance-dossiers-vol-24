Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue Commission (Customs & Inland Revenue Division), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; statutory registrations effected through licensed Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) agents and filed on the VFSC e-Registry portal (www.vfsc.vu) and the Customs & Inland Revenue portal (customs.gov.vu).

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate an International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] via a licensed VFSC registered agent (no minimum capital, no local resident director required); (2) apply for a Vanuatu Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the Vanuatu Revenue Commission under the Revenue Administration Act 2022, attaching certificate of incorporation, registered-agent letter, and due-diligence copies of director and beneficial owner passports (TIN issued ~5 business days); (3) obtain an ASYCUDA World importer/exporter code from Customs for export declarations; (4) apply for a municipal business licence only if a physical presence is established in Port Vila or Luganville (offshore ICs with zero local activity are exempt). No income tax or VAT registration exists because Vanuatu levies no direct tax and no national VAT.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — (1) Incorporate an International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] via a licensed VFSC registered agent (no minimum capital, no local resident director required); (2) apply for a Vanuatu Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the Vanuatu Revenue Commission under the Revenue Administration Act 2022, attaching certificate of incorporation, registered-agent letter, and due-diligence copies of director and beneficial owner passports (TIN issued ~5 business days); (3) obtain an ASYCUDA World importer/exporter code from Customs for export declarations; (4) apply for a municipal business licence only if a physical presence is established in Port Vila or Luganville (offshore ICs with zero local activity are exempt). No income tax or VAT registration exists because Vanuatu levies no direct tax and no national VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections are available because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax. Relevant statutory elections: (i) IC may elect tax-exempt non-resident status, obtaining an Exemption Certificate under the International Companies Act covering all income, capital gains, withholding, and stamp duties; (ii) election of the offshore/exempt business-licence category before the Port Vila or Luganville municipality; (iii) election under the Foreign Exchange Control Act to route foreign-currency settlements through licensed domestic banks or to maintain external foreign-currency accounts with central-bank approval.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT/GST system: None — Vanuatu operates without a national VAT, GST, or general sales-and-use tax; only municipal sales taxes (generally 5% in Port Vila and Luganville) apply to physical domestic retail consumption within municipal boundaries, and such taxes have no nexus over a non-resident IC exporting goods overseas.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Vatu (Vt): there is no national indirect-tax statute and therefore no turnover threshold for VAT/GST registration. The only in-country trigger is the municipal business-licence regime (first Vatu of local trading activity); 100% export sales settled in foreign currency are outside municipal sales-tax nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

De facto permanent 0% export treatment prevails by statutory design, as no VAT/GST is chargeable on exports from Vanuatu. To preserve this treatment for pre-packaged hotpot soup-base bricks (ISIC 4638), the exporter must: (i) ship the goods physically out of Vanuatu customs territory under a lodged ASYCUDA World export entry; (ii) hold a valid IC registration, TIN, and customs exporter code; (iii) sell to a non-resident buyer with title and risk transferring offshore; (iv) settle in freely convertible foreign currency via SWIFT TT or Letter of Credit; and (v) confine all activities to offshore operations so no municipal sales-tax nexus arises.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry) duly processed and cleared
  • Commercial Invoice and packing list (HS headings 2103.90 / 2104.10, seasoning and soup preparations)
  • Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill, or Airway Bill evidencing consignment to an overseas port
  • Certificate of Origin (only where required by the buyer's Letter of Credit)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from the collecting Vanuatu bank, or a foreign-exchange transaction report, evidencing receipt of proceeds within the statutory period

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-clearance, API, or digital-stamping mandate exists for VAT purposes in Vanuatu. Commercial invoices are self-issued; electronic control is limited to ASYCUDA World customs declarations, export-entry processing, and the TIN-based filing framework under the Revenue Administration Act 2022.

Fraudulent export zero-rating or over-invoicing attracts: (i) customs penalties under the Customs Act [Cap 145] of 100%–300% of the under-declared value; (ii) monetary penalties and imprisonment sanctions under the Revenue Administration Act 2022 for knowingly providing false information to the Vanuatu Revenue Commission; (iii) retrospective revocation of the IC's tax-exemption certificate and deregistration by the VFSC; (iv) exchange-control enforcement under the Foreign Exchange Control Act [Cap 67], including fines, restriction of future forex facilities, and compelled repatriation where proceeds are not received within 180 days. Directors and beneficial owners may be pursued jointly and severally.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

VUV is freely convertible for current account and capital account transactions on a de facto basis. Vanuatu maintains no central bank prior-approval regime for profit remittances by non-resident controlled International Companies. Banks require board resolutions, underlying trade contracts, and source-of-funds evidence for each outward remittance to satisfy domestic AML/CFT obligations. Corporate tax exemption does not create any remittance restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or repatriation timeline is imposed on export proceeds in Vanuatu. Exporters may keep foreign currency receipts in offshore or onshore foreign-currency accounts. For prudential recordkeeping, banks may require export declarations and freight documents within 30 to 90 days, but this is a customer due diligence measure, not an exchange control surrender rule.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), exercising prudential and foreign exchange administration under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 322]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) licenses and supervises International Companies and payment service providers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to Medium. Offshore digital EMI providers are not licensed by RBV to hold settlement accounts in Vanuatu unless they obtain a banking or payment-services license under the Financial Institutions Act. A foreign fintech wallet may be used only as an auxiliary channel for e-commerce receipts; direct trade settlement through a non-resident EMI will trigger VFSC scrutiny and may be rejected by counterparty banks due to lack of a Vanuatu clearing relationship.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu's Income Tax Act and International Companies Act do not enact statutory transfer pricing rules and prescribe no Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country Report thresholds in VUV. The IC is nonetheless expected to keep contemporaneous records evidencing arm's-length pricing in purchase and resale transactions.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not apply an Economic Substance Regulations framework equivalent to those of the Gulf Cooperation Council states or the OECD Harmful Tax Practices regime as binding on ordinary wholesale International Companies. However, the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] mandates a physical registered office in Vanuatu, an appointed licensed registered agent, and maintenance of accounting records sufficient to explain all transactions. For the export distribution of pre-packaged hotpot soup base, the accepted substance profile requires real purchase contracts, inventory risk, logistics coordination, and wholesale invoicing from the IC to unaffiliated or affiliated distributors, rather than a bare invoice-passing shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Customs Declaration Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Customs Act [Cap. 145] and the Value Added Tax Act No. 6 of 2015, the offender is liable upon conviction to fines of up to VUV 1,000,000, forfeiture of the exported goods, imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years, and assessment of unpaid duty and VAT at triple the evaded amount. Additionally, the VFSC may strike the IC off for conducting illegal trading.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or direct fraudulent invoicing face personal criminal prosecution, fines, and imprisonment. Civil courts may pierce the corporate veil and order restitution of the evaded customs and tax amounts against a director's personal assets; serious cases may trigger director travel bans and extradition requests from the affected treaty partner.
Shell Company Misrepresentation and Bank Fraud

Statutory sanction: The International Companies Act [Cap. 222] empowers the VFSC to issue compliance directions, impose remedial fines, strike the IC off, and require the terminated registered agent to destroy the certificate of incorporation. Misrepresentation to a financial institution may constitute obtaining a banking facility by deception, punishable by fines and imprisonment under the Penal Code [Cap. 112].

Director personal liability: Directors who sign facility letters, KYC declarations, or trade finance undertakings containing false substance statements are personally liable under common law fraud and breach of fiduciary duty. Remedies include rescission of financing agreements, personal restitution of the lender's losses, disqualification as a director, and travel restrictions pending civil or criminal process.
Money Laundering and AML-CFT Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 41 of 2016, money laundering is punishable by imprisonment for up to 25 years and fines of up to VUV 50,000,000. The VFSC may suspend or revoke the IC registration, while the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze assets pending investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are treated as reporting persons and may incur personal criminal liability regardless of the corporate form. Asset forfeiture orders, travel bans, and personal imprisonment apply where the director arranged or deliberately ignored suspicious settlement patterns; nominee directors are equally liable where they “allow” the IC to be used for money laundering.
