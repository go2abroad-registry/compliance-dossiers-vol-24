Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (official portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.vu)

E-government filing portal

International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt from Vanuatu national income tax; no income tax TIN or VAT ID is issued. A foreign IC is not required to register with Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue unless it undertakes physical import or export clearance, in which case a one-time Customs importer/exporter registration code is obtained via the ASYCUDA World portal. For pure offshore film licensing and royalty receipts, no statutory TIN procurement is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt from Vanuatu national income tax; no income tax TIN or VAT ID is issued. A foreign IC is not required to register with Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue unless it undertakes physical import or export clearance, in which case a one-time Customs importer/exporter registration code is obtained via the ASYCUDA World portal. For pure offshore film licensing and royalty receipts, no statutory TIN procurement is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist. Vanuatu imposes no national income tax and does not distinguish between transparent and entity-level taxed structures; the International Companies Act (Cap. 222) grants the IC an unconditional statutory exemption from all Vanuatu taxes, with no election required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No broad-based VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation is limited to import customs duties, excise duties on specified goods, and border fees

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Vanuatu levies no VAT/GST; there is no turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold. Customs registration is event-based, triggered per import/export transaction regardless of value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the traditional VAT zero-rating sense because Vanuatu does not impose VAT/GST on exports. Export goods are generally free of export duty except certain prescribed agricultural commodities subject to specific export duties. Cross-border film licensing and royalty services are treated as outside the scope of Vanuatu indirect taxation and do not require a zero-rating claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form CED) lodged via ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or other transport document
  • Commercial Invoice and executed Film Licensing Agreement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank credit confirmation evidencing royalty repatriation

No e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate applies. Customs declarations and clearance are processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window system; commercial invoices and supporting documents are submitted periodically for risk-based customs verification, not in real time.

Because no VAT zero-rating refund regime exists, no export refund clawback exposure arises. For physical goods, incorrect export declarations or evasion of export duties may be penalised under the Customs Act (Cap. 257), including monetary penalties, seizure of goods, and liability for underpaid duties. Mischaracterisation of dutiable exports as licensing services would not trigger indirect tax recovery, as the transactions remain outside the scope of Vanuatu indirect taxation; however, false declarations to customs authorities can attract criminal sanctions and revocation of customs clearance privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates an open and broadly non-restrictive foreign exchange environment for non-resident International Companies. The Vatu (VUV) is a managed-float currency; current-account income, including film licensing royalties, distribution advances and residual payments, may be credited directly to a foreign-currency account without prior exchange-control approval. Capital-account inflows and outflows, including initial equity injections, shareholder loans, intercompany debt repayments and liquidation distributions, are likewise free of prior licensing or conversion requirements, subject only to standard bank-level AML/KYC verification, correspondent-bank sanctions screening and the provision of a valid economic and documentary chain for the transaction. Profit remittance is not subject to Vanuatu income tax, withholding tax or an advance exchange permit; the practical hurdles are the compliance standards of the receiving correspondent banks and the requirement to demonstrate a genuine copyright-licensing business behind the VU_IC structure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio applies to the VU_IC under current Reserve Bank of Vanuatu practice. Film-licensing proceeds may be retained in any permitted foreign currency and may remain offshore or in a Vanuatu foreign-currency account without conversion into VUV. There is no specified statutory timeline for repatriation, and no requirement that a fixed percentage of proceeds be converted into local currency. The only bank-level requirement is that the underlying licensing contract and source of funds be documented at the time of credit so that the receipt can be classified correctly for AML/CFT reporting.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), Port Vila; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately supervises International Companies and corporate service providers. No distinct foreign exchange administration department exists; exchange-control powers are exercised by the RBV through authorised dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as the primary treasury home, Medium as a secondary collection rail. Vanuatu has no dedicated e-money institution or payment institution licensing regime, and offshore digital EMIs are not authorised banks. An offshore EMI may lawfully collect and route film-licensing royalties only if it is regulated in its home jurisdiction, does not accept Vanuatu-resident deposits, and settles inwards through a direct SWIFT MT103 to a resident tier-1 bank within the same settlement cycle. The IC should not allow the EMI to be the final custodian of treasury funds, because local substance, correspondent access and audit clarity require a VFSC-compliant bank account in Vanuatu. Any use of an EMI should be documented as a collection agency relationship, with full reconciliation and direct fallback to the resident account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital should be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own licensed bank account directly to the VU_IC's cleared USD account. The remittance information field should state: 'Equity subscription into VU_IC for authorised share capital; ISIC 5911 independent film licensing'. Vanuatu has no national statutory purpose-code registry; banks rely on narrative purpose text and will map the receipt internally to a capital-contribution category; if the receiving bank accepts ISO 20022 purpose codes, use the code corresponding to an intra-group capital injection or 'ANBC' as accepted by the specific institution. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no payroll or social security tax on the profits of an International Company registered under the International Companies Act 2013.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no withholding tax, no dividend tax and no remittance tax on distributions declared by an International Company to its non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — Vanuatu has not enacted transfer-pricing legislation, and no VUV-denominated Local File, Master File or Country-by-Country Report threshold applies to International Companies. As a BEPS Inclusive Framework member, Vanuatu maintains policy commitments against harmful tax practices but currently imposes no domestic mandatory TP documentation on ICs.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance Act, applicable to International Companies and amended most recently in 2021-2022, requires a relevant-activity IC to demonstrate in Vanuatu: (i) direction and management through board meetings held at the registered office; (ii) adequate physical premises and employees; (iii) operating expenditure proportionate to income; and (iv) core-IP management where IP income is derived. An independent film-licensing IC falls within the IP-holding and distribution/service-centre categories; annual economic-substance notification and filing with the VFSC are mandatory. The reduced substance regime applies only to pure equity-holding entities, not to active film-royalty licensees.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Exemption Abuse / Unauthorized Domestic Business

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the exemption certificate and forfeiture of tax-exempt status, administrative fines and penalty surcharges under the ICA Regulations, strike-off by the Registrar, and recovery of uncollected VAT, stamp duty and other indirect taxes on the unlawful domestic transactions.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced when directors knowingly authorize local activity; directors become jointly and severally liable to defrauded creditors and the Vanuatu revenue authority, may be disqualified from managing any Vanuatu company, and may be subjected to a travel restraint pending criminal investigation.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties for ESR non-compliance and false substance declarations; repeat failures attract financial penalties scaled to the IC's gross film-royalty income, corrective orders and strike-off. Adverse findings may be exchanged with OECD and EU authorities under information-sharing instruments.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who certify false substance filings face statutory fines and imprisonment, personal restitution for creditor losses, loss of professional licensing in Vanuatu, and exposure to re-characterization of the IC as a sham by foreign tax authorities, thereby triggering source-country tax, interest and penalties.
Money Laundering / AML-CFT Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Criminal sanctions under the Vanuatu Proceeds of Crime Act and AML/CFT framework: confiscation of laundered film-revenue assets, pecuniary penalties against the corporate entity, and VFSC regulatory enforcement for failure to maintain the statutory AML compliance program.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly direct sham licensing structures face imprisonment for money laundering, travel bans and international asset-freezing orders obtained by treaty partners, joint-and-several civil restitution for the laundered amounts, and disqualification from acting as directors of any VFSC-regulated entity.
