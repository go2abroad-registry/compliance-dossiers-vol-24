Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; official portal: Vanuatu Government e-Government Portal (www.gov.vu)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities are not required to obtain a statutory tax identification number (TIN) for income tax purposes because Vanuatu does not impose income tax. International Companies are statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes under the International Companies Act. For customs purposes, an entity intending to export goods must apply for a Customs Client Code through ASYCUDA World by submitting the certificate of incorporation, registered agent certificate, board resolution, and valid identification of the company officers.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Foreign entities are not required to obtain a statutory tax identification number (TIN) for income tax purposes because Vanuatu does not impose income tax. International Companies are statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes under the International Companies Act. For customs purposes, an entity intending to export goods must apply for a Customs Client Code through ASYCUDA World by submitting the certificate of incorporation, registered agent certificate, board resolution, and valid identification of the company officers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu has no income tax and International Companies are exempt from all domestic taxes on offshore business activities. No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available or necessary. An International Company may elect to redomicile or convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act, but that is a commercial law election with no direct tax consequences.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: Vanuatu does not levy VAT or GST, and therefore has no zero-rating concept. Exports of goods, including recycled scrap copper wire, are generally not subject to any Vanuatu turnover tax or export duty, provided the shipment is properly declared through customs and physically departs Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Scrap Metal Export Permit (where required by Vanuatu Department of Environmental Protection)

No e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance regime. Customs export declarations must be filed electronically through the ASYCUDA World system; no digital stamping requirement applies.

No VAT/export zero-rating clawback mechanism exists due to the absence of VAT. However, false or fraudulent customs declarations are subject to substantial monetary penalties, seizure and forfeiture of goods, and potential imprisonment under the Vanuatu Customs Act. Violations of environmental and waste-export permit conditions may result in additional administrative fines and suspension or revocation of the export licence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains current-account convertibility consistent with IMF membership practice; the VUV is managed against a trade-weighted AUD/EUR/NZD/USD basket, and trade-based settlements for ISIC 4669 scrap-copper imports and exports flow through RBV-licensed authorized dealers without prior exchange-control approval when supported by the standard documentary set. Capital-account entries, including inbound equity subscriptions, inter-company loans, and non-trade outward remittances, require an RBV exchange-control application and settlement through an authorized dealer. Profit and dividend remittance from a tax-exempt International Company to non-resident shareholders is permitted after (i) RBV exchange-control filing, (ii) source-of-funds certification evidencing completed physical consignments of copper scrap, and (iii) confirmation from the authorized dealer that no Vanuatu income tax is attributable to the IC. Residual friction resides in the correspondent-banking layer rather than in statute: banks apply enhanced scrutiny to Pacific-linked metal flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Act [Cap 113] imposes a statutory repatriation duty: all proceeds from the IC's export of recycled copper scrap must be received in Vanuatu through an authorized dealer within 180 days of the bill-of-lading date. No fixed surrender ratio or forced conversion into VUV is applied; proceeds may be retained in USD or AUD in the IC's foreign-currency account if earmarked for the next consignment cycle, but cumulative conversions above VUV-equivalent 10 million per month must be reported to the RBV. The IC must retain the export permit, customs declaration, scale or weighbridge tally, and assay certificate for seven years; failure to repatriate or settle through an authorized dealer exposes directors to penalty under the Exchange Control Act and may trigger VFSC deregistration of the IC.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), the central monetary authority and exchange-control administrator acting under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 113] and the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) registers and licenses the International Company, and the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (VFIU) receives suspicious-transaction reports for the scrap-copper commodity corridor.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW. No domestic EMI or digital-banking sandbox exists in Vanuatu, and RBV does not license pass-through money-service businesses for physical commodity settlement. Offshore EMIs (UK FCA e-money institutions, EU EMIs, Australian neobanks) are constrained by their own CDD policies from onboarding a Pacific IC whose principal activity is high-risk scrap-metal trading, and the correspondent-de-risking climate blocks EMI clearing into Port Vila. An EMI may be retained only as a secondary, low-value disbursement rail after the anchor bank account is live; it must never receive export proceeds, which must route through the RBV-authorized dealer to satisfy exchange-control repatriation.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax; International Companies (ICs) under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] are statutorily exempt from all national income taxation.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu does not levy any withholding tax on dividends, whether paid to residents or non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing is due within 30 days after each anniversary of incorporation; a corporate income tax return has no deadline because no such return is required.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Vanuatu threshold: Vanuatu has no mandatory Local File or Master File regime. Functional compliance benchmark: prepare OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation once related-party cross-border scrap copper turnover exceeds USD 1,000,000, in order to withstand audits in the scrap-origin jurisdiction and the shareholder's residence jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: ESR applies through the International Companies (Economic Substance) (Amendment) Act 2019 and the annual VFSC Economic Substance Return. An IC claiming Vanuatu tax residence must demonstrate substantial activity, including procurement, LC financing, and shipping decisions, within Vanuatu. A pure offshore physical consignment business more prudently files the non-resident ESR exemption declaration stating tax residence in the shareholder's jurisdiction; failure to evidence substance heightens EU blacklist scrutiny, bank de-risking, and group-level CFC assessments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Tax Evasion / CFC Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Full corporate income tax recomputation of all IC profits in the residence jurisdiction at the applicable headline rate, plus evasion penalties of 100% to 300%, interest surcharges, and denial of any foreign-tax credit because the undisclosed income was never declared.

Director personal liability: Personal liability of the controlling shareholder-director for civil restitution of diverted profits, cross-border travel bans pending criminal inquiry, and imprisonment of up to 10 years for fraudulent tax evasion.
Sham Substance / Shell-Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Refusal of the VFSC annual return, default penalties, and compulsory strike-off of the IC; in counterparty jurisdictions the entity is treated as a sham, and deductions, treaty-like benefits, and LC financing are denied on a look-through basis.

Director personal liability: Breach of director duties under the IC Act; court-ordered personal contribution and disgorgement of profits, seizure of personal assets, and director disqualification for up to 15 years.
Trade-Based Money Laundering / AML-CTF Breach

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu AML/CTF Act No. 16 of 2016 (as amended): corporate fines up to VT 100 million, confiscation and forfeiture of the scrap consignments, freezing orders on bank accounts, and revocation of any VFSC license held by related service providers; foreign jurisdictions may impose parallel asset forfeiture and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: Directors acting with willful blindness face imprisonment of up to 20 years, personal fines, travel bans, and civil restitution to defrauded LC issuing banks.
False Filings / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Default fines and daily surcharges under the IC Act, deregistration, and referral to VFSC AML enforcement, which may revoke the resident agent's license and exchange information via CRS/FATCA channels.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability for false certification, fines and imprisonment of up to 5 years, and a statutory bar from serving as a director of any Vanuatu company.
