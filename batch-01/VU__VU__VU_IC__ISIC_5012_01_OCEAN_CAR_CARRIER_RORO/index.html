Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), the unified Department of Customs and Inland Revenue within the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official registration and e-services portal: https://customs.gov.vu. Licensing of International Companies is administered separately by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu levies no income tax, capital gains tax, or wealth tax on any person, so no statutory income-tax TIN is issued. A foreign entity that imports, supplies, or intends to supply taxable goods or services in Vanuatu must register for VAT with the VRC by submitting the official VAT Registration Application Form together with certified constitutional documents, proof of local registered address, list of directors and principal officers, bank references, and a projected turnover declaration. Upon approval, the VRC issues a VAT Registration Number. An International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes and duties and is prohibited from trading with Vanuatu residents; therefore it does not complete this SOP, and its VFSC company registration number serves as the sole regulatory identifier.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Vanuatu levies no income tax, capital gains tax, or wealth tax on any person, so no statutory income-tax TIN is issued. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, fiscal-transparent, or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Vanuatu law because no income-tax regime is in force. Available statutory elections are limited to: (i) voluntary VAT registration under section 7 of the VAT Act No. 16 of 1998 for suppliers whose taxable turnover is below the VT 2,000,000 compulsory threshold; and (ii) conversion or re-domiciliation of an International Company as an onshore company under the Companies Act, which terminates the IC's blanket tax exemption and subjects the entity to local VAT and duties on Vanuatu-source activities. An IC may also elect, in exceptional circumstances, to hold a VAT registration solely to claim zero-rating on eligible export services, although such registration is not generally required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 2,000,000 (approximately USD 16,700) of taxable supplies per annum; registration is mandatory as soon as taxable turnover for any 12-month period has exceeded, or is reasonably expected to exceed, this amount pursuant to sections 6 and 7 of the VAT Act No. 16 of 1998.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for the 0% rate where the goods are physically removed from Vanuatu within the prescribed period and the exporter retains corroborative customs evidence. Services, including deep-sea Ro-Ro vehicle transport between ports located entirely outside Vanuatu, qualify for zero-rating where the supplier belongs in Vanuatu for VAT purposes and the recipient belongs in a foreign country, provided the services are not effectively used or consumed in Vanuatu. Incidental port handling, stevedoring, or terminal charges physically performed within Vanuatu waters or terminals remain standard-rated unless a specific statutory zero-rating or exemption applies. The taxpayer bears the burden of proving physical removal or foreign consumption; failure to retain the statutory export evidence invalidates the 0% rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration approved by Vanuatu Customs and validated at the port of departure
  • Bill of Lading (Ocean) evidencing carriage from a non-Vanuatu loading port to a non-Vanuatu discharge port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or SWIFT MT103 credit advice) proving receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list encoding the overseas consignee
  • Vessel departure certificate or cargo manifest countersigned by the shipping agent

None in force. Vanuatu has not implemented real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping. Registered taxpayers may issue paper or electronic VAT invoices, provided the invoice contains the prescribed particulars, including serial number, supplier VAT registration number, customer details, description of supply, taxable consideration, and VAT amount at the applicable rate. Invoices must be retained for VRC audit inspection.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating claims attract retrospective clawback of any input VAT recovered, default interest, and a civil penalty of 5% per month on the unpaid VAT. Criminal conviction for VAT evasion carries a fine of up to VT 500,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 2 years under the VAT Act. Knowingly false export declarations additionally trigger monetary penalties, forfeiture, and prosecution under the Customs Act. The VRC may reopen and reassess transactions for up to 7 years in cases of wilful default or fraud. Although Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control statute, failure to evidence receipt of export proceeds within the 180-day standard voids the zero-rating election and re-characterises the supply as taxable, exposing the taxpayer to full VAT clawback, penalties, and cancellation of the IC tax exemption if the entity has engaged in unauthorised local trade.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, and the Vatu (VUV) is a free-floating currency with full current-account convertibility. Non-resident International Companies may freely receive charter-party earnings, capital contributions and shareholder loans in USD, AUD, EUR or VUV without prior RBV exchange-control approval, subject only to documentary verification by the authorised dealer bank. Repatriation of profits, dividends and capital is unencumbered by any exchange-allocation mechanism, dividend-blocking rule or quantifiable quota; administration is exercised through balance-of-payments reporting by the receiving bank. Capital-account transactions of a resident character, including resident outward investment and resident foreign-currency borrowing, remain notifiable to the RBV and may require prior approval where the counterparty is a Vanuatu resident or the transaction utilises domestic credit. The absence of sub-jurisdictional fiscal or monetary distinctions in Vanuatu means a single national regime applies to all VU_IC accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender ratio, compulsory VUV conversion, or prescribed repatriation timeline for Ro-Ro vessel freight and charter proceeds. Export receipts must, however, be routed through a Vanuatu licensed authorised dealer bank, recorded for balance-of-payments purposes, and be traceable to the underlying ISIC 5012 activity. Foreign-currency retention on the operating account is expressly permitted. The supporting documentary baseline for each receipt is the charter party, the signed freight invoice or bill of lading, and the identifying vessel name and IMO number; retained funds may be deployed directly for bunkering, port disbursement, crew wages, and LC cover without separate surrender approval.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting together with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); exchange control is administered by RBV under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 68] through licensed authorised dealer banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW. Vanuatu maintains no offshore EMI licensing framework, and the Banking Act [Cap. 62] together with the AML/CFT Act 2020 requires any payment-service provider holding customer money for a VU_IC to hold a VFSC banking or financial-dealer licence. Offshore digital EMIs and BaaS aggregators (including Airwallex-class platforms) generally decline Vanuatu-domiciled International Companies as a direct consequence of the jurisdiction's FATF increased-monitoring status (from June 2024) and the lack of an EMI passporting arrangement. EMIs may at most be used as supplemental pay-in/pay-out gateways where a licensed domestic bank sponsors and retains settlement finality; all primary liquidity rails must terminate at a Vanuatu licensed authorised dealer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections are executed by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for high-value treasury transfers) from the parent shipowner to the VU_IC USD Operational or Capital Escrow Account at a licensed authorised dealer. The ISO 20022 purpose code must be 'EQTY' for paid-up equity with narrative 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - VU_IC OCEAN RORO (ISIC 5012)'; shareholder loans use purpose code 'LOAN' with reference to the executed intercompany loan agreement. The remitting bank must receive a board resolution authorising the escrow allocation, the executed share subscription instrument, the VFSC Foreign Investment approval (where the shareholder is a non-citizen), and the current beneficial-ownership extract. USD routing is performed through the bank's US correspondent leg; field 71A (details of charges) should elect OUR or SHA to ensure full-value arrival, and the receiving bank must tag the credit as non-resident IC capital under its exchange-control reporting obligation.The designated outgoing rails are Swift_LC and Wire_Transfer. Bunker supply payments are effected by MT103 directly to the supplier or via the mandated bunker trader under the bunker supply agreement. Port disbursement accounts are funded by MT103 to the ship agent with the PDA schedule attached; LC settlements, including MT700 issuance, MT742 reimbursement claims and MT202 cover, are processed under ICC UCP 600 and ISBP 745 for the acquisition of vessels, shipyard instalments and major equipment. Charter hire payments, crew payroll and towage fees are cleared by MT103 via the SWIFT GPI network; all VUV-denominated local payments settle through the RBV-operated national payment clearing system. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No transfer pricing legislation or documentation thresholds exist in Vanuatu (VUV-denominated thresholds: N/A); the compliance burden is limited to the annual economic substance declaration rather than arm's-length transfer pricing files

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2022 and the International Companies (Amendment) Act 2022 apply to ICs carrying on 'relevant activities', including shipping and maritime transport (ISIC 5012). A Ro-Ro car-carrier IC must demonstrate: (a) direction and management in Vanuatu; (b) adequate physical premises, full-time employees, and operating expenditure; and (c) performance of core income-generating activities (vessel chartering, freight contracting, cargo administration, invoicing) in Vanuatu. Annual substance declarations are filed with the VFSC; non-compliance attracts financial penalties and deregistration

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Offshore Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu: penalties for false declarations up to VT 10,000,000 and VFSC strike-off; foreign jurisdiction: re-assessment of concealed profits, interest, and penalties up to 100–200% of the tax evaded under CFC and general anti-avoidance rules

Director personal liability: Directors may be held jointly and severally liable for the company's foreign tax debts; travel bans, mutual-assistance asset-freeze orders, and confiscation orders may issue; egregious cases carry extradition and imprisonment for tax fraud in the home jurisdiction
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to VT 10,000,000 under the Economic Substance Act 2022; VFSC deregistration and automatic dissolution; adverse information exchange to foreign tax authorities under CRS and exchange-of-information on request mechanisms

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined up to VT 2,000,000; disqualification orders, personal liability for restoration costs and third-party claims; foreign tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil and impose personal tax penalties
AML / Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to VT 100,000,000; freezing and confiscation of assets; VFSC deregistration; bank de-risking and termination of correspondent banking relationships

Director personal liability: Imprisonment up to 12 years for money laundering; confiscation of personal assets; travel bans; mandatory disqualification from acting as a director; extradition risk under the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act
Abandonment of Annual Compliance Filings

Statutory sanction: VFSC strike-off, late filing surcharges up to VT 100,000, automatic dissolution, and loss of legal capacity to sue or defend claims

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for restoration costs and creditors' claims arising during the default period; further contempt sanctions and prohibition from acting as directors in Vanuatu may apply
