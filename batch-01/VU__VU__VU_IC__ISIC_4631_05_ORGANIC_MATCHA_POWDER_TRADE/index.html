Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — International Companies Registry, Port Vila, Republic of Vanuatu

Governing statute

International Companies Act [Cap. 222] (Vanuatu), as amended, particularly sections 3 (Incorporation), 7 (Memorandum and Articles), 12 (Registered Office), and 18 (First Directors); the Companies Act [Cap. 191] applies to domestic companies only and is not applicable to International Companies.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association (Constitution) executed by the subscriber and filed with VFSC together with the Application for Incorporation of an International Company (Form IC-1)

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 11 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital. The International Company may issue shares of no par value at any nominal consideration; no escrow account or capital deposit is required as a condition of incorporation. Share capital may be denominated in any freely convertible currency.

Funding & unlock path: No industrial fire safety certification or structural inspection is required at formation because the Company operates solely through a serviced office / registered agent address. Upon establishment of any physical office, a general building and fire safety clearance must be obtained from the Vanuatu Fire and Emergency Services and the municipality; office occupancy with fewer than five employees requires no special occupational clearance under the Health and Safety at Work Act.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital. The International Company may issue shares of no par value at any nominal consideration; no escrow account or capital deposit is required as a condition of incorporation. Share capital may be denominated in any freely convertible currency.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Republic of Vanuatu (portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu); International Company regulation and annual supervision by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) (portal: https://www.vfsc.vu).

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes and receives no TIN; its statutory identifier is the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation registration number. An IC engaged purely in offshore procurement and distribution of organic matcha powder is not required to register with the DCIR. If the IC makes taxable supplies inside Vanuatu exceeding VT 10,000,000 per annum, it must apply for VAT registration on DCIR Form VAT-01, submitting the Certificate of Incorporation, certified constitution, registered agent and registered office evidence, director and shareholder KYC details, a valid Vanuatu business licence, and a description of the matcha trading activity; the DCIR then issues a VAT registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu has no corporate income tax, no check-the-box regime, and no hybrid-mismatch election legislation. The IC's tax-exempt status is automatic upon incorporation and a declaration in its constitution under section 8 of the International Companies Act (Cap 222). The only structural election available is conversion of the IC into a domestic Vanuatu company (with VFSC and Ministerial approval), which terminates the tax-exempt zone status and exposes the entity to VAT, business licensing, customs duties, and general Vanuatu fiscal law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Vatu (VT) 10,000,000 (approx. USD 82,000) aggregate annual value of taxable supplies; persons making taxable supplies in Vanuatu above this threshold must register with the DCIR. A non-resident International Company conducting offshore matcha procurement and distribution with no Vanuatu taxable supplies has no indirect tax nexus and no registration obligation. Voluntary VAT registration is available to a non-resident supplier making zero-rated exports where the Commissioner is satisfied as to business purpose and record-keeping capacity.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under section 7(2) of the VAT Act (Cap 302), 0% VAT applies to (i) goods physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of the date of supply (or such extended period as the Commissioner allows); (ii) supplies made in relation to goods for export; and (iii) services physically performed outside Vanuatu or ancillary to international transport. To claim 0%, the supplier must hold proof of export, including the customs export declaration and transport documents, and must issue a VAT invoice bearing a clear zero-rated notation. For the offshore Vanuatu International Company model, where organic matcha powder is procured abroad and re-sold to overseas buyers without the goods entering Vanuatu, no Vanuatu VAT arises at all because the supply occurs wholly outside the indirect tax territory; export zero-rating documentation becomes relevant only if goods are physically shipped from Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD) submitted electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs system to the Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal of the organic matcha powder from Vanuatu
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List matching the customs declaration in description, quantity, and value
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit evidence confirming receipt of foreign export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin, phytosanitary certificate, or organic certification where required by the destination jurisdiction
  • VAT invoice annotated 'Zero-rated – Export' where the supply is exported from Vanuatu

None. Vanuatu does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting, or digital stamping. Taxpayers must issue sequentially numbered VAT invoices (paper or PDF) showing the VAT ID, date, description, zero-rated or exempt indication, and tax amount, and must retain records for at least five years. Customs declarations are cleared electronically through the ASYCUDA World platform operated by the DCIR; no additional e-invoicing clearance applies.

Vanuatu has no exchange-control law, so no statutory FX repatriation deadline is imposed; the 180-day period is the standard banking and export-credit convention, and remittance evidence must be preserved for DCIR audits. Fraudulent export zero-rating, false declarations, and baseless VAT refund claims are prosecuted under the VAT Act (Cap 302) and Customs Act (Cap 155): DCIR may reassess and retrospectively claw back refunded VAT, charge statutory interest, impose fines and imprisonment, forfeit goods, cancel VAT registration, and refer offenders for criminal prosecution. For an International Company, misconduct additionally triggers VFSC administrative sanctions, including strike-off and director disqualification, and personal criminal liability of directors under Vanuatu anti-money-laundering statutes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for current account transactions. Vanuatu has eliminated capital controls and maintains no general exchange control restrictions on inward or outward payments of a trade or investment character. Profit remittances by an International Company (VU_IC) are permitted without prior RBV approval, subject only to anti-money-laundering/know-your-customer (AML/KYC) checks, proof of beneficial ownership, and availability of supporting trade documents. Divergent but well-established Treatment: profit distribution to non-resident shareholders is ordinarily unrestricted given the zero-tax regime, but banks may apply enhanced due diligence where the remittance is classified as equity contribution, shareholder loan repayment, or royalty flows. The practical hurdle is effective liquidity for VUV/USD conversion due to the shallow local interbank market and dependence on Australian (AUD) and United States (USD) correspondent clearing; large disbursements will require settlement through offshore USD or AUD nostro accounts. No surrender of exported proceeds is required and no restrictions apply to holding foreign currency in a VU-licensed bank or in an offshore account of a Vanuatu International Company, provided the account is declared for AML purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable — Vanuatu has no statutory Export Proceeds Surrender Regime. The Exchange Control Act [Cap. 41] does not prescribe a timeline, a minimum conversion ratio, or a mandatory repatriation threshold for export receipts of goods, including culinary and ceremonial grade organic matcha powder. Export earnings may be retained in VUV, USD, AUD, EUR, or JPY in a freely operated trade account. The only compliance requirements are: (1) accurate declaration on the export documentation or commercial invoice to the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue for statistical/reporting purposes; (2) full traceability of the underlying transaction through the commercial bank for AML/CFT obligations under the Vanuatu AML/CFT Act No. 5 of 2017; and (3) no artificial pricing or transfer pricing misstatement if a related-party Japanese or Chinese matcha supplier is involved. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; Surrender timeline: none.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore digital EMIs or payment/e-money institutions domiciled outside Vanuatu. Legal constraint: any deposit-taking, electronic-money issuance, payment processing, or money-transfer activity targeting or operated from Vanuatu requires a license from the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu under the Banking Act [Cap. 73] and the Debit/Credit Card and Other Payment Systems Act; non-resident EMIs are not automatically recognized and cannot access local settlement rails. An offshore EMI may be used solely as a front-end collection tool if the underlying settlement is ultimately booked through a RBV-licensed bank with a local bank account. Conversely, a VU_IC attempting to bank with an unregulated foreign EMI will encounter serious friction from corresponding-bank due diligence and will be treated as a higher AML risk for the organic matcha trade because the counterparty jurisdiction (Japan/China) requires bank-to-bank documentary trade trails. Practical recommendation: adopt a hybrid model; use an established, licensed fintech EMI for e-commerce customer collections if the business has retail/ceremonial-grade matcha direct-to-consumer sales, but ensure EUR/USD balances are swept daily to a VU licensed operational account. Risk rating: Medium-to-High legal/regulatory friction; not viable as primary clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for USD capital injection from non-resident shareholder/investor to VU_IC: remitter instructs SWIFT MT103 (for up to USD 1,000,000) or MT202/MT202COV via the investor's financial institution to the VU bank's USD correspondent, using beneficiary SWIFT BIC (e.g., ANZ VUVUPXVZ or BSP VUVUVUVX) and explicit remittance reference: 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION / SHARE CAPITAL FOR VU_IC ORGANIC MATCHA TRADE', quoting the VU_IC registered agent file number. Accepted narrative purpose code: 'CAPT' (Capital Contribution), 'TREA' (Trade Payment), or the standard ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' if the correspondent bank requires; in Vanuatu, banks primarily rely on the remittance narrative and do not require an RBV import/export license. The funds are initially credited to a capital escrow sub-ledger or segregated inward flows account, blocked until the bank's AML/compliance officer verifies the source-of-funds declaration and the corresponding corporate resolution. All verification documentation (subscription agreement, KYC, constitutional documents, signed board resolution approving capital raising) must be provided before the escrow release into the operational trading account. For payouts to Japanese matcha suppliers, inbound USD is converted to JPY at an onshore VUV rate or via the delivering bank's USD/JPY desk; alternative: maintain a USD-denominated operational account in Vanuatu and settle the supplier invoice via a USD/JPY forward.Outbound settlement for organic matcha powder import/trade from Japan/China is executed through: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct telegraphic transfer for open-account settled orders, with payment details matching the pro-forma/commercial invoice (Invoice number, Incoterms, itemized product codes, and weight in kilograms), cleared through the bank's USD/AUD/JPY nostro accounts; (2) SWIFT MT700/M720 irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit, opened by the VU bank at the supplier's bank, allowing the matcha counterparty to draw against shipping, organic certificate, and phytosanitary documents; (3) SWIFT MT707 amendment trail to manage partial shipments and tolerance clauses; and (4) SWIFT MT752/MT754 authorization/confirmation rails for pre-advised documentary collections. For cross-border high volumes, the VU_IC should standardize on USD clearing via correspondent banks in the United States (e.g., BNY Mellon, JPMorgan Chase, Citibank) or AUD clearing via BSP and ANZ Australian corridors to avoid the limited VUV liquidity pool. All payments must embed the consolidated trade reference code (TRC) enabling reconciliation by both the VU trading entity and the Japanese/Chinese export bank to satisfy end-to-end AML and sanctions screening. No access to CLS settlement for VUV; interbank VUV/USD is executed via telephone/electronic dealing and confirmed with MT300/MT503 confirmation messages. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Nil — Vanuatu has not enacted a statutory transfer-pricing documentation regime with a VUV-denominated Local File or Master File threshold. For cross-border related-party matcha procurement, the IC should nonetheless maintain arm's-length contract and pricing evidence to support economic substance and avoid constructive dividend recharacterization by foreign tax authorities.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Under Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations, an IC engaged in distribution and service centre activity — including cross-border procurement and distribution of matcha powder — must demonstrate economic substance in Vanuatu: physical office, local employees, senior management decisions in Vanuatu, adequate operating expenditure, and an annual economic substance return filed through the registered agent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / VAT and Customs Fraud

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the full VAT/customs duty evaded, statutory penalties not exceeding 100% of the evaded amount, interest surcharges, seizure and auction of matcha shipments, and freezing of the IC bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly caused false declarations become personally jointly and severally liable for restitution. Criminal conviction may result in imprisonment, financial penalty, and courts may pierce the corporate veil to recover assets from personal accounts.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: VFSC rejection of the economic substance return, administrative penalties, deemed loss of International Company exemption, striking-off from the Vanuatu register, and disqualification of the registered agent if the agent certified a false substance filing.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil liability for the IC's outstanding government fees and penalties, director disqualification orders, asset-freezing orders, and travel bans issued by Vanuatu courts in the course of restoring or winding up the struck-off entity.
AML/CFT Breach in Payment Processing

Statutory sanction: Enforcement action under the Vanuatu AML/CFT Act: compliance orders, civil penalties against the company, revocation of the registered agent arrangement, freezing and confiscation of funds, and criminal prosecution for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly processed illicit trade proceeds can be charged personally with money laundering; conviction carries imprisonment, asset forfeiture, international travel bans, and disqualification from holding company offices in Vanuatu.
False Exempt Status / Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Treatment as fraud under the Vanuatu Penal Code and the International Companies Act; VFSC may dissolve the IC, order disgorgement of profits, impose civil monetary penalties, and refer directors to the Public Prosecutor.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for damages suffered by aggrieved commercial counterparties and financial institutions. Courts may impose employment/prohibition orders, travel bans, civil restitution, and custodial sentences proportionate to the fraud.
