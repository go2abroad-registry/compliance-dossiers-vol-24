Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR) — Government of the Republic of Vanuatu; official portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] via a private registered agent and register with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), lodging name reservation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office address in Port Vila, and registered agent details. Step 2: Obtain the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation. Step 3: Apply to the DCIR for the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the DCIR TIN application form, submitting certified copies of the Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent certificate, proof of registered office, and certified passport copies of directors/shareholders. Step 4: Where the IC makes or intends to make taxable supplies exceeding the VAT registration threshold, file Form VAT-001 (VAT Registration Application) with the DCIR, attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, register of directors, service address, and a description of taxable activities. Step 5: Upon approval, the DCIR issues the VAT registration certificate and TIN, which must be quoted on all invoices and customs declarations. Step 6: For the wholesale distribution of pharmaceutical APIs, obtain the applicable trade/business licence and any VFSC endorsement required for the stated commercial model. The IC is tax-exempt by statute and is not obliged to pay income tax or business turnover taxes to the DCIR; voluntary TIN issuance is available for banking, customs clearance, and counterparty identity requirements.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu maintains no check-the-box election, hybrid-mismatch election, or subchapter-S style classification regime. The International Company is automatically classified as an offshore tax-exempt entity under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], subject only to the statutory restriction that it must not carry on business with Vanuatu residents, hold land in Vanuatu, or list securities on the Vanuatu stock exchange. An IC may voluntarily deregister as an International Company and convert to a Domestic Company under the Companies Act, thereby subjecting itself to standard domestic tax treatment (including VAT and customs duties); this is a corporate-status conversion rather than a tax election. No other entity-level tax classification elections are available under Vanuatu law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (ten million Vatu) annual taxable turnover; any person carrying on a taxable activity must register for VAT within 21 days after the end of the month in which the threshold is met, and may voluntarily register at a lower turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated under Section 13 of the Value Added Tax Act No. 13 of 2014 where the supplier exports the goods from Vanuatu within 28 days of the date of supply (or such further period as the Commissioner of Customs and Inland Revenue approves) and retains documentary evidence of export. Services are zero-rated where (a) the services are supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time the services are performed; (b) the services are in respect of exported goods; or (c) the services relate to goods that are temporarily imported into Vanuatu for repair or processing and are subsequently exported. For the pharmaceutical API wholesale model, the IC must demonstrate that the APIs are physically dispatched to a foreign purchaser and leave Vanuatu, or are delivered from an offshore warehouse where the services are supplied for export-related logistics; commissionaire or bill-and-hold arrangements are scrutinised for substance. Relief from VAT on zero-rated exports is effected by crediting input tax against output tax; excess credits are refundable by the DCIR.

  • Customs Export Declaration (approved export entry lodged with the DCIR Customs Division)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List issued by the International Company to the overseas pharmaceutical buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT confirmation demonstrating settlement of the export proceeds in foreign currency
  • Certificate of Origin (where required by the destination jurisdiction or trade agreement)
  • Sales contract, Letter of Credit, or purchase order reflecting the offshore wholesale transaction
  • Customs entry or import documentation from the destination country confirming receipt of the APIs where available
  • Evidence of tax invoice issuance showing the zero-rated supply and VAT ID of the supplier

Vanuatu does not currently operate a real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API-based e-invoicing clearance, or digital tax-stamping platform. The DCIR accepts paper invoices, PDF e-invoices, and electronic records. Suppliers registered for VAT must issue a valid VAT invoice containing: the supplier's name and VAT registration number/TIN, sequential invoice number, date of issue, full description and quantity of goods or services, unit price and total consideration, the VAT amount at 15%, and for zero-rated exports an express statement that the supply is zero-rated under the Value Added Tax Act No. 13 of 2014. The DCIR is progressively digitising tax administration and customs declarations (including the electronic processing of export entries), but no statutory e-invoicing clearance mandate is in force as of the assessment date.

The DCIR conducts targeted audits of zero-rated export refunds, requiring a complete 'export evidence package' for each consignment; failure to produce satisfactory proof of physical export and foreign-currency consideration within the prescribed record-keeping period results in reversal of the zero-rating, assessment of VAT at the standard 15% rate on the full gross consideration, and imposition of interest at the statutory rate from the original due date. The Commissioner may issue default assessments where VAT returns are incorrect or where zero-rating is unsupported, and may raise assessments up to four years from the relevant date (extended in cases of fraudulent or wilful evasion). Penalties under the Value Added Tax Act No. 13 of 2014 include administrative fines of up to VT 1,000,000, additional penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded, and imprisonment of up to three years for individuals involved in making false declarations or knowingly issuing fraudulent zero-rated invoices. The VFSC may additionally deregister or strike an International Company for non-compliance, and a breach of the IC Act's restriction on trading with Vanuatu residents will forfeit the IC's tax-exempt status, exposing the entity to retroactive domestic VAT and customs duty liabilities. Although Vanuatu imposes no statutory exchange-control repatriation timeline, standard banking compliance and the DCIR export-evidence framework effectively require repatriation of export proceeds within 180 days; documentation of later repatriation attracts enhanced scrutiny, potential denial of refund claims, and clawback of any VAT refund previously paid, together with penalties and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal exchange-control regime for both current and capital account transactions. The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is freely convertible, profit remittances are not subject to prior exchange-control approval, and the International Company may repatriate paid-up capital and trading dividends without quantitative ceilings. Practical impediments arise only from bank-level AML/CFT due diligence, enhanced documentation for high-risk pharmaceutical wholesale activity, and RBV/FIU reporting thresholds for large-value or unusual inbound/outbound transfers. Sanctioned jurisdictions and persons remain prohibited under Vanuatu's international financial sanctions framework.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender rule, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed repatriation timeline. Export receipts may be retained in a resident foreign-currency account, converted to VUV at licensed dealer rates, or used directly for import/trade settlements. A dealing bank will require the underlying pro-forma invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, insurance certificate, and customs export declaration for the trade to be processed under the liberalised regime.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - Monetary Authority and Foreign Exchange Administration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low—Vanuatu has no statutory electronic-money institution (EMI) licensing category. Offshore digital banks and EMIs without an RBV banking licence cannot open resident VUV accounts, access the local clearing system, or provide regulated payment services to a Vanuatu International Company. They may be used only as supplementary payment gateways, not as primary treasury or settlement rails, and are legally constrained from issuing VUV-denominated accounts or executing local currency payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions must arrive via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's regulated overseas bank to the Vanuatu International Company's designated escrow/operational account at an RBV-licensed bank. The SWIFT field 70 (Remittance Information) must include an explicit corporate-purpose narrative, for example 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — VU_IC / ISIC 4649 / PHARMACEUTICAL API WHOLESALE', and must never be described as a personal gift or unrelated remittance. The receiving bank will require a board resolution authorising the incoming capital, a copy of the shareholder register, and a source-of-funds questionnaire for any transfer above the bank's threshold. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation and no statutory VUV threshold for Local File or Master File preparation. Best-practice threshold: if the foreign parent group is subject to OECD-style TP rules, prepare a balanced Local File for the Vanuatu IC when annual related-party turnover exceeds VUV 120 million (approx. USD 1 million) or when intercompany financial transactions exceed VUV 50 million (approx. USD 400,000), aligning with common EU/OECD de-minimis rules.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu introduced economic substance requirements via the International Companies (Amendment) Act 2022 and the VFSC Economic Substance Guidance, applicable to 'relevant activities' including distribution and service centres, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding entities, and IP businesses. A pharmaceutical API wholesale IC holding a VFSC special-purpose licence and engaging in related-party distribution must demonstrate in Vanuatu: (i) local board meetings with quorum; (ii) adequate physical premises and equipment; (iii) a minimum of one full-time resident employee; (iv) substantive decision-making at principal level; and (v) annual operating expenditure proportionate to the income booked. An annual economic substance notification must be filed with the VFSC; failure to file or to satisfy substance triggers monetary penalties, loss of licence, and possible deregistration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Abusive Tax Exemption / Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Within Vanuatu, no domestic income tax fraud charge arises because the IC is exempt; however, knowingly making false statements to VFSC constitutes an offence under the International Companies Act Cap 222, attracting a fine of up to VUV 500,000. Outside Vanuatu, the parent jurisdiction may apply CFC rules, GAAR, or transfer pricing recharacterisation, resulting in retroactive tax assessments, interest, and penalties of 100%-200% of the tax underpaid.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false intercompany agreements or knowingly misstate the IC's management location face personal criminal liability in Vanuatu, including a fine and imprisonment of up to 2 years. In the foreign jurisdiction, directors can be personally assessed for the diverted tax, subjected to travel bans, and disqualified from serving as directors under cross-border enforcement mechanisms.
Money Laundering / AML-CTF Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act, Act No. 7 of 2016, a corporate body convicted of money laundering faces fines of up to VUV 20 million, mandatory revocation of the VFSC licence, and confiscation of all connected assets. The Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) may freeze bank accounts and order the surrender of transaction records.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and authorised signatories who knowingly or recklessly participate in laundering API proceeds are liable to imprisonment of up to 15 years, individual fines of up to VUV 5 million, travel bans, and asset seizure under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2001.
Unlicensed / Illicit Pharmaceutical Wholesale Distribution

Statutory sanction: Contravention of the Pharmacy Act Cap 65 and related medicines regulations subjects the company to immediate product seizure, revocation of any distribution authorisation, and fines of up to VUV 10 million per offence; each API shipment or falsified document constitutes a separate offence.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise the import/export of counterfeit or unregistered APIs are personally and criminally liable, facing fines and imprisonment of up to 5 years, permanent exclusion from acting as a director of any licensed pharmaceutical company, and civil claims by downstream patients or distributors for damages.
Shell Company Substance Failure and Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: VFSC may impose an initial fine of up to VUV 500,000 and a continuing penalty of VUV 50,000 per month for non-compliance, suspend the registered agent's services, and strike off the IC. Sanctions violations are referred to the Vanuatu FIU and the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Unit, which can execute restraint orders on the IC's bank accounts and compel repatriation of misappropriated assets.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability for false substance declarations and sanctions evasion, including fines of up to VUV 1,000,000, imprisonment of up to 10 years, director disqualification orders, travel bans, and personal confiscation of profits earned through the offending transactions.
