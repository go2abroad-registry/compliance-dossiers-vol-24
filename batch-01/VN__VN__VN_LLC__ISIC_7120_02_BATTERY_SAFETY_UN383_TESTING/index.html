Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Vietnam imposes no economy-wide statutory minimum paid-up capital for an LLC engaged in technical testing and analysis (ISIC 7120). However, the foreign-invested company must declare a Charter Capital fully subscribed in the Charter and sufficient for the scale of the project, which is a substantive appraisal criterion in the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) proceeding under Article 36 of the Law on Investment. The contributed capital, either in cash or appraised non-cash assets, must be fully paid within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47.4 of the Law on Enterprises); cash contributions must be deposited into a dedicated escrow/transaction capital account at a bank licensed in Vietnam, and the legal representative is required to report capital contribution and account statements to the DPI. If the contributory capital remains unpaid, the LLC must undergo a capital adjustment or capital reduction procedure and the legal representative is subject to administrative sanctions under Decree 122/2021/ND-CP.

Funding & unlock path: The laboratory must obtain Fire Prevention and Fighting Police approval - Certificate of Fire Safety Design Approval and Certificate of Acceptance of Completion of Fire Safety Works (Giay chung nhan tham duyet thiet ke PCCC va Giay xac nhan nghiem thu ve PCCC) issued by the Provincial Police Department under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP, covering electromagnetic testing chambers and battery storage cells with adequate fire suppression and VOC detection systems; structural load and as-built verification must be evidenced by a construction completion certificate or a certificate of eligibility for house ownership under the Law on Construction No. 50/2014/QH13; electrical installation must be inspected by an accredited body to confirm compliance with QCVN 01:2021/BXD on construction safety and electrical system safety; workplace environment monitoring (noise, vibration, electromagnetic field exposure, chemical vapors) must comply with QCVN 24:2016/BYT for health and safety, and occupational hygiene registration must be filed with the Department of Health under Law No. 84/2015/QH13 on Occupational Safety and Hygiene; an Internal Labor Regulation must be registered with the provincial labor authority if the laboratory employs 10 or more persons; employees performing UN38.3 drop tests must receive dangerous goods transport safety (IMDG/UN) training certification and first aid training; all testing equipment must be calibrated and traceable to national or international standards as required by ISO/IEC 17025.

Paid-in capital rule: Vietnam imposes no economy-wide statutory minimum paid-up capital for an LLC engaged in technical testing and analysis (ISIC 7120). However, the foreign-invested company must declare a Charter Capital fully subscribed in the Charter and sufficient for the scale of the project, which is a substantive appraisal criterion in the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) proceeding under Article 36 of the Law on Investment. The contributed capital, either in cash or appraised non-cash assets, must be fully paid within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47.4 of the Law on Enterprises); cash contributions must be deposited into a dedicated escrow/transaction capital account at a bank licensed in Vietnam, and the legal representative is required to report capital contribution and account statements to the DPI. If the contributory capital remains unpaid, the LLC must undergo a capital adjustment or capital reduction procedure and the legal representative is subject to administrative sanctions under Decree 122/2021/ND-CP.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam (Tổng cục Thuế, GDT) under the Ministry of Finance; national e-tax filing and registration portal: eTax system at thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; national e-invoice portal: hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; enterprise registration is separately administered by the provincial Department of Planning and Investment through the National Enterprise Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: For a foreign-invested LLC, obtain the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment; the 10-digit Enterprise Code issued on the ERC automatically constitutes the statutory tax identification number (TIN) under Law on Enterprises 2020 Art. 29 and Circular 105/2020/TT-BTC, so no separate TIN application is required. Step 2: Within 10 working days of ERC issuance, complete and submit the initial tax registration dossier, including the VAT method election (credit versus direct method), legal representative, location, and business line (ISIC 7120 / testing services), through the eTax portal thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; the GDT issues the electronic TIN certificate and taxpayer profile. Step 3: Register a certified digital signature (CA) and create the e-invoice account on hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; register e-invoice templates and obtain GDT approval before first use. Step 4: Open a Vietnamese authorized-bank account for foreign-currency export proceeds, register payroll and social insurance, and set the VAT filing period (monthly or quarterly) with the competent tax sub-department. A foreign investor may also appoint a licensed tax agent to perform the registration; all filings are digital and no physical inspection is required at the registration stage.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the Vietnam LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer under Law on Corporate Income Tax 14/2008/QH12 (as amended) and is taxed on worldwide taxable income at the standard 20% CIT rate. There is no pass-through or fiscal-transparency regime in Vietnamese law; profits are taxed at the LLC level and distributions to foreign shareholders are not subject to any dividend withholding or profit-remittance tax (the former profits remittance tax is repealed). Territorial nuance: a resident LLC is taxed on worldwide income, while non-resident foreign contractors are taxed only on Vietnam-source income. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the Vietnam LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer under Law on Corporate Income Tax 14/2008/QH12 (as amended) and is taxed on worldwide taxable income at the standard 20% CIT rate. There is no pass-through or fiscal-transparency regime in Vietnamese law; profits are taxed at the LLC level and distributions to foreign shareholders are not subject to any dividend withholding or profit-remittance tax (the former profits remittance tax is repealed). Territorial nuance: a resident LLC is taxed on worldwide income, while non-resident foreign contractors are taxed only on Vietnam-source income. No general tax-exempt-zone status applies to the LLC itself; a services-oriented laboratory would need an EPE or industrial-zone incentive certificate to access special regimes, and battery-safety testing services rarely satisfy EPE manufacturing/processing conditions under Decree 82/2018/ND-CP.

Available election forms

Vietnamese law does not operate a United States-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. The elections and regimes which a foreign-invested testing LLC may validly invoke include: (1) VAT credit (deduction) method election at tax registration, mandatory for businesses with annual revenue from VND 1 billion, and voluntary below that threshold; (2) direct VAT method election where the quantitative thresholds are met; (3) CIT incentive election for a new investment project certified in an eligible industrial zone, high-tech zone, or supporting-industry project, which may confer a 10% preferential CIT rate for 15 years, a 4-year CIT exemption and 50% CIT reduction for the following 9 years (Law on CIT Arts. 13-16; Decree 218/2013/ND-CP), provided the IRC records the incentive conditions; (4) global minimum tax registration and top-up tax filing for MNE groups meeting the EUR 750 million consolidated revenue threshold under Resolution 107/2023/QH15 (effective fiscal year 2024); (5) after the first 12 months of compliant operation, election to migrate from tax-authority-coded e-invoices to self-issued e-invoices (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP); (6) foreign contractor tax withholding election and qualified intermediary registration for cross-border payments; and (7) VAT refund election for zero-rated exporters on a monthly or quarterly basis. Hybrid mismatch elections (e.g., dual consolidated loss elections) are not recognized; Vietnam instead applies controlled-foreign-company rules under Circular 96/2015/TT-BTC to re-attribute low-taxed offshore profits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no VAT registration threshold for an LLC: VAT registration is mandatory from the date the ERC/TIN is issued. The operative monetary thresholds are: (i) VND 1,000,000,000 of annual revenue from the sale of goods or services, at or above which the VAT credit (deduction) method is compulsory and below which the direct method applies unless voluntary credit-method registration is elected (Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC Art. 12); and (ii) VND 100,000,000 of annual revenue, which is the simplified compulsory registration threshold applicable only to household and casual businesses, not to corporate LLCs. For foreign contractors supplying cross-border services into Vietnam, the VND 1 billion threshold also governs the option to register for the deduction method; otherwise foreign contractor tax applies by withholding.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Law on VAT Art. 9 and Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC Art. 9, zero-rating (0% VAT) requires, cumulatively: (1) the supply is an export of goods or a service rendered directly to a foreign organization or individual; (2) for services, the service is consumed outside Vietnam, meaning the economic benefit and the test report are used offshore; (3) the foreign party is invoiced with a valid 0% VAT e-invoice; and (4) payment is received from abroad in convertible foreign currency credited to a Vietnamese authorized bank account (bank transfer, letter of credit, or foreign remittance), with proceeds repatriated within 180 days. For the UN38.3 battery-safety and CE-RED electromagnetic testing lab: zero-rating is defensible where battery samples are imported into Vietnam under the temporary-import-for-re-export customs regime (or sourced from an EPE/free-trade zone), each sample batch is traced and either re-exported or destroyed under customs supervision, the test report is addressed exclusively to the offshore principal and used for UN38.3 certification outside Vietnam, and the testing fee is remitted in EUR/USD from abroad. Testing services physically performed in Vietnam for a domestic Vietnamese client, on goods released into the domestic market, or where the report is consumed in Vietnam are standard-rated at 10%; testing services are not within the 5% reduced-rate list. The GDT applies a substance-over-form review: paper exports with actual domestic consumption or deliberate mischaracterization of a domestic service as an offshore service will be re-rated at 10% and subject to penalties.

  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / international transport document evidencing that the goods or tested samples left Vietnamese customs territory or were delivered under customs control to the foreign principal
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank credit advice from an authorized Vietnamese bank showing receipt of the export service fee in convertible foreign currency from abroad
  • Export or cross-border service contract between the VN LLC and the foreign client, identifying the UN38.3 / CE-RED testing scope, place of consumption, and payment terms

  • Packing list, sample custody and logistics records, UN38.3 test report addressed to the foreign client, destruction certificate (if samples are destroyed under customs control), and other evidence of offshore consumption or use of the report

Mandatory structured e-invoicing applies to all enterprises from 1 July 2022 (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP; Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC). A newly established foreign-invested LLC must use e-invoices with a tax-authority code for the first 12 months of operation: each XML invoice is transmitted to GDT in real time via API or portal web service, and GDT clears the invoice by returning a unique tax-authority code (mã của cơ quan thuế) before the invoice is legally valid, which functions as a real-time digital fiscal stamp. After 12 months of compliance, the LLC may apply to issue self-issued e-invoices without a pre-approved code; such invoices must still bear a digital signature from a licensed certification authority (CA) and a QR code, and the full invoice data must be transmitted to the hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn portal no later than the statutory due date of the VAT filing period in which the invoice was issued, with an accelerated 2-hour transmission rule for certain categories of supplies. There is no physical fiscal-printer or paper-stamping regime; the tax-authority code, QR code, and GDT data repository constitute the fiscal clearance and audit trail. All e-invoice templates must be pre-registered and approved by GDT, and invoices must be issued in Vietnamese (with English allowed for exported services).

Export zero-rating claims are a priority audit area for the GDT and the General Department of Customs. Compliance expectations include a complete documentation trail, physical and accounting traceability of sample flows, proof of bank remittance from abroad, and evidence that the service was consumed offshore. Penalties for misstatement, circular round-tripping of funds, undisclosed domestic use of tested goods, or fabricated export documentation include: (1) reversal of the 0% rate and reassessment at the standard 10% VAT with late-payment interest at 0.03% per day from the original due date; (2) administrative fines of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax under Law on Tax Administration 38/2019/QH14 Art. 17; (3) customs fines, confiscation, and tariff reassessment for false export declarations under Decree 128/2020/ND-CP; (4) extension of the tax assessment limitation period from 5 years to 10 years where evasion is established (Law on Tax Administration Art. 42); (5) suspension or denial of VAT refund claims for zero-rated exporters where the input VAT refund pattern shows no domestic output VAT, triggering pre-refund field audits; and (6) criminal prosecution under Penal Code Art. 200 for tax evasion, punishable by fines up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment of up to 7 years where the evaded amount exceeds statutory thresholds, with personal criminal liability for legal representatives and accountants who sign false export invoices.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam maintains current-account convertibility for service-export receipts and import payments: a UN38.3 battery-safety testing laboratory invoicing foreign clients can receive testing-fee income in freely convertible currencies through an SBV-licensed bank without an individual SBV conversion permit. Capital-account transactions are controlled and not freely convertible: foreign equity contributions must flow through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at a licensed bank; foreign loans require debt-registration certificates under the SBV foreign-borrowing circulars; and profit remittance is classified as a current-account payment, but the remitting bank will require evidence that the LLC has prepared audited annual financial statements, fulfilled annual corporate income tax and value-added tax finalization obligations, and has no overdue tax liabilities. Practical convertibility hurdles are procedural rather than statutory: banks may request extensive underlying contracts, invoices, test certificates, and tax confirmation forms, and VND liquidity windows can create temporary conversion delays.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Viet Nam currently imposes a 0% mandatory currency conversion ratio for export/service receipts collected into an SBV-licensed bank; the LLC is not required to sell export proceeds to the bank and may retain foreign currency in its licensed foreign-currency account. The mandatory surrender obligation is replaced by a repatriation obligation: proceeds from supply of battery-safety testing services to overseas customers must be credited to a foreign-currency account at a licensed bank in Viet Nam within the statutory current-transaction repatriation period, commonly 90 days from the completion-of-services date evidenced by the test report and commercial invoice; if the funds are received into a permitted overseas account, they must be repatriated to the Vietnam bank account within 30 days of deposit. The LLC must maintain the signed service contract, commercial invoice, completed UN38.3 test report, customs or freight documents for battery samples, and a bank transaction declaration as supporting documents for the bank's review.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) / Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam, acting through the Foreign Exchange Administration Department and the Credit Institutions Supervision Department, under the Government of Viet Nam. All cross-border foreign-exchange transactions, current-transfer procedures, capital-flow compliance, and licensed-bank FX operations are administered by the SBV under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 06/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended by Law No. 07/2013/QH13, and the implementing circulars of the SBV, principally Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN as amended.
