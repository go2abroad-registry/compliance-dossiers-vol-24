Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (GDT), Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Vietnam; official electronic tax portal: etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn (enterprise self-service e-filing and registration).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested LLC registers for taxation with the Provincial/City Tax Department where the head office is located by submitting the Tax Registration Form (Form 01-ĐK-TCT), Article of Incorporation, investment certificate (IRC/ERC), and legal representative identification. The tax authority issues a single Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST) that functions as the TIN, VAT identification number, and customs identification number, typically within 15 working days from valid submission. Tax registration is automatically integrated with the National Business Registration System under the One-Stop Shop mechanism.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; a Vietnamese resident enterprise (foreign-invested LLC) is taxed on worldwide income at 20% CIT; no pass-through or partner-level taxation is available. — A foreign-invested LLC registers for taxation with the Provincial/City Tax Department where the head office is located by submitting the Tax Registration Form (Form 01-ĐK-TCT), Article of Incorporation, investment certificate (IRC/ERC), and legal representative identification. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a Vietnamese resident enterprise (foreign-invested LLC) is taxed on worldwide income at 20% CIT; no pass-through or partner-level taxation is available.

Available election forms

Vietnam does not operate U.S.-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. The LLC is irrevocably classified as a resident corporate taxpayer. Only limited routine elections exist, such as choosing the accounting period (calendar year or other fiscal year subject to tax authority approval), and selecting between the regular VAT credit method and the direct tax computation method for foreign contractor contexts, but for an ordinary local LLC, VAT is mandatory under the credit method once taxable revenue exceeds the registration threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration turnover threshold applies to companies formed under the Enterprise Law; every foreign-invested LLC must register for VAT immediately. For household and individual businesses the conservative threshold is VND 100,000,000 annual revenue; an LLC with BPO services is always mandatorily registered regardless of revenue level.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Vietnam and to services supplied to and consumed by a foreign customer outside Vietnam, including offshore BPO/call-center services, provided that the service is not consumed or used in Vietnam. The exporter must possess a written contract with the foreign party, documentary evidence of actual service performance, and verified non-cash payment from abroad through a Vietnamese licensed bank or through foreign banks and international payment intermediaries (e.g., Wise B2B, SWIFT wire) with conclusive bank statements. The VAT rate is 0%; input VAT attributable to export activities is refundable under VAT credit method.

  • Customs Export Declaration (both e-declaration and confirmed customs clearance) for physical goods
  • Commercial Invoice / Service Invoice evidencing BPO service scope, customer identity, and transaction price
  • Signed BPO services agreement (in English or Vietnamese) with clear offshore deliverable clauses
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill (only when tangible goods are involved; not applicable to pure call-center services)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or equivalent bank remittance advice evidencing non-cash payment from the overseas counterparty

Mandatory electronic invoicing under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. All invoices must be generated in the national e-invoice portal format and, for invoices issued using the tax-authority-code method, are subject to pre-validation and assignment of a digital tax authority code before issuance. Real-time machine-readable XML (standardized by GDT) and API integration with the e-invoice gateway are compulsory for certified invoicing applications. No physical invoice stamping is required.

Zero-rating claims are audited against export contracts, customs declarations, service performance records, and inbound remittance evidence. False export declarations or fraudulent zero-rating claims attract VAT reassessment plus tax evasion penalties under Circular 166/2013/TT-BTC and criminal liability under Penal Code Article 200 where fraudulent VAT refund is proven. Penalties include retrospective denial of the 0% rate, imposition of the 10% standard VAT on the full gross consideration, interest at 20% per annum, fines ranging from 1 to 3 times the under-declared tax for evasion and up to VND 100,000,000 for administrative violations, and clawback of all refunded input VAT, in addition to suspended or revoked tax identification/customs registration codes in severe cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations and therefore maintains de jure convertibility for current-account transactions, which includes the export of remote BPO and call-center services (booking of fees, commissions, and management charges by the LLC from offshore principals). In practice, however, the convertibility regime is procedural and bank-intermediated: all foreign-currency receipts must flow through an SBV-licensed authorized credit institution; service receivables denominated in USD or EUR must be credited to an authorized foreign-currency account maintained in Viet Nam; and conversion into VND is executed at the commercial bank's counter rate, which is disciplined by the SBV's daily central rate and the prescribed trading band. Capital-account transactions remain fully controlled: equity injections, shareholder loans, and liquidation or exit proceeds are subject to mandatory registration, use of the DICA, and prior documentary verification under Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN. Profit remittance is permitted as a current-account transfer but is effectively conditioned on (i) completion of the audited annual financial statements, (ii) finalization of corporate income tax and all other statutory obligations, (iii) confirmation that all matured liabilities to Vietnamese creditors have been discharged, (iv) a board resolution declaring the distribution, and (v) the commercial bank's verification that the remitted amount does not exceed the accumulated after-tax distributable profit reflected in the audited equity accounts. For this BPO LLC, the material operational hurdle is not legal convertibility but the documentary discipline required for each USD receipt and each VND conversion, and the absolute prohibition against settling service export fees outside the licensed banking channel.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For service exports (remote BPO and call-center activities), the statutory rule is full repatriation rather than surrender: 100% of the foreign-currency proceeds earned from services rendered to offshore clients must be credited to a foreign-currency or VND account held with an SBV-licensed authorized bank in Viet Nam. The applicable repatriation period is 180 days from the date the service is performed or, where the underlying client contract establishes payment milestones, 180 days from each invoice due date; the LLC must monitor each receivable against this deadline and in no case may it retain service proceeds offshore. There is currently no mandatory conversion ratio or export surrender quota applicable to service exporters of this type: the LLC may lawfully retain the full 100% of collected USD in its foreign-currency operating account or convert all or part to VND at the licensed bank. Nevertheless, the SBV retains discretionary authority, in exceptional balance-of-payments stress scenarios, to impose temporary conversion obligations by legal instrument, and the licensed bank is obligated to notify the SBV of any overdue or unrepatriated service receipt, which may trigger administrative sanctions, adverse BoP status, and heightened scrutiny at the next annual account review.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV; Vietnamese: Ngan Hang Nha Nuoc Viet Nam), acting principally through the Foreign Exchange Management Department and the municipal/provincial SBV branches that license, supervise, and receive regulatory filings from the commercial bank selected to host the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) and the operational foreign-currency accounts of the foreign-invested limited liability company.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low, with a constrained secondary role. Offshore digital EMIs and cross-border payment platforms (Wise B2B, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, and similar) cannot hold SBV-regulated VND accounts, cannot provide onshore clearing, and cannot serve as the legal settlement rail for Vietnamese balance-of-payments reporting. VND is not internationally deliverable, and any EMI custody of BPO service receivables creates shadow-accounting risk, non-compliance with the 180-day repatriation rule, and a break in the audit trail required by Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN. The only viable architecture is a collection-rail model: Wise or an equivalent EMI may be used for low-ticket inbound fees collected from offshore end clients, provided that (i) the EMI account is established in the exact name of the Vietnamese LLC or its authorized treasury nominee, (ii) a daily or weekly sweep by SWIFT MT103 transfers the cleared proceeds into the LLC's foreign-currency account at the licensed Vietnamese bank, and (iii) the LLC's books, the BoP filings, and the audited financial statements recognize the principal only at the Vietnamese licensed bank. High-ticket strategic receivables from the BPO principals should be wired directly via SWIFT MT103 to the licensed Vietnamese bank. Feasibility rating: Low for primary treasury and clearing functionality; Medium only as a secondary aggregation point for marginal retail-scale collections.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity capital must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign shareholder's bank account directly to the LLC's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at the licensed Vietnamese commercial bank. The remittance instruction must identify the LLC by its Enterprise Registration Code and Investment Registration Certificate number and must state a purpose code corresponding to direct-investment equity contribution; the bank will credit the funds, register the inflow in the DICA ledger, and report the transaction to the State Bank of Vietnam under the balance-of-payments framework. The LLC may then effect a spot conversion of the USD capital to VND and transfer the VND to its payroll/operating account. Service-export fees from offshore BPO principals must not be commingled with the DICA; they are current-account receipts and must be received into a separate foreign-currency operating account. Where the principals are invoiced monthly, the LLC should include the invoice number and the BPO contract reference in each incoming MT103 so that the bank can map the receipt to the underlying service export and verify compliance with the 180-day repatriation rule.Local disbursements, including employee salaries, social insurance, rent, and Vietnamese vendor payments, are executed in VND through the local interbank clearing system managed by the National Payment Corporation of Viet Nam (NAPAS), using the licensed bank's e-banking platform. Cross-border outbound disbursements, including license fees for cloud telephony and CRM software, foreign supplier payments, and tax advisory retainers, are cleared via SWIFT MT103 direct transfers or, where a correspondent intermediary is required, MT103 plus MT202 cover. Dividends and capital repatriation must be cleared outbound through the DICA via SWIFT MT103, supported by the audited financial statements, the CIT finalization certificate, the board resolution, and the bank's verification that the remittance does not exceed distributable after-tax profit. Commercial letters of credit are not a standard or necessary rail for an ISIC 7820 remote BPO/call-center operation because the underlying transactions are service fees rather than physical commodity shipments; high-value equipment purchases may be settled by documentary collection or MT103 against the supply agreement, reserving the LC structure only if the LLC later acquires physical hardware on a cross-border deferred-payment basis.Outright VND/USD FX forward contracts entered into with an SBV-licensed authorized bank, permitted only where the LLC demonstrates a genuine underlying foreign-currency exposure arising from service export receivables, payroll conversion requirements, or registered shareholder loans; speculative or non-deliverable offshore-linked forward structures are prohibited.VND/USD FX swap transactions (both odd-leg and straight-swap structures) with an SBV-licensed authorized bank, used to manage the timing mismatch between USD service-receipt conversion and periodic VND payroll and rental payment obligations.Cross-currency swaps executed between the LLC and a licensed bank or between the LLC and its foreign shareholder, provided the underlying shareholder loan or intercompany payable has been duly registered with the State Bank of Vietnam and the swap terminates on or before the maturity of the recognized underlying exposure.FX option structures (call or put options on VND) purchased from an SBV-licensed authorized bank, subject to the same genuine-demand rule requiring the notional to fall within the forecast USD service receipts and committed VND expenditure over the option tenor.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs): not permitted as an onshore instrument for a Vietnamese resident entity; the LLC may not novate offshore NDF contracts into VND delivery, may not use offshore NDF desks without prior SBV authorization, and must rely exclusively on deliverable onshore forwards and swaps for lawful hedges.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low, with a constrained secondary role. Offshore digital EMIs and cross-border payment platforms (Wise B2B, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, and similar) cannot hold SBV-regulated VND accounts, cannot provide onshore clearing, and cannot serve as the legal settlement rail for Vietnamese balance-of-payments reporting. VND is not internationally deliverable, and any EMI custody of BPO service receivables creates shadow-accounting risk, non-compliance with the 180-day repatriation rule, and a break in the audit trail required by Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN. The only viable architecture is a collection-rail model: Wise or an equivalent EMI may be used for low-ticket inbound fees collected from offshore end clients, provided that (i) the EMI account is established in the exact name of the Vietnamese LLC or its authorized treasury nominee, (ii) a daily or weekly sweep by SWIFT MT103 transfers the cleared proceeds into the LLC's foreign-currency account at the licensed Vietnamese bank, and (iii) the LLC's books, the BoP filings, and the audited financial statements recognize the principal only at the Vietnamese licensed bank. High-ticket strategic receivables from the BPO principals should be wired directly via SWIFT MT103 to the licensed Vietnamese bank. Feasibility rating: Low for primary treasury and clearing functionality; Medium only as a secondary aggregation point for marginal retail-scale collections.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a Vietnamese LLC to its corporate shareholder if the shareholder is a foreign juridical person under common application of the Law on Enterprise and the CIT Law, provided the LLC's after-tax profit is lawfully distributed; however, a 0.1% registration fee on equity transfers is not a dividend WHT. Dividends to foreign individuals are subject to a 5% personal income tax where applicable, but for corporate shareholders using a Vietnam LLC, the dividend itself is not subject to additional CIT. Where the foreign shareholder is a country without a DTA, domestic rules still do not impose a dividend WHT at the LLC level; instead cross-border payments (interest, royalties, services) require a contractor tax exposure.

Quarterly provisional CIT payments due no later than the 30th day after the end of each quarter; the annual finalization return is due no later than the last day of the 3rd month from the end of the fiscal year (typically 31 March for a calendar-year taxpayer, or the last day of month 3 after the fiscal year ends for approved fiscal years).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File thresholds apply to a related-party transaction taxpayer where total related-party revenue exceeds VND 50 billion (approximately USD 2 million) or where the transaction value exceeds VND 30 billion, under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP and Circular 105/2021/TT-BTC. Even if below thresholds, a taxpayer must prepare a TP dossier by 30 November for the previous fiscal year if it engages in controlled transactions; smaller taxpayers may complete the TP declaration form instead. In practice, all foreign-invested BPO entities must maintain contemporaneous TP documentation to protect against adjustment risk.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not maintain a codified 'Economic Substance Regulations' such as the UAE regime, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under Article 26 of Decree 132/2020/ND-CP and the substance-over-form principle in tax administration allow recharacterization of transactions lacking business purpose. A foreign-owned BPO call center must demonstrate substantive personnel in Vietnam, active decision-making, contractual risk assumption, and actual operational control over telecommunication and CRM systems; failure to evidence substance for intercompany service charges may lead to full disallowance of the cost, especially for fees routed to tax havens.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine up to 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount under Law No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree 125/2020/ND-CP; criminal prosecution under the Penal Code for tax evasion involving amounts over VND 100 million yields fines up to VND 4.5 billion and imprisonment up to 3-7 years for organized or large-scale evasion, plus an obligation to pay the full underpaid tax and interest.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives, financial directors, and authorized signatories risk criminal liability if they knew or ordered the misdeclaration; the court can impose a cumulative sentence, travel bans, freezing of personal assets, and restitution orders; tax administration may issue a coercive collection order against the director's personal bank accounts if the company is dissolved or asset-emptied.
Shell Company and Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the entity's income to the true economic owner; disallowance of all intercompany deductions; application of a deemed profit determination under Decree 132/2020; penalties for false tax declaration up to 3 times the tax shortfall and additional late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: Director may be jointly liable for the corporate tax debt when the corporate veil is pierced under Vietnamese law due to fraud or abusive use of a legal entity; civil damages to tax authorities and creditors; travel restrictions and mandatory appearance orders can be issued to prevent flight.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment by GDT with additional CIT at 20% plus late-payment interest at 0.03% per day; administrative penalty equal to 20% of the underpaid tax where the mispricing is not fraud, and up to 3 times for fraudulent concealment; compulsory filing of TP documentation with adjusted returns.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false TP documentation or authorize circular transactions may be subject to criminal tax evasion provisions; personal assets can be attached through enforcement procedures for the company's unpaid tax; foreign directors can be subject to entry bans or denial of exit visas pending settlement.
AML and Payment-Rail Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering Law (effective 2023), failure to report unusual transactions results in fines up to VND 200 million and suspension of business activities for serious violations; banks must freeze accounts; criminal money-laundering charges attract imprisonment up to 10 years plus fines up to VND 100 million for individuals, and VND 3 billion for organizations.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners of the Vietnamese LLC may be criminally prosecuted for facilitating money laundering if they knowingly process illicit proceeds; personal travel bans, asset forfeiture, and Social Insurance or tax clearance refusal may block departure; the State Bank of Vietnam can blacklist controllers from maintaining payment-service relationships.
