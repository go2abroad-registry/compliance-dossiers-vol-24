Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: http://www.customs.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must apply for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by filing Form TIN-01 with the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department, accompanied by certified copies of the Certificate of Incorporation, Registered Agent certificate, and valid passport for each director. The International Company (IC) is registered as a tax-exempt non-resident entity; no VAT registration is available or required. Processing takes approximately 5 business days.

The International Company (IC) is registered as a tax-exempt non-resident entity; no VAT registration is available or required. Processing takes approximately 5 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. The Vanuatu International Company is statutorily tax-exempt; there is no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election mechanism. No election to be treated as a pass-through or entity-level taxpayer is recognised under Vanuatu law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT, GST, or sales and use tax is imposed in Vanuatu.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs import duties and export duties (where applicable) are administered under the Customs Tariff Act, payable at clearance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – because Vanuatu levies no VAT/GST, there is no zero-rating mechanism, no export refund claim, and no VAT registration trigger. Exports of surplus commercial aircraft parts are exempt from any export duty under applicable customs law, subject to documentation requirements.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice in English
  • Packing List
  • Registered Agent Certificate (for International Company)

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping requirement is imposed. Customs declarations are processed via UNCTAD's ASYCUDA World system for import/export clearance only.

No VAT zero-rating or refund clawback mechanism exists within Vanuatu. Fraudulent customs declarations (including undervaluation or misdescription of exported goods) are subject to penalties of up to 300% of duties evaded, confiscation of goods, revocation of trade licenses, and administrative fines under the Customs Act. False filings under the International Companies Act attract separate civil penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is administered by the RBV through a managed arrangement against a trade-weighted basket of major partner currencies. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted for non-residents, and an International Company (IC) constituted under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is treated as non-resident, exempt from resident capital-account controls; profit and dividend remittances require no exchange-control approval and are exempt from withholding taxes under the IC tax-exemption regime. Trade liquidity is strong because settlement is USD-denominated and may be held offshore in unrestricted foreign-currency accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds earned by non-resident ICs; USD trade receipts may be retained in full. Resident exporters are subject only to administrative reporting through authorized dealers, and the IC's surplus-aircraft-parts receivables may flow directly into USD operational or escrow accounts without VUV conversion.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the Central Bank and residual exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 77]; daily exchange-control administration is delegated to authorized dealers (licensed commercial banks) under RBV oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium, conditional. Select EU/UK-licensed EMIs accept Vanuatu ICs for USD clearing only where the IC supplies a VFSC registered-agent referral, audited financial statements, and a civil-aviation trade dossier; most tier-1 EMIs decline remote offshore ICs under de-risking policies. EMIs cannot deliver commercial LC, aviation escrow, or physical trade-desk instruments, so they are suitable only as capped auxiliary disbursement rails, never as the primary clearing bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% general corporate income tax as no CIT statute is currently enacted; International Companies are explicitly exempt from any form of Vanuatu income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax under ss. 104-108 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%; Vanuatu has no dividend withholding tax and no act governing income tax withholding. Dividends declared by an International Company to foreign shareholders are paid free of Vanuatu tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable under Vanuatu law: no Local File, Master File, Country-by-Country Reporting, or documentary threshold is codified in VUV because Vanuatu does not impose a corporate income tax. Foreign tax administrations may nevertheless impute arm's-length pricing on the IC's outbound aircraft-parts sales under their own transfer pricing regimes.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance (International Companies) Regulations apply to a Vanuatu IC that is tax resident and carries on a 'relevant activity'; distribution and service business, including aviation spares trading, is considered a relevant activity. Such an IC must file an economic substance declaration with VFSC demonstrating that it is directed and managed in Vanuatu, that core income-generating activities (e.g., contract negotiation, procurement, inventory management) take place in Vanuatu, and that it has adequate premises, VFSC-licensed registered agent, and full-time employees or data systems. Failure to satisfy economic substance is not cured by the zero-tax regime and exposes the IC to financial penalties, strike-off, and information exchange.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Sham Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no corporate income tax to evade, but the conduct facilitates evasion abroad. Sanctions are imposed by the destination jurisdiction: reassessment, interest, 100% anti-avoidance penalties, and criminal prosecution for intentional foreign tax evasion. In Vanuatu, VFSC may impose an administrative penalty under the Economic Substance Regulations for filing a false substance declaration.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors can be held personally liable for unlawful trading where the IC is a vehicle of fraud; courts may pierce the corporate veil and issue confiscation orders, travel bans, and custodial sentences of up to seven years for fraudulent tax evasion in the foreign jurisdiction.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: VFSC administrative penalties for non-compliance with the Economic Substance (International Companies) Regulations range from an initial penalty of up to VUV 500,000 and a continuing penalty of VUV 100,000 per month; the IC may be struck off and dissolved and its directors may be disqualified.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to the extent of assets extracted from the shell, joint and several with the company for penalties arising from false declarations, and may be prohibited from leaving Vanuatu pending an economic substance inquiry.
Unlicensed Domestic Trading and Customs Offences

Statutory sanction: Offence under the Business Licence Act [Cap. 163] and the International Companies Act: fines of up to VUV 200,000 per transaction, forfeiture of goods, revocation of the business licence, and if customs duties are evaded, criminal liability under the Customs Act including imprisonment and exclusion from procurement.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise unlicensed domestic sales are individually fined and can be imprisoned for up to 12 months; the Vanuatu Customs Comptroller may issue a travel ban while the company is under investigation and may seize directors' personal assets used in the unlawful business.
AML and Economic Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Financial Transactions Reporting Act (FTRA) 2020: monetary penalties up to VUV 15 million, asset freezing, and imprisonment up to five years. The VFSC can also suspend or revoke the registered agent's licence and cause the IC to be wound up.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners are individually liable if they 'knowingly aid' money laundering; personal criminal fines of VUV 10 million and custodial sentences up to five years apply, and any director under investigation may be barred from travelling abroad.
