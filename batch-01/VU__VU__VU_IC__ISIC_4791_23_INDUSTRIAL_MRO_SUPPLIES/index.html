Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue (VCIR), Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official online portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a general TIN. Upon incorporation, the VFSC assigns a company registration number; the IC's income-tax exemption is automatic and does not require a taxpayer ID. If the IC conducts a taxable activity in or from Vanuatu and meets or elects VAT registration, it must submit the prescribed VAT Application for Registration to the Commissioner of Inland Revenue, attaching the VFSC certificate of incorporation, registered-office consent, business licence, proposed supply details and identity documents of directors and owners. The Commissioner then issues a VAT Registration Number, which serves as the tax ID for VAT purposes. Non-VAT-registered International Companies are not assigned a TIN, and no annual income-tax registration or filing obligation arises.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — An International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a general TIN. Upon incorporation, the VFSC assigns a company registration number; the IC's income-tax exemption is automatic and does not require a taxpayer ID. If the IC conducts a taxable activity in or from Vanuatu and meets or elects VAT registration, it must submit the prescribed VAT Application for Registration to the Commissioner of Inland Revenue, attaching the VFSC certificate of incorporation, registered-office consent, business licence, proposed supply details and identity documents of directors and owners. The Commissioner then issues a VAT Registration Number, which serves as the tax ID for VAT purposes. Non-VAT-registered International Companies are not assigned a TIN, and no annual income-tax registration or filing obligation arises.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime. The International Company is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu direct taxes under the International Companies Act and cannot elect to be ordinarily taxable, pass-through, disregarded, or treated as a tax resident for income-tax purposes. The only relevant election is voluntary VAT registration below the compulsory threshold; a registered IC may also make statutory VAT elections, including zero-rating of exports and recovery of input VAT, under the Value Added Tax Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration applies to a supplier whose taxable supplies in any 12-month period reach or exceed Vatu 5,000,000 (VT 5,000,000). Voluntary registration is available below the threshold. Zero-rated exports count as taxable supplies for threshold measurement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply of industrial MRO goods qualifies for the 0% rate where the buyer is a non-resident, title and risk pass outside Vanuatu, the physical goods are exported from Vanuatu to the destination country within the required period, and the supplier holds contemporaneous export evidence. Certain offshore services, international freight, and services directly related to exported goods also qualify. The zero-rating benefit is lost if goods are diverted to the local Vanuatu market or the exporter fails to maintain proof of export.

  • Customs Export Declaration (approved ASYCUDA World outward declaration showing exporter, consignee, HS code, quantity, value and departure date)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment from Vanuatu to the destination country
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and purchase order or export contract evidencing the international sale at 0% VAT
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or credit-card settlement record evidencing payment by the non-resident customer
  • VAT zero-rated tax invoice issued by the exporter to the customer
  • Copy of the International Company incorporation or VFSC registration and, if registered, the VAT registration certificate
  • Evidence of delivery or order fulfilment from the MRO e-commerce platform, including final tracking status

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing, tax-authority API clearance, or digital fiscal stamping regime is in force. VAT tax invoices must contain the statutory particulars and be issued within the timeline required by the VAT Act. Customs export declarations must be lodged electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs clearance system; an approved electronic export declaration and clearance reference is required to support the zero-rated export and any input VAT refund.

The Commissioner may retrospectively reassess a purported export as a domestic taxable supply at 12.5% and claw back refunds or input credits if export documentation is missing, inconsistent, or goods are diverted into Vanuatu. Penalties include default and penalty tax on the unpaid VAT amount, recovery of overclaimed refunds, and prosecution for fraudulent evasion or knowingly false zero-rating declarations under the VAT Act. On conviction, substantial fines of up to VT 5,000,000 and imprisonment of responsible persons may be imposed, in addition to director liability and loss of zero-rating or export certification. Export substantiation records and FX remittance evidence should be retained for at least 5 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The VUV is freely convertible for current account transactions, trade in goods and services, and remittance of profits to non-residents without quantitative restriction or prior RBV approval. Capital account operations by resident entities are subject to exchange control authorisation, but the target International Company is statutorily exempt: Section 32 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] disapplies the Exchange Control Act to International Companies and their operations. Consequently, the IC may hold, convert, and transfer USD, AUD, EUR, and NZD balances without RBV consent, and profit distribution is wholly free of Vanuatu income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, and exchange control gateways. Practical hurdles are limited to correspondent bank due diligence and UBO transparency requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the general Exchange Control Act [Cap. 96] regime applicable to Vanuatu resident exporters, export proceeds must be received within six months of the date of shipment and surrendered to a RBV-authorised dealer within one month of receipt, with a mandatory conversion ratio of 100% of eligible foreign currency denominated export earnings unless a specific exemption is granted. Critical carve-out: International Companies are outside the scope of the Exchange Control Act by virtue of Section 32 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; therefore the IC is not subject to any statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio and may retain export receivables indefinitely in foreign currency accounts onshore or offshore.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], is the sole monetary and exchange control authority for the Vatu (VUV), acting under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 96] and the Exchange Control Regulations. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the regulatory authority for International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] and serves as the AML/CFT supervisor for financial institutions other than banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility as a supplemental collection and pay-out rail, but not as a substitute for an onshore RBV-regulated bank account. Vanuatu has no EMI or digital banking licensing framework; VFSC does not issue EMI licences. The IC may commercially use reputable overseas EMIs (e.g., FCA- or MAS-authorised) for USD/AUD settlement to end-customers, subject to strict correspondent due diligence, sanctions screening, and the EMIs own risk appetite toward Vanuatu-incorporated entities. Regulatory comfort is limited and many EMIs will decline onboarding until the IC has established a track record with a Tier 1 Vanuatu bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity contributions are routed by SWIFT MT103/MT103 GPI from the shareholder's account to the IC's designated USD account at an RBV-authorised dealer bank in Vanuatu. The wire field 50/remittance information must state 'equity injection / share capital contribution under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]' using ISO purpose code 'CAPT' (Capital) or legacy purpose code 'K', accompanied by the latest certificate of incumbency, a certified board resolution authorising issue and allotment of shares, and written confirmation from the registered agent for VFSC filing. The receiving bank will verify standard settlement instructions (SSI), affirm the exchange-control exemption, perform positive-match UBO and sanctions screening, and credit the funds only after the corporate packet is complete; no RBV consent is required for the International Company to receive capital.Trade settlements are executed through (i) SWIFT MT103/MT103 GPI priority wires for high-volume, low-value MRO purchases and freight disbursements; (ii) commercial documentary letters of credit (sight or usance, on CFR/CIF terms) issued via MT700 and covered by MT202/MT103 cover payments through the bank's global correspondent network for large supplier framework contracts; and (iii) domestic payments to Vanuatu suppliers via the RBV-operated interbank payment and settlement systems to the extent onshore vendors are engaged. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Vanuatu transfer-pricing statute prescribes a threshold; no Local File, Master File, or Country-by-Country Report is payable or filed in Vanuatu. The IC must nonetheless keep at the registered office sufficient accounting records to evidence that MRO resale margins and intercompany service fees are established at arm's length.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not administer a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations filing regime for standard International Companies, but the IC Act imposes substantive territorial constraints: a registered office in Vanuatu, books and records physically present in Vanuatu, and prohibition of any trade with Vanuatu residents. The MRO e-commerce contracting, invoicing, and payment flows must therefore be positioned offshore. If the IC incidentally derives Vanuatu-source revenue or IP-related income, it forfeits the exempt treatment and becomes exposed to VAT at 12.5% and to potential income tax should any future direct-tax law be enacted.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Exemption Abuse / Domestic Trade Under IC Cover

Statutory sanction: Automatic loss of exempt status; the Commissioner of Customs and Inland Revenue assesses VAT at 12.5% on undeclared Vanuatu sales, plus a recovery penalty of up to 150% of the tax due and statutory interest compounding daily.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the prohibited domestic trade are jointly and severally liable for the assessed VAT, penalties, and interest; the court may issue travel bans, freezing orders over personal assets, and disqualification under the International Companies Act.
Shell Company Masking / Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 per infraction plus mandatory striking-off proceedings; intentional concealment also constitutes a predicate offense under the Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act and carries imprisonment of up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Each director is personally liable for the VUV 1,000,000 fine; courts routinely issue departure-prohibition orders preventing directors from leaving Vanuatu while the fine is unpaid, and nominee directors face criminal sentencing equal to that of a natural-person beneficial owner.
AML/CFT Breach in Trade Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Fines up to VUV 30,000,000 per violation, freezing of corporate bank accounts and the e-commerce payment gateway, and administrative strike-off of the IC with consequential nullity of outstanding purchase orders.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly structured payment flows to avoid VFSC-discipline are personally liable to fines of up to VUV 30,000,000 and imprisonment for up to 15 years; laundered corporate assets are recoverable from the directors' personal estates, and travel bans apply pending confiscation.
Intercompany Margin Manipulation / Implied Transfer-Pricing Evasion

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no domestic TP adjustment provision, but the VFSC substance review and the automatic exchange of information under the multilateral transparency framework will flag the margin imbalance to the controllers' home tax authority; the foreign authority may apply secondary adjustments of up to 100% of the underpaid tax, plus late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve a zero-margin intercompany structure knowing that a foreign tax benefit is the driver become exposed to foreign-jurisdiction director liability, including civil tax-evasion judgments enforceable against the IC's assets under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act, travel bans in the home jurisdiction, and imprisonment of up to 7 years for tax fraud.
