Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue Authority (VRA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, successor to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue; primary taxpayer portal: https://vra.gov.vu, with customs declarations processed through the ASYCUDA World national single-window system.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident undertaking Vanuatu retail operations must appoint a registered office/agent, incorporate a local ordinary company under the Companies Act 2012 (or register a foreign company branch), obtain a Business License under the Business License Act [Cap 245] from the municipal licensing body, and complete VRA registration using Form VAT 001 where taxable supplies exceed the statutory threshold; the entity must also obtain an importer code in ASYCUDA World for customs clearance. An International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes and is not required to register for income tax, VAT, or business license purposes because it is prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents; the IC pays only the VFSC annual registration fee and may hold trademarks, offshore procurement contracts, and foreign-purchase logistics, while the physical supermarket trading entity in Vanuatu must be the local ordinary company.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Vanuatu International Companies benefit from a statutory 20-year exemption from all Vanuatu taxes, including income tax, VAT, business license tax, and customs duty on qualifying imports, pursuant to the International Companies Act [Cap 222]; an ordinary Vanuatu company defaults to Territorial_Scope taxation with a nil corporate income tax rate but remains liable to VAT and selected withholding taxes on Vanuatu-source consumption).

Available election forms

Vanuatu does not operate an IRC check-the-box, CFC look-through, or hybrid-mismatch election regime. Available elections include: (i) conversion of an International Company into an ordinary company under Section 66 of the International Companies Act, thereby electing into VAT, business license, and customs duty exposure; (ii) voluntary VAT registration by a taxable person below the compulsory threshold under the VAT Act No. 8 of 1998, as amended; (iii) re-domiciliation election under the International Companies (Amendment) Act allowing a foreign company to enter Vanuatu as either an IC or an ordinary company; and (iv) group VAT registration or designation of a single representative for multi-branch retail and combined warehousing/export operations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 1,000,000 (approximately USD 8,300) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period, or a reasonable expectation of exceeding VT 1,000,000 in the next 12 months; a person importing taxable goods must register for VAT before the first import even when below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Vanuatu no later than 180 days after supply, held outside Vanuatu, and evidenced by an approved customs export declaration qualify for the 0% VAT rate under Section 14 of the VAT Act; zero-rating also applies to services directly connected with exported goods and services supplied to non-residents for use wholly outside Vanuatu. A supermarket grocery hub may zero-rate wholesale exports of Asian condiments and specialty vegetables, ship-store and aircraft-supply sales outside the customs territory, and offshore services, but may not zero-rate over-the-counter retail supplies delivered in Vanuatu. The exporter must be VAT-registered, invoice at 0% with an explicit export statement, and substantiate the physical exit with the statutory document bundle; input VAT credits are recoverable solely through the quarterly VAT refund/offset mechanism supervised by the VRA, which may require export-pool accounting.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Cusdec/ASYCUDA World) endorsed by customs and referencing the Bill of Lading or Air Waybill number
  • Commercial invoice endorsed with the 0% VAT export notation and corresponding packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a Vanuatu bank, or a SWIFT MT103/FX transaction record, evidencing receipt of foreign-currency payment from the buyer
  • Trader's supporting proof of export, including landing certificate or port-outward station records for transshipments through Port Vila or Luganville

Vanuatu does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, certified electronic invoicing, or digital tax stamping. VAT-registered suppliers must issue prescribed tax invoices and maintain a seven-year audit trail; customs export/import and bond movements are cleared through the ASYCUDA World platform with electronic batch filing, while VAT returns and refund claims are submitted via the VRA e-services portal. Anti-fraud controls rely on ASYCUDA audit trails, risk scoring, and reconciliation of border declarations against accounting records rather than on a continuous-control e-invoicing framework.

The VRA conducts post-shipment export refund audits against the seven-year record-keeping obligation. A claim of 0% export VAT without actual export, with fictitious bills of lading, or overstated input credits triggers: (i) repayment of the entire refunded sum together with statutory interest; (ii) a civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded under the VAT Act and the Customs Act; (iii) fines of up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 12 months for false statements or obstruction of tax officers, with criminal prosecution available for forged customs stamps, sham foreign-purchaser arrangements, or fraudulent refund schemes; and (iv) deregistration and immediate reassessment of VAT on the underlying domestic supplies, including clawback of any offset or refund already granted.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is effectively fully convertible. Both current account and capital account transactions are substantially unrestricted: there is no foreign exchange allocation rationing, no surrender requirement, and no export certificate precondition. Profit and dividend remittances, capital repatriation, and liquidation proceeds are freely transferable once corporate records, tax status, and banking compliance (source of funds) are evidenced. The RBV does not impose exchange-rate bands; the VUV trades on a managed floating basis against the US dollar, Australian dollar, and other reference currencies, with market pricing agreed between licensed commercial banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory obligation to surrender export receipts to the RBV or to convert them into VUV within any prescribed timeline. An exporter (including a trading entity that is structurally an International Company) is permitted to invoice in a foreign currency, maintain foreign-currency accounts at a licensed domestic bank, and determine the timing and amount of conversion to VUV based on treasury requirements. There is no mandatory conversion ratio or holding period under the current legal regime.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — also supervises the Vanuatu National Payment System and administers such exchange control and prudential requirements as remain in force under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 81].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility — with significant legal and operational constraints. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed as banks by the RBV and cannot obtain a direct VUV clearing-bank code. An offshore EMI may be used solely as a payment-interface layer funded by transfers to and from a physical, RBV-licensed domestic commercial bank account. Because Vanuatu operates a real-time gross settlement and interbank clearing system limited to licensed banks, all VUV-denominated liquidity must maintain a local banking anchor. The VFSC does not currently confer a direct settlement or clearing membership to pure non-bank EMIs.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections should be routed by SWIFT (MT103/MT202) to a VUV or foreign-currency account held at an RBV-licensed commercial bank, with a compliant purpose code (e.g., capital contribution, shareholder loan, or intercompany working capital) quoted in the payment narrative. Remittance details must clearly reflect the underlying legal basis and match the bank KYC file to avoid freeze or return. For larger injections, pre-advised deposit confirmation, a bank-approved company resolution, and a source-of-funds pack are recommended. The receiving bank has no exchange-control consent requirement because Vanuatu does not require currency allocation permits, but the bank’s internal AML/CFT checks apply.Settlements to foreign suppliers of Asian grocery goods are executed via (i) SWIFT MT103 cash transfers from the VUV/fx treasury account, or (ii) commercial documentary letters of credit (sight or usance) issued by the local bank and advised to the supplier’s bank. Domestic Vanuatu payments may be executed by cheque, POS debit, bank transfer, or the upgraded RTGS/ACH-like rails for interbank settlement. For retained VUV liquidity, overnight deposits and term deposits with the domestic licensed bank may be used; cash conversion into USD/AUD/NZD for international supplier settlement should follow bank treasury desk FX execution.VUV/USD deliverable forward: standard hedging instrument for confirmed supplier invoices and expected trade margins.VUV/AUD deliverable forward: useful given the Australia–Vanuatu import corridor and the AUD-basket peg tendency of the VUV.Cross-currency swap (VUV/USD or VUV/AUD): bilaterally contracted with a licensed commercial bank to fix the exchange conversion on multi-currency treasury balances.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) executed offshore: permissible as an offshore-curated instrument for entities with a strong documented rationale, but not routinely available from domestic Vanuatu banks and subject to the RBV’s absence of a dedicated over-the-counter derivatives market.

Box 1 – Output VAT VAT charged on taxable retail sales of groceries and condiments. Non-resident legal entity must appoint a fiscal representative to file if local VAT registration arises. Late VAT filing attracts a VUV 2,000 fixed penalty plus VUV 1,000 per day; understatement of liability carries a 25% surcharge; fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence.
Box 2 – Input VAT VAT incurred on eligible business purchases and importations of inventory. Only reclaim input VAT directly related to taxable local supplies. Late VAT filing attracts a VUV 2,000 fixed penalty plus VUV 1,000 per day; understatement of liability carries a 25% surcharge; fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence.
Box 3 – Net VAT Payable Positive excess of output VAT over input VAT payable to the Vanuatu Government. Ensure monthly filing reflects actual sales; zero-filing is not accepted where physical retail is occurring. Late VAT filing attracts a VUV 2,000 fixed penalty plus VUV 1,000 per day; understatement of liability carries a 25% surcharge; fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None – Vanuatu does not impose transfer pricing documentation, Local File, or Master File requirements. Customs valuation and VAT rules still require transactions to reflect market value.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) are applicable only to International Companies deriving income from specified relevant activities (such as IP holding, financing, headquarters, or distribution and service centres). Physical grocery retail in Vanuatu is outside the exempt offshore IC regime and is not a permitted relevant activity; it constitutes prohibited local business requiring a domestic retail license and full tax registration. The ESR test is therefore met only as a negative finding: the IC structure itself is invalid for the stated supermarket purpose.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorized Local Business / Ultra Vires Activity

Statutory sanction: Immediate compulsory strike-off, revocation of the licensed agent's authority, forfeiture of the company's assets to the Vanuatu Government, and a fine of up to VUV 1,000,000.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for all contractual debts and statutory obligations arising from the illegal local trade; courts may impose travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment for up to 2 years.
Tax Evasion and VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Evasion penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded plus compound interest, machinery and premises seizure, and criminal prosecution with cumulative fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly direct or approve the fraud are personally liable for the short-paid tax and penalties; continuing misconduct triggers director disqualification, travel prohibitions, and imprisonment up to 3 years.
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Compulsory reclassification of the entity as a local taxable company, retroactive assessment of income tax at 0% is irrelevant, but VAT and all unpaid fees become due, and VFSC may strike off the shell and fine the registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally responsible for the company's unpaid supplier claims and government dues under the 'corporate veil piercing' doctrine, and may be prohibited from acting as directors of any Vanuatu company for 5 years.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Statutory penalty under Vanuatu's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act: fines up to VUV 50,000,000 for the entity, plus confiscation of the tainted property, and suspension or revocation of the retail license.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers face personal fines up to VUV 10,000,000 and custodial sentences up to 14 years for wilful AML breaches; travel bans are automatically available to the authorities to secure prosecution.
