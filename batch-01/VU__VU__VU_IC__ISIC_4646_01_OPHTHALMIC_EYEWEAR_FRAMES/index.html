Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management — portal: https://customs.gov.vu; corporate registry: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — portal: https://vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] through a VFSC-licensed registered agent; the VFSC-issued Certificate of Incorporation (company number) serves as the sole statutory tax-identification document. No TIN or national-tax file is issued or required for a tax-exempt IC. Only if the entity voluntarily engages in a domestic taxable activity must it register for VAT with the DCIR (Form VAT 1) and obtain a municipal business licence (e.g., Port Vila); hiring local staff additionally triggers employer payroll-tax registration at 4%.

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 3,000,000 (Vanuatu Vatu) of annual taxable supplies triggers compulsory VAT registration; voluntary registration is permitted below threshold. A pure offshore wholesale IC distributing ophthalmic eyeglass frames has no domestic taxable nexus and cannot be compelled to register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply and to services supplied to non-residents and consumed wholly offshore. For the IC, all offshore wholesale sales of acetate eyeglass frames are statutorily exempt from any tax, with or without VAT registration; no FX repatriation deadline applies (no exchange-control regime), so export proceeds may be retained offshore indefinitely.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic declaration, Form E1)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export within the statutory period
  • Certificate of Origin (only where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)
  • Evidence of settlement (SWIFT/MT103 or platform-payout record) for refund substantiation

None: no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping regime exists. Export declarations are filed through the ASYCUDA World customs platform; VAT invoices are issued on paper or private software without state validation.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating claims by a VAT-registered exporter trigger de-registration, repayment of refunds, additional assessment plus penalty up to 200% of the VAT evaded, fines, and imprisonment; retroactive clawback may reach back 5 years. Unrepatriated FX is not a clawback trigger: Vanuatu imposes no exchange controls, and the 0-day statutory deadline reflects this nil repatriation obligation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu is an IMF Article VIII member: current-account payments and transfers are free from governmental restrictions. There is no separate exchange-control law; the VUV is managed against a trade-weighted basket of major currencies. Profit remittances, dividends, and internal corporate transfers may be freely effected via authorized banks, subject only to standard AML/CTF and source-of-funds verification. Capital-account transactions are also generally unrestricted, although banks may apply internal due-diligence thresholds and require supporting board resolutions and underlying trade contracts for large inbound equity injections.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement. No mandatory conversion ratio applies, and no fixed statutory timeline exists for repatriation or conversion of export receipts. Authorized banks may require declarative reporting under RBV statistical data-collection powers, but export proceeds may be held in VUV or any freely accepted foreign currency at the exporter's commercial discretion.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — the sole monetary authority and regulator of the Vatu (VUV), operating in conjunction with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for non-bank financial institutions and international company service providers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore digital EMI-only relationships. Vanuatu does not operate a distinct e-money institution (EMI) license track; overseas digital EMIs are not authorised to open domestic settlement accounts or provide local payment services unless separately licensed by the VFSC as a financial institution or international banking entity. Tier-1 commercial banks generally decline to provision operational banking services to offshore EMI platforms for corporate clients due to regulatory ambiguity and correspondent-bank de-risking pressure.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Tax-exempt International Company (IC) with nominee director or shareholder

Defense evidence: Banks require (i) the IC's certificate of incorporation, (ii) a current certificate of incumbency naming the ultimate beneficial owner, (iii) the full registered-agent and registered-office agreement, (iv) an executed declaration of trust or nominee acknowledgment, (v) a group structure chart demonstrating actual control and economic ownership, and (vi) a corporate resolution ratifying the account mandate.
High-value cross-border wires inconsistent with industry risk profile

Root cause: High-value cross-border wires inconsistent with industry risk profile

Defense evidence: Present purchase orders and invoices from reputable eyewear manufacturers, full trade contracts, inspection certificates, shipping logistics documents, customs declarations, and a monthly cash-flow projection aligned with the ISIC 4646 wholesale distribution model.
Source of funds from opaque private investment vehicles

Root cause: Source of funds from opaque private investment vehicles

Defense evidence: Deliver six months of bank statements from the funding entity, audited financial statements, tax receipts in the jurisdiction of origin, a notarised statement of source of wealth signed by the beneficial owner, and independent asset-history documentation where the accumulating capital exceeds USD 1,000,000.
Multiple signatories or directors with politically exposed person (PEP) status

Root cause: Multiple signatories or directors with politically exposed person (PEP) status

Defense evidence: Submit official PEP status declarations, independent risk-assessment reports, proof of legitimate remuneration, legal-source letters from each PEP regime, and documented corporate approval of any high-risk signatory's account authority.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder funding from foreign jurisdictions must be effected via SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) or MT202 (bank-to-bank funds transfer) to a commercial bank account in Vanuatu, with the purpose code clearly marked 'EQUITY CAPITAL INJECTION' or 'LOAN PRINCIPAL UNDER SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT' where applicable. The receiving bank requires a board resolution authorising the capital increase or subordinated loan, the signed shareholder agreement or subscription contract, an updated register of members, and a statement confirming the funds originate from a non-sanctioned banking institution. 0%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends or other corporate distributions. A Vanuatu International Company may distribute profits to non-resident shareholders without any domestic dividend WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No statutory CIT deadline exists. Tax-exempt status does not require periodic income tax declarations. The relevant compliance deadlines are VFSC annual fee and registry deadlines, plus VAT/customs obligations if taxable local activity commences.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual VFSC fee and return are due on or before each anniversary of the International Company's incorporation. A short administrative grace period may apply, but no general extension is granted without VFSC consent.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0. Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and has not enacted a statutory transfer pricing documentation regime. Therefore no Local File, Master File, or CbC reporting threshold applies in Vanuatu. For customs and VAT purposes, related-party import prices must nonetheless be based on the transaction value and an arm's length price to avoid revaluation.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not currently administer a formal Economic Substance Regulations filing regime comparable to BVI or Cayman for International Companies. No annual ESR report is filed with VFSC solely on the basis of holding an International Company. However, under FATF/OECD transparency standards and VFSC regulatory practice, the International Company must avoid being characterized as a shell: it should maintain accounting records, board minutes, a bank account, a licensed registered agent, and commercial documentation for the wholesale eyewear frame transactions. The absence of genuine substance may trigger foreign CFC rules, GAAR adjustments, refusal of banking services, and deregistration risk.
