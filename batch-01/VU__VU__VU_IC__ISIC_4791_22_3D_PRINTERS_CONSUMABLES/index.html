Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), administered under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; online portal: https://customs.gov.vu and https://www.vnurevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity seeking to establish a Vanuatu International Company (IC) must file incorporation documents with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation. As an IC is statutorily exempt from income tax, no national tax identification number is required for income tax purposes. If the IC later makes taxable supplies within Vanuatu or imports/export goods, it must apply to VRC for a VAT identification number and a Customs Business Code; otherwise no TIN/VAT ID is procured.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity seeking to establish a Vanuatu International Company (IC) must file incorporation documents with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation. As an IC is statutorily exempt from income tax, no national tax identification number is required for income tax purposes. If the IC later makes taxable supplies within Vanuatu or imports/export goods, it must apply to VRC for a VAT identification number and a Customs Business Code; otherwise no TIN/VAT ID is procured.

Tax_Exempt_Zone

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Vatu (VT) 4,000,000 annual taxable turnover; however, an International Company engaged exclusively in offshore business is outside the VAT net unless it makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Vanuatu are zero-rated under the Value Added Tax Act No. 12 of 1998, provided the goods are physically removed from Vanuatu within the prescribed period and evidenced by verified export documentation. Offshore services supplied to non-residents outside Vanuatu are also zero-rated. The International Company's tax-exempt status supersedes VAT liabilities but does not create a refund entitlement for input VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (approved by VRC)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing consignee outside Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds

No mandatory electronic invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance regime. VAT-registered businesses may issue manual or electronic invoices. Customs entries are filed electronically via the Vanuatu Customs Automated System (VCAS); no digital stamping requirement is imposed.

False or fraudulent export zero-rating claims are subject to penalties under the VAT Act and Customs Act, including imposition of the unpaid VAT plus a penalty of up to 200% of the tax, monetary fines up to VT 10,000,000, and seizure of goods. In cases of deliberate evasion, VRC may issue retrospective assessments for up to 5 years and institute criminal proceedings with imprisonment not exceeding 7 years.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current-account and capital-account transactions in foreign currency are substantially liberalized. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes and may freely hold, receive, and disburse USD, AUD, EUR, and other convertible currencies in domestic accounts. The domestic Vatu (VUV) has a managed float against a currency basket and possesses a thin offshore market, so NDF-type exposure exists but direct convertibility is effectively unrestricted onshore. Profit and dividend remittance hurdles: nil — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties paid by an IC to non-residents; a board resolution authorizing distribution and standard bank remittance forms are the only practical requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory repatriation timeline, and no conversion ratio for export proceeds earned by an IC. All receipts from the online sale of 3D printers and consumables may be retained in full in foreign currency in the IC's offshore or domestic foreign-currency account, and may be applied directly to supplier disbursements, operating costs, or distributions without RBV approval.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — the sole monetary authority and bank supervisor, administering the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 89] and the International Banking Act [Cap. 251]; all licensed commercial banks act as authorized dealers in foreign exchange.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity or shareholder-loan injection is executed via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's personal or affiliated company account to the IC's USD operational account at the Vanuatu bank. Payment reference must state 'Share capital injection — International Companies Act Cap. 222' or 'Director/shareholder loan' as applicable. No RBV exchange-control approval or filing is required for capital inflow; the receiving bank will perform standard source-of-funds verification and may request the remitter's bank statement, a board resolution authorizing issuance of shares/loan acceptance, and an updated register of members. For sub-USD 50,000 injections, bank exception-approval is generally automatic; larger injections attract enhanced due diligence but remain structurally permissible. If the IC opens a merchant-of-record arrangement abroad, intercompany capital flows should be documented through an intercompany loan or prepayment framework to match corporate treasury records with the payment gateway's settlement ledger.Supplier settlements are effected primarily by SWIFT MT103 outgoing wires from the IC's foreign-currency account for recurring purchase orders below USD 25,000, supported by proforma invoices, packing lists, and courier waybills. For major inventory purchases above USD 25,000, a sight documentary Letter of Credit (Commercial LC) issued by ANZ or BSP Vanuatu against supplier documents (invoice, packing list, clean bill of lading) provides the safest clearing rail and satisfies correspondent-bank trade-compliance checks. USD-denominated payments clear through New York correspondent accounts; AUD-denominated payments clear through Sydney; all are subject to OFAC/UN sanctions filtering. No RBV permit is required for outbound trade payments, but banks require the IC to maintain a complete audit trail of every disbursement, including the underlying sales orders, evidence of stock received, and downstream customer invoices, to rebut trade-based money-laundering queries.Outright FX forwards between VUV and USD/AUD/EUR with RBV-licensed authorized dealer banks (ANZ, BSP) for commercial settlement hedging, maturities typically up to 12 months.Cross-currency swaps on USD/AUD/EUR principal amounts executed bilaterally with licensed commercial banks, permitted for non-resident ICs and treated as balance-sheet hedging rather than speculative dealing.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing the VUV (VUV/USD and VUV/AUD), available through international brokers and booked in HK/SG, considered permissible and legally enforceable against the Vanuatu IC provided the IC retains board-approved hedging policies and documented underlying commercial exposure.Natural hedging through multi-currency accounts (maintaining USD and AUD balances to match supplier and customer currency flows) is expressly permitted and recommended as the primary low-cost treasury posture, given the absence of any requirement to convert settlement proceeds into VUV.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu International Companies (ICs) are fully exempt from corporate income tax, capital gains tax, and any national income or profits tax under Section 2 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222]; no CIT is levied on worldwide or Vanuatu-source income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - Vanuatu has no Transfer Pricing Regulations, and no Local File, Master File or CbC Report thresholds are prescribed in VUV. For practical foreign-audit safety, benchmark studies and OECD TPG-compliant pricing records should be prepared where the IC invoices related offshore entities above VUV 30,000,000 (approx. USD 250,000) annually.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) apply to an IC that performs a 'Relevant Activity'; online retail distribution of 3D printers and resin consumables qualifies as a 'Distribution and Service Centre'. The IC must satisfy the CIGA (core income-generating activity) test in Vanuatu, meaning: (1) management meetings and board decisions physically held in Vanuatu, (2) adequate local staff and premises proportionate to turnover, (3) a local bank account, and (4) annual economic substance return filing to VFSC demonstrating Vanuatu operating expenditure. A pure letter-box or nominee-only operation fails ESR and exposes the IC to regulatory strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Sham Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Foreign CFC/PEG attribution resulting in 200% penalty surcharges on the disguised profit; in Vanuatu, VFSC has power to dissolve the IC for filing false declarations and to exchange beneficial ownership information with foreign tax authorities under TIEA provisions.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors personally liable for civil restitution of the fraudulently avoided tax; cross-border arrest warrants, Interpol red notices, travel bans, and imprisonment in the prosecuting jurisdiction; Vanuatu courts will enforce foreign restitution judgments under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act.
Shell Company and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty regime escalates from VUV 250,000 for non-filing to administrative fines up to VUV 20,000,000 for confirmed lack of substance; VFSC compulsory dissolution and automatic vesting of corporate assets in the Republic; referral of the IC's ownership records to foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors disqualified for up to 5 years from holding office in any Vanuatu entity; personal fines equal to the ESR penalty where the director knowingly signed false substance returns; personal asset forfeiture orders upon strike-off.
AML/CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Body corporate fines up to VUV 10,000,000 per offence for money laundering; freezing orders issued by the VFIU over the IC's Vanuatu bank account; revocation of the registered agent's licence if it tolerated the non-compliant activity.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers personally liable to imprisonment up to 15 years; travel bans preventing departure from Vanuatu pending trial; personal bank accounts and assets subject to confiscation under the Proceeds of Crime Act.
Prohibited Domestic Trading

Statutory sanction: Fine for each discrete offence plus court-ordered winding-up of the IC; mandatory forfeiture of all profits derived from domestic Vanuatu transactions; the registered agent is compelled to report the breach to VFSC.

Director personal liability: Directors ordered to make compensatory restitution to the company for diverted prohibited profits; restricted from departing Vanuatu while proceedings are pending; permanent disqualification from forming future Vanuatu vehicles where the breach was fraudulent.
Recurring Filing Defaults

Statutory sanction: Escalated late fees from VUV 5,000 to VUV 20,000; publication of strike-off notice in the Vanuatu Gazette; dissolution of the IC with all registered assets, bank balances and IP automatically vested in the Republic.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for the IC's outstanding VFSC debts upon dissolution; re-instatement orders require personal payment of all arrears plus a VUV 50,000 restoration fee; directors face an entry ban from registering any new Vanuatu company.
