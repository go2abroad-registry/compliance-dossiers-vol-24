Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Vanuatu International Company; authorised capital is expressed in the Memorandum, and no escrow deposit, capital importation, or paid-up capital verification is required. Shares may be issued for cash, a promissory note, or other property under s. 19 of the International Companies Act.

Funding & unlock path: Exempt for the registered office-only footprint. If a physical office is occupied in Vanuatu, the office must obtain a fire safety clearance from the Vanuatu Fire Service and structural occupancy certification from the Public Works Department, and maintain a workplace health and safety register under the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2008. For mobile container assets transiting or being stored outside Vanuatu, the user operator must comply with the class survey, fire-rated container rules, and marine/rail safety standards of the jurisdiction where the container is located.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Vanuatu International Company; authorised capital is expressed in the Memorandum, and no escrow deposit, capital importation, or paid-up capital verification is required. Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), operating through the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR); official portal: https://www.customs.gov.vu. Corporate and International Company registry: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); official portal: https://www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax, VAT, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, land tax, and all present and future taxes in respect of its offshore income, assets, shares, transactions, and operations, with an express 20-year tax-holiday guarantee from incorporation (International Companies Act [Cap 222], s.12). The pure container-leasing platform is therefore outside the Vanuatu tax net.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, entity classification, or hybrid-mismatch election regime exists because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax. The IC exemption applies automatically with no filing. The only available structural elections are: (a) an IC may elect by special resolution to surrender IC status and re-register as a domestic company under the Companies Act [Cap 191], thereby becoming subject to 12.5% VAT, municipal business licence fees, customs duty, and Vanuatu National Provident Fund obligations - an election to be avoided for this offshore leasing vehicle; and (b) a foreign corporation may elect to redomicile into Vanuatu as an IC under Part XI of the IC Act and thereby inherit the same statutory exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration arises where taxable turnover exceeds VT 10,000,000 (approx. USD 85,000) in any 12-month period for supplies of goods, and VT 5,000,000 (approx. USD 42,500) for supplies of services; voluntary registration is available below these thresholds. The offshore IC does not trigger any registration nexus because its container-leasing supplies to non-resident ocean carriers and foreign operators are zero-rated or outside the scope of VAT, and the IC is tax-exempt.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under the VAT Act [Cap 72] to (i) exported goods removed from Vanuatu within 30 days of supply, and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident located outside Vanuatu at the time of performance, where the services are not supplied for use in a taxable activity carried on in Vanuatu. Intermodal ISO shipping container leasing and hire by the IC to non-resident carriers, freight forwarders, and foreign operators qualifies as an export of services at 0% (or is wholly outside the scope of VAT for the exempt IC). Eligibility requires that the container remains in offshore circulation under an operating lease, title and risk remain with the lessor, and no supply is made for use in Vanuatu. Any supply to a Vanuatu-resident customer would be prohibited for an IC and, if made through a domestic entity, would be taxable at 12.5%.

VAT-registered suppliers must issue serial-numbered tax invoices containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and VAT registration number, recipient details, description, quantity, VAT-exclusive consideration, and the 12.5% VAT component shown separately) and file periodic VAT returns with DCIR. Fraudulent zero-rating claims expose the taxpayer to retrospective clawback of all refunded VAT with interest and penalty tax, reassessment across open statutory periods (extended in cases of fraud), deregistration, criminal fines, and custodial penalties against directors and registered agents under the VAT Act [Cap 72] and the Customs Act [Cap 46], including seizure and forfeiture of assets. For an IC, the VFSC may additionally strike off the company and disqualify officers for filing false documents or conducting prohibited domestic business; the IC Act s.12 exemption does not shield fraudulent conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations since 1982; there are no exchange controls, prior-approval gates, or transfer restrictions on dividends, lease rentals, loan repayments, royalties, or divestment capital. The Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible on current and capital account, with the RBV operating a stabilized arrangement against an undisclosed basket dominated by AUD, USD, and NZD. An International Company (IC) engaged in container leasing may retain 100% of its USD-denominated rental receivables offshore or credit them to onshore multi-currency accounts without conversion or surrender. Practical constraint is market thinness: meaningful USD/VUV conversions must be pre-agreed with the domestic dealing desk, and the IC treasury should remain USD-native, keeping VUV balances only for local administrative spend.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion regime on export proceeds or cross-border services receipts. The colonial-era exchange-control framework has been fully liberalised, and neither the RBV nor the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) requires container-leasing rental income to be converted to VUV within any timeline. The only compliance duty is the receiving bank's classification of the inward credit in its RBV balance-of-payments statistical return, normally under the purpose narrative 'operational leasing services'; funds may remain in external USD accounts indefinitely with zero conversion risk.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; the RBV acts as sole monetary authority, issuer of the Vatu, administrator of the national payment system, and prudential regulator of all domestically licensed banks under the International Banking Act and the Financial Dealers Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rated Low-Medium. Vanuatu banking law has no dedicated e-money institution (EMI) licence category and the VFSC does not license non-bank EMI platforms. Global EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, Mercury) generally decline Vanuatu-incorporated ICs under internal country-exposure policies, largely due to the absence of substantive local presence and correspondent-bank sensitivities. Accordingly, EMIs are feasible only as ancillary collection accelerators for US domestic ACH tenant payments, never as primary concentration accounts. The recommended architecture is a USD treasury spine at BSP or BRED, complemented by an optional US-domiciled ACH collection account that auto-sweeps via MT103/MT101 into the Vanuatu operating account, preserving a fully documented payment chain.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity and shareholder-loan injections clear via SWIFT MT103 for funds transfers or MT202/MT203 for interbank net credits, with no RBV or VFSC approval gate. The remitter must narrate the payment with the IC's exact registered name, the share-subscription reference, and a balance-of-payments purpose code agreed with the receiving bank, typically 'Equity' for share capital, 'Other investment' for shareholder loans, or 'Operational leasing services' for tenant rental income, because BSP and BRED file this code in the RBV statistical return. The IC's share register and subscription resolution must evidence equity injections; executed loan agreements must support intercompany loans. For container-lease rental receivables, tenants should remit via MT103 referencing container BIC numbers and the lease schedule so the bank can match the narration to the declared fleet and avoid frozen-credit reviews. The ordering-customer name must exactly match VFSC registration to pass intermediary-correspondent filters, and funds routed through New York or Sydney correspondents should avoid ambiguous trade names.Outbound treasury disbursements use three rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for T/T remittances to container manufacturers (e.g., CIMC, Singamas) and depot service providers, with narration comprising the container BIC list, purchase-order number, and commercial invoice; (2) irrevocable at-sight commercial letters of credit (L/C) for batch container purchases of 500+ TEU, issued by BSP or BRED with a Singapore or Hong Kong advising/confirming bank, covering bill of lading, container inspection certificate, and inland-drayage documentation; and (3) SWIFT MT101 sweeps from the Vanuatu operating account to the parent treasury centre or group account for consolidated liquidity management. Lease-rental outflows to container pool managers and sub-lessors are paid by MT103 referencing the relevant invoice and sublease agreement. The core commercial cycle is 100% USD, avoiding FX conversion friction; local administration in VUV (office rent, salaries, registry fees) is settled through a dedicated VUV current account debited via the RBV domestic RTGS system. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold exists because Vanuatu has not enacted Local File or Master File obligations for exempt International Companies. Prudent compliance standard: prepare contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation where related-party container leasing revenue exceeds VUV 100,000,000 (approximately USD 830,000) per annum.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu applies economic substance expectations consistent with OECD/G20 BEPS minimum standards and the EU Code of Conduct Group criteria. A container box leasing company conducting finance and leasing activity or operating as a distribution and service centre must demonstrate, through the registered agent and VFSC declarations, a real economic presence proportionate to its activity. The economic substance doctrine requires a Vanuatu office or demonstrable outsourced administration, local or remote management with documented board decisions, adequate run-rate expenditure, and a clear commercial rationale for the Vanuatu domicile; failure to satisfy these requirements exposes the IC to strike-off and reclassification as a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Annual Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to VUV 5,000,000 under the International Companies Act, possible cancellation of the tax exemption certificate, mandatory strike-off and asset vesting in the Republic of Vanuatu, and recovery of all unpaid duties with interest.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and the registered agent making a false statutory declaration are personally liable for civil restitution of amounts lost to the state, disqualification from acting as a director for up to 10 years, travel bans pending investigation, and imprisonment not exceeding 7 years.
Shell Company and Sham Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Designation as a shell entity by the VFSC, withdrawal of the certificate of good standing, administrative penalties up to VUV 2,000,000, forced dissolution, and potential cross-border information exchange exposing the structure to the foreign tax administration.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face personal fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and prosecution for aiding unlawful tax arrangements; beneficial owners may be subpoenaed through mutual legal assistance and subjected to foreign civil anti-avoidance adjustments plus domestic director disqualification and travel restrictions.
AML, CFT, and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act penalties reach VUV 100,000,000 for corporate entities, freezing orders over the container fleet and lease receivables, and mandatory revocation of the IC registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally exposed to fines up to VUV 30,000,000, disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 15 years for money laundering; the corporate veil is pierced where the director was the controlling mind of the violation.
Sanctions Evasion and Forbidden Jurisdiction Processing

Statutory sanction: Asset freezes under Vanuatu financial sanctions regulations, monetary penalties up to VUV 25,000,000, cancellation of VFSC registration, and referral to international enforcement networks including FATF action against the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for knowingly directing transactions with sanctioned parties, face indefinite travel bans while under investigation, confiscation of personal assets, and imprisonment up to 10 years under international sanctions enforcement statutes.
Fraudulent Trading and Thin Capitalisation Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under general company law principles the VFSC may order re-characterisation of the related-party lease and debt arrangements, impose civil penalties up to VUV 15,000,000, and require repayment of unlawful distributions.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly trade with intent to defraud creditors are personally liable without limit for the IC's debts, barred from managing any company for 10 years, subjected to travel bans while civil recovery is pending, and imprisoned for fraudulent trading.
