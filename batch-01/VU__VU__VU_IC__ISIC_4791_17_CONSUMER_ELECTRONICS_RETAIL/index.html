Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue (VCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: vcir.gov.vu. International Company registration and annual maintenance are administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); official portal: vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu levies no income tax, so no TIN is issued for tax-exempt International Companies (ICs) solely on the basis of incorporation. A VAT registration number may be obtained from VCIR only if the entity carries on a domestic taxable activity and its local taxable turnover exceeds VT 2,000,000 in a 12-month period. An IC engaged exclusively in offshore e-commerce retail of consumer electronics is outside the VAT net and instead uses its VFSC registration number and customs business code for export documentation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections are available because Vanuatu has no corporate income tax regime. The International Companies Act confers an automatic exemption from all Vanuatu taxes on ICs; the only relevant corporate elections are non-tax procedural options, such as redomiciliation, conversion to a local company, or continuation under a foreign law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 2,000,000 taxable turnover in any 12-month period for domestic supplies (approximately USD 16,200); exports of goods are excluded from taxable turnover. International Companies conducting only offshore business are not required to register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply, provided the exporter retains proof of export and the buyer is not a Vanuatu resident. Services supplied to non-residents and used entirely outside Vanuatu, including digital e-commerce support services, are also zero-rated. For the online retail of personal electronics and smart gadgets, title and control of the goods must transfer to an overseas customer and the goods must leave Vanuatu (or be supplied into a free zone/foreign trade zone) within the statutory period.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Entry (VU-CED)
  • Commercial invoice issued by the International Company
  • Packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing export from Vanuatu
  • Payment settlement report from Stripe/PayPal or inward remittance certificate from a licensed Vanuatu bank
  • Certificate of Origin or supplier/vendor invoices (where applicability is required to substantiate export value)

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based continuous transaction control regime is in operation. Standard paper or PDF VAT invoices are sufficient; export invoices generated via Stripe or PayPal are accepted for substantiation.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims are subject to VCIR audit, retrospective clawback of VAT (where applicable) for up to five years, civil penalties up to 150% of the tax evaded, and criminal fines under the VAT Act. Customs Act penalties also apply for false export declarations, including confiscation of goods and fines up to VT 5,000,000. Because the IC is tax-exempt rather than a registered VAT trader, the principal audit risk is mischaracterisation of Vanuatu-source domestic activities, which could trigger VAT registration liability and associated penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu is an IMF Article VIII member since 1 December 1982; current-account convertibility is free for bona fide e-commerce receivables, trade services, and profit remittances, with zero withholding taxes and no remittance levy applicable to a non-resident International Company. Capital-account inflows, including equity subscriptions and shareholder loans, are structurally liberal, but any VUV-denominated capital transaction requires routing through an RBV-authorized dealer and may trigger prior central bank approval. Practical friction arises not at central bank level, but at bank-level source-of-funds verification, PSP settlement proof, and beneficial-owner transparency documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender ratio or fixed conversion deadline applies to a non-resident International Company earning offshore foreign currency, as such proceeds are expressly exempt from exchange control under the International Companies Act regime; export receipts may be retained offshore in USD. Resident Vanuatu operating exporters are subject to general RBV exchange-control oversight, which effectively requires repatriation of proceeds through authorized dealers in line with invoice payment terms, customarily within 30 to 90 days; mandatory conversion percentages for resident exporters are not legislated, and the International Company is outside this framework.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 63] with the Minister of Finance, in coordination with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for International Company oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rated High as a supplementary clearing rail and Low-to-Medium as a primary bank relation. Legal constraints: an offshore EMI without a Vanuatu financial-dealer licence may not solicit deposits or provide payment services inside Vanuatu; however, the International Company may lawfully maintain settlement balances with a licensed foreign EMI (for example an FCA-regulated UK EMI) where those funds arise strictly from third-party e-commerce receivables, provided a licensed Vanuatu bank account is concurrently maintained for audit evidence, statutory solvency, and conversion of net proceeds. Unlicensed deposit-taking in Vanuatu is a criminal offense, so the EMI structure is viable only extraterritorially and must never accept Vanuatu-sourced inward payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder-loan injections must be effected by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's verified bank (preferably Hong Kong, Singapore, or United Kingdom) directly to the International Company's USD account at ANZ or Bred, with payment narrative 'Share subscription / shareholder loan, International Companies Act [Cap. 120]'. No prior RBV approval is required for foreign-currency injections into the exempt International Company; the receiving bank will condition release to the operational ledger on delivery of a certified board resolution, updated register of members, source-of-funds letter, and projected e-commerce revenue evidence. Any credit exceeding VUV 1,000,000-equivalent triggers a mandatory threshold transaction report to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit; therefore all injections should be named-account-to-named-account, free of third-party splitting, and documented contemporaneously.Supplier settlements for consumer-electronics procurement from Hong Kong and mainland Chinese distributors should clear via SWIFT MT103 with SWIFT GPI tracking through ANZ or Bred treasury platforms, denominated in USD to eliminate VUV conversion friction; for serial procurement, a confirmed irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued under UCP600 at sight is structurally preferable. The treasury should direct Stripe/PayPal receivable pools in USD to the Vanuatu USD account, then execute same-day USD-to-CNH or USD-to-HKD spot conversions at the bank's dealing desk when making supplier payments; foreign-currency-to-foreign-currency conversions require no RBV approval, while any VUV conversion must run exclusively through the RBV-authorized dealer and be retained in the audit trail.Outright deliverable USD/VUV forward contracts up to 12 months with an RBV-authorized dealing bank (ANZ or Bred), supported by documented trade receivables or payables.USD/CNH deliverable forwards or FX swaps executed offshore (Hong Kong or Singapore dealing room) for the electronics supply-chain settlement, with no Vanuatu counterparty required.USD/HKD cross-currency swaps used to convert long USD receivable positions into HKD to match supplier invoice currency, booked offshore with a top-tier counterparty.Non-deliverable VUV forwards (NDFs) with a global dealer, strictly limited to hedging residual VUV-denominated receivables, noting thin offshore VUV NDF liquidity and therefore small approved notional.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable in Vanuatu: no vatu-denominated transfer pricing documentation threshold is prescribed by statute. Vanuatu has not enacted OECD transfer pricing legislation for International Companies. Nevertheless, prudent compliance under foreign tax laws (e.g., U.S., Australia, EU) may require a Local File / Master File if the VU IC transacts cross-border with related parties and the group turnover exceeds the relevant foreign threshold, commonly EUR 50 million or USD equivalent. Recommended arm's length records: prepare contemporaneous documentation in English if related-party e-commerce transactions exceed USD 1,000,000 per financial year to mitigate foreign audit risk.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) comparable to the CFC/EU listings or the OECD/BEPS standards. The International Companies Act imposes a prohibition on carrying on business in Vanuatu with Vanuatu residents and owning real property in Vanuatu unless licensed. In practice, an IC must demonstrate economic substance only in response to a foreign CFC or anti-hybrid regime (e.g., U.S. GILTI, Australia CFC rules, EU blacklist defensive measures). To be safe, the IC should maintain real economic substance outside its foreign owner's jurisdiction: a Vanuatu registered office, a licensed agent, a local corporate secretary, and a board of directors exercising real decision-making (at least one director resident in Vanuatu). The IC must not be a mere shell in the hands of the foreign parent. The recommended commercial substance benchmark: at least VUV 500,000 (about USD 4,200) of annual local operating expenditure in Vanuatu, or demonstrable management and control from Vanuatu.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abusive Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: In Vanuatu: the IC may be struck off, incur penalties for false filings, and directors may be fined up to VUV 500,000 (approx. USD 4,200) and/or imprisonment for up to 6 months under the International Companies Act for making false statutory declarations. In the foreign market: full back-taxes, compound interest, penalties of up to 100% of the underpaid tax, and potential anti-avoidance reassessments (e.g., OECD BEPS rules).

Director personal liability: Directors face joint and several liability for taxes evaded if they authorize the sham structure. Foreign courts may pierce the veil where the IC is a mere instrumentality. Directors may be subject to civil restitution, asset freezing orders, travel bans, director disqualification for up to 25 years (in Australia), and criminal imprisonment in severe fraud cases. In Vanuatu, directors making false declarations face imprisonment up to 6 months and personal fines; foreign arrest warrants may be executed via international mutual legal assistance.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu may strike off the company for non-compliance with the annual filing requirement. Foreign tax authorities (e.g., ATO, IRS, HMRC) may treat the IC as transparent, re-attribute income to the controlling shareholder, impose penalty tax of 50-75% of the tax-related adjustment, and apply shell-company anti-abuse directives. EU member states may apply defensive countermeasures (withholding tax penalties, transaction denials) against Vanuatu when listed in Annex II.

Director personal liability: Directors of a shell IC risk personal liability for fraudulent phoenix activity; courts may lift the corporate veil if the company lacks economic substance. Directors may be personally assessed for the group's unpaid foreign taxes, cross-border interest, restitutive compensation to minority shareholders, and a travel ban under the Proceeds of Crime Act (Vanuatu) if assets are traced. Criminal imprisonment of up to 5 years can be imposed for money laundering predicates (fraud by false representation).
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Predicate Offense

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu AML/CFT Act (Cap. 271, as amended and VFSC AML Rules), an IC that fails to implement AML internal controls may face a civil penalty of VUV 10,000,000 (approx. USD 84,000) and suspension or revocation of its registered agent's license. The VFSC may also impose an administrative fine of VUV 5,000,000 per contravention. Criminal money laundering carries imprisonment of up to 15 years in Vanuatu under the Proceeds of Crime Act. International Payment Card Networks (Stripe/PayPal) may terminate services, and corresponding banks may freeze funds.

Director personal liability: Directors incur personal criminal liability if they knowingly direct or consent to money laundering. Restitution orders are issued for fraudulent gains; directors may be personally liable to indemnify the company and third parties. Travel bans are routinely issued in mutual legal assistance proceedings; senior managers can be imprisoned for up to 15 years in Vanuatu for laundering proceeds above VUV 1 million, and for up to 10 years in foreign jurisdictions for predicate fraud. The corporate veil is pierced where the IC is used as a sham to launder consumer payment funds.
Beneficial Ownership Registration Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Beneficial Ownership Regulations (2020), the VFSC may impose a penalty of VUV 100,000 (approx. USD 840) per day for continuing non-compliance, and the company may be struck off. False declarations are indictable offenses. The registered agent is also liable for a fine of VUV 5,000,000 and possibly license revocation.

Director personal liability: Directors sign the statutory annual return and beneficial ownership declaration. A dishonest director is personally liable for the penalties and may be convicted of perjury. Courts may impose a travel ban pending investigation, and if the beneficial owner is a non-resident, the VFSC will issue a red flag to financial intelligence units, resulting in asset freezes abroad. Individual directors face up to 12 months imprisonment in Vanuatu for non-compliance; aggravated cases with intent to facilitate tax evasion can attract 5 years.
Trade/Retail VAT and Digital Commerce Non-Compliance (Market Jurisdiction)

Statutory sanction: In Australia: non-registration leads to a penalty of up to 200% of the GST payable, plus interest charges; director penalty notices are issued for unpaid GST. In New Zealand: 100% penalty plus additional tax; the overseas supplier's website may be blocked and payment providers ordered to cease support. In the EU: VAT assessments with a penalty up to 50% of the due VAT and an obligation to appoint a fiscal representative.

Director personal liability: The fiction of Vanuatu tax exemption does not protect foreign VAT liabilities. Directors may receive a Director Penalty Notice (DPN) from the ATO making them personally liable for unpaid GST amounts. Non-payment of DPN leads to civil restitution, travel bans, and cross-border enforcement against personal assets. In severe evasion cases, criminal imprisonment of up to 5 years can be imposed.
