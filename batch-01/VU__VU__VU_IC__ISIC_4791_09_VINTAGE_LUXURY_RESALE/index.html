Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (part of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management), operating through the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs portal; company registration and licensing authority for International Companies is the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing an International Company in Vanuatu must register the company with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), obtaining a Certificate of Incorporation and a VFSC registration number. Because Vanuatu imposes no income tax, no tax identification number (TIN) or VAT ID is issued for direct tax purposes. For customs operations, the entity must separately register as an importer/exporter with the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department and obtain a customs identification code, typically through lodgment of an importer/exporter application in the ASYCUDA World customs system.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity establishing an International Company in Vanuatu must register the company with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), obtaining a Certificate of Incorporation and a VFSC registration number. Because Vanuatu imposes no income tax, no tax identification number (TIN) or VAT ID is issued for direct tax purposes. For customs operations, the entity must separately register as an importer/exporter with the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department and obtain a customs identification code, typically through lodgment of an importer/exporter application in the ASYCUDA World customs system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. The Vanuatu International Companies Act provides no 'check-the-box' or hybrid mismatch election mechanisms. International Companies are categorically exempt from direct and indirect taxation on offshore income; an IC may elect to become a domestic company for restricted onshore activity, which subjects it to business license fees and ordinary customs duties, but no elective tax classification regimes analogous to US or EU systems exist.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no national VAT or GST; indirect taxation limited to customs import duties and specific excise taxes)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. No VAT/GST exists in Vanuatu; therefore there is no registration threshold for such a tax. Customs importer/exporter registration is mandatory for any entity undertaking import or export activity regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Vanuatu does not levy VAT or GST, so there is no zero-rating mechanism for exports. Exports simply fall outside the scope of any national indirect tax; no refund claim process exists for output tax because no output tax is charged. Exporters must comply with Vanuatu Customs regulations to obtain export clearance, but no indirect tax refund is available or required.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank transfer / SWIFT proof of payment

Vanuatu imposes no electronic invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate. Customs export declarations must be processed through the ASYCUDA World electronic customs management system, which requires submission of supporting documentation and generation of export clearance. There are no digital stamping or continuous transaction control requirements for indirect tax purposes.

There are no VAT export zero-rating clawback provisions applicable in Vanuatu because no VAT regime exists. However, false or fraudulent customs declarations are penalized under the Customs Act, including monetary fines, forfeiture of goods, and suspension or revocation of the entity's import/export privileges. For International Companies, failure to comply with VFSC annual renewal and reporting obligations can also result in penalties, deregistration, and loss of the statutory tax exemption.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is formally convertible on both capital and current account with no general restrictions applicable to non-resident International Companies. Vanuatu operates a liberalised exchange control regime; however, banks retain de facto internal controls on the funding of International Company accounts. Dividend and profit remittance from an International Company to non-resident shareholders is generally unrestricted provided the company is not in local currency debt, the remittance is clearly identified as distributable profits, and any applicable annual/original filing obligations with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) are met. Trade-related payments for luxury resale transactions are treated as current account payments and do not require RBV approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline for surrendering export proceeds nor any mandatory conversion ratio. Vanuatu does not impose forced surrender of foreign currency export earnings to the RBV or to an authorised dealer. Proceeds can be retained in foreign currency, deposited in an offshore-capable corporate account, and used for operational disbursements without RBV conversion requirement.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), exercising statutory authority under the Reserve Bank Act [Cap 125], the Exchange Control Act [Cap 86], and the Financial Institutions Act [Cap 279] for the Republic of Vanuatu.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium to Low. Vanuatu does not maintain a distinct electronic money institution (EMI) licensing class, and RBV-regulated banks are the only institutions able to hold client funds domestically. Offshore digital EMIs can clear payments globally but the International Company must still rely on a local bank or an established offshore acquiring bank for actual VUV settlement, merchant services, and escrow integration. Legal constraints include: absence of EMI reciprocity between Vanuatu and EU/UK licensing frameworks, heightened de-risking risk toward Pacific-jurisdiction corporates, and the inability of an unlicensed offshore EMI to open a nostro account with RBV-regulated banks. The optimal pathway is to use a tier-one local bank for fiduciary segregation and a licensed EU/UK EMI (e.g., for USD/EUR payouts) only as an auxiliary disbursement rail, never as the sole treasury facility.

Defense evidence: Treasury policy and FX hedging strategy, detailed cashflow projections, setting out planned conversion volumes, identification of the relevant FX dealer and permitted NDF agreement, and bank-approved account purpose documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections into the International Company must be completed by SWIFT MT103 transfers using the bank routing code (BIC) of the receiving Vanuatu bank, with the memo field citing the purpose code 'EQUI' (equity injection) or 'AN13' (trade-related funding) depending on the correspondent bank's format. All remittances should be accompanied by a board resolution, a shareholder subscription schedule, and a signed remittance mandate to satisfy the bank's source-of-funds verification. Payment instructions must not omit the International Company's registered agent reference number. For escrow advance funding or merchant acquiring rolling reserves, the route is either (a) direct MT103 from the sentiment account of Stripe/Escrow_Pay to the operational account, using the merchant ID and reserve release reference, or (b) a two-step wire through a licensed EU/UK EMI prior to final settlement to the Vanuatu account. Only RBV-authorised banks may convert foreign currency to VUV; conversion requests must be accompanied by an underlying trade or treasury instruction.Trade settlements for procurement of vintage luxury inventory are executed through SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers from the company's operational account to the beneficiary's bank account, with purpose code 'TSUP' (trade services and related commission) or 'AGRT' (goods-related payments), plus commercial invoice number and a unique trade reference. For high-value acquisitions exceeding USD 50,000, an Irrevocable Confirmed Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) drawn on an international bank is the recommended clearing rail to mitigate supplier risk. Escrow-linked disbursements are released only against authenticated delivery acceptance triggers recorded in Escrow_Pay. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable (no Vanuatu transfer pricing rules, no Local File/Master File threshold; local currency VUV but no tax base is assessed).

Economic substance scope: No standalone Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations. An International Company is required to maintain a registered agent, registered office and limited records. Substance criteria of shareholder tax jurisdictions and EU/OECD lists may impose requirements; a shell IC without any operational footprint may be re-characterized.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu International Companies Act: monetary penalties, strike-off and loss of corporate privileges. In the investor's jurisdiction: substantial understatement penalties (e.g. 100–200% of unpaid taxes) and criminal prosecution for willful evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors may be assessed personally under anti-avoidance and fraudulent tax rules; international travel bans and extradition are possible where indictable tax fraud is committed.
Shell Company Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu's VFSC can impose administrative sanctions for failure to maintain registered records; OECD/G20 actions can designate the company as a shell; foreign tax authorities may apply GAAR/CBCR penalties and disregard its existence.

Director personal liability: In civil proceedings, courts may pierce the corporate veil and impose restitution on directors for amounts repatriated; asset freeze and liability for group tax debts can follow.
Money Laundering and AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Offense under Vanuatu AML/CFT framework: fines up to Vt 100 million or higher, imprisonment up to 20 years, revocation of registration and seizure of proceeds; international consequences include loss of correspondent banking and FATF countermeasures.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may face personal criminal prosecution; travel bans, extradition, and personal asset forfeiture are expressly authorized.
