Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A registered office at a licensed agent's physical address in Port Vila satisfies the statutory requirement of the International Companies Act §§ 22–23. A pure virtual (non-physical) office is permissible for statutory presence, but the defense banking and foreign procurement market will treat it as weak substance; the underlying manufacturing phase will necessarily occur outside Vanuatu (e.g., contractual toll manufacturing in a supplier country) because the International Company is prohibited from carrying on domestic business in Vanuatu except with a special license. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: https://customs.gov.vu (ASYCUDA World custom clearance); company registration oversight by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) at https://vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu does not levy corporate or personal income tax, so no general TIN is issued for direct-tax assessment. An International Company (IC) is constituted and registered under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] exclusively through a VFSC-licensed registered agent; there is no statutory minimum capital. For CRS/FATCA identification, the IC may obtain an 8-digit continuous numeric Vanuatu TIN on request from VRC. If the IC physically imports or exports goods through Vanuatu, it must obtain a Customs Importer/Exporter Code by filing an application with VRC Customs supported by certified constitutional documents, registered agent consent, director passport copies, and a business licence where domestic activity is permitted. VAT registration is not applicable as no VAT/GST regime is currently in force.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The International Company is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, levies, and stamp duties on its shares and transfers under Section 3 of the International Companies Act. The entity operates under a strict offshore/non-domestic territorial scope and is statutorily prohibited from carrying on local beneficial business without a licence, preserving the tax-exempt zone character.

Available election forms

No income-tax check-the-box election exists because entity-level taxation is not imposed. Available statutory elections include: (i) continuation of the IC into or out of Vanuatu as a foreign-re-domiciled entity under the International Companies Act; (ii) conversion between an International Company and a domestic company under the Companies Act; (iii) application for a limited local licence electing approved domestic activities; (iv) hybrid mismatch elections are not applicable for Vanuatu direct-tax purposes as no tax on income is imposed.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT/GST is in force; indirect taxation arises from customs import duty and excise under the Customs Act [Cap 77], stamp duty under the Stamp Duties Act [Cap 73], and the tourism/services levy where applicable.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Any person importing or exporting physical goods must file electronic customs declarations through ASYCUDA World irrespective of turnover or value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - since no VAT system operates, exports attract no output tax and no zero-rating claim or refund mechanism exists. Exports of goods from Vanuatu are duty-neutral except for specified controlled commodities; imported materials subsequently re-exported may qualify for duty drawback under the Customs Act.

  • Customs Export Declaration

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API, or digital stamping mandate is enforced by VRC. Customs clearance is electronic only via ASYCUDA World, covering declaration lodgement, risk assessment, duty accounting, and export processing.

Vanuatu imposes no VAT clawback because no VAT is levied. Audit scrutiny targets customs duty evasion, export mis-declaration, mis-description of controlled goods, and unauthorized domestic trading by an IC. Breaches of the Customs Act [Cap 77] expose the entity to forfeiture of goods, fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment; VFSC may strike off the IC or revoke its registration for carrying on unlicensed domestic business or AML/CFT non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is partially convertible. Current account transactions, including legitimate trade payments and service fees, are generally permitted through authorized dealers, subject to underlying documentary verification. Capital account movements and profit remittances require RBV approval or an authorized dealer's exchange control compliance clearance. An International Company is typically treated as a non-resident entity and may maintain foreign-currency external accounts, enabling profit repatriation outside the ordinary VUV clearing system, but any inbound VUV transfers for local operations remain subject to source-of-funds scrutiny and tax-neutrality confirmation. Remittance hurdles include proof of beneficial ownership, underlying contract validity, and the absence of any RBV restrictive designation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no mandatory statutory conversion ratio for export receipts; however, all export proceeds must be lodged with an authorized dealer in Vanuatu expeditiously after collection. Retention of foreign currency and use of external accounts is permissible where the exporter is an International Company, subject to RBV reporting requirements. Standard practice requires export documentation and evidence of shipment to support repatriation; failure to comply with the timing and documentation conditions may trigger exchange control penalties and future banking restrictions.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — central monetary authority and exchange control administrator under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 112]

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rated Medium-to-Low. Vanuatu's licensing framework does not recognize foreign electronic money institutions (EMIs) as authorized dealers for FX or clearing purposes. Offshore digital EMIs may be used for non-settlement ancillary payments, but they cannot satisfy RBV exchange control compliance, Government_Direct_Wire rails, or Diplomatic_LC settlement. A locally licensed EMI or a banking-as-a-service arrangement with a Tier-1 Vanuatu bank is required to achieve compliant cross-border clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed through an authorized dealer in Vanuatu using SWIFT MT103 or MT202 wires, with the purpose code stating capital contribution or inward foreign direct investment. The originating bank must include the International Company's exact registered name, the Beneficial Owner's legal status, the corporate registration number, and a clear reference to the escrow or operational account. The authorized dealer will require the signed board resolution, subscription agreement, contribution schedule, and an exchange control declaration confirming that the funds are derived from lawful sources. Additional verification of the 24-month source-of-funds trail is mandatory for any transfer exceeding the bank's internal reporting threshold.Trade settlements are executed via SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier payments, MT202 for interbank cover payments, and Commercial Letters of Credit subject to UCP600 for defense and tactical-vehicle procurement. Diplomatic_LC transactions require a DC-advising bank in Vanuatu, with presentation of the original LC, shipping and insurance documents, freight forwarder bills, inspection certificates, and final acceptance certificates. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable; Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and therefore imposes no Local File, Master File, or transfer pricing documentation obligation on International Companies.

Economic substance scope: No domestic Economic Substance Regulations apply to Vanuatu International Companies. However, a licensed defense exporter must demonstrate operational substance, including local risk management, procurement control, and compliance capacity, under VFSC licensing criteria.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unlicensed Defense Export and Arms Brokerage

Statutory sanction: VFSC license revocation, seizure of goods, suspension of banking payment rails, monetary penalties under Vanuatu trade control and licensing laws, and criminal prosecution for unlicensed arms dealing.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined, imprisoned, disqualified from acting as directors, and subjected to travel bans while under investigation.
Sanctions and Embargo Evasion

Statutory sanction: Asset freezing, confiscation of financial proceeds, inclusion on consolidated sanctions lists, VFSC deregistration of the International Company, and criminal sanctions for violations of international sanctions directives implemented in Vanuatu.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face personal asset forfeiture, targeted international travel bans, criminal prosecution, and possible imprisonment.
AML/CFT Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under the Vanuatu AML/CFT framework, suspension or loss of the VFSC license, freezing of bank accounts, and referral to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal investigation.

Director personal liability: Senior officers may be subject to civil restitution obligations, personal disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment for willful AML/CFT violations.
Shell Company Abuse and False VFSC Declarations

Statutory sanction: Company strike-off, deregistration, VFSC fines, loss of the IC's limited liability status, forfeiture of exempt status, and prohibition against future re-registration.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced; directors and shareholders may be held personally liable for creditor claims and restitution, with travel bans imposed pending completion of investigations.
