Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum charter capital for a general LLC under Enterprise Law 2020; charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days after the Enterprise Registration Certificate is issued. For foreign investors, the IRC may specify a committed capital amount, and a blocked/escrow bank account is recommended to demonstrate capital availability and to ensure lawful contribution remittance into Vietnam. Capital must be licensed at a commercial bank in Vietnam, and contribution schedule must be maintained.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate (Phê duyệt/ nghiệm thu về phòng cháy chữa cháy) must be obtained from the provincial Fire Prevention and Rescue Police Department under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP before the physical studio is put into operation. Conduct an annual fire inspection of the office floor, ensure fire alarm and extinguishing equipment, emergency exits, and evacuation plans. Obtain a structural load certificate/structural acceptance for the leased floor confirming it can bear the concentrated load of render servers and GPU workstations from a licensed construction inspection company. Comply with Decree 44/2016/ND-CP for construction and equipment quality. Carry out mandatory occupational safety training, workplace health risk assessment (software indicated: low-risk office but still mandatory), electrical safety inspection, and register the facility with the district labor safety authority. In addition, although not a license, file a registration for the use of laser or high-intensity electronic devices if used; otherwise no further clearance is required for non-industrial 3D art production.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tong Cuc Thue) of the Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Viet Nam; official e-portal: thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-Tax Portal) and etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation of a foreign-invested LLC, the provincial Department of Planning and Investment issues the Enterprise Registration Certificate and simultaneously assigns the statutory Tax Identification Number (Ma So Thue). Tax registration is integrated with enterprise registration; the LLC must then activate the e-Tax account on the General Department of Taxation portal within the prescribed statutory window, submit its legal representative authorization and digital certificate, and complete VAT and CIT registration formalities. Non-resident entities without a permanent establishment may separately register under the Decree 126/2020/ND-CP withholding mechanism for indirect tax compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity election is available under Vietnamese law. An LLC is mandatorily taxed at entity level as a resident corporate taxpayer at the 20% CIT standard rate. Statutory elections are limited to: (a) VAT computation method election between the credit/deduction method (khau tru) and the direct method (truc tiep) for eligible taxpayers below prescribed revenue thresholds; and (b) election of the CIT incentives regime (e.g., high-tech, export-processing enterprise or new-investment incentive certificate) where statutory qualification criteria are satisfied. Thin capitalization (30% EBITDA interest cap), transfer-pricing documentation and consolidation elections are not permitted deviations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 100,000,000 annual turnover for household and individual businesses; foreign-invested LLCs are subject to mandatory VAT registration without any turnover threshold upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero percent VAT applies to exported goods physically leaving Viet Nam, including supplies to export-processing enterprises (EPEs), bonded warehouses and non-tariff zones, and to services rendered to foreign organizations/individuals and consumed outside Viet Nam. Conditions include: a written cross-border contract stipulating the supply of goods or services and payment terms; customs export declaration for goods; non-cash settlement through a bank or eligible intermediary; and, for services, evidence that the service is performed entirely or predominantly outside Viet Nam and that the foreign principal bears the cost. For the AAA game 3D art outsourcing model, eligibility is available where the creative BPO service is supplied directly to an overseas principal under contract, delivered electronically, and paid via SWIFT wire transfer or PayPal with an inward remittance record; services consumed domestically or performed within Viet Nam for domestic parties remain subject to the standard 10% rate.

  • Viet Nam e-Customs Export Declaration (electronic V5/V6 declaration with VNACCS clearance)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export
  • Signed overseas export contract / Statement of Work / Master Services Agreement
  • Bank SWIFT MT103 / Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) evidencing foreign-currency remittance

Mandatory nationwide e-invoicing under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC effective 1 July 2022. Taxpayers must issue e-invoices in standardized XML format through GDT-approved e-invoice service providers. Two authentication mechanisms are mandatory: (1) e-invoices with tax authority clearance code — the digital invoice file is transmitted in near-real-time via API to the GDT e-invoice portal, which immediately validates and returns the authority code; and (2) e-invoices without tax authority code bearing a qualified digital signature, with invoice data files transmitted to thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn no later than the statutory VAT return filing deadline (20th of the following month for monthly filers; 30th of the quarter-end for quarterly filers). Real-time fiscal clearance through e-Customs integration (VNACCS/VCIS) applies to export declarations.

Export zero-rating and VAT refund claims are subject to retrospective inspection under the Law on Tax Administration (No. 38/2019/QH14). The ordinary inspection period is five years from the end of the liability year; the limitation period extends to ten years where fraud is identified. Penalties include administrative fines up to VND 500,000,000 per violation, 20% late-payment interest per month on clawed-back refunds, 100% disgorgement of wrongfully claimed offsettings with daily interest surcharge, and suspension or revocation of the e-invoice clearance status. Criminal liability under Penal Code Articles 200 and 201 may attach with fines up to VND 4.5 billion and custodial sentences up to 20 years for organized tax evasion or invoice forgery.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vietnam maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with the Vietnamese Dong (VND) as the domestic currency. Current account transactions, including cross-border service fees and profit remittances arising from licensed foreign-invested enterprises, are generally convertible for legitimate commercial purposes subject to SBV-accredited bank processing and supporting documentation. Capital account transactions, including FDI equity contributions and withdrawals, remain restricted and require prior licensing, the opening of a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA), and annual tax clearance. Profit remittance is permitted once the audited annual financial statements are approved and the corporate income tax finalization obligation is satisfied, but the bank will apply a 0.5% withholding tax rate on profit transfers to foreign shareholders pursuant to the relevant double tax treaty provisions or domestic regulations, as applicable. No significant hurdles arise in the current account conversion for a low-risk creative BPO receiving confirmed purchase orders from international game publishers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SBV Ordinance on Foreign Exchange and Circular 02/2021/TT-NHNN (as amended), all export proceeds derived from the provision of services to non-residents by Vietnamese companies, including game art outsourcing, must be repatriated to Vietnam by crediting the receipts to a foreign currency account held at a licensed domestic bank within no more than 180 days from the date of service completion or contractual settlement date, whichever occurs later. There is no mandatory conversion of foreign currency proceeds into VND at any fixed ratio; however, the enterprise may retain foreign currency balances in its account, and the bank will effect conversions to VND upon request at the prevailing market rate. Export service invoices must be accompanied by the signed service agreement, delivery of digital assets, and payment records to evidence the underlying transaction identity for foreign exchange registration purposes.

Central bank supervision: The State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury usage. Offshore fintech EMIs and digital banking platforms such as Wise, Payoneer, and Airwallex are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in Vietnam and are not permitted to open VND accounts, provide payment aggregation within Vietnam, or act as the settlement rail for inbound FDI repatriation or statutory foreign exchange registrations. These platforms may be used solely as secondary channels for collecting small-value client remittances from abroad, which must then be swept into the licensed domestic bank account through a compliant SWIFT inbound transfer. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections must be executed through the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened in the name of the VN LLC at an SBV-licensed commercial bank after the issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) and the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC). The foreign investor shall wire funds via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own overseas bank account, referencing the IRC number and the purpose code based on the IMF Balance of Payments classification for direct investment equity capital (category 7.1.1 or the SBV-designated code for FDI capital contribution under Circular 06/2019/TT-NHNN). The bank will verify the remitter's name against the registered shareholder, obtain the underlying equity transfer agreement if applicable, and issue a capital contribution voucher upon crediting funds. All inflows must be supported by the written capital injection schedule and board resolution approving the funding, and any funds from PayPal or intermediary platforms cannot be routed into the DICA; PayPal collections must first enter a separate ordinary foreign currency account only after conversion of the sales ledger into verifiable invoices.Routine payments to overseas subcontractors, royalty holders, and software licensors shall be cleared via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers through the domestic licensed bank's corresponding network, with each instruction containing the precise commercial payment narrative and transaction purpose code. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VND 50 billion (total related-party revenue within the year) triggers the requirement to prepare Local File and Master File under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP. Additionally, if the ultimate parent's consolidated revenue exceeds VND 50 billion and the Vietnam entity's related-party transactions exceed VND 30 billion, the Master File is required. Country-by-Country Report filing is required for groups with consolidated revenue above VND 18,000 billion (approximately USD 750 million).

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not have a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime as in hubs like the UAE. However, under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP and the General Anti-Avoidance Rules of the Law on Tax Administration, transactions must have economic substance and commercial rationale. A shell company arrangement without actual employees, office, or operational substance in Vietnam may be re-characterized, adjustments may be imputed, and penalties applied. Thin capitalization: related-party debt-to-equity ratio exceeding 5:1 results in disallowed interest deductions for CIT purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Revenue Suppression

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under Article 200 of the Penal Code: fines up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment up to 20 years depending on the amount evaded. Administrative penalties: up to 3 times the evaded tax amount plus late payment interest of 0.03% per day.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners involved in intentional tax evasion are personally liable; courts may impose imprisonment, travel bans, asset seizures, and civil restitution in addition to piercing the corporate veil.
Transfer Pricing Non-compliance (Shell / Low-Substance Entity)

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment by the Vietnam tax authority to impute arm's length income; penalty of 20% of the under-declared tax, or up to 3 times the evaded tax in grave cases; late payment interest; administrative fines for late document submission up to VND 50 million.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign TP documentation knowingly false or who fail to ensure substance may be jointly liable for tax arrears, subject to travel bans, and criminally liable if tax evasion is demonstrated.
Profit Stripping / Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Interest expenses exceeding the cap are non-deductible for CIT; excess interest may be re-characterized as constructive dividends; FCT and withholding tax may be reassessed. Additional CIT and late payment interest apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute artificial profit-stripping schemes may be held personally liable for resulting tax debts and fines; travel bans and criminal sanctions are possible in cases of fraud.
AML / Money-Laundering Concerns in Cross-Border Receipts

Statutory sanction: Violation of the State Bank AML regulations: fines up to VND 1 billion; criminal money-laundering liability under Article 324 of Penal Code when proceeds from crime are disguised. Accounts may be frozen; the entity may be banned from banking operations.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly permit or facilitate laundering through corporate accounts face imprisonment up to 15 years, personal fines, and asset forfeiture; corporate veil may be pierced to recover proceeds.
Foreign Contractor Withholding / Tax Administration Violations

Statutory sanction: Penalties for failure to withhold and remit: 100% of the tax amount not withheld (or 20%-3 times depending on severity); late payment interest; additional administrative fines.

Director personal liability: If the company cannot pay, directors having knowingly failed to comply may be personally assessed for unremitted withholding tax, subject to travel bans and prosecution for misappropriation of tax money.
