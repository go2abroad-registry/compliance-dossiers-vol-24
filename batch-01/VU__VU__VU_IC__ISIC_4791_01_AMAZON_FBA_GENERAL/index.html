Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management. Official portal: https://www.vrca.gov.vu/

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu levies no income tax or capital gains tax on domestic or foreign entities, so no general corporate TIN procurement exists. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is exempt from all taxes and duties and does not require a tax registration number. Only an IC that makes taxable supplies within Vanuatu exceeding the VAT threshold must register for VAT and obtain a VAT identification number through VRCA Form VAT-001, accompanied by certificate of incorporation, directors'/shareholders' register, business licence, and bank account details. Foreign entities without a physical presence in Vanuatu may not obtain a TIN unless they become VAT-registered or are statutorily required to withhold any colourable levy.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box regime, no CFC rules, no hybrid mismatch elections, and no income tax law to elect pass-through treatment. An IC may voluntarily waive its exempt international status by electing to be registered as an ordinary local company under the Companies Act [Cap. 191], thereby forfeiting the tax exemption and becoming subject to VAT and ordinary business licensing rules. No other tax classification elections are available.

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and international services are zero-rated under the Value Added Tax Act No. 12 of 1998 (s.10) where the goods are physically exported from Vanuatu or the services are supplied to a non-resident and are not connected with land or movable property in Vanuatu. For an Amazon FBA general retail operation, zero-rating is claimable only if the goods are supplied to non-resident consumers and are exported from Vanuatu customs territory; goods held or fulfilled outside Vanuatu do not enter the Vanuatu VAT chain and therefore are outside the scope of Vanuatu VAT rather than subject to zero-rating.

No mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, public API integration, or digital stamping regime is implemented in Vanuatu. VRCA permits paper and electronic tax invoices. Registered VAT suppliers must issue a valid VAT invoice showing supplier VAT number, invoice date, description, quantity, and tax amount, but there is no government-approved clearance platform or prior validation requirement.

VRCA may conduct export VAT refund audits and request full evidentiary documentation. A false zero-rating claim or fraudulent export evidence may result in refund denial, retrospective clawback of input VAT credits, penalty of 100% of the tax evaded or sought to be evaded, negligence penalties up to 50%, monthly interest of 1% on unpaid tax, deregistration from the VAT register, and criminal liability for company directors. Under the International Companies Act, fraudulent activity may additionally lead to striking-off and disqualification of the IC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations; VUV is freely convertible for current account transactions. Capital account operations of resident persons require RBV exchange control approval under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 95], but International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt from exchange control as non-resident entities and may freely hold, convert, and remit foreign currency. Profit remittance for a VU IC is unrestricted: dividends, liquidation proceeds, and shareholder loans are repatriable without VUV conversion imposts and are subject to zero withholding tax under the current zero-income-tax regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio applies to a non-resident VU International Company; Amazon FBA gross receipts in USD may be retained, swept, or converted at will in its foreign-currency operational accounts. Resident domestic exporters are required to receive export proceeds through an authorized dealer within 180 days of shipment and offer such receipts for conversion into VUV unless RBV approval to retain foreign currency is granted. The VU IC's exempt status removes all statutory conversion and repatriation timelines at the entity level.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - central bank and administrator of exchange control delegated under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 95]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) regulates International Companies and registered agents. VUV (Vanuatu Vatu, ISO 4217: VUV) is pegged to the IMF Special Drawing Rights (SDR) basket effective 1 August 2011.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All equity contributions and shareholder loans must be routed by SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire/CHIPS for USD originating in the United States) to the IC's named USD account at BSP or BRED. Remittance information in field 70/72 must state 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION - INTERNATIONAL COMPANY [LEGAL NAME] - VFSC FILE NUMBER' together with the beneficial owner's reference and the approved purpose code for capital injection (e.g., 'G2 - Equity Capital' or the correspondent bank's equivalent). Include the IC's Certificate of Incorporation and VFSC file number to ease correspondent filtering. No VUV-specific clearing rail exists; incoming USD is credited via the correspondent gateway (for example Bank of New York Mellon or Citi) with T+1/T+2 value dating. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold and no Local File or Master File regime exists because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax. However, under the Companies and Financial Institutions (Economic Substance) Act 2019, an IC that carries on the relevant activity of a distribution and service centre must maintain contemporaneous records evidencing economic substance in Vanuatu, including local employees, local premises, local board decision-making and local operating expenditure, irrespective of transaction size. There is no minimum VUV threshold for such records; the obligation is triggered by the nature of the activity, not monetary volume.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations apply to every Vanuatu incorporated company, including an International Company, unless the company is tax resident in a foreign jurisdiction appearing on the OECD list of compliant jurisdictions. An Amazon FBA IC that purchases goods from connected foreign suppliers, resells via marketplace platforms to third-party customers, or provides management services to group entities may be classified under the 'distribution and service centre' or 'headquarters' relevant activity and must satisfy all of: (a) at least 50% of directors' meetings held physically in Vanuatu with a quorum present in Vanuatu; (b) adequate qualified employees working in Vanuatu; (c) adequate physical premises available exclusively to the IC; (d) adequate local operating expenditure; and (e) core income-generating activities performed by staff in Vanuatu. A bare mailbox entity directing e-commerce operations from abroad fails the ESR test and faces automatic penalty and enforcement by the VFSC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Avoidance Abroad

Statutory sanction: VFSC deregistration and compulsory winding-up under the International Companies Act; exchange of information with foreign tax authorities under Vanuatu's TIEA and AEOI commitments; freezing and forfeiture of marketplace receivables; monetary penalties imposed by foreign revenue authorities upon recharacterisation of the IC as a sham.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil where the IC is a mere instrumentality; directors and shadow shareholders are civilly liable to restore diverted funds and pay damages; travel bans, director disqualification orders, and asset restraint orders may be issued by the Vanuatu Supreme Court; custodial sentences up to 2 years for fraud and knowingly making false statutory declarations.
Economic Substance Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Immediate penalty of VT 1,000,000, cumulative monthly penalty of VT 100,000 for continuing contravention, remedial directions compelling substance creation within a fixed period, and ultimate strike-off and dissolution for persistent non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed a false substance declaration are individually liable for the corporate penalties; criminal prosecution may impose fines up to VT 20,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 1 year; foreign principals directing the entity are treated as shadow directors and equally exposed.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Enforcement under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2019: monetary penalties up to VT 10,000,000, mandatory account freezing orders, agent licence revocation, and referral to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal investigation.

Director personal liability: Individual directors face criminal liability for money laundering and terrorist financing predicate offences, with custodial terms up to 5 years and personal financial penalties; directors may also be banned from acting as company officers in any VFSC-regulated jurisdiction.
Shell Company and Sham Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and dissolution by the VFSC without a winding-up process, seizure of residual assets by the Registrar, adverse disclosure to beneficial ownership exchange networks, and disqualification of the registered agent who failed to verify substance and ownership.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors are personally liable for unpaid government fees, agent costs and dissolution expenses; civil restitution orders compel repatriation of corporate assets to creditors; travel restrictions and extradition exposure arise where foreign tax or customs authorities issue criminal charges against the controlling person.
