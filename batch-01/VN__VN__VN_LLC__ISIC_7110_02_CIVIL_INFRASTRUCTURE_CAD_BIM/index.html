Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up charter capital applies to a foreign-invested LLC providing offshore engineering CAD/BIM services under the Law on Enterprises 2020. The charter capital stated in the Company Charter must be fully paid in by all members within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Certificate of Enterprise Registration. No escrowed account is mandatorily required by law; however, capital contributions from foreign investors must be channelled into the company's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened with a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam. Practical substance and administrative discretion favour a charter capital sufficient to sustain payroll, office lease, and bank transfer operations for the offshore service model.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety and firefighting clearance is required for the office premises in accordance with the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting of 2021 and Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP; the landlord or building manager should evidence the valid Fire Safety Certificate for the building, and the company must maintain fire extinguishers, escape signage, evacuation procedures, and staff fire safety training if the building is classified under the statutory fire prevention list. Occupational safety clearance is governed by the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13: the company must perform a workplace risk assessment, issue safety procedures for computer-based work, ensure ergonomic workstations, and contract a licensed occupational health service for periodic health checks. Structural load certification is a building owner obligation; the tenant company should obtain a copy of the building's issuance certificate and occupations permit. Civil defence registration is not a standalone licence for a leased office in an existing permitted building.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up charter capital applies to a foreign-invested LLC providing offshore engineering CAD/BIM services under the Law on Enterprises 2020. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Official portals: Electronic Tax Portal (https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn); National Electronic Invoice Portal (https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn); Electronic Portal for Foreign Suppliers (https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn); National Business Registration Portal (https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn).

E-government filing portal

Vietnam operates a unified TIN/VAT-ID system (Mã số thuế, MST). The standard SOP is: (1) obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment or the relevant Export Processing/Industrial Zone Authority; (2) file for the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) on the National Business Registration Portal; the GDT automatically generates the MST, which is embedded in the ERC and serves concurrently as the tax identification number for CIT, VAT, PIT withholding and foreign-contractor tax returns of the LLC — no separate paper TIN certificate needs to be collected; (3) complete post-registration e-tax formalities: activate the business profile at the competent local Tax Sub-Department, register the electronic tax transaction and payment account on https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn, purchase an accredited digital signature (USB token), and file the e-invoice registration on the National Electronic Invoice Portal; (4) register the LLC's legal-branch/office and bank account details in the national database; (5) if a foreign parent or offshore contractor sells digital/B2C services into Vietnam without a PE, that foreign entity separately registers under the Foreign Contractor Withholding Tax or via the etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn portal — the VN LLC itself does not obtain a separate VAT certificate beyond the MST.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the foreign-invested LLC is a Vietnamese resident enterprise, taxed at entity level on its worldwide CIT income (standard 20% headrate); it is NOT a fiscally transparent or pass-through vehicle and no taxpayer election to be treated as a disregarded entity exists. The territorial scope is a worldwide-residency base: the enterprise's offshore engineering/BIM/CAD export turnover is taxable CIT income in Vietnam (subject to DTT exemptions and the 0% VAT export regime where applicable). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the foreign-invested LLC is a Vietnamese resident enterprise, taxed at entity level on its worldwide CIT income (standard 20% headrate); it is NOT a fiscally transparent or pass-through vehicle and no taxpayer election to be treated as a disregarded entity exists. The territorial scope is a worldwide-residency base: the enterprise's offshore engineering/BIM/CAD export turnover is taxable CIT income in Vietnam (subject to DTT exemptions and the 0% VAT export regime where applicable). EPE/software/export-service tax incentives may reduce effective CIT rates but do not alter the entity-level classification.

Available election forms

Vietnam does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election mechanisms for entity classification. Statutory options available to the LLC include: (i) election between VAT credit/refund method and the direct method of VAT calculation where eligible; (ii) application of preferential CIT/incentive regimes (e.g., EPE status, software-product enterprise, high-technology enterprise) through licensing rather than tax-form election; (iii) under the DTT, election to apply PE-threshold analysis and claim treaty relief/benefits via MAP and APA procedures with the GDT's Treaty Division; (iv) for non-resident service providers acceding as subcontractors, the optional application of the Foreign Contractor Withholding Tax regime under Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC; (v) grouping of related-party documentation under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP with no one-way-up election permitted; and (vi) an election for filing/accounting year calendar-year alignment. No unlimited loss carry-forward election: CIT losses can be carried forward continuously up to five years only.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Corporations/LLCs are mandatorily VAT-registered upon incorporation; no annual revenue threshold applies to a company as legal person. The VND 100,000,000 per annum economic threshold is relevant only to individuals/household businesses opting out of full VAT registration. Foreign digital suppliers or foreign contractors without a PE in Vietnam have no turnover exemption threshold and must register on the GDT's electronic foreign supplier portal (etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn) irrespective of local revenue, provided the supply is made to Vietnamese customers (B2C domestic consumption).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero percent VAT on exported services (Article 9 of the VAT framework and Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC) is available to the LLC where: (1) the service (offshore BIM, structural CAD/drafting, engineering design) is supplied to a foreign organization or individual; (2) the foreign customer has no permanent establishment in Vietnam with respect to the transaction; (3) the service is consumed entirely outside Vietnam and the output/data are used abroad; (4) the sales contract is written and signed with the foreign counterparty; (5) payment is received in a freely convertible foreign currency from an account abroad and is remitted into the LLC's Vietnamese bank account, evidenced by an inward remittance certificate / SWIFT; and (6) the LLC retains a complete export dossier comprising the contract, 0% e-invoice, delivery/production acceptance report, proof of offshore use, and bank documentation. Services expressly excluded from zero-rating (e.g., shipping brokerage, reinsurance/brokerage, training, domestic-consumption services) are not covered; those remain 10% taxable.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic customs declaration E-5/Form HQ/2015/EXP) — applicable only when physical design kits, drawings, or hardware prototypes are exported; for a pure service export no physical customs declaration is filed and this item is substituted by the service delivery certificate
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — applicable only when tangible goods/samples are shipped; for BIM/CAD service deliverables substitute a signed service acceptance and handover certificate (biên bản bàn giao) evidencing offshore consumption
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) from the LLC's Vietnamese licensed bank, or the SWIFT MT103/FX evidence, confirming that the foreign buyer's payment in a freely convertible currency was received from an offshore account
  • Written offshore service contract entered with the non-resident client, defining the BIM/CAD scope and territorial consumption
  • Zero-rated VAT e-invoice (0%) issued to the foreign client in compliance with Decree 123/2020/ND-CP
  • Supporting export-compliance file: delivery logs, IP handover data, transfer records, foreign client's confirmation of the off-shore use, and the LLC's VAT/CIT ledgers demonstrating the export turnover and input VAT reconciliation

All VAT- and CIT-relevant invoices must be e-invoices under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC in XML format, with the mandatory fiscal QR code and the supplier's digital signature. For high-risk classes, the enterprise applies an 'invoice with tax authority code' where the GDT's portal pre-numbers and digitally clears the invoice in real time; for standard enterprise issuance (no tax code), the invoice is generated locally and the data file (XML) must be transferred to the National Electronic Invoice Portal (https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) immediately at issuance (technically near real-time API submission), after which the GDT assigns the invoice serial number confirmation; export zero-rated invoices are also transmitted through the same portal. There is no physical wet-ink stamping; the QR code serves as the fiscal stamp/clearance marker. POS-linked fiscal devices require real-time data streaming to the tax authority. The LLC must maintain its invoice-books ledger (account 5113/3331) reconciled to the portal registers.

The GDT may audit export zero-rating claims for up to five years (and longer for transfer-pricing/related-party issues). Where an export VAT application fails the statutory test, the tax authority recharacterizes the revenue to the standard 10% VAT rate, issues an additional VAT assessment, claws back any input VAT already refunded or offset, and charges late-payment interest at 0.03% per day (per Article 59 of Law 38/2019/QH12). Administrative sanctions under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP include fines up to three times the evaded tax amount (with organizational maximums set by the decree) and forfeiture of refund entitlements; false invoicing/electronic data manipulation can trigger criminal tax-evasion liability under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015, with penalties up to 7 years' imprisonment and fines up to 4.5 times the evaded tax where the evaded amount exceeds the statutory criminal threshold. The LLC's legal representative and chief accountant may bear personal liability for signed false declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam operates a split current/capital account regime. The current account is fully convertible for bona fide trade in services: a resident LLC billing non-resident clients for CAD/BIM engineering work may receipt foreign currency without prior SBV licensing, provided (i) the underlying Master Service Agreement and delivery acceptance are on file, (ii) the proceeds are remitted to an account at a licensed Vietnamese bank within the statutory 180-day repatriation window, and (iii) the servicing bank captures the transaction under its balance-of-payments reporting. The capital account, by contrast, remains controlled: inbound FDI equity and shareholder loans require an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC), charter capital contribution schedule, a dedicated Foreign Investment Capital Account (FICA) issued by the licensed bank, and, for loans, SBV ex-ante registration; outbound remittances of dividends, royalties, and liquidation proceeds require audited Vietnamese financial statements, tax finalization certificates, and board resolutions. Non-resident direct ownership in the LLC is freely permitted with no minimum capital floor under Viet Nam's enterprise regime, but all fund flows must pass through a domestically licensed credit or payment institution; offshore-digital EMI rails are not a recognized channel for satisfying statutory receipt-repatriation rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory conversion ('surrender') ratio applicable to the LLC's export service receipts. Under the 2005 Foreign Exchange Ordinance (as amended) and SBV implementing guidance, receipts from the export of engineering/BIM services must be repatriated to Viet Nam and credited to a FX demand-deposit account (or export proceeds account) at a licensed credit institution within 180 days following the completion of services or issuance of the corresponding invoice, whichever is earlier. A licensed bank may grant a single extension of up to 30 additional days upon verified documentary justification. The LLC may retain 100% of USD receipts in the account without forced sale to VND; conversion to VND for payroll and local operating expenses is voluntary and executed at the onshore prevailing market rate agreed with the bank. The bank is obliged to report any overdue receipt and any failure to repatriate to the SBV as a foreign exchange infringement, exposing the LLC to penalties between VND 80,000,000 and VND 100,000,000 plus possible suspension of the bank authorization.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV; Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam), operating under the Foreign Exchange Ordinance No. 05/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, as amended, together with implementing decrees and SBV circulars regulating foreign exchange, banking, and current/capital account activities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium feasibility as a primary rail. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) are not licensed credit institutions or authorized payment-service settlement providers under the SBV's payment or FX regime; they cannot open a statutory FICA and cannot give the LLC the documentary 'licensed bank credit' that satisfies export-proceeds repatriation compliance. Their permitted use is limited to an ancillary collection and aggregation layer for small-value trial invoices, subject to the mandatory condition that all EMI balances are swept to the LLC's licensed VN bank account no later than the 180-day window; the LLC then must present EMI statements as reconciliation evidence in the bank's annual due-diligence review. Any pattern of lingering offshore balances or direct client-to-EMI billing without a corresponding onshore licensed-bank credit will be treated by the servicing bank as a foreign-exchange breach and trigger remedial KYC intervention.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial and subsequent FDI equity contributions must be routed exclusively by SWIFT MT103 from the offshore parent to the LLC's Foreign Investment Capital Account (FICA) at the licensed Vietnamese bank, using the balance-of-payments purpose code for inward FDI equity capital (approximately code 101a under the SBV international-transaction classification), with the remittance reference quoting the IRC registration number and the charter capital installment number. The servicing bank verifies the credit against the approved IRC and capital-contribution schedule; the FICA funds are then converted or disbursed into the operational VND account only after the bank has sighted the lawful source documentation (MT103 allocation and contributor identity). Any shareholder loan requires SBV ex-ante registration through the servicing bank before remittance and must be contracted at arm's-length interest under Decree 68/2016/ND-CP (transfer pricing) frameworks; loan drawdowns are separately coded and cannot be commingled with equity injections.Service-trade outflows (subcontract design fees, software licenses, royalties, and permitted profit repatriation) are cleared by standard SWIFT MT103/telegraphic transfers through the LLC's licensed bank international desk, supported by (i) the underlying service or licensing contract, (ii) Vietnamese tax receipts evidencing withholding (CIT and Foreign Contractor Tax) where applicable, and (iii) a board resolution capturing dividend or payment approval. Profit/retained-earnings repatriations require completion of the statutory annual independent audit, filing of the audited financial statements and tax finalization to the General Department of Taxation, and the bank's verification of net-after-tax distributable profits; the LLC then remits in convertible currency with the BOP code for repatriation of investment profits. Commercial Letters of Credit are structurally unnecessary for CAD/BIM services, but where a foreign client insists on a documentary rail, the LLC may accept a Standby or Confirmed LC; proceeds so received are treated as export service receipts and remain subject to the identical 180-day repatriation and KYC verification protocol.Deliverable FX forward contracts (outright forwards) with a licensed Vietnamese credit institution, executed only against a genuine underlying service-export or dividend-repatriation transaction, with tenors capped at 365 days under SBV Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN (as amended by Circular 20/2018/TT-NHNN); the bank must retain copies of the underlying contract and invoice.FX swap contracts (same-currency or cross-currency principal exchange) executed bilaterally with the licensed bank to convert USD earnings into VND for operating purposes and re-convert at a future fixed rate, supported by the same underlying transaction documentation.Currency options (call/put) where offered by the licensed bank as structural hedging for capital-account exposures such as FICA conversion or shareholder-loan repayment; the option premium and notional must be derived from the registered loan or contribution schedule.Natural hedging via staggered multi-currency invoicing (USD and EUR tranches) and disciplined timing of outflows within the 180-day repatriation window, plus an offshore parent-level NDF on VND used purely as a portfolio hedge; onshore NDFs are not recognized and SBV-licensed banks will not book them for a VN resident entity.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low-Medium feasibility as a primary rail. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) are not licensed credit institutions or authorized payment-service settlement providers under the SBV's payment or FX regime; they cannot open a statutory FICA and cannot give the LLC the documentary 'licensed bank credit' that satisfies export-proceeds repatriation compliance. Their permitted use is limited to an ancillary collection and aggregation layer for small-value trial invoices, subject to the mandatory condition that all EMI balances are swept to the LLC's licensed VN bank account no later than the 180-day window; the LLC then must present EMI statements as reconciliation evidence in the bank's annual due-diligence review. Any pattern of lingering offshore balances or direct client-to-EMI billing without a corresponding onshore licensed-bank credit will be treated by the servicing bank as a foreign-exchange breach and trigger remedial KYC intervention.
