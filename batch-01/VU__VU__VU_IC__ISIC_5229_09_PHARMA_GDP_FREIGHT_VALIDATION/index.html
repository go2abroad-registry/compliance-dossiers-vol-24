Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate from Vanuatu National Disaster Management Office / Fire and Emergency Services; structural load certification from a licensed engineer for racking and high-bay storage areas; occupational workplace safety clearance issued by the Labour Department under the Health and Safety at Work Act, covering first-aid kits, fire drills, log of maintenance, and certification of powered material handling equipment.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital required; paid-up capital may be nominal (commonly US$1 or VUV 100); no escrow account or capital duty is required for an International Company.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Service (VCIR), an agency within the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management of the Republic of Vanuatu; primary e-portal: VCIR/ASYCUDA World customs portal (www.customs.gov.vu); corporate registry and tax-exemption certificates: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) is incorporated by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] and receives a Certificate of Incorporation together with a statutory tax exemption from all Vanuatu taxes. No TIN or VAT ID is issued because Vanuatu imposes no income tax and no VAT/GST. If the IC physically moves pharmaceutical cargo across the Vanuatu border, it must register with VCIR for an Importer/Exporter Registration Number (RIN) in ASYCUDA World before lodging customs declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or entity-classification election exists; no hybrid-mismatch election is available because no corporate income tax base exists in Vanuatu. An IC may elect to convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act [Cap. 191], voluntarily relinquishing its ring-fenced IC tax exemption, but no additional tax liability would arise.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT, GST, or sales and use tax is imposed. Indirect taxation is limited to customs import/export duties under the Customs Act [Cap. 145], the Customs Tariff Act, and statutory stamp duties.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. For customs purposes, every importer/exporter must obtain a VCIR registration number irrespective of turnover; the standard pharmaceutical logistics import duty rate is calculated on the CIF value under the Customs Tariff Act.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Vanuatu has no VAT/GST, so there is no 0% export VAT rate, no export VAT refund mechanism, and no VAT credit to substantiate. Exports of goods from Vanuatu are outside the scope of any indirect tax (except narrow statutory export duties on specific natural resources), and offshore GDP pharma freight services supplied by an IC are exempt from Vanuatu tax under the International Companies Act by statutory design.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CUS-EXP) lodged via ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement of GDP pharmaceutical cargo
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by the settlement bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed at destination)
  • GDP temperature-lane records and Certificate of Analysis (pharma compliance documents, not tax documents)

Not applicable - Vanuatu imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, API tax clearance, or digital stamping mandate. Customs declarations are processed electronically through ASYCUDA World, which serves as the single window for border clearance.

No VAT clawback is available because no VAT/export refund exists. Audit scrutiny is exercised by VCIR under the Customs Act [Cap. 145]: false export declarations, undervaluation, or forged shipping documents attract forfeiture of goods, fines up to VUV 100,000, and imprisonment. Misuse of the IC tax-exemption certificate is penalized by VFSC under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] via monetary penalties, deregistration, and strike-off. Anti-money-laundering enforcement under the Financial Transaction Reporting Act applies to documentary fraud in cross-border freight payments.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalized current account with full convertibility for trade and service transactions, and a de facto unrestricted capital account for non-resident investors and International Companies (ICs). ICs incorporated under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] are statutorily exempt from Vanuatu exchange-control restrictions on foreign currency accounts and offshore dealings, provided they do not transact in the domestic VUV economy with Vanuatu residents. Profit, dividend, interest, and liquidation remittances are free of RBV prior approval and are not burdened by corporate income tax, withholding tax, or capital gains tax applicable to the IC; the practical constraints are correspondent-bank overlay risk on USD/AUD clearing and the thin, non-international depth of the VUV, making USD or AUD the effective treasury currencies for the ISIC 5229 GDP pharma freight operation, while receipts from clients in France, Australia, New Zealand, or the wider region may be settled across the corresponding clearing systems.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement: the RBV prescribes neither a mandatory conversion ratio into VUV nor a prescribed repatriation timeline for export receipts, and both resident exporters and non-resident International Companies may retain, reinvest, or disburse foreign currency earnings freely. For ISIC 5229 GDP pharma freight service receivables, USD/AUD/EUR invoices may be credited in full to operational foreign-currency accounts; the only formalities are accurate balance-of-payments transaction coding, commercial invoice and airway-bill/waybill evidence supporting the export of services, and record-retention and reporting duties under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act No. 35 of 2000 (as amended), which align with ordinary AML/CFT expectations of the banking counterparties.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [CAP 125], acting as the central bank, sole issuer of the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV), prudential regulator of licensed banks, and administrator of the national exchange-control framework under the Exchange Control Act [CAP 100], with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as corporate registry, AML/CFT supervisor, and offshore licensing authority for international companies and dealers in securities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM (conditional). The RBV does not operate an e-money institution licensing regime, and offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Paysera, Currenxie) hold no passport, equivalence, or local registration right in Vanuatu; in practice most leading EMIs decline or substantially restrict corporate applicants whose registered address or controlling persons are in Vanuatu, citing correspondent overlay risk and lack of VUV clearing support. The EMI may therefore serve only as a secondary, non-exclusive disbursement rail hosted in a top-tier regulated foreign jurisdiction, using USD/SGD/EUR transit accounts for payables in well-covered corridors; it cannot be the primary rail for the GDP pharma freight validation operation, and any VUV-denominated or domestic-Vanuatu activity must remain with an onshore licensed commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity subscription or shareholder-loan capital should be injected by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for high-value transfers) from the shareholder's regulated bank to the IC's operational account at ANZ Vanuatu or BSP Vanuatu, with the beneficiary account denominated in USD or AUD, because VUV settlement is operationally inefficient for capital flows. No prior RBV exchange-control approval is required for non-resident IC funding; however, the remitting institution must classify the transfer under a recognized balance-of-payments purpose code - direct-investment equity (commonly IIF code 221) for share capital, or direct-investment intercompany debt (commonly code 223) for shareholder loans - and the narrative must state 'Contribution to paid-up capital - International Companies Act [CAP 222]' or 'Shareholder loan - intercompany funding' as applicable. The Vanuatu receiving bank will match the wire against the corporate registration, the board resolution approving the capital injection, the register of charges (for loan-funded injections), the executed loan agreement, and the UBO KYC pack on file; a subordination agreement between the shareholder and the IC is recommended to maintain clean equity classification and simplify future remittance of distribution proceeds.Outbound settlements for the GDP pharma freight operation should use three clearing rails: (1) primary SWIFT MT103 payments in USD or AUD for carriers, cold-chain logistics providers, insurers, customs brokers, and other service suppliers, routed through the Vanuatu bank's correspondent corridor (Australian/NZ or US clearing) with full invoice and purchase-order references and the ISIC 5229 freight-logistics service descriptor in the remittance information; (2) documentary commercial Letters of Credit issued as irrevocable sight LCs (SWIFT MT700) where a pharmaceutical importer or contracted carrier requires payment security, with UCP 600 document checking comprising airway bill or bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, GDP certificate, certificate of origin, and insurance policy; and (3) Direct ACH or instant payments via the approved top-tier EMI transit account only where beneficiaries maintain collection accounts in well-covered USD/GBP/EUR corridors and where the transaction does not involve domestic Vanuatu counterparties. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no enacted transfer-pricing documentation rules, no local file/master file thresholds, and no VUV monetary threshold. The zero-income-tax regime removes the need for transfer pricing compliance.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not apply Economic Substance Regulations comparable to BVI/Cayman. However, the VFSC imposes a licensed-agent substance requirement for International Companies, and a VAT/customs-exempt entity engaged in physical GDP logistics must retain real management records and a physical presence where regulated activity occurs; the zero-tax benefit is lost only if the IC is used to shield unlicensed local commercial activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration of Tax-Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: VFSC deregistration, disqualification from re-registration, monetary penalties under the International Companies Act, and recovery of unpaid customs duties and business license fees together with punitive surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may be jointly and severally liable for restitution of avoided revenue; deliberate false declarations trigger criminal prosecution, travel bans, and imprisonment under Vanuatu fraud statutes.
Shell Company / Abuse of Corporate Form

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off, forfeiture of the exempt status, customs and VAT assessments, and regulatory penalties for acting without the required GDP freight license.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil to impose civil restitution on directors and shadow directors; responsible persons face direct civil claims by affected logistics counterparties and regulatory disqualification orders.
Money Laundering / AML-CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Freezing of assets, penalty fines under Vanuatu's Proceeds of Crime and AML/CFT legislation, confiscation of the corporate vehicle, and cancellation of the VFSC registration.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and nominee shareholders who knowingly facilitate laundering face custodial sentences, personal asset forfeiture, travel bans, and post-conviction disqualification from managing any Vanuatu company.
