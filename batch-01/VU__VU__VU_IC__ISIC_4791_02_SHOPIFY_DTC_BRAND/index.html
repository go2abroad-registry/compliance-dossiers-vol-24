Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), Companies Registry — International Companies Section, Port Vila, Vanuatu.

Governing statute

Vanuatu International Companies Act No. 32 of 1992, consolidated as Cap. 222 (IC Act), Part II (Incorporation) ss. 5–10; International Companies (Fees) Regulations; International Companies (Records) Regulations; Companies Act Cap. 191 applies only to domestic companies and is dis-applied to International Companies.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association executed by the subscriber, submitted with Form IC-1 (Application for Incorporation of an International Company) via the VFSC e-Registry portal.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 16 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital; no escrow or capital deposit requirement. The IC Act permits authorized capital of USD 50,000 or such amount as resolved; at least one issued share of USD 1.00 (or no par value) must be allotted to the subscriber upon incorporation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification and structural load inspections are not applicable for the remote-only DTC operation; the registered office is housed in a licensed agent's commercial premises in Port Vila holding a valid Certificate of Occupancy from the Port Vila Municipal Council. Remote employees are subject to the occupational health and ergonomic guidance of Vanuatu's Employment Act Cap. 160; no hazardous machinery, chemical storage, or high-voltage installations are present in the virtual workplace.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital; no escrow or capital deposit requirement. The IC Act permits authorized capital of USD 50,000 or such amount as resolved; at least one issued share of USD 1.00 (or no par value) must be allotted to the subscriber upon incorporation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Department (VRCD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management — Government portal: https://www.gov.vu; corporate and offshore registry: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), portal: https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

The International Company (IC) is formed through a licensed registered agent under the International Companies Act and receives a VFSC corporate registration number; a separate TIN/VAT ID is not required for an IC that conducts no Vanuatu domestic business. A Tax Exemption Certificate is obtained through the registered agent from VFSC/VRCD confirming exemption from Vanuatu income taxes. If the IC later makes or intends to make taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding the VAT threshold, the prescribed VRCD VAT registration application must be submitted with the Certificate of Incorporation, constitutional documents, registered office confirmation, and projected turnover evidence; VRCD then issues a VAT registration number. For a pure offshore DTC Shopify brand, the company should remain outside the Vanuatu VAT registration net.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — The International Company (IC) is formed through a licensed registered agent under the International Companies Act and receives a VFSC corporate registration number; a separate TIN/VAT ID is not required for an IC that conducts no Vanuatu domestic business. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu does not operate a check-the-box entity classification election and has not enacted hybrid mismatch or pass-through taxation elections. The International Company's tax-exempt classification is automatic under statute; no election is available to subject an IC to Vanuatu corporate income tax or to reclassify it for foreign tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 (approximately USD 34,000) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted; an offshore International Company with no Vanuatu local taxable supplies is not subject to the registration obligation

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered supplier may claim the 0% VAT rate on goods physically exported from Vanuatu to a non-resident where the supplier retains proof of export, including a customs-approved export declaration and transport documentation. Specified services supplied to a non-resident and consumed outside Vanuatu may also qualify for zero-rating if the effective use and enjoyment occurs outside Vanuatu. For a Vanuatu International Company engaged only in offshore DTC e-commerce sales, there is no Vanuatu VAT nexus and no zero-rating claim is required; where the IC is VAT-registered, the same export-evidence requirements apply and input VAT attributable to export sales may be refunded. Funds for export sales should be received within 180 days to evidence commercial reality and support the zero-rating claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (lodged and approved through ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill / Master Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and original purchase order
  • Packing List / cargo manifest
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103 / payment credit evidence
  • Proof of non-resident buyer and foreign destination (customer address, IP/country record, delivery confirmation)
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment or treaty benefit is claimed)

No real-time e-invoicing, no digital fiscal stamping, and no continuous API clearance mandate is currently in force. Export declarations must be filed through the ASYCUDA World customs clearance system. VAT invoices, whether paper or electronic, must contain the prescribed particulars under the VAT Act. Shopify-generated invoices are acceptable as record evidence only when fully reconcilable to customs export documents, shipping records, and payment remittances.

VRCD audit scrutiny applies to the complete export chain. Zero-rating is denied and 12.5% VAT is assessed on the full export consideration if the prescribed documentation is incomplete or inconsistent. Any VAT refund obtained on false evidence is retrospectively clawed back by protective assessment and recoverable as tax due with statutory interest. Civil penalties, including penalties up to the amount of the tax shortfall and further default penalties, apply under the VAT Act; criminal liability, including fines and imprisonment, arises for knowingly false customs declarations and fraudulent refund claims. The limitation period for reassessment is extended in cases of fraud, and the IC's tax-exempt status may be revoked or deregistration effected where the regime is abused.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal currency convertibility regime across both current and capital account transactions. There are no statutory restrictions on profit repatriation, dividend distribution, loan repayment, or capital redemption for non-resident shareholders of an International Company. Convertibility hurdles are limited to standard bank-level due diligence, requiring proof of underlying transaction validity, source of funds, and compliance with amounts requiring notification under RBV reporting thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu does not impose mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds. Exporters may retain all export receipts in any freely convertible currency. However, banks must file prescribed foreign exchange and AML/CFT reports with RBV and the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for transactions above established monetary thresholds, ensuring traceability of export-related trade flows.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) is the central bank and monetary authority, responsible for issuing banking licenses, supervising licensed banks, and administering foreign exchange and monetary policy functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to medium feasibility. Vanuatu does not issue electronic money institution (EMI) licenses to non-resident digital payment providers, and offshore EMIs regulated under FCA or EU frameworks may offer multi-currency transaction accounts but cannot directly clear VUV through RBV settlement infrastructure. Their dependence on correspondent banking in Australia or New Zealand introduces latency and execution risk, making them unsuitable for primary treasury operations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share capital or shareholder loan injections should be routed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfers from a reputable regulated bank to the designated Vanuatu operating or escrow account. The transfer instruction must reference a clear economic purpose such as 'International Company capital contribution' or 'Shareholder loan facility - VU_IC', accompanied by board resolutions, capital allocation letters, and source-of-funds evidence.
