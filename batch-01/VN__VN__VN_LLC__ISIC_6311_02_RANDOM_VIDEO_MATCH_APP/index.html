Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation of Viet Nam (Tong Cuc Thue), Ministry of Finance. Electronic tax portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

The foreign-invested LLC is formed by obtaining an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment. Upon ERC issuance, the LLC is automatically assigned a 10-digit Tax Identification Number (Ma So Thue) by the tax authority. The LLC must then complete VAT registration and tax information registration on the GDT e-tax portal, obtain digital certificates for e-invoicing, and activate electronic tax payment. If an automatic TIN is not assigned, the LLC must file Form 01-DK-TCT with the local General Department of Taxation office within 30 days of enterprise registration. For payments to non-residents, a foreign contractor tax registration may also be required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A Vietnamese LLC is a resident corporate enterprise taxed at entity level under Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income. No pass-through taxation; no default tax-exempt zone treatment unless the enterprise is separately licensed as an Export Processing Enterprise (EPE) or located in an industrial/export processing zone with specific incentives.

Available election forms

None available. Vietnam has no check-the-box election, no partnership-classification election for LLCs, and no hybrid mismatch entity election. The LLC is irrevocably a CIT taxpayer under Vietnamese law. Only fiscal-year election and certain accounting-method elections are available; these do not alter entity-level tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 100,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the threshold for business households and individual manufacturers to register for VAT; however, all enterprises, including LLCs, are subject to mandatory VAT registration and must register for VAT under the national tax ID upon enterprise registration, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for goods physically exported under customs supervision, goods and services supplied to export processing enterprises and non-tariff zones, and services supplied and consumed entirely outside Vietnam in accordance with Article 9 of the VAT Law and Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC. For the Video Chat Social App, subscription, virtual gift, and advertising revenue from foreign users located outside Vietnam and consumed offshore qualifies for 0% VAT provided the LLC maintains contracts, platform transaction reports, and non-cash cross-border payment evidence. Revenue from Vietnamese users is subject to the standard 10% VAT rate. Apple IAP and Paymentwall receipts must be supported by remittance records and platform statements demonstrating the foreign purchaser/consumption location, and the absence of Vietnamese consumption.

All Vietnamese enterprises must issue electronic invoices in XML format with a digital signature, transmit invoice data to the General Department of Taxation's e-invoice system, and obtain a 32-digit tax authority invoice code. Tax-authority-coded e-invoices must be transmitted for clearance before issuance; self-generated e-invoices without authority codes must be transmitted to the GDT no later than the end of the business day for digital verification. API integration is mandatory for larger taxpayers and supported on the GDT e-invoice portal. No physical paper stamping applies; the digital stamp/timestamp and the tax authority invoice code constitute clearance. Invoices for revenue from foreign customers qualifying as exported services must be zero-rated; domestic revenue from Vietnamese users is invoiced at 10% VAT.

Export zero-rating claims are highly scrutinized. If the GDT determines that the goods or services did not qualify for export, the VAT is retrospectively assessed at 10% with late-payment interest at 0.03% per day. Administrative penalties range from 100% to 300% of the underdeclared VAT amount depending on tax-fraud severity. Input VAT credits previously claimed on related purchases are clawed back. In cases of fake invoices, fictitious export contracts, or under-declared foreign revenue, the taxpayer faces criminal liability for tax evasion with penalties up to 20 years' imprisonment, fines, and confiscation of assets. All supporting documentation must be retained for at least 10 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam maintains current-account convertibility in accordance with its IMF Article VIII obligations; payments and transfers for current international transactions, including cross-border service fees, advertising disbursements, and profit remittances, may be settled in freely usable foreign currencies through a licensed credit institution without prior central bank approval. Capital-account transactions, however, remain restricted: foreign direct investment equity contributions must be routed through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained at a licensed commercial bank, and external borrowings, intercompany loans, capital redemption, divestment proceeds, and transfer of investment capital require State Bank of Vietnam registration or approval before the funds can be moved offshore. Profit remittance is classified as a current transaction but carries substantive procedural hurdles: the foreign-invested LLC must have (i) fully executed the charter-capital contribution schedule reflected in its Investment Registration Certificate, (ii) obtained a complete set of annual audited financial statements from a State Bank-approved auditing firm, (iii) settled all corporate income tax, foreign contractor tax, value added tax, and social insurance obligations, (iv) received a shareholder resolution approving the dividend or profit distribution, and (v) submitted the quarterly DICA reporting package through its principal bank. In practice, the first profit remittance may be delayed if the audited statement reveals accumulated losses, outstanding intercompany payables, or unresolved tax arrears; contraband profit distribution without audited evidence is treated as an illegal capital-outflow event and may trigger administrative penalties and suspension of the foreign investor's remittance rights.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 06/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP, and State Bank of Vietnam implementing guidance for current transactions, a resident enterprise earning foreign currency from exports of goods or services must channel such proceeds exclusively through the licensed banking system. The statutory mandate vests the State Bank with authority to impose mandatory surrender ratios on export earnings; however, in current routine administration, the State Bank has calibrated the mandatory surrender ratio at zero for ordinary exporters, allowing the enterprise to retain up to 100% of service proceeds in a foreign-currency current account opened at a licensed credit institution. The binding operational requirements are: (i) export and cross-border service receipts must be credited to the enterprise's foreign-currency payment account within 15 working days of the value date; (ii) all domestic payments between residents must be settled in Vietnamese dong (VND), requiring conversion of retained foreign currency at market rates quoted by the licensed bank; (iii) outward transfer of retained export proceeds is prohibited except where supported by underlying current-transaction documentation such as import contracts, tax remittance vouchers, royalty and license agreements, or a lawful profit-remittance dossier; and (iv) the State Bank may, exceptionally, reinstate surrender requirements as an emergency macroprudential measure during balance-of-payments pressure, in which case the ratio and timeline will be fixed by an SBV circular and applied through the principal bank.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Low as a primary corporate treasury rail; Medium only as an ancillary collection channel. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Airwallex, WorldFirst, Payoneer, and similar non-bank payment orchestrators are not 'licensed credit institutions' under the Law on Credit Institutions and cannot lawfully maintain the Direct Investment Capital Account, cannot issue SBV-recognized foreign-exchange declarations, and cannot execute the statutory current-transaction settlement chain required for a Vietnamese foreign-invested LLC. If the EMI attempts to receive the foreign investor's charter-capital injection or service-export collections, the funds would be deemed received outside the SBV-controlled banking channel and would expose the company to foreign-exchange control violations, rejection of profit-remittance dossiers, and AML/CFT sanctions. Such EMIs may lawfully participate only as non-bank payment intermediaries (for example, facilitating App Store merchant settlements) if they operate under an SBV-licensed Payment Intermediary Services arrangement or through a licensed domestic intermediary payment service provider; the definitive treasury ledger and all FX conversion must remain within the onshore licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All foreign charter-capital injections must be routed through the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) that the foreign-invested LLC maintains at a licensed commercial bank. The remitting foreign shareholder must initiate a SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for cover) wire with the beneficiary account equal to the DICA number, beneficiary name exactly matching the Vietnamese LLC legal name, and the remittance information field referencing the Enterprise Registration Certificate number, the Investment Registration Certificate number, and the foreign investor's legal name. The receiving bank will apply its internal foreign-transfer purpose code identifying 'foreign direct investment capital contribution' (góp vốn đầu tư trực tiếp nước ngoài); the investor must not use generic purpose codes such as 'payment for services' or 'business operations capital', as those will be rejected or reclassified as unlawful loans. The contribution must be completed within the schedule stated in the IRC; the statutory default for contribution completion is 90 days from the date of enterprise registration unless the IRC provides a longer timeline. Upon receipt, the principal bank verifies the cumulative funded amount against the registered charter capital and reports the inflow to the State Bank through the quarterly DICA reporting mechanism. Commercial letters of credit (L/Cs) and documentary collections are not appropriate for intangible service transactions and should be reserved only for future equipment or hardware importation. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: Low as a primary corporate treasury rail; Medium only as an ancillary collection channel. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Airwallex, WorldFirst, Payoneer, and similar non-bank payment orchestrators are not 'licensed credit institutions' under the Law on Credit Institutions and cannot lawfully maintain the Direct Investment Capital Account, cannot issue SBV-recognized foreign-exchange declarations, and cannot execute the statutory current-transaction settlement chain required for a Vietnamese foreign-invested LLC. If the EMI attempts to receive the foreign investor's charter-capital injection or service-export collections, the funds would be deemed received outside the SBV-controlled banking channel and would expose the company to foreign-exchange control violations, rejection of profit-remittance dossiers, and AML/CFT sanctions. Such EMIs may lawfully participate only as non-bank payment intermediaries (for example, facilitating App Store merchant settlements) if they operate under an SBV-licensed Payment Intermediary Services arrangement or through a licensed domestic intermediary payment service provider; the definitive treasury ledger and all FX conversion must remain within the onshore licensed bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Vietnam does not impose a separate withholding tax on dividend distributions made by a Vietnamese company to its foreign shareholders. After finalization of CIT and approval of the annual financial statements, after-tax profits can be repatriated freely through the corporate bank account without additional dividend withholding. The only prerequisite is compliance with the Law on Enterprises 2020: dividends are payable only from accumulated after-tax profits, the company must remain solvent after distribution, and the shareholder resolution plus supporting audited financial statements must be maintained. The foreign investor remains subject to any home-country tax on the dividend, offset only where a relevant DTA provision or unilateral foreign tax credit exists.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Vietnam (VND) – Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP and Circular 09/2021/TT-BTC: (i) Local File is mandatory when the total value of controlled (related-party) transactions during the fiscal year exceeds VND 30 billion; (ii) Master File is mandatory when the taxpayer's total annual revenue exceeds VND 50 billion or the total value of controlled transactions exceeds VND 30 billion; (iii) Country-by-Country Report is required when the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue equals or exceeds VND 18 trillion. Taxpayers with total related-party transaction value below VND 30 billion, total revenue below VND 50 billion, and no transactions with parties resident in low-tax jurisdictions are exempt from the Local File requirement but must still file the TP disclosure form (Form 01/QL-DN a/k/a Phụ lục III) with the annual CIT finalization. All VAT-eligible and economically substantive transactions should be documented contemporaneously with the arm's length analysis; documentation must be prepared before the annual finalization and submitted to the tax authority within 30 days of a formal TP audit request.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not adopt the UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations licensing framework, but enforces a strict economic-substance doctrine through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) under Article 5 of Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, Article 19 of the Tax Administration Law 38/2019, and the substance-over-form jurisprudence applied by Vietnamese courts. Tax authorities may disregard transactions, reclassify entities, and re-determine taxable income where intercompany arrangements lack genuine commercial rationale, have no real business purpose, or are designed principally to obtain tax or treaty benefits. A foreign-invested LLC engaging in video matching services must demonstrate: actual management and control in Vietnam, a physical office or demonstrable operational presence, resident employees performing material functions, real business risk assumption, ongoing revenue recognition in Vietnamese books, and a local bank account that receives and disburses commercial payments. Where the LLC is a mere nominal entity with decision-making and value creation outside Vietnam, the tax authority will treat the foreign principals as having a permanent establishment or recharacterize the purported expense deductions, deny treaty benefits under PPT, and apply withholding obligations with penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Revenue Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Administrative sanction of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP; late payment interest at 0.03% per day on the unpaid tax; full disallowance of fraudulent deductions; criminal prosecution under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 (amended 2017): for managing individuals, a fine from VND 100 million up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment from 6 months to 7 years depending on the scale of evasion; for the legal entity, fines up to VND 20 billion, suspension of the enterprise's operation for up to 2 years, or a permanent ban from the video telecommunications and app-ake business. The tax authority may also order public disclosure of the violation.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, CEO, and chief accountant bear personal criminal liability; Vietnamese courts routinely impose travel bans, exit-visa denial, and court-ordered asset freezes on directors while tax evasion investigations are pending. The corporate veil is pierced for the tax debt where the director has signed false declarations or caused the company's insolvency; directors may be ordered to make civil restitution of the full tax shortfall and, upon conviction, face imprisonment up to 7 years, plus a prohibition from holding managerial positions for 1 to 5 years.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration / Thin Capitalization / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may re-determine taxable income under the best-method rule, disregard contrived transactions, impose disallowed interest deductions above the 30% EBITDA ceiling, and adjust transfer prices to the median arm's length result. The additional assessed tax carries late-payment interest at 0.03% per day, plus an administrative penalty of up to 20% of the reassessed tax shortfall for under-declaration; failure to submit TP documentation is separately fined up to VND 20 million under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP. In severe cases, criminal tax evasion charges may be added when TP manipulation crosses the wilful-evasion threshold.

Director personal liability: Directors and chief accountants who sign false TP documentation are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax debt and face personal fines and imprisonment under Article 200 Penal Code; the General Department of Taxation may deny exit visas to the legal representative until the TP audit and settlement is complete; foreign shadow directors operating remotely without physical presence in Vietnam may be personally served through mutual legal assistance and barred from future entry to Vietnam until the liability is settled.
Anti-Money Laundering / Payment Aggregator Bypass / Unlicensed Payment Services

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines under the Law on Anti-Money Laundering 2022 up to VND 200 million for legal entities that fail to perform customer identification, suspicious transaction reporting, or record keeping; the State Bank of Vietnam may suspend account operations and revoke related payment accreditations; immutative criminal liability under Article 324 of the Penal Code for money laundering applies to persons who transfer, convert, or conceal proceeds of crime, with imprisonment up to 15 years for individuals and fines up to VND 20 billion for legal entities; enterprise licenses may be revoked for recurrent violations.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are personally investigated in parallel with corporate sanctions; travel bans and exit denial are routinely imposed on signatories under AML emergency powers; personal assets may be seized and civilly confiscated as proceeds of crime; foreign investors found to have used nominee Vietnamese directors may be criminally charged and faces international arrest risk through INTERPOL notices, while the nominee directors face imprisonment for collusion.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: The GAAR forces re-determination of taxable income from substance over form, denies treaty benefits under the PPT/limitation-of-benefits provisions, reclassifies the company as a mere permanent establishment of the foreign operator, imposes FCT withholding at full domestic rates (10% CIT on royalties, 5% on interest, and 0% dividend WHT with full recharacterized income), and strips all preferential incentives. The taxpayer bears the burden of proving commercial substance; additional assessments carry 20% under-declaration penalties and 0.03% daily interest. The Ministry of Planning and Investment may also revoke the enterprise registration certificate for falsely declaring operational substance.

Director personal liability: The nominee legal representative and the foreign shadow director are jointly and severally liable for the full reassessed tax and penalties; courts can pierce the corporate veil to seize personal bank accounts, freeze residential property, impose exit bans, and in serious cases pursue criminal fraud charges under Article 174 (fraudulent appropriation) or Article 200 Penal Code; the foreign beneficial owner who cannot be served in Vietnam may be subjected to mutual legal assistance, Interpol Red Notice issuance, and civil recognition of Vietnamese money judgments in the owner's home jurisdiction.
