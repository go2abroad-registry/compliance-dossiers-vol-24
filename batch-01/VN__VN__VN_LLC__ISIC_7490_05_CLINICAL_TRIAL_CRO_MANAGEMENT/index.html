Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Viet Nam imposes no general statutory minimum charter capital for limited liability companies; however, a foreign-invested clinical-trial CRO project requires the Department of Planning and Investment to appraise the economic justification of the proposed charter capital at the Investment Registration Certificate stage. The subscribed capital must be remitted through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained at a licensed bank in Viet Nam and fully contributed no later than ninety (90) days after issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate. A blocked or escrow deposit account is not statutorily required; the DICA bank statements serve as the evidentiary basis of lawful capital contribution.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam – electronic administration portals: E-Tax (etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn) for corporate income tax/VAT filings and payments; E-Invoice Portal (hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) for e-invoice registration, transmission, and certification; enterprise formation via the National Business Registration Portal (Dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) administered by the Ministry of Planning and Investment.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Execute corporate authorizations/Power-of-Attorney and certify constituent documents (Charter, legal representative appointment) for the foreign investor. Step 2 — File the investment registration dossier for the foreign-invested LLC (obtain Investment Registration Certificate – IRC) with the provincial Department of Planning and Investment or the export-processing/industrial-zone authority. Step 3 — Concurrently or sequentially file the enterprise registration application for the LLC; upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC), the enterprise code is automatically granted and, by law, the enterprise code is simultaneously the enterprise's 13-digit tax identification number (Mã số thuế – MST); the applicant does not hold a separate tax-registration step with the tax office. Step 4 — Within 10 working days from the ERC issuance, submit the tax-registration docket (Tax Registration Declaration under Circular 105/2020/TT-BTC) to the managing sub-department of the GDT (typically the Tax Sub-Department of the province/city where the head office is located), covering the legal representative, registered address, bank accounts, authorized signatories, and any dependent branches requiring their own sub-identification numbers. Step 5 — Register the VAT calculation method (credit/deduction method versus direct method), adopt a compliant accounting regime (full regime per Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC or SME regime per Circular 132/2018/TT-BTC), obtain a professional digital-signature certificate and e-invoice solution, and pay the annual business license tax (lệ phí môn bài) within 30 days of ERC issuance (annual rates VND 2,000,000–3,000,000 based on statutory capital).

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the Vietnamese LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer subject to Enterprise Income Tax (CIT, thuế thu nhập doanh nghiệp) at the flat rate of 20% under the Law on Enterprise Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12 as amended. There is no transparency or pass-through regime for LLCs: profits and losses are recognized and taxed at the entity level; losses may be carried forward for five years (within 5 consecutive tax years); foreign shareholders are not subject to any Vietnamese dividend withholding tax on after-tax profit remittances, whereas services and royalties paid offshore would be separately scrutinized for foreign-contractor tax and transfer-pricing purposes. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Vietnamese LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer subject to Enterprise Income Tax (CIT, thuế thu nhập doanh nghiệp) at the flat rate of 20% under the Law on Enterprise Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12 as amended. There is no transparency or pass-through regime for LLCs: profits and losses are recognized and taxed at the entity level; losses may be carried forward for five years (within 5 consecutive tax years); foreign shareholders are not subject to any Vietnamese dividend withholding tax on after-tax profit remittances, whereas services and royalties paid offshore would be separately scrutinized for foreign-contractor tax and transfer-pricing purposes. Vietnam applies worldwide taxation to resident corporations, subject to foreign tax credit, and therefore the scope is entity-level worldwide income rather than territorial-only taxation.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not operate a check-the-box or partnership pass-through election. Mandatory entity-level taxation is the default for the LLC. Within the statutory framework, the taxpayer may elect: (i) the VAT calculation method (tax-credit/deduction method under Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC versus the direct method), whereby most CRO service providers elect the credit method to preserve input VAT credits/refunds; (ii) the accounting standard (full accounting regime under Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC or SME accounting regime under Circular 132/2018/TT-BTC) if qualifying as a small or medium entity; (iii) the method and scope of transfer-pricing documentation (master file, local file, and Country-by-Country reporting thresholds under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP); and (iv) CIT incentive elections for qualifying high-tech, scientific-research, or innovation activities. No hybrid-mismatch elections are available domestically; Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree 132/2020 empower the GDT to disregard or re-characterize hybrid arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None for the corporate/LLC form: every enterprise with the ERC is automatically and mandatorily VAT-registered at the moment the enterprise code is granted; there is no economic-turnover threshold triggering separate VAT registration for an LLC. (For completeness, a household/individual business may fall outside the VAT net where annual turnover does not exceed VND 100,000,000, but that household exemption does not apply to a foreign-invested LLC.)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export services, including contract research organization (CRO)/clinical-trial management services, qualify for the 0% VAT rate under Article 9 of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC only if ALL of the following conditions are simultaneously satisfied: (1) the services are supplied to and invoiced to a foreign organization or individual (the foreign sponsor); (2) the foreign sponsor has no permanent establishment or place of business in Vietnam; (3) the services are actually consumed outside Vietnam — for CRO services, the clinical trial data, medical/scientific reports, regulatory dossiers, milestones, and intellectual outputs must be delivered, used, stored, or otherwise enjoyed offshore, while mere local site-monitoring within Vietnam by the Vietnamese CRO will normally be characterized as domestically consumed and exposed to the standard 10% VAT; (4) the services are paid for from abroad in foreign currency through a licensed Vietnamese bank account; and (5) the taxpayer holds the complete documentary evidence bundle described below. Because GDT sub-departments apply a restrictive definition of 'consumed abroad', a CRO must execute a cross-border service agreement with the offshore sponsor and ensure that the contractual and operational substance demonstrates offshore delivery and use of trial results rather than local enjoyment in Vietnam.

  • Signed cross-border service/clinical-trial management agreement with the foreign sponsor, indicating scope, milestones, foreign-currency consideration, and governing law
  • E-invoice with 0% VAT rate issued through the GDT e-invoice portal in statutory XML/QR format
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign-currency bank statement issued by the Vietnamese commercial bank evidencing receipt of the foreign sponsor's payment from abroad
  • Foreign sponsor's written certification/receipt confirming that the services were entirely consumed outside Vietnam (offshore use of data, reports, and trial results)
  • Offshore delivery evidence: accepted milestones, electronic delivery logs, data-transmission records, regulatory filing receipts (e.g., US FDA or EMA submission copies), and correspondence proving the output left Vietnam
  • For any physical items tied to export (reference standards, biological samples, and trial supplies): Customs Export Declaration (E-Form under VNACCS/VCIS), Air Waybill or Bill of Lading, and packing list

Mandatory e-invoicing has applied since 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. Every invoice, including invoices for exported CRO services, must be generated as an electronic invoice in XML format containing the mandatory invoice code, digital signature, and embedded QR code, and must be transmitted to the GDT's E-Invoice Portal (hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn). For invoices without pre-assigned tax-authority codes, the e-invoice data must be submitted to the GDT no later than 8 hours from the time the invoice is issued; the system immediately validates the XML/XSD schema and tax-code registry, and invoices that fail validation are rejected, requiring immediate re-issuance. Invoices with pre-assigned tax-authority codes obtain the tax code in real time through the e-invoice portal's web-service API (SOAP/REST), effectively an authorization-clearance step. Export invoices, customs declarations, and VAT refund dossiers are reconciled electronically, and the VNACCS/VCIS customs system applies the same one-stop electronic clearance for any physical goods movement.

Export zero-rating is a historical audit hotspot. A taxpayer who incorrectly claims 0% VAT must re-invoice the service at 10% VAT, is subject to a deficiency assessment with late-payment interest at 0.03% per day, and is liable for a penalty of 20% of the under-declared VAT in cases of mistaken declaration, or a penalty between 1 and 3 times the evaded tax where deliberate evasion is established, under Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree 125/2020/ND-CP. Retrospective clawback of refunded input VAT with interest is standard when the GDT disallows the offshore-consumption test, and the taxpayer's e-invoice codes may be suspended pending re-moderation. In aggravated cases (fictitious invoices, sham arrangements, documentary fraud, or related-party zero-rating served to shift profits), criminal sanctions for tax evasion apply under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 with fines up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment from 6 months up to 7 years, in addition to administrative fines, revocation of the enterprise's invoice status, and public blacklisting. The GDT may also adjust transfer prices and impose secondary penalties under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP where the CRO invoices zero-rated services to related offshore sponsors without sufficient substance and comparability.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam operates a managed-floating exchange rate regime in which the Vietnamese Dong (VND) is not freely convertible outside the territory. Current-account transactions, including the cross-border export of clinical trial CRO services by a foreign-invested LLC, are fully convertible through SBV-licensed credit institutions, subject to underlying transaction documentation and mandatory statistical purpose codes. Capital-account transactions (initial charter-capital remittance, shareholder loans, capital increases, dividend and profit remittance, transfer of capital/parcels, and residual-asset liquidation transfers) must be settled exclusively via the enterprise's registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) under Circular 23/2014/TT-NHNN. Profit remittance is permitted without a lock-up period after the enterprise obtains audited annual financial statements, completes CIT/VAT/final withholding tax obligations, secures tax-clearance confirmation from the competent sub-department of taxation, and passes the bank's documentary checks (board resolution, audited profit distribution statement, corporate governance approval, and proof of no outstanding tax debt). The SBV retains legal authority to impose exchange restrictions or suspend convertibility only in supreme national emergency circumstances pursuant to the FX Ordinance; no such restrictions are currently in force. Practical hurdles for a CRO LLC include the bank's strict verification of the substance behind service-revenue inflows, the reconciliation of each inbound tranche with the 90-day export-proceeds repatriation window, and the requirement that all underlying clinical trial agreements be legally valid under Civil Code obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds arising from the cross-border provision of clinical trial CRO services must be repatriated to a licensed bank in Viet Nam within 90 days of the export date, as required by Article 17 of the Foreign Exchange Ordinance and Article 19 of Decree 70/2014/ND-CP. The proceeds must be credited to the LLC's foreign currency account (FCA) at an SBV-licensed credit institution; receipt through offshore emoney or third-party payment aggregators outside the regulated banking channel is not permitted. Since the 2015 deregulation, there is no mandatory percentage of export proceeds that must be surrendered to the SBV; the enterprise may retain 100% of its export receipts in USD/EUR or convert voluntarily to VND at the negotiating commercial bank's posted exchange rate. The bank will apply the statutory purpose code for 'export of services' and will verify the supporting dossier (cross-border service contract, invoice signed by the foreign sponsor, clinical trial milestones, and the valid VAT e-invoice registration). If repatriation is not completed within the 90-day statutory period, the enterprise must obtain a written extension from the SBV through its advising bank before the deadline; failure to do so subjects the company to administrative fines under Decree 88/2019/ND-CP (sanction framework for monetary and banking violations) and may force mandatory conversion to VND.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) — Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam, acting under the Law on the State Bank of Vietnam (No. 46/2010/QH12) and the Law on Credit Institutions (No. 47/2010/QH12, as amended). The foreign exchange regime is governed by the Foreign Exchange Ordinance (No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, amended by Ordinance 06/2013/UBTVQH13), Decree 70/2014/ND-CP (guidance on the FX Ordinance), and SBV Circulars, notably Circular 02/2021/TT-NHNN on foreign exchange transactions between credit institutions and customers (as amended by Circular 11/2022/TT-NHNN). External transactions of foreign-invested enterprises are also supervised by the Ministry of Planning and Investment and the Ministry of Finance for investment registration and tax clearance purposes.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity capital contributions must be credited to the LLC's registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at its principal licensed bank, opened under Circular 23/2014/TT-NHNN concurrently with the issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate. Protocol: (1) the LLC issues a capital-call notice and board resolution stating the amount, currency, and scheduled receipt date; (2) the foreign shareholder's remitting bank transmits the wire via SWIFT MT103 with Field 70 (Remittance Information) quoting the IRC number, the enterprise name, the share-purchase agreement reference, and the explicit purpose 'Contribution to charter capital — direct investment'; (3) the receiving Vietnamese bank assigns the statistical purpose code 3211 (direct investment equity) per Decision 02/2018/QD-NHNN and posts the funds to the DICA in the original foreign currency (USD/EUR) or, upon instruction, converts to VND; (4) the bank files the cross-border foreign-exchange report with the SBV within statutory timelines (daily electronic declaration for the DICA ledger). For operational current-account inflows derived from export of CRO services, the sponsor remits directly to the LLC's operating USD FCA (not the DICA) using the statistical services code for 'other business services' (Group 2 — services, best fit under the SBV code table; the bank must validate the exact sub-code per Decision 02/2018, typically code 225/226 for professional and R&D-business services). The LLC must preserve the service contract, invoice, acceptor's confirmation, and clinical trial progress evidence to support each crediting. Additional verification in practice includes a statement of cash-flow matching against the registered revenue forecast and the attempted confirmation that the remitter is the contracting sponsor and not an unlicensed payment intermediary.Outbound cash-flow rails for the CRO LLC are structured to accommodate: (a) service settlements to offshore sub-CROs, central laboratories, and foreign investigators via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers, each accompanied by a purpose code for import of services, the sub-consulting agreement, invoice, and proof of services rendered (lab report, data transfer certificate, or milestone acceptance); (b) trade-related physical imports (clinical trial consumables, investigational medicinal products, medical devices) via documentary collection or irrevocable commercial letters of credit compliant with UCP 600, advised/confirmed by a Vietnamese LC-issuing bank, together with the import permit from the DAV and customs declaration; (c) intra-group recharge and overhead apportionment via MT103 supported by SG&A allocation memoranda and tax-substantiating records; (d) dividend and profit remittances to the foreign shareholders through the DICA, supported by a board resolution, audited annual financial statements, profit-distribution minutes, and tax-clearance evidence; (e) statutory payments (CIT, VAT, PIT, social insurance) executed through the onshore IBPS/VNDRTGS system and the State Treasury network; and (f) covered FX conversions and forward settlements via the principal bank's e-treasury platform. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% (standard CIT rate applicable to Vietnamese limited liability companies under Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12, as amended; effective since 1 January 2016).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Vietnam imposes no additional dividend withholding tax). After-profit remittance to foreign shareholders is permitted once the LLC has satisfied: (i) payment of all CIT liabilities, (ii) audited annual financial statements, (iii) a shareholder meeting resolution approving the distribution, and (iv) year-end tax finalization. The profit transfer is executed through a licensed Vietnamese bank and reported under Circular 05/2015/TT-BLDTBXH (foreign exchange) – bank will require tax clearance certificate.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, a Foreign-Invested LLC is exempt from preparing a Local File and Master File only if its total annual revenue is below VND 200 billion AND the aggregate value of related-party transactions is below VND 30 billion. A Master File is additionally required when the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue in the preceding fiscal year is at least VND 18,000 billion. A clinical-trial CRO with significant sponsor fees from related multinationals should assume the thresholds are met and maintain contemporaneous TP documentation.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not have a stand-alone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) and transfer pricing substance requirements under Article 47 of Tax Administration Law No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP demand real economic presence. The CRO must demonstrate actual clinical personnel, premises, functional ownership of trial data, decision-making capability, and risk absorption. Absent these, the tax authority may recharacterize intercompany service fees, deny tax incentives, and impose VAT and CIT adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent CIT Filing

Statutory sanction: Administrative sanction up to 2.5 times the amount of evaded tax plus late-payment interest of 0.03%/day; criminal prosecution under Penal Code Art. 200: fine from VND 100 million to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment from 6 months to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, CFO, and actual managers are jointly liable for the tax arrears; courts may issue civil restitution, impose travel bans (exit visa suspension), and sentence individual wrongdoers to imprisonment where fraud is proven.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: GAAR application: recharacterization of the entire arrangement, disallowance of tax incentives, re-assessment of profits, imposition of 20% underpayment penalty, late-payment interest, and administrative fines up to VND 60 million for providing false records.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign artificially inflated intercompany contracts or exempt-sale certificates may be personally liable for the recomputed tax, subject to civil damage claims and travel bans; criminal exposure if the shell is used for money laundering.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment to taxable income plus 20% penalty on the under-declared tax; separate penalty of VND 5–25 million for non-submission of TP documents; if intentional, the fine rises to 2.5 times the evaded tax and the matter may be referred for criminal tax investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors responsible for TP policy are liable for the adjusted CIT and penalties; they can be held jointly and severally for misleading the tax authority; travel restrictions may apply until the entire TP debt and penal interest is settled.
