Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (VDCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management — official portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must first incorporate the Vanuatu International Company (IC) through a licensed registered agent at the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); no statutory minimum capital applies. For indirect tax identity, the IC files Form VAT-01 with VDCIR together with the certified Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered agent confirmation, director and shareholder identification, and written turnover projections. VAT registration is compulsory when taxable turnover reaches or exceeds VT 2,500,000 in any consecutive 12-month period, and is available by voluntary election below the threshold where input VAT recovery is desired. A unique VAT Registration Number (VRN) is issued by VDCIR. The IC must also obtain a customs exporter identifier in the ASYCUDA World border declaration system for parcel export clearance. No national income tax TIN is issued because Vanuatu does not impose corporate or personal income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax. The International Company is statutorily exempt from all present and future income taxes, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and exchange control under the International Companies Act [Cap 222]. Applicable elections are limited to: (1) voluntary VAT registration below the compulsory threshold, subject to a 24-month minimum registration commitment from date of approval; (2) election of duty and VAT deferment procedures for imported goods under customs control; (3) election of zero-rating treatment on qualifying exports of goods and offshore services through documentary substantiation rather than formal application.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 2,500,000 (two million five hundred thousand vatu) — aggregate value of taxable supplies made in any consecutive 12-month period, or reasonably expected to exceed that level in the following 12 months, without deduction of input VAT

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated under the VAT Act where: (i) the supplier is VAT-registered; (ii) the goods are supplied to a non-resident purchaser or are otherwise destined for export from Vanuatu; (iii) the goods physically leave Vanuatu within the statutory export tolerance period, generally 28 days from the date of supply unless extended by VDCIR; and (iv) the supplier retains adequate written evidence of physical export. Services connected with the parcel consolidation hub, including sorting, co-packing, warehousing, direct-injection line-haul procurement, and export documentation, constitute exported services and are zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident with no place of business in Vanuatu and the benefit of the service is received entirely outside Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration (approved export entry lodged through ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (master and house versions) evidencing physical departure of the consolidated consignment
  • Consolidation and line-haul manifest generated by the parcel consolidation hub operating system
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued against the consolidated consignment
  • Bank statement, SWIFT MT103, or inward remittance certificate evidencing receipt of foreign currency consideration

No real-time fiscal clearance, cryptographic stamping, or mandatory national e-invoicing regime is in force in Vanuatu. Paper and PDF tax invoices remain lawful and must be issued within 28 days of the supply date and contain: supplier name, address, and VRN; sequential invoice number; date of supply; description and quantity of goods or services; VAT-exclusive consideration; VAT rate; and VAT amount charged. Electronic customs declarations through ASYCUDA World are mandatory for export and import clearance at the frontier, but that system does not operate as a transactional fiscal clearance mandate for domestic or e-commerce invoicing.

VDCIR may conduct retrospective audits across open tax periods and may reassess output VAT at 12.5% with statutory interest and administrative penalties wherever the export documentation bundle is incomplete, inconsistent, or untimely. Fraudulent zero-rating — including false bills of lading, simulated or circular exports, inflated consolidation invoices, or fabricated foreign exchange evidence — triggers full VAT claw-back plus additional penalties under Part VII of the Value Added Tax Act [Cap 246], and criminal liability for false declarations under the Customs Act [Cap 245], with fines, imprisonment, forfeiture of the goods, and deregistration of the VAT registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current- and capital-account convertibility applies. International Companies are non-resident and face no exchange-control licensing for capital injections, profit remittance, dividend payouts, or liquidation proceeds. The VUV is a managed-float currency linked to a trade-weighted basket (AUD, NZD, USD, JPY, EUR); accounts may be held, funded, and disbursed in any foreign currency. The sole statutory constraint is that an IC must not carry on business with Vanuatu residents, which does not affect cross-border trading, FX, or treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender rules exist: there is no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation window for service-export proceeds. Freight aggregation, consolidation, and linehaul service fees may be received and retained 100% in the invoice currency (USD/EUR/AUD) without RBV approval. Only statistical balance-of-payments survey reporting to the RBV may apply for large individual receipts; this is an informational filing, not a conversion or surrender obligation.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - issuer of the VUV and monetary policy authority. Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) licenses offshore banks and supervises AML/CFT under the AML/CFT Act [Cap. 270] and the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High as a supplementary collection, disbursement, and local-clearing rail (matching the target rails: Airwallex and Local Clearing); Low as the sole treasury anchor. Vanuatu has no domestic EMI licensing regime, so the IC must contract with a foreign-licensed provider (e.g., Airwallex, Wise, or an FCA-authorised EMI). The IC Act prohibitions on domestic Vanuatu business do not impede cross-border flows; the main frictions are the EMI's own enhanced due-diligence screen for tax-exempt offshore entities and the requirement to sweep volatile balances back to the BSP anchor account nightly.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - VUV 0 (Vanuatu has no statutory transfer pricing regime, no documentation thresholds, and no Local File / Master File obligation for International Companies)

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (ESR) apply to 'relevant entities' that are tax resident in Vanuatu or have a physical presence there and conduct 'relevant activities'. The parcel consolidation hub, classified as a distribution and service centre activity, falls within ESR only if managed and controlled from Vanuatu or operating through a Vanuatu fixed place of business; a properly structured non-resident International Company with no Vanuatu premises, staff, or management control is outside ESR scope. Any degree of local management or a Vanuatu booking office triggers full ESR reporting, a non-negotiable requirement to demonstrate economic substance with local staff and expenditure, and automatic information exchange with the EU/OECD if substance is not shown.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (GST / VAT Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Under the Value Added Tax Act No. 11 of 2013, monetary penalties up to 200% of the evaded tax, criminal fines up to VUV 10,000,000, and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for the corporate penalty; criminal prosecution attaches personally, with mandatory travel bans pending trial, asset freezing orders, and custodial sentences served on the natural person.
Shell Company / Abusive Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and dissolution under s.289 of the International Companies Act; the company's assets vest in the Registrar, it loses capacity to sue or defend, and restoration requires a court order with payment of all arrears and penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors become personally liable for all debts contracted after strike-off; the corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent or abusive use of the IC structure, exposing directors to civil restitution, disqualification orders, and restrictions on leaving Vanuatu.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 13 of 2014, body corporates face fines up to VUV 100,000,000, responsible individuals face fines up to VUV 25,000,000 and imprisonment up to 15 years; unlicensed money service business activity is separately punishable under the Financial Dealers Licensing Act.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners are subject to personal criminal liability, confiscation of laundered funds, asset seizure, and travel bans; courts may order civil restitution equal to the value of the illicit flows.
Fraudulent Trading and Misrepresentation to VFSC

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 12 years under the International Companies Act and the Penal Code; civil liability extends to the full amount of fraudulent debts and induced third-party losses.

Director personal liability: A court will pierce the corporate veil, rendering directors personally liable for civil restitution; additional sanctions include travel bans, confiscation orders, and disqualification from managing any company in Vanuatu for up to 10 years.
