Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

For a fully virtual operation, no physical workplace clearance is triggered; if staff are engaged in Vanuatu, the Department of Labour occupational health registration applies.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue Authority (VRA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (successor to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue); official portal: https://www.vura.gov.vu. International-Company licensing and annual compliance: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), official portal: https://www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

No TIN or VAT ID is issued for a Vanuatu International Company (IC). Procedure for a foreign-owned IC: (1) the foreign beneficial owner engages a VFSC-licensed registered agent; (2) the agent files the charter/memorandum and pays the statutory registration fee (no minimum capital and no residency requirement for shareholders); (3) VFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation carrying the IC's registration number, which is the only statutory identifier for banking, Stripe onboarding, and contracts; (4) because the IC is statutorily exempt from income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty and VAT (International Companies Act, Cap. 222), no application to the VRA is made and no TIN is generated; if a foreign bank or marketplace demands a tax reference, the IC obtains from VFSC/its registered agent a certificate or status letter confirming the exemption, or relies on the ATI/foreign tax form matched with the VFSC registration number.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — No TIN or VAT ID is issued for a Vanuatu International Company (IC). Procedure for a foreign-owned IC: (1) the foreign beneficial owner engages a VFSC-licensed registered agent; (2) the agent files the charter/memorandum and pays the statutory registration fee (no minimum capital and no residency requirement for shareholders); (3) VFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation carrying the IC's registration number, which is the only statutory identifier for banking, Stripe onboarding, and contracts; (4) because the IC is statutorily exempt from income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty and VAT (International Companies Act, Cap. 222), no application to the VRA is made and no TIN is generated; if a foreign bank or marketplace demands a tax reference, the IC obtains from VFSC/its registered agent a certificate or status letter confirming the exemption, or relies on the ATI/foreign tax form matched with the VFSC registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None available. Vanuatu levies no corporate or personal income tax, so there are no check-the-box classifications, CFC regimes, or hybrid-mismatch elections. The IC's tax-exempt status is automatic and non-elective, conditioned solely on maintaining the IC's offshore restriction (no business with Vanuatu residents) and paying the annual VFSC registered-agent/imposition fees. The only structural election is initial incorporation form: an IC (exempt) vs. a domestic company under the Vanuatu Companies Act (which is within the domestic VAT ring once turnover exceeds the threshold); a domestic company may subsequently 'continue' as an IC, and vice versa, under Part 14 of the International Companies Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (ten million Vanuatu Vatu) of taxable turnover in any 12-month period, triggering mandatory VAT registration; an International Company is exempt from VAT and therefore cannot and must not register, because its permitted overseas-facing supplies are outside the domestic taxable supply definition.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are zero-rated at 0% under the direct-export rule of the VAT Act (goods/offshore services supplied to non-residents and consumed outside Vanuatu). For the AI/LLM API platform, each token or inference usage supplied to a non-resident customer (i) is supplied by an IC that is prohibited from trading with Vanuatu residents, and (ii) is wholly performed offshore (cloud endpoints, no on-island supply chain) and paid from abroad; accordingly, the supply is 'out of scope' and simultaneously qualifies for zero-rating treatment. Eligibility conditions: non-resident recipient with no Vanuatu establishment; payment received in foreign currency or offshore stablecoin settlement (e.g., USDC) into an offshore account; and preservation of the documentation bundle for at least seven years.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World clearance record) - required only for physical exports; for pure AI/LLM API digital services, replaced by an IC registered-agent confirmation that no goods declaration apply
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill - for physical equipment shipments; for digital services, substituted by the executed master subscription / API Terms of Service naming the non-resident customer and Vanuatu IC vendor
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or offshore bank/Stripe payout/USDC on-chain ledger proof - evidencing receipt of consideration from outside Vanuatu
  • Itemized commercial invoice bearing the IC's VFSC registration number and 'International Company' designation, with 0% VAT / 'No VAT' cartouche
  • API usage and token-consumption log tying each invoiced amount to actual AI inference volumes delivered to the non-resident customer

None in force. Vanuatu mandates no real-time fiscal clearance, Government e-invoicing API, machine-readable digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. Customs declarations are lodged through the ASYCUDA World system and VAT/customs returns are filed electronically via the VRA EFACT portal, but no pre-clearance invoice authorization is required for AI/LLM API token supplies.

Vanuatu has no exchange controls, so no statutory FX-repatriation deadline applies; the 180-day benchmark is applied conservatively as internal evidence of offshore export and payment. If a claim to zero-rating/out-of-scope is challenged, VRA may: (i) treat the supply as domestic taxable and assess 12.5% VAT on the full consideration, with a 25% late-payment penalty plus 1% monthly interest; (ii) retrospectively claw back input VAT credits and zero-rating benefits for the whole period of incorrect claims; and (iii) prosecute fraudulent export zero-rating (false customs declarations, forged Bills of Lading, forged remittance vouchers, or sham non-resident customer arrangements) as a criminal offence under the VAT Act and Customs Act — penalties range up to VT 1,000,000 fine and/or two years' imprisonment per false declaration, in addition to VFSC deregistration of the IC and disqualification of its registered agent for breach of the International Companies Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible on both current and capital account. No exchange control act, no prior approval for dividend or profit remittance, and no restriction on non-resident holding of VUV/USD accounts. Capital account liberalization is de jure complete for International Companies. Practical friction originates exclusively from correspondent bank AML/OFAC screening and the thin USD/VUV spot liquidity, which is concentrated in ANZ, BSP, and BRED. USD-denominated treasury accounts are the operational priority for an AI API platform whose revenue rails are USD-denominated (Stripe) and USDC-denominated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None imposed. Vanuatu maintains no statutory timeline for repatriation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation to the RBV for export receipts or software/API service revenues. An International Company may retain 100% of USD proceeds offshore-class with no maturity benchmark or VUV conversion requirement, subject only to each commercial bank's internal enhanced due diligence thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), Port Vila; banking supervision jointly with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for non-bank financial institutions, International Companies registry, and Financial Dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-Medium feasibility. Vanuatu currently issues no dedicated EMI (Electronic Money Institution) licence; offshore digital EMIs overwhelmingly decline VU-incorporated companies because of correspondent de-risking and absence of local RBV recognition. Stripe direct acquiring is unavailable for a VU International Company; indirect merchant-of-record rail is required. USDC treasury operations are viable only through a VFSC-registered Financial Dealer or an OECD-tier exchange custody partner performing on-chain KYC and source-of-funds attestation, with USD off-ramp into the BSP/ANZ USD account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold exists because Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing legislation and no CIT base to protect; no Local File or Master File is legally mandated. Under OECD FHTP / BEPS peer review, an IC deriving geographically mobile income must nevertheless retain contemporaneous arm's-length pricing records; best-practice documentation threshold: related-party transactions exceeding VUV 50,000,000 (approx. USD 400,000) per annum should be documented with a TP policy, invoices, contracts, bank trail and substance file

Economic substance scope: Following the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices peer review and Vanuatu's international tax-good-governance commitments, an International Company carrying on a service business — including an AI/LLM API platform under ISIC 6201 — must demonstrate economic substance in Vanuatu proportionate to its activity: a physical office, qualified employees or engaged contractors, board meetings directed and managed in Vanuatu, genuine operating expenditure, and core income-generating activities performed from Vanuatu. Substance records (employment contracts, board minutes, client contracts, invoices, bank statements) must be maintained at the registered agent's office and updated annually; failure to maintain them exposes the IC to deregistration and adverse international listing measures

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties of up to 150% of tax or fees evaded, statutory fines up to VUV 500,000, forfeiture of untaxed proceeds, and deregistration / compulsory strike-off of the IC under the International Companies Act [Cap. 190]

Director personal liability: Directors, including non-residents, may be personally prosecuted for fraudulent declarations, ordered to make civil restitution for the revenue shortfall, barred from holding office, and imprisoned; travel bans may be imposed during criminal investigation
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Compulsory deregistration, revocation of IC tax-exempt privileges, penalties under the International Companies Act, and adverse EU/OECD classification of Vanuatu; the IC may be struck off with assets forfeited to the State

Director personal liability: Corporate veil piercing: directors personally liable for unpaid obligations and restitution, disqualification from managing companies, removal from the VFSC register, and exposure to home-country tax assessments through automatic exchange of information
Anti-Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines, seizure and confiscation of assets, and imprisonment under the Vanuatu AML/CFT framework; suspension or revocation of the registered agent's licence, IC listing by the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit, and FATF monitoring where systemic deficiencies arise

Director personal liability: Sole directors and shareholders are criminally liable for AML offences: long-term imprisonment, asset confiscation, travel bans, extradition exposure, and permanent bars on holding corporate office in Vanuatu or under mutual-assistance requests
Stamp Duty and Corporate Facade Abuse

Statutory sanction: Stamp duty reassessments with penalty and interest, fines for fraudulent dealings under the Stamp Duties Act, invalidation of the offending instrument, and winding-up petitions initiated by the VFSC

Director personal liability: Directors held civilly liable to defrauded creditors and the revenue; veil-piercing applied where the IC is a mere facade; compensation orders, fines, and imprisonment for fraudulent misuse of the corporate form
