Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), Companies Registry Division, Port Vila, Republic of Vanuatu.

Governing statute

International Companies Act [CAP 222] of the Republic of Vanuatu, as amended, together with the International Companies Regulations; principal provisions govern incorporation, memorandum and articles, name reservation, registered office and registered agent, share capital issuance, and appointment of the first board of directors and officers.

Formation / constitutional filings

Official Application for Incorporation of an International Company (Form IC-1), filed together with the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 9 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an International Company under the International Companies Act [CAP 222]. The Company is formed with a single ordinary share of USD 1 par value subscribed by the sole shareholder. No escrow bank account or upfront capital deposit is mandated at incorporation; authorized capital may be increased by ordinary board resolution without notarization.

Funding & unlock path: Not required for a non-resident trading entity with no physical premises, fire risk, or workforce in Vanuatu. Should the Company later open a Port Vila office or an Efate warehousing facility, it would be required to obtain: (i) an annual fire safety certificate from the Vanuatu Fire and Emergency Services; (ii) a structural load clearance from the Department of Public Works for any racked or bulk storage; and (iii) a workplace occupancy clearance from the Port Vila Municipal Council under the Municipalities Act [CAP 126].

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an International Company under the International Companies Act [CAP 222]. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Finance & Treasury - Inland Revenue Division (Vanuatu Customs & Inland Revenue Service), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; corporate and international-company registry administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); official government portal maintained under the gov.vu domain.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Engage a VFSC-licensed registered agent to incorporate the International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; VFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation containing the statutory company registration number, which is the definitive corporate identity. Step 2: No mandatory direct-tax TIN is issued because the International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax and corporate tax; the entity may optionally obtain an identification certificate from the Inland Revenue Division for banking, LC and KYC purposes. Step 3: Register with the Vanuatu Customs & Inland Revenue Service via the ASYCUDA World electronic single window only if the IC imports dutiable goods into Vanuatu or supplies enumerated prescribed services subject to the 4% Service Tax. Step 4: A Port Vila or Luganville municipal business license is required only if the IC maintains a physical local place of business, which is not required for offshore dry-bulk sulfur brokerage conducted by a non-resident International Company.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None domestically. The International Company exemption from income tax, corporate tax, dividend distribution tax, capital gains tax and withholding tax is automatic and non-elective under Section 3 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]. Vanuatu has no check-the-box, CFC, hybrid-mismatch, fiscal-transparency or minimum-tax election. Foreign tax classifications (e.g., a US check-the-box election) are irrelevant for Vanuatu direct-tax purposes and cannot alter the statutory exemption; no GAAR or anti-deferral election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in force. The 12.5% Value Added Tax (VAT Act No. 8 of 2014) was repealed effective 1 July 2018. Residual consumption taxes are the 4% Service Tax [Cap. 80] on enumerated prescribed services (hotel/tourism accommodation, restaurants, car rental, telecommunications and certain insurance-related or port-type services) and customs import/export duties; none of these applies to FOB/CFR/CIF wholesale trading of granular elemental sulfur mineral ore.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in VUV because the VAT system was repealed on 1 July 2018. Under the residual Service Tax [Cap. 80], any person supplying an enumerated prescribed service must register for the 4% Service Tax irrespective of turnover; customs registration is transaction-driven per import/export. A Vanuatu International Company engaged purely in offshore marine sulfur brokerage outside the enumerated prescribed services has no indirect-tax registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Vanuatu has had no VAT/GST since 1 July 2018, so there is no export zero-rating claim, no output VAT on exported goods and no input-VAT refund mechanism. Under the erstwhile 12.5% VAT (2015-2018), exported goods could be zero-rated with documentary proof; that regime is spent. Exports of sulfur mineral ore from Vanuatu waters or transshipped cargo are presently outside Vanuatu consumption tax entirely, provided the IC supplies no Vanuatu prescribed service. Genuine export documentation remains necessary for Vanuatu customs compliance and for the buyer's or destination-jurisdiction's export VAT/GST relief.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic entry) showing HS 2503 classification for sulfur of any kind other than sublimed, precipitated or colloidal sulfur
  • Bill of Lading (or FI/FO charter-party fixture note) evidencing shipment on a dry-bulk vessel from a Vanuatu port or transshipment point
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank FX settlement statement) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice stating exporter IC, buyer, INCOTERMS, price, weight, quantity and sulfur grade
  • Certificate of Origin (non-preferential or PACER-plus / Melanesian Spearhead Group preferential certificate if claimed)
  • Prior import entry and terminal/warehouse receipt confirming ownership and storage where cargo passed through Vanuatu

No national real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, continuous-transaction-control (CTC), API stamping or digital fiscal-stamp mandate exists in Vanuatu. Paper invoices and ordinary commercial records are legally sufficient. The only electronic clearance interface is the ASYCUDA World customs single window, through which import/export declarations must be filed electronically for physical cargo movements; no real-time digital stamping is required.

Vanuatu has no VAT/export-refund clawback regime because no VAT is in force. Compliance risk sits primarily under the Customs Act [Cap. 145]: false declarations, misdescription or undervaluation of sulfur exports attract forfeiture of goods, duty evaded plus up to double-duty penalty, fines up to VT 200,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years for knowingly false statements. The International Company's direct-tax exemption depends on the absence of Vanuatu-source income; sham pricing or shell-transaction structures could be disregarded by a court, and any future income-tax legislation may authorize retroactive assessment. Vanuatu has no reciprocal tax-collection agreement for foreign clawback orders, but customs records and VFSC beneficial-ownership data remain exchangeable under international tax-transparency commitments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is predominantly free-floating and fully convertible for both current account and capital account transactions. Vanuatu has accepted Article VIII obligations of the IMF, and there are no prior approval procedures or quantitative limits on inward or outward remittances relating to trade, financing, equity, or profit repatriation. Profit remittance hurdles are effectively nil for International Companies registered under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; nevertheless, licensed authorised dealers must record balance-of-payments purpose codes, and source-of-funds documentation will be required for any outward transfer of proceeds generated from sulphur mineral ore trade to ensure compliance with the Anti-Money Laundering and International Financial Transactions Act.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, no prescribed timeline for repatriation, and no authorised-dealer exchange control exemption needed for export receipts arising from the dry bulk marine transport and brokerage of granular elemental sulphur. Exporters retain unfettered discretion to maintain, deploy, or convert foreign currency proceeds in onshore or offshore non-resident accounts. A Vanuatu International Company should nevertheless maintain a complete treasury archive comprising bills of lading, commercial invoices, certificates of origin, weighbridge or laboratory analysis attestations, and payment receipts, as supporting evidence in any future RBV or Financial Intelligence Unit audit or counterparty bank prospective due diligence review.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the monetary authority, financial supervisor, and exchange control administrator under the Reserve Bank Act [Cap. 125] and the Foreign Exchange Administration Act framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low to Medium with material legal and practical constraints. Vanuatu does not maintain a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing category comparable to the EU Revised Payment Services Directive. VFSC-licensed money transmitters or payment service providers are limited and are mainly capped by local correspondent banking relationships; offshore digital EMIs without a physical branch or a VFSC payment-dealer licence are not permitted to clear or settle VUV or hold client-blocked accounts for a Vanuatu International Company. The only bankable alternative is a white-label payment and escrow arrangement delivered through an RBV-licensed authorised dealer (ANZ, BSP, or BRED), combined with SWIFT-based API connectivity and a separate client money deposit account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injection from the ultimate beneficial owner or the holding entity shall be routed exclusively by SWIFT MT103 single-customer credit transfer to the IC's designated operational or escrow account, or, for parent-to-subsidiary flows, by SWIFT MT202/MT202 COV for cover payment. Incoming remittances must be described in the treasury instruction as 'Equity Contribution' or 'Intercompany Loan Drawdown' and matched against a Board Resolution, Share Subscription Agreement, or signed Loan Agreement. The Vanuatu authorised dealer will record the inward transfer under the RBV-monitored balance-of-payments classification as direct investment (category 710) or loan liability (category 715). Cash wiring protocols prohibit bearer deposits, third-party first-party cheque deposits, and any unsupported same-day reversal or linked-party routing through unreviewed shell accounts. Evidence of documented trade intent and the original contract chain for sulphur procurement and resale must be furnished upon first funding to prevent transaction laundering allegations.Outbound disbursements for the purchase of dry bulk granular elemental sulphur and affiliated marine freight shall be effected through the following clearing rails: (i) clean SWIFT MT103 transfers under documentary collection (DP/DA) for routine supplier payments; (ii) irrevocable, conforming, cumulative weekly-use letters of credit issued under SWIFT MT700/MT701 with UCP 600 and eUCP 2.0 terms for high-value cargo shipments; (iii) direct negotiation and discounting of the seller's draft under MT750/MT760 advice when the seller requires cash payment before discharge; and (iv) commercial standby LCs issued in favour of charterers or freight forwarders for demurrage and fixture security. All outward remittances must be tied to a corresponding purchase invoice, pre-settlement marine logistics confirmation, and a certificate of customs-clearance at the destination port. The IC's treasury policy must prohibit the use of cash advances, hawala arrangements, or undisclosed crypto-backed correspondents; each payment must be prefunded in USD or AUD and cleared strictly through the RBV-licensed authorised bank.Short-dated VUV/USD and VUV/AUD forward outrights written by ANZ, BSP, or BRED with a maximum tenor of 12 months, supported by a valid invoice, LC, or charter party cash-flow forecast.USD-denominated structural natural hedging via multicurrency operating accounts where VUV receipts from incidental domestic sources are offset against USD import settlement obligations, thereby reducing the need for separate derivative transactions.USD/VUV cross-currency swap executed through a licensed authorised dealer solely to match the IC's projected net working-capital deficit against the designated sulphur cargo purchase contract.European-style vanilla FX call or put options with a premium cap and no knock-in or knock-out feature, permitted where the notional amount does not exceed 100% of the confirmed trade payable in the corresponding settlement currency.Zero-cost collars (forward band structures) on USD/AUD for marine freight and bunker disbursements, provided the underlying exposure is a signed voyage charter or freight agreement with confirmed payment dates.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax on International Companies under section 43 of the International Companies Act 1992.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing threshold exists because Vanuatu has no corporate income tax. However, under the Vanuatu Economic Substance Rules introduced by the International Companies (Amendment) Act 2021, an International Company engaged in controlled transactions with connected non-residents should maintain intercompany pricing records aligned with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines whenever substance is claimed in Vanuatu; practical substance-file preparation is expected where gross annual revenue exceeds VUV 20,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance Rules mirror the BVI/Caribbean ESR model: every International Company carrying on a relevant activity, unless tax resident in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction, must demonstrate (i) direction and management exercised in Vanuatu; (ii) core income-generating activities performed in Vanuatu; (iii) adequate employees, premises, and expenditure proportionate to the activity. ISIC 4669 wholesale trading of granular elemental sulphur may qualify as 'distribution', and dry bulk marine transport brokerage may qualify as 'shipping'; both are listed relevant activities subject to the mandatory annual VESR declaration and VFSC review.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax offence — fraudulent tax-exemption certificate and evasion of VAT and customs duty

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of up to three times the evaded VAT or customs duty plus a civil fine of up to VUV 2,000,000 under the Value Added Tax Act 2021 and the Customs Act 2013; criminal conviction for making false statements to the Commissioner of Inland Revenue.

Director personal liability: Directors to whom the evasion is attributed are personally liable for the evaded amount; criminal conviction carries imprisonment of up to 2 years; foreign directors are exposed to extradition under Vanuatu's mutual assistance and extradition arrangements.
Shell company operation and economic substance violation

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalty of up to VUV 5,000,000; mandatory reporting and automatic exchange of information with EU and OECD jurisdictions; strike-off of the company from the VFSC registry with frozen assets pending restoration.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid economic substance penalties; a director who signs a false economic substance declaration is subject to disqualification orders, travel restraints, and imprisonment of up to 2 years.
Anti-Money Laundering / counter-financing-of-terrorism failure associated with LC payment rails

Statutory sanction: VFSC administrative penalties of up to VUV 10,000,000 for the corporate entity, in addition to enforcement action against the licensed registered agent; freezing orders and confiscation of proceeds under the Anti-Money Laundering and Proliferation Financing Act.

Director personal liability: Money laundering is a standalone criminal offence punishable by imprisonment of up to 15 years; directors who move or conceal the proceeds with knowledge are prosecuted personally, and foreign directors are exposed to international arrest notices and extradition.
False beneficial ownership reporting and nominee opacity

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to VUV 1,000,000 per false declaration under the Companies and Trust Review Act 2021; the VFSC registry may strike off the company and refuse restoration; beneficial ownership data is exchanged automatically with OECD jurisdictions on request.

Director personal liability: Each director authorising the false filing is jointly and severally liable for the penalty; directors may be disqualified from managing any company in Vanuatu for up to 5 years and imprisoned for up to 12 months.
Annual return default and continued trading while struck off

Statutory sanction: Late penalties of up to VUV 250,000; strike-off after one year of default; a struck-off company is dissolved and cannot act as plaintiff in legal proceedings, and its assets are placed under registrar control pending restoration.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue trading after strike-off are personally liable for debts incurred after dissolution; the Supreme Court may restrain such directors from leaving Vanuatu pending restoration proceedings and may order civil restitution of diverted assets.
