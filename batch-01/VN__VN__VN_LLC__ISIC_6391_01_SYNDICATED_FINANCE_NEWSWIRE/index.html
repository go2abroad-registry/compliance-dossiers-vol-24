Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No general statutory minimum charter capital applies to a limited liability company under the Enterprise Law. Under Article 26 of the Law on Investment, the charter capital committed by the foreign investor must be commensurate with the scale and implementation schedule of the registered ISIC 6391 project, which is assessed by the DPI. The entire subscribed charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 75 of the Enterprise Law 2020). Vietnamese law does not mandate a separate escrow account; however, the contributed funds must be remitted into and maintained in a dedicated capital contribution account in the company's name at a commercial bank licensed in Viet Nam, evidenced by SWIFT/MT103 or local transfer records.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety design appraisal and acceptance certificate (Thẩm duyệt thiết kế về phòng cháy, chữa cháy và Văn bản nghiệm thu) required for the occupied premises under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP, as amended by Decree No. 83/2024/ND-CP; the building must hold a valid fire safety acceptance covering the entire structure and the lessor must evidence annual inspection by the Fire Police. Structural load clearance required for any rack-mounted server or heavy equipment, evidenced by the lessor's as-built structural drawings and a signed engineering confirmation for the intended floor loading (typically not less than 2.0 kN/m² for office uses). Occupational clearance obtained through workplace hazard declaration to the district labor inspectorate under the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH14, appointment of a workplace safety coordinator, documented fire evacuation drill at least annually, first-aid kit and accessible fire hoses/extinguishers, and compliance with Circular No. 23/2022/TT-BLDTBXH on workplace inspection and safety training.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: A resident foreign-invested LLC is taxed on its worldwide corporate income at entity level. There is no pass-through eligibility; profits of the LLC are subject to enterprise income tax before distribution. The LLC is not automatically a tax-exempt zone entity unless it obtains express export-processing-enterprise (EPE), high-tech, or other statutory incentive registration.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or general hybrid mismatch election is available under Vietnamese tax law. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT calculation method election between the deduction/credit method and the direct method for eligible small taxpayers; (ii) election to apply VAT credit method requires proper accounting records and valid input VAT invoices; (iii) CIT incentive registration at project inception for investment projects in eligible sectors and geographical zones; (iv) export-processing-enterprise status election requires prior written approval and creates zero-rated input VAT treatment for the EPE.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold for LLCs or corporate entities; VAT registration and TIN issuance are mandatory upon enterprise incorporation. For household and individual business taxpayers, annual revenue exceeding VND 100,000,000 triggers mandatory VAT registration; below that amount registration is voluntary.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate is available for goods physically exported from Viet Nam, including goods sold to export-processing enterprises, and for international transport and export services actually consumed outside Viet Nam. Required conditions are contemporaneous documentation proving the goods or services crossed the Vietnamese customs frontier, a valid export contract, customs clearance records, and foreign-currency payment evidence. Offshore services supplied to foreign organizations or individuals and consumed outside Viet Nam may qualify if they are not deemed to be consumed in Viet Nam. Failure to substantiate the export status results in mandatory VAT at the standard 10% rate plus non-deductible input VAT reversals.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic customs clearance with customs-signed status)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Export Sales Contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank confirmation of foreign-currency receipt
  • Swift wire transfer evidence matching the invoice value and payment terms

Mandatory electronic invoicing applies under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. E-invoices must be generated in XML with a qualified electronic signature, transmitted to the GDT e-invoice data system, and, where a tax-authority verification code is required, obtain that code from GDT in real time before delivery to the buyer. Invoices without tax-authority codes must be transmitted no later than the day following issuance. APIs and standard data interchange formats are prescribed; all invoice data is centralized in the GDT E-Invoice System for automated tax clearance and reconciliation with customs records.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to post-clearance audit by the General Department of Customs and GDT. Missing or inconsistent export documentation triggers retroactive VAT reassessment at 10%, disqualification of input VAT refunds, and statutory late-payment interest. False export declarations, fabricated customs documents, or fraudulent VAT refund claims attract administrative penalties of up to 20% of the understated tax, penalty multiples of 1-3 times the evaded amount, invoice suspension, forced cancellation of registered invoice series, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion or smuggling with imprisonment penalties up to 20 years under the Vietnamese Penal Code.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese dong (VND) is partially convertible. Current account transactions are generally liberalized, permitting cross-border payments for trade in services, royalties, and software subscriptions without prior SBV licensing. Capital account transactions remain subject to registration, licensing, or prior approval: foreign direct investment capital contributions, foreign loans, equity transfers, and outbound investments require an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) / Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) and bank account designated for capital flows. Profit remittance by a foreign-invested LLC is permitted on an annual basis after the audited financial statements are filed, the corporate income tax (CIT) finalization has been accepted, and all tax and customs obligations have been settled. A registration certificate from the SBV is generally not required for current profit remittance; the bank will verify the published annual audited financials and tax clearance. Dividends or distributions may be remitted in foreign currency from the company's foreign currency account, subject to confirmation that no overdue tax liabilities exist.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters must repatriate full export proceeds into a licensed foreign currency account held with a SBV-licensed commercial bank within 180 days from the date of customs clearance, in accordance with Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN and Decree 70/2014/ND-CP. There is currently no fixed mandatory statutory conversion ratio applied to all export proceeds; however, the SBV retains the right to request credit institutions to require a proportional sale of foreign currency to the bank when systemic foreign currency demand is high. Exporters may either retain the proceeds in a foreign currency account or convert to VND freely for approved purposes. Documentation must include the customs declaration, sales contract, invoice, and transport documents.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) – Foreign Exchange Administration Department (Foreign Exchange Control and Management Division)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) and digital banks not licensed by the SBV cannot independently hold VND settlement territory or provide local clearing accounts. They are not permitted to provide payment services in Vietnam without a local partnership with a licensed bank or under a separate payment intermediary licence issued by the SBV pursuant to Decree 80/2016/ND-CP and Circular 39/2014/TT-NHNN. For a financial newswire syndicated finance API, offshore EMIs such as Stripe (as payment gateway) or TransferWise/Revolut may only serve as technical rails for cross-border receipts and not for domestic VND settlement. Onboarding feasibility rating: Low for autonomous offshore operation; Medium through a licensed local payment intermediary and bank partnership. Legal constraints include mandatory processing of payment transactions through SBV-licensed banks and the prohibition of unauthorized foreign exchange conversion.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound FDI equity injections must be routed through the company's dedicated foreign direct investment (FDI) capital account at a SBV-licensed commercial bank. The founding shareholders shall transfer funds by SWIFT MT103 from the overseas investor's bank to the Vietnamese LLC's capital account. The wire instruction must include: (i) the LLC's IRC/ERC registration number; (ii) a purpose code designating the inflow as FDI equity (BOP code for direct investment); (iii) the name of the investor; (iv) a payment reference referencing the Investment Registration Certificate number and scheduled contribution tranche. The receiving bank will verify the IRC and may require a signed capital contribution declaration and the board resolution approving the capital schedule. Upon receipt, the bank will update the SBV's online registration of foreign investment and report the transaction through the External Transactions Statistics system. It is not permissible to receive inbound capital investments to the ordinary operational account without prior written approval from the SBV or the designating licensed bank.For recurring B2B subscriptions and content licensing fees paid by overseas syndicated finance customers, the LLC's preferred outbound clearing rails include SWIFT MT103 wire transfers and, when required, commercial letters of credit evidenced by MT700/MT710. For trade settlements, the MT103 field 70 must contain an invoice number, contract reference, and a service description consistent with the underlying newswire agreement; the bank may request the signed contract and invoice before executing settlement. For domestic VND collections or when the customer settles into an intermediate digital payment account, funds must be swept into the LLC's licensed bank account and converted to VND only through an authorized bank. All FX conversions for outbound disbursements must be quoted by a licensed local bank; interbank cover payments should use MT202 for US-dollar nostro/loro settlement.Deliverable VND/USD forward contracts offered by SBV-licensed banks, available only for actual commercial underlying exposure (e.g., confirmed future subscription or content licensing receivables/payables), with maximum tenor of 365 days.VND/USD currency swaps (FX swaps) permitted for corporate customers, including simultaneous spot and forward trade, subject to the same underlying economic exposure and 365-day limit.Cross-currency swaps and certain options are available only after the bank holds a conducted derivative licence from the SBV and the end-user fulfills derivative-trading validation; however, non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible onshore for VND, as they are treated as circumvention of exchange controls. NDFs may only be traded offshore by licensed international counterparties, but a Vietnamese LLC is prohibited from directly contracting them domestically.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) and digital banks not licensed by the SBV cannot independently hold VND settlement territory or provide local clearing accounts. They are not permitted to provide payment services in Vietnam without a local partnership with a licensed bank or under a separate payment intermediary licence issued by the SBV pursuant to Decree 80/2016/ND-CP and Circular 39/2014/TT-NHNN. For a financial newswire syndicated finance API, offshore EMIs such as Stripe (as payment gateway) or TransferWise/Revolut may only serve as technical rails for cross-border receipts and not for domestic VND settlement. Onboarding feasibility rating: Low for autonomous offshore operation; Medium through a licensed local payment intermediary and bank partnership. Legal constraints include mandatory processing of payment transactions through SBV-licensed banks and the prohibition of unauthorized foreign exchange conversion.
