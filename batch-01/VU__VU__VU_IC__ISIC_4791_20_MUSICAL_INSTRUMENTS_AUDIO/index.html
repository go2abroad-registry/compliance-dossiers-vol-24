Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital and no escrow or capital deposit requirement exists for a Vanuatu International Company. The sole shareholder/director may subscribe shares at any stated nominal value, and capital is payable directly to the company treasury rather than the Registry.

If all operations are purely virtual/e-commerce with no local premises, these requirements are waived.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official portal: https://vrc.gov.vu; annual IC fees are collected by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is automatically granted an exemption from all Vanuatu taxes on income, profits, and capital; no income tax TIN is issued. If the IC separately conducts a taxable activity in or from Vanuatu, it must file VAT registration form VAT-001 with VRC and obtain a VRC VAT number. Otherwise, no national tax registration is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — An International Company (IC) is automatically granted an exemption from all Vanuatu taxes on income, profits, and capital; no income tax TIN is issued. If the IC separately conducts a taxable activity in or from Vanuatu, it must file VAT registration form VAT-001 with VRC and obtain a VRC VAT number. Otherwise, no national tax registration is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist under the International Companies Act 1992. The IC's tax-exempt status is statutory, automatic, and cannot be waived or converted into a taxed local entity. Voluntary VAT registration is not available for the IC itself; only a separate taxable local activity could be registered.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 20,000,000 (twenty million Vatu) of taxable supplies in any consecutive twelve-month period; registration becomes compulsory upon crossing this threshold for any person carrying on a taxable activity in or from Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Vanuatu, including musical instruments and audio equipment sold online to foreign customers, are zero-rated for VAT where the goods are physically exported from Vanuatu by the supplier or on its behalf and are for use outside Vanuatu. Services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and not used in Vanuatu are similarly zero-rated. The IC must obtain and retain prescribed evidence of export and foreign payment within the statutory period.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping regime is in force for standard VAT invoices. Invoices must contain prescribed particulars and be retained for audit. Export declarations and customs processing are performed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs management system.

VRC may audit export zero-rating claims within five years of filing. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating triggers immediate clawback of the 12.5% VAT not charged, a civil penalty of up to 200% of the evaded tax, monthly interest of 1.5% on the unpaid amount, criminal fines of up to VUV 10,000,000 and imprisonment up to 12 months for responsible officers, plus revocation of the IC's business registration and personal liability of directors where the claim was knowingly false.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is convertible for both current and capital account transactions by non-resident International Companies; inward and outward remittances are generally unrestricted, but banks apply AML/CFT screening before releasing merchant payouts or foreign supplier wires. Profit remittance is not subject to withholding tax for an International Company, though each transfer must align with declared corporate purpose, beneficial ownership filings and a local tax-domicile certificate to avoid de-listing by correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio; an International Company may retain sales proceeds in AUD, USD or VUV. However, the RBV requires all export settlements, including Stripe/PayPal marketplace payouts, to be routed through a licensed Vanuatu commercial bank for record-keeping; any conversion from USD/AUD to VUV is voluntary and driven by creditor payment needs.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for a standalone offshore EMI account in Vanuatu. VFSC does not license EMIs as domestic payment institutions, and non-resident digital EMI accounts cannot access the local RBV clearing and settlement system; foreign EMIs should be used only as secondary payout channels for merchant acquiring proceeds, with primary settlement still repatriated to the Vanuatu IC's licensed bank account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold exists because Vanuatu has not enacted transfer-pricing legislation. Prudential OECD-aligned benchmark: prepare Local File and Master File when the IC's annual related-party transactions exceed VUV 25,000,000 (approx. USD 210,000) or when an upstream jurisdiction's local transfer-pricing law imposes documentation duties on the Vanuatu entity.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations require a Vanuatu IC performing relevant activities, including distribution and service centre, financing and leasing, IP holding, shipping, headquarters, or pure equity holding, to maintain local management and premises, adequate full-time employees, proportionate local expenditure, and board meetings managing core income-generating activities in Vanuatu. A pure online retail operation that drop-ships musical instruments and audio gear, holds no Vanuatu inventory, and has no local employees generally falls outside the relevant-activity definition; however, every IC must file the annual ESN and maintain a licensed registered office. If the IC assumes purchasing, inventory risk, or logistics control, it will be treated as a distribution and service centre and must meet full economic substance in Vanuatu.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false fiscal representation

Statutory sanction: Domestically: VFSC strike-off plus statutory fines under the International Companies Act. Internationally: foreign revenue authorities recharacterize the structure under CFC, permanent establishment, and general anti-abuse rules, impose full tax, penalties often reaching 100-200% of tax evaded, and pursue collection assistance under TIEAs with Australia, New Zealand, France, and other partners.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are personally liable for foreign tax, penalties, and interest; criminal prosecution, travel bans, and international arrest warrants may follow; fraudulent trading liability under the Companies Act [Cap 191] exposes directors to personal liability for obligations incurred with intent to defraud creditors.
