Competent corporate registry

Business Registration Office under the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) of the province/city where the registered head office is located; online submission via the National Business Registration Portal at https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 (Articles 22-29, 47-60, 75-83 on LLC registration, capital contribution, corporate governance and legal representative); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 (Articles 23-24, 38 on investment registration for foreign investors and issuance of Investment Registration Certificate); Decree 01/2021/ND-CP detailing the Law on Enterprises and Enterprise Registration; Decree 31/2021/ND-CP detailing the Law on Investment.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Enterprise Registration (Form I-1 issued under Decree 01/2021/ND-CP) accompanied by the Company Charter and List of Members; the Investment Registration Certificate must first be obtained for a foreign-invested LLC.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an ordinary LLC administering an internal ESOP trust under ISIC 6430. Charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate pursuant to Article 75 of the Law on Enterprises 2020. There is no escrow or blocked-account requirement at the incorporation stage; after incorporation, foreign capital contributions must be remitted through a Foreign Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened with a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam.

Funding & unlock path: The registered office must obtain a fire prevention and fighting clearance or acceptance certificate under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP before operation. Structural load inspection is not required unless the entity alters load-bearing elements of the building. Occupational safety clearance includes compliance with workplace conditions under the Labor Code 2019, provision of employee risk assessments, and registration of any hazardous working conditions, though ordinary administrative ESOP administration duties will generally fall below the threshold for mandatory occupational hazard registration.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an ordinary LLC administering an internal ESOP trust under ISIC 6430. Charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate pursuant to Article 75 of the Law on Enterprises 2020. There is no escrow or blocked-account requirement at the incorporation stage; after incorporation, foreign capital contributions must be remitted through a Foreign Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened with a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (GDT / Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance of Viet Nam; primary taxpayer portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; national e-invoice portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; Local Tax Departments (Cục Thuế) of provinces/centrally affiliated cities serve as the operational registration and audit offices.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested limited liability company obtains, through the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) one-stop service, an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC). The 10-digit enterprise code recorded on the ERC simultaneously constitutes the statutory Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST) under Law on Enterprise Registration (2019) and Law on Tax Administration (38/2019/QH14). The LLC must (i) complete supplementary tax registration for VAT, personal income tax, payroll withholding and any dependent branch codes via the GDT e-portal within 30 days of ERC issuance; (ii) procure a licensed digital certificate (chữ ký số) for the legal representative and the enterprise; (iii) register for the mandatory e-invoice scheme on the GDT portal; and (iv) open a Vietnamese foreign-currency and VND settlement bank account for tax payment and export remittance collection. No separate application for the tax code is required after ERC issuance; the tax code becomes legally active upon DPI issuance, and a tax registration certificate is auto-generated in the e-tax system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity election is recognized under Vietnamese law for a domestic LLC; corporate income tax at the entity level is compulsory. The only available elections concern tax mechanics rather than entity classification: (i) VAT method election between the credit (input-deduction) method and the direct percentage method on gross revenue, available to taxpayers with annual revenue below VND 1 billion; (ii) for a foreign contractor without a permanent establishment, the election between deemed Foreign Contractor Tax collection at source (fixed VAT/CIT rates) versus self-assessment where the contractor maintains compliant accounts and obtains registration; (iii) export processing enterprise (EPE) status election, which yields customs VAT deferral/exemption privileges but does not create a separate tax person; and (iv) transfer pricing methodological elections (including advance pricing agreement applications) under Circular 05/2025/TT-BTC, which do not affect entity classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Organizations and business households with annual revenue from taxable production or trading of goods and services of VND 1 billion (approx. US$40,000) or more are mandatorily registered for VAT under the credit method; taxpayers below this threshold may voluntarily register or apply the direct method on revenue. Foreign contractors and foreign organizations deriving Vietnamese-source revenue are subject to deemed VAT withholding (Foreign Contractor Tax) from the first Vietnamese dong of gross turnover, irrespective of the VND 1 billion threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods and services exported from Viet Nam, including offshore services supplied to foreign organizations or individuals and consumed wholly outside Vietnamese territory, are eligible for the 0% VAT rate (Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC, Article 9). Conditions: (i) a valid export contract with a non-resident customer (or a contract directly with a foreign outsourcer for processing/services); (ii) for goods, presentation of a customs export declaration or an EPE delivery note; (iii) a commercial invoice and, for services, a service completion certificate and documentary evidence of actual consumption abroad; (iv) payment received in freely convertible foreign currency through a licensed Vietnamese bank (or offset/clearing arrangement specifically approved by the State Bank of Viet Nam); and (v) the service is not one statutorily excluded from zero-rating (e.g., labor supply into Viet Nam, services attached to Vietnamese real estate, reinsurance/insurance of Vietnamese assets, international transport of passengers/transit). For an ESOP administration LLC, administrative, custodial and trustee-support services rendered to an overseas corporate trustee, sponsor or employee benefit plan, where the services are performed for and consumed outside Viet Nam and no onshore beneficiary exercise occurs, constitute zero-rated export services; the taxpayer must maintain a precise service-delivery log demonstrating the offshore place of consumption.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Tờ khai hải quan) as electronically cleared, or EPE in-country delivery note for deemed exports
  • Bill of Lading (sea freight) or Air Waybill (air freight) evidencing actual physical departure from Vietnamese territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank foreign-currency collection confirmation (chứng từ thanh toán qua ngân hàng) proving proceeds credited from abroad
  • Export services contract (for ESOP administration) specifying foreign customer, scope, place of performance, and offshore consumption
  • Commercial invoice and service completion/acceptance certificate signed by the foreign recipient
  • Certification of actual use/utilization of the service abroad, together with the foreign customer's tax invoice or equivalent third-party evidence
  • Packing list (for physical goods) and certificate of origin if a preferential duty rate claim is made

E-invoicing is mandatory for all Vietnamese enterprises from 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. Businesses must issue validated e-invoices using certified software in standardized XML format, sign them with a legally recognized digital certificate, and transmit the invoice data to the GDT e-invoice portal within 24 hours of issuance through secured REST/SOAP web-service APIs or the GDT-hosted T-WEB interface. The majority of taxpayers self-generate 'e-invoices without tax-authority code'; higher-risk taxpayers and specific transaction types (e.g., those involving tax refund applications) are directed by GDT to use 'e-invoices with tax-authority code', whereby the authority issues a digital fiscal code for each invoice before issuance. Periodic (monthly) aggregate declaration of e-invoice data is prohibited for refund purposes. No physical fiscal stamping or hardware fiscalization module exists; the GDT performs digital cross-clearance by reconciling e-invoice records against customs, banking, and monthly VAT return (GTGT01) data in real time.

The GDT subjects export VAT refunds to automated pre-refund and post-refund risk screening: e-invoice output, customs exit data, banking FX receipts, and service-export substantiation are reconcilable in the TMS analytics layer. Where documents are missing or inconsistent, the refund is suspended under anticipatory control, and a full documentary audit may be initiated. Any zero-rating or refund found to be unsupported or fraudulent is subject to retrospective clawback: the VAT officer issues an additional assessment recovering the refunded amount with late-payment interest charged at 0.03% per day from the original refund date, plus an administrative evasion penalty of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount (Law on Tax Administration 38/2019/QH14, Article 141; Decree 125/2020/ND-CP). Criminal exposure arises under Penal Code Article 200 (tax evasion) for organized or repeated schemes, with fines up to VND 4.5 billion and imprisonment up to 7 years; forged export invoices, fake customs declarations, or false service contracts additionally expose responsible managers/beneficial owners to penalties for smuggling (Article 188), producing counterfeit documents (Article 201), and property-related fraud (Article 341), and the enterprise may be disqualified from the expedited refund channel for up to 5 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vietnam operates a managed floating exchange rate regime. Current account transactions are generally convertible for trade and service-related payments, subject to underlying documentation and SBV reporting. Capital account transactions require registration, licensing, or prior approval. Profit remittances to foreign investors are permitted after the completion of audited financial statements, tax finalization, satisfaction of statutory reserve requirements, and issuance of a profit remittance approval by the licensing authority. All remittances must be routed through a licensed domestic commercial bank with a valid Foreign Investment Account (FIA).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated to Vietnam through an authorized bank within 180 days of the date of export shipment completion, unless an extension is approved by the SBV. Mandatory surrender/conversion ratios are determined by the SBV on a case-by-case basis; however, certain export receipts may be subject to a statistically targeted conversion requirement to maintain national foreign exchange equilibrium. For the ESOP trust entity, which is not primarily engaged in export trade, this requirement applies only to incidental export-related receipts and is not the core operational burden.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) – Supervision Department and Foreign Exchange Control Department; Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI) for investment registration; General Department of Taxation (GDT) for tax-related FX filings.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI licenses do not confer a legal passport to provide banking or payment services in Vietnam. A foreign fintech may only act as an ancillary technology provider and must route all transactions through an SBV-licensed intermediary payment service. Capital contributions and profit repatriation must be executed through a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank and properly designated foreign investment accounts.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Offshore holding parent or trust-like structure with non-transparent ultimate beneficial ownership

Root cause: Offshore holding parent or trust-like structure with non-transparent ultimate beneficial ownership

Defense evidence: Organizational tree showing direct ownership; trust deed and ESOP trust rules; certified copies of passport and proof of residential address for all natural-person UBOs; source of wealth and source of funds statements; audited financial statements of the trust and contributing parent company; legal opinion on the trust's validity and non-profit nature.
Incoming capital remittance without a clearly linked purpose code or investment registration certificate

Root cause: Incoming capital remittance without a clearly linked purpose code or investment registration certificate

Defense evidence: Investment Registration Certificate (IRC); Economic Organization Registration Certificate (EORC/ERC); SBV Foreign Investment Account (FIA) registration confirmation; signed share subscription or capital contribution agreement; board resolution approving capital drawdown; bank remittance instruction referencing the IRC number and correct purpose code.
Outgoing disbursements to offshore employee-beneficiaries or trustees triggering payroll or dividend red flags

Root cause: Outgoing disbursements to offshore employee-beneficiaries or trustees triggering payroll or dividend red flags

Defense evidence: ESOP plan document; award agreements and vesting schedules; payroll ledger; tax filings with GDT showing personal income tax compliance; legal memorandum confirming that the trust is administrative in nature and does not conduct licensed financial services; underlying invoice or settlement statement for each disbursement.
Director or shareholder appearing on sanctions or PEP lists

Root cause: Director or shareholder appearing on sanctions or PEP lists

Defense evidence: Sanctions screening report from each bank's compliance screening tool; proof of identity documents; explanation of business rationale for any PEP status; approvals per internal escalation policy; enhanced due diligence questionnaire signed by the ultimate beneficial owner.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound investment capital must be routed through a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank into a Foreign Investment Account (FIA) established under the foreign-invested LLC. All remittances must use SWIFT MT103 and require an acceptable purpose code. The bank will verify the IRC, the capital contribution schedule in the investment registration dossier, and the foreign investor's identity. Funds received must be recorded in the FIA before conversion to Vietnamese Dong or transfer to the operational account.
