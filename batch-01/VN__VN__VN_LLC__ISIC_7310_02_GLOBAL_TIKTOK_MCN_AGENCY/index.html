Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance of Viet Nam; official electronic portal: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested limited liability company first obtains an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment; the tax authority automatically issues the Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST) upon data synchronization with the national business registry. The LLC must then submit a tax registration file (Form 01-ĐK-TCT) to the local tax office to activate CIT and VAT obligations. For the supply of digital services, e-commerce, or online platform services into Viet Nam without a permanent establishment, the foreign or offshore entity (e.g., an MCN agency receiving TikToK creator payouts) must register a foreign contractor presence through the ETC portal of the General Department of Taxation (etaxportal.gdt.gov.vn) under Decree No. 85/2021/NĐ-CP and Circular No. 80/2021/TT-BTC, obtaining a dedicated foreign supplier tax code and fulfilling monthly/quarterly declaration duties.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not apply check-the-box partnership pass-through elections or hybrid mismatch elections. A VN LLC is mandatorily taxed at entity level at the 20% Enterprise Income Tax (CIT) rate. Election available: VAT method election between deduction method (input credit) and direct method (levy on value-added) subject to accounting standards compliance. Also electable: simplified transfer pricing documentation for qualifying low-risk/related-party transactions under Decree No. 132/2020/NĐ-CP; Export Processing Enterprise (EPE) status election approved by customs authorities granting 0% VAT on export sales and non-applicability of VAT on imported production inputs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Business households and individuals are required to register VAT once annual revenue exceeds VND 100 million; all corporations and foreign-invested LLCs must register VAT mandatorily upon incorporation regardless of revenue. Foreign suppliers of e-commerce, digital services, and platform intermediary services (including TikTok payout services to Vietnamese creators) are in all cases required to register, declare, and pay tax through the dedicated ETC portal of the General Department of Taxation even without a fixed place of business, with no de minimis turnover exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods and services supplied to foreign organizations or individuals and physically delivered/consumed outside Viet Nam are eligible for the 0% VAT export rate. For digital creator-manager services (ISIC 7310 / MCN agency), zero-rating applies when the service is performed offshore, the recipient is a foreign party (e.g., TikTok Pte. Ltd. outside Viet Nam), and the contract, service delivery records, and offshore performance evidence demonstrate the service is not consumed in Viet Nam. Goods exported through an Export Processing Enterprise additionally qualify for export VAT zero-rating. Disbursements, intermediary commissions, or platform payouts routed to local creators remain subject to standard-rate VAT to the extent such services are consumed domestically.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Tờ khai hải quan hàng hóa xuất khẩu) with customs clearance confirmation stamp
  • Bill of Lading (original or certified true copy) or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank foreign-currency receipt certificate (chứng từ thanh toán qua ngân hàng) confirming actual remittance from the foreign buyer within the statutory repatriation window

All VAT invoices must be issued as electronic invoices (hóa đơn điện tử) under Decree No. 123/2020/NĐ-CP and Circular No. 78/2021/TT-BTC. Invoices must be transmitted near-real-time to the General Department of Taxation via the hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn system; taxpayers normally generate invoices with a self-generated code after achieving compliance status, except high-risk taxpayers whose invoices are pre-coded with the tax authority's invoice code. Machine-to-machine API integration is mandatory for high-volume enterprises and for foreign digital suppliers reporting through the ETC portal; no physical stamping exists, but digital signatures (provided via USB token or cloud signing service) and standardized XML/JSON formats are obligatory; invoice data must include a verification number and be cleared by the tax authority's central invoice database prior to use.

Any false, fraudulent, or erroneously substantiated export zero-rating claim triggers retrospective VAT reassessment and forced refund clawback, late-payment interest at 0.03% per day (approximately 10.95% per annum), administrative fines up to three times the evaded tax amount, and penalty charges under Decree No. 125/2020/NĐ-CP. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days (or an approved extension under Circular No. 05/2017/TT-NHNN and the Law on Foreign Exchange) also attracts customs and tax administrative sanctions. The tax authority may audit VAT refunds and export records for up to five years from the date of violation, or longer where criminal indicia exist. Aggravated, premeditated evasion invokes criminal liability under Articles 200 and 201 of the Penal Code No. 100/2015/QH13, including imprisonment of up to 20 years for the responsible officers and corporate-level criminal fines, confiscation of assets, and permanent denial of export zero-rating eligibility.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

VND is only partially convertible in the international sense. Current-account transactions are legally convertible through SBV-licensed credit institutions under the Foreign Exchange Ordinance (Ordinance 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, as amended) and Decree 70/2014/ND-CP; however, practical clearing requires full supporting documentation through a Vietnamese bank. Capital-account transactions are controlled: equity contributions, shareholder loans, profit repatriation, and liquidation proceeds must be routed through a registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at an SBV-licensed bank. Profit remittance to the foreign parent is permitted only after the audited annual financial statements have been approved, corporate income tax (CIT) finalisation has been completed and all taxes paid, statutory reserves are established, the members' meeting passes a distribution resolution, and the servicing bank verifies tax clearance. VND is not freely deliverable in offshore FX markets; offshore non-deliverable transactions cannot be used as the settlement currency for local VAT, CIT, payroll, or payments to domestic counterparties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For an export-service business such as a TikTok/MCN agency, the statutory rule is repatriation, not a fixed percentage surrender. Export proceeds from services must be credited to a licensed bank account in Vietnam within 90 days from the service completion date unless an SBV-approved longer contractual deferral is arranged. There is no current mandatory percentage conversion ratio for MCN service receipts under Circular 05/2021/TT-NHNN; however, the SBV may impose emergency surrender of up to 100% in exceptional balance-of-payments situations, and under normal administrative practice any foreign-currency balance not backed by permitted FX expenditures (offshore creator payments, platform licensing fees, foreign loan service, permitted profit repatriations) must be converted to VND through the collecting bank. In effect, the LLC should plan for 100% conversion of net export-service proceeds after deducting approved FX-denominated operating needs.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) / Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as an ancillary collection/payout aggregator, but Low as a primary treasury settlement channel. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise B2B, Payoneer, Airwallex, and Currencycloud are not licensed by the SBV to open bank accounts, accept deposits, or provide VND clearing services in Vietnam. They may be used only as an offshore payment conduit so long as the final economic settlement lands in an SBV-licensed bank account of VN_LLC via a compliant SWIFT MT103 or a correspondent banking transfer. Legal constraints include risk of the EMI being treated as an unlicensed payment intermediary in Vietnam if used as the local collection account; therefore all export revenue must be booked and declared in Vietnam, and tax authorities will require bank statements from the licensed Vietnamese bank. The LLC should not maintain its primary treasury or tax-payment account outside the Vietnamese banking system.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All equity capital injections must be routed as cross-border wire transfers to the beneficiary's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at the chosen SBV-licensed bank. The remitter must be the foreign shareholder in whose name the equity contribution is registered under the IRC; third-party payments are not accepted. The SWIFT MT103 should include the shareholder's full name, the beneficiary account in the name of VN_LLC, and a purpose/remittance reference such as 'FDI equity contribution under Investment Registration Certificate No. [reference]'. The LLC must provide the IRC, ERC/charter, member capital contribution schedule, board resolution approving the capital call, and source-of-wealth pack. The local bank will verify that the incoming amount matches the committed capital schedule and will report the inflow to the SBV under the balance-of-payments framework. If the capital is received in a non-VND currency, it can remain in a foreign-currency sub-account or be converted to VND at the bank's telegraphic-transfer rate; no surrender ratio applies to capital injections.The primary outbound clearing rail for MCN creator settlements is SWIFT MT103 for cross-border services and the domestic NAPAS/FAST/VietQR rails for VND payments to Vietnamese creators. Commercial letters of credit are not the appropriate settlement mechanism for intangible creator services; if a large client insists on documentary security, use a standby LC or bank performance undertaking, with the final payment still effected by MT103 against invoices and milestone deliverables. Recurring international creator payments should be pre-approved as a beneficiary list, with each beneficiary's identity, contract, and tax status maintained in the treasury system. Local creator payouts should be made to verified Vietnamese bank accounts using service-purchase or fee-for-service codes, never as anonymous cash, and the bank will conduct sanctions screening and SBV balance-of-payments reporting. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Since 1 January 2016, Vietnam abolished the Profit Remittance Tax on after-tax earnings distributed to foreign corporate shareholders. A foreign-invested LLC may remit annual post-CIT profits to its foreign corporate owner without Vietnamese withholding tax, provided the distribution is approved by the members' meeting, based on audited financial statements, and after full CIT finalization. Non-resident individual shareholders remain subject to 5% Personal Income Tax withholding on dividend distributions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP Article 19: (i) Local File is mandatory when the taxpayer's aggregate related-party transaction value exceeds VND 30 billion (~USD 1.2 million) in a fiscal year; (ii) Master File is mandatory when the ultimate parent's worldwide consolidated revenue reaches approximately VND 500 billion and the Vietnamese taxpayer engages in related-party transactions; (iii) Country-by-Country Report applies to groups with consolidated revenue of at least VND 18,000 billion (~USD 750 million). Taxpayers with total annual revenue below VND 50 billion and related-party transactions below VND 30 billion are exempt from preparing formal documentation but must still complete the Form 01 related-party disclosure attached to the CIT finalization return.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, Article 74 of the Tax Administration Law No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP mandate a substance-over-form assessment. Transactions lacking commercial rationale or conferring purely artificial tax benefits may be recharacterized by the General Department of Taxation. The BEPS MLI Principal Purpose Test additionally denies DTA benefits where the arrangement was designed primarily to secure treaty relief. In practice, the LLC must demonstrate physical office presence, locally resident management, payroll substance, enforceable creator contracts, active business decision-making in Vietnam, and assumption of commercial risk.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - deliberate understatement of CIT

Statutory sanction: Administrative: 20% penalty on the understated CIT plus late-payment interest of 0.03% per day (Decree 125/2020/ND-CP). Criminal: when evaded tax equals or exceeds VND 100 million and constitutes at least 10% of the true tax liability, Penal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 (amended 2017) Article 223 imposes on the legal entity a fine up to VND 5 billion, suspension of operations up to 5 years, or forced dissolution for serious recidivism.

Director personal liability: The board chairman/managing director signing false CIT returns faces personal criminal fine of VND 100-500 million, up to 7 years' imprisonment, and restitution of the evaded tax. Under Decree 126/2020/ND-CP, temporary exit suspension automatically applies to the legal representative where the company's tax debt exceeds VND 10 million and remains overdue for more than 90 days; personal assets can be attached where transfers to nominee offshore entities were made to frustrate collection.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Tax officers recharacterize the arrangements under OECD-compliant arm's-length analysis, disallow the inflated deductions, assess additional CIT, apply a 20% understatement penalty, and levy 0.03%/day interest on the deficiency for the entire period from the original deadline; the statute of limitations extends to 10 years where fraudulent concealment of related-party transactions is proven.

Director personal liability: Directors cannot shield behind the LLC veil when sham contracts are the operative mechanism: the managing director is jointly liable for the additional CIT and penalties, the DTA/PPT relief is revoked, the e-Tax declaration is treated as knowingly false, and the responsible individual is exposed to criminal tax evasion charges under Article 223 and to exit ban enforcement under Decree 126/2020.
Shell Company or Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The General Department of Taxation invokes Article 74 Law 38/2019 to deny the corporate taxpayer characterization for the arrangement, reattribute the Vietnamese-source income to the foreign beneficial owner, and assess additional CIT with a 20% penalty and daily interest. Enterprise registration sanctions under Decree 122/2021/ND-CP apply for false registered information, and the LLC may be forced into dissolution where it has no real business; tax debt enforcement includes asset seizures and bank account freezes.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors remain personally liable for the accuracy of registered filings and may be sanctioned with fines of VND 50-100 million; where the shell structure is used to evade tax or launder creator payouts, directors face criminal prosecution under Articles 223 and 324 of the Penal Code, travel bans, and personal restitution orders when the LLC's assets prove insufficient to satisfy the tax judgment.
Non-Resident Contractor Tax (FCT) Withholding Default

Statutory sanction: The LLC is jointly assessed for the un-withheld FCT at 15% (royalty: 5% VAT plus 10% CIT) or 5% (interest) on the gross remittance, plus a penalty of 20% of the under-withheld amount under Decree 125/2020 and late-payment interest of 0.03%/day backdated to each payment date; the corresponding expense is disallowed in the LLC's CIT computation.

Director personal liability: The managing director who signs remittance instructions without satisfying FCT obligations is personally and jointly liable for the tax arrears under the Tax Administration Law; enforcement measures include travel bans, suspension of bank payment authorization, and, where the omission was intentional and part of a tax evasion scheme, criminal liability under Penal Code Article 223 with imprisonment of up to 7 years.
AML and Foreign Exchange Violations through Offshore Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Foreign exchange violations are fined under the State Bank sanctions regime at up to VND 1 billion for organizations; AML due-diligence infractions attract fines of VND 400 million to 1 billion under Decree 19/2023 sanction provisions; unlicensed cross-border money services can trigger criminal money-laundering charges under Penal Code Article 324, resulting in 1-10 years' imprisonment for responsible parties; all undeclared offshore receipts are additionally treated as undeclared taxable revenue subject to reassessment.

Director personal liability: Directors who establish or authorize offshore payout accounts in the LLC's name are primary subjects of criminal AML enforcement, face bank account freezes, exit suspensions, and fines of up to VND 500 million or imprisonment of up to 10 years; the corporate veil is pierced for restitution when creator proceeds are diverted to personal or foreign-entity accounts in a manner designed to evade Vietnamese tax and foreign-exchange control.
