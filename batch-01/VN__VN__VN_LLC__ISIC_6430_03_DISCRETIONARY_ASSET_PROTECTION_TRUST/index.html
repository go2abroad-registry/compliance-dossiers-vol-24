Name reservation / lock period

Viet Nam does not provide a separate advance name-reservation mechanism; the proposed name is reviewed as part of the enterprise registration dossier and, if approved, is fixed upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate. If the name is rejected, the enterprise registration agency must state the specific grounds in writing. The applicant has the right to amend the proposed name and resubmit the corrected dossier without additional government filing fees within the prescribed response period. Administrative appeals may be lodged with the Director of the Department of Planning and Investment of the province within 30 days of rejection, and further appeals to the Minister of Planning and Investment under Law No. 02/2011/QH13 on Complaints. Judicial remedies are available before the Administrative Court under Law No. 93/2015/QH13 on Administrative Procedures.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

There is no statutory minimum charter capital under Vietnamese general enterprise law for a standard limited liability company; however, if the company will provide fiduciary trust administration services, the State Bank of Vietnam may require a higher legal capital and escrow/deposit maintenance under its special licensing conditions. The member(s) must fully contribute their committed charter capital within 90 days from the date the Enterprise Registration Certificate is issued, and capital used for trust administration must be segregated from client assets held in escrow accounts.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) of the Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Viet Nam; national e-tax portal — https://etax.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice portal — https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; tax registration/TIN lookup — https://tracuunnt.gdt.gov.vn.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested Viet Nam Limited Liability Company obtains its Tax Identification Number (TIN; Mã số thuế) automatically upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate and Investment Registration Certificate by the provincial Department of Planning and Investment. The LLC must activate tax obligations and submit the tax/accounting registration dossier electronically via the GDT e-tax portal within 10 working days after enterprise registration. The 10-digit TIN serves as the VAT identification number; separate VAT ID certificates are not issued. Branches/dependent accounting units obtain 13-digit sub-TINs. A foreign contractor without a permanent establishment is not issued a full TIN; the Vietnamese payer instead applies the foreign contractor tax (FCT) withholding regime using the payer's TIN, unless the contractor independently registers under the same FCT procedure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A resident foreign-invested LLC is a per se Vietnamese corporate income tax and VAT taxpayer; no pass-through/transparent treatment is available. Territorial scope: resident LLCs are taxed on worldwide income; non-resident suppliers are taxed only on Viet Nam-source income. Tax-exempt-zone status is not automatic and arises only from an investment incentive certificate under the Law on Investment with specific conditions.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not provide a US-style check-the-box election, a partnership-style pass-through election, or a domestic hybrid-mismatch pass-through election. The LLC is automatically classified as an entity-level CIT taxpayer. Available statutory elections are limited to: (i) VAT computation method election between the credit method and the direct-calc-on-turnover method where law allows; (ii) registration of investment incentives (high-tech, supporting industry, special economic zones, export-processing enterprises) in the Investment Registration Certificate; (iii) application for an Advance Pricing Agreement for transfer pricing; and (iv) treaty-based relief under relevant double tax agreements. Anti-hybrid mismatches are addressed by substance-of-arrangement standards under transfer-pricing/anti-abuse rules, not by an election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For an LLC, no VAT registration threshold applies; all corporate taxpayers must be VAT/TIN registered before carrying on taxable activity. For household/individual business taxpayers, annual turnover of VND 100,000,000 or less from VAT-able goods/services is exempt from VAT and triggers no registration/payment obligation. Non-resident foreign contractors without a PE are not separately VAT-registered unless they elect direct registration; the Vietnamese customer/beneficiary withholds foreign contractor VAT at the statutory rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate is available for (i) exported goods physically leaving Viet Nam for consumption abroad, evidenced by a customs export declaration; and (ii) exported services supplied to foreign organizations/individuals and consumed entirely outside Viet Nam. For an offshore discretionary asset protection trust, zero-rating applies only if the trust is non-resident, the administration service is provided under a written offshore service contract, payment is received in foreign currency from abroad, and no benefit/use is enjoyed in Viet Nam. Services performed or consumed in Viet Nam, and transactions with domestic parties, remain taxable at 10% (or exempt if classified as financial/credit services). Financial/fiduciary services that are VAT-exempt do not enjoy input VAT creditability and cannot also be zero-rated; therefore classification under Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC as either an exempt financial service or an export service determines the outcome.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic export declaration, customs e-system) evidencing goods passing out of Viet Nam (for goods exports)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing delivery/offshore destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a licensed commercial bank in Viet Nam evidencing receipt of foreign currency from the foreign buyer/offshore trust
  • Export sales contract or offshore service agreement with the non-resident trust/beneficiary
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (goods) or service invoice/time-sheet certification (services)
  • Transport/insurance certificates, if any
  • Copy of the zero-rated e-invoice issued under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and associated bank SWIFT/payment records

All taxable supplies must be supported by e-invoices in the GDT XML format (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC). E-invoices are signed by qualified digital signatures; physical stamping is abolished. Where the e-invoice uses a tax authority code/transaction code, the invoice is cleared through the GDT e-invoice portal in near-real-time before/at issuance; code-less e-invoices must be transmitted to the GDT within the prescribed periodic schedule. API/portal integration is mandatory for large sellers, and all taxpayers must maintain invoice records on the GDT system to support VAT refunds and zero-rating claims.

Viet Nam's GDT and Customs cross-match export declarations, bills of lading, e-invoices, bank receipts and FX remittance records. If a zero-rated export or VAT refund cannot be substantiated, the refund is clawed back in full with late-payment interest at 0.03% per day, and administrative penalties apply under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP up to three times the evaded tax. Where false invoices, forged customs documents, or non-existent exports are used, criminal prosecution may follow under Article 200 of the Penal Code (VAT/tax fraud), particularly when the evaded tax exceeds VND 100,000,000. The 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation deadline is a specific compliance condition; missing it (absent lawful extension) exposes the taxpayer to FX-control sanctions and recapture of the VAT refund. The taxpayer bears the burden of proving offshore consumption, actual export, and payment repatriation.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Corporate non-resident member distributions: 0% dividend withholding tax (CIT) on after-tax profits remitted by a Vietnamese LLC to a foreign corporate shareholder. Profit remittance is permitted only after audited financial statements, full CIT payment, and tax finalisation. Non-resident individual shareholders receiving profit distributions are subject to a final 5% personal income tax withheld at source, unless a treaty rate reduces that liability.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than the 90th day after the end of the fiscal year; for a calendar-year company this is 31 March. The fiscal year-end may be adjusted only with prior tax authority approval.

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than the 30th day of the first month of the following quarter: 30 April for Q1, 30 July for Q2, 30 October for Q3, and 30 January for Q4 of the subsequent year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Same as the annual CIT finalization deadline: no later than 90 days after the fiscal year-end.

Late filing penalties under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP: VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 for 1 to 30 days late; VND 5,000,000 to 8,000,000 for 31 to 60 days; VND 8,000,000 to 15,000,000 for 61 to 90 days; VND 15,000,000 to 25,000,000 for more than 90 days. Any late or underestimated payment also attracts late-payment interest at 0.03% per day on the outstanding tax amount.
Box C - Deductible expenses Expenses actually incurred with lawful invoices, vouchers, and contracts, allocated to the generation of assessable revenue; statutory caps on advertising, entertainment, and management fees apply; donations and provisions exceeding statutory thresholds are non-deductible. Recharges paid to an offshore trustee or trust administrator are deductible only where the contract is substantiated, services are actually performed, payment is made through a banking institution, and the foreign contractor tax obligations for the payee are settled. Late filing penalties under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP: VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 for 1 to 30 days late; VND 5,000,000 to 8,000,000 for 31 to 60 days; VND 8,000,000 to 15,000,000 for 61 to 90 days; VND 15,000,000 to 25,000,000 for more than 90 days. Any late or underestimated payment also attracts late-payment interest at 0.03% per day on the outstanding tax amount.
Box D - Loss carry-forward Prior-year tax losses carried forward chronologically and offset against assessable income, limited to 5 consecutive years. Loss carry-forward belongs to the Vietnamese LLC and is not transferred with ownership interests; a change in non-resident members or trust beneficiaries does not reset the 5-year clock. Late filing penalties under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP: VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 for 1 to 30 days late; VND 5,000,000 to 8,000,000 for 31 to 60 days; VND 8,000,000 to 15,000,000 for 61 to 90 days; VND 15,000,000 to 25,000,000 for more than 90 days. Any late or underestimated payment also attracts late-payment interest at 0.03% per day on the outstanding tax amount.
Box E - CIT payable Final CIT assessed after incorporating provisional quarterly payments, preferential tax rate adjustment, and additional tax payable or refundable. After-tax profit may be remitted abroad only after the audited financial statements are completed and the CIT finalization return is filed; profit remittance by a corporate member is not subject to dividend withholding tax. Late filing penalties under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP: VND 2,000,000 to 5,000,000 for 1 to 30 days late; VND 5,000,000 to 8,000,000 for 31 to 60 days; VND 8,000,000 to 15,000,000 for 61 to 90 days; VND 15,000,000 to 25,000,000 for more than 90 days. Any late or underestimated payment also attracts late-payment interest at 0.03% per day on the outstanding tax amount.
Box I - Estimated quarterly CIT Estimated quarterly taxable profit multiplied by the company's applicable CIT rate, with adjustments for incentives and non-deductible expenses. Provisional tax must be based on a conservative annual projection; quarterly declarations do not need to be exact but total advance payments must reach at least 80% of the final CIT liability. Late quarterly filing attracts the same tiered fixed penalties as the annual return under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP. If the taxpayer pays less than 80% of the final CIT through provisional returns, the shortfall is treated as deficient payment and subjected to late-payment interest at 0.03% per day plus an administration penalty of up to 20% of the under-declared tax.
Box II - Year-to-date cumulative income Cumulative revenue and deductible expenses to the end of the reporting quarter, using the matching principle. If the LLC receives trust income on behalf of an offshore discretionary beneficiary under a deposition contract, such sums must be recorded as fiduciary liabilities rather than revenue unless risk of ownership has passed to the LLC. Late quarterly filing attracts the same tiered fixed penalties as the annual return under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP. If the taxpayer pays less than 80% of the final CIT through provisional returns, the shortfall is treated as deficient payment and subjected to late-payment interest at 0.03% per day plus an administration penalty of up to 20% of the under-declared tax.
Box III - Advance payment due The amount payable in cash to the state budget, determined as the difference between the cumulative tax due and prior quarterly advances. Advance payments must be completed by the annual deadline; underpayment beyond the 80% threshold triggers penalty interest and statutory underestimation penalties. Late quarterly filing attracts the same tiered fixed penalties as the annual return under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP. If the taxpayer pays less than 80% of the final CIT through provisional returns, the shortfall is treated as deficient payment and subjected to late-payment interest at 0.03% per day plus an administration penalty of up to 20% of the under-declared tax.
Part I - Enterprise information Identifies the taxpayer, global organisation chart, related parties, transfer-pricing legal entity details, and total revenue plus total related-party transaction value. Foreign trust protectors, settlors, beneficiaries, and offshore corporate trustees must be included as related parties where they are able to control or substantially influence the VN LLC's pricing, funding, or commercial decisions. Failure to attach Form 03-7/TNDN where related-party transactions exist attracts fixed penalties from VND 8,000,000 to VND 15,000,000 plus possible recharacterisation by the tax authority. A TP adjustment may result in additional CIT, late-payment interest at 0.03% per day, and penalties of up to 20% of the additional tax found after inspection.
