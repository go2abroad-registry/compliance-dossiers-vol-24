Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital requirement and no escrow account is required. The authorized share capital is chosen by the incorporator and set out in the Memorandum and Articles; the first share may be issued at par value without capital deposit.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Vanuatu Fire Services is required for any warehouse exceeding the internal floor-area threshold. Structural load inspections must confirm racking capacity for stored electronic inventories, and occupational clearance must verify ESD-safe flooring, adequate ventilation, and emergency egress per the National Building Code.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital requirement and no escrow account is required. The authorized share capital is chosen by the incorporator and set out in the Memorandum and Articles; the first share may be issued at par value without capital deposit.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; customs/revenue filings via the national ASYCUDA World single-window portal; exempt International Company registration via Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

No TIN or VAT ID is obtainable or required for an offshore International Company (IC). The IC is automatically exempt from all Vanuatu taxes under s.134 of the International Companies Act [Cap.222] and is statutorily prohibited from domestic taxable activity under s.135; the VFSC-issued registration certificate ('IC-xxxxx') is the sole tax-relevant identifier. A VAT registration number is issued only to domestic traders exceeding VUV 2,000,000 turnover; such registration is legally unavailable to an exempt IC.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (statutorily exempt non-resident offshore company; Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax or stamp duty, so entity-level taxation is nil and the s.134 exemption applies automatically while the IC avoids local resident trading). — No TIN or VAT ID is obtainable or required for an offshore International Company (IC). The IC is automatically exempt from all Vanuatu taxes under s.134 of the International Companies Act [Cap.222] and is statutorily prohibited from domestic taxable activity under s.135; the VFSC-issued registration certificate ('IC-xxxxx') is the sole tax-relevant identifier. A VAT registration number is issued only to domestic traders exceeding VUV 2,000,000 turnover; such registration is legally unavailable to an exempt IC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (statutorily exempt non-resident offshore company; Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax or stamp duty, so entity-level taxation is nil and the s.134 exemption applies automatically while the IC avoids local resident trading).

Available election forms

None tax-related. Vanuatu has no income tax, so no check-the-box, hybrid-mismatch, CFC or group election exists. The only corporate election is conversion of an IC into a domestic company under the Companies Act [Cap.191]; conversion forfeits the s.134 exempt status and makes the entity subject to VAT, business licence fees and customs duties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 2,000,000 annual taxable turnover (VAT Act registration threshold); inapplicable to an exempt offshore IC, which has no domestic VAT nexus and cannot register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated under the VAT Act where goods are physically removed from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply and the supplier retains export evidence. A pure offshore IC distributing passive electronic components dropshipped from foreign principals to foreign buyers never enters the Vanuatu VAT net, so no output VAT, input credit or export refund mechanics arise. Vanuatu has no exchange controls; the 180-day inward remittance window is the bank/audit substantiation benchmark.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed in ASYCUDA World (HS 8532 for ceramic capacitors and HS 8533 for fixed resistors)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Vanuatu within the 28-day statutory window
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign-currency proceeds within the 180-day window

No real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal API clearance or digital stamping mandate exists. VAT is evidenced by prescribed paper/PDF tax invoices, while customs declarations must be submitted electronically through ASYCUDA World.

No income-tax clawback exists. Enforcement risks: (i) false VAT zero-rating/refund claims attract civil penalty of up to 200% of tax evaded plus interest and possible imprisonment; (ii) false customs export declarations trigger seizure and penalty assessments; (iii) an IC that trades with Vanuatu residents or in Vatu forfeits s.134 exemption and is liable to VFSC fines and retrospective VAT/import assessments on domestic-connected supplies.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is generally freely convertible for legitimate current account transactions including trade settlements, service payments, and dividend or profit remittances, subject to prevailing anti-money laundering (AML) documentation. Capital account transactions, including inbound equity injections, shareholder loans, and repo-style liquidity arrangements, are not subject to restrictive prior central bank approval for International Companies, but local commercial banks will independently apply source-of-funds verification and foreign investment due diligence. There are no exchange-controlled queues, surrender requirements, or differentiated current/offshore market rates; resident and non-resident accounts may be maintained in VUV or major foreign currencies such as USD, AUD, EUR, and GBP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu does not impose any statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. A VU International Company (IC) is permitted to retain export receipts in foreign currency accounts domestically or offshore without force-sale conversion to VUV. For statistical and balance-of-payments reporting, commercial banks must submit transaction-level data to the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu within their regular reporting cycle; no export certificate or central bank permit is required solely for retention or deployment of export receivables.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act, operating in conjunction with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for licensing and corporate compliance matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability for offshore digital electronic-money institutions (EMIs). VFSC does not recognize a generic EMI licence, and the Financial Dealers Licensing Act restricts unauthorized electronic money issuance; however, a VU International Company may use a VFSC-licensed remittance dealer or a fully licensed overseas EMI for auxiliary receivable collection in USD/AUD, provided the EMI does not become the primary settlement bank for trade disbursements. Major tier-1 commercial banks in Vanuatu remain the only acceptable clearing counterparties for documentary credits, structured trade finance, and escrow-capital injection.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection shall be routed as a SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the overseas shareholder's bank directly into the VU IC's Capital Escrow Account at a tier-1 Vanuatu commercial bank. The wire must clearly state the economic purpose code as either 'Direct Investment Equity Capital' or 'Intercompany/Shareholder Subordinated Loan'; the receiving bank will apply value-dated funds verification and request a board resolution, shareholder loan deed, VFSC corporate extract, and know-your-customer confirmation for the ultimate funding source. Funds must remain in the escrow account and may be funneled into the Operational Account only after compliance sign-off; for capital above customary thresholds, the bank may require evidence of Foreign Investment Registration if the IC is undertaking branch-like physical activity, though a purely passive distribution IC without a domestic physical trade presence is generally exempt under Vanuatu International Companies legislation.Trade disbursements for passive electronic inventory purchases will be cleared predominantly through SWIFT MT103 direct remittances for open-account supplier payments and through documentary commercial letters of credit (sight or usance) issued under ICC UCP 600 for negotiated procurement from OEM/EMS suppliers. USD payments to Chinese, Taiwanese, Korean, and North American component manufacturers will route via the bank's New York correspondent (Fedwire/CHIPS); EUR-denominated obligations will clear via SEPA through the bank's European correspondent; and Australian-dollar payments will clear via the bank's Australian branch network or an AUSTRAC-regulated correspondent. For VUV-denominated local expenses, settlement occurs through RBV's interbank clearing platform. The bank's trade finance desk will require underlying purchase orders, supplier invoices, and logistics proof before executing each disbursement; prohibited strings include payments to sanctioned entities, cash-sweep transfers, and invoices not clearly tied to ISIC 4652 component transactions.Onshore OTC Vanuatu vatu (VUV) forward contracts with RBV-licensed commercial banks, limited to bona fide trade and balance-sheet exposure in passive electronic component distribution.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in USD/VUV or AUD/VUV using the RBV indicative mid-rate, entered through reputable global banks for hedging forecasted cash flows where onshore forward liquidity is insufficient.Cross-currency swaps (VUV against USD, AUD, or EUR) executed between the VU IC and a licensed commercial bank for period-end treasury netting, subject to underlying exposure documentation and bank credit approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing legislation and prescribes no Local File or Master File threshold in any local currency. Multinational groups should nonetheless compile OECD-aligned transfer-pricing documentation (functional analysis and benchmarking appropriate to the passive electronic component distribution function) to defend the intermediary margin in the counterparty jurisdiction and to resist recharacterisation of the IC as a conduit.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not currently operate Economic Substance Regulations equivalent to BVI or Cayman ESR. Statutory substance duties under the International Companies Act are limited to maintaining a registered office and licensed agent, keeping books and records, and filing the annual declaration. Foreign tax authorities may disregard or pierce a 'brass-plate' IC lacking real operational substance when distributing multilayer ceramic capacitors and resistors, particularly where the IC holds no inventory title, maintains no bank account in its own name, employs no staff, and exercises no board authority over the distribution transactions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Prohibited Domestic Activity (International Companies Act)

Statutory sanction: Imposition of statutory fines on the company and each officer as prescribed by the International Companies Act, forced deregistration and winding-up orders; the offending transaction is void and unenforceable.

Director personal liability: Veil-piercing applies: every director and officer who knowingly authorised the prohibited act is jointly and severally liable for the company's debts and losses, subject to criminal fines, disqualification orders and civil restitution.
Annual Licence Default / Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: Automatic 10% late-payment surcharge; after two consecutive annual defaults the Registry strikes the company off and dissolves it; company assets escheat to the Vanuatu Government subject to liquidation costs.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders lose the statutory shield of limited liability for debts and liabilities incurred after dissolution; creditors and the Registrar may bring civil claims directly against them.
Shell Company Abuse / Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: No direct Vanuatu income-tax sanction exists, but the IC may be recharacterised as a sham by foreign courts, Vanuatu may face countermeasures under the OECD Global Forum and Inclusive Framework, and the VFSC may revoke exempt status for breach of good standing.

Director personal liability: Directors may be named in foreign criminal tax proceedings, subjected to extradition requests, asset-freezing orders and travel bans, and held personally liable under civil conspiracy, fraudulent-trading or dishonest-assistance theories.
AML / CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Offences under the Vanuatu Proceeds of Crime Act and the Financial Dealers Act 2020: fines up to VUV 30,000,000 (approximately US$250,000), imprisonment up to 20 years, confiscation of assets and revocation of the registered agent's licence.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face imprisonment, travel bans, confiscation orders and long-term prohibition from acting as officers of any Vanuatu company.
Misrepresentation and Fraud on the Registry

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties for false statements under the International Companies Act, immediate cancellation of the IC's registration, and referral for prosecution for fraud under the Vanuatu Penal Code.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for fines and imprisonment; the court may order restitution to defrauded trade creditors, disgorgement of profits and payment of liquidation costs.
