Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) – corporate registry and IC licensing; Department of Inland Revenue (DIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management – VAT and customs revenue. Portals: https://www.vfsc.vu and https://www.gov.vu (DIR e-services gateway).

E-government filing portal

A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is incorporated electronically via a licensed VFSC agent and receives a Certificate of Incorporation containing the VU Company Registration Number; no separate income-tax TIN is issued because ICs are exempt from Vanuatu tax. For VAT, if voluntary or mandatory registration is triggered, the IC files VAT Registration Form (VAT-1) with DIR, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered office details, UBO identification, and projected annual turnover; DIR issues a VAT Account Number within approximately 10–15 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is incorporated electronically via a licensed VFSC agent and receives a Certificate of Incorporation containing the VU Company Registration Number; no separate income-tax TIN is issued because ICs are exempt from Vanuatu tax. For VAT, if voluntary or mandatory registration is triggered, the IC files VAT Registration Form (VAT-1) with DIR, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered office details, UBO identification, and projected annual turnover; DIR issues a VAT Account Number within approximately 10–15 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax and has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime. The IC may elect in its constitutional documents to operate under the International Companies Act with an International Company (exempt) status; no election alters tax treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 annual taxable turnover (compulsory); voluntary registration available for turnover below this threshold where the person is carrying on a taxable activity

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods exported from Vanuatu by the supplier within 28 days of invoice/payment, evidenced by a Customs export declaration and transport documentation, and to services supplied to a non-resident outside Vanuatu or directly connected to exported goods. For a Vanuatu IC conducting offshore medical-device assembly without local taxable activity, zero-rating is structurally immaterial because the supply is outside the territorial scope of Vanuatu VAT; if an IC elects voluntary VAT registration, the same export zero-rating conditions apply.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or House Airwaybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (where required)

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API e-invoicing mandate currently applies. A regular VAT invoice with prescribed content (invoice number, date, supplier/recipient VAT ID, goods description, VAT-exclusive value, VAT amount and rate) is required for all taxable supplies; records must be kept for seven years.

DIR may audit export zero-rating claims and may revoke zero-rating where documentary evidence or foreign exchange repatriation is not in place within the 180-day benchmark. The consequence is assessment of VAT at 12.5%, with default interest of 2% per month and penalties of up to 200% of the tax payable; fraudulent claims are criminally prosecutable with fines and imprisonment, and refunds received are clawed back for four years under the VAT Act and Proceeds of Crime Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible for current-account transactions and largely free for capital-account transactions. International Companies (ICs) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] may hold, borrow, and remit funds in any foreign currency without RBV prior approval. Profit and capital remittances are unrestricted for non-resident ICs, subject only to a valid VFSC Certificate of Good Standing and an annual compliance return. Practical friction arises not from statutory controls but from correspondent-bank due diligence on VUV-denominated wires; therefore USD or AUD clearing accounts are the recommended treasury vehicle.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists. Exporters may retain 100% of foreign-currency proceeds offshore or onshore in external accounts. Obligation is limited to statistical reporting: monthly BOP-01 return to RBV for aggregated receipts exceeding VUV 10 million equivalent, and an annual survey for related-party trade flows. No mandatory conversion to VUV applies; where domestic VUV settlement is required, conversion is executed at the RBV-published mid-rate plus a commercial margin.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 282], is the sole monetary and exchange-control authority, administering the Exchange Control Act [Cap 57] as substantially liberalized in 2006, together with the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Vanuatu has no electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing category; only deposit-taking institutions licensed under the Financial Institutions Act [Cap 356] may lawfully hold client funds. Foreign digital EMIs cannot clear VUV and face closed correspondent routes; their use for receivable factoring or interim float creates AML red flags and potential unlicensed-banking exposure under VFSC supervision. Direct licensed-bank rails are the sole compliant and operationally viable path.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profit tax, no payroll tax, and no social security contribution tax at the national level; it is a zero-direct-taxation jurisdiction).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, no branch remittance tax, no distribution tax, and no capital gains tax on share disposals. An International Company may repatriate profits to non-resident shareholders by dividend, loan repayment, intercompany settlement, or management fee without any Vanuatu tax cost.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 30 days after each anniversary of incorporation; the annual government franchise fee is payable simultaneously. VFSC applies a progressive late-filing fee (10-20% of the annual fee per month elapsed, capped at 100%) and may strike off the company after sustained default.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and payment due on or before the last calendar day of the month following the end of the taxable period; exports of surgical suture products are zero-rated and typically create a refund credit position.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing statute, no Local File / Master File requirement, no Country-by-Country reporting regime, and no advance pricing agreement mechanism. The closest analogous registration threshold is the VAT registration threshold of VT 5,000,000 (approximately US$41,000) of annual taxable turnover for physical supplies made in Vanuatu; no filing threshold applies for purely cross-border intercompany transactions.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not operate a Gulf-style Economic Substance Rules code, but as an OECD Inclusive Framework member it applies CRS reporting, beneficial-ownership registers, and VFSC administrative substance expectations. The IC must maintain adequate substance: a licensed resident registered agent, a physical registered office in Vanuatu, and available books and records. The VFSC annual return includes an economic-substance self-assessment; enhanced review applies to relevant activities of banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, IP holding, and distribution/service-centre. Pure manufacturing of surgical sutures and needle assembly (ISIC 3250) is not a listed relevant activity, but real operational direction (board minutes, intercompany agreements, a Vanuatu bank account, and financial statements) must be preserved to avoid shell-company recharacterisation, foreign CFC inclusion, and VFSC strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Breach of International Company tax-exempt perimeter (local trading)

Statutory sanction: Invalidation of the s.33 International Companies Act exemption; recharacterisation as an ordinary Vanuatu company; imposition of VAT at 12.5% on local supplies; municipal business-licence assessment; and VFSC strike-off proceedings. Regulatory fines apply under the non-compliant conduct provisions.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise local business may be personally liable for unpaid VAT and civil restitution to the company; VFSC may issue a prohibition order barring management of any Vanuatu entity for up to five years.
Shell company / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC refusal to accept the annual return, public default listing, administrative strike-off and dissolution, asset-freezing orders on Vanuatu bank accounts, and red-flag reporting to OECD and APG peer jurisdictions under TIEA exchange mechanisms.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed registered agent face fines and imprisonment for false declarations under the Companies (Amendment) Act 2013; the Vanuatu Supreme Court may order restitution, costs, and a travel ban while the dissolution is investigated.
AML/CFT and sanctions evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Act 2007 (as amended): Supreme Court confiscation and freezing orders, statutory financial penalties against the corporate entity, and criminal fines plus imprisonment for individuals; unlicensed financial dealing additionally triggers VFSC enforcement and criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Limited liability is pierced: directors, compliance officers, and the registered agent are personally liable for money-laundering and false-declaration offences, with custodial sentences of up to 15 years, personal asset confiscation, travel bans, and mutual-assistance extradition within the APG network.
Chronic non-filing and franchise-fee default

Statutory sanction: Progressive late fees of 10-20% per month (capped at 100% of the annual fee); automatic strike-off and dissolution; VAT non-filing adds the fixed per-return penalty and monthly compounding interest on arrears; the registered agent is prohibited from continuing to act for the company.

Director personal liability: Directors remain civilly liable for debts incurred after dissolution; VFSC may certify personal liability for outstanding fees and tax arrears, and directors may be barred from incorporating new Vanuatu entities for up to five years.
