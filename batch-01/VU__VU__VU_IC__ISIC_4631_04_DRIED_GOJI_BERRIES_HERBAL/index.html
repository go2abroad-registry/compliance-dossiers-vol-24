Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA) – official portal: https://vrca.gov.vu; International Company registration authority: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) is registered under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) through a registered agent and obtains a VFSC Company Number. No statutory TIN is issued solely for tax purposes to a pure offshore IC. If the IC conducts domestic taxable supplies or seeks zero-rated export clearance to substantiate VAT-exempt export turnover, it must apply to VRCA for VAT registration using the VFSC certificate, constitutional documents, registered-office address, and board resolution; approval is generally issued within 5 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned International Company (IC) is registered under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) through a registered agent and obtains a VFSC Company Number. No statutory TIN is issued solely for tax purposes to a pure offshore IC. If the IC conducts domestic taxable supplies or seeks zero-rated export clearance to substantiate VAT-exempt export turnover, it must apply to VRCA for VAT registration using the VFSC certificate, constitutional documents, registered-office address, and board resolution; approval is generally issued within 5 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box, CFC, or hybrid mismatch election regimes. An International Company automatically qualifies as a tax-exempt offshore entity exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, stamp duties on offshore transactions, and VAT on offshore supplies. An IC may, however, elect voluntarily to register for VAT under Section 21 of the Value Added Tax Act 2011 solely to claim zero-rating on export sales and recover input credits; such an election is irrevocable for at least 12 months.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT 4,000,000 (four million vatu) per annum on taxable turnover; voluntary registration is permitted; export-only supplies can be zero-rated even if the supplier does not exceed the local turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the supply of goods that are physically exported from Vanuatu to a non-resident destination, and to services rendered offshore or directly to a non-resident for use outside Vanuatu. An International Company exporting dried goji berries and herbal botanicals must demonstrate, through contemporaneous documentation, that the goods left Vanuatu in accordance with the VAT Act and that the customer is a non-resident; the supplier must retain proof of export and payment.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin

Vanuatu does not mandate real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, API filing, or digital stamping. Taxpayers must issue paper or electronic VAT invoices containing the mandatory particulars set out in the VAT Act and retain supporting documents for at least 5 years for audit purposes.

VRCA and Vanuatu Customs conduct post-clearance audits and may reverse zero-rated treatment where export evidence is deficient or fraudulent. Penalties include understatement penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded, interest accruals, criminal fines for fraudulent invoices, and revocation of VAT registration; although Vanuatu has no exchange-control legislation, failure to evidence legitimate repatriation is treated by VRCA as a red flag in export substantiation and may trigger an audit and retrospective clawback.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates an open, non-exchange-controlled regime. The Vatu (VUV) is officially floating and freely convertible through RBV-licensed authorised dealers for both current-account and capital-account transactions. No RBV approval is required for outward profit remittance, dividend distribution, repatriation of equity, or settlement of bona fide intercompany debts by an International Company. Practical frictions are confined to correspondent-bank de-risking and bank-level documentary checks (board resolution, solvency certificate, underlying export invoices), not statutory conversion quotas. Capital-account convertibility is unrestricted; the only legal obstacle is the completion of client due diligence under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 23 of 2020 (AMLCFT Act).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio apply. An International Company may retain export proceeds indefinitely in foreign currency (USD, AUD, EUR) in onshore or offshore accounts without repatriation deadlines. The AMLCFT Act 2020 imposes record-keeping and transaction-monitoring duties only; there is no RBV-imposed 90-day or 180-day liquidation rule for trade receivables.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; sole monetary authority, currency issuer, and supervisory authority for all licensed banks and authorised foreign-exchange dealers in Vanuatu.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Global digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, Mercury) generally decline Vanuatu-incorporated International Companies due to jurisdiction-risk appetite, residual FATF/EU listing history, and inability to obtain reliable remote UBO verification. Legally, a non-resident EMI may onboard a VU IC only if the EMI holds a valid home-jurisdiction licence and executes full AMLCFT-compatible due diligence; Vanuatu offers no passporting or EMI licensing that grants such providers direct access to VUV clearing rails. A practical alternative is a segregated multi-currency account at a Tier 1 bank with SWIFT access.
