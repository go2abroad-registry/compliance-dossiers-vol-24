Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum charter capital applies to ISIC 7020 management consultancy under Vietnamese law; market-entry consulting is not a conditional business line and is not subject to the legal capital requirements of Decree 01/2021/ND-CP. The founders freely determine the charter capital in the corporate charter. However, under Articles 47 and 75 of the Law on Enterprises 2020, the member or members must fully pay the registered charter capital within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate; unpaid capital triggers a mandatory reduction of charter capital to the paid-up amount. For a foreign-invested LLC, the equity contribution must be remitted by wire transfer in VND to the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened at a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam pursuant to Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN; no escrow account is statutorily required, but the DICA is monitored by the State Bank of Vietnam and the bank must issue a capital-contribution receipt evidenced by the incoming remittance.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the building owner must hold the fire protection design appraisal and fire safety acceptance certificate under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP covering the leased premises; the tenant must comply with the building's fire safety plan, maintain escape routes, provide fire extinguishers on each floor, and conduct annual fire drills and self-inspection records under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP. Occupational safety: the Company must comply with the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13 by providing workplace induction and safety training, establishing a safety inspection book, and arranging periodic health checks for employees; no prior physical-clearance certificate is required for rented office use, but the annual fire-safety inspection by the Fire Prevention and Rescue Police is mandatory and can result in temporary suspension of activity until remedial measures are completed.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance; official portal: etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested limited liability company obtains its tax identification number (Mã số thuế) automatically upon successful enterprise registration with the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI). No separate TIN application is required for the entity itself. The LLC must then register for VAT, e-invoice issuance, and periodic tax declarations on the General Department of Taxation's electronic portal. Foreign contractors or suppliers without a local entity are subject to a separate registration and withholding process under Vietnamese tax law.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Vietnam does not provide a check-the-box entity classification regime. A Vietnamese LLC is always treated as a separate corporate taxpayer for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) purposes. Certain elective regimes exist for investment incentives (e.g., high-tech enterprise status, export processing enterprise status), for accounting basis (accrual vs. cash in exceptional thresholds), and for foreign tax relief (election to claim a foreign tax credit instead of a deduction for foreign taxes paid). Hybrid mismatch elections are not locally available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None for LLCs; all enterprises, including foreign-invested LLCs, must register and file VAT regardless of turnover. A VND 100,000,000 annual revenue threshold applies only to household businesses and individuals, not to LLCs.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Consultancy and other exportable services qualify for the 0% VAT rate under Article 1 of Circular 130/2016/TT-BTC (amending Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC) when all of the following conditions are satisfied: (i) the service is supplied directly to a foreign organization or individual under a written service contract; (ii) the foreign recipient has no permanent establishment in Vietnam; and (iii) the service is physically and legally consumed outside Vietnam. For an overseas market entry consultancy paid by a non-resident client, the 0% rate applies if the service is provided from Vietnam to an offshore customer and the out-of-Vietnam consumption condition is duly documented. The provider must retain the export service contract, correspondence, payment evidence, and any supporting deliverables confirming the offshore consumption.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for goods; not directly applicable to pure services but required when accompanying software or documents in physical form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment of any tangible goods or deliverables to overseas recipient
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement demonstrating non-cash payment received from abroad through a licensed Vietnamese bank

All VAT invoices must be issued as electronic invoices through the General Department of Taxation's e-invoice system (xml format), bearing a unique digital signature. For invoices requiring the tax authority's code, the XML data must be transmitted to the GDT portal in real-time before issuance; the GDT approves and affixes a tax authority code within 30 minutes. For enterprises permitted to issue e-invoices without a tax code, the XML files must still be transmitted to the tax authority within a prescribed period. No physical stamping is required; digital authentication via GDT's system is mandatory.

Export zero-rating claims are intensely scrutinized by the Vietnamese tax authority. If the statutory documentation is incomplete, if the overseas payment is not received within the 180-day repatriation deadline, or if the claimed export service is deemed to have been consumed in Vietnam, the tax authority will: (i) reverse the 0% VAT treatment and re-assess standard 10% VAT on the full export revenue; (ii) disallow any corresponding input VAT credit; (iii) impose late-payment interest at 0.03% per day; (iv) apply administrative fines for tax evasion of up to 3 times the tax evaded; and (v) refer the matter for criminal prosecution if intentional fraud is established. Additionally, the exporter's e-invoice blocks may be suspended, and the enterprise may be placed on the 'high-risk' list for future customs and tax audits.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese dong (VND) operates under a managed float and is not freely convertible. Current account transactions are broadly liberalized: payments and transfers for trade in goods and services, including consulting fees, royalties, interest, and the annual remittance of profits by foreign-invested enterprises, may be executed through authorized credit institutions upon production of underlying commercial documents and tax compliance evidence. Capital account convertibility is restricted. Inward foreign direct investment equity contributions and shareholder loans, and outward remittances of dividends, loan principal, and liquidation proceeds, must pass through a registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained at an SBV-licensed bank. Profit remittance hurdles include the completion of the annual statutory audit, finalization of corporate income tax (CIT) liabilities, settlement of all Foreign Contractor Tax and social insurance obligations, a resolution of the Members Council approving the dividend distribution, and the practical availability of distributable retained earnings as confirmed by the auditor. No additional dividend withholding tax is imposed under domestic law on a Vietnamese LLC distributing profits to a corporate shareholder, but the bank requires the certified DICA documentation, and the payment is reviewed against the reported tax position. The ordinary timeliness risk is approximately one to four weeks per remittance cycle; notification to the local tax authority of the remittance plan is required by practice.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters of goods and services, including the consulting services performed by this LLC for overseas clients, must repatriate export proceeds into a foreign currency account maintained at a licensed credit institution in Vietnam within 90 days from the date of export or completion of the service, pursuant to Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP; an extension to 180 days may be granted by the managing bank for justifiable commercial reasons. Vietnam abolished mandatory export surrender ratios for ordinary exporters in the post-2015 liberalization: there is currently no fixed statutory percentage of export receipts that must be converted into VND, and exporters may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency. In the event the enterprise requires VND for domestic operating expenses, conversion is executed at the authorized bank's spot rate. The bank reconciles export proceeds against customs declarations, service agreements, and invoices, and the SBV retains the authority to re-impose compulsory surrender at specified percentages during balance-of-payments emergency conditions, although no such ratio is presently in force.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV; Vietnamese: Ngan hang Nha nuoc Viet Nam). Foreign exchange administration is exercised by the SBV under the Foreign Exchange Ordinance No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP, and implementing circulars, notably Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN (as amended by Circular No. 03/2018/TT-NHNN) for current account transactions, Circular No. 19/2014/TT-NHNN for the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) regime applicable to foreign-invested enterprises, and Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN (as amended by Circular No. 01/2024/TT-NHNN) for foreign loan registration and repayment. The SBV also administers Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP for administrative sanctions in the foreign exchange and banking sphere.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary banking and DICA purposes; MEDIUM-LOW as a supplementary collection or disbursement rail for low-value overseas payments. Offshore digital payment institutions (Wise Business, Airwallex, Revolut Business, Payoneer) do not hold a State Bank of Vietnam license under the Law on Credit Institutions 2024 or Decree No. 101/2012/ND-CP, as amended by Decree No. 80/2016/ND-CP, and therefore cannot open VND settlement accounts, act as the Direct Investment Capital Account bank, or lawfully provide domestic payment services to a Vietnamese LLC. Funds that are receivable by the LLC may be collected through an offshore EMI in limited circumstances, but such receipts must be swept into the licensed onshore DICA or the operating account within the 90-day repatriation window to avoid an administrative sanction under Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP and to satisfy the bank's underlying-documentation review. Vietnamese commercial banks may treat credits originating from EMIs as third-party or unexplained flows and request additional declarations, source-of-fund confirmation, and copies of the service contract before accepting the funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All charter capital contributions and shareholder loans are remitted from the offshore investor's banking account via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the DICA of the Vietnamese LLC maintained at a licensed commercial bank. The SWIFT narrative (field 70) must expressly state the purpose of the remittance, referencing the full legal name of the LLC, the Investment Registration Certificate number, and the capital contribution schedule, for example: CONTRIBUTION OF CHARTER CAPITAL TO {LLC NAME} UNDER IRC NO. {XXX} DATED {DD/MM/YYYY}. The sending bank account name must exactly match the name of the foreign investor recorded in the IRC; nexus mismatches are the most frequent cause of quarantine by the compliance department of the receiving bank. Upon receipt, the bank credits the DICA and reports the inflow to the SBV's foreign exchange management information system, and may request the closing capital certificate or bank confirmation of the contribution to complete the IRC contribution milestone. Where the LLC receives a shareholder loan above the statutory threshold, a separate foreign loan registration must be obtained from the SBV via the authorized bank under Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN before the drawdown is credited; short-term trade credit facilities under one year are exempt from registration but remain subject to bank verification.Cross-border disbursements for consultancy operations, including payments to overseas subcontracting consultants, market research agencies, software and data platform subscriptions, licensing fees, and foreign marketing agents, are settled by SWIFT MT103 from the VND or USD operating account, or directly from the DICA where the payment relates to a registered capital item. Banks require the underlying service contract or invoice and a completed Foreign Contractor Tax withholding form (Form 01/NTNN under Circular No. 80/2021/TT-BTC) together with evidence of payment of the deemed VAT (5%) and CIT (5%) for pure consulting services before executing the remittance; alternative relief certificates under a double tax treaty must be filed with the tax authority to reduce the CIT component. Payments above the bank's regulatory threshold (USD 10,000 equivalent in most institutions) trigger enhanced documentary review. Where the LLC's scope expands to include procurement of equipment or goods for foreign clients, commercial letters of credit (MT700) may be issued at the same bank, with subsequent settlement via MT103 or MT202; domestic VND funding of the settlement account is executed through the National Payment Corporation of Vietnam's clearing house, ACH, or instant VietQR transfer, after which the foreign currency leg is effected through the bank's correspondent network (USD via CHIPS/Fedwire, EUR via TARGET2, and JPY via FXYCS/BOJ-NET).Onshore FX Forward Contracts (VND/USD, VND/EUR) with a licensed credit institution, maximum tenor 365 days, supported by genuine underlying trade or service cash flows and registered capital payment obligations; the forward must be documented with the relevant contract and bank facility agreement.Onshore FX Swaps (spot/forward combinations and forward/forward swaps) used for short-term liquidity rebalancing between VND and foreign currency where the LLC holds dual-currency operating positions, executed with the same licensed bank under the master FX agreement.Cross-Currency Interest Rate Swaps (CIRS) for hedging long-term foreign-currency shareholder loans, permitted where the underlying loan has been registered with the State Bank of Vietnam pursuant to Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN and the bank's credit approval contemplates the derivatives exposure.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% on taxable worldwide income derived through the Vietnamese limited liability company

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% under Vietnam’s domestic law for dividends paid from after-tax profits to foreign corporate shareholders of a Vietnamese LLC. Vietnam does not impose a separate profit remittance tax on foreign investors. Remittance is permitted only after audited financial statements and the annual CIT finalization are completed and all tax liabilities are paid.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, Local File documentation is exempt below: VND 30 billion of related-party transactions where the taxpayer’s annual revenue is below VND 50 billion; VND 50 billion where revenue is below VND 100 billion; and VND 100 billion where revenue is below VND 200 billion. Above these baskets, contemporaneous Local/Master File documentation is mandatory. Country-by-Country reporting applies to ultimate parent groups with consolidated revenue of at least VND 18,000 billion. Furthermore, an annual related-party transaction declaration must be submitted with Form 03/TNDN where related-party transaction value exceeds VND 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Viet Nam does not apply a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations law, but the General Anti-Avoidance Rules and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP establish a substance-over-form doctrine. The tax authority may disregard transactions or entities lacking commercial substance, including shell LLCs with no employees, office premises, or local decision-making; round-tripped capital; or intercompany service fees with no demonstrable benefit. The taxpayer bears the burden of proving genuine economic substance and arm’s-length pricing.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and false CIT declaration

Statutory sanction: Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14: administrative penalty of 1 to 3 times the under-declared tax amount; late-payment interest at 0.03% per day; business registration cancellation for repeated serious violations. Criminal referral where evaded tax is at least VND 100 million; under Penal Code 2015 (as amended) fine up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The legal representative or general director who personally certifies false returns faces criminal liability, civil restitution to the tax authority, prohibition from leaving Vietnam during investigation, and disqualification from acting as a director of any Vietnamese enterprise.
Transfer pricing violations

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may re-determine taxable income at arm’s length, impose additional CIT at 20%, charge late-payment interest of 0.03% per day, and issue administrative fines under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP for inadequate documentation, generally up to 20% of the underpaid tax (or higher where intentional distortion is proven).

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrated artificial intercompany pricing, moved assets abroad, or instructed accountants to submit false TP disclosures are personally liable for unpaid taxes and may be prosecuted for tax evasion with asset freezes, travel bans, and custodial sentences.
Shell company and lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: GAAR and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP allow the tax authority to disregard the entity and re-attribute revenue to the actual beneficial owner, resulting in additional CIT, back-withholding taxes, late-payment interest, fines, and revocation of enterprise registration.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be lifted under fraud and civil restitution principles. Directors involved in nominee arrangements face personal repayment orders, travel bans, criminal liability for falsifying enterprise registration documents, and prohibition from forming additional Vietnamese entities.
Improper profit repatriation and false e-invoice schemes

Statutory sanction: The tax authority treats any unlawful remittance as undeclared taxable income, imposes additional CIT at 20% with late-payment interest, fines the taxpayer under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP, and blocks future profit remittance until all liabilities are discharged; criminal liability arises when false invoices are used to evade tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize premature or fraudulent profit distributions are personally liable for restitution of funds, subject to exit-control/travel bans while under investigation, and may receive custodial sentences for tax evasion.
AML and money-laundering red flags

Statutory sanction: Law on Anti-Money Laundering 2022 imposes administrative fines up to VND 3 billion for failure to conduct customer due diligence, maintain records, or report suspicious transactions; repeated violations may lead to business license suspension and referral for money-laundering prosecution.

Director personal liability: Senior officers who knowingly facilitated layered transfers for third parties face criminal prosecution under Penal Code Article 324 for money laundering, with penalties including up to 15 years’ imprisonment, asset confiscation, travel bans, and lifetime disqualification from corporate management.
