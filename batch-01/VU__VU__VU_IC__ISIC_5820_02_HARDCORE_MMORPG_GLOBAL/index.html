Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue – Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu (official portal: https://customs.gov.vu)

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is generally tax-exempt and is not automatically issued a TIN. If VAT registration becomes necessary (e.g., for local taxable supplies or for zero-rating export claims), the foreign entity must submit a completed VAT registration application (Form VAT 01) to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, together with the IC registration certificate, certified passport copies of all directors/shareholders, a board resolution appointing a local tax representative, and evidence of relevant business activity. The Department issues a VAT identification number upon approval; the same ID serves as the withholding tax and customs reference number where applicable.

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists under Vanuatu law. The International Company enjoys automatic income tax exemption under the International Companies Act, subject to complying with IC activity restrictions. An IC may optionally elect to register for VAT under the Value Added Tax Act if it makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu above the registration threshold; this election does not affect income tax exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) exports of goods physically removed from Vanuatu, (ii) international transport and ancillary services, and (iii) services supplied to non-residents that are performed wholly or predominantly outside Vanuatu or that directly relate to offshore business activity. For an MMORPG digital publishing model, digital services and in-app purchases delivered through Apple App Store and Google Play to non-resident users outside Vanuatu can be treated as zero-rated offshore services where the place of supply is outside Vanuatu and the services are not consumed in Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Entry/Form CED)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or equivalent shipping document
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, bank statement, or payment provider settlement report evidencing receipt from overseas counterparties
  • VAT zero-rated supply declaration including exporter TIN, recipient name, and statutory attestation

No real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping regime is currently in force in Vanuatu. Taxpayers must issue VAT/ tax invoices containing prescribed particulars (including supplier name, VAT identification number, supply description, VAT amount, and date). Records must be maintained for audit purposes.

Vanuatu imposes strict anti-fraud measures on export zero-rating claims. False declarations, forged export documents, or excluded goods/services improperly zero-rated trigger a VAT clawback at 12.5% plus interest and administrative penalties, typically up to VUV 200,000 per offence and/or imprisonment of up to 5 years under the Value Added Tax Act. Customs-related misdeclarations additionally attract fines and forfeiture under the Customs Act. Retrospective assessments may be issued by the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue upon audit, and abuse of the International Company regime may lead to loss of statutory tax exemption and reclassification for income tax purposes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberalised exchange control framework for International Companies (ICs). Current account payments and capital account transfers are effectively convertible and do not require prior RBV approval for non-resident-owned ICs. Profit remittance is freely permitted provided the bank receives standard supporting documentation evidencing the underlying transaction and the company has no unsatisfied domestic tax or statutory obligations. The practical remittance hurdle is enhanced compliance due diligence at the commercial bank level, principally de-risking pressure from correspondent banks and the need to substantiate the source of funds from digital app store payout statements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement in Vanuatu. Export receipts generated from MMORPG in-app purchases and distributed via Apple App Store and Google Play may be retained in foreign currency or converted to VUV at the discretion of the company. The RBV AML/CFT directives require reporting of suspicious and large cash-equivalent transactions, and banks must retain documentary proof of the commercial or digital service export, although no mandatory repatriation timeline or percentage conversion ratio is imposed on ICs.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility as a primary banking rail. RBV does not maintain a dedicated EMI (Electronic Money Institution) licensing regime, and offshore fintech EMIs that operate without an in-country banking licence or a licensed agent cannot offer stable settlement into a Vanuatu IC. Such instruments may be used solely as supplementary payout channels if the underlying funds are first cleared through a licensed domestic ADI and provided the EMI complies with the applicable foreign correspondent bank restrictions.

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax; distributions by an International Company to non-resident shareholders are statutorily exempt under the International Companies Act [CAP 222].

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No domestic transfer-pricing statute and no Local File or Master File threshold exist in Vanuatu. The only documentation-linked obligation is Country-by-Country Reporting under Vanuatu's implementation of OECD BEPS Action 13: an MNE group with consolidated group revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750 million (≈ VUV 93,000,000,000) must file a CbCR return and notification where the Vanuatu IC is the ultimate parent or a constituent entity. Below that threshold, no transfer-pricing documentation is required under Vanuatu law.

Economic substance scope: The International Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act applies to Vanuatu International Companies carrying on relevant activities, administered by the VFSC. An MMORPG publisher performing IP licensing and monetisation is conducting an 'Intellectual Property Business', a statutorily relevant activity subject to the full nexus approach. The company must demonstrate: (1) management and control in Vanuatu, with board meetings physically held in-country; (2) adequate local employees, premises, and equipment; (3) local expenditure proportionate to the income level; and (4) annual ESR filing. A pure holding vehicle that merely registers game IP and collects Apple or Google payouts without local activity is a 'cash box' and fails the substance test, making dividend and royalty flows through the Vanuatu IC a primary target for foreign CFC and anti-BEPS rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Abusive Offshore Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: In Vanuatu: ESR failure triggering penalties of up to VUV 1,000,000 and strike-off. In source and residence states: disallowance of royalty deductions, CFC inclusions under Subpart F and GILTI in the United States or Part 9A CTA 2009 in the United Kingdom, digital services taxes of up to 15%, penalties of up to 200% of the tax understated, and denial of treaty benefits under the BEPS Multilateral Instrument Principal Purpose Test.

Director personal liability: Civil courts will pierce the corporate veil for fraud or sham, ordering shareholders and directors to pay restitution of shifted profits plus interest. Criminal evasion prosecutions have generated travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment, with custodial exposure of up to 5 years in the United States and up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion in the United Kingdom. In Vanuatu, a director who signs a false ESR declaration faces imprisonment of up to 5 years and a fine of up to VUV 250,000.
Shell Company — Off-the-Shelf IC Abuse

Statutory sanction: VFSC strike-off and deregistration, restoration fees, and administrative penalties. Apple App Store and Google Play may terminate payout accounts and freeze held funds; correspondent banks de-risk the entity; foreign courts may disregard the corporate personality entirely in enforcement proceedings.

Director personal liability: Where the shell conceals ownership or evades taxes, courts lift the veil and hold directors personally jointly and severally liable for the company's obligations; directors may be disqualified for up to 10 years from managing any Vanuatu company; criminal fraud prosecutions attract custodial sentences of up to 5 years and confiscation orders.
