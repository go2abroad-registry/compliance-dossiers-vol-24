Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Cautiously permissible. The registered address of an LLC in Viet Nam must be a definite physical premises within Vietnamese territory; a post-office box or a pure virtual maildrop is not accepted. Licensed serviced-office and co-working space addresses in Hà Nội and Hồ Chí Minh City are widely accepted (approximately 30% of newly incorporated FDI LLCs use them) provided the operator is authorized to receive official correspondence and can confirm the tenant's presence. Pure home-office addresses are accepted only for Vietnamese natural person sole-member LLCs and are not advisable for a foreign-invested MedTech regulatory consulting LLC; provincial DPIs may require proof of landlord consent, legal title, and fire-safety clearance for certain premises. For an ISIC 7490 consultancy, an address with a functional reception capability is strongly recommended to withstand address verification reviews.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The Provincial Department of Planning and Investment of the province or centrally governed city in which the registered office is located; for a project in an export processing zone, industrial zone, or economic zone, the competent authority is the Management Board of that zone. Administrative oversight is exercised through the Agency for Business Registration under the Ministry of Planning and Investment and the National Enterprise Registration Information Portal.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14, as amended, in particular Articles 22-36 (enterprise registration), Articles 44-52 (charter capital and LLC governance), and Articles 74-80 (members' council and legal representatives); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, Articles 37-42 (foreign investment certificates); Decree No. 01/2021/ND-CP on enterprise registration; Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP guiding the Law on Investment; and Circular No. 216/2016/TT-BTC on fees and charges in enterprise registration activities.

Formation / constitutional filings

Investment Registration Certificate application dossier followed by the Enterprise Registration application. The primary filing instrument is the Enterprise Registration Application together with the company charter, submitted through the National Enterprise Registration Information Portal; for a foreign-invested LLC, the Investment Registration Certificate is a mandatory precondition.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 42 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum charter capital is prescribed for a limited liability company engaged in ISIC 7490 professional consulting. Charter capital is freely agreed by the members and stated in VND in the charter, Investment Registration Certificate, and Enterprise Registration Certificate. There is no escrow account requirement for this non-conditional activity. Under Article 47 of the Law on Enterprises 2020, charter capital must be contributed in full within 90 days of the Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance; capital not contributed bears interest for the account of the defaulting member, and the company must adjust its charter capital or take remedial measures if contributions are not completed.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the office must satisfy the fire-prevention and rescue requirements under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP, including portable extinguishers, emergency lighting, exit signage, smoke detectors, and an evacuation plan; if the building requires a Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate, a copy from the landlord or provincial Fire Prevention and Rescue Police must be kept on site. Structural load: no separate inspection is issued for ordinary office occupancy; the landlord's construction completion acceptance and building safety certificate must be maintained. Workplace clearance: comply with the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13, register employee labor details with DOLISA, and maintain occupational risk assessments and first-aid arrangements. Civil defense: comply with the Law on Civil Defense No. 71/2023/QH15 and maintain an emergency response plan appropriate to an office workplace.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum charter capital is prescribed for a limited liability company engaged in ISIC 7490 professional consulting. Charter capital is freely agreed by the members and stated in VND in the charter, Investment Registration Certificate, and Enterprise Registration Certificate. There is no escrow account requirement for this non-conditional activity. Under Article 47 of the Law on Enterprises 2020, charter capital must be contributed in full within 90 days of the Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance; capital not contributed bears interest for the account of the defaulting member, and the company must adjust its charter capital or take remedial measures if contributions are not completed.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Vietnam; eTax portal at https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn and https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-invoice subsystem: hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested Limited Liability Company must first obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment or the Export Processing Zone / Industrial Park Authority, and thereafter an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC). Upon ERC issuance, the tax authority automatically issues a 13-digit Tax Identification Number (MST/TIN). The LLC must then activate its eTax account and complete VAT registration via the national eTax portal (Form 02/GTGT), file an initial tax statement, register the e-invoice issuer profile, and, where cross-border services are provided to Vietnamese clients, register for Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) under either the Direct Method or the Deduction Method.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - a foreign-invested LLC is a Vietnam tax resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax (CIT) on its worldwide income at the standard rate of 20%; no pass-through is permitted; non-resident foreign corporations with a Vietnam permanent establishment are instead subject to FCT on Vietnam-source income. — A foreign-invested Limited Liability Company must first obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment or the Export Processing Zone / Industrial Park Authority, and thereafter an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC). Upon ERC issuance, the tax authority automatically issues a 13-digit Tax Identification Number (MST/TIN). The LLC must then activate its eTax account and complete VAT registration via the national eTax portal (Form 02/GTGT), file an initial tax statement, register the e-invoice issuer profile, and, where cross-border services are provided to Vietnamese clients, register for Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) under either the Direct Method or the Deduction Method.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - a foreign-invested LLC is a Vietnam tax resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax (CIT) on its worldwide income at the standard rate of 20%; no pass-through is permitted; non-resident foreign corporations with a Vietnam permanent establishment are instead subject to FCT on Vietnam-source income.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists. Foreign contractors may elect between the Direct Method (VAT and CIT assessed on gross revenue at deemed rates) and the Deduction Method (full bookkeeping, input VAT credit, and CIT on net income) for FCT purposes. Small and medium enterprises may elect simplified presumptive taxation under Circular 40/2021/TT-BTC. Tax incentive zone/industry elections require prior approval under Decree 218/2013/ND-CP; interest deduction and transfer pricing elections follow OECD BEPS-aligned rules rather than elective regimes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 1,000,000,000 (one billion Vietnamese Dong) of annual taxable turnover for mandatory VAT registration; however, foreign contractors with any Vietnam-sourced taxable supply must register and file FCT on a per-transaction basis irrespective of threshold, and non-resident e-service suppliers must register through the Ministry of Finance portal under Decree 85/2021/ND-CP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of services, including MedTech FDA 510(k) and European CE Mark regulatory consulting, qualifies for the 0% VAT rate under Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC Article 9 where: (a) the service is provided to a foreign organization or individual having no Vietnam establishment; (b) the service is consumed entirely outside Vietnam (the consulting output, deliverables, and decision-making are used abroad); (c) payment is received from abroad in convertible foreign currency into a licensed foreign currency account maintained with an authorized Vietnamese commercial bank; and (d) the service is not listed in the excluded categories (e.g., telecommunications, insurance, domestic financial services). Adequate documentary evidence must be retained to support zero-rating.

  • Executed export service contract for FDA 510(k) / CE Mark regulatory consulting explicitly stating service delivery, performance, and consumption located outside Vietnam
  • Electronic VAT invoice (with tax authority code) issued to the foreign client
  • Foreign Currency Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the authorized local commercial bank evidencing receipt of the export service fee
  • Foreign exchange transaction record and bank statement from the collecting bank confirming credit to the LLC's foreign currency account
  • Foreign client's signed Service Utilization / Acceptance Certificate confirming the service was consumed abroad
  • Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) and Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) of the exporting Vietnam LLC

Mandatory electronic invoicing for all enterprises since 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. All e-invoices must be transmitted in real-time to the General Department of Taxation's eInvoice system. Two regimes exist: (i) e-invoices with tax authority codes (có mã), which require online clearance and a 12-digit cryptographic code before issuance; and (ii) e-invoices without tax authority codes (không mã), which require strict pre-registration of the issuer's information technology system and batch data transmission to the GDT. API interoperability with the GDT's VietTIS system is mandatory for licensed e-invoice issuers; no physical stamping is required.

The General Department of Taxation conducts targeted audits on service exporters claiming 0% VAT and input VAT refunds, requiring full production of service contracts, foreign-currency bank receipts, and offshore-client substantiation. Under the Law on Tax Administration 38/2019/QH14 (Articles 16-17) and Decree 125/2020/ND-CP, fraudulent zero-rating or refund claims trigger: (i) administrative fines of 1 to 3 times the evaded or over-refunded VAT amount; (ii) retrospective clawback of all input VAT refunded plus default interest at 0.03% per day; (iii) suspension of e-invoice issuance status and zero-rate export eligibility for up to 6 months; and (iv) criminal prosecution under the Penal Code (100/2015/QH13, Article 200) for tax evasion, punishable by up to 7 years' imprisonment where organized or involving amounts exceeding VND 100 billion. The statute of limitations for retrospective tax adjustment is 5 years, extended to 10 years for fraud.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for a corporate foreign shareholder under Vietnamese domestic CIT law; Vietnam does not impose withholding tax on dividends or profit remittances by a resident LLC to a non-resident corporate investor. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, a Vietnamese LLC is not exempt from transfer pricing documentation unless its aggregate related-party transaction value for the year is below VND 30 billion and its total annual revenue is below VND 50 billion. Consequently, a Local File and Master File are mandatory in any year when related-party transactions reach VND 30 billion or group revenue exceeds the simplified safe-harbor test. A Country-by-Country Report is additionally required when consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent reaches VND 18,000 billion (approximately EUR 750 million).

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not maintain a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations registration regime; however, Vietnamese tax law (Law on Tax Administration 2019 and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP) incorporates substance-over-form and general anti-abuse doctrines. To obtain deductible treatment for cross-border FDA/CE regulatory consulting fees, the LLC must prove the foreign service provider has actual personnel, track record, project documents, communication records, and that the fee is at arm's length and supports the LLC's revenue-generating activity. A transaction lacking economic substance is grounds for recharacterization, expense disallowance, and transfer-pricing adjustment.
