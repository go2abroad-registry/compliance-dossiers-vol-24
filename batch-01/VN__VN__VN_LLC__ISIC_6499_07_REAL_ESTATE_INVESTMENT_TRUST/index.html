Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (GDT, Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Electronic tax administration portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-filing, e-payment, e-refund); national e-invoice clearance portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; national enterprise registration and TIN issuance portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Obtain a real estate/REIT statutory license as required by the Law on Real Estate Business 29/2023/QH15 (and securities licensing if publicly offered) and secure the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) under the Law on Investment 61/2020/QH14 for the foreign-invested LLC. Step 2: Submit the enterprise registration application online at https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn; the DPI issues the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) within 3 working days, and the 13-digit Enterprise Tax Identification Number (MST) is automatically generated and printed on the ERC. Step 3: Tax registration becomes effective automatically with the ERC; the taxpayer then activates the e-tax account on https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn, obtains a digital signature certificate (eCA token), and submits the VAT-method election and tax registration confirmation dossier (Form 04/ĐK-TCT) within 10 working days. VAT registration is integrated with the ERC; no separate VAT registration exercise exists. Step 4: A foreign entity not establishing a Vietnamese entity but earning Vietnamese-source income (e.g., a foreign asset manager or offshore REIT promoter) must apply separately for a Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) TIN at the competent GDT sub-department within 5 working days (Form 04/ĐK-TCT), and if operating through a branch/permanent establishment, obtain a local tax file under Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC. Step 5: Open a foreign-currency account at a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank for export/leasing proceeds, and register the e-invoice solution and digital signing mechanism with the GDT before the first invoice is issued.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Foreign-Invested LLC is a resident tax enterprise under Article 2 of the Law on Corporate Income Tax (CIT) No. 14/2008/QH12, as amended (Law 32/2013/QH13 and Law 71/2014/QH13) and Decree 218/2013/ND-CP, and is taxed at the entity level at the flat 20% CIT rate on worldwide income. No pass-through or REIT-specific tax transparency is recognized under Vietnamese law; profit distributions are made from after-tax profits and dividends remitted abroad by a foreign corporate investor are not subject to Vietnamese dividend withholding tax (domestic law imposes no dividend WHT). Territorial scope: Vietnam operates a worldwide CIT system with a unilateral foreign tax credit for foreign-source income under Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC and available double-tax-treaty relief. Tax-exempt-zone treatment is limited to licensed export-processing enterprises (EPEs) and designated high-tech/economic zones and is inapplicable to a domestic real-estate portfolio REIT. No sub-national income taxes exist; the tax system is unitary at the national level.

Available election forms

Vietnam does not provide U.S.-style check-the-box or entity-classification elections. Available statutory elections and regimes for the VN LLC REIT include: (i) VAT method election — the LLC may elect the input-credit (deduction) method versus the direct method (3% or 5% deemed turnover tax) under the Law on VAT, subject to GDT registration; a foreign-invested LLC generally must adopt the credit method upon registration; (ii) Foreign Contractor Tax election — a non-resident service provider or property manager may elect the 'deduction method' (net assessment on branch accounts approved by the GDT) instead of the deemed gross-receipts 'direct method' withholding regime; (iii) CIT incentive registration election — preferential CIT rates (e.g., 10% for 15 years and additional exemptions) are available only for projects in qualifying sectors (high-tech, socialized infrastructure, large-scale industrial parks), which a REIT's passive leasing and financial income ordinarily does not satisfy; (iv) Foreign tax credit election — unilateral credit for foreign taxes paid on foreign-source income, capped at the Vietnamese CIT rate, is available in lieu of treaty credit; (v) Loss-carryforward election — confirmed right to carry tax losses forward for 5 consecutive years (no carryback) under Article 9 of Decree 218/2013/ND-CP; (vi) Interest limitation and transfer-pricing rules — the 30% EBITDA interest-cap and the transfer-pricing documentation regime under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP apply, and Vietnam has not adopted BEPS Action 2 hybrid-mismatch rules, so no hybrid-mismatch election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold for incorporated taxpayers: each enterprise with an Enterprise Registration Certificate is automatically subject to VAT registration and periodic declaration, regardless of revenue or leasing turnover; failure to file a VAT registration dossier is separately sanctionable. For household and individual business taxpayers not incorporated as enterprises, annual revenue of VND 100,000,000 or less is exempt from VAT registration; above that threshold registration is mandatory. Foreign contractors performing in-Vietnam property-management or related services are subject to FCT registration and withholding with no de minimis threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT treatment is strictly limited under Article 9 of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC and Article 10 of Law 48/2024/QH15 to: (i) goods physically exported from Vietnam, including goods sold to export-processing enterprises (EPEs), nontariff zones, and bonded warehouses; and (ii) export services supplied to foreign organizations or individuals that are physically performed and consumed wholly outside Vietnam. A REIT's domestic rental income is never zero-rated, even if the tenant is a foreign entity, because the real property is situated in Vietnam and the supply is consumed in Vietnam; such income is subject to VAT at 10%. Dividend, interest, and other portfolio financial income of the REIT is outside the VAT scope (exempt from VAT) rather than zero-rated. The LLC-REIT may validly claim 0% VAT only for genuinely offshore services, such as property-management, brokerage, or advisory services rendered to a foreign fund for assets located abroad, where the services are performed abroad, consumed abroad, invoiced to a non-resident, and settled in foreign currency, supported by the full documentary evidence set below.

  • Customs electronic export declaration (VNACCS/VCIS) bearing a lawful 'cleared' customs status, or for EPE/deemed-export supplies, the EPE-issued certificate and customs manifest
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or equivalent international consignment documentation evidencing physical shipment from a Vietnamese port/airport to a destination outside Vietnam
  • Commercial invoice and the underlying export sales contract (or offshore service agreement) specifying Incoterms, applicable prices, and payment terms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank, or the foreign-currency account statement reflecting actual receipt of the foreign-currency proceeds from the overseas buyer into Vietnam within the applicable repatriation window
  • Packing list, customs weight certificate, and, where applicable, certificate of origin or processing certificate for goods exported from an EPE or bonded processing facility
  • For offshore services: the foreign customer's business registration or tax-identification certificate, a service-delivery/work-completion acceptance certificate, and independent documentation proving that the services were wholly performed and consumed outside Vietnam
  • The statutory state-budget refund request form (Form 01/ĐNHT under Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC) together with the corresponding e-invoice XML records transmitted to the GDT portal and the VAT refund computation worksheet

Mandatory e-invoicing applies to all enterprises since 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP. Each e-invoice must be transmitted in XML format to the GDT's national e-invoice portal (https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) within 24 hours of issuance. Newly established enterprises and high-risk taxpayers — a class that includes large B2B real-estate lessors and taxpayers with a history of VAT refund claims — must use invoices carrying a GDT-issued authorization code (mã của cơ quan thuế) obtained in real time per invoice. After 12 months of compliant filing, the taxpayer may elect to use self-issued invoice codes without pre-authorization but must continue to transmit invoice-issuance data in near-real time. The GDT mandates API (REST/Web-service) integration with the central e-invoice database for large-scale taxpayers, implemented for enterprises with annual revenue above approximately VND 300 billion and for all taxpayers seeking export-related VAT refunds; invoice data is automatically cross-checked against customs declarations (VNACCS/VCIS), bank payment reports, and the enterprise's tax returns. Failure to transmit or tampering with transmitted invoice data triggers automatic suspension of invoice issuance privileges.

Wrongfully claimed or fraudulent export zero-rating triggers: (i) retrospective reassessment for 5 years (extended to 10 years where fraud/evasion is established), re-collecting 10% output VAT on the affected consideration plus late-payment interest at 0.03% per day (approximately 10.95% per annum); (ii) administrative penalties under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP — a penalty equal to 20% of the under-collected tax for ordinary errors, rising to 1x, 2x, or 3x the under-declared or over-refunded amounts for intentional evasion, together with suspension of e-invoice self-issuance privileges for 12–24 months and forfeiture of expedited VAT-refund eligibility for repeat offenders; (iii) criminal liability under Articles 200 and 202 of the Penal Code 2015 (amended 2017) where the evaded tax or fraudulently obtained refund is VND 100 million or more — commercial legal entities may be fined up to VND 20 billion and suspended from operations for up to 3 years, and responsible directors, CFOs, or chief accountants face imprisonment (statutory scale extending to 10 years in aggravated organizational cases); (iv) clawback of any cash VAT refund already disbursed, with collection enforcement including bank-account freezes, asset seizure, and recovery of receivables under the Law on Tax Administration 38/2019/QH14; and (v) independent foreign-exchange-control penalties for failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days — administrative fines up to VND 500 million for legal entities — which the GDT treats as a red flag that the underlying export and its 0% VAT claim are not commercially genuine.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam maintains current-account convertibility for registered trade, service, and investment-income transactions, while capital-account transactions remain selectively controlled and formalised through registration and purpose-code reporting. A foreign-invested LLC exercising REIT/trust activities is fully convertible on distributions once statutory tax filings are complete: profit remittance is permitted once per fiscal year, after CIT finalisation, submission of audited financial statements, payment of residual tax liabilities, and issuance of the tax authority's confirmation of profit remittance eligibility. The principal administrative hurdle is the mandatory ring-fencing of all inbound direct investment capital through a registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA); after funding, conversion of VND into USD/EUR for profit remittance, capital reduction, and liquidation proceeds is effectuated by an authorised bank without separate central-bank approval, provided the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) remains valid and the enterprise has filed all required balance-of-payments reports with the SBV.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN, all export proceeds arising from current transactions must be repatriated into a foreign-currency payment account or DICA held with an SBV-licensed bank within 180 days from the date of completion of the export customs declaration. There is no ordinary-case mandatory conversion ratio into VND; however, the SBV may prescribe conversion obligations where expressly provided by statute, and any deferred repatriation requires prior written approval of the SBV. The enterprise must keep a reconciliation file matching each SWIFT credit to the applicable customs declaration, export contract, and commercial invoice, and must notify the bank of the purpose code 'export proceeds from sale of goods'.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV; Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam), the sole central bank and principal foreign-exchange administration authority, acting through the Foreign Exchange Management Department and provincial branches. Core legal instruments include the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 06/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, as amended by Ordinance No. 15/2013/UBTVQH13; Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN (repatriation of export and current-transaction proceeds); Circular No. 09/2018/TT-NHNN (Direct Investment Capital Account – DICA); Circular No. 32/2015/TT-NHNN (foreign-exchange transactions between credit institutions and customers); and Circular No. 02/2021/TT-NHNN (external borrowing and repayment).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound FDI equity and shareholder-loan capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 directly into the enterprise's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained with a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank, quoting in the wire narrative the full enterprise legal name, the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) number, the ERC number, and the standardized balance-of-payments purpose code 'Direct investment – capital contribution to enterprise'. Remittances should be sent with charges marked OUR to ensure full value is credited to the DICA. The bank then matches each credit to the registered capital schedule under Circular No. 09/2018/TT-NHNN and reports the transaction to the SBV within the statutory window; no drawdown is permitted until the incoming credit is validated against the IRC and the enterprise's capital-contribution schedule.Operational outflows (construction costs, property-management fees, import of equipment and services) settle via SWIFT MT103 from the entity's VND or foreign-currency operating accounts, with each payment supported by an underlying invoice, customs import declaration where applicable, and the relevant BOP purpose code. For large purchases of land and building assets, a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) is used for construction, equipment, and completed-project disbursement. Distribution and profit-remittance wires are executed as SWIFT MT103 using purpose code 'remittance of profits to foreign investors', preceded by issuance of the tax-authority-approved profit-remittance notification and the bank's FX dealing confirmation; the corresponding debit to the DICA must be reported to the SBV.FX Forwards executed with an SBV-licensed credit institution, tenor capped at 365 days and strictly linked to underlying trade, service, or investment cashflows under Circular No. 32/2015/TT-NHNNFX Swaps between VND and USD/EUR for managing cashflow timing mismatches on rental income and distribution datesCross-Currency Interest Rate Swaps (CCIRS) between the REIT and an authorised bank to hedge long-dated interest-rate and currency exposure on property-financing facilitiesSpot FX transactions for immediate settlement of operating payments and ongoing dividend/conversion cyclesNDFs (Non-Deliverable Forwards) are not permitted onshore without an express SBV licence; offshore NDF access by the foreign parent is not enforceable as an onshore hedge and may not be documented as part of the Vietnamese REIT's treasury plan
