Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (MFEM), Port Vila; corporate and licensing registry: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) via VFSC-licensed registered agents; customs single window: ASYCUDA World (VRC Customs Division).

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] through a VFSC-licensed registered agent; Certificate of Incorporation is issued within approximately 24 hours. 2) The incorporation filing automatically triggers allocation of a Vanuatu Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by VRC; no in-person interview or minimum capital is required. 3) Apply to VRC Customs Division for a Customs Business Registration (CBR) certificate to obtain an exporter code. 4) Obtain an annual Business Licence under the Business Licence Act. There is no tax file interview, no economic substance filing, and no direct tax registration obligation for an IC; the TIN and CBR are used for customs, stamp duty, and statutory returns.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: An International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] is statutorily exempt from all present and future Vanuatu taxes, including income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, VAT/sales tax, and stamp duty on IC instruments, provided it does not carry on business with Vanuatu residents and does not own Vanuatu land. The entity is a separate limited-liability juridical person; although structurally an entity-level taxpayer, its statutory exemption results in an effective 0% direct tax burden. — 1) Incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] through a VFSC-licensed registered agent; Certificate of Incorporation is issued within approximately 24 hours. 2) The incorporation filing automatically triggers allocation of a Vanuatu Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by VRC; no in-person interview or minimum capital is required. 3) Apply to VRC Customs Division for a Customs Business Registration (CBR) certificate to obtain an exporter code. 4) Obtain an annual Business Licence under the Business Licence Act. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: An International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] is statutorily exempt from all present and future Vanuatu taxes, including income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, VAT/sales tax, and stamp duty on IC instruments, provided it does not carry on business with Vanuatu residents and does not own Vanuatu land. The entity is a separate limited-liability juridical person; although structurally an entity-level taxpayer, its statutory exemption results in an effective 0% direct tax burden.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist because Vanuatu has no income tax statute. Available statutory elections under the IC Act [CAP 222] include: (i) election to redomicile/continue a foreign company into Vanuatu as an IC; (ii) election to convert an IC into an ordinary domestic company and vice versa with VFSC approval; (iii) election to issue par-value or no-par shares and maintain multi-currency share capital; and (iv) elective appointment of directors, shareholders, and registered agent. No passthrough election exists, as the IC regime simply imposes zero direct taxation at entity level.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in force (VAT/GST repealed 1 August 1998). Residual indirect taxes: customs import and export duties under the Customs Act [CAP 232], stamp duty under the Stamp Duties Act [CAP 74], municipal and tourism levies. There is no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax regime applicable to air cargo charter brokerage.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST exists, so no turnover threshold can trigger VAT registration. For customs indirect tax purposes, every exporter/importer must hold a Customs Business Registration (CBR) regardless of turnover; there is no de minimis threshold. Air charter brokerage service fees are outside the scope of any Vanuatu indirect tax, so no taxable-turnover threshold is triggered.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Statutory export zero-rating is not applicable because Vanuatu does not impose VAT/GST on exports; air cargo charter brokerage services rendered to offshore clients are simply outside any Vanuatu indirect tax charge. No export duty is payable on services. For physical cargo or aircraft spares exported from Vanuatu, most goods qualify for duty-free export treatment; the exporter claims such treatment by lodging the Export Declaration through ASYCUDA World and obtaining customs endorsement. There is no VAT refund mechanism because no output tax is ever charged.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Export Entry lodged electronically via ASYCUDA World and endorsed by Vanuatu Customs
  • Air Waybill (Master and House AWB) or Bill of Lading evidencing outbound movement
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List with full description and HS classification
  • Certificate of Origin where preferential or destination-country origin treatment is claimed
  • Charter Agreement or Brokerage Contract evidencing the terms of the air cargo charter transaction

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government API clearance mandate is in force. Export and departure formalities are processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs management system: electronic Export Declaration, cargo manifest, and aircraft departure clearance are lodged via the VRC single electronic window with risk-based and intelligence-led inspection. The broker must retain the customs-endorsed export entry and original supporting documents for audit.

No VAT zero-rating claim or refund mechanism exists, so there is no direct VAT clawback exposure. Scrutiny focuses on customs and licensing compliance: (1) false or misleading customs declarations attract penalties under the Customs Act [CAP 232] of up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 10 years, plus forfeiture of goods; (2) an IC that transacts with Vanuatu residents, engages in prohibited business, or acquires Vanuatu land breaches the International Companies Act [CAP 222], exposing the company to strike-off and retrospective loss of its statutory tax exemption; (3) unstamped agreements are inadmissible as evidence and attract penalty interest under the Stamp Duties Act [CAP 74]; (4) failure to renew the CBR or Business Licence results in suspension of export privileges and administrative fines; and (5) breaches of the VFSC anti-money laundering regime applicable to brokers can trigger additional regulatory penalties. No statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline applies because Vanuatu has no exchange control legislation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is a partially convertible currency. Current-account payments incident to the air-cargo charter brokerage (charter hire, fuel, ground handling, dispatch fees, broker commissions) are liberalized under the RBV standing General Permission and may be settled through licensed banks against documentary evidence (charter contracts, invoices, air waybills, IATA settlement statements). Capital-account transactions - non-resident share subscriptions into a Vanuatu International Company - are permitted in VUV or foreign currency, but borrowing in VUV by external enterprises and on-lending to Vanuatu residents require RBV specific approval. Profit remittances are not subject to statutory withholding, exchange surcharge, or mandatory conversion; the practical hurdle is the FATF increased-monitoring (grey-list) regime, which triggers enhanced due diligence, beneficial-ownership verification, and robust source-of-funds documentation for every outward credit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, no statutory conversion ratio, and no forced repatriation timeline. A VU International Company acting as the invoicing and clearing principal for air-cargo charter service exports may retain 100% of foreign-currency earnings in an RBV-licensed foreign-currency account. Conversion into VUV for local operating expenses occurs on the interbank market at the prevailing rate. All export-service receipts must be supported by underlying files (signed charter party/ACMI agreements, commercial invoices, master air waybills or house air waybills, and CASS settlement records) for the bank's documentary archive and for periodic balance-of-payments/statistical reporting to the RBV.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - monetary authority and exchange-control administrator under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125] and the Exchange Control Act [Cap 103]; AML/CFT supervision is shared with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2002.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as the primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as an ancillary collection/disbursement channel capped at 5% of monthly flows. Vanuatu has no PSD2-style e-money directive, no cross-border EMI passporting regime, and offshore digital EMIs cannot carry on business in or from Vanuatu without a VFSC financial-dealer licence under the Financial Dealers Licensing Act [Cap 248]; such licences are operationally difficult to obtain for speculative fintech issuers. In addition, an International Company licensed under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is prohibited from offering financial-dealer services to Vanuatu residents. The IC should therefore use only an FCA- or EU-authorised EMI with segregated client funds, a direct SWIFT gateway, and an approved-name client relationship, while directing at least 95% of inbound and outbound value through RBV-licensed banks or top-50 global correspondent banks.

Supporting pack to be delivered pre-wire: board resolution authorising the allotment, signed share certificates, updated beneficial-ownership register entry in the VFSC portal (filed within 14 days), and source-of-funds evidence. For amounts above the bank's in-house threshold (commonly USD 250,000 or USD 1,000,000), pre-notify the compliance officer with an MT199 bank reference and PDF copy of the capital certificate so the credit is cleared before release. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax; dividends distributed by an IC to non-resident shareholders are free of all Vanuatu tax and there are no exchange control restrictions on repatriating USD dividends by Swift or Fedwire.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The renewal and prescribed annual government fee come due on the anniversary of incorporation (or at incorporation for the first year); the statutory calendar is unrelated to any corporate fiscal year-end because the IC is outside the income tax filing universe.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - Vanuatu has no transfer pricing statute, no arm's length codification, and no Local File or Master File monetary thresholds in vatu (VUV) applicable to ICs. The only documentation discipline is the economic substance record-keeping obligation: readily available books, contracts, bank statements, and operational evidence must be produced to the VFSC or Tax Information Authority on request.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Vanuatu's 2021 economic substance reforms to the International Companies Act and the accompanying Economic Substance Regulations require ICs engaged in a relevant activity to submit an annual Economic Substance Declaration through the VFSC. Relevant activities include banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, intellectual property, and distribution and service centres. An air cargo charter broker would be in scope if it provides services to foreign connected enterprises (distribution and service centre) or acts as the group's headquarters; a purely third-party brokering vehicle is likely outside the core categories but must nevertheless file the declaration to confirm non-applicability. In-scope ICs must show a physical office in Vanuatu, adequate local employees, proportionate local operating expenditure, and management and control exercised in Vanuatu; failure results in financial penalties, deregistration, and automatic information exchange to foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Interposition

Statutory sanction: The foreign jurisdiction may recharacterize income, deny treaty benefits, apply CFC or GAAR rules, and sanction the IC under anti-avoidance legislation; Vanuatu authorities may strike off the IC for false declarations and report the matter through exchange-of-information channels.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face civil liability for restitution of unpaid taxes, restriction orders, travel bans during investigation, and imprisonment where conduct amounts to fraudulent trading or perjury.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Escalating financial penalties per relevant activity, daily default fines, deregistration from the VFSC register, and exchange of filed substance information with treaty partners under the applicable TIEA.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly liable for unpaid penalties; in severe cases the VFSC may obtain a court disqualification order, an asset freeze, and a travel ban pending restoration or liquidation.
