Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Business Registration Office (BRO) of the Provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) under the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI), operating through the National Enterprise Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) and the one-stop division of the Provincial Administrative Center (Trung tâm Phục vụ Hành chính công).

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14, Articles 22-35 and 166-197 (limited liability company formation, charter capital contribution, Members' Council, and legal representation); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, Articles 22-38 (investment registration and conditional business lines); Government Decree No. 01/2021/ND-CP on Enterprise Registration, Articles 21-34 and 43-48; Government Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP detailing the Law on Investment, Articles 36-49; Government Decree No. 46/2017/ND-CP as amended on education cooperation, Articles 96-102 (study-abroad consultancy conditions).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Enterprise Registration (Giấy đề nghị đăng ký doanh nghiệp) filed together with the Investment Registration Certificate dossier (Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư); the ERC and IRC collectively form the statutory incorporation instruments.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 37 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a service LLC under Vietnamese law; charter capital is freely agreed by the members and recorded in the Charter. The foreign investor must nevertheless demonstrate sufficient financial capacity at the IRC application stage. Charter capital must be fully contributed to a bank account in Viet Nam within 90 days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Decree 31/2021/ND-CP, Article 47). No escrow account is mandatory for incorporation, but the capital contribution account is required, and defaulting members forfeit rights to the un-paid portion.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety: the office premises must obtain fire prevention and fighting (PCCC) settlement, namely a written certification of fire safety eligibility (Giấy chứng nhận đủ điều kiện an toàn về PCCC) or a written acceptance of fire safety requirements issued by the provincial Fire Prevention and Fighting Police under the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting No. 27/2001/QH10 (as amended) and Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP, including fire doors, extinguishers, emergency lighting, and evacuation plans. Structural clearance: occupancy of the rented office is evidenced by the building owner's construction completion certificate and compliance with local building-code requirements for offices and administrative premises. Occupational clearance: the workplace must comply with the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13, including workplace risk assessment, annual employee health checks, first-aid provisions, fire drills, and mandatory personal protective equipment rules; a copy of the safety-eligibility certificate and occupational health records is kept at the registered office for inspection by the Department of Labor, Invalids and Social Affairs.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a service LLC under Vietnamese law; charter capital is freely agreed by the members and recorded in the Charter. The foreign investor must nevertheless demonstrate sufficient financial capacity at the IRC application stage. Charter capital must be fully contributed to a bank account in Viet Nam within 90 days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Decree 31/2021/ND-CP, Article 47). No escrow account is mandatory for incorporation, but the capital contribution account is required, and defaulting members forfeit rights to the un-paid portion.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam — Central portal: www.gdt.gov.vn; e-filing portal: thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice portal: hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; National Public Service Portal: dichvucong.gov.vn; business registration portal: dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested LLC is automatically assigned its Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST) upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) by the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI). The MST is identical to the 10-digit Enterprise Code and is generated ex officio via the National Business Registration Portal. Post-incorporation compliance steps: (i) upload the electronic tax registration dossier through thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn within 10 working days of ERC issuance; (ii) obtain a Class-3 digital signature certificate (chữ ký số) from an approved certification authority (e.g., Viettel-CA, VNPT-CA, FPT-CA) for e-filing and e-invoice transmission; (iii) notify the tax office of the corporate seal specimen and legal representative; (iv) register the opening of a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at an authorized commercial bank for inbound equity and foreign-currency receipt; and (v) register for Social Insurance, Health Insurance, and Unemployment Insurance with the provincial Social Insurance Agency using the same MST. No separate registration with the GDT is required to obtain the TIN; the MST is the single taxpayer identifier for all corporate taxes, customs, e-invoices, and social insurance contributions.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not provide a check-the-box or US-style pass-through regime; an LLC is conclusively a separate CIT and VAT taxpayer. Statutory elections available to the LLC include: (i) election to apply the deemed input-VAT credit method under Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC (5% of output VAT for goods and 3% for services at the 10% VAT rate) where actual input credit tracing is impractical; (ii) election to file consolidated monthly VAT and provisional CIT declarations for dependent branches located within the same provincial taxing district; (iii) election of quarterly provisional CIT instalments at not less than 80% of the estimated annual liability, with a finalisation return by the 90th day after fiscal year-end; (iv) election to enter a unilateral, bilateral, or multilateral Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) for transfer-pricing certainty; (v) election to claim an investment incentive exemption/reduction of CIT for projects located in eligible industrial parks or under new investment law thresholds; and (vi) election by a foreign contractor supplying services to the LLC to apply the Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) mixed or turnover-based withholding method, which interacts with the LLC's obligation to withhold at source.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold applies to incorporated LLCs — every LLC is mandatorily VAT-registered upon ERC issuance irrespective of revenue. Unincorporated household businesses and individual entrepreneurs are exempt from VAT registration and output tax collection if annual turnover from VAT-liable goods/services does not exceed VND 100,000,000 (approximately USD 4,100). For the ISIC 7810 study-abroad/immigration-consulting scope, the LLC must register for the standard VAT method (khấu trừ) rather than the deemed method, given its corporate status and anticipated input invoices.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% VAT rate applies to services exported to foreign organisations or individuals that are consumed entirely outside Viet Nam, pursuant to Article 9(2) of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC and Circular 26/2015/TT-BTC. For ISIC 7810 study-abroad placement and immigration/visa consulting, zero-rating is available only when all cumulative conditions are satisfied: (i) the contracting counterparty is a person resident outside Viet Nam — e.g., a foreign university, overseas foundation, or non-resident applicant; (ii) the service is contractually rendered, performed, and delivered abroad; (iii) the end-beneficiary and actual consumption occur wholly offshore; (iv) payment is received in freely convertible foreign currency from abroad; and (v) the service is not one of the statutorily excluded categories (e.g., reinsurance, transportation, technology export restrictions). Critically, advisory services provided to Vietnamese-resident students or parents — even if ultimately facilitating overseas study or visa acquisition — are domestic standard-rated supplies at 10% VAT, not zero-rated. Mixed contracts must segregate domestic and export elements; a single contract containing both domestic and offshore deliverables is generally standard-rated in its entirety unless separately invoiced and substantiated.

  • Executed service agreement/contract with the foreign counterparty expressly identifying the cross-border nature of the supply, place of performance, and overseas consumption
  • E-invoice issued under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP bearing the 0% VAT rate and GDT invoice code
  • Foreign-currency inward remittance certificate or bank credit advice evidencing crediting of export service consideration to the LLC's DICA or a permitted foreign-currency account at an authorised bank
  • Notarised/legalised certificate or formal declaration of non-resident tax status of the foreign service recipient (e.g., foreign tax ID registration, certificate of incorporation, or overseas address proof)
  • Service delivery report and work-product documentation (e.g., submitted visa dossiers, university admission applications lodged abroad) demonstrating performance and consumption outside Viet Nam
  • Internal transfer-pricing call memo confirming the contractual terms align with arm's length substance

All enterprises must issue fully electronic invoices under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. The LLC must apply to the GDT for an e-invoice issuing code (kỳ hóa đơn) and transmit each invoice to the GDT e-invoice portal (hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) in real time at issuance. Two types of e-invoices exist: (a) invoices bearing the GDT computer-generated tax-authority code (hóa đơn có mã của cơ quan thuế), which require pre-issuance data transmission and digital stamping approval; and (b) invoices without tax-authority codes, used mainly by in-house circulation and suitable only for enterprises holding the GDT's permission. In practice, service-consulting LLCs use coded e-invoices. A qualified digital signature (chữ ký số) from an authorised CA is mandatory on each invoice. E-invoice data must be preserved for 10 years; non-compliant invoice issuance can result in suspension of invoice codes and severe administrative penalties.

The GDT and local tax departments conduct targeted horizontal audits of zero-rated service exporters claiming input-VAT refunds, with particular focus on professional-services enterprises. Adverse determinations trigger: (i) VAT reassessment at the 10% standard rate on the full invoice amount, plus late-payment interest of 0.03% per day calculated for the period from the original filing date (statutory assessment period: 10 years for incorrect declarations and 5 years for omission under Law No. 38/2019/QH14); (ii) administrative fines under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP — for under-declaration, 10%-20% of the understated tax; for fraudulent tax evasion, 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount (minimum sanction VND 5,000,000 for individuals, doubled for organisations); (iii) clawback of any previously refunded input VAT, together with interest, and denial of refund eligibility for a 12-24-month bar period if the refund-regime adverse-conclusion criteria are met; (iv) criminal prosecution under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 (as amended 2017) where the evaded amount is VND 100,000,000 or more, with fines up to VND 4,500,000,000 or imprisonment of up to 7 years for organized, sophisticated, or repeated schemes; (v) civil and administrative sanctions by the State Bank of Viet Nam for violation of the 180-day export-earnings repatriation obligation under the Foreign Exchange Ordinance 06/2015/PL-UBTVQH13 and Circular 15/2016/TT-NHNN, including fines up to VND 200,000,000 and compulsory repatriation; and (vi) revocation of the enterprise's e-invoice code, and public blacklisting in the GDT's non-compliance notice where systematic zero-rating abuse is found.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese dong (VND) is not a freely convertible currency; the SBV operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with an official daily central rate. Current-account transactions are fully convertible in administrative practice: a foreign-invested enterprise may receive and pay cross-border service revenues and expenses (educational consultancy fees, international student placement commissions, tuition deposits, and application disbursements to foreign universities) through a licensed credit institution without prior SBV case-by-case approval, provided each remittance is supported by an underlying contract/invoice, carries a documented purpose code, and passes through an authorized bank account (Direct Investment Capital Account for capital flows, Foreign Currency Account for current-account service flows). The capital account remains restricted. Inward equity contributions, shareholder-loan drawdowns, and outward profit/loan remittances must be executed exclusively through the licensed-bank Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) monitored under the SBV balance-of-payments reporting system. Profit remittance hurdles for the LLC are: (1) completion of the annual corporate income tax finalization and settlement of all outstanding tax liabilities; (2) issuance of audited financial statements showing distributable post-tax profits and no accumulated losses; (3) a corporate resolution of the member approving the profit distribution; (4) registration of every DICA with the bank and declaration to the SBV; and (5) payment instruction via the DICA with conversion from VND to FX at the bank’s prevailing rate. No additional withholding tax is imposed on dividends or profit remittances to foreign shareholders (Vietnam abolished the profit-remittance tax; dividends are paid from after-tax profits). A decisive constraint for the golden-visa dimension of this business: outward remittances by a Vietnamese resident enterprise for the purpose of acquiring foreign real estate, residence-by-investment securities, or immigration-investment program participations are not recognized as permissible current-account payments under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange. Such outbound capital movement requires, in law, an Overseas Investment Registration Certificate issued under Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP, which in practice is not obtainable for residence-advisory products. Therefore the VN_LLC must not route, hold, or settle client funds destined for foreign investment migration products; its lawful treasury model is limited to invoicing advisory/placement fees, holding student tuition money under a segregated escrow/trust arrangement only where permitted by its statutory license, and disbursing such money strictly to accredited educational institutions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vietnam does not currently impose a mandatory percentage conversion quota on export receipts; the effective surrender ratio for exporters, including service exporters such as an education-consultancy LLC, is 0%, meaning 100% of foreign-currency earnings from cross-border services (foreign university placement commissions and offshore consulting fees) may be retained in a Foreign Currency Account (FCA) at a licensed Vietnamese bank. The statutory discipline operates through repatriation and declaration rather than conversion: (i) all proceeds earned from services supplied by a resident enterprise to non-resident counterparties must be brought onshore into the FCA or DICA within the payment terms of the underlying contract and in any event by the annual audit cut-off; banks apply an internal monitoring window of approximately 12 months before classifying an unrepatriated receivable as a foreign-exchange irregularity; (ii) each inflow must be matched to the corresponding signed service agreement, service-completion certificate, export VAT invoice (if applicable under the VAT law on exports of services), and student placement documentation; and (iii) retained FX may be applied exclusively for permitted current-account disbursements (tuition payments to foreign universities, application fees, bank charges), spot-sold to the licensed bank for VND operating liquidity, or used as the base for permissible onshore hedging transactions. No statutory VND conversion floor or ceiling applies; the enterprise is free to retain its FX for subsequent lawful payments without a forced sale timeline. Non-compliance exposes the LLC to administrative penalties under Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP (as amended) for unauthorized foreign-exchange activities, inaccurate balance-of-payments declarations, and offshore settlement outside licensed credit institutions.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) – Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam. The SBV is the exclusive central monetary authority and foreign-exchange administrator for the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam. Its regulatory command chain for this blueprint comprises the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended by Law No. 70/2014/QH13; Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP on foreign-exchange control; Circular No. 02/2021/TT-NHNN on foreign-exchange administration of foreign investment activities in Vietnam; Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN on foreign-exchange transactions between credit institutions and customers; Law on Anti-Money Laundering No. 14/2022/QH15 (effective 1 March 2023); Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP on implementation of the Law on Investment with respect to outbound investment; and Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP on administrative sanctions in the monetary and banking sector.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, the Vietnamese LLC is exempt from preparing a Local File when its annual revenue is below VND 50,000,000,000 AND its total value of related-party transactions is below VND 30,000,000,000 in the same tax period. A Local File becomes mandatory when either threshold is crossed. A Master File is required when the group’s ultimate parent company, wherever resident, has consolidated annual revenue exceeding VND 3,000,000,000,000. A Country-by-Country Report is required when the consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent exceeds VND 18,000,000,000,000 (EUR 750 million equivalent).

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, the tax administration applies a ‘substance over form’ doctrine through Article 5.2 of Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, disregarding related-party transactions where there is no economic substance, no commercial purpose, or the arrangement is artificial. The LLC must maintain real local personnel, physical office premises, a board of directors, an independent legal representative, and demonstrable control over the provision of visa and immigration consulting services. Payments out to shell affiliates are re-characterised, added back to CIT, and may trigger the anti-avoidance provisions of the Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Under-Declaration of Revenue

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty equal to 20% of the under-declared tax amount plus late payment interest at 0.03% per day; where the evaded amount equals or exceeds VND 100,000,000, criminal prosecution under Article 200 of the Penal Code: individual legal representatives face fines up to VND 4,500,000,000 or imprisonment from 6 months to 7 years; the LLC itself faces fines up to VND 3,000,000,000, suspension of operations, and confiscation of illegal proceeds.

Director personal liability: The General Director and legal representative are jointly and severally liable for unpaid CIT, civil restitution of the State’s loss, travel bans prohibiting exit from Vietnam, and criminal imprisonment for intentionally directing or knowingly acquiescing in the suppression of revenue.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Intra-Group Service Fees

Statutory sanction: Re-determination of taxable income by the General Department of Taxation at the arm’s length profit level; additional CIT assessed together with late payment interest at 0.03% per day and a 20% misdeclaration penalty; administrative fines up to VND 25,000,000 for failure to produce documentation; aggravated cases are referred to the criminal authorities for tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false transfer pricing reports or instruct artificial intercompany pricing are personally exposed to administrative penalties, civil restitution of the re-assessed tax, travel bans, and criminal prosecution where fraudulent intent is proved.
Shell Company, Sham Substance, and Illegal Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the enterprise’s tax code and business registration; disallowance of all expenses backed by counterfeit invoices; fines up to VND 500,000,000 for illegal invoicing; criminal liability under Article 204 of the Penal Code for issuing or using illegal invoices with imprisonment up to 20 years for organisers; the LLC may be subjected to revocation of the investment registration certificate and dissolution.

Director personal liability: The charter legal representative and Directors are personally liable to creditors and the tax authority, face civil restitution, prohibition from leaving Vietnam, confiscation of personal assets traceable to the sham scheme, and imprisonment for fraud, tax evasion, or money laundering.
AML Non-Compliance and Escrow/Wire Payment Misconduct

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines of up to VND 400,000,000 under the Law on Anti-Money Laundering 2022 (effective 1 December 2023); freezing of escrow and bank accounts; if criminal money laundering under Article 324 of the Penal Code is established, the organisation is fined up to VND 5,000,000,000, suspended from operations for up to 3 years, and managers are imprisoned from 1 to 15 years depending on scale.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer face personal fines, restrictions on holding management positions in Vietnam, exit prohibitions, confiscation of illicit assets, and criminal liability where they knowingly facilitated the laundering of client proceeds through escrow arrangements or wire transfers.
