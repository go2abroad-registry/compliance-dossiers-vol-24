Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue (VCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management – official portal: https://vcire.vu

E-government filing portal

For a Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC), no income-tax TIN applies because Vanuatu imposes no national income tax. The foreign-owned IC must apply directly to VCIR for VAT registration using the VFSC-issued certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, passport copies of directors and beneficial owners, and a local agent's letter of authorization. VCIR issues a VAT registration number upon completion of the standard application form and compliance review, normally within 10–15 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC), no income-tax TIN applies because Vanuatu imposes no national income tax. The foreign-owned IC must apply directly to VCIR for VAT registration using the VFSC-issued certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, passport copies of directors and beneficial owners, and a local agent's letter of authorization. VCIR issues a VAT registration number upon completion of the standard application form and compliance review, normally within 10–15 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No income tax law or check-the-box entity classification exists in Vanuatu. International Companies are statutorily exempt from national and municipal taxes. Under the Value Added Tax Act, an International Company may make a voluntary VAT registration election if it makes taxable supplies, but VCIR does not recognize any elective pass-through or hybrid-mismatch entity classification for tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover (Value Added Tax Act Cap. 186), with voluntary registration permitted below threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies where the International Company supplies goods that are physically exported from Vanuatu to a foreign buyer and the export is evidenced to VCIR's satisfaction. Services to non-residents are zero-rated when supplied and performed wholly outside Vanuatu or provided for use outside Vanuatu and the supply qualifies under the VAT Act as an exported service. Bakery products and central-kitchen goods produced in Vanuatu and sold to overseas franchise stores qualify only if legal ownership passes to a non-Resident purchaser and the goods are removed from Vanuatu within the statutory export period.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No nationwide real-time electronic fiscal clearance or mandatory tax-authority API stamping regime is currently in force. Taxpayers must issue VAT tax invoices (paper or electronic) containing the supplier's VAT registration number, invoice date, description of supply, amount payable, and VAT charged. Export tax invoices must be retained together with customs export evidence; electronic record keeping is permitted and the electronic customs declaration system is integrated with the Vanuatu Customs Service, but no real-time tax clearance at invoice level is required.

VCIR may audit export zero-rating claims retrospectively for a 7-year period. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating declarations attract a civil penalty of up to 150% of the VAT evaded, a fine not exceeding VUV 1,000,000, imprisonment not exceeding 2 years, and mandatory retrospective reversal of all credits and refunds with compound interest at the statutory rate from the date of claim.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalised exchange-control structure: the Vatu is convertible on both current and capital account for bona fide commercial, investment and remittance flows, and no prior RBV approval is required for IC dividend, royalty, franchise-fee or intercompany-loan outflows provided the remittance is effected through an RBV-licensed authorised dealer and is supported by the underlying commercial documentation. Profit remittance from the Vanuatu International Company is structurally unrestricted because the International Companies Act [Cap. 240] grants a full tax exemption from income tax, capital gains tax and withholding taxes; the residual hurdles are practical rather than statutory, namely (i) the shallow onshore VUV interbank market with wide spreads set by the large commercial banks, (ii) correspondent-bank due-diligence friction caused by the tax-exempt IC form, and (iii) the recurring requirement to evidence commercial substance connecting the Vanuatu master-franchisor to the actively operating Chinese bakery chain before the bank will permit regular cross-border royalty flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio on export receipts: 100% of export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in foreign-currency receivable or suspense accounts at a licensed authorised dealer without RBV conversion. The compliance burden is evidentiary: each export-credit entry must be matched with the underlying commercial contract, the Vanuatu customs entry, the bill of lading, and the bank's export/proceeds purpose classification, and the authorised dealer must reflect material transactions in its periodic Exchange Control Act returns to the RBV. For the pastry-chain treasury, intra-group master-royalty receipts must be supported by the executed franchise/master-licence agreement and a monthly royalty-backup schedule, while physical-goods export receipts from central-kitchen equipment sales require the standard shipping-document package; there are no statutory timelines, percentages or conversion windows beyond the bank's own reporting-cycle conventions.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — the statutory central bank with exclusive currency issuance authority, administration of the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 91] through licensed authorised dealers, and prudential supervision of all commercial banks and credit institutions; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately supervises the International Companies Office (ICO) which administers the International Companies Act [Cap. 240].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Vanuatu has no domestic e-money institution (EMI) licensing category and the RBV does not yet authorise non-bank payment service providers, so a locally regulated EMI cannot be used as the primary treasury vehicle; instead, the IC may engage a UK-FCA-authorised or EU-licensed EMI as a secondary collection/disbursement apron, but the legal reality is that most tier-1 EMIs apply restrictive country policies to tax-exempt Vanuatu International Companies and either decline onboarding or require the UBO to be a passport holder backed by a regulated-bank reference. VUV is non-deliverable on virtually all EMI platforms, forcing USD or AUD settlement and introducing conversion lag; therefore the blueprint channels all material master-royalty and capital flows through the licensed Vanuatu commercial banks using SWIFT-cleared rails, with the EMI limited to low-value POS settlement aggregation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: all shareholder equity injections and intercompany loans are remitted in USD via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's regulated bank (typically Hong Kong, Singapore or Australia) directly into the IC's USD Capital Escrow sub-account at the Vanuatu authorised dealer; the payment narrative must state a clear purpose code classified as capital contribution, shareholder equity injection or intercompany loan, and must be accompanied by a board resolution or signed loan agreement held on the bank's file. Verification performed by the receiving bank includes OFAC/sanctions screening of all counterparties, source-of-funds review, beneficial-owner AML checks, and confirmation that the incoming wire has not been routed through an unlicensed money-service business or shell processor; where the wire is denominated in a currency other than USD or AUD, the authorised dealer converts at its prevailing rate and retains the conversion ticket, and the funds remain in foreign currency unless a specific payroll need in VUV is documented.Outbound settlements use three primary rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for master-royalty fees, initial franchise fees and central-kitchen equipment payments to the Chinese bakery-chain operator's bank accounts in Hong Kong, Singapore or Shanghai, with the underlying franchise agreement and invoice numbers cited in the payment narrative; (ii) irrevocable commercial letters of credit — either sight or 90-day usance — issued by the Vanuatu bank and routed through its correspondent network (typically ANZ Australia, the BRED/Credit-Agricole group, or US-based correspondents) for large import purchases, with USD cleared through New York CHIPS/Fedwire; and (iii) domestic VUV payroll, supplier and tax-related payments processed through the authorised dealer's local clearing arrangement, while intra-group intercompany transfers between the IC's own sub-accounts are effected as internal ledger movements without leaving the bank.Deliverable FX forward contracts (VUV/USD and VUV/AUD) with the onshore authorised dealer against documented trade and royalty exposuresNon-deliverable VUV forwards (NDFs) executed with an offshore investment-grade counterparty under an ISDA Master Agreement where physical VUV settlement is unavailableCross-currency swaps (USD/AUD versus VUV) for asset-liability and escrow-balance matching on the IC's capital and operating positions

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax and no secondary tax on distributions of an International Company. Dividends and capital distributions may be declared and remitted to non-resident shareholders free of any Vanuatu tax, and there is no requirement to obtain a tax clearance certificate before repatriation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The IC Annual Return and accompanying annual government fee fall due within one month after each anniversary of the date of incorporation. The Vanuatu business licence, where applicable, is renewed on or before 31 December of each year for the succeeding calendar year. No income tax computation, no estimated-tax instalment, and no corporate tax return filing deadline exists because Vanuatu levies no tax on corporate income.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable in Vanuatu. The Republic of Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax and has not enacted Local File or Master File documentation thresholds expressed in Vanuatu Vatu. Nevertheless, the Chinese bakery-franchise group must satisfy the transfer-pricing documentation thresholds of the People's Republic of China (notably the related-party transaction declaration and contemporaneous documentation standards under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42), because the Vanuatu IC is a related party of the Chinese operating companies. Intercompany royalty rates, central-kitchen management fees, procurement margins, and franchise service charges must be priced at arm's length and supported by written agreements, benchmarking studies, and board approvals.
