Commercial Banking & Treasury Pooling

Central bank current account FX control diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs. offshore digital EMIs matrix, and risk rejection defense guide.

FX Control Flexibility The Vietnamese Dong (VND) is a limited-convertibility currency: the current account is substantially convertible for trade in services, meaning that payments by resident service providers such as an IP patent prosecution LLC to non-resident payees (foreign patent offices, WIPO, overseas associate attorneys) are permitted without prior SBV approval provided the underlying service contracts, invoices and documentary proof of service are presented to the authorized bank; the capital account, by contrast, remains tightly regulated and requires prior investment licensing, with all direct investment capital contributions, foreign loans, and profit/dividend remittances flowing through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at an authorized bank; profit remittance to the foreign investor is permitted only annually after finalization of corporate income tax, statutory audit, and settlement of all tax obligations; VND is not a fully convertible offshore currency and offshore NDFs are not legally available to Vietnamese resident corporates

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum charter capital applies to an intellectual-property agency activity classified under ISIC 7490 under the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14, the Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, or the Law on Fees and Charges. Members freely fix the charter capital in the Company Charter; the foreign-invested company must contribute the full subscribed capital, in cash or lawful assets, within ninety (90) days after issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate; foreign members must remit cash contributions into a licensed direct-investment capital account in a bank authorized to conduct foreign-exchange activity. No escrow account or blocked-account statutory rule is imposed for service-sector LLCs; a failure to contribute in due time triggers interest, compulsory capital adjustment or capital deregistration under Article 47 of the Law on Enterprises.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the company must occupy leased premises within a building that holds the required Fire Prevention and Fighting (PCCC) design approval (văn bản thẩm duyệt thiết kế PCCC) and fire-safety acceptance certificate (văn bản nghiệm thu về PCCC) issued by the competent Fire Police under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP; the tenant must appoint a fire-safety person-in-charge, post escape and signage plans, equip the premises with portable extinguishers, organize annual fire-drill and evacuation training for at least a representative proportion of staff, and maintain the required fire-safety records for the commune-level police inspection. Structural load: the tenant retains from the landlord the building-operation/construction certificate under the Construction Law No. 50/2014/QH13; interior fit-out works that do not affect the structural frame do not require a construction permit, but any structural renovation of the leased floor must be approved by the district-level construction authority. Occupational safety and health: the company is subject to the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13; it must conduct an occupational-hazard inventory, provide office ergonomics, implement annual workplace environmental monitoring to the extent required for office exposure limits (noise per QCVN 22:2016/BYT; lighting per QCVN 23:2016/BYT), and register any lifting or pressure equipment with the provincial Department of Labor, Invalids and Social Affairs where installed; no hazardous production equipment is deployed in a patent-prosecution agency, and no fixed-facility license beyond the general duties is imposed.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum charter capital applies to an intellectual-property agency activity classified under ISIC 7490 under the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14, the Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, or the Law on Fees and Charges. Official National E-Tax Portal: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn; National E-Invoice Data System (Cổng hóa đơn điện tử): https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn. Local administration rests with the provincial Departments of Taxation (Cục Thuế) and district-level Sub-Departments of Taxation (Chi cục Thuế) of the province or centrally managed city in which the LLC holds its tax registration, which in practice coincides with the location of its head office and business registration with the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI).

E-government filing portal

STEP 1: File for an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC, Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư) with the provincial DPI or the management board of an export processing zone, industrial zone, hi-tech zone, or economic zone, because a 100% foreign-owned patent prosecution agency in ISIC 7490 requires an IP project registration and a capital contribution/membership plan. STEP 2: After the IRC (or concurrently in the one-stop licensing path), file the enterprise registration dossier (charter, list of members, legal representative appointment) with the Business Registration Office of the DPI; the resulting Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC, Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký doanh nghiệp) states a single 10- or 13-digit enterprise registration code that is simultaneously the enterprise's statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN/MST) and the VAT registration ID; no separate TIN application is made to the tax authority. STEP 3: Within 10 working days after ERC issuance, the LLC must complete its tax registration file on the National E-Tax Portal using a qualified legal digital signature (Chữ ký số), file the initial tax registration information, declare the tax calculation method exclusively for VAT purposes (credit method versus direct method election), register its bank account, and list the authorised legal representatives; failure to activate exposes the LLC to administrative penalties. STEP 4: Obtain the mandatory patent agency practice licence from the National Office of Intellectual Property of Vietnam / Ministry of Science and Technology, as the business requires statutory licensing; the licence is independent of, but complementary to, tax registration. STEP 5: Complete e-invoice template registration under Decree 123/2020 and Circular 78/2021, open an offshore/inward remittance capable foreign-currency bank account for export service proceeds, and begin monthly or quarterly provisional CIT declarations and monthly VAT/e-invoice reporting. STEP 6: Any branch or place of business located in another province must obtain its own dependent TIN, while the head office retains the parent TIN and centralises VAT refund and CIT finalisation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: A foreign-invested Limited Liability Company is a Vietnamese resident enterprise taxed on its worldwide income at the headline CIT rate of 20% under the Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12 (as amended); profits are taxed at entity level and distributions to foreign corporate shareholders are not subject to an additional corporate dividend withholding tax. No pass-through, partnership flow-through, or fiscal transparency is available. Territorial_Scope caveat: the LLC's liability covers Vietnamese-source income and also foreign-source income attributable to its offshore contracts; conversely, non-resident service providers dealing with the LLC are exposed to the separate Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) regime. Tax_Exempt_Zone: By default the LLC is not located in a tax-exempt zone and must pay standard CIT and standard VAT; however, if the patent prosecution activity is embedded in an export processing enterprise (EPE) or a qualifying economic/hi-tech zone with a separate incentive certificate, alternative CIT/VAT treatment may apply.

Available election forms

Vietnam has no US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election under the Tax Administration Law or Decree 132/2020 on transfer pricing. The available statutory elections are: (i) VAT method election: the credit (input-output offset) method is mandatory if the LLC maintains full accounting books and demonstrates annual revenue over VND 1 billion, but micro/small enterprises may elect the direct method on turnover; once elected, the method must be used for at least the full tax year; (ii) FCT method election: when the LLC subcontracts offshore patent associates, it may elect the deduction method under Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC for its foreign contractor payments, provided the foreign contractor maintains accounting records, otherwise the direct withholding method (VAT + CIT deemed rates) applies; (iii) CIT investment incentive election: the investor may apply for the standard 20% rate or petition to apply the incentivised 10%/15% rates, tax holidays, and reductions, in each case only if the investment project and location qualify under the Law on Investment and Decree 218/2013; (iv) No consolidated fiscal unity, group registration, or loss-transfer election exists: the LLC files standalone tax returns.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no economic turnover threshold for compulsory VAT registration of a legal entity: every LLC, including a patent prosecution agency in ISIC 7490, must register for VAT from the date of enterprise registration regardless of revenue. The only revenue-based threshold in the Vietnamese VAT system is VND 100,000,000 per annum of revenue, which concerns households, individual businesses, and cooperatives subject to the direct method; foreign contractors maintaining no permanent establishment in Vietnam must register for FCT/VAT within 10 working days of commencing taxable activities irrespective of turnover. For VAT refund purposes, the effective working credit must exceed the statutory threshold of VND 300,000,000 after a consecutive 12-month period to qualify for an export input VAT refund.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% VAT rate applies to services exported to foreign organizations or individuals and consumed entirely outside Vietnam, per Article 9(3)(c) of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC, as reinforced by Circular 130/2016/TT-BTC and official guidance. For the patent and trademark prosecution agency, the following cumulative conditions must be satisfied: (i) the contractual counterparty is a foreign organization or a foreign individual with no permanent establishment or Vietnamese residence, and the contract is concluded through the LLC's Vietnamese establishment; (ii) the service deliverable is used and consumed abroad, which is met for PCT international patent applications, international design registrations, Madrid Protocol trademark filings, and representation before foreign national/regional patent offices; services consisting in representation before the National Office of Intellectual Property of Vietnam (NOIP) in connection with Vietnamese domestic patents or trademarks are deemed consumed in Vietnam and are taxable at 10%, even if the client is foreign; (iii) payment is made in a freely convertible foreign currency and is actually remitted to the LLC's Vietnamese bank account; (iv) the LLC retains complete contemporaneous documentation, including the contract, PCT/WIPO receipts, foreign office correspondence, and remittance evidence; (v) the exported services are not covered by the anti-abuse list for services deemed consumed in Vietnam (advertising, education, transportation of persons, restaurants, etc.), which does not apply to patent prosecution. The LLC must never claim VAT credit on input invoices that directly relate to domestic Vietnamese trademark representation; those are subject to standard 10% VAT and may be credited normally. Export turnover exceeding the 20% CIT rebasing thresholds will additionally require transfer-pricing documentation under Decree 132/2020.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Hải quan điện tử) — only when physical goods, samples, priority documents, or certified copies are exported; for electronic-only services this declaration is replaced by the service contract and service delivery proof
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (vận đơn đường biển/đường hàng không) — required solely to evidence physical movement of exported goods/documents
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Giấy báo có) issued by a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank, or an original foreign bank SWIFT credit advice, evidencing receipt in the LLC's account of the export proceeds in convertible foreign currency from abroad
  • Service Export Contract concluded between the Vietnam LLC and the foreign client, in English or a foreign language, identifying the PCT/Madrid patent or trademark services and the foreign invoicing party
  • Official e-invoice issued by the LLC stating the 0% VAT rate and referencing the export contract
  • PCT Receipt, International Bureau filing receipt, WIPO Notice, or foreign national/regional patent office filing confirmation demonstrating that the service was rendered before a foreign authority and consumed outside Vietnam
  • Signed acceptance certificate, work-delivery record, or client email confirmation evidencing actual completion of the offshore service
  • Certificate of incorporation, commercial registration, or tax identification document of the foreign client proving its non-resident status
  • Foreign-currency bank account statement of the LLC demonstrating repatriation of the export proceeds within the statutory 180-day deadline
  • Written declaration/undertaking by the LLC that the service was not consumed within Vietnam, accompanied by a detailed calculation of attributable input VAT

Mandatory e-invoicing under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC: every seller must issue a digitally signed e-invoice through a certified e-invoice supplier (or through the tax authority's free web portal) and transmit it immediately upon issuance to the National E-Invoice Data System operated by the General Department of Taxation; transmission is near real-time, and each invoice is attributed a unique transaction code and an embedded encrypted QR code. There is no discretionary digital stamping; for taxpayers without proprietary systems, the tax authority generates a verification code (mã số của cơ quan thuế) before the invoice is legally issued. Large issuers must integrate via secured API/machine-to-machine interfaces. For export invoices at 0%, the invoice must state the export contract number, the foreign party's name, the method of payment, and indicate the export customs declaration number when physical freight is involved. Non-compliance with transmission timing or template registration renders invoices invalid for input credit and shifts the entire VAT burden to the exporter.

Export zero-rating is a high-risk audit area: the tax authority may re-examine export dossiers for a period of up to 5 years from the violation date for tax evasion, and may enforce arrears within 10 years from the statutory payment deadline. Unsustained zero-rating is retroactively reclassified as domestic VAT at 10%, with the VAT deficiency assessed from the invoice date; late-payment interest runs at 0.03% per day on every unpaid day (Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14). Wrongfully obtained VAT refunds are clawed back in full with statutory interest from the date the refund was paid, plus an administrative penalty of 100% of the refunded amount, and the LLC's refund priority is suspended. For deliberate fraud or forgery of export documents, the evasion is punishable as tax evasion under Penal Code No. 100/2015/QH13, Article 200: if the evaded tax is from VND 100,000,000 up to under VND 300,000,000, the responsible legal representative faces a fine up to 3x the evaded tax or imprisonment from 6 months to 7 years; a legal entity can be fined up to VND 4.5 billion and have its license suspended for up to 3 years. In addition, the Ministry of Science and Technology may suspend or revoke the agency's patent agent practice license for records falsification. There is no de minimis safe harbour for zero-rating; each transaction stands or falls on its own documentary integrity.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese Dong (VND) is a limited-convertibility currency: the current account is substantially convertible for trade in services, meaning that payments by resident service providers such as an IP patent prosecution LLC to non-resident payees (foreign patent offices, WIPO, overseas associate attorneys) are permitted without prior SBV approval provided the underlying service contracts, invoices and documentary proof of service are presented to the authorized bank; the capital account, by contrast, remains tightly regulated and requires prior investment licensing, with all direct investment capital contributions, foreign loans, and profit/dividend remittances flowing through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at an authorized bank; profit remittance to the foreign investor is permitted only annually after finalization of corporate income tax, statutory audit, and settlement of all tax obligations; VND is not a fully convertible offshore currency and offshore NDFs are not legally available to Vietnamese resident corporates.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Foreign Exchange Ordinance 2005 (as amended) and Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN dated 24 February 2015, a resident service exporter must repatriate the full amount of foreign-currency export earnings into a foreign-currency account opened at an authorized credit institution in Vietnam within 90 days from the date the export service is completed; where the contract provides for deferred payment, the proceeds must be credited to the authorized-bank foreign-currency account within 90 days from the contractual payment due date; no mandatory statutory conversion or surrender ratio is currently imposed on service export receipts, so the LLC may retain 100% of the foreign-currency proceeds in its foreign-currency account or voluntarily sell such proceeds to the authorized bank at the prevailing market exchange rate within the SBV-published daily central-rate band; documentary evidence comprising the export service contract, invoice, proof of service delivery (e.g., WIPO filing receipts, patent number confirmation) and bank transaction receipts must be retained for at least five years for SBV foreign-exchange inspection, corporate income tax finalization, and AML record-keeping purposes.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV - Ngan hang Nha nuoc Viet Nam), being the sole central bank and foreign-exchange administration authority under the Foreign Exchange Ordinance No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended by Ordinance No. 08/2013/UBTVQH13; the SBV operates through its Foreign Exchange Department (formerly the Foreign Exchange Control Division) and licensed authorized credit institutions; the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI) holds complementary competence for foreign direct investment registration and the Ministry of Finance (MOF) for fiscal, customs and tax-related cross-border reporting.

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury purposes; tolerated only as an ancillary rail. Vietnam does not maintain a statutory e-money institution (EMI) or digital-bank licensing regime equivalent to EU/UK EMIs, and offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Stripe, and similar platforms) are not authorized credit institutions, payment intermediaries, or DICA custodians under SBV regulations. Such EMIs may be used for low-value, high-frequency operational disbursements (for example, payment of official fees to WIPO, USPTO, or national intellectual property offices) and for collection of small client remittances from foreign applicants, provided funds are immediately mirrored through the licensed Vietnamese bank account for audit, tax-deduction, and FX-control evidence; EMIs cannot accept direct investment capital, cannot serve as the DICA, cannot be used for profit repatriation, and cannot replace the licensed-bank clearing leg; reliance on unregulated EMIs creates material tax-deductibility, transfer-pricing, and AML compliance risk and is therefore strictly supplemental.
