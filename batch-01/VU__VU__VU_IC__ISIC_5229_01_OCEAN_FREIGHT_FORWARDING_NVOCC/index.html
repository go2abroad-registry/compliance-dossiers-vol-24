Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA) - Port Vila, Vanuatu; official web portal: https://www.vrca.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must apply to VRCA for VAT registration using Form VF-1 (VAT Registration Application) supported by certified certificate of incorporation, International Company (IC) registration certificate from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission, statutory declaration of intended taxable activities, projected turnover statement, and identification documents of directors and beneficial owners. Where the entity is engaged solely in offshore business and makes no local taxable supplies, it may still voluntarily register for VAT to recover input tax. VRCA typically issues the VAT identification number within 10 business days. No income-tax ID is required because Vanuatu does not levy income tax.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity must apply to VRCA for VAT registration using Form VF-1 (VAT Registration Application) supported by certified certificate of incorporation, International Company (IC) registration certificate from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission, statutory declaration of intended taxable activities, projected turnover statement, and identification documents of directors and beneficial owners. Where the entity is engaged solely in offshore business and makes no local taxable supplies, it may still voluntarily register for VAT to recover input tax. VRCA typically issues the VAT identification number within 10 business days. No income-tax ID is required because Vanuatu does not levy income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None material. Vanuatu does not have a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. An International Company cannot elect to become subject to income tax; its tax-exempt status is mandatory under the International Companies Act. Tax classification is statutory and deemed permanent.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 4,000,000 (approximately USD 34,000) taxable turnover in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to exports of goods removed from Vanuatu under customs control; international transport of goods; services directly connected to exported goods; freight forwarding and NVOCC services provided for goods consigned from Vanuatu to overseas destinations; and services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and where the benefit of the service is wholly outside Vanuatu. The exporter or service provider must retain sufficient evidence to prove the goods were exported or the service was rendered offshore.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Entry) endorsed by VRCA customs
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document showing shipment from Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank MT103 evidence of foreign currency receipt
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Freight forwarding contract or NVOCC booking note evidencing the offshore routing

No mandatory electronic invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance system is in operation. Invoices issued in Vanuatu must contain the supplier name, VAT identification number, invoice date, description of supply, consideration, and VAT amount. There is no API-based digital stamping or real-time government clearance requirement.

VRCA may audit export zero-rating claims within 5 years from the end of the calendar year in which the claim was made. If goods were not exported or services were not genuinely supplied to non-residents, the uncollected output tax becomes immediately due, plus a default penalty of 25% of the tax and monthly interest at 1%. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims are subject to civil penalties up to 300% of the tax evaded and criminal fines up to VT 10,000,000, with imprisonment for up to 5 years under the Value Added Tax Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV, ISO 4217) is fully convertible on both current and capital account for residents and non-residents; Vanuatu operates a liberalized exchange regime with no IMF Article VIII restrictions on payments and transfers. There are no statutory exchange-control limits on foreign direct investment, dividend distribution, profit repatriation, loan servicing, or liquidation proceeds payable to a VU International Company (IC), and the IC is tax-exempt under the International Companies Act No. 32 of 1992. Practical convertibility hurdles are bank-level rather than statutory: the authorized dealer must sight the underlying board resolution, the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, and a compliant purpose declaration before effecting conversion; the offshore VUV market is thin, so large USD-to-VUV conversions (above VUV 20 million) may suffer spread widening or require 24–72 hours for dealer pricing. Profit remittance out of Vanuatu carries zero withholding tax for the IC, but the remitting bank will require evidence of good standing, updated beneficial-ownership filing, and confirmation that no Vanuatu-source taxable activity (beyond the licensed NVOCC freight-forwarding scope) has occurred.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline on export proceeds or cross-border service income. An NVOCC freight-forwarding IC may retain 100% of its foreign-currency earnings (freight, consolidation fees, surcharges, and commission income) in USD, AUD, NZD, or VUV accounts with any authorized dealer. RBV prudential circulars only require the authorized dealer to record the underlying commercial invoice, house bill of lading, or service contract to satisfy source-of-funds verification; there is no central-bank prescribed time window for bringing receipts onshore. Bank policy, however, expects export-related credits to be physically received within a commercially reasonable period (typically 30–90 days) to align with trade-cycle documentation, and credits arriving beyond that window will trigger enhanced due diligence rather than a statutory violation.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 282], is the sole central bank and prudential supervisor of authorized foreign-exchange dealers; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately licenses the International Company (IC) and supervises AML/CFT obligations under the Financial Institutions (AML/CFT) Act 2016. The Ministry of Finance retains high-level residual exchange-control policy power, but day-to-day FX supervision is delegated to RBV through its Banking and Exchange Control divisions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Ramp Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW (legally usable only as an ancillary railway, never as a primary treasury account). Vanuatu has no dedicated e-money institution (EMI) or principal-to-principal payment institution licensing regime under VFSC; the Fintech Act 2021 establishes only a regulatory sandbox (limited scope, capped volumes, and mandatory bank partnership) which cannot support the recurring SWIFT settlement volumes of an NVOCC. Offshore digital EMIs (UK FCA-authorised, Singapore MAS major payment institutions, or EU-licensed e-money institutions) are not prohibited from servicing a VU International Company, but correspondent banks frequently refuse VUV-clearing instructions from EMI accounts, and EMIs themselves apply a de-risking overlay to Vanuatu-domiciled offshore ICs, requiring a locally registered bank as the on-ramp. Legal constraint: VFSC Banking Act requires any entity taking deposits or executing remittance business on a domestic basis to be licensed; an unlicensed international EMI circumventing this triggers a criminal offence; therefore, the blueprint mandates a licensed domestic commercial bank for clearing and allows a foreign EMI only for precategory payouts to US carriers via ACH-Direct rails after explicit written consent from the primary relationship manager.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route all inbound shareholder equity via a single SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's home bank into the VUV/USD Capital Escrow Account opened at ANZ Vanuatu or BSP Vanuatu. The wire narrative must unambiguously state 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION — [Company Name] — INVOICE [Reference]' in field 72; the beneficiary bank will map the credit to IMF BPM6 functional category 'Direct Investment — Equity (DII)' in its RBV statistical reporting. The remitting bank must include the Vanuatu authorized dealer's SWIFT BIC and the beneficiary account number; the initiating institution must complete the purpose-of-payment field using SWIFT category code 'CAPI' (capital) or domestic equivalent, not a generic goods/services code, to prevent an 'ambiguous credit' hold. Prior to the transfer, the receiving bank requires (i) the board resolution authorizing the capital call, (ii) the executed subscription agreement, and (iii) the source-of-funds statements; the dealer will issue a Foreign Exchange Transaction Report to RBV if conversion into VUV is requested. To avoid an unnecessary 24–72 hour compliance hold, the injection must not exceed 150% of the projected first-quarter operating expense; any larger credit will automatically trigger strengthened due diligence. ACH Direct is not a permitted inbound rail for cross-border capital injection because ACH is a domestic US clearing mechanism; all cross-border equity must use the SWIFT wire network.Use SWIFT MT103 for operational outbound payments (carrier freight, terminal handling charges, demurrage, port congestion surcharges, and agent commissions) to underlying ocean carriers and foreign agents; use SWIFT MT202 COV for interbank cover payments in large settlement batches. For structured transactions, utilise Commercial Letters of Credit (documentary credits UCP 600) with sight or usance drafts drawn on the Vanuatu issuing bank as a means of settling NVOCC receivables/payables where the counterparty requires bankers' participation; the issuing bank will typically require 100% cash cover for a first-year IC. ACH Direct is viable only for USD-denominated disbursements to a US resident payee (e.g., a US port agent or US consolidator) because ACH operates solely within the US automated clearing network; it must not be used for non-US counterparties. All outbound payments must carry the corresponding BPM6 transaction code (e.g., 'Transportation — Sea Freight' for freight payments and 'Other Business Services' for agency commissions) so that the RBV statistics remain consistent and the Medium-Risk-Enhanced rating is not escalated. Standing settlement instructions for recurring carrier payments should be pre-agreed to eliminate the need for repeated compliance re-screening; payments to new counterparties require first-time enhanced due diligence consisting of the commercial contract, the carrier's proof of vessel ownership, and sanctions screening.Outright Forwards (deliverable VUV/USD) via an authorized dealer (ANZ Vanuatu, BSP Vanuatu) with standard 10–20% margin posting and physical delivery at maturity; permitted without RBV pre-approval for underlying trade transactions under the Foreign Exchange Act and RBV dealer guidelines; tenor normally capped at 12 months for IC accounts.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) structured through a Singapore or Hong Kong licensed dealer because the offshore VUV market is illiquid and VUV is treated as a limited-convertibility currency outside Vanuatu; NDFs are permitted as a hedge against VUV/USD volatility affecting collected freight revenue; settlement is in USD or AUD on the notional differential; dealer requires a bilateral ISDA 2002 Master Agreement.Cross-Currency Swaps (VUV/USD) executed with an offshore licensed dealer under a CSA-covered ISDA Master Agreement to swap fixed-rate VUV liabilities or receivables into USD floating rate; permitted for balance-sheet hedging of the escrowed capital and long-term carrier payment obligations; subject to risk-appetite approval by the relationship bank and RMB 10 million equivalent notional minimum at most dealers.Vanilla European FX Options (VUV calls/puts) permitted for hedging the conversion of freight receivables into VUV or for protecting escrow liquidation values; RBV does not impose a statutory ban, but dealers require the underlying trade exposure to be evidenced at -trade date; exotic options and speculative FX derivatives are prohibited for a Medium-Risk-Enhanced IC and will cause account review.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax and no dividend tax. An International Company (IC) is expressly exempt from all taxes on its income, profits, distributions, transactions and operations under Section 68 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax. Distributions paid by the IC to non-resident shareholders are exempt from all Vanuatu taxation and may be remitted freely by SWIFT wire or ACH without deduction at source (Section 68, International Companies Act; Vanuatu has no exchange controls).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The IC-AR and annual government fee fall due on each anniversary of incorporation; the registered agent ordinarily requires the signed return 30 days before the anniversary so that the fee is remitted on time. No tax assessment year exists. A newly incorporated NVOCC IC must complete its first annual return within 12 months of registration.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - Vanuatu prescribes no turnover-related threshold and no Local File or Master File obligation because it imposes no corporate income tax. The practical documentation threshold is foreign-led: prepare OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation wherever the NVOCC group operates in high-tax jurisdictions with local disclosure thresholds (e.g., the local-currency thresholds for Local File or Country-by-Country Report in partner states). Maintain intercompany agreements for freight rebilling, commission splits and cost allocations.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted BVI/Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulations and has no CFC rules directed at International Companies. However, the IC must maintain (i) a registered office in Vanuatu, (ii) a licensed registered agent, (iii) a Beneficial Ownership Return on file with the VFSC, and (iv) credible substance indicators such as real board authority over freight contracts, evidence of commercial decision-making, and adequate books and records held in Vanuatu. Absence of these indicators exposes the IC to re-characterization as a shell company by foreign tax authorities, correspondent banks and CRS counterparts, and may impair the validity of its NVOCC agreements and the availability of treaty benefits claimed in foreign jurisdictions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abuse of Tax-Exempt IC Status

Statutory sanction: Loss or revocation of IC tax-exempt status; assessment of undeclared profits as if derived by an ordinary Vanuatu company; monetary penalties under the IC Act; mandatory deregistration. Where the unlawful local revenue constitutes a taxable supply, the 12.5% VAT becomes exigible with interest and default surcharges under the Value Added Tax Act.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders who knowingly cause the IC to trade locally are jointly and severally liable for restitution of concealed profits and unpaid VAT. Courts may issue personal asset-freezing orders and travel bans pending enforcement, and imprisonment may be imposed for fraud-type offences; civil asset forfeiture may reach the freight receivables and bank balances of the IC.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC penalties for late or non-filing of Beneficial Ownership Returns; administrative strike-off with assets vesting in the Registrar; adverse international classification by OECD/EU/GAFI, which materially impairs the IC's banking relationships and the commercial enforceability of its NVOCC contracts.

Director personal liability: Controllers and directors may be disqualified by the VFSC, held personally liable for restoration costs and company debts, prohibited from leaving Vanuatu through travel bans where enforcement proceedings are pending, and subject to criminal fines and imprisonment for knowingly submitting false declarations of substance.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution with substantial fines and imprisonment for natural persons; confiscation of freight-payment funds and the IC's bank accounts; VFSC revocation of the registered agent's license and of the IC's registration; FATF/CRS reputational damage resulting in de-risking by correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal liability for wilful blindness in freight-payment flows; civil restitution orders may attach to personal assets; travel bans and mutual legal assistance cooperation with requesting foreign states apply under Vanuatu law.
Registration Fee and Annual Return Default

Statutory sanction: Late surcharges; administrative strike-off of the NVOCC IC; vesting of all assets, including freight receivables from shippers, in the Registrar; restoration fees and arrears must be paid to resume lawful operation; the Section 68 tax exemption is unavailable during the period of strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade after strike-off are personally liable for all debts and freight liabilities incurred; the Court may issue travel bans and commit directors for contempt; criminal fines apply where the company is falsely represented as being in good standing.
Transfer Pricing / Sham Intercompany Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu itself has no transfer pricing penalty regime, but foreign tax authorities will re-characterize the IC as a sham conduit, deny deductibility of intercompany freight charges, and impose cross-border penalties and interest on the group. The VFSC may penalize the IC for filing false information, and the IC's bank accounts may be suspended for suspicious transaction structuring.

Director personal liability: Directors may be pursued by foreign revenue authorities for negligent misrepresentation; in Vanuatu, knowingly signing false invoices or false confirmations attracts personal fines and potential imprisonment for fraud; joint and several civil restitution of underpaid foreign taxes may be ordered by foreign courts and enforced against personal assets in Vanuatu under mutual assistance.
Unlicensed NVOCC Operation / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Foreign-regulator penalties, debarment and bonding forfeitures; in Vanuatu, violation of sanctions legislation carries fines and imprisonment, and the VFSC may strike off the offending IC; cargo owners may bring civil claims for breach of bailment and fraudulent misrepresentation.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly involved in unlawful bookings face personal fines, imprisonment and travel bans and may be disqualified from acting as officers in Vanuatu; freight assets may be forfeited and directors may be ordered to compensate affected cargo interests; personal liability attaches where the IC is treated as an alter ego in fraudulent shipping schemes.
