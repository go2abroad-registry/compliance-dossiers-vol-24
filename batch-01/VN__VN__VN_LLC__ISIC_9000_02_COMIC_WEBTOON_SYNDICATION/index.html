Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum charter capital applies because the webtoon digital publishing activity is not a conditional business line and the enterprise profile carries a self-declared charter capital. The charter capital must be expressed in VND and paid in full within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47.2 of Law on Enterprises 59/2020/QH14). No escrow account or capital-blocking account is required; contributed capital must be physically deposited into a bank account of the LLC opened at a licensed commercial bank or foreign bank branch in Viet Nam. If a member fails to contribute on time, the unpaid portion becomes a debt of that member owed to the LLC, and the Members' Council must adjust the registered charter capital by filing a change with the Business Registration Office within 30 days after the payment deadline.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety: the company must obtain Fire Prevention and Rescue Police approval of the fire safety design for its office fit-out before construction works, and after completion a Fire Safety Acceptance Certificate (Văn bản nghiệm thu về PCCC) is required under Decree 136/2020/ND-CP as amended by Decree 83/2023/ND-CP; annual self-inspection and maintenance of fire-fighting equipment and evacuation routes are mandatory. Structural load: the office lease must be supported by the building's construction acceptance certificate and the floor-load capacity must accommodate server racks, data cabinets and uninterruptible power supplies; a structural compliance certificate from the building owner may be requested. Occupational safety: the company must conduct a workplace hazard assessment, occupational hygiene monitoring (microclimate, noise, lighting, electromagnetic fields), provide safety training to employees, and register occupational safety and health self-assessment results with the provincial DOLISA in accordance with the Law on Occupational Safety and Health 2015 and Decree 44/2016/ND-CP; ergonomic risk prevention for employees working with video display terminals is required as good practice and industry standard. Workplaces with employees engaged in screen-based activities are subject to the general workplace environmental quality thresholds: lighting levels per QCVN 22:2016/BYT, microclimate per QCVN 26:2016/BYT, and ventilation per QCVN 27:2016/BYT.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum charter capital applies because the webtoon digital publishing activity is not a conditional business line and the enterprise profile carries a self-declared charter capital. The charter capital must be expressed in VND and paid in full within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47.2 of Law on Enterprises 59/2020/QH14). No escrow account or capital-blocking account is required; contributed capital must be physically deposited into a bank account of the LLC opened at a licensed commercial bank or foreign bank branch in Viet Nam. If a member fails to contribute on time, the unpaid portion becomes a debt of that member owed to the LLC, and the Members' Council must adjust the registered charter capital by filing a change with the Business Registration Office within 30 days after the payment deadline.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; national e-tax portals: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (domestic taxpayers) and https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn (foreign suppliers without a Vietnamese establishment); e-invoice portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested LLC obtains its Tax Identification Number (MST) through the enterprise registration process as follows: (1) apply for an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI), an Industrial Zone Authority, or the Special Investment Supervisory Agency where applicable; (2) submit the online enterprise registration dossier to the provincial DPI to obtain an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC); (3) the Enterprise Code printed on the ERC is automatically the Tax Identification Number (MST) pursuant to the Enterprise Law 2020 (Law 59/2020/QH14) and the Law on Tax Administration (Law 38/2019/QH14), so no separate lottery-style TIN application exists; (4) within 30 days of ERC issuance, complete supplementary tax registration via the online tax registration system (đăng ký thuế lần đầu) at the tax authority's one-stop portal and receive the electronic Tax Registration Confirmation (Giấy xác nhận đăng ký thuế); (5) activate an e-tax account on the GDT e-tax portal, register for mandatory e-invoices under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP, and register for the declared tax payment method (khai thuế, nộp thuế điện tử).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a foreign-invested LLC is mandatorily a tax resident of Vietnam for corporate income tax (CIT) purposes under the Law on Corporate Income Tax (Law 14/2008/QH12 as amended by Law 32/2013/QH13 and Law 106/2016/QH13), subject to a 20% CIT on worldwide income with a foreign tax credit available under bilateral tax treaties; the LLC is not a pass-through entity and no partnership-style flow-through is recognized under Vietnamese law.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid entity election regime exists in Vietnam. A foreign-invested LLC cannot elect to be taxed as an individual, partnership, or branch; full entity-level taxation is mandatory. There are no hybrid mismatch election mechanisms; BEPS Action 2 outcomes are not domestically enacted, but related-party certainty is governed by Decree 132/2020/ND-CP on transfer pricing and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule contained in Article 8 of Law 38/2019/QH14, which authorizes the tax authority to disregard transactions without economic substance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover exemption threshold applies to LLCs: every enterprise must register for VAT upon ERC issuance regardless of revenue level. For comparison, households and individual businesses are subject to VAT registration only if annual revenue exceeds VND 100,000,000 (per the Law on Personal Income Tax and VAT administration guidance). Foreign digital suppliers of e-commerce goods/services without a Vietnamese establishment must register, declare, and pay VAT and CIT through the GDT foreign supplier portal (etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn) with no minimum registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

An exported digital webtoon/electronic publishing supply (including subscription-unlocking services and multilingual chapter-license content) qualifies for 0% VAT under Article 9.3 of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC when ALL of the following conditions are satisfied: (i) a written foreign service contract is concluded with a foreign party (or a binding online terms-of-service with non-resident end-users); (ii) the service is actually performed and consumed outside Vietnam, evidenced by subscriber geolocation/IP-address logs, platform-analytics reporting, and foreign bank receipt; (iii) the consideration is received in a freely convertible foreign currency via electronic transfer into an account at a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank; and (iv) the LLC retains a complete substantiation file for its service-export claims. Service supplies to foreign parties that are attributable to domestic Vietnamese users or that form part of a domestic value chain are NOT zero-rated and are subject to 10% VAT. Revenue routed through Apple IAP / Google Play Billing must be carefully split: the app-store commission is a separately imported international digital service and the Vietnamese LLC must concurrently zero-rate the net proceeds of non-resident consumer subscriptions; domestic consumer purchases remain standard-rate supplies, with the app store's commission withheld by the platform subject to the foreign contractor tax regime.

  • Customs Export Declaration (HQ/2015-XK electronic declaration) — required only if physical merchandise or tangible media is co-exported with the digital webtoon content; zero-rating of pure intangible digital services does not use a customs declaration.
  • Export Service Contract or binding online terms-of-service agreement with the foreign party / non-resident end-user, with a clear statement of offshore consumption and pricing schedule per chapter or subscription tier.
  • Commercial invoice and zero-rated VAT e-invoice issued under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP, with the 0% VAT line item, VAT rate code, and mention of the Article 9 Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC zero-rating basis.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Chứng từ thanh toán qua ngân hàng) or bank credit advice evidencing net proceeds received in a convertible foreign currency from abroad into an account at a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank.
  • Offshore-consumption evidence pack: non-resident subscriber reports from Apple IAP / Google Play Billing, IP-geolocation logs, viewer-country analytics, and user-identification metadata demonstrating that content is delivered outside Vietnamese territory.
  • App-store commission substantiation: Apple/Google merchant-of-record statements and foreign contractor tax registration evidence, to support the app-store commission as a separate imported digital service with its own VAT/GST treatment.

Mandatory e-invoicing with fiscal data transmission to the General Department of Taxation is fully effective for all Vietnamese enterprises from 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. E-invoices must be issued in XML format with a digital signature and a two-dimensional QR data matrix; invoices must be issued with a tax authority-issued code for B2C sales. For e-invoices without a tax code, the full e-invoice data file must be transmitted to the GDT e-invoice portal within 24 hours of issuance; for coded invoices, the code assignment is performed real-time by the GDT system before issuance to the buyer. Cash-register-type invoices, if used in a physical retail scenario, must transmit data in real time from the fiscal data device to the GDT. There is no VAT-group consolidation or fiscal-warehouse for digital content: the invoice must record the placing of free-to-paid subscription conversion, VAT breakdown, and the customer's VAT ID or passport number, and all e-invoice data must be archived for 10 years.

The GDT maintains a high-scrutiny refund-control list for zero-rated service exporters and applies retrospective post-refund audits. For fraudulent zero-rating or VAT-refund claims, the legal penalties are: (i) evasion penalties under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP of 1 to 3 times the evaded VAT amount, plus late-payment interest at 0.03% per day; (ii) false-underclearance of VAT that is not criminal evasion attracts a 20% supplementary tax penalty plus late-payment interest; (iii) the VAT refund is clawed back in full with interest from the date of refund disbursement; (iv) criminal liability under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 (as amended 2017) — tax evasion from VND 100,000,000 or with aggravating circumstances carries imprisonment of 6 months to 7 years, with evasion exceeding VND 1,000,000,000 attracting up to 20 years' imprisonment, plus fines up to VND 4,500,000,000; (v) a claim for VAT refund of export services that fails the offshore-consumption documentation test within the 180-day repatriation deadline results in automatic rejection of the zero-rate and conversion of the supply to 10% standard-rated output, with under-declared VAT recovered, penalty, and interest; and (vi) the responsible legal representative and chief accountant may be administratively fined and, in egregious schemes, criminally prosecuted for refund fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

VND is not freely convertible offshore. Current-account transactions, including cross-border webtoon licensing royalties, syndication fees, and profit remittances after audited financial statements and tax clearance, are generally convertible through authorized banks with appropriate underlying documentation. Capital-account transactions, such as charter capital injections and cross-border shareholder loans, require SBV registration or permission and are strictly monitored. Foreign investors are permitted to remit profits in VND-convertible foreign currency once annually after completion of statutory tax and audit requirements. All FX conversions must take place onshore through a licensed credit institution; offshore market trading in VND is restricted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds, including digital content syndication revenue from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing, must be repatriated to a foreign-currency account held at an authorized bank in Vietnam within the contractual settlement period, generally not exceeding 120 days from the service performance or invoice date unless a longer payment term is approved by the bank. The mandatory conversion ratio for private-sector exporters is currently set at 0% of export receipts under normal conditions; however, the SBV may impose a temporary surrender ratio during balance-of-payments emergencies. The LLC should retain USD receipts offshore only to the extent expressly permitted, but standard compliance requires all proceeds to flow into an onshore VND/USD operational account and conversion to VND to occur only for local operating expenditures.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) - Ngan hang Nha nuoc Viet Nam, acting through the Foreign Exchange Management Department and authorized commercial banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low for primary treasury operations. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, and Revolut cannot act as the main corporate banking rail for a Vietnamese LLC because they are not authorized credit institutions under SBV regulations and cannot provide onshore VND or VND-denominated current accounts. They may be used only as ancillary collection vehicles for micro-inbound IAP/PayPal receipts of foreign platforms, subject to strict enterprise bookkeeping and tax reconciliation, but all explicit foreign-exchange conversion and Vietnamese statutory disbursements must be conducted through an SBV-licensed bank. Any unlicensed payment service activity in Vietnam carries criminal and administrative enforcement risk.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for primary treasury operations. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Corporate shareholders: no domestic dividend withholding tax is imposed on dividends paid by a Vietnamese LLC out of after-tax CIT profits. Individual shareholders, resident or non-resident, are subject to final personal income tax at 5% on dividend distributions; the LLC must withhold and remit that 5% to the tax authority. Therefore, for an LLC owned by foreign corporate entities, after-tax profits may be remitted overseas without additional corporate-level Vietnamese withholding.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due no later than the last day of the first month following each calendar quarter. For a calendar-year filer, the deadlines are 30 April, 31 July, 31 October, and 31 January. The LLC must pay at least 80% of the annual CIT liability through the quarterly provisional payments by the final quarter; shortfall between quarterly installments and final assessed CIT is subject to late-payment interest.

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 90 days from the end of the fiscal year. For a calendar-year taxpayer, the deadline is 31 March. A written application may allow a maximum 30-day extension. The annual return reconciles the four quarterly provisional payments with the final tax liability.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VND 30 billion in total annual related-party transaction value or VND 50 billion in total annual turnover (exemption thresholds). If a taxpayer's annual revenue is below VND 50 billion and total related-party transaction turnover is below VND 30 billion, it is exempt from preparing the Local File and Master File, but it must still file the annual Form 01 Related-Party Transaction Disclosure with the CIT finalization return. Above either threshold, a compliant Local File, Master File, and TP benchmarking study must be prepared and retained within 30 days of a tax authority request.

Economic substance scope: Viet Nam does not operate a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations regime; however, the Vietnamese tax administration applies substance-over-form, beneficial ownership, and general anti-avoidance principles. A digital webtoon syndication LLC must demonstrate real people, actual office facilities, functional decision-making, contractual assumption of risk, and value-creating activities in Viet Nam (editorial curation, platform management, licensing, subscriber support). An entity staffed solely by nominees or routing transactions through Viet Nam to exploit DTAs or defer tax will be disregarded, with income reallocated to the true economic owner and FCT assessed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Underdeclaration of Digital Sales Revenue

Statutory sanction: Under the Tax Administration Law and Decree 125/2020/ND-CP, penalty equal to 20% of the underdeclared tax plus daily late-payment interest of 0.03%. Criminal exposure under Penal Code Article 200 includes fines up to VND 4,500,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for large-scale tax evasion. Tax authority may freeze bank accounts, attach assets, and suspend tax codes.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, general director, and chief accountant are jointly and severally liable for short-paid tax. Directors may be subjected to temporary exit suspension (travel ban), asset seizure, and criminal prosecution. Imprisonment up to 7 years is available for deliberate and organized evasion schemes.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may disregard the legal form, recharacterize all income earned by the shell as income of the foreign beneficial owner, disallow expenses, impose FCT on the deemed cross-border payments, assess additional CIT, apply a 20% penalty on the shortfall, and charge 0.03% daily interest. Administrative fines for false declarations or failure to keep books reach VND 25,000,000 and may be applied together with criminal sanctions.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and beneficial owners who sign false substance declarations face civil restitution, personal tax liability, and exit bans. Courts may pierce the corporate veil where the LLC is a facade. Criminal false-certification liability may result in imprisonment for the officers who certified non-existent operations.
Non-Arm's-Length Transfer Pricing and Disguised Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: Vietnam tax authority may adjust prices to the arm's-length standard, disallow excess interest and royalty deductions, reclassify disguised distributions, impose 20% penalty on additional CIT assessed, charge 0.03% daily late payment interest, and issue criminal referral for intentional manipulation. Excessive interest deductions are additionally capped by thin capitalization rules under Decree 20/2017/ND-CP.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who knowingly certify false related-party transaction prices are personally liable for additional tax and penalties within the scope of their supervision. They may face asset freezing, exit bans, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion where the TP adjustment is accompanied by deliberate false documentation.
AML / Payment Rails Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the Law on Anti-Money Laundering 2022, administrative fines for failure to implement AML, KYC, and reporting obligations may reach VND 500,000,000, in addition to suspension of digital payment channels. Money laundering under Penal Code Article 324 carries fines up to VND 5,000,000,000 and imprisonment up to 15 years. Payment processors may terminate the LLC's merchant accounts for AML non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML compliance officers who knowingly structure or fail to report suspicious payment behavior are personally exposed to criminal fines, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 15 years. The corporate veil is pierced in cases of deliberate misuse of the payment rails; directors also face civil restitution for illegally generated revenue.
Abusive DTA Relief and Erroneous FCT Withholding

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will deny treaty relief, require full domestic FCT withholding (effective 15% on royalties, 5% on interest), assess late-payment interest, and impose a 20% penalty on the additional tax. Fraudulent abuse of DTAs may escalate to tax evasion prosecution with fines and imprisonment, and the payment itself may be redetermined as taxable profit of the Vietnamese LLC.

Director personal liability: The CFO, legal representative, and any officer certifying false TRCs or beneficial-ownership declarations are personally liable for the unpaid withholding tax and penalties. They may be banned from leaving Viet Nam and may face criminal imprisonment for forging tax documents or making false statements to tax authorities.
