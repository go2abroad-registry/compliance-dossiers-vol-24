Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs & Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu (Port Vila); official portal: customs.gov.vu. Responsibility covers VAT administration, excise, and customs duties. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately incorporates and supervises International Companies through licensed registered agents.

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no income tax, so no corporate income-tax TIN is issued; the operative identifier is the VFSC incorporation number for an International Company (IC). VAT identification numbers are procured as follows: (1) Incorporate the IC under the International Companies Act 1992 via a VFSC-licensed registered agent (no minimum capital, 100% non-resident ownership permitted, registered office in Port Vila or Luganville); (2) If the IC will make taxable supplies within Vanuatu or wishes to claim input VAT credits on local purchases, file the prescribed VAT registration application with the Department of Customs & Inland Revenue, appointing a resident tax representative when no local office exists; (3) The Department validates the constitutional documents, registered-agent confirmation, and premises evidence, then issues a VAT registration certificate and VAT identification number; (4) The IC activates the VAT ID by issuing sequentially numbered tax invoices from the effective registration date.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. An International Company is a non-resident, tax-exempt limited company: exemption from corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding taxes, stamp duty, and inheritance/gift taxes, with a statutory 20-year immunity from future Vanuatu taxation after incorporation. The IC may not carry on business with Vanuatu residents, own land, or transact domestically in Vatu; all income is offshore-source and, when supplied goods/services are exported, falls outside the domestic VAT net.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist because Vanuatu has no direct tax regime. Elective mechanisms available: (i) voluntary VAT registration below the statutory threshold to recover input VAT on export-support costs; (ii) redomiciliation/continuation of a foreign or domestic company as an International Company, or reverse redomiciliation, subject to VFSC approval under the International Companies Act 1992; (iii) an IC may obtain a written compliance ruling from the Department confirming the zero-rating or exemption treatment of a defined export program.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover for supplies of goods or VUV 3,000,000 annual taxable turnover for supplies of services, measured on a rolling 12-month prospective test in Vanuatu Vatu (VUV). An IC engaged exclusively in offshore export activity will generally remain below threshold absent a domestic supply nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods (FPV racing drones, carbon-fiber airframes, brushless motors, spare parts): zero-rated where the supplier exports, or supplies to an exporter who exports, the goods from Vanuatu for delivery to a non-resident, with physical export completed within 28 days of supply (or an extended period approved by the Commissioner) and freight evidenced to a final destination outside Vanuatu. Services: zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and the benefit or use is wholly outside Vanuatu (e.g., international logistics, engineering design for overseas assembly, software downloads, warranty support), or where services are supplied directly in connection with exported movable goods. The exporter must retain conclusive export evidence to support the 0% rate and any input VAT refund claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (finalized Vanuatu Customs export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (or courier manifest for postal/express exports) evidencing physical export and destination
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit confirmation of foreign-currency payment settlement

No real-time fiscal clearance, no electronic fiscal device mandate, no API-based clearance, and no digital stamping regime. Registered VAT suppliers must issue sequentially numbered tax invoices (paper, PDF, or ERP-generated) containing the prescribed particulars: supplier name, address, VAT ID, invoice date, description, quantity, unit price, VAT amount, and a visible 0% notation for zero-rated exports. VAT returns are filed electronically through the Department's online portal; invoices and underlying records must be retained for the statutory retention period and produced on audit.

The Department of Customs & Inland Revenue reconciles zero-rating claims against customs export entries, freight documentation, and banking settlement records. Unsupported or non-exported supplies are re-assessed at 12.5% VAT with statutory late-payment interest, a late-payment penalty, and a civil penalty commonly set up to 150% of the tax shortfall for fraud or gross negligence; criminal prosecution under the Value Added Tax Act and Customs Act may result in significant fines and terms of imprisonment. Excess input VAT refunds are withheld pending audit verification and are clawed back with penalties where the underlying export evidence fails. An International Company that abuses its exemption through prohibited domestic trading is exposed to VFSC strike-off, loss of statutory tax-exempt status, and personal civil liability of directors.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains an open currency regime for both current and capital accounts. The Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for most transactions, and profit remittances by International Companies to non-residents are generally permitted without quantitative restrictions. Capital account inflows and outflows above material thresholds require approval from an authorised dealer bank or the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu, but no prior foreign exchange regime licence is required for equity injection or trade disbursements. The practical constraint is bank-level compliance rather than statutory convertibility limitations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory percentage conversion ratio for export proceeds in Vanuatu. The Exchange Control Act imposes an obligation on resident exporters to offer foreign currency to an authorised dealer within one month of receipt unless an extension is granted; International Companies are generally treated as non-resident entities and may retain export proceeds in a designated foreign currency account without surrender to the Central Bank. For commercial goods leaving Vanuatu, the exporter must repatriate payment within 180 days of shipment and maintain supporting shipping documentation for bank verification.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation, no arm's-length documentation requirements, and no Local File/Master File concept. The de facto threshold is VT 0; related-party sales of drone components and intercompany IP licences are documented only under the IC's general books-and-records duty and the registered agent's AML/KYC file.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act No. 9 of 2024 and the 2025 Regulation Order impose substance obligations only on ICs undertaking defined relevant activities: banking, insurance, investment fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution and service centres, holding companies, and IP exploitation. Carbon-fibre milling, brushless-motor integration, and retail sale of FPV racing drones are manufacturing and trading activities and do not constitute a relevant activity unless the IC separately contracts as a Vanuatu-licensed distribution or service centre for affiliated foreign entities. A pure third-party e-commerce IC satisfies the regime with a registered office, a resident registered agent, and books and records maintained in Vanuatu; no local employment or physical premises are required.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abuse of IC Tax-Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of the s.138 exemption; retrospective assessment and clawback of all taxes that would have applied had the entity been a domestic Vanuatu company; criminal fine up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years on conviction under s.130.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorised local trading are jointly and severally liable for clawed-back taxes and fines; courts may order civil restitution of offshore profits, impose travel bans, and sentence directors to imprisonment up to 2 years.
Shell Company / Registry Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Late fee of VT 25,000 plus VT 5,000 per month; compulsory strike-off and dissolution under ss.104–105; bank balances, receivables, and IP assets vest in the State; restoration requires a court order, payment of all arrears, and fresh AML vetting.

Director personal liability: Directors of a struck-off company lose standing to defend proceedings in Vanuatu courts; they become personally liable for the IC's outstanding obligations, may be disqualified from holding office for up to 5 years, and face travel restrictions when the VFSC files enforcement proceedings.
Economic Substance Misrepresentation / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2024 (up to VT 5,000,000 per default), mandatory deregistration of the IC, nullification of the false certificate, and compulsory reporting of the misrepresentation to foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors, registered agents, and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for penalties; criminal conviction carries imprisonment up to 5 years, travel bans, passport surrender, and confiscation of the IC's assets.
AML / Money Laundering on Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Money laundering conviction: imprisonment up to 20 years and fines at the court's discretion; corporate AML compliance breaches: penalties up to VT 25,000,000; VFSC may freeze e-money accounts, issue transaction blocking orders, and order civil confiscation of laundered funds.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for directors and beneficial owners who knowingly control laundering operations; they face personal criminal prosecution, civil restitution and disgorgement, travel bans, asset-freezing orders, and imprisonment up to 20 years.
