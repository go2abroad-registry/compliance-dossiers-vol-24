Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Department (Department of Customs and Inland Revenue), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management — official portal: https://www.gov.vu and customs/revenue single-window via ASYCUDA World at https://customs.invanuatu.vu

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is a tax-exempt offshore vehicle excluded from the domestic revenue net; it is not eligible or required to obtain a Vanuatu TIN/VAT ID for pure offshore dropshipping operations conducted exclusively with non-residents. Any foreign entity that instead carries on domestic taxable activity must apply to the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Department on Form VAT-REG (Application for Registration), attach the certificate of incorporation, a Business Licence application, evidence of projected taxable turnover, and a resident-agent appointment; where annual taxable turnover exceeds or is reasonably expected to exceed VT 4,000,000, VAT registration is mandatory, and the Commissioner issues a TIN printed on the VAT registration certificate. An IC that illegally engages in domestic supplies may be removed from the exempt registry and retrospectively subjected to the same registration obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu has no check-the-box entity-classification election, no hybrid-mismatch election, no CFC attribution regime, and no fiscal consolidation election. The IC's tax-exempt status is statutory, automatic, and unconditional upon valid incorporation under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; it cannot be waived, opted out of, or varied by shareholder resolution. The only elective feature in the indirect tax system is voluntary VAT registration for a separate domestic trading entity with turnover below the VT 4,000,000 threshold, which is inapplicable to a properly licensed IC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration where the value of taxable supplies in any 12-month period exceeds or is reasonably expected to exceed VT 4,000,000 (approximately USD 33,000); voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold only with Commissioner's consent.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the VAT Act, the export of goods is zero-rated at 0% where the supplier removes the goods from Vanuatu within 28 days of the supply and retains transport documentation; international services supplied to a non-resident for use wholly outside Vanuatu also qualify. Because an IC is wholly exempt from VAT and cannot lawfully make local taxable supplies, it does not charge VAT on export dropshipping flows and has no VAT refund entitlement. The 0% export mechanism is relevant only where a separate registered Vanuatu trading company routes physical goods through Vanuatu customs territory, in which case the exporter must substantiate that it acted as principal, that the goods left Vanuatu within 28 days, and that the customer is a non-resident.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic declaration with unique consignment reference and port departure stamp)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing despatch to the foreign customer within 28 days
  • Commercial tax invoice describing the goods and confirming zero-rating status
  • Packing list and shipping carrier manifest proving physical exit from Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or bank-issued payment evidence connecting sale proceeds to the invoiced export

No mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, tax-stamping, SAF-T, Peppol, or state-certified API filing regime is in force in Vanuatu as of the current tax year. Tax invoices are issued in paper or PDF form and must state 'tax invoice', supplier name and TIN, sequential invoice number, date, description, quantity, VAT-exclusive amount, VAT rate, VAT charged, and total price. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through ASYCUDA World; however, an IC engaged in automated dropshipping with no goods landing in Vanuatu customs territory has no local invoicing, clearance, or stamping trigger.

The Revenue and Customs Commissioner may audit VAT and export records within 5 years of the end of the relevant accounting period under the VAT Act. Where zero-rating is claimed on an export that did not genuinely occur, or where exported goods are diverted back into the local market, the Commissioner may raise an assessment at the standard 12.5% rate on the full supply value, add default interest, and impose civil penalties of up to 150% of the VAT evaded; fraudulent documentation additionally triggers criminal prosecution with fines and imprisonment of up to 5 years. For a Vanuatu IC, any violation of the International Companies Act by conducting prohibited domestic dealings may cause forfeiture of the statutory tax exemption, retrospective application of business-license presumptive taxation, customs import duties on landed goods, and deregistration by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains liberal and de facto full convertibility of the Vatu (VUV) for both current and capital account transactions. The jurisdiction has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, meaning no exchange control restrictions apply to payments and transfers for current international transactions. Capital injections, profit remittances, dividends, and liquidation proceeds are freely repatriable without prior central bank approval or withholding tax. The only practical hurdles are standard commercial bank AML/KYC verification, source-of-funds documentation, and transaction threshold reporting to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None in active force. Vanuatu does not require exporters to surrender export proceeds to the central bank or any domestic bank. There is no mandatory conversion ratio, no statutory repatriation deadline, and exporters may retain receipts in VUV or any freely convertible foreign currency. The historical Exchange Control Act provisions are not applied to international companies, and no RBV approval is needed for offshore settlement.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the central bank and foreign exchange administration under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 61] and the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], together with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for licensing and AML/CFT supervision

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low feasibility for pure offshore EMI solutions. Vanuatu has no dedicated e-money institution (EMI) licensing framework; a foreign EMI holding or transmitting e-money locally would need to liaise with a licensed domestic bank for custody and integrate with local AML/CFT requirements. Offshore digital wallets have no direct access to local clearing rails and cannot be used as Virul substitute bank accounts. A workable structure is to use an onshore Vanuatu operational bank account as the funding anchor and an offshore merchant processor (e.g., Stripe/PayPal) solely as a receivables-collection tool with periodic settlement into the Vanuatu bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from shareholders are routed by SWIFT MT103 from the investor's overseas bank to the VU_IC Capital Escrow Account. The remittance field must identificate the beneficiary as 'VU_IC - International Company', the commercial purpose as 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' and, where requested, the BoP purpose code 'EC' (equity capital). The receiving bank will verify sanctions status, source of funds through financial institution wire statements, and beneficial ownership data. If the remittance exceeds local reporting thresholds, statutory FIU filings are triggered. No prior RBV approval or exchange-control permit is required; however, to prevent the tracker being treated as a director's loan, the client file must include a share subscription agreement, directors' resolution, certificate of incumbency, and confirmation the funds are credited to a non-interest or escrow-designated account pending allocation.Outbound supplier settlements are cleared through SWIFT MT103 for straightforward open-account payments and trade invoices, or through commercial letter-of-credit operations via SWIFT MT700 (issuance) and MT707 (amendments) where the supplier requires a documentary credit. Because VUV is not a heavily traded currency, cross-border disbursements are typically effected through USD, AUD, or EUR nostro accounts and then converted to the supplier's settlement currency at the bank's prevailing spot rate. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax on International Companies; no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax, and no minimum corporate tax. The schema-default 5-year loss carryforward is inapplicable because no CIT computation arises.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends or distributions made by an International Company to non-resident shareholders; dropshipping profits may be repatriated freely without Vanuatu fiscal formalities.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return due within 30 days after the anniversary of incorporation; the annual government fee is due on the anniversary date. A 60-day grace period applies before the Registrar of International Companies initiates strike-off.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Part 1 (Box 1-2) - Company Identification Exact legal name and VFSC/Registrar registration number of the International Company as shown on the Certificate of Incorporation. Non-resident owners must reproduce the name exactly as registered in Latin characters; mismatches with Stripe/PayPal or bank names trigger FATF-style screening failures. Late filing triggers a statutory surcharge of 10% of the annual fee; non-payment beyond the 60-day grace period empowers the Registrar to strike off the IC without further notice. Reinstatement requires all arrears, a reinstatement fee up to VUV 50,000, and agent approval. False statements in the annual return are punishable by fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years, with continuing daily default fines until corrected.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - Vanuatu has not enacted transfer-pricing legislation, Local File or Master File requirements, CbCR thresholds, or any VUV-denominated documentation threshold applicable to International Companies. Foreign tax authorities in customer and supplier markets may nonetheless apply OECD transfer-pricing and permanent-establishment tests to the ISIC 4791 model.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations analogous to BVI/Cayman; however, the International Companies Act [Cap 222] confines the IC to offshore business, and VFSC good-standing review, the AML/CTF Act 2014, and Stripe/PayPal underwriting impose substance expectations: genuine order documentation, supplier contracts, decision-making minutes, active payment-rail usage, and auditable profit attribution. Operation as a pure shell results in de facto shell treatment for fiscal-crime and payment-rail termination purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] and the Penal Code [Cap 135]: fines of up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years for false declarations; foreign tax penalties can reach 200% of the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil where the IC is a façade; directors are jointly and severally liable for full restitution of evaded taxes, face personal asset freezes, domestic and INTERPOL-coordinated travel bans, extradition under mutual legal assistance, and custody for the statutory term.
Shell Company Misuse and Deficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Registrar strike-off after 60-day fee default; VFSC revocation of good standing; reinstatement fee up to VUV 50,000 plus arrears; global MATCH-list blacklisting by Stripe and PayPal; retroactive revocation of the tax exemption for abuse of the IC regime.

Director personal liability: Directors who trade after strike-off are personally liable for all post-strike-off debts; disqualification orders, seizure of personal assets under civil recovery, and loss of personal banking relationships in OECD correspondent networks apply.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 11 of 2014: body corporate fines up to VUV 100,000,000; natural-person fines up to VUV 30,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 15 years for money-laundering predicates; confiscation and freezing orders over IC assets and payment-rail balances.

Director personal liability: Knowingly involved directors are individually criminally liable; personal bank and crypto-wallet balances are confiscable, travel bans are imposed pending extradition, imprisonment up to 15 years applies, and the director may be added to global de-risking lists.
Unlicensed Financial Services and Dropshipping Payment Fraud

Statutory sanction: Merchant account termination and MATCH-list blacklisting; VFSC fines up to VUV 5,000,000 for IC misuse; Penal Code fraud sanctions; foreign regulators may impose unlicensed money-transmitter penalties in customer markets.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for restitution to processors and consumers; civil judgments enforce against foreign assets; travel bans and border-stop orders may issue; asset forfeiture applies; imprisonment up to 12 years for financial fraud is available under the Penal Code.
