Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu does not maintain an income tax administration. The relevant authorities are (1) Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for International Company registration and licensing, www.vfsc.vu; (2) Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue (VCIR), a division of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, for customs and municipal business licence administration; and (3) municipal councils in Port Vila and Luganville for local business licences.

E-government filing portal

There is no statutory national TIN or VAT ID in Vanuatu. A foreign-owned International Company (IC) obtains its corporate identifier through the following procedure: (1) an approved VFSC registered agent files the incorporation documents and registered office details under the International Companies Act (Cap. 222); (2) the VFSC issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a VFSC Company Number; (3) if the IC maintains a physical presence in Vanuatu, it obtains a municipal business licence from the relevant local council; (4) if the IC imports or exports goods, it registers with Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue for customs processing and obtains an importer/exporter code; and (5) where a bank account is opened, the bank carries out customer due diligence and may record local tax identifiers of beneficial owners, but no central tax registration number is issued.

It is not subject to income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, VAT, or stamp duty on its offshore business transactions, provided it does not trade within Vanuatu or engage in domestic prohibited activities. — There is no statutory national TIN or VAT ID in Vanuatu. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: a Vanuatu International Company is a non-resident offshore entity treated as tax-exempt in Vanuatu. It is not subject to income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, VAT, or stamp duty on its offshore business transactions, provided it does not trade within Vanuatu or engage in domestic prohibited activities.

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no income tax and therefore no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch tax elections. An International Company may elect, by special resolution, to adopt or amend its constitutional documents, change its share structure, convert to a company limited by guarantee, or deregister and move to another jurisdiction. No national tax entity-classification election exists because all International Companies have a single tax-exempt classification; nevertheless, an IC may voluntarily register as a Vanuatu domestic company under the Companies Act if it wishes to carry on local commercial activity, which would then bring it into municipal licensing and customs obligations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None: Vanuatu does not operate a VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax system. The only indirect revenue measures are customs import duties, excise taxes on specified goods (tobacco, alcohol, fuel), stamp duty on immovable property transfers, and municipal business licence fees.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: there is no VAT/GST registration threshold because no VAT/GST is imposed. Any business trading in Vanuatu must obtain a municipal business licence from the first day of operation regardless of turnover, and any importer/exporter must register with Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: because Vanuatu has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating mechanism and no VAT refund substantiation process. Exports of automotive aftermarket parts and tuning accessories by an International Company are outside the Vanuatu indirect tax net and are not subject to any export sales tax. Customs requires a formal export declaration for goods leaving Vanuatu for statistical and enforcement purposes, but no VAT form or VAT zero-rate claim is required.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged through ASYCUDA World
  • Commercial invoice showing exporter, importer, description, quantity, and value
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or air waybill
  • Certificate of origin (if required by the destination customs authority or preferential trade claim)
  • Inward remittance or bank transfer record evidencing settlement of the export sale, where retained by the exporting IC's licensed Vanuatu bank

Vanuatu has no electronic invoicing mandate, no real-time fiscal clearance system, and no digital stamping requirement for invoices. Commercial transactions are documented with ordinary paper or electronic invoices. Customs import and export declarations are processed through the ASYCUDA World electronic customs management system, but this does not constitute a tax-authority pre-clearance or invoice-clearing regime for indirect tax purposes.

There is no export VAT zero-rating to audit, so no VAT clawback penalty exists. However, false customs declarations, undervaluation, misdescription, smuggling, or attempted exportation contrary to Vanuatu Customs legislation may result in seizure and forfeiture of the goods, monetary penalties based on evaded duties, criminal prosecution, and civil recovery by the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue. Fraudulent representations to the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission in connection with International Company registration can result in fines, de-registration of the IC, and disqualification of directors; beneficial owners making deliberately false statements may also face professional negligence or money-laundering related sanctions under the Vanuatu AML/CFT framework. Vanuatu imposes no statutory foreign-exchange repatriation or clawback deadline on export proceeds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal current-account convertibility regime; the Vatu is convertible for all bona fide trade and service transactions. Capital-account inflows and outflows, including shareholder capital injections, loan repurchases and certain portfolio investments, require either RBV prior approval or delegated approval through an authorised dealer. Profit remittance for a tax-exempt International Company is generally permissible without exchange-control tax clearance, provided the company can evidence beneficial ownership, source of funds, and compliance with all applicable anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing requirements. The principal practical hurdle is documentary proof of the underlying trade, not conversion capacity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds for online retail of automotive replacement parts and accessories must be repatriated to Vanuatu within 90 days from the date of export shipment, unless the exporter obtains prior deferred-payment approval from an authorised dealer. There is no mandatory conversion ratio into Vatu; exporters may retain foreign currency in approved foreign-currency accounts at a licensed commercial bank. However, the exporting entity must report all foreign-currency balances to the authorised dealer upon request and make the proceeds available for sale to the banking system if RBV deems it necessary in the national interest. Digital e-commerce proceeds are classified as current receipts and are subject to normal repatriation and record-keeping obligations, not special surrender licences.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) – Exchange Control Department, acting under the Vanuatu Exchange Control Act [Cap.173] and delegated authorised-dealer arrangements with licensed commercial banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium as a sole banking solution. Vanuatu does not currently operate a purpose-built e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime; non-bank fintechs may only undertake money-transfer activity under VFSC financial-dealer licensing and must demonstrate substantive physical presence. Offshore digital EMIs are therefore legally viable only as complementary settlement or disbursement gateways, not as primary custodians of treasury liquidity. The preferred architecture is a domestic commercial bank account as the principal account, with foreign EMIs used downstream solely for customer payouts or supplier payments in jurisdictions where such EMIs are licensed and regulated.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0: no local file or master file is required because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax. However, any material intercompany transaction with a related party in another jurisdiction should be supported by contemporaneous arm's-length documentation for the partner jurisdiction's transfer-pricing audit; the effective threshold is zero where a foreign tax authority may recharacterise the IC's commercial substance.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not maintain a statutory Economic Substance Regulation regime. However, VFSC requires each International Company to maintain a licensed registered agent and physical registered office in Vanuatu and to keep accounting records at that office. The anti-abuse doctrine treats inadequate substance (no assets, no staff, no income records) as a non-residence red flag, exposing the IC to strike-off, cancellation of certificate, or taxation in the shareholder jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (VAT and Customs Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Under the Customs and Excise Act and the Value Added Tax Act: forfeiture of goods, a civil penalty of up to 200% of the duty or tax evaded, a criminal fine of up to VT 10,000,000 (approximately USD 84,000), and imprisonment for up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Courts may disregard the IC and impose personal liability on each director who signed or directed the false entry for the full amount of unpaid duty and penalty; directors may also be barred from leaving Vanuatu pending restitution and are personally subject to imprisonment.
Shell Company (Unauthorised Domestic Trading)

Statutory sanction: In addition to a VFSC penalty of VT 2,500,000 per violation (approximately USD 21,000), the IC registration is struck off and the certificate cancelled; each prohibited contract becomes voidable and the corporation loses the benefit of the tax exemption.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly conducted domestic activities are jointly and severally liable for the company's debts and must restore all profits to creditors; the Court may impose a personal penalty of VT 1,000,000 and a travel ban for two years.
Anti-Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act: entity-level penalty of VT 5,000,000, officer penalties of VT 1,000,000, seizure of all proceeds, exclusion from any regulated payment rail (Stripe or PayPal), and referral to the Public Prosecutor for criminal money-laundering indictment.

Director personal liability: Officers and directors may face personal fines up to VT 2,000,000 and imprisonment up to ten years; where funds have been laundered, the Court can order recovery against personal assets and impose a travel ban pending determination.
Sanctions and Export Control Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu's Sanctions Act 2006 empowers the Minister to freeze accounts, seize goods, and impose an administrative fine of VT 15,000,000 (approximately USD 126,000); criminal conviction for deliberate violation carries imprisonment up to ten years.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the controlled transaction are personally liable for the amount frozen or forfeited; a director may be imprisoned for up to ten years and may be made subject to a departure prevention order pending trial.
