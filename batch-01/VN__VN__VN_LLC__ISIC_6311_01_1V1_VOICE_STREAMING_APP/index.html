Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office (i.e., a postal-only address without a physical desk/lease in Viet Nam) is NOT permissible for the registered head-office address of a foreign-invested LLC. The Enterprise Registration Certificate requires the exact address of the LLC's head office 'tru sở chính' located in Viet Nam territory, with a legally valid lease or ownership certificate and payment of local business taxes. However, a licensed shared-space or serviced-office arrangement (coworking operator providing a dedicated room/desk and lease invoice) is accepted by most DPIs if accompanied by (i) the operator's business registration extract and (ii) a notarized lease/service agreement with a term exceeding the incorporation window. For an interactive voice-streaming app relying on remote developers, the registered office may host administrative/back-office functions only; the content/service servers may be cloud-hosted offshore, but the legal seat must be a verifiable physical location in Viet Nam staffed during working hours, and the app operator must be able to produce records at that address within inspection timelines.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for a pure digital voice streaming social platform under the Law on Enterprise 2020 and Law on Investment 2020. The subscribed charter capital is freely agreed by the members and must fully be contributed within 90 days from the ERC issuance date. For foreign-invested projects, the investor must evidence financial capacity (bank statements or audited financials) at the IRC application stage. Funds contributed must be remitted to the Vietnamese legal entity's corporate bank account; an escrow arrangement is not mandatory but is commonly used in practice for inter-member capital commitments.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) of the Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Vietnam; central administrative and filing portal: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn (e-tax portal for tax declaration, assessment and payment); electronic invoice system portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; national business registration portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn; official English portal: https://www.gdt.gov.vn

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Obtain an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) for the foreign-invested project from the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) or the industrial-zone/EPZ management authority under Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 and Decree No. 31/2021/ND-CP. Step 2: Apply for enterprise registration via https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn to obtain the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC); under the integrated one-stop mechanism of Decree No. 01/2021/ND-CP, the enterprise code stated on the ERC is simultaneously the Tax Identification Number (MST / TIN). Step 3: Within 10 working days of ERC issuance, submit Tax Registration Declaration Form 01-ĐK-TCT through the e-tax portal https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn; the competent tax authority issues the Certificate of Tax Registration and opens the taxpayer account, which automatically registers the LLC for Value Added Tax (VAT, credit method), Corporate Income Tax (CIT), Personal Income Tax finalization duty, and electronic invoice obligations. Step 4: Register the electronic invoice template with the local tax office and activate the certified e-invoice application under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular No. 78/2021/TT-BTC. Step 5: Open a foreign-currency and Vietnamese-dong settlement account at a licensed bank in Vietnam, notify the tax authority of the banking details, and obtain the customs/import-export code if the entity undertakes cross-border movement of goods; the foreign-invested LLC receives a single consolidated tax code covering VAT, CIT, and related sub-codes for the head office, branches, and participating operations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the foreign-invested LLC is a Vietnamese resident enterprise under Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 14/2008/QH12 as amended, taxed at the entity level on worldwide income at the standard CIT rate of 20%; the owners do not file Vietnamese individual income tax on LLC profits and no pass-through or transparent treatment is available

Available election forms

Vietnam has no IRS-style check-the-box or hybrid transparent-entity election. The LLC is mandatorily an entity-level CIT taxpayer and VAT registrant. Statutory elections available: (i) VAT method election — an enterprise with annual revenue from sale of goods/services of VND 1 billion or less may elect the Direct Method (VAT payable at 1% to 3% of turnover) instead of the Credit Method; however, LLCs with full accounting books and invoices are required to apply the Credit Method; (ii) CIT incentive election — the taxpayer may elect to apply preferential CIT rates (10% or 17%) and exemption/reduction schedules for qualifying software production, high-technology, data-processing/hosting services (relevant to ISIC 6311), subject to the roadmap conditions in Decree No. 218/2013/ND-CP and Circular No. 96/2015/TT-BTC; (iii) investment incentive elections — new investment projects may elect between different CIT incentive roadmaps (2-year exemption and 4-year 50% reduction, or 4-year exemption and 9-year 50% reduction in qualifying zones/sectors); (iv) transfer-pricing documentation status election — filing of Form LT-STP-LTP, Form TP-01, and related elections for country-by-country reporting thresholds under Decree No. 132/2020/ND-CP; (v) hybrid mismatch alignment — no elective deduction is permitted for hybrid mismatch arrangements; interest expenses exceeding 30% of earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) are non-deductible under Decree No. 20/2017/ND-CP as amended by Decree No. 68/2020/ND-CP

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 100,000,000 in annual taxable turnover triggers compulsory VAT registration for household and individual businesses; a foreign-invested LLC must register for VAT from the moment of enterprise registration with no de minimis turnover threshold (effectively zero-threshold mandatory registration). VAT returns are filed monthly or quarterly depending on turnover; if output VAT in a period exceeds input VAT, VAT refund claims are processed under Articles 72-75 of Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export VAT status is available under Article 9 of Decree No. 209/2013/ND-CP as amended by Decree No. 49/2022/ND-CP and Section II.1, Article 9 of Circular No. 219/2013/TT-BTC. For the ISIC 6311 voice-streaming social application, the supply must satisfy all of the following evidential conditions: (i) the service is rendered to a foreign organization or individual that is not a Vietnamese resident (offshore end-consumer or the overseas app-store operator acting as distributor of record); (ii) the service is consumed wholly outside Vietnam, demonstrated by the end-customer's billing country, IP geolocation, SIM/device country codes, platform country-of-sale analytics reports, and the app's territorial terms of service; (iii) payment is received in a convertible foreign currency from outside Vietnam through a licensed bank, which includes remittances from Apple Inc., Apple International Distribution Limited, Google Ireland Ltd / Google Asia Pacific Pte Ltd after commission deduction, supported by platform payout statements; (iv) a written or electronic agreement exists with the overseas counterpart, including the Apple Developer Program License Agreement and Google Play Distribution Agreement; (v) the taxpayer retains documentation that the counterparty is non-resident (foreign corporate number, registered offshore office address, VAT/GST overseas registration); (vi) for services considered exported software/digital content, the LLC must maintain the IT service export dossiers required by Circular No. 48/2023/TT-BTC, including technical service descriptions and server logs evidencing offshore content delivery. Services supplied to Vietnamese resident end-customers, including in-app purchases made from Vietnamese App Store/Play Store accounts, cannot be zero-rated; such revenue bears 10% VAT. The LLC may reclaim input VAT and file for VAT refund of excess input tax where export zero-rated revenues exceed domestic taxable revenues, subject to quarterly or annual refund procedures and a pre-refund audit by the General Department of Taxation

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronically cleared through the VNACCS/VCIS customs system) for any embedded goods or hardware components
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill for goods, or a formal Services Delivery and Acceptance Certificate signed by the offshore customer or platform counterparty confirming that the streaming, chatroom, and virtual-gift services were fully delivered and consumed outside Vietnam
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (xác nhận giao dịch ngoại tệ) issued by a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank evidencing the actual receipt of convertible foreign currency from the offshore buyer or App Store marketplace into the LLC's Vietnamese foreign-currency account
  • Commercial invoice and the executed service or digital-content distribution agreement with the foreign purchaser of record (including Apple Developer Program License Agreement and Google Play Distribution Agreement)
  • Bank statements and foreign-exchange allocation records reconciling the specific imported foreign currency amount against each export invoice number and the corresponding VAT zero-rating period
  • Platform sales-analytics and geolocation evidence (country-of-sale reports, IP addresses, Apple Store and Google Play financial reports) demonstrating that customer consumption occurred outside Vietnam
  • Withholding tax or tax identification certificates issued by the foreign payer (e.g., IRS Form W-8BEN-E, Irish Revenue VAT certificate for Apple, Singapore IRAS certificate for Google) establishing the non-resident status of the counterparty
  • Export VAT zero-rate supporting ledger and export file index prepared in accordance with Circular No. 219/2013/TT-BTC and Tax Administration Law No. 38/2019/QH14

Mandatory electronic invoicing effective 1 July 2022 under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular No. 78/2021/TT-BTC. All sales invoices, including export invoices, must be issued through a certified e-invoice application connected to the GDT e-invoice portal. The application transmits invoice data to the tax authority in real time or within one working day; the GDT generates an invoice verification code (mã của cơ quan thuế) for consumer-facing e-invoices, and the code must be printed or digitally embedded on the invoice (digital stamping). Invoices for export of services may be self-issued without a tax-authority verification code when the taxpayer has registered the e-invoice template and the export conditions are met, but the un-coded invoice must still be transmitted to the GDT portal for data acceptance within the reporting period. API integration: the taxable person or its e-invoice service provider uses the published web-service API of the GDT e-invoice system (HoaDonDienTu.GDT.Gov.Vn) for template registration, invoice issuance, replacement, adjustment, cancellation, and encipherment; failure to transmit within the prescribed period results in rejection of the invoice code and imposition of administrative fines up to VND 20,000,000 per violation

The General Department of Taxation and Customs Administration jointly audit export zero-rating claims with heightened scrutiny on digital-service exporters using app-store distribution. Where the statutory documentation bundle is incomplete, fabricated, or where the remitted foreign currency is not allocated to specific export invoices, the tax authority retrospectively treats the revenue as domestic supply at 10% VAT, disallows the zero-rating, claws back any previously refunded input VAT, and reassesses the output VAT shortfall. Legal consequences under Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14 and Decree No. 125/2020/ND-CP include: (i) late-payment interest at 0.03% per day on all underpaid amounts from the original statutory due date until full payment; (ii) a fine of 20% of the underdeclared/underpaid tax where the error was not fraudulent; (iii) a fine of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount for intentional tax evasion, with the multiplier depending on aggravating circumstances; (iv) administrative fines up to VND 60,000,000 for invoice and documentation violations; (v) suspension of the taxpayer's e-invoice verification code and removal from the e-invoice system; (vi) publication of the taxpayer on the public non-compliant taxpayer list; (vii) criminal prosecution for tax evasion under Article 200 of the Penal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 as amended, carrying fines of up to VND 20,000,000,000 and custodial sentences of up to 20 years for organized fraud, and penalties for money laundering under Article 324 where proceeds are disguised; (viii) retrospective clawback periods of 5 years for VAT underdeclaration and 10 years for tax fraud; and (ix) joint liability of legal representatives and tax-compliance officers where false certificates of offshore consumption were knowingly issued. If foreign currency export earnings are not repatriated to Vietnam within 180 days without an approved State Bank of Vietnam extension, the LLC is also exposed to foreign-exchange sanctions under Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN as amended and Decree No. 88/2019/ND-CP, including fines and suspension of foreign-currency accounts

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% corporate income tax on dividends distributed by a Vietnamese LLC to its corporate shareholder (dividends are paid from after-tax profits). Individual shareholders receiving dividends pay 5% personal income tax. Branch profit remittance tax: 0% for most sectors, but 5% may apply for certain natural resource activities.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual finalization return: within 90 days after the end of the fiscal year (e.g., by March 30 if fiscal year ends December 31). Quarterly provisional tax returns: within 30 days after the end of each quarter. The final CIT payment must be made no later than the finalization deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VND 30 billion aggregate related-party transaction value, or VND 50 billion total revenue, or VND 3 billion value of related-party service payments/interest; however, all taxpayers with related-party transactions must complete Form 01/TKQ regardless of threshold. Local File and Master File required if thresholds exceeded. Country-by-Country Report filing applies for groups with consolidated revenue ≥ VND 18 trillion (approx. EUR 750 million).

Economic substance scope: Decree 132/2020/ND-CP requires that related-party transactions reflect actual commercial substance, not merely legal form. Tax authorities may disregard arrangements without economic substance (e.g., shell offices, back-to-back arrangements, no personnel/assets/risk). Functional analysis must demonstrate that the Vietnamese LLC performs material functions (development, marketing, customer service, content moderation, user acquisition) and bears real economic risks. Profit allocation must align with the value-contributing activities. The Global Minimum Tax (Pillar Two / QDMTT) applies to MNEs with global revenue ≥ EUR 750 million, imposing top-up tax to 15% effective in Vietnam from January 2024.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent CIT Declaration

Statutory sanction: Penal Code Art. 200: fine up to VND 4.5 billion and/or imprisonment 6 months to 7 years. Administrative penalty: 100–200% of evaded tax plus interest at 0.03%/day. Tax authority may enforce asset freezes and VAT invoice cancellation.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives and beneficial owners are criminally liable if they knowingly orchestrate evasion. Directors may face imprisonment, travel bans, and personal civil liability for restitution of evaded taxes. Corporate veil may be pierced where the LLC is a mere instrumentality.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authorities may recharacterize intercompany pricing, adjust taxable income upward, impose a 20% penalty on the additional underpaid tax, and assess 0.03% per day late-payment interest. For thin-capitalization (interest expense exceeding 30% of EBITDA), disallowed and reclassified. Documentation failures trigger fines up to VND 50 million.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve fraudulent TP policies can be held personally liable for penalties if they knowingly caused false filings. Criminal liability may attach under Penal Code for tax evasion if TP adjustments exceed thresholds. Travel bans and asset seizures possible during audit.
Failure to Withhold Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT)

Statutory sanction: Withholding agent is liable for unpaid FCT plus 20% penalty and 0.03%/day interest. If Apple/Google are treated as the seller and the LLC is merely reselling, the whole revenue may be reclassified as Vietnamese-source and subject to CIT. Penalties up to VND 100 million for incorrect withholding.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable if they knowingly fail to withhold. Criminal tax evasion charges may be brought if the failure is part of a scheme to reduce Vietnamese tax, leading to imprisonment and restitution.
Shell Company / Substance Deficiency (Anti-Avoidance)

Statutory sanction: Under Decree 132 and GAAR principles, the tax authority may disregard the entity or reattribute profits to the Vietnamese economic employer. Additional CIT up to 20% (or 100-200% if fraudulent), plus interest. The LLC may also face revocation of IRC/ERC for non-compliance with investment law.

Director personal liability: Shareholders/directors who intentionally maintain an artificial structure to evade tax may have the corporate veil pierced; they become personally liable for tax debts and penalties. Travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and criminal prosecution under Penal Code Art. 200 or Art. 217 (false registration) apply.
Money Laundering / AML Red Flags in Voice Streaming

Statutory sanction: Penal Code Art. 324: imprisonment 3–15 years and fines up to VND 5 billion for money laundering. Administrative AML fines under Decree 11/2023/ND-CP up to VND 500 million. The enterprise may be forced to terminate operations; licenses revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face criminal indictment if they knowingly allowed laundering activities. The corporate veil can be pierced for fraud, subjecting personal assets. Travel bans and extradition treaties with foreign authorities may be triggered.
Global Minimum Tax (Pillar Two) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Enforcement of 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT) under Resolution 711/2023/QH15. Penalties for non-payment: late-payment interest 0.03%/day and administrative fines. The parent jurisdiction may impose its own IIR/Pillar Two top-up if Vietnam does not collect.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for ensuring that the top-up tax is properly calculated and reported; failure can result in civil liability to the group, administrative fines, and travel restrictions pending resolution of tax debt.
