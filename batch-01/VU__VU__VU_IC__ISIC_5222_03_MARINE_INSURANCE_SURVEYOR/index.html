Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue Authority (VRA) (successor to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue); official fiscal portal: https://www.vra.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

International Companies (ICs) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] are statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, and VAT and are not required to obtain a TIN or VAT registration number. The sole fiscal identifier is the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and IC registration number. If an IC anomalously undertakes taxable supplies in Vanuatu, it must submit the VAT registration application (Form VAT-1) to the VRA together with constitutional documents, registered-office certification, and evidence of the Vanuatu supply, with the VRA issuing a VAT registration number within approximately 10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — International Companies (ICs) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] are statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, and VAT and are not required to obtain a TIN or VAT registration number. The sole fiscal identifier is the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and IC registration number. If an IC anomalously undertakes taxable supplies in Vanuatu, it must submit the VAT registration application (Form VAT-1) to the VRA together with constitutional documents, registered-office certification, and evidence of the Vanuatu supply, with the VRA issuing a VAT registration number within approximately 10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or entity-classification election exists under Vanuatu law. An IC that wishes to abandon the exempt offshore zone may apply to the VFSC to convert into an ordinary local company, thereby becoming subject to VAT and local licensing obligations; otherwise the exempt status is automatic and irrevocable while the IC remains in good standing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 7,000,000 taxable turnover per 12-month period (mandatory registration); an International Company performing exclusively offshore marine survey services falls entirely outside the Vanuatu VAT net and is not required to register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of services rendered to a non-resident recipient (offshore marine underwriter or reinsurer) and performed substantially outside Vanuatu qualify for zero-rating as export services. A marine cargo and hull claims surveyor must substantiate the non-resident status of the insurer, the location of the vessel or cargo, the offshore delivery of the survey report, and the receipt of consideration from abroad. The International Company structure is deemed to operate wholly offshore and therefore invoices without charging VAT rather than claiming refunds; a voluntarily registered local company may zero-rate qualifying surveys and reclaim attributable input VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (required only where surveying involves temporary export of equipment or goods; not required for pure intangible marine survey services)
  • Bill of Lading (applicable where the engagement includes cargo/equipment movement verification; otherwise replaced by the survey assignment contract and vessel/cargo location certificate)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/telegraphic transfer confirmation evidencing foreign-currency payment repatriated from the offshore underwriter

None. Vanuatu does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance regime, continuous-transaction-control (CTC) network, API-based e-invoicing platform, or digital stamping system. Conventional paper or PDF invoices suffice; the VRA may require production of underlying export contracts, foreign-currency remittance advices, survey assignment logs, and vessel/cargo location evidence during audit.

Zero-rating claims are presumed to be domestic taxable supplies absent contemporaneous documentation. At audit, the VRA may reassess VAT at 12.5% with statutory interest and late-payment penalties of up to 200% of the tax due, claw back any input VAT credits claimed on survey vessels or testing equipment, and impose criminal sanctions for fraudulent evasion, including substantial fines and terms of imprisonment. For International Companies, the VFSC may additionally suspend or strike off the licence for tax misdeclaration or participation in a Vanuatu-titled supply chain.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains an open, fully convertible current account and a liberalized capital account with no statutory impediments to cross-border payments. The Vatu (VUV) is a floating currency managed by the RBV against a trade-weighted basket of major partner currencies (USD, AUD, EUR, GBP, JPY). There are no RBV approvals, quotas, or prior-authorization requirements for trade settlement, foreign direct investment, dividends, or profit repatriation. The only practical friction consists of commercial bank documentation checks (invoices, beneficial ownership declarations, and source-of-funds evidence); tax-exempt International Companies are not required to obtain tax clearance from the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department for remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil. Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export proceeds. Marine insurance surveying fee receivables may be retained indefinitely in VUV or held in foreign-currency accounts (USD, AUD, EUR, GBP) without RBV consent.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — sole central bank and monetary authority administering the Vatu and the national payments system; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) exercises ancillary supervisory oversight over licensed insurance intermediaries but holds no FX regulatory mandate.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility as a primary treasury rail. Vanuatu-domiciled International Companies are not supported by most offshore EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, Currencycloud) due to the perceived offshore financial center risk profile; none currently issue VUV-denominated local account details, and VUV itself is not a clearing currency on these platforms. An EMI account in AUD/USD/GBP may be used only as a supplementary collection or disbursement channel after substantive domestic bank onboarding is completed, and only where the EMI expressly accepts Pacific island OFC entities.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
CIT Form Code: N/A No taxable income schedule. The VFSC Annual Return records company name, IC number, registered office, resident agent, registered address, director and shareholder particulars, and a confirmation that the company has not carried on prohibited activities with Vanuatu residents. Non-resident shareholders and directors need not supply any local tax computation. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No threshold applicable in Vanuatu vatu; Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and has not enacted local-file or master-file documentation thresholds. If a Vanuatu IC is challenged by a foreign tax administration, contemporaneous records should still demonstrate arm's-length pricing to avoid recharacterization.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not maintain a domestic Economic Substance Regulations regime for International Companies. An IC must nevertheless maintain continuous substance through a registered office, resident agent, licensed marine surveying activity records, corporate records, and actual decision-making. Foreign tax authorities may apply CFC, controlled-foreign-company, or anti-hybrid rules to attack an IC lacking real economic substance; VFSC licensing and local board oversight are the primary Vanuatu-side substance controls.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Foreign Recharacterization

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu itself imposes no income tax penalty, but foreign tax authorities may recharacterize profits as deemed taxable income of shareholders or a permanent establishment, impose understated-tax penalties, interest, and back taxes, and apply general anti-avoidance provisions. In Vanuatu, false declarations to the VFSC in support of the tax-exempt status attract fines and cancellation of the IC registration.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in orchestrating sham arrangements may be subject to foreign joint-and-several liability for corporate taxes, penalties, and interest; they may face personal criminal tax prosecution, travel bans, and imprisonment in the foreign jurisdiction where the economic activity occurred.
Shell Company / Abusive Offshore Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Vancuatu VFSC may refuse license renewal, strike the company off, or dissolve it under the International Companies Act. The company may also be exposed to foreign compulsory deregistration under shell-company and economic-substance enforcement regimes.

Director personal liability: Directors and nominee directors may be disqualified, ordered to pay restitution to creditors or government agencies, and subjected to money-laundering charges where the shell structure is used to move illicit premiums or fraudulent claims.
Unlicensed Insurance Surveying and Marine Claims Adjusting

Statutory sanction: Operating without required licensing under Vanuatu financial services and insurance laws attracts monetary penalties, injunctive relief, prohibition orders, and revocation of any affiliated license. Repeat or fraudulent conduct may lead to criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for unlawful revenue received, civil fines, and court costs; serious cases involving fraudulent surveys supporting inflated insurance claims can result in custodial sentences and travel-restriction orders.
AML / CFT Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu AML/CFT regime and international bench requirements, licensed entities must conduct CDD, beneficial-ownership verification, and suspicious-transaction reporting; breaches attract substantial fines, licence suspension or revocation, asset restraint, and regulatory winding-up.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly permit AML breaches may be prosecuted individually, fined, imprisoned, banned from managing companies in Vanuatu, and subjected to personal asset seizure and travel bans pending investigation.
False Filings and Misrepresentation to VFSC

Statutory sanction: The VFSC may impose fines, revoke the certificate of incorporation, publish the strike-off, and refer the matter to the Public Prosecutor. The International Companies Act treats knowing false submissions as a criminal offense with fine and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false returns are personally liable for fines, statutory restitution, disqualification as a director, and imprisonment; if the discovered misconduct caused third-party loss, the corporate veil may be pierced for civil damages.
