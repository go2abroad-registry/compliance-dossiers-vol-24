Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a foreign-invested LLC providing office-based UI/UX design subscription services under ISIC 7410. The charter capital is freely declared by the members in Vietnamese Dong; it must be fully contributed within ninety (90) days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47, Law on Enterprises 2020). No escrow account or pre-funded bank verification account is required by the registry at incorporation; however, contributed capital must be remitted through a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam and recorded in the Company's statutory accounting books. If the enterprise later elects export processing enterprise (EPE) status, investment capital contribution is subject to the compliance schedule under the Law on Investment and its implementing decrees.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Prevention and Fighting clearance administered by the provincial Fire Prevention and Rescue Police under Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting and Rescue No. 56/2024/QH15 and Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP (as amended by Decree No. 83/2024/ND-CP). The Company must hold or rely on the lessor's certificate of fire safety acceptance for the building, maintain fire extinguishers, emergency exits and adequate lighting, and evidence an annual fire escape drill and staff training register. Occupational safety clearance is registered with the provincial Department of Labour, Invalids and Social Affairs under Law No. 84/2015/QH13, including ergonomic evaluation of computer workstations, annual health examination of employees, and designation of an occupational safety officer. No structural load inspection is required because the Company undertakes no structural modification of the leased premises; if the Company modifies load-bearing structures or mezzanines, a construction permit and technical appraisal by the Department of Construction would become mandatory under Decree No. 15/2021/ND-CP.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam; primary business portals: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (electronic tax declaration and payment) and https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn (national e-invoice clearance portal); enterprise incorporation flanks via the National Enterprise Registration Information System at https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn operated by the Ministry of Planning and Investment.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested Limited Liability Company receives its statutory tax identification number (Mã số thuế, 10-digit MST) automatically upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate by the provincial Department of Planning and Investment or the relevant Economic Zone / Industrial Zone Authority; no separate tax authority pre-approval is required. Procedure: (1) obtain the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) via https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn, processing within 3–15 working days; the ERC states the MST, which is simultaneously activated in the GDT taxpayer registry; (2) within 10 working days of the ERC, file supplementary tax registration information, open a foreign-currency and VND bank account at a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank, and declare the legal representative and authorized signatories; (3) obtain a qualified e-signature certificate (chữ ký số) from a licensed certification authority; (4) register the e-invoice issuing method through a certified e-invoice service provider or direct GDT portal API; (5) register the VAT declaration cycle (monthly or quarterly where annual revenue is below VND 50 billion) and the corporate income tax finalization cycle; (6) pay the annual business license fee (Lệ phí môn bài) under Decree 22/2020/ND-CP — VND 2,000,000 for charter capital ≤ VND 10 billion, VND 3,000,000 above, VND 1,000,000 per branch, with first-year exemption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Vietnam administers no US-style check-the-box election and no domestic hybrid-mismatch election; a foreign-invested LLC is per se a resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide income at the standard 20% corporate income tax rate. Available statutory elections and mechanisms include: (i) VAT computation method election — the credit (deduction) method is compulsory for enterprises keeping full accounting records, while the direct-on-added-value method is statutorily available only to micro taxpayers with revenue below the threshold; (ii) election to offset prior-year tax losses against future taxable income within 5 consecutive years (non-discretionary election to preserve the loss benefit); (iii) foreign tax credit election under an applicable double taxation treaty, filed via Form 01/TNT with GDT confirmation; (iv) for related-party transactions, election to prepare a simplified transfer-pricing file under Circular 132/2020/TT-BTC when intercompany transactions and Belgian-style thresholds are met; (v) no election to be treated as a transparent entity exists, and the 30% EBITDA interest deduction cap applies mandatorily where financing is used.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 0 for all limited liability companies — every foreign-invested LLC, regardless of forecast turnover, must register for VAT immediately upon incorporation and file periodic VAT returns. The only statutory turnover exemption is VND 100,000,000 per year, and it applies exclusively to household businesses and individual sole proprietors, not to LLC legal entities. A quarterly VAT declaration window is available where annual revenue is below VND 50 billion, but registration itself is unconditional.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to UI/UX design and digital design subscription services (ISIC 7410) supplied to a foreign customer if and only if all statutory conditions of Article 9, Decree 209/2013/ND-CP and Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC are cumulatively met: (i) the service recipient is a foreign organization or individual that does not have a permanent establishment in Vietnam in respect of the subscribed design services; (ii) the design service is actually consumed outside Vietnam — the deliverable library, design system, UI asset files, and UX specifications must be deployed or used for the client's operations outside Vietnamese territory, evidenced by acceptance certificates, IP assignment/licenses, time-stamped digital handover records (e.g., Figma, Jira, GitHub access logs), and overseas project correspondence; (iii) payment is received from abroad in convertible foreign currency through a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank, with clean SWIFT/MT103 inflows traceable to the foreign client; subscription settlements routed via Stripe Billing or Wise must ultimately land in the LLC's foreign-currency account at a Vietnamese bank — if the collection chain is deemed to occur in Vietnam in VND through a local payment aggregator, the 0% condition fails and 10% VAT is due; (iv) the LLC maintains a complete export-service dossier containing the subscription agreement, e-invoice, acceptance evidence, and foreign currency remittance certificates. Input VAT attributable to the exported service is fully creditable and refundable on a monthly/quarterly basis, with refund claims subject to pre-refund GDT examination. Services performed in Vietnam for consumption in Vietnam — such as local user-testing workshops for a domestic audience — do not qualify for 0% and are taxed at 10%.

  • Export service contract (Hợp đồng dịch vụ xuất khẩu) with the foreign subscriber, specifying the scope of UI/UX subscription deliverables, offshore consumption, payment currency, and settlement rails
  • Recurring e-invoice for each subscription billing cycle issued in conformity with Decree 123/2020/ND-CP, showing 0% VAT and a clear reference to the underlying export contract
  • Certificate of completion / acceptance handover (Biên bản nghiệm thu) electronically signed or acknowledged by the foreign client, evidencing receipt and offshore utilization of design deliverables
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or original bank statement issued by the Vietnamese commercial bank confirming receipt of each subscription payment in convertible foreign currency from an overseas bank account within the statutory period
  • Proof of offshore consumption: time-stamped deliverable access logs (Figma, GitHub, Miro), IP transfer or license documents, cloud deployment certificates, and client-side correspondence demonstrating utilization outside Vietnamese territory
  • For any physical deliverables or software media that accompany the electronic design subscription: customs export declaration (Tờ khai hải quan), commercial packing list, bill of lading or airway bill

Mandatory since 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. Every recurrent design-subscription invoice must be an e-invoice in GDT-prescribed XML/TXML format bearing a qualified digital signature and a sequential invoice-symbol/number. Two clearance paths exist: (a) e-invoices with Tax Authority Code — invoice data is transmitted via a certified e-invoice service provider or direct GDT portal API and a validated GDT code is returned in near-real-time before legal issuance, constituting effective fiscal clearance; and (b) self-generated e-invoices (with code) — authorized enterprises issue invoices under their own sequence and must transmit invoice data to the national e-invoice portal within 5 days of issuance (aggregation permitted with GDT approval). The digital stamp is the enterprise's qualified e-signature; no physical fiscal stamp is used. E-invoice data must reconcile with the monthly VAT return (Form 01/GTGT) and the annual CIT finalization; mismatch triggers automatic GDT red-flag analysis.

Export-service zero-rating is a high-audit-risk area for the GDT, especially for recurring digital subscriptions, because eligibility rests on subjective offshore-consumption evidence. Penalties under the Law on Tax Administration 2019 and Penal Code 2015 (amended 2017) include: (i) late-payment interest of 0.03% per day on any VAT under-declared; (ii) administrative sanctions of up to 3 times the evaded tax amount for fraudulent zero-rating declarations; (iii) full retrospective clawback of input VAT previously credited against export sales, reversal of output VAT at the 10% standard rate, and disallowance for CIT of related expenditures; (iv) criminal prosecution under Article 200 for tax evasion from VND 100 million, carrying fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years (or higher where aggravated amounts exceed VND 1 billion); (v) suspension or revocation of e-invoice self-issuance rights, migration to the Tax Authority Code regime, and downgrading of the taxpayer compliance rating, which triggers mandatory 100% pre-refund audit for all subsequent VAT refund claims; (vi) in the cross-border subscription scenario, GDT scrutiny of the Stripe/Wise payment chain may also reclassify net settlement flows as taxable commission income unless the merchant-of-record and foreign-exchange traceability documentation is clear.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese dong (VND) is conditionally convertible. Current account transactions, including service export proceeds, subscription fees, software licensing, and profit remittances arising from permissible underlyings, are generally convertible without prior SBV approval, provided they are supported by verifiable commercial documents and settled through authorized credit institutions. Capital account transactions, including inbound foreign direct investment contributions, shareholder loans, and repatriation of invested capital upon liquidation, are subject to stricter SBV registration, approval, and reporting obligations and must flow through the Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) of the foreign-invested LLC. Profit remittance after the first year of operation is permitted once the annual financial statements are audited, tax liabilities are settled and evidenced by tax clearance or payment receipts, and a board resolution authorizing the distribution is issued. In practice, full and immediate convertibility cannot be assumed for capital outflows; advance consultation with the servicing licensed bank and the SBV-supervised foreign investment monitoring system is mandatory.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Vietnam's Foreign Exchange Ordinance and Decree 70/2014/ND-CP, as implemented by SBV Circular 05/2021/TT-NHNN, a resident organization exporting goods or services must receive all export proceeds through authorized credit institutions and repatriate those proceeds to its foreign currency account at an authorized bank within 90 days from the date of service completion (or customs clearance for goods, or the date of the export invoice for services, whichever is later under the relevant underlying contract). There is no mandatory surrender ratio currently applicable to export receipts in convertible foreign currencies; the LLC may retain the foreign currency in its account or sell it to the authorized bank at the prevailing market exchange rate. For design-subscription services delivered to overseas clients, the LLC must ensure that subscription collections, whether received directly or via digital payment facilitators, ultimately settle into the bank account of the Vietnamese entity within the 90-day statutory timeline and are properly reported with the corresponding service-export transaction purpose code.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam). The SBV holds exclusive authority over monetary policy, foreign exchange administration, cross-border payment supervision, and the licensing of authorized banks, credit institutions, and foreign exchange dealers in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW AS A BANK ACCOUNT SUBSTITUTE; MODERATE AS A COLLECTIONS-ONLY FACILITATOR. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Stripe, Payoneer, Airwallex) are not licensed credit institutions under Vietnam's Law on Credit Institutions and cannot receive statutory FDI capital contributions or act as the primary bank account for the LLC. SBV foreign-exchange regulations require export service proceeds and capital account flows to be received through an authorized bank in Vietnam; therefore, using an offshore EMI as the principal treasury account would constitute a breach of the Foreign Exchange Ordinance and the AML law, exposing the entity to fines, suspension of banking privileges, and criminal exposure in severe cases. EMIs may be used only as operational gateways for receiving low-value subscription collections, provided all funds are swept into the Vietnamese authorized bank account within the 90-day export-receipt window and are correctly mapped to the underlying service-export purpose code. Any direct delinking of subscription billing from a Vietnamese bank account is not compliant.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All foreign-direct-investment capital for the LLC must be routed through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened at an authorized commercial bank in Vietnam, in VND or a permitted foreign currency. The LLC must first obtain the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC), and then the bank will open the DICA and register the foreign-investment capital schedule with the SBV's monitoring system. The foreign investor must issue a capital-call notice and board resolution specifying the exact amount and currency. The remittance must be sent via SWIFT MT103 with the correct foreign-exchange transaction purpose code (the charter-capital contribution code per the SBV reporting directory). Upon credit, the bank will issue a purpose-code confirmation or foreign-exchange certificate; the funds may be transferred to the operational account only after the bank verifies the documentary package (IRC, ERC, capital call, and investor KYC). Shareholder loans require prior registration with the SBV or the authorized bank and must be credited to the DICA under the loan purpose code; the loan agreement, SBV loan registration confirmation, and drawdown notice are mandatory.For the design-subscription business, outbound payments primarily comprise service fees to overseas design contractors, software licensing fees, royalties, and dividend repatriations. The standard clearing rail is an international wire via SWIFT MT103, executed by the authorized bank against a supporting set: underlying service/IP contract, invoice, proof of delivery or acceptance, board resolution, and declaration/certificate of Vietnamese tax withholding where required. A Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) is not a customary or suitable rail for service exports; settlement is effected on an open-account basis using MT103. For low-value subscription payouts, the LLC may use digital facilitators (e.g., Wise, Stripe) to initiate payments, but final treasury and regulatory reporting must remain on the authorized bank's books; the LLC must reconcile every payout with the corresponding service-export transaction code and ensure that all inbound collections are repatriated within the 90-day statutory window.Onshore deliverable foreign-currency forward contract with a licensed bank, tenor up to 365 days, tied to a genuine USD/VND cash flow from export subscriptions or foreign-currency liabilities (per SBV Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN). Non-deliverable forward (NDF) structures are not permitted onshore for VND.Onshore foreign-currency swap (FX swap) between VND and a major convertible currency, used for short-term treasury liquidity bridging and rollover of USD receivables into VND working-capital needs.Onshore foreign-currency option contracts (call/put) with physical delivery, entered into with an authorized bank, provided the options are supported by an underlying current-account or capital-account exposure and are not speculative (subject to the bank's internal product authorization).Cross-currency interest-rate swap (CCIRS) for the hedging of a long-term USD-denominated shareholder loan, available at major banks after credit approval and demonstration of the underlying borrowing and repayment schedule.Forward agreements on the interbank FX market are not available to non-bank customers; all hedging must be executed bilaterally with an authorized commercial bank under express SBV FX-regulatory frameworks.
