Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Business Registration Office (Phòng Đăng Ký Kinh Doanh) of the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (Sở Kế Hoạch và Đầu Tư), operating under the Agency for Business Registration of the Ministry of Planning and Investment through the National Business Registration Portal at dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprise No. 59/2020/QH14 (effective 01 January 2021), notably Articles 9 (management authority), 22 (enterprise registration), 24 through 33 (enterprise registration procedure and dossiers), 39 (contents of company charter), 47 (schedule for capital contribution), and 74 through 83 (single-member limited liability company); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, Articles 21 through 26 and Appendix IV (conditional sectors), confirming that ISIC 7020 management consultancy is unconditioned and permits 100% foreign ownership; implementing regulations: Decree No. 01/2021/NĐ-CP on Enterprise Registration and Decree No. 31/2021/NĐ-CP detailing the Law on Investment.

Formation / constitutional filings

Enterprise Registration Application Dossier for establishment of a foreign-invested single-member limited liability company, headed by the standardized Enterprise Registration Certificate Application Form (Form No. 01, promulgated with Decree No. 01/2021/NĐ-CP), accompanied by the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC), the Company Charter, the List of Members, and notarized constituent documents.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 36 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum charter capital is fixed by law for ISIC 7020 management consultancy services (non-conditional sector under Appendix IV of the Law on Investment 2020); the sole member must state charter capital in the Company Charter and must fully pay in the committed capital within 90 days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47, Law on Enterprise 2020), failing which the company is obliged, under the same Article, to register either a reduction of charter capital or a supplementary capital contribution within an additional statutory period. No escrow account is statutorily mandated for this service industry; for banking and investment-registration proof, the capital contribution is tracked through a dedicated Vietnamese bank account opened in the company name.

Funding & unlock path: Civil defense and occupational clearance for the office premises: obtain fire-protection design approval and acceptance verification from the provincial Fire Prevention and Rescue Police (Bộ Công an) under Decree 136/2020/NĐ-CP if the leased office is in a building not covered by a previously issued acceptance certificate; maintain a registered self-inspection checklist, internal fire-fighting equipment, emergency exit signs, and periodic evacuation drills; obtain a completion inspection certificate for building works from the local Department of Construction under Decree 06/2021/NĐ-CP where a new building envelope is occupied, and an occupational-safety declaration and workplace health risk assessment under Article 18 of Law on Occupational Safety and Hygiene 2015, including medical surveillance and safety lighting in the workplace.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum charter capital is fixed by law for ISIC 7020 management consultancy services (non-conditional sector under Appendix IV of the Law on Investment 2020); the sole member must state charter capital in the Company Charter and must fully pay in the committed capital within 90 days from issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Article 47, Law on Enterprise 2020), failing which the company is obliged, under the same Article, to register either a reduction of charter capital or a supplementary capital contribution within an additional statutory period. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) of the Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Viet Nam; national e-tax portal https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn and national electronic invoice portal https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn.

E-government filing portal

Under the one-stop enterprise registration mechanism, a foreign-invested LLC is issued a 13-digit Tax Identification Number (Mã số doanh nghiệp) automatically together with the Enterprise Registration Certificate by the provincial Department of Planning and Investment via the National Business Registration Portal (https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn). The LLC must then (i) activate the e-tax account at https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn using the legal representative's PKI digital certificate; (ii) update tax registration information using Form 01-ĐK-TCT (Circular 105/2020/TT-BTC) for any subsequent change within 10 working days; (iii) obtain a tax-authority-approved electronic invoice template registered on the Electronic Invoice Portal; and (iv) open a direct investment capital account if applicable. For an offshore ESG/carbon advisory vendor without a Vietnamese legal presence, the Vietnamese client is the withholding agent for Foreign Contractor Tax and no standalone TIN is needed unless the foreign vendor registers directly with the GDT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation – the foreign-invested LLC is a Vietnamese tax resident subject to corporate income tax at the 20% statutory rate on worldwide income. Viet Nam does not recognize pass-through/LLC tax transparency; income is always taxed at the entity level. A Territorial_Scope carve-out applies only to non-resident foreign contractors, whose Vietnam-source income is taxed via withholding (Foreign Contractor Tax). The LLC may additionally qualify for Tax_Exempt_Zone preferential CIT rates if located in an export processing, industrial, high-tech or economic zone, but a stand-alone ESG consulting LLC has no automatic exemption.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not operate a US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. The available statutory elections are: (i) VAT accounting method election: the deduction (credit) method is mandatory once annual revenue equals or exceeds VND 1 billion; a small taxpayer may elect the direct method while below that threshold; (ii) accounting currency and fiscal year election for the CIT file; (iii) investment-incentive elections recorded on the Investment Registration Certificate (preferential rates, tax holidays, import duty exemptions); (iv) consolidated transfer pricing documentation election under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP for the economic-statistical analysis of related-party services; and (v) optional micro-enterprise simplified accounting treatment. Hybrid mismatch adjustments are not available as an election; Viet Nam's interest-limitation rules follow the BEPS approach with no elective de-minimis for related-party interest.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 1,000,000,000 (approximately USD 43,000) of annual turnover from taxable supplies triggers mandatory VAT registration and the deduction method; registration is compulsory irrespective of turnover for any entity liable to corporate income tax or Foreign Contractor Tax, including foreign-invested LLCs commencing activities in Viet Nam.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to exported goods, goods and services supplied to export processing enterprises, and exported services. For ESG/carbon footprint audit and offset brokerage, the service must (i) be contractually performed for a foreign organization or individual; (ii) be actually rendered and consumed outside Viet Nam (domestic consumption, even under a foreign contract, is excluded); (iii) be paid for in convertible foreign currency from abroad (bank transfer evidence required); (iv) meet the Article 9.1 Circular No. 219/2013/TT-BTC documentation test, including a written service contract, a 0% VAT invoice, evidence of foreign-client non-residence and offshore use, and, for offset brokerage, carbon-credit registry transaction reports. Services that involve on-site data collection in Viet Nam or provide benefits to a Vietnamese entity remain subject to 10% VAT unless the entirety of the output is consumed outside Viet Nam.

  • Export service contract (or purchase order) expressly identifying the foreign counterparty and stipulating that the ESG carbon auditing/offset brokerage output is consumed entirely outside Viet Nam
  • Coded 0% VAT e-invoice and, for any tangible deliverables or equipment embedded in the service, a Customs Export Declaration with the electronic customs seal
  • Foreign-currency inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103 bank statement evidencing actual payment received from the non-resident client abroad
  • Notarized certificate of non-residence and offshore consumption, including the foreign client's tax-residency evidence, work-paper/time records demonstrating Vietnam-offshore performance, and carbon registry transaction reports for offset brokerage

Since 1 July 2022 all Vietnamese taxpayers must issue electronic invoices under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular No. 78/2021/TT-BTC. An LLC using the deduction method must issue invoices bearing the seller's e-certificate in standardized XML and transmit every invoice data file to the General Department of Taxation's e-invoice portal (https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn) in near-real-time to obtain an exclusive tax-authority transaction code (coded e-invoice) before delivery to the client; service invoices must be issued at the moment of completion and transmitted within the prescribed deadline. Non-coded e-invoices are permitted only for qualifying transactions and require summary transmission within 1 working day. The GDT portal exposes an API enabling real-time fiscal clearance and risk profiling; the tax authority can block issuance of invalid invoices and trigger automatic desk audits. E-invoice hardcopy conversion and digital stamping are not permitted substitutes.

Zero-rating and refund claims are high-risk targets of the General Department of Taxation's export-audit programme; the GDT cross-matches e-invoice data, customs declarations, bank FX dashboards, and foreign-client residency evidence. False declarations (fictitious export contracts, circular or round-trip payments, invoices without overseas consumption) expose the LLC to: (i) denial of the VAT refund and mandatory prospective clawback of previously credited input VAT, compounded with late-payment interest of 0.02% per day on underpaid VAT; (ii) administrative fines under Decree No. 125/2020/ND-CP of 1 time the under-declared tax for negligent underestimation and 1.5 to 2.5 times the evaded amount for intentional evasion; (iii) suspension or revocation of the e-invoice code authorization and of the refund entitlement; and (iv) criminal liability under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 (amended 2017) where evasion exceeds VND 100,000,000, with fines up to VND 4,500,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 12 years for the most serious export-VAT fraud, in addition to retrospective assessment of all years under the 5-year audit window and collection of dunning penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese Dong (VND) is convertible on the current account for legitimate trade and service transactions, including cross-border corporate advisory services related to ESG carbon audit. Capital-account convertibility is restricted and requires a documented underlying FDI contribution, intra-company loan, or registered investment project. Profit remittances by a foreign-invested LLC are permitted on an annual basis after the company completes audited financial statements and satisfies all corporate income tax, VAT, and other statutory obligations; the distribution must be evidenced by the applicable tax revenue declaration, an independent auditor's report, and minutes of the member's meeting. The residual profits may be converted and transferred abroad by a commercial bank acting as a licensed agent. In practice, capital-account transactions face detailed KYC, purpose-code, and SBV electronic reporting requirements, and there is no active offshore VND foreign-exchange market, meaning that conversion timing and USD/EUR position-taking are strictly limited by the onshore interbank rate and the exchange-rate band regulated by SBV.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN and SBV administrative practice, all export proceeds derived from the provision of services, including ESG advisory, carbon audit, and offset brokerage, must be credited to a foreign-currency account at a licensed Vietnamese bank within 15 calendar days from the date the payment is received. There is no automatic and unconditional mandatory surrender-to-VND requirement for service exporters; however, the bank may require conversion for repayment of VND debts if so triggered under the loan documentation, and the use of retained foreign currency is subject to prior declaration and must be monetized in a purpose-compliant manner. Exporters are obliged to upload the underlying sale contract, service invoice, and SWIFT credit advice to the bank for receipt verification and SBV reporting, failing which the remittance will be held in a suspense account and may be rejected if the documentary trail is insufficient.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV), exercising foreign exchange administration powers through the Foreign Exchange Control Department and monetary authority under the Law on the State Bank of Vietnam, the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange, and Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN. SBV is the sole approving authority for licensing credit institutions that may clear international payments and hold foreign currency on behalf of Vietnamese residents.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with hard regulatory limits. Offshore digital EMI providers such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and Revolut can serve as ancillary collection accounts for USD/EUR receipts from non-Vietnamese clients, but they cannot act as a licensed banking venue for indie Vietnamese entity because they lack an SBV credit-institution license and are not admitted to the domestic VND interbank clearing system. All FDI equity contributions, statutory tax payments, and the main operational clearing for salary and vendor settlement in Vietnam must be executed through a licensed Vietnamese bank. An offshore EMI account may raise SBV reputational risk if used to present false payment locality or to conceal Vietnamese-source revenue, so it should be limited strictly to foreign-source cash sweeps feeding into the licensed Vietnamese bank and never used for domestic collections or tax deposits.

Each equity capital contribution must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the offshore subscribing shareholder to the VN LLC's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) held at the licensed Vietnamese bank. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File are mandatory if the Vietnamese enterprise has related-party transactions and meets at least one of the following thresholds under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP: total revenue from all activities in the tax period reaches VND 50 billion or the aggregate value of related-party transactions reaches VND 30 billion. Country-by-Country Report obligations apply to a Vietnamese constituent entity when the ultimate parent group has consolidated revenue of at least VND 18,000 billion and the Vietnamese group meets the additional thresholds set out in Decree 132/2020/ND-CP.

Economic substance scope: Vietnam does not operate a standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to common law DTA gateways, but substance-over-form principles are embedded in the Law on Tax Administration 38/2019/QH14, Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, and the General Anti-Avoidance Rules. For this ESG carbon accounting and offset brokerage LLC, the tax authority may disregard transactions or structures lacking real economic substance, deny treaty benefits to shell arrangements, recharacterize artificial transfer pricing, and require that the Vietnam entity demonstrate actual staff, office premises, management decision-making, and operational capacity commensurate with the income booked.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Revenue Suppression

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of understated CIT and VAT, late payment interest at 0.03% per day, administrative penalties of one to three times the under-declared tax amount, and criminal prosecution under Article 200 of the Criminal Code 2015 (as amended) with fines up to VND 4.5 billion and imprisonment of up to 7 years for serious intentional evasion.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, general director, or compliance director who signed false tax returns may be personally liable for civil restitution, may be prohibited from leaving Vietnam through temporary exit suspension, and may face criminal imprisonment if the evasion is attributable to knowing direction.
Shell Company and Artificial Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authorities may disregard the legal form, reattribute income to the true controlling party, deny CIT incentives and treaty benefits, impose reassessment penalties, and recommend revocation of enterprise registration under the Law on Enterprises for falsified incorporation declarations.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and the factual controller may be jointly liable for unpaid taxes and penalties; criminal charges for fraud or tax evasion can attach where the shell structure was erected solely to mislead Vietnamese tax authorities.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Vietnam tax authorities may make an arm's-length adjustment by recharacterizing the actual transaction, disallow non-deductible payments, impose late payment interest, and issue penalties for inaccurate declarations. Administrative penalties for transfer pricing documentation failures are separate from underpayment penalties and may be combined with criminal penalties if the mispricing is fraudulent.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed or procured related-party contracts without economic justification can be personally assessed for resulting tax shortfalls in the context of civil restitution; criminal liability arises where the transfer pricing scheme amounts to tax evasion.
Unauthorized or Premature Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: The State Bank of Vietnam may impose foreign exchange penalties; the tax authority may recharacterize the disguised service fee as a dividend, disallow the deduction, assess 20% CIT plus late payment interest, and impose administrative fines for incorrect invoicing. Criminal tax evasion sanctions apply where the disguise is intentional.

Director personal liability: The signing director and the foreign parent's local representative may be liable for the illegal foreign exchange remittance and for the tax consequences; exit suspension may be applied until the full tax liability is settled.
AML and Payments Facilitation for Carbon Offset Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Law on Anti-Money Laundering 2022, designated entities must report suspicious transactions to the State Bank of Vietnam and maintain customer due diligence records; violations attract administrative fines, suspension of certain activities, and criminal liability under Article 324 of the Criminal Code for money laundering if proceeds of another crime are knowingly processed.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may face personal criminal liability for knowingly allowing the LLC to be used as a conduit for fraudulent carbon credits or proceeds of illegal activity; imprisonment terms for money laundering facilitation can be severe, and directors may also be disqualified from serving as corporate officers.
