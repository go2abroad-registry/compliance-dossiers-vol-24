Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Business Registration Office of the provincial-level Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) of the province or centrally-run city where the LLC's registered head office is located, operating through the National Business Registration Portal (https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) administered by the Agency for Business Registration, Ministry of Planning and Investment.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14, effective 1 January 2021 — Chapter III on Limited Liability Companies, Article 47 on capital contribution and payment schedule, Articles 66-93 on governance of multi-member LLCs and Article 73-75 on single-member LLCs; Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, Articles 25-26 on investment registration procedures, Articles 28-29 on market access conditions; Decree 01/2021/ND-CP on enterprise registration — Articles 27-28 (dossier and issuance deadline), Article 33 (publication of registration content), Articles 38-45 (post-registration formalities); Decree 31/2021/ND-CP on Conditional Investment Sectors — Annex IV (line of business not listed as conditional for trade-remedy defense consulting).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Enterprise Registration (Giấy đề nghị đăng ký doanh nghiệp) filed electronically via the National Business Registration Portal, together with the Company Charter, list of members, and constitutive documents required by Article 27 of Decree 01/2021/ND-CP.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an LLC engaged in ISIC 7490 trade-remedy defense consulting, as the sector is not a conditional investment sector under Decree 31/2021/ND-CP Annex IV and carries no legal capital requirement under the Law on Enterprises 2020 or sectoral regulations. The members must pay the subscribed charter capital in full within 90 days from the date the Enterprise Registration Certificate is issued, in accordance with Article 47 of Law on Enterprises 2020. No escrow account, regulatory-capital deposit, or bank-blocked account is mandated for this non-financial, non-brokerage consulting activity. If the company later expands into licensed law-practice services, the capital and licensing conditions of the Law on Lawyers 2006 and Decree 123/2013/ND-CP may apply.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

For non-manufacturing trade-remedy defense consulting in ISIC 7490, no industrial manufacturing license is issued by an industrial licensing board. The general operating authority is the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) under the Law on Enterprises 2020. For the substantive trade-defense affairs, the competent policy authority is the Trade Remedies Authority of Viet Nam (Cục Phòng vệ thương mại), Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT), which receives and administers anti-dumping and anti-subsidy disputes; that authority does not license the consulting firm itself. If the firm expands to courtroom legal representation, the competent licensing authority is the provincial Department of Justice under the Law on Lawyers 2006.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (GDT) under the Ministry of Finance of Viet Nam; online portal: thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-Tax Portal) and Dich vu cong quoc gia (National Public Service Portal at dichvucong.gov.vn)

E-government filing portal

Foreign-invested limited liability companies are required to obtain a Tax Identification Number (MST) via the enterprise registration process at the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI). The DPI issues the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC), which simultaneously serves as the tax registration certificate referencing the tax ID. The enterprise must subsequently complete tax code activation and file an initial declaration using the e-Tax portal (thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn) through a valid digital signature (USB token or e-Signature) within statutory timelines. A separate VAT registration is not required; the corporate TIN functions as the VAT identification number.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: The foreign-invested LLC is a separate tax resident entity subject to Corporate Income Tax (CIT) at the standard rate of 20%. It is also a VAT-registered taxable person subject to output VAT, input VAT crediting, and VAT finalization where applicable. Territorial_Scope: Tax residence in Viet Nam applies to entities incorporated under Vietnamese law with legal seat or place of effective management in Viet Nam; worldwide income (including foreign-source income) is subject to CIT with foreign tax credit relief. Tax_Exempt_Zone: Not automatically applicable; eligibility for preferential tax zones, industrial zones, export processing zones (EPZs), high-tech zones, or economic zones requires separate approval and satisfaction of investment incentive conditions under Decree 218/2013/ND-CP and the Law on Investment 2020. — Foreign-invested limited liability companies are required to obtain a Tax Identification Number (MST) via the enterprise registration process at the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The foreign-invested LLC is a separate tax resident entity subject to Corporate Income Tax (CIT) at the standard rate of 20%. It is also a VAT-registered taxable person subject to output VAT, input VAT crediting, and VAT finalization where applicable. Territorial_Scope: Tax residence in Viet Nam applies to entities incorporated under Vietnamese law with legal seat or place of effective management in Viet Nam; worldwide income (including foreign-source income) is subject to CIT with foreign tax credit relief. Tax_Exempt_Zone: Not automatically applicable; eligibility for preferential tax zones, industrial zones, export processing zones (EPZs), high-tech zones, or economic zones requires separate approval and satisfaction of investment incentive conditions under Decree 218/2013/ND-CP and the Law on Investment 2020.

Available election forms

Viet Nam generally does not operate a full check-the-box classification regime comparable to the U.S. Treasury Regulations. However, the following statutory elections and mechanisms are available: (i) election of small- and medium-sized enterprise CIT incentives or high-tech/export-processing enterprise incentives based on qualifying investment certificates; (ii) election to apply the foreign tax credit method for foreign-source income under Circular 78/2014/TT-BTC; (iii) election of VAT deduction method versus direct method based on registration and accounting records; (iv) consolidated VAT offsetting in accordance with joint circulars; (v) hybrid mismatch election treatment limited to treaty non-discrimination and double taxation agreement (DTA) relief, including the possible use of the Mutual Agreement Procedure (MAP) under applicable DTAs; and (vi) for cross-border consulting service income, election or reliance on the Permanent Establishment (PE) threshold and profit attribution rules under DTAs and Decree 132/2020/ND-CP on transfer pricing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general registration threshold in local legal currency (VND) for enterprises; all companies incorporated and operating in Viet Nam are subject to VAT registration and must obtain a VAT identification number regardless of turnover, except that foreign contractors and foreign organizations without a Vietnamese establishment may be subject to Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) withholding VAT in lieu of full registration. For household businesses and individuals, the VAT registration and declaration threshold is annual turnover exceeding 100 million VND. For a foreign-invested LLC engaged in consulting services, the de minimis threshold effectively does not apply; registration is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to exported goods and services, including export-processed goods, offshore services provided to foreign organizations/individuals for consumption outside Viet Nam, goods sold to export processing enterprises (EPEs), and services supplied to foreign parties for use outside Viet Nam, provided that (i) the transaction qualifies as an export under customs or service export regulations; (ii) the enterprise holds full documentation proving the goods were physically exported or the service was actually rendered offshore; (iii) payment is received in convertible foreign currency from abroad or from an EPE; and (iv) the enterprise has a real economic transaction and sufficient supporting contracts. Consulting services for international anti-dumping trade defense provided to foreign clients are zero-rated only if the services are consumed outside Viet Nam and documentary evidence of offshore consumption and foreign-currency payment is preserved.

  • Viet Nam Customs Export Declaration (Tờ khai hải quan hàng hóa xuất khẩu) duly electronically filed through the VNACCS/VCIS national single window
  • Original or certified true copy of commercial invoice
  • Original or certified true copy of Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB) evidencing international carriage
  • Packing list and any other customs-required shipping documents
  • Export contract or purchase order with foreign buyer
  • Foreign remittance/telegraphic transfer advice evidencing actual receipt of foreign currency payment in convertible currency
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Inward Remittance Certificate or Bank Export Confirmation/Certificate of Foreign Currency Receipt issued by the authorized bank
  • Service export supporting contract, Foreign Contractor Tax withholding certificate (if applicable), proof of offshore consumption, and any non-processing attestation issued by Vietnamese authorities for services supplied to offshore clients
  • VAT zero-rating declaration and e-invoice bearing 0% VAT code where applicable

Viet Nam has implemented a mandatory e-invoicing (Hóa đơn điện tử) system under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. All VAT-registered enterprises must use data-standardized electronic invoices generated via certified e-invoice service providers or the GDT's e-invoice portal; each invoice must be digitally signed using the enterprise's registered digital certificate and transmitted to the e-invoice system for data validation and registration. For sales to tax authorities, state agencies, and in certain high-risk scenarios, invoice-by-invoice real-time transmission to the GDT is required; otherwise, periodic batch transmission is permitted under current regulations. The GDT is progressively implementing the national e-invoice data integration system (Hệ thống hóa đơn điện tử) with application programming interface (API) connections from business accounting systems to the GDT's central portal, and is expanding real-time invoice clearance and digital stamping for verifiable invoices linked to customs, banking, and corporate tax records.

Claims for zero-rated export VAT are subject to stringent GDT and customs audit scrutiny, especially for service exports. The General Department of Taxation may require supplementary documentation proving actual offshore consumption, bank statements evidencing foreign-currency inflow, and the commercial substance of the recipient. Any failure to substantiate zero-rating within the statutory deadlines results in retrospective VAT assessment, accrual of late-payment interest computed at 0.03%/day on the under-assessed VAT, and administrative fines of up to 20 times the amount of the tax evasion under the Law on Tax Administration 2019 (in severe cases, criminal sanctions under the Penal Code 2015, including fines up to VND 4.5 billion and imprisonment up to 20 years). Additional penalties apply for incorrect e-invoice issuance, including suspension of e-invoice usage, fines between VND 4 million and VND 20 million per invoice, and disqualification from VAT refunds for periods under audit. Export VAT refunds are subject to pre-refund audit (pre-assessment) for high-risk taxpayers, and clawbacks of refunded amounts are levied together with penal interest and administrative sanctions if false documentation is discovered.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam maintains de jure current-account convertibility for service exports and profit remittances, but de facto convertibility is subject to documentary compliance by the licensed bank. Capital-account transactions, including shareholder equity injections, inbound loans, and cross-border guarantees, require mandatory registration with the SBV through a licensed bank (e.g., Direct Investment Capital Account – DICA regime). Profit remittance is permitted once the LLC satisfies annual CIT finalization, has audited financial statements, distributable after-tax profits approved by the Members' Council, and presents a tax clearance/confirmation from the tax authority. Dividend repatriation also requires the bank to receive the appropriate purpose code and a certification that no outstanding tax obligations exist. The VND is not fully convertible offshore; all profit and capital repatriation must be settled through an onshore licensed bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 (as amended) and SBV operational guidelines, export receipts, including service-export fees for anti-dumping trade-defense consulting, must be repatriated to the LLC's foreign-currency account at a licensed Vietnamese bank within 90 days from the date of customs clearance for goods or from the contractual payment-due date for services; an extension of up to 180 days in aggregate may be approved by the SBV on a case-by-case hardship basis. The mandatory conversion (surrender) ratio for export proceeds is 0% – no forced sale to the SBV or authorized bank is required. The LLC may hold export proceeds in a foreign-currency account indefinitely, or sell them to licensed banks at negotiated rates, and must ensure that the export service invoices and contracts clearly evidence the foreign-source payment to avoid reclassification as capital inflows.

Central bank supervision: The State Bank of Vietnam (SBV / Ngan Hang Nha Nuoc Viet Nam), acting as the central bank and the sole competent authority for foreign exchange administration, together with the Ministry of Finance for tax-linked remittance and foreign trade remedies coordination. The SBV issues the daily VND/USD reference rate, manages the managed-floating exchange rate band, and licenses authorized banks for FX trading and cross-border payment clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-moderate viability for treasury operations but strictly supplementary to a licensed onshore bank account. Offshore digital EMIs (multi-currency payment platforms domiciled in Singapore, the UK, or the EU) cannot hold VND, do not have access to the Vietnamese interbank payment system, and cannot clear statutory DICA obligations, CIT payments, or employee payroll. They may be used as collection wallets for inbound retainer fees from international clients and as payment conduits for overseas expert-witness or expert-report disbursements, but all funds must ultimately be swept to the LLC's onshore account to meet repatriation and audit trail requirements. The SBV only licenses domestic fintech payment intermediaries acting as agents of licensed banks; an offshore EMI holding or processing VND executes no legally valid Vietnamese FX transaction. Therefore, classify offshore EMI viability as 'Low' for primary treasury, 'Medium' as an ancillary cross-border receivable accelerator with strict reconciliation controls.

Defense evidence: Every material inbound transfer must be paired with: (i) the signed service agreement and its payment schedule; (ii) the original VAT-eligible invoice issued by the LLC as a Vietnamese service exporter; (iii) the client's purchase order or instruction letter confirming the retainer reference; (iv) substantive evidence of services delivered, including substantive filings to the investigating authority (case docket numbers), expert economic reports, legal memoranda, or hearing transcripts; (v) a completed bank-issued 'Know Your Client's Client' form for any payment exceeding USD 50,000; (vi) the LLC's reconciliation spreadsheet mapping the SWIFT reference to the receivable ledger; (vii) confirmation from the client's bank that funds derive from the client's own operating account, thereby avoiding third-party-payment suspicion. The LLC should also prepare an annual internal audit trial of all trade-defense engagements to evidence that profit margin is arm's-length and non-circular.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections from foreign investors must be routed only through SWIFT MT103/MT202 instructions to the LLC's DICA at a licensed Vietnamese bank, with the wire message containing the SBV-approved purpose code corresponding to 'contribution of charter capital to an FDI enterprise' and explicit referencing of the IRC number, the investing entity's name, and the capital-to-be-contributed. The receiving bank will verify that the remitter is identical to the shareholder listed in the company's IRC and the Vietnamese enterprise registration, and will require a payment-credit confirmation or a subsequent capital-contribution certificate to be submitted within the statutory window. Inbound loans from foreign shareholders (except exempt short-term loans of less than one year with no accrued interest) must undergo mandatory SBV registration via the Direct Lending Facility from authorized banks by the borrower before drawdown, with the purpose code 'FF – foreign loan proceeds'. After crediting, the LLC must report on the SBV's periodic FDI information system (system for statistics) and ensure that no mixture of operating cashflows with capital-account ledger entries occurs.Consultancy fee disbursements to overseas experts, co-counsel, economic consultants, and translation agencies are made via SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer) referencing the appropriate service-payment purpose code (e.g., 'payment for imported services') and supported by a copy of the relevant service contract, VAT invoice assessment, and withholding tax status. Where a foreign client or partner insists on documentary credit, a Commercial Letter of Credit (MT700) may be issued by the LLC's bank with the beneficiary presenting a benign set of documents (invoice, delivery certificate of legal services, expert report acceptance) under UCP600. Profit distribution to the foreign shareholder must be a clearing transfer from the operational account after CIT finalization, using purpose code 'dividend remittance to foreign investor' and attachment of the audited financial statements, Members' Council meeting minutes, and tax clearance confirmation.
