Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) of the province or centrally governed city where the head office is located (for Ho Chi Minh City: DPI-HCMC; for Hanoi: DPI-Hanoi), or the Management Board of Industrial Zones / Export Processing Zones if the registered office is located inside an industrial zone; all applications are submitted electronically through the National Business Registration Portal (NBRP) at https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn, with the investment component processed on the National Investment Registration System (VIET SIC).

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 (effective 01 January 2021), Articles 25, 47-60, 73-89 (formation of limited liability companies, charter contents, members' council powers, and legal representative rules); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14, Articles 36-38 (foreign investment registration procedure and Investment Registration Certificate issuance); Law on Securities No. 54/2019/QH14, Articles 74-76 and 85-90 (licensed securities services, professional certification of personnel, and operational conditions applicable to an equity crowdfunding portal selling or facilitating startup equity offerings); Decree No. 01/2021/NĐ-CP, Articles 23-28 (dossier and procedures for enterprise registration); Decree No. 155/2020/NĐ-CP (conditions and licensing criteria for securities intermediaries); draft Decree on Equity Crowdfunding (Huy động vốn cộng đồng) administered by the State Securities Commission under the fintech regulatory pilot programme.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Enterprise Registration (Giấy đề nghị đăng ký doanh nghiệp, Form I-1 prescribed under Decree No. 01/2021/NĐ-CP), accompanied by the Company Charter (Điều lệ công ty), the List of Members, copies of the approved Investment Registration Certificate, and legal-entity or identity documentation of all contributing members.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

There is no statutory minimum charter capital for a general foreign-invested LLC under the Law on Enterprises; however, the equity crowdfunding portal service is a conditional business line requiring a licensed intermediation for securities-related crowdfunding, and the State Securities Commission's draft Crowdfunding Decree and the fintech pilot programme require a minimum paid-up charter capital of VND 10,000,000,000 (approximately USD 400,000), which must be fully contributed in cash within 90 days after the Enterprise Registration Certificate is issued. The capital must be escrowed at a licensed commercial bank in Vietnam until the portal operating license is granted, with the bank issuing a written confirmation of the escrow balance to the licensing authority; withdrawal of the escrow funds prior to licensure is strictly prohibited without SSC written consent.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế, 'GDT') of the Ministry of Finance (Bộ Tài chính), Socialist Republic of Vietnam; online taxpayer service portals: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn (e-tax filing and account activation), https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-invoice clearance and stamping); national enterprise registration system: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn.

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor first secures an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) where required and then applies for the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) through the National Business Registration Portal. The statutory Tax Identification Number (MST - Mã số thuế) is automatically granted simultaneously with the ERC by the integrated national registration system under the Enterprise Law No. 59/2020/QH14 and the Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14. After incorporation, the foreign-invested LLC must (i) file any supplementary tax registration via Form 01-ĐK-TCT with the provincial tax department (Cục Thuế) having jurisdiction over the registered head office, including registration of branches and secondary establishments; (ii) procure a qualified electronic signature (chữ ký số) from a licensed certificate authority; (iii) activate its e-tax account at https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn; and (iv) register its e-invoice template and digital signature on the GDT e-invoice system under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP before issuing any invoices.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a resident corporate taxpayer (foreign-invested LLC) is subject to Corporate Income Tax at the 20% standard rate on worldwide income under the CIT Law No. 14/2008/QH12 as amended by Law No. 32/2013/QH13, Law No. 71/2014/QH13 and Law No. 130/2024/QH15; income derived outside Vietnam remains taxable unless relieved under a double-tax treaty; income from foreign digital intermediation services retains Vietnamese source character where the service is performed in Vietnam. Territorial_Scope concessions and Tax_Exempt_Zone regimes (export processing zones, high-tech parks, economic zones under Decree 82/2021/ND-CP) exist but are not inherent to a licensed equity crowdfunding portal and would require physical establishment in the designated zone plus a separate investment incentive certificate.

Available election forms

Vietnam has no check-the-box classification regime. Available statutory elections and safeguards include: (i) CIT loss carry-forward election for up to 5 consecutive years (Law No. 71/2014/QH13); (ii) VAT credit-method vs. direct/deduction-method election (Law No. 106/2016/QH13; Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC); (iii) CIT incentive election for research/innovation activities under high-tech enterprise rules (Decree 82/2021/ND-CP); (iv) unilateral/bilateral/multilateral Advance Pricing Agreement application under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP and Circular 127/2024/TT-BTC; (v) hybrid mismatch limitation and related-party adjustment analysis under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP; and (vi) election to substantiate 0% VAT on qualifying offshore services with the statutory documentary bundle.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VND 100,000,000 (one hundred million Vietnamese Dong) per annum of taxable turnover is the statutory threshold above which compulsory VAT registration applies (Decree 49/2022/ND-CP amending Decree 209/2013/ND-CP). An equity crowdfunding portal holding a State Securities Commission license and charging share-issuance success fees is a registered enterprise under the Enterprise Law, so VAT registration and credit-method taxation are mandatory from inception; no de minimis threshold applies to corporate taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies where goods are physically exported through Vietnam customs procedures and where services are supplied to foreign organizations or individuals and are consumed wholly outside Vietnam (Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC as amended by Circular 25/2018/TT-BTC; Law No. 106/2016/QH13). For the crowdfunding portal, zero-rating on platform-orchestration services furnished to non-resident issuers is claimable only if the contract, service-completion record, and bank remittance documentation persuasively prove the service was performed and consumed outside Vietnam; Vietnamese tax administrative practice excludes domestic intermediation, brokerage, and liaison services from zero-rating, so substantial in-Vietnam platform infrastructure, local due-diligence, or investor solicitation will rebut the offshore-consumption test and recharacterize the fee as taxable at 10%.

  • Customs Export Declaration with electronic customs clearance receipt (Tờ khai hải quan hàng xuất khẩu) issued by the General Department of Vietnam Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export of goods (applicable to goods transactions only)
  • Inward Remittance Receipt or Bank Payment Confirmation (Xác nhận thanh toán qua ngân hàng) issued by the authorized settlement bank evidencing foreign-currency remittance from the foreign buyer or foreign issuer
  • Foreign purchase contract or offshore service agreement, together with a service completion acceptance record signed by the foreign counterparty
  • Commercial Invoice and complete international shipping documentation for freight transactions
  • E-invoice at 0% VAT transmitted to the GDT with the corresponding export voucher and payment references

Mandatory electronic invoicing under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. The portal must transmit each e-invoice in XML format in real time to the GDT e-invoice system at https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn over authenticated HTTPS APIs. For invoices requiring tax-authority clearance, the GDT assigns a tax authority code (mã của cơ quan thuế) before the invoice is legally effective; taxpayers satisfying Decree 123/2020/ND-CP conditions may issue code-less e-invoices but must transmit complete invoice data to the tax authority no later than one working day after issuance. All e-invoices must bear the taxpayer's registered digital signature, and the GDT performs automated cross-matching of invoice data against VAT returns, zero-rated export declarations, and bank payment documentation.

Post-refund audits (kiểm tra, thanh tra sau hoàn thuế) are conducted under Chapter VA of the Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14 and Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC through documentary desk review and on-site inspection of the portal's books, underlying contracts, and FX remittance records. Unlawful refunds are retroactively clawed back with interest at 0.03% per day (or at the refinancing rate if higher), and an administrative penalty of 1 to 3 times the evaded or fraudulently refunded tax amount is imposed (Decree 125/2020/ND-CP as amended). Fraudulent export zero-rating or refund claims constitute tax evasion under Article 200 of the Penal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 as amended (fines up to VND 4.5 billion and imprisonment up to 7 years) and invoice forgery under Article 203; additionally, the State Securities Commission may suspend or revoke the crowdfunding platform license under Decree 10/2024/ND-CP for material compliance violations, and the State Bank of Vietnam may apply currency-control sanctions where FX repatriation exceeds the 180-day statutory deadline unless the governing export contract expressly stipulates a longer repatriation term permitted by Circular 05/2024/TT-NHNN.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vietnamese Dong (VND) is a managed-float, partially convertible currency. Current-account receipts are generally accessible through authorized banks but are subject to underlying-documentation review, while capital-account flows are restricted, registration-heavy, and transaction-locked. Equity contributions into a foreign-invested LLC must enter through a registered Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at an SBV-licensed bank and must be mapped to the Investment Registration Certificate, Enterprise Registration Certificate, and capital-contribution schedule. Profit remittance is a supervised current-account outflow permitted only after audited financial statements and final tax settlement, executed through an authorized bank at the prevailing SBV reference rate. Offshore trading of VND is not permitted; all exchange transactions must occur inside the domestic authorized-bank market.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Vietnamese foreign-exchange control, any resident entity receiving foreign-currency proceeds from exports or service revenues must repatriate the gross amount to a foreign-currency account held at an SBV-licensed bank and must surrender or convert 100% of those proceeds to VND within 15 days from the date of receipt, unless the authorized bank has approved a formal foreign-currency retention facility supported by underlying currency liabilities. The conversion rate is the bank's published buying rate on the value date, and the bank retains the export contract, customs declaration, invoice, or service agreement as part of the current-account file. For the crowdfunding portal, platform fees received in foreign currency are subject to the same repatriation and surrender discipline.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV / Ngan hang Nha nuoc Viet Nam) is the central bank and the exclusive foreign-exchange regulator, operating under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange and the implementing SBV circulars. For an equity crowdfunding portal classified as ISIC 6499, the SBV in coordination with the State Securities Commission of Vietnam (SSC) must confirm the permissible scope of the portal, the statutory escrow treatment of investor funds, and the bank authorizations required before any cross-border treasury transaction is opened.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility as a primary treasury or escrow rail. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed to hold Vietnamese resident funds, operate payment accounts, or execute domestic VND clearing without an SBV authorization as a credit institution or licensed intermediary payment service provider. An offshore EMI may function only as a non-regulated technology vendor for treasury accounting or as a foreign-currency disbursement capability strictly outside the Vietnamese domestic payment system. Statutory crowdfunding investor funds must remain in an explicitly designated client-money escrow account at an SBV-licensed commercial bank, never commingled with the portal's proprietary treasury.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity injections must be routed by SWIFT MT103 or local interbank transfer to the LLC's registered DICA at an SBV-licensed bank. The remitter field must identify the legal investing entity, the message purpose code must align with FDI equity contribution or capital participation, and the reference field must contain the Investment Registration Certificate number. The bank verifies the amount against the approved capital-contribution schedule, converts foreign currency into VND at the authorized daily rate, and credits the operating or designated escrow account. For crowdfunding investor subscriptions, funds must be received into a statutory client-money escrow account at the licensed bank, individually attributed to each investor and segregated from proprietary liquidity; no internal netting or commingling is permissible.Cross-border vendor payments and distribution payments to non-residents are executed through SWIFT MT103 from the operating FX account with the underlying contract, invoice, tax clearance, and board authorization retained for audit. Trade-based goods settlements may be supported by an irrevocable commercial letter of credit issued under UCP600 through a nominated local or foreign bank. Domestic disbursements to portfolio companies, issuers, and service providers are settled in VND through the SBV interbank electronic payment system or NAPAS ACH, subject to batch authorization, duplicate-funds verification, and a full audit trail. Escrow outflows require a signed disbursement instruction matching the completed crowdfunding offering and the exact investor-allocation schedule.Deliverable FX forward contracts against VND with an SBV-licensed credit institution, supported by documented trade or financial exposure and a maturity cap under SBV derivative regulations.FX swaps between VND and foreign currencies, including spot-forward and forward-forward combinations.Cross-currency swaps for long-dated foreign-currency liabilities, subject to SBV derivative licensing and underlying-deal verification.FX options, including vanilla options and simple collar structures, used strictly for hedging commercial exposures and not for speculative trading.Onshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not an authorized instrument for VND; offshore NDFs cannot be used by Vietnamese residents for hedging, so all permissible hedging must be deliverable through an SBV-licensed bank.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low feasibility as a primary treasury or escrow rail. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed to hold Vietnamese resident funds, operate payment accounts, or execute domestic VND clearing without an SBV authorization as a credit institution or licensed intermediary payment service provider. An offshore EMI may function only as a non-regulated technology vendor for treasury accounting or as a foreign-currency disbursement capability strictly outside the Vietnamese domestic payment system. Statutory crowdfunding investor funds must remain in an explicitly designated client-money escrow account at an SBV-licensed commercial bank, never commingled with the portal's proprietary treasury.
