Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; corporate registry and licensing: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Primary portals: dcir.gov.vu for customs/import-export codes and vfsc.vu for International Company registration.

E-government filing portal

No national TIN exists because Vanuatu imposes no income tax. A foreign-owned International Company (IC) obtains its unique legal identity via the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation under the International Companies Act Cap 222. For physical movement of dangerous goods cargo, the IC must additionally obtain a Customs Importer/Exporter Client Code from DCIR (lodged through ASYCUDA World) and hold the HF/VFSC-licensed dangerous goods forwarding authorization before filing the first export declaration. Municipal Service Tax registration is triggered only if the IC later supplies prescribed onshore services (accommodation, food and beverage, vehicle hire) inside Port Vila or Luganville, which is outside the offshore DG freight model.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (International Company under Cap 222, carrying on business wholly outside Vanuatu; statutory 20-year income tax exemption guarantee; non-resident owned; zero minimum capital).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections are available because no direct tax is enacted. The sole classification mechanism is the IC status election under the International Companies Act, including voluntary conversion to/from domestic company form via VFSC filing and payment of fixed registration/stamp fees; no tax rulings or advance pricing agreements are issued by DCIR.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (no national VAT/GST or sales and use tax enacted); indirect levies are limited to customs import/export duties, excise on alcohol/tobacco, and municipal Service Tax on prescribed onshore hospitality services. Nationwide VAT reform has been repeatedly deferred.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no national VAT/GST threshold exists. Municipal Service Tax registration attaches to prescribed onshore services (accommodation, food and beverage, vehicle hire) rather than to turnover; the offshore IC's forwarding services fall outside this nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – with no VAT in force, there is no 0% export VAT claim or VAT refund mechanism. The IC's freight services are offshore-source and wholly outside the Vanuatu tax net. Tangible exports are exempt from customs duties, except a narrow list of primary products (copra, kava and similar) subject to export duty; dangerous goods forwarding services are not dutiable.

  • VU Customs Export Declaration (lodged electronically in ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading (sea) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing the DG consignment
  • Dangerous Goods Shippers' Declaration (IMDG certificate for sea; IATA DGR Declaration for air)
  • Valid VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and Good Standing Certificate of the International Company
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and underlying Letter of Credit or SWIFT MT103 remittance where applicable

None – no real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime. DCIR operates the ASYCUDA World electronic customs single-window for declarant filing, but clearance is not real-time and no API-based fiscal data feed is required for exporters.

No export VAT clawback exists absent VAT. DCIR and VFSC scrutiny focuses on substance and source: if the IC carries on business with Vanuatu residents or earns Vanuatu-source income, the Cap 222 exemption is void ab initio, exposing the IC to civil fines, deregistration, and recovery of all taxes that would have applied. Customs false-export declarations attract seizure of goods, monetary penalties up to three times the duty evaded, and criminal prosecution for smuggling. Failure to maintain offshore-source books and records triggers VFSC license suspension and director-level liability.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible on the current account consistent with Vanuatu's IMF Article VIII obligations; the capital account is highly liberalized and non-resident investment into an International Company (IC) does not require prior RBV exchange-control approval. The International Companies Act (Cap. 222) expressly confers banking and exchange-control privileges on a compliant IC, permitting unrestricted foreign-currency deposits and remittance of profits, dividends, and liquidation proceeds to non-resident shareholders. Vanuatu imposes no income tax, no withholding tax, and no capital gains tax on the IC or its shareholders, so no tax-clearance certificate is required as a precondition to repatriation; the only practical frictions are correspondent-bank fees, domestic liquidity depth for VUV, and the bank's own AML/KYC documentary gates for high-risk dangerous-goods forwarding.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 46), export receipts earned by a Vanuatu-domiciled exporter of record are required to be repatriated through an authorized dealer bank within three (3) months of shipment and are subject to a mandatory conversion ratio to VUV of up to 100%, unless a Foreign Currency Retention Account has been pre-approved by the RBV-authorized dealer. A Vanuatu International Company engaged purely as a specialized DG forwarding agent is not the exporter of record; therefore, statutory surrender attaches only to domestically earned VUV-denominated commission and agency fees, while USD/AUD charter, freight, and dangerous-goods handling disbursements received under principal-to-principal offshore contracts may be retained in foreign currency without conversion. Where the IC nevertheless books trade receivables in VUV, the full 100% surrender and three-month repatriation timeline applies, and a written retention facility should be obtained from the authorized dealer in advance to avoid breach.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV); exchange control administration is additionally vested in the Ministry of Finance of Vanuatu under the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 46), with supervisory authority over authorized dealer banks exercised solely by the RBV pursuant to the Financial Institutions Act (Cap. 58).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low; the Vanuatu Financial Institutions Act (Cap. 58) requires any business carrying on banking or deposit-taking activity in or from Vanuatu to hold an RBV license, and no separately regulated digital Electronic Money Institution (EMI) regime exists locally. Global or offshore digital EMIs cannot clear VUV, cannot access SWIFT settlement in Vanuatu for the IC's purpose, and routinely decline high-risk ISIC 5229 hazardous-chemical forwarding clients. An offshore EMI may be used only as a non-core auxiliary collection wallet and can never satisfy the 'authorized dealer bank account' evidence required by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission, insurers, or downstream counterparties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 in USD, AUD, or EUR from the shareholder's remitting bank into the IC's Capital Escrow Account held with the Vanuatu authorized dealer; the remittance instruction must include the purpose descriptor 'direct equity investment for international dangerous-goods freight forwarding working capital', which maps to the Vanuatu balance-of-payments classification for equity shares in an International Company under the financial account direct-investment category. On receipt, the funds are either credited to the IC's foreign-currency account or converted into VUV at the prevailing dealer rate. The IC must deliver within five business days (i) the share-allotment board resolution, (ii) the updated register of members and UBO register, (iii) the registered agent's confirmation of corporate good standing, (iv) the remitting bank's source-of-funds statement, and (v) a notarized UBO declaration; failure to deliver these records will trigger a compliant hold-back and potential account restriction under the bank's AML/CFT policy.Operational trade settlements clear through SWIFT MT103 priority wires from the Vanuatu operational account using the purpose description 'freight forwarding and logistics services'; this rail supports payments to ocean and air carriers, port authorities, customs brokers, hazmat-certified warehousing, and contracted DG handling agents. Recurring charter or consolidated-cargo payments above the USD-equivalent of 50,000 are further supported by irrevocable documentary letters of credit (UCP600) issued or confirmed by the Vanuatu authorized dealer, while standby letters of credit are deployed as customs-bond guarantees to the Vanuatu Department of Customs. The IC exporting via this blueprint must maintain strict principal-versus-agent fund segregation between client-controlled cargo receipts and its own commission income so that clearing flows do not create constructive taxable service revenue inside Vanuatu and do not compromise the IC's tax-exempt status.Deliverable forward contracts in VUV/USD executed with a Vanuatu authorized dealer bank, tenor up to twelve months, supported by board-approved treasury policy and documented exposure from freight invoices or charter-party payments.Deliverable VUV/AUD and VUV/EUR forward contracts and cross-currency swaps concluded with the Vanuatu authorized dealer to hedge operating-cost corridors and European or Australian counterparty flows, each supported by a board treasury resolution and a confirmed underlying commercial transaction.Offshore non-deliverable forwards referencing the VUV/USD exchange rate executed through the shareholder group's treasury or a licensed offshore broker outside Vanuatu, permissible strictly on a hedging-only basis with a dedicated hedge-file evidencing each underlying exposure and retained for review by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission on substance assessments.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return and annual fee are due on the anniversary date of incorporation. There is no fiscal year-end income tax filing deadline because the entity is outside the corporate income tax net. If the IC has voluntarily elected to file a nil declaration with the Vanuatu Inland Revenue, such declaration must be filed within 30 days after the fiscal year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None – Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation and imposes no local file/master file thresholds on International Companies. However, a foreign tax authority with jurisdiction over a related freight forwarding counterparty may impose its own documentation thresholds, so the IC should be prepared to produce contemporaneous arm’s length evidence for any intercompany charges.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act and the VFSC regulatory guidance require an IC carrying on a geographically mobile relevant activity to demonstrate economic substance in Vanuatu. Since ISIC 5229 dangerous goods freight forwarding is a cross-border service activity, the entity must show Vanuatu management and control; maintain board minutes in Vanuatu; retain all accounting records accessible in Vanuatu; and appoint a local compliance officer. If the core functions, risks, and assets of the DG forwarding chain remain overseas, the IC must nonetheless prove at least a minimal Vanuatu presence, otherwise it will be classified as a shell and subject to penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abuse of IC Exemption

Statutory sanction: The IC exemption is void ab initio under Section 95 of the International Companies Act. The company becomes immediately liable to all applicable domestic impositions, including VAT registration, customs duties, and social insurance contributions. Civil penalty under the Value Added Tax Act may reach 150% of the unpaid VAT, and the Supreme Court may levy an additional fine of VT 50,000,000 for fraudulent exemption claims.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid VAT and customs duties during their tenure. They face director disqualification orders, civil restitution to the revenue authorities and creditors, travel bans while the assessment is pending, and imprisonment up to 7 years under Vanuatu's fraud and false statements legislation.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: The VFSC can impose a penalty of VT 20,000,000 for the first non-compliant year and VT 400,000 for each subsequent month of continued non-compliance. The IC may be struck off summarily, its bank accounts restricted by the Financial Intelligence Unit, and its details communicated to the OECD and FATF, causing severe correspondent banking de-risking.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal penalties up to VT 15,000,000 for certifying false substance declarations, a 10-year disqualification order, and a compensatory order requiring restitution of all fees earned through the sham structure. Continued non-substance activity can lead to criminal charges for negligent mismanagement under the Companies Act and imprisonment of up to 3 years.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Breach

Statutory sanction: The Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act [Cap. 260] imposes fines of VT 50,000,000 per contravention. Assets may be subject to a Preservation Order and receiver appointment for up to 90 days. The entity's special licence for dangerous goods forwarding is automatically suspended, and all letters of credit are rendered void at the issuing bank's option.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face civil restitution to all victims of the underlying fraud, 25-year imprisonment for money laundering, mandatory travel bans, and extradition to jurisdictions where the underlying hazardous goods shipment has caused environmental damage or personal injury. The corporate veil is pierced where the IC is used as a façade, and legal advisors who assisted in the layering may have their professional fees confiscated as tainted property.
Sanctions and Export Control Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Customs Act and the United Nations Sanctions Act, the aircraft or vessel may be seized, the shipment forfeited, and the IC fined up to VT 100,000,000. The Vanuatu Dangerous Goods Transport Commission will revoke the licence, and the VFSC can dissolve the IC without the standard six-month wind-down period.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for sanction violations and may be extradited under the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act. Travel bans are mandatory. The Proceeds of Crime Act allows confiscation of personal assets held through family trusts. A director who issued the shipping instruction is treated as principal offender and can face life imprisonment where the goods were to be used in the manufacture of weapons of mass destruction.
