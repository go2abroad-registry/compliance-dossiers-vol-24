Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (Department of Customs and Inland Revenue), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official portal: https://customs-inlandrevenue.gov.vu; customs clearance through ASYCUDA World; company registration through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) at https://www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] via a VFSC-licensed registered agent; obtain the VFSC company number, Certificate of Incorporation, and registered office. Step 2: Because Vanuatu levies no income tax, no income TIN exists; the IC is automatically tax-exempt on offshore business and files no tax returns. Step 3: If the IC makes or intends to make taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding the threshold, or elects voluntary registration to reclaim input VAT on export purchases, file the VAT registration application with Vanuatu Revenue and Customs together with incorporation documents, registered agent confirmation, and turnover projections; the Commissioner issues a VAT registration certificate bearing a unique VAT registration number. Step 4: For physical movements, enrol in ASYCUDA World for electronic export/import declarations; thereafter maintain VFSC annual license renewal and registered agent records.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Vanuatu law. The IC is statutorily non-resident and exempt from income tax, VAT, stamp duty, and exchange control on offshore business. The only available election is a Ministerial permit to carry on business in Vanuatu or with Vanuatu residents, which confines the automatic exemption to offshore activity and subjects the permitted local activity to standard laws; otherwise, no entity classification elections apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 3,000,000 of taxable turnover in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted for exporters to recover input VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated where supplied to a non-resident buyer, the goods are physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply, and the export is evidenced by customs declarations and transport documents. Services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident for use wholly outside Vanuatu, including international freight. For the offshore battery-metals arbitrage model, a non-resident IC conducting procurement and re-invoicing entirely outside Vanuatu makes no Vanuatu taxable supply and remains outside the scope of VAT with an effective 0% rate; if goods are routed through Vanuatu, the exporter must substantiate zero-rating with full customs and banking records. Excess input VAT credits arising from zero-rated export supplies are refundable upon submission of the VAT return and supporting export evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document filed via ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing departure from Vanuatu customs territory
  • Commercial Invoice issued by the VAT-registered exporter
  • Packing List and Certificate of Weight/Quality for battery-grade lithium carbonate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing foreign currency settlement
  • Original supply/purchase contract for the cross-border lithium carbonate transaction
  • Certificate of Origin (only where required by the destination state)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping mandate is currently in force. Registered businesses must issue sequential tax invoices (paper or electronic) containing prescribed particulars; all customs transactions are processed electronically through ASYCUDA World with digital submission of the Single Administrative Document.

Vanuatu imposes no exchange control or statutory FX repatriation deadline; 180 days is applied as the maximum evidentiary window for matching inward remittance certificates to zero-rated export invoices. Zero-rating refund claims are scrutinised against ASYCUDA World export declarations, bank statements, and remittance certificates. Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract retrospective reassessment (3 years standard; extended where misrepresentation or fraud is established), clawback of refunded input VAT with interest, monetary fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years for knowingly false statements under the VAT Act; false customs export declarations attract forfeiture of goods and penalties of up to 3 times the duty value under the Customs Act [Cap 145]; directors and officers are further exposed to VFSC licence revocation and anti-money-laundering proceedings under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act, with penalties including substantial fines and imprisonment up to 20 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is convertible for all current account transactions, consistent with long-standing IMF Article VIII obligations. For an International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], the capital account is effectively unrestricted: ICs are statutorily exempt from the Exchange Control Act, so equity injections, intercompany loan disbursements, and profit/principal remittances require no prior RBV authorization. The practical constraint is market depth, not law: VUV is thinly traded offshore, and settlement for a lithium-carbonate procurement house is therefore denominated in USD, AUD, or CNH. The IC should maintain zero residual VUV exposure and a documented policy that all borrowings, invoices, and liquidity buffers are held in settlement currencies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation applies. There is no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation deadline for export or trading receivables booked by an IC. Foreign-denominated proceeds may be retained indefinitely in the IC's licensed bank accounts in any currency. The only compliance duties are customer due diligence verification of the underlying trade contract (invoice, bill of lading, packing list) and retention of transaction records for a minimum of five years under Vanuatu's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) is the monetary authority and exchange-control administrator under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 63]. Policy oversight vests in the Minister of Finance, while day-to-day regulatory and licensing coordination for offshore vehicles is performed by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). All licensed commercial banks in Vanuatu operate as authorized dealers under RBV supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Vanuatu has no e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime, and the VFSC does not license standalone payment-service aggregators for ICs. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., UK FCA-authorised or Lithuanian EMIs) may be tolerated only for ancillary operational disbursements below USD 10,000 per transaction and are rejected as clearing conduits for high-value battery-grade lithium carbonate trade settlements. Tier-1 banks and their correspondents classify unlicensed aggregators as medium-high risk; any EMI usage must be pre-disclosed to the Relationship Manager, earmarked in the liquidity policy as non-core, and backed by a full audit trail to the underlying commercial contract.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All upstream funding is transmitted via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the shareholder's tier-1 bank (Hong Kong, Singapore, or Australia) into the IC's USD operational account at ANZ or BSP in Port Vila. The wire must carry an explicit purpose narrative: 'Direct investment equity contribution for battery-material procurement under loan/equity agreement dated [date]'; where the correspondent bank requires balance-of-payments classification, the narrative identifies the flow as BPM6 Direct Investment Equity Capital. The supporting bundle, delivered to the Relationship Manager before value date, comprises the certified board resolution, signed shareholder funding or loan agreement, UBO and source-of-funds declarations for every funder, and the IC's certificate of incumbency. Wires exceeding USD 1 million must be pre-advised 48 hours in advance. No RBV prior approval is required, but the receiving bank must report the flow in its Exchange Control Act statistical return; funds should first arrive in USD and be immediately converted or held rather than channeled through VUV to avoid conversion friction.Trade settlements run on two rails. Rail one: SWIFT MT103 direct covers for supplier invoices below USD 500,000, including CNH-denominated payments cleared through CNH clearing banks in Hong Kong (e.g., via HSBC or Standard Chartered) with the ISIC 4662 business code and the supply contract reference stated in the narrative. Rail two: irrevocable documentary letters of credit governed by UCP 600 for cargo volumes of USD 500,000 or more, issued at sight or 90-day usance from the IC's operational account, and confirmed by a top-50 international bank outside Vanuatu to eliminate correspondent and country-bank risk. Payments to regional Asian banks outside the standard Vanuatu correspondent network are layered through a third-party correspondent (HSBC, Standard Chartered, or JPMorgan) to guarantee clearing finality. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax; International Companies are statutorily exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Vanuatu does not impose dividend withholding tax on distributions paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders, and no domestic withholding, exchange-control, or capital-gains charge applies to profit repatriation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory monetary threshold exists in VUV for Local File or Master File obligations because Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and no transfer-pricing filing regime. However, under the VFSC economic substance compliance framework, a Vanuatu IC engaged in a Relevant Activity such as distribution or service-center business with gross operating income above VUV 30,000,000 must maintain contemporaneous transactional records, margin analyses, and arm's length evidence in Vatu to demonstrate that its cross-border lithium carbonate purchases and resales are commercially substantive and properly priced.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu International Companies (Economic Substance) Act applies to an International Company carrying on a Relevant Activity. Cross-border procurement, distribution, and resale of battery-grade lithium carbonate constitutes distribution and service-center activity. The IC must demonstrate substance in Vanuatu through local management meetings, adequate physical offices, qualified employees engaged in procurement documentation, and core decision-making records held in Vanuatu. Failure to file the annual economic substance return may result in a VFSC fine of up to VUV 4,000,000, strike-off, and disqualification orders.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abusive Offshore Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu Penal Code and VFSC enforcement powers, the IC may be subject to fines up to VUV 1,000,000, deregistration, asset restraint orders, and compulsory restitution of economic benefits. Cooperation with foreign tax authorities under a TIEA may trigger civil assessment and penalties abroad.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly files a false annual declaration or signs sham trading documentation faces personal civil restitution, disqualification from acting as a director, a travel restriction, and imprisonment for up to five years under Vanuatu law.
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC may impose a penalty of up to VUV 2,500,000, order immediate strike-off of the company, prohibit the registered agent from representing the IC, and refer the company to the Vanuatu director disqualification registry.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's economic-substance penalty if the failure is willful; additional personal sanctions include director disqualification for up to ten years, civil recovery orders, and travel bans while enforcement proceedings are active.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: The Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2016 imposes criminal fines up to VUV 100,000,000 on the IC and VUV 50,000,000 on senior officers; VFSC may suspend or revoke any license, freeze accounts, and confiscate the proceeds of suspicious wire transfers.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who participate in or knowingly facilitate money-laundering arrangements are personally liable to imprisonment up to fifteen years, civil forfeiture of assets, travel bans, and disqualification from holding financial-sector offices.
Fraudulent Preferences and Insolvent Trading

Statutory sanction: A court may set aside the fraudulent preference transaction under Vanuatu's insolvency and International Companies Act provisions, order restitution of the transferred value, and impose a fine on the company for unlawful distributions to shareholders.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid trade debts, ordered to reimburse corporate funds personally, subjected to asset forfeiture and travel restrictions, and imprisoned for up to seven years if fraudulent trading is established.
