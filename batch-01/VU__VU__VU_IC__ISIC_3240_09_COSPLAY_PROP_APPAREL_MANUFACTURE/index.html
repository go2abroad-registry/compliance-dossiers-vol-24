Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), Registry of International Companies, Port Vila.

Governing statute

International Companies Act No. 17 of 1992, Chapter 222, as amended (International Companies (Amendment) Acts 1994-2014); sites of principal operations: ss.4-6 (incorporation), s.10 (Certificate of Incorporation), s.11 (registered office), ss.21-23 (registered agent), ss.86-92 (directors and secretary); International Companies and Trusts (Fees) Regulations 2010; Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Rules 2019.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form IC-1 'Application for Registration as an International Company', accompanied by the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association and registered agent's consent; Certificate of Incorporation is issued under s.10 upon VFSC approval.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 13 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum authorized or paid-up capital is prescribed for a Vanuatu International Company; the sole founding subscriber may subscribe for one ordinary share of par value USD 1.00 or an equivalent in any freely convertible currency. No escrow bank account or capital deposit is required at incorporation; the share capital requirement is nil and shares may be issued fully or partly paid. Beneficial ownership particulars, including the subscriber shareholding, must be declared to the VFSC within 14 days of incorporation in accordance with the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Rules 2019.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable to the offshore International Company. If a domestic subsidiary were later formed, the premises would require (i) a fire safety clearance certificate from the National Fire Service of the Vanuatu Police Force under the Fire Services Act [Cap 80]; (ii) building authorization and structural load inspection clearance from the Public Works Department; and (iii) workplace registration with the Department of Labour, including an OHS plan, fire exits, first aid provisions, machine guarding for sewing equipment, and particulate control for synthetic fibre dust.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum authorized or paid-up capital is prescribed for a Vanuatu International Company; the sole founding subscriber may subscribe for one ordinary share of par value USD 1.00 or an equivalent in any freely convertible currency. No escrow bank account or capital deposit is required at incorporation; the share capital requirement is nil and shares may be issued fully or partly paid. Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable to a Vanuatu International Company conducting offshore trading only. An International Company is statutorily prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents, owning local land, or operating a physical domestic establishment; any on-island tailoring workshop or warehouse would instead require a domestic company incorporated under the Companies Act [Cap 191] and licensing by the Department of Industry, Ministry of Trade, Tourism, Industry and Cooperatives, together with a municipal business license.

Category: Not applicable. If, in the future, a domestic Vanuatu subsidiary were established, it would require a 'Business License' issued by the Port Vila Municipality or other municipal authority and, for knitwear/textile production, a factory registration with the Department of Labour under workplace safety legislation.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Inland Revenue Division, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (MoFEM), Port Vila; official government portal: https://gov.vu (with VRC/ASYCUDA e-services modules)

E-government filing portal

STEP 1 - Foreign beneficial owners incorporate the International Company through a licensed Registered Agent under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission issues the Certificate of Incorporation. STEP 2 - The Registered Agent lodges a taxpayer identification application with VRC; Vanuatu does not levy income tax, so the issued taxpayer number functions as the VAT/statistical identifier and is required for a Certificate of Tax Exemption. STEP 3 - The IC applies on Form VAT-01 (if it will transact or import in Vanuatu) for VAT registration; the certificate of incorporation, registered agent details, director/shareholder identity documents and local registered office address are attached. STEP 4 - The IC obtains a s. 52 International Companies Act Certificate of Tax Exemption from VRC, confirming a 20-year exemption from all Vanuatu taxes, duties and levies for offshore operations. STEP 5 - If show props or raw materials physically enter Vanuatu customs, the IC obtains an importer/exporter code in ASYCUDA World; for purely offshore e-commerce and overseas drop-ship sales, steps 3 and 5 are unnecessary.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. Relevant elections for an International Company are: (1) election to apply the Companies Act [Cap. 191] governance rules in place of certain IC Act provisions; (2) election under s. 52 to hold a Certificate of Tax Exemption (20 years); (3) election to dispense with audited accounts; (4) election to redomicile to or from Vanuatu; (5) annual economic-substance self-classification election under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act/Rules, reserving offshore tax-exempt status for a pure IC with no relevant activity in Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration for a person carrying on business in Vanuatu with annual taxable turnover above VUV 5,000,000 in a 12-month period (approx. USD 42,000); voluntary registration is open to exporters to claim input VAT credits. An offshore International Company manufacturing and delivering cosplay apparel outside Vanuatu does not cross a Vanuatu VAT nexus and is not required to register; nexus arises only on substantive Vanuatu establishment or on import of goods into Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under the VAT Act where (a) goods are exported from Vanuatu and leave the country, generally within 28 days of supply, supported by customs and shipping evidence; or (b) services are supplied to a non-resident and the benefit is enjoyed entirely outside Vanuatu. The cosplay prop/apparel manufacturing business qualifies either as non-scope (goods manufactured and sold overseas) or zero-rated export if goods physically pass through Vanuatu. Input VAT incurred locally (sampling, prototypes, design services, freight) is creditable in proportion to documented zero-rated export sales.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry, with the HS code corresponding to ISIC 3240 manufactured theatrical wear/props)
  • Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of the goods from Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank MT103/SWIFT credit confirmation or foreign exchange conversion certificate evidencing receipt of export payment)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • VAT zero-rating claim form (export schedule prescribed by VRC) with underlying purchase invoices and forwarder receipts
  • International Company Certificate of Incorporation and valid Certificate of Tax Exemption

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-API clearance or digital stamping regime exists in Vanuatu. VAT invoices must show the seller's TIN/VAT number, buyer, description, VAT-exclusive amount and 12.5% VAT. Export clearance is executed through the ASYCUDA World single-window customs system, which records the export declaration and serves as the evidential anchor for zero-rating; there is no pre-clearance invoice approval or continuous transaction control for B2B/B2C invoices.

Vanuatu has no exchange-control enactment, so the 180-day repatriation benchmark is a commercial/bank standard rather than a statute deadline. Unsubstantiated zero-rating claims are recharacterized as domestic taxable supplies, permitting a 5-year retrospective assessment of 12.5% VAT plus interest. Civil penalties range from 50% to 200% of the tax shortfall; corporate fines can reach VUV 5,000,000; directors, officers and the Registered Agent risk imprisonment of up to 2 years for fraudulent declarations. Misuse of an International Company tax exemption or economic-substance self-certification triggers revocation of the Certificate of Tax Exemption and VFSC deregistration. In addition, Vanuatu's inclusion on the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions and the OECD/EOIR standards requires full beneficial-ownership, transfer-pricing and export-documentation trails, and artificial arrangements designed merely to obtain zero-rating are susceptible to general anti-avoidance challenge.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a fully liberalized capital and current account regime. The Exchange Control Act has been effectively abrogated; no exchange control approval is required for inward capital subscription, outward profit remittance, dividend distribution, or trade settlement by an International Company (IC). The Vatu (VUV) is a floating currency, not freely traded offshore, but resident and non-resident entities may freely hold, convert, and transfer VUV and foreign currency accounts. Profit remittance to shareholders is subject to zero withholding tax under the IC's Tax-Exempt Zone classification; the only practical friction is correspondent bank compliance rather than statutory RBV restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists. Exporters of manufactured goods (including cosplay prop apparel) may retain 100% of foreign currency proceeds in onshore or offshore foreign-currency accounts indefinitely. There is no mandated conversion of USD, AUD, or EUR receipts into VUV, and no RBV declaration or permitting step is required for repatriation of trade receivables. ICs are additionally prohibited from conducting domestic business with Vanuatu residents, which reinforces that proceeds must derive exclusively from export or offshore commercial activity.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], acting as the sole monetary authority and regulator of licensed financial institutions in the Republic of Vanuatu.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low-Medium. Vanuatu International Companies face elevated de-risking scrutiny from offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) due to the jurisdiction's historical tax-haven classification and thin correspondent banking footprint; most mainstream EMIs either decline VU-incorporated entities outright or impose multi-tier enhanced due diligence. Direct Stripe and Shopify Payments onboarding is also unsupported for a Vanuatu-domiciled merchant, so the practical architecture is: (i) anchor a physical USD account with BSP or ANZ; (ii) route e-commerce settlement through a supported intermediate payment facilitator or a US/UK sales entity; and (iii) use the EMI only as a pre-funded disbursement wallet, never as primary treasury storage.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injections should be wired via SWIFT MT103 from the beneficial owner's personal or corporate account to the IC's USD account at BSP or ANZ with the word 'Share Capital Subscription' and the IC registration number in the remittance information field. No RBV approval or exchange control form is required; however, the receiving bank will require an advance copy of the capital introduction letter and the IC constitution's share issuance resolution to classify the credit as non-export revenue. Avoid lump-sum cash-equivalent transactions and always source funds from the same jurisdiction/bank as declared in the KYC file to prevent a 'split-transfer' flag.Primary rail is SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier payments (e.g., raw fabric, wig fiber, and hardware procured from China/Hong Kong/Vietnam), settled in USD or CNY depending on the supplier's bank's willingness to accept USD. For large procurement orders, a confirmed Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) issued by ANZ/BSP and advised through a Chinese or Vietnamese confirming bank is recommended to secure shipping terms; for lower-value disbursements, the IC should use bank-to-bank telegraphic transfer (TT) with full invoice and packing list cross-referenced in the payment narrative to avoid trade-based money laundering (TBML) flags. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax under domestic law; neither resident companies nor International Companies (ICs) are subject to a national corporate profits tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu has no dividend withholding tax; a Vanuatu IC can distribute untaxed international profits to foreign shareholders without deduction at source, and no advance dividend tax or dividend tax balance arises.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return is due within the statutory period following each anniversary of incorporation (administered as a 30-day filing window); the annual government fee is payable simultaneously. No extension is available for non-resident convenience.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Licences run on the calendar year; renewal must be submitted before 31 December for the licence to be valid from 1 January of the following year. Late renewal attracts an incremental penalty and materially delays merchant-account KYC re-validation.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Returns are due within the prescribed period after the end of each taxable period (standard quarterly cycle ending 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December); payment accompanies the return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing statute; there is no Local File or Master File threshold (nil VUV filing duty) because there is no corporate income tax base to allocate. Cross-border transactions with related factories or brand owners should nonetheless be documented at arm's length as best practice and to preserve integrity in any Economic Substance review.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Vanuatu amended the International Companies Act to introduce Economic Substance Requirements administered by the VFSC, aligning with OECD standards. A Vanuatu IC performing a relevant activity — notably 'distribution and service centre', 'holding business', or 'intellectual property business' — must demonstrate (i) physical presence and premises in Vanuatu, (ii) full-time employees proportionate to the activity, (iii) core income-generating activities performed in Vanuatu, and (iv) adequate local expenditure; an annual economic substance declaration is required. For the ISIC 3240 cosplay apparel structure, physical contract manufacturing typically occurs offshore; the IC's local function is limited, so B2C online retail to unrelated customers should fall outside the relevant-activity definition, but a written position paper should be filed with the substance declaration. Purchasing from a related factory and reselling to related distributors would trigger the substance test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion via false tax-exemption claims / VAT fraud

Statutory sanction: Back-assessment of 15% VAT plus statutory interest and misdeclaration penalties (commonly up to 100% of the tax), disqualification from the IC regime, licence cancellation, and criminal fines; persistent fraud leads to prosecution and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for knowing participation: directors are jointly and severally liable for assessed tax and penalties, exposed to restitution orders, criminal conviction, and, where proceedings risk flight, travel bans and asset-freezing orders.
Shell company / artificial substance avoidance

Statutory sanction: VFSC monetary penalties for false declaration, administrative strike-off and dissolution, loss of the IC's statutory tax benefit, and regulatory referral to OECD/EU listing mechanisms endangering the entity's banking and payment-rail relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face disqualification orders, administrative financial penalties, unfitness declarations taking effect across Vanuatu's regulated sectors, and personal liability for debts of the shell if it traded while unable to meet obligations.
AML / counter-terrorist financing failure

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime Act and Vanuatu's AML/CFT statutory framework: confiscation of proceeds, monetary penalties, suspension of the entity's licence, and imprisonment at the higher end for intentional money laundering.

Director personal liability: Senior managers and the AML compliance officer are personally accountable; sanctions include criminal conviction, travel bans pending seizure proceedings, personal bankruptcy orders, and civil restitution to defrauded stakeholders.
Failure of annual return / fee / substance filing discipline

Statutory sanction: Escalating monthly late fees, automatic strike-off and dissolution, forfeiture of assets to the State as bona vacantia, and permanent loss of the ability to operate Stripe or Shopify Payments merchant accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who continued to trade after strike-off are personally liable for contracts entered in the company's name, may be sued by creditors, and are barred from acting as directors of any Vanuatu company without court leave.
Unlicensed e-commerce / payment channel misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Fines per day of unlicensed trading, closure of the payment account by the regulator, VAT assessments with penalties, and director prosecution; acquirers may blacklist the entity's KYC profile.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorised the payment integration and the local representative who certified the licence application are personally jointly liable for fines and restitution; travel restrictions may be imposed pending resolution of the assessment.
