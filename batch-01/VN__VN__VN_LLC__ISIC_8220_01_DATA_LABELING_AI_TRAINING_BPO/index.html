Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum charter capital applies to an AI data-labeling BPO because the activity is not a conditional business and is not subject to the legal capital (vốn pháp định) regime; accordingly no escrow account or capital-blocking requirement is imposed. The charter capital is freely determined by the Members' Council and must be paid in full in VND or freely convertible foreign currency within 90 days from the date of issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate; a foreign-invested LLC must make capital contributions through a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) maintained at a licensed bank in Viet Nam, and the completed contribution must be reported to the DPI together with bank statements to update the business registration file.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (GDT, Tổng cục Thuế) under the Ministry of Finance (Bộ Tài chính); electronic administration via the National E-Tax Portal (https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn) and the GDT E-Invoice Portal (https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn). Provincial-level administration is delegated to local Tax Departments (Cục Thuế) such as Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested LLC automatically obtains a single statutory Tax Identification Number (MST, Mã số thuế) upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate by the local Business Registration Office (Sở Kế hoạch và Đầu tư) through the National Enterprise Registration Portal (https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn). The MST serves as the unified CIT, VAT, payroll, and customs identifier. Post-incorporation steps: (1) update full supplementary tax registration with Form 01-ĐK-TCT on the e-tax portal; (2) declare the VAT filing method and accounting regime; (3) obtain and register a digital signature (Token/CA) and a licensed e-invoice solution under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP; (4) open a licensed commercial-bank current account (both VND and foreign currency) and inform the GDT; (5) complete any CIT/VAT incentive registration if locating in a high-tech, IT park, or export-processing zone.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the VN_LLC is a Vietnamese tax-resident enterprise subject to enterprise income tax (CIT) on worldwide income at the standard headline rate of 20% under Law No. 14/2008/QH12 (as amended by Laws No. 32/2013/QH13 and No. 71/2014/QH13). There is no partnership pass-through or disregarded-entity regime recognized; profits are taxed at the LLC level, and outbound distributions/payments to offshore owners will bear separate dividend/interest/royalty WHT. The 'software products and digital data services' characterization of ISIC 8220 data-labeling activities may, if properly certified in a qualifying IT/high-tech or EPE zone, support a 10% CIT rate, a 4-year CIT exemption, and a 9-year 50% reduction under current incentive law, but requires pre-approval by the competent investment authority.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not operate a US-style 'check-the-box' system; the LLC is mandatorily taxed at entity level with no hybrid-entity election. Statutory elections and structured choices include: (1) election to carry forward CIT losses against future taxable profits for a maximum of 5 years; (2) election of Foreign Tax Credit crediting rules under Circular 08/2019/TT-BTC for foreign-sourced AI-training revenue; (3) election to apply 0% export VAT on qualifying offshore service contracts under Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC; (4) transfer-pricing method election (most favored: transactional net margin method) and advance pricing agreement application under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP; (5) election for global minimum top-up tax compliance under Resolution 107/2023/QH15 if the consolidated MNE group exceeds EUR 750 million revenue; (6) on a transactional basis, election of the deemed DNT/foreign-contractor regime where services are resold into Viet Nam by an offshore principal.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold applies to the VN_LLC; a foreign-invested enterprise is mandatorily VAT-registered from the moment of enterprise registration regardless of pre-revenue status. A VND 100,000,000 (one hundred million dong, approx. USD 4,000) annual revenue threshold applies only to household business establishments and individuals under Decree 209/2013/ND-CP, not to LLCs. Data-labeling BPO services exported to foreign clients are not subject to any domestic registration threshold beyond the enterprise's compulsory VAT identity.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

AI data-labeling, bounding-box annotation, and RLHF data-curation BPO services qualify for the 0% VAT export rate under Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC only when all conditions are cumulatively satisfied: (1) the service is provided under a written contract to a foreign organization or individual without a Vietnamese permanent establishment; (2) the service is consumed outside Viet Nam, evidenced by contract terms, data-flow infrastructure, and the absence of domestic end-users; (3) the VN_LLC performs the service inputs in Viet Nam but delivers/transfers the benefit to the offshore party; (4) consideration is paid in foreign currency through licensed Vietnamese banks via inward remittance; (5) an e-invoice is issued at 0% VAT showing the correct export service wording and GDT transmission code. Services provided to Vietnamese-onshore counterparties, services physically delivered in Viet Nam, and services embedded in a domestic value chain cannot use 0% and are taxable at the 8-10% standard/reduced rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Tờ khai hải quan xuất khẩu) - mandatory only where physical storage media, embedded servers, or hardware prototypes are shipped abroad in support of the data-labeling service
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (Vận đơn đường biển/đường hàng không) - evidencing cross-border movement of physical media, if any
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Chứng từ thanh toán qua ngân hàng có xác nhận của ngân hàng) issued by a Vietnamese licensed bank, evidencing receipt of export service proceeds in foreign currency remitted from the foreign client
  • Export Service Contract (Hợp đồng dịch vụ xuất khẩu) clearly defining delivery scope, acceptance criteria, offshore consumption, intellectual-property treatment, and payment terms
  • Service Completion and Acceptance Report (Biên bản nghiệm thu hoàn thành công việc) signed by the offshore counterparty or platform evidencing delivery of annotation/RLHF data outputs
  • Zero-rated VAT e-invoice (Hóa đơn điện tử thuế suất 0%) bearing the GDT invoice code and QR stamp, with proof of immediate transmission to the tax authority
  • Internal substance and cost file evidencing actual AI-training data processing capacity, headcount, and delivery infrastructure in Viet Nam for transfer-pricing and economic-substance verification

Electronic invoicing with mandatory transmission to the GDT has been obligatory for all VAT taxpayers, including the VN_LLC, since 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. Each e-invoice must be issued in XML format, bear a QR code, a seller digital signature, and be transmitted to the GDT e-invoice portal immediately at the point of issuance (near-real-time RTIR-style clearance). Taxpayers classified as high compliance risk, or those identified for fiscal control, must use a GDT 'coded invoice' (hóa đơn điện tử có mã của cơ quan thuế) in which the tax authority assigns a validation code before the invoice becomes legally effective. There is no physical stamping or paper seal regime; the GDT coded/QR invoice data serves as the fiscal clearance marker. Failure to transmit invoice data in the mandatory format with valid certificate or failure to include the 0% export VAT indicator will make the invoice legally ineffective and may trigger penalty under Decree 125/2020/ND-CP.

The GDT may conduct tax audits and VAT-refund post-clearance reviews within the 5-year statute of limitations under Law No. 38/2019/QH14. If export zero-rating or VAT refund claims are disallowed due to missing inward remittance proof, absence of foreign-consumption evidence, presence of a Vietnamese PE, circular/false documentation, or non-compliant e-invoice data transmission, the consequences are cumulative: (1) clawback of the entire VAT refund plus reassessment at the standard 10% rate on the gross invoiced amount; (2) administrative penalty equal to 20% of the under-declared or fraudulently refunded tax under Article 17 of Decree 125/2020/ND-CP, capped only by the actual unlawful benefit and augmented by late-payment interest at 0.03% per day (approx. 10.95% per annum); (3) invalidation of zero-rated invoices resulting in cascade liability across all affected tax periods; (4) economic-substance and transfer-pricing recharacterization under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP, including a deemed profit uplift for the BPO activity, which may eliminate any claimed refund or incentive; (5) where the fraudulent evasion or refund-claim abuse causes state revenue loss of more than VND 100 million, criminal prosecution under Article 200 of the Penal Code 2015 is available, with corporate and individual penalties ranging from monetary fines (up to VND 4.5 billion) to suspended imprisonment and compulsory restitution. Exporters must therefore retain the complete 'export file' for a minimum of 10 years and ensure all FX receipts repatriated within the mandatory 180-day window and reflected in both bank records and tax returns.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vietnam maintains a current-account-convertible but capital-account-controlled foreign exchange regime under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange (No. 05/2017/PL-UBTVQH14) and implementing circulars, principally Circular No. 02/2021/TT-NHNN. Legal current-account payments, including cross-border service fees, staff salaries for expatriates, and profit remittances to foreign investors, are freely executable through licensed banks upon presentation of supporting documentation. However, VND is not fully convertible offshore; all conversion must take place through the onshore interbank market via a licensed commercial bank. Profit remittance is conditioned on completion of annual audited financial statements, tax finalization, and the use of a registered Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) capital account (Direct Investment Capital Account - DICA). Remittance may be made once annually or in stages, with advance remittance requiring an auditor's confirmation of distributable profits. Capital-account flows remain controlled: foreign borrowing must be registered with the SBV unless exempt, equity injections must enter through the DICA, and VND lending to foreign-invested enterprises is subject to net-debt/equity ratio and risk-limitation regulations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Decree No. 70/2016/ND-CP and Circular No. 02/2021/TT-NHNN, export proceeds from services must be collected through licensed banks and repatriated into the exporter's foreign-currency account within 180 days from the date of the invoice or the export customs clearance date, whichever is applicable, unless an extension is granted by the SBV for force majeure or documented international payment delays. There is no mandatory conversion ratio currently applicable; the obligation is satisfied whether the proceeds are retained in a foreign-currency account at a licensed bank or sold into VND. For AI data-labeling and BPO cross-border service contracts, each invoice should contain the contract reference, delivery milestone evidence, and the bank's electronic SWIFT credit confirmation to support the 180-day repatriation narrative. Failure to repatriate on time triggers SBV inspection, fines, and restrictions on subsequent foreign-currency account usage.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Digital EMIs and payment platforms such as Wise, PayPal Business, and multi-currency neobank accounts are viable for low-value recurring disbursements and international client aggregations, but they cannot legally replace a supervisory bank in Vietnam. Feasibility rating: High for daily operational micro-payments (under approximately USD 10,000) and for receiving high-volume small-labeling invoices through Wise/PayPal; Medium when used as a supplementary collection rail; Low as the sole treasury backbone. Legal constraints: Offshore EMIs are not licensed by the State Bank of Vietnam to hold VND balances or provide local payment accounts; any payment service that establishes accounts for Vietnamese residents requires a SBV license. Cross-border payments initiated by EMI platforms must ultimately be converted and repatriated through a licensed Vietnamese bank to comply with export-proceeds surrender rules. Concentrated use of EMIs for invoicing does not satisfy statutory DICA or VAT invoicing requirements, and the LLC should maintain a local VND account for all VAT-paid local disbursements and salary payments.

Wise Fit: High
