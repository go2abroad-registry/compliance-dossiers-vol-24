Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (MFEM), Government of Vanuatu — official portal https://customs.gov.vu; corporate registry and International Company (IC) licensing: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — https://vfsc.vu; investment facilitation: Vanuatu Investment Promotion Authority (VIPA).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Incorporate the IC under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] through a licensed VFSC agent and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation with the statutory Company Number. Step 2 — Register with DCIR's Customs and Excise Division to obtain a Customs Client Code (exporter/importer identifier) enabling electronic export entries in ASYCUDA World. No national income-tax TIN is issued in Vanuatu because no income tax or VAT is operational. Step 3 — If a physical presence is established, obtain a Business Licence under the Business Licence Act and register for social obligations (VNPF, GI) only where local staff are engaged; an IC trading exclusively offshore does not require these registrations. Step 4 — Pay only the VFSC annual licence fee and applicable customs entry/registry fees; the IC is statutorily exempt from corporate tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty and all other present or future Vanuatu taxes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Vanuatu International Company is statutorily per se exempt from all Vanuatu taxes under s.144 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222], including income, capital gains, dividends, interest, royalties, stamp duty and any transaction or wealth tax. The exemption requires the IC not to conduct domestic onshore business; no economic-substance threshold is prescribed by the ICL for offshore trading income.

Available election forms

Not applicable — Vanuatu has no check-the-box, transparent-entity, or hybrid-mismatch election regime; the IC's tax-exempt classification is automatic and non-electable. Optional corporate-law mechanics available: (a) redomiciliation/continuation into Vanuatu as an IC; (b) election of share capital denomination in VUV or a foreign currency; (c) member-consent election to maintain records and registers in a foreign language or at a foreign location. No elective tax status exists pending possible future commencement of the Vanuatu VAT Act 2022.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-and-use tax is in force. The operative indirect-tax regime is the Vanuatu customs border regime under the Customs and Excise Act [Cap 144]: customs duty (ad valorem on imported goods) and excise-equivalent 'fiscal duty' (applied principally to imported alcohol, tobacco and petroleum). Exported baijiu (HS 2208) is outside the charge; no export duty is levied.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no operational VAT/GST registration threshold. The enacted but not yet commenced VAT Act 2022 prescribes a registration threshold of VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover; the implementation date has been repeatedly deferred, with a prospective start thereafter. For customs export registration, zero turnover threshold applies and every exporter must also obtain a Customs Client Code. For a domestic Business Licence, a small-trader simplified schedule exists, but an offshore IC is not required to hold one.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — export zero-rating presupposes a VAT, and Vanuatu does not operate one. All offshore export sales of Chinese baijiu consigned from Vanuatu are entirely outside the Vanuatu indirect-tax net by operation of law; there is no refund claim, export-completion certificate or repayment mechanism. The exporter remains obliged to evidence the genuine offshore wholesale transaction (commercial invoice, shipping evidence and banking settlement) under AML/CFT trade-screening controls; failure to do so attracts AML penalties, not tax clawbacks.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World Electronic Export Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT MT103 / Letter of Credit settlement documents
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Analysis / product specification (recommended for foreign customs and importing-country clearance; not a Vanuatu customs prerequisite)

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping or digital duty-stamp mandate exists. Export and import entries are lodged electronically through ASYCUDA World by licensed customs brokers, with an electronic entry number and risk-based selectivity assigned at clearance. Commercial invoices, bills of lading and packing lists must be retained for customs audit; there is no B2B fiscal reporting to the tax authority.

Vanuatu Customs retains audit authority over ASYCUDA transactions and may conduct post-clearance verification of export entries under the Customs and Excise Act [Cap 144]. No VAT refund clawback is currently possible; if a deferred VAT regime commences, misdeclared export claims would be subject to retrospective assessment, interest and penalties. Present sanctions for false export declarations or under-declaration include forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties as prescribed, and/or imprisonment. VFSC may revoke International Company status for fraud or misrepresentation, terminating the s.144 exemption and exposing the entity to standard business licence fees and penalties. Trade-based money-laundering offences under the AML/CFT Act are prosecuted with substantial Court-determined fines and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu is an IMF Article VIII member; the Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible for current-account payments (trade settlement, dividends, interest, fees) without prior RBV approval. Capital-account transfers (equity injections, intra-group loans, repatriation of capital) are freely permitted when executed through an authorized dealer (AD) within delegated bank limits; larger transfers require RBV Exchange Control consent, which is routinely granted for licensed ICs. For a tax-exempt International Company, profit/dividend remittance attracts zero Vanuatu withholding tax; the only real hurdle is bank-level source-of-funds verification (board resolution, register of members, VFSC Certificate of Good Standing, underlying trade contracts). No statutory profit-remittance ceiling applies to ICs.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the RBV Exchange Control Manual, proceeds from Chinese baijiu exports must be repatriated to a Vanuatu external account held with an authorized dealer within 180 days from the bill of lading date. There is no mandatory surrender or conversion ratio into VUV: exporters may retain 100% of USD/RMB proceeds in a foreign-currency account with the AD. The AD must receive a 'Proceeds of Export' declaration, commercial invoice, bill of lading, and the corresponding SWIFT credit advice; late repatriation or non-declaration can trigger RBV administrative sanctions and adverse bank risk ratings.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - Exchange Control Section, administering the Exchange Control Act [Cap 65] and the Financial Dealers Act [Cap 73]. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) incorporates the International Company (IC), but all banking, FX licensing, and cross-border payment oversight sit exclusively with RBV.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. Vanuatu has no regulated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing class; only RBV-licensed banks (as authorized dealers) and licensed Financial Dealers may hold client funds or transmit money. Offshore digital-only accounts (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business, Mercury, Airwallex) are not viable primary rails because they are not ADs in Vanuatu, the IC lacks economic nexus in the EMI's home jurisdiction, and most US/EU/UK EMIs automatically decline VU-registered offshore ICs. An RBV-licensed Financial Dealer may be used as a limited inward-remittance agent, never as the primary clearing account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing statute, no advance pricing agreement procedure, and no prescribed Local File/Master File threshold in VUV. Nevertheless, prudent ICs engaging in controlled transactions with related Chinese entities should maintain contemporaneous arm's-length benchmarking for banks, CRS reviews, and home-country tax audits; an operational benchmark threshold of VUV 30,000,000 in controlled transactions is recommended.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted a formal Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime of the type applied in other zero-tax jurisdictions; however, the VFSC imposes substance expectations under the International Companies Act and AML/CFT legislation. An IC wholly lacking real management, records, or banking activity may be recharacterized as a shell by foreign tax authorities under CFC, CRS, and substance-over-form doctrines, exposing the Chinese beneficial owners to imputed income in the People's Republic of China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under Vanuatu Penal Code, fines, and deregistration of the IC; cross-border exchange of information under CRS and tax information exchange agreements exposes the underlying PRC taxpayer to Chinese penalties of 50% to 500% of tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where fraud is proven; directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution, subject to asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment for related tax offenses.
Shell Company / Lack of Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC refusal of annual renewal and strike-off of the IC; simultaneous adverse CRS reporting to the PRC State Taxation Administration may trigger CFC anti-deferral rules, resulting in current taxation of undistributed IC profits in China.

Director personal liability: Directors of a shell IC face personal responsibility for wrongfully continued trading and unpaid sovereign fees; Chinese shareholders may be subject to individual asset preservation measures and exit bans while under investigation.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Vanuatu AML/CFT Act 2019 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Directive: fines up to VUV 30 million, asset forfeiture, suspension or revocation of the IC's VFSC license and bank facilities.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly orchestrate money laundering face imprisonment up to 5 years, civil confiscation, travel bans, and blacklisting from financial sectors in Vanuatu and China.
Unlicensed Liquor Export / Customs Breach

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu Customs legislation and Liquor Licensing Act: seizure and condemnation of the Baijiu consignment, revocation of export privileges, and monetary penalties of up to VUV 1,000,000 per consignment, in addition to excise liabilities.

Director personal liability: Beneficial directors who direct or knowingly participate in unlicensed liquor trade are personally responsible for customs penalties and may be prosecuted criminally; personal travel restrictions and disqualification from acting as directors may be imposed.
