Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Service (VCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; corporate licensing and registration of the International Company (IC) is administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Primary portals: https://vcir.gov.vu (tax and customs) and https://www.vfsc.vu (IC corporate e-Registry and annual returns).

E-government filing portal

An International Company is a non-resident, tax-exempt legal person; no national income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax TIN is issued or required. Where a VAT identity is needed for voluntary registration, the IC files Form VAT-01 with the VCIR attaching the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, constitutional documents, director identification, registered-agent confirmation, and turnover projections. Compulsory VAT registration applies when taxable turnover in any 12-month period equals or exceeds VT 5,000,000. For customs purposes, the IC registers as a trader in the ASYCUDA World portal to obtain a Customs Client ID for export declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The Vanuatu International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax, VAT on offshore operations, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and inheritance tax under the International Companies Act 1992; it is prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents and from owning Vanuatu real property, so its offshore custom-manufacturing and export of keyboard keycaps falls entirely outside the Vanuatu VAT and income tax net.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, fiscal unity, or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Vanuatu. The IC may voluntarily apply for VAT registration even when below the compulsory threshold, which permits input VAT recovery on locally purchased materials, while exported goods remain zero-rated. An IC may elect to convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act 1991, thereby forfeiting its tax-exempt status, but this election is commercially inadvisable and rarely used.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply, and to services supplied to a non-resident recipient where the direct benefit is enjoyed wholly outside Vanuatu. Custom double-shot PBT keycaps manufactured in Vanuatu and sold to offshore buyers via Shopify Payments or Stripe qualify for 0% VAT, provided the IC retains proof of export and non-resident status of the purchaser; goods supplied while the IC remains non-resident and goods never entering Vanuatu are outside the scope of VAT entirely.

  • Customs Export Declaration processed electronically in ASYCUDA World
  • Bill of Lading or House Airway Bill evidencing physical removal of goods from Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 / bank credit advice evidencing foreign-currency receipt of consideration

No national e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or CTC (continuous transaction control) mandate exists. Export consignments must be cleared electronically through ASYCUDA World, which is mandatory for all export declarations. Printed or PDF VAT invoices used for domestic supplies must meet minimum content requirements under the VAT Act; no digital fiscal stamping applies.

Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control regime and no statutory FX repatriation deadline; exporters generally document payment within 180 days as a prudential benchmark for zero-rating substantiation. VCIR may audit zero-rating claims retrospectively and any erroneous or fraudulent claim triggers repayment of VAT, statutory late-payment penalties and interest, and criminal fines plus imprisonment for knowingly false declarations under the VAT Act; directors of the IC remain personally liable for fraudulent customs documentation, and the tax-exempt status of the IC does not shield against prosecution for customs or VAT fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu observes IMF Article VIII obligations: the vatu is freely convertible for current-account and capital-account payments, with no prior approval required for trade-related FX conversion, dividend repatriation, or capital injections by an International Company. The current account is fully open for the keyboard-caps exporter: sales proceeds remitted by payment aggregators (Stripe, Shopify Payments) may be credited to VUV or foreign-currency accounts and converted at market rates. The capital account is equally open for an IC: share capital, shareholder loans, and intercompany transfers may enter and leave without RBV consent, and there are no profit-remittance hurdles other than standard AML/KYC documentation (source-of-funds certificates, underlying sales/supply contracts, CRS/FATCA self-certifications). The only practical convertibility friction is the thinness of the offshore VUV market; USD is the effective settlement currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable to the VU_IC. The International Companies Act legislatively exempts International Companies from the exchange-control regime, meaning there is no statutory timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no forced sale of export proceeds to the RBV or an authorized dealer. Export receipts from custom mechanical keyboard keycap sales, including aggregated Stripe and Shopify Payouts in USD, AUD, and EUR, may be retained, swept, netted, or hedged at the IC's discretion. Administrative expectation: maintain a six-year trail of export invoices, logistics documents, and payout reconciliation reports for the bank's AML file and for any future economic-substance or tax-residency examination.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), the statutory central bank constituted under the Central Bank Act, holds exclusive authority over monetary policy, vatu (VUV) issuance and convertibility, administration of exchange-control rules, and licensing/oversight of authorized-dealer banks. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) acts as the corporate registry and AML/CFT supervisor for International Companies. The RBV operates the domestic payments and settlement infrastructure and jointly administers the policy framework applicable to the Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC) with the VFSC.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. A Vanuatu IC will almost certainly be declined by mainstream offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Mercury, Airwallex, Relay) because Vanuatu triggers higher-risk jurisdiction filters, non-resident beneficial-ownership flags, and correspondent-bank de-risking policies; in addition, no offshore EMI clears VUV. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Remit via SWIFT MT103 only, from the beneficial owner's or parent company's bank into the IC's operational USD or VUV account at ANZ or Bred, with the remittance narrative stating 'capital contribution / shareholder loan - International Companies Act' and no ambiguous third-party intermediaries. The receiving bank will require a board resolution authorizing the injection, the VFSC register extract showing the shareholder, a source-of-wealth certificate, and CRS/FATCA self-certification. No RBV exchange-control approval is needed. For VUV-denominated legs the funds clear through the RBV-operated payments system; for USD legs they clear through the New York or Sydney correspondent network. Confirm the value date before issuing supplier purchase orders.Two clearing rails are recommended: (1) direct SWIFT MT103 transfers for PBT resin, molds, packaging, and Asian supplier invoices, each tied to a commercial invoice and packing list; and (2) irrevocable sight or standby commercial letters of credit issued through ANZ or BSP for large production batches. Digitally delivered keycap designs (STL and 3D-print files) are invoiced as services and cleared by MT103 against a service agreement. Every disbursement must be accompanied by the complete export pack comprising the commercial invoice, packing list, airway bill or bill of lading, and, where relevant, certificate of origin, to avoid sanctions-screening holds. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Nil) — Vanuatu does not impose corporate income tax; an International Company (IC) is contractually and statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu national taxes under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu has no dividend withholding tax, no general withholding-tax statute, and no taxation of distributions paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders; dividends from the IC are repatriated free of any Vanuatu deduction or levy.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Anniversary-based: the Annual Renewal Record and fee are due on each anniversary of the Certificate of Incorporation. A 30-day administrative grace period is applied by the VFSC; thereafter a 10% flat late-payment surcharge accrues, and continued default for three months triggers strike-off proceedings.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold: Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and has not enacted Local File, Master File, or country-by-country reporting obligations applicable to International Companies. Defensive benchmark: prepare contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation whenever annual intercompany transactions (purchases, royalties, service fees) exceed VUV 10,000,000 (approx. USD 85,000), in order to withstand foreign CFC, GAAR, and substance challenges.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance (Implementation) Act 2021 applies to every Vanuatu-registered entity, including ICs, carrying on a 'relevant activity'. Cross-border e-commerce sale of keyboard keycaps constitutes a distribution and service centre. The IC must be directed and managed in Vanuatu, perform core income-generating activities in Vanuatu, maintain adequate premises and full-time employees in Vanuatu, and file an annual ESR return with the VFSC. A letterbox IC with only a registered agent fails the test; non-compliance exposes the IC to civil penalties, cancellation of registration, and criminal referral of directors.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: VAT Act No. 40 of 1998: civil penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded, plus late-payment penalties of up to 20% of the tax; criminal conviction: fines up to VUV 2,000,000 (approx. USD 17,000) and/or up to two years' imprisonment per count; the VFSC concurrently cancels the IC's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false VAT returns or direct the evasion are jointly and severally liable for civil penalties; the corporate veil is pierced upon fraud; directors may be made subject to asset-freeze orders and prohibited from leaving Vanuatu pending criminal trial; extradition is available under the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance (Implementation) Act 2021: civil penalty up to VUV 10,000,000 (approx. USD 84,000) per failure, with continuing penalties for ongoing default; VFSC strike-off and disqualification of the registered agent; referral to the OECD Global Forum may blacklist the structure and trigger international exchange of information.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly misstate substance face criminal liability with imprisonment up to two years; after strike-off, directors remain personally liable for obligations contracted subsequent to dissolution; foreign tax authorities may pierce the IC under CFC or GAAR rules and assess the beneficial owners at home-country rates with interest and penalties.
AML/CFT and Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2017 and the Proceeds of Crime Act [Cap. 280]: fines up to VUV 10,000,000 (approx. USD 84,000) for record-keeping and suspicious transaction reporting failures; money-laundering offences carry custodial sentences of up to 20 years; the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze all bank and payment-rail accounts.

Director personal liability: Individual directors and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution; personal assets may be frozen and travel bans imposed pending investigation; directors who sign false UBO declarations are personally liable to creditors and to the registered agent for resulting damages, and may be disqualified from managing any Vanuatu company.
Misrepresentation to VFSC / False Annual Declaration

Statutory sanction: International Companies Act [Cap. 222]: offence punishable by fines up to VUV 500,000 (approx. USD 4,200) and cancellation of the certificate of incorporation; reinstatement, if granted, requires court approval, payment of all arrears, and restoration fees of approximately VUV 100,000.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly or recklessly provide false statements are personally criminally liable; VFSC may seek civil penalties and disqualification orders barring management of any Vanuatu company for up to 10 years; upon strike-off the Registrar may obtain a receivership over the IC's worldwide receivables.
Continued Trading After Strike-off / Unpaid Annual Fees

Statutory sanction: Automatic dissolution of the IC; all contracts entered into post-dissolution are voidable by counterparties; unpaid government fees, surcharges, and penalties remain due as a Crown debt enforceable in the Vanuatu courts.

Director personal liability: Every director is jointly and severally personally liable for debts and liabilities contracted while the company was struck off; shareholders lose limited-liability protection; counterparties may sue directors personally in Vanuatu and enforce foreign judgments against them; the Registrar may obtain asset-preservation orders, including travel restrictions, until the debt is satisfied.
