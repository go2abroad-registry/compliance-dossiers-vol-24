Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for company and international company registration; Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, for customs and indirect levies; registered-agent portal administered by licensed VFSC agents.

E-government filing portal

No national TIN or VAT identification number exists in Vanuatu because there is no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or VAT/GST. A Vanuatu International Company obtains its sole statutory identifier through the VFSC registration certificate issued by a licensed registered agent upon incorporation. For physical bitumen export/import clearance, the IC must obtain a Customs Importer/Exporter Code from Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue by presenting the VFSC certificate of incorporation, registered-agent certificate, and commercial shipping documentation. No direct-tax PIN is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. The Vanuatu International Company tax exemption is automatic and mandatory under the International Companies Act, not elective. There is no check-the-box regime, no pass-through election, no hybrid mismatch election, and no fiscal-transparency election in Vanuatu domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Vanuatu has no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; the only indirect indirect-type levies are customs import duties, export duties where applicable, and selective consumption duties on specified goods, none of which apply to the offshore wholesale supply of containerized bitumen by an International Company.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in VUV — there is no VAT/GST registration threshold because no VAT/GST legislation exists. For customs purposes, an Importer/Exporter Code is required for any physical shipment of bitumen, regardless of turnover value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Vanuatu does not impose VAT/GST; therefore there is no zero-rating mechanism and no refund substantiation obligation. Exports of drummed or bagged penetration-grade bitumen by a Vanuatu International Company are outside the scope of Vanuatu tax, and the offshore income is tax exempt by statute. No prior ruling is required for the 0% effective rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration in ASYCUDA World reflecting containerized bitumen movement
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing carriage from Vanuatu to foreign buyer
  • Commercial Invoice issued by VU_IC to the overseas purchaser
  • Packing List specifying drum/bag count, gross/net weight, and HS classification (HS 2713/2715)
  • Certificate of Analysis confirming penetration-grade bitumen specifications

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing, tax-authority API clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Vanuatu. Customs declarations are processed through the ASYCUDA World system, and a standard commercial invoice and packing list accompanying the containerized export are sufficient.

No VAT clawback or export-refund repayment is applicable because no VAT/GST is levied. Customs and VFSC audits may nonetheless scrutinize export truthfulness, trade statistics, and the IC's exempt status. False declarations, understating export values, or misuse of the International Company exemption may result in seizure and forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties assessed under Vanuatu customs law, deregistration of the IC, and criminal prosecution where fraudulent intent is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a de facto current account convertibility regime: trade-related payments are cleared by RBV-licensed authorized dealers without prior approval, but every transaction requires an exchange control declaration routed through the dealer. Capital account flows (direct investment, shareholder loans, portfolio transfers) are permissible but subject to dealer-level verification and RBV notification; borrowing by Vanuatu-resident entities from non-residents requires RBV approval. The Vatu (VUV) is managed against a trade-weighted basket (USD, EUR, AUD, NZD) with predictable intra-USD band behavior. Profit remittance for an International Company is hurdle-free at the tax level (IC is statutorily tax exempt and no dividend withholding is imposed), but the bank will require proof that (i) the IC conducted no domestic business in Vanuatu, (ii) all proceeds originate from external trade in the USD-denominated clearing rail, and (iii) no VUV conversion takes place beyond incidental operating costs. Principal remaining hurdle: final counterparties and cargo ports must not be OFAC/EU/UN-sanctioned or the dealer will freeze the outward leg.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender or conversion obligation applies to an International Company because it is deemed non-resident for exchange control purposes and statutorily prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu. Resident exporters must convert export receipts to VUV within 2 business days or obtain prior RBV authorization to retain foreign currency for up to 90 days. The IC treasury policy must therefore maintain only non-resident foreign-currency accounts at the authorized dealer, never route into a resident VUV account, and document the offshore origin of all USD receipts for audit trail purposes.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), Port Vila, under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 62] and the Financial Institutions Act [Cap. 210]. Ancillary authority over company registration and financial services is the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Vanuatu has no EMI-specific licensing regime; the RBV does not recognize offshore digital EMI licences (e.g., E-Money licenses from EEA/UK) for local treasury purposes, and the major Vanuatu banks will reject EMIs as intermediary settlement parties. Any attempt to settle bitumen LC or high-value MT103 disbursements through a fintech EMI introduces clearing risk and AML rejection; the compliant route is direct SWIFT correspondent banking via ANZ/Bred Bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Vanuatu statutory threshold; no Local File or Master File is required under VU law. For bank and foreign tax due diligence, maintain board-approved intercompany pricing evidence where cumulative related-party wholesale transactions exceed VUV 50,000,000 (approx., not statutory) per annum.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted local Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) equivalent to BVI or Cayman. Nevertheless, ICs used for bitumen/asphalt trading should maintain genuine administrative substance in Port Vila (registered office, agent, board minutes, books and records) because banks and foreign tax authorities may apply OECD-style economic substance or CFC tests; the VFSC AML/CFT regime requires adequate record-keeping and beneficial-ownership transparency.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration to Government

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the VAT Act or Customs and Excise Act where triggered, recovery of unpaid duty and tax, criminal fines and imprisonment for fraud; because Vanuatu has no income tax, the underlying liability is typically assessed under VAT, customs, or the International Companies Act for misrepresentation; courts may impose a monetary penalty based on the amount involved plus costs.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal exposure for knowingly signing false declarations; civil restitution to government, disqualification as a director, travel bans and custodial sentences under the Vanuatu Penal Code for forgery or fraud; the corporate veil is pierced where the IC is a mere facade for the director's trading activity.
Shell Company or Abuse of Offshore Structure

Statutory sanction: VFSC refusal or revocation of IC status, striking-off from the register, freezing orders obtained by counterparties, court-ordered winding-up, fines under the International Companies Act and Penal Code for false statements; in serious cases, confiscation of proceeds of illegal and fraudulent transactions.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the veil where the IC is a sham; principals and nominee directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid LC liabilities and indemnities, may be ordered to restore misused assets to creditors, and may face travel bans pending restitution and criminal sanctions if fraud is established.
AML/CFT Violations in Trade Payment Rails (SWIFT LC / TT Wire)

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act, FIU administrative fines, account freezes, AML consent orders, and potential criminal liability for money laundering; banks may terminate the IC's banking relationship and report suspicious transactions to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: AML violations attach personally to directors and officers; personal criminal liability for money laundering can include imprisonment; directors can also be disqualified by VFSC or FIU and may be subject to cross-border arrest, travel bans, and international financial sanctions coordination with overseas regulators.
Falsified or Absent Records / Accounting Fraud

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties for false accounting and forgery; civil claims by LC issuing banks for presentation of fraudulent documents; VFSC may impose fines, suspend the IC, or strike it off; tax or VAT assessments may be reconstructed and enforced if customs import records reveal misreporting.

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally liable for fraudulent misrepresentation to banks and for knowingly allowing false records; restitution orders, disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment apply; in insolvent trading situations, court-appointed liquidators may recover preferences and compel director contribution.
