Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila, Vanuatu; taxpayer and customs portal: ASYCUDA World / Vanuatu Customs Online (www.gov.vu, www.customs.gov.vu)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities obtain a Vanuatu International Company identification number from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) rather than a national TIN. A DCIR VAT registration number is required only when the IC makes, or intends to make, taxable supplies in Vanuatu in excess of the VAT threshold, or voluntarily registers to zero-rate export supplies. The registration application is submitted to DCIR with the certificate of incorporation, VFSC extract, directors'/shareholders' identification and address proof, business licence (if required), expected turnover declaration, and evidence of activity; DCIR issues a VAT ID upon satisfactory verification.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available in Vanuatu. A foreign-owned International Company is outside the Vanuatu income tax base as a non-resident exempt entity. The only practical elections are voluntary VAT registration for exporters seeking input VAT recovery and the choice to operate entirely from outside Vanuatu so no local VAT, business licence, or customs nexus arises.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 of taxable supplies in any period of 12 consecutive months; voluntary registration is available for exporters.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered VAT person may zero-rate supplies of goods exported from Vanuatu to a destination outside Vanuatu, and certain services performed outside Vanuatu for an overseas customer, provided the supplier retains evidence of physical export within the statutory period, the buyer is outside Vanuatu, and the goods are not retained for local consumption. For the semiconductor wholesale and brokerage model, zero-rating is available only where the goods move through Vanuatu customs under a valid export declaration and title passes to an offshore buyer; pure offshore-to-offshore brokerage without a Vanuatu supply remains outside the VAT net and does not require VAT registration.

  • Customs Export Declaration / SAD lodged in ASYCUDA World with export clearance stamp
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Vanuatu to the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list identifying SSD and server processor products, quantities, unit prices, and HS classification
  • Supplier VAT tax invoice and export certificate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT credit / bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API filing, or digital stamping regime is in force. Tax invoices must include the supplier's name and VAT ID, invoice date and number, description, quantity, amount excluding VAT, VAT rate and VAT payable. Exports are cleared through the ASYCUDA World customs electronic declaration; transport documents and commercial invoices must be retained as substantiation.

DCIR may audit export zero-rating and raise a VAT assessment at 12.5% on the export invoice, plus interest and penalties of up to 200% of the tax due, if documentary proof is missing, goods do not leave Vanuatu, or the transaction is found to be a sham. Customs misdeclaration penalties and VFSC licence suspension may apply; fraud may attract criminal prosecution and retrospective clawback of input VAT credits previously refunded.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal convertible-currency regime: the Vatu (VUV) is independently floating, current-account payments are free from central-bank pre-approval, and capital-account repatriations are subject only to bank-level AML/EDD documentary review rather than statutory quotas. An International Company operating in offshore mode is exempt from exchange-control restrictions under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] provided it does not carry on domestic business in Vanuatu; consequently, profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital withdrawal encounter no convertibility hurdles or profit-franking requirements. Practical friction is limited to correspondent-banking scrutiny: USD-clearing banks may impose enhanced review or fee surcharges on VUV/VU-flagged flows unless trade documentation, beneficial-ownership declarations, and purpose codes are complete and coherent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender ratio, conversion timeline, or repatriation deadline applies to International Companies in Vanuatu. RBV retains statutory powers under the Exchange Control Act to direct domestic traders to convert export proceeds, but the IC is automatically exempt when it has no Vanuatu-source business, and USD, AUD, or EUR receivables may be retained indefinitely in the IC's operating account. Where the IC settles local expenses (registered-agent fees, VFSC filing fees, or legal retainers), the bank converts only the precise VUV-denominated amount at prevailing spot rates, leaving all trade balances in the original contract currency.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 63] and as banking and payment-system supervisor under the Banks Act [Cap. 64] and the National Payment System Act 2022; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) administers the International Companies registry and licensing under the Financial Dealers Licensing Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low — not viable as the sole treasury rail. Global EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) generally decline Vanuatu-registered International Companies due to limited local substance and offshore-style opacity; specialist APAC or UK EMI/PSP providers may offer USD/GBP/EUR IBANs, but high closure risk, elevated loading fees, and absence of LC or documentary-collection capability make them unsuitable for wholesale SSD/CPU settlement. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital and pre-sale working capital should enter as SWIFT MT103 wires from the beneficial owner's own corporate or personal account in an OECD/G20 jurisdiction to the IC's USD operating account at ANZ or BSP. Each wire must include: beneficiary account number and IC registration number, ISO 20022 purpose code 'TRAD' or 'GOOD' with narration 'capital injection for wholesale semiconductor trading, ISIC 4651', and reference to the underlying capitalization resolution. The receiving bank will apply exchange-control record-keeping and may require: board resolution approving the capital call under the IC's constitutive documents; updated register of members evidencing share issuance; and source-of-wealth evidence for the remitting shareholder, typically six months of bank statements plus a contract of employment or prior-year tax assessment. Third-party or family-member contributions must be supported by formal loan agreements and equivalent EDD documentation; all receipts are reconciled against the capital table before any trading disbursement is permitted.Outbound settlement is executed through three reinforcing rails: (1) Swift TT (MT103) for open-account and escrow-inspection-anchored settlements to pre-vetted suppliers, with narration containing contract and invoice references; (2) Documentary Letter of Credit (MT700 issuance, MT707 amendments) established by ANZ or BSP against 100% cash cover — an IC will not obtain unsecured LC lines, but cash-backed LCs are issuable within two to three business days, enabling supplier-side mitigations (partial shipment, tolerance, inspection clause); and (3) Documents Against Payment (CAD/D/P) collection, under which the settlement bank releases title documents strictly against payment or acceptance, suitable for mid-value consignments and the Escrow_Inspection_Wire rail when release is conditioned on an inspection certificate. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax on International Companies; the International Companies Act (Cap. 222) grants a complete exemption to non-resident-owned ICs, and no income tax is levied on their offshore profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or liquidation proceeds paid by an International Company to its non-resident shareholders, irrespective of beneficial ownership.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing legislation, permanent establishment threshold, or thin-capitalization rules; no Master File, Local File, or Country-by-Country reporting obligations apply to International Companies in Vatu or any other currency.

Economic substance scope: No Economic Substance Regulations are in force in Vanuatu; nevertheless, an IC must maintain a registered office and records in Vanuatu, act through a licensed registered agent, and refrain from prohibited domestic business. In practice, substance is evidenced by board minutes, trade contracts, and payment flows held at the registered office. Foreign tax courts may apply their own CFC, GAAR, or sham doctrines where the IC lacks economic substance and is de facto managed from the beneficial owner's residence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Cross-Border Tax Evasion and Treaty/GAAR Abuse

Statutory sanction: Foreign revenue authorities may reassess profits, impose penalty interest and penalty taxes up to 200% of the tax avoided, and apply general anti-abuse rules under MLI/GAAR; the Vanuatu VFSC may also fine the IC VT 1,000,000 for knowingly filing false declarations.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors personally liable for restitution of evaded tax plus punitive civil penalties; serious cases trigger cross-border arrest warrants, travel bans, asset-seizure orders, and imprisonment of up to 7 years for tax fraud in the residence jurisdiction.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC strike-off from the International Companies Register, cancellation of the certificate of incorporation, confiscation of the registered office, and fines up to VT 2,000,000; internationally, the structure triggers EU/AML risk-rating and termination of correspondent banking relationships.

Director personal liability: The veil of incorporation is pierced for abuse of the corporate form; shareholders and directors become jointly and severally liable for the company's trade debts and foreign tax liabilities, face disqualification as directors for up to 5 years, and may be barred from re-entering Vanuatu pending regulatory investigation.
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorism Financing (FTRA)

Statutory sanction: The Financial Transactions Reporting Act (as amended) criminalizes money laundering with imprisonment of up to 10 years and/or fines of up to VT 10,000,000; the instrumentalities of the offence are subject to forfeiture, and a conviction triggers automatic revocation of the IC's registration by the VFSC.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and beneficial owners who knowingly participate, consent, or are wilfully blind face personal criminal liability, confiscation of personal assets, cross-border travel bans, and extradition; corporate assets are frozen pending trial and disgorgement of profits is mandatory.
Prohibited Local Business and Unlicensed Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: Every prohibited transaction is void as against the State; the Registrar of International Companies may cancel the certificate of incorporation and impose a fine of VT 500,000 per violation; revenue derived from illegal domestic trade is subject to full recoupment and penalties.

Director personal liability: Each director who participated in a prohibited act is individually liable for all money received, damages suffered by third parties, and VFSC enforcement costs; the Court may order restitution, grant injunctive relief to the Registrar, and imprison defaulting directors for up to 2 years.
