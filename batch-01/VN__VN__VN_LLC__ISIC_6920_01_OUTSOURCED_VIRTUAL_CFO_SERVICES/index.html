Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum charter capital applies to a foreign-invested limited liability company providing professional virtual CFO and bookkeeping services. The charter capital may be freely determined by the founders but must be fully paid in within 90 days after issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate, in accordance with Article 26 of the Law on Investment. For service businesses, capital must be sufficient to cover implementation costs and registered operational scope.

Funding & unlock path: The office must obtain a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate / Fire Protection Acceptance (Giấy chứng nhận thẩm duyệt thiết kế và nghiệm thu về PCCC) from the Provincial Fire Prevention and Firefighting Police under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP. The site must be structurally sound for ordinary office use (no industrial load requirements), have emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, and clearly marked evacuation routes. The company must register the workplace with the Occupational Safety and Health Authority if there are any risk-prone machines, and provide basic occupational safety gear, first-aid equipment, and organization of annual occupational safety training under Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; operational portals: e-Tax Online Filing (https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn), Electronic Invoice System (https://hoadon.dientu.gdt.gov.vn), and the National Business Registration Portal (https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn); primary local supervisor is the provincial/city Tax Sub-Department of the LLC's registered head office.

E-government filing portal

Foreign investors first file an investment dossier (where required under the Law on Investment 2020 for conditional sectors or large-scale projects) or proceed directly to the Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) for an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) under the Law on Enterprises 2020 (No. 59/2020/QH14), submitting the LLC charter, legal representative authorization, foreign-investor identity papers, and evidence of the registered head-office premises in Vietnam. Within 3 working days after ERC issuance, the DPI feeds data to the General Department of Taxation, which automatically assigns a 13-digit Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST); the LLC then completes its electronic tax registration in the e-Tax portal, opens a local corporate bank account, obtains a qualified electronic signature for tax submissions, and registers its electronic invoice template within statutory timeframes. For foreign entities that do not establish a permanent establishment in Vietnam and transact through local counterparties, Vietnam instead applies Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) withholding on remittances, with no separate TIN privilege; however, for the resident VN_LLC the MST is mandatory, automatic, and irrevocable.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; a Vietnamese foreign-invested LLC is a standalone corporate income tax (CIT) payer at the standard 20% rate and cannot elect pass-through treatment; no U.S.-style check-the-box election exists. Tax domicile follows a worldwide-income system for residents, with a foreign tax credit (FTC) for foreign-source income pursuant to the Law on Corporate Income Tax (amended by Law 71/2014/QH13), capped by the applicable Vietnamese CIT rate; there are no tax-exempt zones for the LLC's head-office model, and the special Export Processing Enterprise (EPE) regime is not available for service-sector companies. — Foreign investors first file an investment dossier (where required under the Law on Investment 2020 for conditional sectors or large-scale projects) or proceed directly to the Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) for an Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) under the Law on Enterprises 2020 (No. 59/2020/QH14), submitting the LLC charter, legal representative authorization, foreign-investor identity papers, and evidence of the registered head-office premises in Vietnam. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a Vietnamese foreign-invested LLC is a standalone corporate income tax (CIT) payer at the standard 20% rate and cannot elect pass-through treatment; no U.S.-style check-the-box election exists. Tax domicile follows a worldwide-income system for residents, with a foreign tax credit (FTC) for foreign-source income pursuant to the Law on Corporate Income Tax (amended by Law 71/2014/QH13), capped by the applicable Vietnamese CIT rate; there are no tax-exempt zones for the LLC's head-office model, and the special Export Processing Enterprise (EPE) regime is not available for service-sector companies.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is available under Vietnamese law. Statutory choices affecting tax identity include: (i) election of intra-group transfer-pricing methodology and documentation (e.g., TNMM or profit split on a profit-level indicator) under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP; (ii) election of the currency used in licensed service contracts, with VAT and CIT returns mandated in VND under the government's reference exchange rate; (iii) election to apply the treaty foreign tax credit for offshore CFO fees subject to source-country taxes; and (iv) for multinational groups covered by global minimum tax rules with effect for fiscal years from 2024, election of the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT) safe harbour under Decree 154/2024/ND-CP.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Registration is compulsory for every LLC from the effective date of its Enterprise Registration Certificate; there is no turnover-based VAT registration threshold for corporate taxpayers. As statutory background, households and individual business households with annual revenue not exceeding VND 100 million are excluded from VAT under the VAT Law; this de minimis threshold is not available to a limited liability company and does not affect the mandatory, automatic registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Article 9(4) of the Ministry of Finance Circular No. 219/2013/TT-BTC, export services provided to a foreign organization or individual and not consumed in Vietnam are subject to 0% VAT. The LLC's fractional CFO advisory, multicurrency bookkeeping, and financial analytics qualify only if (i) the purchaser is a non-resident of Vietnam and has no fixed establishment in Vietnam through which the services are received; (ii) the services are wholly or substantially supplied outside Vietnam, with substantive value generated offshore; (iii) the LLC retains contract, e-invoice, inbound foreign-currency receipt, and work-product evidence; and (iv) the payment is received through a bank transfer denominated in foreign currency, with Stripe_Billing and Wise settlement flows satisfying this condition only when the receiving Vietnamese bank statement identifies the offshore payer and the credited FX amount. Input VAT on costs attributable to such zero-rated supply is creditable, and excess credit is refundable, with refund applications exceeding VND 300 million attracting in-person audit.

  • Customs Export Declaration (required when any tangible deliverables accompany the services; for pure services it is substituted by service invoice and work-product records)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (only for physical exports of documentation or media)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Giấy báo Có) issued by the Vietnamese bank for the foreign-currency payment received from each offshore client
  • Service Export Contract or Statement of Work identifying the foreign non-resident buyer, service scope, place of consumption, and fee schedule
  • Valid electronic service invoice issued to the overseas client and registered with the e-invoice system, including the authority-coded XML record
  • Bank statement or settlement report from Stripe_Billing or Wise showing the payer name and the FX transfer being credited to the LLC's Vietnamese bank account
  • Work-product or engagement deliverables evidencing offshore utilization of the CFO advice and accounting records

E-invoicing is mandatory nationwide since 1 July 2022 under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC. Every invoice issued by the LLC must be generated as structured XML data, digitally signed with an authorized electronic signature, and transmitted to the General Department of Taxation's centralized e-invoice portal (hoadon.dientu.gdt.gov.vn). The tax authority assigns an invoice authentication code to each e-invoice, and taxpayers must register their e-invoice template and issuance principles before first use; invoices issued without the authority-assigned code are not fiscal receipts, and businesses using ERP systems must integrate the e-Tax API for batch transmission of invoice metadata within the applicable tax declaration cycle. Digital stamping is realized through the tax-authority-issued invoice code string and the nationwide centralized archive, which gives the tax authority real-time visibility of each invoice; Stripe_Billing and Wise settlement reports are cross-checked against invoice codes and inward FX remittance records.

The tax authority reconstructs claimed export zero-rating whenever an invoice lacks matching FX inward remittance within 180 days; Stripe_Billing and Wise settlement flows are high-scrutiny because the taxpayer must prove that the beneficiary of record is the Vietnamese LLC and not an aggregate payment intermediary. Mis-constructed zero-rated invoices are recharacterized as taxable domestic services, with output VAT reassessed at 10% of the full gross fee; the tax authority adds late-payment interest at 0.03% per day, administrative penalties of VND 20–25 million for defective documentation, and 1–3 times the understated tax for deliberate under-declaration under Law 38/2019/QH14. Criminal liability for organized export-invoice fraud arises under the Penal Code on tax evasion, with imprisonment up to 20 years, confiscation of illegally obtained VAT refunds, extension of the statute of limitations from 5 years to 10 years, and publication of the compliance default. The clawback reach is retroactive to the tax period of the mis-claimed export fee, and the recovered VAT cannot be credited against subsequent refundable input VAT.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with current-account convertibility for VND. Under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange (No. 06/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended) and SBV Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN, all payments, transfers and remittances for current-account transactions (trade in services, including management consultancy and virtual CFO services) are freely convertible and may be settled through SBV-licensed credit institutions without prior SBV approval. Capital-account transactions, including inbound equity contributions into a foreign-invested LLC and shareholder loans, require prior investment registration, opening of a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA), and compliance with the SBV's foreign-exchange control procedures. Profit remittance abroad is permitted once per year, after the audited financial statements and final corporate-income-tax settlement for the fiscal year, provided the LLC has no accumulated losses, has satisfied all tax and social-insurance obligations, and remits via the licensed foreign-currency account or DICA. VND is non-deliverable onshore; offshore NDF referencing VND is not recognized by SBV and may not be booked through a Vietnamese licensed bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Service-export proceeds (including cross-border virtual CFO advisory fees) denominated in foreign currency must be transferred to a licensed bank foreign-currency account in Viet Nam within 180 days from the date the service is completed and invoiced, as set out in Article 10 of Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN. There is no mandatory statutory conversion ratio: the LLC may retain the foreign currency in its account, sell it to the licensed bank, or use it for approved outbound payments. However, SBV reporting on the foreign-currency flow must be submitted via the bank's electronic reporting system using the appropriate balance-of-payments purpose code. Any extension of the 180-day repatriation timeline requires a written SBV approval obtained before the deadline, supported by underlying service contracts and documentary evidence of the delay.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam – SBV), acting through its Foreign Exchange Administration Department and provincial branches, together with the Ministry of Finance for concurrent tax-related FX remittance controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM with legal constraints. Offshore digital EMI platforms (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) may be used as ancillary collection channels for receiving small-value cross-border client payments, but they cannot substitute for a licensed Vietnamese bank account and cannot legally open a domestic VND clearing account without an SBV credit-institution licence. For a foreign-invested LLC, all service-export proceeds must be ultimately repatriated to an onshore licensed-bank foreign-currency account under Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign investor must first obtain the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC). The LLC then opens a Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at a licensed Vietnamese commercial bank in the name of the LLC. The share capital contribution must be wired via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's bank account in which the remitter name matches the investor name stated in the IRC; the wire must reference the 'Capital contribution to the LLC' purpose code prescribed under SBV's statistical reporting regime (the bank will map it to the applicable BoP classification). The bank will credit the DICA, issue a Foreign Investment Confirmation or capital-contribution receipt, and report to SBV. No physical remittance is required if the contribution is made in-kind; however, for cash injections each transfer must be registered in the DICA ledger. For any subsequent increase in charter capital, an amended IRC is required before additional wires are accepted. The LLC may also receive shareholder loans in foreign currency, but such loans must be registered under SBV regulations and cannot exceed 30% of charter capital for shorter-term intra-group loans, otherwise they are subject to the enterprise loan ratio and loan-registration requirements.Outbound settlement for imported software subscriptions, subcontracting services, or international licensing fees uses the SWIFT MT103 channel via the licensed bank, with the LLC's foreign-currency account or the conversion of VND at the interbank rate. For each disbursement, the bank requires the underlying service contract, the vendor invoice, a certificate of service completion where applicable, and the correct BoP purpose code (e.g., 'payment for consulting services', 'software licensing royalties'), together with the Vietnamese corporate income tax and VAT obligations — including 1% or 5% FCT (foreign contractor tax) withholding for non-resident vendors, as applicable. Commercial letters of credit (LCs) are generally not used for services; they are reserved for physical trade, and the LLC shifting to multicurrency ledger bookkeeping is unlikely to require LC issuance. Payment orders should be batched and reported through the bank's corporate e-banking platform to maintain a clean audit trail. For cross-border service disbursements exceeding USD 500,000 equivalent, the bank may require a board resolution / members' council resolution authorising the payment and, for payments to related parties, transfer-pricing documentation or a TP policy statement to comply with Circular 132/2020/TT-BTC.Onshore VND/USD FX forward contracts pursuant to SBV Circular 02/2021/TT-NHNN, with a maximum tenor of 365 days, requiring a genuine underlying current-account or capital-account exposure; used to lock the exchange rate on expected service-export proceeds or import payments.Onshore VND/USD FX swap contracts (both principal and interest) with a licensed bank, permitted for up to 365 days on the same underlying-real-demand basis; commonly used to convert temporary USD liquidity into VND and reverse the position on maturity.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps between USD and VND are available at the bank's product committee discretion for sophisticated borrowers, but SBV treats such structures as ordinary OTC derivatives to be documented under ISDA or a local master agreement, and in practice most banks restrict availability to tenors under 365 days with collateral requirements.Offshore nondeliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing USD/VND are NOT permissible onshore and may not be entered into by an SBV-licensed credit institution; an onshore VND corporate may only hedge with deliverable onshore products, and the LLC should prohibit any Vietnamese counterparty from booking an NDF. The LLC may use offshore NDFs only indirectly through its foreign parent for risk-sharing, provided no Vietnamese entity is a party and settlement is made wholly outside Viet Nam without affecting the DICA.
