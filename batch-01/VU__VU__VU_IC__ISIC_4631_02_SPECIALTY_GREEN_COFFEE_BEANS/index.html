Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), Customs & Excise Division; online filing via ASYCUDA World single window. Company registration and regulatory licensing are administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) under the International Companies Act, Cap 222, with licensed registered agents as mandatory intermediaries. Official portals: www.vrca.gov.vu and www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) with VFSC through a licensed registered agent; no minimum paid-up capital and no resident director requirement. 2) Apply to VRCA for a Customs Importer/Exporter Code via the ASYCUDA World module only if goods will physically enter or transit Vanuatu customs territory; the VFSC registration number remains the primary legal identifier for the IC. 3) No income tax TIN is issued because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, no withholding tax, and no capital gains tax; an IC holding a valid Certificate of Exemption is statutorily outside the national income tax net. 4) For pure offshore trading (coffee sold ex-Vanuatu origin to non-Vanuatu buyers), no VRCA registration is triggered; the IC operates on principal-to-principal contracts with title passing outside Vanuatu.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — 1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) with VFSC through a licensed registered agent; no minimum paid-up capital and no resident director requirement. 2) Apply to VRCA for a Customs Importer/Exporter Code via the ASYCUDA World module only if goods will physically enter or transit Vanuatu customs territory; the VFSC registration number remains the primary legal identifier for the IC. 3) No income tax TIN is issued because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, no withholding tax, and no capital gains tax; an IC holding a valid Certificate of Exemption is statutorily outside the national income tax net. 4) For pure offshore trading (coffee sold ex-Vanuatu origin to non-Vanuatu buyers), no VRCA registration is triggered; the IC operates on principal-to-principal contracts with title passing outside Vanuatu.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None applicable. Section 112 of the International Companies Act, Cap 222, grants an absolute, non-assessable tax exemption to ICs on all income, profits, distributions, transfers, and capital transactions; no check-the-box election, no hybrid mismatch election, and no residence-based election exists because Vanuatu has no income tax statute. An IC may elect under its constitutional documents to adopt enhanced governance provisions, but such elections have no tax consequence. If the IC engages in prohibited domestic Vanuatu business, the exemption is voidable and penalties under the Act apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT or GST in force. Vanuatu operates a customs duty (import tariff), excise, and stamp duty regime administered by VRCA under the Customs and Excise Act, Cap 121; the IC's offshore green coffee trading does not attract any Vanuatu indirect tax where goods do not enter Vanuatu customs territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. A Customs Importer/Exporter Code in VUV is required only upon the first physical import or transshipment of goods through Vanuatu customs territory; an IC trading coffee wholly offshore without Vanuatu-landfall has no fiscal registration or turnover threshold obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Formally inapplicable - Vanuatu imposes no VAT/GST, therefore no 0% export VAT claim mechanism exists. However, Vanuatu levies no export duty on green coffee beans, and an IC operating as a pure offshore conduit is entirely outside the customs duty base: goods transshipped or sold free on board (FOB) at a Vanuatu port under bond or free-zone procedures attract no indirect tax. To preserve this neutral position, the IC's documentary evidence must demonstrate the goods were exported or shipped to a foreign destination, the coffee never entered home-use consumption in Vanuatu, and no value was added within Vanuatu customs territory.

  • ASYCUDA World Electronic Customs Export Declaration (cargo manifest or export entry, if goods physically use Vanuatu customs territory)
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing foreign destination
  • Signed Commercial Invoice (green coffee contract proceeds with reference to the VU_IC entity)
  • Packing List and Certificate of Origin / phytosanitary certificate where applicable
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing receipt of export sales proceeds
  • VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and International Company Act Section 112 tax exemption certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, no digital stamping, and no mandatory e-invoicing/API clearance regime is in force. Electronic customs declarations are lodged through the ASYCUDA World system for all goods entering or leaving Vanuatu; risk assessment is data-driven but not connected to live invoicing. Commercial invoices and packing lists are submitted as structured PDF attachments; there is no continuous transaction control (CTC) clearance protocol.

No clawback of export zero-rating exists given the absence of VAT. Fraudulent customs declarations, misdescription of goods, or false use of duty-free/transshipment status are prosecuted under the Customs and Excise Act, Cap 121: fines up to VUV 1,000,000, forfeiture of goods, vessel or aircraft seizure, and imprisonment up to 5 years. An IC that breaches the International Companies Act Cap 222 by engaging in prohibited domestic Vanuatu business loses its Section 112 exemption retroactively for the offending transactions, with recovery of unpaid customs and excise duties and cancellation of registration by VFSC. Anti-money laundering scrutiny applies through licensed registered agents and banks under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 13 of 2014, with penalties for suspicious transaction reporting failures including monetary penalties and sanctions against the entity's license.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None. No statutory transfer-pricing documentation, Local File, Master File or CbC reporting thresholds apply in Vanuatu because no corporate income tax exists. Related-party pricing of green-coffee imports remains relevant only for customs valuation under the Customs Act (duty and 12.5% VAT base at import).

Economic substance scope: No formal Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) in the BVI/Cayman model. Substance proxies are nevertheless enforced through: (i) the IC Act requirement of a licensed resident agent, registered office and physical records in Vanuatu; (ii) the prohibition on conducting business with Vanuatu residents; and (iii) AML/CFT beneficial-ownership recording. The VFSC, OECD Global Forum and foreign tax authorities may disregard the IC if the coffee trade is executed without real functions, assets, risks and decision-making in Vanuatu.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and sham use of IC exemption

Statutory sanction: Loss of the IC tax exemption; prosecution under the International Companies Act for prohibited local business; assessment of 12.5% VAT plus interest and penalties by Customs and Inland Revenue; deregistration by VFSC; foreign anti-avoidance adjustments with interest and penalties in the shareholder's jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permitted the prohibited activity are personally liable for statutory fines and the company's unpaid penalties; courts may pierce the veil and order civil restitution; directors face travel bans and removal from the Vanuatu register.
Shell-company and false-substance declaration

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration with asset vesting in VFSC; fines for false declarations under the IC Act; correspondent banks terminate SWIFT/LC facilities; transactions become unenforceable where the IC has ceased to exist.

Director personal liability: Veil piercing where the IC is a facade; directors personally civilly liable for corporate obligations; disqualification from acting as directors for up to 5 years; travel bans while outstanding fines are unresolved.
AML/CFT non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 30 of 2014: fines up to VUV 100 million for bodies corporate and imprisonment up to 25 years for individuals; VFSC administrative fines; freezing and confiscation of container proceeds; termination of banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and the appointed MLRO face personal criminal prosecution, imprisonment, extradition under mutual legal assistance treaties, and personal asset-confiscation orders.
Prohibited local business and unlicensed activity

Statutory sanction: Offence under the International Companies Act for prohibited local business, with fines and daily default penalties; contracts with Vanuatu residents void or unenforceable; VFSC strike-off; Business Licence Act penalties for unlicensed trade.

Director personal liability: Every director and officer participating in the prohibited transaction is personally liable for the statutory fine; the corporate veil is pierced for debts arising from the unlawful contracts; directors face imprisonment for continued non-compliance and travel restrictions pending payment.
