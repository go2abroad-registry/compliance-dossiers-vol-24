Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official portal https://dcir.gov.vu; corporate registry and licensing authority: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), portal https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from income tax, stamp duty, capital gains tax, and withholding tax and is not automatically entered on the VAT register. No TIN is required for pure offshore wholesale trading. If the IC derives taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding VT 5,000,000 annually (a scenario excluded under the International Companies Act but permissible for voluntary registration), the IC must submit the VAT-01 registration form to DCIR accompanied by the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles, directors' and shareholders' details, a non-resident declaration, and projected turnover; DCIR then issues a 15-digit VAT identification number prefixed 'VU'. For customs purposes the IC may obtain an Importer/Exporter Code via ASYCUDA World at the DCIR Customs Division.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no general income tax law, therefore no check-the-box, entity classification, or hybrid mismatch elections exist. The IC's exemption is automatic under Part X of the International Companies Act Cap 222 (sections 101-105). The only available statutory election is voluntary VAT registration under section 9 of the VAT Act No. 21 of 2023 to recover input VAT, which is tactically inadvisable for an offshore fertilizer chartering model because the IC's supplies are outside the Vanuatu VAT net.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 annual taxable supplies (approximately USD 42,000) for any person, resident or non-resident, making taxable supplies in Vanuatu; compulsory registration applies to suppliers making or intending to make taxable supplies above this threshold in any 12-month period

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under section 24 of the VAT Act No. 21 of 2023 for (i) goods exported directly from Vanuatu by the VAT-registered supplier, (ii) services supplied to a non-resident person for use wholly outside Vanuatu, and (iii) supply of a business or its assets to another VAT-registered person. For the present IC offshore chartering model, prilled urea and potassium chloride are purchased and resold between non-Vanuatu ports and never enter Vanuatu territory; such supplies are outside the scope of Vanuatu VAT rather than zero-rated. If goods were transiently cleared through Vanuatu en route to a foreign buyer, the IC would claim 0% by presenting export evidence; if the goods first enter Vanuatu and then are re-exported, the import VAT may be deferred or zero-rated under customs warehousing rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World ED with manifest approval)
  • Bill of Lading or House Bill of Lading (original negotiable copy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit advice or SWIFT MT103 confirmation evidencing export proceeds received in VUV or foreign currency)

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital signature mandate is currently enacted in Vanuatu. VAT-registered businesses must issue serially numbered tax invoices in paper, PDF, or standard ERP format. All import and export declarations are processed through ASYCUDA World customs clearance software; no mandatory government fiscal clearance is imposed on commercial invoices beyond customs valuation in ASYCUDA World.

DCIR may retrospectively assess unsubstantiated zero-rated exports at 15% VAT on the export value plus interest at 2% per month. The VAT Act No. 21 of 2023 imposes a penalty equal to 100% of the tax shortfall for false or fraudulent export declarations and a criminal fine of up to VT 1,000,000 and/or up to five years' imprisonment. The limitation period for VAT clawback and assessment is six years from the filing date, extended indefinitely where fraud or willful evasion is established. Separate penalties apply under the International Companies Act for failure to maintain records evidencing offshore transactions, including removal of the IC's tax-exempt status upon disqualification, and under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act for trade-based money laundering connected to fertilizer commodity billing fraud, with fines up to VT 50,000,000 for the IC and imprisonment of directors.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is a non-internationally-traded and administratively managed currency. Current account transactions (trade in goods, freight, insurance, and related payments) are fully convertible without prior RBV approval when routed through an authorized dealer. Capital account inward investment by non-resident shareholders is generally freely permitted, but outbound transactions involving a 'resident' counterparty may require an Exchange Control Act endorsement by the appointed authorized dealer. An International Company (IC) that conducts its business wholly outside Vanuatu and does not transact with Vanuatu residents enjoys the statutory IC exemption from the Exchange Control Act [Cap 222], allowing the IC to hold and settle currency in any overseas denomination without RBV clearance. Profit remittance by the IC is unimpeded: Vanuatu withholds no dividend tax, and distributions may be made directly from offshore accounts without buy-side local regulatory approval, provided the corporate resolution and minute trail are retained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Act [Cap 13] places a statutory obligation on a 'resident' person acquiring foreign currency to offer it for sale to an authorized dealer without undue delay. In RBV administrative practice this obligation is applied as a 21-day offer/turn-in requirement for resident exporters receiving VUV-convertible receipts, unless the proceeds are credited to an RBV-approved foreign-currency account. No fixed percentage surrender ratio is legislated; Vanuatu permits 100% retention of export proceeds in foreign currency accounts for offshore-transacting businesses. A properly structured Vanuatu International Company is outside the resident reporting net for offshore receipts because the IC exemption in the International Companies Act disapplies the Exchange Control Act to its foreign-currency holdings, offshore bank accounts, and non-resident shareholder equity. For the fertilizer commodity flow, the IC should therefore retain urea/potash sale proceeds in USD at an authorized dealer or an offshore correspondent account.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 98] and the Exchange Control Act [Cap 13], with supplementary AML/CTF oversight by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for licensed banks and securities dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Global digital EMI/neobank platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Wise Business, Currencycloud) generally refuse or restrict onboarding of Vanuatu-domiciled entities due to perceived Pacific-offshore jurisdiction risk and the absence of an RBV passporting regime. Domestically, any deposit-taking or payment institution must hold a licence under the Banking Act [Cap 265]; unlicensed foreign EMIs cannot legally service a Vanuatu-incorporated IC as a primary Treasury channel. The IC may open accounts offshore at licensed EMIs in Singapore or Australia, but such rails are inadequate for fertilizer trade finance where documentary credits, MT7xx messaging, and commodity-grade compliance are required. A tier-1 correspondent bank corridor should therefore be the sole operational rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The IC is not subject to any minimum paid-up capital; the shareholder may inject equity or an intercompany loan via SWIFT MT103 or MT202. The onward routing bank will require the IC to evidence the source of funds (six months of shareholder bank statements, signed source-of-wealth declaration, and a certified corporate resolution approving the capital call). If the inward remittance passes through the Vanuatu banking system, the receiving authorized dealer will classify the credit as 'Inward Investment — Share Capital' and may issue an Exchange Control consent letter evidencing a polite, non-approval-required inward flow; for pure offshore treatment, the credit should land in the IC's USD account at a Singapore/Hong Kong correspondent with narrative 'Share Subscription — VU IC' to avoid any false residency trigger. Loan principal and interest returns must map to a dated intercompany loan agreement with arm's-length pricing to withstand AML/EDD review.Primary clearing rail for fertilizer trade settlements is SWIFT MT103 for open-account payments under USD 2 million per shipment; large urea/potash parcels should settle via an irrevocable documentary credit issued by the buyer's bank in MT700 format, with MT701 amendments, MT750 advice, and MT754 confirmation/reimbursement routed through a Singapore or Hong Kong confirming bank to secure same-day value. The Vanuatu IC should maintain its USD liquidity pool at ANZ Vanuatu or an offshore correspondent with daylight-overdraft facility and a backup MT910/MT920 mirror account. A dual-rail discipline is recommended: (i) LC rail for first-time or sovereign-risk counterparties; (ii) MT103 with 2-of-3 signature authority for established suppliers, always accompanied by the bill of lading, weight certificate, and commercial contract in the compliance file.Outright USD/VUV forward contracts of up to 12 months with an authorized dealer for any VUV-denominated operating costs.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/VUV for offshore institutions unable to take physical VUV settlement, subject to authorized-dealer intermediation.Cross-currency swaps (USD/VUV and USD/USD basis swaps) with an RBV-licensed dealer for tenor-matched hedging of long-dated trade receivables.Natural hedging through matched USD receivable/payable booking and multi-currency accounts; commodity-linked price swaps are not FX instruments and require separate internal dealing approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable under Vanuatu domestic law; Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax and has no transfer pricing documentation rules, Local File, or Master File obligations for International Companies. However, if the company is resident for tax purposes elsewhere (e.g., as a controlled foreign corporation in a foreign jurisdiction), the foreign jurisdiction's transfer pricing documentation thresholds shall apply based on that jurisdiction's law.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not administer Economic Substance Regulations similar to Caribbean regimes. However, International Companies must adhere to the International Companies Act 1992, which requires maintenance of a registered office and registered agent in Vanuatu, filing of an annual return, and prohibition of domestic business conduct. Foreign jurisdictions applying CFC legislation may impute substance requirements, requiring the non-resident owner to demonstrate that the Vanuatu IC has adequate substance in Vanuatu or that the controlled foreign company income is attributable to the foreign parent under that jurisdiction's rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Unlicensed Domestic Operations

Statutory sanction: The International Company may be struck off the register, fined up to VUV 10,000,000, and any person acting on behalf of the company may be fined up to VUV 2,000,000 or imprisoned for up to two years, or both, under the International Companies Act 1992 and the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission Act 2017.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit unlawful domestic business may be personally liable for fines, restitution of any gains, disqualification from acting as director, and travel bans pursuant to court orders; persistent contravention may lead to imprisonment.
Money Laundering / AML Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2015 may reach VUV 100,000,000 for a body corporate; revocation of the registered agent's license; confiscation of proceeds; and criminal prosecution may be initiated by the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may incur civil liability for loss arising from AML failures, personal criminal fines, and imprisonment for up to ten years for money laundering; they may also be subject to civil penalties and overseas travel bans while an investigation is pending.
Shell Company / Substance Avoidance in Source Jurisdictions

Statutory sanction: Under the foreign source jurisdiction's CFC or anti-hybrid rules, all profit attributable to the Vanuatu IC may be imputed to the foreign parent; penalties may include back taxes, interest, and accuracy-related penalties of 20% to 40% or more; in civil fraud cases, additional penalties may reach 75% of the understatement.

Director personal liability: Foreign tax authorities may pierce the corporate veil where the IC is a sham; directors may be held personally liable for the unpaid taxes of the foreign parent, subjected to travel bans upon enforcement of civil judgments, and criminally prosecuted for willful evasion leading to imprisonment.
False Declarations to VFSC

Statutory sanction: The VFSC may impose administrative sanctions, cancel the registrant of the IC, strike the company off, and impose fines; knowingly providing false information constitutes an offense punishable by imprisonment and continuing daily fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who authorize a false filing are jointly and severally liable for damages sustained by any person relying on the register; they may be disqualified from managing any Vanuatu entity and subject to criminal penalties including imprisonment for up to two years.
Failure to Maintain Registered Agent / Office

Statutory sanction: The company is subject to an automatic annual fee increase, late penalties, liquidation or strike-off proceedings by the VFSC, and loss of separate legal personality upon dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors may incur personal liability for transactions undertaken after the company has been struck off, including joint and several liability for all debts of the company contracted during the period of default; civil proceedings may result in personal judgment enforcement and travel bans.
