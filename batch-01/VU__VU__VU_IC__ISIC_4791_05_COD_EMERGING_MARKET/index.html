Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the statutory company registry and International Company licensing authority, operating the e-registration portal at www.vfsc.vu; Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA) administers customs duties, stamp duties, service tax, and residual revenue functions through its single-window portal at www.vrca.gov.vu.

E-government filing portal

A foreign ultimate beneficial owner appoints a VFSC-licensed registered agent who files incorporation with the VFSC online registry; within three to five business days the VFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation and a statutory Tax Exemption Certificate under the International Companies Act [CAP 222]; because the International Company is tax-exempt, no separate TIN, VAT ID, or taxpayer registration is triggered, and the VFSC company number on the exemption certificate functions as the official identifier for banking, AML/KYC, customs, and logistics settlement purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign ultimate beneficial owner appoints a VFSC-licensed registered agent who files incorporation with the VFSC online registry; within three to five business days the VFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation and a statutory Tax Exemption Certificate under the International Companies Act [CAP 222]; because the International Company is tax-exempt, no separate TIN, VAT ID, or taxpayer registration is triggered, and the VFSC company number on the exemption certificate functions as the official identifier for banking, AML/KYC, customs, and logistics settlement purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists in Vanuatu law. The International Company automatically receives a non-discretionary exemption from income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty on transfers of shares, and all transaction-based taxes, conditional upon not carrying on business with Vanuatu residents and not holding land in Vanuatu; the only statutory election that would forfeit the exemption is voluntary conversion or redomiciliation into the domestic Companies Act [CAP 191] regime, which is not recommended for the target offshore COD e-commerce structure.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Vanuatu operates no VAT or GST system; the indirect levies in force comprise customs import duties, excise duties on alcohol and tobacco, stamp duties on domestic instruments, and a narrowly-scoped service tax on tourism and telecommunication services, none of which attach to an exempt International Company's offshore mail-order retail and cash-on-delivery turnover.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Vanuatu imposes no turnover-based VAT/GST registration threshold; the International Company's zero indirect tax exposure is statutory rather than threshold-based, derived from section 9 of the International Companies Act [CAP 222], which exempts all income, distributions, transactions, and instruments of the International Company from Vanuatu taxes and stamp duties.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating is inapplicable because Vanuatu has no VAT against which a zero rate or refund can be claimed. Instead, the International Company enjoys automatic tax-exempt status for all offshore income and transactions under the International Companies Act [CAP 222]; no application for zero-rating or refund substantiation is required, although banks, payment facilitiators, and the VFSC expect the IC to maintain evidence of the offshore nature of sales, including commercial contracts, logistics proof for goods, and COD remittance records, to preserve the exemption certificate.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through the VRCA SIGTAS electronic single window
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List matching the ISIC 4791 mail-order COD shipment description
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued by the carrying carrier for cross-border goods movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 record evidencing receipt of offshore sale proceeds
  • VFSC Tax Exemption Certificate confirming the International Company's statutory export exemption status

None - Vanuatu mandates no real-time fiscal clearance system, no e-invoicing API, and no digital stamping requirement for commercial invoices; customs export declarations for physical goods are processed through the VRCA's SIGTAS electronic single window, but this clearance obligation does not extend to the International Company's externally issued sales documentation, COD settlement records, or digital invoicing to offshore customers.

Misuse of the International Company exemption by trading with Vanuatu residents, holding Vanuatu land, or misrepresenting the offshore character of sales to the VFSC attracts fines of up to VT 1,000,000, cancellation of the tax exemption certificate, strike-off of the company, and director disqualification under the International Companies Act [CAP 222]; fraudulent customs declarations separately trigger forfeiture, penalties, and prosecution under Vanuatu customs legislation; Vanuatu imposes no statutory 180-day remittance deadline, but VFSC and VRCA audits treat prolonged non-repatriation or diversion of export proceeds as circumstantial evidence of domestic trading, which can expose the International Company to retrospective clawback of duties, transaction taxes, and registration fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) operates under a managed exchange-rate arrangement referencing a trade-weighted composite basket and is not freely convertible offshore. All onshore foreign-exchange activity must pass through RBV-licensed Authorized Dealers: current-account payments (trade, freight, logistics fees) are approved on production of documentary evidence, while capital-account outflows require RBV approval unless delegated to an Authorized Dealer. The International Company (VU_IC) is exempt from exchange-control restrictions under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] and may lawfully denominate its business wholly in USD or AUD without forced conversion into VUV. The IC is zero-rated for income tax and subject to no withholding taxes; profit remittance is therefore legally untaxed. The only practical convertibility friction is correspondent-bank de-risking, which is mitigated by maintaining a USD-centric treasury and using an Authorized Dealer solely for local operating costs such as registered-agent and government fees.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Act empowers the RBV to direct the timing, collection, and surrender of export proceeds. Resident exporters must repatriate receipts through an Authorized Dealer and convert foreign currency into VUV within the period prescribed by RBV directions unless a retention or offset approval has been obtained. The VU_IC is treated as a non-resident entity for exchange-control purposes, so the mandatory conversion ratio is nil (0%) for proceeds received into its designated foreign-currency operational account. For cash-on-delivery e-commerce inflows remitted by emerging-market logistics aggregators, the treasury must retain order-level evidence (customer orders, dispatch records, proof-of-delivery, and aggregator settlement files) so that inward MT103 credits are classified as genuine export sales rather than unexplained remittances.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) as the monetary authority and administrator of the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 91], acting in conjunction with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as banking and AML/CFT supervisor under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act (Act No. 6 of 2000, as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-conditioned. Global EMIs such as Airwallex generally decline Vanuatu-incorporated International Companies at onboarding because Vanuatu presents an elevated offshore-center risk profile and the IC lacks local substance; Airwallex is therefore not viable as the primary collection rail. Legal constraint: Airwallex holds no RBV payment license and cannot clear VUV or domestic COD collections in the target market. Permitted architecture: (i) local logistics remittance aggregators licensed in each emerging market collect COD cash and remit batch MT103 credits to the IC's RBV-bank USD account; (ii) an offshore EMI, if obtained on a case-by-case basis after 12 months of clean banking history, may serve only the outbound supplier-disbursement leg; and (iii) all VUV conversion must route through an onshore Authorized Dealer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder funding must arrive by SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's bank or the holding company into the IC's BSP or ANZ USD operational account, tagged with the OECD/BPM6 direct-investment equity purpose code (for example code 5025) stated as 'Share capital injection into Vanuatu International Company'. The IC should credit the receipt against the Share Register, minute the issuance through a board resolution, and retain a source-of-funds declaration for the Authorized Dealer; no RBV prior approval is required because the IC is exchange-control exempt and the credit is foreign-currency denominated. Practical controls: use a dedicated inbound-only capital receipt ledger for the first 180 days, reject any third-party payer not identified in the shareholder resolution, and never accept cryptocurrency or cash-equivalent funding into the account.Supplier and logistics payments execute through SWIFT MT103 (with MT202COV cover payment where correspondent routing is required) from the IC's USD clearing account. For bulk inventory procurement where supplier trust is unproven, the treasury should use a sight Commercial Letter of Credit issued by BSP or ANZ, documents against payment, naming the IC as applicant. Inbound COD receivables are collected by in-market logistics aggregators and remitted in batched MT103 credits to the IC's settlement ledger; the treasury must match each credit to order-level settlement files before releasing funds, maintain a 30-day operating-expense float, and prohibit any payout to unidentified beneficiaries. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable: Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and therefore no statutory transfer-pricing documentation thresholds in VUV. Nonetheless, International Companies must maintain contemporaneous books and records evidencing cross-border commercial terms to support banking, AML, and economic substance reporting.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has substantive registration and substance requirements for International Companies under the International Companies Act and related AML/CFT rules. Entities conducting e-commerce or distribution activities through a Vanuatu vehicle must demonstrate real presence in Vanuatu, a locally retained managed and directed office, and adequate personnel and expenditure. Non-resident International Companies that are not tax-resident elsewhere and that do not undertake local economic activity must nonetheless maintain arms-length evidence and comply with VFSC information requests; the substance threshold is low but not nil.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abusive Offshore Structuring

Statutory sanction: The foreign tax authority may impose back taxes, interest, penalties, and VAT or customs assessments on the full supply chain; Vanuatu itself does not impose tax but may terminate the IC's exempt status if it finds local economic activity exceeding the offshore allowance.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly direct onshore sales through a sham offshore wrapper may be personally liable for the foreign company's tax debts under lifting-the-veil doctrines; travel bans and asset freezes are commonly issued by the source-country revenue authority.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC may strike off the IC, impose administrative fines up to VT 1,000,000, and refer the matter to the Public Prosecutor; the entity may be listed on a non-compliance registry and denied future licensing.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified for up to 10 years, jointly and severally liable for the company's debts and fees, and personally fined where the shell structure is used to defraud creditors or regulators.
AML / Cash-on-Delivery Proceeds Handling

Statutory sanction: Financial Intelligence Unit and central bank AML/CFT sanctions can include monetary penalties, freezing of the IC's bank accounts, revocation of the VFSC registration, and criminal prosecution under Vanuatu's AML/CFT Act.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal liability, imprisonment, and personal fines for knowingly facilitating money laundering through unexplained COD collections; civil asset recovery can reach personal bank accounts and real estate.
False Submission to VFSC

Statutory sanction: VFSC imposes an administrative penalty of up to VT 500,000 per false statement, can immediately suspend the IC, and may strike off the company without notice.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are personally liable for restitution, may be disqualified, and may be imprisoned for up to 7 years for fraud or perjury in regulatory filings.
