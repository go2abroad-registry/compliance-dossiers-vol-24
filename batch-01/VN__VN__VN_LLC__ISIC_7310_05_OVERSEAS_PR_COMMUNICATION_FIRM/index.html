Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A purely virtual or mailing-only address is not permissible for the registered headquarters of a Vietnamese limited liability company. The registered office must be a physical address within Vietnam with a specific house number, street, ward/commune and province; the enterprise must hold a legal right to use that address. A serviced office or business center with a genuine lease agreement and a dedicated workspace can qualify, but a conventional virtual office without a physical presence is rejected by provincial business registration authorities.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum charter capital applies to a foreign-invested LLC engaged in media relations and public relations advisory under ISIC 7310. The declared charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days from the date of Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance as required by Article 47 of the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14. No escrow account or bank-blocked capital requirement is imposed for this non-conditional business line.

Funding & unlock path: Office premises require fire prevention and fighting design approval and a certificate of satisfactory fire safety conditions under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP where applicable; the lessor's valid fire safety certificate must be on file. Structural load approvals for leaseholds are not required beyond the landlord's own compliance; nonetheless, a workplace safety inspection by the provincial Department of Labor, Invalids and Social Affairs is required for employee occupational health, especially computer-screen working conditions, emergency exits, and first-aid provisions. The company must maintain an occupational safety and health risk assessment and appoint a safety officer.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum charter capital applies to a foreign-invested LLC engaged in media relations and public relations advisory under ISIC 7310. The declared charter capital must be fully contributed within 90 days from the date of Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance as required by Article 47 of the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14. No escrow account or bank-blocked capital requirement is imposed for this non-conditional business line.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế — GDT), Ministry of Finance of Viet Nam; electronic platforms: eTax Portal (etax.gdt.gov.vn) for e-filing and e-payment, e-Invoice Portal (hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) for invoice data clearance, and National Business Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) for enterprise registration and automatic TIN issuance.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested LLC obtains its statutory Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế — MST) automatically upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate through the National Business Registration Portal, normally within 3 working days of submission. Immediately after establishment, the LLC must complete supplementary tax registration with the governing Tax Sub-Department (Chi cục Thuế) within 30 days, declaring the VAT calculation method (credit/deduction method or direct method), managing tax authority, bank accounts for e-tax refunds, and e-invoice registration patterns through the GDT e-Invoice Portal prior to issuing any invoices. A foreign enterprise without a permanent establishment must separately obtain a Foreign Contractor Tax registration code with the Vietnamese counterparty's tax authority before invoicing under the Foreign Contractor Tax regime.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Vietnamese-resident corporate taxpayer subject to Corporate Income Tax at 20% on worldwide income, including offshore PR retainer income; non-resident shareholders are separately subject to 0.1% withholding on dividend distributions. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — Vietnamese-resident corporate taxpayer subject to Corporate Income Tax at 20% on worldwide income, including offshore PR retainer income; non-resident shareholders are separately subject to 0.1% withholding on dividend distributions. Territorial VAT zero-rating applies only to qualifying exported PR/communication services; no pass-through or transparent status is available.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not provide US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. Available statutory elections: (1) VAT method election between the credit/deduction method and the direct method on deemed revenue; the credit method is mandatory for taxpayers with annual revenue above VND 1 billion, while newly established LLCs with full accounting records may elect the credit method upon registration; (2) FCT election under Circular 04/2022/TT-BTC for foreign contractors between the direct method (deemed profit rates) and the deduction method (full CIT/VAT registration); (3) election of the 5-year CIT loss carryforward period; (4) simplified transfer-pricing documentation election for low-risk related-party service transactions under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Corporate taxpayers, including foreign-invested LLCs and foreign contractors, are mandatorily registered for VAT upon establishment with no turnover threshold exemption. For small households and individual businesses, the statutory VAT registration threshold is VND 1,000,000,000 (1 billion VND) of annual taxable turnover. Advertising, media relations and crisis PR services (ISIC 7310) were temporarily subject to an 8% reduced VAT rate under Decrees 15/2022/ND-CP, 94/2023/ND-CP and 180/2024/ND-CP through 30 June 2025, reverting to the 10% standard headline rate from 1 July 2025 under VAT Law No. 48/2024/QH15.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VN LLC providing international media relations, crisis PR, and communication consultancy to offshore clients may zero-rate its services under Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC Article 9 only if all of the following tests are met: (1) the services are supplied under a written service contract to a foreign organization or individual; (2) the services are performed in Viet Nam but used and consumed by the foreign client outside Viet Nam, with deliverables such as press kits, media monitoring reports, crisis communication protocols and strategy documents transferred to the non-resident principal; (3) consideration is received in freely convertible foreign currency and credited to the Vietnamese bank account of the LLC via wire, ACH or SWIFT transfer; and (4) contemporaneous substantiation comprising contract, invoices, service acceptance certificates and bank remittance vouchers is retained. Services in fact consumed in Viet Nam or benefiting a resident related party of the overseas client do not qualify for 0% VAT and are taxable at 10%.

  • Written export service contract between the VN LLC and the non-resident client evidencing offshore consumption
  • Commercial invoice and detailed fee schedule (retainer/ACH or wire) carrying the 0% VAT indicator
  • Bank payment voucher or official Inward Remittance Certificate proving receipt in convertible foreign currency
  • Signed service completion/acceptance certificate or deliverable transmittal statement issued by the foreign client
  • Proof of the counterparty's non-resident status (foreign tax registration certificate or certificate of incorporation)
  • Customs Export Declaration (Form HQ/2015-XK) and Bill of Lading/Air Waybill — applicable only where ancillary goods or promotional collateral accompany the exported service

Viet Nam operates a post-issuance centralized data-clearance regime rather than real-time pre-approval: every seller must generate e-invoices in certified XML format, apply a certified digital signature, and transmit the XML file to the GDT e-Invoice Portal (hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) no later than 24 hours after each invoice issuance under Decree 123/2020/ND-CP, enhanced by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP effective 1 June 2025. The GDT performs automated data-matching and timestamp indexing; buyers query invoices for input-VAT credit, and QR-code validation is mandatory for paper conversions. Certified e-invoice software must integrate with GDT APIs through approved service providers. POS e-invoices from cash registers must be transmitted immediately upon each sale. No 0% export VAT may be applied unless a compliant e-invoice bearing the 0% VAT indicator has been transmitted and confirmed by the GDT system.

The GDT cross-checks e-invoice XML data, customs declarations (VNACCS/VCIS), bank FX remittance records and related-party positions using data analytics to flag fictitious export zero-rating. Where a 0% export VAT claim or refund is disallowed, the taxpayer must repay the refunded or credited VAT with late-payment interest at 0.03% per day from the due date under Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14 Article 59, as amended by Law 56/2024/QH15, plus an administrative penalty of 20% of the under-declared tax under Article 17; deliberate concealment attracts penalty multiples of 1x to 3x the evaded amount and an extended 10-year statute of limitations for assessment under Article 8. Serious or organized invoice and export fraud triggers criminal liability under Penal Code Articles 200 (tax evasion — up to 10 years for amounts exceeding VND 1 billion) and 203 (illegal invoice issuance/use — up to 20 years), and may also result in revocation of the e-invoice right, suspension of VAT refund status, and customs blacklisting of the company and its principals.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vietnamese dong (VND) is not freely convertible. Current-account transactions for trade in services, including cross-border PR and communication services, are generally permissible and may be cleared through licensed banks without prior licensing; however, a valid underlying contract, invoice, and tax compliance evidence must be presented. Capital-account transactions, including inward equity contributions, offshore loan drawdowns, and profit repatriation, are tightly controlled and require registration documentation such as the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC), Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC), and in-letters of compliance, and often require pre-registration with the SBV under Law on Foreign Exchange. Profit remittance by a foreign-owned LLC is permissible after corporate income tax finalization, audited annual financial statements, and full settlement of tax and customs obligations; the remittance itself is executed through an authorized bank only upon submission of audited statements, auditor report, tax payment certificates, and the board/owner resolution declaring distributable profit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a service exporter such as an overseas public relations and communications firm, all export service proceeds received from foreign clients must be transferred to a foreign-currency account held with an authorized bank in Viet Nam within the statutory time frame. Under SBV practice for service exports, the proceeds should be repatriated within 90 days from the date of invoice issuance or payment due date, unless an extended period is granted by the SBV. There is no current mandatory conversion ratio for services; export foreign currency can be retained in the foreign-currency account and converted voluntarily to VND at the prevailing interbank exchange rate through the authorized bank, or used directly to settle documented foreign-currency expenditures, provided each transaction is reported with the underlying FX transaction purpose code.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV), through its Foreign Exchange Management Department and Provincial Branches, is the sole monetary and foreign exchange authority in Viet Nam.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Conditional viability — Low to Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed by the State Bank of Vietnam and therefore cannot provide independent local clearing, VND settlement, or licensed FX conversion. They may serve only as ancillary rail for inbound/outbound cross-border wires where the underlying settlement and tax-effective trail must be supported by a fully licensed Vietnamese authorized bank. Direct crediting of VND to the EMI is prohibited; all VND clearing and corporate account core banking must be hosted by an SBV-licensed institution.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the Vietnam LLC must be wired to a capital escrow account opened in the exact name of the LLC at an SBV-licensed authorized bank. The wire must use the SWIFT MT103 network and be annotated with the SBV FX transaction purpose code for foreign direct investment capital contribution (typically FDI code), together with the LLC's investment certificate number and tax ID. A copy of the ERC, IRC, investment charter, board or owner resolution approving capital contribution, and the capital contribution schedule are required at the receiving bank. Upon receipt, the bank may convert the foreign currency to VND on the LLC's instruction or maintain it in a foreign-currency capital account; disbursement from the capital escrow account requires submission of the payment plan, invoices, or contractual commitments and the relevant tax registration documents, satisfying SBV tracking of FDI disbursement records.Cross-border disbursements for the PR firm's operational outflows — overseas retainer payments to sub-contracted communication agencies, media buying, adtech platform subscriptions, research and survey fees, and software licensing — should be made by SWIFT MT103 direct transfers from the LLC's operational bank account to third-party overseas beneficiary accounts. Underlying documentary evidence, namely the valid service contract, commercial invoice, tax memorandum, and in cases of prudence the completed and signed KYC form, is lodged with the paying bank. For physical trade flows involving merchandise, corporate-branded promotional goods, or event materials, a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) may be used with a Vietnamese issuing bank and an advising/confirming bank abroad, governed by UCP 600, but routine PR service fees are normally conducted via MT103 bank-to-bank wire rather than LC. All outbound payments must be reported under the applicable SBV FX transaction purpose code and any cross-border service payment is subject to Vietnamese withholding tax formalities (foreign contractor tax) at source before final remittance.FX forward contract (deliverable) with a licensed authorized bank, based on a documented underlying current or capital transaction in foreign currency.FX swap (currency swap between VND and foreign currency) entered with an SBV-licensed bank against a real commercial exposure.Cross-currency interest rate swap or simple currency swap where both legs correspond to a lawful underlying debt, investment, or service flow registered or approved with the SBV.Offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) entered into through an overseas licensed bank, only as a risk overlay on top of an onshore settlement account; directly placing NDFs with unauthorized offshore counterparties is not permitted for Vietnamese resident entities, and the contract must be segregated from local cash flow for accounting and tax transparency.
