Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management — Port Vila Central Office; online portal: https://www.customs.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

A Vanuatu International Company (IC) formed under the International Companies Act is not a domestic VAT/TIN-bearing taxpayer. A foreign-controlled IC must register with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) at incorporation, obtaining a Certificate of Incorporation and a VFSC company number rather than a domestic tax TIN. If the IC nonetheless carries on, or intends to carry on, a local taxable activity (which is generally prohibited for an IC), the officer or director must submit VRC Form VAT REG 01 together with constitutional documents, a VFSC Extract, notarised identification for directors, a Vanuatu business address, and overseas parent information; a VAT registration number is issued after verification and, where applicable, provision of a security bond. Non-resident exporters with no local fixed establishment need not obtain a domestic VAT identification number but must obtain a Vanuatu Customs supplier/importer code for export declaration processing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or fiscal-transparency election is available in Vanuatu. An International Company automatically receives tax-exempt-zone treatment under the International Companies Act and is exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, including VAT on qualifying offshore supplies; the only statutory 'election' is the annual government maintenance filing and a possible application to VRC for a certificate of tax-exemption or VAT zero-rating endorsement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 (Vatu four million) annual taxable turnover; registration is compulsory when turnover exceeds this threshold and voluntary below it if VRC considers registration appropriate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT-registered exporter may zero-rate a supply of goods if the goods are physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days, or within such extended period as VRC approves, documentary evidence of export is produced, and title and risk pass to the non-resident recipient; services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident and the services are performed outside Vanuatu or consumed wholly outside Vanuatu. An International Company's offshore sales of electronic patient monitors and ECG telemetry hardware must be routed through a registered exporter or local agent to support a VAT refund claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World processed)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (non-negotiable original or certified copy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT TT credit advice from a Vanuatu bank evidencing export payment
  • Commercial invoice and zero-rated VAT tax invoice
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)
  • Packing list and container or serial-number records for ECG devices and calibration instruments

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing, tax-stamping, or continuous transaction clearance regime is currently implemented. Registered persons must issue serially numbered VAT tax invoices containing all statutory particulars and retain records for the applicable VAT period; export transactions are reported through the Vanuatu Customs E-Single Window (ASYCUDA World) using customs export declarations.

VRC may audit exporters and disallow zero-rating where export documentation is incomplete or title or risk did not pass outside Vanuatu; penalties include recapture of VAT at 12.5%, late-payment penalties at 20% per annum, monetary penalties as prescribed in the VAT Act, and additional tax equal to 100% of the evaded VAT in cases of fraud; directors and principals are jointly and severally liable. Fraudulent use of export zero-rating may result in criminal prosecution, deregistration, customs penalties under the Customs Act, and public naming of the taxpayer by VRC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a largely liberalized exchange control regime with full current account convertibility for trade and service transactions and no prior RBV approval for remittances of profits, dividends, interest, or royalties by non-resident investors. Capital account transactions, including inward direct investment and repatriation of capital, require only classification disclosure through an authorized dealer and are not subject to quantitative restrictions. Profit remittance is unimpeded provided the International Company has filed its annual exemption certificate and maintains no local tax liabilities; no withholding tax applies on dividend distributions by Vanuatu International Companies. The national currency, the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV), is pegged to a reference basket of major currencies, but is not an offshore deliverable currency; practical USD account capacity is available in-country.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no delivery timeline obligation in Vanuatu. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency in an authorized dealer account or held offshore at the exporter's discretion, subject solely to bank-level record-keeping obligations under the AML/CFT Act [Cap. 299] and VFSC/RBV anti-money laundering directives.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - Central Bank and Monetary Authority; Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) handles corporate and financial licensing; Foreign Exchange operations are administered by RBV under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 62] as delegated to authorized dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Low for licensing in Vanuatu. Vanuatu does not operate a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) or digital bank licensing regime; offshore digital bank/EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Wise, Mercury) are not locally regulated or supervised by the RBV or VFSC and cannot serve as the primary corporate treasury account for trade settlements routed through Vanuatu. They may be used only as auxiliary collection or payment rails if the entity also maintains a domestic account at an authorized dealer; local banks will require the primary account relationship to be with a physically licensed Vanuatu bank. Foreign-owned EMIs typically refuse to onboard Vanuatu International Companies due to correspondent banking restrictions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder loan capital must be routed via the SWIFT network to the Vanuatu transit account at an authorized dealer using MT103 or MT202 with the purpose code classification 'Direct Investment' / 'Capital Contribution to Non-Resident Enterprise'. The remitter must include the company name, registered VFSC number, and a brief trade narrative (e.g., 'INVESTMENT CAPITAL FOR ECG PATIENT MONITOR ASSEMBLY OPERATIONS'). The receiving bank will complete an Exchange Control Form DCL (Dealer Classification) for RBV statistical purposes; no prior approval is required for amounts below VUV 100 million equivalent, but supporting board resolutions and shareholder loan agreements must be retained on file for verification. Identity verification of the remitting beneficial owner will be requested if the transfer originates from a non-bank financial intermediary.Primary clearing rails include (a) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for advance payments, deposits, and settlement balances up to established transaction limits; (b) SWIFT MT700 commercial letters of credit issued by Vanuatu authorized dealers in favor of suppliers in Asia, principally covering assembly components for patient monitors; and (c) documentary collections (SWIFT MT400/MT750) for low-volume or repeat purchases. All disbursements must carry the trade purpose code for 'Imports of Medical Equipment and Accessories', and the underlying commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading must be presented to the bank within the statutory record-keeping window. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no national corporate income tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — no dividend withholding tax is imposed on distributions by a Vanuatu International Company (Vanuatu does not operate a withholding tax regime for dividends).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within thirty (30) days after each anniversary of the date of incorporation. No CIT annual return is filed because the IC is tax-exempt; the VFSC annual return is the only compliance filing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold in Vanuatu Vatu (VUV): Vanuatu has not enacted transfer pricing or BEPS documentation legislation, and International Companies are tax-exempt. Local File/Master File preparation is therefore not mandatory in Vanuatu. Practical compliance benchmark for cross-border ECG monitor intercompany sales: maintain an OECD-aligned benchmarking note covering 3–6 comparable distribution transactions to withstand foreign downstream TP audits.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not apply Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) equivalent to BVI/Cayman. A VU-IC must engage a VFSC-licensed registered agent and maintain a registered office in Port Vila, but no statutory requirement exists for local physical office, local employees, local management, or an economic substance test. The IC must nevertheless refrain from trading with Vanuatu residents, holding Vanuatu real property, or carrying on banking, insurance, or trust business within Vanuatu; any such conduct violates the IC Act declaration and exposes the company to sanctions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

False Declaration / Corporate Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Making a false statement in any document required under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is an offense punishable on conviction by a statutory fine and/or imprisonment of up to two years; the VFSC may refuse renewal of the IC license, strike the company off the register, and require rectification filing. Proceeds of tax evasion abroad may also trigger foreign consolidated GAAR/CFC assessments with penalties of 50–100% of tax understated plus interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are personally prosecutable in the Vanuatu Supreme Court; civil fraudulent-trading remedies allow the court to pierce the corporate veil and order restitution from the directors' personal assets. Criminal travel bans and passport surrender can be imposed pending trial; upon conviction, disqualification from managing any Vanuatu company and custodial sentence apply.
Shell Company / Sham Conduit Misuse

Statutory sanction: No standalone Vanuatu ESR penalty exists; however, VFSC may decline the annual license fee renewal and strike off the IC where it appears to have no legitimate economic purpose. In the destination market, the recharacterization of 100% of remote ECG monitor sale profits typically triggers corporate income tax at rates up to 30–40%, plus late-payment interest and negligence penalties of up to 100%, and customs valuation penalties for overstated invoice values.

Director personal liability: Courts applying the doctrine of piercing the corporate veil may fix personal liability on directors who structured the sham; freezing orders over personal bank deposits, travel bans, and seizure of travel documents have been granted in asset-preservation proceedings. Foreign directors remain extraditable for fraud where predicate offenses fall within applicable extradition treaties.
AML / Money Laundering and Financial Circuit Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act No. 13 of 2001 (as amended), money laundering is punishable by a fine of up to VUV 10,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to twenty years; the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze all bank balances held by the IC; the VFSC may suspend or revoke licenses held by the registered agent and ban the company from reincorporation. Banking rails (Swift/TT) will be terminated by Vanuatu correspondent banks for AML breach.

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners and directors risk criminal conviction, confiscation of proceeds under the Proceeds of Crime Act, personal fines, imprisonment, statutory travel bans, and prohibition from re-entering Vanuatu; the AML Act also pierces the corporate veil to attach the personal assets of directors who directed suspicious transfers with knowledge or wilful blindness.
Unlicensed Medical Device Manufacturing and Unapproved Export

Statutory sanction: Carrying on business without the required license is a criminal offense under the Business Licence Act, carrying statutory fines per contravention plus forfeiture of relevant equipment and closure of premises; customs authorities may seize misdeclared ECG monitor consignments; importing regulators can order recalls, impose monetary penalties under the EU MDR or US FDA civil monetary penalty regimes, and institute product-liability claims for patient harm.

Director personal liability: Directors and principal officers can be named personally in product-liability and personal-injury suits, with unlimited damages exposure in the destination jurisdiction; culpable negligence or manslaughter charges prosecuted against officers in serious patient-death cases lead to imprisonment, alongside Vanuatu-court travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and permanent director disqualification in both Vanuatu and the importing market.
