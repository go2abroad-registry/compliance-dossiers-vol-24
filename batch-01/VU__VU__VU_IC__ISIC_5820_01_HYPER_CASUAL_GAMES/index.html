Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: vrca.gov.vu. Offshore company incorporation and annual licensing registry: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); official portal: vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity creates a Vanuatu International Company (IC) by engaging a VFSC-licensed registered agent, filing constitutive documents, and obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation. No tax-clearance certificate is required for incorporation. After incorporation, the IC is automatically classified as an offshore exempt company; no income-tax registration exists in Vanuatu. If the IC makes or intends to make taxable supplies in or into Vanuatu exceeding VUV 10,000,000 in any 12-month period, it must apply to VRCA for VAT registration. The application requires the certificate of incorporation, registered agent acceptance, statement of business activities (including hyper-casual game publishing and digital advertising), expected value and type of supplies in Vanuatu, and bank account details. VRCA issues a VAT Registration Certificate bearing a VAT identification number/TIN on a single common identification framework. Non-resident principals can sign documents by any lawful electronic or notarized signature acceptable to the registered agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: a Vanuatu IC is a non-resident/exempt offshore company with separate legal personality and limited liability; it is not subject to Vanuatu income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax. It cannot elect pass-through treatment because no entity-level income tax regime exists; liability is not imputed to shareholders. For indirect-tax purposes, the IC is outside the normal Vanuatu VAT net except to the extent of taxable supplies connected with Vanuatu or imported services subject to reverse charge.

Available election forms

None comparable to check-the-box. Vanuatu has no entity classification election, no hybrid-mismatch election, and no transparent or partnership election for an International Company. The IC's exempt status is statutory and automatic. The only relevant election is voluntary VAT registration under the VAT Act for a person below the VUV 10,000,000 threshold, and the ability to apply for a different VAT accounting/tax year or input-credit allocation method with VRCA approval. Offshore status cannot be waived as a tax election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 10,000,000 of annual taxable supplies in or into Vanuatu (registration is compulsory once this threshold is met or exceeded in any 12 consecutive months); the imported-services reverse charge applies regardless of threshold when a Vanuatu IC receives services from a non-resident supplier that would have been taxable if supplied in Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered IC may zero-rate exported goods if the goods are exported within 28 days after supply or import or within such longer period allowed by the Commissioner, and evidence of export is retained. Services are zero-rated when supplied to a recipient who carries on business outside Vanuatu and the services relate to business carried on outside Vanuatu, or the services are performed wholly outside Vanuatu and are not consumed or enjoyed in Vanuatu. Hyper-casual game publishing and ad-monetization services sold to non-resident platforms such as AdMob or AppLovin qualify for 0% VAT if the IC holds platform contracts, recipient tax-residency evidence, and payout data demonstrating offshore receipt and use. Input VAT attributable to such zero-rated export supplies is refundable; mixed-use inputs must be apportioned.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Form) endorsed by VRCA Customs for any physical goods movement; for pure digital services, a digital-services export declaration or statement is substituted.
  • Bill of Lading or House Air Waybill for physical exports; for hyper-casual digital services, a signed platform agreement with AdMob or AppLovin and a service completion report.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency from the offshore payer.
  • AdMob and AppLovin payout statements and transaction logs showing monetization revenue, app identifier, date, and settlement currency.
  • Zero-rated VAT invoice with prescribed particulars and a statement of export or offshore service provision.
  • General ledger extraction for the IC's digital advertising export income and input VAT apportionment.

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, pre-clearance e-invoicing API, or digital stamping mandate exists. VAT invoices must be issued within 28 days and contain the supplier's VAT number, recipient details, date, description, tax-exclusive amount, VAT amount, and total price. Customs export clearances use electronic single-window declarations (ASYCUDA World), but these are not linked to real-time VAT invoice clearance. Tax and invoice records must be retained for at least 7 years.

VRCA may audit zero-rated export claims and issue a revised assessment, clawing back any incorrectly claimed zero rating and disallowed input VAT credits. For careless or deliberate misstatement, the VAT Act authorizes civil penalties up to 200% of the tax avoided, default interest computed monthly, and prosecution of directors or the registered agent for offences. Knowingly false export declarations may attract fines and imprisonment of up to 12 months or two years, and the IC may lose its VFSC compliance certificate. The limitation period for a revised assessment is generally 5 years, extended without restriction in cases of fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a substantially liberalised currency regime. The Vatu is managed against a multi-currency basket but is freely quotable against USD, AUD, NZD, EUR and GBP. Current account convertibility is unrestricted: international companies may receive foreign currency revenue from gaming platforms and remit royalties, service fees and dividends without prior RBV approval. Capital account treatment is also open for a Vanuatu International Company: non-resident shareholders may inject equity or shareholder loans, and the proceeds may be held in offshore currency accounts. The practical remittance hurdle is not exchange control approval but bank-level source-of-funds scrutiny and documentary verification. No tax clearance certificate or profit remittance permit is required for an International Company that has no Vanuatu-source income and that has maintained its exempt status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio. An International Company may retain export or service revenues in USD, EUR, AUD or any other freely usable currency in RBV-licensed banks or approved offshore accounts. There is no statutory timeline for conversion and no requirement to repatriate hard currency into Vatu. The receiving bank must report inward remittances for balance-of-payments statistics to the RBV, and the treasury should adopt an internal policy of converting Vatu balances only when operational local disbursements require VUV.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as the sole primary treasury account; Medium as a supporting outbound disbursement rail. An offshore digital EMI without an RBV banking licence may not hold statutory capital escrow, may not issue VUV-account servicing for a Vanuatu entity, and cannot provide the full audit trail required for a capital injection. Cross-border EMIs such as Airwallex, Currencycloud or multi-currency B2B platforms may be used only for non-VUV operational payouts after the primary account is established with an RBV-licensed bank. Legal constraints include Financial Institutions Act licensing, the prohibition on deposit taking without a licence, and the need to ensure the EMI does not create an unauthorized Vanuatu permanent establishment.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound wiring requires the shareholder to remit SWIFT MT103 from a regulated bank account in the shareholder's name to the dedicated Capital Escrow Account at an RBV-licensed tier-1 bank. The purpose narrative must clearly state 'Equity capital subscription into Vanuatu International Company' and include, where the correspondent bank requires it, an ISO 20022 purpose code corresponding to direct investment, such as G300 for establishment or equity capital, and the Vanuatu balance-of-payments statistical classification for inward share capital. The receiving bank must receive the corporate due-diligence file, board resolution authorising the capital increase, updated register of members, and source-of-funds evidence. The treasury should ensure the remitter name, the share subscription agreement and the SWIFT sender line are identical; no third-party deposit, cash, crypto or unexecuted loan agreement will be accepted. For shareholder loans, a signed loan agreement with commercial terms, an RBV-compliant purpose narrative, and a board treasury policy must accompany the wire.The primary clearing rail for outbound trade and operational payments is SWIFT MT103 bank-to-bank priority transfer for vendor payments, platform service fees, group royalties and user-acquisition agency disbursements. Escrow-style disbursement instructions should be executed only through the tier-1 bank's electronic banking portal with dual authorisation. Trade settlement documents, monthly payout reports, invoices and board-approved treasury limits must be retained to support every outbound transfer. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions paid by an International Company to its non-resident shareholders; a distribution out of an IC's profits may be remitted free of any Vanuatu tax, stamp duty, or exchange control.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within one calendar month immediately following each anniversary of the date of incorporation. The registered agent must procure the signed declaration and pay the annual government fee before the statutory deadline; a foreign beneficial owner may sign electronically or by power of attorney appointing the agent.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Part A Box 1 - Company Name and Registration Number Legal name of the International Company as shown on the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, followed by the unique IC registration number. Enter the exact English transliteration of the name; no local-language characters are accepted. The non-resident shareholder does not file this form directly; the agent files on the IC's behalf. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A in VUV — Vanuatu has not enacted transfer pricing legislation and imposes no Local File or Master File threshold for International Companies. However, consolidated groups must respect arm's length pricing when contracting the Vanuatu IC with foreign related parties, such as a payable for publishing services or a royalty for game IP, because the ultimate parent's tax authority will apply its own CFC and transfer pricing rules; maintain contemporaneous pricing files in USD covering the IC's intercompany agreements.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance (Implementation) Act applies to International Companies carrying on relevant activities. For a hyper-casual games publisher, any of the following constitutes a relevant activity: (i) intellectual property business where the IC owns or exploits the game's copyright, trademark, or software for income; (ii) distribution and service centres supporting the ad-network contracts; or (iii) pure equity holding. The IC must report substance in its annual VFSC declaration and, where relevant, meet the 'adequate substance' test: a physical registered office or place of business in Vanuatu, permanent local employees with suitable qualifications conducting the core income-generating activity, and board meetings in Vanuatu with a local quorum. Game development, ad inventory management, and bank signatory authority carried out solely in foreign locations while booking income in the IC will breach the substance doctrine and expose the zero-tax status.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Misrepresentation (ESR Offence)

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Economic Substance (Implementation) Act, administrative penalty per falsified declaration, disgorgement of the exempt status benefit, and deregistration of the IC by the VFSC.

Director personal liability: Directors and the beneficial owner who signed the false declaration face joint and several civil responsibility for unpaid foreign taxes assessed on the group, personal fines, a Court-imposed travel restriction pending prosecution, and imprisonment for the knowing false statement.
Tax Evasion through Sham Offshore Routing (Foreign CFC/PE Recharacterization)

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu: cancellation of IC status, VFSC penalties, and potential strike-off. Foreign jurisdictions: recharacterization of the IC as a domestic resident or permanent establishment, inclusion of its profits in the parent's taxable base, fraud and negligence penalties of up to 100-200% of the avoidance, plus interest.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced: directors and controlling shareholders are jointly and severally liable for the recaptured tax and penalties; in serious cases foreign courts issue travel bans, extradition requests, and custodial sentences for willful evasion.
AML/CFT and Financial Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fines and asset forfeiture under Vanuatu's AML/CFT legislation; VFSC suspension of the registered agent; bank account freezes by correspondent banks; the IC may also be delisted from AdMob or AppLovin payment rails for AML failure.

Director personal liability: Directors and the nominee shareholder are personally accountable for AML compliance failures and may be prohibited from acting as directors, subjected to confiscation orders over personal assets, and prosecuted criminally; travel bans are issued when flight risk is established.
False Statement, Forgery, and Contempt of VFSC Notices

Statutory sanction: Fine not exceeding VUV 100,000 per offence and/or a term of imprisonment under the International Companies Act (Cap 222); the registration may be cancelled retroactively so the entity loses its limited liability and tax exemption from inception.

Director personal liability: The director or beneficial owner in default is personally liable for all debts of the company contracted during the period of invalidity, subject to restitution orders to the VFSC and aggrieved creditors, and may be barred from obtaining any future licence in Vanuatu.
