Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No general statutory minimum charter capital applies to limited liability companies under the Law on Enterprises 2020; however, because the captive reinsurance SPV is an insurance-business enterprise, Decree No. 46/2023/ND-CP Article 6 sets a legal capital floor of VND 300,000,000,000 (approximately USD 12.7 million at prevailing exchange rates) for a reinsurance enterprise. The charter capital must equal or exceed this legal capital. The full charter capital must be irrevocably deposited into a blocked escrow account (tài khoản phong tỏa) at a licensed commercial bank operating in Vietnam prior to submission of the Insurance Establishment License dossier; the bank's balance-freeze certificate is attached to the dossier, and the escrowed funds are released for licensed operational use only after the Ministry of Finance issues the Insurance Establishment License.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Design Appraisal and Fire Fighting Acceptance (thẩm duyệt thiết kế phòng cháy chữa cháy và nghiệm thu) issued by the provincial Fire Prevention and Rescue Police under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP and the Law on Fire Prevention and Fighting; the enterprise must obtain a fire-safety certificate for the premises, install an approved fire alarm and suppression system, maintain a fire-response plan, and appoint a fire brigade for the workplace. Structural clearance and safe-building-use confirmation from the local construction inspectorate under the Construction Law No. 50/2014/QH13 (as amended) confirming the building's structural load capacity for the intended office/data-center occupancy. Occupational safety and health clearance under the Law on Occupational Safety and Health No. 84/2015/QH13 and Decree No. 39/2016/ND-CP, including a baseline risk assessment, workplace environment monitoring, periodic health checkups for employees, and a certification that the premises comply with labor safety standards; additionally, a civil-defense preparedness check confirming the office has emergency evacuation routes, a shelter plan, and a first-aid station in accordance with Decree No. 03/2019/ND-CP (civil defense law) is recommended, given the enterprise's designation as a financial market infrastructure participant.

Paid-in capital rule: No general statutory minimum charter capital applies to limited liability companies under the Law on Enterprises 2020; however, because the captive reinsurance SPV is an insurance-business enterprise, Decree No. 46/2023/ND-CP Article 6 sets a legal capital floor of VND 300,000,000,000 (approximately USD 12.7 million at prevailing exchange rates) for a reinsurance enterprise. The charter capital must equal or exceed this legal capital. The full charter capital must be irrevocably deposited into a blocked escrow account (tài khoản phong tỏa) at a licensed commercial bank operating in Vietnam prior to submission of the Insurance Establishment License dossier; the bank's balance-freeze certificate is attached to the dossier, and the escrowed funds are released for licensed operational use only after the Ministry of Finance issues the Insurance Establishment License.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế, GDT) under the Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Viet Nam. Primary e-tax portal: https://etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn (e-filing, e-invoicing, taxpayer account management) and https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (legacy electronic tax portal). Import/export tariff and customs clearance are administered by the General Department of Vietnam Customs (Tổng cục Hải quan) at https://www.customs.gov.vn. The insurance and reinsurance licensing authority is the Ministry of Finance (Insurance Supervisory Authority).

E-government filing portal

For the foreign-invested LLC captive reinsurer: (1) obtain an Insurance Enterprise Establishment and Operation License from the Ministry of Finance, which is a statutory precondition for any captive reinsurance entity under the Law on Insurance Business 2022 (08/2022/QH15), including satisfying minimum charter capital, governance, and solvency capital requirements; (2) after the insurance license is granted, file the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) application with the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI); (3) the General Department of Taxation automatically issues the 10-digit Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế) concurrently with ERC issuance under the integrated business and tax registration mechanism (Decree No. 122/2021/ND-CP), so no separate initial TIN application is required for locally incorporated entities; (4) activate the GDT e-tax account on etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn, elect CIT/ VAT finalization cycles, and complete e-invoice registration with tax-authority codes under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP; (5) register the foreign currency account and export/import FX payment codes with an authorized bank, and notify the State Bank of Vietnam as needed for cross-border premium and reserve remittances; and (6) complete payroll, social insurance, unemployment, and health insurance registration with the provincial social insurance agency. A foreign corporate that operates without a permanent establishment in Viet Nam does not obtain a domestic TIN through this route but instead registers with the GDT for Foreign Contractor Withholding Tax (FCT) and receives a temporary tax code.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the LLC is a Vietnamese tax resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax at the standard 20% rate on worldwide income under the Law on Enterprise Income Tax (14/2008/QH13, as amended); no pass-through or transparent treatment is available to limited liability companies; dividends and distributions are not deductible; the captive's premium income, investment income, and gains are taxable at the entity level. Territorial_Scope: non-resident insurers and reinsurers are taxed only on Vietnam-source income through FCT or withholding tax, but a locally licensed captive is a resident taxpayer. Tax_Exempt_Zone: not available; export processing enterprise (EPE) zero-VAT and customs-duty incentives do not apply to a financial-services captive, and no special economic-zone VAT/CIT exemption is available for captive reinsurance in Viet Nam. — For the foreign-invested LLC captive reinsurer: (1) obtain an Insurance Enterprise Establishment and Operation License from the Ministry of Finance, which is a statutory precondition for any captive reinsurance entity under the Law on Insurance Business 2022 (08/2022/QH15), including satisfying minimum charter capital, governance, and solvency capital requirements; (2) after the insurance license is granted, file the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) application with the provincial Department of Planning and Investment (DPI); (3) the General Department of Taxation automatically issues the 10-digit Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế) concurrently with ERC issuance under the integrated business and tax registration mechanism (Decree No. 122/2021/ND-CP), so no separate initial TIN application is required for locally incorporated entities; (4) activate the GDT e-tax account on etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn, elect CIT/ VAT finalization cycles, and complete e-invoice registration with tax-authority codes under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP; (5) register the foreign currency account and export/import FX payment codes with an authorized bank, and notify the State Bank of Vietnam as needed for cross-border premium and reserve remittances; and (6) complete payroll, social insurance, unemployment, and health insurance registration with the provincial social insurance agency. A foreign corporate that operates without a permanent establishment in Viet Nam does not obtain a domestic TIN through this route but instead registers with the GDT for Foreign Contractor Withholding Tax (FCT) and receives a temporary tax code.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the LLC is a Vietnamese tax resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax at the standard 20% rate on worldwide income under the Law on Enterprise Income Tax (14/2008/QH13, as amended); no pass-through or transparent treatment is available to limited liability companies; dividends and distributions are not deductible; the captive's premium income, investment income, and gains are taxable at the entity level. Territorial_Scope: non-resident insurers and reinsurers are taxed only on Vietnam-source income through FCT or withholding tax, but a locally licensed captive is a resident taxpayer. Tax_Exempt_Zone: not available; export processing enterprise (EPE) zero-VAT and customs-duty incentives do not apply to a financial-services captive, and no special economic-zone VAT/CIT exemption is available for captive reinsurance in Viet Nam.

Available election forms

Viet Nam has no check-the-box or partnership classification election; all LLCs and JSCs are mandatorily entity-level CIT taxpayers. Available statutory elections and mitigation mechanisms: (a) CIT incentive election for qualifying new investment projects in eligible sectors or disadvantaged regions under Decree No. 218/2013/ND-CP (e.g., 10% preferential rate for up to 15 years, 4-year full exemption, 9-year 50% reduction), available only if the captive substantively satisfies the incentive conditions; (b) election of the foreign tax credit method to offset taxes paid on foreign-source insurance/reinsurance income against Vietnamese CIT (default is credit, not deduction); (c) thin-capitalization election to operate within the 30% EBITDA related-party interest cap under Decree No. 132/2020/ND-CP, which is a mandatory fixed-ratio rule rather than an elective safe harbor; (d) transfer-pricing elections: bilateral/unilateral Advance Pricing Agreements, and MAP invocation under Decree No. 126/2020/ND-CP where the BEPS MLI applies; (e) no hybrid mismatch election exists under Vietnamese domestic law; the Law on Tax Administration and Decree No. 132/2020/ND-CP recharacterize hybrid entities and instruments statutorily, and CFC rules must be assessed for any offshore captive affiliate.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No minimum turnover threshold applies to incorporated taxpayers: every enterprise is automatically VAT-registered upon issuance of its Tax Identification Number, and VAT liability arises on any taxable supply in Viet Nam without de minimis exception. The only statutory threshold is for business households and private individuals with annual taxable revenue equal to or below VND 100,000,000, which are not required to register or charge VAT (Article 5.24 of the VAT Law); companies, including the LLC captive reinsurer, cannot invoke this exemption. A captive issuing taxable auxiliary services (e.g., management, underwriting support to affiliates) must charge 10% VAT on those supplies from the first dong of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) is available only for genuinely exported goods and services meeting the conditions of Article 9 of the VAT Law and Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC: (i) the service is supplied to a foreign entity under a written contract; (ii) the service is consumed entirely outside Viet Nam; (iii) payment is received from abroad; and (iv) the export settlement is made through an authorized bank. A Vietnamese captive reinsurer that assumes risk from a foreign-related captive would, in principle, be performing a service consumed outside Viet Nam; however, reinsurance itself is already VAT-exempt under the insurance exempt-supply list, and exempt services cannot be zero-rated, so the premium income enters neither the 10% nor the 0% VAT base and the associated input VAT is irrecoverable. 0% VAT treatment is claimable only for a genuine and severable export business line (e.g., fee-based actuarial or underwriting consultancy supplied to a foreign affiliate), which would require the full substantiation bundle. VAT refund of excess input credits is granted only for purchases attributable to taxable zero-rated or 10% export sales, and the refund claim (under Circular 80/2021/TT-BTC) requires the exporter to have bank evidence of foreign payment within 90 days of the refund application and to have submitted e-invoice data with prior tax compliance confirmed.

  • Signed reinsurance contract/cover note between the Vietnamese captive LLC and the foreign captive or ceding insurer, evidencing assumption of risk located outside Vietnam
  • Commercial invoice or reinsurance statement of account (Phiếu tính phí / Đề nghị thanh toán) issued by the Vietnamese captive
  • Reinsurance policy schedule, underwriting documentation, and actuarial or claims reserve report evidencing that the insured risk and loss events are situated outside Vietnam
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Giấy báo Có) or bank Swift message confirming the premium remittance is credited to the captive's foreign-currency or VND account at an authorized bank in Vietnam
  • Foreign insurer/captive certificate of incorporation and, where relevant, tax residence certificate of the offshore recipient
  • Board resolution approving the captive reinsurance arrangement and transfer-pricing documentation substantiating an arm's-length premium

Mandatory structured e-invoicing applies 100% from 1 July 2022 under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP. Insurers and reinsurers must issue e-invoices with tax-authority codes (hóa đơn điện tử có mã của cơ quan thuế): the invoice is sent through an approved e-invoice intermediary to the GDT etaxvn platform via API; the GDT assigns a digital code in near-real-time and returns the digitally stamped invoice to the issuer, constituting fiscal clearance. Every e-invoice must contain a QR code and the GDT-defined JSON/XML schema. The taxpayer must transmit invoice data and summary ledgers to the GDT on a periodic basis and include them in CIT/VAT finalization. Exempt reinsurance premium income must still be documented on coded e-invoices stating the VAT-exempt basis; input VAT recovery is evidenced exclusively through matching e-invoices and non-cash payment records.

The GDT applies statistical profiling to VAT refund/zero-rating files, with captive reinsurers subject to heightened tax-risk review because premium flows move between related parties across borders and the service is intangible. Substantial penalties apply: wrongfully claimed refunds or zero-rating are clawed back in full together with late-payment interest at 0.03% per day (approximately 10.95% per annum) under the Law on Tax Administration (38/2019/QH14); administrative fines for tax evasion range from one to three times the evaded tax amount under Decree No. 125/2020/ND-CP; criminal liability attaches under Article 200 of the Penal Code (15/2017/QH14) where evaded VAT exceeds VND 100,000,000, with fines up to VND 4.5 billion, imprisonment of up to 20 years for the most aggravated bracket, and direct exposure of the legal representative, chief accountant, and external tax preparer; retrospective assessments may reach back ten years for fraudulent, concealed, or unregistered transactions; the tax authority may also apply 'red-list' customs debt suspension, freeze VAT refunds, publish non-compliance rankings, and impose additional penalties on the e-invoice intermediary. In addition, failure to repatriate export service proceeds within the 180-day foreign-exchange deadline violates State Bank of Vietnam regulations (Ordinance on Foreign Exchange 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11; Circular No. 15/2015/TT-NHNN, as amended), exposing the entity to administrative fines, forced conversion of foreign currency, and suspension of tax refund processing until repatriation is completed.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam maintains current-account convertibility for permitted trade and services transactions while subjecting capital-account flows to registration, licensing, and the use of designated accounts. A captive reinsurance SPV under ISIC 6512 is treated as a service exporter for premium income received from overseas cedants; repatriated reinsurance premiums must be credited to a foreign-currency account at an SBV-licensed credit institution. Profit remittance hurdles include: (i) completion of audited annual financial statements prepared under Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) or IFRS as adopted for foreign-invested enterprises; (ii) corporate income tax finalization and production of the tax settlement certificate or tax clearance confirmation; (iii) submission of a foreign-currency withdrawal application to the licensed bank evidencing post-tax retained earnings; and (iv) compliance with the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC), the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC), and the solvency-reserve and capital-maintenance requirements applicable to insurance and reinsurance activities under the Law on Insurance Business 2022 and Decree 73/2016/ND-CP. Dividends and profit transfers are permissible only after the annual financial statements have been approved and the statutory audit is completed; the captive SPV must maintain an actuary-certified technical reserve position and, where applicable, a security/collateral deposit for the benefit of overseas cedants.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Ordinance on Foreign Exchange Control (No. 05/2005/PL-UBTVQH11, as amended) and SBV Circular No. 05/2021/TT-NHNN, all export earnings—including cross-border reinsurance premium receipts—must be remitted into a foreign-currency account held at an SBV-licensed credit institution. The statutory surrender rule requires the mandatory sale to the licensed bank of 80% of the export foreign-exchange receipts (net of eligible deductions) as promptly as possible upon credit to the account; the remaining 20% may be retained in the foreign-currency account for working-capital and permitted trade purposes. Any exemption, deferral, or reduced surrender ratio must be approved in advance by the State Bank of Vietnam. Reinsurance claims, loss-adjustment expenses, and reserve releases payable to overseas cedants are remitted through the same licensed bank with underlying reinsurance treaty contracts, bordereaux, loss advices, and tax invoices retained for the statutory five-year record-keeping period.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) – Foreign Exchange Control Department (FXCD)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for use as a primary treasury account; Medium only as an ancillary FX/payment channel. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut) are not licensed credit institutions in Viet Nam, cannot open VND-denominated accounts, and cannot clear VND payments through the SBV or the National Payment Corporation of Vietnam (NAPAS). The SBV does not recognize offshore EMI licenses for deposit-taking or local payment intermediation; cross-border transfers originating from such EMIs may be rejected by correspondent banks absent a clear underlying trade/reinsurance document trail. The captive reinsurance SPV must therefore maintain its principal account at an SBV-licensed commercial bank and use any EMI only for peripheral USD/EUR collections, with full reconciliation into the licensed-bank records and compliance with SBV reporting obligations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocols require all inbound equity injections to be remitted via SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident shareholder's account into the SPV's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) held at an SBV-licensed bank. The SWIFT remittance purpose/field 70 must expressly reference an FDI equity contribution ('gop von dau tu truc tiep') and the IRC registration number; the receiving bank posts the inflow to the registered legal-capital schedule and submits the transaction to the SBV balance-of-payments statistical reporting module. Shareholder loans must be wired into a separate SBV-registered loan account under a loan agreement filed with the SBV; equity injections and shareholder-subordinated loans must never be co-mingled in a single SWIFT. On high-value credit, the bank may request a source-of-funds confirmation (board resolution, shareholder financial statements, and recent bank statement) before the credit is made available.Outbound clearing rails: (i) reinsurance claim payments, retrocession premiums, and brokerage fees settled by SWIFT MT103/MT202 to overseas beneficiaries; (ii) collateral posting for reinsurance letters of credit executed through SWIFT MT760/MT799 (standby LC issuance) or MT700-series where a commercial/standby LC is used as settlement collateral; (iii) domestic VND obligations—taxes, statutory fees, payroll, and local service charges—settled through the NAPAS ACH system or the State Treasury collection platform. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File / Master File if total related-party transactions exceed VND 50 billion (~USD 2.1 million) per fiscal year, or total revenue exceeds VND 200 billion (~USD 8.5 million), or total assets exceed VND 50 billion; entities below thresholds must still keep contemporaneous TP documentation if a related-party transaction exists and the tax authority requests for benchmarking.

Economic substance scope: General anti-avoidance rules under the Tax Administration Law apply; Vietnam does not maintain formal Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as in UAE/BCI. However, a captive reinsurance entity must be commercially rational, have actual decision-making and risk-bearing people in Vietnam, and not be a conduit entity. Face-to-face control, adequate capital, and actual underwriting activity are required; failure will trigger recharacterization, or denial of TP concessions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing / Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Tax authority has the power to adjust taxable income, deny TP benefits, impose 20% penalty on under-declared tax, plus late-payment interest at 0.03%/day. Failure to maintain documentation may lead to administrative fines up to VND 50 million and subsequently tax adjustments based on the authority's own determination.

Director personal liability: Directors may be responsible for false declarations if they sign incorrect tax returns; criminal liability from deliberate tax evasion can reach imprisonment up to 7 years. Civil restitution of unpaid taxes and interest may be enforced against personal assets of the legal representative if the company is dissolved without settlement.
Shell Company / Substance-By-Pass

Statutory sanction: Tax authorities may disregard the entity for tax purposes (substance-over-form) or recharacterize transactions; addition of tax and penalties as above. Insurance regulator may revoke license, impose administrative fines up to VND 1 billion, or order shutdown for operating without a valid license.

Director personal liability: Registered legal representative's personal civil liability arises for unlawful management; foreign directors can be subject to travel bans if assets are not remitted to settle tax arrears, and possible criminal prosecution for insurance business fraud or tax evasion.
Anti-Money Laundering and Non-Remittance Compliance

Statutory sanction: AML fines up to VND 500 million for violations; criminal charges for laundering proceeds of crime up to 15 years imprisonment. Foreign-exchange control violations carry fines of up to VND 400 million; banking account freezes and license suspension possible.

Director personal liability: Direct scans may be criminally liable for money laundering if knowingly involved; travel bans and asset attachment are common pre-trial measures; personal accountability for restitution in civil actions.
Hide/Evasion of Withholding Tax

Statutory sanction: 20% penalty on under-withheld tax, plus interest; fine up to VND 200 million, criminal prosecution for tax evasion if over VND 100 million unpaid.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize unlawful distributions without tax clearance become personally liable for the unpaid WHT; criminal sanctions including imprisonment up to 5 years under the Penal Code.
