Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR); official portal: https://dcir.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities incorporating a Vanuatu International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] do not obtain a tax identification number from DCIR because ICs are statutorily tax-exempt. Registration with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is required for incorporation, which issues a company registration number. If the IC voluntarily registers for VAT or becomes liable for VAT through local taxable activities, it must apply to DCIR using Form VAT-01, providing the certificate of incorporation, foreign/domestic address details, and director identity documents. For purely offshore operations, no TIN is issued; an Exemption Certificate is available on request.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Vanuatu law. The International Company is categorically tax-exempt. Voluntary VAT registration election is available under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap 248] where the IC elects to make local taxable supplies. There are no hybrid mismatch elections.

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Vanuatu within the prescribed period, provided the supplier retains documentary evidence of export and the goods are not later returned. Export of plush mascot stuffed animals qualifies if title passes to a non-resident and the goods are shipped abroad. Offshore services must meet the exported-services test, such as services directly connected with exported goods or performed and supplied for use wholly outside Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration or Single Administrative Document approved by Vanuatu Customs
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill evidencing physical export from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Evidence of transfer of possession to the international carrier
  • Certificate of origin (where required)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate/foreign bank SWIFT credit evidencing payment by non-resident buyer
  • Copy of the zero-rated VAT invoice

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system is currently mandated. VAT-registered persons must issue prescribed tax invoices and file returns via DCIR electronic system (VITARA); no API-based real-time clearance.

DCIR may launch audits and retrospective reviews of zero-rating claims. If substantiation is inadequate, VAT is clawed back with interest from the filing date. Fraud penalties may include fines up to VUV 5,000,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years; further civil penalties include up to 150% of evaded tax and cancellation of VAT registration for repeat non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates an open, de facto fully convertible regime: both current-account (trade, services, income remittance) and capital-account (direct equity, loans, liquidation proceeds) flows are free from quantitative restrictions, with no prior approval or licensing requirement for non-resident International Companies. The VUV is independently floated without an official peg, and profit remittance by ICs is legally unrestricted. Practical hurdles arise from VUV's shallow offshore liquidity and global correspondent-bank de-risking: banks require an RBV Exchange Control form, board-resolved dividend or withdrawal minutes, beneficial ownership attestation, and tax-clearance confirmation for every outward remittance, with standard processing delays of one to three business days.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation, mandatory conversion ratio, or prescribed repatriation timeline exists in Vanuatu. An IC exporting plush mascot goods under HS 9503 may retain 100% of USD export proceeds in a Foreign Currency Account at a domestic bank or offshore without conversion into VUV. However, proceeds from physical shipments must be credited through the domestic banking system and matched to customs export declarations, bills of lading or airway bills, and commercial invoices; unsupported or purely offshore pass-through invoicing will be treated as an AML/CFT red flag and may result in account suspension.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; sole monetary authority, issuer of the Vatu (VUV), and statutory administrator of exchange controls pursuant to the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 128]. Corporate registry and non-bank AML/CFT supervision are administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Rating: Low-Medium. Vanuatu has no statutory fintech EMI or e-money licence track; deposit-taking from within Vanuatu requires a bank licence under the Financial Institutions Act or the International Banking Act. No income tax return penalty attaches because Vanuatu does not assess income tax on ICs.
Section A — Employer Registration Employer tax identification number, legal entity name, physical trading address, and contact details. Required only if the IC employs at least one Vanuatu citizen or permanent resident. An IC managed wholly offshore with no local payroll need not file but must retain payroll records to prove the absence of local employment. Late payment penalty of 1% per month on unpaid payroll tax, an additional 25% penalty for failure to notify chargeability, default assessment where no return is filed, and criminal liability for evading payroll tax punishable by fine and imprisonment for responsible officers. The VRC may impose a 100% surcharge on deliberately unpaid or concealed payroll tax.
Section B — Taxable Remuneration Total wages, salaries, allowances, and other payments subject to payroll tax; tax at 1%; less exempt payments; net payroll tax payable. Non-resident or expatriate employees are excluded from the 1% local payroll tax to the extent they are not Vanuatu citizens or permanent residents, but statutory deemed monthly taxable wages may apply where such employees are physically present in Vanuatu; amounts are payable quarterly with the return. Late payment penalty of 1% per month on unpaid payroll tax, an additional 25% penalty for failure to notify chargeability, default assessment where no return is filed, and criminal liability for evading payroll tax punishable by fine and imprisonment for responsible officers. The VRC may impose a 100% surcharge on deliberately unpaid or concealed payroll tax.
Section C — Provident Fund Cross-Reference Declaration of Vanuatu National Provident Fund (VNPF) contributions: employer 4% and employee 2% of insurable wages for all employees under the VNPF age threshold. VRC and VNPF data are cross-matched; under-declaring payroll heads to avoid payroll tax while remitting VNPF contributions is treated as deliberate evasion. Late payment penalty of 1% per month on unpaid payroll tax, an additional 25% penalty for failure to notify chargeability, default assessment where no return is filed, and criminal liability for evading payroll tax punishable by fine and imprisonment for responsible officers. The VRC may impose a 100% surcharge on deliberately unpaid or concealed payroll tax.
