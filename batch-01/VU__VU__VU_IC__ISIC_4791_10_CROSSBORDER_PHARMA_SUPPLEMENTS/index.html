Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu maintains no national income tax authority because no corporate or personal income tax statute is in force. Fiscal and corporate governance is allocated as follows: (a) Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC, www.vfsc.vu) - sole registrar and regulator of International Companies (IC), responsible for incorporation, annual renewal, beneficial ownership filings and economic substance supervision; (b) Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management), operating the ASYCUDA World electronic customs system, customs duties, excise and Multiple Purpose Levy (MPL) on qualifying imports; (c) Municipal councils of Port Vila and Luganville - domestic business license fees, from which ICs are statutorily exempt.

E-government filing portal

No national TIN or VAT identification number is issued in Vanuatu because no income tax or VAT law exists. A foreign-owned IC obtains its fiscal identity as follows: (1) engage a VFSC-licensed registered agent (mandatory statutory requirement); (2) file the Memorandum and Articles of Association and Certificate of Incorporation application with the VFSC registry; (3) receive the Certificate of Incorporation bearing the IC registration number, typically within 1-3 business days upon payment of the VFSC statutory government fee plus registered agent fees; (4) maintain the mandatory registered office and registered agent in Vanuatu; (5) pay the annual VFSC renewal fee to preserve the tax exemption; (6) file beneficial ownership details and any economic substance returns when triggered; (7) if physical goods cross the Vanuatu border, obtain a VRCA Customs client code and file export/import entries in ASYCUDA World. No municipal business license is required for an IC, and no revenue authority tax file number is assigned.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu operates no check-the-box election, no hybrid mismatch election, and no statutory mechanism to waive, limit or opt into taxation. Tax status is fixed by the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]: an IC is exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, imposts and charges, including income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, business license fees and land transfer tax, irrespective of ownership, turnover or place of management. The IC must not carry on business with Vanuatu residents and must not hold Vanuatu land or licensed banking/insurance activities without separate authorization; cross-border nutraceutical e-commerce sales to non-residents do not disturb the exemption. No U.S.-style entity classification or hybrid translocation election exists in Vanuatu law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Vanuatu has no VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax system in force. Indirect fiscal levies are limited to customs import duties, excise on specified goods, and the Multiple Purpose Levy (MPL) on qualifying physical imports into Vanuatu; none apply to export sales of dietary supplements or nutraceuticals by a Vanuatu International Company.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST law is enacted. An IC is exempt from all indirect taxation regardless of turnover. Domestic traders become liable for municipal business license fees from the first vatu (VUV) of local turnover, but s.77 of the International Companies Act expressly disapplies such fees for ICs in good standing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Vanuatu imposes no VAT/GST on domestic sales, exports, or offshore services, so there is no export zero-rating claim or refund mechanism to assert. Cross-border nutraceutical sales by the IC sit entirely outside the Vanuatu indirect tax base; no export equalization levy, turnover-based export tax, or stamp duty applies. Customs duties and the Multiple Purpose Levy are chargeable only on physical imports into Vanuatu territory, never on outbound shipments, digital supplies, or offshore card-not-present transactions.

Physical exports are cleared through the ASYCUDA World electronic customs declaration system administered by VRCA; invoices issued by an IC for offshore nutraceutical sales are not subject to any Vanuatu fiscal e-reporting or digital validation requirement.

No VAT export-fraud clawback regime exists because no VAT is imposed. Enforcement risk concentrates on corporate and customs compliance: (1) VFSC annual renewal default triggers statutory late penalties, loss of good standing and potential strike-off; (2) failure to maintain a registered agent or beneficial ownership records exposes the IC to fines and director liability; (3) prohibited trading with Vanuatu residents can cause forfeiture of the IC exemption, retroactive municipal license fee assessments and director personal liability; (4) false customs declarations attract fines and imprisonment under the Customs Act [Cap. 121], plus seizure of goods; (5) default on economic substance obligations for relevant activities may trigger penalties and cancellation of the IC certificate. Vanuatu has no exchange control law and therefore imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day reference reflects standard high-risk merchant acquirer settlement and rolling-reserve cycles applicable to nutraceutical card-not-present payment processing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is a floating currency with current-account convertibility consistent with Vanuatu's IMF Article VIII status (accepted 1981); there are no statutory restrictions on dividends, interest, or profit remittance by International Companies. Practically, fund transfers are routed through authorized dealers with documentary proof of legitimate source of funds, and a bank-level source-of-funds review is mandatory under the AML/CFT regime. Capital-account flows are liberalized for ICs, but the RBV requires statistical declaration forms for cross-border investment transactions. No withholding tax is imposed on outbound dividends in Vanuatu, though the high-risk nature of nutraceutical e-commerce triggers enhanced due diligence and possible de-risking by correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act [CAP 51], export proceeds from the sale of goods must be received through an authorized dealer in Vanuatu within 6 months of the date of shipment. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to VUV; exporters may retain 100% of proceeds in a foreign-currency account at a licensed commercial bank. The exporter must submit a completed Form EX-1 (Exchange Control Declaration), a customs export declaration, sales invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, and proof of receipt within the prescribed window. Failure to comply may result in enforcement action by the RBV under exchange control regulations.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [CAP 124] and the Exchange Control Act [CAP 51]; the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (VFIU) supervises AML/CFT compliance for all authorized dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. Vanuatu has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) license framework comparable to the FCA or MAS; the VFSC Financial Dealers License covers only limited dealing activity, not a full EMI passport. Offshore digital EMI providers must either partner with a Tier 1 Vanuatu bank as a sponsor or rely on an unrestricted foreign license, which most VU banks will reject under de-risking policies. The RBV does not issue EMI authorizations, and the absence of a national payment systems sandbox further constrains fintech-only clearing in VUV. Viability is acceptable only as a secondary e-wallet layer, not as the primary treasury rail.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No corporate income tax filing deadline exists. The VFSC license must be renewed within 12 months of the date of issue; late renewal incurs penalties. Proof of renewal is required to maintain standing to enforce contracts and protect assets.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Renewal Resource Record Confirmation of company name, registration number, registered agent, registered office address, authorized share capital, beneficial ownership register status, and payment of the annual VFSC license fee. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation, no Local File or Master File mandate, and no threshold expressed in VUV. Multinational groups must, however, comply with OECD-aligned documentation requirements of foreign parent jurisdictions (e.g., EUR 750,000 to EUR 50 million thresholds).

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations comparable to the BVI or Cayman Islands. Nevertheless, the VFSC licensing framework for online nutraceutical retail (ISIC 4791) requires demonstrable physical presence, a substantive board process, and core income-generating activities conducted from Vanuatu. Failure to maintain such substance may expose the structure to foreign anti-abuse challenges (CFC rules, economic substance recharacterization, and shell-company denial claims).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Structuring

Statutory sanction: The International Companies Act Cap 222 permits VFSC to strike off offending companies. Criminal prosecution for fraud may lead to fines up to VT 1,000,000, restitution orders, and forfeiture of assets through mutual legal assistance channels with foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in tax evasion or misrepresentation are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes, penalties, and interest; courts may pierce the corporate veil to impose civil restitution, travel bans, and imprisonment for statutory fraud (maximum term 7 years).
Unlicensed Regulated Activity — ISIC 4791

Statutory sanction: VFSC may levy administrative fines up to VT 500,000, suspend or revoke the Special License, and prohibit further e-commerce operations. Additional penalties may be imposed under the Vanuatu Pharmacy and Medicines Act for distribution of unapproved health products.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for any fine if the unlicensed activity arises from their direction or neglect; egregious cases trigger director disqualification orders, asset freezes, and travel bans pending full compliance.
AML / Beneficial Ownership / Sanctions Compliance

Statutory sanction: AMLTFA 2014 imposes fines on legal persons up to VT 2,000,000, license revocation, and mandatory dissolution; the Vanuatu FIU may issue freezing orders and initiate criminal prosecutions.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly fail to report suspicious activity or who permit sanctions violations face personal fines up to VT 500,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; personal asset forfeiture and travel bans are routinely granted by the Vanuatu courts.
