Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR) for domestic fiscal matters; Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as the registry authority for International Companies (portal at https://www.vfsc.vu).

E-government filing portal

Foreign shareholders incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] through a VFSC-licensed registered agent. The VFSC issues a Certificate of Incorporation bearing a unique company registration number. No separate TIN or VAT registration number is issued because an IC is expressly exempt from all Vanuatu national taxes; the only recurring fiscal obligation is the annual VFSC license fee payable through the registered agent.

Default tax classification

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST regime is currently in force; a proposed future VAT would apply to taxable supplies above VUV 10,000,000 annual turnover, but the legislation is not yet operative.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — absent a VAT/GST regime, there is no zero-rating mechanism to claim. An International Company's offshore income, including TikTok Shop retail sales proceeds, is categorically outside Vanuatu's tax jurisdiction and generates zero output tax liability and zero refund entitlement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (only if physical goods transit Vanuatu customs territory)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical logistics corridors)

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API data exchange, or digital stamping requirement exists in Vanuatu. International Companies are not connected to any national e-invoicing mesh or fiscal telemetry system; private commercial records are sufficient.

No statutory FX repatriation deadline or VAT clawback exists because Vanuatu has no exchange controls and ICs are fully tax-exempt. However, false declarations or misuse of the International Company regime are prosecuted under the International Companies Act and VFSC enforcement rules; penalties include fines up to VUV 500,000, deregistration, director disqualification, and retrospective revocation of tax-exempt status if the company engages in prohibited domestic business activity.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is convertible on current and capital account; Vanuatu maintains a liberal exchange control regime with no general restrictions on non-resident transactions. Qualifying International Companies dealing in foreign currency with non-residents are substantially exempt from exchange control formalities. Inward capital subscriptions and outward profit remittances are permitted without prior RBV approval, but banks require documentary proof of IC good standing, registered agent confirmation, beneficial ownership declarations, and appropriate SWIFT purpose codes. VUV operates under a managed-trade-weighted-basket arrangement, so USD/AUD/EUR-denominated treasury accounts are recommended to avoid ADB conversion spreads. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal for the tax-exempt IC, but each outbound transfer is subject to the ADB's sanctions, anti-money-laundering, and source-of-funds screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no required repatriation timeline. Exporters and cross-border e-commerce operators, including International Companies, may retain foreign currency export proceeds indefinitely in foreign currency accounts at Authorized Dealer Banks and convert to VUV only as needed at commercial rates. An IC may not transact with Vanuatu residents unless domestically licensed, so VUV conversion should generally be avoided for treasury efficiency.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) as the Central Bank and Foreign Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 155], with day-to-day exchange control administration delegated to Authorized Dealer Banks (ADBs); the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately licenses International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital is injected via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for interbank credit) to the IC's USD, AUD, or EUR account at an Authorized Dealer Bank, with the payment narrative stating 'Equity injection and paid-in capital to International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap 222]'. The remitting bank must supply a funds-origin declaration, and the receiving ADB will verify the source of wealth, corporate good standing, and registered agent confirmation. No prior RBV approval is required for a qualifying IC; the ADB records the inflow in its exchange-control reporting. To avoid recharacterization risks, the IC should maintain capital escrow and operating sub-ledgers and keep shareholder loans in separately documented loan agreements with stated interest terms, notwithstanding the IC's tax exemption.Supplier and logistics payments are cleared through ADB-originated SWIFT MT103 wires in USD, GBP, or EUR. For TikTok Shop affiliate commissions and repeat supplier settlements, batch API-driven payout rails such as Wise B2B or Payoneer Payouts are permissible where the IC's merchant settlement account is the documented originator and beneficiary accounts are on a pre-approved supplier list. For high-value inventory procurement above approximately USD 50,000, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit at sight or usance, issued through the ADB and advised or confirmed by a correspondent bank in the supplier's jurisdiction, is the preferred clearing rail. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or branch profits tax at the national level; International Companies registered under the International Companies Act, Cap. 222 are expressly exempt from all present and future taxes on income, profits, and distributions.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu does not impose withholding tax on any distribution, including cash dividends, stock dividends, deemed distributions, or liquidation proceeds paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — Vanuatu has no domestic transfer pricing legislation, no arm's length statutory rule, and no Vatu-denominated documentation threshold for Master File or Local File; International Companies are exempt from corporate income tax and therefore cannot obtain a tax advantage through artificial transfer pricing.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), effective for International Companies engaged in relevant activities, require adequate physical presence, local employment, core income-generating activities in Vanuatu, and annual substance reporting to VFSC; a TikTok Shop retail operation substantially conducted from Vanuatu may fall within 'distribution and service centre' scope where purchasing, inventory management, order processing, and logistics functions occur on-island, requiring a local office, staff, and directors' meetings in Vanuatu; purely offshore sellers without Vanuatu operational substance must nonetheless document that their relevant activities are not attributable to Vanuatu.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Offshore Tax Fraud Abroad

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no domestic income tax to sanction, but the foreign resident jurisdiction may impose tax fraud penalties, civil fraud fines of up to 200% of understated tax, criminal prosecution, and joint liability orders; Vanuatu authorities may also cooperate through exchange of information arrangements, exposing the structure to cross-border enforcement.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and beneficial owners acting as de facto or shadow directors face personal liability for fraudulent trading, foreign criminal tax penalties, and confiscation orders; travel bans may be issued by the foreign tax authority and judicial freezing orders may pierce the Vanuatu corporate veil on grounds of sham or façade.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

AML/CFT Breaches and Unlicensed Financial Activity
