Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Vanuatu Government, Port Vila; taxpayer online services portal administered by DCIR

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned International Company (VU_IC): (1) Complete VFSC incorporation or re-domiciliation under the International Companies Act; (2) lodge the incorporation application and certified constitutional documents with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); (3) after incorporation, submit the DCIR Taxpayer Registration Form together with the certified certificate of incorporation, passport copies, registered office certificate, resident agent appointment, and the statutory bunkering licence; (4) receive the statutory TIN/VAT registration certificate. A pure offshore International Company is tax exempt and need not obtain a VAT registration number unless it makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu; voluntary VAT registration is available to support input VAT credits or export zero-rating substantiation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election, classification election, or hybrid mismatch election is available under Vanuatu domestic tax law because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax. An International Company may elect to redomicile or migrate into or out of the International Companies regime, and may voluntarily elect for VAT registration under the Vanuatu VAT Act to claim credits for zero-rated export and international shipping supplies; there is no fiscal transparency or partnership election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months; voluntary registration is permitted for persons making or intending to make only zero-rated supplies

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for goods physically exported from Vanuatu and for marine fuel and lubricants supplied as ship's stores under Customs supervision to oceangoing vessels engaged in international trade, including offshore bunkering and refuelling operations. The operator must hold the statutory bunkering licence and evidence that the vessel departed Vanuatu or is engaged in foreign-going carriage; associated offshore services supplied to non-resident ship operators are also zero-rated as international service exports.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Vanuatu does not presently impose a real-time fiscal clearance mandate, mandatory API integration, certified fiscal device, or digital tax stamp requirement for e-invoicing. Registered taxpayers must issue full, sequentially numbered VAT invoices and retain export documentation, including Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, and Inward Remittance Certificate, for DCIR inspection.

DCIR may reverse zero-rating if the exporter cannot substantiate physical export or foreign-going vessel supply, or if the foreign currency sale proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days, converting the supply to taxable at the standard 12.5% VAT rate. Assessments may be reopened within five years; unpaid VAT carries a default penalty of up to 25% plus gazetted interest. Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract a civil penalty of up to double the tax evaded, criminal fines and imprisonment under Vanuatu fraud legislation, suspension or revocation of the VFSC or DCIR bunkering licence, and director debarment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu adopts a liberalized current and capital account convertibility regime. The Vatu is freely convertible for non-resident investors; profit remittances, dividends, and liquidation proceeds are not subject to Central Bank approval. However, all flows must transit through a resident licensed bank and satisfy source-of-funds and beneficial ownership verification under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act. Cash payments and large value transactions above statutory thresholds must be declared and reported by the bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu does not impose exchange control surrender obligations. Export proceeds derived from ship bunkering and refueling may be retained in foreign currency accounts with resident banks or converted to Vatu freely. There is no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no concentration requirement for export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), in coordination with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Vanuatu has no dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing class under the VFSC regime. Offshore digital EMIs may serve as ancillary receivables / payables corridors for low-value transactions, but they cannot hold regulated treasury balances or issue a locally domiciled account. The International Company engaged in ship bunkering must maintain primary operational liquidity in a resident commercial bank to comply with AML/CFT supervision and to maintain documentary integrity for trade settlements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Vanuatu threshold; for a zero-tax IC, the effective threshold is VUV 0 because no transfer-pricing rules are enforced by the Vanuatu tax authority. Multinational groups should nevertheless maintain contemporaneous documentation equal to the Local File and Master File thresholds applicable in the foreign operating jurisdictions.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu International Companies remain offshore zero-tax vehicles, but OECD/EU-aligned substance reforms require an IC engaged in a relevant activity to demonstrate adequate economic substance. For marine bunkering and lubricant replenishment, substance is assessed through physical or contractual presence, risk management, bank accounts, contract execution, and decision-making records; the activity should not be carried on inside Vanuatu's domestic market in order to preserve the IC exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through misuse of the zero-tax IC wrapper

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and penalties under the International Companies Act; in the operating jurisdiction, reassessment, up to 100% civil penalties, interest, CFC/GAAR adjustments, and criminal tax evasion fines plus imprisonment where fraud is established.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil to hold directors personally liable for the company's unpaid foreign tax; civil restitution, asset freezes, travel bans, extradition risk, and imprisonment in the source jurisdiction.
Shell company / no economic substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC deregistration and cancellation of the certificate of incorporation; heightened adverse classification by OECD/EU; denial of zero-tax status if the entity engages in Vanuatu domestic business.

Director personal liability: Directors of a struck-off company remain personally liable for new obligations; courts may impose disqualification, personal liability for debts, and travel restrictions if the entity was used as an instrument of fraud.
AML/CFT deficiencies in ship-bunkering payments

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu's Proceeds of Crime Act and VFSC AML rules: monetary penalties, revocation of registration, confiscation of tainted property; correspondent banks may close accounts and report suspicious activity; civil asset forfeiture follows.

Director personal liability: Directors can be prosecuted for money laundering, conspiracy, and facilitation of sanctions evasion; penalties include heavy personal fines, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 20 years in serious cases.
Unlicensed or unauthorized bunkering activity

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist orders, seizure of fuel and vessels, fines for each unlicensed fuel supply operation, and prohibition orders against the company and its officers in Vanuatu or the port state.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly direct unlawful bunkering may be held personally liable for environmental damage, fuel theft, damages to the charterer, and criminal fines; personal arrest or travel bans may be imposed pending criminal proceedings.
False or fabricated transfer pricing records

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no domestic transfer-pricing penalty, but false documents filed with VFSC or foreign tax authorities trigger cancellation, foreign reassessment with 40% accuracy-related penalties, and interest; criminal false-document charges may follow.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for signing or directing false books and records; conviction can lead to imprisonment for false accounting or forgery, civil restitution to affected tax authorities, and disqualification from serving as directors.
