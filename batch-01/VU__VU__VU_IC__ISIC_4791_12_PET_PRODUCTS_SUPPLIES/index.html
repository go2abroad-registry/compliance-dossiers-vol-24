Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permitted for this industry with statutory constraints: every International Company must maintain a fixed registered office in Vanuatu, which can be provided by the licensed registered agent's physical address. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) – International Companies Registry; Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (CIRD) has only residual customs duty administration powers (no direct tax or VAT competency)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities incorporating an International Company (IC) must engage a VFSC-licensed registered agent to file the Incorporation Application under the International Companies Act, Chap. 222. No separate TIN/VAT ID is issued because Vanuatu levies no income tax, corporation tax, or consumption tax. The VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, together with the registered agent appointment, constitutes the sole statutory tax-identity instrument, supplemented by any goodwill or regulatory references from the VFSC company search upon request.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – Inward investment vehicle wholly exempt from all national taxes, capital gains taxes, and stamp duties by virtue of Part 12 of the International Companies Act, Chap. 222; no territorial or residency-based taxation trigger exists for ICs.

Available election forms

None – Vanuatu recognizes no check-the-box entity classification regime, no hybrid mismatch election, and no utilization of tax treaty attributes; the IC status is a self-executing full tax immunity, with no elective downgrade into a taxable corporate vehicle domestically.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST regime – Vanuatu operates only a limited customs import duty system under the Customs Act [Cap. 233]; exports and offshore services are completely outside the indirect tax perimeter

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – there is no indirect tax registration threshold, no fiscal numbering for VAT, and no dealer registration requirement; ICs importing physical goods into Vanuatu for local delivery would trigger standard customs duties (typically 8%–15% ad valorem depending on tariff classification, plus possible stamp duty on customs entries), but pure e-commerce export sales from Vanuatu or fulfilment outside Vanuatu incur zero indirect tax obligations

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – because no VAT/GST is levied in Vanuatu, the 0% export VAT concept does not exist; export sales of pet supplies and related goods from (or originated in) Vanuatu by an IC are wholly outside any indirect tax charge, with no requirement to substantiate a zero rate and no refund procedure to invoke.

  • Export customs declaration (if goods physically consigned from Vanuatu and subject to export documentation under the Customs Act [Cap. 233])
  • Commercial invoice with HS tariff classification and shipment terms
  • Packing list / weight certificate
  • Bill of Lading or House Air Waybill evidencing cross-border dispatch
  • Certificate of origin (only where requested by the destination jurisdiction or trade agreement; no Vanuatu zero-rating purpose)

None – Vanuatu has no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration with tax administration, or digital stamping/marking requirement for invoices; the CIRD maintains a manual customs electronic data interchange (ASYCUDA) solely for import goods declaration, not for sales invoices or VAT clearance.

No VAT clawback or retrospective export zero-rating adjustment is applicable as Vanuatu imposes no indirect tax on exports. However, the VFSC may impose administrative fines (up to VT 1,000,000), cancellation of the IC licence, and deregistration for the filing of false declarations concerning offshore status, registered office, or agent details under the International Companies Act. For customs purposes, under-declaration or misdescription of goods in an import entry triggers civil penalties up to double the duty short-paid and potential criminal forfeiture under the Customs Act. E-commerce merchants should also maintain in-country agent records and auditable transaction flow evidence to demonstrate that the IC is not engaged in prohibited local trading, which would otherwise violate the IC's exemption terms.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a fully open and unrestricted exchange-control regime; current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible and no RBV approval, exchange-control licence, or profit-repatriation permit is required for cross-border remittances. The national currency is the Vatu (VUV), which is administered on a managed-float basis against a trade-weighted currency basket. Profit and capital remittances by an International Company are unhindered by income tax, capital gains tax, or dividend withholding tax because Vanuatu imposes none of those taxes; the material constraints are therefore operational rather than regulatory, namely limited offshore VUV liquidity, increased KYC/EDD requirements imposed by understaffed compliance teams, and correspondent-bank documentation demands for USD/AUD clearing. E-commerce settlement is best conducted entirely in USD to eliminate convertibility friction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None statutorily applicable. Vanuatu imposes no surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export receipts. Exporters, including electronic retailers of pet supplies and devices, may retain 100% of foreign-currency proceeds in Foreign Currency Accounts (FCAs) at licensed commercial banks and may freely deploy those funds for imports or treasury purposes. In practice the RBV requires commercial banks to apply AML screening to inward SWIFT credits and to record purpose-of-remittance data; the recommended treasury policy for the Stage 4 mandate is to invoice in USD via Stripe/PayPal, settle net proceeds into a USD FCA, and defer any VUV or AUD conversion until discrete local expenditure is scheduled.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) under the Currency Act, the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act and the Exchange Control Act [Cap 56]; banking licensing and AML/CFT supervision are jointly administered by the RBV and the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Moderate as a settlement-enabler but low as a replacement for a Vanuatu licensed bank account. Vanuatu has no domestic Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime; foreign EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, and Stripe Treasury are not recognised deposit-takers by the RBV and cannot hold clearing accounts in the national payments system. Legal constraints require that a foreign EMI, if used, holds a valid e-money/PSP authorisation in its home jurisdiction, provides a complete transaction audit trail, and causes all merchant settlement to be swept via SWIFT to the IC's physical USD account at ANZ or BSP within strict timelines; domestic banks will refuse to perpetuate a 'nested' account where funds remain offshore beyond 60 days, citing correspondent de-risking. Stripe and PayPal are treated as unregulated collection PSPs and are permissible for front-end D2C card capture; the recommended architecture is Stripe/PayPal payout to BSP or ANZ USD FCA, then scheduled MT103 wires for supplier and logistics disbursement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: the shareholder or parent entity wires paid-up capital or an intercompany shareholder loan from its own bank account to the IC's USD settlement account at ANZ or BSP using SWIFT MT103 with OFAC-screened messaging. Vanuatu imposes no central-bank approval and no fixed purpose-code table for inbound remittances; the remittance must include a free-text reason-for-payment such as 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - IC REG NO VU_IC' or 'INTERCOMPANY LOAN - INV/LOAN NO.', and the bank's reconciliation team will match the MT103 field 70 against a board resolution and signed capital-escrow instruction letter. For operational e-commerce liquidity, Stripe/PayPal settlement arrives via international ACH/SWIFT into the same USD FCA in sweeps; all inward credits are screened by the bank on a positive-pay reconciliation basis. Third-party deposits are prohibited: the remitter must be a named shareholder or lender, and the supporting file must include the board resolution authorising the capital, the remitter's certified identity documents, and proof of source of funds for any single credit exceeding USD 100,000, which automatically triggers enhanced due diligence.Primary rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 corporate wires from the IC's USD FCA to supplier accounts in China, the United States, and the European Union, each wire carrying the invoice reference and a clear remittance instruction ('PET SUPPLY IMPORT / INVOICE NO.') to avoid trade-description flagging; (2) commercial sight letters of credit, confined to bulk periodic purchases of pet-care devices exceeding approximately USD 25,000 per shipment and issued with confirmation from a correspondent negotiating bank because the local LC book is thin; (3) host-to-host or SWIFT FileAct batched payment files for recurring logistics and fulfilment suppliers; and (4) PSP-initiated payouts (e.g., Stripe Treasury, PayPal MassPay) only as a fallback, subject to the 60-day nesting rule noted above. For local VUV disbursements, the RBV-operated Vanuatu Automated Transfer System (VATS) is the domestic clearing rail; however, the Stage 4 treasury policy is to remain fully USD-denominated and to execute any required VUV conversion at the same domestic bank with a three-day booking window to preserve margin.USD/VUV outright forward contracts (up to 12 months) executed with ANZ or BSP under a standard corporate master FX confirmation, supported by underlying trade or expense documentation for RBV audit purposes.AUD/USD and EUR/USD cross-currency swaps or rollover structures via the bank's own risk book, permitted only above a practical notional of USD 5 million and thus normally unavailable to a small e-commerce IC; natural currency matching is preferred.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on VUV are not bankable because an offshore VUV NDF market does not exist; a USD-based NDF on AUD or EUR for procurement hedging is permissible only through an FCA-protected clearing line with full disclosure to VFSC and the domestic bank.Vanilla call/put options and structured swap collars are not permissible for an unlicensed International Company without a VFSC investment-dealer or financial-licence extension; the standard prudent policy for this asset-light e-commerce model is multi-currency FCA matching (USD invoicing, USD supplier payout) with strict 10-day FX budgeting and no speculative derivative exposure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Moderate as a settlement-enabler but low as a replacement for a Vanuatu licensed bank account. Vanuatu has no domestic Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime; foreign EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, and Stripe Treasury are not recognised deposit-takers by the RBV and cannot hold clearing accounts in the national payments system. Legal constraints require that a foreign EMI, if used, holds a valid e-money/PSP authorisation in its home jurisdiction, provides a complete transaction audit trail, and causes all merchant settlement to be swept via SWIFT to the IC's physical USD account at ANZ or BSP within strict timelines; domestic banks will refuse to perpetuate a 'nested' account where funds remain offshore beyond 60 days, citing correspondent de-risking. Stripe and PayPal are treated as unregulated collection PSPs and are permissible for front-end D2C card capture; the recommended architecture is Stripe/PayPal payout to BSP or ANZ USD FCA, then scheduled MT103 wires for supplier and logistics disbursement.
