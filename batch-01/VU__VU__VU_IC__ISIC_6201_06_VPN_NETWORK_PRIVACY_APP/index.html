Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), Port Vila; official portal: https://vrca.gov.vu (VAT registration and TIN services administered under the Value Added Tax Act No. 3 of 1998 and the Tax Administration (Penalties) Act)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) obtains a statutory TIN/VAT ID by applying to the VRCA on the prescribed VAT registration form, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation under the International Companies Act Cap. 222, the registered agent details, director identity documents, and proof of the registered office in Vanuatu. VAT registration is compulsory within 21 days after the taxable turnover of the IC supplier reaches or exceeds VT 10,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is available below the threshold. The IC is assigned a 9-digit VAT registration number and, where applicable, payroll/income tax accounts are not required because the IC is tax-exempt.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned International Company (IC) obtains a statutory TIN/VAT ID by applying to the VRCA on the prescribed VAT registration form, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation under the International Companies Act Cap. 222, the registered agent details, director identity documents, and proof of the registered office in Vanuatu. VAT registration is compulsory within 21 days after the taxable turnover of the IC supplier reaches or exceeds VT 10,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is available below the threshold. The IC is assigned a 9-digit VAT registration number and, where applicable, payroll/income tax accounts are not required because the IC is tax-exempt.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections are available under Vanuatu law. The International Company enjoys a statutory 20-year exemption from all Vanuatu income, corporate, capital gains, withholding, and stamp taxes under the International Companies Act Cap. 222, renewable for further 20-year periods upon re-registration. The IC may elect to remain entirely outside the domestic VAT net by restricting all supplies to non-resident customers of offshore-network services, and no optional VAT grouping or special fiscal-regime election is provided.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (ten million vatu) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; mandatory VRCA registration within 21 days of exceeding the threshold, with voluntary registration permitted below it

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% applies under the VAT Act where (i) goods are exported by the supplier in the course or furtherance of a taxable activity, or (ii) services are supplied to a non-resident customer and are physically performed entirely outside Vanuatu. For the VPN Network Privacy App SaaS, the IC must establish that the digital service is rendered to non-resident subscribers, the servers/usage occur outside Vanuatu, and the consideration is received in foreign currency and attributable to offshore supplies; services merely consumed incidentally in Vanuatu or supplied to Vanuatu residents are excluded from zero-rating and remain taxable at 15%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (outward manifest / export entry form filed with the Vanuatu Customs Division)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export movement of any associated hardware/media
  • Commercial Invoice describing the VPN service and confirming the non-resident recipient
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/Stripe-Paymentwall settlement statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Non-resident Customer Declaration and IP/usage-location log demonstrating offshore consumption of the digital service

No real-time electronic-invoicing clearance, fiscal API, or digital stamping mandate exists in Vanuatu. Taxable suppliers may issue manual or printed VAT invoices and must retain all books, invoices, and records for a minimum of 7 years; the VRCA relies on post-audit invoice verification and bank-record cross-checks rather than transaction-level digital clearance.

Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control repatriation deadline for IC export proceeds because the IC operates wholly in foreign currency and is outside the domestic tax net; nonetheless, the VRCA may disregard zero-rating where export evidence is deficient. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims trigger reassessment at 15% VAT on the full supply value, additional tax up to 25% of the unpaid VAT for late payment, a 100% civil penalty on the tax shortfall for deliberate misdeclaration, and retrospective clawback of any input credits, plus interest accruing from the original due date. Knowingly submitting false declarations, forged export documentation, or wilfully obstructing the VRCA is a criminal offence punishable by fines up to VT 500,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years, with directors and the registered agent jointly and severally liable in the case of an International Company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

VUV is fully convertible on both current and capital account; Vanuatu has no exchange control legislation, no capital-movement approval mechanism, and no restriction on profit, dividend, or capital repatriation. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is expressly exempt from exchange control requirements and may freely transfer funds in and out of the jurisdiction. Practical remittance friction is limited to commercial AML/KYC screening by correspondents, not regulatory approval; there is no black-market premium, no surrender obligation, no approval for inward foreign direct investment, and no repatriation timeline. The de facto settlement currency for the VPN SaaS business is USD, with VUV conversion available at authorized-dealer interbank rates.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Vanuatu imposes no mandatory surrender of export or service-rendering proceeds, no holding-period requirement, and no forced conversion ratio for export receipts. The RBV prescribes no statutory timeline for disposition of foreign currency earnings; VPN SaaS export receivables may be retained indefinitely in USD or converted at the prevailing authorized-dealer rate.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; statutory monetary authority for the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV), regulator of licensed financial institutions and authorized foreign exchange dealers, and operator of the Vanuatu Clearing System.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low direct viability. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) shareholders' capital contribution resolution and board minutes authorizing the injection; (2) remitting bank executes SWIFT MT103 with purpose code referencing 'equity injection into International Company' (BPM6 category 5M2 - equity capital); (3) beneficiary line must exactly match the IC legal name and the Tier-1 Vanuatu account number; (4) reference field carries the share-allotment serial number; (5) remitter's bank must be the UBO's actual bank, with 6 months of account statements attached to the KYC file; (6) no exchange-control permit is required because Vanuatu imposes no inward-remittance approval; (7) if the remittance is characterized as shareholder loan rather than equity, a signed loan agreement, interest-rate policy, and intercompany documentation must be retained to prevent FIU mischaracterization; (8) funds settle in USD or VUV via SWIFT correspondent, typically T+1–T+2 from Australia or Singapore.Primary rail: SWIFT MT103 for operating disbursements (cloud hosting, CDN bandwidth, software licenses, legal, accounting, registered-agent fees, director costs) with field 70 (Remittance Information) containing a precise commercial invoice reference; no outward exchange-control permit required. For high-value infrastructure procurement exceeding USD 250,000, a sight or usance Commercial Letter of Credit can be arranged through ANZ or BSP, though availability is limited and typically requires 50–100% cash margin from an IC with no track record; confirm whether the beneficiary accepts documentary collections. Local VUV clearing runs through the RBV-operated Vanuatu Clearing System on a T+1 basis; cross-border clearing uses SWIFT correspondents in Australia (AUD), New Zealand (NZD), Singapore (USD), and Europe (EUR). Reconciliations are reinforced by MT940/MT942 statements; payment disputes are traced via MT195/MT199 and reversed via MT192/MT292.Outright spot and forward contracts in VUV/USD, VUV/AUD, and VUV/NZD (tenors up to 12 months) through authorized dealers (ANZ, BSP, BRED) — permissible without RBV approval because VUV is fully convertible and no corporate derivative-licensing regime appliesCross-currency swaps (USD/VUV and AUD/VUV) — legally permissible for hedging subscription revenue timing mismatches; market-making is narrow and banks quote bilaterally with credit support/collateral for tenors of 3–12 monthsMulti-currency account netting and natural hedging — matching USD receivables from Stripe/Paymentwall against USD payables to hosting/CDN providers to eliminate excessive VUV conversionPlain-vanilla interest-rate or currency swaps written with a licensed Vanuatu bank as end-user hedge — permissible provided the swap relates to genuine commercial exposure and is documented under an ISDA Credit Support AnnexNon-deliverable forward (NDF) on VUV — legally unnecessary and commercially impractical because VUV is not a restricted or inconvertible currency requiring NDF treatment; reliance on deliverable forwards and natural hedging is the preferred compliance posture

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low direct viability. Leading digital EMIs and neo-banks (Stripe Treasury, Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, Mercury) either do not support Vanuatu-incorporated entities or apply hard jurisdiction blocks due to Vanuatu's prior FATF grey-listing remediation status, the offshore IC structure, and the VPN/privacy industry's Medium-Risk-Enhanced profile. Feasible architecture: (1) open a Tier-1 Vanuatu bank account (ANZ/BSP/BRED) as the primary settlement account; (2) aggregate Stripe and Paymentwall payouts into the Vanuatu account via SWIFT MT103; (3) use the IC's VFSC-registered agent and substance documentation to satisfy any second-tier EMI application, assessing each provider's country list individually before commitment. Direct reliance on a single offshore EMI as the primary treasury channel is not legally or operationally defensible.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None prescribed. Vanuatu has not enacted transfer pricing legislation or Local File/Master File thresholds. A prudent VPN group should nonetheless maintain contemporaneous intercompany pricing documentation and a transfer pricing report if cross-border controlled transactions exceed VUV 100,000,000 to defend against foreign taxes and substance audits.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's economic substance requirements apply to International Companies carrying on relevant activities, including intellectual property holding, distribution and service centres, and financing. A VPN/network security SaaS company deriving revenue from software licensing or global subscriptions must show Vanuatu-based direction and control, staff, premises, and adequate operating expenditure; otherwise the tax-exempt status can be challenged, and the company may face annual ESR filing and penalty obligations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Sham Offshore Structure

Statutory sanction: Fraudulent use of an International Company is sanctionable under the International Companies Act and general criminal law; penalties may include strike-off, disqualification of the registered agent, a fine up to VT 10 million, and/or up to two years' imprisonment; in cross-border cases, the underlying foreign tax offense remains separately prosecutable.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where the company is a façade. Directors and beneficial owners can be ordered to make civil restitution of diverted profits, are subject to travel bans while under investigation, and may serve imprisonment for tax fraud in jurisdictions receiving Vanuatu's mutual legal assistance.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The Vanuatu ESR regime imposes specific penalties for failure to file substance declarations or for false declarations, including monetary penalties of up to VT 5 million per annum, forced dissolution of the International Company, and referral to the Financial Intelligence Unit if evasion is suspected.

Director personal liability: Directors of a shell entity are individually liable for knowing participation in false ESR filings; courts may disallow the exemption, hold directors personally liable for debts incurred with no genuine business, and ban them from acting as directors in Vanuatu for up to 10 years.
AML / Payment Processing Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Counter Financing of Terrorism Act, non-compliance with CDD, record-keeping, and suspicious transaction reporting is a criminal offence subject to fines applicable to the company and imprisonment for compliance officers; the Proceeds of Crime Act permits freezing and confiscation of assets gained from unlawful conduct.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in sanctions evasion or money laundering face personal criminal prosecution, financial penalties, travel restrictions, and confiscation of personal assets; beneficial owners may also be subjected to foreign OFAC/EU equivalent listings, triggering VFSC de-registration.
Transfer Pricing Fraud / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: No direct domestic Vanuatu transfer pricing penalty applies because there is no income tax; however, the sham transaction can trigger foreign anti-avoidance legislation, withholding tax reassessments, and severe penalties equal to 20% to 40% of adjusted profits in the counterparty jurisdiction, plus interest.

Director personal liability: Foreign revenue courts may pierce the veil of the Vanuatu IC and impose joint civil liability on directors for the hidden tax, along with personal payment orders, contempt-of-court sanctions, and the risk of international arrest where intentional fraud is established.
