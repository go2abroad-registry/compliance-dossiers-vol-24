Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), the unified tax and customs administration of Vanuatu; official digital portal administered at www.vrca.gov.vu, complemented by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for International Company incorporation.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] via a VFSC-registered agent; obtain Certificate of Incorporation and IC registration number. 2) Apply to VRCA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using Form TIN-01, supported by certified incorporation documents, registered office address, and director/officer particulars; issuance occurs within 3-5 business days and is mandatory even for tax-exempt entities. 3) Because the IC is prohibited from conducting domestic business with Vanuatu residents, it remains outside the VAT net; a separate VAT registration is only triggered if the IC pivots to permitted domestic taxable supplies. 4) Obtain the IC Tax Exemption Certificate from VFSC evidencing exemption under s.122 of the IC Act. No minimum capital, local shareholding, or physical office is required for IC status.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — International Companies are statutorily exempt from Vanuatu corporate income tax (Vanuatu currently imposes no income tax regime), capital gains tax, withholding tax, dividend tax, and exchange control; VAT applies only upon domestic taxable supplies, which an IC may not ordinarily undertake. — 1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] via a VFSC-registered agent; obtain Certificate of Incorporation and IC registration number. 2) Apply to VRCA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using Form TIN-01, supported by certified incorporation documents, registered office address, and director/officer particulars; issuance occurs within 3-5 business days and is mandatory even for tax-exempt entities. 3) Because the IC is prohibited from conducting domestic business with Vanuatu residents, it remains outside the VAT net; a separate VAT registration is only triggered if the IC pivots to permitted domestic taxable supplies. 4) Obtain the IC Tax Exemption Certificate from VFSC evidencing exemption under s.122 of the IC Act. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — International Companies are statutorily exempt from Vanuatu corporate income tax (Vanuatu currently imposes no income tax regime), capital gains tax, withholding tax, dividend tax, and exchange control; VAT applies only upon domestic taxable supplies, which an IC may not ordinarily undertake.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or transparent-entity election exists in Vanuatu. Available statutory elections are limited to: (a) voluntary VAT registration for suppliers below the VUV 5,000,000 threshold (s.6 VAT Act [Cap 248]); (b) irrevocable election to be governed as an International Company under the IC Act, securing the statutory tax exemption; and (c) optional issuance of a VFSC Tax Exemption Certificate to facilitate counterparty due diligence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover (approximately USD 42,000), mandatory registration threshold; voluntary registration permitted below the threshold but imposes full VAT compliance obligations for a minimum of 24 months.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to goods (including designer blind box toys) physically exported from Vanuatu by the supplier, provided the goods are removed within the statutory export period and the supplier holds prescribed documentary proof of export (s.8 VAT Act); services supplied to non-residents are zero-rated where the benefit is wholly received outside Vanuatu; zero-rated refund claims are processed via periodic VAT return, not separate refund filing.

  • Customs Export Declaration (VRCA Export Entry / EDIFACT electronic export lodgement)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from a Vanuatu port
  • Commercial Invoice and export sales contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank settlement confirmation evidencing foreign currency payment
  • VAT invoice marked 'Zero-Rated Export' bearing supplier TIN

No real-time fiscal clearance, government API stamping, or mandatory e-invoicing network has been implemented in Vanuatu; taxpayers must issue paper or digital VAT invoices containing prescribed particulars under s.21 of the VAT Act and retain fiscal records for 7 years; no digital fiscalisation or invoice clearance certification is currently required.

VRCA audit applies longitudinal scrutiny of export documentation-to-remittance matching; fraudulent zero-rating or refund misstatement triggers a monthly default penalty of 10% of unpaid tax (s.35 VAT Act), criminal liability with fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 10 years (s.38 VAT Act), retrospective reassessment within 5 years and unlimited limitation in fraud cases; clawback of refunded VAT plus interest applies, and destruction of export records within the mandatory 7-year retention period is independently sanctionable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu abolished exchange controls in 1988. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are legally unrestricted, and no exchange-control approval is required for inbound equity, outbound trade settlements, dividends or cross-border loan servicing. The vatu (VUV) operates under an RBV-managed float referenced to a USD/AUD basket; VUV is effectively non-deliverable offshore, but on-island conversion between VUV, USD, AUD, NZD and EUR is freely available at commercial bank rates. The Vanuatu International Company (IC) pays zero corporate income tax and zero withholding tax on distributions, so profit repatriation is tax-free. The practical residual hurdle is counterparty de-risking by foreign correspondent banks, not local statute.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None imposed. There is no statutory timeline for repatriation of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio into vatu; designers and manufacturers may retain USD/AUD export earnings in foreign-currency accounts. Banks nonetheless file international funds transfer reports with the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (VFIU) for wire transfers above the prescribed threshold (currently approximately VUV 1,000,000) and will require underlying documentation — pro-forma invoice, commercial invoice, bill of lading and customs declaration (HS code 9503.00) — to evidence genuine designer-blind-box trade flows.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — sole monetary authority, prudential banking regulator and foreign-exchange administrator; Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — regulator and corporate registry for Vanuatu International Companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium feasibility rating. Vanuatu has no dedicated domestic EMI licensing regime, so a VU International Company cannot obtain a full-service local electronic-money licence. Offshore EU/FCA-ADGM or Australian-regulated EMIs (including global FX wallet and mass-payout platforms) can be engaged for USD/AUD/HKD collections and Shopify settlement, but they routinely apply heightened due diligence to zero-tax Vanuatu ICs, require proof of substance, and face unstable correspondent access under global Pacific de-risking. The nominated Vanuatu commercial bank should therefore remain the primary liquidity spine, with EMIs used only as secondary collection rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No transfer pricing documentation threshold is prescribed under Vanuatu law because the IC is exempt from income tax. For prudent group reporting, prepare a Master File where consolidated related-party transactions exceed VUV 1.3 billion (approximately EUR 10 million) or global revenue exceeds USD 50 million.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted UK-style Economic Substance Regulations. The International Companies Act requires a licensed registered agent in Vanuatu and compliance with beneficial ownership reporting under the Companies and Trusts Service Providers (Amendment) Act 2019. A passive holding of brand IP and collection of e-commerce revenue for designer blind box toys is not subject to Vanuatu economic substance tests.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Sham Offshore Structure

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu: derecognition of the IC for false representations. The beneficial owner's home tax authority may re-attribute all profits to the resident individual or parent company, imposing tax penalties of 50–100% of the unpaid tax plus interest at the prescribed statutory rate.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where the IC is used as a sham. Directors become jointly and severally liable for the full tax debt, are subject to travel bans preventing departure, and face imprisonment of up to 5 years for fraudulent trading.
Shell Company with No Commercial Substance

Statutory sanction: Under foreign general anti-avoidance and CFC rules, the shell IC may be disregarded and all profits attributed to the parent or principal. Vanuatu does not assess substance penalties, but the VFSC may require the registered agent to produce records and may strike off the company for supplying inaccurate information.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil claims for breach of fiduciary duty, quantification of unreported taxes from the deemed taxable presence, personal bankruptcy orders, and, where false accounting is knowingly certified, criminal imprisonment of up to 5 years under applicable company offences legislation.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Filings

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu AML/CFT legislation, failure to provide accurate beneficial ownership information attracts fines of up to VT 5,000,000 and removal of the registered agent; intermediaries may terminate banking relationships if the IC cannot produce its IC registration number or equivalent tax identifier.

Director personal liability: Registered directors, including nominees, are personally liable to fines of up to VT 5 million and imprisonment of up to 5 years for knowingly supplying false statements; travel restrictions may be imposed pending investigation.
Customs and Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu Customs and Excise Act penalties include seizure of the goods, fines of up to three times the duty evaded, and revocation of the customs broker license. The foreign importing jurisdiction may also impose customs penalties of up to 200% of the goods' value.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false import/export declarations become personally liable to customs recovery orders, monetary penalties, civil fraud damages, and imprisonment of up to 5 years for fraudulent use of invoices.
