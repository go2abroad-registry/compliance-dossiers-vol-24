Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED that the subscribers' allotment of shares be confirmed and the subscribed shares be recorded; that the registered office and registered agent proposed in the application be approved and adopted; that the persons named as director(s), the corporate secretary and the resident agent be appointed; that the execution of banking, freight-forwarding and project-logistics agreements be authorized; that the seal and electronic signature protocols be adopted; that the binder of statutory registers be kept at the registered office; and that the directors instruct the registered agent to file all necessary declarations with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial licensing authority applies to an offshore International Company in Vanuatu; the company is prohibited from conducting domestic physical business in Vanuatu and therefore is not eligible for an inward industrial or manufacturing licence.

Category: Not applicable — International Companies operate purely offshore; any heavy-haul or project-cargo physical operations must be performed through foreign counterparties, special-purpose vehicles, or in the destination jurisdiction under its applicable transport licensing regime.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue (VCIR), Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila (official portal: www.customs.gov.vu); corporate registry and International Company supervision: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), Port Vila (www.vfsc.vu).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) via a licensed VFSC registered agent (memorandum/articles, registered office and registered agent in Port Vila, directors and shareholders registers). Step 2: Obtain a VFSC Certificate of Good Standing; no income tax registration is required because Vanuatu levies no income tax on ICs. Step 3: If the IC imports OOG equipment or clears export zero-rated supplies through Vanuatu ports, obtain a Customs Client/Importer Code in ASYCUDA World by filing the Customs Client Registration Form with the incorporation certificate, VFSC good-standing certificate, passport copies of directors, agent confirmation and bank reference. Step 4: Register for VAT with the Commissioner (form R1) only if taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceed the VUV 5,000,000 threshold, or voluntarily to recover input VAT; the Commissioner issues an 8-digit VAT/TIN number and VAT registration certificate. Step 5: Maintain the annual VFSC return and entity fee; no corporate tax return is filed because no income tax is in force.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the IC is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes on income, assets, distributions, capital gains and stamp duty (International Companies Act (Cap 222) s.130 exemption, routinely evidenced by a Minister-issued exemption certificate covering the statutory exemption period, commonly marketed as a 20-year guarantee); Vanuatu currently imposes no corporate income tax, no withholding tax, no capital gains tax and no payroll tax on any company; the draft 2023 proposals (10% CIT on turnover above VUV 15 million and a 2% service charge) were deferred by the Council of Ministers in February 2024 and are not in force; VAT (12.5%), customs duty and municipal licence fees continue to apply to onshore imports and consumption, but an IC is prohibited from domestic trading in Vanuatu, so its OOG project cargo operations are offshore/export-scope only.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or elective entity classification exists under Vanuatu law; the IC tax-exempt status is automatic and non-elective. Available elections: (i) voluntary VAT registration to recover input VAT attributable to zero-rated export services; (ii) election between invoice-basis and payment-basis VAT accounting with the Commissioner's consent; (iii) election to file customs entries through an in-house ASYCUDA user account or a licensed customs broker; (iv) temporary-import bond election for foreign heavy-lift equipment with re-export within the statutory bond period under the Customs Act; and (v) application for the IC tax exemption certificate for production to banks, L/C issuing banks and charterers.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 (five million vatu) of annual taxable turnover in any 12-month period; registration is mandatory once the threshold is met or is reasonably expected to be met; a non-resident IC whose Vanuatu supplies are exclusively zero-rated is not obliged to register but may do so voluntarily; importers must hold a Customs Client Code and pay import VAT at the border at the standard 12.5% rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: a supply of export goods (including OOG modules, cranes and heavy machinery) is zero-rated under s.19 of the VAT Act [Cap 241] where the IC exports the goods to a place outside Vanuatu, the export entry is lodged in ASYCUDA World, and the goods are removed from Vanuatu within the statutory period of 28 days from the time of supply (or such longer period as the Commissioner approves for project logistics). Services: a supply of services is zero-rated where supplied to a non-resident foreign principal and (a) the services are physically performed or benefitted outside Vanuatu, or (b) the services are ancillary to exported goods, including ocean and air international freight, port stevedoring, project cargo forwarding, chartering, survey and pre-shipment inspection of export cargo. The IC must obtain and retain the export evidence before claiming the 0% rate; input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports is refundable through the periodic VAT return (input-credit/refund mechanism).

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World SAD Form) validated and cleared for the OOG heavy-lift consignment
  • Bill of Lading (ocean liner or charter-party bill for out-of-gauge project cargo) or Air Waybill naming the foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT MT103 or Letter of Credit negotiation advice proving receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice (VAT 0% basis), packing list and OOG cargo survey report with dimensions, gross weight and stowage certificate
  • Insurance certificate (marine cargo/liability) for the international transit; freight contract, charter party or booking note linking the supply to the foreign principal

No real-time fiscal clearance, no mandatory government-authorisation e-invoicing API, and no digital stamping regime exists in Vanuatu as of the reporting date. VAT tax invoices must be issued within 28 days of the time of supply and contain the 14 statutory particulars (supplier/recipient names and TIN, description, quantity, unit price, VAT amount, zero-rating citation). All export and import declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World (UNCTAD EDI) and must be customs-cleared before goods are laden or released; OOG heavy-lift consignments require pre-lodgement of the export manifest, stowage plan, cargo survey certificate, and overweight/over-dimensional verification at Port Vila or Luganville port.

The Commissioner may disallow the 0% rate and claw back refunded or credited VAT plus interest at 20% per annum where the export documentation bundle is incomplete, inconsistent or untimely; penalty tax of up to 150% of the VAT evaded applies for negligence or fraud (VAT Act [Cap 241]), and officers may face criminal prosecution (fine and/or up to 2 years imprisonment) for fraudulent zero-rating declarations; the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit can impose freezing, disgorgement or anti-money-laundering penalties for false trade invoicing; reassessment is open for 5 years from the end of the tax period and is unlimited in cases of forgery or fraud. Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control repatriation rule, so the 180-day collection milestone follows trade-finance practice, Incoterms and L/C tenors; overdue export proceeds may trigger substance and commercial-reality audits by the Commissioner and VFSC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal exchange control regime. The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible on both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange control regulations applicable to non-resident-owned International Companies (ICs), and no restrictions on inward capital injections, dividend distributions, or profit remittance to foreign shareholders. Profit remittance may be effected directly through the commercial banking system without prior central bank approval, subject only to standard bank due diligence and tax clearance confirmation (tax-exempt IC status).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender requirement for export proceeds. Export receipts may be retained freely in VUV or any foreign currency, credited directly to the exporter's operational account without any mandatory conversion ratio or specified repatriation timeline. The only compliance expectation is that receipts pass through the formal banking sector for AML/CFT traceability.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs and virtual account providers are not licensed depository institutions in Vanuatu and cannot serve as primary treasury rails for project cargo settlement. They may be used only as supplementary collection gateways where the underlying receivable is pre-funded or where the EMI maintains a correspondent account with a licensed Vanuatu bank. The legal constraint is that no entity may hold client funds or perform money transmission without a banking licence or a valid Electronic Money Institution authorisation under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act, which is not available to purely offshore fintech providers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injection should be routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 ADV from the parent group treasury bank to the Vanuatu IC's operational VUV or USD account held at a licensed reserve bank-cleared commercial bank. The wire must use purpose code 'G' (general business / capital contribution) or an explicit narrative stating 'IC Shareholder Capital Injection – ISIC 5229 Project Cargo Services' to avoid exchange-control misclassification. For LC-backed capital injections, the LC issuing bank should advise the Vanuatu beneficiary bank on MT710/MT720 with a clear LC reference; the destination account must match the registered beneficiary name exactly. The receiving bank will obtain a statement of purpose and may require the shareholder resolution approving the capital contribution to be furnished before credit is irrevocably released.Outbound trade settlements for subcontractor heavy-haul logistics, ocean freight, crane rental, port handling, and OOG transport services should be executed through the domestic clearing rail of SWIFT MT103 direct transfers from the Vanuatu IC's operational account to the counterparty's beneficiary bank. For larger milestone settlements, a commercial Letter of Credit (MT700 issued by a first-class international bank; MT710/MT720 for assignment) is the preferred rail. The commercial LC route must name the Vanuatu logistics IC as beneficiary and require presentation of: full set clean on-board ocean bill of lading, consular or transhipment certificate, packing list, weight certificate, and the project milestone completion certificate. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None (VUV 0): Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and no statutory transfer pricing documentation regime. A VU IC is not required to prepare OECD-style Local File, Master File, or country-by-country report in Vanuatu. Foreign-parent compliance obligations are governed solely by the foreign jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has adopted Economic Substance Regulations aligned with OECD standards. A VU IC carrying on a 'relevant activity' must demonstrate economic substance in Vanuatu, including a physical office, employees, and core income-generating activities locally, and must file an annual ESR return. The out-of-gauge heavy project cargo logistics business may be treated as a distribution and service centre or may otherwise be tested for a relevant activity. A passive holding company or low-risk logistics vehicle must still avoid functioning as a shell; substantive activities conducted outside Vanuatu will not satisfy the ESR test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / abuse of zero-tax status

Statutory sanction: False declarations and deceptive filings under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] attract VFSC administrative penalties, potential strike-off, retroactive loss of exemption where relevant, and referral to Vanuatu prosecution authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable where the corporate veil is pierced for fraud or sham. Courts can order civil restitution of diverted profits, impose asset seizure and travel bans, and directors may face imprisonment in associated fraud or money laundering prosecutions.
Shell company / absence of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Sanctions under Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations and VFSC enforcement practice include administrative financial penalties, enforced strike-off, disqualification of the registered agent, and adverse exchange-of-information notifications to treaty partners.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent who knowingly permit the shell structure may be disqualified from managing VFSC-regulated entities, ordered to pay restoration costs and penalties, and face civil liability for losses sustained by counterparties or foreign tax authorities.
AML / trade-based money laundering

Statutory sanction: Violations of the Financial Transactions Reporting Act and VFSC AML Rules carry significant fines, asset forfeiture, and imprisonment of up to 12 years for substantive money laundering; the VU IC may also be struck off.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the money-laundering reporting officer can be criminally liable for knowing involvement or negligent failure to report suspicious transactions. Remedies include confiscation of proceeds, travel restrictions, and custodial sentences.
Unlicensed financial or regulated activity

Statutory sanction: Carrying on regulated financial business without a licence breaches the Financial Services Commission Act and the International Companies Act; the VFSC can issue cease-and-desist orders, impose monetary penalties, wind up the company, and refer the matter for prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal civil liability to customers and counterparties, disqualification from managing VFSC-licensed entities, and criminal sanctions including fines and imprisonment for knowingly conducting unlicensed financial business.
