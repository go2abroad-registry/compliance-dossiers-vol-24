Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None available. Vanuatu has no income-tax check-the-box or entity-classification election regime. An International Company automatically holds a perpetual tax exemption under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; the only statutory election of relevance is the election to convert to a local company under the Companies Act, which would extinguish the exemption. No hybrid mismatch elections exist as Vanuatu imposes no income or withholding tax on ICs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 3,000,000 (three million Vanuatu Vatu) of aggregate taxable supplies in a 12-month period; voluntary VAT registration is permitted below this threshold, and zero-rated export supplies count toward the threshold for registration but are not subject to VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply qualifies for zero rated VAT where (i) goods are physically exported to a destination outside Vanuatu, including the supply of aircraft stores/in-flight meals delivered to an international airline for consumption on an international flight; (ii) services are supplied to a non-resident airline or in connection with international carriage; and (iii) the supplier retains contemporaneous documentary proof of export and foreign payment. The bonded in-flight catering kitchen should treat direct ramp deliveries as exports only when the meals are loaded on to a departing international aircraft and covered by a customs export endorsement.

  • Customs export declaration (Vanuatu Customs export endorsement for bonded catering stores)
  • Air waybill / bill of lading evidencing the international carriage of the in-flight catering load
  • Inward remittance certificate / foreign currency bank credit advice evidencing payment received from the overseas airline

No national real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate currently applies in Vanuatu. VAT-registered businesses must issue a compliant VAT invoice (prescribed minimum invoice content under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 251]) within 28 days of supply. For bonded/export supplies, invoices must be cross-referenced with customs export declarations and maintained for a minimum of 7 years for VCIR audit.

The VCIR can reassess any claimed zero-rated supply at the standard 12.5% VAT if the exporter cannot substantiate the physical export or the receipt of proceeds within 180 days. Late payment or misdeclaration penalties run at up to 25% of the net tax due plus 1.5% monthly interest; fraudulent zero-rating claims are criminal offences under the VAT Act and Customs Act, punishable by fine and imprisonment (maximum on indictment up to VT 5,000,000 and/or 5 years). Retrospective clawback extends to all VAT, penalties, interest, and audit costs.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is fully convertible for current international payments under Vanuatu's IMF Article VIII status; capital transfers and profit repatriation by International Companies are unrestricted, except for bank-level source-of-funds verification. Foreign-currency account retention is permitted, and there is no compulsory conversion of export receipts into VUV. Remittance of dividends, royalties, and management fees to non-residents may proceed via normal commercial wire, accompanied by board resolutions and audited financial statements where required by the receiving bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender ratio or mandatory conversion timeline applies to export receipts for International Companies in Vanuatu. However, export/delivery proceeds from inflight-catering sales must be banked within the contractual settlement period, ordinarily not exceeding 120 days, with supporting sales invoices, airway bills, proof of delivery, and airline statements retained for VFSC inspection and bank transaction-monitoring review.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) – the statutory monetary and banking regulator under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 283]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) supervises financial service providers and the International Company registry. For Vanuatu International Companies, no exchange-control approval is required for operational transfers, but banks must apply RBV prudential guidelines and Vanuatu AML/CFT obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low. Offshore digital EMIs have no Vanuatu banking licence and cannot be used for primary clearing or settlement of VUV or foreign exchange in Vanuatu. They may only serve as supplementary invoicing/collection gateways if the EMI is already engaged by the airline payer and the Vanuatu IC maintains a dominant domestic banking relationship; no VFSC/RBV regulatory permission is granted for an unlicensed EMI to act as a depository. Any locally domiciled EMI must obtain a Category 3 Financial Services licence under the Vanuatu Financial Services Dealers Act and comply with RBV licensee obligations.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0%: Vanuatu levies no domestic corporate income tax on International Companies; the IC is a tax-exempt offshore entity subject to fixed VFSC annual fees, VAT on local consumption where legally triggered, customs duties on imported catering inputs, and annual business licence fees.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%: Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, no advance corporate tax, and no distribution tax on dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the anniversary date of incorporation; the annual government fee is payable within 30 days of the anniversary and the return must be certified by the registered agent on the same date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold exists in Vanuatu law; best-practice threshold: prepare a full Local File and group Master File when related-party in-flight catering turnover exceeds VUV 200,000,000 (approximately USD 1,650,000) per annum, and maintain a simplified economic-substance file at all turnover levels.

Economic substance scope: Applicable: the Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations 2022 (as amended) require every International Company, including tax-exempt ones, to satisfy an economic substance test for 'relevant activities'. An airline in-flight catering kitchen selling under a captive airline group contract is captured under the 'distribution and service centre' head if the services are provided to a related party in another jurisdiction, and under general rules if the kitchen performs intra-group procurement, processing and logistics. The IC must demonstrate in its annual ESR return to the VFSC: (a) the catering business is directed and managed in Vanuatu through local board meetings and documented management decisions; (b) a qualified full-time workforce physically operates the kitchen, including chefs, HACCP officers, quality controllers and ramp logistics staff; (c) the kitchen owns or leases its premises, cooking equipment and delivery vehicles in Vanuatu; (d) monthly operating expenditure in Vanuatu is proportionate to the group service fee; and (e) core income-generating activities, namely menu design, procurement, food production, quality control and direct ramp delivery, occur in Vanuatu. Failure to file the ESR return or failure to satisfy the substance test results in removal of the tax-exempt certificate, a monetary penalty, and possible strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell company / tax evasion abuse

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under the International Companies Act 2022, withdrawal of the tax exemption certificate, revocation of the catering and bonded-warehouse licences, and reassessment of all concealed VAT and customs duties with a penalty equal to 300% of the tax foregone.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution to the VFSC and the VRC; the VFSC may impose travel bans and asset-freezing orders; continuing to trade after strike-off notice is a criminal offence punishable by imprisonment of up to 12 months.
Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Fixed administrative penalty of up to VUV 1,000,000 for ESR non-compliance plus a daily default penalty of VUV 50,000; the VFSC may strike off the IC and exchange information with the airline group's home tax authority, triggering a CFC review abroad.

Director personal liability: Each director and the registered agent bear personal liability for the penalties; directors may be disqualified from holding office in any Vanuatu company for up to 5 years; intentional misdeclaration is an offence punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years and a personal fine of VUV 2,000,000.
Customs / bonded warehouse diversion

Statutory sanction: Seizure and forfeiture of the catering inventory and cooking equipment, an import-duty penalty of three times the duty evaded, cancellation of the customs bond, and a 5-year blacklisting of the operator from bonded catering licences.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the unpaid duty and forfeited assets; criminal conviction for smuggling carries a fine of up to VUV 5,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 10 years; travel bans and pre-trial asset-protection orders may be issued.
Anti-money laundering / beneficial ownership transparency (AML/CFT)

Statutory sanction: The IC is a reporting institution under Vanuatu's AML/CFT framework and may be fined up to VUV 10,000,000 for a serious breach; the registered agent's licence is suspended; the Financial Intelligence Unit may obtain a provisional freezing order over all catering receivables and bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face criminal liability: imprisonment of up to 10 years for money laundering, a personal fine of up to VUV 5,000,000, disqualification from directorships, and joint liability for the airline group's foreign tax penalties where the concealment causes a misdeclaration in the shareholder's home jurisdiction.
