Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

There is no statutory minimum charter capital for an ordinary limited liability company under the Law on Enterprises 2020. However, for the licensed cross-border remittance activity, Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP requires a minimum licensed chartered capital of VND 1,000 billion for cross-border remittance service providers, and Decree No. 80/2016/ND-CP requires a minimum legal capital of VND 50 billion for payment intermediary service providers. Charter capital must be fully paid within 90 days after issuance of the Certificate of Enterprise Registration, with funds held in a blocked capital contribution account at a licensed commercial bank in Viet Nam and verified by an independent audit before SBV licensing.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certification issued by the Fire Protection and Rescue Police for the registered office and data-processing premises; civil defense coordination approval confirming the building siting, means of escape, automatic fire suppression, and emergency access routes; structural load clearance from a licensed inspection entity confirming floor loading capacity for IT racks and safes; workplace safety and occupational health clearance under the Law on Occupational Safety 2015 including annual health checks for employees and safety training records; physical security and access-control clearance under SBV Circular No. 09/2020/TT-NHNN to protect customer data, cash-handling areas, and payment system infrastructure.

Paid-in capital rule: There is no statutory minimum charter capital for an ordinary limited liability company under the Law on Enterprises 2020. However, for the licensed cross-border remittance activity, Decree No. 70/2014/ND-CP requires a minimum licensed chartered capital of VND 1,000 billion for cross-border remittance service providers, and Decree No. 80/2016/ND-CP requires a minimum legal capital of VND 50 billion for payment intermediary service providers. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation of Viet Nam (Tổng cục Thuế), an agency of the Ministry of Finance; primary electronic portal: https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn; e-invoice clearance portal: https://hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn; National Business Registration Portal: https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn.

E-government filing portal

Viet Nam uses a single Tax Identification Number (Mã số thuế, MST) for all taxes, including corporate income tax (CIT), VAT, fringe benefit/PIT withholding and license fees. For a foreign-invested limited liability company, procurement is automatic: (1) the Ministry of Planning and Investment's provincial Department of Planning and Investment issues the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and the Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC); (2) the DPI synchronizes the incorporation record with the General Department of Taxation through the National Business Registration Information System, and the tax authority issues the 13-digit MST for the LLC without a separate application; (3) within the statutory calibration period the LLC must complete supplementary tax registration through the Electronic Tax Portal using Form 01-ĐK-TCT (Circular No. 105/2020/TT-BTC), designating the competent local/sub-tax office, legal representative, branches, bank accounts and the accounting regime; (4) the LLC must register its electronic invoice scheme and obtain the digital signature (chữ ký số) before issuing e-invoices. Non-resident foreign entities without a PE in Viet Nam that supply services to the LLC do not need an MST: the Vietnamese payer must register, declare and withhold Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT, comprising CIT and VAT) on each payment and remit it through the payer's own MST under Decree No. 126/2020/ND-CP. The reverse direction is equally relevant: when the LLC receives fee income from foreign remittance partners for payout/cash pick-up services performed in Viet Nam, that income is taxable in Viet Nam in the LLC's own MST because the LLC is a domestic resident enterprise; no FCT applies to that inward stream.

Territorial scope: residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed only on Viet Nam-source income. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the LLC is a Vietnamese resident corporate taxpayer taxed at entity level on worldwide income under corporate income tax at a standard headline rate of 20%; there is no pass-through/fiscally transparent status for a Vietnamese LLC, and foreign shareholders are not taxed by reference to the LLC's income until repatriation events. Territorial scope: residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed only on Viet Nam-source income. Tax-exempt-zone/special investment regimes (e.g., export processing enterprises, high-tech parks, special economic zones) are available only upon separate certification and licensing; a licensed remittance LLC operating cross-border payment rails will ordinarily remain outside such preferential zones and is classified as an ordinary taxable resident enterprise.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not have a US-style 'check-the-box' election, and its anti-hybrid rules (contained in Decree No. 132/2020/ND-CP on transfer pricing and controlled transactions) provide no elective hybrid-mismatch regime. The elections lawfully available to the LLC are: (i) VAT calculation method: the deduction/credit method is compulsory for taxpayers with annual turnover of VND 1 billion or more; smaller taxpayers may elect between the direct method and the deduction method; (ii) fiscal year: election of any 12-month accounting year differing from the calendar year, provided the tax declarations and financial statements align to that elected year and the tax administration is notified; (iii) investment-based CIT incentive elections: eligible new investment projects (high-technology, supporting industry, industrial park/economic zone projects) may elect the preferential CIT rate and tax holiday regime under the Law on CIT and implementing regulations, with the incentive registered/harmonized through the annual tax settlement; (iv) FCT settlement: a foreign contractor may elect to be placed on the 'declaration, assessment and payment' track rather than pure withholding only if it maintains a PE and a VAT-deduction status in Viet Nam; a remittance LLC as payer should document the non-resident status of each offshore counterparty accordingly. No consolidated group or fiscal unity election exists in Viet Nam; each licensed legal entity files standalone CIT and VAT returns.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Viet Nam accepted IMF Article VIII obligations in 2017, underpinning current-account convertibility for trade, services and income, yet the VND remains managed and the capital account is administered. Inward direct investment, shareholder loans, intercompany liquidity and profit repatriation are permitted only through a Foreign Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) opened at an SBV-licensed bank and registered with the SBV; remittance-service fees from non-resident senders qualify as current-account service receipts settleable through standard banking channels. Profit remittance requires (i) audited annual financial statements, (ii) Corporate Income Tax finalization, (iii) updated Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC), and (iv) DICA registration, and must be executed within the permitted annual cycle; documentary delays, tax-clearance prerequisites and administrative discretion create residual repatriation hurdles, but no systemic convertibility embargo exists for lawful current-account flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters, Decree 70/2014/ND-CP and Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN impose a statutory duty to credit export proceeds to a foreign-currency account at an authorized bank and to surrender 80 percent of export receipts to a licensed credit institution within 30 calendar days of receipt; 100 percent foreign-invested export processing enterprises and entities granted SBV exemption rulings are outside this mandatory surrender ratio. This VN_LLC money-transmission company operates a service-receipts model rather than an exporter, so the 80 percent surrender rule does not govern its inbound remittance floats; however, any ancillary foreign-currency receipts must either be converted to VND through authorized banks or held in an approved foreign-currency account, and the company must make timely inward lodging to avoid deemed-noncompliance penalties.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (SBV / Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam), acting through its Foreign Exchange Management Department and Banking Supervision Agency; complementary authority over foreign direct investment licensing and trade-linked foreign exchange resides with the Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI) and the General Department of Vietnam Customs.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low direct feasibility. An offshore digital EMI cannot hold VND clearing accounts, issue payment instruments or operate a payout network in Viet Nam without an SBV-issued intermediary payment service license under Decree 101/2012/ND-CP (as amended by Decree 80/2016/ND-CP), which requires a locally incorporated entity, a statutory minimum charter capital (approximately VND 50 billion for intermediary payment services), proven technical infrastructure, board and UBO fitness, and a demonstrated AML/CFT program; such licenses are rarely granted absent a strategic local banking partner. The viable architecture is a partnership or agency model: the offshore EMI originates remittance instructions and settlement outside Viet Nam, while a licensed Viet Nam credit institution or licensed payment intermediary performs VND payout, cash-pickup clearing and domestic compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inward equity and shareholder-loan capital must be wired by SWIFT MT103/202 to the DICA maintained at an SBV-licensed bank, referencing the IRC/ERC number and the inward-direct-investment purpose code under Circular 15/2015/TT-NHNN; the bank registers the DICA with the SBV Foreign Exchange Management Department before first utilisation, and each capital draw is reconciled to the charter-capital schedule and subsequent capital-contribution reports to MPI. Remittance operating float from overseas money-transmitters enters a separate operational service-revenue foreign-currency account coded for current-account service receipts; the company then converts to VND at authorized-bank rates and posts VND collateral or prefunding into the payout bank's designated agent-clearing account, with daily reconciliation against beneficiary payout files.Operational and trade disbursements use (i) SWIFT MT103 for overseas partner settlement, agent commissions, technology and licensing fees; (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700/MT707) for documentary trade in corporate treasury operations; and (iii) domestic rails, namely NAPAS ACH and Quick Pay, together with the SBV Interbank Electronic Payment System (IBPS), for VND payouts to beneficiaries, cash-pickup agent prefunding and interbank treasury settlement. Cross-currency disbursements must adhere to the authorized-bank window and retain underlying contracts for the FX-need assessment.Deliverable FX forwards against VND for genuine current-account exposures, maturing within 365 days, executed with an SBV-licensed credit institution.FX swaps (VND against USD, EUR and other freely convertible currencies) with a licensed counterparty, tenor within the SBV-authorized maximum, on documented commercial-need substantiation.Cross-currency swaps for matched mismatches between USD-denominated remittance inflows and VND payout obligations, subject to SBV derivative-framework eligibility.European-style FX options purchased from an authorized bank for transaction hedging, where permitted by SBV regulations, with no speculative net position.Onshore NDFs and offshore NDF-indexed hedging are not permissible for residents absent explicit SBV approval; hedging is therefore confined to deliverable onshore instruments.
