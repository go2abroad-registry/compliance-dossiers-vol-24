Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Business Registration Office, Department of Planning and Investment (DPI) of the province/city where the head office is located; given the cross-border character, the Ho Chi Minh City DPI or Hanoi DPI will be the likely registry; online filing via the National Business Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) administered by the Agency for Business Registration of the Ministry of Planning and Investment.

Governing statute

Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 (effective 1 January 2021): Articles 43–57 (limited liability company rules), Article 26 (enterprise registration processing), Articles 74–77 (charter capital and contribution); Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14: Articles 37–42 (investment registration for foreign investors); Decree No. 01/2021/ND-CP on Enterprise Registration; Circular No. 01/2021/TT-BKHDT (forms and fees).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Enterprise Registration (Form I-1) and Application for Investment Registration (Form I-1/A) as prescribed by Circular No. 01/2021/TT-BKHDT and Circular No. 03/2021/TT-BKHDT respectively, filed through the National Business Registration Portal.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 40 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital applies to a government-relations advisory LLC (ISIC 7020) under Article 74 of Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14; the registered charter capital must be rationally commensurate with the scale and objectives of the business, must be fully contributed in cash within 90 days from the issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate, and must be remitted from the foreign investor's account to the company's corporate bank account in Viet Nam; no escrow account is required, the activity being a wholly unrestricted service sector open to 100% foreign ownership.

Funding & unlock path: Fire prevention and fighting certificate (approval/acceptance) issued under Decree No. 136/2020/ND-CP for the head office, with annual maintenance and re-validation; structural load and occupancy safety confirmed by the building owner's certificate; occupational safety and hygiene clearance under Law No. 84/2015/QH13 including employee health checks, hazard identification, workplace risk assessment, safety signage and designation of a workplace safety officer; civil defense coordination through the local ward-level police for the building evacuation plan.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital applies to a government-relations advisory LLC (ISIC 7020) under Article 74 of Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14; the registered charter capital must be rationally commensurate with the scale and objectives of the business, must be fully contributed in cash within 90 days from the issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate, and must be remitted from the foreign investor's account to the company's corporate bank account in Viet Nam; no escrow account is required, the activity being a wholly unrestricted service sector open to 100% foreign ownership.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế), Ministry of Finance, Socialist Republic of Viet Nam; official digital portals: www.gdt.gov.vn, thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-tax filing), hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn (e-invoice clearance), dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn (National Business Registration Portal, administered jointly with the Department of Planning and Investment).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested LLC obtains a single enterprise tax identification number (Mã số thuế - MST) automatically upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate (Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký doanh nghiệp) through the National Business Registration Portal (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn). This MST functions as the unified TIN and VAT ID for all tax administrations. Required follow-up steps: (1) file a statutory tax registration dossier (Form 01-ĐK-TCT) with the local Tax Department (Cục Thuế) within 10 working days after the ERC date; (2) activate the e-tax taxpayer account at thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn and register all transactional bank accounts with the tax authority within 15 days of account opening; (3) register an electronic-invoice principal mailbox at hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn and either self-generate invoice codes or be assigned GDT invoice codes for each issued e-invoice; (4) submit the official-seal registration and digital signature certification to the tax office; (5) finalize the LLC's tax registration dossier within 10 working days of any subsequent amendment. No separate VAT certificate is issued; the MST and Enterprise Registration Certificate are conclusive evidence of tax registration for a resident LLC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope - A VN-incorporated LLC is a resident corporate taxpayer subject to enterprise income tax (CIT) at the standard 20% rate on worldwide income; no pass-through or transparent fiscal treatment exists under the Enterprise Law No. 59/2020/QH14 and the Tax Administration Law No. 38/2019/QH14. Non-resident shareholders' Vietnam-source income is separately taxed through the Foreign Contractor Tax withholding mechanism where applicable. The LLC's status cannot be altered to a partnership or disregarded entity. Certain investment-incentive projects may enjoy CIT exemption/reduction as an entity-level benefit, but the fundamental entity-level taxation model is immutable.

Available election forms

Viet Nam does not provide a U.S.-style 'check-the-box' election, hybrid-mismatch election, or transparent-entity election for an LLC whose legal form is fixed by the Enterprise Law. Available elections and electable regimes include: (A) segment-by-segment CIT incentive elections (e.g., high-tech, supporting-industry, or special-zone projects) including exemption-and-reduction schedules to be claimed on the final CIT annual return; (B) elective quarterly VAT declaration (instead of monthly) where the prior-year revenue does not exceed VND 50 billion, under Article 9 of Circular 219/2013/TT-BTC as amended; (C) election of a 365-day fiscal year differing from the calendar year, with prior approval; (D) election of the Advance Pricing Agreement route under Decree 132/2020/ND-CP for related-party transactions; and (E) voluntary application of the GDT's pre-clearance e-invoice scheme for low-risk taxpayers. All elections are procedural or incentive-based; they do not alter entity-level tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For resident LLCs there is no economic turnover threshold: VAT registration is mandatory at incorporation and effective immediately upon issuance of the Enterprise Registration Certificate. For unincorporated household businesses, the statutory registration threshold is VND 100,000,000 (one hundred million) annual revenue. Non-resident digital suppliers without a Vietnamese legal presence must register for VAT through the GDT foreign-supplier portal (etaxvn.gdt.gov.vn) once they derive Vietnam-source revenue, without a statutory turnover threshold under Decree No. 85/2021/ND-CP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate is available strictly under Article 9 of Circular No. 219/2013/TT-BTC for (i) exported tangible goods and goods sold to non-tariff-zone traders/export-processing enterprises, and (ii) services supplied to foreign organizations or individuals which are consumed outside Viet Nam. For a Government-affairs-lobbying and trade-policy advisory LLC (ISIC 7020 / NAICS 541820), zero-rating requires a contractual supply to a non-resident principal with documentary proof that the service is performed and conceived for use exclusively offshore. Advisory output, policy analysis, or advocacy that is delivered, used, or benefited by persons within Viet Nam, including lobbying of Vietnamese authorities on behalf of a foreign principal, is not zero-rated and bears the 10% standard VAT. The taxpayer bears the full burden of producing an 'export service dossier' proving the non-resident counterparty, the offshore place of consumption, and the actual receipt of consideration in foreign currency.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronically lodged on VNACCS/VCIS and validated by Customs) - mandatory for goods exports
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / international carriage document evidencing physical departure of goods from Viet Nam
  • Export sales contract between the LLC and the foreign buyer, including payment terms and Incoterms (for services: contract with the non-resident principal stipulating offshore consumption)
  • Commercial invoice together with the GDT-coded e-invoice expressly stating the 0% VAT rate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Giấy báo Có / Swift / NH Form) issued by the Vietnamese bank or foreign correspondent bank confirming receipt of the export proceeds from abroad
  • For advisory services: statement/certificate from the foreign recipient confirming exclusive consumption outside Viet Nam, supported by underlying work product and performance records

Mandatory electronic invoicing under Decree No. 123/2020/ND-CP and Circular No. 78/2021/TT-BTC, fully effective from 1 July 2022: every invoice must be issued as an e-invoice, digitally signed and transmitted to the GDT e-invoice portal (hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn) in real time upon issuance. For the default regime, each e-invoice must carry a unique GDT-generated tax-authority code issued through the central vAT-einvoice clearing system; qualifying low-risk taxpayers may self-generate invoice codes but remain obligated to transmit 100% of invoice XML data to the GDT immediately after issuance. Fiscal 'stamping' is replaced by the tax-authority invoice code and the digital signature of the seller; no physical fiscal stamp is affixed. Customs export declarations must be processed through the VNACCS/VCIS national single-window system. Retail-cash-register-linked e-invoicing may apply to certain high-risk B2C activities, but for advisory services zero-rating claims, the governing obligation is full electronic traceability of 0% invoices in the GDT portal.

The GDT and Customs Service conduct targeted post-export audit campaigns on professional services and advisory zero-rating claims, applying strict substance-over-form examinations. Consequences of disallowance or fraud: (a) under-declared VAT is assessed as a deficiency of 20% of the understated tax plus late-payment interest at 0.03% per day under Articles 141 and 143 of Law No. 38/2019/QH14; (b) fraudulent zero-rating or refund fabrication constitutes tax evasion subject to an administrative penalty of 1 to 3 times the evaded tax amount, suspension of the taxpayer's e-invoice codes, and issuance of forced-collection decisions under Decree No. 125/2020/ND-CP; (c) criminal liability may attach under Article 200 of the Penal Code No. 100/2015/QH13 (as amended in 2017) for the LLC's responsible officers where the evasion exceeds VND 100 million, with imprisonment penalties of up to 7 years; (d) wrongly received VAT refunds are clawed back in full with 0.03% daily interest under a five-year general lookback period, extended to ten years for fraud; (e) failure to repatriate export earnings within the 180-day statutory deadline triggers foreign-exchange-control fines of VND 50 million to VND 100 million, compulsory repatriation, and late-repatriation interest under Circular No. 05/2018/TT-NHNN. All export substantiation files must be retained not less than 5 years (10 years where fraud indicators exist).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

VND is not fully freely convertible. Current-account transactions, including service fees, advisory retainers, and cross-border consultancy disbursements, are generally convertible and may be settled through SBV-licensed credit institutions without advance SBV approval, provided the underlying commercial contracts, invoices, tax records, and FX purpose declarations are complete. Capital-account transactions, including FDI equity contribution, share transfer proceeds, medium-term and long-term external loans, and capital repatriation, are subject to evidentiary restrictions and must be routed through the enterprise's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at a licensed bank. Profit remittance abroad is legally permitted after compliance with annual audited financial statements, corporate income tax finalization, statutory reserve allocations, settlement of all tax and social insurance obligations, and submission of the required bank-facing documentary set. In practice, unresolved tax audits or transfer-pricing exposure and foreign contractor tax filing gaps constitute the principal remittance hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For export receipts of goods or services, the Vietnamese foreign-exchange framework imposes an inward-repatriation obligation: export proceeds must be collected and brought into the Vietnamese banking system within the statutory deadline, generally 90 days from the export date or the contractual payment due date, and must be credited to the LLC's foreign-currency account at a SBV-licensed authorized bank. There is no mandatory conversion ratio for private export earnings of a foreign-invested LLC under the current regime; the LLC may retain the proceeds in foreign currency, but any conversion to VND must be executed through a licensed bank at the prevailing spot rate. If the statutory repatriation deadline cannot be met, a written extension application must be submitted to the SBV before the deadline lapses, supported by the underlying cross-border contract, collection status report, and bank transaction proof. Offshore retention, offshore set-off against payables, or evasion of the repatriation route is not permitted.

Central bank supervision: State Bank of Vietnam (Ngân hàng Nhà nước Việt Nam), acting as the monetary and foreign-exchange administrative authority through its Foreign Exchange Administration Department, supported by the Foreign Exchange Ordinance No. 28/2005/PL-UBTVQH11 as amended by Ordinance No. 06/2013/UBTVQH14, the Law on Credit Institutions, and subordinate SBV Circulars, including Circular No. 09/2022/TT-NHNN on Direct Investment Capital Accounts.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Payoneer cannot act as the primary corporate treasury or cross-border clearing bank for a Vietnamese foreign-invested LLC. Vietnamese law reserves the provision of payment accounts, settlement services, and foreign-exchange clearing activity to SBV-licensed credit institutions and certain licensed payment intermediaries. An offshore EMI may serve only as an ancillary collection channel for small external service fees; it cannot lawfully receive the LLC's FDI equity capital, cannot maintain an onshore VND settlement account, and cannot issue SBV-recognized FX documentation. The LLC must establish and maintain its root clearing relationship through an SBV-licensed bank.

Revolut Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound FDI equity subscriptions must be wired into the LLC's Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) at an SBV-licensed bank. The foreign investor issues a SWIFT MT103 with remittance information containing the investor's legal name, the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) number, the purpose of payment, and the relevant purpose code 'Foreign Direct Investment — Equity Capital'. The receiving bank verifies: (i) the IRC is authentic and unexpired; (ii) the contribution is within the charter-capital schedule and does not exceed the registered capital; (iii) the investor's corporate identity and UBO; (iv) compliance with the statutory capital-contribution deadline, generally 90 days from the Enterprise Registration Certificate issuance; and (v) the source-of-funds documentation. Once cleared, the funds are booked onshore: the LLC may hold the foreign currency or convert into VND at the applicable spot rate. Withdrawals from the DICA to the operating account are permitted only for lawful expenditure supported by invoices, contracts, payroll records, and tax filings. No offshore conversion, no settlement through a third-party offshore account, and no capital credit directly to the operating account is permissible. If the injected amount exceeds the original charter capital, an updated IRC and Department of Planning and Investment approval must be obtained before the remittance.Disbursements to foreign advisory firms and trade counterparties are executed from the LLC's operational bank accounts, not from the DICA. The standard clearing rail is SWIFT MT103: the LLC submits a payment order with a purpose code reflecting the nature of the cross-border transaction, such as business consulting or advisory services, together with the underlying commercial contract, itemized invoice, service acceptance certificate, and, where applicable, evidence of Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) withholding and settlement. For imported goods, the LLC may open an irrevocable commercial letter of credit through its credit line, or use a sight documentary collection where no credit line exists. Every outbound payment must be matched to one invoice and one engagement; the LLC must maintain continuously updated treasury files containing VAT receipts, tax remittance confirmations, FX spot confirmations, and proof of bank-authorization references. The LLC should aggregate USD-denominated payables and settle them in a single spot purchase through the licensed bank to minimize conversion cost and demonstrate real-demand compliance.
