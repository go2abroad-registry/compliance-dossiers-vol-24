Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (VCIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; statutory VAT registration, VAT returns, customs duties and trade clearances are administered under the Value Added Tax Act and the Customs Act through the ASYCUDA World electronic customs declaration portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company may obtain a Vanuatu VAT/TIN reference after completing incorporation under the International Companies Act and receiving approval of the registered agent and registration confirmation from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). The IC must submit the statutory VAT registration application to VCIRD together with the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, VFSC certificate of good standing, registered agent letter, description of taxable activities, and evidence of intended import/export transactions. VCIRD processes the registration, issues a VAT registration number, and activates the customs operator code in ASYCUDA World. No income tax registration is required because Vanuatu imposes no income tax and an International Company is statutorily tax-exempt.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company may obtain a Vanuatu VAT/TIN reference after completing incorporation under the International Companies Act and receiving approval of the registered agent and registration confirmation from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). The IC must submit the statutory VAT registration application to VCIRD together with the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, VFSC certificate of good standing, registered agent letter, description of taxable activities, and evidence of intended import/export transactions. VCIRD processes the registration, issues a VAT registration number, and activates the customs operator code in ASYCUDA World. No income tax registration is required because Vanuatu imposes no income tax and an International Company is statutorily tax-exempt.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None applicable. Vanuatu has no general income tax, no check-the-box classification, no hybrid mismatch election, and no fiscal transparency election. The International Company's tax exemption is statutory and automatic. VAT classification is determined solely by the Value Added Tax Act; a registered IC may voluntarily apply for VAT registration below the compulsory turnover threshold, but entity-level income tax classification elections are not available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million vatu) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; compulsory VAT registration arises when actual or expected annual taxable turnover exceeds the threshold, and voluntary VAT registration is available below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered International Company may zero-rate an export supply if: (1) the goods are exported by or on behalf of the supplier from Vanuatu to a destination outside Vanuatu; (2) the goods are consigned under a customs export declaration and leave the Vanuatu customs territory within the prescribed period; (3) the transaction is at arm's length with a non-resident buyer; (4) the supplier retains title and export risk until departure; and (5) the supporting export documentation is complete. The frozen beef and seafood import business may also zero-rate a direct re-export or offshore sale, provided the goods are not consumed in Vanuatu and physical export is fully evidenced. Services supplied to a non-resident and performed outside Vanuatu are similarly zero-rated if the statutory conditions and proof of foreign receipt are satisfied.

  • Customs export declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry) authenticated by Vanuatu Customs
  • Bill of lading or air waybill showing departure from Vanuatu and destination outside Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and packing list with HS codes, quantity, weight and product description
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank SWIFT/telex confirmation showing receipt of export proceeds
  • VAT registration certificate and import entry/import declaration evidencing customs duty and import VAT paid at import
  • Certificate of origin if required by the destination jurisdiction for tariff preference purposes

Vanuatu does not currently operate a mandatory real-time electronic invoicing or digital fiscal stamping clearance system for domestic VAT invoices. Registered persons must issue and retain VAT invoices containing the prescribed particulars under the VAT Act. All import and export entries are cleared electronically through ASYCUDA World; import VAT must be declared and settled at customs clearance as a condition of release, and export entries are validated against the zero-rating evidence package.

VCIRD and Vanuatu Customs scrutinize export zero-rating claims by reference to the full documentary trail and may suspend refunds pending verification. If an export zero-rating claim fails, the VAT is reassessed at 12.5% with retrospective clawback of any input VAT credits already refunded; statutory penalty tax and interest accrue on the under-declared VAT. Fraudulent zero-rating documentation attracts criminal prosecution, monetary penalties, and direct liability for directors and officers; a knowingly false export declaration may also result in customs seizure, forfeiture, and revocation or suspension of the IC's VAT registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible in both current-account and capital-account channels. Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control approval, no currency rationing and no repatriation restriction for non-resident-owned International Companies. Dividend payments, profit remittances, loan repayments and investment capital may be wired freely through any RBV-licensed commercial bank. The only practical hurdles are (1) bank-led source-of-funds documentation; (2) the AML/CFT Act reporting thresholds for cash and suspicious transactions; and (3) customs and statistical documentation if the goods enter the domestic customs territory. If the IC operates purely in a bonded import/re-export or international wholesale modality, no domestic profit-remittance bottleneck arises at all.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu maintains no statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. An IC may retain all re-export receipts, including proceeds from frozen beef and pelagic fish trades, in VUV, USD, AUD or other cleared currencies. The only obligations are commercial-bank recordation for balance-of-payments purposes and, where goods are re-exported under bond, customs verification via transit or customs-warehouse documentation. No percentage of hard-currency export earnings must be converted into VUV.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) under the Central Banking Act [Cap.125]; no dedicated foreign-exchange control bureau exists; RBV performs monetary regulation, payments-system oversight and balance-of-payments statistics, with delegated supervision under the Payments and Clearing Systems Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW. A Vanuatu International Company may not lawfully operate an unlicensed local payment business; a third-country digital EMI (Airwallex, Currencycloud, Ebury or similar) is not a substitute for an RBV-licensed bank because it lacks local deposit-licence status and cannot support a supply-chain LC or customs-bond guarantee. Such EMIs are viable only as a supplementary FX engine for incidental operating expenses and small supplier prepayments, and only where (a) the EMI holds a regulated licence in an FATF-compliant jurisdiction (AUSTRAC, FCA, MAS or FINMA); (b) every transaction is mirrored by an underlying invoice; and (c) the contractual stamp is attached to a domestic bank record. Continued sole reliance on an offshore EMI exposes the IC to de-risking, frozen funds and unavailability of Letters of Credit.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All equity or shareholder-loan funds are wired by the overseas parent in USD or AUD via SWIFT MT103 from an OECD or ASEAN licensed bank directly to the VU IC account held at a tier-1 RBV-licensed bank. The MT103 field 70 must state the corporate purpose, for example Capital Increase pursuant to board resolution date, and the beneficiary account must be pre-notified. No RBV prior approval is required, but the receiving bank will file balance-of-payments statistical returns, and large customer due-diligence checks of USD 1 million or above are anticipated; a pre-positioned source-of-funds file, corporate group chart and audited parent accounts reduce clearing time to one to two business days. For tax-exempt status, the inbound capital is recorded in the International Companies Act records and must not be mischaracterised as trading revenue subject to any Vanuatu duty.Clearing rails: (1) Irrevocable confirmed Letter of Credit under UCP600 for the underlying frozen meat and seafood consignment, issued by ANZ or BSP and advised through the seller's bank, being the primary rail for high-value cargo; (2) SWIFT MT103 for freight, insurance, customs-bond and pre-approved open-account payments up to USD 100,000 per shipment; (3) documents-against-payment at sight only for established supplier relationships and re-export modalities. Disbursements above VUV 10 million are automatically routed to the bank's senior compliance officer; each payment is matched against quarantine permit, commercial invoice, shipping manifest and real-time cold-chain temperature record. The IC must maintain a hard segregation of trade disbursements and treasury hedge settlements to avoid piercing tax-exempt substance.Spot VUV/USD and VUV/AUD conversion at the RBV daily benchmark rate through tier-1 domestic banks for operational working capital.Offshore USD/AUD and USD/NZD deliverable forwards and exchange-traded options executed by the parent treasury desk of an OECD regulated bank, with no local VUV leg.USD/VUV non-deliverable forward (NDF) contracted with an offshore licensed bank; the NDF is permissible only where the domestic account bank confirms that the IC has no VUV credit exposure and the fixing fallback clause is based on the RBV published rate.Cross-currency swap (CCS) between USD and AUD/VUV to match long-term capital and shareholder debt refinancing, provided the hedge is commercial-risk based, booked under IFRS 9 hedge documentation, and arranged through a VFSC-licensed dealer if booked onshore.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu has no national corporate income tax; International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt from all income tax on both domestic and offshore income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu does not impose withholding tax on dividends; IC dividends may be repatriated to non-resident shareholders free of any deduction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Vanuatu has no codified transfer-pricing law and no VUV threshold (VUV 0 / N/A) triggering mandatory Local File or Master File preparation; related-party transactions nevertheless must be supported by contemporaneous records if the IC declares an ESR relevant activity.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019) apply to ICs carrying on relevant activities, which include a Distribution and Service Centre business such as wholesale import and trading. The IC must show management and control exercised in Vanuatu, adequate local premises, sufficient local employees, and proportionate local operating expenditure, and must file an annual Economic Substance return with the VFSC; the cold-chain import business must therefore either maintain genuine substance in Vanuatu or demonstrate that distribution activities are not core to its commercial model.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations to VFSC

Statutory sanction: Revocation of IC registration, penalty assessments up to VUV 500,000 per offence, and compulsory strike-off or liquidation; referral to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations face civil restitution orders, travel bans pending investigation, disqualification from management, and imprisonment up to 3 years for fraud offences.
Shell Company and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR non-compliance fines of VUV 1,000,000 for the first month of default and VUV 2,000,000 for each subsequent month, cancellation of IC status, and direction to wind up.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, disqualification orders, travel restrictions pending ESR correction, and imprisonment up to 12 months for continued willful breach.
Anti-Money Laundering and Shell-Pipeline Abuse

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to VUV 10,000,000, licence revocation, asset forfeiture, and public deregistration; breach of threshold reporting can trigger referral to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Individual directors and compliance officers are criminally liable for willful AML breach, facing imprisonment up to 15 years, personal confiscation of benefits, and civil liability jointly and severally with the company.
Fraudulent Trading and Veil Piercing

Statutory sanction: Winding-up orders, fines up to VUV 200,000 per contravention, and claw-back of misapplied corporate assets under the International Companies Act.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: directors are jointly and severally liable for company debts, subject to civil restitution, travel bans under enforcement orders, and imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent trading.
