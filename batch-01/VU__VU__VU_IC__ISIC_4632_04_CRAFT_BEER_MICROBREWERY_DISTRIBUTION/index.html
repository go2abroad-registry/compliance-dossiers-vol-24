Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila, Vanuatu; official e-services portal: https://vrc.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor organizing as a Vanuatu International Company (IC) must procure its statutory tax identifier through the following SOP: (1) appointment of a VFSC-licensed registered agent and lodgement of incorporation documents under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; (2) issuance by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission of the Certificate of Incorporation and IC registration number, together with a tax exemption certificate confirming the entity's exempt status; (3) where the IC will make taxable supplies in or into Vanuatu, submission of Form VAT Reg-001 to the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs with the IC certificate, registered agent's certificate, board resolution, beneficial ownership particulars and projected turnover; (4) issuance by VRC of a Taxpayer Identification Number / VAT Registration Number; and (5) for import-export operations, application for a Customs Importer/Exporter code. No national income tax TIN or employer tax registration is required because Vanuatu has no corporate income tax or PAYE on non-resident employees.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign investor organizing as a Vanuatu International Company (IC) must procure its statutory tax identifier through the following SOP: (1) appointment of a VFSC-licensed registered agent and lodgement of incorporation documents under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]; (2) issuance by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission of the Certificate of Incorporation and IC registration number, together with a tax exemption certificate confirming the entity's exempt status; (3) where the IC will make taxable supplies in or into Vanuatu, submission of Form VAT Reg-001 to the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs with the IC certificate, registered agent's certificate, board resolution, beneficial ownership particulars and projected turnover; (4) issuance by VRC of a Taxpayer Identification Number / VAT Registration Number; and (5) for import-export operations, application for a Customs Importer/Exporter code. No national income tax TIN or employer tax registration is required because Vanuatu has no corporate income tax or PAYE on non-resident employees.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Vanuatu law. An International Company is statutorily classified as a non-resident, tax-exempt entity without the need for any entity-level election. No hybrid mismatch election, branch registration, or classification election is prescribed. If the IC engages in a domestic VAT-triggering activity, it may elect to register for VAT voluntarily (subject to VRC approval); however this election affects VAT treatment only and does not alter the entity-level income-tax exemption. The Minister of Finance may issue a tax-exemption certificate in connection with contract lender due diligence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 2,000,000 (approx. USD 17,000) of annual taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold. A non-resident IC is not required to register merely because it earns foreign-source income, but an economic nexus arises if it supplies goods to customers in Vanuatu (including online sales) or imports goods/equipment into Vanuatu, as VAT is chargeable at importation regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for a registered supplier's direct export of physical goods (including beer/kegs) from Vanuatu to a non-resident purchaser, provided the goods are consigned out of Vanuatu and exported within the period stipulated by VAT regulations or contracts. Offshore services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident and wholly consumed/used outside Vanuatu; international freight and transport services also qualify. The exporter must be a VRC-registered supplier, maintain credible evidence of the export (not merely intra-island carriage), and obtain banking evidence of payment in foreign or VUV currency from the non-resident buyer. On-shipment to Vanuatu consumers, abandoned cargo, or export that later re-enters Vanuatu does not qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Cus. Exp.) validated by the Vanuatu Customs desk officer
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing consignor/exporter name and foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a licensed Vanuatu bank or SWIFT MT103 credit evidence
  • Commercial invoice and packing list reconciling line items, keg count, batch numbers, and declared export value

No mandatory electronic invoicing, continuous transaction controls (CTC), government-licensed clearance gateway, API filing, or digital stamping regime is currently in operation. VRC accepts paper-based VAT returns and invoices generated by standard accounting software, provided each registered supplier issues sequentially numbered tax invoices and keeps the audit trail. The VRC maintains electronic risk-based verification for exports, but there is no real-time fiscal clearance or oil-tax/GST dashboard that must be integrated as of the current assessment period.

VRC export and refund controls are strict: zero-rating is provisional until all export documentation is produced and the sale proceeds are repatriated through a Vanuatu licensed bank within 180 days from the shipment date. If that deadline is missed, or if declarations are false, VRC will disallow the export and claim all VAT at 12.5% on the original invoice, reverse any input VAT refunds already paid, and add statutory interest and default penalties. A knowingly fraudulent export zero-rating claim is a criminal offence under the Value Added Tax Act and Customs Act, exposing the entity to fines up to VUV 1,000,000 (or such higher amount as the variance penalty rules provide) and imprisonment of officers; management also risks cancellation of the IC registration and blacklisting from future customs clearance. Proof of currency repatriation and custody of export evidence must be maintained for at least seven years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The VUV is freely convertible for both current and capital transactions; there are no exchange controls on dividends, capital repatriation, or loan servicing. A Vanuatu International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] may hold and remit funds in any currency without RBV approval. The only remittance hurdle is commercial bank source-of-funds and UBO due diligence; there is no regulatory conversion licensing, no profit remittance quota, and no tax clearance prerequisite for outbound transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The IC may retain 100% of cold-chain beverage export proceeds indefinitely in USD, AUD, or NZD accounts; there is no statutory conversion timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to declare export contracts or shipping documents to the RBV or Customs.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — sole monetary authority and banking supervisor under the Financial Institutions Act and the International Banking Act. No separate foreign exchange administration exists; the VFSC separately licenses International Companies. Vanuatu is an IMF Article VIII member and applies no exchange-control approval regime.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Vanuatu ICs are generally unsupported by offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Revolut Business) due to the absence of a machine-readable VFSC registry validation channel, entity-type AML profiling, and the fact that the VFSC does not license or recognise standalone EMI authorisation under the International Banking Act. Core treasury must run through tier-1 SWIFT-connected banks; EMIs, where available, are limited to destination-market collection facilitation via locally incorporated paying agents.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital and subordinated loans are injected via SWIFT MT103 from the investor's home jurisdiction into the IC's designated USD or AUD operational account at ANZ or BSP. Wire remarks must state the beneficiary reference 'VU_IC_ISIC_4632' and cite 'capital subscription under International Companies Act [Cap 222]'. No RBV approval, exchange-control form, or tax clearance is required. The receiving bank requires a board resolution authorising account opening and the capital subscription agreement for source-of-funds sign-off. Inward wires above approximately VUV 30 million (~USD 250,000) attract standard enhanced due diligence, not a regulatory ceiling. The IC must not receive domestic VUV trade receivables from Vanuatu residents due to the non-resident trading prohibition; local direct-debit collections must be executed by onshore distribution agents in the destination market.Brewery payments, freight, and cold-chain logistics are settled via (i) SWIFT MT103 (gpi-tracked) for open-account invoice payments; (ii) SWIFT MT700 documentary letters of credit for high-value brewery purchase contracts; and (iii) MT101/MT940 liquidity-sweep instructions for internal treasury concentration. Each outbound instruction cites the commercial invoice number, Incoterm, and purchase-order reference. No stamp duty, remittance tax, or withholding applies to outbound wires from Vanuatu ICs. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Vanuatu does not impose corporate income tax at the national level; a Vanuatu International Company is taxed at nil on its worldwide income, subject only to VFSC annual fees).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%; Vanuatu does not levy dividend withholding tax, secondary corporate tax, or branch remittance tax on a Vanuatu International Company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0 statutory threshold because Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and therefore no domestic transfer pricing documentation obligation. For good governance and foreign tax defence, contemporaneous documentation should be prepared when annual related-party transactions involving craft beer distribution, keg leasing, logistics, or brand royalties exceed VUV 50,000,000; the OECD-aligned Local File and Master File are recommended where group revenue exceeds USD 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The VFSC Economic Substance Regulations require a Vanuatu International Company carrying on a relevant activity, including distribution and service centre business, to demonstrate economic substance in Vanuatu. A craft beer wholesale and export entity must maintain its principal place of business in Vanuatu through a physical registered office and adequate staff presence, keep books and records locally, ensure core income-generating activities such as contract negotiation, order management, inventory control, shipping arrangements, and invoicing are conducted from Vanuatu, and file an annual economic substance return. A broad self-declaration of no activity will be insufficient if the company is earning distribution and commission income; failure to satisfy the substance test exposes the company and directors to fines, strike-off, and international regulatory adverse action.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Profit Repatriation Abuse Through Zero-Rate Jurisdiction

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu-side penalties include a VFSC economic substance administrative penalty of up to VUV 5,000,000, a daily continuing default penalty of VUV 100,000, licence revocation, and strike-off from the register. In the foreign shareholder's jurisdiction, tax authorities may reallocate profit under controlled foreign corporation and transfer pricing rules, impose underpayment interest, and apply penalty taxes of up to 100% of the additional assessment.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or approve the artificial distribution may be jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of diverted corporate funds; courts may issue asset freezes, travel bans, disqualification orders, and, where fraudulent evasion is shown, imprisonment of up to seven years in the jurisdiction asserting criminal tax fraud.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Evasion

Statutory sanction: A false economic substance declaration constitutes an offence under the VFSC Economic Substance regime; penalties escalate from a first-offence fine of VUV 2,500,000 to a second-offence fine of VUV 5,000,000, with a daily non-compliance surcharge of VUV 50,000. VFSC may order compulsory strike-off and public naming of the company on the compliance default list.

Director personal liability: Every director and signatory of the false return is personally liable for the outstanding penalty if the company does not pay it, and each responsible officer may be fined up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisoned for up to five years; nominee directors cannot rely on contractual indemnities to avoid criminal sanctions.
AML / CFT Breach in Craft Beer Export Receivables

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu AML/CFT framework, failure to comply with beneficial ownership, record-keeping, and suspicious transaction reporting obligations is punishable by a fine of up to VUV 10,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 10 years for the company and responsible officers; VFSC may suspend or revoke the International Company licence and make the company's accounts subject to a confiscation order.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally exposed to criminal money-laundering liability of up to 15 years' imprisonment and civil liability for traceable proceeds; courts may issue urgent travel bans, freezing orders, and worldwide asset seizure against directors, and any bankruptcy arising from forfeiture may cause personal disqualification from managing Vanuatu or foreign companies.
