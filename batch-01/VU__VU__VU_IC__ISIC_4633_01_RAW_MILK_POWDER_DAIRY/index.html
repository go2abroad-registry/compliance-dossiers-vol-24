Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Department of Finance and Treasury, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; online portal: https://www.gov.vu and https://www.customs.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, so foreign entities are not issued a general taxpayer identification number. A VAT-registered business obtains a unique VAT registration number from VRC by filing the prescribed VAT registration application with incorporation documents, director identification, and local contact details when applicable. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is exempt from all national taxes and does not ordinarily obtain a VAT ID; it evidences its tax identity through the Certificate of Incorporation, IC status certificate, and exemption certificate issued by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, so foreign entities are not issued a general taxpayer identification number. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch, or entity reclassification elections exist under Vanuatu law. An International Company may, by special resolution and VFSC approval, continue as an ordinary company under the Companies Act [Cap 191], which would remove the automatic tax-exemption status but would not create corporate income tax liability. Hybrid mismatch elections are inapplicable due to the absence of income tax; export zero-rating is applied mechanically under the VAT Act and cannot be elected or optimized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

A supplier must register for VAT when taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceed or are likely to exceed VT 4,000,000 (four million vatu) in any consecutive 12-month period; registration is voluntary below this threshold. Supplies by an International Company that are made outside Vanuatu, or that are exported, are outside the scope or zero-rated and therefore do not create a VAT registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Vanuatu are zero-rated where the goods are physically removed from Vanuatu within the time limit prescribed under the VAT Act and the supplier retains documentary evidence. Services supplied to non-residents are zero-rated if the services are not used or enjoyed in Vanuatu and are supplied for consideration to a recipient outside Vanuatu. For whole and skimmed milk powder traded as a cross-border commodity, supplies that never enter Vanuatu are outside the scope of Vanuatu VAT; if goods are exported through Vanuatu or supplied and shipped from a Vanuatu bonded warehouse to a foreign buyer, the supply qualifies for zero-rating.

  • Vanuatu Customs Export Declaration (outward clearance form)
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill evidencing physical export from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the milk powder commodity
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (where required for preferential tariff purposes)
  • VAT zero-rating substantiation schedule retained for audit

Vanuatu does not currently mandate real-time electronic invoicing, continuous transaction control, digital stamping, or API-based fiscal clearance. Invoices must contain prescribed VAT content, be serially numbered, and be filed/retained for audit. Export transactions must be supported by customs declarations, commercial export invoices, transport documents, and, where applicable, inward remittance evidence; no fiscal e-invoicing clearance is in force.

The Vanuatu Revenue and Customs may audit zero-rated export claims and require documentary proof of export and foreign payment. An unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating claim is treated as a taxable supply, triggering a clawback of 12.5% VAT with late-payment penalties and interest; deliberate evasion attracts criminal prosecution and director/personal liability under the VAT Act and Customs offences legislation.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu applies a liberal exchange-control regime to non-resident International Companies. Current-account convertibility for trade in whole and skimmed milk powder is unrestricted; capital-account contributions from shareholders may be received without RBV prior approval. The vatu (VUV) is convertible onshore but is not a deliverable offshore currency, therefore settlement is normally effected in USD/AUD/EUR through authorized dealers. Profit remittance is not subject to Vanuatu withholding tax or an exchange-control permit; the only practical hurdle is the receiving bank's source-of-funds verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, conversion ratio, or statutory timeline applies to export proceeds received by a Vanuatu International Company. The Exchange Control Act only requires that export proceeds be collected through an authorized dealer, that the full invoice value be received within agreed commercial terms, and that the exporter retain the sales contract, commercial invoice, and transport document for exchange-control inspection. Proceeds may be held indefinitely in foreign currency; no VUV conversion quota is triggered.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - central bank and exchange control administrator under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act and the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 68]

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium. Offshore fintech and electronic-money institutions are not licensed by the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu and cannot provide VUV-denominated accounts or documentary/LC services to a Vanuatu International Company. Such EMIs may support ancillary low-value USD/EUR remittances, but they are legally restricted from taking deposits as part of Vanuatu banking business and are unsuitable as primary clearing rails for dairy trade settlements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None. Vanuatu has no statutory transfer pricing regime for International Companies and no local currency threshold for Local File / Master File preparation. However, a dairy commodity trader holding a VFSC broker or dealer licence may be required to maintain contemporaneous pricing records to demonstrate arm's length revenue under AML/CFT fit-and-proper requirements.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's ESR framework applies to International Companies carrying on specified 'relevant activities', including distribution and service centre activities. Pure wholesale trading of raw milk powder under ISIC 4633 may trigger 'distribution' substance requirements if the company derives income from purchasing goods from related parties and reselling to third parties outside Vanuatu. The IC must then demonstrate CIGA (directed and managed in Vanuatu), physical office presence, full-time staff and core income-generating activities in Vanuatu, or risk penalty of VUV 20,000 plus cancellation of the company's exemption status. Given the dairy commodity model, substance is recommended at the level of the operating distribution subsidiary and not the non-resident IC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu International Companies Act and the penal code, penalties reach VUV 1,000,000 and compulsory de-registration. The IC loses its tax-exempt status retrospectively, and the entire income is treated as taxable in the beneficial owner's country of tax residence.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil restitution for unreported profit amounts, travel bans under mutual assistance orders, and up to 7 years' imprisonment for fraudulent false statements.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations, failure to meet the substance test triggers a penalty of VUV 20,000, a daily default penalty of VUV 500, and possible automatic cancellation of the IC's certificate of incorporation. The VFSC also has power to strike the company off for failure to demonstrate substance within a remedial period.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid penalty amounts; the court may order disqualification of the director from acting as a director in Vanuatu for up to 5 years and may impose a travel restriction where the director attempts to leave Vanuatu while penalties are outstanding.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu International Companies (Amendment) Act and the Anti-Money Laundering Act, fines of up to VUV 100,000 apply to the company and VUV 50,000 per director; funds and assets may be frozen under orders of the Vanuatu Supreme Court.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly fail to comply with UBO disclosure orders face civil liability for the full value of any funds laundered, exclusion from the dairy trading sector under VFSC industry directives, and imprisonment for up to 10 years for wilful money laundering.
