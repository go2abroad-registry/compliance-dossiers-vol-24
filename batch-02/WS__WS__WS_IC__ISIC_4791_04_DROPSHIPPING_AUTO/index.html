Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) - official portal: https://www.sra.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC) obtains a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by registering with the Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) through its online taxpayer portal. The applicant submits a certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, trust deed (if any), and a completed SRA registration form. ICs are, however, exempt from income tax and VAGST on offshore activities; a Certificate of Tax Exemption can be issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour / Registrar of International Companies. If the IC makes domestic taxable supplies or meets the turnover threshold, it is required to file a VAGST registration application within 14 days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC) obtains a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by registering with the Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) through its online taxpayer portal. The applicant submits a certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, trust deed (if any), and a completed SRA registration form. ICs are, however, exempt from income tax and VAGST on offshore activities; a Certificate of Tax Exemption can be issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour / Registrar of International Companies. If the IC makes domestic taxable supplies or meets the turnover threshold, it is required to file a VAGST registration application within 14 days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election under domestic tax law. An IC may voluntarily elect to register for VAGST to recover input tax on local expenses, but such election does not convert the IC's tax-exempt status into a taxable entity for income tax. No hybrid mismatch elections are available; the International Companies Act imposes an automatic statutory tax exemption on qualifying ICs, subject to compliance with anti-avoidance provisions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 250,000 (annual taxable turnover)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAGST-registered supplier may zero-rate exported goods if the goods are physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply and appropriate customs evidence is retained. Offshore services (including dropshipping facilitation, platform services and logistics coordination) supplied to a non-resident person who is outside Samoa at the time of supply and not connected with land/goods located in Samoa are also zero-rated. The claim must be supported by documentary proof of export or offshore recipient status.

  • Samoa Customs Export Declaration (C2X)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank Transfer Evidence for the cross-border settlement
  • VAGST Export Certificate or zero-rating attestation (where claimed)

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or e-invoicing clearance mandate is currently enforced in Samoa. Registered persons must issue VAGST tax invoices in the prescribed form, retain them for seven years, and file electronic returns through the SRA online platform.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims or fictitious export documentation are subject to detailed audit scrutiny by the SRA. Penalties include recovery of any refunded VAGST plus interest (at the statutory rate), a civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax underpaid, a fine not exceeding WST 50,000, and imprisonment of up to seven years for responsible officers. The SRA may also suspend or cancel the entity's tax registration and require enhanced reporting for subsequent periods.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is non-internationally traded and subject to a managed exchange rate arrangement. Current-account convertibility is granted for genuine trade transactions upon submission of documentary evidence, while capital-account transactions require prior Central Bank of Samoa approval under the Exchange Control Act 1955 and Exchange Control Regulations 1960. Profit remittances by an International Company require exchange-control clearance, evidence of after-tax available reserves, and are vulnerable to CBS liquidity checks; the CBS may impose queueing in times of foreign-exchange shortage. Samoa has no formal capital-account liberalization for offshore hedge funds or passive treasury vehicles, so repetitive funding and distributions are scrutinized to ensure the IC is not a mere conduit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Samoa's Exchange Control Regulations, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days from the date of shipment. Every exporter must offer the foreign currency proceeds to an authorized dealer bank within 30 days of receipt; mandatory conversion into WST at the official exchange rate applies unless the Central Bank of Samoa grants a foreign-currency retention-account exemption. For an automated dropshipping operation, careful distinction must be made between 'export of goods' receipts and 'service fees' or 'sales commission' flows, as the latter may face stricter surrender requirements and require a separate CBS ruling.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Samoa does not license foreign-owned electronic money institutions for domestic WST settlement, and the Central Bank of Samoa treats any Samoa International Company holding client funds through an offshore EMI as a high-risk, non-transparent treasury arrangement. Offshore digital EMIs unlicensed in Samoa cannot provide compliant clearing through the local banking system and may create unlicensed banking or payment-services exposure. Such EMIs can only be used for low-value non-Samoan currency collection if the transaction path does not involve Samoan residents; this is impractical for an operating IC in Samoa.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions and intercompany funding must be received by an authorized dealer bank in Samoa via SWIFT MT103 with proper purpose codes (e.g., 'Share Capital Contribution' or 'Loan Intercompany' with CBS reference). Prior approval from the Central Bank of Samoa is required for WST-equivalent amounts above 100,000 or for any unusually structured debt/equity hybrid; a signed Form A or approved Legal Tender Advice must be lodged. The bank will verify the underlying International Company Act 2012 registration, current directors, and that the source funds are easily identifiable. No domestic inward routing through EMI settlement or PayPal business balances will be accepted as the sole proof of injected liquidity.Outbound trade settlements must use the formal banking clearing rails: SWIFT MT103 will be executed for third-party supplier payments, and Commercial Letters of Credit (L/C at sight or usance) are recommended for high-value automated inventory acquisitions. Each disbursement requires an approved underlying commercial invoice, packing slip, shipping order or courier manifest, and a valid purpose code identifying dropshipped goods. Payments cannot be released to wholly unrelated shell payees; all payee names must match the supplier agreement. WST balances generated from export conversions can be used for operating costs, but margins held in WST are subject to regulatory 'at most 30 days' holding periods unless allocated for approved local expenses.FX forward contracts with authorized dealer banks for up to 12 months against bona fide underlying imports/exportsNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in offshore markets for WST/USD exposure where the settlement amount is netted in USD outside SamoaCurrency swaps restricted to matching actual trade cash flows, executed only through an authorized dealer bank in Samoa with CBS transparency filings

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not triggered for a fully tax-exempt International Company; should the IC nonetheless become Samoa-taxable by earning Samoan-source income or trading locally, the Samoa Income Tax Act's arm's-length rules require a Transfer Pricing Documentary Certificate when related-party transactions exceed WST 2,000,000 per year and a Master File when aggregate connected transactions exceed WST 20,000,000 per year

Economic substance scope: The Samoa International Companies Act imposes no mandatory local economic substance test, and a pure offshore IC does not require employees or premises in Samoa; however, an IC that is managed and controlled from Samoa while claiming foreign tax exemption may be treated as resident for income tax purposes, creating a real substance exposure. Automated dropshipping arrangements should be structured to ensure procurement, storage, logistics, and board decision-making occur outside Samoa, and directors should avoid any factual nexus to Samoan customers or payment processing in order to preserve the exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Exemption Abuse / Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Retrospective assessment of the underlying income at the 27% corporate rate, a 100% late payment/persistence penalty, monetary penalties up to WST 100,000, deregistration of the International Company, and revocation of the income tax exemption certificate

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall and penalties; where fraud or willful evasion is proved, directors may face criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to 5 years, travel bans, and asset seizure orders after the corporate veil is pierced
Shell Company / Sham Commercial Substance

Statutory sanction: Deregistration under the International Companies Act, administrative strike-off, regulatory fines up to WST 50,000, forced repatriation or freezing of payment-rail assets, and disqualification of the registered agent

Director personal liability: Courts may disregard the limited liability structure and hold directors personally liable for creditor claims and regulatory penalties; personal asset tracing, charging orders, and director disqualification orders are available to the Samoa Registrar
AML/CFT Violations in Payment Rail Integration

Statutory sanction: Under the Samoa AML/CFT Act 2017, fines up to WST 500,000 for the corporate entity, cancellation of access to licensed payment aggregators, and freezing of funds; repeat violations trigger extradition and mutual legal assistance exposure

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally subject to fines up to WST 100,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; senior management may be banned from acting as directors and prohibited from leaving Samoa pending investigation
