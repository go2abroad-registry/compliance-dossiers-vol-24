Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), operating as the successor to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue; official e-Services portal: https://www.customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is incorporated by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No statutory income tax TIN is issued because an IC is entirely outside Vanuatu's direct tax net. If the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu or imports goods subject to VAT, it must obtain a VAT registration number (VAT ID) by filing Form VAT-01 with VRCA, providing certified constitutional documents, passport copies of shareholders/beneficial owners, registered office certificate, and a declaration of projected turnover. Approval is typically granted within 7–14 working days; VAT IDs are issued electronically and must be quoted on all VAT invoices.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: An International Company under the Vanuatu International Companies Act is wholly exempt from income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, and estate duty. It has no tax residence for Vanuatu domestic income tax purposes and is not taxed on worldwide or Vanuatu-source income. Its only indirect tax exposure arises from Vanuatu VAT registration in respect of taxable supplies made in Vanuatu. — An International Company (IC) is incorporated by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: An International Company under the Vanuatu International Companies Act is wholly exempt from income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, and estate duty. It has no tax residence for Vanuatu domestic income tax purposes and is not taxed on worldwide or Vanuatu-source income. Its only indirect tax exposure arises from Vanuatu VAT registration in respect of taxable supplies made in Vanuatu.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, entity classification election, or hybrid mismatch election exists under Vanuatu law for International Companies. The offshore tax-exempt status is mandatory and non-elective. A foreign-owned IC may voluntarily register for VAT to facilitate input credit recovery on local business expenses, but this does not alter its direct tax exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Vatu (VT) 5,000,000 aggregate taxable turnover in any consecutive 12-month period. A non-resident IC is also required to register for VAT from the first day it makes a taxable supply in Vanuatu, unless the supply is wholly zero-rated as an export; imports of goods are subject to VAT at the border regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero percent VAT is claimable on: (i) goods physically exported by the supplier in the course or furtherance of the taxable activity, (ii) goods supplied to a non-resident where title passes outside Vanuatu and goods are removed from Vanuatu within 28 days, (iii) services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply, provided the services are not performed substantially in Vanuatu, and (iv) services directly related to international transport. The exporter must retain sufficient documentary evidence demonstrating actual exportation and receipt overseas; otherwise the supply is taxable at 12.5%.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time electronic fiscal clearance, government e-invoicing, API-based clearance, or digital stamping regime is currently mandated under Vanuatu VAT law. Taxpayers may issue paper or electronic VAT invoices, provided each invoice contains the prescribed particulars: invoice number, date, supplier VAT ID, recipient details, description, tax-exclusive amount, VAT amount, and VAT rate. There is no fiscal device or online authorization requirement.

VRCA auditors rigorously review export zero-rating claims, applying a substance-over-form analysis. If the exporter cannot produce the mandated documentation or if funds are not repatriated within the 180-day deadline, the zero-rating is retrospectively reversed and VAT becomes payable at 12.5% on the supply, plus interest at 2% per month on the underpaid tax. Input VAT credits previously attributed to the export claim are clawed back. Deliberate or reckless misstatement attracts an administrative penalty of up to 200% of the VAT undercharged and, for fraudulent evasion, criminal prosecution with fines up to VT 5,000,000 and/or imprisonment for up to seven years under the Value Added Tax Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalized exchange control regime. The domestic currency is the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV), which is not officially pegged to a hard currency but is managed against a basket of trading-partner currencies. Current account transactions are generally unrestricted, subject to reporting thresholds. Capital account transactions are permitted but require prior RBV authorization through an authorised dealer (commercial bank) for certain cross-border investments and external borrowings. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and liquidation proceeds may be freely transferred abroad for International Companies (ICs) after verification of source funds and tax compliance, provided the entity is not engaged in domestic business. Convertibility risk is minimal for non-resident ICs operating offshore. Foreign currency accounts may be held in USD, EUR, GBP, or AUD within the domestic banking system. No central bank guarantee or deposit insurance exists for non-resident IC accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists in Vanuatu. Exporters and offshore services providers, including regulatory consulting firms, are not required to convert foreign currency receipts into VUV within any timeline. Receipts may be retained in the original currency in foreign-currency bank accounts. However, entities must report inward remittances above VUV 100,000 to the authorised dealer for exchange-control recordkeeping and maintain underlying contractual evidence, invoices, and regulatory service agreements for six years. No mandatory conversion ratio applies.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) viability is Moderately Low. Vanuatu is not part of a Passporting Framework such as SEPA or the UK FCA Temporary Permissions Regime. EMIs incorporated in other jurisdictions cannot access the local VUV clearing system and are not supervised by the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu. Non-resident ICs should use global EMIs (e.g., US MSB/FinCEN-registered, Singapore MPI, or EU EMI) for USD/EUR/GBP settlement; however, RBV exchange-control reporting requires incoming wire evidence to be routed through a local authorised dealer if funds need to be converted into VUV. Segregation between EMI operational accounts and Vanuatu treasury functions is legally permissible only if no VUV clearing or domestic banking services are provided, which materially reduces utility. A Cayman/Channel Island or Singapore licensed digital bank holding a correspondent banking relationship with BSP/ANZ provides a safer intermediary for cross-border dollar clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial capital injections and operating liquidity must enter Vanuatu via SWIFT wire from the beneficial owner's corporate or personal bank account matching the name on the VFSC registration and bank account. Recommended routing: Originator bank (US/EU/HK/SG) to a US Dollar correspondent of ANZ Vanuatu or BRED Vanuatu, using SWIFT MT103. The payment method must include Remittance Information field (Field 70): 'SHARE CAPITAL - VU_IC / INVOICE' with purpose code EXCHANGE CONTROL APPROVED - CAPITAL INJECTION. For amounts above VUV 100,000, the authorised dealer bank will request an Source of Wealth Statement, certified copy of the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, and a board resolution authorising the capital deposit. The RBV Balance of Payments classification should be 'Direct Investment: Equity Capital' or 'Other Capital Transfer' if operating expense. For monthly expense funding, the purpose code 'OTHER PRIVATE SERVICES - REGULATORY FEES' may be used, but the beneficiary's invoice must be retained. No local clearing of ACH or Faster Payments is available; all inbound routing must be SWIFT. Avoid overboxed remittance descriptions containing 'FDA', 'CE', 'MONEY LAUNDERING', or 'MEDICAL DEVICE' alone; use neutral yet accurate commercial description 'CONSULTING FEES' or 'CORPORATE SETTLEMENT' controlled by the underlying agreement.Outbound trade disbursements for regulatory filing fees (FDA user fees, EU Notified Body fees), subcontractor consultants, and purchase of software subscriptions shall be conducted via SWIFT MT103 through a foreign currency account maintained at ANZ or BRED. For Vanuatu VUV operations, the bank will apply FTT (Foreign Transaction Tax) as applicable; for USD/EUR/AUD, no conversion fee is imposed. Commercial Letters of Credit are not appropriate for intangible consulting disbursements; instead, MT103 with irrevocable payment order and a detailed Commercial Invoice is the correct rail. Chinese suppliers or technical testing labs that require LC may be settled through a confirmed bank-provided LC using the Vanuatu Bank's correspondent network, but the underlying ISIC 7490 activity does not generate physical merchandise trade. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The VFSC annual exemption fee, signed declaration of particulars, and registered agent confirmation are due on or before 30 June of each calendar year. For an IC incorporated mid-year, the first return is due on the anniversary date set in the certificate or the statutory renewal date prescribed by the VFSC; the registered agent's renewal notice confirms the exact date. There is no CIT deadline because no CIT return is assessed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abusive Shell Company

Statutory sanction: The IC may be struck off by the VFSC for failing to maintain a licensed agent or records; the foreign tax jurisdiction may apply CFC, anti-avoidance, or section 482-type reallocation rules, imposing back tax, interest, and accuracy-related penalties of up to 20-40% of the understated tax and criminal referral for offshore tax fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly caused the offshore attribution of profits are personally exposed to civil restitution, piercing of the corporate veil, director disqualification, travel restrictions pending tax investigation, and imprisonment in the beneficial-owner's home jurisdiction for tax evasion offences.
AML / Shell Company Banking Red Flag

Statutory sanction: Correspondent banks may block the account, file a STR/SAR, and terminate the banking relationship; under Vanuatu AML/CFT legislation the VFSC may fine the IC, suspend the registered agent, and strike off the company. The company's assets may be frozen during a suspicious transaction investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors or beneficial owners who conceal the economic substance of transfers risk civil asset forfeiture, personal fines, travel bans imposed as part of an AML investigation, and imprisonment for wilful money laundering or aiding tax evasion.
Defective Transfer Pricing Documentation / False Intercompany Charges

Statutory sanction: Although Vanuatu has no domestic transfer pricing penalty regime, the US/EU parent jurisdiction can deny deductions for payments to the Vanuatu IC, impose transfer-pricing penalties of 20-40% of the adjustment, apply CFC inclusion of the IC's earnings, and challenge the tax-exempt status of the structure as a sham.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false documentation or fail to oversee substance may be jointly and severally liable for corporate tax liabilities by piercing the veil, subject to director disqualification and criminal prosecution for false statement in the jurisdiction that asserts the tax.
Unlicensed On-Shore Activity / False No-Local-Business Declaration

Statutory sanction: The IC loses its exempt-character status, becoming liable to corporation-type licensing fees, VAT on local supplies, and strict liability offences under Vanuatu's business licensing and VAT framework; VFSC may impose fines and cancel the IC's registration for knowingly false annual declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the false declaration of no local business face civil fines, repayment of unpaid government fees, disqualification from acting as officers, court-ordered travel bans where the state is pursuing recovery, and imprisonment for perjury or fraud.
