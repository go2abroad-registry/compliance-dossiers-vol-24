Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA); online portal: SRA Taxpayer Access Point (TAP)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident entity intending to carry on taxable activity in Samoa must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Samoa Revenue Authority. The application is submitted online or in person using Form TIN-01, accompanied by certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, directors' register, and evidence of physical or agent address in Samoa. An International Company (IC) is generally exempt from income tax registration and does not require a TIN for tax-exempt offshore activities, but a TIN and VAGST registration are required if the IC undertakes taxable domestic supplies or imports goods for local consumption.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident entity intending to carry on taxable activity in Samoa must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Samoa Revenue Authority. The application is submitted online or in person using Form TIN-01, accompanied by certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, directors' register, and evidence of physical or agent address in Samoa. An International Company (IC) is generally exempt from income tax registration and does not require a TIN for tax-exempt offshore activities, but a TIN and VAGST registration are required if the IC undertakes taxable domestic supplies or imports goods for local consumption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. An International Company is statutorily exempt from all Samoan income, capital-based, and stamp taxes. It may elect to become a domestic ordinary company under the Companies Act 2006, thereby waiving the IC tax-exempt status, but no subchapter-S-style pass-through election exists. No hybrid entity election is available for tax purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated for VAGST when they are exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply, or when they are supplied for use in an export free zone. Offshore services supplied to a non-resident recipient for use entirely outside Samoa are zero-rated. The exporter must hold documentary evidence proving physical export, the recipient's non-resident status, and receipt of foreign consideration. Deliveries to Samoan customers, including online auto-parts sales delivered within Samoa, are taxable at 15% VAGST.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime is currently in force in Samoa. VAGST-registered suppliers must issue standard tax invoices containing prescribed particulars; invoices may be paper or electronic. The SRA does not operate an API-based e-invoicing clearance system as of the current stage.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims are subject to full VAGST clawback plus interest at the statutory rate. Penalties include a late or false penalty of up to 100% of the tax shortfall and an additional monetary penalty not exceeding WST 10,000 per offence. The SRA may retrospectively audit up to six years and, in cases of wilful evasion, criminal prosecution and director liability may be imposed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a managed currency without full convertibility. Current-account payments for genuine goods/services are cleared through authorised dealers upon presentation of invoices, contracts and shipping documents, with no CBS pre-approval for standard trade transactions; capital-account flows — equity injections by non-resident shareholders, shareholder loans, dividend repatriations, outward investments and secondary-market securities flows — require CBS notification or prior approval via the authorised dealer. For WS_IC (a non-resident Samoan International Company under the International Companies Act 1987), the optimal classification is an offshore/non-resident entity that settles in USD and avoids unnecessary WST conversion. Dividend/profit remittance hurdles: distributions require (i) audited accounts or the SIFA tax-exempt certificate; (ii) a Certificate of Good Standing; and (iii) a CBS-approved outward dividend form; the hurdle is reduced where the dividend is paid in original foreign currency from a CBS-approved foreign-currency estate, as no WST sale is triggered.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa's Exchange Control Regulations require resident exporters to receive and collect export proceeds within six months (180 days) of shipment. Export proceeds invoiced in foreign currency must be surrendered to an authorised dealer and converted to WST at 100% of the receipt value within the settlement windows set by CBS, unless a Foreign Currency Account (FCA) or retention approval has been granted in advance. For the ISIC 4791 online aftermarket-parts exporter, the mandated compliance path is to obtain a CBS retention approval through the settlement bank before receiving gateway sweeps, allowing USD platform payouts to be held as USD for direct re-payment of overseas parts suppliers; otherwise every Stripe/PayPal payout is forcibly converted into WST. A structurally non-resident IC conducting pure offshore e-commerce should nonetheless obtain a written authorised-dealer classification confirming it is not a Samoa-resident exporter, in which case the surrender obligation does not apply to its foreign-currency settlement, but supporting documentation of the offshore operating model must be filed with CBS.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS; Samoan name: Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa) — the sole exchange-control administrator under the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations 1983 and the Banking Ordinance 2008; CBS licenses authorised dealers and money remitters, approves non-resident and foreign-currency accounts, issues exchange-control permits, and receives balance-of-payments reporting from commercial banks on all cross-border flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium as a collection/payment rail, but never as the settlement bank. Neither Stripe nor PayPal holds a Samoan banking licence; Stripe does not support Samoa as a merchant market, and PayPal permits Samoan merchants only to receive USD credits that must be withdrawn to an authorised-dealer account via SWIFT. Offshore digital EMIs (including Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie and Payoneer) are not licensed by CBS, cannot accept deposits with a Samoan nexus under the Banking Ordinance 2008, do not settle WST, and routinely screen out Samoa International Companies on territorial-availability and e-commerce policy grounds. Legally viable design: use EMI/PSP accounts strictly as pass-through collection wallets from which funds are swept by SWIFT MT103 into the BSP Samoa USD account; the last-mile settlement must always land in a CBS-licensed authorised-dealer account. Any deposit-taking or WST-denominated ledger with an unlicensed EMI would constitute unauthorised banking business under Samoan law.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound protocol: (1) board resolution approving the equity subscription or shareholder loan; (2) allotment/subscription registered by the SIFA-licensed agent with the Samoan company registry; (3) shareholder bank executes SWIFT MT103 credit to BSP Samoa USD capital sub-account, beneficiary identified as WS_IC; (4) field-70 remittance information must read: /CAP/ subscription under International Companies Act 1987 / capital contribution for online auto-parts trading; (5) sending bank applies BOP/ISO purpose classification of equity capital (code EQU or equivalent); (6) verification package filed with the receiving bank: certified board minutes, application for allotment, registered-agent allotment certificate, source-of-funds evidence and UBO chart; (7) CBS inward-capital declaration is lodged by the authorised dealer where the value exceeds the dealer's exchange-control threshold (plan for review at USD 50,000 per wire); (8) the funds are held in a dedicated USD capital/escrow sub-ledger and released only against documented inventory purchase commitments or platform liquidity needs.Trade settlement uses two rails. (A) SWIFT MT103 commercial wires for routine prepayment/TT inventory purchases from auto-parts suppliers in China, Japan and the USA; remittance purpose code states 'trade payable — automotive replacement parts', with commercial invoice, purchase-order and dispatch references retained on file; USD clearing runs over the authorised dealer's New York correspondent nostro. (B) Irrevocable commercial documentary letters of credit for bulk sourcing above USD 100,000: issued at sight through BSP (or an offshore issuing bank), requiring seller presentation of commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, certificate of origin and inspection certificate; documentary credits settle via SWIFT MT700/MT103/MT202 series. Inward Stripe/PayPal platform sweeps arrive as MT103 credits from the payment processor's payout bank with e-commerce sales purpose coding; those USD receipts must be reconciled at order level in the treasury ledger before being re-disbursed to suppliers. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% – International Company (IC) is exempt from Samoan corporate income tax on all offshore-sourced income; the standard domestic corporate rate is 27% (Income Tax Act 2015) but is inapplicable to a compliant IC deriving no Samoan-source income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory dividend withholding tax under the Income Tax Act 2015 applies only to dividends paid out of Samoan-source profits by a taxable company. A compliant offshore-exempt IC distributing non-Samoan-source profits to non-resident shareholders incurs 0% Samoan dividend WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 30 days after each anniversary of incorporation; the reporting period is the preceding 12-month period ended on the anniversary date. No audited financial statements are required to be filed for a private IC unless it has opted into the standard taxable regime.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No specific monetary threshold is prescribed in Samoan law for ICs. The transfer pricing rules in the Income Tax Act 2015 apply to related-party transactions only when the IC has opted into the standard tax regime or has derived Samoan-source income. In administrative practice, an IC must maintain transfer pricing documentation when the gross annual value of related-party cross-border transactions exceeds WST 500,000; below this level a light-touch contemporaneous file is recommended.

Economic substance scope: Samoan law does not yet impose OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) on International Companies. However, OECD Harmful Tax Practices reviews and European Union listing criteria have created market pressure for ICs to exhibit real substance, including a qualified registered agent, a physical registered office, board records, bank account activity, and accurate payroll or professional service arrangements. A letterbox IC without any demonstrable activity is exposed to financial institution de-risking, foreign tax authority recharacterisation, and treaty denial.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Domestic Source Activity

Statutory sanction: Retroactive cancellation of the IC tax exemption; assessment of Samoan corporate income tax at 27% on all Samoan-source profits for the open tax years; monthly late-payment penalty of 2%; civil penalty of 50% to 150% of the tax evaded under the Tax Administration Act 2017; criminal prosecution in the Supreme Court of Samoa.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved or concealed the domestic activity are jointly and severally liable for the assessed tax; the corporate veil may be pierced by court order; Ministry for Revenue may issue distress warrants restricting travel where assets are dissipated; imprisonment of up to 3 years may be imposed for fraudulent tax evasion.
Shell Company / False Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off by the Registrar of International Companies for false declarations; disqualification orders barring responsible persons from incorporating in Samoa for up to 5 years; civil penalties on the registered agent up to WST 10,000 for failing to verify beneficial ownership and substance; exposure to OECD and EU listing consequences, which terminate correspondent banking and high-risk payment processing agreements such as Stripe and PayPal accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil liability to creditors for fraudulent trading; travel bans issued by court order; exclusion orders from Samoa, New Zealand, and Australia under mutual assistance instruments; criminal conviction for perjury if the annual offshore declaration is knowingly false.
AML / Financing of Illegitimate Activity

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Samoa AML/CFT Act 2018 and SIFA directives: fines up to WST 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for money laundering; freezing orders by the Central Bank of Samoa over all platform payouts; forfeiture of proceeds; cancellation of the IC registration where the company is used as a money-laundering vehicle.

Director personal liability: Directors who control the payment gateways personally can be prosecuted and imprisoned; personal bank accounts linked to the corporate cash flow may be subject to civil forfeiture; Interpol red notices may issue if the activity crosses borders; directors forfeit the benefit of limited liability where fraud is proven.
Indirect Tax Circumvention in the Auto-Parts Market

Statutory sanction: Foreign tax authorities may recharacterise the IC as the taxable seller and issue assessments for uncollected VAT/GST, plus penalties of 15% to 100% of the tax due; Stripe and PayPal will permanently terminate the merchant account for misrepresenting business domicile; Samoa may revoke the IC's exemption where the activity is deemed a sham contrary to the offshore declaration.

Director personal liability: Directors may be added as responsible persons in foreign indirect-tax assessments; travel restrictions may be imposed by issuing jurisdictions for collection enforcement; personal guarantees signed with payment processors expose directors to direct recovery; the corporate veil can be pierced in cases of deliberate misrepresentation.
