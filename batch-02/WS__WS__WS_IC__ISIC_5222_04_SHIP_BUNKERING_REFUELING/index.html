Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed under Samoan law; the stated authorised share capital is to be fully subscribed by the initial subscribers. No escrow or capital deposit requirement applies. The Registrar levies an annual government fee (currently USD 300) after incorporation.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue, Government of Samoa (MOR); online portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity seeking to establish a taxable presence or obtain a tax identification number must file the Business Registration Form (Form MOR-BR1) with the Ministry of Revenue, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, proof of registered office address, and notarised passport copies of officers/shareholders. For VAT purposes, the entity must submit the VAT Registration Application (Form MOR-VAT1) and, where applicable, a bunkering licence from the Samoa Ports Authority and Marine Safety Administration. The Ministry of Revenue issues a combined TIN-VAT registration number upon verification of incorporation records and confirmation of a physical or licensed presence in Samoa.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity seeking to establish a taxable presence or obtain a tax identification number must file the Business Registration Form (Form MOR-BR1) with the Ministry of Revenue, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, proof of registered office address, and notarised passport copies of officers/shareholders. For VAT purposes, the entity must submit the VAT Registration Application (Form MOR-VAT1) and, where applicable, a bunkering licence from the Samoa Ports Authority and Marine Safety Administration. The Ministry of Revenue issues a combined TIN-VAT registration number upon verification of incorporation records and confirmation of a physical or licensed presence in Samoa.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

International Companies are generally treated as tax-exempt offshore entities and are excluded from the ordinary income tax regime under the International Companies Act 1987 and the Income Tax Act 2012. No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available for Samoa domestic law; an International Company may, however, elect in writing to be treated as a resident company for tax purposes if it obtains a Samoa business licence and derives income from Samoa sources, thereby triggering standard corporate income tax and VAT registration obligations. Hybrid mismatch rules under the Anti-Avoidance provisions apply to deny double non-taxation benefits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 60,000 taxable turnover in any 12-month period; voluntary registration permitted for lower turnover where taxable supplies are being made

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of goods exported from Samoa and directly connected services (including ship bunkering and lubricant replenishment for vessels operating in international trade) are zero-rated. To claim the zero rate, the supplier must be a VAT-registered person and obtain documentary evidence proving the supply has been exported or provided to a non-resident for use in international commerce, including evidence of the vessel's departure from Samoa waters, proof of payment from the non-resident, and confirmation that title and risk passed outside Samoa.

  • Samoa Customs Service Export Declaration (Customs Form CED-01)
  • Bill of Lading or other shipping document evidencing export of bunker fuel
  • Bunker Delivery Note (BDN) signed by the master or chief engineer
  • Port clearance certificate issued by Samoa Ports Authority
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank notification evidencing payment in foreign currency
  • VAT zero-rated supply schedule accompanying the VAT return

Samoa has not yet introduced a real-time electronic fiscal clearance or continuous transaction control system. VAT-registered persons are required to issue a tax invoice containing the words 'tax invoice', the supplier's name, address and VAT registration number, the recipient's name and address, a description of the supply, the date, the consideration exclusive of VAT, and the VAT charged. Tax invoices must be retained for five years. There is no API-based e-invoicing mandate, digital stamping requirement, or pre-clearance of invoices through the Ministry of Revenue system; the Ministry of Revenue may, however, require electronic submission of VAT returns and supporting schedules through its online portal.

Zero-rating claims are subject to audit scrutiny by the Ministry of Revenue, which may require production of the full documentation bundle within 30 days. Where the export documentation is incomplete or fraudulent, the VAT claimed as zero-rated is clawed back with interest at the statutory rate, the taxpayer is liable for a penalty of 200% of the tax underpaid for fraudulent claims and 100% for negligent claims, and criminal prosecution may result in a fine of up to WST 50,000 or imprisonment of up to three years. Additionally, the Customs Service may impose forfeiture of the goods and disqualification of a bunkering licence for false declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa maintains an exchange control regime under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Exchange Control Regulations, administered through authorized dealer commercial banks with the Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) as the principal regulator. The Samoan tala (WST) is a partially convertible currency. Current account transactions (e.g., trade payments, service fees, and operating remittances) are generally permitted, subject to documentary evidence of underlying trade and CBS-authorized dealer reporting. Capital account transactions, including capital injections, loans, and repatriation of share capital from a non-resident International Company, fall under CBS exchange control approval and require prescribed declaration forms and transactional back-up documentation. Dividend and profit remittance is legally permissible for an International Company that is tax-exempt under the International Companies Act 1987; however, the remitting agent must demonstrate tax clearance/confirmation from the Ministry of Revenue, audited financial soundness, identified beneficial ownership, and compliance with CBS reporting thresholds. There are no standing impediments to profit remittance for a compliant non-resident-owned Samoa International Company provided all filings are made through an authorized dealer and CBS approval is obtained where the underlying obligation is classified as a capital transfer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Samoa's exchange control framework, export proceeds from a Samoa International Company's foreign-currency bunkering sales must be repatriated through an authorized dealer bank in Samoa within 180 days from the date of shipment or date of completion of bunkering delivery. All export receipts are required to be reported to the Central Bank of Samoa. The prescribed mandatory conversion ratio into Samoan tala (WST) is 100% for export receipts unless the CBS grants a specific written exemption on a case-by-case basis, ordinarily available for an offshore International Company whose business receipts are retained in foreign currency for operational settlement in marine fuel procurement. Documentary evidence required includes a commercial invoice, bunker delivery receipt (BDN), vessel IMO number, charter contract, and a CBS export declaration form.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Samoa has no domestic fintech EMI (electronic money institution) licensing framework that would allow an offshore digital EMI to be treated as a licensed deposit-taking institution or authorized dealer. An offshore digital EMI is legally constrained by the Banking Act 2000 and CBS licensing and exchange control regulations from opening a banking account on a standalone basis for a Samoa International Company. Practical workarounds, such as receiving funds through a foreign-licensed EMI and then routing onward to a Samoa-authorized dealer bank, create sanctions-screening and counterparty-liability gaps in respect of marine-fuel trades; therefore, the treasury blueprint requires all liquidity to settle via licensed commercial banks or regulated clearing banks with SWIFT connectivity rather than EMI rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections into the Samoa International Company must be funded by the ultimate non-resident beneficial owner's own foreign-currency bank account. The compliant protocol uses an SWIFT MT103 wire transfer narrative with purpose code 'CAPITAL / EQUITY CONTRIBUTION' and reference to the CBS Exchange Control approval number (where required; CBS approval is required if the country of remittance treats the transaction as a capital transfer or where the amount exceeds the CBS threshold). The beneficiary bank will screen the remitter, correspondent banks, and beneficiary against UN, OFAC, EU, UK-HMT, and CBS watchlists before crediting. A complete verification set must include the sanctioned wiring instruction from a regulated introducing bank, the signed board resolution evidencing acceptance of the share premium or equity contribution, a source-of-funds declaration, and a CBS Capital Import Form or Exchange Control Declaration applicable at the time of remittance. The capital escrow account is segregated from operational cash roll and cannot be used to support short-term marine fuel payment; it must remain as evidence of minimum capitalization and standby liquidity support. Once cleared, the funds may be converted to the bunker supply operating account through an authorized dealer with an FX purchase contract and customs/exchange-control filing referencing the business purpose code for marine fuel inventory acquisition.Outbound disbursements for bunker fuel, lubricant replenishment, port agency fees, and surveyor costs are settled via SWIFT MT103 for onshore fuels and service invoices below USD 250,000 invoice value and via a Commercial Letter of Credit issued through the International Company's account in Samoa (MT700/MT707) for bulk marine fuel procurement where the bank has a trade-finance relationship with the supplier's bank. Standby LCs (MT760) are used as a performance guarantee for the delivery of bunker fuel or as an indirect backing for trade credit lines. Before each outbound MT103, the operations controller must forward the commercial invoice, bunker receipt copy, BDN, reconciliation of pipeline quantity, and payment-clearing warrant confirming payee background and absence of sanctions/red flags. The bank uses the SWIFT CMS-Payment purpose code '1097' (marine fuel payments) where permitted, or the general trade-payment code for commercial services, with mandatory inclusion of invoice number on the payment narrative. Settlement to high-risk maritime suppliers is limited to direct bank transfers to named payee accounts previously vetted and added to the bank's payee security list; no payments to payable-through accounts, cash or bearer instruments, or third-party transporters without pre-approval are allowed.Onshore WST/USD forward contracts executed through an authorized dealer for documented trade payables, provided that a physical trade contract exists and the CBS underlying-deal principle is metNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) hedged offshore via an international bank not licensed in Samoa, with valuation references to Samoan tala (WST) official exchange rate, used exclusively for net position balancing where no deliverable WST market is economically viableCross-currency swaps (WST/USD) arranged through an offshore treasury counterparty for alignment of long-term bunker storage purchase contracts and terminal facility lease obligationsOption contracts (over-the-counter) written by a major international bank against USD/WST where the Cambodia structure would recognize the underlying fuel procurement as a genuine commercial exposure, obtaining a legal negative-clearance legal opinion that such derivative does not violate CBS exchange-control restrictions on unauthorized back-to-back currency conversions

Section A: Entity Particulars IC name, registration number, registered agent, date of incorporation, director and shareholder details. Non-resident directors are permissible; the agent must maintain current UBO details on the register. Failure to file the ESR notification attracts an administrative penalty of WST 5,000 plus daily accrual, with strike-off or deregistration under the International Companies Act; the registered agent can be fined and the IC's name disclosed to the Samoan tax authority and exchange-of-information partners.
Section B: Relevant Activity Declaration Declaration of relevant activities under ESR Rules, here marine fuel bunkering and lubricant replenishment, categorised as shipping-related maritime services. Declare the activity even where all income is non-Samoan; the ESR regime is activity-based, not profit-based. Failure to file the ESR notification attracts an administrative penalty of WST 5,000 plus daily accrual, with strike-off or deregistration under the International Companies Act; the registered agent can be fined and the IC's name disclosed to the Samoan tax authority and exchange-of-information partners.
