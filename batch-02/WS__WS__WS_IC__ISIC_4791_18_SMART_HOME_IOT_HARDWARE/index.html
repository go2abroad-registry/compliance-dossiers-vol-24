E-government filing portal

For a Samoa International Company, the company automatically obtains a Certificate of Incorporation from the Samoa International Companies Registrar (Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour). To procure a statutory Tax Identification Number, the IC or its registered agent must file an application with the Ministry for Revenue, providing the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office address in Samoa, and details of the authorised representative. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa International Companies are statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax, VAT, and stamp duty. No check-the-box election exists under Samoan domestic law. An IC may, by issuing a formal notice to the Ministry for Revenue, voluntarily file for tax residence if it intends to claim treaty benefits, but the default position is automatic tax exemption for offshore income.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods and certain services supplied for direct export outside Samoa qualify for zero-rating at 0% VAGST, provided the exporter is a registered person and holds evidence that the goods have been physically removed from Samoa within 90 days of supply. Offshore services supplied to non-residents are generally treated as outside the scope of VAGST.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDEC) duly stamped by the Samoa Customs Service
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing removal from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer confirmation showing receipt of foreign currency
  • Proof of export under the VAGST regulations (Form VAGST-E)

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime is currently in force for Samoa. Invoices may be issued in paper or electronic format and must contain the supplier's VAGST registration number, date, description, and amount of tax.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit export claims retroactively within the statutory limitation period. Any fraudulent claim for zero-rating, misrepresentation of export documents, or failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days is punishable as a tax offence under the Tax Administration Act 2012, exposing the IC to a clawback of the wrongly claimed zero-rated input credits, a penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded, and imprisonment of up to 10 years for responsible officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a managed float with current-account convertibility available through CBS-licensed authorized dealers upon presentation of standard trade or service documentation. Capital-account transactions generally require CBS approval for resident entities, but the Samoa International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from the Exchange Control Act and may lawfully conduct all treasury operations in USD or any permitted foreign currency. Profit remittance, capital repatriation, and dividend distribution by the IC are unrestricted and carry no conversion obligation. Practical constraints: WST liquidity is thin, USD settlement requires correspondent banks in Australia or the United States, and counterparties in the local market may require a letter of comfort from the Samoan registered agent confirming the IC's non-resident exchange-control exempt status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must repatriate export proceeds and surrender them to an authorized dealer within 90 days from the date of shipment, with conversion into WST at the prevailing CBS reference rate unless a specific exemption is granted by the CBS. The Samoa International Company is treated as a non-resident for exchange-control purposes and is expressly exempt from surrender and mandatory conversion requirements, retaining a 100% foreign-currency retention ratio in its USD/heavy operational and escrow accounts.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) - Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa, acting jointly with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) for offshore/international company registration and AML/CFT supervision; the Exchange Control Act 1975 and Exchange Control Regulations 1978 are administered exclusively by the CBS.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium direct viability. Stripe and Shopify Payments maintain no licensed merchant-acquiring presence in Samoa and will not settle directly into a Samoan IC account without a verified Merchant of Record (MoR) arrangement. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to an International Company because of its zero-tax exemption. In the unlikely event an IC is found to be carrying on taxable Samoan-source activity, the Income Tax Act 2015 general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR) imposes arm's length pricing on related-party transactions with a de facto materiality threshold of WST 0 for any transaction.

Economic substance scope: Samoa does not enact OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations. Nevertheless, the International Companies Act requires the maintenance of a registered agent and registered office in Samoa. To avoid reclassification by foreign tax authorities or Samoan anti-avoidance measures, the IC should demonstrate practical substance through board meeting minutes, operational decision-making, and the holding of corporate records outside the country.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Revocation of exempt status; immediate assessment of corporate income tax at 25% on all income, plus a 50% civil penalty and 20% per annum interest under the Tax Administration Act 2015

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; the Commissioner may issue a travel ban and asset restraint order against any director
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative strike-off, dissolution, and a fine of up to WST 50,000 against the company; directors may be disqualified

Director personal liability: Civil liability to creditors for debts incurred after the shell company ceased substance; director disqualification for a minimum of 5 years and possible criminal imprisonment of up to 2 years
AML / Anti-Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to WST 1,000,000 for the entity and a further WST 500,000 for each non-compliant officer; imprisonment of up to 10 years for deliberate violations

Director personal liability: Personal fines, corporate asset forfeiture, and up to 10 years imprisonment where directors knowingly participate in laundering proceeds or facilitate sanctions evasion
Insolvency / Fraudulent Preference

Statutory sanction: Voidable transaction; fine of up to WST 10,000; Court may order reinstatement of assets and immediate dissolution of the IC

Director personal liability: Directors may be ordered to make restitution to the company, personally liable for the debts arising from the fraudulent preference, and sentenced to imprisonment of up to 5 years
