Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) — official internet portal: https://www.sra.gov.ws, with taxpayer e-Services (TIN registration, VAGST filings, customs payment gateway).

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Samoa International Company, the statutory route is: (1) file Form TIN-1 with the SRA presenting the certificate of incorporation issued by the Samoa International Companies and Trust Registry, a certified copy of the memorandum and articles of association, and evidence of appointment of a Samoa-registered agent; (2) if any taxable supply will be made in Samoa, additionally lodge Form VAGST-1 to obtain a VAGST registration number; (3) any non-resident entity not physically in Samoa must maintain a local registered agent and a resident tax contact to receive notices; (4) the SRA issues the TIN within approximately 5 business days after satisfactory compliance checks.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Samoa International Companies are statutorily exempt from Samoan national income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax on all activities, income, or profits arising in or derived from outside Samoa. They are also exempt from stamp duty, are outside exchange-control restrictions, and are not regarded as carrying on business in Samoa if they refrain from transactions with Samoan residents and from holding Samoan land or certain excluded assets. — For a foreign-owned Samoa International Company, the statutory route is: (1) file Form TIN-1 with the SRA presenting the certificate of incorporation issued by the Samoa International Companies and Trust Registry, a certified copy of the memorandum and articles of association, and evidence of appointment of a Samoa-registered agent; (2) if any taxable supply will be made in Samoa, additionally lodge Form VAGST-1 to obtain a VAGST registration number; (3) any non-resident entity not physically in Samoa must maintain a local registered agent and a resident tax contact to receive notices; (4) the SRA issues the TIN within approximately 5 business days after satisfactory compliance checks.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Samoa International Companies are statutorily exempt from Samoan national income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax on all activities, income, or profits arising in or derived from outside Samoa. They are also exempt from stamp duty, are outside exchange-control restrictions, and are not regarded as carrying on business in Samoa if they refrain from transactions with Samoan residents and from holding Samoan land or certain excluded assets.

Available election forms

Samoa does not have a US-style check-the-box, partnership classification, or hybrid mismatch election. The only relevant statutory choices are the capacity to elect a segregated account company under the International Companies Act 2008 and the election to re-register as a local/domestic company, which extinguishes the tax-exempt status. No double-taxation treaty elections are available for the tax-exempt International Company structure.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory VAGST registration arises when the value of taxable supplies by an entity in Samoa equals or exceeds WST 100,000 within any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is available to a person making taxable supplies below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies where VAGST-registered persons export goods from Samoa to a place outside Samoa within the statutory timeframe, retain acceptable transportation evidence, and prove the goods were not used, consumed, or resupplied in Samoa before export. Offshore services supplied to a non-resident recipient are also zero-rated if the effective use and enjoyment takes place outside Samoa and the supplier carries contemporaneous contract, service delivery, and payment documentation. The International Company's tax-exempt status does not itself confer VAGST input credits, but compliance with the Substantiation Rules for Exports permits net refunds of VAGST input tax on exported goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/Form F-15) stamped by the Samoa Customs Division
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill showing physical export from Samoa to the destination market
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and underlying purchase order/contract
  • Inward remittance certificate (IRC) issued by a SAMOA-licensed bank confirming receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency
  • Bank credit advice and SWIFT MT103/TT wire confirmation matching the invoiced export consignment
  • VAGST export certificate (Commissioner-prescribed export evidence form) endorsed by the exporter

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping of invoices. VAGST invoices must carry the supplier's TIN, a sequentially numbered invoice reference, an itemized supply description, the VAGST amount shown separately, and the date of supply. The SRA operates an electronic filing portal for returns and customs entries, but there is no API-based third-party invoice clearance duty. The Asycuda World customs single-window is used for import and export declarations, but that is not a revenue invoicing clearance chain.

The SRA may retrospectively deny input tax credits and claw back the entire refund where export evidence is missing, forged, or non-compliant. Under the Revenue Administration Act 2016 and the VAGST Act 2003, a fraudulent export zero-rating claim attracts a civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, default interest, and, in egregious cases, criminal prosecution with fines and imprisonment. Directors and the beneficial owner of the International Company can be held jointly and severally liable for any refund received or offset on the strength of false documentation. Repeated falsification results in mandatory deregistration from VAGST and a statutory debarment period for future export-credit claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a non-fully-convertible currency administered under a strict, discretionary exchange-control regime. Current-account payments (trade imports, contract-manufacturing fees, logistics, insurance, and professional charges) are generally permissible through authorized dealers upon completion of CBS-prescribed declaration forms, whereas capital-account transactions (inward portfolio investment, offshore borrowing, repatriation of capital injected by non-residents) require prior CBS approval unless a general exemption applies. A Samoa International Company holding non-resident status is deemed outside the exchange-control perimeter for all-foreign-currency transactions occurring offshore; it may lawfully maintain USD external accounts, remit dividends and repay shareholder loans to non-residents, and convert only genuine Samoan-source earnings into WST. Profit-remittance hurdles are minimal for an offshore-trading IC, but any onshore Samoan-source revenue would require CBS 'good standing' certification, WST conversion through an authorized dealer, and international-company audit evidence of distributable reserves.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1977, resident physical exporters must obtain payment for exported goods within 180 days of shipment and must offer all foreign-currency export proceeds for sale to an authorized dealer within 30 days of receipt, converting into WST, unless a specific or general CBS exemption is obtained. For the WS_IC International Company, CBS accords a general exemption from surrender and offer-for-sale obligations in respect of exports of goods that do not originate in, and are not shipped from, Samoa, provided all proceeds are received in the IC's designated USD external account and are supported by the underlying commercial invoices, bills of lading, SWIFT credit advices, and the registered agent's certificate of non-resident status. The Company is nonetheless advised to maintain a standing compliance file evidencing the general-exemption position to pre-empt bank-level questioning on each inward credit.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, being the designated exchange-control authority administering the Exchange Control Act 1976, as amended, and the Exchange Control Regulations 1977, as amended in 2019, together with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) in respect of international-company licensing and supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

LAW_RATED_LOW_FEASIBILITY - Offshore digital EMI or neo-bank accounts (for example, Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, or USD-only digital platforms) are, in the majority of cases, legally unavailable to a Samoa-registered International Company. While the non-resident IC is not caught by the resident prohibition on offshore accounts, global EMI providers apply internal country-exclusion lists and correspondent-bank de-risking policies that treat Samoa as a small-island low-volume jurisdiction; onboarding is usually declined. Any WST conversion or Samoan-source transaction through an EMI is prohibited without CBS approval.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Share-capital and shareholder-loan injections are effected via SWIFT MT103 or equivalent Fedwire/CHIPS transfers in USD directly into the WS_IC's external USD account at ANZ Bank (Samoa) Limited or Bank of South Pacific (Samoa) Limited. The payment message must state the IMF BPM6 financial-account purpose 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' and include the explanatory reference 'WS_IC Share Capital Injection – [Name of Subscriber]'; the receiving Samoan authorized dealer will record the inward foreign-currency credit against the IC's international-company registration number and apply the CBS general exemption applicable to non-resident international companies. The compliance verification pack comprises a board resolution approving the share issuance, share-allotment certificates entered in the IC's register, a receipted bank credit advice, a Certificate of Incumbency from the registered agent, and source-of-funds declarations from each subscriber. No prior CBS specific licence is required solely to receive foreign-currency equity offshore; any subsequent conversion into WST or application of funds to onshore Samoan business requires CBS exchange-control approval.Procurement disbursements for polyethersulfone (PES) hollow-fiber resin, polyurethane potting compound, spin-pack components, and contract manufacturing services are cleared through (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for advance deposits (typically 10-30% against pro-forma invoices, with the balance settled after shipping-document inspection) and (ii) irrevocable commercial documentary Letters of Credit issued by the IC's Samoan bank through SWIFT MT700 in favor of Taiwanese and Chinese suppliers, including MT707 amendments for quantity/price adjustments and MT752 reimbursement authorizations. Outbound sales of finished disposable dialysis hemofilters to distributors and hospital groups are collected through MT103 T/T remittances with a 30-60-day tenor or through MT700-documented import LCs opened by the buyer, all cleared via the IC's USD nostro correspondents. Each outward payment above USD 500,000 is supported by the underlying trade contract, commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, and, where Samoan involvement is absent, a written reliance memo confirming the IC's non-resident exchange-control exemption.

Any WST conversion or Samoan-source transaction through an EMI is prohibited without CBS approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None for exempt International Companies; Samoa does not impose local file or master file thresholds on ICs. Tax-resident companies engaging in connected cross-border transactions above WST 1,000,000 are expected to maintain contemporaneous OECD-compliant transfer pricing documentation.

Economic substance scope: Samoa applies Economic Substance Requirements to International Companies under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019. A company carrying on relevant activities must have an office, employees, and core income-generating activities in Samoa. Manufacturing and distribution of dialysis products qualify as relevant activities if they are directed and managed in Samoa; otherwise the IC must file an annual economic substance notification declaring no relevant activity and providing supporting documentation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abuse of Tax Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Loss of exemption and reclassification as a taxable resident company; reassessment at 27% corporate income tax on all profits plus civil penalty up to 200% of the tax liability and monthly compounding interest.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax and penalties where the exemption was obtained through fraudulent or misleading declarations; fraudulent evasion is a criminal offense carrying imprisonment up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Samoa may strike the company off the Register, impose civil fines, and disclose information to foreign tax authorities under the multilateral competent authority agreement; the entity becomes vulnerable to being characterized as a shell by OECD/FATF.

Director personal liability: Directors may face disqualification orders, travel bans, and personal fines for failing to comply with economic substance and beneficial ownership filing obligations.
AML / Money Laundering through Medical Goods Mis-Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and national AML/CFT regulations, penalties include fines up to WST 2 million, seizure of assets, and imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally criminally liable for deliberate failure to file suspicious transaction reports or for participation in money laundering; personal bank accounts may be frozen and travel restricted.
Transfer Pricing / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: While Samoa itself applies no TP sanction to exempt ICs, the counterparty jurisdiction may recharacterize the transactions, adjust taxable profits, and impose a penalty equal to 100% of the additional tax plus interest.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable if they knowingly issue false invoices or manipulate accounting records, leading to administrative fines and exposure to cross-border extradition under mutual legal assistance treaties.
