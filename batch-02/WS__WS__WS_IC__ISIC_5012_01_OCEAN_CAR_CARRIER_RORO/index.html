Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the International Companies Act 1987, an International Company is exempt from Samoa domestic income tax and capital gains tax, provided it does not carry on business in Samoa other than offshore business. An IC may elect to be treated as an ordinary local company under the Companies Act 2001, thereby becoming subject to Samoa corporate income tax on worldwide income; alternatively, it may elect to maintain tax-exempt status by adhering to the annual licence fee filing and limitation on local-source activities. Samoa has not adopted check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; entity classification follows the statutory International Company regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (SAT 100,000) annual taxable turnover of goods or services supplied in Samoa

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to goods exported from Samoa within 30 days of the date of supply, to international transport services directly related to goods carried from Samoa to a destination outside Samoa (including deep-sea RoRo vehicle transport), and to services supplied directly for or in connection with exported goods. The supplier must retain documentary evidence proving export and must not be a registered person in Samoa making local consumption. Repair and maintenance of ships used in international traffic also qualify where the customer is a non-resident.

  • Customs Export Declaration (approved by Samoa Customs Service)
  • Bill of Lading (signed by the ocean carrier or carrier's agent evidencing carriage of vehicles to a foreign port)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank SWIFT MT103 showing foreign currency credit of the export sale proceeds)

Samoa does not currently operate a mandatory real-time e-invoicing fiscal clearance system. VAGST-registered taxpayers must issue tax invoices containing prescribed particulars, retain electronic or paper records, and file periodic VAGST returns through the Ministry for Revenue e-services portal. No digital stamping or API clearance is required.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit zero-rated export claims and require full substantiation at any time within 5 years after the taxable period. For fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims, Samoa law imposes a shortfall penalty of 150% of the unpaid VAGST plus interest at the prescribed late payment rate, civil penalties up to WST 100,000, retrospective clawback of the VAT input credits claimed in connection with the export, and criminal sanctions including imprisonment of up to 3 years for tax evasion or falsifying documents. Customs undervaluation or misdeclaration penalties also apply under the Customs Management Act 2014.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tala (WST) is not a freely internationally convertible currency. The CBS administers a regulated exchange-control regime under the Exchange Control Act; current-account payments are convertible through authorised dealers for genuine trade and operating expenses. Capital-account inflows into an International Company are generally exempt from CBS prior approval when the shareholder is non-resident and the proceeds are credited to a designated foreign-currency account. Profit remittances and dividend distributions to non-resident members are permitted without exchange-control restriction once the International Company confirms its exempt status under the International Companies Act, and no Samoan tax or withholding obligation attaches to such distributions. Local WST conversion is reserved for authorised-dealer transactions and should be limited to actual Samoan operating needs to avoid unnecessary convertibility friction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a resident Samoan exporter, the statutory rule is repatriation of foreign-currency export proceeds within 30 days and mandatory conversion of 100% of such proceeds into WST through an authorised dealer unless a CBS exemption is obtained. For a non-resident-owned International Company engaged in RORO vehicle transport, freight revenue earned outside Samoa is treated as offshore foreign-currency income and is not subject to the resident surrender regime. Where voyage or freight receipts are collected in a Samoa bank account, the compliant structure is to maintain a CBS-approved Foreign Currency Account and apply for a general or specific exchange-control exemption so that the mandatory WST conversion ratio does not apply. Any local disbursement requiring WST should be funded through a controlled conversion executed by the authorised dealer, documented by the underlying invoice and board resolution.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Foreign Exchange and Banking Supervision Department; Samoa International Financial Services Authority (SIFA) as the licensing authority for International Companies

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating is Low as a primary treasury rail and Medium only as an ancillary collection channel. An offshore digital EMI that is neither licensed nor supervised by the CBS cannot lawfully maintain a Samoa operating account, act as capital escrow custodian, or open a documentary letter of credit. A foreign EMI authorised in a reputable jurisdiction may be used to collect freight receivables and route funds by SWIFT to the Samoan bank, but it cannot replace the underlying CBS-licensed banking relationship. The EMI must apply equivalent AML/KYC diligence, verify the International Company charter and UBO structure, and all onward payments must be made to the named operational or escrow account at an authorised dealer.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No formal Samoan transfer pricing threshold for exempt International Companies; a transfer pricing documentation set, including Local File and Master File, is prudent where gross transactions with related parties exceed WST 1,000,000 or where the company materially shifts profits through charter hire arrangements.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to Samoa International Companies carrying on relevant activities, with deep-sea Ro-Ro vehicle transport classified as a relevant activity. The company must be managed and controlled in Samoa, maintain adequate physical premises, employ competent local staff, and incur sufficient operating expenditure; an annual ESR notification and substance report must be submitted to the Samoa Registrar of International Companies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Abusing International Company Exemption

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 150% of unpaid Samoan tax; default interest at 5% above government lending rates; criminal fine up to WST 50,000; confiscation of revenues attributable to the Samoan-source shipping leg.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are personally liable for the unpaid tax; travel bans may be imposed; management disqualification for up to 10 years; imprisonment for up to 7 years for tax fraud.
Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Fine up to WST 250,000 for failing the economic substance test; annual penalty up to WST 50,000 for non-compliance; Registrar may strike off the company and exchange information with foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may be jointly liable for the corporate fine; disgorgement of profits; travel restrictions; director disqualification for up to 5 years; imprisonment up to 3 years for submitting false substance information.
