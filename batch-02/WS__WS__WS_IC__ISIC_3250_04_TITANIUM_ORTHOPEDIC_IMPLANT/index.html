Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or disregarded-entity election exists in Samoa. Available statutory elections: (i) election to operate as an exempt International Company under the International Companies Act 1987; (ii) voluntary VAGST registration for non-residents supplying connected services in Samoa; (iii) filing of a notice with the Registrar to revoke the IC exemption and become a domestic taxable company; (iv) hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized, and Samoa applies entity-level characterization under the Income Tax Act 2015.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 annual taxable turnover; the same threshold applies to non-resident offshore suppliers of digital and connected services to Samoan consumers, below which registration is voluntary.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Samoa by the supplier or under the supplier's instructions qualify for the 0% VAGST rate. Offshore services supplied to a recipient outside Samoa at the time of performance are zero-rated. Supplies made by an International Company to foreign clients, and transactions among International Companies in Samoa, are treated as offshore and outside the VAGST net; the IC's Certificate of Exemption operates as conclusive evidence of non-liability for domestic tax on such exports.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World CusDec)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Samoan foreign exchange record evidencing FX repatriation
  • International Company Certificate of Exemption and Certificate of Good Standing

No real-time e-invoicing fiscal clearance, API transmission, or digital stamping mandate is currently in force. VAGST-compliant paper or electronic tax invoices must be issued and retained for 7 years. Export declarations are processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs clearance system.

Fraudulent export zero-rating claims attract recovery of the unpaid VAGST, monthly late-payment interest at 1%, late-return penalties of WST 100 per return, and fines of up to WST 200,000 with imprisonment for up to 7 years under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2021. The Customs Act 2014 also authorizes seizure of goods and retrospective seven-year reassessment for false declarations. The SIFA/Registrar may revoke an International Company's Certificate of Exemption for material misrepresentation in connection with tax-refund schemes, exposing the former-IC to entity-level tax under the Income Tax Act 2015.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa observes IMF Article VIII current-account convertibility: the Samoa tala (WST) is freely convertible for trade, services, dividends, and current transfers. Capital-account operations, including non-resident equity injections, intercompany loans, derivative contracts, and capital repatriation, require prior CBS exchange-control approval obtained through an authorized dealer. Profit remittance from a Samoa International Company is permissible once audited financials, statutory filings, and any Samoan registration or health-ministry levies are settled, and after lodgement of a CBS EC-X application with resolutions and distributor agreements evidencing the underlying orthopedic-implant trade.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days from the shipment date and surrendered to an authorized dealer within 5 business days of receipt, with 100% of foreign-currency earnings mandatorily converted into WST at the dealer's prevailing buying rate. Foreign-currency retention is granted only by advance CBS written exemption for documented trade offsets or debt service; breaches attract fines and compulsory forfeiture under the Exchange Control Act, with monthly EC-X compliance returns filed by the exporter's bank.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), the sole monetary authority and foreign-exchange regulator, operating under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, the Exchange Control Act 1976, and CBS binding directives on authorized dealers and cross-border capital flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Samoa has no electronic-money-institution licensing regime; cross-border payment services are reserved to CBS-licensed commercial banks and registered money-transfer operators. Offshore digital EMIs cannot open WST settlement accounts or access local clearing, and would require full BSP/SCB bank sponsorship with a corresponding AML/CFT agent agreement; the treasury blueprint therefore relies on commercial-bank SWIFT rails for all material inbound and outbound liquidity.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% standard corporate income tax for Samoan-resident companies on Samoan-source and foreign-source income attributable to an onshore business; effective rate 0% for a qualifying International Company (IC) registered under the International Companies Act 2012 (as amended), which is fully exempt from income tax on all income other than income derived from carrying on business in Samoa.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory dividend withholding tax on dividends paid by a Samoan onshore-resident company to a non-resident; dividends declared by an International Company are entirely outside the Samoan withholding tax net because the IC is an exempt entity and does not have a Samoan tax status for distribution purposes, enabling tax-free repatriation to the trust beneficiary.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and annual license fee are payable within 30 days of the anniversary of incorporation; a company incorporated multiple years ago must file on the same anniversary date each year even if no business activity occurred.

Deadline: · Frequency:

For onshore corporations: return due on or before 30 June following a 31 December income year (i.e., not later than six months after the income year end).

Box 3 - Authorized Share Capital and Share Classes Aggregate authorized capital, number of issued shares, and class rights distinguishing voting, non-voting, and bearer shares (bearer shares may be held only by a licensed custodian). No minimum capital requirement; non-resident founders commonly declare authorized capital of USD 50,000 to minimize the annual fee tier. Failure to file Form IC-AR within 30 days of the anniversary: late penalty of 10% of the annual license fee plus 1% per month of continued default; after three months the Registrar may strike the IC off the register without court order, with consequential loss of legal capacity and forfeiture of the Samoan registered office; personal liability may then attach to directors who continue to trade.
Box 4 - Register of Directors and Shareholders Names, addresses, and nationalities of all directors and shareholders, and confirmation that the statutory registers are maintained at the registered office in Samoa. Nominee director details may be shown, but the beneficial ownership declaration under the Beneficial Ownership Act 2018 must separately disclose the ultimate settlor and beneficiaries. Failure to file Form IC-AR within 30 days of the anniversary: late penalty of 10% of the annual license fee plus 1% per month of continued default; after three months the Registrar may strike the IC off the register without court order, with consequential loss of legal capacity and forfeiture of the Samoan registered office; personal liability may then attach to directors who continue to trade.
Box 5 - Declaration of No Onshore Business Statutory declaration that the IC has not carried on business in Samoa during the preceding year other than transactions incidental to its offshore status. Any local administrative activity (paying the agent, holding board minutes) is permissible; sales, procurement, or payroll for operations performed in Samoa is a violation that voids the exemption. Failure to file Form IC-AR within 30 days of the anniversary: late penalty of 10% of the annual license fee plus 1% per month of continued default; after three months the Registrar may strike the IC off the register without court order, with consequential loss of legal capacity and forfeiture of the Samoan registered office; personal liability may then attach to directors who continue to trade.
Box 6 - Economic Substance Self-Assessment Self-declaration of whether the IC conducts a 'relevant activity' (IP holding, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service center, banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, or pure equity holding). A pure equity holding IC must declare 'low substance' and will be exempt from ESR tracking but must still be outside the financial intermediary sector; an IP-holding IC for titanium implant patents must declare high substance and is subject to sectoral review. Failure to file Form IC-AR within 30 days of the anniversary: late penalty of 10% of the annual license fee plus 1% per month of continued default; after three months the Registrar may strike the IC off the register without court order, with consequential loss of legal capacity and forfeiture of the Samoan registered office; personal liability may then attach to directors who continue to trade.
Box 7 - Registered Agent Compliance Certificate Confirmation by the licensed Samoan registered agent that the IC is in good standing, has paid the annual fee, and holds current AML/CFT client due diligence records. The agent must approve any wire transfers (SWIFT) and letter of credit documents for which it provides corporate authorization; the agent's consent to each export LC is mandatory if the agent is a signatory. Failure to file Form IC-AR within 30 days of the anniversary: late penalty of 10% of the annual license fee plus 1% per month of continued default; after three months the Registrar may strike the IC off the register without court order, with consequential loss of legal capacity and forfeiture of the Samoan registered office; personal liability may then attach to directors who continue to trade.
Box 8 - Annual Fee Computation Computation of the annual registry fee on a sliding scale by authorized capital (approximately USD 100 for capital up to USD 10,000, USD 200 up to USD 1,000,000, USD 500 up to USD 10,000,000, and USD 1,000 above). A late filing fee of 10% of the annual fee is applied for the first month of delinquency and 1% per additional month; continued default permits summary strike-off and asset forfeiture to the Crown. Failure to file Form IC-AR within 30 days of the anniversary: late penalty of 10% of the annual license fee plus 1% per month of continued default; after three months the Registrar may strike the IC off the register without court order, with consequential loss of legal capacity and forfeiture of the Samoan registered office; personal liability may then attach to directors who continue to trade.
Line 1 - Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and Registered Name Samoan TIN and legal entity name as registered onshore. Not applicable to an IC; an IC has no Samoan TIN unless it mistakenly registers for onshore tax, which would expose it to reclassification. Late filing penalty of USD 200 per month up to a maximum of USD 2,000, plus interest at 12% per annum on any unpaid tax, and additional penalties of up to 100% of the tax where the Commissioner determines willful evasion.
Line 2 - Assessable Income Reconciliation Total Samoan-source gross income, deductions, taxable income, and loss carryforward calculations subject to the 27% rate. If an IC unintentionally derives Samoan-source income, the exemption is fully lost for such income and the 27% rate plus 12% per annum late payment interest applies. Late filing penalty of USD 200 per month up to a maximum of USD 2,000, plus interest at 12% per annum on any unpaid tax, and additional penalties of up to 100% of the tax where the Commissioner determines willful evasion.
Line 3 - Non-Resident Withholding Tax Schedule Schedule of dividends, interest, and royalties paid to non-residents, with the respective 15% withholding tax summarized. Irrelevant for a pure IC, which is exempt from dividend withholding; relevance arises only in the defunct onshore-operation scenario. Late filing penalty of USD 200 per month up to a maximum of USD 2,000, plus interest at 12% per annum on any unpaid tax, and additional penalties of up to 100% of the tax where the Commissioner determines willful evasion.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: SAT 1,000,000 (approximately USD 380,000) aggregate annual cross-border related-party transactions per taxpayer: transactions above this threshold trigger the obligation to maintain contemporaneous Local File and Master File documentation under the Income Tax Act 2015 and Tax Administration Act 2015; however, an exempt International Company has no Samoan tax liability and therefore no enforceable Samoan TP filing obligation - the operative TP burden resides in the manufacturing jurisdiction where the titanium implant plant operates and where intercompany royalties, management fees, and finished-good sales are priced.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's International Companies (Economic Substance) Amendment Act 2020, aligned with the EU Code of Conduct Group requirements, applies to ICs carrying on relevant activities: banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, pure equity holding, holding of intellectual property, and distribution and service centers. Manufacturing of titanium orthopedic implants is not itself a listed relevant activity; nevertheless, an IC that owns the patent, trademark, or process IP for the electron-beam 3D melting technology is classified as an IP holding company and must satisfy a two-prong test: (i) core income-generating activities (i.e., active patent management, grant of licenses, acceptance of financial risk, negotiation of contracts) must be performed by personnel physically present in Samoa on a fit-and-proper basis; and (ii) adequate physical assets, qualified staff, and annual expenditures must be proportionate to the IP-derived income. A pure equity holding IC that merely owns shares of the manufacturing subsidiary is subject only to a reduced 'substance-in-minimal-requirements' standard, requiring a Samoan registered office and annual confirmation. Failure of ESR cascades into automatic reclassification as an onshore resident company, retroactive 27% tax on revenues, and de-registration of the IC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Onshore Business Prohibition Breach

Statutory sanction: Loss of all IC exemptions retroactively; the Commissioner of the Samoa Revenue Authority assesses tax at 27% on all onshore-derived revenue plus late-payment interest at 12% per annum and a civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged; cumulative fines of up to SAT 50,000 and forfeiture of assets to the State.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permitted the onshore operations are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax and penalties; the Court may issue a freezing order over personal bank accounts and a travel ban pending full settlement, with imprisonment of up to seven years for willful submission of false declarations to the Registrar.
Shell Company - Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registrar of International Companies strikes the IC off after 30 days' notice; the tax exemption is revoked and the company is taxed on worldwide source income at 27% under the Income Tax Act 2015; additional ESR penalty of SAT 10,000 per month of non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed Samoan registered agent are jointly liable for the unpaid ESR-specific fine and for the costs of the strike-off; the beneficial owner may be barred from operating any Samoa-incorporated vehicle for a period of five years, and the Court may order a restitution obligation equal to the taxes avoided, plus a travel restriction pending the assessment.
Trade-Based Money Laundering and Export Misinvoicing

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter Financing of Terrorism Act 2018: civil penalties up to SAT 200,000 per transaction, revocation of the licensed agent, forfeiture of the entire transaction value to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit, and asset restraint orders.

Director personal liability: Directors, bank signatories, and the trust protector are exposed as principals in the money laundering offense punishable by imprisonment of up to twelve years; in cross-border letters of credit, each L/C endorsement carries personal liability to the confirming bank for the full face value, and the offender is subject to international extradition and a SAMOA High Court asset freeze over their New Zealand and Australian properties.
Unlicensed Medical Device Manufacturing and Export

Statutory sanction: Prohibition of the manufacturing facility, public recall of the implant lots, criminal fines of up to SAT 100,000 for the corporation, cancellation of the company's import/export license, and a directive to the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour to dissolve the IC.

Director personal liability: Each director who signed the declaration of the device trial data is personally liable for wrongful certification; the court may order civil restitution to every patient harmed by the titanium implant and a term of imprisonment of up to three years for the director of production; the exporting director additionally faces personal suit in the destination jurisdiction under product liability law.
Breach of Beneficial Ownership Disclosure and AML Registration

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Beneficial Ownership Act 2018 of up to SAT 25,000 for the company and SAT 5,000 per director per day of continuing non-disclosure; the Registrar's right to strike off the IC and confiscate the shares held by the non-disclosing principal.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent are personally jointly and severally liable for the daily fines; the court may issue a personal disqualification order restricting the director from managing any company in Samoa or New Zealand for up to six years and may impose a detention order preventing the director from leaving Samoa until the beneficial ownership information is corrected.
