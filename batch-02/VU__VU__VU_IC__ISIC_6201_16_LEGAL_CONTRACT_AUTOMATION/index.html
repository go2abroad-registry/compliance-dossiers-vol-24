Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; taxpayer portal at https://vrc.gov.vu; company registry at Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) with VFSC under the International Companies Act Cap 222 via a licensed local registered agent (same-day or next-day incorporation); 2) Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, Company Code, and stamped Memorandum and Articles of Association; 3) Pay the annual VFSC business licence fee (flat fee, revenue-independent, mandatory for every IC); 4) Apply to VRC for VAT registration ONLY if the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding VUV 5,000,000 in any 12-month period or voluntarily opts in; an offshore-only SaaS platform (ISIC 6201) exporting to non-residents is not VAT-registrable and requires no VRC TIN; 5) No income-tax TIN, no employer PAYE registration, and no corporate income-tax filing obligation exist for ICs under the Vanuatu zero-corporate-tax regime.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — 1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) with VFSC under the International Companies Act Cap 222 via a licensed local registered agent (same-day or next-day incorporation); 2) Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, Company Code, and stamped Memorandum and Articles of Association; 3) Pay the annual VFSC business licence fee (flat fee, revenue-independent, mandatory for every IC); 4) Apply to VRC for VAT registration ONLY if the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding VUV 5,000,000 in any 12-month period or voluntarily opts in; an offshore-only SaaS platform (ISIC 6201) exporting to non-residents is not VAT-registrable and requires no VRC TIN; 5) No income-tax TIN, no employer PAYE registration, and no corporate income-tax filing obligation exist for ICs under the Vanuatu zero-corporate-tax regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box, US S-corp, or hybrid-mismatch entity classification election. An International Company automatically receives the unconditional statutory tax exemption under Section 148 of the International Companies Act Cap 222 (no tax on income, assets, capital gains, dividends, interest, or royalties). For VAT purposes, an IC may elect voluntary registration under the VAT Act Cap 248 even below the VUV 5,000,000 threshold, and may elect zero-rating for exported services to non-resident recipients; conversely it may elect to remain wholly outside the VAT net by supplying only offshore services.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 (approximately USD 41,700) of annual taxable supplies made within or connected to Vanuatu; an International Company whose entire business is the export of LegalTech SaaS services to non-resident customers has no Vanuatu VAT nexus and is neither required nor eligible to register in respect of those offshore revenues

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under the VAT Act Cap 248 for (a) goods physically exported from Vanuatu evidenced by customs documentation, and (b) services supplied to a recipient who is not resident in Vanuatu and who consumes the services wholly outside Vanuatu, provided no local economic use arises; qualifying categories expressly include cloud document automation, e-signature authentication, and other digital business-to-business services delivered offshore. The IC must retain proof that the recipient is non-resident, that payment originates offshore, and that the service is not enjoyed in Vanuatu; input VAT credits may be claimed on costs attributable to zero-rated export activity where the IC is VAT-registered.

  • ASYCUDA World Customs Export Declaration (XGD) with customs receipt or export clearance stamp
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export movement (goods)
  • Non-resident customer agreement / SaaS subscription terms and delivery records (services)
  • Foreign-currency bank statement or wire transfer advice evidencing inbound settlement (no formal Inward Remittance Certificate exists in Vanuatu due to the absence of exchange controls)

None. Vanuatu does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, digital stamping, or clearance-server validation. VAT invoices must be issued in paper or PDF format and contain the statutory fields prescribed by the VAT Act Cap 248 Section 25; VRC operates an IFMIS modernisation programme but no third-party electronic invoice clearance obligation exists as of the current tax year.

VRC audit review focuses on export substantiation, non-resident counter-party identity, and absence of local consumption. Fraudulent or erroneous zero-rating claims, false VAT refund claims, or misuse of the IC export exemption trigger: (i) civil assessment of underpaid VAT plus a civil penalty of up to 200% of the VAT evaded; (ii) criminal liability under the VAT Act Cap 248 with fines of up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years; (iii) retrospective clawback of input VAT credits and export refunds plus interest; and (iv) administrative sanctions by VFSC including deregistration or strike-off of the International Company for abuse of the tax-exempt licence. Although the IC itself has no corporate income-tax exposure, inaccurate export declarations may also attract Customs offences under the Customs Act and material prejudice to annual VFSC licence renewal.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for current account and most capital account transactions. There are no general currency convertibility restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, loan repayments, or repatriation of foreign direct investment. Inward and outward payments are not subject to routine prior central bank approval, but authorized dealer banks must apply customer due diligence and statistical reporting thresholds under RBV regulations and the Exchange Control Act. Capital account flows are treated as permissible unless the transaction implicates UN/OFAC sanctions, anti-money-laundering suspicion, or a false trade declaration.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory mandate exists requiring surrender of export proceeds to the central bank and no minimum percentage of foreign currency earnings must be converted into VUV. Exporters may maintain foreign currency accounts with licensed banks in Vanuatu. However, all export receipts must be reported to the authorized dealer bank for statistical balance-of-payments purposes, and substantial cash transactions must be declared to the RBV Financial Intelligence Unit under the AML/CFT regime. Non-compliance with reporting may result in administrative penalties.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) acting under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 68] and the AML/CFT statutory framework, in conjunction with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for licensing and corporate compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium. Vanuatu does not regulate foreign electronic money institutions (EMIs) as a separate passportable class. Offshore EMIs can generally be used to acquire SaaS card payments and to facilitate cross-border payouts in USD/EUR/GBP without a Vanuatu banking license, provided they do not hold themselves out as carrying on banking business in Vanuatu. However, tier-1 Vanuatu banks treat unregulated EMI balances with heightened source-of-funds scrutiny and may reject them for primary treasury settlement. A licensed card acquirer and a recognized correspondent bank are preferred for the LegalTech SaaS model.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital transfers must be routed through an authorized dealer bank in Vanuatu using the SWIFT MT103 message format. The sending bank should use purpose field code describing equity injection or intercompany capital contribution; common international purpose codes include 6010 (FDI equity) or a descriptive text consistent with OECD Balance of Payments classification. The recipient Vanuatu bank will require a board resolution, a copy of the investment agreement, the International Company's certificate of incorporation, beneficial ownership declaration, and, for sums exceeding statutory thresholds, an AML source-of-funds statement. No prior RBV exchange control approval is required, but the receiving bank must register the transaction in its balance-of-payments reporting to the Reserve Bank.Trade settlements for SaaS licensing, professional fees, and cloud infrastructure costs should be executed via SWIFT MT103 messages using correspondent clearing through ANZ/BRED and their global intermediaries. A commercial letter of credit is generally unsuitable for intangible services, so documentary collection is customarily replaced by a bank-to-bank wire against invoices and electronic acceptance certificates. For high-value settlements in USD or EUR, the treasury may use correspondent bank accounts at JPMorgan, Citibank, or BNP Paribas via the Vanuatu bank's nostril account network; intraday liquidity discipline should be maintained by pre-funding the operating account one day prior to scheduled outbound payments.Vanuatu vatu/USD deliverable forward contracts with licensed commercial banks onshore, provided the underlying commercial exposure is evidenced by signed SaaS contracts or supplier invoices.Cross-currency swaps between VUV and USD/AUD/EUR executed through a licensed authorized dealer bank for treasury risk management, subject to the new ISDA/CPS standard master agreement.Offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) contracts referencing VUV, permitted for Vanuatu corporates when used solely to hedge expected non-VUV cash flows and when the counterparty is a reputable investment-grade bank; no onshore NDF market exists.Foreign exchange option collars or vanilla options on USD/VUV offered by the local commercial bank treasury desks, possible when bilateral credit limits are established and the positive accounting treatment is confirmed under IFRS 9 hedge accounting.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold under Vanuatu law because no corporate income tax regime exists. However, the Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations require a filing for ICs engaged in geographically mobile activities if the IC is treated as tax resident in Vanuatu; the gross income threshold for ESR designation is 3,000,000 Vatu (approx. USD 25,000) per annum.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to companies tax resident in Vanuatu. A Vanuatu International Company is generally non-resident if its central management and control are outside Vanuatu. If the IC is controlled from Vanuatu, ESR requires physical presence, local employees, and expenditure proportionate to the income from covered activities. For remote/digital LegalTech SaaS operations, the covered activity test typically fails if no core income-generating functions are performed in Vanuatu.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Representation

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to VT 5,000,000 (approx. USD 42,000) and/or imprisonment for up to 12 months under the International Companies Act and Income Tax (Amendment) provisions.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and penalties if they knowingly authorized the false tax-exemption claim; a criminal conviction can trigger a travel ban and asset forfeiture.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the International Companies Register, voiding of all contracts signed under the IC, and a direct VFSC fine of VT 200,000 (approx. USD 1,700) for failure to supply economic substance information.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal restoration orders for all liabilities incurred after the IC became shell in substance; revocation of the right to act as a director of any Vanuatu company for 5 years.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalty of up to VT 10,000,000 (approx. USD 85,000) and imprisonment of up to 15 years under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for AML penalties and are prohibited from leaving Vanuatu pending investigation; foreign directors may face extradition assistance requests.
Illegal Dividend or Return of Capital

Statutory sanction: Excess distribution voidable; director liability for the full amount distributed plus interest. VFSC may order restoration of corporate records and a public reprimand.

Director personal liability: Directors become personally liable for reprobating debts; in cases of fraudulent trading, civil restitution is extended to family trusts or related underlying companies, and imprisonment up to 7 years may be imposed.
