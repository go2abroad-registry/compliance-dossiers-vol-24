Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (Samoa Tax Administration), Apia, Samoa, headed by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue within the Ministry of Finance framework; official tax and customs portal: https://www.mof.gov.ws.

E-government filing portal

For a Samoa International Company (IC): (1) engage a SIFA-licensed registered agent (trustee or corporate service provider) to incorporate under the International Companies Act 2009; (2) the Registrar of the Samoa International Financial Authority (SIFA) issues a Company Registration Number (CRN) within 1-3 business days, which serves as the sole tax reference number because the IC is statutorily tax-exempt and no separate TIN or VAGST number is issued for offshore operations; (3) file the statutory tax-exemption declaration with the Ministry for Revenue to obtain a Certificate of Tax Exemption, which evidences exemption to banks, customs, and payment rails such as Amazon Pay and Stripe; (4) if the IC nevertheless undertakes domestic Samoan taxable supplies (normally prohibited by the ring-fence), apply for a standard TIN and, above the WST 100,000 turnover threshold, compulsory VAGST registration; (5) if the IC employs staff in Samoa, register as a PAYE employer; (6) maintain a registered office and licensed agent in Apia at all times for tax and regulatory correspondence; constitutional documents may lawfully be drafted wholly in Chinese, supporting the traditional Chinese offshore family-trust client base.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The Samoa International Company is ring-fenced from the national tax net, being exempt from income tax, Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST), stamp duty, and all other Samoan taxes, duties, and fees on income, capital, distributions, and instruments arising outside Samoa; the exemption is statute-backed and government-guaranteed for 20 years from incorporation, provided the IC does not carry on prohibited domestic business (transactions with Samoan residents, local asset ownership, or local-source revenue); the IC is additionally exempt from exchange-control and banking-licence requirements, is not subject to entity-level taxation in Samoa, and has no economic-substance, local-director, or minimum-capital obligation, making it eligible as a passive holding and e-commerce export vehicle for ISIC 3240 baby Montessori wooden blocks.

Available election forms

Statutory elections and mechanisms: (i) formal election to activate and evidence the IC Act 2009 tax-exemption through a declaration filed with the Registrar, producing a Certificate of Tax Exemption required by banks, customs, and online payment settlement providers; (ii) shareholder-level pass-through election by non-resident beneficial owners, so the IC is treated as transparent in the beneficial-owner jurisdiction, with Samoa imposing no withholding tax, CFC charge, or exit tax on such electing shareholders; (iii) voluntary VAGST registration election for a non-exempt Samoan trading subsidiary to recover input credits on local freight, warehousing, and utility costs, whereas the exempt IC itself cannot charge or reclaim VAGST; (iv) no check-the-box classification regime exists under Samoan domestic law, and Samoa provides no domestic anti-hybrid election because the IC's full exemption eliminates any Samoan-side hybrid mismatch.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 per annum in taxable supplies (compulsory VAGST registration for domestic suppliers); voluntary registration permitted below the threshold, and zero-rated exporters may register to obtain input-credit refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods (including beechwood, non-toxic Montessori sensory toddler blocks under ISIC 3240) are zero-rated under the VAGST Act 1993 where: (i) the goods are physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply or such further period as the Commissioner approves; (ii) the exporter holds a valid electronic export declaration in ASYCUDA World; (iii) payment is received in foreign currency from a non-resident, including settlement proceeds remitted through Amazon Pay or Stripe; and (iv) the complete export-documentation bundle is retained. The Samoa IC operates under pure tax-exempt-zone treatment: it charges no VAGST on export sales, and any input VAGST embedded in local costs (port handling, Apia warehousing) is not creditable; a non-exempt or voluntarily registered exporter would instead claim input-credit refunds via periodic VAGST returns.

  • ASYCUDA World electronic Export Declaration (Customs Export Declaration)
  • Commercial invoice / supplier's tax invoice cross-referenced to the export declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing port of loading in Samoa and foreign destination
  • Foreign-currency inward remittance certificate (bank MT103/MT950 or Amazon Pay/Stripe settlement statement evidencing receipt in WS Tala equivalent)
  • Packing list and, if preferential tariff treatment is claimed, certificate of origin
  • Copy of the IC Certificate of Tax Exemption evidencing exempt-zone status to customs and banks

No real-time fiscal-invoice clearance, government e-invoicing API, or digital-stamping mandate is currently in force in Samoa; registered VAGST suppliers must issue a compliant tax invoice containing supplier and recipient names, both TINs, serial number, date, supply description, and VAGST amount within 28 days of supply; export consignments are declared electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs clearance platform using the Export Declaration form; exporters must retain electronic records, including Amazon Pay and Stripe settlement reports, for a minimum of 5 years for audit control purposes.

Retrospective customs and VAGST audits may cover up to 5 tax years, with full document-production powers over e-commerce books and records. If the export substantiation file is incomplete, zero-rating is disregarded and VAGST is clawed back at 15% on the full invoice value, plus statutory late-payment penalties and monthly interest on unpaid tax. Fraudulent export claims (false export declarations, fictitious foreign remittances, or sham exemption certificates) constitute tax evasion under the VAGST Act 1993 and the Income Tax Act 1974, exposing directors and officers to criminal fines per offence and imprisonment of up to 5 years, automatic revocation of the IC's tax-exempt status, and retrospective taxation of all previously exempt income and distributions; breach of the domestic ring-fence (supplies to Samoan residents or local-source revenue) triggers full clawback of the 20-year exemption guarantee.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is not freely convertible offshore. Current-account transactions are liberally approved by authorized dealers upon documentary evidence; capital-account transactions require CBS approval unless an International Company (IC) exchange-control exemption applies. An International Company incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is exempt from Exchange Control Act restrictions in respect of all non-WST denominated dealings, provided it maintains no resident-linked WST account, transacts no business in Samoa with residents, and channels all equity, trade proceeds and dividends through external USD accounts. Dividend and profit remittances to non-resident shareholders from the IC's approved USD operational account are unrestricted and require no CBS permit; any incidental conversion of USD dividends into WST for onshore disbursement requires an authorized dealer exchange-control form and CBS post-transaction verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the CBS Exchange Control Notice to Exporters and regulation 8 of the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, exporters must receive full payment for exported goods within six months from date of shipment; foreign-currency export proceeds received by a resident must be surrendered to an authorized dealer within 30 days of receipt or be credited only to a CBS-approved foreign-currency account. For the Samoan International Company (ISIC 3240 baby Montessori wooden blocks exporter), a standing CBS exemption applies to external sales: the IC may retain 100 percent of export proceeds in its offshore-class USD account with an authorized dealer and has no WST surrender ratio because the export transaction is concluded with non-resident buyers in foreign currency. The authorized dealer reports all external receipts and account movements to CBS under the monthly exchange-control return.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Apia, Samoa - sole monetary authority and foreign exchange controller under the Exchange Control Act 1975 and Exchange Control Regulations 1976; CBS licenses authorized dealer banks and supervises the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2018 (AMLCFT Act 2018).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None – Samoa has not enacted general transfer pricing documentation rules or local-currency thresholds. International Companies must nevertheless maintain contemporaneous records supporting arm's length pricing for any related-party transactions, especially where the Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply. Standard arm's length principles are expected by the Samoa Revenue Authority for domestic and cross-border dealings.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply to International Companies. An IC conducting a relevant activity (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, holding company, intellectual property, distribution and service centre) must demonstrate economic substance in Samoa by performing core income-generating activities there, maintaining adequate employees and premises, and filing an annual economic substance return with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour. The manufacturing and online sale of wooden Montessori toys may trigger the distribution and service centre category if purchasing and reselling through e-commerce platforms.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2015 and International Companies Act 1987: fines up to WST 500,000, payment of all evaded taxes and penalties, deregistration of the IC, and forfeiture of the corporate benefit.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for resulting restitution; criminal conviction can lead to imprisonment up to 10 years and a travel ban preventing departure from Samoa pending investigation.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations 2019: administrative penalties up to WST 100,000 per failure; mandatory strike-off; the company's name may be published as non-compliant and the IC certificate may be cancelled.

Director personal liability: Directors may be fined personally up to WST 50,000 and disqualified from being directors of any Samoan company; repeated non-compliance can trigger criminal liability and a prohibition on re-entry to Samoa for up to 5 years.
AML Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007: fines up to WST 1,000,000 for the entity, forfeiture of assets connected to the violation, and suspension of the company's financial license or bank account access.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be criminally prosecuted with imprisonment up to 14 years for willful money laundering; personal bank guarantees may be called; directors are subject to international travel restrictions and extradition risk.
Non-Resident Trading Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act 1987: fines up to WST 250,000, revocation of the IC exemption, and an order to repatriate all prohibited profits to Samoa for potential income tax assessment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorized prohibited local trade are personally liable for the repatriated profits and statutory damages; the Samoan High Court may impose a personal financial penalty and a travel ban until all amounts are paid.
