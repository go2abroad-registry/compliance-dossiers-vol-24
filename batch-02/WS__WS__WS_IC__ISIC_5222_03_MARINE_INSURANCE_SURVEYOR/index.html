Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Step 2: The IC is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax, VAGST, stamp duty and all other Samoan taxes under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended), provided it transacts no local business with Samoan residents and holds no Samoan land; therefore no MfR income-tax registration is triggered. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box classification election exists in Samoa. The International Company exemption is automatic by statute. Available elections: (i) the IC may elect by special resolution under the International Companies Act to convert into a domestic company, thereby becoming taxable under the Income Tax Act 2015; (ii) a person making exclusively zero-rated offshore supplies may elect not to register for VAGST, and a registered person may elect to apply for deregistration when turnover is below the WST 80,000 threshold; (iii) no hybrid mismatch or entity-classification election is enacted in Samoa; Samoa's international tax legislation does not provide a section 7701-style check-the-box analogue.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 80,000 (Samoa tala) annual taxable turnover; non-resident International Companies supplying exclusively offshore marine insurance surveying services are outside the VAGST system regardless of turnover

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAGST zero-rating applies to goods exported and services supplied to a non-resident for use or consumption wholly outside Samoa, including marine hull, cargo and insurance damage survey services to foreign underwriters, P&I clubs and cargo owners, where the service is performed overseas or benefits a non-resident and no Samoan nexus exists; for the exempt International Company, offshore surveying income is wholly outside the VAGST net and 0% treatment is evidenced by the MfR exemption certificate rather than a VAT refund mechanism.

  • Customs Export Declaration (where surveying equipment, dunnage samples or cargo remnants are physically exported from Samoa)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or vessel call data confirming the offshore or international location of the surveyed hull/cargo
  • Signed marine survey report and service contract with the non-resident underwriter, broker or cargo owner
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing settlement by the non-resident in foreign currency within 180 days

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping or API mandate applies in Samoa. Registered operators must submit VAGST returns electronically via MfR's Tax and Duty Administration System (TDAS) and issue prescribed-format paper or PDF invoices under the VAGST Regulations; no QR-coded fiscal receipts or government clearance platform is currently prescribed.

MfR may audit VAGST records within the statutory assessment period and reclassify improperly zero-rated supplies as taxable at 15%, with penalty tax up to 50% of the assessed amount, statutory interest, and criminal fines up to WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment for fraudulent export claims. A Samoa International Company that breaches the non-resident nexus and transacts with Samoan residents may be stripped of its exemption, struck off, and fully assessed under the Income Tax Act 2015, with directors jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current-account convertibility is available for services trade; capital-account convertibility is restricted for residents, but the Samoan International Company (IC) is non-resident and automatically permissive when holding external accounts in USD at an authorised dealer. The Samoan Tala (WST) is an administered currency with thin interbank liquidity; conversion to/from WST onshore is legal but subject to authorised-dealer rates and CBS reporting when the counterparty is a resident. For the marine insurance surveying IC, receipts are best denominated and retained in USD; profit and capital remittance to shareholders is unrestricted because the IC is non-resident, requiring no CBS dividend or surplus-remittance approval. The practical hurdles are liquidity, documentation (source of funds, purpose codes), and the bank's internal risk review, rather than statutory convertibility limits.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For residents, the Exchange Control Act 1976 and Regulations 1978 require exporters to repatriate proceeds within 90 days of shipment and surrender 100% of foreign-currency receipts to an authorised dealer (credited and converted to WST) unless exemption is granted; services exporters (including ISIC 5222 surveying) must receive settlement within 180 days of invoice and surrender any foreign currency. The Samoan International Company is non-resident: survey fees are receivable into a USD external account, are not Samoan exports, and are outside the statutory surrender and conversion ratios. The IC must not perform surveying for Samoan resident clients, which would re-characterise the fee as domestic business and trigger surrender, WST conversion, and the domestic-business restrictions of the International Companies Act 1988 (as amended).

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Fanuatupu o Samoa), the apex monetary authority and exchange control administrator under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Financial Institutions Act 2012; licensed commercial banks are designated authorised dealers for foreign exchange, WST market-making, and external-account administration.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% statutory corporate income tax rate for resident companies under Samoa's Income Tax Act 2015 (as amended). A Samoa International Company (IC) registered under the International Companies Act 2008 is statutorily tax-exempt, resulting in a 0% effective corporate income tax rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends and distributions paid by an International Company to its non-resident shareholders, granted directly by the International Companies Act 2008 statutory exemption. For an ordinary resident Samoan company, the statutory dividend withholding tax rate is 15% under the Income Tax Act 2015.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory WST-denominated Local File or Master File threshold exists in Samoa. The Income Tax Act 2015 imposes an arm's length standard on all cross-border related-party transactions of resident taxpayers, but a tax-exempt IC is outside the resident taxpayer net and has no transfer pricing documentation obligation. The IC must, however, preserve general business records (survey contracts, inspection reports, fee invoices, and bank statements) for at least 7 years, which the Commissioner may examine if the exemption is challenged.

Economic substance scope: Samoa does not apply Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), but it enforces an equivalent offshore-substance regime under the International Companies Act 2008: an IC must not carry on business with Samoan residents, must not derive Samoan-source income, and must maintain a Resident Agent and registered office in Samoa. Consistent with Samoa's OECD/G20 BEPS and Global Forum commitments, a marine insurance surveyor IC must demonstrate genuine activity — actual hull and cargo damage inspection contracts with foreign underwriters, qualified survey staff or subcontractors, professional indemnity coverage, and real performance of claims assessments. A bare paper entity that merely invoices underwriting commissions without operational capacity will be re-characterized as a resident company and taxed at 27%.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Abusive Tax-Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Re-characterization of the IC as a resident company; re-assessment of the entire income at 27% CIT with 15% dividend WHT on deemed distributions; penalties of up to 200% of the unpaid tax under the Tax Administration Act 2015; criminal fines of up to WST 500,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years for the responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Resident Agent are jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax and penalties; the Commissioner may obtain civil restitution orders, freeze corporate and personal bank accounts, and impose travel bans preventing departure from Samoa pending resolution.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Striking-off and dissolution under the International Companies Act 2008; loss of tax exemption with back-assessment of 27% CIT and all withholding taxes; disqualification of directors for up to 5 years; monetary penalties of up to WST 100,000 under the International Companies Act.

Director personal liability: Under the fraudulent trading and veil-piercing provisions of the International Companies Act 2008, courts may declare directors and shadow controllers personally liable for all debts and restitution; asset tracing orders and travel bans may issue.
AML/CFT and Unlicensed Insurance Surveying

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to WST 1,000,000 for body corporates and imprisonment of up to 10 years for individuals under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007; revocation of the surveying license; closure of the IC's bank accounts; the remuneration derived is forfeitable as proceeds of a predicate offense.

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed AML/CFT compliance officer face personal criminal liability, fines, imprisonment, confiscation orders against personal assets, and travel bans or international arrest where cross-border wire proceeds are involved.
Failure to File Annual Return and False Offshore Declaration

Statutory sanction: Automatic striking-off under the International Companies Act 2008; restoration only by court order with payment of all arrears, reinstatement fees, and interest; false declarations attract criminal penalties of fine and imprisonment under the Crimes Act 2013 and the International Companies Act.

Director personal liability: During the struck-off period the company is dissolved and its assets vest in the State; directors who continue trading are personally liable for all contracts and debts, and the Resident Agent may be delisted where collusion in a false declaration is proven.
