Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of Samoa, Apia; official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company (IC) is formed under the International Companies Act 1987 through a licensed registered agent. As a tax-exempt offshore entity, the IC does not obtain a domestic income-tax TIN or file income tax returns. If a TIN or GST identifier is required for customs, banking, or counterparty compliance, the registered agent submits a Samoa Taxpayer Registration application to the Ministry for Revenue, attaching certificate of incorporation, IC license, memorandum and articles of association, registered office address, beneficial ownership declarations, and passport copies of officers/shareholders. The IC must declare that it does not receive Samoa-source income and does not make taxable supplies in Samoa. Approval is administrative and typically granted within 5-7 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa has no check-the-box or U.S.-style entity classification election. A Samoa International Company cannot elect to become a taxable entity while remaining an IC. The only available mechanism is to revoke the IC status and re-domesticate as a Samoa company under the Companies Act 2006, which makes the entity subject to the 25% domestic corporate income tax rate and all Samoa taxation and anti-avoidance rules. No hybrid-mismatch election is available to an IC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 20,000 annual taxable turnover in Samoa; a tax-exempt International Company performing offshore fulfillment, kitting, assembly and logistics services for non-Samoa customers is not subject to GST registration unless it imports goods into Samoa or supplies goods/services to Samoa customers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A GST-registered supplier may apply the 0% rate for goods physically exported from Samoa, for international transport, and for certain offshore services performed wholly outside Samoa, provided the supplier retains transport and remittance evidence proving the goods/services left Samoa. For the tax-exempt International Company in this scope, offshore kitting and fulfillment outputs are outside the Samoan GST base entirely, which is equivalent to zero-rating without the compliance burden of GST registration or refund filing.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Samoa does not currently impose mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping on ordinary commercial invoices. GST compliance is enforced through periodic GST returns, customs declarations, bank statements, and audit examinations. Where physical goods cross the Samoan border, the Samoa Customs Single Window/ASYCUDA World system captures import and export declarations for duty and GST purposes.

Misuse of export zero-rating or submission of false export documentation is treated as GST evasion or tax fraud. Penalties include 150% of the tax shortfall, monthly late-payment interest, fines up to WST 20,000, imprisonment up to 2 years, and retrospective GST reassessment at the 15% headline rate for the full invoice value. For a tax-exempt International Company, engaging in Samoa-sourced trading activity or deriving income from Samoa residents can trigger loss of exempt status, retroactive corporate tax assessment at 25%, and additional penalties under the Tax Administration Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a managed float with an administrative distinction between current and capital account transactions. Current account flows (trade settlements, service fees, and ordinary operating disbursements) are effectively convertible through authorized dealers upon submission of underlying invoices and contracts. Capital account flows (equity injections, intercompany loans, loan repayments, and liquidation distributions) require CBS approval or authorized-dealer certification with supporting board resolutions and audited financials. Profit remittances are permissible upon proof of tax clearance and audited accounts. An International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987 may denominate contracts and maintain accounts in foreign currency, materially reducing WST conversion friction; however, any physical conversion into WST must transit a licensed bank and comply with CBS reporting thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 180 days of the date of shipment. For IC-designated foreign currency accounts, the mandatory conversion ratio into WST is zero, provided the authorized dealer files the prescribed exchange control declaration and the IC submits an annual foreign currency account recertification to CBS. Proceeds may be retained in the approved foreign currency account to fund operational disbursements, including supplier payments, subcontractor settlements, and intercompany treasury movements.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) — Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa, established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984; the Monetary Board administers exchange control pursuant to the Exchange Control Act 1975 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1976. All WST-denominated and cross-border transactions through Samoa require intermediation by a CBS-licensed authorized dealer.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium. Samoa maintains no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) or neobank licensing category; non-bank payment service providers require CBS authorization under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984. No international EMI, including Airwallex, currently holds a Samoan money transmission license. Offshore digital EMIs may be used as collection-side global accounts for USD, EUR, and AUD receivables and for vendor payments outside Samoa, but any physical remittance into or out of the Samoan banking channel must transit a licensed authorized dealer. The WS_IC should designate a Samoa-licensed commercial bank as the primary settlement and escrow account holder, with the Airwallex global account serving as an auxiliary receivable conduit subject to weekly sweeps.

Schedule C Directors, shareholders, and beneficial owner registry Beneficial ownership information must be kept at the registered agent in Samoa and be available to SIFA on demand. Failure to file by the due date triggers automatic strike-off after 90 days, a reinstatement fee, and a SIFA penalty of up to WST 10,000; the registered agent may also be fined for non-compliance.
Schedule D Economic substance declaration Confirm whether a relevant activity (distribution and service centre, fulfilment, or logistics) is conducted in Samoa and detail premises, employees, and expenditure. Failure to file by the due date triggers automatic strike-off after 90 days, a reinstatement fee, and a SIFA penalty of up to WST 10,000; the registered agent may also be fined for non-compliance.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File is mandatory where aggregate related-party turnover equals or exceeds WST 1,000,000 or where total gross revenue equals or exceeds WST 5,000,000. A Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue of WST 50,000,000 or more. Documentation must be filed with the IRD within 30 days of an official request and must be prepared before the tax return is lodged.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 and the Economic Substance Regulations 2019, administered by the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA), require International Companies carrying on relevant activities to demonstrate genuine economic presence in Samoa. Fulfillment, kitting, and third-party logistics assembly are classified as a 'Distribution and Service Centre' relevant activity. The company must satisfy the CIGA test: (i) management and control exercised in Samoa, (ii) full-time qualified employees physically present in Samoa, (iii) adequate operating expenditure incurred in Samoa, and (iv) physical premises leased or owned in Samoa. An annual Economic Substance Return (Form ESR-1) must be filed through the registered agent; failure to file results in automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities and enforcement action.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 200% of the tax evaded plus interest at 2% per month; criminal conviction carries a fine of up to WST 200,000 and imprisonment of up to 7 years

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution; the court may impose travel bans, freeze personal assets, and, upon fraud conviction, impose imprisonment. The corporate veil is pierced where the company is used as a mere facade for tax evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: SIFA may impose a penalty of up to WST 250,000, strike off the company from the register, and compulsorily exchange all commercial and beneficial ownership information with foreign revenue authorities

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from acting as officers of any Samoa company for up to 5 years, subjected to court-ordered travel restrictions, and held personally liable for unpaid penalties where they wilfully certified false substance declarations.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Full transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length price, a 50% substantial understatement penalty on the resulting tax shortfall, monthly interest at 2%, and, for repeat offenses, referral for criminal tax evasion prosecution

Director personal liability: Directors who sign defective TP documentation or knowingly approve sham recharging arrangements face personal civil liability for the shortfall, disqualification orders, and criminal sanctions where fraud is proven.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Breach

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines of up to WST 1,000,000, revocation of any financial services registration, asset forfeiture, and blacklisting by the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit

Director personal liability: Officers may be imprisoned for up to 14 years; individual fines up to WST 100,000; directors may be disqualified from managing any entity in Samoa and are subject to preventive travel restraints pending investigation.
Chronic Non-Filing and Registry Default

Statutory sanction: Compounding fines, automatic strike-off after 90 days of default, confiscation of the company's assets by the Registrar, and a minimum reinstatement fee of WST 1,000 plus all outstanding fees

Director personal liability: The registered agent may sue directors for reimbursement of penalties; directors remain personally liable for liabilities incurred after the date the company ceased to be in good standing, and may be prohibited from departing Samoa pending settlement.
