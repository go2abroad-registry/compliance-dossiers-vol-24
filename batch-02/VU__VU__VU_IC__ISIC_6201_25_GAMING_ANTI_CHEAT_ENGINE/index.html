Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official PIN: PMB 9019, Port Vila; digital access via the Vanuatu Trade Information Portal (tradeportal.gov.vu) and DCIR taxpayer service counters. International Company (IC) constitution is administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) International Companies Registry.

E-government filing portal

An International Company is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu direct taxation; no income tax TIN is required for offshore operations. The VFSC Registry issues a Certificate of Incorporation bearing a unique IC registration number, which functions as the entity's primary legal and fiscal identifier. If the IC voluntarily opts for value added tax (VAT) registration, it must file Form VAT-01 with DCIR together with the VFSC certificate, constitutional documents, registered-office proof, director and shareholder identification, and a local contact address; DCIR then issues a dedicated VAT registration number and certificate. Pure offshore gaming anti-cheat middleware activities require no further tax ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no corporate income tax, no check-the-box regime, and no hybrid-mismatch or OECD BEPS participation elections. Available statutory elections are: (i) voluntary VAT registration under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap 246] for entities making taxable supplies in Vanuatu notwithstanding fiscal neutrality; and (ii) entity conversion or redomiciliation under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], including continuation as a domestic company, which would preserve income tax neutrality but extend full VAT exposure. An IC cannot elect pass-through taxation because the IC category is exempt at entity level by statutory operation (section 169 of the International Companies Act).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 in annual taxable supplies (compulsory registration threshold); voluntary registration available below threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% VAT where they are physically exported from Vanuatu by the supplier within 28 days of supply, or within a longer period permitted by the Commissioner. Services qualify at 0% where supplied to a non-resident for use and enjoyment wholly outside Vanuatu, supported by documentary proof of offshore benefit. Gaming anti-cheat middleware supplied electronically as SaaS or licensed software to foreign game operators and paid via SWIFT or Stripe falls outside the territorial scope of Vanuatu VAT; an IC transacting exclusively offshore is outside the VAT net entirely and need not claim zero-rating unless voluntarily VAT-registered.

  • Approved Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export departure from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (for tangible goods)
  • Signed offshore service contract and beneficiary-use statement (for anti-cheat middleware/SaaS services)
  • Overseas bank remittance or payment receipt from the foreign gaming operator (recommended evidence)

No real-time fiscal clearance, government fiscal-API, or digital stamping mandate is in force. VAT-registered suppliers issue free-format sequentially numbered tax invoices containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and VAT number, invoice date, description, and tax-exclusive/inclusive amounts). VAT returns are filed periodically; customs export declarations are cleared digitally through the ASYCUDA World platform, which constitutes the export evidence record for zero-rating.

Vanuatu has no exchange-control regime and imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline; export proceeds may be held offshore indefinitely. False zero-rating claims, under-declaration, or fraudulent misstatements expose the taxpayer to recovery of uncollected VAT, statutory default interest, monetary penalties up to VUV 1,000,000, imprisonment up to five years, and cancellation of VAT registration. An International Company that breaches the International Companies Act offshore restrictions (e.g., transacting with Vanuatu residents or holding local property) risks loss of its section 169 exemption, retrospective tax clawback, fines, and deregistration by the VFSC.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalized exchange-control regime administered by the RBV under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 54]. The Vanuatu International Company, being a non-resident class entity under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], is statutorily exempt from exchange-control licensing approvals; the VUV is fully convertible for current and capital account transactions conducted outside Vanuatu, and outbound dividend and profit remittances are unrestricted and free of Vanuatu withholding taxes. The only practical constraint is market depth: the VUV is a thinly traded Pacific currency, so the treasury should remain USD-naturalized and avoid material VUV conversions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation applies. Vanuatu imposes neither a statutory timeline nor a mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts; the International Company may retain 100% of all export and merchant proceeds in USD-denominated accounts, whether held domestically or offshore, without obligatory repatriation or conversion into VUV.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no payroll tax, no estate or gift tax, and no social security surcharge on any resident or non-resident taxpayer.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu enacts no dividend withholding tax, no branch remittance tax, and no undistributed profits tax; an IC may distribute its entire anti-cheat license revenue to shareholders free of Vanuatu tax.

Tax Evasion by False Return
