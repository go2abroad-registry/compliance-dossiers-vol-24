Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR) of the Government of Samoa, Apia — the national revenue authority administering income tax, VAGST, and customs; taxpayer-facing portal and e-filing services are delivered through the MfR e-Tax system, while International Company incorporation and the statutory register are maintained by the Registrar of International Companies under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour.

E-government filing portal

Foreign applicants: (1) incorporate the Samoa International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 through the Registrar of International Companies; (2) obtain Certificate of Incorporation, constitutional documents, and registered agent appointment; (3) apply for a 9-digit TIN at the Ministry for Revenue on Form TR-1, attaching certified incorporation documents, constitutional documents, director and beneficial-owner registers, and the Samoan registered-agent certificate; (4) no further tax registration is required because the IC is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax and VAGST on international income; (5) pay the annual International Company government fee to the Registry to preserve tax-exempt status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Samoa International Company is a non-resident, tax-exempt vehicle; international wholesale brokerage income from SSD and server CPU chip trading is excluded from Samoan entity-level taxation, and the IC has no Samoan territorial tax nexus beyond its registered office and agent.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Samoan law. Available mechanisms: (i) application to MfR for a Certificate of Residence or Non-Resident Tax Certificate evidencing exempt status; (ii) a constitutional-election to convert or redomicile the IC into a domestic taxable company, thereby accepting entity-level domestic taxation (generally inadvisable); (iii) US beneficial owners may file Form 8832 to treat the IC as a disregarded entity or partnership on US returns, which has no Samoan tax effect; (iv) Samoa, as an OECD Inclusive Framework member, applies general anti-avoidance recharacterization under the Tax Administration Act 2015 to hybrid mismatch arrangements, with no elective hybrid mismatch rule currently legislated.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory VAGST registration arises when taxable supplies made in Samoa exceed WST 100,000 within any 12-month period. An International Company conducting purely offshore wholesale of semiconductor goods normally has no Samoan taxable supplies and therefore falls outside this threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAGST-registered supplier may zero-rate: (a) goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of the time of supply to a recipient outside Samoa, provided contemporaneous proof of export is preserved; and (b) services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply, where such services are not performed in Samoa and are not directly connected with Samoan land or goods located in Samoa. The International Company may claim zero-rating only where the chips or services actually transit or are supplied from Samoa; principal-to-principal trading executed wholly offshore outside Samoa does not enter the Samoan VAGST base and requires no zero-rating claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA SAD) as cleared by Samoa Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export consignment to the foreign buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/MT103 confirmation evidencing foreign currency repatriation
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • VAGST export declaration / zero-rating worksheet maintained under the VAGST Act 2003

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control (electronic-invoicing/API) mandate is currently in force in Samoa. VAGST returns are filed electronically via the MfR e-Tax portal and customs declarations are processed through the ASYCUDA World platform, with no statutory API invoice-clearance obligation as of the current regime.

Unsupported or fraudulent export zero-rating claims trigger: (i) reassessment and retrospective clawback of refunded VAGST with statutory interest under the Tax Administration Act 2015; (ii) civil shortfall penalties of up to 150% of the avoided tax for evasion; (iii) criminal fines of up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment for responsible directors or officers; (iv) extended audit windows of up to 10 years (unlimited in fraud cases); and (v) potential revocation of the International Company's tax-exempt status with reclassification to full domestic taxation of historical profits.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations; the Samoan Tala (WST) is administratively convertible for bona fide current-account transactions through licensed authorized dealers, but the WST is not freely traded internationally and has no genuine offshore interbank market. Capital account transfers, resident foreign-currency accounts, and any WST conversion by a resident require CBS application-based approval. The WS_IC is a non-resident, tax-exempt International Company (IC); all SSD/CPU trade proceeds are collected and paid in USD outside Samoa. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is executed USD-to-USD without entering the WST market and therefore does not trigger CBS exchange-control approval. The only practical remittance hurdle is documentary: the bank will require proof of the IC's good standing, tax-exemption status, and a representation that no Samoan resident holds ownership or benefits from the remitted profits.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutorily, under the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, a resident exporter must repatriate full export proceeds within 90 days of shipment and surrender 100% of the foreign currency to an authorized dealer unless an approved foreign-currency account or a CBS exemption certificate is held. The target operation is structured as an offshore open-market relay: the IC purchases SSD components and server CPUs from, and sells them to, non-resident counterparties, with title passing outside Samoa and no goods entering Samoan customs territory. In that configuration, no Samoan export declaration arises and the 90-day surrender rule is not engaged. If any future physical export from Samoa occurs, the mandatory 100% surrender timeline will apply unless a CBS-approved USD account exemption is pre-arranged.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 2005, exercising foreign exchange authority under the Exchange Control Act 1975 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, operating through licensed authorized dealers (commercial banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low - structurally non-viable as a settlement spine. Samoa has no electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing or passporting regime; the Money Remitters Act 2016 licenses only domestic CBS-supervised remittance businesses. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise- or Payoneer-type platforms) are neither recognized nor accepted as source-of-funds evidence by Samoan banks, and the IC's wholesale purchase and sale obligations must clear over SWIFT through a CBS-licensed authorized dealer to preserve an audit-grade trail. An EMI may be used only as an ancillary commission-collection layer, never as the primary clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders remit paid-in capital by SWIFT MT103 in USD to the IC's dedicated Capital Escrow Account at the Samoan authorized dealer. The remittance must be labeled as direct investment - paid-in capital of an International Company, and the account package must include the board capital-call resolution, shareholder register, Registered Agent good-standing letter, and a non-resident representation confirming that no Samoan resident holds an interest. Because no WST conversion and no resident payment is involved, CBS exchange-control approval is not triggered; the bank nevertheless records the debit in its CBS-reportable balance-of-payments capital ledger. Any shareholder subordinated loan must be notified to the CBS if aggregate external debt exceeds the Samoan reporting threshold or if funds are subsequently converted into WST.Supplier payments are executed by SWIFT MT103 priority transfers from the IC's USD account, cleared through ANZ Bank's Australia relationships or BSP's Pacific-region correspondent network. For delivery-assured wholesale purchases, an irrevocable sight documentary letter of credit is opened with ANZ or BSP Samoa and may be confirmed by a top-tier Singapore or Hong Kong bank, with payment made against compliant presentation of transport and warranty documents. Outbound trade disbursements are purpose-coded as general merchandise imports (or trade-related services for commissions) and supported by commercial invoice plus airway bill or bill of lading. Disbursements above CBS monitoring thresholds require an exchange-control application form (CBS EC-type filing) prior to dispatch; the bank will impose a 24-hour hold on unusually large or mismatched drafts pending CBS clarification. For escrow-protected deals, buyer funds are cleared into the bank's segregated escrow ledger, released in favor of the IC only after documentary inspection, and then settled onward to the supplier by MT103.Outright deliverable USD/WST forward contracts with tenors of up to 12 months through Samoan authorized dealers, limited to trade-backed conversion needs.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) contracts on WST/USD with offshore bank counterparties, permitted on a hedge-only basis supported by underlying commercial exposure; speculative NDF books are prohibited by the CBS.Cross-currency swaps (EUR/USD, CNY/USD, AUD/USD) with licensed bank counterparties where an underlying trade payable or receivable exists; post-trade CBS notification is required.FX swap rollovers and spot-next executions for working-capital alignment between payable and receivable value dates using the same authorized-dealer relationship; options and exotic derivatives are not routinely permitted and require prior CBS approval.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for qualifying International Companies (IC) under the International Companies Act 2015; the general Samoan corporate income tax rate for taxable resident companies is 27%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by a qualifying International Company to non-resident shareholders. For ordinary resident Samoan companies, the statutory non-resident dividend withholding tax rate is 20%, unless reduced by a double taxation agreement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory WST threshold is codified for transfer pricing documentation; Samoa applies the arm's-length principle to all controlled transactions. In administrative practice, the Revenue Department will request contemporaneous Local File / Master File documentation where aggregate controlled turnover exceeds WST 2,000,000 in a fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Samoa's International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations apply to ICs engaged in relevant activities, including wholesale distribution and service centre operations. A semiconductor/CPU/SSD wholesale trading IC must report (i) core income-generating activities performed in Samoa; (ii) adequate physical premises in Samoa; (iii) full-time local employees proportionate to the activity; and (iv) local operating expenditure sufficient to support the trade. A distribution and service centre classification will not be satisfied by passive invoicing alone.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Abuse of the International Company Exemption

Statutory sanction: Automatic loss of the IC exemption; assessment of corporate income tax at 27% on all undeclared Samoan-source profits; civil penalty of 100% to 200% of the tax understated; daily interest of 2% per month; criminal fine up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years for intentional under-reporting.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax. The Supreme Court may issue travel bans, confiscation orders, and director disqualification for up to 10 years; the corporate veil is pierced where the IC acted as the direct instrument of the directors' fraud.
Shell Company / Inadequate Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Economic Substance Regulations: fine up to WST 100,000 for a false substance declaration, plus continuing penalties of WST 10,000 per month; possible default assessment of tax on deemed Samoan-source income; the company may be struck off or de-registered.

Director personal liability: Directors may be named in a tax claim, subject to civil restitution of all avoided taxes, banned from leaving Samoa until the fine is settled, and disqualified as officers of any Samoan company for up to 10 years.
AML – Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Money laundering offence under Samoa's Crimes Act 2013 and Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act: fine up to WST 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 14 years; asset freezing and forfeiture; multiple offences carry cumulative penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors face separate criminal prosecution, personal confiscation of assets, travel bans, designation as high-risk persons, and prohibition from holding financial or corporate office. A director who knowingly signs false wires or invoice records is criminally liable as a principal.
False Beneficial Ownership / Nominee Concealment

Statutory sanction: Fine up to WST 20,000 per false entry; company strike-off; referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit; prosecution for false declaration carries imprisonment up to 5 years and confiscation of benefits derived from the concealed ownership.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for all penalties; the court may issue a travel ban pending full disclosure; the nominee director is jointly liable and may be prohibited from acting as an officer in Samoa or abroad under mutual legal assistance measures.
