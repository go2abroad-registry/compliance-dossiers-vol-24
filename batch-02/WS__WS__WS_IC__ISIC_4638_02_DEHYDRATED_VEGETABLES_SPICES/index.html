Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax, VAGST and stamp duty under s.219 of the International Companies Act 1987 and is not required to obtain a TIN. Practical SOP: (1) incorporate with the Registrar of International Companies via the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (2) optionally apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a voluntary TIN to facilitate offshore bank account opening or foreign tax credit relief; (3) if the IC elects (or is required) to conduct domestic taxable activity, mandatory VAGST registration is required within 21 days after crossing the WST 100,000 annual taxable turnover threshold; for pure offshore dehydrated-spice trading, no registration is required.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to a registered person's supply of goods that are exported from Samoa directly to a non-resident and are not subject to later Samoan VAGST, and to services supplied to a non-resident performed wholly or predominantly offshore (VAGST Act 2007). A Samoa International Company, being exempt from VAGST, is outside the charge to VAGST entirely; therefore no indirect tax is levied on its offshore bulk dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper sales, and no input VAT refund is claimable because no input VAT is incurred. To evidence eligibility, the exporter must hold a customs export declaration and documentary proof that the goods physically departed Samoa within 30 days.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World Cus-01 Entry)
  • Commercial Invoice (full description, customs value, Incoterms)
  • Bill of Lading (or Airway Bill / Sea Waybill)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Samoan licensed commercial bank
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (when required by the destination jurisdiction)

No real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital tax stamp regime exists. Taxpayers must file VAGST returns electronically through the Ministry for Revenue 'Oloa' portal. Export goods clearance is mandatory through ASYCUDA World, and physical inspection is risk-based; electronic customs declarations are required for all export consignments.

Samoa Customs and the Ministry for Revenue may reassess zero-rated exports within five years of the supply. A false or fraudulent zero-rating claim triggers immediate clawback of the 15% VAGST, default interest at 12% per annum, and civil penalties of up to 200% of the tax liability for intentional evasion. Criminal liability under the Tax Administration Act 2015 exposes directors to fines up to WST 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to seven years. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days is a breach of the Exchange Control Regulations and can incur fines up to WST 100,000 or three times the unremitted amount, plus revocation of the IC's foreign investment or trading permits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is managed by the CBS under a discretionary managed-float arrangement. Current-account convertibility for bona fide trade and service transactions is generally available to resident businesses, but capital-account transactions involving resident persons and domestic regulated entities require CBS approval or a commercial bank foreign-exchange permit. An International Company under the International Companies Act 1988, as amended, is classified as a non-resident entity and benefits from a statutory exemption from all exchange-control restrictions: capital subscriptions, dividends, interest, management fees, and liquidation proceeds may be converted through a licensed commercial bank and repatriated offshore without CBS license or prior exchange-control approval. This creates full de facto convertibility for the WS_IC treasury footprint, subject only to bank-level AML/KYC verification and documentary substantiation of the underlying transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies to a Samoan International Company. The International Companies Act 1988 (as amended) expressly displaces the Exchange Control Act 1975 and the Exchange Control Regulations with respect to international companies; therefore, export receipts earned by WS_IC from bulk dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper sales may be retained in non-WST currencies (USD, EUR, AUD, NZD) in offshore or onshore foreign-currency accounts without being required to be converted to WST or surrendered to the CBS. Resident counterparties remain subject to standard exchange-control repatriation timelines and any central-bank-imposed conversion obligations; WS_IC is outside that perimeter.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low: global fintech EMIs and digital cross-border payment platforms generally include Samoa in higher-risk OFC screening when the underlying entity is a tax-exempt International Company. EMIs may open payment/e-money accounts only where the IC can provide locally registered physical substance, audited financials, beneficial-ownership transparency to PICP depth and independent confirmation of the dehydrated-spices trade. The entity should therefore treat fintech EMIs as supplementary collection/disbursement rails, not as a substitute for a full commercial bank account with a licensed Samoan bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inward share-capital or shareholder-loan payments to the WS_IC account must be sent as a SWIFT MT103 through a licensed Samoan commercial bank or its overseas correspondent; the beneficiary should be named exactly as per the corporate account mandate and the remittance should use a clearly stated purpose code such as 'Share subscription - capital contribution to Samoa International Company' or 'Shareholder loan - deed-poll reference'. No CBS exchange-control license is required. The receiving bank will apply standard source-of-funds verification, so the remitter must provide the corporate bank with a board resolution, the shareholder register, and a funds-origin certificate before credit is posted; funds will typically be value-dated same-day for TAL/foreign-currency settlement, if T+0 clearing, otherwise T+1, in line with SWIFT and local clearing-house cut-offs.For settlements with third-party suppliers, freight forwarders and overseas agents, the WS_IC treasury should use (i) commercial letters of credit issued on SWIFT MT700/MT710 by the Samoan bank's trade desk, with documents negotiated under UCP 600, for large bulk spice shipments; and (ii) SWIFT MT103 direct telegraphic transfers for smaller or regular payments to counterparties with established credit. Payments should be sourced only from the corresponding foreign-currency account funded by trade receivable collections, and all invoices, purchase orders and shipping documents must be file-matched to the payment instruction. Leave nil for exempt ICs; if any Samoan-source income is inadvertently returned, only arm's-length, documented expenses are deductible. Late filing attracts a fixed penalty of WST 2,000 plus a monthly surcharge of 5% of the tax outstanding (capped at 24 months); late payment interest accrues at 2% per month. Tax-exempt ICs face nominal fixed penalties for delayed nil returns but risk loss of good standing.
Box 5 - Taxable income Gross income less allowable deductions. Exempt ICs enter WST 0. Late filing attracts a fixed penalty of WST 2,000 plus a monthly surcharge of 5% of the tax outstanding (capped at 24 months); late payment interest accrues at 2% per month. Tax-exempt ICs face nominal fixed penalties for delayed nil returns but risk loss of good standing.
Box 6 - Corporate income tax at 25% Statutory tax computed on taxable income. Exempt ICs enter WST 0 and attach the directors' declaration of IC exempt status. Late filing attracts a fixed penalty of WST 2,000 plus a monthly surcharge of 5% of the tax outstanding (capped at 24 months); late payment interest accrues at 2% per month. Tax-exempt ICs face nominal fixed penalties for delayed nil returns but risk loss of good standing.
Box 7 - Declaration and signature Signed declaration by a director or the licensed resident agent certifying accuracy. A non-resident director must co-sign through the Samoan resident agent; MOUs and POAs must be filed with SIFA. Late filing attracts a fixed penalty of WST 2,000 plus a monthly surcharge of 5% of the tax outstanding (capped at 24 months); late payment interest accrues at 2% per month. Tax-exempt ICs face nominal fixed penalties for delayed nil returns but risk loss of good standing.
Box A - Company name and registration number Official IC name and certificate of incorporation number. Match exactly the SIFA registry records; no tax assessed but the return evidences legal good standing. Arrear fees accrue at WST 50 per month up to WST 1,000; after six (6) months of default the IC is liable to administrative strike-off, a reinstatement fee of WST 300, and payment of all outstanding Government fees.

Arrear fees accrue at WST 50 per month up to WST 1,000; after six (6) months of default the IC is liable to administrative strike-off, a reinstatement fee of WST 300, and payment of all outstanding Government fees.
Box C - Register of directors and shareholders Full names, addresses, shareholdings, and appointment dates. Nominee shareholding and trust arrangements must be disclosed; underlying beneficial owners noted separately. Arrear fees accrue at WST 50 per month up to WST 1,000; after six (6) months of default the IC is liable to administrative strike-off, a reinstatement fee of WST 300, and payment of all outstanding Government fees.
Box D - Beneficial ownership declaration Ultimate beneficial owner(s) of the shares and control rights. Maintain a current beneficial ownership register at the registered office for MLPA inspection within 24 hours of demand. Arrear fees accrue at WST 50 per month up to WST 1,000; after six (6) months of default the IC is liable to administrative strike-off, a reinstatement fee of WST 300, and payment of all outstanding Government fees.
Box E - Confirmation of no Samoan-source business Declaration that the IC did not trade within Samoa during the year. If answered 'No', the IC is taxable and must instead file a full CIT-100. Arrear fees accrue at WST 50 per month up to WST 1,000; after six (6) months of default the IC is liable to administrative strike-off, a reinstatement fee of WST 300, and payment of all outstanding Government fees.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File where related-party transactions exceed WST 10,000,000 (approx. USD 3.8 million) in an income year; Master File and Country-by-Country Report thresholds follow CbC guidance (consolidated group revenue above WST 100,000,000); all exempt ICs must retain contemporaneous transfer pricing records to prove arm's-length pricing on intercompany sales of dehydrated vegetables and spices.

Economic substance scope: International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2020 (ESR) apply. A trading IC conducting a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity (storage, inventory, contract negotiation, invoicing for dehydrated garlic flakes and chili pepper exports) must maintain in Samoa: a physical office presence, adequate full-time employees, board meetings and records demonstrating active management and control, and sufficient operating expenditure for core income-generating activities. Exempt ICs file an annual ESR notification (nil where pure equity-holding); failure to meet substance results in denial of exempt status, assessment to 25% CIT, and penalties up to WST 50,000.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Act 2016: shortfall penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded, civil interest at 2% per month, criminal fines up to WST 50,000, and imprisonment up to three (3) years; MOU/agent facilitating fraud is also sanctionable.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent are jointly and severally liable for the company's tax and penalties; fraudulent evasion exposes directors to personal fines, a court-issued travel ban (departure prohibition order), and custodial sentences up to 3 years.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Withdrawal of the IC exemption, reassessment to 25% CIT on worldwide income, ESR penalty up to WST 50,000, administrative strike-off from the Registry, and adverse listing on EU/OECD high-risk grey-lists resulting in enhanced AML due diligence by correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for CIT and ESR penalties; the Supreme Court may issue asset-freezing orders, travel bans, and disqualification from directorship of any Samoan company for up to 5 years.
AML/CFT and Record-Keeping Failure

Statutory sanction: Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2007 (as amended): corporate fines up to WST 100,000, revocation of the IC's registration, and freezing orders on the IC's bank accounts; the resident agent's license may also be suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent face individual criminal fines up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for willful AML non-compliance; departure prohibition orders may be issued during investigation.
Carrying on Business in Samoa / Loss of IC Exemption

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the IC as a taxable domestic company; assessment of Samoan-source profits at 25% CIT plus late payment interest; retroactive assessments for up to 5 years (10 years in fraud cases) and 200% shortfall penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors participating in the misdeclaration are jointly and severally liable for the tax debts; the court may restrain personal bank accounts and assets and prohibit departure from Samoa until all liabilities are settled.
