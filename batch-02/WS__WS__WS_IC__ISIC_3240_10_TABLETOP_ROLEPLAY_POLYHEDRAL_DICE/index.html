Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (Matagaluega o Tupe Maua), Apia, Samoa; online service portal at https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is statutorily exempt from all Samoan national taxes and duties; no TIN is required for tax purposes. The IC obtains an annual Tax Exemption Certificate from the Ministry for Revenue (supported by the certificate of incorporation from the Registrar of International Companies under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour). If a TIN is nevertheless required for banking, customs, or counterparty due-diligence purposes, a non-resident entity files an Application for Registration for Income Tax / VAGST with the Ministry for Revenue, attaching certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, director passport pages, and a local agent appointment form. The exemption certificate is issued within 5 working days; TIN issuance for non-residents takes 3-10 working days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Samoan law. An IC may voluntarily apply to the Registrar to convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act 2006, thereby prospectively losing the IC tax exemption; no hybrid mismatch or fiscal-transparent election is available. An IC cannot elect to be taxed on a resident basis. Special Economic Zone incentives are not applicable to ICs under the current regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration where annual taxable turnover exceeds WST 50,000 (approximately USD 19,000); voluntary registration permitted below threshold. ICs carrying on offshore business only are outside the charge to VAGST.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply and to certain services supplied to non-residents (services performed wholly outside Samoa or directly in connection with exported goods). The supplier must retain proof of export, and the recipient must be outside Samoa; the claim must be made in the VAGST return with the statutory declaration and documentary annexures attached.

  • Cancelled Customs Export Declaration (CusDec) bearing sacnned Samoa Customs stamp
  • Original Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Samoan port
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the CusDec
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no government digital stamping, and no mandatory e-invoicing API regime in Samoa. Registered taxpayers must issue a tax invoice in the statutory form for every taxable supply and file VAGST returns electronically via the Ministry for Revenue online portal; electronic records are admissible, but a physical printed tax invoice remains the contemporaneous evidence required for input credit and export claims.

The Ministry for Revenue audits export zero-rating claims against customs manifests and CBS banking records. Under the Tax Administration Act 2012 and VAGST Act 2008, fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating attracts penalty tax up to 150% of the shortfall, civil penalties of WST 10,000, imprisonment of directors up to 5 years, and retrospective clawback of the wrongly zero-rated VAT with interest accruing from the original filing date; input credits claimed on related acquisitions are also disallowed for up to 5 prior tax periods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tālā (WST) is not fully convertible under classical exchange control. Current-account transactions (trade, services, royalties, and routine operating payments) are generally authorised by licensed commercial banks under delegated authority from the CBS. Capital-account transactions including equity injections, dividend distributions, and intercompany loans require either prior CBS approval or conformity with an automatic exemption. A Samoa International Company (IC) is treated as a non-resident entity for exchange control purposes; it may freely maintain and operate foreign-currency accounts in USD, EUR, AUD, and WST and may remit profits to non-resident shareholders without individual CBS pre-approval, provided that funds are sourced from offshore business operations and not from domestic Samoan sales. Practical remittance approvals are standard within 2–5 business days when the sampled documentation is complete.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations, resident exporters must surrender export proceeds to a licensed Samoan bank within 6 months from the date of shipment and must convert into WST unless a specific exemption is granted. A Samoa International Company is explicitly carved out from the resident definition in the International Companies Act and is therefore exempt from the surrender obligation and conversion mandate. All proceeds from the export of handmade RPG dice may be retained in designated foreign-currency accounts. The exemption must be evidenced by the IC registration certificate, a central bank acknowledgement (export code), and commercial invoices listing the consignee and shipment details. The CBS may require an annual update on the volume and currency mix of export receipts for statistical maintenance but no mandatory conversion ratio applies.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) – Exchange Control Division

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for the collection of payments from Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal in USD and EUR, and for onward disbursement to suppliers using offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Payoneer, Wise, and Currenxie. Legal constraints: EMIs are not recognised as deposit-taking institutions under the Central Bank of Samoa Act and cannot hold WST balances, issue local payment cards, or execute Samoan domestic transfers. Offshore EMI accounts may be used as virtual treasury vehicles for e-commerce settlements and supplier payments, but the Samoa IC must maintain a licensed local settlement account (or a corresponding bank account in a permissible jurisdiction) for any capital flows that touch the Samoan banking system. The CBS imposes no direct ban on EMI use for non-resident ICs; however, the IC should ensure that the EMI's KYC and AML regime is deemed equivalent to the SAMOA AML/CFT framework, and that no Samoan-resident beneficiary receives funds through the EMI without a local banking intermediary.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial capital injection for a Samoa International Company (standing capital may be zero if no distribution has been made) must be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder/beneficial owner. Field 70 (Remittance Information) should contain the purpose code 'INVC' (Investment Capital) along with the company name and share subscription reference. The bank may request a certified copy of the board resolution authorising the issuance of shares and the register of members. To avoid triggering anti-money-laundering reviews, the transfer amount must be consistent with the issued share capital stated at onboarding; any material difference must be supported by a written share subscription agreement and a certificate of incumbency. For payments above USD 100,000 or the WST equivalent, a pre-onboarding declaration to the CBS under the automatic exemption for non-resident ICs is advised, and the bank will issue a receipt confirming the foreign-currency status of the funds.Supplier and logistics payments will be cleared primarily using SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers with ISO 20022 purpose code 'TRAD' (Trade) or 'GDS' (General Trade) in field 77B. For shipments of epoxy resin, silicone moulds, polyhedral dice parts, pigments, and freight forwarding services, the following rails are available: (1) Standard wire transfer for amounts up to USD 50,000 with a scanned invoice as an attachment to the bank's digital portal; (2) For orders above USD 50,000, an irrevocable standby commercial L/C issued by a licensed bank is acceptable, but in practice the bank will require a pro-forma invoice, shipping schedule, and customs valuation report; (3) For high-frequency small-value disbursements (e.g., PayPal payouts to supply chain artisans), an approved prepaid card or an EMI virtual account can be used, with periodic reconciliation to the operational account. The CBS retains the authority to request supporting trade documents for any transaction exceeding USD 100,000 per month, but for an IC the timeline for verification is generally one to two business days.USD/WST vanilla forward contracts (1–12 months tenor) executed with ANZ Bank Samoa Limited or BSP Samoa with no CBS pre-clearance for trade-hedging purposesNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/WST referencing the CBS fix, permitted for an IC when the underlying exposure does not involve repeated local conversionsCross-currency swaps between USD and EUR for procurement expenses, subject to prior CBS notification because the tenor exceeds 12 monthsCurrency options on major crosses (EUR/USD) available only through a registered offshore broker with a local settlement agent

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% (general resident-company CIT rate); 0% effective statutory rate for International Companies (ICs) under the International Companies Act 1987, s.144 exemption.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a Samoan International Company to non-resident shareholders (statutory IC exemption); the general statutory rate for resident companies is 15% on dividends paid to non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 30 days after each anniversary of the date of incorporation, together with payment of the annual government fee.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months after the end of each accounting year; the IC must notify SRA of its accounting year-end no later than seven months after incorporation or within 30 days of changing it.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six months after the end of the fiscal year, with full self-assessment payment of any CIT and a P&L schedule annexed.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa prescribes no statutory Local File/Master File monetary threshold for exempt ICs; best-practice contemporaneous documentation (Local File, Master File, CBCR exemption analysis) is mandatory where intercompany payments exceed WST 1,000,000 (approximately USD 370,000) per annum or where the IC derives more than 50% of gross income from related-party sales of dice through a Hong Kong or New Zealand distribution hub.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply to all ICs carrying on relevant activities. Tabletop RPG dice production and distribution falls within the 'distribution and service centre' category (and the IP brand holding is scrutinised under the IP-business substance test). The IC must maintain a Samoan office, resident employees proportionate to income, local board control, and minimum annual Samoan expenditure, and must file the annual ESR return; failure to meet the test is fatal because SRA will deem the profits taxable at 27% and exchange information with the shareholder's residence country.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / filing a false economic substance return

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to WST 50,000 (100 penalty units) and daily default penalties of WST 1,000 per day; reassessment of 27% CIT on the entire undeclared revenue with a 100% evasion uplift and 8% per annum interest; referral to the Samoa Attorney General for criminal proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent who signed the false return are jointly and severally liable for the company's tax debts; the Commissioner may issue travel restraints (departure objection certificates) against each director; conviction carries imprisonment of up to three years and disqualification from acting as a director in Samoa for ten years.
Shell company / economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: SRA may set aside the s.144 exemption, assess 27% CIT on gross receipts with penalty surcharges, order deregistration of the IC, and automatically transmit the file to the beneficial owner's residence jurisdiction under the Global Forum and CRS exchange framework; registration of the IC as a 'non-compliant' entity can trigger additional annual penalties of WST 10,000 per year.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: directors face civil restitution of diverted profits, personal fines of up to WST 100,000, obtaining a departure ban preventing them from leaving Samoa by air or sea pending payment, and imprisonment of up to five years for fraudulent evasion where the PayPal and Stripe balances were concealed.
AML / beneficial ownership transparency breach

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties under Samoa's AML/CFT Act of up to WST 100,000 for the IC and WST 50,000 per responsible officer; the Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze funds held in remittance accounts and impose record-keeping penalties of 5 penalty units per day until the BO register is corrected.

Director personal liability: Directors and agents who knowingly permitted anonymous control are personally liable for AML fines, subject to criminal prosecution carrying imprisonment of up to five years, forfeiture of the sales proceeds, and cancellation of the registered agent's licence.
Transfer pricing / profit-stripping sham

Statutory sanction: SRA may make a transfer pricing adjustment to restore arm's length profit in Samoa, impose 27% CIT on the adjusted profit, and add penalties equal to 50%-100% of the underpaid tax plus interest; in the alternative, the IC exemption is revoked for failure to demonstrate substance.

Director personal liability: Directors authorising the related-party contracts are personally liable for the tax shortfall as shadow directors; courts may issue worldwide freezing orders, travel bans, and restitution orders against the founder; aggravated fraud convictions carry imprisonment of up to ten years.
