Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue – Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws; SRA administers income tax, VAGST, and customs duties.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities obtain a Samoa Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN/IRD Number) by filing the SRA registration application (Form IRD-001), accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, International Companies Registry certificate, directors' and shareholders' particulars, registered office proof, and business description; the SRA issues the TIN upon verification, typically within 10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone – the Samoa International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is exempt from Samoa income tax, VAGST, and stamp duty while it remains non-resident, derives no Samoan-source income, and carries on no business within Samoa; the IC's tax-exempt status is evidenced by a tax exemption certificate issued by the International Companies Registry and recognized by the SRA. — Foreign entities obtain a Samoa Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN/IRD Number) by filing the SRA registration application (Form IRD-001), accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, International Companies Registry certificate, directors' and shareholders' particulars, registered office proof, and business description; the SRA issues the TIN upon verification, typically within 10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – the Samoa International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is exempt from Samoa income tax, VAGST, and stamp duty while it remains non-resident, derives no Samoan-source income, and carries on no business within Samoa; the IC's tax-exempt status is evidenced by a tax exemption certificate issued by the International Companies Registry and recognized by the SRA.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election is available for a Samoa non-resident IC; an IC may elect under s. 12 of the International Companies Act 1987 to request a tax exemption certificate confirming its Tax_Exempt_Zone status; it cannot elect into Samoa entity-level taxation while non-resident, and hybrid mismatch rules only apply to resident controlled foreign corporations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 annual taxable turnover for compulsory registration; voluntary registration is available to businesses below the threshold; foreign exporters without a fixed place of business in Samoa generally have no VAGST registration nexus unless they make taxable supplies in Samoa.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply qualifies for zero-rated VAGST where goods are physically exported from Samoa by the supplier, delivered to a destination outside Samoa, and supported by documentary evidence of export; granular sulfur mineral ore exported under ISIC 4669 falls within the zero-rated export category; offshore services supplied to non-residents and not used or consumed in Samoa may also qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Form EX1) certified by Samoa Customs at export clearance
  • Bill of Lading (or airway bill/applicable ocean transport waybill) evidencing delivery outside Samoa
  • Commercial invoice and contract of sale issued by the IC
  • Certificate of origin and any required export license/permit for granular sulfur mineral ore
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank statement) evidencing repatriation of export proceeds into Samoa

No real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, API, or digital stamping mandate exists in Samoa; taxpayers may issue paper or electronic commercial invoices and file VAGST returns through the SRA electronic tax filing portal; full audit trail integrity must be maintained under the Tax Administration Act 2012.

Zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective audit by the Samoa Revenue Authority; if the supporting export documentation is incomplete, misstated, or fraudulent, the claimed zero-rating is reversed, VAGST becomes payable on the full invoice amount, interest accrues, late payment penalties of up to 25% of the unpaid tax are imposed, and individual/officer fines up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years may apply under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1992 and Tax Administration Act 2012; the clawback period extends to all refunds paid within the prior 6 assessment years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is fully convertible for current-account international trade and service transactions, subject to documentary verification and standard reporting to CBS through licensed commercial banks. Capital-account movements, including direct investment, portfolio transfers, and debt-related cross-border flows, require selective CBS approval where the transaction involves resident counterparties or Samoan-domiciled assets. Profit remittance hurdles for an International Company are minimal provided the company is non-resident, maintains no Samoan source income, and remittances are made from an offshore-funded escrow or operational account; there is no withholding tax on non-resident IC distributions, and CBS control is exercised only at the level of the paying bank via AML/KYC verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds generated by any entity physically operating within Samoa, including branches of International Companies conducting domestic trade, must be repatriated through a CBS-licensed commercial bank within 180 days of shipment and sold for WST at the applicable market rate unless a surrender exemption has been issued by CBS. For International Companies operating purely on a non-resident trade basis, where the underlying contract is executed outside Samoa and no Samoan goods or services are exported, the surrender requirement does not apply; however, if the entity directly invoices from a Samoan branch or utilises Samoan port facilities for transhipment, the 180-day repatriation rule and a minimum mandatory conversion of 30% of gross proceeds into WST applies, reducible to 10% on documentary evidence of immediate conversion to supplier settlements.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), formally the Central Bank of Samoa, exercising delegated authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the FSI Act 2012.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable in Samoa as a standalone licence class. CBS does not issue electronic money institution (EMI) or digital payment service licences to non-bank technology firms; offshore digital EMIs can only provide services by partnering with a CBS-licensed commercial bank as a bare account provider and technology gateway, with no direct access to the Samoan clearing system. The commercial bank retains full AML/CTF liability, and all KYC records must reside with the Samoan entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injection for the Samoan International Company must be routed through the Samoan banking system as an inward foreign direct investment (FDI) wire. For an IC, the capital need not be deposited in Samoa unless a Samoan bank account is used as treasury hub; if so, the wire must be labelled SWIFT MT103 or MT202 with purpose code GRCT (General Capital Transfer) or CFPP (Capital Contribution/Subscription to an International Company). The remitting bank must include a full beneficial owner explanation, and the receiving Samoan bank will complete a CBS Foreign Exchange Approval Form (FEA-01) when the inward transfer exceeds WST 500,000. The necessary verification pack comprises the IC certificate, the board resolution for capitalisation, a source-of-funds declaration, and a no-disbursement undertaking until the account is activated.Trade disbursements from the Samoan IC treasury are cleared via two principal rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct payments for prepayments, open-account settlements, broker commissions, and minor demurrage claims, with purpose code S102; (2) documentary letters of credit using SWIFT MT700/MT720 series, confirmed by top-tier Far East/European correspondent banks, for bulk sulfur cargo disbursements under the ISIC 4669 commercial model. Where a Samoan bank issues the LC, it will typically co-accept using a funded escrow arrangement, with the WST/USD conversion executed at spot or under a pre-sold forward contract. Erroneous payment risk is mitigated by dual verification of the vessel, port, and discharge certificate prior to release. The Samoan clearing system is unable to process same-day high-volume payments; therefore, all outbound disbursements greater than USD 250,000 must be queued 48 hours in advance, unless a dedicated USD clearing account is held at ANZ or BSP Samoan correspondent network.Deliverable FX forwards against USD, AUD, NZD, and EUR, up to 12-month tenor with a CBS-licensed commercial bank, provided the underlying trade exposure is evidenced by a signed cargo sale/purchase contract.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on WST/USD, executed with offshore banks for hedging Samoan costs or WST-denominated commitments; CBS approves such structures only where the hedge does not create any onshore WST liabilities.Cross-currency swaps between WST and USD, exclusively for matching capital injection/repayment obligations of an IC having a registered Samoan investment project.Imported commodity-hedge rolling contracts for sulfur price exposure, structured as zero-premium swaps via a licensed securities/investment adviser, subject to CBS notification requirements when the notional amount exceeds the underlying trade transaction value by 20%.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% (standard Samoa corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2012); Samoa International Companies are statutorily exempt from all income tax, producing a 0% effective rate for WS_IC.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% final withholding tax under the Income Tax Act 2012 on dividends paid by a Samoa-resident taxable company. An exempt International Company is outside the tax net, and distributions by WS_IC to its non-resident shareholders are not subject to any Samoan dividend withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before the anniversary of incorporation; the annual return and annual government fee must be lodged within 60 days after that anniversary.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Generally due within 12 months after the end of the fiscal year, unless extended by the Commissioner; a taxable company must also pay provisional tax in two installments.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold in WST; Samoa has no mandatory Local File or Master File regime. The Income Tax Act 2012 imposes a general arm's-length standard and empowers the Commissioner to adjust cross-border related-party transactions. A tax-exempt IC has no Samoan transfer pricing return or documentation obligation, but should retain contemporaneous pricing evidence for sulfur offtake, freight, and brokerage contracts to withstand foreign tax authority scrutiny.

Economic substance scope: Samoa does not impose Economic Substance Regulations of the BVI/Cayman type. The International Companies Act 2019 instead requires a licensed registered agent, a registered office, an annual return, an annual government fee, and prohibits bearer shares. The IC must not transact business with Samoa residents or derive Samoa-source income. For dry-bulk sulfur trading and brokerage, substance is evidenced by genuine charter parties, sales contracts, SWIFT/Letter of Credit settlement records, retained price risk, and decision-making by officers outside Samoa. Absence of these indicia risks recharacterization as a shell under Samoa's general anti-avoidance rule and under foreign CFC/economic substance legislation in the beneficial owner's jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Sham Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Immediate recapture of the tax exemption for the relevant year; corporate income tax at 27% on the concealed income; penal tax of up to 100% of the avoided tax; default interest; deregistration of the company; prosecution fine of up to WST 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax, face imprisonment of up to 5 years, travel bans, and civil restitution orders.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Exercise of the general anti-avoidance rule permitting the Commissioner to disregard the IC and assess the true economic position; penalties up to 200% of tax avoided; strike-off from the Samoa register.

Director personal liability: Judicial piercing of the corporate veil: directors liable for civil restitution, personal disqualification, and criminal prosecution for fraud if documents are forged.
Failure to File Annual Return and Pay Annual Fee

Statutory sanction: Automatic strike-off after Registrar's notice; the company loses its limited liability shield and tax exemption; restoration requires payment of all arrears, compounded late penalties, and a restoration fee of WST 500 to WST 1,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade after strike-off become personally liable for all debts incurred after dissolution; officers are liable to fines for each day of default.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Offences under the International Companies Act 2019 and the Financial Intelligence Unit Act 2016: fines of up to WST 500,000 for the entity and WST 100,000 for a responsible officer; freezing orders over bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors individually fined, disqualified from managing any company in Samoa, and for repeat breaches, imprisonment of up to 3 years.
Non-Arm's-Length Transfer Pricing in Foreign Jurisdiction

Statutory sanction: Samoa imposes no adjustment on an exempt IC, but the foreign jurisdiction may recharacterize the arrangement, apply its own corporate income tax (typically 25-35%) on the adjusted profit, and levy transfer pricing penalties of 20-40% of the underpayment.

Director personal liability: Directors in the parent chain may face controlled foreign company attribution, personal liability under foreign tax statutes, and cross-border disqualification; Samoa's tax exemption provides no defense to foreign revenue claims.
