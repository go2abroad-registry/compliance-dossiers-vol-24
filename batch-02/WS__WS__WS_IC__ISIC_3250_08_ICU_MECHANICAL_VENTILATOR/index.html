Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (Matagaluega o Tupe Maua), operating through the Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA); taxpayer e-Services portal at www.sra.gov.ws; customs clearance interface via Samoa Customs ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned Samoa International Company, the licensed registered agent files incorporation with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) and obtains the Certificate of Incorporation. The agent then files Form SRA-01 (Taxpayer Registration) with the Samoa Revenue Authority, enclosing the Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered agent certificate, particulars of directors and shareholders, and passport copies of beneficial owners. The SRA issues a 9-digit Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) in approximately 5-7 business days. The same TIN is used for VAGST, customs, PAYE, and business licence interactions. In parallel, the entity registers with Samoa Customs (ASYCUDA World) to obtain an importer/exporter code. If actual or expected taxable supplies exceed WST 100,000 in any consecutive 12-month period, the IC must file Form VG-1 for Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) registration under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2007.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. A Samoa International Company incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is a non-resident offshore-exempt vehicle: it is exempt from Samoan income tax, capital gains tax, and stamp duty on all income, profits, and transactions not sourced in Samoa, and it is not subject to entity-level taxation on global export manufacturing profits. No annual income tax return is required; the annual SIFA licence fee is the only state charge. The exemption is lost if the IC carries on business in Samoa, owns Samoan land, or transacts with Samoan residents in breach of the Act, in which case domestic resident-taxpayer rules apply.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Samoa. Available statutory mechanisms include: (1) section 158 of the International Companies Act 1987 - conversion of an IC into a local company with Ministerial consent, changing the entity to resident, fully taxable status; (2) voluntary VAGST registration below the WST 100,000 threshold; (3) VAGST group registration and cash-basis accounting elections permitted by SRA determination for related and small enterprises; (4) customs warehouse licensing under the Customs Act 2014 allowing duty and VAGST deferral on imported ventilator components; (5) election to maintain accounting records in a foreign currency under the Tax Administration Act 2012. Absent hybrid-mismatch rules, the SRA applies general anti-avoidance, transfer-pricing, and thin-capitalisation (3:1 debt-to-equity) provisions to cross-border financing structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoan Tala) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; a person commencing business must register if taxable turnover is reasonably expected to exceed WST 100,000 in the first 12 months; voluntary registration is permitted. A non-resident supplier is registerable only where it has a Samoan fixed establishment or otherwise supplies goods physically in Samoa.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods exported by a registered VAGST person from Samoa to a destination outside Samoa, provided the goods are physically exported within 28 days of supply and the supplier retains documentary proof of export. Offshore services supplied to non-resident persons and performed entirely outside Samoa (including engineering, R&D, and assembly-support services for the ventilator line) are also zero-rated. The ventilator manufacturer receives a full input-tax credit on VAGST paid on imported components, circuit boards, sensors, and assembly machinery; where zero-rated outputs exceed taxable inputs, a cash refund is payable, subject to documentation checks.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic ASYCUDA World declaration accepted by Samoa Customs)
  • Bill of Lading, Master Air Waybill, or through bill of lading evidencing physical export from Samoa
  • Commercial invoice and sequentially numbered VAGST zero-rated tax invoice
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by the collecting Samoan commercial bank
  • SWIFT MT103 wire transfer advice or documentary letter-of-credit evidence of foreign-currency payment
  • Packing list, freight booking confirmation, and endorsed cargo movement record
  • Medical device export permit issued by the Samoa Ministry of Health where goods are classified as controlled health goods

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory e-invoicing API regime is in force in Samoa. VAGST-registered suppliers must issue sequentially numbered VAGST tax invoices containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and TIN, VAGST registration number, invoice date, description, quantity, and price exclusive and inclusive of tax). Returns are filed electronically through the SRA taxpayer e-Services portal, and payments are cleared electronically. For exports, ASYCUDA World provides the electronic customs declaration and clearance platform; invoice-level real-time clearance by the tax authority is not mandated.

The SRA audits zero-rated export refund claims and may reopen assessments within the statutory period, which is extended without limit in cases of fraud. If goods are not exported within 28 days, or supporting documentation is incomplete or false, the SRA disallows zero-rating, assesses VAGST at 15% on the supply, and claws back any refund paid with statutory interest at 20% per annum. Penalty tax is imposed under the Tax Administration Act 2012: 100% of the tax shortfall for intentional misstatement, 50% for careless misstatement, 20% for minor errors, and 150% for deliberate evasion. Fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence punishable by a fine of up to WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment for a term of up to five years. The SRA may also deregister the exporter's VAGST number, disqualify the entity from future zero-rating, and refer the case to the Revenue Criminal Investigation Unit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is convertible for bona fide current-account transactions through CBS-authorised dealers; capital-account movements (equity injections, cross-border loans, outward capital remittances) require CBS approval or a statutory exemption. A Samoa International Company conducting business exclusively in foreign currency with non-residents is outside the core exchange-control net under the International Companies Act 1987, permitting free maintenance and disposition of USD/EUR accounts. Convertibility friction materialises only where the IC transacts with Samoan residents in WST or remits to a resident beneficiary. Profit-remittance hurdles: dividends and capital distributions out of offshore foreign-currency earnings are remittable without a CBS licence, but the paying authorised dealer customarily requires a Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) tax-clearance certificate, audited financial statements, and a board dividend resolution; remittances funded from resident-source WST receipts remain blocked unless CBS approval is obtained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory baseline: export proceeds must be credited to a Samoan account with an authorised dealer within 90 days of export and surrendered (converted to WST) at the prevailing market rate unless a CBS-approved retention account or exemption is granted. For WS_IC exporting ICU ventilators, the 90-day repatriation and conversion obligation is disapplied where the IC holds an exemption certificate confirming no resident business and maintains offshore USD collection accounts; if any export sale is settled in WST through a Samoan resident buyer, 100% of that WST receipt is subject to mandatory surrender. Operational protocol: obtain the CBS written exemption through the designated authorised dealer before first shipment and attach it to each export declaration; non-compliance exposes the IC to compulsory sale of foreign exchange, civil penalties up to twice the undeclared amount, and criminal liability under the Foreign Exchange Act 1975.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), constituted under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, exercising exchange-control administration under the Foreign Exchange Act 1975 and the Foreign Exchange Regulations 1976; enforcement is coordinated with the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2020 and with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL) which registers Samoa International Companies (WS_IC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium as primary clearing rail. Offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, Currenxie, Wise Business, Mercury) cannot hold WST nor clear Samoa-domestic payment instruments, as CBS licenses only domestic banks and authorised financial institutions to operate payment and settlement systems. EMIs are viable solely as ancillary USD collection and concentration accounts feeding the licensed Samoan account, provided the EMI's upstream correspondent accepts the Samoan IC's full beneficial-ownership KYC. Legal constraint: EMIs cannot advise, issue, or settle commercial letters of credit or government instruments for Samoan public-health tenders; all LC and SIPS/WST-denominated flows must terminate at a CBS-licensed commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: the originating shareholder bank transmits a SWIFT MT103 to ANZ Bank Samoa or BSP Samoa for crediting the IC's USD account in Apia. The receiving authorised dealer holds funds for one to two days pending source-of-funds verification before final credit; any WST conversion is executed only on a CBS Foreign Exchange Declaration (EA01) filed by the authorised dealer. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Company under the Samoa International Companies tax-exempt offshore regime; resident domestic companies carrying on business in Samoa are taxed at the 27% corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2018.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends and distributions paid by an International Company, because the IC is a tax-exempt offshore entity outside the Samoan income tax net; the domestic statutory dividend WHT of 15% applies only where a resident Samoan company pays dividends to a non-resident under the Income Tax Act 2018.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation) is mandatory where related-party transactions exceed WST 5,000,000 in a tax year; Master File is required where offshore group consolidated revenue exceeds WST 100,000,000 or where the group files a Samoan consolidated return; tax-exempt International Companies are excluded from income tax transfer pricing documentation unless they unlawfully carry on business with Samoan residents.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2020 requires every Samoa International Company undertaking a relevant activity to demonstrate in Samoa: (i) effective management and control, (ii) adequate physical premises, (iii) adequate qualified employees, and (iv) adequate operating expenditure proportionate to profit. A turbine-driven ICU ventilator manufacturer operating through an IC, holding IP, or earning service and distribution income must satisfy this test; a pure equity holding exemption is available only where the IC performs no active trading and holds only passive investments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Prohibited Domestic Dealings

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and loss of International Company status; retrospective assessment as a resident taxpayer at 27% corporate tax on all Samoan-source income, plus penalty tax up to 150% of the shortfall and interest at 10% per annum under the Income Tax Act 2018; company fines up to WST 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent who signed the false annual return are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, penalties and interest; criminal conviction for tax evasion carries imprisonment up to 7 years, personal fines up to WST 50,000, a travel ban pending tax clearance, and disqualification from acting as a director in Samoa.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: WST 50,000 penalty plus WST 10,000 per month of continuing default; strike-off and forfeiture of IC assets; automatic exchange of information with EU Member States and Global Forum members; referral for transitional listing as a non-cooperative jurisdiction where systemic failures persist.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to pay substance penalties where the company defaults, face disqualification from the Samoa company registry for up to 5 years, and, for shell structures used to evade foreign tax, may be subjected to mutual-assistance travel bans and extradition proceedings.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Income adjustment to the arm's length price under anti-avoidance rules; 27% corporate tax on the adjusted amount plus penalty of up to 150% and interest; cancellation of the IC tax-exemption certificate; referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit for trade-based money-laundering investigation; fines up to WST 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false cross-border invoices face criminal liability for false accounting under the Samoa Criminal Law Act with imprisonment up to 5 years, and each knowingly participating director is liable up to WST 100,000 for the resulting tax shortfall.
Anti-Money Laundering and Corruption in Government Procurement

Statutory sanction: Offences under the Anti-Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act 2018: fines up to WST 1,000,000, confiscation of the ventilator shipments and proceeds, freeze and forfeiture orders, permanent vendor blacklisting from Samoan government procurement, and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners and the registered agent are jointly liable for restitution of corrupt payments; travel bans and asset restraint orders issue automatically upon charge; individual sentencing may reach 14 years' imprisonment and permanent exclusion from public procurement.
Medical Device Regulatory and Licensing Violations

Statutory sanction: Seizure and destruction of non-compliant devices; customs penalty up to 200% of the goods' duty-paid value; revocation of the exporter license; referral to the medical products regulator; public disclosure of the enforcement action.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines up to WST 250,000 and criminal liability for endangering public health with imprisonment up to 10 years where devices fail life-safety standards; travel bans may be imposed pending investigation, and directors leaving Samoa remain subject to extradition under mutual assistance treaties.
