Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — https://www.vfsc.vu — registers International Companies; the Department of Finance and Treasury (VAT Office), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management — https://www.finance.gov.vu — administers all indirect tax. No income-tax authority exists because Vanuatu levies no income tax.

E-government filing portal

No TIN or income-tax registration exists. VAT registration (Form 01 to the Commissioner of VAT, Section 5, VAT Act [Cap 293]) is inapplicable to a bona fide IC, which is barred from carrying on business in Vanuatu and is exempt from VAT. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. Section 10, International Companies Act [Cap 222]: the IC is exempt from all income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, estate/inheritance tax, stamp duty and VAT on all transactions conducted under the Act. Conditionality: the IC must not carry on business in Vanuatu, transact with residents outside the IC framework, or own Vanuatu real estate; breach causes retroactive loss of exemption.

Available election forms

None. Vanuatu has no corporate income tax statute and therefore no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. Exemption is automatic and unconditional. The only administrative options are voluntary VAT registration (inapt for an exempt IC) and migration/deregistration under the IC Act; neither creates tax liability absent a breach.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 in taxable supplies in any 12-month period (approx. USD 42,000); compulsory for domestic taxable activities; inapplicable to an exempt offshore IC

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Section 48, VAT Act [Cap 293] zero-rates: (a) exports of goods with proof of physical export; (b) services to non-residents directly connected with exported goods; (c) services connected with movable property outside Vanuatu; and (d) international transport. SaaS SEO/content-analytics services supplied to non-resident customers for consumption wholly abroad qualify as exported services and would be zero-rated if within the VAT net; a bona fide IC is, however, outside the scope of the VAT Act entirely and charges no 12.5% VAT, the IC exemption superseding the zero-rating mechanism. Cross-border digital-services taxes in customer jurisdictions are collected by Stripe/Paddle as merchant-of-record payment facilitators — not by Vanuatu.

  • Vanuatu Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World entry) — needed only for physical exports; for pure SaaS no customs entry arises, and the functional substitute is the executed SaaS subscription agreement plus server-access or usage logs evidencing overseas consumption
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill — required only on a physical-goods export leg; for SEO analytics the equivalent evidence is the signed master service agreement/order form identifying the non-resident customer and the cloud-infrastructure hosting location
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or Stripe-Paddle payout settlement statements — evidencing foreign-currency receipt of export proceeds, reconcilable to the invoice ledger and customer base

None. Vanuatu does not mandate e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API filing, or digital stamping. The VAT Act requires only a Commissioner-approved tax invoice containing supplier name and VAT number, date, description, quantity, price, and VAT amount. Records must be kept in English, retained 7 years, and available in Vanuatu. The IC issues its own contractual invoices from its registered-office address, charging no Vanuatu VAT.

An unsubstantiated zero-rating claim triggers default VAT assessment at 12.5% plus statutory interest, penalties up to twice the tax shortfall, fines up to VUV 500,000 and/or imprisonment for intentional false statements under the VAT Act. For an IC, the graver clawback is substantive: proof of local business, resident dealing, or registered-agent/office default forfeits the IC exemption retroactively, making all prior transactions taxable and exposing assets to court-ordered recovery. Vanuatu has no exchange-control statute; the 180-day figure reflects bank AML/KYC export-proceeds documentation benchmark practice, not a statutory repatriation deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains full capital and current account convertibility. There is no exchange control legislation and no requirement for prior RBV approval of capital injections, profit remittances, or subscription receipts. The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is freely convertible, and an International Company may denominate accounts and contracts in USD, EUR, AUD, or other currencies. Profit remittance is unrestricted by statute; the only substantive hurdles are bank-level source-of-funds due diligence and maintaining a valid registered agent and Good Standing certificate with VFSC. This yields a Tier-1 unrestricted convertibility profile for a cross-border SaaS exporter.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender rules. SaaS subscription and SEO analytics export receipts may be retained indefinitely in any convertible currency, and there is no mandated conversion timeline or mandatory conversion ratio into VUV. Receipts are subject only to ordinary bank recordkeeping and the Vanuatu AML/CFT suspicious transaction reporting regime, with no central bank form filing for export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) as the central bank and monetary authority, with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) acting as the licensing and AML/CFT supervisor for banks and financial service providers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Vanuatu has no recognised e-money institution (EMI) or dedicated digital EMI licensing category. An unlicensed offshore EMI seeking to hold or transmit client funds would constitute unauthorised financial business under the Vanuatu Financial Services Framework, which creates immediate regulatory and banking-risk exposure. Offshore EMI settlement rails are therefore not a viable substitute for a VFSC-licensed bank account. The preferred architecture is to use regulated SaaS payment acquirers (Stripe and Paddle) for commercial receipts and an RBV-licensed commercial bank as the underlying treasury settlement account.

Vanuatu does not mandate a national balance-of-payments code, but the bank requires the payment to be clearly labelled as equity contribution so it can be distinguished from trade proceeds under its internal AML reporting. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; the Income Tax Act is functionally dormant for CIT purposes, and an International Company is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes on offshore income under s.4 of the International Companies Act Cap 222.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — no dividend withholding tax exists in Vanuatu. An International Company may distribute dividends, return capital, and redeem shares free of Vanuatu withholding tax, capital gains tax, or exchange-control approval, without restriction on foreign currency settlement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No local transfer pricing statute or documentation threshold applies because no corporate income tax exists. However, the ESR framework functionally imports OECD transfer pricing principles: a Vanuatu IC acting as a low-substance IP owner of the SEO analytics technology will be deemed to lack economic substance unless it demonstrates local people, premises, and expenditure commensurate with the intercompany royalty receivable. For any IC with an intercompany principal or limited-risk model, benchmarked master file and local file documentation is prudently prepared once gross annual revenue exceeds VT 10,000,000 (approximately USD 85,000) or once royalty inflows from a related party exceed USD 500,000, mirroring the OECD DEMPE analysis.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's Economic Substance (Regulations) Act 2019 applies to International Companies. The SEO content analytics software platform is the highest-risk category: any income derived from proprietary software, algorithms, keyword-data crawlers, trademarked brand use, or licence streams is an intellectual property (IP) business, which cannot rebut the presumption of low substance by outsourcing to foreign affiliates. Compliance requires: (i) board meetings and strategic IP decisions held in Vanuatu; (ii) a physical office with contemporaneous records; (iii) adequate qualified full-time employees resident in Vanuatu performing product development, deployment oversight, and data governance; (iv) local operating expenditure commensurate with IP-generated gross income; and (v) annual ESR01 and ESR02 filings. A pure equity holding classification is available only if the IC passively holds the SaaS-IP subsidiary; active use of the software in sales disqualifies passive holding treatment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Misrepresentation of International Company status / unlawful domestic business

Statutory sanction: The IC forfeits its tax-exempt status and becomes liable to 12.5% VAT on local supplies, business licence fees, and customs duties; VFSC may impose compliance penalties and strike the company off after three months of default, activating the asset-vesting provisions of the IC Act.

Director personal liability: A director signing a false ASR declaration faces a fine up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years; the Ministry of Finance may pursue civil restitution including clawback of distributed tax-free profits plus statutory interest.
Economic substance evasion (shell-company abuse)

Statutory sanction: Entity penalty up to VT 10,000,000 plus continuing daily penalties; mandatory exchange-of-information disclosure to OECD member tax authorities; de-risking and account closure by correspondent banks and payment processors including Stripe and Paddle KYB refusal.

Director personal liability: Senior officers liable to a fine up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years; directors face disqualification orders under the Companies Act and travel bans under AML/CFT provisions where money laundering or tax evasion predicates are alleged.
VAT evasion via direct consumer sales

Statutory sanction: In each customer jurisdiction, the prior-year tax liability plus penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded, statutory interest, and merchant-account termination by payment platforms. Because no Vanuatu CIT offset exists, all offshore indirect tax exposure remains with the IC.

Director personal liability: Directors may be pursued by foreign tax authorities through civil garnishment and administrative co-operation; where VAT fraud is established, the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit can act on mutual legal assistance requests, resulting in account seizure, travel bans, and extradition exposure under the Extradition Act.
Beneficial ownership / AML non-disclosure

Statutory sanction: Entity fine up to VT 10,000,000 for UBO non-compliance; VFSC power to suspend financial services permissions; daily default fines for continued non-filing.

Director personal liability: Individual officers face fines up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years; personal director accounts used for company fund flows may be frozen; directors face five-year banking debarment and immigration-related travel bans where money-laundering indicators arise.
