Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital or escrow account is required for an International Company. Share capital may be denominated in any convertible currency and may be issued at par, at a premium, or partly paid as stated in the Memorandum; non-cash consideration must be recorded in a board resolution.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue (MoR), Government of Samoa — official e-Services and compliance portal at revenue.gov.ws (e-filing at eservices.revenue.gov.ws); customs functions administered under the Samoa Customs Service within the same ministry through the ASYCUDA World platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Obtain from the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) the Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing for the International Company (International Companies Act 1987, as amended). Step 2: File the MoR taxpayer registration application (Form IR-1) via the Ministry of Revenue e-Services portal, attaching the SIFA certificate, certified board resolution appointing the registered agent, and the registered agent's Samoa physical address. Step 3: MoR issues a nine-digit Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) within five working days of a complete application. Step 4: Complete Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) exchange-control registration as an exporter for inward remittance clearance. Step 5: For VAGST purposes, the International Company must register only if it makes or intends to make taxable supplies in Samoa exceeding the annual threshold (SAT$100,000); an offshore dental ceramic block manufacturer with no Samoa economic activity should obtain a written non-registration confirmation from MoR.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate an IRS-style check-the-box election. The Income Tax Act 2015 charges resident-company profits but expressly excludes International Companies, which are statutorily exempt from income tax, VAGST (absent domestic taxable activity), stamp duty, and capital duty under the International Companies Act 1987. Available elections: (i) an elective articles amendment under s.16 of the International Companies Act 1987 to adopt tax-indemnity and distribution provisions; (ii) if voluntarily VAGST-registered, an election under the VAGST Act 2008 to account for output tax on an invoice/accruals basis rather than a payments basis; and (iii) BEPS-aligned CFC attribution rules apply automatically, with no hybrid-mismatch election available, ensuring the offshore manufacturing and export structure remains fiscally transparent-neutral.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAGST registration applies where taxable supplies exceed or are likely to exceed SAT$100,000 in any 12-month period (threshold raised from SAT$80,000 by the VAGST Amendment Act 2019); voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold. A Samoa International Company manufacturing dental CAD/CAM zirconia blocks exclusively for export and with no domestic taxable supplies remains outside the VAGST registration net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available where the entity is a VAGST-registered person and either (i) goods are physically exported from Samoa within seven days of supply (twenty-eight days with Customs approval) and the export is made in the course of a taxable activity, or (ii) services are supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply and the services are not connected with Samoa land, goods, or personal enjoyment in Samoa. Offshore dental CAD/CAM design-data services and internationally shipped zirconia blocks qualify, and input VAT credits (raw zirconia, milling consumables, laboratory equipment) may be claimed on a pro-rata basis apportioned to zero-rated export supplies.

  • Samoa Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export declaration, approved and validated by the Samoa Customs Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical shipment from Apia or another Samoa port to the overseas consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by the exporting entity's licensed commercial bank, evidencing receipt of foreign-currency export proceeds through the Central Bank of Samoa exchange-control system

Samoa has not enacted a real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or continuous transaction-control regime; customs clearance is electronic only via ASYCUDA World. The binding mandates under the Tax Administration Act 2012 and VAGST Act 2008 are: (i) every VAGST-registered supplier must issue sequential tax invoices containing supplier name, address, TIN and VAGST registration number, quantity, unit price, and VAGST amount; (ii) simplified retailer receipts are allowed only for supplies under SAT$1,000; (iii) VAGST returns and payments must be filed electronically through MoR e-Services; and (iv) export transactions require a validated electronic Customs Export Declaration in ASYCUDA World as the pre-export clearance step.

MoR audits zero-rated export refunds by cross-matching the customs export declaration, the inward remittance certificate, and supplier input-credit documentation. A fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claim triggers: (i) full retrospective clawback of the VAGST refund; (ii) an evasion penalty of 150% of the tax shortfall under the Tax Administration Act 2012; (iii) monthly late-payment interest of 2%, compounding; and (iv) criminal prosecution for knowing false statements, attracting fines up to SAT$100,000 and imprisonment of up to seven years. Non-repatriation or late repatriation of export proceeds additionally exposes the exporter to Central Bank of Samoa penalties, exchange-control licence suspension, and customs penalties under the Customs Act 2014 for false or misleading export declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tala (WST) is a managed-float currency pegged to a trade-weighted basket of the currencies of Samoa's principal trading partners. Current-account payments for bona fide trade and services are freely executable through authorised dealers without prior central-bank approval. Capital-account movements, including equity injections by foreign shareholders into an International Company (IC), are permitted but subject to written notification to the Central Bank of Samoa, source-of-funds verification, and reporting through authorised-dealer exchange-control forms. Profit and dividend remittances require an audited determination of available distributable reserves and the absence of any Samoan tax debt for remittance certification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds derived from the sale of dental CAD/CAM zirconia blocks and milling discs must be received into a Samoan authorised-dealer account within 180 days of shipment and surrendered to that dealer within 7 business days of crediting. The mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of gross foreign-exchange export proceeds converted into WST unless the exporter has been granted a foreign-currency account permit by the Central Bank of Samoa. Retention beyond the statutory deadline requires a written exemption application supported by letters of credit, forward sales contracts, or evidence of unavoidable banking delays.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Samoa's reserve monetary authority, administering exchange control under the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations 1978, and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2018)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility — Samoa's legal framework recognises only authorised financial institutions and authorised dealers under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1978. Non-resident electronic-money institutions are not licensed to hold deposits or provide treasury settlement services for Samoan International Companies. An offshore EMI may be used solely as an auxiliary remittance-referencing layer, not as the primary liquidity depository, and must always be backed by a domestic Samoan authorised-dealer account to preserve exchange-control compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital wires must be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign investor's bank to the International Company's Samoan authorised-dealer non-resident account. The payment narrative field must reference the Samoan International Company Registry number and use the standard balance-of-payments purpose classification for direct investment equity capital as prescribed by the Central Bank of Samoa. The receiving bank must obtain the board resolution, verify remitter identity, ensure the deposit is not in a conflict-affected high-risk currency, and complete Central Bank notification form EXC-04 for all receipts above 100,000 WST or USD equivalent. The acquisition of WST from the foreign-currency leg must be executed at the daily authorised-dealer mid-rate.Clearing of outbound trade disbursements for dental zirconia blocks and CAD/CAM milling discs is executed through SWIFT MT103, directly to the beneficiary bank, with a documentary letter of credit offered as the preferred rail for higher-credit-risk or first-time buyers. Letters of credit must conform to the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600); Samoan authorised banks may add their confirmation to a sight or deferred-payment LC. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (International Company statutory exemption); standard resident corporate headline rate is 25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Local File or Master File filing is statutorily triggered for the exempt IC; Samoa's Income Tax Act 2015 transfer pricing documentation duty attaches to resident taxpayers with aggregate related-party transactions above WST 1,000,000 per tax year, but the IC has no assessable income. Group treasury should nevertheless maintain a Master File, a Local File and a comparables search for audit defence.

Economic substance scope: Samoa has not enacted BVI-style Economic Substance Regulations. The equivalent doctrine is embedded in the International Companies Act: the IC must not carry on business with Samoa residents, must derive no domestic-sourced income, and must maintain a licensed registered agent capable of evidencing offshore character. Failure converts the entity into a taxable resident company and voids the exemption; the Samoa General Anti-Avoidance Rule additionally recharacterizes any local operational presence, such as a Samoan milling facility, as a taxable permanent establishment of the dental block manufacturing group.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through misuse of the International Company exemption

Statutory sanction: Immediate withdrawal of the exemption; deemed assessment on the full hidden turnover; penalty equal to 200% of the tax evaded; maximum fine of WST 500,000; imprisonment up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and penalties; travel bans issued by the Ministry of Revenue; freezing of personal bank accounts; disqualification from managing any Samoa company for 10 years.
Shell company / sham corporate facade

Statutory sanction: Judicial piercing of the corporate veil; revocation of the certificate of incorporation; winding-up order; restitution order equal to the gross sales proceeds rerouted to the real owner; WST 100,000 civil penalty.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution without discharge in bankruptcy; personal liability for all debts of the sham entity; 5-year director disqualification; criminal prosecution for fraudulent incorporation with imprisonment up to 7 years.
AML/CFT breach and false beneficial ownership declaration

Statutory sanction: FIU-imposed monetary penalty of up to WST 1,000,000; account freezing and confiscation orders; deregistration of the IC; referral to the Samoan criminal prosecution service for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers personally fined up to WST 150,000; imprisonment up to 10 years for engaging in predicate money-laundering; confiscation of personal assets tracing to the tainted dental-block revenue.
Transfer pricing manipulation and false documentation

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length reallocation of the entire margin to the Samoan taxable entity; 150% penalty on the resulting tax shortfall; compounding interest at 10% per year; disallowance of all expenses connected to the sham supply chain.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign incomplete or false TP declarations are personally fined WST 150,000; extradition exposure for fiscal fraud; personal guarantee for the unpaid group tax debt if transfers drained the taxable entity's cash reserves.
