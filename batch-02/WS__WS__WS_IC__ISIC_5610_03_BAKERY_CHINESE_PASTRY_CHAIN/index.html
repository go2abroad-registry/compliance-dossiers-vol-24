Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A Samoa International Company must maintain a physical registered office in Samoa at a street address (not a post office box), provided by a licensed registered agent. A virtual office is permitted only for supplementary business correspondence and remote-management administrative support outside Samoa; it cannot replace the statutory registered office for service of process or official notices. For the bakery franchise, virtual board meetings and overseas operational offices are acceptable provided the Samoan registered agent's address remains the sole legal seat.

Competent corporate registry

Governing statute

International Companies Act 1987 (Samoa), as amended, ss. 6-12 (incorporation), s. 14 (registration), s. 24 (registered office and agent); International Companies Regulations 1998; Samoa International Financial Services Authority Act 2007.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None required by law; the International Companies Act 1987 imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital. The Constitution declares authorized capital (commonly USD 100,000), and issued shares may be fully or partly paid at the subscribers' discretion; no escrow account or capital deposit obligation applies to International Companies.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (Samoa), the national tax administration (successor to the Samoa Revenue Authority), with taxpayer registration, e-filing, and VAGST administration via the official portal https://www.revenue.gov.ws; Samoa Customs Service discharges customs-related fiscal controls under the same ministry.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated investor or its licensed Samoan registered agent lodges the TIN Application Form with the Ministry for Revenue together with: (i) the Certificate of Incorporation or International Company registration certificate, (ii) the certificate of registered office in Samoa, (iii) identity documents for all directors and ultimate beneficial owners, and (iv) the memorandum and articles of association if requested. The Ministry issues the statutory Tax Identification Number within approximately 5-10 business days; the same TIN functions as the VAGST registration number upon registration. An International Company with wholly offshore activity may instead obtain the conclusive tax-exemption certificate issued under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended), which eliminates the requirement to hold a TIN for purely international business income.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Samoa International Company under the International Companies Act 1987; all international business income is exempt from Samoa income tax; the entity is exempt from stamp duty and exchange-control restrictions; engaging in local business with Samoa residents within the domestic market would destroy the exemption and subject the company to the ordinary corporate tax and VAGST regime).

Available election forms

(1) Domestic-conversion election: the board may elect in the company's constitution to become a domestic company governed by the Companies Act 2012, thereby entering the normal Samoan income-tax and VAGST net; (2) Voluntary VAGST registration election under the VAGST Act 2005, permitting input-tax credit recovery while offshore exports remain zero-rated; (3) Hybrid-status certification: the Ministry for Revenue's certificate of tax status may be tendered to foreign tax authorities to evidence tax-transparent or tax-exempt character of the International Company. Samoa does not operate a US-style check-the-box (Form 8832) election regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoan tala) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; registration is compulsory once the threshold is exceeded, while voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold; offshore royalty and master-franchise supplies made by a non-resident-facing International Company lie outside the Samoan taxable-supply perimeter.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for the 0% export rate where they are physically exported from Samoa within the prescribed period under a customs export declaration, sold to a non-resident purchaser, and the consideration is received from outside Samoa. Services (including master-franchise royalties and offshore central-kitchen know-how supplied by the International Company) qualify for zero-rating where the recipient is a non-resident and the services are supplied for use and benefit wholly outside Samoa. Where the International Company relies on the statutory exemption under the International Companies Act 1987 in respect of its offshore activities, the supply is outside the VAGST system altogether rather than taxed at 0%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export of the goods from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign-currency proceeds
  • Commercial invoice issued in the VAGST-prescribed format
  • Certificate of Origin where required by the importing jurisdiction
  • International Company tax-exemption certificate issued by the Ministry for Revenue (for claims founded on the IC statutory exemption)

Samoa has not enacted real-time fiscal clearance, API-based continuous transaction controls, or mandatory digital stamping. A registered person must issue tax invoices and credit notes containing the particulars prescribed by the VAGST Regulations (supplier identity, TIN, date, description, VAGST-exclusive amount, and VAGST-inclusive amount), self-assess, and file periodic VAGST returns electronically through the Ministry for Revenue e-filing portal; invoices and source records must be retained for the statutory retention period and produced in audits, including to substantiate any zero-rating position.

The Ministry for Revenue may audit export-zero-rating claims within the statutory assessment window, disregard incomplete documentation, re-assess output VAGST, deny refunds, revoke VAGST registration, and apply statutory interest together with shortfall penalties of up to double the tax evaded for false or misleading declarations; fraudulent export claims expose responsible officers to criminal fines and imprisonment under the VAGST Act 2005 and the Tax Administration Act 2012, with retrospective clawback of improperly credited refunds permitted for the entire open assessment period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tālā (WST) is partially convertible. Current-account payments for trade and services are generally permissible through authorized dealers, with prior CBS filing for amounts above WST 1,000,000. Capital-account transactions are subject to case-by-case CBS approval. For a non-resident International Company (IC), profits and dividends may be remitted freely where the underlying funds originate from foreign-currency accounts and are not converted into WST; conversion into WST or settlement through a resident Samoan clearing system triggers exchange control documentation and, for dividend remittances, an approval of non-resident interest. The IC's tax-exempt status does not automatically exempt distributions from exchange-control scrutiny.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa Exchange Control Notices require resident exporters to repatriate export proceeds to an authorized commercial bank within 90 days of the bill of lading date and to convert 50% of export receipts to WST within 14 days of receipt. A non-resident International Company with no Samoan-resident obligor and whose entire revenue arises from overseas is classified as non-resident for exchange-control purposes and is eligible for a full exemption from the conversion quota, provided CBS Form XR-12 'Declaration of Non-Resident Export Proceeds' is filed on an annual basis.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1975 and the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, with delegated authority to licensed commercial banks for routine exchange control approvals.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low-Medium. Offshore digital EMI (e-money institution) licenses are not recognized as deposit-taking licenses in Samoa. An offshore EMI may be used solely for cross-border receivables collection or disbursement in a non-WST corridor, but the Samoa IC must still maintain an operational WST or foreign-currency account with a licensed Samoan commercial bank for all regulated clearing. The Central Bank of Samoa has not issued any EMI or digital-bank license; use of an offshore EMI without a corresponding local licensed banking relationship may raise unlicensed-banking-law risk.
