Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) - the national tax and customs administration of the Government of Samoa (successor to the Ministry for Revenue, 'Maota o Tupe Maua'), Apia; official e-services portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws; customs declarations and clearance via the ASYCUDA World single window.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 through a licensed registered agent and local registered office at the Registrar of International Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL), Apia; 2) Submit the TIN application (Form TIN-A) to the SRA with the Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent consent, director/shareholder particulars, and passport copies - statutory TIN is issued within 5-10 business days; 3) Register with Samoa Customs as an importer/exporter and obtain a Customs Client Code plus a Bonded Warehouse Operator Licence under the Customs Act 2014; 4) Register for VAGST (Form VR-1) only where the IC makes taxable supplies in Samoa exceeding SAT 200,000 (WST 200,000) per rolling 12 months - offshore-only ICs remain outside the VAGST net; 5) File the MCIL annual declaration and maintain the statutory tax-exemption status; no income tax return is required for the tax-exempt offshore business.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Samoan International Company is statutorily a non-resident offshore entity exempt from income tax, stamp duty, and business licence fees under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended), read with the Income Tax Ordinance 2015; the bonded wine/spirits export business is therefore outside the territorial income tax net.

Available election forms

Not applicable - Samoa has no check-the-box or entity-classification election. Tax classification is fixed by statute: the International Company is non-resident and tax-exempt and cannot elect pass-through or entity-level taxation. No hybrid mismatch election is available. The only elective step is voluntary VAGST registration under the VAGST Act 2009 where the IC chooses to make domestic taxable supplies, plus the administrative application for a Certificate of Tax Exemption / tax-status confirmation letter from the SRA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

SAT 200,000 (Samoa Tala / WST 200,000) of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period triggers compulsory VAGST registration (VAGST Act 2009, as amended); voluntary registration is available to non-resident and export-only suppliers. The bonded trader must in all cases hold Customs Client and excise-suspension registrations regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and offshore supplies of services are zero-rated under the VAGST Act 2009. For bonded wine/spirits wholesale, 0% VAGST applies where: (a) the bonded goods physically depart Samoa under a Customs export declaration; (b) title and risk pass to a non-resident buyer; (c) the operator holds a valid Bonded Warehouse Operator Licence (Customs Act 2014) and Liquor Wholesale Licence (Sale of Liquor Act 2011); (d) removal occurs within the bond period such that excise duty is abated; and (e) the full documentary evidence bundle is retained. Input VAGST on freight, storage, and agency services attributable to export supplies is creditable or refundable through the SRA refund mechanism.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World) matched to the bonded removal permit
  • Bonded Warehouse Removal/Transfer Permit issued by Samoa Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Samoa
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin/analysis (as applicable)
  • VAGST tax invoice marked 'Zero-Rated Export'
  • Bonded stock ledger and excise duty-suspension reconciliation report
  • Inward Foreign Currency Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT advice of payment from the non-resident buyer

No mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or Continuous Transaction Controls exist in Samoa. Registered VAGST suppliers must issue compliant tax invoices and preserve records for 7 years, with returns filed through the SRA e-services portal. All bonded movements and export declarations are lodged electronically in ASYCUDA World, which performs risk assessment and customs clearance; ASYCUDA lodgment is not a fiscal stamping regime.

Samoa Customs and the SRA may audit export and bond records for up to 7 years. If zero-rating is claimed without genuine export, bonded wine/spirits are diverted to domestic consumption, or the export proceeds are not received within 180 days: (i) the full 15% VAGST is assessed as output tax with monthly late-payment penalties and prescribed interest; (ii) excise duty is clawed back at the applicable spirits/wine rate plus a penalty not exceeding twice the duty; (iii) false declarations attract fines up to SAT 100,000 and/or imprisonment under the VAGST Act 2009 and Customs Act 2014, with aggravated evasion punishable by up to 7 years' imprisonment and forfeiture of goods; (iv) the IC's tax-exempt status, customs client code, and bonded warehouse licence may be revoked and the operator disqualified from future bonded facilities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a managed-float currency pegged by the CBS to a trade-weighted basket of the currencies of Samoa's major trading partners (USD, NZD, AUD, EUR, GBP, JPY, TWD) with active central-bank intervention. For current-account transactions - payments for genuine bonded wine purchases, storage, insurance, freight, and distributor commissions - the WST is freely convertible through licensed authorized dealers under the CBS general authority, requiring only documentary support (commercial invoice, warehouse release, transport documents) and the bank's standard AML/CFT checks. For capital-account transactions - initial equity subscription, shareholder subordinated loans, capital reductions, profit distributions, and redemption of shares - a prior CBS specific permit is mandatory; the authorized dealer files the application on behalf of the IC with board resolutions, source-of-funds documentation, and destination account details, with an observed approval cycle of 5 to 10 business days. Profit and dividend remittances by an exempt International Company carry no Samoan withholding tax, but the CBS requires a certificate of good standing from SIFA, a Ministry of Revenue tax-clearance confirmation (where applicable), and audited or CPA-reviewed management accounts demonstrating distributable profits derived exclusively from offshore bonded-trade activity. No domestic restriction applies to a non-resident IC holding foreign currency; the operating currency of the treasury should be USD/EUR/GBP because offshore WST liquidity is thin and the WST is not a deliverable settlement currency outside the Samoan banking system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1977, resident Samoan exporters must receive export proceeds through an authorized dealer within 90 days of shipment and surrender the full WST-equivalent to the banking system unless a surrender exemption is formally granted. For non-resident International Companies holding CBS external-account status, the applicable rules are materially different: (i) proceeds from bonded re-export trades must be credited to a CBS-approved external foreign-currency account at a Tier-1 authorized dealer (ANZ Bank Samoa or BSP Samoa) within 14 days of receipt from the buyer; (ii) no mandatory conversion into WST applies, so in practice 0% of bonded-trade proceeds are surrendered; (iii) every deposit must be accompanied by the trade invoice, warehouse release certificate, and transport document, retained for six years for CBS audit; and (iv) the IC must file an annual External Account Declaration reconciling all inbound and outbound trade flows through the authorized dealer. Balances in the external account may be freely remitted to bonded warehouse custodians, customs-bonded operators, insurers, and wholesale buyers worldwide without further CBS approval, provided the IC does not transact with Samoan residents and does not carry on business within the Samoan customs territory.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) / Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa, Exchange Control Division - the sole monetary and foreign-exchange licensing authority in Samoa, operating under the Exchange Control Act 1976, Exchange Control Regulations 1977, Central Bank of Samoa Act 2003, Financial Institutions Act 1996, and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2017. The CBS grants all authorized-dealer licenses, approves capital-account transactions, maintains the external-account designation regime for non-resident International Companies, and supervises the Samoan International Finance Authority (SIFA) interface for IC corporate records.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility with binding legal constraints. The CBS does not license electronic-money institutions or standalone third-party payment providers; the Financial Institutions Act 1996 restricts the taking of deposits and the operation of payment accounts to licensed commercial banks. Offshore digital EMIs cannot hold settlement accounts at Samoan banks, and CBS Exchange Control practice requires the IC to route all material inbound and outbound treasury flows through a Samoan authorized dealer in order to preserve the external-account audit trail and the capital-account evidence file. An offshore EMI may be used only as an auxiliary invoicing wallet in non-Samoan jurisdictions, provided it never holds IC share capital, never executes WST-denominated payments, and is not relied upon as the primary clearing rail; such an arrangement materially raises AML and correspondent-banking red flags and is not recommended as the primary treasury architecture. The preferred execution is a CBS-licensed multi-currency account at ANZ or BSP, complemented where necessary by a parallel EUR account at a Tier-1 EU bank connected through the same SWIFT infrastructure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All equity and shareholder-loan injections must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for interbank cover) to the CBS-licensed external account at ANZ or BSP Samoa, with field 70 purpose narration reading 'SHARE SUBSCRIPTION WS_IC [date]' or 'INTERCOMPANY LOAN AGREEMENT [reference]'. The authorized dealer must receive, within five business days of credit, the board capitalization resolution, the share allotment form or executed loan agreement, and the source-of-funds declaration for the CBS capital-account record. In-kind capital contributions consisting of title to wine parcels in bonded warehouses are recognized as lawful IC assets; they are evidenced by an independent valuation report, the warehouse custodian's written confirmation of title transfer to WS_IC, and registration of the parcels in the IC's fixed-asset schedule held by the Samoan registered agent; no physical movement of goods to Samoa occurs. Escrowed subscription funds are released to the operating sub-ledger only after the CBS capital-import permit is issued; for loan injections, the interest rate, maturity, and repayment schedule must be declared on the authorized dealer's capital-import reporting form to prevent recharacterization of shareholder debt as equity.(1) Commercial Letters of Credit: for first-time supplier relationships, a confirmed irrevocable LC issued by ANZ or BSP Samoa in favor of the bonded warehouse custodian or fine-wine merchant, governed by UCP 600, requiring presentation of the warehouse release certificate, bonded stock number, and excise-suspension documents; this is the mandatory rail for purchases from new French and British merchants. (2) Open-account SWIFT MT103 wires from the IC external USD/EUR/GBP account to the supplier's clearing bank, with field 70 coded to the Bonded Parcel reference and field 72 referring to the purchase agreement; same-day value is achieved through the SWIFT network. (3) Intra-group and working-capital transfers to the IC's EU, UK, or Asia-Pacific sub-accounts are permitted under the external-account general authority if coded 'TRADE FINANCE - BONDED WINES' and supported by the underlying warehousing agreement. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low feasibility with binding legal constraints. The CBS does not license electronic-money institutions or standalone third-party payment providers; the Financial Institutions Act 1996 restricts the taking of deposits and the operation of payment accounts to licensed commercial banks. Offshore digital EMIs cannot hold settlement accounts at Samoan banks, and CBS Exchange Control practice requires the IC to route all material inbound and outbound treasury flows through a Samoan authorized dealer in order to preserve the external-account audit trail and the capital-account evidence file. An offshore EMI may be used only as an auxiliary invoicing wallet in non-Samoan jurisdictions, provided it never holds IC share capital, never executes WST-denominated payments, and is not relied upon as the primary clearing rail; such an arrangement materially raises AML and correspondent-banking red flags and is not recommended as the primary treasury architecture. The preferred execution is a CBS-licensed multi-currency account at ANZ or BSP, complemented where necessary by a parallel EUR account at a Tier-1 EU bank connected through the same SWIFT infrastructure.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% (Income Tax Act 2012, as amended). A licensed Samoa International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from income tax, stamp duty and exchange control under s.209 of the International Companies Act 2008 while it remains non-resident in conduct and derives no Samoa-source income, resulting in a 0% effective tax rate for the bonded trade.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% final withholding tax under the Income Tax Act 2012 on dividends paid by a Samoa resident company to non-resident shareholders. Dividends distributed by a qualifying International Company to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Samoan tax and paid without WHT deduction while the IC licence and non-residence conditions are continuously satisfied.
