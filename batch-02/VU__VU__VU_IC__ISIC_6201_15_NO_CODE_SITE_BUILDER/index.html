Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not permissible as the statutory registered office for an International Company. Section 8 of the International Companies Act 1992 requires a physical registered office in Vanuatu maintained by a licensed registered agent, with physical access by VFSC and service of process. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila, Vanuatu; administrative portal administered by the Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Service (VCIR) via the official government e-services gateway.

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu International Companies (ICs) are tax-exempt and are not ordinarily required to obtain a domestic VAT or income tax identification number. If a physical customs or employer registration need arises, a foreign entity must file a written application with the VCIR using the prescribed registration form, accompanied by: (i) certificate of incorporation and IC registration certificate issued by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); (ii) copy of the registered office address certificate issued by a licensed registered agent; (iii) particulars of directors and shareholders; (iv) beneficial ownership declaration; and (v) a statement confirming the intended activities in Vanuatu. Processing is typically completed within 5–10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Vanuatu International Companies (ICs) are tax-exempt and are not ordinarily required to obtain a domestic VAT or income tax identification number. If a physical customs or employer registration need arises, a foreign entity must file a written application with the VCIR using the prescribed registration form, accompanied by: (i) certificate of incorporation and IC registration certificate issued by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); (ii) copy of the registered office address certificate issued by a licensed registered agent; (iii) particulars of directors and shareholders; (iv) beneficial ownership declaration; and (v) a statement confirming the intended activities in Vanuatu. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists under Vanuatu law because Vanuatu does not impose income tax. The International Company may statutorily elect to remain a non-resident exempt entity under the International Companies Act [CAP 222], which serves as the de facto exemption election. Hybrid mismatch rules under BEPS Action 2 have not been enacted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 3,000,000 Vatu of taxable supplies per annum for resident businesses; not applicable to International Companies, which fall outside the chargeable scope of Vanuatu VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the VAT Act, exports of goods are zero-rated when the goods are physically exported from Vanuatu within the applicable time limit and the exporter retains prescribed evidence of export. Services are zero-rated if supplied to a recipient outside Vanuatu, enjoyed wholly outside Vanuatu, or supplied directly in connection with goods situated outside Vanuatu. A Vanuatu International Company providing software-as-a-service or website-builder tools to non-residents is outside the charge to VAT and is not eligible for input VAT refunds; a resident VAT-registered vendor seeking zero-rating must satisfy the documentary export bundle.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bank inward remittance certificate or SWIFT payment receipt evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds
  • Contemporaneous contract or purchase order showing delivery to a non-resident

Vanuatu has no real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no e-invoicing API requirement, and no digital stamping for standard invoices. Registered VAT suppliers must issue paper or electronic tax invoices containing the prescribed VAT Act fields and retain them for at least 10 years. Customs export declarations are processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs platform; exempt International Companies serving offshore clients are outside the VAT invoicing and clearance regime.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective assessment by the Comptroller of Customs and Inland Revenue: the unpaid VAT becomes immediately due together with interest at the statutory rate, and a civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded may be imposed. In cases of deliberate fraud, criminal prosecution may result in a fine of up to VT 5,000,000 and/or imprisonment for up to five years. The Comptroller may extend the assessment period to eight years in fraud or wilful default cases and four years in ordinary cases. An International Company that improperly holds itself out as VAT-exempt for domestic supplies remains personally liable for VAT, customs duties, and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations. The vatu is freely convertible for all current international transactions, and profit remittance is unrestricted for International Companies engaged in offshore software services, subject to standard bank documentation (audited financial statements, tax clearance from the Vanuatu Customs & Inland Revenue Department, and, if applicable, the Vanuatu Investment Promotion Agency (VIPA) certificate for foreign investors). Capital-account inflows require notification or approval under the Foreign Investment Act 1998 via VIPA; no exchange-control licensing or repatriation quotas apply. Material FX consideration: the vatu is officially pegged to the Australian dollar at approximately 1 VUV = 0.01 AUD, creating structural currency risk for USD-denominated SaaS subscription revenue that should be managed through treasury hedging.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio exist under RBV rules. Export receipts for software, licensing, and SaaS services may be retained indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts (USD/EUR/AUD) at licensed commercial banks, subject only to bank-level AML/CFT activity monitoring and ongoing economic-substance evidence.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the monetary authority, national regulator of licensed credit institutions, and operator of the Vanuatu National Payment System.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with a structural legal constraint. Offshore/foreign-regulated digital EMIs are not licensed by the RBV to take deposits or maintain local IBANs for Vanuatu International Companies, as the RBV licenses credit institutions under the Financial Institutions (Amendment) Act 2020 and prohibits unlicensed deposit-taking. In practice, the entity may use global payment processors (Stripe Billing, Paddle) for SaaS collections, settle those flows into local VUV/USD bank accounts at ANZ or BSP, and employ a foreign EMI strictly as an auxiliary payment-card or FX rail — never as primary treasury custody.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0 / Not applicable. Vanuatu has no domestic transfer pricing legislation, no statutory TP documentation thresholds, and no local file/master file requirement for International Companies. However, to manage source-country transfer pricing risk, a no-code SaaS/website builder entity with cross-border related-party transactions should maintain contemporaneous TP documentation where intercompany charges exceed the destination jurisdiction's filing thresholds (commonly USD 1 million for master file or local file triggers in the counterparty jurisdiction).

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu International Companies are subject to limited economic substance expectations only to the extent of maintaining a registered office, registered agent, and adequate records. Vanuatu has not enacted EU-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR). For a no-code website builder (ISIC 6201), the substance requirement in any high-tax parent jurisdiction may require actual management, employees, and servers in that jurisdiction. If the IC is treated by a foreign tax authority as a controlled foreign company or shell, it may be subject to CFC tax, economic substance tests, or denial of benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Offshore Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu International Companies Act 1992 and applicable foreign tax laws, the entity may be struck off or dissolved; criminal fines for false declarations; in foreign courts, the company's tax-exempt status may be disregarded to impose domestic corporate tax, interest, and penalties up to 200% of the understated tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may face civil restitution of unpaid taxes, asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment for fraudulent evasion where the corporate veil is pierced as a sham or mere façade.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Foreign anti-avoidance rules (CFC, transfer pricing, substance-based denial) may recharacterise the income as taxable in the shareholder's jurisdiction; penalties for inaccurate transfer pricing documentation typically 25-40% of the adjustment; EU blacklisting consequences may trigger enhanced due diligence and possibly listing of Vanuatu as non-cooperative.

Director personal liability: Shareholders and directors who create artificial arrangements without substance can be assessed jointly and severally for the tax and penalties; director disqualification and travel restrictions may apply.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Proliferation Financing Act 2020, fines up to VUV 200 million for body corporates and imprisonment up to 15 years for individuals; non-monetary penalties include suspension of the registered agent license and the company's deregistration.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal liability, civil penalties, and disqualification if knowingly involved in or willfully blind to money laundering, fraud, or terrorist financing.
Annual Return and License Fee Default

Statutory sanction: Late filing penalties accumulate from the due date, the company may be struck off the Register of International Companies, and all assets vested in the company escheat to the Crown or VFSC until restoration; restoration fees and court orders may be required.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue trading after strike-off are personally liable for the company's debts and contractual obligations; directors may be fined or imprisoned under the International Companies Act for providing false statements.
Illegal Local Business Activity

Statutory sanction: Loss of international company status and conversion to a domestic Vanuatu company, immediate tax liability on income derived from Vanuatu at the standard rate, monetary penalties for each offending transaction, and potential deregistration.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit unauthorised local business activity can be held personally liable for penalties, taxes, and indemnification of the company's losses.
