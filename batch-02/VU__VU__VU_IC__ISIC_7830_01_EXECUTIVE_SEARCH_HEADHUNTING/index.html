Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official portal: https://www.vrca.gov.vu. Company and International Company (IC) registry functions are administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) at https://www.vfsc.vu.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the Vanuatu International Company through a VFSC-licensed registered agent; obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, a registered office in Port Vila, and, where the IC will hold any local physical presence or employ staff, secure the applicable VIBO business license for executive-search services. Step 2 - If the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu or its taxable turnover (including zero-rated export retainers) exceeds VT 5,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period, apply to VRCA on the prescribed form for a VAT registration number, attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office certificate, business license (if applicable) and details of the resident registered agent; registration must be applied for within 21 days after the end of the month in which the threshold is exceeded. Step 3 - Voluntary VAT registration is available, but input VAT recovery is restricted where output consists exclusively of zero-rated offshore executive-search services. Step 4 - If the IC employs personnel physically working in Vanuatu, register separately with VRCA for PAYE withholding before the first payroll run. Step 5 - Obtain a written tax-status confirmation letter from VRCA confirming the IC exempt status and the zero-rating of offshore headhunting supplies; no income-tax TIN is issued because Vanuatu imposes no corporate, capital gains, withholding or personal income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No domestic check-the-box, pass-through, or hybrid-mismatch election exists because Vanuatu levies no income tax at either entity or shareholder level; the exemption applicable to an International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] applies automatically. Foreign owners remain subject to home-jurisdiction classification (e.g., U.S. IRC Section 7701 check-the-box and CFC/GILTI rules may classify the IC and impute its income). Domestically, an IC may elect to re-domicile as an ordinary company under the Companies Act, thereby surrendering IC exempt status, and may reverse that election with VFSC consent; the pre-1992 exempt-company category was consolidated into the International Company regime. Separately, an IC may elect the monthly VAT filing cycle to accelerate refunds on its zero-rated export service lines.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million vatu) of taxable turnover in any 12-month period; zero-rated export supplies count as taxable turnover for threshold purposes, so a headhunting IC whose offshore retainers and success fees exceed VT 5,000,000 must register for VAT even though all output is zero-rated, principally to reclaim input VAT on local overhead; the threshold is measured on a rolling 12-month basis and registration is mandatory within 21 days after the end of the month in which the threshold is exceeded

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Schedule 2 of the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 245], a supply is zero-rated when it is (a) an export of goods, (b) a supply of services to a non-resident recipient who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and whose place of business or fixed establishment receiving the service is outside Vanuatu, or (c) a supply of services directly related to international transport. For the executive-search and headhunting scope: the retainer contract must be executed with the foreign employer; the C-suite or specialist candidate placement must be for a position located outside Vanuatu; the VAT-exclusive retainer and success-fee invoices must be issued to the offshore client and marked 'Zero-rated - export of services'; and the IC must not be supplying local recruitment or human-resources placement services to a Vanuatu taxable person (such domestic supplies attract 15% VAT). Pure offshore headhunting retainers and placement success fees satisfy zero-ratng because the service is consumed at the employer's establishment outside Vanuatu.

  • Customs export declaration (ASYCUDA SAD filing) capturing shipment details where physical goods form part of the transaction
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing the movement of goods out of Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of hard-currency export proceeds in Vanuatu
  • Signed executive-search retainer agreement and engagement letter identifying the non-resident employer and the jurisdiction in which the services are consumed
  • Commercial tax invoice marked 'Zero-rated - export of services' carrying the client's registered business address
  • Evidence of client non-residence (foreign certificate of incorporation, overseas utility bill, or foreign tax ID) and evidence that the placed position is located outside Vanuatu
  • SWIFT MT103 or Wise B2B wire confirmation matching the invoiced retainer or success fee
  • Placement report and candidate acceptance note evidencing that the executive's employment is with a non-resident employer outside Vanuatu

No real-time fiscal clearance, government-authorization API, digital stamping or QR-code invoice mandate is in force for VAT as at the relevant stage. Taxpayers issue standard paper or PDF tax invoices containing the seller name, VAT registration number, sequential invoice number, date, description, VAT-exclusive amount, VAT rate and VAT amount. VAT returns are filed electronically through the VRCA e-services portal on a monthly or quarterly cycle, with monthly filing phased in for larger taxpayers. ASYCUDA World is used only for customs declarations of physical goods. VAT invoices and underlying export documentation must be retained for at least 7 years for audit and input-credit substantiation.

Zero-rating and VAT-refund claims are verified through desk audits, bank-record cross-checks and field examinations. If VRCA disallows zero-rating because documentation is missing or inconsistent (e.g., no inward remittance within 180 days, backdated contract, or evidence that the actual beneficiary is a Vanuatu resident), the supply is re-characterized as taxable at 15%, the net VAT is assessed on the gross retainer, associated input VAT is clawed back, and statutory penalty tax plus interest is imposed. Fraudulent or careless export zero-rating claims attract civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated (up to 200% for repeat offenders), and willful evasion carries criminal fines up to VT 500,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years under the Value Added Tax Act, with directors personally liable under 'knowingly concerned' provisions. VRCA may reopen assessments within the statutory limitation period, without time limit where fraud is demonstrated, and refunds are withheld until the substantiation bundle is fully delivered.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The VUV is convertible on a partially controlled basis. Current account transactions are freely permitted through authorised dealers, while capital account transactions by residents require RBV exchange control approval. A Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC) is statutorily treated as a non-resident entity under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], and enjoys a ministerial exchange control exemption; consequently the entity may hold and settle entirely in foreign currency (USD/EUR/AUD) with no VUV conversion burden and no profit repatriation blocking. Dividends and branch remittances from the IC are tax-exempt in Vanuatu and outflow is unrestricted subject only to bank-level anti-money-laundering verification of the beneficiary.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters, Exchange Control Notices issued by the RBV require export receipts to be remitted to Vanuatu through an authorised dealer within 180 days of shipment or invoice maturity, and the VUV equivalent must be offered for sale if the exporter has VUV obligations. The VU_IC is not a resident exporter: ISIC 7830 executive search services are intangible cross-border service exports settled directly into offshore foreign-currency accounts of the IC, and mandatory VUV surrender does not apply. If the IC ever receives a payment in VUV from a Vanuatu resident (prohibited under the IC regime), conversion to foreign currency would require RBV concurrence and is deemed an exceptional licensed act.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; the RBV is the statutory regulator for money, banking, foreign exchange control and the Vatu (VUV) currency, with the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 64] administered jointly by the RBV and the Ministry of Finance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-Moderate. No Vanuatu-licensed electronic money institution (EMI) exists, and the Wise B2B rail is not legally operable for a Vanuatu-incorporated IC because Wise does not accept VU corporate registrations and cannot settle VUV. A foreign EMIs (e.g., UK FCA-authorised Wise/Currencycloud) may be used only as an upstream collection point funded by overseas clients, provided the EMI does not carry on unlicensed payment business in Vanuatu; the chain must end in a settlement account held at a Vanuatu authorised dealer or a reputable offshore bank to satisfy the RBV definition of deposit-taking.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0 - not applicable. Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax and has no mandatory Local File or Master File legislation. Non-resident shareholders must maintain transfer-pricing and substance documentation required by their home jurisdiction, particularly CFC and anti-abuse rules.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu applies Economic Substance Regulations through the VFSC under the Economic Substance Implementation legislation. A VU International Company must file an economic substance notification and, if undertaking a relevant activity, demonstrate adequate physical presence, employees, expenditure and management and control in Vanuatu. Executive search and headhunting are not automatically specified relevant activities unless the company operates as a group service centre or headquarters. A standalone offshore headhunting agency billing foreign clients should still maintain board minutes, client contracts, staff records and expense evidence to rebut any shell-company or sham characterisation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Offshore Concealment

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu itself has no income tax offence, but the VFSC and the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit may investigate, strike off the IC and exchange information under the TIEA. The investor's home tax authority may impose tax evasion penalties, interest, confiscation orders and criminal fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate shareholders may be subject to civil restitution, asset-tracing orders, travel restrictions and imprisonment under the applicable foreign tax fraud legislation.
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC cancellation of International Company status, monetary administrative penalties, disqualification of directors and mandatory strike-off under the economic substance regime.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for penalties, barred from acting as directors of any VFSC-regulated company, and ordered to make full civil restitution of assets stripped through the shell structure.
AML/CFT Violations by the Executive Search Agency

Statutory sanction: VFSC AML/CFT enforcement action, monetary penalties, license suspension or revocation, freezing of bank accounts and referral to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and the designated compliance officer may face civil penalties, confiscation orders, travel bans and imprisonment for money laundering or failure to report suspicious transactions.
Unlicensed or Unauthorised Headhunting Activities

Statutory sanction: Stop orders, denial of business licence, monetary penalties and revocation of the VU IC's right to operate; the company may lose the statutory limited-liability shield.

Director personal liability: Directors and authorised officers may be jointly and severally liable for contracts entered into while unlicensed and may be subject to criminal fines or imprisonment for carrying on a regulated business without lawful authority.
False Filings and Misrepresentation to VFSC

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the International Companies Act and AML/CFT laws, administrative strike-off, monetary penalties and permanent disqualification of directors.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will normally be pierced; directors and signatories are personally liable for civil restitution, travel bans, immigration-listing and imprisonment for the period prescribed by the applicable Vanuatu criminal statute.
