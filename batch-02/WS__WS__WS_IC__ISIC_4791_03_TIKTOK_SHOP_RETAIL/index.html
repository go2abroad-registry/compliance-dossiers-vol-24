Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Independent State of Samoa - the single national tax and customs administration administering the Income Tax Act 2015, the Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 2011 (VAGST Act), the Customs Act 2014, and the Tax Administration Act 2012; official portal: www.revenue.gov.ws; customs declarations are processed on the ASYCUDA World platform. International Companies are incorporated by the Registrar of International Companies at the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL); export-proceeds repatriation and exchange control are supervised by the Central Bank of Samoa (CBS).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987: appoint a licensed resident agent, submit the memorandum and articles, name reservation, Samoan registered office, and director/shareholder particulars; no minimum capital is required, non-resident ownership is fully permitted, and the Certificate of Incorporation is issued by SIFA. Step 2 - Obtain the Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN): file the MfR TIN registration application attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, charter documents, and identification of directors and beneficial owners; the TIN is a single reference used for VAGST, PAYE, customs, and tax correspondence. Step 3 - Customs registration: apply to the Customs Division (MfR) under the Customs Act 2014 for the importer/exporter code and ASYCUDA World access to lodge export declarations. Step 4 - VAGST registration: because zero-rated exports count as taxable supplies, the IC must register for VAGST once its actual or prospective 12-month taxable turnover exceeds WST 60,000; a newly formed exporting IC should register voluntarily at once to claim input VAGST credits on platform fees, logistics, and overheads; non-resident companies may register through the resident agent. Step 5 - Banking and exchange control: open a foreign-currency account with a licensed Samoan bank (the IC is deemed non-resident for Exchange Control Act 1975 purposes), receive TikTok Shop payouts, and maintain CBS records of export proceeds for repatriation within 180 days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Samoan International Company is an entity-level limited-liability corporation placed in a statutory offshore exemption zone: under the International Companies Act 1987, as amended, the IC, its income, assets, securities, and transactions are exempt from all Samoan taxes and duties (income tax, capital gains tax, estate duty, stamp duty); the IC is deemed non-resident for exchange control purposes; it bears no Samoan income tax on global e-commerce profits; distributions to non-resident shareholders are free of Samoan withholding tax; for VAGST purposes the IC is a taxable person only if it carries on a taxable activity in Samoa, and its cross-border retail supplies are zero-rated exports. The classification is not pass-through; it is a corporate tax exemption zone, not a transparency regime.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Samoa; the IC's Tax_Exempt_Zone status is automatic under statute. Available statutory elections and choices: (i) conversion/re-registration as a domestic company under the Companies Act 2001, by special resolution filed with SIFA, which elects Entity_Level_Taxation and surrenders the offshore exemption; (ii) application for a licence to carry on domestic business with Samoan residents, which submits the IC to the Income Tax Act 2015 on Samoan-source income and to VAGST on local supplies; (iii) voluntary VAGST registration below the WST 60,000 threshold to recover input credits, with deregistration only by approval of the Commissioner once the taxable activity ceases; (iv) constitutional election of restricted or unrestricted IC status, which affects permitted activities and SIFA fees only; (v) no hybrid-mismatch election regime has been enacted - Samoa has not adopted the OECD/G20 hybrid mismatch rules, and the Commissioner's anti-avoidance powers are exercised under the Tax Administration Act 2012.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a managed-float regime referenced against a weighted currency basket dominated by the NZD, AUD, USD, and EUR. Current-account convertibility for genuine trade in goods and services is generally accommodated through authorized dealers without ad hoc CBS licensing, subject to submission of standard supporting documentation (commercial invoice, transport documents, tax clearance, and a completed FX application form). Capital-account transactions — including inbound foreign direct investment, share subscription, shareholder loans, assignment of intellectual property, and any cross-border transfer of funds exceeding the regulatory reporting threshold — require advance CBS exchange-control approval; approvals are discretionary, typically require 1-3 weeks of review, and may impose conditions on the timing or amount of remittance. Dividend and profit remittance by a non-resident International Company is legally permitted, provided the company holds a valid tax-exemption certificate under the International Companies Act 2007, has filed its annual return with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA), and presents a tax clearance certificate; however, in practice the remittance can be delayed by bank-level compliance review and is subject to a CBS declaration which can take up to 10 business days. WST is not deliverable or freely convertible offshore; there is no offshore WST interbank market, so any non-onshore hedge must be cash-settled in USD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1977, a resident exporter must negotiate export proceeds through an authorized dealer in Samoa within three (3) months of the date of shipment and surrender 100% of the proceeds to a Samoan bank, converting them into WST at the prevailing authorized-dealer rate unless CBS grants a written exemption. For a Samoa International Company that is non-resident owned, does not carry on business with Samoan residents, and derives income exclusively from foreign commerce (including social-commerce sales through TikTok Shop), Section 187 of the International Companies Act 2007 disapplies these resident-based exchange-control obligations; consequently the IC is not statutorily required to surrender or convert TikTok Shop payout proceeds. Nevertheless, where the IC elects to maintain a Samoan bank account, the authorized dealer may still require: (i) a SIFA certificate of non-resident status, (ii) a declaration that no Samoan-resident business is being conducted, and (iii) a CBS Exchange Control form (EC-01) for any cumulative inbound remittance exceeding WST 1,000,000 (approximately USD 360,000) in a rolling 12-month period.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984, acting as the monetary authority and the administrator of the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1977. All foreign exchange transactions in Samoa must be conducted through CBS-licensed authorized dealers (commercial banks). The CBS-operated national real-time gross settlement (RTGS) system is the exclusive settlement venue for domestic Samoan Tala (WST) transfers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Wise B2B (Wise Europe SA / Wise US Inc.) is assessed as Low-Medium feasibility for the primary settlement rail. Wise does not support WST balances, does not maintain a Samoan regulatory passport, and cannot issue local WST account details; a Samoa-registered International Company may open a Wise Business account only if it can additionally evidence a registered address or permanent establishment in a Wise-supporting jurisdiction (e.g., New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong, or the United States). The recommended architecture is: TikTok Shop USD payout (via Wise or direct SWIFT) to a Wise USD balance held by a supporting operating entity, followed by same-day SWIFT sweep to the Samoa IC's USD account at Samoa Commercial Bank; legally, the Wise account must not hold the Samoa IC's principal trade settlement balance because Wise is an e-money institution, not a licensed Samoan bank, and its regulatory status would create an unlicensed deposit-taking exposure under the Central Bank of Samoa Act. Other offshore digital EMIs (Payoneer, Airwallex, Dukascopy) are likewise not CBS-licensed; they may be used exclusively as collection convenience accounts with mandatory daily sweeps to the licensed bank, and any ATM/expense use in Samoa must be avoided to prevent the characterization of a Samoan place of business.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Wise B2B (Wise Europe SA / Wise US Inc.) is assessed as Low-Medium feasibility for the primary settlement rail. Wise does not support WST balances, does not maintain a Samoan regulatory passport, and cannot issue local WST account details; a Samoa-registered International Company may open a Wise Business account only if it can additionally evidence a registered address or permanent establishment in a Wise-supporting jurisdiction (e.g., New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong, or the United States). The recommended architecture is: TikTok Shop USD payout (via Wise or direct SWIFT) to a Wise USD balance held by a supporting operating entity, followed by same-day SWIFT sweep to the Samoa IC's USD account at Samoa Commercial Bank; legally, the Wise account must not hold the Samoa IC's principal trade settlement balance because Wise is an e-money institution, not a licensed Samoan bank, and its regulatory status would create an unlicensed deposit-taking exposure under the Central Bank of Samoa Act. Other offshore digital EMIs (Payoneer, Airwallex, Dukascopy) are likewise not CBS-licensed; they may be used exclusively as collection convenience accounts with mandatory daily sweeps to the licensed bank, and any ATM/expense use in Samoa must be avoided to prevent the characterization of a Samoan place of business.
