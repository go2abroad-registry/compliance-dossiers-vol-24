Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Samoa — Commissioner of Revenue; official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws (MfR e-Services platform for TIN issuance, GST and income tax returns, and customs-related filings).

E-government filing portal

For a Samoa International Company (IC), the licensed registered agent submits the prescribed tax registration application to the Ministry for Revenue together with the Certificate of Incorporation issued under the International Companies Act 1987, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered-agent consent, certified passport copies of directors/shareholders, and registered office address confirmation; no physical presence or local employees are required for an offshore-only IC. The MfR issues the Tax Identification Number (TIN) within 3-5 working days. Separate GST registration is triggered only where the entity makes or intends to make taxable supplies in Samoa exceeding the WST 100,000 threshold; an IC conducting wholly offshore beauty DTC retail is not obliged to register for GST unless goods are imported into or supplied within Samoa.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch classification election exists under Samoa law. An IC is statutorily exempt from income tax, GST, stamp duty, and other taxes on international business under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended). Optional elections include voluntary GST registration under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2003 (enabling input credit recovery but taxing Samoa supplies) and conversion into a domestic company under the Companies Act 2006, which subjects the entity to standard domestic entity-level taxation (corporate income tax at the prevailing 27% rate) and extinguishes IC statutory exemptions. The General Anti-Avoidance Rule under the Tax Administration Act 2012 and CFC rules in the Income Tax Act 2012 apply to prevent abuse of IC status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoa Tala) of taxable supplies in Samoa in a 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under section 9 of the Goods and Services Tax Act 2003: (i) goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of the time of supply (or such longer period as the Commissioner approves), provided the supplier retains documentary proof of export; and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply and where the benefit is used or consumed entirely outside Samoa. For ISIC 4791 online cosmetics retail, direct-to-consumer shipments delivered to overseas customers qualify for the 0% GST rate if the IC is GST-registered; where goods are sourced and dropshipped entirely outside Samoa, no Samoa GST arises because no taxable supply occurs in Samoa. Zero-rating requires simultaneously holding contemporaneous export documentation and, for refund claims, that any excess input credits are substantiated and claimed in the GST return.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDEC) lodged electronically with Samoa Customs (Ministry for Revenue)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exportation from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds through the Samoa financial system
  • Commercial invoice and purchase order or sales contract (matched to the CusDEC)

No national real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate is currently in force (no API clearance, digital stamping, or fiscal cash-register integration). GST-registered suppliers must issue prescribed tax invoices in paper or PDF form under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2003 and file GST returns electronically (monthly, with quarterly arrangements available) via the MfR e-Services portal.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to MfR post-clearance audit. A false or unsupported zero-rating claim triggers immediate clawback of the unremitted GST plus interest and shortfall penalties under the Tax Administration Act 2012: 20% for failure to take reasonable care, 40% for recklessness, and up to 150% for intentional evasion, together with monthly late-payment penalties of 1.5%. Criminal prosecution for tax evasion carries fines up to WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years. The Customs Act 2014 separately imposes false-declaration penalties of up to 200% of duty evaded and forfeiture of goods. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days (administered by the Central Bank of Samoa under the Exchange Control Act 1975) may result in loss of zero-rating, customs valuation adjustments, and monetary penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is non-internationalised and administratively managed. Current-account payments may be processed by authorised dealers with CBS reporting forms, whereas capital-account transactions (equity injections, shareholder loans, dividend and profit remittances) require prior CBS approval or an authorised-dealer coded authorisation. Profit remittance by a WS International Company requires audited financial statements, tax and economic-substance confirmation, and a completed Exchange Control outbound form; there is no deep offshore WST market, so USD-denominated settlement through correspondent chains is recommended.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters must repatriate export proceeds into Samoa through an authorised dealer within the CBS-prescribed timeline, generally within three months from shipment, and surrender foreign currency at the mandatory conversion ratio of 100% where proceeds fund domestic WST obligations. A CBS 'direction to retain' may permit an offshore-model International Company to hold receipts in an external foreign-currency account, subject to monthly returns and supporting export documentation; late surrender invokes CBS penalty administration.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) — the sole exchange-control authority, administering the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations, and CBS directions issued to licensed commercial banks acting as authorised dealers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low. Samoa has no EMI licensing regime, and offshore digital EMIs are not authorised dealers under CBS exchange control; they cannot hold WST or perform Samoan clearing. A WS International Company may use Shopify Payments and PayPal strictly as front-end collection aggregators, but all net treasury sweeps must settle into a CBS-licensed authorised-dealer account; routing core liquidity through an offshore EMI would constitute an exchange-control breach.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 1,000,000 per annum for related-party transactions triggering mandatory Local File / Master File under SRA Transfer Pricing Guidelines; IC-specific threshold applies only when Economic Substance filing is activated.

Economic substance scope: International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply. For online beauty retail distribution and service centre activities, the IC must demonstrate core income-generating activities, physical regulated premises, full-time qualified employees, and local management in Samoa; annual ESR notification and substance report must be filed with the Samoa International Companies Registry.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to WST 100,000 plus 200% of understated tax; imprisonment up to 7 years under Samoa Tax Administration Act 2012.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax through civil recovery; travel restriction orders; disqualification as director; criminal prosecution of each signing director.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Annual penalty of WST 20,000 per IC, plus WST 5,000 per director and registered agent for ESR non-compliance; involuntary strike-off and deregistration of the IC.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors personally liable for all profits reclassified as Samoa-source taxable income; personal liability for statutory penalties; prohibition from re-incorporation in Samoa for 5 years.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment to arm's length consideration; taxation of adjusted profit at 27%; additional penalty of 40% on the additional tax due.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify materially incorrect local file or TP documentation expose personal liability to SRA for recovery; referral to criminal prosecutor for certification offences.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance
