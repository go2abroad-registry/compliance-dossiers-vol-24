Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Government of Samoa — online portal: Tax Administration System (TAS), https://www.mfr.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the International Company (IC) via a licensed Samoan registered agent under the International Companies Act 2007; (2) the registered agent submits to the Ministry for Revenue an Application for Taxpayer Identification Number accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, Certificate of Registered Office, Registered Agent Consent, and certified passport copies of directors and shareholders; (3) MfR validates the IC's tax-exempt status and issues a TIN within approximately five business days; (4) the TIN is activated on the TAS portal and is thereafter used for VAT filings, customs transactions, and any withholding obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Samoa International Company is a non-resident, tax-privileged entity under the Income Tax Act 2015 and International Companies Act 2007; an IC that does not carry on business within Samoa and derives income from sources outside Samoa is exempt from corporate income tax, VAT, and stamp duty; a small annual government fee is payable to the Registrar of International and Foreign Companies.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Samoa. Available elections: (a) an IC may elect to be continued and re-registered as a domestic Samoan company, thereby opting into the ordinary income tax and VAT regime; (b) an IC may apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a binding private tax ruling confirming the scope of its exemption; (c) the IC may elect its reporting currency and financial statement basis under the International Companies Act; and (d) hybrid mismatch rules in the Income Tax Act 2015 may require re-characterization where a foreign jurisdiction treats the IC as fiscally transparent.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration where WST$500,000 of annual taxable turnover is met or exceeded in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available below the threshold; VAT applies at customs on imported goods regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available to a VAT-registered person where: (a) goods are physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply, or such longer period as approved by the Ministry for Revenue, and the exporter retains customs-validated proof of export; (b) services are supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Samoa at the time the services are performed and the benefit of the services is not enjoyed in Samoa; cross-border express courier and international freight transport of small parcels from Samoa to foreign destinations qualifies as an exported service where the contractual recipient is a non-resident and the transit terminates outside Samoa; supporting evidence must include the contract, airway bill or customs declaration, and proof of receipt of funds from a foreign-source account.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CED) validated in the ASYCUDA World customs system
  • Master and House Airway Bill or Bill of Lading evidencing outbound movement from Samoa
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or foreign-currency credit advice from a Samoan licensed commercial bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Proof of non-resident status of the service recipient (e.g., foreign VAT/GST registration certificate, overseas registered address, and place-of-incorporation evidence)

No real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate is currently in force in Samoa. VAT-registered persons must issue tax invoices in the prescribed form under the VAT Act 2018; VAT returns and schedules are filed electronically through the TAS portal; customs declarations are processed in the ASYCUDA World system; digitally issued invoices are acceptable provided they contain the prescribed particulars and remain verifiable on audit.

The Ministry for Revenue may conduct retrospective audits of export zero-rating claims for up to five years; where zero-rating is claimed fraudulently or without adequate substantiation, the taxpayer must repay the refunded VAT with interest and is liable to a civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax shortfall; criminal prosecution for tax evasion may attract a fine of up to WST$1,000,000 and imprisonment of up to seven years; directors and the registered agent who participate in false export declarations may be held jointly and severally liable; the Registrar of International and Foreign Companies may also strike off or revoke the IC's tax-exempt status for repeated serious non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is convertible for current account transactions (trade in goods, services, and remittances of profits) under the Foreign Exchange Act 2020 and Exchange Control Regulations. Capital account transactions, including inbound share capital injections above certain thresholds and outbound investment repatriation, are subject to CBS licensing, case-by-case approval, and documentary substantiation. Profit remittances are permissible after compliance with corporate tax clearance and annual return filings; practical hurdles include the need for audited financial statements, tax compliance certificates, and foreign exchange application forms submitted through an authorised dealer. The CBS retains discretionary power to defer or reject remittances if the applicant has unresolved tax or regulatory obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations, all exporters (including cross-border courier operators with invoiced export service earnings) must repatriate export proceeds to Samoa within 180 days from the date of export. There is no mandatory statutory conversion ratio into WST; however, where the exporter requires local currency to meet operational expenses, the authorised dealing bank will convert the foreign currency at the prevailing market rate. Export proceeds must be received through a CBS-licensed commercial bank and supported by a customs or service declaration form; failure to repatriate within the stipulated timeline triggers a mandatory surrender order by the CBS and may result in fines or revocation of the exporter's licence.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low - Offshore digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) accounts are not directly viable for a Samoa International Company due to (i) Samoa's Foreign Exchange Act requiring all FX transactions to be routed through authorised CBS-licensed banks, (ii) the absence of a designated EMI licensing framework for non-resident corporate service providers, and (iii) the practical refusal of correspondent banks to clear through unregulated EMIs. An offshore EMI may be used only as a supplementary collection rail where the underlying treasury settlement and FX conversion occur through a domestic commercial bank; this structure requires a formal memorandum of understanding and approval from the CBS.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share capital injections must be routed through a CBS-licensed commercial bank. The remitter must use SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'Capital contributions to nonresident controlled company' (corresponding to Samoa Exchange Control Form 1) and include the company registration number. The bank will verify that the IC is maintained in good standing with the Samoa International Companies Registry, and that the funds originate from a KYC-compliant source. Aggregate inbound capital above SAT 1 million requires prior CBS approval; the authorised bank will issue a foreign exchange transaction certificate evidencing compliance with Article 17 of the Foreign Exchange Act 2020.Disbursements for outbound trade settlements (freight forwarding, terminal handling, and international courier line-haul charges) are cleared via (i) SWIFT MT103 direct wire transfers to the beneficiary's bank, or (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) issued by the Samoan bank against the underlying courier invoices. The bank requires submission of a Samoa export entry, a courier airway bill (AWB), a commercial invoice, and a customs declaration schedule. All FX conversions for disbursements are executed at the CBS daily reference rate, and settlements to low-tax or OFAC-sanctioned counterparties trigger enhanced due diligence delays.Plain vanilla forward contracts against USD with maturity up to twelve months and requiring a 20% margin depositNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing the USD/WST rate where settlement is made in USD based on the CBS official fixingCross-currency swaps subject to CBS approval for tenors exceeding six months and limited to hedge of trade flowsOptions embedded in forward instruments only where the underlying courier receivables are evidenced by confirmed invoices

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa does not impose local-file/master-file transfer pricing documentation on tax-exempt International Companies; for taxable resident enterprises, contemporaneous TP documentation would be required when related-party transactions exceed WST 10 million annually

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) require an International Company conducting relevant activities — which may include logistics, distribution, and service centre operations — to demonstrate substantial activities in Samoa. The company must maintain premises in Samoa, an adequate number of full-time employees, and local management; file annual economic substance declarations; and ensure the courier revenue is commensurate with physical substance. Pure letterbox structures without local substance are liable to penalties and recharacterization

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid taxes where the false declaration was made knowingly; the court may impose imprisonment of up to 7 years, monetary restitution, and asset freezing orders
Shell Company and Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations for each relevant activity, possible default deemed taxable profit assessment, loss of IC tax-exempt status, and strike-off by the Registrar

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for ESR penalties and default assessments if they knowingly certified false substance information; management interference or personal benefit may lead to piercing the corporate veil and disqualification orders
AML/CFT Breach and Money Laundering

Director personal liability: Senior officers face severe criminal penalties including imprisonment up to 15 years, personal fines, travel bans, and freezing of personal and corporate assets; the corporate veil is pierced for breaches committed with the directors' knowledge or reckless indifference
Unlicensed Express Courier Operations
