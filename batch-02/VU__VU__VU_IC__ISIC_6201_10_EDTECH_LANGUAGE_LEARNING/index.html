Governing statute

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Vanuatu; official portal: https://customs.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must first be incorporated or registered as an International Company with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Submission of the completed VAT/TIN registration application (Form VAT-1) is then required to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, registered office certificate, constitution, director and shareholder identification documents, and proof of registered agent in Vanuatu. The Department issues the statutory VAT registration number and Tax Identification Number within approximately five business days after due compliance.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity must first be incorporated or registered as an International Company with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Submission of the completed VAT/TIN registration application (Form VAT-1) is then required to the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, registered office certificate, constitution, director and shareholder identification documents, and proof of registered agent in Vanuatu. The Department issues the statutory VAT registration number and Tax Identification Number within approximately five business days after due compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An International Company may, by special resolution filed with the VFSC and Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, elect to become a resident company under Part 16 of the International Companies Act Cap 222 and thereby forgo the statutory tax exemption and become subject to domestic VAT and any future income tax measures. No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists; hybrid mismatch elections are not applicable as Vanuatu does not impose corporate income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 10,000,000 annual aggregate taxable turnover (excluding zero-rated supplies) in Vanuatu; registration is mandatory within 14 days from the end of the month in which the threshold is exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods supplied to a person outside Vanuatu and physically exported within 60 days, and to services supplied to non-residents where the place of supply is outside Vanuatu, including online EdTech subscription services delivered via Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, or internet-based automated tutoring platforms. The supplier must possess documentary evidence that the goods or services were provided to a recipient outside Vanuatu, payment was received in foreign currency, and the value was not consumed or used in Vanuatu. Associated input VAT may be refunded.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Electronic Export Manifest (for physical goods)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or equivalent proof of export (for physical goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/merchant settlement statement evidencing receipt of export consideration in foreign currency
  • Signed export contract / online subscription agreement evidencing the non-resident recipient and offshore delivery
  • System usage or delivery logs demonstrating that the EdTech service was accessed and consumed outside Vanuatu

Vanuatu does not yet mandate real-time fiscal clearance, electronic digital stamping, or API-based invoice clearance. Taxpayers must issue prescribed VAT invoices (in English or French), retain electronic and paper records for seven years, and file periodic VAT returns; the Department may conduct on-site audits of invoicing processes.

The Department of Customs and Inland Revenue may conduct retrospective assessments up to 6 years (unlimited in fraud), and may claw back zero-rated input credits and output VAT if the exporter has not substantiated physical departure, offshore delivery, or foreign-currency receipt within the statutory period. Penalties include fines up to VUV 5,000,000 and imprisonment for tax evasion, civil penalties up to 200% of the evaded VAT, cancellation of the zero-rating registration, and personal liability of directors and officers involved in fraudulent export refund claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains full current and capital account convertibility under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. There are no exchange controls, no prior approval requirements for foreign currency transactions, and profits, dividends, and other returns on investment may be freely repatriated. The domestic currency is the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) with a floating exchange rate; a licensed local bank may require a declaration for cash transactions exceeding threshold but will not block approved wire transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement in Vanuatu. Exporters may hold, convert, or remit overseas earnings freely, subject only to normal anti-money laundering and counter-financing of terrorism verification and the bank's internal risk-based monitoring.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

No dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework exists in Vanuatu, and overseas digital EMIs cannot directly hold bank charters or access local payment rails without a Vanuatu banking license or an explicit correspondent arrangement through a licensed domestic bank. Feasibility for a foreign-regulated EMI as a primary treasury rail is Low; the recommended pathway is a local commercial bank account with SWIFT connectivity and a regulated fintech payment orchestration layer for merchant settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inward remittance must be executed via SWIFT MT103 from the ultimate shareholder's bank account. The payment should carry the purpose code corresponding to 'Shareholder Loan' or 'Equity Contribution' depending on the Internal Company's capital and loan records; the receiving bank in Vanuatu is the licensed domestic commercial bank holding the capital escrow or operational account. Verification will require a board resolution authorising the borrowing, a signed loan agreement or equity subscription contract, and a certification from the company secretary confirming the latest registered shareholding. The value date should not be split into multiple small transfers unless each tranche references the original purpose code to avoid structuring concerns.Outbound trade settlements for the EdTech platform (hosting fees, marketing, app store fees, content licensing, software development services) should be cleared through SWIFT MT103 wires from the Vanuatu operating account. For high-value or contractually significant purchases, a Commercial Letter of Credit (documentary credit, UCP 600) issued by ANZ or if required cleared through a corresponding bank is permissible, along with regular telegraphic transfer (TT) and Resource Payment Orders where supported by the beneficiary jurisdiction. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (permanent zero-rate regime; an International Company is exempt from all corporate income tax under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222])

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by an International Company to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Transfer Pricing law, Local File, or Master File obligation exists in Vanuatu for International Companies; no VUV threshold is prescribed. Arm's-length pricing is nonetheless recommended to protect foreign-payer deductions and avoid foreign CFC/PE challenges.

Economic substance scope: The 2018 economic substance framework (International Companies Act [Cap. 222] as amended, administered by VFSC) applies to ICs carrying on relevant activities, including headquarters, distribution and service centers, and IP holding. An IC deriving EdTech subscription and licensing income must evidence real management control in Vanuatu (local office, employees, expenditure, board meetings) and file an annual substance return; passive IP-holding and conduit structures without Vanuatu substance are vulnerable to VFSC scrutiny and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Source Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Civil reassessment for unpaid VAT with late penalties and statutory interest; anti-avoidance penalties and publication orders under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 241]; criminal prosecution for false declarations with fines and imprisonment on conviction.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in the misdeclaration are jointly and severally liable for restitution; the Court may issue departure prohibition (travel ban) and asset-freezing orders pending tax and criminal proceedings.
Shell Company and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC monetary penalties for failure to file the economic substance declaration; removal of good standing; and strike-off/dissolution under the International Companies Act, with referral to foreign competent authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors may be held personally liable for losses caused by substance misstatements, disqualified from managing Vanuatu companies, and exposed to civil restitution in foreign tax proceedings.
AML/CTF and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties and regulatory sanctions under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2019; suspension of the corporate registration; asset-freezing orders by the Vanuatu FIU; criminal prosecution for money laundering.

Director personal liability: Individuals concerned in management commit an offence and face personal fines and custodial sentences on conviction; civil liability attaches for restitution and directors may be subject to travel restrictions.
Offshore Conduit Misuse and Foreign GAAR Exposure
