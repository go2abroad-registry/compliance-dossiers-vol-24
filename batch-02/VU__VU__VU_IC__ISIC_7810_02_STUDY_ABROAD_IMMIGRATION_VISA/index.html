Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), established under the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority Act No. 36 of 2011; unified administrator of the Value Added Tax Act No. 9 of 1998 [Cap 245], the Customs and Excise Management Act No. 11 of 2010 and stamp duties; official portal and e-services: https://www.vrca.gov.vu; customs declarations processed through ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

Domestic path: a foreign entity with a Vanuatu taxable presence files the VRCA TIN registration form attaching the home-jurisdiction certificate of incorporation, notarised passport copies of directors, a board resolution appointing a local tax agent, evidence of registered physical address and a municipal business licence (where applicable); VRCA verifies the file and issues the TIN, and VAT registration is separately determined against the VT 4,000,000 turnover threshold. Offshore path: an International Company (IC) is incorporated by a VFSC-licensed registered agent under the International Companies Act [Cap 222]; the IC automatically receives tax-exempt status and is not required to obtain a TIN or VAT registration because the IC Act prohibits carrying on business with Vanuatu residents; the only annual obligation is the VFSC annual return filing.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — by operation of the International Companies Act [Cap 222], the VU_IC is an offshore juridical person exempt from all Vanuatu taxes (no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, dividend/interest/royalty withholding tax, VAT or stamp duty on worldwide offshore activities); non-resident ownership is unrestricted; the exemption is automatic and requires no election, but the IC remains subject to VFSC annual filing and anti-money-laundering/beneficial-ownership substance obligations. — Domestic path: a foreign entity with a Vanuatu taxable presence files the VRCA TIN registration form attaching the home-jurisdiction certificate of incorporation, notarised passport copies of directors, a board resolution appointing a local tax agent, evidence of registered physical address and a municipal business licence (where applicable); VRCA verifies the file and issues the TIN, and VAT registration is separately determined against the VT 4,000,000 turnover threshold. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — by operation of the International Companies Act [Cap 222], the VU_IC is an offshore juridical person exempt from all Vanuatu taxes (no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, dividend/interest/royalty withholding tax, VAT or stamp duty on worldwide offshore activities); non-resident ownership is unrestricted; the exemption is automatic and requires no election, but the IC remains subject to VFSC annual filing and anti-money-laundering/beneficial-ownership substance obligations.

Available election forms

Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, so no check-the-box entity classification and no hybrid-mismatch election is available; an IC cannot elect into the domestic VAT regime because the IC Act forbids supplies to Vanuatu residents; the only relevant corporate elections (conversion to a domestic company under the Companies Act [Cap 191], change of registered agent or voluntary striking-off) do not create any tax election and would, in the case of conversion, forfeit the statutory tax-exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 4,000,000 in annual taxable turnover (VAT Act [Cap 245] s. 4); registration becomes compulsory once taxable supplies exceed VT 4,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period, equivalent to approximately USD 33,000 at prevailing VUV/USD market rates; non-resident suppliers of cross-border services to Vanuatu residents are outside the net unless the services are physically performed or exploited inside Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under VAT Act [Cap 245] s. 6 and Schedule 2 for: (i) goods physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply and documented through customs; (ii) services supplied to non-resident recipients where the place of consumption and benefit is wholly outside Vanuatu and the services do not relate to land or goods situated in Vanuatu; and (iii) international transport and freight. For the ISIC 7810 study-abroad and immigration-visa consultancy, advisory and placement fees invoiced to overseas students, foreign universities and migration clients qualify for zero-rating provided the recipient is a non-resident, the services are performed offshore, and no domestic lodging, tutoring or relocation element is supplied in Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through ASYCUDA World with VRCA clearance status
  • Commercial invoice and VAT tax invoice addressed to the foreign buyer or service recipient
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or postal despatch receipt evidencing physical export from Vanuatu (goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by an authorised Vanuatu bank, or SWIFT MT103/telegraphic transfer credit advice, evidencing foreign-currency payment
  • Packing list, certificate of origin and export permit where applicable
  • For services: signed consultancy agreement, scope of work, student enrolment and visa-submission records demonstrating performance entirely outside Vanuatu

Vanuatu does not operate a real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-clearance or digital-stamping mandate; VAT-registered suppliers must issue tax invoices carrying the supplier's name, TIN, VAT registration number, description and taxable value, VAT amount and supply date, and retain records for 7 years; imports are cleared electronically via ASYCUDA World, but no statutory API-based fiscal data transmission is required for domestic B2B or consumer invoicing.

VRCA applies risk-based post-clearance and post-refund audits of exporters claiming zero-rating; deficient or fraudulent substantiation triggers repayment of the VAT previously refunded or credited together with statutory penalty tax and interest from the date of the erroneous claim; criminal prosecution for fraudulent zero-rating or false customs declarations may attract substantial fines and imprisonment; customs may seize and forfeit the goods, cancel exporter bonds and the VFSC may strike off the entity for non-compliance. Export proceeds must be repatriated through the Vanuatu banking system within 180 days; default triggers Reserve Bank of Vanuatu exchange-control sanctions and retrospective disallowance of the zero-rating; supporting records must be retained for 7 years.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu is a free-floating, fully convertible currency for current-account payments effected through RBV-authorised dealers; capital-account transfers by resident entities require an RBV-prescribed application, but the non-resident International Company is statutorily excluded from the exchange-control regime. Profit remittance and dividend repatriation to non-resident owners are unrestricted, subject only to the disbursing bank's AML/CFT checks and CRS self-certification; the IC's zero percent corporate and withholding tax status removes all tax-clearance friction, while the bank will require audit-grade evidence of accrued service revenue for a study-abroad and immigration-consulting account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory surrender timeline: not applicable; mandatory conversion ratio: 0%. The VU_IC, being a non-resident international company under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], may retain 100% of consulting and placement fees in any settlement currency in Vanuatu or offshore accounts. Authorised dealers are required to report prescribed cross-border transactions to the RBV in periodic exchange-control returns and to retain transaction records for seven years; no RBV licence or prior approval is needed to hold or spend foreign currency at the corporate level.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting as the exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 112]; Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as the licensing, AML/CFT and conduct supervisor of banks, financial dealers, and registered agents.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW as a primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as an ancillary payment wallet for small client top-ups. Legal constraints: an offshore EMI not licensed by the VFSC may not solicit deposits or provide payment services in or from Vanuatu under the Financial Dealers Act [Cap 70] and the International Banking Act [Cap 163]; holding client funds in an unlicensed EMI is an automatic enhanced-risk flag at the local clearing bank and may breach the statutory client-money trust duty. A VFSC-licensed money changer or EMI can support low-value conversion, but escrow of student fees and the corporate treasury account must sit with a licensed Vanuatu commercial bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; an International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is statutorily tax-exempt on all foreign-source and domestic-source income except where a specific indirect tax applies.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to resident or non-resident shareholders; dividend distributions from the tax-exempt IC are fully free of Vanuatu tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return and annual fee are due within 30 days after the anniversary date of the certificate of incorporation; the annual licence fee is due on the anniversary date. VAT returns, if the IC has local taxable supplies, are due according to the prescribed taxable period deadline under the VAT Act; exported study-abroad and immigration consultancy services are typically zero-rated.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation, no Local File or Master File requirements, and no country-by-country reporting obligations. The operative compliance threshold is instead the VFSC Economic Substance declaration, which applies to all ICs irrespective of turnover; the threshold in local currency is therefore VUV 0 for TP documentation and 'any IC undertaking a relevant activity' for substance purposes.

Economic substance scope: Applicable — Vanuatu enacted the International Companies (Economic Substance) (Amendment) Act 2019 and associated Economic Substance Regulations, administered by VFSC. An IC carrying on a 'relevant activity' from a permanent establishment in Vanuatu must demonstrate economic substance: premises in Vanuatu, adequate full-time employees, sufficient local expenditure, and direction and management exercised in Vanuatu. For the ISIC 7810 study-abroad and golden visa advisory business, the relevant activity is most likely classified as a 'distribution and service centre'. An IC that is tax resident in a foreign jurisdiction is exempt from the Vanuatu substance test but must file an annual declaration identifying that jurisdiction and attach supporting evidence. Failure to file or to satisfy substance requirements results in administrative penalties, deregistration, and adverse information reporting.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Misuse of Zero-Tax Status

Statutory sanction: Under the Value Added Tax Act 1998 (as amended), incorrect returns and evasion attract penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded plus interest; under the ESR Regulations, VFSC may impose substantial administrative fines, cancel the company's certificate of incorporation, and refer the matter for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors and registered agents may face personal civil restitution of evaded amounts, asset freezing and travel bans or non-departure orders, and imprisonment of up to 7 years for fraud, forgery, or false declaration offences.
Shell Company — Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC may issue compliance notices with escalating financial penalties, impose an economic substance assessment fee, deregister the IC for repeated default, and pass negative information to foreign exchange-of-information authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may be ordered to restore assets, be disqualified as directors of any Vanuatu entity, and face travel restrictions pending compliance; deliberate concealment may lead to custodial sentence.
AML/CFT Deficiency and Unlicensed Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu's AML/CFT Act and VFSC AML Rules, financial and conduct penalties may reach VUV 25,000,000 (approximately USD 210,000); the business licence may be suspended or revoked, and money laundering offences carry custodial penalties of up to 15 years.

Director personal liability: Directors personally exposed to civil restitution to defrauded clients, licence prohibition, travel bans pending investigation, and imprisonment for money laundering, dishonest receipt of client funds, or conspiracy to defraud.
Client-Fund Misuse — Escrow Conversion in Visa Transactions

Statutory sanction: Civil claims for breach of fiduciary duty, professional licence cancellation by the Vanuatu Business Licence Office, restitutionary orders equal to the full amount misapplied, and criminal charges under the penal code for obtaining money by deception.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil applies directly: directors and shareholders who controlled the funds are jointly and severally liable for restitution; travel bans may be imposed to secure assets; conviction can result in imprisonment of up to 10 years.
Abandonment, Strike-Off and Director Disqualification

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the International Companies Register; restoration requires a court order or VFSC consent, payment of arrears, penalties, and restoration fees; creditors may seek appointment of a liquidator against the struck-off company's assets.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from managing any Vanuatu company; civil restitution orders can attach to personal assets; travel bans may be issued pending repayment; fraudulent abandonment with intent to defraud creditors carries custodial penalties.
