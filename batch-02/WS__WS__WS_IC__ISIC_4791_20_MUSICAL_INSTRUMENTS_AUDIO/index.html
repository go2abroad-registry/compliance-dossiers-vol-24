Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue (Maota o Tupe Maua), Government of Samoa, Apia — administered by the Commissioner of Revenue; official online taxpayer portal and electronic filing system at https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1988 is automatically recognised as a tax-exempt offshore entity and is not required to obtain a domestic income tax TIN for its offshore business. If the IC conducts activities in Samoa that create VAGST obligations, it must submit the VAGST Registration Application to the Ministry of Revenue together with the certificate of incorporation, registered office and resident agent appointment certificate, notarised passport copies of all directors and shareholders, proof of physical or registered address, and bank account details; the Ministry issues the VAGST registration number which functions as the taxpayer identification number for indirect tax purposes. An IC with no Samoan taxable supplies may apply for voluntary VAGST registration to support zero-rated export treatment and input tax credit recovery.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1988 enjoys a statutory offshore income tax exemption in Samoa; territorial tax charge and VAGST apply only to activities with a Samoan nexus.

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity classification election and no hybrid mismatch or hybrid instrument election exists under Samoan law. The tax-exempt territorial treatment of an International Company arises automatically by statute and cannot be modified by election. Taxpayers may only elect voluntary VAGST registration under the VAGST Act 2008 where not compulsorily registrable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period; registration is compulsory at or above this threshold and voluntary below it, and non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies to Samoan consumers are subject to the same nexus rules where the supply occurs in Samoa

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under s.14 of the VAGST Act 2008, goods supplied by the IC — including musical instruments and studio audio equipment sold online — qualify for the 0% rate only when the goods are exported by the supplier from Samoa in the course of a taxable activity and are physically exported within the statutory period, ordinarily 28 days from the date of supply or within such extended period as the Commissioner of Revenue allows. The sale must be supported by customs export evidence, and any delivery to a Samoan address is treated as a domestic taxable supply at 15%. Services ancillary to exported goods follow the export zero-rating treatment if performed before export and if properly recorded on the customs documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs export entry duly processed and released by the Customs Division of the Ministry of Revenue)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (non-negotiable copy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, bank SWIFT MT103, or payment-rail settlement statement from Stripe or PayPal evidencing offshore receipt of export proceeds
  • Order and payment records from the online store (order ID, SKU list, shipping address outside Samoa)
  • Carrier freight documentation showing Incoterms (EXW/FOB) and shipped weights

Samoa has not implemented a real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, API-based e-invoice clearance, or digital stamping regime. Taxpayers must issue serially numbered VAGST tax invoices containing the supplier's name, address, TIN/VAGST registration number, invoice date, description, quantity, consideration, and VAGST amount, and must file VAGST returns and make remittances electronically through the Ministry of Revenue's online portal; no government-validated electronic stamping of invoices is currently mandated.

The Ministry of Revenue cross-verifies each zero-rated export claim against the Samoa Customs export declaration, carrier waybills, sales contracts, and bank or payment-rail receipts; any misdeclared export, failed export within the statutory period, over-valued invoice, or non-arm's-length arrangement is recharacterised as a domestic taxable supply and assessed VAGST at 15% from the date of registration plus statutory interest. The Tax Administration Act 2012 authorises shortfall penalties of up to 150% of the tax shortfall for fraudulent evasion, initial late-payment penalties and monthly incremental late-payment penalties, criminal fines up to WST 100,000, and imprisonment for up to 7 years for the officers of the IC. Retrospective clawback assessments may be raised within 10 years for fraud or wilful misstatement, and any zero-rating refund is set off first against outstanding VAGST, income tax, or penalty liabilities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa Tala (WST) is pegged to a composite basket of trading-partner currencies; resident current-account convertibility is administered through authorised dealers, while resident capital-account flows require CBS approval. An International Company is a non-resident entity for exchange-control purposes: it must conduct all business in foreign currency, hold no WST balances and transact with no Samoan resident. Consequently, the IC enjoys full current- and capital-account convertibility with no CBS prior approval, unrestricted foreign-currency banking, and unencumbered profit remittance and capital repatriation. Remittance friction is limited to AML/KYC documentary clearance (source of funds, beneficial ownership, economic-substance compliance) imposed by the correspondent bank chain. Samoan income-tax and withholding-tax exemptions apply to IC dividends, interest, royalties and asset sales, so no tax-clearance certificate is required for outbound profit flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters are statutorily required to repatriate export proceeds through an authorised dealer within six months of export and to offer the foreign currency for sale against WST to the extent mandated by CBS; retention in foreign currency requires CBS-appointed approval. A non-resident IC is wholly outside this regime: online-sales proceeds (ISIC 4791 musical-instrument and audio-gear trade) may be accumulated indefinitely in USD/EUR/GBP/AUD settlement accounts, the mandatory conversion ratio is 0%, and no surrender or repatriation obligation arises, conditional on preserving the IC's non-resident firewall (no Samoan-resident sales, no WST receipts, no domestic place of business).

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984; administrator of the Exchange Control Regulations 1984; prudential regulator of authorised dealers under the Financial Institutions Act 1996; supervisor of AML/CFT obligations under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 as amended. The Registrar of International Companies within the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour supervises the International Company charter, registered agents and annual-fee obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium as a standalone treasury rail: Samoa has no domestic e-money-institution licensing regime, no fintech charter and no EMI passporting; CBS does not authorise e-money issuance. International digital EMIs generally impose enhanced due diligence on Samoan offshore companies and prefer a prime bank relationship. The compliant architecture is a hybrid: core USD liquidity at ANZ or BSP Samoa, with global merchant collections through PayPal Business (receive-supported in Samoa) and through a payment-facilitator or merchant-of-record arrangement in a Stripe-supported jurisdiction (Stripe does not list Samoa as a supported merchant country). Settlement flows from these PSP rails sweep in USD directly to the IC's Samoan bank account, avoiding reliance on EMI clearing.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% for a standard resident Samoa company; 0% for an International Company (IC) holding an active International Companies Act 1987 exemption

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid out of a Samoan International Company to non-residents; the statutory final withholding rate of 15% applies only to resident standard Samoan companies paying dividends to non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No statutory IC tax return deadline. If a domestic Samoan company becomes taxable, Form CT1 is due within three months after the fiscal year-end, typically 31 March for a 31 December balance date.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
N/A No income tax line entries apply to the WS_IC because the entity is a tax-exempt International Company; the exemption operates automatically under the International Companies Act 1987 and the IC is not a resident Samoan taxpayer. Non-resident shareholders should not include WS_IC profits in a Samoan tax return. They should retain clear documentation of the IC exemption and evidence of the company's management and economic activity outside Samoa to support tax residence treatment in their own jurisdiction. No late-filing penalty attaches to a validly exempt International Company. If an IC were erroneously considered taxable or lost its exemption, failure to file a corporate return would trigger a 20% late-filing penalty on assessed tax, a monthly 2% surcharge capped at 24 months, and prosecution of responsible officers with fines of up to WST 10,000.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File/Master File threshold is currently enacted in Samoa. For risk-egularity and prudent compliance, an International Company engaged in related-party purchase and resale of musical instruments and studio audio gear should maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation where aggregate cross-border connected-party turnover exceeds WST 1,000,000 per annum.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (International Companies) Act 2019 applies to ICs engaged in relevant Samoan activities, including distribution and service centres. The IC must maintain substance in Samoa or in another jurisdiction with adequate economic presence. Substantive requirements include Samoan-registered offices, local board meetings, core income-generating activities directed by board members, qualified staff, and annual substance returns. An online musical instrument and audio gear sales vehicle that operates entirely from non-Samoan premises and has no local employees will be treated as a substantive non-compliance risk.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / sham tax-exempt status misuse

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of all domestic-source profits at the 27% corporate income tax rate, imposition of a 20% late-filing penalty, 2% monthly late-payment surcharge (up to 24 months), and criminal prosecution under the Tax Administration Act 2012 with fines up to WST 10,000 and imprisonment up to two years.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and beneficial owners may be jointly and severally liable for assessed tax where assets have been transferred out of the company; the court may impose civil restitution, travel bans, asset freezing orders, and disqualification from serving as a Samoan director.
Transfer pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: Reallocation of income by the Samoa Ministry of Revenue on an arm's-length basis, 40% additional assessment penalty on the understated tax, plus interest; the penalty increases to 100% for deliberately concealed related-party transactions.

Director personal liability: Knowing directors and those who sign false certificates are personally liable for the penalty amount under the tax administration law; criminal conviction can lead to imprisonment up to five years, travel restrictions, and personal civil restitution to the tax authority.
Shell company / economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties of WST 5,000 for the first failure and WST 20,000 for repeated non-compliance under the Economic Substance (International Companies) Act 2019; the company may be struck off the Samoa International Companies Register and the file will be exchanged with foreign tax and law-enforcement authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly file false substance declarations face personal fines up to WST 10,000, director disqualification, and travel bans; the corporate veil is pierced where the company is a mere facade and assets in the name of the IC are available for judgment creditors.
AML / sanctions violations in payment processing

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007, fines up to WST 2,000,000 and imprisonment up to fourteen years; freezing or confiscation orders may attach to all bank accounts and digital payment settlement funds; the financial institution or payment processor may terminate the merchant agreement and file a suspicious transaction report.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and payment beneficiaries with actual or constructive knowledge are individually liable; remedies include criminal imprisonment, travel bans, restraint orders over personal assets, and extradition under mutual legal assistance arrangements.
Beneficial ownership concealment

Statutory sanction: Daily default fines of WST 1,000 up to a maximum of WST 100,000, automatic information exchange under Samoa's international obligations, and compulsory deregistration of the International Company; persistent breach may trigger criminal fines and forfeiture of the company's assets.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors are personally liable for materially false beneficial ownership declarations, facing imprisonment up to seven years, civil penalties, and disgorgement of all fees; the trust structure may be pierced, exposing family trust settlors to personal judgments and travel restrictions.
