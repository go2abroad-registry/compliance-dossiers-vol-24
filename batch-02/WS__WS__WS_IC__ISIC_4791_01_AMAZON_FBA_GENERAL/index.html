Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Inland Revenue Division (Samoa); official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws/

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Samoa International Company is statutorily tax-exempt and is not ordinarily required to obtain a national tax identification number (TIN) for income tax. If the IC nonetheless registers for VAGST (voluntarily or due to taxable local supply), it must file Form VAGST-REG with the Ministry for Revenue, supported by the Certificate of Incorporation, Registered Office Declaration, and identification of the Samoa agent/registered office provider. The Ministry issues a TIN / VAGST registration number upon approval. For non-tax-exempt resident entities, a TIN is issued through the Revenue Register within the same Ministry.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Samoa International Company is statutorily tax-exempt and is not ordinarily required to obtain a national tax identification number (TIN) for income tax. If the IC nonetheless registers for VAGST (voluntarily or due to taxable local supply), it must file Form VAGST-REG with the Ministry for Revenue, supported by the Certificate of Incorporation, Registered Office Declaration, and identification of the Samoa agent/registered office provider. The Ministry issues a TIN / VAGST registration number upon approval. For non-tax-exempt resident entities, a TIN is issued through the Revenue Register within the same Ministry.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, transparent-entity, or hybrid-mismatch elections are available for Samoa International Companies under the International Companies Act 1987. The entity is absolutely exempt from Samoa income tax, and there is no mechanism to elect into entity-level taxation. Registered VAGST persons may elect to group related entities for VAGST purposes under the discretion of the Ministry for Revenue, but this does not alter the IC's underlying tax-exempt classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 per 12-month period (or such gazetted higher amount) of taxable supplies; monthly tracking is required once cumulative supplies exceed 50% of the threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 2003, a supply is zero-rated if the goods are exported from Samoa by or on behalf of the supplier, or the services are physically performed outside Samoa and supplied to a non-resident. An International Company engaged in cross-border e-commerce/FBA may claim zero-rating only if it is a registered VAGST person and the goods leave Samoa and are delivered to an offshore marketplace warehouse or customer; pure offshore digital/fulfilment services without local leakage may be outside the VAGST net entirely.

  • Samoan Customs Export Declaration (export entry form)
  • Bill of Lading or Airwaybill evidencing physical removal from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank FX receipt showing export proceeds credited to the IC's Samoa bank account
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Third-party evidence of delivery into the offshore FBA/Amazon warehouse

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance APIs, or digital stamping. Registered VAGST suppliers must issue sequential VAGST tax invoices (paper or electronic PDF) containing the seller's name, registration number, date, description, amount, and VAGST component. No pre-clearance or government API clearance exists.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating attracts a tax shortfall penalty of 100% of the VAGST sought to be refused or refunded, plus an additional 50% in the case of intentional evasion, compound interest from the due date, and full retrospective clawback of any refund paid. Criminal prosecution may extend to directors and the registered agent, with maximum fines of WST 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment. Audited customs export records, courier manifests, and bank receipts must be retained for at least 7 years.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a conventional peg to a trade-weighted basket dominated by USD, AUD, NZD and EUR. Current-account payments are convertible through authorised dealer banks upon presentation of underlying invoices and customs or service documentation. Capital-account transactions (equity, loans, portfolio flows) and resident foreign-currency accounts require CBS approval. For a non-resident Samoa International Company (WS_IC), the International Companies Act 1988 confers a statutory exemption from the Exchange Control Act 1976 for offshore business conducted in foreign currency; therefore profit remittance, dividends and liquidation proceeds payable to non-residents from offshore foreign-currency accounts are freely remittable and are not subject to Samoa withholding tax. Material hurdles arise only if funds are converted into WST or passed through a domestic-resident account, in which case CBS Form EC applications, an authorised dealer, and a tax clearance certificate are required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, the full foreign-currency value of exported goods must be received within six months of the export date and must be tendered for sale to an authorised dealer within one month of receipt. No fixed statutory percentage of conversion into WST is prescribed; the CBS may direct conversion on a transaction-by-transaction basis. For the WS_IC Amazon FBA model, physical export of goods into Samoa is de minimis (inventory moves from supplier warehouses directly to Amazon fulfilment centres), so the surrender rule is practically engaged only on cargo landed in Samoa. Export receipts credited to an exempt offshore foreign-currency account of the IC are outside the surrender obligation provided the account is properly designated and reported through the IC's licensed registered agent.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa / CBS), acting as the sole exchange control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations 1976, and the Financial Institutions Act 1996.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% general corporate income tax rate; International Company (IC) operating in the tax-exempt zone: 0% on non-Samoa-source income

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Samoa's Income Tax Act 2015 does not impose a withholding tax on dividend distributions, whether to resident or non-resident shareholders; an exempt International Company may distribute profits to its non-resident beneficial owners free of Samoan dividend WHT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No codified statutory Local File / Master File threshold exists in Samoa. Prudent compliance benchmark: associated-party cross-border transactions exceeding WST 100,000 (approximately USD 38,000) per fiscal year should be supported by contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation applying the arm's length principle under Section 101 of the Income Tax Act 2015.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Samoa's International Companies Act 1987, as amended by the Economic Substance Requirements regime effective from 2019, requires an International Company carrying on a relevant activity (including IP holding, banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and other activities aligned with OECD/EU substance criteria) to demonstrate real economic presence in Samoa: Samoan registered office, board meetings held in Samoa, adequate staff and operating expenditure, and core income-generating activities performed in Samoa. A cross-border Amazon FBA e-commerce IC claiming the 0% tax-exempt status must maintain this substance, otherwise the tax exemption is denied and the company is subject to Registrar enforcement, penalties, and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner of Revenue may issue a default assessment on estimated income, apply a shortfall penalty of up to 100% of the tax avoided, and charge compound monthly interest; criminal prosecution for intentional evasion or falsification of records carries statutory fines and imprisonment for responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false exemption declarations or direct the diversion of Samoa-source revenue into the exempt IC are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax; the Samoan High Court may issue asset-freezing orders, forfeiture orders, travel bans, and director disqualification upon conviction.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Withdrawal of the IC tax exemption; the Registrar may impose economic substance non-compliance penalties, require rectification, and strike the company off the register where substance is absent for consecutive reporting periods; assets may escheat to the Registrar upon strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and licensed Registered Agents facilitating a sham substance arrangement face fines and de-registration; upon winding-up, directors may be personally liable for the IC's debts where trading was fraudulent or the shell was used to mislead revenue authorities.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018 and the Beneficial Ownership Act 2018: substantial financial penalties, confiscation of funds and property, and imprisonment for money laundering offences; continued non-disclosure of beneficial owners triggers regulatory enforcement, bank account freezes, and enhanced FATF/APG scrutiny.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit laundering or conceal beneficial ownership are personally exposed to criminal conviction, confiscation orders, travel bans, and extradition; the corporate veil may be pierced to recover illicit proceeds from individual controllers of the IC.
Transfer Pricing / Non-Arm's Length Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner of Revenue may exercise Section 101 of the Income Tax Act 2015 to substitute arm's length amounts, disallow excessive deductions, reassess the shifted margin at the 27% corporate rate, and apply shortfall penalties and monthly interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who architect an abusive transfer pricing scheme may be jointly and severally liable for the additional tax and penalties; the arrangement is treated as tax evasion where documentation is fabricated, exposing directors to criminal sanctions, disqualification, and cross-border exchange of information under Samoa's DTA and tax transparency commitments.
