Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is statutorily tax-exempt. No income tax TIN or VAT registration ID exists or is required. The sole statutory identifier is the VFSC registration number obtained through a licensed Registered Agent (filing of memorandum and articles of association plus declaration of compliance). If a physical Port Vila presence is established, a municipal business licence is required, but this does not trigger tax registration. Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu observes IMF Article VIII obligations for current-account convertibility; the VUV is the sole legal tender, but capital-account and current-account transactions by qualifying International Companies are effectively liberalized. There is no general withholding tax, capital-gains tax, or dividend tax in Vanuatu, and a VU International Company may repatriate profits by ordinary SWIFT remittance. Practical hurdles are not FX-control ceilings but rather AML/CFT documentation, source-of-funds evidence, and the domestic requirement that the paying bank verify the IC’s solvency certificate, board dividend resolution, and non-resident-source income status before releasing funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu does not impose compulsory export-proceeds surrender, a mandatory conversion ratio, or a statutory repatriation deadline for International Companies. Export and service receipts, including programmatic advertising and platform settlement proceeds, may be retained in the invoiced foreign currency in the IC’s external or domestic multi-currency account. Where a receiving bank files regulatory returns for RBV balance-of-payments statistics, the only practical obligation is accurate narrative classification of the remittance as service export (AdTech/SSP) rather than physical-goods export; no conversion to VUV is required.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) as the central monetary and exchange-control administrator, acting alongside the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for AML/CFT supervision and International Company corporate administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-medium feasibility. Vanuatu has no dedicated electronic-money institution (EMI) licensing framework; a fully offshore digital EMI is not authorized to provide local clearing, hold client funds in VUV, or act as a settlement agent in the Vanuatu banking system. An offshore EMI may be used only as an ancillary merchant-funds conduit for non-VUV settlement if it has no physical nexus in Vanuatu, obtains its own correspondent-banking relationships, and does not present itself as a VFSC-licensed institution. Any claim of local regulatory approval must be verified through the VFSC registry; otherwise the domestic bank will treat the EMI as an unregulated third-party and apply enhanced due diligence.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no payroll tax, and no withholding tax. A Vanuatu International Company (IC) registered under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) is statutorily exempt from all taxation on income, profits, distributions, and capital.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders. Dividends, distributions, and redemption proceeds may be repatriated without prior government approval and without deduction of domestic WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None — Vanuatu has not enacted transfer pricing legislation and there is no Local File, Master File, or CbCR threshold. However, the Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations require ICs carrying on relevant activities to demonstrate adequate local substance; for non-resident AdTech principals, the relevant test is managed-and-directed from Vanuatu with adequate premises, employees, and expenditure proportionate to the platform's income.

Economic substance scope: Yes — Vanuatu enacted Economic Substance Regulations applicable to International Companies. Entities engaged in relevant activities (banking, insurance, shipping, financing and leasing, IP holding, headquarters, distribution, or service businesses) must satisfy a substance test: active Vanuatu registered office, local directors/employees with board independence, adequate physical assets and annual expenditure. A programmatic AdTech IC structured as a pure IP-holding/royalty-collecting shell without a real Vanuatu operational presence is directly exposed to ESR sanctions and automatic information exchange to OECD/EU jurisdictions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abuse of the Tax-Exempt IC Privilege

Statutory sanction: Revocation of IC status; strike-off from the Vanuatu register; imposition of VAT and customs penalties up to VT 4,000,000 (approximately USD 34,000) plus interest under the Value Added Tax Act and the International Companies Act; automatic exchange of information to the beneficial owner's taxing authority.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute false annual returns or knowingly permit unlawful local trading face a personal fine of up to VT 1,000,000 and imprisonment not exceeding 2 years; in cross-border CFC disputes, directors of the parent group may be subject to civil restitution, travel bans in the foreign jurisdiction, and disqualification from board service.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulation non-compliance declarations; fines of VT 500,000 to VT 2,000,000 per relevant activity; administrative strike-off; negative determinations shared under the network of international tax transparency and rendered to the EU/OECD. The company may also be subjected to compulsory dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal penalties up to VT 2,000,000 and disqualification from holding any VFSC-regulated office; in the residence state of the beneficial owner, the IC's directors and shadow directors can be jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and penalties of the deemed CFC.
AML / Financial Transactions Reporting

Statutory sanction: Penalties and fines of up to VT 10,000,000 under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act No. 23 of 2006 for anti-money-laundering failures; freezing of funds and revocation of the registered agent's licence; referral to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit; contractual loss of US correspondent banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for AMLA/FTRA offenses; imprisonment up to 15 years for laundering the proceeds of predicate crimes; personal confiscation orders against foreign and local assets; blanket travel bans and extradition risk for principals who knowingly allow the prosecution of sanctioned advertising counterparties.
Unauthorized Banking & Unlicensed Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: Fines up to VT 5,000,000; immediate strike-off of the IC; criminal prosecution under the Financial Services Providers and International Companies Acts; all contracts involving the unlawful financial activity are voidable, and foreign PSPs/KYB institutions will terminate payment-rails access.

Director personal liability: Directors who carry on banking, deposit-taking, or money transmission without a licence commit a strict-liability criminal offense; imprisonment up to 12 years and personal liability for the return of any third-party funds; piercing of the corporate veil for the full value of the unauthorised settlement flows.
