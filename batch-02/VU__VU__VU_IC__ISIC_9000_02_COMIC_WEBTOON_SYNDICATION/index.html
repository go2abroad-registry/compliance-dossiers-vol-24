Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — Registry of International Companies, 35 Ave de la Côte d'Azur, Port Vila, Vanuatu.

Governing statute

International Companies Act [Cap 222] of Vanuatu, as amended by the International Companies (Amendment) Act 2022; key provisions — ss. 5–12 (incorporation procedure and Certificate of Incorporation), ss. 13–20 (Memorandum and Articles of Association), ss. 27–29 (directors and secretary/officers), ss. 47–52 (registered agent, registered office and service of process); read together with the Companies (International Company) Regulations 2023.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form CON-1 (Application for Incorporation of an International Company) together with the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association, lodged electronically via the VFSC Online Corporate Registry by a VFSC-licensed registered agent.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

No statutory income-tax TIN or VAT ID exists in Vanuatu. For a foreign-owned International Company (IC): (1) engage a VFSC-licensed registered agent; (2) file articles of incorporation, registered-office particulars and agent consent with the Registrar of International Companies at VFSC; (3) pay gazetted incorporation fees (no paid-up capital requirement, minimum capital nil); (4) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and Company Registration Number (CRN); (5) use the CRN for all bank, contract, and regulatory dealings. A VRC customs client identifier is optional and relevant only if physical goods are imported/exported.

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Nil — Vanuatu does not impose VAT, GST, or sales and use tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in law — Vanuatu does not operate a VAT/GST system, so there is no 0% export rating to claim and no refund mechanism to trigger. Revenue from offshore webtoon syndication (Apple IAP, Google Play Billing) is sourced outside Vanuatu and remains outside the Vanuatu indirect tax net. If the company later exports physical printed editions, tangible exports are not subject to export VAT; a standard customs export entry is the only documentation needed.

  • No Vanuatu customs export declaration, bill of lading, or inward remittance certificate is statutorily required for digital webtoon export; the cross-border supply of intangibles is not a dutiable transaction.
  • Prudent evidence for company standing and audit defense: Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent confirmation, Apple IAP / Google Play settlement statements, distribution/licensing agreements, and bank credit advices (retain for at least 7 years).

Not applicable — Vanuatu has no e-invoicing mandate, no real-time fiscal clearance API, no continuous transaction controls, and no digital tax stamping requirement

No VAT zero-rating clawback regime exists because Vanuatu has no VAT. However, abuse of International Company exemption — e.g., carrying on business in Vanuatu without a VFSC permit, unauthorized interaction with Vanuatu residents, or false filings — is punishable by fines up to VUV 100,000 and possible deregistration. False declarations to Vanuatu Revenue and Customs under the Customs Act (Cap 75) may trigger seizure, forfeiture, and monetary penalties. ICs owe record-keeping and beneficial-ownership filing duties; registered agents and banks apply AML/CFT checks. Because Vanuatu participates in OECD Global Forum exchange of information on request, misrepresented cross-border fund flows can be scrutinized and result in regulatory and reputational consequences.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu accepted International Monetary Fund Article VIII obligations, Sections 2, 3 and 4, with effect from 19 December 1996; accordingly, there are no restrictions on payments and transfers for current international transactions, which covers royalty, licensing and digital content syndication remittances of the Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC). Capital account flows remain nominally subject to exchange control approval, but the VU_IC is treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes and may freely maintain and operate foreign-currency accounts, convert foreign currency and repatriate capital and profits without prior RBV consent. Profit remittance is unencumbered: the International Company is exempt from income tax, dividend withholding tax, capital gains tax and stamp duty on profit distributions. Practical hurdles are limited to correspondent-bank KYC document review, 2 to 5 business day settlement for USD/EUR flows through Port Vila clearance networks, and the fact that VUV is not freely deliverable outside Vanuatu; hence the treasury should denominate liquidity in USD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender ratio, conversion ratio or fixed repatriation deadline for export proceeds derived from digital services, and no monetary export order applies to webtoon licensing receipts. Export proceeds of a non-resident International Company may be retained indefinitely in a foreign-currency external account with an RBV authorised dealer. Where a payment is erroneously credited in VUV, the authorised dealer permits conversion back to foreign currency only with source documentation evidencing the underlying service export; VUV balances may otherwise only be used for permitted local disbursements through an external account, not for telegraphic transfer abroad.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act No. 2 of 1981 (as amended); exchange control administration is conducted under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 66], the Banking Act [Cap. 62], the International Banking Act [Cap. 64] and applicable RBV Exchange Control Notices and Authorised Dealer directives.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW. Vanuatu has no domestic Electronic Money Institution licensing regime, so offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer and Currencycloud are engaged only under their home-state licences (UK FCA, Singapore MAS, EU EBA passport). EMIs typically classify Vanuatu International Companies as higher-risk offshore vehicles and commonly require a registered-agent introduction letter, VFSC beneficial-ownership filing confirmation, a physical or virtual office address, bank-verified source of funds and platform settlement evidence. Wise and Payoneer remain operationally viable for creator payouts in 40+ currencies, but they cannot provide VUV clearing, RBV nostro settlement or statutory Vanuatu corporate banking capacity and must not substitute for a tier-1 licensed bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: originating shareholder or group treasury wires USD via SWIFT MT103 to the VU_IC beneficiary account at the selected Port Vila authorised dealer using the bank's BIC and the VU_IC operational account number; field-70 must state 'Share capital contribution per board resolution dated YYYY-MM-DD'; for group treasury originations an ISO purpose code of 'INTC' (intra-company payment) is preferred, while external investors use 'COMC' (commercial payment); the receiving bank applies standard identification checks, source-of-funds verification and board-resolution review; no RBV prior approval is required because the VU_IC is non-resident and the remittance is in foreign currency. For capital above USD 500,000, route the wire through the relationship manager in advance to pre-clear enhanced due diligence and prevent return-wire delays.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for all creator, studio, localisation vendor and sub-licensor settlements, using the IC's USD account to avoid VUV conversion friction. VUV-denominated domestic payments clear through the authorised dealer's local clearing cell on a T+1 basis and should be minimised. For large one-off distribution advances or sub-licensing advances exceeding USD 250,000, the VU_IC may issue an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit confirmed by a Port Vila authorised dealer or a Hong Kong/Singapore correspondent under UCP 600, with the licensor receiving payment against commercial invoice, signed sub-licence agreement and platform revenue schedule. For micro-royalty payments below USD 2,000, batch settlement via Payoneer or Wise provides netting and lower transaction cost, subject to EMI risk appetite.Outright FX Forwards (USD/VUV, USD/EUR, USD/IDR, USD/KRW) with licensed Vanuatu authorised dealers and their global correspondent banks, maturities up to 12 months, permitted without RBV approval where the underlying receivable is evidenced by platform settlement statements.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) executed offshore through Singapore, Hong Kong or Frankfurt trading desks for restricted or illiquid Asian cash currencies (PHP, CNY, IDR, KRW), cash-settled in USD; Vanuatu exchange control does not restrict non-resident NDF contracts.Cross-Currency Swaps for treasury-scale revenue programmes above USD 1,000,000, exchanging USD against the settlement currency of a licensed distributor, platform partner or studio, conditional on documented future receivables and approved by the relationship bank's global markets desk.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: VUV 0 (Vanuatu has no statutory TP or master-file/local-file requirement because no corporate income tax base exists). As a matter of cross-border prudence, formal OECD-aligned TP documentation should be maintained when intercompany webtoon licence fees, platform commissions or management charges exceed VUV 50,000,000 (approximately USD 410,000) per annum, to satisfy upstream tax audits in the shareholder's residence jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019, integrated with the International Companies Act [Cap 222], imposes a substance requirement on ICs conducting relevant activities. Digital webtoon syndication entailing ownership or licensing of IP is an 'intellectual property business'; if the IC also hosts download/transcoding infrastructure or handles distributive logistics, it may be a 'distribution and service centre'. The IC must demonstrate adequate premises, qualified employees, local operating expenditure and the conduct of core income-generating activities in Vanuatu. Failure to satisfy the ESR test results in VFSC penalties, penalty tax action, mandatory deregistration and potential criminal sanction for directors.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Offshore Concealment

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu law, knowingly making a false declaration in the annual return attracts a fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 and imprisonment for up to 12 months. In the shareholder's residence jurisdiction, penalties may reach 200% of the underpaid tax, plus civil fraud fines and asset forfeiture orders.

Director personal liability: Directors may be cited as co-conspirators in the foreign tax evasion; personal restitution orders, travel bans and surrender of passports, and imprisonment (typically 5-15 years) in jurisdictions applying the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act or equivalent criminal tax statutes.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: VFSC may levy a penalty of up to VUV 500,000, impose penalty tax on deemed relevant income, and strike the company off the International Companies Register; the company's assets may be vested in the Registrar of Companies.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal disqualification for up to five years, civil liability for the company's outstanding penalties, and potential criminal prosecution for knowingly filing a false ESR return; foreign tax authorities may disregard the IC and assess the directors directly.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Civil and administrative penalties of up to VUV 2,000,000 under the Vanuatu AML/CFT framework, freezing orders on bank accounts, revocation of the registered agent's licence, and referral to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for regulatory fines if the breach results from their wilful neglect; imprisonment for up to 10 years may be imposed for intentional money laundering; foreign corporate travel restrictions and visa denial may follow.
Misrepresentation to the VFSC

Statutory sanction: A fine of up to VUV 25,000 per false statement and imprisonment for up to 12 months; the International Company may be struck off and its certificate of incorporation cancelled retroactively.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the false declaration are jointly and severally liable for corporate debts incurred after the misrepresentation; they may also be subject to foreign extradition requests and exclusion from Vanuatu corporate administration.
