Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue (MaF – Malo o Tupe), Apia, Samoa; official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws; customs processing is coordinated through the same Ministry via the ASYCUDA World clearance platform.

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 obtains its statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) through the Ministry of Revenue taxpayer registration process. The IC's licensed registered agent submits Form TR-1 together with: (i) the Certificate of Incorporation; (ii) the Memorandum and Articles of Association; (iii) the offshore tax exemption certificate or ICA status letter issued by the Registrar of International Companies; (iv) director and shareholder particulars; (v) registered agent consent; and (vi) a board resolution authorizing the registration. The Ministry issues the TIN within 5–10 business days. No minimum capital, local director, or residency condition applies to an IC. The same TIN is used for VAGST participation if the IC elects to register as a taxable person.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – The Samoa International Company is statutorily tax-exempt: no Samoa income tax, corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax on distributions, or stamp duty applies to its offshore business. Exemption is automatic upon incorporation and may be evidenced by a tax exemption certificate issued under the International Companies Act 1987.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch classification elections exist under Samoa domestic law; the IC's tax-exempt status is mandatory and non-elective. The only statutory conversion routes are: (a) re-domiciliation of a foreign company into Samoa as an IC; (b) conversion of an IC into a domestic Samoa company, which subjects it to the ordinary corporate income tax and VAGST regime; and (c) limited-life company elections available under the International Companies Act. Foreign shareholders must make any upstream entity classification elections in their home jurisdiction (e.g., US check-the-box), but such elections do not alter the IC's Samoa classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 60,000 – compulsory VAGST registration threshold based on the value of taxable supplies (other than capital supplies) made in any 12-month period. An IC with exclusively zero-rated export turnover may nonetheless register for VAGST to recover input tax credits, while an IC with no taxable activity in Samoa has no registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the VAGST Act 2013, a Samoan vendor supplying online maternity and infant-care commodities (ISIC 4791 / NAICS 458110) applies the 0% rate where title passes to a non-resident purchaser and the goods are physically removed from Samoa within 28 days (or an approved extension) of the time of supply. Services qualify for 0% where the recipient is outside Samoa at the time of supply and the benefit is consumed outside Samoa. The seller must hold contemporaneous evidence of export and payment settlement. The Samoa IC's offshore income is separately exempt from income tax under the International Companies Act 1987, making the principal VAGST objective recovery of input credits on eligible business expenses.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export declaration with export entry number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing Samoa port/airport of departure and a consignee outside Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Samoa licensed bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency
  • Commercial invoice and packing list cross-referenced to the customs declaration
  • Sales contract, purchase order, or e-commerce platform settlement statement identifying the non-resident buyer

Samoa imposes no real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API, or digital stamping mandate. Monthly VAGST returns are filed electronically through the Ministry of Revenue E-Services portal. VAGST invoices must state the supplier's name and VAGST registration number, customer identity, invoice date, description, quantity, taxable value, and VAGST amount. Export clearances are processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs declaration system.

The Ministry of Revenue and the Customs Division conduct post-clearance audits of zero-rated export claims. Deficient or fraudulent substantiation triggers: (i) reversal of input credits and reassessment of output VAGST at the statutory 15% rate with interest from the due date; (ii) a civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded under the Tax Administration Act 2012; (iii) criminal prosecution of the IC and responsible directors, with fines up to WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 5 years; and (iv) customs forfeiture and contravention penalties for false or incomplete export declarations. The retrospective reassessment window is generally 5 years, extended where fraud or wilful default is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala is not a fully convertible offshore currency; current account payments for goods and services are generally free from exchange control and settle freely through authorised dealers, while capital account inflows and outflows (including equity subscriptions, loans and profit repatriation) are subject to prior CBS approval or post-transaction notification. Profit remittance is permitted through an authorised dealer in WST or foreign currency, conditioned on audited financial statements, tax clearance, satisfaction of the 15% resident withholding tax (or reduced treaty rate), and confirmation of the International Company's tax exemption status under the International Companies Act 1987.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters must repatriate export proceeds into Samoa within 30 days from the date of shipment and deposit them in a Samoa-based bank account; a mandatory conversion ratio is not statutorily fixed, but proceeds must be offered for conversion to WST or retained as approved foreign currency with an authorised dealer, and any exemption requires advance CBS documentation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Samoa's legal framework does not recognise foreign digital e-money institutions (EMIs) as regulated entities for treasury clearing; a non-resident EMI may not open a correspondent account or issue digital payment instruments without a licensing and permanent establishment analysis. Such EMIs may only be used as a payment aggregator at the upstream e-commerce gateway level, while all WST-clearing and foreign exchange settlements must be executed through a Samoa licensed commercial bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 250,000 aggregate annual related-party transactions trigger Local File preparation; Master File is required where the ultimate group’s turnover is above WST 100 million. An e-commerce IC must also prepare contemporaneous TP documentation if its intra-group cross-border sales exceed WST 150,000.

Economic substance scope: Samoan ESR (international exchange of information laws and economic substance regulations) require any IC engaged in 'distribution and service centre' activity to prove substantive economic presence: physical office in Samoa, at least one board meeting per year in Samoa, payroll generated for local employees, and core income-generating activities performed in Samoa. Offshore-only management is not sufficient; failure to meet the test triggers administrative penalties, information exchange to the foreign tax authority, and possible deregistration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Exemption Abuse / De Facto Residence

Statutory sanction: Reassessment as a resident company at 25% corporate income tax on worldwide income, interest and penalty of up to 100% of the tax shortfall, and late payment surcharge of 2% per month under the Samoa Revenue Administration Act.

Director personal liability: Directors individually liable for the unpaid tax when the IC is treated as a sham; travel bans are available to Revenue for non-cooperation, and directors may face a custodial sentence of up to 7 years for wilful evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR financial penalty up to WST 20,000 per year of non-compliance, additional fixed civil penalty of WST 5,000 per infraction, and referral to the Registrar for strike-off; the IC’s license may also be revoked.

Director personal liability: The Registrar may disqualify directors from managing any Samoa company for up to 5 years; directors of the registered agent can be personally sanctioned for mis-certifying substance.
AML / KYC Deficiencies in E-Commerce Rails

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to WST 100,000 for the company and WST 15,000 for each culpable officer under the Samoa Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism Act; summary suspension of the registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal prosecution, imprisonment for up to 5 years, and a personal demerit record that prohibits future directorship in regulated businesses.
