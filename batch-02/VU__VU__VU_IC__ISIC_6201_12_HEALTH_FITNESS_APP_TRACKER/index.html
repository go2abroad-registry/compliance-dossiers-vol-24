Governing statute

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Vanuatu International Company; shares may be issued with or without par value and may remain partly paid. No escrow account deposit or capitalisation undertaking is required by the VFSC at incorporation or on an ongoing basis.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Department (VRCD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; taxpayer portal: https://vrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must submit Form VAT 1 to the VRCD Commissioner of Revenue together with certified copies of the entity's certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, passports of directors and beneficial owners, an original utility bill or lease agreement proving a Vanuatu address, evidence of foreign tax identification number, and a recent bank reference. A TIN or VAT registration number is issued only when the entity makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu; a pure International Company carrying on business wholly outside Vanuatu is statutorily exempt and does not require a TIN unless it voluntarily registers for VAT. Approval generally takes 5–15 business days after complete submission.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone_Exempt_International_Company: An International Company incorporated under the Vanuatu International Companies Act is exempt from all Vanuatu income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and stamp duty on offshore activities, regardless of beneficial ownership or place of control. It is not resident for domestic income tax purposes and is not eligible for pass-through treatment under Vanuatu law; the entity is simply outside the tax net for non-Vanuatu-source transactions.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Vanuatu law. The tax exemption for International Companies is automatic and non-elective. An exempt IC wishing to engage in local Vanuatu business must convert to or register as a Domestic Company (under the Companies Act) and may then be required to register for VAT; such conversion is the only statutory 'election' relevant to tax status. Hybrid mismatch rules are not enacted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Registration is compulsory for any supplier making taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding VT 1,000,000 in any 12-month period. A foreign entity without a local establishment and making supplies to Vanuatu customers is not ordinarily required to register unless it physically supplies goods or services within Vanuatu; International Companies are exempt from VAT on offshore supplies and are not subject to compulsory registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export VAT zero-rating applies to goods exported from Vanuatu by a registered supplier where the goods are shipped or delivered outside Vanuatu within a reasonable period and documentary evidence of export is maintained. Zero-rating also applies to services performed for and enjoyed by non-residents outside Vanuatu, including digital health and fitness application subscriptions and biometric telemetry services delivered to non-resident customers, provided the supplier retains records showing the recipient is non-resident, the service is rendered from outside Vanuatu, and no benefit is derived from a Vanuatu domestic establishment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDec Entry 3B) for physical goods
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing export from Vanuatu
  • Commercial Invoice signed by exporter
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Vanuatu-licensed bank proving receipt of foreign exchange
  • Copy of the underlying contract or app store settlement report showing non-resident recipients
  • Zero-rated VAT invoice justifying the 0% treatment

Vanuatu does not operate a real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, API, or digital stamping regime. Registered VAT suppliers may issue manual or computer-generated invoices and retain paper or PDF records for audit. No government e-invoice clearance is required.

Fraudulent export zero-rating claims attract liability to the VAT withheld plus penalties up to 200% of the tax sought, daily default interest, fines of up to VT 500,000, and imprisonment of directors for up to 3 years under the Value Added Tax Act. The Commissioner may retrospectively reassess export claims for up to 5 years and reverse input credits previously claimed where export documentation proves false. Misdeclarations are also prosecuted as money-laundering offences under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV, ISO 4217) operates under a managed floating regime pegged to a trade-weighted basket of AUD, USD, NZD, EUR and JPY. Current-account payments (royalties, license fees, service fees, dividend remittances) are freely convertible through RBV-authorized dealers subject only to standard transaction documentation; capital-account outflows (loan redemptions, share buy-backs, liquidation distributions) require prior RBV exchange-control approval for resident entities. A non-resident International Company is statutorily exempted from exchange-control formalities under the IC Act, giving de facto full convertibility for USD-denominated treasury flows and eliminating profit-remittance hurdles for the Vanuatu holding vehicle of the health-fitness-app group.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must collect export proceeds within six months of shipment and surrender balances to an authorized dealer within one month of receipt, with a statutory mandatory conversion ratio of 100% of VUV proceeds unless a specific exemption is issued. The Vanuatu International Company is outside this regime: it may retain receivables in USD or any permitted foreign currency, without conversion to VUV, provided it does not transact with Vanuatu residents, derive Vanuatu-source income, or acquire VUV-denominated assets. App-store proceeds from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing therefore flow directly into the IC's USD settlement account with no surrender obligation.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — the statutory Central Bank and delegated Foreign Exchange Controller under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 74]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately licenses banks and supervises the International Company registry under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility rat. Vanuatu has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI), digital-bank, or neobank licensing regime; only VFSC-licensed deposit-taking institutions may hold customer funds. Offshore EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Mercury) commonly decline Vanuatu-domiciled ICs because of the jurisdiction's recent FATF grey-listing history (2021–2023) and global correspondent-bank de-risking. A compliant alternative is the hybrid structure: the IC maintains its primary treasury and settlement account at ANZ or BRED Vanuatu, while regulated EMIs in Australia, New Zealand, or Singapore are used only as downstream pay-out rails for user refunds and contractor disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound injections are cleared via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers from the shareholder's or investor's bank to the IC's USD settlement account at ANZ or BRED (Port Vila), with the BoP purpose code 'G100 — equity capital injection' (or 'general corporate working capital' for shareholder loans) and the memo field citing the IC name and 'International Companies Act [Cap. 222]'. The receiving bank requires the certificate of incorporation, VFSC register extract, UBO declaration, and, for amounts above approximately USD 50,000, a source-of-funds letter from the remitter; the IC exchange-control exemption means an RBV pre-approval letter is not required unless the remittance is classed as resident-connected. Verification follows standard remittance-checking (RFI/name-screening) with cleared funds available on a T+1 basis under normal VFSC settlement convention.Outbound operating disbursements (cloud hosting, marketing, contractor fees, sub-processor licensing, and Apple/Google payout sweepdowns) settle through SWIFT MT103 customer transfers, with MT202/MT205 cover payments for interbank legs across USD, AUD, EUR, and VUV corridors; US-technology-vendor payments use Fedwire/ACH fallbacks through the bank's USD correspondent. Large procurement items (e.g., server hardware, biometric-data processing equipment, or M&A of data assets) can be supported by documentary collections or commercial letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT700, while recurring app-store settlements flow natively through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing rails into the IC's USD account. The annual government fee and return are due on or before each anniversary of incorporation (typically the last day of the month of incorporation). No income tax deadline applies because no operating income, profit, or gain is assessable to Vanuatu income tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory threshold exists. Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and no transfer pricing statute, so no Master File, Local File, or Country-by-Country Report is required from an International Company. Bookkeeping must still satisfy Section 32 of the International Companies Act, including records sufficient to explain transactions and financial position.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not impose tax-based economic substance filings on International Companies. However, under the International Companies Act and the VFSC beneficial ownership regime, an IC must not be a naked shell: it must maintain a registered office, retain a licensed registered agent, and maintain board minutes and accounting records. If the company is managed and controlled outside Vanuatu and is tax resident in a foreign state, no additional Vanuatu economic substance filing is due.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company and Artificial Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and dissolution under Sections 85-89 of the International Companies Act; default penalties on the annual fee; loss of the protection of limited liability in respect of creditors; denial of good standing certificates for banking or licensing purposes.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for fraudulent or reckless trading, may be disqualified from managing Vanuatu entities, and may face civil restitution to creditors. Where deliberate concealment or fraud is proven, criminal liability under the Vanuatu Penal Code can result in imprisonment.
Abusive Transfer Pricing and Foreign Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no domestic tax anti-avoidance rule, but the foreign tax authority may impose adjusted tax, interest, and penalties, deny foreign treaty benefits, and trigger CRS/FATCA disclosures. Vanuatu regulators, upon evidence of misuse of the International Companies Act, may strike off or suspend the entity and exchange information through the Offshore Governance Act and mutual assistance channels.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling beneficial owners who orchestrate sham arrangements may be charged with tax fraud abroad; foreign civil judgments can be enforced against them through Vanuatu courts where fraud is shown. Travel bans and asset-freezing orders may be issued through the Vanuatu Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters framework.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act and the Financial Transactions Reporting Act, the company and directors are liable to substantial monetary fines, forfeiture of assets, and imprisonment. The VFSC also has power to revoke the IC's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners may face criminal imprisonment for money-laundering offences, asset-freezing orders, and travel bans. The corporate veil is pierced where the company is used as an instrument of crime, making shareholders jointly and severally liable.
Failure to File Annual Returns and Pay Government Fees

Statutory sanction: Default penalty and late fees accrue; after repeated default the company is struck off and dissolved under Sections 85-89 of the International Companies Act. Struck-off status invalidates the company's right to contract, hold assets, or maintain bank accounts in Vanuatu; restoration requires payment of arrears and court or administrative costs.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for debts incurred after the IC has lost good standing, and may be disqualified from managing any Vanuatu-registered company for a prescribed period. Where the default accompanies fraudulent trading, directors also face criminal fines and imprisonment under Vanuatu Law.
