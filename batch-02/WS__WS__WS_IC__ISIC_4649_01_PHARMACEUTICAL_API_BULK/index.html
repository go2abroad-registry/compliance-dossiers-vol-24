Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), the unified national revenue and customs administration established under the Samoa Revenue Authority Act 2017; taxpayer portal: SRA e-Services / MySRA at www.sra.gov.ws. Ancillary regulators for International Companies: Registrar of International Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL), and Central Bank of Samoa (exchange control).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate under the International Companies Act 1987 via MCIL to obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; the International Company (IC) is a non-resident, tax-exempt offshore vehicle and no SRA income-tax TIN is required for its offshore operations. Step 2: Open a licensed offshore foreign-currency account with a Samoan commercial bank; the bank files the exchange-control notification with the Central Bank of Samoa under delegated authority. Step 3: Satisfy the annual government fee and economic substance filing obligations imposed by the International Companies Amendment Act 2021, which are the only fiscal impositions on a pure offshore IC. Step 4: If the IC imports pharmaceutical samples or equipment into Samoa or makes any domestic supply, register with SRA for a TIN and VAGST using Form VAGST-01 within 21 days of the first taxable supply, and obtain an ASYCUDA World customs importer/exporter code. Step 5: For foreign bank and counterparty due-diligence, obtain a Certificate of Good Standing from MCIL and, where a tax identification number is contractually required, an SRA non-resident/inactive taxpayer certificate confirming exempt status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Samoa International Company is outside the Samoan income tax net (all non-Samoan source income exempt), is not a VAGST taxpayer, and is not Samoan tax resident provided central management and control is exercised outside Samoa; the tax-exempt status is preserved only while economic substance requirements under the International Companies Amendment Act 2021 are met in good standing.

Available election forms

Samoa has no check-the-box entity regime. Available statutory elections: (1) election under Part IX of the International Companies Act 1987 to convert to a domestic company, which brings the entity fully into Samoan corporate income tax and VAGST; (2) ring-fencing election to operate a separately registered Samoan domestic branch for island sales while the offshore pharmaceutical API wholesale book remains exempt; (3) application for a binding private ruling under the Tax Administration Act 2015 to confirm no-nexus or zero-rating treatment of a specific API export flow; (4) where hybrid-financing or treaty documentation is required by foreign counterparties, a written residency/classification certification under the Income Tax Act 1974 (as amended) to support anti-hybrid and OECD BEPS outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 60,000 (approximately USD 21,500) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period under section 8 of the VAGST Act 2008; nil threshold applies to non-resident suppliers of enumerated digital and remote services; a pure offshore pharmaceutical API wholesale IC is outside this registration net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods (pharmaceutical APIs): 0% VAGST applies to supplies to non-resident purchasers where the goods are physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of the earlier of invoice date or payment date, supported by a completed ASYCUDA World export declaration. Services (trading, logistics, and commission services ancillary to the API flow): 0% applies where the recipient is a non-resident, the recipient is outside Samoa at the time of supply, and the benefit is enjoyed wholly outside Samoa. Since the standard IC is exempt and unregistered, its offshore sales are entirely outside the VAGST base; only upon the mixed-activity registration election do the export zero-rating and input-credit refund mechanisms become operative, with excess input tax attributable to zero-rated exports refundable after SRA return processing.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World total export declaration with release reference, showing pharmaceutical API classifications under HS Chapters 29/30)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export from Samoa to a non-resident consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/MT300 evidencing receipt of export proceeds into a Samoa-licensed foreign-currency account within the 180-day repatriation window
  • Commercial invoice reconciled to the customs declaration and stating the governing Incoterm and zero-rating basis
  • Non-resident counterparty due-diligence proof (overseas TIN or tax residence certificate) substantiating offshore destination and enjoyment

No real-time fiscal clearance, government digital stamping, or continuous transaction-control mandate exists in Samoa. Customs export declarations must be lodged electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window system and pass risk-based selectivity before clearance. VAGST-registered businesses must issue sequential tax invoices within 28 days of supply, retain records for 10 years, and file invoice-line summary schedules with monthly VAGST returns via the SRA portal; SRA may require SAF-T-style or spreadsheet audit extracts. Invoices issued by an exempt IC for offshore transactions are outside the tax-invoice regime and may be issued in any commercially agreed format.

A zero-rating claim that is unsupported, falsely documented, or lacking economic substance triggers reassessment at the full 15% VAGST rate, imposition of statutory shortfall penalties (escalating to 200% for intentional fraud or wilful misstatement), and retrospective clawback of any input credits claimed; the SRA may reopen returns for four years generally and up to eight years where fraud is alleged. Criminal false-declaration offenses under the Customs Act 2014 and Crimes Act 2016 carry substantial corporate fines and imprisonment for responsible officers, and misuse of the IC exemption can result in revocation or non-renewal of the Certificate of Good Standing with consequential loss of exempt status on all accumulative profits. SRA integrated audits triangulate ASYCUDA export data, VAGST returns, and offshore currency receipts, so pharmaceutical wholesalers must maintain full chain-of-custody evidence including batch records, certificates of analysis, Incoterm contractual matrices, and customer tax-residence certificates.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tālā (WST, ISO 4217) is a partially convertible, administered currency. Current-account payments are processed freely through CBS-licensed Authorized Dealers upon presentation of supporting trade documentation and exchange-control declarations; capital-account transactions by resident companies require prior CBS approval unless a valid IC exemption applies. A qualifying International Company (IC) may remit dividends and repatriate capital through its Authorized Dealer under the exchange-control exemption without per-payment CBS clearance, conditioned on: a current Exchange Control Exemption Certificate; no Samoan-resident beneficial owners; audited financial statements; and a director's solvency and source-of-funds declaration. WST is a physically non-deliverable currency outside Samoa, so all cross-border pharma trade is quoted, cleared, and settled in USD/EUR via correspondent banking, with WST used only for local operating costs.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For non-exempt Samoan resident exporters, the Exchange Control Regulations impose a surrender timeline of 30 days from receipt: 100% of the foreign-currency export proceeds must be offered to a CBS-licensed Authorized Dealer and converted into WST at the prevailing dealing rate, unless the CBS grants a prior written deferral. Qualifying International Companies engaged exclusively in offshore API wholesale are generally exempted from mandatory conversion and may retain export proceeds in a designated IC Foreign-Currency Account at an Authorized Dealer, provided the exports do not originate from Samoan domestic goods and the bank reports aggregate balances to CBS annually. The statutory conversion ratio for non-exempt flows is 100% of gross receipt value, less verified freight, insurance, and direct selling commissions.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) (Samoan: Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa) — the sole monetary, exchange-control, and banking-supervisory authority established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984 and empowered by the Exchange Control Act 1977 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1980 (as amended). The CBS supervises all licensed commercial banks as Authorized Dealers and administers the applicable International Company exchange-control exemption instruments, which relax statutory exchange-control approvals for qualifying Samoan International Companies while preserving CBS reporting and enforcement jurisdiction.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low — no offshore digital EMI or virtual-asset service provider is licensed or passportable into Samoa; the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and the CBS AML/CFT Guidelines restrict currency exchange and wire clearing to licensed banks and licensed money remitters. A foreign fintech/EMI may be used only as an ancillary third-country collection or disbursement conduit outside Samoa (e.g., receiving USD from buyers and paying Indian/Chinese API suppliers), provided no WST is held or converted offshore; it cannot satisfy the IC's covenant to maintain its treasury and capital escrow accounts at a licensed Samoan Authorized Dealer, and any pure-EMI structure will trigger immediate CBS supervisory concern.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder-loan injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 to the IC's USD-designated account at ANZ or BSP, carrying the BPM6-compliant purpose code for equity injection into a direct-investment enterprise or for loans from the direct investor, with the Samoa IC registration number quoted in field 72 and the exact IC legal name in field 59. The receiving Authorized Dealer executes a capital-inflow verification pack consisting of: board resolution authorizing share allotment or loan drawdown; executed loan agreement or subscription documents; valid IC exchange-control exemption certificate; complete KYC/EDD file; and funds-origin statement — after which the Capital Escrow Account is credited without separate CBS consent because the IC falls outside the resident exchange-control net. The first credited capital tranche triggers the bank's account-activation review and the commencement of transaction monitoring.Trade settlement to foreign API suppliers is executed through SWIFT MT103 direct transfers (and MT202 COV for high-value correspondent reimbursements) in USD or EUR from the IC's operational account via the bank's global correspondent network. For large confirmed orders, the standard clearing rail is an irrevocable at-sight Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700) issued by ANZ/BSP and confirmed by a top-50 global bank (e.g., HSBC, BNP Paribas, or Standard Chartered), given the limited strength of Samoan bank LCs in overseas beneficiary markets. The documentary presentation under UCP 600 includes the commercial invoice, full set of clean-on-board bills of lading, certificate of analysis, and GMP/WHO certification; all SWIFT-based USD clearing terminates in the Authorized Dealer's nostro account, with no settlement permitted through unlicensed fintech conduits.Spot foreign-exchange transactions (T+2 settlement) executed with a licensed Samoan Authorized Dealer, subject to CBS transaction reporting and underlying trade verification.Outright forward contracts (up to 12 months tenor) against committed trade invoices, permitted for exempt International Companies without prior CBS approval where a genuine underlying USD/WST or USD/EUR commercial exposure exists.Cross-currency swaps (fixed-for-fixed USD/WST or USD/EUR) with prior written CBS approval, granted only for genuine balance-sheet hedging and never for speculative positions.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referenced to WST/USD, executed exclusively outside Samoa through a licensed international counterparty, on condition that no WST delivery occurs in Samoa and settlement is net-settled in USD.Currency options on major trade currencies (USD/EUR) booked through the bank's treasury line, supported by an annual hedge-effectiveness declaration filed with CBS.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File/Master File thresholds exist in Samoa; Section 59 of the Income Tax Act 1974 empowers the Commissioner to adjust non-arm's length transactions with associated non-residents. A safe harbour documentation package comprising a functional analysis, benchmark study, and pricing policy is recommended for pharmaceutical API wholesale flows exceeding WST 2,000,000 per annum

Economic substance scope: Samoa has not enacted standalone OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, under OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices commitments and the AML/CFT (Companies) Rules, the registered agent must verify the IC's economic purpose, maintain current beneficial ownership records, and confirm the absence of domestic Samoan business activity. A pharmaceutical API wholesale IC must hold a valid wholesale/distribution licence under Samoa's Pharmacy and Drugs legislation, maintain active bank and trade documentation for each API supply chain, and pay the annual SIFA compliance fee to retain its tax-exempt status

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / abusive use of tax exemption

Statutory sanction: Retroactive revocation of the Section 198 exemption; assessment of 27% corporate income tax on all undeclared profits; civil tax penalties of up to 150% of the underpaid tax

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unpaid tax and penalties; criminal conviction for tax fraud carries imprisonment of up to 7 years
Shell company / false or misleading annual return

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to WST 50,000; compulsory strike-off of the company; 10-year disqualification from acting as a director of any Samoan international company

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines of up to WST 25,000 and imprisonment of up to 3 years under the offence provisions of the International Companies Act 1987
AML / pharmaceutical licensing breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to WST 100,000; seizure of pharmaceutical stock; cancellation of the registered agent's licence; referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit for money-laundering investigation

Director personal liability: Directors face departure prohibition orders (travel bans), asset-freezing orders, and imprisonment of up to 10 years under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018
Annual return and government fee default

Statutory sanction: Late filing surcharges; 10% per annum interest on arrears; administrative strike-off and dissolution of the company after 12 months of persistent default

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally liable for company debts incurred during the default period under the fraudulent trading doctrine applicable to international companies
