Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The WS_IC, as a Samoa International Company, is deemed non-resident for income tax purposes and is exempt from Samoan income tax and stamp duty on all foreign-source income; the effective corporate income tax rate is 0%. Samoan-source turnover from bonded airport feeder trucking is excluded from income tax where the transport billing is structured offshore with the international carrier, but the VAGST treatment of the domestic road feeder leg must be independently determined under the indirect tax rules (standard-rated 15% unless zero-rated as an integral component of international carriage of goods).

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a check-the-box entity classification regime. Available elections: (i) election at incorporation to assume 'International Company' status under the International Companies Act 1987, which is irrevocable without Ministerial consent; (ii) voluntary VAGST registration for a person whose taxable supplies fall below the compulsory turnover threshold; (iii) application by related registered persons to be treated as a single group for VAGST purposes with consolidated returns, subject to Ministry for Revenue approval; (iv) no hybrid mismatch or BEPS entity-equity elections are enacted; residence and place-of-supply determinations follow the Goods and Services Tax Act 2012 and the Income Tax Act 2012.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 40,000 (Samoan Tala) annual value of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period. The GPS-sealed bonded truck fleet operator is mandatorily registrable because the combined domestic airport feeder and export-related transport turnover will exceed WST 40,000 per annum; voluntary registration below the threshold is available and advisable to recover input VAGST on fleet and maintenance costs.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAGST rate applies to: (i) goods physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply; (ii) international transport of goods or passengers; and (iii) ancillary bonded road feeder services supplied directly in connection with the international carriage of cargo, including the airport feeder leg, provided the WS_IC contracts with the international airline, ocean carrier, or licensed customs agent, the service is performed exclusively between the customs control area of the international airport and a customs bonded terminal, and the supply is properly documented as part of the international consignment chain. Merely domestic warehouse-to-warehouse cartage or courier distribution within Samoa remains standard-rated at 15% and does not qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export entry)
  • Master Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill evidencing international carriage
  • House waybill and bonded truck manifest with GPS-seal log
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a licensed Samoan commercial bank evidencing receipt of the foreign transport consideration
  • Bonded transport licence and customs-approved truck registration documentation
  • Signed service agreement between the WS_IC and the airline, ocean carrier, or customs bonded warehouse licensee

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or government digital invoicing mandate is currently in force in Samoa. Tax invoices must contain the prescribed particulars under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2012, must be issued at the time of supply, and must be retained for the statutory record-keeping period. VAGST returns are filed electronically through the Ministry for Revenue eServices portal. For export cargo, ASYCUDA World requires an electronic export declaration and digital goods release; the bonded truck fleet must additionally maintain a GPS-sealed electronic movement record linking the airport customs control area to the bonded terminal, together with a truck manifest certified by Customs, and no cargo movement is legally cleared without that digital trail.

The Commissioner of the Ministry for Revenue may audit export zero-rating claims retrospectively within the statutory limitation period. If any documentary requirement is missing or the foreign-currency consideration is not repatriated to Samoa within 180 days, the zero-rating is reversed and VAGST becomes payable at 15% on the full supply value, with monthly interest and an administrative shortfall penalty of up to 200% of the avoided tax. Fraudulent or reckless claims additionally trigger forfeiture of the customs security bond, suspension or revocation of the bonded truck fleet licence, director disqualification, and criminal prosecution of the company and its officers under the Tax Administration Act and the Customs Act, with penalties including substantial fines and terms of imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is not freely convertible offshore; CBS maintains a managed float and requires all WST transactions to be routed through licensed domestic commercial banks. Current-account payments (freight, transit fees, fuel, maintenance) are generally approved with documentary support, while capital-account flows (equity injection, loan repatriation, dividend/redemption remittance) require CBS case-by-case authorization and cumulative threshold reporting. The International Company (IC) is classified as a non-resident offshore entity, which exempts it from certain domestic capital controls, but the licensed bank must still evidence underlying economic substance and the authorised purpose of every transfer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Section 18 of the Exchange Control Act and CBS directives, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within six months of shipment, with 100% mandatory conversion into WST unless a CBS-approved foreign-currency retention account is granted. A customs-bonded truck fleet earning USD freight under transhipment contracts may apply for a CBS retention approval to preserve working capital in USD; otherwise all export-related receipts must be surrendered within the statutory period, with the underlying invoice, customs export declaration, signed B/L or CMR, and proof of delivery required for verification by the settling bank.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), administering the Exchange Control Act 1930 (Samoa), the Exchange Control Regulations 1952, and CBS foreign-exchange directives; licensing of banks is separately administered by the CBS under the Financial Institutions Act 1996.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Samoa operates no electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing framework, and CBS has not issued any regulated digital-banking licence to offshore EMIs. Offshore digital payment providers cannot access Samoa's local settlement systems and would require a sponsorship agreement with a licensed domestic bank plus CBS non-objection, which is highly unlikely under current APG mutual-evaluation scrutiny. The treasury design therefore relies on licensed Samoan commercial banks as the primary account and clearing ledger.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: There is no explicit Samoa statutory TP documentation threshold for International Companies; TP rules apply to persons with associated enterprises engaging in cross-border controlled transactions. The Samoa Income Tax Act 2012 incorporates the OECD arm's length principle. Documentation is recommended where aggregate related-party turnover exceeds WST 500,000 or where cross-border intercompany transactions exceed WST 1 million.

Economic substance scope: Samoa is a non-harmful jurisdiction within the OECD Forum; International Companies must demonstrate economic substance if conducting international business. The Customs Bonded Truck Fleet activity requires: (1) physical presence in Samoa – licensed warehouse/garage or contractual access, (2) full-time employees in Samoa managing logistics and fleet, (3) real assets – bonded trucks registered in Samoa or regional fleet management, (4) actual airport feeder operations generating core income. Shell companies with no substance are liable to penalty of WST 100,000 or revocation of licence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to WST 50,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 7 years (Income Tax Act 2012); civil penalty of 100% of tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of evaded tax; travel bans issued for outstanding assessments; imprisonment up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Immediate revocation of IC status; fine up to WST 100,000; automatic termination of tax exemption; directors disqualification for 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for unpaid taxes of the company (up to 3 years of tax); personal restitutions orders; cross-border asset freezing.
AML / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to WST 2 million for corporate entity; licence revocation; asset forfeiture under Proceeds of Crime Act; anti-money laundering compliance orders.

Director personal liability: Directors fined up to WST 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 14 years for willful blindness; personal travel bans; prohibition from holding directorship in any Samoa company.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment to taxable income; penalty of 50% of additional tax liability; late documentation penalty of WST 5,000 per month.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable where they signed false declarations; pecuniary penalties up to WST 10,000; disqualification for 5 years.
