Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), Ministry for Revenue; official online portal: SRA Taxpayer Online Services (https://sra.ws)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated entity must first obtain a Certificate of Incorporation as an International Company (IC) from the Registrar of International Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour. The IC then files SRA Form REG-01 (TIN/VAGST registration application) together with certified constitutional documents, certificate of incorporation, and a local registered agent address. Because the IC is a tax-exempt offshore entity, the SRA issues a Taxpayer Identification Number for administrative and customs purposes; no annual income tax return is required unless the IC undertakes onshore taxable activities in Samoa.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-incorporated entity must first obtain a Certificate of Incorporation as an International Company (IC) from the Registrar of International Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour. The IC then files SRA Form REG-01 (TIN/VAGST registration application) together with certified constitutional documents, certificate of incorporation, and a local registered agent address. Because the IC is a tax-exempt offshore entity, the SRA issues a Taxpayer Identification Number for administrative and customs purposes; no annual income tax return is required unless the IC undertakes onshore taxable activities in Samoa.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not apply the check-the-box classification regime. An International Company may elect to be treated as a tax-exempt offshore company under the International Companies Act 1987, provided it does not conduct business with Samoan residents or derive Samoa-source income. If the IC fails the off-shore conditions, it is taxed at the standard corporate income tax rate of 27%. No hybrid mismatch election is available under Samoa domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 80,000 annual taxable turnover (approx. USD 29,500). Non-resident suppliers without a fixed establishment in Samoa must register for VAGST only if they make taxable supplies in Samoa exceeding this threshold or if they hold goods in Samoa for supply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of tangible goods, including pre-packaged spicy tallow hotpot soup base, qualifies for the 0% VAGST rate where: (i) title and risk in the goods pass to the purchaser outside Samoa; (ii) goods are physically exported within 30 days after supply; (iii) the exporter retains a customs export declaration evidencing departure outside Samoa; and (iv) directly related offshore services provided to a non-resident purchaser are performed mainly outside Samoa.

  • Samoa Customs Export Declaration (CUSEX-01) with SRA clearance reference
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the International Company
  • Bill of lading or airway bill evidencing discharge at a port outside Samoa
  • Certificate of origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT/TT credit advice from the overseas purchaser
  • Export sales contract or purchase order linked to the corresponding commercial invoice

No real-time e-invoicing clearance or fiscal stamping mandate is currently deployed in Samoa. VAGST invoices must include the SRA Taxpayer Identification Number, serial invoice number, date, supplier and recipient names, description of goods, tax-exclusive price, VAGST amount and total consideration. Taxpayers must retain paper or electronic invoices for six years and provide transaction records to the SRA during audit or investigation.

Fraudulent or negligent zero-rating claims are treated as tax evasion under the Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 1992 and the Customs Act 2010. The SRA may assess 100% of tax forgone, levy late payment and evasion penalties up to 150% of the assessed tax, and charge daily interest. Directors and the registered agent may be subject to fines up to WST 100,000 and/or imprisonment. Export zero-rating claims are open to retrospective clawback for up to five years in cases involving false documentation or deliberate misrepresentation.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa maintains a partially convertible currency regime. Current-account payments, including trade settlement and service fees, are routinely processed through authorised dealers with underlying documentary evidence. Capital-account transactions, such as non-resident direct investment, equity injections, shareholder loans, and portfolio transactions, require CBS approval unless expressly exempted under the International Companies Act 1987. Profit and dividend remittance for an International Company is generally permissible where the underlying income is not sourced in Samoa, but the clearing bank will require a board resolution authorising the distribution, audited or management accounts, an IC certificate of good standing, and, where relevant, a tax clearance issued by the Ministry for Revenue. Practical hurdles include CBS verification of purpose codes, anti-money-laundering source-of-funds checks, and the absence of freely tradable offshore WST markets.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters must obtain an Exchange Control licence or exemption and route all export proceeds through an authorised dealer in Samoa. The statutory expectation is that full invoice proceeds are repatriated within 30 days from the date of shipment or export, unless a specific extension or retention approval is granted by the CBS. There is no blanket fixed percentage surrender ratio for non-traditional manufactured goods such as packaged hotpot soup base; the authorised dealer will convert into WST any portion required to meet local tax, license, payroll, and operational obligations. Foreign currency retention is permitted only with CBS approval and must be supported by forecast import or trade-payment needs. Direct offshore collection without repatriation to Samoa is a compliance breach and will trigger exchange-control, banking, and tax scrutiny.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1977, Exchange Control Regulations, and the Central Bank Act 1984. The CBS licenses authorised dealers and supervises all inward/outward remittances, foreign currency accounts, and trade settlement documentation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally non-structural. Offshore fintech EMIs and digital banks are not licensed or authorised by the Central Bank of Samoa and cannot hold WST or clear Samoan import/export documents. They may be used only as a payment overlay or referral channel, but the legal treasury account, SWIFT clearing, exchange-control filing, and LC issuance must sit with a CBS-licensed commercial bank. Use of an unauthorised EMI for settlement of Samoan production, logistics, or export receipts would create a licensing violation and undermine the exchange-control audit trail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must be received by wire transfer through the licensed Samoa bank's correspondent network. The remitting bank should use SWIFT MT103 with a precise purpose code and structured remittance reference such as 'WS/IC/CAP/2025/001'. The payment message must identify the Samoa International Company account, name the ultimate remitter, and cite the underlying board resolution. The receiving bank will require the matching board resolution, capital subscription agreement, and source-of-funds evidence before crediting the account. Purpose codes must be selected as 'Capital Contribution' rather than 'Trade Settlement' to avoid misclassification under exchange control. No crypto or non-bank channels may be used. If the share capital is denominated in USD or CNY, the conversion to WST or retention as foreign currency must be agreed with the bank before receipt.All supplier and trade payments for pre-packaged hotpot soup base bricks should be executed through SWIFT MT103 for open-account settlements or through a commercial letter of credit (MT700/MT707) where contractual security is required. The export distribution company should request each Samoan bank to issue documentary LCs against warehouse receipts, factory inspection certificates, health certificates, and shipping documents. For wholesale seasoning and edible-fat product distribution, the bank will require the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading/air waybill, and product safety certification. Payments to Chinese and regional suppliers may be cleared in USD, EUR, CNY through the correspondent bank, but WST conversion must be done only at the Samoan authorised dealer.Outright FX forwards with CBS-licensed authorised dealer banks for matching USD/WST trade and dividend flows; requires documented underlying trade exposure.FX options for trade or working-capital hedging, subject to CBS/authorised dealer approval and completion of an economic-need declaration.Cross-currency swaps between USD and AUD/NZD/EUR for liquidity matching, permitted only when tied to identifiable trade liabilities and approved by the Central Bank of Samoa.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No express statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to an exempt International Company. Samoa does not maintain mandatory Local File or Master File rules for ICs. However, the Income Tax Act 2014 authorizes the Commissioner to adjust non-arm's-length prices between related parties. In practice, an IC engaged in export distribution of pre-packaged hotpot soup base should maintain contemporaneous intercompany agreements, pricing policies, and margin benchmarks in WST/USD to evidence arm's-length conduct, even though a documented threshold is not prescribed.

Economic substance scope: Samoa International Companies are not subject to the Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as those imposed by the EU Code of Conduct Group or OECD peer-reviewed jurisdictions. Samoa nonetheless requires licensed registered agents and ICs to demonstrate economic presence (registered office, registered agent, and adequate records) for AML and international standards purposes. An IC distributing hotpot soup base should maintain physical board minutes, bank accounts, and transactional records in Samoa or accessible to the registered agent. Pure shell status without any decision-making or record-keeping can expose the entity to director and shareholder liability under anti-abuse doctrines and international tax transparency frameworks.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration in Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act 1988 and Income Tax Act 2014, fines up to WST 100,000 and cancellation of International Company status; reassessment of Samoan-source income at the headline 27% corporate rate (or applicable prevailing rate) plus interest at statutory late-payment rates.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false declarations are liable for restitution of unpaid taxes, civil penalties, and possible imprisonment of up to 7 years. Travel bans and asset freezing orders can be issued where the Commissioner or Attorney General credibly alleges dissipation of assets.
Shell Company / Sham Transaction

Statutory sanction: Under anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing laws, the Central Bank of Samoa and SIFSA may revoke the registered agent's license, strike the IC from the registry, levy fines of up to WST 200,000, and report the entity to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where the IC is a façade or agent for its shareholder, rendering directors and shadow directors personally liable for creditors' claims, restitution of secret profits, and, for predicate offenses such as money laundering, imprisonment up to 14 years.
AML Deficiency (Unlicensed Activity / Dirty Funds)

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering and Prevention of Terrorism Financing Act 2007, a conviction for money laundering attracts fines and imprisonment of up to 14 years; corporate fines are unlimited at the court's discretion. The IC's bank accounts and assets in Samoa may be frozen or confiscated under proceeds-of-crime provisions.

Director personal liability: Each director who authorizes, acquiesces to, or negligently facilitates a dirty money flow is individually criminally liable. The court may issue restitution orders, travel bans, shipping bans, and prison sentences; professional directors acting as puppets are treated as principals where they had knowledge of the criminal origin.
Failure to Maintain Mandatory Records / Registered Agent Duties

Statutory sanction: SIFSA may impose a penalty of WST 100 per day of non-compliance, suspend the IC's status, and the registered agent may be fined up to WST 50,000. The IC may be struck off the register after a final notice.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for filing deficiencies and for any taxes, penalties, or interest arising from the inability to reconstruct income. Persistent deliberate non-compliance can lead to a director disqualification order and a personal fine of WST 20,000 per violation.
Excessive Interest / Royalty Stripping to Shift Profits

Statutory sanction: Samoa's Income Tax Act 2014 thin-capitalization and transfer-pricing adjustment rules allow the Commissioner to disallow excessive interest deductions, recharacterize the payment as a dividend, and impose withholding tax at 15% plus penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors who implement artificial pricing structures with knowledge of the tax avoidance purpose are personally liable for the corporate tax shortfall under anti-avoidance provisions, subject to civil recovery proceedings, travel bans, and, where the arrangement involved fraud or forgery, imprisonment up to 7 years.
