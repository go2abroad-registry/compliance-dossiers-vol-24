Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of Samoa (Samoa Inland Revenue Service); official portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws with Taxpayer Online Services

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity or Samoa International Company without local-source activity is generally not required to register for income tax; however, a TIN may be obtained voluntarily. The applicant submits a completed registration form (TR-1), certified constitutional documents, registered office certificate, particulars of directors/secretary, and proof of physical address to the Ministry for Revenue. Approval is issued within 5–10 business days. VAT registration is required separately if the entity imports goods into Samoa, supplies goods/services in Samoa, or expects annual taxable turnover to exceed WST 200,000.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa International Companies may elect tax-exempt status under the International Companies Act 1987 for all offshore business activities; local-source income remains taxable on a territorial basis. No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election exists; hybrid mismatch rules in the Tax Administration Act 2012 apply for cross-border arrangements. An IC may elect voluntary VAGST registration for input VAT recovery, but this is limited to local taxable activities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 200,000 annual taxable turnover; compulsory registration once exceeded, voluntary registration available below threshold for persons carrying on a taxable activity

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated when they are exported from Samoa within 28 days (or an approved extended period) under customs control, sold to a customer outside Samoa, and supported by documentary evidence of export. Services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident and the benefit of the services is received outside Samoa, including offshore services relating to export goods. The supplier must retain all evidence proving the export occurred and the consideration was received in Samoa.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CUS/ASYCUDA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Foreign Exchange Receipt
  • Original or certified copy of International Company registration certificate
  • VAGST export evidence documentation (e.g., VAGST Form 14 or equivalent)

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control mandate is currently implemented in Samoa. VAGST invoices must contain prescribed particulars and records must be maintained within Samoa. Customs declarations are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World. No mandatory e-invoicing API clearance is required for exports or domestic supplies, although cross-border digital services may be subject to reverse charge for registered business recipients.

Export zero-rating claims are audited by the Ministry for Revenue. Inability to substantiate export, delivery, or repatriation of foreign exchange within 180 days triggers reassessment at 15% VAGST on the full export value. Late payment penalties of up to 20% of the assessed tax and default interest apply. Fraudulent or reckless claims may incur civil penalties up to 200% of the tax avoided and criminal prosecution under the Tax Administration Act 2012; directors and officers are jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, so the Tālā (WST) is freely convertible for current-account payments by residents; the CBS manages the WST against a trade-weighted basket of major partner currencies. Capital-account transactions (external loans, securities investment, profit and capital repatriation) require prior CBS exchange-control approval when the deploying entity is a Samoa-resident company. The WS_IC, as a licensed International Company under the International Companies Act 1987, is statutorily treated as non-resident for exchange control, enjoying unrestricted current- and capital-account convertibility: share subscriptions may be applied in foreign currency, dividends and redemption proceeds may be remitted to non-resident members without CBS licensing, and no Samoan withholding tax is imposed on IC distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Samoa-resident exporters, the Exchange Control Regulations require that proceeds be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days of shipment and surrendered to a CBS-licensed commercial bank for Tālā within 30 days of realization, unless an individual exemption applies. A licensed International Company (WS_IC) is outside this resident regime: export proceeds from silicone fidget-toy sales may be retained indefinitely in USD or other convertible currencies, may be applied directly toward foreign supplier payments and platform-pay charges, and are subject to no mandatory conversion ratio or surrender timeline.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) — the statutory monetary authority exercising exchange-control administration, commercial-bank licensing, and AML/CFT supervision under the Central Bank of Samoa Act, the Exchange Control Act 1973 and the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007; IC licensing administration is shared with the Samoa International Financial Services Centre within the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Practical rating: LOW-to-MEDIUM. Established global EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer and Revolut Business) generally do not support a Samoa-incorporated International Company as a primary treasury counterparty; the tax-exempt offshore classification automatically triggers enhanced due diligence and most EMI platforms omit Samoa from corporate onboarding menus. Platform-Pay marketplaces may, however, disburse sales settlements through licensed payments facilitators into the IC's CBS-licensed bank account. Legal constraint: the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and CBS CDD guidance require the IC to maintain a registered office, a local registered agent, and a natural-person compliance point in Samoa — a branchless EMI cannot discharge those structural obligations, so an EMI/acquiring-facilitator may serve only as a collection conduit, not as the liquidity ledger.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% standard Samoa resident corporate rate; 0% for International Companies under the International Companies Act 1987, section 220 exemption

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for International Companies; dividends paid by an exempt Samoa International Company to non-resident shareholders are statutorily exempt from Samoa withholding tax. The standard Samoa resident company dividend rate is 15%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Samoa transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to an exempt International Company. If the IC were reclassified as taxable, Samoa domestic law does not prescribe a specific WST turnover threshold for Local File or Master File; OECD-aligned documentation is expected for controlled transactions exceeding WST 3 million on an annual aggregated basis.

Economic substance scope: Samoa applies Economic Substance Regulations to International Companies carrying on relevant activities under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2023. Physical manufacturing and distribution of silicone stress-relief fidgets are high-substance activities; the IC must maintain board meeting minutes, employment records, physical premises, and expenditure records in Samoa demonstrating adequate direction and management. A simplified annual ESR notification is required.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / abuse of International Company exemption

Statutory sanction: Up to 5 years imprisonment, a fine of WST 250,000, and recovery of avoided tax plus a civil penalty of up to 200% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and the registered agent who knowingly certified tax-exempt status may be held personally liable for the unpaid tax, subjected to asset preservation orders, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 5 years.
Shell company / artificial substance arrangement under ESR

Statutory sanction: Automatic strike-off and dissolution, financial penalty equal to WST 60,000 or 10% of relevant income, whichever is higher, and referral to the Samoa International Financial Services Authority for license revocation.

Director personal liability: The court can pierce the corporate veil and declare directors personally liable for all forfeited income and penalties; travel bans may be issued until the penalties are satisfied.
AML / Counter-Terrorism Financing non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Fine up to WST 3 million and imprisonment up to 15 years under the Samoa AML/CFT Act 2018; suspension of the International Company's bank accounts and forfeiture of the funds.

Director personal liability: Senior managers and directors face up to 15 years imprisonment, personal asset seizure, and placement on the regional financial watchlist with preventive travel restrictions.
Failure to maintain current registered office and registered agent

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine of WST 5,000; the company cannot maintain bank accounts or execute contracts; continued default causes strike-off and forfeiture of company assets to the Crown.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for all liabilities incurred after termination of the registered agent, including trade debts, bank overdrafts, and shipping costs, with the possibility of bankruptcy proceedings.
Transfer pricing manipulation of cross-border inventory sales

Statutory sanction: If the Samoa GAAR is invoked, the Commissioner may disregard the contractual price, reallocate the profit at the 27% corporate rate, impose a 50% adjustment penalty, and charge monthly interest at 2%.

Director personal liability: Directors who designed or approved the mispricing may be assessed personally for the tax shortfall and penalties, and migration restrictions may be imposed on any director exiting through Apia airport while a liability certificate is outstanding.
