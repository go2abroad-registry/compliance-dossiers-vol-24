Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of Samoa (formerly Samoa Revenue Authority) – operational tax and customs administration under the Tax Administration Act 2015; electronic services portal: tax.gov.ws; customs declarations via ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is not automatically issued a TIN. The IC must first be incorporated and licensed by the Samoa International Financial Authority (SIFA). To obtain a Samoan TIN/VAT ID for any limited domestic fiscal interaction, the IC files the business registration form with the Ministry for Revenue together with the Certificate of Incorporation, the SIFA International Company licence, registered agent details, and certified constitutional documents; a TIN is issued within 5-10 working days. The IC remains outside the normal income tax net and is not required to file Samoan income tax returns while validly licensed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under Samoan law. The IC tax-exempt status is statutory and automatic under the International Companies Act 1987; the only election is to revoke/convert the entity into a domestic company under the Companies Act 2001, which would trigger full Samoan income tax and VAGST exposure.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 60,000 annual taxable turnover; however, a licensed International Company engaging exclusively in offshore business is exempt from VAGST and is not required to register unless it also makes taxable supplies of goods or services in Samoa.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of jewellery and timepieces are zero-rated under the VAGST Act 2008 where the goods are physically exported from Samoa within 28 days of supply and the supplier retains proof of export. Supplies of offshore services by an International Company to non-residents are outside the scope of Samoan VAGST entirely. Zero-rating is available only to a VAGST-registered supplier; an exempt IC is not entitled to claim input credits.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged via ASYCUDA World to the Samoa Customs Service
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Samoa
  • Commercial invoice with full description and HS Chapter 71/91 classification of jewellery and timepieces
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign exchange record from a licensed bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based invoicing, or digital stamping for VAGST. Tax invoices may be issued in paper or secure PDF format and must contain the supplier's TIN, invoice number, date, supplier and recipient details, description of goods, and the VAGST amount shown separately. No government e-invoicing clearance platform is in operational service.

The Ministry for Revenue audits zero-rated export claims against ASYCUDA customs records, bank remittance evidence, and transport documents. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims attract an assessment for the full VAGST evaded plus a minimum evasion penalty of 50% and up to 200% of the tax shortfall, late-payment penalties of 5% per month on outstanding amounts, and criminal liability including fines and imprisonment for directors under the Tax Administration Act 2015. Retrospective clawback extends to all related input credit claims and may disqualify the exporter from future zero-rating privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tala (WST) operates under a managed convertibility framework. Current-account transactions are freely supported through CBS-authorised dealer banks, while capital-account transfers, including shareholder injections and profit remittances, require prior CBS approval or an authorised dealer's exchange-control consent. Profit remittance hurdles include documentary proof of the International Company's tax-exempt status, original financial statements, and confirmation that no tax obligations are outstanding in Samoa. Offshore convertibility of WST is limited; settlement is practically effected through correspondent banks of the local authorised dealer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export receipts from online jewelry and timepiece sales must be repatriated to a CBS-authorised dealer bank within 180 days of shipment unless an exemption is granted for an approved offshore account. The default statutory requirement requires that 100% of the export proceeds be converted into WST at the authorised dealer rate; however, a deviation to retain receipts in USD may be approved if the International Company demonstrates a direct operational need for foreign currency to pay non-resident suppliers and e-commerce platform fees.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Samoa has no dedicated fintech EMI or payment-institution licensing category; only CBS-licensed commercial banks may perform foreign exchange, money transmission, and customer credit operations. Offshore digital EMIs cannot independently clear WST or hold local settlement accounts, and EU/UK passporting does not extend to Samoa. A foreign EMI may be viable only through a white-label partnership with a local licensed bank acting as sponsor and clearing gateway.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Company holding a valid Tax Exemption Certificate on all foreign-source income; standard domestic Samoan corporate income tax rate of 25% applies only to Samoa-source trading income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% withholding tax on dividends and other profit distributions paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders when the company is tax-exempt. Domestic Samoan source dividends paid to resident shareholders are statutorily subject to a 15% dividend withholding tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 1,500,000 (approx. US$580,000) aggregate controlled transactions in a fiscal year; this threshold is not applicable to International Companies that maintain a valid Tax Exemption Certificate and exclusively derive foreign-source income. A domestic taxable entity crossing the threshold must prepare Local File and Master File in accordance with Samoa's Income Tax Act framework.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations 2021, as amended, impose a substance test on International Companies undertaking relevant activities, including 'distribution and service centre' business. Online retailing of fashion jewelry and watches from a Samoa-registered International Company using overseas distribution hubs is characterized as a relevant activity. The IC must demonstrate Samoan economic direction, maintain a physical registered office in Samoa, hold at least one board meeting in Samoa in each financial year, keep adequate accounting records in Samoa or in a form accessible in Samoa, and justify that booking, procurement, and customer contracting decisions have Samoan involvement. A purely passive letterbox arrangement without substance will fail the Economic Substance Regulations test, nullify the exemption, and expose the company to sanctions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False Exemption Status

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the Tax Exemption Certificate; full assessment of undeclared profits at 25% corporate income tax; an evasion penalty of 200% of the tax evaded; and an administrative fine of up to WST 500,000. The company may also be struck off the International Companies Register.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorized or recklessly allowed the false exemption claim are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of the unpaid tax and penalties. The court may issue a travel ban preventing departure from Samoa, and convicting directors may face imprisonment of up to 14 years under the Crimes Act 2013 on charges of tax fraud or false declaration.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of up to WST 250,000 for each non-compliant financial year; in addition, the Registrar may strike off the International Company without prior notice; tax exemption is retroactively forfeited and a corporate fine of WST 100,000 applies.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from serving as directors of any Samoa company for up to 5 years, required to personally refund annual fee savings obtained through the sham arrangement, and subject to a 12-month travel ban pending the outcome of strike-off proceedings. Imprisonment is generally not triggered for substance failure alone, but contempt of a Registrar's production order carries a custodial sentence of up to 2 years.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to WST 1,000,000 under the Money Laundering and Prevention of Terrorism Financing Act 2021; ordering of forfeiture of the assets or their equivalent value; revocation of the registered agent's license.

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed compliance officer may be criminally fined up to WST 250,000 individually, issued with travel bans pending investigation, and imprisoned for up to 7 years for money laundering; application of asset freezing orders to personal bank accounts is common in cross-border Swiss and Hong Kong banking investigations.
Disguised Profit Repatriation and Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment recharacterizing such payments as dividends; disallowance of all artificial deductions; assessment at the full 25% corporate tax rate on deemed distributions; imposition of penal interest at 6% per annum; and a 15% withholding tax on the recharacterized dividend to the foreign shareholder.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the sham agreements are liable to repay the amount of the tax adjustment out of their own assets where the company lacks funds, cannot obtain discharge in bankruptcy under Samoan company law, and may be canceled from future directorship positions. In egregious cases where bank records are falsified, directors face imprisonment of up to 10 years for false accounting.
