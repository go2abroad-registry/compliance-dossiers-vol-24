Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA); official portal: https://sra.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987 via the Samoa Registrar of Companies (MCIL) and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation. A wholly tax-exempt IC is not registered for income tax purposes and does not obtain a TIN from SRA solely for offshore activity. If the IC enters the domestic tax net (e.g. local payroll, lease, or importation), the registered agent must submit the SRA TIN application and VAGST registration form through the SRA online services portal, attaching certified constitutional documents, identity documents of directors, and the registered-office certificate. The statutory registration threshold for VAGST is WS$100,000 of taxable supplies; below this threshold, registration is voluntary but requires SRA approval.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Foreign entities incorporate an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987 via the Samoa Registrar of Companies (MCIL) and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation. A wholly tax-exempt IC is not registered for income tax purposes and does not obtain a TIN from SRA solely for offshore activity. If the IC enters the domestic tax net (e.g. local payroll, lease, or importation), the registered agent must submit the SRA TIN application and VAGST registration form through the SRA online services portal, attaching certified constitutional documents, identity documents of directors, and the registered-office certificate. The statutory registration threshold for VAGST is WS$100,000 of taxable supplies; below this threshold, registration is voluntary but requires SRA approval.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box or equivalent deemed-entity election. The International Companies Act 1987 provides the sole classification election: registration as an International Company automatically confers statutory tax-exempt status, with no ability to 'check out' of the exemption. The Act also permits election to become a protected cell company (PCC) or a company limited by guarantee, but these forms do not alter the tax-exempt characterization. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available to Samoa ICs; Samoa's Income Tax Act contains CFC and potentially anti-hybrid rules that apply independently of entity status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WS$100,000 annual taxable supplies in Samoa Tala

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAGST-registered person may zero-rate (i) goods physically exported from Samoa and evidenced by customs export entry; (ii) services supplied to a non-resident and consumed wholly outside Samoa; (iii) international transport and related services; and (iv) supplies to prescribed offshore enterprises. An International Company engaged solely in offshore re-invoicing typically does not make a supply in Samoa and therefore falls outside the VAGST net; zero-rating may be claimed only if the entity is registered for VAGST and the supplies clear Samoan customs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic printout)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill consigned to the offshore buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT payment advice proving settlement
  • Original commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of Origin (where applicable)

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate is in force for domestic VAGST invoices. VAGST-registered businesses must issue serially numbered tax invoices and retain records for SRA inspection. Import and export declarations are processed through ASYCUDA World customs automation; no separate SRA e-invoicing API is required.

The SRA cross-matches ASYCUDA export declarations with VAGST returns and banking records. False or unsupported zero-rating claims result in an immediate return to 15% VAGST liability, clawback of any refund, interest, a late payment penalty at 2% per month, a civil shortfall penalty up to 100% of tax, and criminal prosecution for knowingly presenting false documents (fine of up to WS$100,000 and/or imprisonment). Exchange-control penalties and deregistration of the International Company also apply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa tala (WST) is not freely convertible. Current account payments for bona fide trade transactions are permissible through licensed authorized dealers subject to supporting documentation. Capital account transfers, including equity injections, intercompany loan advances, and non-resident profit remittances, require prior exchange control approval or must be effected through a CBS-licensed commercial bank under delegated authority. Profit remittance hurdles include proof of source of funds, tax clearance certification, corporate KYC documentation, and compliance with Samoa's withholding tax and annual filing obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa exchange control regulations require exporters to surrender export proceeds to an authorized dealer within a statutory timeline, normally not exceeding 90 days from shipment or receipt, unless a specific exemption is granted by the Central Bank of Samoa. Mandatory conversion into WST applies to export receipts unless the exporter is approved to retain designated foreign currency balances for operational trade settlement. All export transactions must be documented on the prescribed CBS exchange control declaration or customs export form, and the physical trade and invoice trail must be retained for verification.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), operating under the Central Bank Act 2001 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1978

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs are not viable as the primary or sole banking channel for a Samoa International Company because Samoa exchange control, the CBS supervisory framework, and WST settlement requirements mandate the use of a domestic authorized dealer for regulated financial services. An offshore EMI may be considered only as a secondary treasury concentration vehicle for non-WST receivables, provided that the entity submits to a robust independent AML review, maintains transaction transparency, and keeps the primary clearing corridor through a CBS-licensed bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Tax-exempt offshore company structure with non-resident ownership and re-invoicing trade flows

Root cause: Tax-exempt offshore company structure with non-resident ownership and re-invoicing trade flows

Defense evidence: Independent legal opinion on the Samoa International Company formation, current certificate of incorporation and certificate of good standing, board resolutions establishing the re-invoicing trade hub, economic substance or tax transparency memorandum, full beneficial ownership register, corporate organizational chart, and a narrative flow-of-funds document describing each reinvoicing leg and its commercial purpose.
High-velocity invoice transactions between related or affiliated trading parties

Root cause: High-velocity invoice transactions between related or affiliated trading parties

Defense evidence: Master trade agreements, individual purchase orders, supplier invoices, customer invoices, shipping and logistics documents such as bills of lading and customs certificates, inspections and quality certificates, insurance certificates, detailed transaction-level audit trail, and an explanation of the commercial mark-up and operating expenses supporting the reinvoicing margin.
Sanctions and jurisdiction risk flags on counterparties or payment channels

Root cause: Sanctions and jurisdiction risk flags on counterparties or payment channels

Defense evidence: Sanctions screening reports for all counterparties and ultimate beneficial owners, denial and debarment list checks, adverse media review, proof of source of wealth, negative PEP declarations, bank reference letters, and evidence of compliance with Samoa's AML/CFT statutory obligations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions must be routed through a CBS-licensed authorized dealer bank in Samoa using SWIFT MT103 cash transfers or, where appropriate, MT202 cover payments. The SWIFT field 70 narrative should clearly state 'Share Capital Contribution' or 'Increase in Paid-Up Capital' and reference the relevant corporate resolution. Prior exchange control approval must be obtained where the amount exceeds prescribed thresholds or where the capital transfer is treated as a non-resident inward direct investment. The onboarding bank must receive the corporate KYC pack, source-of-funds certification, bank statements of the remitting entity, notarized board resolution, and confirmation of the capital account entry in the Samoa International Company's statutory ledger.Outbound trade disbursements for suppliers and trade creditors should be executed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfers through the Samoa bank's correspondent banking network. For larger and more structured trade settlements, confirmed irrevocable commercial letters of credit opened by the Samoa bank are preferred, supported by transport documents, commercial invoices, and insurance certificates. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% resident company rate under the Income Tax Act 2012; effective headline rate of 0% for an International Company (IC) on foreign-source offshore income by statutory exemption.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory dividend withholding tax applies to non-resident shareholders under the Income Tax Act 2012; the effective rate is 0% on dividends declared by an exempt International Company out of foreign-source reserves, provided the IC does not carry on business in Samoa.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No express monetary threshold is published in the Income Tax Act 2012; the arm's length principle applies to all controlled transactions. The practical administrative documentation threshold for related-party re-invoicing flows is WST 1,000,000 per annum; contemporaneous benchmarking, transfer pricing policy documentation, and records must be maintained in English for 7 years.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 impose a substance test on ICs conducting 'relevant activities'; a re-invoicing hub falls under 'distribution and service centre' where revenue is derived from goods or services supplied to or purchased from group companies. The IC must show: (1) core income-generating activities are directed and managed in Samoa; (2) adequate physical premises are maintained in Samoa; (3) adequate full-time or equivalent employees are engaged in Samoa; and (4) adequate operating expenditure is incurred in Samoa. An annual economic substance declaration must be filed with MCIL; failure attracts a penalty of up to WST 30,000, additional daily surcharges for continuing default, and automatic exchange of information notification to the beneficial owner's home tax authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abuse of IC Exemption

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of undeclared profits at 27% CIT; default interest of 1% per month; civil penalty of up to 200% of tax evaded; deregistration and strike-off under the International Companies Act 1987.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for taxes where the entity is operated as a sham or cloak; criminal conviction on indictment attracts a fine of up to WST 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 2 years; the court may impose travel bans and asset freezing orders.
Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to WST 30,000 plus daily surcharges for continued non-compliance; mandatory exchange of information to the ultimate parent jurisdiction; deregistration by the Registrar of International Companies.

Director personal liability: Directors face disqualification from acting as directors of any Samoa entity for up to 5 years; personal fines for knowingly filing a false substance declaration; information exchange may trigger foreign prosecution for tax evasion in the home jurisdiction.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: SIRD may adjust taxable income to arm's length levels; penalty of up to 50% of the tax avoided; late payment interest applied from the original due date; possible suspension of the taxpayer's right to object to the assessment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign or approve false transfer pricing accounts are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties; fraudulent intent may lead to criminal prosecution with imprisonment of up to 7 years.
AML / CFT Violations and Beneficial Ownership Defaults

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to WST 1,000,000 under the Samoa AML/CFT Act 2018; licence revocation and strike-off; freezing of assets under regulatory orders; inclusion of the entity on an international sanctions or high-risk notification list.

Director personal liability: Individuals convicted of money laundering face imprisonment of up to 14 years; directors may be subject to personal fines, travel bans, and confiscation orders; breach of the beneficial ownership filing duty triggers personal liability for continuing daily penalties.
