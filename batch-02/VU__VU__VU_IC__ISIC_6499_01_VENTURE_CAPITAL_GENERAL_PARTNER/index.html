Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official e-services and VAT return filing via the DCIR Online VAT Portal (https://dcir.gov.vu); separate licensing/registration of International Companies is administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC).

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap 222] is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu income tax, withholding tax, capital gains tax, and stamp duty. Consequently, no domestic Tax Identification Number is mandatory for standard GP offshore operations. Procedure for any optional DCIR registration: (1) identify whether the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu (an offshore management fee invoiced to a non-resident venture fund is not a taxable supply); (2) if VAT registration is nonetheless required or voluntarily desired, file Form VR-1 with DCIR accompanied by the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent confirmation, minutes of directors' resolution, and local business address proof; (3) DCIR issues a VAT registration number and VAT certificate. For CRS/FATCA purposes, the IC, if considered a financial institution in its capacity as investment manager, must register with the Vanuatu Competent Authority through the DCIR CRS portal and obtain a Global Intermediary Identification Number (GIIN) with the IRS where required. No business license is payable by an IC in Vanuatu.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu domestic law contains no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election mechanism. Section 68 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222] confers automatic and unconditional exemption from any tax or duty imposed on companies, including income tax and stamp duty, so long as the IC is validly registered and does not carry on business with Vanuatu residents (except to obtain professional services). For U.S. federal income tax purposes of the venture capital GP, the IC may elect on IRS Form 8832 to be disregarded/treated as a partnership in order to achieve U.S. pass-through structure; absent such election the IC is treated as a corporation. No Vanuatu statutory election is required or available to vary the IC's exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 in any 12-month period of taxable supplies; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold; supplies made by an IC to non-residents are exported services outside the VAT net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under sections 15–17 of the VAT Act [Cap 247], a supply is zero-rated where the supplier receives consideration and the goods are exported by the supplier, or the services are supplied to a non-resident and are reasonably regarded as supplied outside Vanuatu (exported services). A Vanuatu IC acting as general partner/investment manager of a non-resident venture capital fund will qualify for zero-rating on management and carried-interest related advisory fees provided the services are performed for and consumed by the offshore fund outside Vanuatu, invoicing is directed to the non-resident fund, and consideration is received in foreign currency. Input VAT incurred locally (e.g., travel, professional services) is generally non-creditable for an exempt IC unless the IC is registered and makes taxable/zero-rated supplies; if the IC chooses registration, it may claim refunds of creditable input VAT on the export of services, subject to DCIR verification.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Entry) lodged with the Vanuatu Customs Service (DCIR) for any physical goods exported
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing departure of goods from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice together with the underlying investment management agreement / limited partnership agreement
  • Inward remittance or proof of payment such as SWIFT MT103, bank credit advice, or fund capital-call funding evidence in support of the export consideration
  • VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and registered agent confirmation establishing the IC's exempt offshore status

No real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping regime exists in Vanuatu. There is no mandatory e-invoicing platform. Taxpayers may issue paper or electronic invoices in the prescribed form; such invoices and supporting records must be retained for at least 7 years for DCIR audit purposes. The zero-rated export claim is substantiated via documentary evidence retained by the IC rather than through any electronic government clearance mechanism.

Vanuatu operates no exchange-control legislation and imposes no statutory foreign-currency repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark reflects prudent compliance practice for export consideration substantiation. DCIR may audit zero-rated export claims and, where the export status is not adequately documented, recharacterize supplies at 12.5% VAT with retrospective assessment. Civil penalties for incorrect returns, under-assessment, or evasion range up to 200% of the tax evaded plus interest of 1% per month on unpaid VAT. Criminal liability for fraudulent zero-rating claims or knowingly providing false information to DCIR attracts fines up to VT 2,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 10 years under the VAT Act; for an International Company, breach of the International Companies Act trading restrictions can additionally lead to VFSC deregistration and immediate loss of the statutory tax exemption, with all previously exempt income becoming assessable to the extent permitted by law, and directors/agents subject to sanctions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for current and capital account transactions. International Companies may maintain foreign-currency accounts in USD, AUD, EUR and other major currencies, and may repatriate dividends, capital, interest, management fees, and liquidation proceeds without RBV approval. No withholding tax on outward remittances applies to Vanuatu International Companies. The only affirmative obligations are anti-money-laundering recordkeeping, beneficial ownership reporting under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2020, and sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender rule, no minimum conversion ratio, and no fixed time period for export proceeds in Vanuatu. Exporters are not required to convert foreign currency receipts into VUV or to remit them to a domestic account. Any repatriated proceeds flow through normal banking channels and remain subject only to standard customer due diligence and AML monitoring. This regime is immaterial to the venture-capital general partner treasury model.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 253]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) supervises International Companies and licensing, while the RBV administers banking and payment-system oversight. Vanuatu maintains no active exchange-control permit regime for International Companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, Revolut Business) are operationally viable as secondary disbursement rails for USD/EUR payments, but they cannot replace a licensed VFSC-supervised Vanuatu bank relationship. Legal constraints: a Vanuatu International Company must not hold out as a bank, money-service business, or EMI in Vanuatu; investor capital should not be parked solely on an unregulated foreign EMI because this triggers enhanced money-laundering risk, negative shell-company indicators, and correspondent de-risking. Recommended use is limited to small operating payments and FX execution, not as the principal custodial account for capital calls.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no dividend tax, and no remittance tax on International Companies (ICs).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or capital distribution to shareholders, whether resident or non-resident.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable - Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation, no thin capitalization rules, and no statutory arm's length documentation thresholds because the income tax base does not exist. No Local File, Master File, or CbCR is required domestically.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act No. 37 of 2022 applies fully. For a venture capital general partner, 'fund management' is a relevant activity. The IC must: (a) be managed and controlled in Vanuatu, with board meetings substantively held in-country; (b) have adequate qualified employees physically present in Vanuatu; (c) incur adequate local operating expenditure proportionate to income; and (d) maintain physical premises. A standalone economic substance return must be filed annually with VFSC. Penalties for non-compliance reach 2,000,000 VUV plus daily default fines, and the VFSC may strike off, disqualify directors, and require cessation of relevant activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Violation of the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act; VFSC may impose financial penalties up to 2,000,000 VUV, suspend the IC's registration, order strike-off, and recommend criminal prosecution for false economic substance declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for economic substance penalties; disqualification from managing any Vanuatu company for up to five years; court-ordered civil restitution of benefits; foreign directors may face travel bans upon entering Vanuatu and extradition risk for fraud.
Tax Evasion / Facilitation of Foreign Tax Abuse

Statutory sanction: Common law fraud, offenses under the Vanuatu Penal Code, and the anti-avoidance measures of foreign treaty partners; financial penalties of up to 10,000,000 VUV for serious economic crime, forfeiture of proceeds, and corporate dissolution.

Director personal liability: Piercing of the corporate veil where the IC is used for fraud or evasion; directors face civil restitution, custodial sentences up to 12 years under the Penal Code for conspiracy to defraud, travel bans, and restraint orders over personal and corporate assets.
Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Offenses under the Companies and International Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Act; monetary penalties per day of default and criminal conviction; refusal to register any further ICs or transactions; VFSC administrative strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent are each liable; imprisonment up to 15 years for knowingly providing false beneficial ownership information; travel bans pending investigation; disqualification from holding directorship in Vanuatu.
AML/CFT Failure (Medium-Risk Enhanced)

Statutory sanction: Offenses under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act No. 21 of 2019, as amended; fines up to 30,000,000 VUV for corporate entities, suspension of the IC's operations, freezing orders, and potential designation as a high-risk financial business.

Director personal liability: Individual fines for responsible officers; imprisonment up to 20 years for willful blindness or money laundering; personal liability to investors for consequential losses; confiscation of director personal assets derived from the illicit flows; travel bans and extradition exposure.
Unlicensed Financial Business Conduct

Statutory sanction: Carrying on a regulated financial business without a VFSC license is unlawful under relevant Vanuatu financial services legislation and the International Companies Act; cease-and-desist orders, penalty levies per day of operation, forfeiture of fees charged, and criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize unlicensed activity are personally liable for all investor losses; fines and imprisonment up to 10 years; exclusion orders from the Vanuatu financial sector; civil suits by investors with reversed burden of proof on the director's knowledge.
