Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue, Samoa (Inland Revenue Division); online portal: eRevenue / Samoa Tax Administration System (TAS)

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC): (1) incorporate with the Samoa International Companies Office (ICO); (2) file the certificate of incorporation and a certified registry extract with the Inland Revenue Division to obtain a Samoan Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for administrative purposes; (3) if the IC commences a taxable activity in Samoa or supplies taxable goods/services to Samoan residents, register for Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) within 21 days of commencement of activity or once the WST 80,000 registration threshold is met; (4) an IC that remains wholly offshore and does not trade with Samoan residents may be treated as a nil-assessable non-resident entity and is not required to file VAGST returns.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC): (1) incorporate with the Samoa International Companies Office (ICO); (2) file the certificate of incorporation and a certified registry extract with the Inland Revenue Division to obtain a Samoan Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for administrative purposes; (3) if the IC commences a taxable activity in Samoa or supplies taxable goods/services to Samoan residents, register for Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) within 21 days of commencement of activity or once the WST 80,000 registration threshold is met; (4) an IC that remains wholly offshore and does not trade with Samoan residents may be treated as a nil-assessable non-resident entity and is not required to file VAGST returns.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available under Samoan law. The International Companies Act 1987 confers a statutorily insulated offshore tax-exempt status. An IC may elect to convert to or redomicile as a Samoan Domestic Company under the Companies Act 2001, which would subject it to income tax and VAGST. Hybrid mismatch rules are not adopted in Samoa, and no tax election can produce deductible shareholder payments or otherwise alter the IC's exempt classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 80,000 (Eighty thousand Samoan Tala) annual taxable turnover

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of goods are zero-rated when the goods are physically exported from Samoa and delivered to a destination outside Samoa within 28 days of the supply. Services, including high-end tea-service and lounge hospitality supplied to non-resident cruise or airline clients, are zero-rated when supplied under a contract with a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of performance, or when the services are performed on goods located outside Samoa. The supplier must retain export evidence, a non-resident recipient declaration, a tax invoice marked 'Zero-Rated Export,' and proof of the export destination.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Cus-2 form)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank debit confirmation evidencing settlement of export proceeds in a Samoan bank account
  • Original VAGST tax invoice marked 'Zero-Rated Export'
  • Non-resident recipient declaration and proof of export destination

No mandatory real-time electronic invoicing clearance or cryptographic fiscal stamping regime is currently in force. VAGST-registered suppliers must issue tax invoices in a form approved by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue, retain source documents for seven years, and file electronic periodic VAGST returns. The Ministry of Revenue is progressively deploying an online invoice verification and electronic payments portal, but there is no API-connected fiscal device mandate and no digital stamping requirement yet.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rated export claims are disallowed and treated as taxable output tax. The Commissioner may retrospectively claw back VAGST refunds, impose a late-payment penalty of up to 5% per month plus interest, and assess a shortfall penalty up to 150% of the tax evaded. Deliberate furnishing of false export documentation is a criminal offence carrying a fine not exceeding WST 100,000 and possible imprisonment up to ten years under the Samoa Tax Administration Act 2011. The International Company's tax-exempt status does not shield these penalties where the exporter seeks VAGST refunds on improperly claimed zero-rated supplies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) is a managed-floating currency administered under the Exchange Control Act 1977. Current-account transactions, including trade payments, service fees, and visa-related settlements, are freely permitted through licensed authorized dealers upon documentary support. Capital-account operations, including equity injections, intercompany loans, and profit distribution, require CBS exchange control approval and are scrutinized for source-of-funds, economic substance, and repatriation capacity. For an International Company (IC) conducting a physical specialty tea salon lounge serving local consumers, the presumption of offshore exemption under the International Companies Act 1988 is diminished; profit remittance is permissible only after audited financial statements, valid tax clearance, and registration of the IC as a licensed local business are confirmed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1977 and subsidiary Exchange Control Regulations, exporters must repatriate foreign currency receipts to Samoa within 180 days of shipment and surrender 100% of the proceeds to a CBS-licensed authorized dealer for conversion into WST at the prevailing interbank rate. Specific exemptions may be granted by the Central Bank of Samoa for international companies operating under a valid offshore business certificate; however, a domestic-facing tea salon and lounge cannot rely on such exemptions and must strictly comply with the 180-day repatriation and full-surrender requirement for any export revenue derived from tea product resale.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), in coordination with the Samoa International Finance Authority for international company licensing and offshore banking matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-nil statutory viability. Samoa has no domestically recognized electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime; offshore digital banks, non-bank EMI providers, and unlicensed payment aggregators are not permitted to open host accounts or issue virtual IBANs for Samoan International Companies. A licensed offshore fintech may only interact with the local clearing system through a CBS-authorized dealer, and can never provide a standalone treasury account without a Samoan commercial bank partner. For the specialty tea salon lounge, fintech EMIs should be used solely as downstream card-acquiring or FX quotation utilities, not as primary liquidity rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing requires an external corporate bank to transmit a SWIFT MT103 credit to the IC's WST/USD settlement account held at a CBS-licensed authorized dealer. The incoming payment must quote a valid Exchange Control Application reference issued by the accepting Samoan bank and be categorized under an inward-investment purpose code covering equity subscription or shareholder loan. Capital injections intended for working capital must first be lodged to a Capital Escrow Account, converted to WST after the authorizing dealer confirms CBS approval for amounts exceeding the applicable exchange control threshold, and then released through the Operational Account. The accepting bank must file post-investment reporting with the Central Bank of Samoa within five business days of credit.Trade-related disbursements flow primarily over SWIFT MT103 for recurring low-value supplier payments—such as loose-leaf tea purchases and ceramic dinnerware—accompanied by proforma invoices and CBS-compliant narrative purpose codes. Commercial Letters of Credit are used for high-value consolidated shipments, with the advising bank issuing SWIFT MT700/MT710 messages and the IC's account charged against a pre-approved FX line. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 2,000,000 (for taxable domestic entities only; International Company tax-exempt status does not generate transfer pricing filing obligations, but supporting retention of arm's-length records is advisory)

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations apply to relevant entities engaged in geographically mobile activities (banking, insurance, shipping, IP holding, financing, distribution, service centers). A specialty tea salon lounge is not a relevant activity, but the International Company must still maintain a Samoa registered agent, comply with the International Companies Act prohibition on domestic business with Samoa residents, and avoid being treated as a shell company where actual management and commercial substance are wholly outside Samoa.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Inaccurate Declarations

Statutory sanction: Back assessment of 25% corporate income tax plus penalties of up to 200% of tax evaded; fines up to WST 100,000; mandatory strike-off of the International Company and forfeiture of assets used in the offense

Director personal liability: Directors and officers knowingly signing a false declaration are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, civil restitution of shifted profits, and may face imprisonment of up to 7 years and travel bans pending investigation
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Immediate reclassification as a taxable domestic company, repeal of the tax exemption, imposition of 25% corporate tax on all accumulated profits, interest at 10% per annum, and a penalty of WST 50,000

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil, making shareholders and directors personally liable for the entire tax assessment, bank account freezing, disgorgement of dividends received, and a prohibition from serving as director of any Samoa company for 10 years
AML / Breach of Beneficial Ownership Rules

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties of up to WST 250,000; enforced closure of the registered office; suspension of the licensed agent; referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal anti-money-laundering prosecution

Director personal liability: Directors may be criminally indicted for money laundering, fined up to WST 500,000, and imprisoned for up to 14 years; assets in Samoa and foreign bank accounts may be seized under confiscation orders
Unlicensed Commercial Activity

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of all revenue earned by the unlicensed business, daily penalty of WST 1,000 from the date of the violation, cancellation of the International Company certificate, and prohibition on future registration for 5 years

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unpaid license fees, consumer refunds, and government fines; egregious violations may result in custodial sentences of up to 5 years and personal disqualification from managing any Samoan entity
