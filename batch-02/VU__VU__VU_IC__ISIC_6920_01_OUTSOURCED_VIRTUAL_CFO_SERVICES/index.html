Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (DCIR); official portal: www.customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu; customs declarations processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window; VAT registrations and returns are administered directly at DCIR offices in Port Vila and Luganville, with no separate national VAT e-portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company (IC) is issued a DCIR VAT Identification Number, which serves as the operative TIN. Procedure: lodge Form VAT-01 (Application for Registration) with DCIR, accompanied by a certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered office and registered agent details, director and beneficial owner KYC records under Vanuatu's AML/CFT legislation, and a projected annual turnover declaration. Compulsory registration arises only when annual taxable local turnover exceeds VUV 5,000,000; a non-resident IC making only zero-rated offshore supplies to non-residents is not required to register, though it may voluntarily register to recover input VAT. DCIR typically issues the VAT ID/TIN within 3–5 business days of receiving a complete application.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company (IC) is issued a DCIR VAT Identification Number, which serves as the operative TIN. Procedure: lodge Form VAT-01 (Application for Registration) with DCIR, accompanied by a certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered office and registered agent details, director and beneficial owner KYC records under Vanuatu's AML/CFT legislation, and a projected annual turnover declaration. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None applicable. Vanuatu has no income tax or capital gains tax and no check-the-box or comparable entity classification regime. International Companies are treated conclusively as non-resident tax-exempt corporations. Under the VAT Act, the only available elections are (1) voluntary VAT registration below the VUV 5,000,000 compulsory threshold, and (2) the substantive election to characterize eligible cross-border services as zero-rated exports by maintaining the required offshore documentation. No hybrid mismatch elections or statutory hybrid rules are legislated in Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period (compulsory registration threshold); voluntary registration is available below VUV 5,000,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Value Added Tax Act 1998, Schedule 2, zero-rating applies to: (1) exports of goods physically removed from Vanuatu, evidenced by customs documentation within the prescribed period; and (2) services supplied to a non-resident of Vanuatu where the recipient is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply, the services are not directly connected to land or goods situated in Vanuatu, and the benefit is consumed wholly outside Vanuatu. The outsourced Virtual CFO advisory and multicurrency ledger bookkeeping services qualify as zero-rated offshore services where the client is a non-resident, the engagement is performed for an offshore business, and the invoice is paid from offshore bank accounts. The taxpayer must substantiate both the non-resident status of the recipient and the offshore receipt of the consideration.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry) for any physical goods or digital media shipped out of Vanuatu, where applicable
  • Commercial VAT invoice marked 'Zero-Rated Export' showing the nature of the offshore services and the non-resident client's identity
  • Signed service agreement / statement of work demonstrating that the services are supplied to a non-resident and performed for a business located outside Vanuatu
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for physical exports) evidencing departure from a Vanuatu port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 payment advice from the offshore-paying bank, showing receipt of funds into the Vanuatu IC's bank account within the substantiation period
  • Evidence of the client's non-residency, such as a foreign business registration extract, tax residence certificate, or utility bill in the name of the foreign client

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Vanuatu. VAT-registered businesses are required to issue itemized VAT tax invoices in paper or PDF and file periodic VAT returns with DCIR in paper/PDF. The only mandatory electronic filing window is ASYCUDA World, which is used exclusively for customs export/import declarations. No government API clearance regime applies to VAT invoices.

DCIR audits zero-rating claims through verifiable export evidence, including the customer's non-residency, the locus of service performance, and the incoming offshore settlement. Where the 0% rate is not substantiated within the 180-day evidentiary window, DCIR recharacterizes the invoice as a taxable local supply at 15% VAT and assesses the tax together with monthly interest at 2% per month on unpaid tax, late-payment penalties, and additional penalties. Willfully false export claims carry penalties of up to VUV 10,000,000 and/or up to five years' imprisonment, and any VAT refunds previously paid in respect of the fraudulent zero-rating are clawed back in full with retrospective effect.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible on the current account and Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, thereby prohibiting exchange restrictions on current international transactions. Capital-account transactions are broadly liberalised for International Companies: the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] expressly exempts an IC from the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 64]. Profit, dividend and service-fee remittances by a non-resident-owned IC are unrestricted and free of any Vanuatu withholding tax, subject only to bank-level KYC verification of board resolutions, client service agreements and source-of-funds records.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender regime and no mandatory conversion ratio for any entity, resident or exempt. Service-export receipts, including virtual CFO consulting fees, may be retained indefinitely in any foreign currency. For an IC the exemption is absolute: no RBV approval, no surrender timeline, no repatriation obligation and no conversion requirement applies.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], together with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as prudential and AML/CFT regulators; the RBV administers exchange control under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 64] and publishes the official VUV daily reference rates.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium practical viability with strict legal constraints. Wise (UK FCA-regulated electronic money institution) will onboard a Vanuatu IC remotely for multicurrency collection and disbursement, but it holds no Vanuatu licence and must not be used for VUV-denominated local settlement, which under Vanuatu law requires an RBV-licensed banking or payment institution. Stripe does not support Vanuatu as a merchant market, so Stripe Billing must be fronted by a supported-jurisdiction gateway or by Wise with the IC identified as the underlying beneficiary and daily treasury sweeps into an RBV-licensed domestic bank concentration account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing regime, Local File, Master File, or contemporaneous documentation obligation exists in Vanuatu. Practical OECD-aligned risk benchmarks for the Virtual CFO structure: prepare a Local File where related-party service revenue exceeds approximately VUV 400 million (circa EUR 3 million) and a Master File where group revenue exceeds approximately VUV 1.5 billion (circa EUR 12 million). Even below these thresholds, maintain a basic arm's length evidence file when the Vanuatu IC invoices related controlled entities abroad, in order to preempt foreign audit adjustments.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations comparable to UAE standards. However, the International Companies Act prohibits carrying on business with Vanuatu residents, and VFSC AML Rules require a beneficial ownership and economic purpose file. Foreign CFC, permanent establishment, and substance-over-form doctrines will disregard the IC if strategic management, employees, client contracting, and multicurrency ledger operations are performed abroad. The Virtual CFO IC should evidence board meetings, client contracts, invoice trails via Stripe and Wise, and substantive bookkeeping output in Vanuatu or a recognized offshore back-office to survive judicial scrutiny.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion abroad / sham arrangement

Statutory sanction: Foreign criminal tax penalties, civil fraud penalties up to 200% of the unpaid tax, restitution orders, and imprisonment under cross-border evasion statutes. Vanuatu cooperates through the OECD Multilateral Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance and the FATCA IGA.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution, worldwide asset freezing, travel bans, extradition risk, and imprisonment in the prosecuting jurisdiction.
Trading with Vanuatu residents / domestic market violation

Statutory sanction: Offence under the International Companies Act: fines, daily default penalties, cease-and-desist orders, and strike-off of the IC from the VFSC register.

Director personal liability: Directors personally fined, disqualified from management in Vanuatu, and barred from serving in any VFSC-regulated entity.
Shell company lacking economic substance

Statutory sanction: Foreign courts and tax agencies may reattribute the IC's profits to shareholders, impose CFC tax, deny treaty-like benefits, and treat the entity as void for fiscal purposes; VFSC may also audit and withhold certificates of good standing.

Director personal liability: Director-level exposure in foreign discovery proceedings, joint asset-tracing orders, contribution orders in settlement agreements, and travel restrictions imposed by foreign courts.
AML / Beneficial ownership non-compliance

Statutory sanction: VFSC AML Rules enforcement: administrative monetary penalties as prescribed by law, suspension or revocation of the registered agent, mandatory BO filing corrections, and foreign bank de-risking caused by FATF grey-list scrutiny of Vanuatu.

Director personal liability: Management liability for AML breaches, travel bans in aggravated cross-border cases, disqualification as responsible persons, and personal fines.
False declaration in annual return

Statutory sanction: Criminal offence under the International Companies Act: fine per offence, imprisonment of directors up to 12 months, and discretionary strike-off of the entity.

Director personal liability: Imprisonment, personal restitution of fees and costs, and a ban on holding any directorship in Vanuatu.
Insolvent trading and financial misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu insolvency and fraudulent trading rules impose civil liability for company debts, criminal penalties, and up to 2 years imprisonment for dishonest trading.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution for debts incurred dishonestly, personal bankruptcy orders, travel bans, and disqualification orders.
