Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Vanuatu law prescribes no minimum paid-up or issued share capital for International Companies and no statutory escrow account. For SPAC vehicles, the underwriting agreement must however contractually require IPO/offering proceeds to be deposited into a Vanuatu licensed bank trust escrow account before closing; the escrow amount is not part of the incorporation filing.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Vanuatu Police Force Fire and Rescue Services covering the registered office/fit-out; structural load clearance from the Vanuatu Public Works Department for any office alterations; and occupational health and safety clearance from the Vanuatu Department of Labour if employees are engaged. The SPAC must also maintain a business continuity and emergency evacuation plan.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. An International Company is automatically tax-exempt. The only statutory elections relevant here are: (1) filing a notice under the International Companies Act confirming non-resident/offshore status; and (2) conversion or deregistration as an ordinary company under the Companies Act (Cap 191), which terminates automatic tax exemption. For VAT, voluntary registration is available but does not trigger any hybrid entity election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 per annum (approx. USD 45,000) of taxable turnover from onshore supplies

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered VAT supplier may zero-rate: (a) goods physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of invoice, supported by customs export declarations and transport documents; and (b) services supplied to a non-resident recipient where the direct benefit is wholly outside Vanuatu and the services do not relate to Vanuatu land or goods. Financial services (including escrow, share dealing, and SPAC acquisition services) are generally exempt from VAT, but may be zero-rated as an export of services where the recipient is non-resident and the benefit is realized offshore. An International Company operating as an offshore SPAC is normally outside the VAT net and does not register. Input VAT can only be recovered if the IC registers voluntarily and makes a taxable supply in Vanuatu.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List

No mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping regime is in force in Vanuatu. VAT invoices may be issued in hard copy or PDF and must contain prescribed particulars (supplier TIN, VAT rate, gross consideration). Taxpayers are not required to pre-clear invoice series with the VRC, but must retain invoices and supporting documentation for 7 years.

The VRC may audit zero-rated export claims for up to 7 years. Non-documentary compliance attracts a monthly penalty of 5% of the VAT understated plus interest at 10% p.a. Knowingly false or fraudulent claims trigger a penalty equal to 200% of the tax evaded, criminal fines of up to VUV 10,000,000, imprisonment of officers for up to 10 years, retrospective reassessment and clawback of all zero-rating relief. In addition, the Registrar under the International Companies Act may revoke an IC's tax-exempt status where the entity is found to have engaged in onshore business activity.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalised, no-exchange-control regime. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible in Vatu (VUV), which floats against a currency basket. International Companies (ICs) face no restriction on capital injection, profit remittance, dividend repatriation, or liquidation proceeds transfer. No RBV approval is required for foreign exchange conversion or outward remittance for a SPAC escrow vehicle. Convertibility is practically unlimited; the domestic banking system clears transactions in USD, AUD, EUR, and VUV, with the VUV quoted freely against all major currencies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender or conversion of export proceeds. All export receipts, including service fees and transaction revenues, may be retained in any foreign currency or converted on the entity's own discretion. There are no mandatory timelines, minimum conversion ratios, or RBV directives requiring repatriation into VUV for International Companies or SPAC structures.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) as the monetary authority and custodian of foreign exchange policy; Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) as the corporate and securities regulator responsible for International Company licensing, SPAC listing conduct, and AML/CFT supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low — Vanuatu law does not recognise non-resident e-money institutions as escrow custodians or deposit-takers. Offshore digital EMIs can provide only payment information facilitation on top of a licensed RBV clearing bank; they cannot hold SPAC proceeds in trust or maintain title to capital subscriptions. Use of an unlicensed EMI would create significant AML/CFT and insolvency exposure for the SPAC.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant sponsors' inbound wires use SWIFT MT103 with a transparent purpose code, preferably 9210 (Internal transfer) for subscribed capital or 5620 (Acquisition financing) for SPAC sponsor loans. Each wire must reference the SPAC trust deed and the escrow account number as the ultimate beneficiary. The Vanuatu receiving bank will verify against the VFSC registry and match the remitter with the SPAC's disclosed UBOs. No RBV exchange-control approval is needed, but thorough KYC and beneficial-ownership records must be annexed at first injection and periodically refreshed.Outbound settlement rails for SPAC completion and redemptions consist of SWIFT MT103 wire transfers routed through our nostro correspondents in Australia, Singapore, and New Zealand, or Commercial Letters of Credit where the series-B merger consideration is conditioned on documentary compliance. For redemption events, funds flow by MT103 to the investors' recorded banks, matched to a fund-level share register. VUV/USD clearing on domestic books is executed via the same SWIFT rail with cover payment to preserve an independent audit trail and mitigate settlement risk.Offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) contracts referencing VUV/USD or the acquisition currency, settled in USD net cash at maturity — permissible for non-resident SPAC vehicles.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) under ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a qualified international dealer — permitted despite the absence of an onshore derivatives market.Plain-vanilla FX forwards with an RBV-licensed commercial bank, generally limited to a term matching a fixed SPAC acquisition or redemption date.Currency options may be embedded in structured escrow products only when the bank acts as licensed principal; OTC options between unlicensed third parties are not permissible onshore in Vanuatu.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; International Companies under the International Companies Act Cap 222 are expressly exempt from all taxation on income, profits and distributions)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company, whether to residents or non-residents; dividend distributions from SPAC trust or escrow proceeds are therefore tax-free at source in Vanuatu.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Vanuatu statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold exists because no CIT is levied. However, under the IC ESR Regime, any relevant entity with annual gross income of VUV 10,000,000 (approximately USD 83,000) or more, or with intra-group cross-border transactions above VUV 5,000,000 (approximately USD 42,000), should maintain a Local File and Master File as best practice. Foreign parent jurisdictions (for example the United States, Hong Kong or EU Member States) will apply their own documentation thresholds and penalties to the Vanuatu SPAC, commonly USD 10,000,000 to USD 20,000,000 of gross revenue or covered transactions.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2022 requires every Vanuatu International Company, including an ISIC 6499 SPAC blank-check vehicle, to satisfy the economic substance test applicable to its relevant activity. A pre-deal SPAC holding IPO proceeds will normally be assessed as a pure equity holding entity and therefore subject to light substance requirements: own registered office, adequate records and full compliance. The moment the SPAC earns management fees, receives material interest on escrow, or executes a business combination, classification shifts to financing and leasing or headquarters, with full substance requirements: Vanuatu board control, local employment, local premises and proportionate local expenditure. Failure to meet the test invalidates the IC's claim to the zero-tax regime and exposes directors to disclosure to the OECD and EU.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abusive Conduit Arrangements

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2022: penalty up to VUV 10,000,000 (approximately USD 83,000), daily default penalty of VUV 50,000, disqualification orders and strike-off. Under Vanuatu's anti-money-laundering framework, false statutory declarations attract imprisonment up to 12 years and fines up to VUV 15,000,000. The foreign parent additionally faces GAAR recharacterisation, CFC inclusions, withholding assessments and civil penalties in its residence jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are personally liable for penalties where they knew or were reckless as to the false declaration; courts may lift the corporate veil, impose travel bans and asset-freezing orders, order unjust enrichment restitution equal to the profits routed through the IC, and in aggravated cross-border fraud cases imprison directors for up to 12 years.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC deregistration and notation on the public Non-Compliant Entities register; a VUV 500,000 penalty for each year of non-compliance up to VUV 10,000,000; mandatory adverse OECD/EU cross-border information-exchange reports, resulting in loss of banking relationships and de-risking at the escrow bank.

Director personal liability: Each director is jointly and severally liable for restoration costs, ESR penalties and the costs of judicial liquidation; personal sanctions include a five-year ban on acting as a director of any VFSC-licensed entity and, where escrow monies were misapplied, criminal conversion convictions with imprisonment up to 7 years.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act and VFSC AML Rules: fines up to VUV 25,000,000 (approximately USD 208,000) per violation, revocation of the IC's registration and of the Registered Agent's licence, confiscation of escrow and merger proceeds, and referral to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit and foreign law enforcement.

Director personal liability: Directors, the Registered Agent and the escrow trustee face personal criminal liability for consenting to suspicious transactions; statutory penalties include imprisonment up to 12 years, forfeiture of personal assets under civil recovery, a 10-year banking ban and travel restrictions preventing departure from Vanuatu.
False Economic Substance Reporting / Fraudulent Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Fines of VUV 10,000,000 and daily default penalties; criminal prosecution for false declaration before the VFSC; deregistration; and mandatory exchange of information with the OECD/EU Global Forum, leading to blacklisting of the Vanuatu jurisdiction and removal of concessionary status.

Director personal liability: The signatory director and the Registered Agent officer are personally liable; courts can impose civil restitution of all fees and interest earned by non-resident sponsors, a 10-year director disqualification, a travel ban pending investigation, and imprisonment up to 12 years for fraud.
