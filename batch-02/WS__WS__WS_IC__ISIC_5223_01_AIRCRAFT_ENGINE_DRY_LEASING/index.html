Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Government of Samoa, Apia; official e-services and filing portal: Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) — https://www.mfr.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) registered under Samoa's International Companies Act 1987 is not automatically issued a Samoan TIN. To obtain statutory VAT/TIN identification, the foreign-owned IC applies to the Ministry for Revenue on the prescribed TIN application form (processed through the ITAS e-portal or in paper at the Apia office), attaching the Registrar-issued Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered agent consent letter, director and beneficial owner passports, and proof of the registered office in Apia. The MfR issues a unique taxpayer identification and VAGST registration number; alternatively, the IC obtains from the Registrar of International Companies a Certificate of Tax Exemption evidencing that no Samoan tax registration is required for purely offshore activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Samoa International Company (IC) is a statutorily ring-fenced exempt vehicle under the International Companies Act 1987; it is exempt from income tax, stamp duty, corporate filing duties, and foreign exchange controls, and only a Samoan fixed place of business engaged in local-source commercial activity could attract territorial assessment under the Income Tax Act 2012.

Available election forms

Samoa does not maintain a 'check-the-box' or hybrid-mismatch election. The only relevant statutory mechanisms are (i) voluntary VAGST registration below the monetary threshold under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1992, and (ii) the IC's statutory exemption certificate regime, which is not elective once the International Companies Act 1987 conditions are satisfied. No pass-through election is available to an International Company.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoa Tala) taxable turnover in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available to exporters and international services providers below the threshold; a foreign lessor with no Samoan fixed establishment and no local taxable activity remains outside the VAGST net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The dry lease of an aircraft engine qualifies for the 0% VAGST rate only where the engine is supplied to, and continuously used by, a non-resident lessee outside Samoa and the lease constitutes an export of goods or an internationally connected service under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1992. The IC must demonstrate that the engine was exported from Samoa or was never imported into Samoa, that the commercial benefit of the lease is enjoyed wholly offshore, that the lessee is not a resident of Samoa, and that consideration is received in foreign currency. For a tax-exempt IC with no VAGST registration, no output tax arises and the gross lease rentals remain outside the Samoan indirect tax base.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDec) processed by the Customs Division of the Ministry for Revenue, or an approved customs transshipment/export endorsement evidencing physical export of the engine from Samoa
  • Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill and related transport documents evidencing dispatch of the engine to the non-resident lessee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/MT202 bank records evidencing receipt of the foreign-currency lease rental in a Samoan or offshore IC account
  • Executed dry-lease agreement, lease schedule, and engine log book or serial-number documentation identifying the non-resident lessee and offshore user
  • Zero-rated VAGST tax invoice issued with all prescribed particulars, together with a certificate of non-residence for the lessee

Samoa has no real-time e-invoicing clearance, fiscal stamping, or mandatory digital API clearance regime. The MfR relies on periodic VAGST returns filed electronically through ITAS; a registered supplier must issue a tax invoice containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and registration number, invoice date, description and quantity, consideration, and VAGST shown separately) and must retain export evidence for audit.

The MfR may conduct export-substantiation audits within the statutory limitation period. If the required customs, transport, lease, and foreign-exchange documentation cannot be produced, the zero-rated supply will be recharacterized as a local taxable supply at 15% VAGST, with retrospective clawback assessment, statutory late-payment and late-filing penalties, and default interest under the Goods and Services Tax Act 1992 and the Tax Administration Act 2012. Knowingly false zero-rating declarations constitute a criminal offense punishable by fine and/or imprisonment, and directors, beneficial owners, and the IC's registered agent may be subject to joint and several liability. Breach of the International Companies Act 1987 conditions can additionally trigger forfeiture of the IC's tax-exempt status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa tala (WST) is convertible for current account payments subject to CBS notification and, in some cases, exchange control approval. Capital account transfers, including inbound equity injection and outward investment, require prior CBS exchange control authorization. An International Company (IC) may lawfully maintain and settle obligations in foreign currency, which materially reduces conversion friction. Dividends and profit remittances to non-residents are permitted once funded from genuinely audited profits and after any domestic tax claim is settled or waived; in practice the key hurdles are KYC/AML clearance, bank-level source-of-funds verification, and CBS notification above prescribed thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa does not operate a general surrender requirement for export proceeds. Exporters of goods and services must declare the transaction and ensure that receivables are collected through an authorized dealer within the statutory period, ordinarily not exceeding 180 days from shipment or performance. For cross-border engine dry-leasing income, lease invoices and SWIFT remittances are treated as export earnings; the IC may retain the foreign-currency proceeds in an approved foreign-currency account with a licensed bank, avoiding mandatory conversion into WST. Non-compliance with receipt timelines attracts CBS penalties and may trigger enhanced bank review of the underlying leasing agreement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), acting under the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations 1979, the Financial Institutions Act 2002, and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter Financing of Terrorism Act 2007.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Samoa has no dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework. Offshore fintech EMIs and digital banks cannot independently hold Samoa bank-licensed accounts or access local clearing and settlement rails. A foreign EMI may only operate as an unregulated payment facilitator, routing funds through a sanctioned correspondent bank; this structure exposes the IC to heightened AML scrutiny and reputational risk. A Samoa IC should rely on a licensed local commercial bank with robust SWIFT connectivity rather than an offshore EMI for treasury-critical payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder loan funding is transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the Samoa IC's operational account at a licensed bank. The wire instruction must include the IC name, the unique corporate identifier, the purpose reference 'WS_IC_ISIC_5223_01', and a clear balance-of-payments purpose code: for equity use 'Equity capital other than reinvestment of earnings'; for shareholder loans use 'Other investment – loans'. When the remittance exceeds the CBS notification threshold, the Samoa bank must be given a signed declaration form, evidence of source of funds, a corporate resolution authorizing the injection, and either the escrow agreement or the aircraft-engine lease agreement where the injection supports lease pre-funding. WST conversion, if any, is executed by the authorized dealer at the prevailing market exchange rate and recorded on the authorized dealer transaction form.Treasury disbursements for engine lease settlements, security deposits, maintenance reserves, and third-party payments are primarily cleared via SWIFT MT103 using the IC's directly held correspondent account at the tier-1 bank. Where funds are moved between internal treasury accounts, SWIFT MT202 is used. Commercial letters of credit are not standard for dry-lease rent; however, a standby letter of credit (SWIFT MT760) is permissible when the lessee or lessor requires a security instrument. Escrow release triggers require the presentation of an execution letter, invoice, maintenance receipt, engine log confirmation, and the mutual release instruction.
