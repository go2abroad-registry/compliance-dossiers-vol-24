Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue Authority (VRA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: https://www.vra.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

International Companies incorporated under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) are statutorily exempt from domestic income tax and therefore do not obtain a corporate TIN. If the IC voluntarily elects or becomes required to register for VAT (e.g., by undertaking chargeable supplies in Vanuatu), it must submit the VAT registration application (Form VAT-01) to the Vanuatu Revenue Authority, accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, registered office address, director and shareholder particulars, local registered agent attestation, and bank account details, after which a VRA VAT registration number is issued. No corporate tax identification number exists because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No local check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under Vanuatu law. An International Company is statutorily treated as a non-resident exempt entity within Vanuatu's tax-exempt zone. The entity may, however, be classified for foreign tax purposes under the relevant foreign jurisdiction's check-the-box rules (e.g., U.S. Form 8832), but such election has no Vanuatu domestic tax effect.

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are eligible for zero-rated VAT when they are exported from Vanuatu under a valid customs export declaration and the exporter retains evidence of physical removal from Vanuatu within the prescribed period. Services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident person who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply, the service is not used or consumed in Vanuatu, and the supply does not relate to land or goods situated in Vanuatu. An International Company engaged solely in passive equity holding generally supplies no local taxable supplies and is outside the scope of Vanuatu VAT; zero-rating applies only to VAT-registered persons undertaking qualifying export supplies.

Vanuatu has no real-time fiscal clearance regime, no API-based e-invoicing clearance, and no digital stamping requirement. VAT-registered businesses must issue VAT invoices in the prescribed format and file periodic VAT returns electronically via the Vanuatu Revenue Authority's e-services portal, but no State-coded QR or centralized invoice clearance is currently mandated.

Fraudulent or improper zero-rating claims and export VAT refunds are subject to the Vanuatu Revenue Authority's audit scrutiny, retrospective assessment, and recovery for up to five years. Penalties under the Value Added Tax Act include monetary penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded, criminal fines, imprisonment of directors and officers, mandatory revocation of VAT registration, and civil clawback of all refund amounts plus interest. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission may additionally restrict or cancel an International Company's license if tax fraud or misrepresentation is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is not subject to exchange controls; the capital and current accounts are fully liberalized. Dividends, interest, and profit remittances by non-resident International Company shareholders are freely transferable in any convertible currency. The only de facto hurdle is the documentary burden imposed by commercial banks for anti-money-laundering (AML) counterparty due diligence; there is no regulatory exchange-recognition barrier. In practice, the RBV does not require prior approval for dividend distributions or share-capital repatriation by International Companies, although the transacting bank must verify the corporate status and tax-exempt notation of the IC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu maintains no statutory mandate requiring export receivables to be surrendered, converted, or repatriated within any timeline. There is no compulsory rate of mandatory conversion for export proceeds; approved commercial banks may credit foreign-currency export proceeds directly to the exporter's foreign-currency account. Statutory reporting obligations relate to the Reserve Bank's balance-of-payments data collection only, not to surrender or conversion ratios.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Limited for a Vanuatu International Company. Offshore digital EMIs generally refuse pure holding vehicles because they cannot independently verify economic substance and the jurisdiction's OFC profile raises correspondent-banking risk. Only a small number of EMI platforms under a full European EMI licence may accept a Vanuatu IC if the IC provides audited financial statements, a transactional rationale for the account, and a signed beneficial-ownership declaration. Legal constraints include absence of a local EMI licensing framework under Vanuatu law and a ban on engaging in domestic banking business without a banking licence, so the EMI must remain a wholly non-resident service provider.

Defense evidence: Twelve months of bank statements of the sending entity/originator, the share subscription agreement or board resolution evidencing the equity injection, audited financial statements or management accounts showing the source of the remitted funds, a notarised source-of-wealth affidavit, and confirmation of the tax identification number of each contributing party.
Application profile matching a 'mailbox-like' passive shell with no real presence

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions should be routed as SWIFT MT103 wires from the parent entity's bank account to the VU IC's account held with a licensed Vanuatu bank. The remitter must enter in field /70 (Remittance Information) the narrative 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' and in field /56/F the SWIFT/BIC of the Vanuatu receiving bank. The Vanuatu bank will not require prior exchange-control approval, but the sender's bank may apply a purpose code for foreign-direct investment statistical purposes, typically 'G30' under the IMF Balance of Payments classification. Before crediting, the Vanuatu receiving bank will validate that the IC is not under liquidation and will request a board resolution, the share certificate, and the UBO declaration to distinguish the injection from an incoming loan.Trade settlements and dividend outflows must be executed via SWIFT MT103 on a T+0/T+1 basis, using the Vanuatu IC's designated foreign-currency clearing accounts with the local correspondent network. For documentary trade where the IC is a purchaser under a commercial invoice, a commercial letter of credit (documentary LC) can be issued through a Vanuatu commercial bank or, more efficiently, through a Pacific regional issuing bank (e.g., BSP, ANZ) with reimbursement under SWIFT MT700. Due to the IC being tax-exempt, outbound dividends are also cleared by MT103; the bank will obtain a distribution statement and a solvency test to prevent an unauthorised reduction of capital. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% – Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax. No national income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profits tax, and no inheritance or estate duty is levied on International Companies (ICs).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Vanuatu levies no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to any shareholder, whether resident in Vanuatu or non-resident. Dividends, distributions, and returns of capital are entirely free of Quellensteuer at source. Consequently, a pure equity holding SPV's dividend upstream to its non-resident parent is untaxed in Vanuatu.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual Return must be delivered to the Registrar of International Companies within one month after the return date, being the anniversary of incorporation. The annual VFSC license fee is simultaneously payable on the anniversary date; a grace period is granted by the VFSC fee schedule before late penalties accrue.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual municipal license renewal (if any incidental local activity is admitted) follows the calendar year or the municipal council's fiscal year; ICs that remain purely passive are not subject to renewal and require no payment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A – No local currency threshold. Vanuatu's Income Tax Act has no transfer pricing documentation, Local File, or Master File requirements, and no country-by-country reporting obligation is imposed on International Companies. The VUV threshold triggering transfer pricing documentation is nil because the obligation does not legally exist; nevertheless, foreign parent jurisdictions (ASX-listed parents, OECD jurisdictions) may require global Master File inclusion.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) No. 9 of 2019, effective 1 July 2019, apply to entities carrying core income-generating activities in defined 'relevant activities'. A pure equity holding SPV (ISIC 6420) that derives income exclusively from dividends and capital gains on shares of subsidiaries is NOT a 'relevant activity' for ESR purposes and is therefore not subject to the economic substance test. The company remains subject to the minimum substance baseline: (a) a physical registered office in Vanuatu; (b) a licensed resident Registered Agent; (c) adequate books and records maintained in Vanuatu; and (d) compliance with the IC Act, the AML/CFT regime, and the VFSC beneficial ownership filing. Caution: if the SPV later converts part of its activity to intra-group financing, leasing, or intellectual property holding, ESR full substance requirements (board meetings in Vanuatu, adequate premises, full-time employees, and operational expenditure in Vanuatu) will apply to the relevant income, and the SPV would be required to file an annual ESR return with the VFSC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Facilitation – Offshore Conduit Abuse

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu has no income tax offence, but the VFSC may impose administrative penalties, strike off the company and refer to Vanuatu Police for offences of fraudulent misrepresentation under ss. 92 and 110 of the International Companies Act (Cap. 222). Foreign tax authorities can invoke exchange of information under TIEAs, reassess the parent with penal interest, and impose civil penalties equivalent to 100-200% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed false statutory declarations or aided foreign tax evasion face personal civil restitution orders, confiscation of assets, director disqualification by VFSC, and in egregious cases extradition risk to the source jurisdiction; Vanuatu courts can also impose imprisonment under the Penal Code for conspiracy to defraud.
Abuse of Corporate Personality / Shell Company Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu International Companies Act, non-compliant bearer share issuance is prohibited; VFSC may strike off the company, refuse to renew the registered agent's license, and report to the Financial Intelligence Unit. Financial institutions will freeze Swift wire transactions as suspicious; the entity is flagged under enhanced due diligence.

Director personal liability: Directors and nominee shareholders risk being held personally liable for all company liabilities on a piercing-of-the-veil theory in the jurisdictions where the foreign subsidiary creditors suffer loss; travel bans and asset freezing orders are commonly issued by foreign courts against nominee directors of shell SPVs.
AML/CFT Violations – Beneficial Ownership and Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: The VFSC may levy administrative fines on the Registered Agent and the company, and the Vanuatu courts may impose criminal fines and custodial sentences for money laundering for natural persons; the SPV is additionally subject to immediate account termination by correspondent banks and a recommendation for listing by the FATF.

Director personal liability: Directors who are the controlling minds of a shell SPV that launders proceeds face imprisonment under the ML/FT Act; the Personal liability extends to civil confiscation of all assets traceable to the offending account; directors are also exposed to personal travel bans imposed by foreign AML authorities and to regulatory disqualification across VFSC and overseas registries.
False Statements to VFSC and Non-Response to EOI

Statutory sanction: False statements are an offence under s. 99 of the International Companies Act (Cap. 222), punishable upon conviction by a fine not exceeding VUV 250,000 and imprisonment for up to two years. Failure to produce records to an EOI request can trigger deregistration and loss of the IC's legal personality.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally criminally liable for the false declaration, incurring fines and terms of imprisonment; the VFSC may also issue personal recovery orders against directors for regulator costs and professional compensation; repeated non-compliance also results in the jurisdiction's re-listing on the EU non-cooperative list and subsequent collapse of all correspondent banking access.
