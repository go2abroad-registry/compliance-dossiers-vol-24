Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), Ministry of Revenue, Malifa, Apia; online taxpayer portal: SRA Taxpayer Access Portal (TAP) via https://sra.gov.ws; customs and VAGST administration are processed through SRA's Customs Division and ASYCUDA World electronic platform.

E-government filing portal

A Samoa International Company (IC) does not require income-tax registration solely by reason of incorporation because it is a tax-exempt entity under the International Companies Act 1987. Where a foreign project-cargo entity needs a TIN for customs clearance, VAGST registration, local disbursements or input-credit claims, it must file Form TIN-1 (Non-Resident Taxpayer Registration) with SRA together with the IC Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent certificate, beneficial ownership declaration, directors'/shareholders' identification, and evidence of any Samoan place of business. SRA issues the TIN within 3-10 business days; a VAGST number is separately obtained through the VAGST Registration Form once the entity meets the taxable-supplies nexus. A foreign IC with no physical Samoan economic presence and no Samoan-source income is generally not required to file income tax returns.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Samoa International Company is an exempt entity, not treated as a Samoan tax resident, and is not subject to Samoan income tax, capital gains tax, transfer duty, stamp duty, or withholding tax on distributions to non-resident shareholders. Samoan law does not characterize the IC as a pass-through entity; foreign jurisdictions must independently classify it.

Available election forms

Samoa has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime. The IC may, by special resolution, elect to continue or re-domicile as a domestic company under the general Companies Act, which automatically terminates its International Company tax-exempt status and makes it subject to the Samoan Income Tax Act 2014. No statutory mechanism exists to make an IC partially taxable or transparent under Samoan domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

SAT 300,000 (WST 300,000) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; border-imported goods are subject to VAGST regardless of turnover. Non-resident suppliers of services received in Samoa may be required to register or comply through reverse-charge accounting where the recipient is not a registered VAGST person.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export treatment applies to goods physically exported from Samoa within the Commissioner-prescribed period (normally 28 days), to international freight and offshore transport services, and to services supplied directly for the handling, storage, loading, crane-age, escort or logistics of exported/imported out-of-gauge project cargo, provided the supply is to a non-resident outside Samoa or is in respect of goods being exported and the service is not separately consumed in Samoa. The exporter must keep verifiable export and foreign-receipt evidence and may claim input credit for Samoan VAGST attributable to the zero-rated export supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export entry) endorsed by SRA Customs
  • Original or negotiable Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill, intermodal/CMR waybill and heavy-haul cargo manifest for multimodal OOG movements)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 bank credit advice evidencing actual receipt of freight and ancillary logistics proceeds from the non-resident consignor, consignee or charterer within 180 days
  • VAGST tax invoice issued by the registered Samoa logistics entity describing the zero-rated export/freight supply
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and OOG dimension/weight calculation sheet supporting the project cargo
  • Services contract, letter of indemnity (LOI), or project-cargo charter-party assigning freight responsibility and payment terms

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time electronic fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping for ordinary VAGST invoices. Registered businesses must issue tax invoices containing prescribed particulars; electronic PDF invoices are accepted. Export and import entries must be filed electronically through ASYCUDA World and cleared by SRA Customs before goods move, but there is no mandatory national e-invoicing API or service-by-service tax clearance gateway.

Zero-rating claims on heavy-haul project cargo are risk-assessed. If the documentation bundle is incomplete, goods are not actually exported, or foreign-currency proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days, SRA will claw back the zero-rating, reassess VAGST at 15%, and impose interest plus late-payment penalties of 2% per month. Deliberate falsification or abusive export zero-rating constitutes evasion under the VAGST Act 2014, exposing the taxpayer to civil shortfall penalties of up to 250%, registration cancellation, refund forfeiture, and, upon criminal conviction, a fine not exceeding SAT 200,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years. Records must be retained for 7 years, and failure to maintain them shifts the burden against the taxpayer.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a managed-float currency pegged to a trade-weighted basket comprising the NZD, AUD, USD, EUR, JPY and CNY. Current-account items (trade in goods and services) are freely convertible through authorized dealers with documentary support, while capital-account movements (equity injections, shareholder loans and portfolio transactions) require CBS licensing or case-by-case approval only where the counterparty is a Samoan resident. A Samoa International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is treated as non-resident; its external capital flows with non-residents are therefore outside the ordinary convertibility licensing net. Profit and dividend remittances by a properly tax-exempt IC are not subject to Samoan exchange-control permits or withholding taxes, provided the receiving bank obtains board resolutions and audited financial evidence; the only meaningful friction is enhanced correspondent-bank due diligence in the USD clearing chain. Onshore WST liquidity is shallow, so large-value treasury balances should be held in USD, AUD or NZD accounts to avoid conversion slippage.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters under the Exchange Control Regulations 1978 must repatriate the full export value within six months of shipment and offer 100% of the foreign-currency proceeds to an authorized dealer within one month of receipt, converting into WST unless a CBS retention license permits holding foreign currency or crediting a designated foreign-currency account; there is no graduated statutory conversion percentage because the standard mandate captures the full proceeds subject to exemption. A non-resident Samoa IC providing OOG project-cargo and heavy-haul logistics services outside Samoa is not a resident exporter: freight and logistics service fees collected offshore are not surrenderable, and the IC may retain and disburse foreign currency through its Samoa bank accounts without conversion. Any WST credit arising from local agent billings must be supported by genuine invoices and service contracts, and the receiving bank must apply the CBS transaction-reporting and AML/CFT regime to all inward and outward remittances.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984 and acting as the sole exchange-control and monetary authority under the Exchange Control Act 1977 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1978; prudential supervision is supplemented by the Financial Institutions Act 1996 and the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 (as amended). All foreign-exchange transactions involving Samoan residents must be routed through authorized dealers, being the four licensed commercial banks operating in Samoa.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Samoa does not license electronic money institutions, and the Financial Institutions Act 1996 confines deposit-taking and payment services to licensed commercial banks. Offshore digital EMIs (for example Airwallex, Wise Business or Mercury) have no CBS authorization, no WST settlement capability and no access to Samoa's local clearing infrastructure; they may be used only as peripheral receivables or payables rails in third-country jurisdictions. The primary treasury structure must therefore rest on a Samoa-licensed commercial bank account, complemented by top-tier correspondent accounts (USD in New York, AUD in Sydney, NZD in Auckland) for cross-border settlement certainty.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan funding is routed by SWIFT MT103 (single customer credit transfer) or MT202 (interbank transfer) from the parent bank to the WS IC's account at a Samoa-licensed commercial bank, cleared through standard correspondent chains: USD via a New York clearing bank, AUD via Sydney and NZD via Auckland. The payment instruction must contain the beneficiary bank's SWIFT/BIC code, the IC's account number, and a purpose narrative such as Capital contribution to Samoa International Company or Shareholder loan under loan agreement dated [date]. Samoa imposes no inbound exchange-control licence on non-resident IC capital, but the receiving bank will require a board resolution authorizing the account, the executed share allotment or loan agreement, an ultimate-beneficial-owner declaration, and source-of-funds evidence; high-value injections above the bank's internal threshold (typically USD 1 million) are subject to CBS transaction reporting and may trigger enhanced review. Funds may be held in WST, USD, AUD or NZD without forced conversion; any conversion to WST for local project expenses is executed at the authorized dealer's daily retail rate, and same-day value is achieved for local WST credits through the Samoa electronic clearing system.Outbound disbursements for OOG project cargo and heavy-haul logistics are executed through the following clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 messages for milestone payments to subcontractors, freight forwarders, crane and trailer contractors, port authorities and surveyors, settled in USD, AUD or NZD via the commercial bank's correspondent windows; (ii) irrevocable documentary commercial Letters of Credit, confirmed where required by a top-tier international bank, for the procurement of heavy machinery, marine charters and specialized transport equipment; and (iii) demand guarantees or bank payment undertakings issued by the Samoa bank for port-entry and performance obligations. Each disbursement must be supported by the underlying commercial contract, tax invoice, proof of performance or bill of lading, and customs documentation, enabling the bank to satisfy CBS transaction monitoring, FATF customer due diligence and sanctions-screening obligations. WST-denominated local payments clear same-day through the Samoa clearing house, while foreign-currency payments are batched through the correspondent network and normally settle within one to three business days.Onshore deliverable forward contracts in USD/WST, AUD/WST and NZD/WST offered by licensed authorized dealers for maturities up to 12 months, available only where the IC demonstrates a genuine underlying commercial exposure supported by invoices and contracts.Natural hedging through multi-currency operating accounts, allowing the IC to retain USD, AUD and NZD receivables without immediate conversion into WST and thereby eliminating conversion risk on major disbursement currencies.Foreign-currency term deposits and invoice-and-collect pass-through matching of payables and receivables, being the most cost-effective permissible strategy given the shallow onshore forward market.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return must be furnished to the Commissioner within six (6) months after the end of the income year; for a standard calendar-year IC, due no later than 30 June of the following year. Approved substituted accounting periods are due six months after that period's end. Provisional tax installments for resident companies are due on 30 June, 30 September, and 31 December during the income year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory WST threshold is codified for Local File/Master File. The Commissioner's transfer-pricing powers (Income Tax Act 2015, s 55; Tax Administration Act 2015) require any cross-border related-party transaction to be priced at arm's length on request. Administrative benchmark: contemporaneous TP documentation (functional analysis, comparability study, intercompany agreements) is expected where annual related-party transactions exceed WST 1,000,000 (c. USD 370,000); a simplified TP record is recommended below that level. Thin capitalization restricts interest deductions on related-party debt to 60% of assets (2:3 debt/asset ceiling) unless the taxpayer proves an arm's-length debt level.
