Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Government of Samoa – Inland Revenue and Customs Divisions; official taxpayer portal and customs information at https://www.revenue.gov.ws and https://www.customs.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A Samoan International Company is prima facie exempt from local income tax and therefore is not required to hold a domestic TIN unless it carries on taxable activity within Samoa. If the entity makes taxable supplies, records export claims, or seeks input VAT recovery, it must submit the prescribed Business/Taxpayer Registration application to the Ministry for Revenue under the Tax Administration Act 2015, accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and proof of non-resident ownership from the Registrar of International Companies under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Samoan International Company is prima facie exempt from local income tax and therefore is not required to hold a domestic TIN unless it carries on taxable activity within Samoa. If the entity makes taxable supplies, records export claims, or seeks input VAT recovery, it must submit the prescribed Business/Taxpayer Registration application to the Ministry for Revenue under the Tax Administration Act 2015, accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and proof of non-resident ownership from the Registrar of International Companies under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists in Samoa. The International Company's offshore tax exemption is automatic. For indirect tax purposes, the entity may elect to register for VAGST on a voluntary basis to zero-rate exports and recover input tax; this election does not alter the entity-level tax-exempt classification under the International Companies Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoan Tala) of annual taxable supplies; voluntary VAGST registration is permitted below this threshold for exporters

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to a VAGST-registered supplier where tangible goods such as optical frames are physically exported from Samoa within the prescribed export disposition period, title passes to a non-resident purchaser, and the goods are consigned outside Samoa supported by Customs export entries. Offshore services qualify where the recipient belongs outside Samoa, the services are performed for the benefit of that foreign recipient, and no local value is enjoyed in Samoa. All zero-rated claims must be supported by contemporaneous commercial and banking records.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a licensed Samoan bank
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin where requested by the buyer or correspondent bank
  • VAGST export claim schedule reconciling each zero-rated invoice to foreign exchange receipts

No mandatory electronic invoicing, no fiscal clearance API, and no digital stamping requirement. A registered business may issue VAT invoices electronically as long as content satisfies the VAGST invoice requirements. Tax records must be retained for five years; the Ministry for Revenue continues to roll out online return filing, but there is no real-time tax-authority invoice clearance.

Samoa conducts risk-based audits of export zero-rating, including source-of-funds review. If export documentation or forex repatriation evidence is deficient, the claimed zero rate is reversed and VAGST is assessed at 15% on the full invoice value, with late payment penalties and statutory interest. Intentional evasion or false substantiation triggers shortfall penalties up to 150%, criminal fines, and imprisonment under the Tax Administration Act 2015, and may additionally jeopardize the International Company's regulatory licence and exempt-status renewal.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is convertible for bona fide current account transactions on a documentary basis through authorised dealer banks. Capital account transactions require prior approval from the Central Bank of Samoa on a case-by-case basis. Profit and dividend remittances are permitted after audited financial statements are submitted and applicable dividend withholding tax has been deducted, but no blanket conversion right exists for non-resident shareholders. International Companies operating under the Samoan International Company regime must channel all foreign exchange through a licensed commercial bank and cannot access offshore EMI platforms due to the strict exchange control framework.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act 1970 and applicable Central Bank of Samoa directives, exporters must repatriate all export proceeds to Samoa within 90 days from the date of shipment. 100% of foreign currency export receipts must be offered for conversion into WST to an authorised dealer within 30 days of receipt, unless the Central Bank grants a specific exemption for retentions or working capital. Supporting documentation including commercial invoices, bills of lading/airway bills, packing lists, and customs declarations must be retained and produced to the authorised dealer for verification.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Samoa does not maintain a fintech or Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs, virtual account providers, and non-bank payment companies are not recognised by the Central Bank of Samoa and cannot operate as authorised dealers. Remittances routed through such entities are treated as high-risk and are generally rejected by Samoan commercial banks, creating a mandatory reliance on SWIFT through licensed correspondent banks.

Defense evidence: Signed purchase orders, supplier contracts, itemised commercial invoices under HS code 9003, Bills of Lading or Airway Bills, customs export declarations, warehouse receipt and inventory movement reports, and detailed reconciliation between trade invoices and shipping evidence.

Defense evidence: Corporate governance minutes authorising the specific payment, employment contracts and remuneration policies, independent supplier verification letters, third-party bank references, source-of-wealth statement for all personal-account touches, and audited audit trail tying every payment to the underlying eyewear trade transaction.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The parent shareholder must remit via SWIFT MT103 from a qualifying corporate bank account to the International Company's USD or WST account at an authorised dealer in Samoa. Payment narrative must clearly state 'Share subscription – capital contribution' with the Central Bank of Samoa purpose code for inbound equity investment. The receiving bank will require a board resolution approving the capital injection, an updated register of members, a source-of-funds statement from the remitting shareholder, and a certificate of good standing from the Samoa International Companies Registry.
