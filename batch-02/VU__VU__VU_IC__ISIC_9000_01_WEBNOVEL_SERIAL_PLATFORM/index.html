Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs & Inland Revenue Department (CIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu; company formation administered by Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), https://www.vfsc.vu/

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is statutorily exempt from Vanuatu income/corporate/withholding taxes and exchange control under the International Companies Act 1993 (Cap 222), so no national income tax ID is issued to the entity; the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation constitutes its tax-exempt registration identity. Should the IC import goods into Vanuatu or make local taxable supplies through a Vanuatu branch, it must apply to CIRD for VAT registration on Form VR-01, submitting the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office lease evidence, memorandum/articles, director and shareholder particulars, and bank account confirmation; CIRD issues a VAT registration number within 7-10 business days. For the described offshore-only webnovel subscription model with revenue settled through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing, no VAT/TIN registration is triggered unless Vanuatu importation (e.g., office equipment, software hardware) occurs, in which case customs entry handling may require a temporary importer ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone; ICs are exempt for 20 years from registration from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, and exchange-control requirements (International Companies Act 1993, Cap 222, s.54), including no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no stamp duty on IC instruments; the entity is not a tax resident for income tax purposes since Vanuatu has no general income tax regime; the IC is permitted only offshore business activity and cannot carry on business with Vanuatu residents without VFSC consent.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, CFC, hybrid-mismatch, or entity classification election exists in Vanuatu because the system levies no income tax; an IC may voluntarily elect to register for VAT under the Value Added Tax Act No. 10 of 2013 if engaging in local taxable supplies (this election is irrevocable for 24 months), may elect to redomicile into a foreign jurisdiction, or may convert to a local ordinary company under the Companies Act 2009 (Cap 191); the tax-exempt status is automatic and mandatory for ICs, not elective; foreign status of beneficial owners is evidenced at incorporation and does not require annual tax elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory for any person carrying on a taxable activity with annual taxable turnover of VT 5,000,000 or more (approx. USD 41,700); registration is also mandated for importers on the first import unless exempt; offshore digital sales by an IC to non-resident consumers do not count toward the Vanuatu turnover threshold because the supply is deemed made outside Vanuatu; voluntary registration is permitted for businesses below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% VAT applies to (i) goods exported from Vanuatu with proof of physical departure, and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident person who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply where the services are not connected with land or goods situated in Vanuatu (VAT Act No. 10 of 2013, Schedule 1). Subscription access to the serialized webnovel platform via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing qualifies as an offshore digital service provided the platform maintains contemporaneous evidence that each consumer's billing address, IP geolocation, app-store territory, and payment instrument are outside Vanuatu; app-store settlement reports showing the Apple/Google deduction of commissions and remittance to the IC are the primary evidence; no physical customs export declaration is required for intangible digital delivery.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CusDec) required only for physical goods supporting the platform (e.g., books, merchandise, or equipment)
  • Bill of Lading/Airway Bill evidencing physical export (for goods); for digital services, the app-store settlement report and server/geolocation logs substitute for carriage documents
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 confirmation evidencing receipt of subscription proceeds from Apple/Google treasury centers or foreign banks

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control regime is in force in Vanuatu; CIRD accepts standard paper or PDF tax invoices and does not require pre-approval of invoice numbering; registered suppliers must issue compliant tax invoices showing supplier VAT ID, invoice date, description, VAT-exclusive amount, VAT amount, and total; for zero-rated exports, the invoice must carry a statement of zero-rating with the export justification; Apple IAP and Google Play Billing commission statements are treated as standard settlement documentation, not subject to any Vanuatu electronic fiscal clearance.

CIRD may audit VAT and zero-rating claims for up to 5 fiscal years; fraudulent zero-rating declaration or deliberate mischaracterization of domestic supplies as exports attracts a penalty of up to 200% of the tax sought to be evaded plus interest at 18% per annum, and where the fraud involves forged customs or banking documents, criminal prosecution with fines up to VT 10,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years is possible; retrospective output VAT reassessment and disallowance of all associated input tax credits are applied for the entire audit period where the exporter cannot substantiate non-resident recipient status or overseas delivery; for digital platforms, failure to maintain app-store territory reports and payment-rail settlement records for 5 years creates a rebuttable presumption of domestic taxable supply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal foreign-exchange regime; the Vatu is convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions. There are no quantitative exchange-control limits on non-resident International Company accounts, and no general requirement for central bank licensing of inward equity injections. Profit and dividend remittance is permitted after the presenting bank completes source-of-funds, AML/KYC, and corporate-validity verification. Because the International Company is not a Vanuatu tax resident under the International Companies Act, no Vanuatu withholding taxes apply to such outbound remittances; the only practical hurdles are bank-level documentary requirements and the need to demonstrate that no local Vanuatu business is being conducted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Vanuatu does not impose any statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio. Digital platform subscription receipts, app-store proceeds, publishing royalties, and other cross-border service receipts may be retained in foreign currency in an authorised dealer account or converted to Vatu at the prevailing market rate, subject only to the presenting bank's AML and reporting obligations.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), operating under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act 1981 and the Financial Dealers Licensing Act; exchange control policy is administered by RBV for authorised banks and the Ministry of Finance retains statutory oversight functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium as a principal clearing rail. Vanuatu does not recognise any foreign EMI passporting regime. A non-resident digital EMI may be used as a supplementary payment aggregator to consolidate Apple IAP and Google Play Billing receivables, but any entity accepting deposits or conducting money transmission within Vanuatu must be licensed by the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu. The International Company should maintain its ultimate liquidity and disbursement account at an RBV-licensed commercial bank and must not rely on an offshore EMI as the primary treasury account.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (nil). Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax on any taxpayer class. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is additionally granted an express statutory income tax exemption by Section 34 of that Act, which provides that no tax is payable to the Government of Vanuatu in respect of the IC's income or profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Vanuatu levies no dividend withholding tax at either statutory or practical level. An International Company is free to declare and pay dividends to its non-resident shareholders from the webnovel platform's retained earnings without any Vanuatu tax deduction, clearance, or exchange-control approval.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

The annual return must be delivered to the registered agent on or before the anniversary date of incorporation in each year; the registered agent is thereafter obligated to submit the return to VFSC with the applicable annual government fee. No legislative grace period exists; late submissions attract late lodgement charges.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable (no statutory threshold exists). Vanuatu has no corporate income tax and no transfer-pricing legislation; the International Company exemption means no Local File, Master File, or country-by-country reporting threshold can arise under Vanuatu domestic law. In practice, foreign tax authorities of the shareholder's residence jurisdiction may impute arm's-length remuneration to the IC in respect of webnovel licensing, in-app purchase commissions, and API/data services intercompany flows; the foreign threshold (typically EUR 750M consolidated group revenue for TP documentation under OECD standards) will then govern the preparation of documentation.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted Economic Substance Regulations in the form adopted by the Cayman Islands or BVI. Nevertheless, the functional equivalent is enforced through the VFSC's beneficial ownership regime and the OECD's Harmful Tax Practices peer review. A VU_IC operating the webnovel serial platform must demonstrate real economic nexus where challenged by foreign tax authorities: (i) board meetings and strategic decision-making recorded in Vanuatu minutes; (ii) maintenance of books and records within the registered agent's custody or accessible from Vanuatu; (iii) a genuine Vanuatu-registered agent and office address not used solely as a mail-drop; and (iv) contractual substance for IT hosting, payment gateway, and content-licensing arrangements. Absent such substance, the IC may be reclassified as a shell conduit, and foreign CFC or controlled-foreign-corporation rules may attribute the subscription income to the ultimate non-resident shareholder.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent VAT Declarations

Statutory sanction: Under the VAT Act default surcharge rules: 5% monthly surcharge on unpaid tax, capped at 25%, plus interest. Criminal prosecution for false VAT statements carries a fine of up to Vt 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 2 years. VFSC may simultaneously deregister the IC, extinguishing the s.34 tax exemption and requiring restoration proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false VAT returns are subject to personal criminal prosecution; courts may impose restitution orders for unpaid VAT, 10-year director disqualification, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and imprisonment in cases of aggravated fraud where the amount concealed exceeds Vt 5,000,000.
Shell Company Abuse / Non-Filing of VFSC Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Late-lodgement monetary penalties (accumulating beyond Vt 50,000), administrative suspension, and strike-off from the International Companies Register. A struck-off IC loses all statutory powers, its assets vest in the custody of the VFSC, and its contracts become unenforceable until restoration with full penalty payment.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade under a struck-off name incur personal liability for all debts contracted after deregistration; criminal penalties include fines and up to 12 months' imprisonment, and the court may freeze the director's foreign bank accounts through mutual legal assistance requests initiated by the Vanuatu Public Prosecutor.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Violations

Statutory sanction: Enforcement under the Beneficial Ownership Act includes administrative fines scaled to the number of violations and penalty notices issued by the VFSC. In money-laundering cases, the Financial Intelligence Unit may order freezing of the merchant settlement accounts; the registered agent is obliged to file a suspicious transaction report and may terminate its agency, forcing the IC into non-compliance and automatic strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate beneficial owners face criminal liability with fines and imprisonment for MLAT-assisted offences; the corporate veil is pierced to permit confiscation of the unlawfully concealed platform revenues, and a travel ban / stop-order can be issued pending asset-return undertakings.
Substance Deficiency / Foreign Recharacterization as a Deviator from Arm's-Length Conduct

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu imposes no direct financial sanction on the IC for foreign recharacterization. The operative sanction is extra-statutory and external: the foreign revenue authority reassesses the ultimate shareholder's tax base, applies transfer-pricing adjustments and penalty rates of up to 200% of the underpaid foreign tax, and denies any credit for the IC's zero Vanuatu tax. Fatal contractual consequence: the foreign group may be blacklisted from regulated app-store payout programs for a sham corporate structure.

Director personal liability: Under foreign anti-abuse doctrines, the IC's income is attributed directly to the controlling directors as undisclosed beneficiaries; personal deficiency penalties, U.S. FBAR-style civil fraud penalties, criminal tax referrals, and international travel restrictions may be imposed; in the EU branch of the activity, disqualification orders prohibit the directors from acting as managers of any digital-commerce entity for up to 5 years.
