Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MfR), Samoa — statutory tax administrator under the Tax Administration Act 2012, the Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) Act 1992 and the Customs Act 2014; headquartered at Apia with the official taxpayer e-Services portal (eMfR) at www.revenue.gov.ws. Samoa Customs Service (export/import clearance, ASYCUDA World) operates within the MfR.

E-government filing portal

Samoa International Companies (ICs) under the International Companies Act 1987 are automatically tax-exempt on all international business and are not required to hold a Samoan Taxpayer Registration Number (TRN) for pure offshore lithium carbonate procurement/re-invoicing (ISIC 4662 / NAICS 423510) because no income is sourced in Samoa and no taxable activity is carried out there. Where a domestic nexus is created (local warehousing, domestic sales, resident employees, or imports for local consumption): (1) submit the MfR TRN application with the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles, Certificate of Good Standing, registered agent/office confirmation and the IC's tax exemption status documents; (2) verify identity through the eMfR/eServices portal; (3) file VAGST registration if Samoan taxable supplies reach WST 100,000; (4) receive TRN and VAGST certificate from MfR. Foreign non-resident companies without a Samoan fixed establishment may obtain VAGST registration only through a Samoan tax representative authorized by MfR.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

International Companies Act 1987 grants ICs an automatic, renewable 20-year guaranteed exemption from Samoan income tax, VAGST, stamp duties, withholding taxes and exchange control on non-Samoan-sourced business; no Samoan domestic entity-classification election exists because the exemption is statutory. For U.S. federal tax purposes, a wholly-owned Samoan IC elects its classification via IRS Form 8832 check-the-box (corporation by default; disregarded entity or partnership available), filed before the first U.S. tax return. Hybrid-mismatch considerations: the IC may operate as a reverse-hybrid conduit but must be documented against OECD BEPS Action 2 and Samoan General Anti-Avoidance Rules (Tax Administration Act 2012) to preserve the tax-exempt position in cross-border battery-metals arbitrage.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAGST zero-rating is restricted to registered persons supplying: (a) goods physically exported from Samoa within the statutory time limit (~28 days from supply, extendable by MfR) supported by customs export evidence; (b) international transport services; (c) services supplied to non-residents for consumption wholly outside Samoa. The target IC (cross-border procurement of battery-grade lithium carbonate powder, goods moving directly from third-country producer to downstream buyer without entering Samoa) makes no supply in Samoa and has no VAGST nexus: no zero-rating claim, refund or credit mechanism is engaged because no Samoan output tax arises on offshore arbitrage margin. If goods are ever physically introduced into Samoa and re-exported, the IC would need VAGST registration and full export substantiation to apply 0%; otherwise the supply is taxed at 10%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic via ASYCUDA World, Samoa Customs Service / MfR)
  • Commercial Invoice (HS 2836.91 / ISIC 4662 description, incoterms, WST or USD value)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (proof of physical export from Samoa when applicable)
  • Certificate of Origin (only where preferential trade treatment is claimed)
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / foreign-exchange receive evidence (for Samoan-registered exporters and applicable exchange permits)
  • VAGST export certificate or zero-rating substantiation memorandum maintained for MfR post-clearance audit

Samoa does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, e-invoicing APIs, or digital stamping. Binding digital obligations: (i) e-filing of VAGST returns and tax payments via the MfR eServices/eMfR portal; (ii) electronic customs declarations and manifests through ASYCUDA World for any physical Samoan import/export; (iii) prescribed VAGST tax invoice content (issuer TRN, recipient TRN where registered, supply description, date, quantity/price and VAGST amount), with electronic records retention subject to MfR audit access.

Samoa enforces export zero-rating through MfR post-clearance audit and ASYCUDA data analytics. Penalties for fraud or sham export/re-invoicing: (i) civil shortfall penalties of 50%-150% of tax avoided under the Tax Administration Act 2012; (ii) late-payment interest at the prescribed statutory rate plus a 20% late-filing penalty; (iii) criminal liability under the VAGST Act 1992 and Customs Act 2014 — fines up to WST 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to seven years for fraudulent evasion; (iv) retrospective clawback of all zero-rated benefits, denial/recapture of input credits and re-assessment of prior periods at 10% VAGST; (v) suspension of eServices/ASYCUDA privileges, increased audit frequency, and enforcement against tax representatives who sign false export certificates. Exchange-control cross-checks expect receipt of export proceeds within 180 days (unless formally extended); tax-exempt ICs are outside Samoa exchange-control requirements but should hold their exemption certificate and documentary chain (purchase order, supplier invoices, shipping records, SWIFT/LC settlements) to rebut any sham-arbitrage assertion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tala (WST) is partially convertible. Current-account transactions and normal trade payments are permitted through CBS-licensed authorized dealers, but capital-account transfers, external borrowing, and non-trade remittances require CBS approval or registration. Profit and dividend remittance offshore by an International Company is generally permitted after audit and tax clearance; remittance requests above reporting thresholds require CBS exchange-control notification and evidence of an underlying trade or capital transaction. For the battery-grade lithium carbonate procurement vehicle, USD-denominated receipts should be retained in foreign-currency accounts and settled outside Samoa where possible to minimize WST conversion friction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa's Exchange Control Act 1976 imposes a 30-day window after export proceeds are credited to a Samoan bank account for an exporter to surrender foreign currency to an authorized dealer; the mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of export proceeds for goods physically exported from Samoa customs territory. Where the International Company operates as an offshore trading intermediary and the lithium carbonate is delivered from a third-country refinery directly to an overseas buyer, no surrender obligation arises in Samoa provided settlement is completed through an approved foreign-currency account and reported as an offshore trading receipt. If proceeds are repatriated into Samoa, a written CBS exemption is recommended to avoid the automatic 100% WST conversion requirement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Exchange Control Division, Apia, Samoa

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as the primary settlement rail. Samoa has no local EMI digital banking license and the Central Bank does not treat foreign EMIs as authorized dealers. Platforms such as Wise, Airwallex, or global neo-banks may be used only as auxiliary collection or payment channels for foreign-currency disbursements outside the Samoan regulatory perimeter, provided legal title to funds remains in the International Company's account at a CBS-licensed correspondent bank. EMI accounts inside the Samoa IC structure are not viable for LC issuance, import settlement, or regulatory filings.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No fixed statutory threshold is published in Samoa for International Companies. The Commissioner of Taxation may call for contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation under the Tax Administration Act 2012 whenever cross-border related-party transactions are material; in practice, an IC claiming tax exemption with annual lithium carbonate invoicing exceeding WST 100,000 (approximately USD 38,000) must be able to substantiate arm's length margins. The Economic Substance Regulations 2020 additionally mandate retention of a substance file regardless of turnover.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations 2020, enacted under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2020, require every tax-exempt International Company carrying on a relevant activity, including a distribution and service centre, to demonstrate: (i) direction and management in Samoa evidenced by board meetings held in Samoa and strategic decisions made from Samoa; (ii) adequate physical premises, employees, and expenditure proportionate to the activity; and (iii) core income generating activities physically performed in Samoa. A battery metals arbitrage IC that contracts offshore, holds title offshore, and books margin in Samoa without local management commits a per se substance failure; non-IC resident companies are similarly exposed where their exemption claim generates taxable profit in a foreign parent jurisdiction under CFC or substance-over-form rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure (Shell Company)

Statutory sanction: Fixed penalty of WST 10,000 and daily penalty of WST 1,000 up to WST 200,000 under the ESR Regulations; mandatory strike-off and deregistration by the Registrar; referral of the IC's particulars to the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices; reassessment of foreign-source income at the 27% corporate rate where the exemption is pierced.

Director personal liability: Each director is jointly and severally liable for the ESR penalty and reassessed tax where they knowingly permitted non-compliance; director disqualification for up to 10 years; international travel bans under the Samoa Absconding Debtors Act; criminal conviction for false ESR declaration carries imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may adjust taxable income under the general anti-avoidance provisions of the Income Tax Ordinance 2015 at 27%, impose a 50% shortfall penalty under the Tax Administration Act 2012, and apply monthly interest of 1%; criminal tax evasion prosecutions attract fines up to WST 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 3 years per count; parallel CFC and anti-abuse adjustments in China and Hong Kong create double taxation exposure and late-payment surcharges abroad.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve mispriced purchase or resale invoices are personally liable for the resulting tax shortfall under the vicarious liability provisions of the Tax Administration Act 2012; civil restitution of the mispriced margin; cross-border asset freezing orders; extradition risk on egregious fraud.
AML and Trade-Based Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018 of Samoa: corporate fines up to WST 1,000,000, freezing orders and forfeiture of arbitrage proceeds, suspension or revocation of the licensed agent's registration, and inclusion of the IC on the Samoan Financial Intelligence Unit suspicious activity watchlist with consequential loss of correspondent banking access.

Director personal liability: Directors face imprisonment of up to 7 years for money laundering predicate offences; civil restitution orders against personal estates; asset preservation orders; international travel bans; exclusion from future directorships in Samoa and DTA partner states; regulatory information sharing with HKMA and FATCA exchange networks.
Fraudulent Trading and Abuse of Corporate Form

Statutory sanction: Under s.181 of the International Companies Act 1987 and the Companies Act 2001, the Court may order any director knowingly party to fraudulent trading to be personally responsible for all debts and obligations of the IC, including reassessed tax and penalties; the IC may be wound up and struck off; restraint orders may issue over Swift LC and wire transfer proceeds.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution equal to the full amount of the misapplied arbitrage margin; director disqualification orders of up to 10 years; travel bans; criminal liability for fraud with imprisonment of up to 7 years; personal liability extends to shadow directors and de facto controllers resident in Hong Kong or China even without Samoan nationality.
