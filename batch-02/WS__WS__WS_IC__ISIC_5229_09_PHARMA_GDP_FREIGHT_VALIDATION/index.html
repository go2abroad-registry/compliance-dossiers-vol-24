Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity shall apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the prescribed application form to the Ministry for Revenue. The application must be submitted within 30 days after the entity becomes liable for any Samoa tax, or before making its first taxable supply for VAGST. The entity must submit certified copies of its certificate of incorporation, registered office confirmation, director identification documents, and an authorised business activity declaration. The TIN and, if applicable, VAGST registration certificate are issued upon verification; foreign entities may apply in person, by mail, or through a licensed tax agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the International Companies Act 1987, the tax exemption is automatic and does not require an election. Samoa domestic law does not provide a check-the-box mechanism. Voluntary VAGST registration is possible where a person makes or intends to make taxable supplies below the mandatory threshold. Samoa has no specific hybrid mismatch election legislation under OECD BEPS.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 500,000 (Samoan Tala and Sene) in taxable turnover within a 12-month period; non-resident suppliers of digital services to Samoa may be subject to special registration thresholds as prescribed by the Ministry for Revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported from Samoa within 30 days of supply, and to services supplied to non-resident persons where the services are consumed entirely outside Samoa, including international freight and related GDP logistics services. The taxpayer must retain evidence of export, such as customs endorsement, bills of lading, or air waybills, and demonstrate that the recipient is not resident in Samoa and is not registered for VAGST in Samoa. For freight services, the supply must relate to transportation of goods between Samoa and a foreign country, or where transport commences or terminates in Samoa and the recipient is a non-resident.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate requires an API-based submission or digital stamping. Tax invoices must satisfy the prescribed particulars under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2003, including supplier TIN, recipient TIN, invoice date, description of supply, consideration, and VAGST amount. The Ministry for Revenue accepts paper invoices and electronic invoices issued through approved commercial systems; no fiscal stamp or real-time clearance API is currently in force.

Fraudulent claims for export zero-rating or refunds attract a penalty of up to 200% of the evaded tax, a monetary penalty not exceeding WST 50,000, and/or imprisonment for a term up to three years under the Tax Administration Act 2019. Retrospective clawback applies to any refund paid in respect of a supply subsequently found not to satisfy zero-rating conditions, together with statutory interest at the prescribed rate. Customs and revenue audits verify export endorsements, foreign currency repatriation, and supporting transactional records.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a managed-float regime. Current-account payments, including consultancy fees, freight, insurance, and similar bona fide service transactions, are normally market-convertible upon documentary verification. Profit remittance requires exchange-control authorization when the entity is a Samoan resident; an International Company incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987 is exempt from Samoan exchange control on foreign-currency transactions, provided it does not trade with Samoan residents and maintains comprehensive beneficial ownership and source-of-funds records. Capital-account convertibility is selective: inbound direct investment and share subscriptions generally require no separate CBS permit for an International Company, but outward capital repatriation and conversion of surplus WST assets require an exchange-control authorization and an up-to-date tax-exemption certificate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

A resident exporter must repatriate export proceeds within 90 days from shipment and surrender foreign currency to an authorized dealer for conversion into WST unless a CBS exemption or a retained-foreign-currency-account permission is granted. Non-resident International Companies used for GDP pharma freight are generally outside the resident surrender rule; export income may remain in designated USD, EUR, or WST accounts if the licensed bank files a standing exemption certificate with CBS and maintains fully documented trade files.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), acting also as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1979 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1980; the Minister of Finance retains residual licensing and exemption powers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Samoa does not have a dedicated electronic-money institution licence, and CBS does not permit standalone fintech EMIs to participate directly in the WST clearing settlement. A licensed cross-border EMI can act only as a channelling payment provider passing funds into a real Samoan bank account, not as a clearing-rail settlement institution. Any offshore EMI balance should be treated strictly as credit exposure, not as alternative liquidity infrastructure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire transfers routed through an authorised dealer with clear ISO 20022 purpose codes such as INVEST for direct-investment capital contributions or TRAD for trade facilitation. No WST bearer instruments and no local cash injections are acceptable for an International Company foreign-currency account. The receiving bank requires the share-subscription board resolution, a written capital-introduction letter from the beneficial owner, the Samoan registered agent's AML file, and confirmation that the funds are not sourced from a jurisdiction under FATF monitoring. Funds should pass directly to the licensed IC bank account; intermediary chains should be limited to no more than one correspondent hop and any offshore EMI must be disclosed as a payment processor rather than a settlement bank.Pharmaceutical freight suppliers should be settled by irrevocable documentary letter of credit issued through a Samoan licensed bank and transmitted by SWIFT MT700, or by a direct SWIFT MT103 with full beneficiary due diligence and all supporting trade-contract references. For domestic Samoan pharmacies, local WST obligations are cleared through the Samoan interbank clearing system using Direct ACH/credit transfer in WST. Cross-border supplier payments remain dependent on the SWIFT correspondent banking network, and every MT103 must carry the purchase order, LC reference, and GDP freight-tracking reference to avoid post-transaction exchange-control queries.Plain-vanilla USD/WST forward contracts with an authorised dealer, maturity up to 12 monthsNon-deliverable WST forwards (NDFs) executed offshore with approved financial institutions when local WST forward liquidity is insufficientCross-currency swaps between USD, EUR, and WST for medium-term treasury receivables and payablesCostless option collars, subject to prior CBS notification for resident entities and internal bank credit approval for International Companies

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 1,000,000 annual aggregate controlled transactions (for resident companies under Samoa's Transfer Pricing Regulations 2012). ICs are not subject to CIT TP documentation; however, if the IC is an ESR-resident entity for substance purposes, related-party transactions must be reported to the Registrar for substance assessment.

Economic substance scope: Yes. Samoa's Economic Substance Regulations 2020 (ESR) apply to International Companies carrying on 'relevant activities.' The GDP clinical logistics and pharma freight business qualifies as a 'distribution and service centre' activity if revenues are derived from related-party logistics services. The IC must maintain in Samoa: (i) a physical office or premises; (ii) full-time employees with appropriate GDP and logistics qualifications; (iii) CORE income-generating activities (dispatch, quality control, temperature validation) managed in Samoa; and (iv) operating expenditure proportionate to the level of activity. An annual ESR notification and report must be filed with the Registrar; failure attracts penalties and automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Misuse of IC Exemption)

Statutory sanction: Loss of tax exemption; retroactive reassessment at 27% corporate tax on all Samoa-source profits; penalty of 150% of unpaid tax under s.32 Taxes Administration Act 2015; criminal fine of up to WST 200,000.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, interest, and penalties; the Ministry of Revenue may issue a certificate of debt and impose a travel ban; conviction for tax evasion carries imprisonment of up to 5 years.
Economic Substance Non-Compliance (Shell Company)

Statutory sanction: Penalties from WST 20,000 to WST 100,000 per annum per relevant activity; the Registrar may strike off the IC after 6 months of non-compliance; information is automatically exchanged with EU and OECD member states.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are personally liable for the ESR penalty; disqualification from acting as director in Samoa for 5 years; if the activity is also linked to money laundering, criminal liability extends to imprisonment for up to 10 years.
AML / Sanctions Violation (Dual-Use Pharma Cargo)

Statutory sanction: Seizure and forfeiture of cargo under the Customs Management Act 2016; monetary penalty up to WST 500,000 or three times the value of the goods; revocation of the GDP freight logistics licence by the Samoa Pharmacy and Poisons Board.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly involved face criminal liability under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act 2018, including imprisonment of 3 to 7 years and a personal restitution order for all proceeds traceable to the shipment.
Missing Registered Agent / Failure to File Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Monthly penalty of WST 500 per month; automatic strike-off from the International Companies Register; assets of the company escheat to the Government of Samoa.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable to indemnify the company for any loss caused by the default; a court may issue a restitution order and a travel prohibition for entry into Samoa.
