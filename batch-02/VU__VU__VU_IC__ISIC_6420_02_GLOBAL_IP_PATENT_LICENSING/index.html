Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Vanuatu; e-service portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no income-tax TIN requirement, and an International Company (IC) is statutorily tax-exempt. A VAT/TIN registration number is obtained from DCIR using Form IRV-001 via the ePortal when aggregate taxable supplies reach VT 10,000,000 in any rolling 12-month period or when voluntary registration is elected. Foreign applicants file a certified copy of the certificate of incorporation, registered-agent certificate, director and ultimate beneficial owner passport copies, and a declaration of intended supplies; the licensed registered agent facilitates filing, and DCIR issues the VAT/TIN number within 7–14 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Vanuatu imposes no income-tax TIN requirement, and an International Company (IC) is statutorily tax-exempt. A VAT/TIN registration number is obtained from DCIR using Form IRV-001 via the ePortal when aggregate taxable supplies reach VT 10,000,000 in any rolling 12-month period or when voluntary registration is elected. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist. The IC automatically enjoys the statutory offshore tax exemption under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], covering income, withholding, capital gains, and duties. It may elect in its constitution to become an ordinary Vanuatu company, thereby forfeiting the exemption, and may voluntarily elect VAT registration before the statutory threshold; no hybrid entity election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 aggregate taxable supplies (including zero-rated export supplies) in any rolling 12-month period, approximately USD 83,000; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated when physically exported from Vanuatu within 60 days of supply and evidenced by customs documentation. Services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident recipient located outside Vanuatu and the benefit is enjoyed wholly outside Vanuatu; accordingly, cross-border royalty and patent sub-licensing streams invoiced by the IC to foreign licensees qualify for 0% VAT. Exempt financial and offshore services remain outside the VAT net.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World) for goods; zero-rated services schedule in VAT return for IP royalties
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and underlying patent/trademark licence or sub-licence agreement identifying the non-resident licensee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT credit advice evidencing receipt of the royalty from outside Vanuatu

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital-seal regime is in force as of the classification date. Registered taxpayers must issue full VAT tax invoices within 21 days of supply, retain records in the DCIR ePortal, and comply with phased e-invoicing guidance; ASYCUDA World is used for customs declarations.

DCIR audits export evidence, licence agreements, and inward payment flows. Incorrect zero-rating attracts a late-payment penalty of 25% and misdeclaration penalties of up to 100% of the tax avoided; fraudulent zero-rating claims are criminal offences punishable by fine and imprisonment, with retrospective assessments unlimited in cases of fraud or wilful misrepresentation. The Commissioner may claw back VAT, penalties, and interest from the entity and, where applicable, its directors.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalised exchange control environment for non-resident-owned International Companies. Current account payments, including royalty and licence fee repatriations, are freely convertible without prior RBV approval. Capital account inflows and outflows, including shareholder equity injections and capital returns, are also generally unrestricted for ICs; however, practical hurdles arise from the limited depth of the VUV/USD market, which requires banks to pre-book large conversions and to apply the RBV-sanctioned dealing rates. No profit remittance tax or capital gains tax applies to an IP HoldCo, but the bank will require a complete economic-substance and source-of-funds record before processing large licence-related payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu does not impose a fixed statutory timeline for export proceeds surrender, and there is no mandatory conversion ratio for foreign-currency export receipts. A Vanuatu International Company may retain royalty and licensing income in original currency, usually USD, in a clearing account. Where a local VUV transaction is necessary, conversion occurs at the commercial bank's prevailing buying rate. The licensing entity is not subject to any requirement to convert export receipts into VUV, provided the funds are used for legitimate offshore treasury purposes.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Vanuatu does not have a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime, and foreign offshore digital EMIs cannot obtain deposit-taking or clearing status under the RBV banking supervision framework. A foreign EMI may be used only as a supplementary payment channel after the core treasury banking relationship is established; all inbound and outbound funds must remain in the name of the actual Vanuatu International Company to avoid creating an unlicensed money-services business. The primary clearing route must remain a tier-1 licensed domestic bank with correspondent banking relationships.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections should be routed as a single SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's or parent entity's own account, strictly in the corporate shareholder's legal name, with the remit as 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION' or 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' and the Vanuatu entity's registered account reference. The bank will code the transaction under its internal capital-contribution purpose category and will require a board resolution, updated share register, investor KYC pack, and a registered-agent statutory declaration confirming the IC's good standing. If the injection is received in USD and the operating account is USD-denominated, no FX conversion occurs; if a VUV conversion is needed, the domestic bank's FX ticket and dealing confirmation must be preserved as part of the treasury audit trail.Royalty and sub-licensing fees are cleared principally through SWIFT MT103 payment instructions from the Vanuatu entity's USD account to the beneficiary's bank. For higher-value licence transactions, the beneficiary's importing bank may require a documentary credit; in such cases, a Commercial Letter of Credit issued under UCP600 from a licensed banking institution can be used as collateral-backed settlement rail, usually through the Vanuatu bank's correspondent network in Australia, Singapore, or Hong Kong. All MT103s must carry the underlying licence agreement reference, quote number, and breakdown of principal and taxes, with the outbound purpose narrative describing the applicable patent or trademark royalty settlement.Permissible forwards: plain-vanilla VUV/USD and VUV/EUR outright forwards with an RBV-authorised dealing bank, up to 12 months, supported by documented future royalty cash-flow exposure and an approved treasury hedging policy.Permissible NDFs: non-deliverable VUV/USD forwards executed with an international banker only where offshore VUV liquidity is insufficient, provided the underlying exposure is evidenced and no speculative open position is created.Permissible currency swaps: fixed-to-fixed cross-currency swaps matched to the principal and interest or royalty payment schedule of the licence treasury plan, subject to the domestic bank's credit approval and to a board-approved match-funding structure.Permissible FX options: vanilla annual or semi-annual US/foreign currency options may be used defensively to cap royalty-conversion exposure, but only after the licensed bank confirms the derivative is valid under RBV regulatory expectations and the transaction passes suitability review.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Vanuatu statutory threshold exists (VUV 0 - the regime does not legislate transfer pricing). Practical group-level threshold: maintain a contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation package (Local File and benchmarking study) whenever annual intercompany royalty flows exceed VUV 12,000,000 (approx. USD 100,000), to support the foreign parent's deduction and CFC defence.

Economic substance scope: Applicable - Vanuatu's economic substance legislation (enacted 2021 under the International Companies Act framework and administered by the VFSC) classifies IP holding and licensing as a 'relevant activity' attracting the full substance test. The IC must annually demonstrate: (i) management and control exercised in Vanuatu (quorate board meetings physically held in Vanuatu with minutes); (ii) adequate physical premises; (iii) adequate qualified employees performing all core income-generating activities (CIGA); and (iv) operating expenditure in Vanuatu proportionate to IP-derived income. The reduced equity-holding test does not apply to IP HoldCos. The ESR return is filed annually; outside the ESR framework, the pre-2021 rule exempting ICs from all taxes remains in force.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Sham Transaction (Tax Evasion Pattern)

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu: ESR assessment with penalty up to VUV 5,000,000, daily default fines, and strike-off; VFSC may also report the IC to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU). Parent jurisdiction: the entire royalty flow may be recharacterised as a sham dividend or as attributable to the parent's permanent establishment, triggering full corporate tax, interest, and 20-40% accuracy penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign knowingly false ESR/UBO filings face fines up to VUV 10,000,000 and/or 2 years' imprisonment; in the foreign parent state, courts may pierce the corporate veil and impose civil restitution, travel bans, and criminal tax-fraud sentences on the parent's officers.
Beneficial Ownership / AML Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Fines under Vanuatu's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act up to VUV 10,000,000 for the entity and up to 5 years' imprisonment for officers; the FIU may freeze the IC's SWIFT accounts and require production of all licence agreements and underlying IP registrations.

Director personal liability: Knowing contravention triggers director-level prosecution, personal fines, asset-freezing orders, immigration watchlisting and travel bans, and disqualification from acting as a director in any VFSC-regulated entity.
Prohibited Domestic Business Activity

Statutory sanction: Revocation of IC registration; the entity is reclassified as a domestic company under the Companies Act and becomes potentially liable to Vanuatu VAT (12.5%), business licence fees, stamp duty, and penalties in addition to fines for the unauthorised activity.

Director personal liability: Officers knowingly permitting domestic business may be personally liable for the unpaid taxes and penalties of the company and for criminal fines; the VFSC may impose director bans and seek civil recovery.
Transfer Pricing Distortion / Royalty Over-Charge

Statutory sanction: No Vanuatu CIT consequence (zero-rate), but the foreign tax authority will adjust the royalty deduction, recharacterise the excess as an equity contribution or payment for intangibles, impose 10-20% accuracy penalties, and may assert accrued withholding or diverted-income taxes.

Director personal liability: Signing officers of the group that prepare or rely on unsupported break-up valuations risk civil restitution of the underpaid foreign taxes and criminal exposure for tax evasion where false pricing documents are knowingly produced.
