Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila, Vanuatu; official portal: https://customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, or annual corporate tax; therefore an International Company (IC) obtains no income-tax TIN at incorporation. The sole statutory indirect-tax identifier is the VAT registration number issued by DCIR under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap 246]. Compulsory registration arises only if the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu and annual taxable turnover reaches VUV 5,000,000; an offshore IC selling data/API services exclusively to non-residents remains outside the VAT net and does not register. Procedure: file VAT-01 registration form, certificate of incorporation issued by Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), certified passport copies of directors and beneficial owners, proof of registered agent address in Vanuatu, and a statutory declaration confirming offshore status under s.33 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222]; DCIR issues the VAT number within 5–10 business days. Imported services consumed in Vanuatu are reverse-charged by the local recipient (no non-resident supplier registration required).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — an IC certified under s.133 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222] enjoys absolute exemption from Vanuatu income tax, VAT, stamp duty, customs duties, and all transaction-based taxes for 20 years from incorporation, renewable; all foreign-source API/data revenue is territorially exempt; the IC may not carry on domestic trade with Vanuatu residents without VFSC written consent, preserving zero domestic fiscal nexus.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch mechanism exists in Vanuatu. The operative statutory election is the renewable 20-year tax-exemption certificate issued ex officio by the VFSC at incorporation. The IC may irrevocably elect to convert to a domestic company (thereby becoming VAT-registrable and subject to domestic law) only with VFSC approval; absent such approval no further classification election is available or required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover (approx. USD 42,000) for goods and services supplied in Vanuatu; imported services used in Vanuatu are reverse-charged by the local recipient without supplier registration; cross-border data/API services supplied to non-residents do not count toward the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (i) goods physically exported from Vanuatu and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident person where the services relate to movable or offshore property located outside Vanuatu or are wholly consumed outside Vanuatu (VAT Act [Cap 246] s.6 and Schedule 3). A commercial web-scraping/proxy API subscription sold to non-resident clients qualifies as an export of services invoiced at 0%, provided supporting evidence demonstrates overseas consumption: non-resident contractual counterparty, foreign billing address, delivery through non-VU servers/IP nodes, and absence of domestic usage. No VAT refund is needed because no output VAT is charged on qualifying exports; input VAT incurred by the exempt IC is not creditable but is immaterial given the IC's zero domestic input exposure. Vanuatu maintains no exchange controls; the 180-day treasury convention for inward remittance is applied as a banking-standard evidentiary norm rather than a statutory repatriation deadline.

  • VU Customs Export Declaration (electronic lodgement via Vanuatu National Single Window)
  • Commercial/Pro-forma Invoice showing 0% VAT
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical-goods exports; not applicable to pure API/data services)
  • Signed contract/service order with non-resident counterparty (for data/API services)
  • Delivery and consumption evidence: server/IP geolocation reports, API call logs, and client certification of offshore use
  • Bank Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT receipt evidencing foreign-currency settlement (administrative FX record)

None — Vanuatu operates no mandatory e-invoicing regime, no real-time fiscal clearance platform, no government API clearance gateway, and no digital fiscal stamping. DCIR accepts self-issued paper or PDF tax invoices containing the statutory content (seller name, address and VAT number; buyer name and address; invoice number and date; description; quantity; unit price; VAT rate 0% or 12.5%; VAT amount; total); retained records must substantiate any zero-rating claim.

DCIR audit window is 3 years, extended to 6 years in cases of fraud or wilful evasion (VAT Act [Cap 246] s.30). Wrongful zero-rating or VAT evasion attracts a civil penalty of 200% of the tax evaded plus interest at 12.5% per annum, and individuals are liable on conviction to imprisonment of up to 5 years; any incorrectly paid refund is clawed back immediately with interest. For ICs, breach of s.33 of the International Companies Act (domestic trading without VFSC consent) voids the s.133 tax exemption retrospectively from the date of breach, exposing the entity to 12.5% VAT on all domestic taxable supplies plus a civil penalty of up to VUV 500,000; renewal of the 20-year exemption certificate is conditioned on a clean compliance review.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Vanuatu maintains no general exchange-control regime applicable to International Companies, and there is no prior RBV approval requirement for equity injections, dividend remittances, service fees, or liquidation proceeds. Non-resident-owned ICs may hold multi-currency accounts with resident licensed banks and freely remit funds abroad, subject only to bank-level KYC/AML due diligence and record retention. Profit repatriation is unrestricted and is not subject to any withholding tax under Vanuatu law.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender regime exists under the Reserve Bank Act or the International Companies Act. There is no mandatory conversion ratio, no forced repatriation period, and no requirement to sell foreign currency proceeds to an RBV-designated dealer. The only compliance obligations are transactional record keeping, reasonable invoice/contract retention, and accurate disclosure to the resident receiving bank for AML and sanctions screening purposes.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), acting jointly in an oversight capacity with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for International Company licensing, AML/CFT supervision, and financial-sector fit-and-proper controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Risk but legally permissible. Vanuatu has no domestic FinTech or EMI licensing regime; therefore cross-border digital EMI account providers may onboard the Vanuatu IC solely as a non-resident corporate client. Such providers must hold a valid e-money or payment-institution license in a recognized FATF jurisdiction, must apply full CDD, and must not be used for VUV clearing. An EMI account can function as a receiving rail for Stripe gateway settlements and as a conversion gateway for USDC, but the principal treasury and counterparty relationship should remain with a RBV-licensed tier-1 bank to satisfy substance and banking-risk requirements.

The Annual Return is due within 3 months after the anniversary of incorporation; the annual government refresher fee is payable on or before each anniversary date. No corporate income tax return is filed because no income tax is levied on International Companies.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Issued within 3-7 business days of application; valid only as evidence of due registration and payment of annual fees up to the date of issue.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu's domestic law does not contain a transfer-pricing code, and no Local File or Master File documentation threshold exists under Vanuatu statutory instruments. The VFSC and the Financial Intelligence Unit nevertheless review cross-border related-party flows as part of ESR and AML examinations.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019 and the Economic Substance Regulations apply to International Companies carrying on a relevant activity: banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centre, or holding-company and IP functions. A Vanuatu IC licensing a public web-scraping/proxy API must file an annual ESR return with VFSC declaring its activities; if classified as a distribution and service centre or headquarters, it must demonstrate in Vanuatu: (i) management and control exercised through duly recorded board meetings; (ii) adequate physical office premises; (iii) an adequate number of full-time employees proportionate to the API turnover; and (iv) minimum annual operating expenditure. A passively held offshore IP-owning IC must maintain 'adequate' staff and premises for its IP assets and cannot rely on a pure nominee structure to satisfy ESR.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the VAT Act 1998, evasion of tax attracts a criminal fine of up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 3 years, plus recovery of the unpaid VAT, a late-payment penalty, and a daily surcharge. TIEA cooperation permits exchange of banking and beneficial-ownership records with foreign tax authorities, materially increasing cross-border detection risk.

Director personal liability: A court may hold directors personally liable for the unpaid VAT where the corporate form was used as a sham or where they knowingly made false declarations; on criminal conviction, directors face imprisonment, disqualification orders, and civil restitution orders up to the full evaded amount.
Shell Company and ESR False Declaration

Statutory sanction: VFSC may impose a civil penalty of VT 500,000 per offence plus VT 20,000 per day of continuing breach; severe or repeated failure to meet economic substance triggers a report to the Vanuatu revenue authority and to the OECD/EU Code of Conduct, loss of IC tax-exempt status, and administrative strike-off/dissolution with no statutory compensation to shareholders.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a knowingly false ESR declaration are criminally liable with fines up to VT 5,000,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; the VFSC may also seek a court order requiring directors to disgorge profits gained by the shell structure and may petition for a travel ban or securities order freezing their departure from Vanuatu during the investigation.
AML/CFT Breach, Money Laundering and UBO Concealment

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cap 296), an individual convicted of money laundering faces a fine up to VT 100,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 25 years; the Financial Intelligence Unit may impose supervisory penalties, suspend the registered agent relationship, and freeze connected accounts; the VFSC may cancel the IC's registration and refer the matter for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: AML offences attach to the natural-person controller as much as to the entity; directors are severally liable and face restraint orders, confiscation of proceeds, travel bans, extradition to treaty partner jurisdictions, and up to 25 years' imprisonment without the possibility of sheltering behind the corporate veil.
Fraudulent Trading and Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: The Vanuatu Companies Act (Cap 191) fraudulent-trading provisions, applied by analogy to ICs under the International Companies Act 1993, render directors responsible for all debts contracted during the fraudulent period; the court may order civil restitution, disqualify directors for up to 5 years, and impose criminal fines up to VT 1,000,000 plus imprisonment up to 7 years for dishonest conduct.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced automatically on a fraudulent-trading finding: directors become personally jointly and severally liable for the IC's liabilities, may be ordered to repay all amounts so obtained, and a departure-prohibition order may prevent them from leaving Vanuatu or partner-jurisdiction airports until restitution is made.
