Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — sole registrar for International Companies under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], online portal at www.vfsc.vu; Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue (Ministry of Finance and Economic Management) — responsible for customs duties and any revenue administration, portal at www.customs.gov.vu. No national income tax authority registration exists because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, no personal income tax, and no VAT.

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, pass-through, or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Vanuatu law. The IC automatically holds a statutory exemption from all current and future taxes, duties, stamp duties, and government charges on its income, assets, shares, and transactions for a period of 20 years from incorporation under Section 137 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], on condition that it does not carry on business with Vanuatu residents or acquire Vanuatu real property. The exemption is automatic and non-elective; no classification form is filed with VFSC or the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax is enacted in Vanuatu. Indirect taxation is limited to import customs duties under the Customs Act [Cap. 71], selective export duties on certain physical commodities (not applicable to digital services), municipal business licence fees, and a 12.5% hotel/accommodation levy (irrelevant to B2B email infrastructure SaaS). Cross-border digital services supplied by an offshore IC are outside the scope of Vanuatu indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Vanuatu has not enacted a VAT/GST or digital-services tax; there is no turnover threshold (VT 0), no VAT registration process, and no obligation to account for output tax on overseas newsletter and transactional email API revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Vanuatu levies no VAT/GST on domestic or exported goods and services, there is no 0% export rating to claim and no output tax to refund for the IC's overseas newsletter hosting and email API subscription revenue. The revenue is outside the Vanuatu indirect tax net by statutory design; no prior VAT registration is required for the exemption to apply.

For any physical import of servers, laptops, or office equipment, the IC should retain a Customs Import Entry, commercial invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, and payment evidence to support customs duty assessment.

None — Vanuatu operates no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing platform, tax-relevant API gateway, or digital stamping requirement. The IC may issue standard electronic invoices to foreign customers without government pre-clearance; commercial invoice records must be retained for corporate good-standing and audit purposes.

No VAT zero-rating clawback is possible because no VAT exists. Principal integrity and penalty risks: (1) if the IC conducts business with Vanuatu residents or acquires Vanuatu land without a permit, the statutory tax exemption is lost and VFSC enforcement, fines, and potential strike-off apply under the International Companies Act; (2) false declarations to Customs for imported infrastructure goods attract penalties, forfeiture, and fines under the Customs Act [Cap. 71]; (3) unlicensed financial, banking, or money-transfer activity connected to payment processing rails would trigger penalties under the Vanuatu Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2000 and VFSC sanctions; (4) misstatements of beneficial ownership in the registered agent records are punishable by fines and imprisonment under the company regulatory framework; (5) if the IC holds monetized IP, international substance expectations require an adequate economic footprint in Vanuatu to preserve the exemption's international standing. No retrospective VAT assessment can be made given the absence of an indirect tax statute.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The official currency is the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV). There are no comprehensive foreign exchange control laws imposing blanket restrictions on current account transactions or capital account movements. The Reserve Bank of Vanuatu maintains a liberalised exchange control regime, but certain monitoring and reporting obligations apply to licensed banks. Profit remittance and dividend distribution by International Companies are generally unrestricted, subject only to anti-money laundering compliance verification and the completion of standard corporate formalities. There are no meaningful legislative hurdles to full profit remittance for non-resident shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement compelling the surrender of export proceeds to the central bank. Vanuatu operates under a market-determined exchange rate framework, and no mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts. Licensed banks may require documentary support to confirm the commercial nature of receipts for FATF-compliant record keeping, but no timeline or percentage-based surrender mandate exists.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms may be used selectively for low-value ancillary disbursements; however, they generate elevated correspondent banking scrutiny. The primary operating treasury should reside within a VFSC-regulated and Vanuatu-licensed bank with full SWIFT membership. Offshore EMI accounts should not be used as the sole liquidity rail due to enhanced frequency of freezes and delayed SAR-driven reviews.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: 0 VUV — Vanuatu does not maintain a transfer pricing documentation regime; no local file/master file/Country-by-Country reporting thresholds are prescribed for International Companies. Intercompany pricing is nevertheless subject to foreign tax authority review in the counterparty's jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu's International Companies (Economic Substance) framework — administered by VFSC — requires an IC that carries on a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate economic substance proportionate to its activity. Digital newsletter/email infrastructure typically falls within 'service business' or 'intellectual property business' if the IC derives subscription or licensing income. The IC must be directed and managed in Vanuatu, maintain adequate physical premises, local full-time employees, and operating expenditure, and file an annual ESR declaration through the registered agent. ICs that do not carry on a relevant activity, or are tax resident in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction, may be excluded from ESR filing upon proof.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu's International Companies Act and criminal law, false declarations and false records attract fines, imprisonment, and the court's power to wind up the IC; VFSC may also impose monetary administrative penalties and refer the matter to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).

Director personal liability: Directors/officers who knowingly participate in fraud can have the corporate veil pierced; they may be jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of diverted profits, subjected to travel bans and asset freezing orders, disqualified, and in egregious cases imprisoned.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: VFSC can assess economic substance penalties, require information exchange with foreign tax authorities, strike the IC off the register, and impose daily fines until compliance. The IC may also lose its statutory tax-exempt status if the exemption was procured by misrepresentation.

Director personal liability: A director who authorises a false ESR filing or knowingly allows a shell structure can be personally fined and disqualified; courts may make a 'costs and restoration' order against directors requiring them to pay all expenses of reinstatement.
Money Laundering and AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Violations of Vanuatu's AML/CFT framework and VFSC AML Code expose the IC and its beneficial owners to FIU investigation, civil forfeiture of funds, fines, and closure of payment processing. Banks/payment processors may terminate the merchant account, and the VFSC may remove the registered agent's licence if the agent has facilitated the breach.

Director personal liability: Directors with actual control over the payment infrastructure may be criminally prosecuted for laundering, face imprisonment, have personal bank accounts frozen, and be treated as principals of the offending company under the doctrine of shadow directorship.
Misrepresentation of Corporate Exemption and Unlicensed Activity

Statutory sanction: Carrying on regulated financial business without a VFSC licence is an offence under the Financial Services (Regulation) framework; penalties include daily fines and cessation orders, plus VFSC power to strike off and refer for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors may be subject to personal civil liability for any investor/merchant loss, exclusion from acting as director, and imprisonment for continued unlicensed activity; travel bans may be attached to enforcement proceedings in Vanuatu courts.
