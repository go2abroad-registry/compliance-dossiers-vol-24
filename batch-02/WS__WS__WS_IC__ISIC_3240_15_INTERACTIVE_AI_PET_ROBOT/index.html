Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue of Samoa (MOR), the national revenue authority of the Independent State of Samoa, operating the MOR e-Services online portal at https://www.revenue.gov.ws; customs functions are administered by the Samoa Customs Service under the Ministry of Revenue pursuant to the Customs Act 2014, utilising the ASYCUDA World electronic customs declaration platform.

E-government filing portal

For an International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1987, tax-exempt status is conferred by registration with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) rather than by issuance of an income-tax TIN. To import components, export finished robotic products and claim VAGST input credits, the IC must nevertheless obtain a Ministry of Revenue registration as follows: (1) submit Form MOR-VAGST/TIN application through the MOR e-Services portal or the MOR One-Stop Shop; (2) attach the Certificate of Incorporation, IC Registry extract, registered office address in Samoa, director and shareholder registers, the local registered agent acceptance letter, and a Customs importer/exporter code application; (3) receive the TIN and VAGST registration number, generally within 3 to 5 business days for a low-risk standard incomer; and (4) file periodic VAGST returns (if registered) and maintain the annual IC Government fee. No minimum capital contribution, local shareholding requirement or tax guarantee deposit applies to IC incorporation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – The International Company is statutorily exempt from Samoan income tax, capital gains tax and withholding tax on distributions to non-residents under sections 218 and 219 of the International Companies Act 1987, provided the IC is non-resident-owned and does not carry on business in Samoa. The entity remains within the VAGST net only to the extent it makes taxable supplies in Samoa; a pure export-oriented assembly operation sits outside the Samoan income-tax net and treats exports as VAGST zero-rated supplies.

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a check-the-box entity classification election or hybrid-mismatch election mechanism. The IC tax-exempt status is statutory and automatic upon valid incorporation under the International Companies Act 1987 and continued compliance with the non-resident ownership and no-local-business conditions. The entity may elect to become VAGST-registered upon exceeding the registration threshold, or may elect to remain unregistered or cancel registration where local taxable supplies fall below the threshold. Reclassification as a fully taxable domestic resident company is possible only by converting to an ordinary company under the Companies Act 2001, which would subject the entity to standard Samoan corporate income tax and general anti-avoidance rules.

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoa tala and sene) of taxable supply value in any 12-month period; non-resident suppliers may also be required to register if they have a deemed taxable presence in Samoa, but a purely offshore export seller of physical goods with no Samoan fixed establishment and no local sales is not required to register merely for making zero-rated export supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Samoa by a VAGST-registered supplier qualify for zero-rating where the supplier holds conclusive documentary proof that the interactive AI pet robots left Samoa, namely a customs export declaration, bill of lading or airway bill, and banking or remittance evidence of payment from the non-resident buyer. Services supplied to a non-resident and used wholly outside Samoa, including offshore AI software configuration, design and logistics support, may also be zero-rated. The IC must separately identify zero-rated export sales from any local taxable supplies on the VAGST return, apply a fair and reasonable input-credit apportionment where mixed use exists, and claim excess input VAGST on export purchases as a refund in accordance with the VAGST refund procedure.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate (evidencing export clearance, transport and receipt of foreign-currency proceeds)
  • Commercial invoice detailing the ISIC 3240 robotic toy products, HS classification under HS 9503 (toys, games and models) or HS 8543 (electrical machines with individual functions) as applicable, including quantities, unit values and Incoterms
  • Packing list
  • Certificate of origin (where required by the destination jurisdiction) and any preferential origin certificate only if a relevant trade agreement applies
  • Customs bonded warehouse or temporary importation records where components are imported, processed and re-exported
  • VAGST refund computation worksheet together with supplier tax invoices supporting the input-credit claim

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-connected e-invoicing for standard VAGST registrants. All VAGST tax invoices must contain the mandatory particulars prescribed in the VAGST Act 2003, including supplier name, TIN/VAGST number, invoice date, description, quantity, unit price, VAGST amount and total consideration. Registered taxpayers must file VAGST returns electronically via the MOR e-Services portal, and exporters must lodge export entries electronically in ASYCUDA World, with risk-based customs inspection. Electronic copies of invoices, customs entries and transport documents must be retained for a minimum of seven years to substantiate zero-rating upon audit.

The Ministry of Revenue may scrutinise export zero-rating claims in post-clearance audits, customs valuation reviews and VAGST refund verifications. Where documentation is absent or false, the zero-rated treatment is reversed, the uncollected VAGST at 15% is assessed, and the taxpayer becomes liable for late-payment penalties at 2% per month or part thereof, plus statutory interest on the unpaid amount. Knowingly false declarations, forged customs or remittance documents and schemes to obtain fraudulent refunds constitute offences under the VAGST Act 2003, the Customs Act 2014 and Samoa tax administration legislation, carrying substantial fines, forfeiture of goods and imprisonment. The audit statute of limitations permits retrospective assessment and clawback of input credits for open years, generally extending to a period of at least seven years where fraud or gross negligence is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) enjoys full current-account convertibility for trade and service payments, but WST is not freely tradable offshore and lacks an active interbank FX market outside the domestic authorized-dealer network. Capital-account transactions (including repatriation of contributed capital, intra-company loans, and portfolio investments by non-residents) require prior CBS approval under the Exchange Control Act 1976. Profit and dividend remittances by an International Company are permitted under the International Companies Act 1987, subject to (i) a tax clearance certificate, (ii) audited financial statements evidencing distributable reserves, (iii) completion of CBS Exchange Control Form E1, and (iv) CBS no-objection where the remittance exceeds WST 1,000,000 (or FX equivalent) per calendar year. Dividends are remittable in the original currency of receipt only if the profits were earned in that currency; otherwise conversion through a licensed Samoan authorized dealer is mandatory.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBS Exchange Control Notices issued pursuant to the Exchange Control Act 1976, export proceeds must be repatriated to Samoa within 90 days from the date of shipment. Unless the IC obtains a CBS foreign-currency retention approval (typically valid for 180 days and renewable for operational hedging needs), 100% of convertible export receipts must be either (i) converted into WST at an authorized dealer within 5 business days of receipt, or (ii) credited to a WST-denominated surrender account at a licensed commercial bank. For an International Company that operates outside Samoa and maintains an approved offshore foreign-currency account, repatriation obligations are suspended only if the CBS grants written non-resident exchange control status; absent such status, the full surrender rule applies to all trade receivables settled through Samoan banks. Failure to surrender is an offence attracting fines and potential denial of future dealer services.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Samoa does not license standalone electronic-money institutions and has no EMI regulatory passporting regime. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Stripe Treasury) cannot hold WST settlement accounts or access the Samoan local clearing infrastructure without a licensed sponsor bank. Such platforms may lawfully collect USD, AUD, or NZD receivables for the IC from customers outside Samoa, but the IC must thereafter route the aggregated funds through a Samoan authorized dealer to satisfy exchange control repatriation. Use of an EMI as a primary treasury rail is legally fragile because the CBS does not recognize EMI balances as satisfying export-proceeds surrender or substance requirements. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire protocol: founder or institutional investor initiates a SWIFT MT103 from the originating bank, with the beneficiary being the IC's capital escrow account at ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa. The remittance must carry a clear purpose code: 'L1301 Share Capital Contribution', and the originator field must show the actual funding entity; 'Covers' payments via the intermediary are permitted only if the ordering customer name is transmitted in Field 50K. The IC's banker will complete an inward remittance report to the CBS for amounts above WST 100,000. Prior to release of funds to the operational account, the bank verifies (i) the directors' resolution attaching a funding schedule, (ii) the shareholder registry update, (iii) source-of-funds documentation for each remitter, and (iv) confirmation that the capital does not originate from a jurisdiction under CBS countermeasures. The capital escrow account is then swept into the operational account only after the bank completes its enhanced due diligence, generally within two to five business days of receipt. Equity registration with the Samoa Registrar and an updated share register are prerequisites for tier release of the funds beyond WST 250,000.Primary clearing rail for component procurement from China and other manufacturing jurisdictions is a direct SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer for purchase orders below USD 50,000, and an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (MT700) for bulk robotic toy component shipments, particularly where the supplier requires a confirming bank (e.g., HSBC Singapore or ANZ China) to mitigate country risk. For usance arrangements, 90-day deferred LC terms are permissible with a CB-approved line. Documentary collections (D/P and D/A) are viable for low-risk, repeat suppliers but require the bank's prior credit approval. All FX disbursements are executed via the bank's USD/AUD/NZD Nostro accounts because WST is not a SWIFT-deliverable currency; the Samoan bank debits the IC's WST account at its internal FX desk and credits the overseas beneficiary via SWIFT. Settlement orderly processing: the IC submits Form E2, a pro-forma invoice, an underlying purchase contract, and a compliance certification that the cargo will be used for AI pet robot assembly; beneficiary bank detail validation follows the Deutsche Bank SWIFT anti-fraud payment validation protocol; and for amounts exceeding USD 50,000, a second approver sign-off is required.Outright FX forwards for up to 12 months against USD, AUD, and NZD, available through ANZ Samoa/BSP Samoa only where a confirmed commercial purchase order or sales contract exists; the contract must be deposited with the bank and margins of 10% of notional are standard; CBS approval is required for tenors exceeding 6 months.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing WST: legally permissible only with prior CBS authorization, but in practice the WST offshore NDF market is highly illiquid and local authorized dealers rarely quote positions; the IC should rely on USD/WST forwards instead.Cross-currency swaps: permitted on a case-by-case basis for hedging the AUD/USD and NZD/USD exposure on component imports and consumer receivables; the IC must execute the swap through a licensed dealer, disclose the underlying principal stream, and give CBS prior notice; swaps with a maturity beyond one year require a separate capital-account approval.Currency call/put options: not prohibited, but no Samoan authorized dealer offers a regular local options market; the IC may purchase options from an offshore dealer for AUD/USD only, provided option premiums are declared as current-account payments to the CBS.Natural hedging via multi-currency accounts: the IC may hold segregated USD, AUD, and NZD operational accounts with CBS retention approval to offset receivables against payables in the same currency, thereby reducing forced WST conversion and re-purchase spreads.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low. Samoa does not license standalone electronic-money institutions and has no EMI regulatory passporting regime. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Stripe Treasury) cannot hold WST settlement accounts or access the Samoan local clearing infrastructure without a licensed sponsor bank. Such platforms may lawfully collect USD, AUD, or NZD receivables for the IC from customers outside Samoa, but the IC must thereafter route the aggregated funds through a Samoan authorized dealer to satisfy exchange control repatriation. Use of an EMI as a primary treasury rail is legally fragile because the CBS does not recognize EMI balances as satisfying export-proceeds surrender or substance requirements. Recommended role: limited-use collection convenience for retail AI pet robot sales, with weekly sweep to ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No express Local File or Master File threshold is prescribed under Samoa's Income Tax Act 2015; the arm's-length principle governs all controlled transactions. As best practice, contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation should be maintained when aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed WST 5,000,000 or any single related-party transaction exceeds WST 500,000.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies Act 1987 (as amended by the Economic Substance Amendments) requires ICs carrying on enumerated relevant activities — IP holding, distribution and service centres, financing, banking, insurance, and pure equity holding — to demonstrate adequate economic substance in Samoa through a local registered office, resident agent, qualified local personnel, local board meetings, and local expenditure. Manufacturing and retail trading of bionic interactive AI pet robots (ISIC 3240) is not an enumerated relevant activity; nevertheless, the IC must maintain genuine commercial substance to preserve the 20-year tax exemption and avoid recharacterisation as a shell entity by the Samoa Inland Revenue Department or offshore regulator.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Material Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Shortfall penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, plus late-payment penalties and statutory interest (Tax Administration Act 2012). Criminal conviction carries fines up to WST 500,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; the tax exemption is revoked with retroactive assessment at the 27% corporate rate.

Director personal liability: Directors who instigate, direct, or knowingly participate in the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall and penalties. Civil restitution of diverted corporate assets may be ordered, the Commissioner may issue a departure prohibition order blocking travel until the debt is settled, and directors may be criminally prosecuted and imprisoned.
Shell Company and Abusive Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of the 20-year tax exemption, recharacterisation as a taxable Samoa-resident company at 27%, recomputation of profits with shortfall penalties up to 200%, cancellation or strike-off of the IC registration, and disqualification orders.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability for the reconstructed tax and penalties where the corporate veil is pierced; disqualification from serving as directors for up to 5 years, travel bans pending settlement of revenue claims, and criminal charges where the sham structure masks fraud or money laundering.
AML and Anti-Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and related FIU directives: corporate fines up to WST 1,000,000, asset freezing, termination of banking facilities, and mandatory reporting of suspicious transactions; regulatory non-compliance can result in licence restrictions and IC strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally subject to fines up to WST 100,000, confiscation orders, and imprisonment up to 14 years for laundering offenses; travel restrictions may be imposed during FIU investigations, and personal assets are subject to civil forfeiture.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Stripping Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Income adjustment by the Commissioner, shortfall penalties of up to 100% (200% for intentional disregard), statutory interest, and denial of interest deductions under thin-capitalisation limitations (Income Tax Act 2015).

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false or unsupported pricing returns may be personally assessed; the corporate veil is pierced where intercompany pricing is a facade for fraud or asset stripping, exposing directors' personal assets to civil restitution, travel bans, and potential criminal prosecution.
