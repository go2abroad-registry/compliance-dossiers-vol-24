Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], an International Company is statutorily exempt from corporate income tax, capital gains tax, dividend/interest/royalty withholding tax, property tax, stamp duty and all other direct national taxes. No income tax return or corporate tax payment obligation exists. The only fiscal exposure is indirect VAT at 15% on supplies made in Vanuatu; offshore zero-rated export services (creator management, MCN agency services, digital advertising) attract 0% VAT. Compliance obligations are limited to the annual VFSC licence fee and, where in scope, the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act No. 37 of 2019 substance declaration.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax. Available elections: (i) voluntary VAT registration under s.7 of the VAT Act [Cap 246] for ICs below the VT 10,000,000 threshold, enabling input VAT recovery on local overheads; (ii) election of cash vs invoice basis of VAT accounting under s.25 VAT Act, subject to VRC approval; (iii) election of registered agent and registered office under the International Companies Act; (iv) election to keep VAT records in a foreign currency for predominantly offshore activities, subject to VRC direction; (v) no OECD hybrid mismatch election is available - hybrid mismatch rules are not domestically enacted, but an IC carrying on a 'relevant activity' (including a service centre) must file the annual VFSC economic substance declaration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (approx. USD 84,700) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available to zero-rated exporters for input VAT refund purposes

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rated where physically exported from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply and supported by customs evidence. Services: zero-rated where supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and the services are performed and wholly consumed outside Vanuatu; supplies relating to land or movable goods situated in Vanuatu are excluded. For the Creator_MCN_Agency model, creator incubation, talent representation commissions, advertising/content-monetization services, royalty administration and platform management supplied to TikTok, foreign creators and overseas brand clients qualify for 0% VAT provided the contract is governed offshore, the benefit is realized outside Vanuatu and consideration is remitted in foreign currency from abroad.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (physical exports only); for services, the executed Talent Representation / MCN Agency Agreement, TikTok platform service-order records and creator payout statements evidencing offshore delivery

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists. Vanuatu operates no API-based e-invoicing clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC) or digital stamping regime. Taxpayers must issue sequential VRC-compliant VAT invoices showing supplier name and VIN, recipient details, invoice date and number, description, tax-exclusive value, VAT amount and, for exports, a zero-rating legend. VAT returns and payments are lodged electronically via the VRC e-Services portal.

VRC carries out post-refund audits of zero-rated exporters. If the offshore character of creator-management services is not substantiated within the documentation period, the supply is recharacterized as taxable at 15% VAT, the input VAT refund is clawed back with statutory interest, and a late-payment penalty of up to 20% of the VAT due is imposed. Knowingly false declarations, forged invoices or fabricated IRC/MT103 evidence constitute an offence under s.64 of the VAT Act, punishable by criminal fines of up to VT 10,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 3 years, with potential personal liability for directors and the registered agent. Recurrent breaches may result in VFSC striking-off of the IC, loss of tax-exempt status and freezing of accounts by the Vanuatu FIU for AML/CFT irregularities in cross-border payouts. Although Vanuatu has abolished statutory exchange controls, the 180-day repatriation benchmark is retained as a prudent evidentiary standard for zero-rating substantiation and bank AML/CFT compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible under a completely open exchange-control framework. Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control legislation on current-account or capital-account transactions; non-resident International Companies (ICs) may hold VUV and foreign-currency accounts (chiefly USD, AUD, and EUR) without prior RBV authority, and profits, dividends, royalties, and liquidation proceeds may be remitted offshore without restriction. The VUV is managed by the RBV against a reference basket of trading-partner currencies (with a strong effective weighting toward the USD), but the onshore interbank market is thin, so practical convertibility outside the Vanuatu financial system is limited.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion requirement on export proceeds or cross-border service receipts. A Vanuatu IC in the global creator-management sector (ISIC 7310) may retain 100% of TikTok creator-platform payouts, agency commissions, and ancillary licensing or revenue-share income in USD or any chosen foreign currency without RBV conversion, time-limit, or surrender obligations. The only statutory friction is AML/CFT reporting under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act, which requires the receiving bank to file threshold and suspicious-transaction reports; it does not affect FX availability.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - central monetary authority and banking supervisory body for the Republic of Vanuatu; issues the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV), regulates licensed financial institutions under the Financial Institutions Act, and administers balance-of-payments and financial intelligence policy. Vanuatu has no separate foreign-exchange administration; the RBV acts as the de facto exchange-control authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-High as a payment-disbursement layer, but Low as a primary deposit or settlement spine. Vanuatu ICs are non-resident in the EEA and UK, so EU/UK Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licenses cannot extend core banking functions to them; local law also treats deposit-taking as a regulated activity under the Financial Institutions Act. The compliant architecture is therefore a hybrid model: (i) a Tier-1 Vanuatu bank (ANZ or BRED) acts as the licensed settlement anchor and SWIFT receiving entity for TikTok creator payouts; (ii) global B2B fintech PSPs (Wise Business, Airwallex-class rails) are engaged solely as outbound disbursement processors to individual creators. Legal constraints: creator-platform payout agreements typically require a bank (not an EMI) as the receiving account; EMIs may terminate third-country intermediary flows at any time; and EMI-only treasury is commercially unworkable for a non-resident MCN.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections (equity subscription or shareholder loans) are executed by SWIFT MT103 direct credit in USD or VUV to the IC's Tier-1 settlement account. The remitter must cite a recognized SWIFT purpose code: for equity use 'P019 - Capital Contributions' or, where the bank requires a trade and capital services code, 'P110 - Investment Income'; for intercompany loans use 'P140 - Loans/Bonds' with a signed loan agreement on file. Verification set required by the receiving bank: board resolution authorizing the capital injection; current IC certificate; VFSC shareholder register extract; and a notarized declaration confirming that no RBV exchange-control approval is required. The named beneficiary on the MT103 must match the VFSC corporate register spelling exactly, and the sending bank must confirm the remitter's legal capacity to fund a non-resident IC.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 corporate credit transfer for creator-payout settlements and operating disbursements, denominated in USD, EUR, or AUD. To avoid misclassification of pass-through creator revenue as dividends, each outbound MT103 must carry the purpose code 'P215 - Payment of Commission' or 'P304 - Fees for Services' and reference the underlying creator invoice. The IC maintains a segregated disbursement sub-ledger and executes low-value creator payouts through an API-driven PSP (e.g., Wise B2B) funded from the ANZ or BRED anchor account; the PSP is never granted deposit-holding powers, only disbursement instructions. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Vanuatu TP threshold exists due to the 0% CIT regime; prudent transfer pricing documentation is recommended for intercompany transactions exceeding VUV 50,000,000 (approximately USD 420,000) or where foreign tax authorities assert PE/CFC risk.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not impose formal Economic Substance Regulations like the UAE ESR, but international tax standards and foreign CFC regimes require real substance for an IC. The TikTok MCN agency must maintain local board decisions, commercial contracts with creators, payment records, and a foreign management office; otherwise it may be treated as a shell company by TikTok's payment processors, Wise, or foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Sham Tax-Exempt Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu Penal Code, false declarations to VFSC and use of the IC structure to evade foreign tax may lead to criminal prosecution. Foreign jurisdictions may impose back taxes, 75% accuracy penalties, interest, and CFC recharacterization.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly direct operations from abroad are personally liable for foreign tax assessments and penalties under CFC and permanent establishment rules; civil recovery, travel restrictions, and extradition exposure may follow.
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC may strike off the IC under the International Companies Act and impose fines up to VUV 1,000,000; foreign tax agencies and payment processors may disregard the IC and treat the owner as conducting business directly.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors personally liable for all corporate revenue earned by the TikTok MCN; director bans and assets freezing orders are common recovery tools.
AML/CFT Breach and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act imposes fines up to VUV 100,000,000, freezing orders, and de-risking by payment intermediaries; the registered agent may lose its license for CDD failures.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors face criminal liability for laundering proceeds, imprisonment up to 12 years, and personal financial restitution to defrauded creators or tax authorities.
Unlicensed Money Transmission and Unauthorized Payout Facilitation

Statutory sanction: Violation of Vanuatu's Financial Transactions Regime and foreign payment regulations; sanctions include VUV 50,000,000 fines, OFAC penalties if prohibited countries are involved, and forced termination of TikTok and Wise banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Directors and authorized signatories may be personally liable to repay all unlicensed remittances; criminal sanctions and three-year banking bans are imposed in cross-border enforcement cases.
False Statement to Registered Agent and VFSC

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu Companies Act and International Companies Act, false statements attract disqualification of directors, deregistration of the IC, and criminal penalties including imprisonment up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Every director signing the false declaration is jointly and severally liable for resulting damages, foreign tax evasion penalties, and VFSC administrative fines; travel bans may be imposed to secure prosecution.
