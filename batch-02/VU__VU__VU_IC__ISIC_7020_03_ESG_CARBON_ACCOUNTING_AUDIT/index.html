Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; operational portal: https://www.customs.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, so an International Company (IC) does not obtain an income-tax TIN. A foreign-owned entity registers with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and receives an IC registration number. If the entity enters the indirect-tax net by making taxable supplies in or into Vanuatu, it must apply to VRC for a VAT identification number (VATIN) using the VRC VAT registration form, with the VFSC certificate, articles/incorporation certificate, registered office address, expected 12-month taxable turnover, principal place of business and bank details, and a written local fiscal representative appointment where there is no Vanuatu fixed establishment. Exporting physical goods additionally requires an ASYCUDA World Customs exporter code; a pure offshore service IC that conducts no Vanuatu domestic taxable activity remains outside the VAT register and may simply rely on its IC exemption certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity classification election and no hybrid mismatch or treaty election exist under Vanuatu law. An IC's tax-exempt classification is automatic upon VFSC incorporation and cannot be altered. A business below the VAT threshold may elect voluntary VAT registration, and an exporter may apply for a ruling confirming zero-rating; neither election changes the IC's income-tax exempt status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 (four million vatu) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is available for smaller turnover

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT-registered supplier may zero-rate the export of goods physically removed from Vanuatu and specified offshore services supplied to a non-resident recipient for use or enjoyment outside Vanuatu. For corporate ESG/carbon footprint auditing and offset brokerage, zero-rating is claimable where the client is a non-resident, the service is not performed on land or property located in Vanuatu, no local consumption occurs, and the contract/invoice states the offshore destination. If the carbon offset brokerage is characterized as arranging financial products, it may fall within an exempt financial-services category rather than zero-rated supply, so a binding VRC interpretation should be obtained where this is material.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed electronically in ASYCUDA World
  • Commercial invoice and signed export/offshore service contract
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or other transport document proving physical export
  • Packing list and Certificate of Origin where a preferential origin claim is made
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank SWIFT/MT103 / foreign exchange confirmation evidencing receipt of offshore funds
  • VRC VAT export schedule / zero-rated supplies schedule, if requested on audit

Vanuatu does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, API e-invoicing, or digital stamping. VAT-registered suppliers must issue prescribed tax invoices and retain supporting documents. Export control is managed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs declaration and clearance system, which functions as the fiscal clearance event for exported goods; no separate API invoicing clearance is imposed.

VRC auditors may compare export declarations, transport documents, bank remittance evidence, and service contracts before refunding or recognizing input credits. Any unsupported zero-rating is retrospectively clawed back: the supply is reassessed at 12.5% VAT, input credits are disallowed, and late-payment interest and penalties accrue from the original due date. Knowingly false or fraudulent zero-rating claims and customs misdeclarations expose the entity and responsible officers to civil penalties, imprisonment under the VAT/Customs Acts, loss of IC tax-exempt status, and VFSC enforcement action.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible in both current and capital accounts. The Exchange Control Act [Cap 91] remains on the statute book, but section 126 of the International Companies Act [Cap 222] expressly exempts all International Companies from exchange-control regulations. No RBV approval, licence, or reporting is required for capital injections, profit repatriation, dividend payments, directors' fees, or cross-border service fees in any currency. The VU_IC, as a tax-exempt International Company engaged in Scope 1-3 carbon auditing and offset brokerage, derives service income in USD/EUR/AUD and faces no remittance hurdles, no withholding taxes, and no restrictions on holding foreign-currency bank accounts onshore or offshore.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Vanuatu imposes no statutory deadline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds or cross-border service receipts. The residual surrender provisions of the Exchange Control Act [Cap 91] apply only to non-IC resident entities and are not enforced by RBV in current practice. The VU_IC may retain 100% of its export-service and brokerage receipts in the original invoice currency, hold them in USD/EUR/AUD accounts, and deploy them without conversion to VUV.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) acts as the parallel licensing and AML/CFT supervisor for non-bank financial services and International Companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury rail. Vanuatu has no domestic e-money institution (EMI) or payment-institution licensing category, and VFSC financial-dealer licences do not authorise digital wallet issuance. The VU_IC may lawfully hold balances with reputable offshore EMIs, but such accounts are outside RBV/VFSC regulatory perimeter and are treated as heightened-risk by correspondent banks in Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and Hong Kong. Feasibility: supplementary receivable-collection channel only, with full beneficial-owner disclosure, exact invoice matching, and monthly sweep to the primary ANZ/BRED Vanuatu account; not suitable for capital escrow or settlement of offset purchases.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions and subscription proceeds are remitted via SWIFT MT103 from the beneficial owner's bank to the VU_IC account at ANZ Vanuatu or BRED Vanuatu. The beneficiary reference must read 'Capital Contribution to Vanuatu International Company [legal name]' and the remittance narrative in field 70 must cite the International Companies Act [Cap 222], section 126 exchange-control exemption. No RBV filing, import certificate, or prior approval is necessary. The receiving bank will verify current registration and good standing with VFSC, confirm the remitter appears on the beneficial-ownership register, and reconcile the amount against the filed registered capital (no minimum capital applies). For carbon-audit fee income, each inbound credit must reference a defined ESG deliverable — Scope 1-3 carbon inventory, carbon-footprint verification, or offset-brokerage commission — so the MT103 narration matches the underlying commercial contract. Settlement verification comprises SWIFT GPI tracking alerts, same-day bank credit confirmation, and monthly reconciliation against the custom treasury ledger.Primary outbound clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for direct advisory-fee disbursements to overseas consultants, verification bodies, and audit partners, and for carbon-credit purchase settlements with registry counterparties in USD, EUR, or AUD. Where the IC acts as principal in a bundled offsets transaction and funds a third-party registry or trustee account, SWIFT MT202 COVER is used to settle correspondent obligations in conjunction with the beneficiary's MT103. Commercial letters of credit are not market standard for ESG advisory services but may be requested for large offset-purchase programmes exceeding USD 2,000,000; in such cases a sight LC confirmed by a Schedule-1 OECD bank is executed through ANZ Vanuatu's trade-finance desk. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu abolished corporate income tax under the Income Tax (Abolition) Act No. 34 of 2005; an International Company (IC) holding a valid VFSC Certificate of Incorporation is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu income taxation.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no domestic dividend withholding tax; an IC may distribute all profits to non-resident shareholders free of Vanuatu tax, and no stamp duty applies to dividend resolutions or transfer of dividend proceeds.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Return and annual government maintenance fee are due within 6 months of each anniversary of incorporation. The initial Annual Return must be filed within 6 months of incorporation; a nil-activity declaration is permissible only where no transaction has been undertaken.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold in Vatu because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax and has no domestic TP law. Foreign threshold triggers apply only where a foreign tax authority recharacterizes the IC as a tax resident or permanent establishment of a related enterprise; in such cases the OECD-recommended thresholds govern (e.g., Local File above EUR 1,000,000 or Master File above EUR 50,000,000 of related-party turnover). For carbon offset brokerage commissions of USD 500,000 or more annually, prudent compliance requires preparing a contemporaneous transfer pricing memorandum in the buyer's jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has no UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, Section 111 and Section 174 of the International Companies Act Cap 222 impose equivalent substance conditions on ICs: (a) maintain a VFSC-licensed Registered Agent; (b) maintain a physical registered office in Vanuatu; (c) keep accounting records at the registered office that reflect the company's financial position with reasonable accuracy; (d) refrain from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents or holding real property in Vanuatu. The annual return includes an Economic Substance Declaration confirming the company's activities do not create a local economic presence. Failure to satisfy these conditions enables the Registrar to strike off the company and exposes the IC to characterization as a shell entity by foreign courts, particularly in U.S. and EU anti-abuse rules (CFC, GAAR, and DAC6).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Foreign Jurisdiction)

Statutory sanction: Foreign court recharacterization of the IC as a tax resident; reattribution of 100% of profit with interest; penalties of 100-200% of the tax understated; publication on the foreign tax authority's non-cooperative entity list; VFSC cross-sanction for false declaration under Section 230 of the International Companies Act, including revocation of the IC's exemption.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for the foreign unpaid tax and penalties; civil restitution orders equal to the full tax deficiency; 5-10 year travel bans; extradition risk; imprisonment of up to 14 years under the foreign state's criminal tax code.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC strike-off and dissolution under Section 263 of the International Companies Act; liquidation of bank accounts through court order; forfeiture of the company's assets to the Vanuatu State after 7 years; inclusion on the Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information adverse review report, causing loss of correspondent banking.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for up to VT 25,000,000 of the IC's outstanding debts if the company continued trading after the strike-off notice; 5-year disqualification from acting as a director of any VFSC-licensed entity; prohibition from reincorporating an IC in Vanuatu.
AML / Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of up to VT 25,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 10 years under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act; freezing and confiscation orders over the IC's entire asset base; revocation of the Registered Agent's license and immediate deregistration of the IC.

Director personal liability: Directors individually prosecuted where knowledge of the illicit source of funds is proven; personal fines of up to VT 10,000,000; seizure of personal bank accounts; travel restriction orders of up to 3 years; personal restraint orders under the Vanuatu Proceeds of Crime Act.
Unauthorized Domestic Trading / Business License Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to VT 1,000,000 plus VT 10,000 per day of continuing default; immediate suspension of the IC's tax-exempt status; criminal prosecution under the International Companies Act; unlawful contracts rendered unenforceable before Vanuatu courts.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for unpaid license fees, court costs, and daily fines; civil orders to disgorge all profits derived from the Vanuatu-sourced business; imprisonment of up to 2 years for continuing to trade after a suspension order.
