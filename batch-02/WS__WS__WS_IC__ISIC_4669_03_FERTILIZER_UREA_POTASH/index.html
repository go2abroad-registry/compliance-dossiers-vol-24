Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of Samoa (Matagaluega o Tupe Maua), Apia; taxpayer eFiling portal https://efiling.revenue.gov.ws/; customs clearance via Samoa Customs Service (ASYCUDA World); International Company registry administered by the Samoa International Financial Services Centre (SIFSC).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) must apply through its licensed Samoan registered agent by lodging the TIN registration form with the Ministry for Revenue together with: certificate of incorporation, memorandum & articles, IC operating licence issued by SIFSC, particulars of the registered office/agent in Samoa, and beneficial ownership declarations. The Ministry issues a single Business TIN within 5 business days; separate VAGST registration (VAGST number) must be obtained where annual taxable supplies reach WST 100,000. ICs engaged exclusively in offshore wholesale (sale of urea and potash to non-residents) require no income-tax registration and no minimum capital import.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt (International Companies Act 1987, ss.125-127) from income tax, VAGST on offshore supplies, stamp duty, estate duty and gift duty for 20 years from incorporation; a flat annual government licence fee applies in lieu of income tax. — A foreign-owned International Company (IC) must apply through its licensed Samoan registered agent by lodging the TIN registration form with the Ministry for Revenue together with: certificate of incorporation, memorandum & articles, IC operating licence issued by SIFSC, particulars of the registered office/agent in Samoa, and beneficial ownership declarations. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt (International Companies Act 1987, ss.125-127) from income tax, VAGST on offshore supplies, stamp duty, estate duty and gift duty for 20 years from incorporation; a flat annual government licence fee applies in lieu of income tax.

Available election forms

Samoa has no check-the-box classification regime. The available elections are: (i) re-domestication/continuance of an IC as a domestic company (becoming assessable at Samoa's 27% corporate income tax rate and VAGST at 15%) or of a domestic company as an IC (obtaining the 20-year exempt-zone status); and (ii) VAGST voluntary registration to obtain input credits on exports. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are curtailed by statutory anti-avoidance rules (Income Tax Act 2012, ss.99-100).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of taxable supplies in any 12-month rolling period; entities making solely zero-rated export supplies (e.g., urea and potash sold to non-resident buyers) may register voluntarily to claim refunds of input VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAGST) is available for goods physically exported from Samoa within the statutory window under VAGST Act 1994 s.17, for international freight and for qualifying supplies to non-residents; for an exempt-zone IC, offshore wholesale turnover of prilled urea and potassium chloride is entirely outside Samoa's VAGST tax base, resulting in a 0% effective charge with no VAGST payable and credit entitlement for input tax attributable to export supply.

  • Single Goods Declaration (SGD) – customs export declaration lodged in ASYCUDA World with customs-certified exit
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal of urea/potash from Samoa
  • Commercial Invoice and packing list matching the declared commodity (HS 3102 urea; HS 3104 potassium chloride)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency from the non-resident buyer
  • Copy of IC licence and TIN certificate for exporter identification

Samoa imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous e-invoicing mandate. VAGST-registered businesses must issue compliant tax invoices (VAGST Act 1994, s.29) and file returns electronically via the Ministry for Revenue eFiling portal. All export consignments of fertilizers are cleared through the ASYCUDA World customs platform using the Single Goods Declaration; no additional fiscal seal or clearance API applies.

Export zero-rating claims are audited by the Ministry for Revenue for up to 5 years (unlimited on fraud) and may be clawed back with interest. Shortfall penalties under the Tax Administration Act 2012 rise from 20% (lack of reasonable care) to 40% (recklessness), 100% (intentional disregard) and 150% (evasion), plus a 2% monthly late-payment penalty and prosecution fines/imprisonment for fraudulent VAGST refund claims. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days breaches the Exchange Control Regulations 1974 and can trigger Central Bank of Samoa monetary penalties and debt action in addition to the VAT clawback; sham export documentation also triggers criminal prosecution under the Crimes Act 2016.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

WST is a non-internationally-traded, managed-float currency; convertibility is administered through authorised dealer commercial banks. Current-account transactions (trade in goods, services, and routine profit remittances) are broadly liberalised, requiring only CBS-prescribed EC forms submitted via the receiving/remitting bank. Capital-account transactions, including equity injections by non-residents and cross-border financing, require CBS approval or an exemption. For a Samoa International Company (IC), the International Companies Act 1987 (s.204) exempts the IC from the Exchange Control Act 1976 for any transaction denominated in a foreign currency and involving no Samoan-resident counterparty; therefore the IC's USD-denominated capital injections, charter proceeds, and profit remittances are not subject to WST conversion or CBS prior-approval where the funds do not transit a Samoan bank account. Where funds do pass through a Samoan bank, the bank applies EC-1/EC-4 filings and CBS balance-of-payments reporting, but no convertibility restriction is imposed if the account is designated as a non-resident foreign-currency account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a Samoan resident exporter, CBS rules require (i) full repatriation of export proceeds through an authorised dealer within 3 months of shipment (extendable to 6 months with CBS consent), and (ii) mandatory conversion to WST of 100% of repatriated proceeds unless a CBS-approved foreign-currency retention account is held with an authorised dealer. A Samoa IC conducting an offshore physical charter trade in prilled urea and potassium chloride (ISIC 4669) is a non-resident for exchange-control purposes under the International Companies Act 1987 s.204; consequently the statutory mandatory-conversion ratio is 0% for foreign-currency receipts retained offshore. The IC may hold USD proceeds freely in its offshore treasury account; if the IC ever books export proceeds into Samoa, the bank will treat the IC as a non-resident and apply the retention-account protocol rather than the 100% surrender rule, provided the EC-4 declaration and IC certificates are lodged.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), established under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 1984 and the designated authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1977. CBS licenses and supervises authorised dealer banks, administers the Samoa Tala (WST) managed-float currency system, and issues all exchange-control permits and foreign-currency account approvals.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Samoa has no statutory framework for fintech e-money institutions (EMIs), no sandbox, and no domestic EMI licence class; a Samoa IC cannot obtain local e-money authorisation. Offshore EMIs (EEA/Singapore/HK licensed) may in principle hold funds for the IC, but most mainstream platforms decline Samoa-IC clients due to enhanced-risk classification and opaque beneficial-ownership optics. EMIs are therefore viable only for immaterial operational expenses (sub-USD 5,000 per item) and cannot serve as the primary clearing rail for fertilizer trade settlements or LC operations. The strategic posture is to run the primary treasury at a regulated international correspondent bank outside Samoa and use no Samoan domestic clearing for the offshore charter trades.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by an exempt International Company. For a resident company not falling within the IC regime, the statutory dividend withholding tax rate is 15% for non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No CIT filing deadline applies to an exempt International Company. For a taxable resident company, the return is due within six months of the financial year-end, normally 31 March or the selected balance date approved by the Ministry of Revenue.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of up to 7 years and monetary fines of up to WST 100,000, with additional tax equal to 100% of the evaded amount; further administrative penalties and surcharges apply.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally and jointly liable for unpaid taxes and penal tax where they knowingly participated in false returns; travel bans and director disqualification orders can also be imposed.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalties, deregistration, dissemination of the International Company licence, and potential inclusion in international harmful-tax blacklists; SIFA may impose fines and require remedial substance steps.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced; directors can be made personally liable for aggregate tax losses to Samoa, prohibited from acting as directors for a defined period, and subjected to civil recovery actions.
AML/CFT Failures

Statutory sanction: Fines under Samoa's AML/CFT regime, freezing orders, closure of bank accounts, and revocation of the International Company registration; beneficial-ownership reporting breaches carry separate monetary penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors may face personal civil penalties, criminal prosecution where money laundering is proven, and imprisonment of up to 10 years; assets can be restrained or confiscated.
Sham Intermediary / Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of the arrangement, recharacterisation under Samoa's anti-avoidance rules, assessment to Samoan tax at 27%, plus late-payment penalties and statutory interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who design or approve artificial intermediary structures face personal liability for the additional tax and interest, and criminal sanctions if the arrangement is prosecuted as tax fraud.
