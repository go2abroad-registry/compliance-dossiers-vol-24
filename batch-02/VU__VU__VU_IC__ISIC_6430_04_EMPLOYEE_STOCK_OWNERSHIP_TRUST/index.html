Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), operating through the official VRCA portal (www.vcra.gov.vu) as the single agency administering the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 343], the Customs Act [Cap. 145] and residual fiscal duties; international company incorporation and standing are administered separately by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) (www.vfsc.vu) under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222].

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate an International Company (IC) with VFSC and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and the unique VFSC company number; (2) file the annual return and pay the fixed annual government fee to VFSC (no annual corporate income tax return exists because Vanuatu levies no income tax, capital gains tax, estate tax, withholding tax, or stamp duty on international companies); (3) apply to VRCA for VAT registration under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 343] on the standard VRCA form when the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu and annual taxable turnover exceeds VT 10,000,000, or electively below that threshold; (4) obtain from VRCA the VAT registration number / TIN and, where goods are imported or exported, register on the VRCA ASYCUDA World electronic customs declaration platform; (5) for an ESOP trust business, confine all trustee, administration, payroll, and equity-transfer services to non-resident employers, grantors, and beneficiaries so that the IC retains its tax-exempt and zero-rated offshore character.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — (1) Incorporate an International Company (IC) with VFSC and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and the unique VFSC company number; (2) file the annual return and pay the fixed annual government fee to VFSC (no annual corporate income tax return exists because Vanuatu levies no income tax, capital gains tax, estate tax, withholding tax, or stamp duty on international companies); (3) apply to VRCA for VAT registration under the Value Added Tax Act [Cap. 343] on the standard VRCA form when the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu and annual taxable turnover exceeds VT 10,000,000, or electively below that threshold; (4) obtain from VRCA the VAT registration number / TIN and, where goods are imported or exported, register on the VRCA ASYCUDA World electronic customs declaration platform; (5) for an ESOP trust business, confine all trustee, administration, payroll, and equity-transfer services to non-resident employers, grantors, and beneficiaries so that the IC retains its tax-exempt and zero-rated offshore character.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no income tax statute and therefore no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election comparable to US or EU regimes; the relevant statutory elections are (i) voluntary VAT registration below the VT 10,000,000 threshold under the VAT Act, (ii) election to convert or re-domicile an exempted domestic company as an International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], thereby acquiring the statutory tax exemption guarantee, and (iii) optional application for a Financial Dealers or Securities Dealer licence under the Financial Dealers Licensing Act [Cap. 70] where custody or dealing in employer securities exceeds the IC's exempt activity perimeter; none of these elections alters the entity-level zero-tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 annual taxable turnover (mandatory registration when cumulative taxable turnover in any consecutive 12-month period exceeds VT 10,000,000; voluntary registration permitted below the threshold for an IC making taxable supplies in Vanuatu)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under section 10 of the Value Added Tax Act applies to (i) goods exported from Vanuatu physically removed within the statutory timeframe and evidenced by customs documentation, and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident person for direct use and enjoyment outside Vanuatu, including offshore administration, trustee, payroll, and equity-transfer services rendered by an ESOP trust to non-resident employers, grantors, and beneficiaries; the supplier must hold valid VAT registration, issue a compliant tax invoice, retain proof of export or offshore service performance, and receive consideration in foreign currency from outside Vanuatu; domestic supply of trustee services to Vanuatu-resident beneficiaries is taxable at 15% and would erode the IC tax-exempt character.

  • VRCA Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic declaration bearing exit clearance stamp)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of consideration from the non-resident payor
  • Commercial invoice, export contract, and VAT zero-rating schedule
  • VFSC Certificate of Incorporation, annual return receipt, and valid International Company certificate for the ESOP trust

No real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, API interconnection, or digital stamping mandate is currently operative in Vanuatu; VAT invoices must contain the prescribed particulars under the VAT Act and are issued in paper or PDF form; exported goods and customs declarations are lodged electronically through the VRCA ASYCUDA World single-window declaration system, which generates the electronic Customs Export Declaration required for exit clearance.

Vanuatu imposes no exchange-control repatriation rules; the 180-day benchmark is adopted as a default audit tolerance. On review, VRCA may assess unpaid VAT at 15% on the full consideration where zero-rating documentation is defective, add statutory interest, and impose civil penalties under the VAT Act (fines up to VT 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 12 months for fraudulent evasion or false declarations); false customs declarations trigger penalties and forfeiture under the Customs Act [Cap. 145]; refunds previously paid on zero-rated export supplies are retrospectively clawed back with statutory interest, and VFSC may suspend or strike off an IC for inconsistent offshore trust activity.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is legal tender and convertible for current-account transactions; the capital account is liberalised for offshore-licensed and International Company structures. A VU-IC ESOP trust has full freedom to hold, convert and remit foreign currency without prior RBV approval. Profit and capital repatriation (dividend, redemption, trust distribution, employee proceeds) is unrestricted and carries zero withholding tax because Vanuatu levies no income tax, no capital gains tax and no remittance tax. The practical friction is limited offshore VUV liquidity; ESOP funding and disbursements are normally transacted in USD or a group base currency through Port Vila correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable to a VU-IC ESOP trust. Section 56 of the International Companies Act (Cap. 222) excludes International Companies from the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 102); therefore no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no surrender obligation attach to ESOP receipts, employer contributions or investment sale proceeds. Domestic Vanuatu exporters are also subject to a liberalised RBV reporting regime rather than a fixed surrender ratio; an IC enjoys a stricter statutory exemption than domestic entities.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act (Cap. 125), is the central bank and exchange-control administrator. In practice, exchange control for international companies is waived: Section 56 of the International Companies Act (Cap. 222) exempts every International Company from the operation of the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 102). The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) supervises trust companies, financial dealers and the financial services industry supporting ESOP treasury operations, but holds no monetary authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to Medium. No Vanuatu-licensed standalone electronic money institution (EMI) exists; offshore digital EMIs (UK, EU, Australian and Singapore-based) generally classify Vanuatu International Companies as higher-risk offshore jurisdictions and reject pure IC non-licensed structures, especially for holding or disbursing employee equity funds. Account approval is materially improbable unless the EMI accepts a VFSC-licensed trust company as the onboarding agent and the account is used only for low-velocity outbound benefit payments with strict source-of-funds files. An EMI may be used as a satellite disbursement rail for individual employee payments under USD 10,000 per transaction, but the core treasury should remain with ANZ Vanuatu or BSP Vanuatu commercial banking lines.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, and no branch profits tax; International Companies (ICs) are expressly exempt from any present or future tax on income, dividends, or capital under the International Companies Act (Cap 222), s.46, provided the IC does not carry on business within Vanuatu.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax; profits, distributions, and ESOP trust payouts to non-resident beneficiaries are remitted free of any statutory deduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return and license fee are due within one month after each anniversary of incorporation; the license fee itself is payable on or before the anniversary date of registration of the International Company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing legislation and prescribes no Local File or Master File threshold (statutory threshold: VUV 0); multinational groups involving a Vanuatu IC must instead satisfy documentation and arm's-length requirements in the foreign parent or operating jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not enact Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) of the BVI/Cayman type. Substance for an International Company is a negative statutory obligation: under s.5 of the International Companies Act the IC must not carry on business with or derive income from Vanuatu residents or domestic sources, and must maintain a registered office, a licensed registered agent, and accounting records. For an ESOP trust, administration may be entrusted to a licensed Vanuatu trust company under the International Trusts Act; foreign tax authorities, not Vanuatu, will apply foreign economic-substance or CFC rules to the trust.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Unauthorised Domestic Commercial Activity

Statutory sanction: Ilsant violation of s.5 of the International Companies Act: statutory fine of up to VUV 5,000,000, forced deregistration, compulsory winding-up, and retrospective VAT assessment with a default penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Knowing directors and trustees are personally liable for civil restitution of all profits derived, are disqualified from acting as directors, face asset-freezing orders and travel bans, and may be imprisoned for up to 12 months under the corporate-offences provisions.
Shell Company / False Compliance Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of up to VUV 1,000,000 plus daily default fines; VFSC may strike off the entity without further notice, render its shares void, and disqualify the local registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations incur personal criminal liability, compensation liability to creditors and employee-beneficiaries, and the court may pierce the corporate veil to attach the directors' personal assets.
AML/CFT Breach (Money Laundering)

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act: corporate fines of up to VUV 100,000,000, confiscation and forfeiture of trust assets, revocation of the trust company's license, and blacklisting of the registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors, trustees, and compliance officers face imprisonment of up to 15 years, personal criminal fines, travel bans pending trial, and disqualification orders.
Beneficial Owner Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Failure to comply with the beneficial-ownership information law attracts a corporate penalty of up to VUV 1,000,000 and suspension of the IC's good-standing certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for fines of up to VUV 500,000 and imprisonment of up to 12 months; the court may impose exit-control orders (travel bans) on non-compliant directors and trustees until the declaration is remedied.
