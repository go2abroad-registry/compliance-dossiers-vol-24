Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue (MOR) of the Independent State of Samoa, Apia; online e-Services tax portal at https://www.revenue.gov.ws; International Company registry and annual government license fee administered by the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA).

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities do not directly self-register. A licensed Samoa registered agent is appointed to (a) incorporate or register the entity with SIFA, (b) obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and, if required, (c) submit the TIN/VAGST registration application (Form R1) to the Ministry of Revenue together with the certificate of incorporation, constitution, agent consent letter, and passport copies of directors and shareholders. MOR issues a TIN within approximately 5-10 business days. A purely offshore International Company trading exclusively outside Samoa is statutorily exempt and is not required to hold a Samoan TIN or VAGST registration; its only annual obligation is the SIFA government license fee.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: An International Company (WS_IC) is excluded from the Samoan income tax and VAGST base. Under Section 175 of the International Companies Act 1987, as preserved under the Income Tax Act 2017, the entity is exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, dividend and interest withholding tax, gift duty, and stamp duty on all offshore business activities and profits, regardless of foreign ownership; the only periodic charge is the annual SIFA license fee. — Foreign entities do not directly self-register. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: An International Company (WS_IC) is excluded from the Samoan income tax and VAGST base. Under Section 175 of the International Companies Act 1987, as preserved under the Income Tax Act 2017, the entity is exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, dividend and interest withholding tax, gift duty, and stamp duty on all offshore business activities and profits, regardless of foreign ownership; the only periodic charge is the annual SIFA license fee.

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a US-style check-the-box entity classification regime and has not enacted a statutory hybrid-mismatch election. Entity status is elected at registration: applicants elect either an International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 or a local company under the Companies Act 2001. An International Company may, in its constitution, elect to be limited by shares, limited by guarantee, or unlimited, and may elect to denominate its issued capital and accounts in any foreign currency. The Income Tax Act 2017 contains a general anti-avoidance rule that counters hybrid mismatch outcomes in international arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 50,000 (approximately USD 18,500) annual taxable turnover; compulsory VAGST registration applies when rolling 12-month taxable turnover exceeds WST 50,000; voluntary registration is available to smaller suppliers. Wholly offshore sales of oversized furniture and home decor by an International Company are outside the scope of Samoan VAGST and do not count toward the registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the VAGST Act, supplies of goods physically exported from Samoa are zero-rated provided the goods are exported within 28 days of the date of supply; certain international services and services performed outside Samoa are also zero-rated. The exporter must be VAGST-registered, retain documentary proof of export, and show input tax was paid to substantiate a refund. For the offshore International Company conducting pure e-commerce furniture sales with no physical movement of goods through Samoa, no Samoan VAGST arises and the zero-rating/refund mechanism is not triggered.

  • Validated electronic Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry with export permit)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment and export within 28 days of supply
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign currency payment evidence from a licensed Samoan bank
  • Certificate of Origin where a preferential tariff claim is made, plus input tax invoices supporting the VAGST refund claim

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoice validation, or digital stamping mandate is currently enforced in Samoa. VAGST-registered suppliers must issue tax invoices in paper or PDF containing the prescribed invoice particulars, file VAGST returns electronically through the MOR e-Services Portal on a monthly or two-monthly cycle, and lodge customs declarations through ASYCUDA World at export.

The Commissioner of Revenue may audit export zero-rating claims, require production of shipping and banking records, and reassess within up to 6 years (with no time limit in fraud cases). Erroneous or fraudulent zero-rating and refund claims attract shortfall penalties of 20% for lack of reasonable care, 50% for gross negligence, and 150% for intentional evasion, plus late payment interest; refunds are clawed back, may be set off against current tax debts, and criminal prosecution may lead to substantial fines and imprisonment under the Tax Administration Act 2017.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa operates a managed-float currency regime for the Samoa Tālā (WST). Current-account transactions for bona fide trade are freely processed through authorised dealers, but capital-account movements, including equity injections and loans, require CBS approval or an authorised-dealer declaration. For a non-resident International Company, profit and dividend remittances are permitted where the company has no local-source income and produces its tax exemption certificate, audited financial statements, and an Application for Purchase of Foreign Exchange; no systematic convertibility blockage exists absent fraud or AML concern.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be repatriated to an authorised dealer in Samoa within 180 days from the bill of lading or shipping date. The default rule is 100% mandatory conversion into WST unless the CBS has approved a foreign-currency retention account, direct settlement of verified imports, or a documented hedging and working-capital exemption.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) and the Foreign Exchange Administration under the Exchange Control Act 1975

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low domestic viability. Samoa does not confer a standalone EMI licence on foreign digital-payment firms; offshore EMIs may be used only as front-end card or BNPL processors. All WST-denominated settlement and clearing must ultimately occur at a CBS-licensed commercial bank, so a local correspondent account remains mandatory.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory WST threshold is prescribed for tax-exempt Samoa International Companies. Transfer pricing documentation is triggered through the ESR filing when the IC has cross-border related-party transactions; a Local File should be maintained for controlled transactions, with a practical documentation threshold of WST 1,000,000 or more per annum. A group Master File should be maintained where the group meets the Samoa Revenue Administration consolidated revenue filing threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Samoa International Companies (Economic Substance) Amendment Act 2018 and its subsidiary regulations apply. A Samoa IC engaged in online retail of oversized furniture and home decor is likely to be treated as a 'distribution and service centre' if it receives orders, controls inventory, contracts carriers, and records revenue through the Samoan entity. The IC must demonstrate a physical office in Samoa, qualified full-time employees, adequate local expenditure, local board/management presence, and core income-generating activities performed in Samoa. Passive holding companies face reduced substance requirements but must prove passive status by filing the annual declaration. Non-compliance can lead to financial penalties, deregistration, automatic exchange of information, and referral to AML authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion by misuse of International Company exemption

Statutory sanction: A conviction under Samoa's Income Tax Administration Act carries fines, a civil tax assessment, an evasion penalty of up to 100% of tax undercharged, interest, and imprisonment of up to 7 years; the IC may also be struck off with assets recovered by the State.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign or approve false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax and penalties; they may face civil restitution, asset freezing, travel bans, disqualification, and custodial sentences.
Shell company and sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: The Registrar or SIFSC may impose a monetary penalty up to WST 1,000,000, order daily default penalties, strike off and dissolve the company, require disgorgement of economic benefits, and disclose the entity to Samoa's Financial Intelligence Unit; the registered agent may also be fined or suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified, ordered to contribute to company debts on fraudulent-trading grounds, subjected to travel restrictions during investigation, and convicted where the sham was used to defraud creditors or tax authorities.
AML/CFT and beneficial ownership non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's AML/CFT legislation, penalties can reach WST 2,000,000 or twice the value of laundered property; bank accounts may be frozen and forfeited; imprisonment of up to 12 years may be imposed; and payment providers may terminate access to the IC's merchant accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners, and AML compliance officers face criminal prosecution for money laundering, civil asset forfeiture, personal fines, travel bans, imprisonment, and veil-piercing where the IC is used as an instrument of crime.
Transfer pricing misstatement and profit shifting

Statutory sanction: If Samoa's transfer pricing and arm's-length principles apply, the Revenue will adjust income to arm's-length levels, impose interest and penalties of up to 20% of adjusted tax, or 100% for deliberate error; the IC may also lose its tax-exemption status, face ESR penalties, and be deregistered.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly implement artificial intercompany arrangements can be held personally liable for tax adjustments and responsible-officer penalties; courts may order restitution, impose travel bans, disqualify directors, and impose imprisonment for fraudulent profit shifting.
