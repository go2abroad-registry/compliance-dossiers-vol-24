Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

No. A Vanuatu International Company must maintain a registered office at a physical address in Vanuatu provided by or through a licensed registered agent. The registered office cannot be a post-office box or a virtual-only address. However, operational and administrative functions may be conducted remotely, and the director and shareholder meetings may be held by telephone, video conference, or written resolution in accordance with the company's constitution. The registered agent's address will be the official service of process and statutory mailing address; no separate business activity, employees, or physical premises are required for the International Company unless the company later obtains a VFSC license.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Government of Vanuatu; digital portal at https://dcir.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

Foreign entity must first register as an International Company with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). The VFSC registration number serves as the foundation identifier. Thereafter, apply for a tax identification number (TIN) and VAT ID through the DCIR VATeam online registration system by submitting the certificate of incorporation, registered office address, director/shareholder details, and proof of VFSC registration. A local tax representative may be required for non-resident registrants. Because an International Company is exempt from income tax, the TIN is primarily used for VAT, customs, and statutory reporting obligations under Vanuatu's value-added tax regime.

The VFSC registration number serves as the foundation identifier. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu does not operate a check-the-box pass-through regime. International Companies may elect for a tax exemption certificate under section 15 of the International Companies Act. No hybrid mismatch election is available, as Vanuatu has no income tax treaty network, no CFC attribution rules, and no corporate income tax for International Companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT 10,000,000 (VUV) per calendar year for local taxable supplies. Non-resident digital service suppliers are registrable when their supplies to private consumers in Vanuatu exceed this turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of goods where ownership passes to a non-resident and the goods are physically exported within 28 days. Zero-rating also applies to the supply of services to a non-resident where the services are consumed wholly outside Vanuatu, including software-as-a-service, cloud-based financial portfolio tracking, data analytics, and other offshore digital services. The supplier must be registered for VAT, retain documentary evidence of the export nature, and declare the supply as zero-rated on the VAT return.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CUS) approved by the Vanuatu Customs Authority.
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate proving receipt of foreign exchange.
  • Signed contract, purchase order, or subscription agreement identifying the non-resident recipient.
  • Service Completion Certificate or performance evidence for offshore services.

No real-time e-invoicing clearance or digital stamping mandate is currently in operation. Registered businesses must maintain VAT-compliant tax invoices and file periodic VAT returns through the DCIR VATeam portal. Customs import and export declarations are processed via ASYCUDA World. Full API-based fiscal clearance and continuous transaction control are not yet statutory requirements.

Zero-rating claims are subject to DCIR audit. False or unsupported claims require repayment of VAT previously zero-rated, plus interest from the date the VAT was first claimed. Penalties include an additional tax of up to 50% of the unpaid amount, a fixed penalty of VT 500,000 for fraudulent evasion, and criminal prosecution for knowing misstatement. International Companies may also be stripped of their tax-exempt status and face deregistration by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible across both current and capital accounts. No exchange control legislation or RBV approval is required for inward or outward remittances, dividend distributions, loan repayments, or capital repatriation. Profit remittance by an International Company is unrestricted subject to commercially reasonable documentation (audited financial statements, tax registration confirmation, and shareholder resolution). The absence of capital account controls makes Vanuatu a low-friction jurisdiction for treasury flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rules exist. There is no mandatory conversion ratio and no timeline requiring export or service revenue to be repatriated or converted into VUV, enabling natural-currency treasury holding.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low direct viability. Non-bank foreign digital EMIs (e.g., global fintech platforms) that are not licensed by the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu may not provide segregated client escrow accounts in VUV and are constrained by Vanuatu's Banking Act and Financial Institutions Act. The International Company should rely on Tier-1 commercial banks or RBV-licensed banks for clearing and settlement; foreign EMIs may only be used for pure card-acquiring or SaaS subscription settlement subject to VFSC licensing assessment, and any such EMI wallet must be positioned as a downstream collection channel, not as the primary treasury account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No general transfer pricing documentation law exists because there is no corporate income tax. The mandatory documentation-triggering thresholds that do apply: (i) Economic Substance Regulations Act 2020 - ESR return required where gross income from a relevant activity exceeds the gazetted de minimis threshold (approximately VUV 30 million / USD 260,000); (ii) Country-by-Country reporting - an IC belonging to a multinational group with consolidated revenue above EUR 750 million is reportable through the Vanuatu competent authority exchange mechanism.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations Act 2020: an International Company carrying on a relevant activity - including financing/leasing, distribution and service centre business, pure equity holding, or intellectual property business - must demonstrate in Vanuatu: (i) board meetings and minutes held in Vanuatu; (ii) physically present employees with adequate qualifications; (iii) adequate operating expenditure incurred in Vanuatu; and (iv) core income-generating activities performed in Vanuatu. An annual ESR return must be filed with VFSC. A software-as-a-service wealth-tracking platform holding proprietary code as IP must either prove genuine Vanuatu IP substance or pivot to a low-risk agency/service characterization.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Fictitious Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Economic Substance Regulations Act 2020 up to VUV 15,000,000; court-ordered strike-off from the company register; referral to the Public Prosecutor for criminal enforcement; and publication of the entity on a non-compliant list shared with the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices.

Director personal liability: Directors are exposed to civil restitution orders, director disqualification by the Vanuatu Supreme Court, personal fines, and travel bans of up to 25 years where the court finds knowing participation in a sham substance arrangement.
Tax Evasion (Home Jurisdiction)

Statutory sanction: Home-jurisdiction liability: accuracy penalties up to 150% of the evaded tax, interest, anti-money-laundering fines, and criminal prosecution. In Vanuatu, making false statements to the Financial Intelligence Unit is an offence under the Penal Code (Cap 135) punishable by fine or imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Personal prosecution in the shareholder's home country for tax fraud; CFC-attribution of the IC's income to the individual; Vanuatu directors may face extradition under the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters legislation and personal asset freeze orders.
AML / Unlicensed Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: Conviction for unlicensed securities dealing or possession of client funds: fines up to VUV 50,000,000, confiscation of proceeds, mandatory revocation of the company registration, and inclusion in FATF grey-list risk communications.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly control the unlicensed activity face criminal liability for money laundering under the Penal Code with imprisonment up to 20 years, personal asset confiscation, and a prohibition on acting as a director of any VFSC-regulated entity.
VAT Evasion / Unregistered Local Supply

Statutory sanction: VAT default penalty of 50% of the tax concerned; default assessment for up to 36 months; penalty of VUV 50,000 plus daily penalty up to VUV 10,000; criminal prosecution and fine if non-compliance continues after notice.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the unpaid VAT and penalties under the VAT Act enforcement provisions; the Commissioner of Taxes may issue a travel restriction order against directors until the VAT debt is settled.
