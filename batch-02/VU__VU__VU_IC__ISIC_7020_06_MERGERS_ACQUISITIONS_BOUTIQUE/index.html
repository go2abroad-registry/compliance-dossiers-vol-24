Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Finance and Treasury, Inland Revenue Division, Vanuatu (VRMS online portal: https://vrms.gov.vu); VFSC handles incorporation and licensing.

E-government filing portal

Incorporate as an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No separate TIN is issued for direct tax because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax for ICs. If the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu, register for VAT under the Value Added Tax Act No. 29 of 2019 via VRMS. VAT registration requires the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office certificate, resident agent details, and details of taxable activity; the VRMS assigns a VAT registration number used as the indirect tax identifier.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or direct tax classification election exists under Vanuatu law. A VAT-registered entity may elect to file VAT returns electronically, and a business that makes only zero-rated supplies may elect to register for VAT to recover input VAT, subject to Commissioner approval. Hybrid mismatch and transfer-pricing elections are not applicable because no direct income tax regime is operative.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 10,000,000 in taxable supplies during any consecutive 12-month period; registration is compulsory within 28 days after the month in which the threshold is exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Vanuatu and supply of services to a non-resident recipient are zero-rated where the recipient is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and the services are performed outside Vanuatu or are not consumed directly in Vanuatu. For an M&A advisory boutique, pure offshore advisory services, success fees, and cross-border deal management services to non-resident clients qualify for 0% VAT, provided the contract, invoicing, and payment flows clearly indicate the non-resident destination. Any local onshore services or services connected to Vanuatu property or VU-domiciled target entities must be separately assessed and may be subject to 15% VAT unless the recipient is non-resident and the service is exported.

  • Customs export declaration or ASYCUDA World electronic filing (for goods transactions; for pure services, this is substituted by a service export declaration/statement)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (only where tangible goods are involved; not required for advisory services)
  • Signed service agreement/engagement letter evidencing non-resident client and distinct offshore deliverables
  • Tax invoice issued in compliance with VAT Act, clearly showing 0% export rating and basis of zero-rating
  • SWIFT/remittance certificate or inward remittance advice evidencing payment from outside Vanuatu
  • Work papers, board resolutions, and transaction closing records substantiating the M&A advisory services and success fee accrual

No real-time electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate is currently implemented. VAT-registered businesses must issue tax invoices in paper or electronic form containing required particulars; returns are filed through VRMS electronically. No API-based clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control (CTC) regime is yet operational.

The Commissioner may conduct audit review of export zero-rating claims. If a zero-rating claim is unsupported or the documentary evidence is deficient, VAT at 15% is assessed on the transaction value, with penalty interest and penalties up to 200% of the tax evaded for fraudulent claims. Criminal prosecution may follow for knowingly false statements. Input VAT recovered in reliance on a zero-rated export may be clawed back, and VAT registration benefits may be revoked. Export proceeds ordinarily should be received in the IC's bank account within 180 days of invoice date; failure to receive remittance without a documented bona fide trade dispute may cause the zero-rating to be reversed and trigger assessment, interest, and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for current-account payments and remittance flows consistent with IMF Article VIII obligations; advisory and service fees may be paid abroad without restriction. Capital-account flows are liberalized but must be executed through an authorized dealer, with statistical reporting to the RBV above the prescribed threshold. An International Company (VU_IC) registered under the International Companies Act is treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes and may hold USD, AUD, and EUR accounts onshore or offshore without RBV convertibility approval; dividend and profit remittance is subject to no material statutory hurdle provided the IC derives no domestic Vanuatu-source business income. Practical constraint: offshore VUV non-deliverable forward (NDF) liquidity is negligible, so settlement and hedging should be denominated in USD or AUD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender ratio or time-bound repatriation obligation applies in Vanuatu. Under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 102], service receipts of a non-resident IC may be retained indefinitely in foreign currency with an authorized dealer or in an offshore bank account. The only compliance duty is passive: the receiving authorized dealer records the credit on the RBV statistical exchange-control return and performs standard AML/CFT vetting. For the M&A boutique, success-fee wires are current-account service receipts and fall outside any conversion or surrender requirement.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - statutory central bank and exchange-control administration under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 102]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately licenses the M&A advisory IC and supervises its AML/CFT compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-to-MEDIUM with a hard legal cap. Global fintech EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex and similar) generally decline native onboarding of Vanuatu International Companies on jurisdiction-risk grounds, the entity's statutory tax-exempt status, and the enhanced scrutiny attached to a VFSC-licensed M&A advisory. Such EMIs are not authorized deposit-taking institutions under RBV law and cannot provide domestic Vanuatu clearing in VUV. Where an EMI is used strictly as a non-VUV offshore wallet, the IC must still anchor the treasury structure with at least one authorized-dealer account in Vanuatu, and all EMI disbursements must mirror the approved fee schedule with verifiable source-of-funds evidence.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital must arrive by SWIFT MT103 in USD, AUD, or EUR. Vanuatu does not use an IBAN standard, so the receiving authorized dealer requires the full beneficiary name, account number, and bank BIC. The remittance reference must state: 'Capital contribution to VU_IC per shareholder resolution [date]'. Verification pack: certified incorporation documents; extract of the register of members and share-allotment resolution; registered-agent certificate of good standing; and, where gross proceeds exceed the RBV statistical ceiling, the dealer files the applicable exchange-control statistical return. Capital intended for offshore M&A fee working capital must not be applied to domestic Vanuatu transactions, preserving the IC's non-resident tax-exempt status.Outbound settlements for the M&A advisory boutique should be cleared via SWIFT MT103 from a USD or AUD concentration account; authorized dealers require a disbursement file containing the signed engagement mandate, date-issued invoice, and closing or fee-trigger evidence. For escrowed M&A consideration or deferred payment obligations, an irrevocable, confirmed, fully transferable commercial letter of credit advised by a tier-1 Vanuatu bank is the applicable documentary rail, with standby LCs available to back completion undertakings.
