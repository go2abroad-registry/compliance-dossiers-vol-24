Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue (MOR), Government of Samoa, headed by the Commissioner of Revenue; official portal: www.revenue.gov.ws; policy oversight by the Minister for Revenue.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: A licensed registered agent in Samoa incorporates the International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act 1987 through the Samoa International Finance Authority / corporate registry, obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation. Step 2: The registered agent files the TIN application (Form TR-1) with the MOR, attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office address, and director/shareholder particulars; the MOR issues a company TIN within 3-5 business days. Step 3: VAGST registration is required only if the IC makes taxable supplies in Samoa exceeding WST 100,000 per rolling 12 months; an IC dealing exclusively in offshore cane sugar commodity trading (goods never landing in Samoa) is outside the scope of VAGST and may remain unregistered, or may voluntarily register solely to recover VAGST on local costs.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Samoan law. The IC is statutorily tax-exempt under the International Companies Act 1987 (exemption from income tax, VAGST on offshore-facing activities, stamp duty, and all other Samoan taxes and exchange controls), so no entity-classification election is required or available in respect of that exemption. An IC may, by special resolution, elect to redomicile or convert to a local company under the Companies Act 2006, thereby losing its exempt status and becoming subject to the Income Tax Act 2014; this election is irreversible except by reincorporation as a new IC. Samoa's Income Tax Act 2014 contains controlled-foreign-company rules and a general anti-avoidance provision addressing hybrid arrangements, but no formal hybrid-mismatch election is provided; where shareholders' home jurisdictions treat the IC as fiscally transparent, Samoan law applies a purely domestic legal-form characterization and does not recharacterize the exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period (mandatory registration); voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Schedule 2 of the Goods and Services Tax Act 1992: (i) a supply of goods physically exported by the supplier from Samoa is zero-rated, provided the goods are removed from Samoa in the course of the supply and are not subsequently used locally; (ii) a supply of services to a non-resident recipient is zero-rated where the services are not physically performed in Samoa and are not directly in connection with land or goods situated in Samoa; (iii) international transport and freight services are zero-rated regardless of the supplier. For the WS_IC raw cane sugar arbitrage model (VHP raw cane centrifugal sugar shipped FOB/CIF from an origin port directly to a destination port with no physical presence in Samoa), the supply is outside the scope of Samoan VAGST because no part of the supply is made, utilized, or delivered in Samoa; consequently, no VAGST, zero-rating election, or refund procedure applies to the principal sale. If a sugar parcel is ever transhipped or warehoused in Samoa, the supply becomes zero-rated only upon proof of continuous export, evidenced by the customs export entry, through bill of lading, warehouse receipts, and discharge certificates. Input VAGST attributable to exported goods or outside-scope supplies is refundable only if the refund claim is filed within 12 months of the end of the taxable period and the supplier holds the complete documentary bundle listed below.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs Service export/submission entry)
  • Original or negotiable copy of the Bill of Lading plus mate's or tally receipt naming the IC as shipper
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/forex statement proving receipt of export proceeds in a Samoan licensed commercial bank
  • Fully executed commodity sale contract together with the pro-forma and final commercial invoice
  • Independent polarization / SGS inspection certificate for raw cane sugar grading as contractually agreed
  • Certificate of Origin and shipping instruction/cargo manifest (where required by the importing jurisdiction)

No real-time electronic fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC) regime, or digital tax stamp applies in Samoa as of the knowledge cutoff. Registered persons must file VAGST returns electronically via the MOR e-Services portal by the 28th day of the month following the end of the taxable period; monthly filing applies to all registered persons. Each taxable supply exceeding WST 100 must be documented by a tax invoice in the statutory form containing the supplier's VAGST registration number, invoice date, description, and tax amount. The MOR permits one-time bulk return uploads via API for large taxpayers; no per-invoice government clearance, digital stamping, or pre-approval is imposed.

The Tax Administration Act 2014 imposes: (i) a shortfall penalty of 50% of the tax undercharged for failure to take reasonable care, and 150% for intentional disregard, fraud, or wilfully misleading statements; (ii) a late-payment penalty of 2% per month or part of a month on unpaid VAGST, capped at 100% of the tax; (iii) a late-filing penalty of WST 500 per month for registered persons, capped at WST 5,000; and (iv) interest at the gazetted MOR rate from the due date. The Commissioner may make retrospective assessments up to 5 years, extended to 10 years where fraud or wilful default is proven, and may claw back any VAGST refunded or credited with compound interest from the original refund date. Criminal prosecution for export zero-rating fraud, including false declarations, forged export documentation, or knowingly claiming refunds without physical export, renders the IC and its responsible officers liable on conviction to a fine of up to WST 200,000 and imprisonment of up to 3 years, plus forfeiture of the goods concerned. Exporters must retain the entire substantiation bundle for 10 years from the date of export; failure to produce records upon demand carries a separate penalty of WST 2,000 per failure. In practice, the MOR audits sugar and commodity traders under elevated scrutiny, applying substance tests to confirm that risk of loss and title passed offshore and that hard-currency proceeds were repatriated through a licensed Samoan commercial bank within the 180-day contractual/financing window.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a non-internationally traded currency under a managed float; current-account payments for merchandise trade are liberalized, while capital-account inflows and outflows (shareholder loans, repatriation of capital, portfolio movements) remain subject to CBS screening. A Samoa International Company holding a CBS exchange-control exemption certificate is treated as a non-resident offshore entity: it may hold, receive, and remit USD freely for sugar purchases, freight, charter hire and profit distribution without prior CBS approval and with zero tax leakage inside the Tax-Exempt Zone. The practical limitations are liquidity and correspondent access: the WST interbank market is shallow, USD clearing depends on ANZ Australia and BSP regional loops, and every capital movement must be evidenced by board resolutions and registered-agent confirmations to avoid triggering CBS reporting assumptions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a non-exempt Samoa trading company, export proceeds from the physical shipment of VHP raw cane sugar must be repatriated to Samoa within three months from the bill-of-lading date, with 100% of the foreign exchange surrendered to an authorized dealer at the prevailing CBS-sanctioned rate unless a specific exemption is granted. A duly licensed International Company with an offshore exemption certificate is relieved of the mandatory conversion and surrender obligation; nevertheless, it must file the export declaration with the CBS for balance-of-payments statistical purposes and must maintain a complete invoice, bill-of-lading, and vessel documentation trail to sustain classification of the cargo as bona fide offshore trade rather than domestic export.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), Apia — the statutory regulator of foreign exchange under the Exchange Control Act 1952, the Exchange Control Regulations 1952 (as amended), and the Financial Institutions Act 2016. All foreign-exchange transactions by residents must be executed through licensed authorized dealers, which are the CBS-approved commercial banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low for primary treasury rails. The Central Bank of Samoa does not license standalone electronic-money institutions or payment-institution platforms, and the Exchange Control Act requires all foreign-currency transactions to pass through licensed authorized dealers. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie and other multi-currency IBAN providers) cannot serve as the clearing bank of record for sugar-trade LC disbursements or vessel payments, and their use as a shadow USD treasury creates tri-party AML/CFT corroboration risk and CBS reporting gaps. Permissible deployment is strictly ancillary: EMIs may be used for executive expenses and minor overheads, provided the Samoa commercial bank is fully disclosed; the core USD liquidity must remain domiciled at ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Because the Samoa International Company has no statutory minimum capital, equity is subscribed or advanced through SWIFT MT103/MT202 transfers from the beneficial owner's home bank to the IC's USD account at ANZ Samoa or BSP Samoa via the Australian and New York correspondent corridors. Field 70 (Remittance Information) must carry the purpose identifier 'Capital Contribution / Shareholder Loan' or 'G301 – Trade in Goods' as applicable; concurrently, the IC files the CBS exchange-control exemption declaration, a board resolution authorizing the inward investment, source-of-wealth evidence, and the registered-agent confirmation of the updated shareholder register. Funds are released for trade disbursement only after cleared credit and after the Samoa bank's AML unit completes verification and assigns a compliant internal purpose code.Outbound settlements for VHP raw cane sugar rely on two clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 wires for clean payments, documentary collections, freight, and demurrage; and (ii) irrevocable sight or 60-day usance letters of credit issued by the counterparty's bank and advised or confirmed by a tier-1 bank in Singapore or London, with the Samoa bank acting strictly as advising bank. USD settlement is executed through the ANZ and BSP international correspondent loops (SWIFT to CHIPS/Fedwire via New York correspondents). Because Samoan commercial banks lack dedicated sugar-documentary credit desks, LC document examination, bill-of-exchange negotiation, and discrepancy handling are delegated downstream to the confirming bank under a back-to-back issuance framework, preserving the Samoa IC's riskless intermediary position in the physical sugar trade.USD/WST deliverable currency forwards up to 12 months, executed through an authorized dealer with an underlying trade invoice and CBS exemption declarationUSD/WST non-deliverable forwards (NDF) booked on the Samoa bank's or its offshore head-office trading desk, cash-settled in USD with no physical WST conversionUSD/AUD and USD/NZD cross-currency swaps for freight, port, insurance, and financing-expense hedging, permitted for exempt International Companies with a CBS declarationVanilla USD/WST foreign-exchange options (puts and calls) with the authorized dealer, with premiums payable from the IC's USD operational account

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Company (IC) on all non-Samoa-source income under the International Companies Act 1987, s.193; the general Samoa corporate income tax rate is 27% for assessable companies under the Income Tax Act 2012

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders (exempt under the International Companies Act 1987, s.193); the general non-resident dividend withholding tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2012 is 15%

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

IC-AR must be filed within 90 days of the financial year-end (31 December for calendar-year filers), i.e., by 31 March; where an IC becomes assessable for Samoa-source income, Form C is due by 30 June following the tax year, with provisional tax instalments payable quarterly

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa law prescribes no monetary TP documentation threshold; however, the Income Tax Act 2012 empowers the Commissioner to assess arm's-length pricing on any controlled cross-border transaction. Practical compliance benchmark for the sugar wholesale sector: prepare a Local File where annual related-party sales exceed WST 5,000,000 (approx. USD 1,850,000) and a Master File where global consolidated turnover exceeds WST 100,000,000

Economic substance scope: Samoa, as a member of the Global Forum and the BEPS Inclusive Framework, applies Economic Substance Regulations through SIFA and the International Companies Act 1987. An IC deriving trading or wholesale income must demonstrate either (a) real economic substance in Samoa (physical office, qualified staff, board management, core income-generating activities performed locally) or (b) tax residence and substantive activities in a foreign jurisdiction; bare-bone mailboxes without any substance declaration are subject to strike-off and reporting to the Samoan FIU

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2012: administrative penalty of 200% of the tax underpaid plus interest at 1% per month; criminal conviction punishable by a fine of up to SAT 500,000 (approx. USD 185,000) and imprisonment of up to 5 years

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the evaded tax; the Court may issue a travel ban and asset freezing order; a conviction carries custodial sentence with no eligibility for parole in offenses above SAT 200,000
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and deregistration by SIFA; forfeiture of the IC name; a director of a deregistered company may be disqualified from managing any Samoan company for 5 years; reactivation requires the payment of all arrears, penalties (SAT 100 per day), and re-registration fees of SAT 2,500

Director personal liability: Where the corporate veil is pierced, directors incur personal liability for unpaid annual licensing fees, third-party trade debts, and consequential damages; the Central Bank of Samoa may ban the director from holding or controlling any Samoan licensed financial or trust institution
AML

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 (Samoa): a fine of up to SAT 1,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 10 years for money laundering; additional civil forfeiture of the entire proceeds of the offending transactions and freezing of related Swift wire transfers

Director personal liability: Prosecutors may indict directors as principals for laundering funds through the IC; personal assets are subject to confiscation orders; travel bans and surrender of passports are automatic while an anti-money laundering investigation is pending; directors face lifetime disqualification from regulated financial services
