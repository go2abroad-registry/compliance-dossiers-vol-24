Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue, Government of Samoa (Samoa Revenue Authority / Inland Revenue and Customs administration); official taxpayer portal and e-services: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC) is first incorporated through a licensed registered agent with the Samoa International Finance Authority / Registrar of International Companies under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended). Because the IC is structured as a non-resident tax-exempt offshore entity, it is not automatically registered with the Samoa Ministry for Revenue and is generally not required to obtain a domestic taxpayer identification number or file income tax returns for foreign-source income. If the IC intends to conduct any taxable activity in Samoa, import medical-device components, employ local staff, or claim zero-rating/refund of Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) on export sales, it must apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and obtain separate VAGST registration. The application requires submission of the IC certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, registered-agent certificate, directors and beneficial-owner identity evidence, a local business licence if applicable, and the value of expected annual taxable supplies. The Ministry then issues the TIN and, upon meeting the registration threshold or applying voluntarily, a VAGST registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election exists under Samoa law. A Samoa International Company is automatically tax-exempt on all offshore income by statute and is not a pass-through entity for Samoan income-tax purposes; the exemption applies unless the IC converts to a domestic company under the Companies Act or exercises a permitted local business activity. The IC may voluntarily register for VAGST even below the statutory turnover threshold in order to zero-rate exports and recover input tax, and may apply to the Ministry for Revenue for a binding ruling or private clearance on the export zero-rating eligibility of medical-imaging supplies. No hybrid-mismatch elections or dual-residency tax elections are available in the Samoa domestic regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 taxable annual turnover in Samoan Tālā (compulsory registration); voluntary registration permitted for exporters, with the threshold measured over any consecutive 12-month period

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export sale of goods if the goods are supplied to a person who is outside Samoa, are physically exported from Samoa within the statutory period and are not subsequently re-imported for local use, and the exporter retains sufficient evidence of the international supply chain. Zero-rating also covers certain services supplied directly to a non-resident recipient and consumed entirely outside Samoa, including installation, testing, calibration and software support associated with an exported medical-imaging console, provided the services are not performed for a Samoa-resident benefit. The Color Doppler Ultrasound Console must be sold under an export contract, consigned to a foreign hospital or distributor, and cleared by Samoa Customs under the export declaration; mere local delivery to a related party will not qualify. Input VAT incurred on local purchase of components, consumables, calibration equipment and freight services is recoverable against the zero-rated export output; any input tax attributable to domestic taxable supplies remains creditable only to the extent of the VAGST return and invoice-substantiation requirements.

  • Samoa Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic CusDec clearance record)
  • Commercial Invoice issued by the Samoa International Company to the overseas buyer
  • Packing List identifying the ultrasound console, serial number, HS classification 9018.13 and export quantity
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of the goods from Samoa
  • Certificate of Origin or manufacturer's compliance certificate if required by the importing jurisdiction
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of the foreign-currency sales proceeds from the overseas buyer

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time electronic fiscal invoicing, government API clearance, or digital stamping for commercial invoices. A VAGST-registered exporter must issue a compliant VAGST tax invoice in paper or PDF format containing prescribed particulars within 28 days of the supply. Export clearance is handled through the Customs Division using the ASYCUDA World electronic customs management system, which requires an approved electronic export declaration before the Color Doppler Ultrasound Console consignment is loaded. The Ministry for Revenue provides an e-filing portal for VAGST returns and tax payments, but that portal is not an invoice-clearance or e-invoicing fiscal gateway.

Samoa Revenue Authority may subject export zero-rating claims to desk or field audit, document verification through ASYCUDA Customs records, bank-inward-remittance reconciliation, and inspection of warehouse/logistics records. If the exporter fails to produce the complete export documentation bundle, fails to prove shipment from Samoa, or fails to bring the export proceeds into the country within the 180-day statutory repatriation window without a Ministry-approved foreign-exchange waiver, the zero-rated treatment is retrospectively reversed and the full 15% VAGST becomes payable together with late-payment interest and penalties. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims, false Customs declarations, forged bills of lading, artificial round-tripping of goods, or misstatement of the destination or buyer attract additional civil shortfall penalties of up to the amount of tax evaded, prosecution under the Tax Administration Act and Customs law, fines, imprisonment of responsible directors/off-icers, and cancellation or suspension of the International Company's registration by the Registrar of International Companies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is convertible for current-account trade settlements through authorized dealers; capital-account transactions require prior CBS approval or exemption. International Companies in Samoa operate in the Tax-Exempt Zone and have no Samoan income-tax or withholding-tax on profit remittances. However, dividends, loan repayments, liquidation proceeds, and other capital-related remittances must be supported by audited financial statements and submitted through a licensed commercial bank. The CBS monitors cross-border flows under its exchange control mandate, and non-resident owners must demonstrate source of funds upon capital injection. The practical remittance hurdle is not taxation but documentary compliance with CBS forms, BO registers, and compliance with anti-money-laundering requirements of the banking channel.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Samoa requires all export proceeds to be repatriated to an authorized dealer within six months from the date of export shipment. Unless a foreign-currency account retention exemption is granted by the CBS under the Exchange Control Act, 100% of export receipts must be offered for conversion into WST through an authorized dealer within one month of receipt. For International Companies that operate as offshore export vehicles, the CBS may permit foreign-currency retention where the proceeds are contractually committed to foreign-currency trade liabilities; any net surplus not so committed must be surrendered. All export documents, customs declarations, bills of lading, and commercial invoices must be retained for five years and presented upon CBS inspection.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) – Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa, acting under the Exchange Control Act 1970, the Financial Institutions Act 1996, and the CBS Exchange Control Guidelines

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Samoa does not maintain an electronic-money institution (EMI) licensing regime. Non-bank digital EMI providers are not authorized to conduct foreign-exchange clearing or hold client funds unless separately licensed as banks under the Financial Institutions Act 1996. Offshore digital EMIs cannot settle WST transactions or access Samoa's domestic interbank clearing infrastructure without a licensed bank partner. An International Company should therefore use a tier-1 Samoan commercial bank or a reputable global banking provider with a Samoa-located branch.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections and shareholder loans must be routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the beneficial owner's bank to the International Company's designated WST or USD account at a Samoan authorized dealer. The wire must state the CBS-approved transaction purpose code — 'EM1 Equity Capital Direct Investment' for shares or 'EL1 Direct Investment Loans' for shareholder borrowings, or the corresponding Samoan exchange-control nomenclature. Documentation includes: board resolution approving the capital injection, executed subscription agreement or loan agreement, proof of source of wealth, CBS exchange-control notification form where applicable, and a SIFA beneficial ownership declaration. Funds will be credited only after the receiving bank completes inbound funds verification and confirms the capital-account classification.The primary clearing rails for outbound trade settlements are SWIFT MT103 'Our' or 'Cover' payments for TT wire transfers and irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued under UCP600 via SWIFT MT700/MT710. Where a supplier requires an LC, the Samoan issuing bank opens the credit through its correspondent bank and settles via SWIFT MT756/MT202 when documents conform under the LC. For export collections, SWIFT MT400 or MT410 may be used through the CBCA network. All outbound trade payments require invoicing in USD, AUD, NZD, or EUR when the bank cannot settle WST; the bank will verify the underlying purchase order, commercial invoice, cargo documents, and CBS form for the specific trade. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends, distributions, and liquidation proceeds paid by a Samoan International Company to any non-resident shareholder; the law expressly prohibits any withholding or other tax on shareholder distributions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Nil / Not Applicable for a tax-exempt International Company. Because the IC has a 0% corporate tax rate, Samoa's transfer pricing documentation thresholds (Mandatory Country-by-Country Report threshold of WST 500,000,000 and Local File/Master File threshold of WST 5,000,000) do not apply. However, if the IC enters into transactions with a Samoan resident entity or a treaty-protected ordinary company, contemporaneous documentation must still be prepared to prove arm's length pricing in case the IC is re-characterized as a resident taxpayer.

Economic substance scope: Samoa's International Companies (Economic Substance) Amendment Act 2018/2020 applies to ICs conducting 'relevant activities' — namely banking, insurance, shipping, distribution, service center, finance/leasing, headquarters, fund management, and intellectual property holding. Manufacturing of medical diagnostic scanners and the export of color Doppler ultrasound consoles, if conducted through a Samoan entity with physical operations elsewhere, is generally NOT a 'pure equity holding' or IP-only activity; nevertheless, a 3% minimum economic substance test may apply. The IC must demonstrate: (a) actual management and control in Samoa; (b) adequate physical presence (staff, premises, export logistics); and (c) core income-generating activities performed in Samoa. If the IC is a pure holding company for the medical equipment IP, it qualifies for the reduced substance test and must maintain: registered office, Samoan-resident director, and minutes of board meetings held in Samoa.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Loss of International Company Exempt Status

Statutory sanction: Retrospective revocation of tax exemption; assessment of corporate income tax at the headline rate of 27% (or the applicable Samoan corporate rate) on all income from the date of first domestic activity; imposition of 20% late-payment interest; compounded penalties up to 200% of tax under-assessed; monetary penalty of WST 50,000 for unlicensed trading; a daily penalty of WST 1,000 until the offense ceases.

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners, and shadow directors become jointly and severally liable for all corporate tax arrears, penalties, and interest where the company's exemption is voided. Courts may pierce the corporate veil and seize personal assets. Directors of a void exempt IC may also be subject to travel bans under Samoa's Revenue Administration Act 2015 if tax debts exceed WST 25,000, and imprisonment for up to 5 years for willful tax evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Amendment Act, the Registrar may impose an economic substance penalty of WST 100,000 for the current year and WST 50,000,000 for repeated violations (in exceptional cases). The company may be struck off the international register and automatically converted to a domestic company. The Samoa Ministry of Revenue may also notify the OECD Global Forum, leading to adverse tax transparency ratings and automatic exchange of information (CRS/FATCA) disclosures to the shareholder's home jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate beneficial owners each face personal monetary fines of up to WST 150,000 and disqualification from holding directorship of any Samoa company for 10 years. The court may order the payment of all compliance costs and restitution of hidden profits. Where false declarations are made to the Registrar in respect of economic substance, directors are liable to imprisonment up to 7 years.
AML/CFT Breach (Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing)

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act 2021, a body corporate may be fined up to WST 2,000,000 for a first offense and WST 5,000,000 for a subsequent offense. The FIU (Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit) may freeze bank accounts, patient hospitals paying the IC, and associated funds. The IC also faces civil forfeiture of all assets valued in connection with the offending transaction.

Director personal liability: Individual directors and compliance officers are personally liable for fines up to WST 500,000 and imprisonment not exceeding 10 years for willful failure to report suspicious transactions. Travel bans and extradition agreements with foreign States (including China, USA, Australia, New Zealand) apply where the medical-imaging export proceeds are alleged to be tainted. Directors cannot rely on the corporate veil or overseas residency to escape prosecution.
Trading While Struck Off / Non-registration Annual Renewal

Statutory sanction: Trading with a struck-off company is an offense under the International Companies Act 1987. The company's agent, directors, and any person who physically trades in the name of the struck-off company commits an offense punishable by a fine of WST 30,000 plus repayment of all fees. Contracts entered into are voidable at the counterparty's option, making all LC/TT proceeds restitutable to the payer.

Director personal liability: Every director at the date of striking-off is personally liable for all debts and liabilities of the company incurred after the date of striking-off. The Samoan High Court may order restitution, award exemplary damages, and issue a director disqualification order for up to 12 years. Non-resident directors are still subject to personal jurisdiction via the registered agent and can be served internationally; their assets in any DTA or ECT treaty country may be enforced.
