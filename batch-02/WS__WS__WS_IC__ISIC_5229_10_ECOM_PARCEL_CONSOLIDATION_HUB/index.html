Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not permissible as the sole registered office for an International Company. The International Companies Act 1988 requires the company to maintain a registered office in Samoa at a physical street address maintained by the licensed registered agent. This address serves as the official service of process location, and cannot be a post office box or virtual co-working desk. The registered agent's physical office address is used for registry filings, statutory records, and receipt of official notices. An operational physical facility for the parcel consolidation hub is not separately mandated for tax status, but for the efficient conduct of the ISIC 5229 cross-border parcel linehaul business the Samoan entity may maintain only a registered-agent address in Samoa and foreign administrative/operations offices abroad. However, the Samoa International Companies regime must not be used for tax evasion; substance requirements are minimal but require at least a registered agent, registered office in Samoa, and maintenance of certain corporate records at the registered office.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry for Revenue of the Independent State of Samoa (MOR), National Taxpayer Registration Unit; taxpayer portal: Samoa Online Tax Services (SOTS)

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned International Company (IC), the registered agent must file Form IR-01 with the Ministry for Revenue, attaching the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office confirmation, and AML/CFT due diligence. The Ministry will issue a Tax Identification Number (TIN) upon approval. In practice, an IC wholly conducting offshore business is not subject to income tax and holds a tax exemption certificate issued under the International Companies Act 1987; this certificate is filed with MOR as the governing tax status document. Separate VAGST registration is required only if the IC makes non-exempt taxable supplies in Samoa exceeding the statutory threshold; a non-resident IC may apply for VAGST registration on Form VAGST-REG through its local agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a check-the-box or comparable entity classification election. An International Company is a separate legal person and is conclusively treated as the taxpayer for any Samoan-source activities, but it enjoys a statutory exemption from income tax under the International Companies Act 1987; no hybrid mismatch election is available. A VAGST election to register voluntarily is permitted where the entity supplies taxable goods or services in Samoa, subject to standard rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from Samoa qualify for zero-rating if they are exported within 28 days of the date of supply, the exporter obtains and retains proof of physical export, and the goods leave Samoa for a destination outside Samoa. Services supplied to a non-resident are zero-rated when the benefit is provided outside Samoa and the services do not relate to land or tangible goods in Samoa; the consolidation-hub services must be performed for a non-resident counterparty, invoiced in a foreign currency or Tala without VAGST, and supported by written contracts demonstrating offshore delivery.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for direct-injection linehaul
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/MT103 foreign-currency receipt evidence

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based clearance, or digital stamping mandate is currently imposed by Samoa. Registered persons must issue compliant fiscal invoices showing the VAGST amount, maintain electronic or physical records prescribed under the Tax Administration Act 2012, and report VAGST on the prescribed periodic return.

Comprehensive audit scrutiny applies to all zero-rated export claims. The Ministry for Revenue may assess unrecovered or unsupported VAGST at the standard 15% rate, impose shortfall penalties under the Tax Administration Act 2012 of up to 100% of the tax shortfall for intentional evasion, issue late-payment penalties and interest, and initiate criminal proceedings in respect of knowingly false or fraudulent zero-rating declarations, with fines and imprisonment under the VAGST Act 2007 and Tax Administration Act 2012. There is no limitation period for clawback of refunds obtained by fraud, and principals may be held jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The WST is not freely convertible on the capital account; current-account WST transactions require authorized-dealer intermediation and CBS documentary reporting, while capital-account WST conversions generally require CBS approval. International Companies (ICs) are statutorily deemed non-resident and non-domestic under the International Companies Act 1987: all IC treasury flows must be denominated in a permitted foreign currency (USD preferred), rendering capital and current account convertibility effectively unrestricted for USD flows with zero CBS/Exchange Control approval thresholds. Profit remittance of IC dividends is freely permitted from audited retained earnings with no Samoan withholding tax, no capital gains tax, and no remittance licensing; only a standing FIU-reportable wire protocol applies for amounts exceeding the USD 10,000 transaction-reporting threshold.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Samoan resident exporters, Section 3 of the Exchange Control Regulation 1977 requires full surrender of export foreign-currency proceeds to an authorized dealer within 90 days of shipment. ICs are wholly exempt via the Exchange Control (Exemption of International Companies) Notice 1988 because they are non-resident and prohibited from trading with Samoa residents or transacting WST; therefore, USD receivables generated by the parcel-consolidation hub (ISIC 5229 principal in-bound and out-bound linehaul) may be retained offshore or in the IC's USD Samoa account indefinitely with no surrender ratio or conversion timeline.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS; Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa) acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976 and as bank supervisor under the Financial Institutions Act 1996; the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) oversees AML/CFT reporting under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007; the Ministry of Finance grants exchange control exemption orders for offshore entities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: High, subject to the digital EMI's own jurisdictional risk matrix. Airwallex (operating under Australian or UK EMI/ADL licenses, not under a CBS license) will accept a Samoan IC as the Account Holder if the IC provides a full corporate kit, UBO declaration, and source-of-funds evidence; legal constraint: Samoa has no fintech sandbox and no EMI licensing category, so the EMI is not Samoa-supervised, WST-denominated accounts are unavailable, and all flows must be routed in USD or other supported currencies via the EMI's global clearing network. The IC should hold a BSP or ANZ Samoa USD account as a fallback liquidity buffer because EMIs may freeze accounts pending enhanced CDD when Samoan jurisdictions appear on any EU/FATF watchlist tranche.
