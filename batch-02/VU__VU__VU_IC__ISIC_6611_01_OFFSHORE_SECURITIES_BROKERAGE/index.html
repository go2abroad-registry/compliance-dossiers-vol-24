Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement under Cap. 222 for an International Company; nominal authorized capital (standard US$ 50,000) with at least one issued share at par. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA) — the national competent authority for value added tax, excise, and customs; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the licensing and corporate registry authority for International Companies (ICs). The VRCA operates the VAT registration and return portal; the VFSC maintains the IC register under the International Companies Act Cap. 222.

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; a Vanuatu International Company does not obtain an income-tax TIN. The statutory tax identity for an IC is the VFSC International Company registration number issued at incorporation. Where the entity makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding VT 10,000,000 in any 12-month rolling period, it must apply to the VRCA for a VAT Registration Number (VRN) using Form VAT-01, accompanied by the VFSC certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered office consent, director identification, and the proposed effective date of registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — the International Companies Act Cap. 222 confers complete statutory tax exemption from corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, dividends, interest, royalties, estate duty, gift duty, and stamp duty. The only recurring fiscal charges are the VFSC annual license fee and government registration fees. — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax; a Vanuatu International Company does not obtain an income-tax TIN. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the International Companies Act Cap. 222 confers complete statutory tax exemption from corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, dividends, interest, royalties, estate duty, gift duty, and stamp duty. The only recurring fiscal charges are the VFSC annual license fee and government registration fees.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, pass-through, or hybrid mismatch election mechanism exists because Vanuatu has no income tax law and no controlled-foreign-company or transfer-pricing legislation. The sole significant election is voluntary VAT registration for an entity below the VT 10,000,000 threshold; once registered, the entity must remain registered for a minimum of two years unless deregistration criteria are lawfully met. There are no treaty election procedures as Vanuatu has no double tax agreements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (ten million Vatu) of annual taxable turnover in the current or preceding 12-month period, approximating USD 84,000 at prevailing exchange rates.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Value Added Tax Act 2014, exported services are zero-rated. An offshore securities brokerage is eligible for 0% VAT where: (i) the brokerage, execution, and CFD/spread services are contractually supplied to a non-resident client; (ii) the services are wholly performed and consumed outside Vanuatu; (iii) consideration is received from the client's offshore account in a foreign currency or into a segregated client wire account; and (iv) the IC holds contemporaneous documentary evidence of the export nature of the supply. Pure offshore-to-offshore brokerage income therefore falls outside Vanuatu's 12.5% VAT net and carries full input-credit recovery eligibility.

  • VFSC International Company certificate of incorporation and valid annual license
  • Executed securities brokerage or services agreement identifying the non-resident client and their jurisdiction of tax residence
  • VAT invoice annotated 'Zero-Rated Export Services' bearing the valid VRN
  • SWIFT MT103, client wire instruction, or bank statement evidencing inward remittance of commission from a foreign bank account
  • Certified KYC file, including client passport, tax residence certificate, or equivalent government-issued non-resident identification
  • Trading platform ledger extract and trade tickets matching the invoice amounts and remittance records

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate, digital stamping, or state-run e-invoicing API is currently imposed by the VRCA. Taxable persons must issue prescribed VAT invoices disclosing the VRN, sequential invoice number, date, supplier and customer details, description, VAT-exclusive and inclusive amounts, and the VAT charged in Vatu. A simplified invoice is permitted for retail supplies below VT 2,500. Import procedures and customs documentation are processed through the ASYCUDA World electronic customs system, but domestic VAT issuance is not subject to live electronic clearance.

The VRCA may audit zero-rating claims for up to five years from the end of the VAT period and may recharacterize supplies as taxable at 12.5% where export evidence is incomplete, leading to retrospective clawback of the full output VAT. Civil penalties include an initial 5% late-payment penalty on the assessed tax plus 2% monthly interest on arrears; fraudulent zero-rating claims attract a civil penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded and criminal fines up to VT 5,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to three years. Company directors are jointly and severally liable where the offense was committed with their knowledge or consent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts in line with Vanuatu's IMF Article VIII obligations. There are no approval, registration, tax or waiting-period hurdles on repatriation of dividends, trading profits, capital, or shareholder-loan repayments by a licensed International Company. The practical constraint is market microstructure: the offshore VUV market is thin, so the brokerage should denominate clearing and treasury liquidity in USD, AUD and NZD multi-currency accounts, using VUV only for domestic operating expenses. Banks may apply internal reasonableness checks and request a board resolution for large outward remittances, but no statutory profit-remittance restriction exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no retention limit for export proceeds, brokerage commissions, service fees, or settlement receipts. The VU_IC may lawfully retain 100% of revenues in foreign-currency accounts indefinitely without RBV notification or conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), Port Vila — sole monetary authority, issuer of the Vatu and prudential supervisor of licensed banks; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) concurrently administers the securities-dealer license for the brokerage. Vanuatu has no exchange-control certification or permit regime, and the RBV does not pre-authorize cross-border payments.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Vanuatu has no domestic Electronic Money Institution (EMI) charter; the VFSC does not issue EMI, sandbox or e-money licenses, and the Financial Dealers Act does not recognize foreign EMIs as custodians of client money. EU/UK or Asia-Pacific licensed EMIs will in practice decline a Vanuatu offshore high-risk retail derivatives broker as a treasury client without substantiated local licensing and substance. Foreign EMI and money-transfer rails may be used only as limited-cap settlement-extraction channels for client payouts, never as the primary custody or liquidity platform.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A - Vanuatu has no transfer pricing legislation imposing Local File or Master File requirements. A statutory audit threshold of VUV 30,000,000 turnover or VUV 10,000,000 assets applies for financial statement filing, but this audit threshold does not create a transfer pricing documentation duty.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act and the Economic Substance Regulations apply to International Companies carrying on relevant activities. For a retail securities brokerage, the activity is assessed under the categories of 'financing and leasing' or 'distribution and service centre'. The company must demonstrate that core income-generating activities, board meetings and management decisions are conducted in Vanuatu. Merely being tax-exempt does not remove the economic substance filing obligation; annual ESR declarations must be filed with the VFSC regardless of zero corporate tax.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the International Company registration; penalty up to VUV 10,000,000 under the Economic Substance Regulations; criminal prosecution for false declarations under the Penal Code with imprisonment up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution to affected shareholders and creditors. The court may pierce the corporate veil, issue travel bans, order asset freezing, and impose personal imprisonment for directors found to have orchestrated phantom substance and tax evasion.
Unlicensed securities and derivatives brokerage

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist order; criminal fine up to VUV 5,000,000; revocation of the company's IC status; nullity of all client-facing brokerage agreements entered into while unlicensed.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for customer losses through court-ordered restitution; the VFSC has power to disqualify directors for up to 5 years; travel prohibitions and imprisonment up to 3 years may be imposed under the Securities Dealers Act.
AML/CFT breach in retail brokerage

Statutory sanction: Liability under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Act; regulatory fines up to VUV 20,000,000; license suspension; detailed adverse findings in FATF and APG evaluations; criminal confiscation of proceeds and imprisonment for money laundering up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed compliance officer face personal fines, criminal money-laundering convictions, confiscation of gains, travel bans, and imprisonment. The corporate veil will be pierced where nominee arrangements are used to hide the beneficial owner.
False tax residence certificate and international misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Fraud under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act; certificate revoked with immediate effect; penalty up to VUV 1,000,000; notification of the offence to foreign regulators and law enforcement agencies.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil restitution in favour of deceived financial institutions and investors, judicial travel bans, a personal director disqualification order, and imprisonment for fraud where the misrepresentation induced third-party reliance.
