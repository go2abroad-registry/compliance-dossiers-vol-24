Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) for International Company (IC) incorporation and annual filings (https://www.vfsc.vu); Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR) for customs and inland revenue matters (https://customs.gov.vu); Ministry of Finance and Economic Management as policy owner.

E-government filing portal

A foreign parent establishes a Vanuatu International Company by engaging a VFSC-licensed registered agent, who files the incorporation application, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and registered office/agent consents with the VFSC. The VFSC issues a Certificate of Incorporation containing a unique IC registration number; this is the company's sole statutory identifier. Because Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no income-tax TIN exists and no tax registration is required for the IC's offshore activities. Where physical goods are imported into or exported from Vanuatu, the IC must additionally obtain a Customs Importer/Exporter Code from DCIR. The only recurring statutory obligation is the annual IC exemption fee payable to VFSC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None available. Section 198 of the International Companies Act Cap 222 grants an absolute statutory exemption to an IC from all Vanuatu taxes, including income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and stamp duty, for a period that extends to the company's lifespan. No check-the-box, flow-through, or hybrid-mismatch election exists or is necessary because Vanuatu has no direct tax base and therefore no hybrid mismatch rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales tax applies in Vanuatu; the 12.5% VAT was abolished on 1 July 1998. Current indirect levies are limited to customs import duties, port and harbour dues, and provincial/municipal business (trade) licence fees. International Companies conducting qualified offshore business are exempt from these imposts; local in-scope business would require an onshore Business Licence.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST is levied. A DCIR Customs Importer Code is triggered only by physical importation of goods into Vanuatu; no turnover threshold applies to the IC's offshore clinical-trial CRO services invoiced to foreign sponsors.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT sense because Vanuatu has no VAT and therefore no zero-rating mechanism or export VAT refund regime. The IC's clinical-trial management and CRO services supplied to non-resident sponsors are wholly outside the scope of Vanuatu taxation by virtue of the offshore IC regime; income arising outside Vanuatu is exempt regardless of documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Vanuatu ASYCUDA export entry, applicable only where goods are physically shipped out of Vanuatu)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill evidencing the export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or SWIFT MT103 / bank credit evidence showing the sponsor's foreign currency payment)

No real-time fiscal clearance, state-run e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists, as Vanuatu has no VAT. Customs clearance for physical goods is processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World system administered by the Vanuatu Customs Service; service exports by the IC require no fiscal clearance.

No VAT-export clawback regime exists because Vanuatu imposes no VAT. The core integrity risk for an IC is regime abuse: if the company conducts local business with Vanuatu residents, holds prohibited local assets, or otherwise breaches the offshore restrictions of the International Companies Act Cap 222, the VFSC may strike off the company, impose fines (up to VT 100,000 per contravention on the company and its officers), and retrospectively revoke the tax-exemption privilege for the period of disqualification. For any dutiable physical exports, the Customs Act applies with seizure powers, penalties up to three times the duty evaded, and criminal liability for false declarations or smuggling.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is non-fully convertible and is managed by the RBV against a trade-weighted basket of partner currencies (AUD, USD, NZD, EUR, GBP, JPY). Under the International Companies Act [Cap 222], the IC is excluded from the Exchange Control Act [Cap 65]; both current-account and capital-account convertibility are therefore unrestricted for the VU_IC. Dividend, interest, and service-fee remittance face no approval requirements and no withholding tax. Non-IC resident entities require authorized-dealer/RBV approval for certain capital outflows, which is irrelevant to the IC's offshore CRO treasury structure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil and not applicable. Vanuatu imposes no export-proceeds surrender, no compulsory conversion ratio, and no statutory conversion timeline on International Companies. Clinical-trial milestone receipts may be retained in the invoice currency (USD/EUR/AUD) in the IC's operational account; no RBV notification, conversion mandate, or filing requirement applies.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) — established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 42] — is the central bank and administrator of the Exchange Control Act [Cap 65]. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) issues and supervises the International Company (IC) certificate, the beneficial-ownership register, and financial-dealer licenses.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Vanuatu has no domestic electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing regime; foreign EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) cannot confer Vanuatu banking substance, frequently reject tax-exempt offshore ICs, and cannot clear VUV through the RBV-authorized-dealer system. Use of a foreign EMI is acceptable only as a supplementary receivables-collection channel backed by an executed CRO service agreement and a clear audit trail to the primary Vanuatu bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Sponsor and parent-group capital injections route via SWIFT MT103 to the IC's USD settlement account at an RBV-authorized dealer (ANZ or BSP). Remittance fields must cite the contract and invoice number and use the purpose description 'Clinical trial CRO management services' for service fees or 'Share capital contribution' for equity injection; no RBV prior approval is required. The dealer codes the flow as a business-services credit for RBV balance-of-payments statistics. Debt injections require an executed intercompany loan facility and amortization schedule; Vanuatu imposes no loan-to-equity restrictions and no withholding tax on interest.Settlements to investigational sites, sub-CROs, and trial suppliers execute via SWIFT MT103 for milestone fees, MT202/MT202COV for interbank liquidity positioning, and MT700 (documentary commercial letter of credit) or MT760 (standby letter of credit) where procurement contracts or sponsor agreements require bank-issued credit support. Disbursements are made in the billing currency directly from the IC's operational account, eliminating VUV conversion risk; payments to non-residents require no RBV approval. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The VFSC Annual Return is due on or before the anniversary of incorporation, or within such extension as the VFSC Registrar may grant in writing. There is no corporate income tax return deadline because no CIT liability exists.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 / Company Name and Registration Number Legal name of the IC and the VFSC registration number exactly as stated in the certificate of incorporation. Non-resident owners must ensure the name on the filing matches the certificate; no local tax identifier is required due to the IC's tax-exempt status. Failure to file the VFSC Annual Return subjects the IC to late filing penalties imposed by the Registrar, suspension of good standing, administrative fines for continued default, and ultimately automatic striking-off/dissolution. No daily surcharge applies under the IC Act, but directors may be separately fined for knowingly permitting non-compliance.

For a clinical-trial CRO management IC, confirm where management decisions and trial-monitoring activities are physically performed and maintain contemporaneous records in Vanuatu to evidence substance. Failure to file the VFSC Annual Return subjects the IC to late filing penalties imposed by the Registrar, suspension of good standing, administrative fines for continued default, and ultimately automatic striking-off/dissolution. No daily surcharge applies under the IC Act, but directors may be separately fined for knowingly permitting non-compliance.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable. Vanuatu has no corporate income tax, no transfer-pricing code, and no statutory Local File/Master File documentation threshold. Intra-group benchmarking and documentation may still be prepared for foreign parent/audit purposes, and the VFSC economic substance review may request supporting evidence of intercompany arrangements, but there is no vatu-denominated threshold.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The International Companies (Economic Substance) Requirements apply to Vanuatu ICs carrying on a relevant activity. Clinical-trial CRO management is best classified as a service center, or as a headquarters activity if the IC coordinates a multinational group's CRO functions. The IC must be managed and directed in Vanuatu, maintain adequate physical premises, employ adequate local staff, incur adequate local operating expenditure, and perform its core income-generating activities in Vanuatu, including contract negotiation, trial monitoring, risk review, and milestone decisions. Annual substance filings are required; non-compliance exposes the IC to administrative penalties, deregistration, and automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Foreign Permanent Establishment Abuse

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu authorities may strike off the IC and impose administrative fines for false filings; the foreign jurisdiction may assess full back taxes and interest, gross negligence penalties, and criminal fines for deliberate concealment or fraudulent structures.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who operate a façade company for diversion of profits may face personal civil restitution, lifting of the corporate veil, disqualification orders, travel bans during tax/fraud investigations, and imprisonment where fraudulent evasion is proven.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: The VFSC may issue monetary penalties in line with the statutory economic substance regime, impose an administrative fine that escalates for continuing default, strike off the IC, and refer the case to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit and foreign regulators through the TIEA network.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false annual substance declarations may be held personally liable for penalties, court-ordered contribution to unpaid taxes, and costs; a court may impose a travel ban on directors pending remediation or striking-off proceedings.
Money Laundering and AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Under Vanuatu's Anti-Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime framework, the FIU or relevant court may impose substantial financial penalties, confiscation of proceeds of crime, and imprisonment of individuals for willful failure; corporate fines can be multiple times the laundered transaction value.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and the appointed AML compliance officer/MLRO are separately liable; they may be criminally prosecuted, personally fined, prohibited from leaving Vanuatu, and ordered to repatriate funds or property traceable to milestone payments.
False Filings and Reckless Tax Representations

Statutory sanction: Unsworn false declarations attract liability for administrative penalties, VFSC deregistration, and director disqualification; where fraud is proved, the responsible parties may face criminal fines and terms of imprisonment under Vanuatu law.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise or participate in false filings are jointly and severally liable for restitution and penalties; courts may issue asset freezes, travel bans, and custodial sentences for fraud on conviction.
