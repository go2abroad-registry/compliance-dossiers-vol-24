Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance and Economic Management (MFEM) / Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) – Corporate Registry; fiscal administration portal: https://www.mfem.gov.vu and https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu does not impose direct income tax on International Companies (ICs); therefore no national TIN or VAT ID is procured for income tax purposes. A foreign captive reinsurance SPV incorporated as a Vanuatu International Company must register with the VFSC Registry, obtain a Certificate of Incorporation, and pay the annual registration and business licence (where applicable) through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and the Ministry of Finance. Upon incorporation, an Internal Revenue compliance reference may be issued only if the entity engages in import/export activities subject to customs, but no statutory tax ID number is mandatory for the tax-exempt offshore captive operation.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Vanuatu does not impose direct income tax on International Companies (ICs); therefore no national TIN or VAT ID is procured for income tax purposes. A foreign captive reinsurance SPV incorporated as a Vanuatu International Company must register with the VFSC Registry, obtain a Certificate of Incorporation, and pay the annual registration and business licence (where applicable) through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and the Ministry of Finance. Upon incorporation, an Internal Revenue compliance reference may be issued only if the entity engages in import/export activities subject to customs, but no statutory tax ID number is mandatory for the tax-exempt offshore captive operation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch elections exist under Vanuatu law. The International Companies Act provides an absolute statutory exemption from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, and levies (including stamp duty) for a minimum of 20 years, extendable by petition. No entity-level or pass-through election is offered because the captive reinsurance IC is automatically treated as a tax-exempt offshore entity within a zero-tax jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No national VAT/GST; indirect taxation is limited to customs duties under the Customs (Amendment) Act 2017 and stamp duty under the Stamp Duties Act [Cap. 68]. Reinsurance services are not imported goods and fall outside the indirect tax base.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST regime exists in Vanuatu. Customs duty registration is required only for physical importers/exporters; offshore reinsurance services and premiums have no local economic turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Vanuatu has no VAT/GST export zero-rating regime. As the captive reinsurance SPV derives all underwriting income from offshore sources and is statutorily exempt from tax, there is no scope to claim a zero rate on premium income or claims payments. The business structure is a tax-exempt export of financial services by operation of law, not by zero-rated VAT mechanics.

  • No export zero-rating supported; mandatory documentation is limited to the VFSC annual filing and corporate records: Certificate of Incorporation, register of members, board minutes evidencing reinsurance contracts, and audited financial statements showing premium income retention.
  • For any tangible or incidental movement of goods, a Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World) and commercial invoice are required, but these are not VAT refund substantiation instruments.

No national e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists for tax-exempt ICs. The Vanuatu Customs Service operates ASYCUDA World for customs declarations; however, this applies exclusively to cross-border movement of tangible goods and does not apply to the SPV's reinsurance contracts, premium flows, or claims payments. No digital stamping or API-based fiscal clearance is imposed on captive reinsurance documentation.

No export VAT repayment exists, so no clawback penalty regime is legislated. However, if a captive IC is misused for fraudulent import/export practices or engages in trade activity not permitted under its international company licence, the VFSC may invoke anti-money laundering (AML) supervisory sanctions, including fines up to VUV 10,000,000 and revocation of the certificate of incorporation. The Customs Act imposes penalties for false export declarations of up to double the duty evaded plus forfeiture of goods. The absence of VAT refunds means no retrospective VAT clawback is applicable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vatu (VUV) is a floating, freely convertible currency for current international transactions and is not subject to exchange-control licensing restrictions for insurance premiums, claims, profits, dividends, interest, or repatriation of capital when cleared through an RBV-licensed bank. Vanuatu has an open capital account in practice, but VUV is not a globally traded deliverable currency; USD and AUD accounts are operationally preferred. Principal remittance hurdles are documentary: banks will require the captive insurance licence, board resolutions, UBO disclosures, source-of-funds records, reinsurance treaty evidence, and confirmation that the International Company does not conduct domestic VUV business with residents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio is currently imposed by the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu. Reinsurance premium income received from offshore cedents is treated as an invisible-trade earning and may be retained indefinitely in USD, AUD, EUR, or other approved foreign currency. There is no statutory timeline requiring conversion of such proceeds into VUV. Commercial banks may request an inward remittance purpose declaration identifying the reinsurance treaty and policy year for AML/CFT statistical purposes, but no RBV conversion mandate, surrender requirement, or custodial direction is triggered.

Central bank supervision: The Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 322], is the central bank and exchange-control administrator. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) acts as the licensing and prudential supervisor of the captive reinsurance vehicle under the Insurance (Captive Insurance) regime and the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission Act No. 10 of 2017.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI solutions have medium feasibility as supplementary collection or payment rails, but low viability as the primary liquidity custodian. Legal constraints: an EMI not licensed by RBV cannot lawfully hold funds for, or provide deposit-taking services to, persons in Vanuatu under the Banking Act [Cap. 162]; use of a non-resident EMI as a bottleneck for premium or claims flows also weakens the bank’s source-of-funds audit trail and may be treated as an unregulated payment intermediary under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act [Cap. 251]. The optimal architecture is a fully regulated Vanuatu bank account with SWIFT and correspondent-bank clearing, supplemented by an EMI only where a cedent is in a restricted jurisdiction and the funds immediately flow onward to the licensed VU account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital and premium injections must be routed by SWIFT MT103, or an equivalent real-time correspondent wire, to the captive’s RBV-licensed bank account in the currency specified by the treasury plan, normally USD or AUD. The instruction must carry the captive’s full legal name, the beneficiary bank’s SWIFT BIC, the account number, and a payment-details memo in the form: ‘Premium or capital contribution under [Reinsurance Treaty No. / Shareholder Resolution No.], risk period [dates], cedent [name]’. The originator’s bank should apply an ISO 20022 purpose code reflecting an insurance premium or intercompany funding; if the beneficiary bank uses domestic inward-remittance codes, the field should state ‘Reinsurance premium’ rather than ‘investment’ or ‘services’. Verification includes the underlying treaty or capital subscription resolution, board authorisation, VFSC licence, source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership records, and confirmation that no mandatory VUV conversion is required.Claims and retrocession settlements should be cleared through the SWIFT network using an MT103 message for third-party beneficiary payments and an MT202 or MT205 message for interbank movement between the captive’s own accounts or group treasury accounts. USD disbursements are commonly routed through the relevant U.S. correspondent using CHIPS or Fedwire; EUR transactions through TARGET2; GBP transactions through CHAPS. The underlying claim file or retrocession instruction must be retained for the receiving bank’s AML/KYC review. Commercial letters of credit are not routine for reinsurance claims but may support collateralised-reinsurance obligations; a captive can arrange a standby letter of credit or bank guarantee to collateralise outstanding loss reserves in favour of a cedent, subject to VFSC prudential rules applicable to invested reserves and approved credit facilities.Plain-vanilla deliverable foreign-exchange forwards in USD/VUV, AUD/VUV, and EUR/VUV executed with an RBV-licensed commercial bank, limited to expected premium, claims, and reserve exposures.Non-deliverable foreign-exchange forwards in VUV, used offshore for the non-deliverable tail of VUV currency risk.Cross-currency swaps in VUV/USD or USD/AUD matching the asset and liability cash-flow profile of the captive reinsurer.Purchased foreign-exchange options, subject to board-approved treasury policy, where asymmetric hedging is justified by reserve requirements and the writer is a licensed bank.Interest-rate and cross-currency swaps under ISDA and CSA documentation where the captive maintains collateralised credit support and does not speculate beyond its hedged non-VUV liabilities.

Strike-off trigger

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax on International Companies (ICs).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax and has no deemed distribution regime; captive reinsurance profits may be remitted to the offshore parent group free of deduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The renewal cycle is fixed by the registration anniversary: the annual return and government fee are due by 30 June for ICs registered in the first half of the year, and by 31 December for ICs registered in the second half. A prescribed late fee surcharge accrues from the day after the due date; no extension is available as of right.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Vanuatu transfer-pricing statute, Local File / Master File threshold, or arm's-length adjustment rule exists in VUV. Compliance pressure arises extraterritorially: the foreign parent group may trigger OECD Country-by-Country Reporting at the EUR 750 million (approximately VUV 97 billion) consolidated revenue threshold and must satisfy home-jurisdiction transfer pricing documentation rules covering the captive's reinsurance pricing and underwriting margins.

Economic substance scope: Applicable for International Companies under the economic substance amendments introduced in 2019 as part of Vanuatu's OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices and FATF commitments. A captive reinsurer performing a relevant activity must demonstrate: (i) board direction and management exercised in Vanuatu, (ii) adequate physical premises, (iii) adequate full-time employees, and (iv) adequate annual operating expenditure. A standalone economic substance return must be filed annually with the VFSC; passive, dummy, or mailbox-only structures are prohibited.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Sham Risk Transfer / Tax Evasion Facilitation

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the International Companies Act and ancillary financial-services legislation: monetary penalties per offense, forfeiture of the IC's exempt status, compulsory strike-off, and referral to the OECD FHTP as a harmful preferential regime.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face personal civil restitution for funds funneled through the sham arrangement, imprisonment for tax evasion facilitation, director disqualification for up to 10 years, and court-imposed travel bans preventing departure from Vanuatu pending investigation.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Immediate VFSC strike-off and cancellation of the captive structure, administrative fines for non-filing of the ESR return, revocation of the registered agent's authorization, and secondary inclusion on international blacklists that triggers global correspondent-bank de-risking.

Director personal liability: Personal liability for company debts contracted after the strike-off date, confiscation of assets vesting in the Vanuatu Government, travel bans, and a statutory bar from acting as director or promoter of any Vanuatu international company.
Unlicensed Insurance / Reinsurance Business

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties per violation, injunctions halting premium collection, revocation of the IC registration, and compulsory liquidation of the captive's Vanuatu trust assets.

Director personal liability: Officers personally liable to policyholders for unpaid claims, restitution of all unearned premiums, imprisonment for conducting unlicensed financial business, and travel bans during the civil enforcement proceedings.
Money Laundering / AML-CFT Breaches

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act (Cap. 258, as amended): corporate fines up to VUV 50,000,000, confiscation of tainted funds, suspension or revocation of the VFSC license, and inclusion in the Financial Intelligence Unit's public enforcement register.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed compliance officer face criminal fines, imprisonment for aggravated money laundering, personal asset forfeiture, international travel bans, and permanent disqualification from financial-services management in Vanuatu.
