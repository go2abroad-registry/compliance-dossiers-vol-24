Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Customs and Inland Revenue Department (CIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; official portal: https://www.customsinland.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Vanuatu International Company (IC) must obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the CIRD. A company with a registered office and licensed agent in Vanuatu files the required incorporation documentation and obtains a TIN concurrently with company registration. If the IC intends to make taxable supplies above the VAT registration threshold, it must separately apply for VAT registration using Form VAT-001, providing the Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles, agent confirmation, and expected revenue declarations. No capital gain or income tax registration is required for an IC because Vanuatu operates a territorial, zero-income-tax system for offshore entities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Vanuatu law, as Vanuatu does not impose an income tax on companies. The only relevant elective mechanism is voluntary VAT registration under the Value Added Tax Act, which may be chosen even when turnover is below the mandatory registration threshold. Hybrid mismatch elections are not applicable because there is no entity-level income tax in Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 10,000,000 (approximately USD 90,000) of annual taxable turnover in the customs territory of Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply is zero-rated for Vanuatu VAT if goods are exported from Vanuatu directly under customs supervision or if services are supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and the services are wholly performed or used outside Vanuatu. For the Offshore Development Centre, qualifying software services and maintenance provided to overseas clients are treated as exported services where no local counterparty, local server, or domestic consumption exists.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/Form C10)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical removal from Vanuatu
  • Inward Remittance Certificate demonstrating receipt of consideration in a Vanuatu bank account or recognised offshore settlement account
  • Commercial Invoice and underlying service contract
  • Certificate of Origin (when required by the importing jurisdiction)

Vanuatu does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or full-volume digital stamping. VAT returns are filed manually or through the CIRD's simplified e-filing facility. Customs export declarations are processed through the ASYCUDA World system, but there is no pre-clearance approval or live fiscal API integration for self-issued invoices for standard export transactions.

The CIRD may claw back any VAT input credit or zero-rated treatment if documentary substantiation is incomplete or falsified. Penalties include: VAT assessment at the standard 12.5% rate plus 100% late-payment penalty; civil penalties up to 150% of the evaded tax for reckless or fraudulent claims; cancellation of VAT registration and consequential loss of zero-rating privileges; and criminal fines up to VT 1,000,000 or imprisonment for up to five years for the knowing supply of false documents in an export zero-rating claim.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is a managed-float currency pegged to a trade-weighted basket (USD, AUD, EUR, JPY) with RBV intervention. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted, and capital-account convertibility is de facto free for International Companies: Section 88 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] expressly exempts ICs and their non-resident owners from the Exchange Control Act. Non-resident shareholders may hold 100% equity, inject share capital, repatriate dividends, and repay shareholder loans without RBV prior approval. Vanuatu imposes no income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax on an IC, so dividend and liquidation remittances are tax-free. The practical remittance hurdles are documentary and correspondent-bank driven: the IC must maintain Good Standing with VFSC (annual return filed), keep an up-to-date register of beneficial owners for AML/CTF purposes, and give the settlement bank advance notice of large wires. There is no statutory administrative approval gate for an ODC receiving software-export service income.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no mandatory surrender, no statutory conversion timeline, and no prescribed conversion ratio for export proceeds. An ODC may retain 100% of its software-export receipts in VUV or in any foreign currency (USD, AUD, EUR, NZD) in onshore or offshore accounts. Only statistical-reporting obligations apply: licensed banks must submit balance-of-payments returns to RBV, and individual receipts above approximately VUV 1,000,000 (about USD 8,500) trigger the bank's internal FX declaration and source-of-funds verification workflow. The ODC should instruct foreign clients to include a payment narrative such as 'ISIC 6202 software maintenance services - invoice reference' so the bank auto-classifies the credit as service-export revenue rather than capital or remittance, which materially shortens clearing time.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - the statutory central bank and the delegated Exchange Control Administrator under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 77]. RBV licenses and supervises all commercial banks and deposit takers, administers the national payment and settlement system, and compiles balance-of-payments statistics. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the corporate registrar and regulator of International Companies (ICs) and non-bank financial institutions; it does not administer FX policy but issues the Certificate of Good Standing and the International Company license-records that banks require for onboarding.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a tactical collection-only rail; Low as a primary treasury venue. Wise Business and Payoneer do not hold a license from the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu, do not support the VUV, and are not participants in the Vanuatu depositor-protection framework; funds held with them are e-money claims under the foreign license of the applicable receiving entity. Wise B2B will generally perform an enhanced manual review before onboarding a Vanuatu International Company and may reject files where the IC appears shell-like despite an active software-operations profile. Payoneer's standard terms do not prohibit ISIC 6202 service businesses, but Vanuatu-registered entities face periodic re-review. Legal constraints: neither rail can receive VUV, execute Vanuatu payroll, issue a capital-injection purpose code, or serve as evidence of local substance. Recommended architecture: use Wise B2B for client invoicing in USD/EUR/AUD from US/EU/AU counterparties; use Payoneer for marketplace payouts; cap aggregate EMI balances at USD 25,000; sweep all balances to the BSP or BRED onshore account at least weekly; and require all project clients above USD 10,000 per invoice to remit directly by SWIFT MT103 to the onshore account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound shareholder equity must be wired by SWIFT MT103 to the IC's USD Operational Account at BSP or BRED, with the payment narrative formatted as: 'EQUITY INJECTION - [IC legal name] - Board Resolution [date] - IMF BoP code 5211 (Direct Investment-Equity)'. The IC's bank must receive a pre-advice email from the Relationship Manager 24-48 hours before any wire above VUV 10,000,000 (about USD 85,000), including the full remitter details, the settlement currency, and the compliance pack. The verification pack to be delivered on or before settlement date: board resolution authorising the share issuance, signed share subscription agreement, updated register of shareholders, certified passport copies of the subscribing beneficial owner, and the bank reference of the remitting institution. Funds may arrive in VUV, USD, AUD, EUR, or NZD; if the settlement currency does not match the designated account currency, the bank converts at interbank mid-rate plus a spread of 0.5-1.0% unless a standing conversion instruction is lodged in advance. No RBV approval is required by virtue of the International Companies Act exemption, but the bank records the transaction on its RBV statistical return, so the IC must also file a corresponding share-issuance return with VFSC to keep the capital account reconciled.Three clearing rails are deployed in parallel. (i) Domestic VUV clearing: payroll and local supplier payments settle through the domestic interbank system via settlement accounts at RBV - only an onshore Vanuatu bank account can execute this rail; the ODC should keep a VUV buffer equal to eight weeks of payroll to avoid forced daily FX conversion. (ii) International disbursements: SaaS subscriptions, cloud providers, overseas contractors, and dividend payments are executed by SWIFT MT103 for transfers above USD 5,000 and by SWIFT MT202/MT202 COV for agent-funding top-ups of the ODC's foreign-currency clearing accounts; every MT103 must carry a correct purpose narrative (royalty-free software services, professional fees, or dividend distribution) because erroneous narratives are the leading cause of correspondent-bank holds. (iii) Trade and performance instruments: UCP600-compliant commercial letters of credit may be opened for hardware or furniture procurement, while ISP98 standby letters of credit (SBLCs) should be requested from BSP or BRED in favour of large enterprise clients as milestone-based performance bonds; the SBLC facility must be pre-approved by the bank's credit committee. Small recurring subscriptions (e.g., cloud tools) may be paid via a BSP or BRED corporate Visa/Mastercard, capped at USD 15,000 per month to control card-FX spread leakage.Deliverable FX outright forward (USD/VUV, AUD/VUV, EUR/VUV) up to 12 months, booked with an onshore licensed bank (BSP or BRED) under an ISDA 2002 FX master or bank master agreement; the primary onshore instrument for hedging the ODC's USD-revenue versus VUV-payroll mismatch.VUV non-deliverable forward (NDF) against USD, transacted with a licensed offshore counterparty and settled in USD, permissible where the offshore parent or a foreign client needs to hedge VUV exposure without touching the onshore VUV market.Cross-currency basis swap (USD/VUV) available only for large notional tickets (above USD 2,000,000 equivalent) and subject to bank credit and collateral approval; not actively quoted in the onshore market and therefore classified as conditional-permissible.Tom-next and spot FX conversion of operational receipts with a 0.5-1.0% bank spread, executed as standing monthly instructions to convert only the budgeted VUV payroll amount, leaving USD invoice balances intact to create a natural hedge.

Wise Business and Payoneer do not hold a license from the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu, do not support the VUV, and are not participants in the Vanuatu depositor-protection framework; funds held with them are e-money claims under the foreign license of the applicable receiving entity. Wise B2B will generally perform an enhanced manual review before onboarding a Vanuatu International Company and may reject files where the IC appears shell-like despite an active software-operations profile. Payoneer's standard terms do not prohibit ISIC 6202 service businesses, but Vanuatu-registered entities face periodic re-review. Legal constraints: neither rail can receive VUV, execute Vanuatu payroll, issue a capital-injection purpose code, or serve as evidence of local substance. Recommended architecture: use Wise B2B for client invoicing in USD/EUR/AUD from US/EU/AU counterparties; use Payoneer for marketplace payouts; cap aggregate EMI balances at USD 25,000; sweep all balances to the BSP or BRED onshore account at least weekly; and require all project clients above USD 10,000 per invoice to remit directly by SWIFT MT103 to the onshore account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — Vanuatu has no transfer pricing statute, no Local File or Master File thresholds, and no country-by-country reporting obligations; OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation may nevertheless be required by the customer's or parent company's foreign tax jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The International Companies (Economic Substance) Amendment Act 2020 requires an International Company carrying on geographically mobile activities, including digital and software services hubs, to demonstrate credible substance in Vanuatu: physical premises, adequate qualified employees, locally controlled board or management, and expenditure proportional to income. Failure to satisfy or to file the annual economic substance declaration subjects the company and directors to penalties, deregistration, and adverse EU or OECD listing consequences affecting international bankability.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Facilitation (Foreign Tax Abuse)

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu Proceeds of Crime Act (as amended) and the Mutual Assistance in Criminal Matters Act, the VFSC may freeze assets, cancel the certificate, and compel restitution; foreign tax judgments may be enforced via Letters of Request. Monetary penalties reach VUV 50,000,000 plus forfeiture of all correlated funds.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil where the International Company is a mere facade; directors and beneficial owners face civil restitution suits, travel bans under departure prohibition provisions, and custodial sentences of up to 15 years for money laundering or fraudulent evasion offenses.
Shell Company — Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Economic Substance Amendment Act 2020 attracts tiered administrative penalties of up to VUV 50,000,000, forced deregistration of the International Company, and disqualification from re-registration, in addition to EU or OECD blacklisting consequences that destroy correspondent banking access.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are personally liable for uncollectable company obligations where the veil is pierced; VFSC may impose a bar on acting as an officer for up to 10 years and issue an international travel-ban notice pending investigation.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with the Vanuatu AML/CFT framework (Anti-Money Laundering Act 2020 and FIU directives) triggers monetary penalties of VUV 1,000,000 to VUV 100,000,000 against the company and its resident agent, plus immediate suspension of the VFSC license and account freezing orders.

Director personal liability: Each director commits an offense of strict liability; upon conviction, personal fines of up to VUV 10,000,000, confiscation of personal assets under proceeds-of-crime orders, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to 15 years apply.
Fraudulent Trading and Misrepresentation to Creditors

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act and general directors' duties, the VFSC may impose disqualification orders, compulsory winding-up, and criminal fines of VUV 2,000,000 per offense; contracts procured by fraud are void and all consideration must be restituted.

Director personal liability: Directors incur personal civil liability to creditors for the full value of fraudulent transactions, forfeiture of personal tax exemptions, and a maximum criminal term of 12 years for fraudulent trading; charging orders and travel bans are routinely imposed.
