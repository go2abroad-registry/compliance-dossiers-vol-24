Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital requirement; capital may be expressed in any currency; no escrow bank account or statutory deposit obligation is imposed on an International Company.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Vanuatu Fire Services is required before occupying any commercial office premises; a structural load inspection certificate and an occupational workplace clearance are obtained from the building owner or the Public Works Department where fit-out is carried out, and the employer must register with the Vanuatu National Provident Fund for workplace insurance coverage.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Customs and Inland Revenue (DCIR), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Republic of Vanuatu — official portal: https://www.dcir.gov.vu; digital TIN and VAT administration under the Tax Administration Act No. 4 of 2013 and the Value Added Tax Act No. 10 of 1998.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident International Company (VU_IC) obtains its TIN/VAT ID by filing a TIN/VAT registration form with the DCIR through its licensed resident agent, accompanied by: certificate of incorporation and status excerpt from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), registered-agent licence and address, constitutional documents, director/shareholder particulars, and the prescribed registration fee. Registration for VAT is mandatory where taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceed VUV 10,000,000 in any consecutive 12-month period; below that, voluntary VAT registration may be approved. Because the localization services are rendered wholly offshore, the IC's routine supply activity does not create a Vanuatu VAT registration obligation; a TIN is nonetheless issued for customs, payroll, and administrative purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — an exempt International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu national direct taxes (no corporate income tax, personal income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, estate or inheritance duty, or stamp duty), and, being outside the domestic VAT net for offshore services, is not required to charge Vanuatu VAT; exemptions are strictly territorial and do not displace source-country or residence-country taxation of the beneficial owners. — A non-resident International Company (VU_IC) obtains its TIN/VAT ID by filing a TIN/VAT registration form with the DCIR through its licensed resident agent, accompanied by: certificate of incorporation and status excerpt from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), registered-agent licence and address, constitutional documents, director/shareholder particulars, and the prescribed registration fee. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — an exempt International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu national direct taxes (no corporate income tax, personal income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, estate or inheritance duty, or stamp duty), and, being outside the domestic VAT net for offshore services, is not required to charge Vanuatu VAT; exemptions are strictly territorial and do not displace source-country or residence-country taxation of the beneficial owners.

Available election forms

At incorporation, the VU_IC may elect 'exempt' or 'non-exempt' status by filing the prescribed election with VFSC; an exempt IC pays registration and annual maintenance fees in lieu of all Vanuatu taxes. Vanuatu has no domestic check-the-box, classification, or hybrid-mismatch election because no income tax exists; any flow-through or entity-level classification (e.g., US 8832, EU/UK rules) is a function of the foreign jurisdiction's law, not Vanuatu law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 10,000,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period (compulsory registration triggered after exceeding the threshold; voluntary registration available below the threshold; registration application due within 21 days of month-end in which threshold is crossed).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the VAT Act, supplies qualify for zero rating where: (i) goods are physically exported from Vanuatu within the statutorily prescribed export window (generally 28 days from supply, or an extended period approved by the DCIR) with prescribed export documentation; or (ii) services are supplied to a non-resident for use wholly outside Vanuatu, including offshore professional, translation, interpretation, transcription, subtitling, dubbing, and localization services delivered to overseas clients. The supplier must retain objective evidence of the offshore recipient, offshore performance/use, and payment from abroad. Input VAT borne on goods/services acquired in Vanuatu and used in making zero-rated exports is creditable and refundable, subject to a DCIR compliance review.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CED) accepted by the Vanuatu Customs Division of the DCIR
  • VAT tax invoice / commercial invoice identifying the foreign buyer and export consideration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exportation (goods) or signed delivery/performance report (services)
  • Packing list and certificate of origin (where requested by DCIR audits)
  • Signed service agreement or localization contract specifying delivery to a non-resident and offshore use
  • Bank SWIFT/remittance credit advice or Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing receipt of foreign consideration
  • VAT return and supporting working papers supporting the input-VAT refund claim

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-clearance controller, API-based clearance, or digital-stamping regime is currently in force in Vanuatu. VAT invoices may be paper or electronic and must contain the prescribed particulars (sequential invoice number, issue date, supplier name and VAT/TIN registration number, recipient name, description and quantity, consideration exclusive/inclusive of VAT, and the VAT amount). VAT registration, returns, and payments are processed electronically through the DCIR portal.

Zero-rated export claims are scrutinized by the DCIR through document-based audits, third-party banking information, and beneficiary-owner tracing. Where zero rating is unsupported, fraudulent, or fails documentary substantiation, the DCIR may: reassess VAT at the 12.5% rate on the full consideration; charge interest until full payment; impose administrative penalties of up to double the tax evaded (with higher penalties for deliberate concealment or falsified documentation); claw back any refunds paid with interest; and pursue criminal prosecution, fines, and imprisonment of directors/agents under the VAT Act, Customs Act, and Tax Administration Act. Vanuatu has no exchange-control restriction on export proceeds, but the 180-day repatriation benchmark aligns the documentary evidence chain with the audit and litigation windows.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has no standalone foreign-exchange-control statute and no requirements for central-bank approval of current or capital account transactions. The Vatu is de facto fully convertible for an International Company (IC); profits, dividends, liquidation surpluses and service revenues may be freely remitted to any jurisdiction. The practical constraints are limited to anti-money-laundering / counter-terrorist-financing (AML/CFT) checks by commercial banks, documentary evidence of the economic substance of the receipts, and the absence of a deep interbank FX market requiring banks to use cross-border counterparties for forward pricing. Profit remittance from the IC to its non-resident parent or shareholders is lawful without withholding tax, but the remitting bank may require a board resolution, the IC's tax-exemption certificate, financial statements and a declaration from the beneficial owner to ensure that the remittance is not used to disguise illicit flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu does not impose any statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, mandatory conversion ratio or repatriation timetable. A Vanuatu International Company performing professional multilingual translation and media dubbing services may invoice clients in foreign currency and retain 100% of export receipts in a VUV- or foreign-currency denominated bank account at a licensed commercial bank. No exchange-control licence is required to hold USD, EUR, GBP, AUD or other currencies offshore. The only conventional requirement is that such receipts be routed through a licensed banking channel consistent with the AML/CFT (Financial Transactions Reporting) Act, which imposes record-keeping and suspicious-transaction reporting duties.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), the national central bank and financial-services regulator responsible for monetary policy, bank supervision and the issuance of the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for cross-border disbursement aggregation, MEDIUM for regulatory certainty. Vanuatu does not maintain a local electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing category; offshore EMIs such as Wise and Payoneer are not locally regulated as banks or deposit-takers. Their services can be used as payment clearing and collection rails for a Vanuatu International Company, provided that (i) the EMI holds a requisite money-transmitter licence in its home jurisdiction, (ii) the IC maintains a compliant relationship with a licensed Vanuatu commercial bank for any VUV-denominated settlement, and (iii) the EMI account is not used to circumvent Vanuatu banking supervision, capital-market promotion restrictions or the IC's corporate-lending prohibition. The IC may lawfully receive USD/EUR client transfers via EMI rails and disburse to global linguists and studios, but capital injections and dividend routes should remain in the formal SWIFT banking ecosystem to avoid intermediary-bank rejection due to apparent lack of substance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing for an equity injection into a Vanuatu International Company: (i) the shareholder executes an outward SWIFT MT103 from a bank account held in its legal name, stating the purpose code 'Capital contribution/Equity investment in non-resident company' and the beneficiary as the IC's foreign-currency (USD) account at a Vanuatu commercial bank. (ii) The IC forwards to the receiving bank the board resolution approving the issuance of shares, Certificate of Incumbency, Certificate of Good Standing, the registered agent's confirmation of share allotment, and UBO/source-of-funds documentation. (iii) The receiving bank performs AML screening and, for amounts above its internal threshold, may seek confirmation from the registered agent that the contribution aligns with a valid share issue. (iv) Upon credit, the proceeds are recorded as paid-up share capital in the IC's register of members; they need not be converted into VUV because the account is a permitted foreign-currency account and Vanuatu imposes no forced conversion. (v) For injections from a related group company, the purpose code should be 'Intercompany loan' or 'Equity investment' consistently, and an intercompany loan agreement must be maintained to avoid recharacterisation by correspondent banks.The preferred clearing rails for outbound disbursements by the localization agency are, in order of use: (a) SWIFT MT103 single customer credit transfers for all material payments to vendors, studios and enterprise partners; (b) Wise and Payoneer multi-currency disbursement rails for micro-payments to independent linguists and dubbing talent, particularly in EUR, GBP, AUD and USD; (c) commercial irrevocable letters of credit (UCP600) where a contractual guarantee is mandatory for a media client; and (d) only in exceptional cases, international cards and RippleNet or similar crypto-fiat rails, subject to the IC's compliance policy. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A - Vanuatu has no domestic transfer pricing legislation, no Local File/Master File threshold, and no requirement to lodge transfer pricing documentation with VFSC. However, for cross-border related-party localization contracts, the IC should maintain contemporaneous OECD-aligned evidence of arm's length pricing to defend against foreign jurisdiction transfer pricing and CFC adjustments, particularly where the counterparty is in a jurisdiction with documentation thresholds such as EUR 1,000,000 or USD 1,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu Economic Substance regulations apply to International Companies carrying on relevant activities, including services, IP, banking, leasing, financing, shipping and insurance. A multilingual localization and dubbing agency entered on ISIC 7490 clearly falls within the 'services' category. It must demonstrate, for each financial year, that in Vanuatu it maintains adequate physical office space, premises or property; employs full-time employees proportionate to its activity; incurs local operating expenditure; and exercises board-level direction and management in Vanuatu. If the IC is tax resident in another recognized jurisdiction, it may instead satisfy the regime by submitting credible proof of foreign tax residence; otherwise VFSC will treat the IC as non-resident and require full substance reporting. Failure to meet economic substance obligations is a strict liability offense and may result in financial penalties, sanctions, and ultimately strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorized Domestic Business By IC

Statutory sanction: VFSC may impose a monetary penalty, issue a compliance direction, and ultimately strike the IC off the register. The IC forfeits its tax exemption and becomes liable for all taxes that apply to an ordinary Vanuatu company, though Vanuatu has no income tax to assess.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly allow domestic business to be transacted face personal liability for amounts owed and for any loss suffered by third parties. The corporate veil is pierced, and the court may issue domestic asset freezing orders, director disqualification, and restrictions on travel where funds are dissipated.
VAT Evasion and False Returns

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the VAT Act; fines presently set at up to VT 10,000,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years on conviction. Civil penalties automatically apply and may reach 200% of the unpaid tax plus daily interest on arrears.

Director personal liability: Senior managers personally who directed or permitted the false return are personally and jointly liable for the unpaid VAT, penalties and costs. Directors may also be prosecuted under the directors' duty provisions; a criminal conviction triggers travel bans, sequestration of assets, and disqualification from managing any Vanuatu-registered entity.
Shell Company and Deficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC may levy an ESR-specific financial penalty per year of default, cancel the IC's registration, and report the entity to international tax enforcement partners. An IC that remains non-compliant for consecutive periods is automatically entered into a public strike-off list and may later be dissolved by court order.

Director personal liability: Directors receive personal violation notices and, after warning, may be made personally liable for outstanding ESR penalties and restoration costs. The court may disqualify directors for up to 5 years and issue a personal financial conduct prohibition applicable within Vanuatu and in jurisdictions granting mutual legal assistance.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Enforcement under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 2 of 2014; fines are imposed per contravention and can include amounts up to VT 5,000,000 for natural persons and VT 20,000,000 for legal persons, plus confiscation proceedings. The VFSC may refer the matter to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: A director who authorized or was willfully blind to the omission is personally criminally liable and may be imprisoned for up to 12 months per count. The court can also make a restraint order against the director's personal bank accounts and assets, and the director can be added to an international financial restrictions list.
Transfer Pricing and Foreign CFC Enforcement

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu itself imposes no domestic transfer pricing penalty. However, the foreign tax administration may re-determine the arm's length income, deny deductions, apply withholding tax on recommputed income, impose 10%-40% transfer pricing penalties, and initiate joint tax audits. Repeat violations can lead to the IC being characterized as a controlled foreign corporation inside the shareholder's jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: If the transfer pricing manipulation constitutes fraudulent evasion of foreign tax, directors may be named in foreign civil recovery proceedings, and courts may pierce the IC in cases where the directors personally authorized unrewarded profit stripping. Personal liability attaches to directors who sign false transfer pricing certificates, and certain jurisdictions allow extradition or travel bans for serious tax abuse.
