Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, administers the Value Added Tax Act [CAP 245] and customs duties through its Inland Revenue and Customs divisions (portal: www.vrca.gov.vu). The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) (www.vfsc.vu) is the statutory regulator and corporate registry for International Companies and licensed electronic money institutions.

E-government filing portal

Because Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, no general TIN exists for International Companies. Step 1: Incorporate the International Company (IC) with the VFSC and obtain a unique IC registration number. Step 2: If the IC is statutorily exempt and transacts only with non-residents, no fiscal registration is required and the VFSC number is the sole identifier. Step 3: If the entity also makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu or intends to claim 0% VAT on exports, register for VAT with the VRCA and obtain a VAT Identification Number under the BE-1 registration procedure, with monthly or quarterly filing obligations. Step 4: Obtain the VFSC statutory electronic money institution license separately for virtual IBAN issuance and digital wallet reserve management.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: International Companies are non-resident and fully exempt from Vanuatu income tax, corporate tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax on dividends, royalties and interest, stamp duty, and exchange control. VAT applies only where the IC makes physical or domestic taxable supplies within Vanuatu.

Available election forms

Vanuatu does not operate US-style check-the-box classification or hybrid mismatch elections. The International Companies Act provides an irrevocable election upon incorporation for International Company status, which grants complete tax and stamp duty exemption. An IC may forfeit this status by carrying on business with Vanuatu residents or deriving Vanuatu-source domestic revenue, which triggers ordinary local-company VAT obligations. No CFC, thin capitalization, or hybrid instrument rules currently apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million vatu) annual taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months for mandatory VAT registration, equivalent to approximately USD 42,000 at standard exchange rates. An International Company making only offshore supplies has no Vanuatu VAT nexus and no registration requirement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For a Vanuatu International Company operating an electronic money institution (virtual IBAN issuance and digital wallet reserve management), 0% VAT applies to: (i) exported tangible goods that physically leave Vanuatu within 28 days of invoice and are evidenced by customs documentation; and (ii) cross-border financial and payment services supplied to non-residents, where the recipient is outside Vanuatu, the service is wholly consumed offshore, and the consideration is received in convertible foreign currency. Because the IC is statutorily tax-exempt, a practical zero-rating claim arises only if the EMI voluntarily registers for VAT or converts to local company status; otherwise offshore turnover remains outside the Vanuatu VAT net.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document or Form C) approved and stamped by VRCA Customs for goods exports
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing departure from a Vanuatu port or airport and arrival at a foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT MT103 / bank credit advice showing foreign-currency funds received into the Vanuatu bank account
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin
  • For services: executed agreement identifying the non-resident customer and a service attestation that digital wallet and IBAN services are accessed and used wholly outside Vanuatu

No mandatory electronic invoicing system, real-time fiscal clearance API, tax device certification, or digital stamping obligation is currently legislated in Vanuatu. VAT-registered businesses must issue prescribed VAT invoices showing the price, VAT component, BE-1/VAT identification number, and supplier details, and retain records for 7 years. The VRCA may require electronic document upload only during an audit, not as a clearance precondition.

The VRCA blocks refunds for 0% rated exports until the complete customs and remittance documentation bundle is submitted. Unsubstantiated claims give rise to a default VAT assessment with a 25% surcharge and monthly interest of 1.5%. Fraudulent export zero-rating or VAT invoice falsification is criminally punishable by fines up to VT 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years. Directors of a VFSC-licensed electronic money institution additionally risk license revocation, disqualification, and statutory debarment under the International Companies Act. Retrospective reassessment may claw back up to 8 years of exempted VAT and render the IC taxable as a local company if prohibited domestic business activity is discovered.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberal exchange-control regime: the Vatu (VUV) is convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions without prior exchange-control approval for equity injections, service fees, dividends, or loan repayments, provided the initiating commercial bank acts as an authorized dealer under the Exchange Control Act. Profit remittance from an International Company (VU_IC) is not subject to withholding tax because Vanuatu has no income tax on corporate distributions, and there is no foreign-investment profit-remittance cadence; the residual procedural hurdles are documentary only, namely UBO evidence, source-of-funds verification, and standard foreign-exchange statistical declarations, whereas the material practical hurdle is correspondent-bank de-risking of VUV-denominated fintech flows rather than any domestic legal restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable in Vanuatu. There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio into VUV, and no requirement to repatriate export earnings within a fixed period. An Electronic Money Institution receiving subscription fees, transaction fees, float-holding income, or technology-service revenue offshore may lawfully retain those proceeds in USD, AUD, EUR, or VUV without a forced conversion, subject only to contractual arrangements with its RBV-licensed sponsor bank and to standard exchange-control reporting thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), constituted under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], is the designated monetary authority and exchange-control administrator under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 20]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately supervises the Financial Dealers Licensing Act [Cap. 173] under which the proposed electronic-money and virtual-IBAN operation will obtain its principal license. The RBV licenses commercial banks and oversees the domestic clearing and settlement infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-to-High for a licensed Financial Dealer operating as a virtual-IBAN issuer and digital-wallet operator rather than as a licensed bank. Vanuatu has no dedicated electronic-money institution statute, no EU/EEA passporting regime, and no native SEPA participant status; consequently an offshore EMI established under the International Companies Act can lawfully serve non-resident customers, hold float in USD/AUD, and route settlement via global correspondents, but it must (i) hold a Financial Dealer's Licence from VFSC, (ii) maintain a settlement account with an RBV-licensed bank for any VUV leg, (iii) obtain SEPA reachability through an EEA-licensed partner EMI or bank, and (iv) avoid deposit-taking language and banking activities that would trigger the Banking Act. The commercial model is therefore viable only in a sponsor-bank arrangement for virtual-IBAN issuance and domestic clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share capital and subordinated loan proceeds are routed by SWIFT MT103/MT202 to the EMI's settlement account held at an RBV-licensed commercial bank, with beneficiary narration 'VU_IC share capital' or 'VU_IC subordinated loan advance' and the BPM6 purpose classification for equity investment or intercompany lending. No prior RBV exchange-control approval is required, but the receiving authorized dealer will complete a foreign-exchange statistical declaration and may require an anti-money-laundering source-of-funds form if the amount exceeds its domestic reporting threshold; for same-bank VUV transfers, the domestic electronic credit rail is used, and for USD/AUD settlement at the sponsor bank, ISO 20022 camt.054 statements are retained for VFSC and RBV audit.Outbound disbursements settle through the sponsor bank's corresponding network using SWIFT MT103 (customer credit), MT202 (bank-to-bank liquidity), and MT700-series commercial letters of credit under UCP600 for trade-related settlements. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu imposes no general corporate income tax; an International Company (IC) is tax-exempt on offshore income under the International Companies Act [Cap.222] as a Tax_Exempt_Zone structure.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu law imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions from an IC to any shareholder, including foreign beneficial owners.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No domestic transfer-pricing statute or documentation threshold exists in VUV. Prudent compliance threshold: prepare Master File and Local File when related-party cross-border transactions (management fees, software licensing, recharges) exceed VUV 30,000,000 per annum, anticipating OECD TP review by counterparty-source jurisdictions.

Economic substance scope: No standalone Economic Substance Regulation statute is in force; however, commitments under the EU Code of Conduct Group and VFSC prudential licensing conditions require a licensed IC to demonstrate physical presence in Vanuatu, local management, and real income-generating activity when carrying on licensed financial business. Minimum substance markers: physical registered office in Port Vila, licensed agent, resident compliance officer, board records, and accounting records maintained in Vanuatu. An IC lacking these elements is classified as a shell by the FIU, exposing the VFSC license to revocation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unlicensed Electronic Money and IBAN Operation

Statutory sanction: Fine up to VUV 100,000 on conviction plus VUV 5,000 for each day the offense continues; confiscation of unlawfully collected fees; mandatory cease-and-desist directions; license refusal if application made ex post.

Director personal liability: Directors personally subject to fines and imprisonment up to 6 months under the Financial Dealers Act Cap.70; VFSC disqualification orders; civil restitution to injured wallet clients, severally.
VAT Evasion and False Export Zero-Rating

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 200% of the VAT evaded, 20% default interest, and fines on conviction up to VUV 5,000,000; confiscation and disclosure of records to foreign tax authorities under the OECD MAAC.

Director personal liability: Directors liable for restitution of evaded VAT; imprisonment up to 7 years under the VAT Act and Criminal Code; travel bans and asset freezes pending criminal trial.
Shell Company and Nominee Director Misconduct

Statutory sanction: Registrar-initiated strike-off and deregistration; restoration only by court order with all fees paid; AML penalties for beneficial-ownership non-disclosure up to VUV 15,000,000; license revocation.

Director personal liability: After strike-off, company debts may be enforced personally against directors; FIU travel bans; money laundering conviction punishable by fine up to VUV 25,000,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years.
AML/CFT Reporting Failure on Digital Wallets

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to VUV 25,000,000; corrective directions, independent AML audit, and suspension or revocation of the VFSC license; forfeiture of misused e-money reserves.

Director personal liability: Individual fines up to VUV 5,000,000; prohibition from holding directorships in any regulated financial institution; custodial sentence; civil liability for client losses resulting from unreported reportable flows.
