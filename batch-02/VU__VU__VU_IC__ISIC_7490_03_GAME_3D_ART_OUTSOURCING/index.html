Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), a department of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; official government portal: https://www.gov.vu; VRC service portal: https://www.vrc.gov.vu

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned International Company (IC): (1) incorporate under the International Companies Act Cap 191 through a licensed agent registered with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; (2) file the registered office, director, and shareholder particulars with the VFSC registry; (3) apply to the Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC) for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) using the prescribed TIN application form, supported by the Certificate of Incorporation, certified passport copies of beneficial owners, and proof of the registered agent address; a non-resident IC with no physical place of business in Vanuatu may appoint a local tax agent to file the application; (4) if annual taxable turnover attributable to Vanuatu exceeds VT 5,000,000, file the VAT registration application simultaneously. No income tax registration is required because Vanuatu imposes no national corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None comparable to US check-the-box. Under the International Companies Act Cap 191, an IC is statutorily tax-exempt and automatically treated as non-domiciled; it cannot elect to be taxed as a Vanuatu-resident company. The Value Added Tax Act Cap 247 permits voluntary VAT registration for turnover below the compulsory threshold, but no hybrid mismatch elections, entity classification elections, or income tax grouping mechanisms exist under Vanuatu law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million Vatu) annual taxable turnover; a supplier making taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding this threshold in any 12-month period must register for VAT; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold; exports of goods and offshore services are zero-rated and count toward the threshold for registration purposes

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Schedule 2 of the VAT Act Cap 247, 0% VAT applies to: (a) goods physically exported from Vanuatu by the supplier, evidenced by customs export entries and shipping documents; (b) services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and who consumes the benefit wholly outside Vanuatu, including digital 3D art creation, modelling, sculpting, animation, and character rigging services delivered electronically to overseas game publishers; (c) services directly connected with exported goods; and (d) international transportation of passengers or goods. The supplier must retain contemporaneous documentation proving the recipient's non-residence, the offshore place of consumption, and, for goods, physical export from Vanuatu within the statutory record-keeping period.

  • Vanuatu Customs Export Declaration (electronic entry lodged in ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or House Airway Bill evidencing physical export out of Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice and signed service or licensing agreement with the non-resident client
  • Inward remittance credit advice or bank statement evidencing receipt of foreign-currency payment
  • Valid VAT tax invoice issued under the VAT Act Cap 247
  • Written declaration or certificate from the non-resident client confirming non-residence and exclusive offshore consumption

No real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory e-invoicing regime. Vanuatu does not operate a government e-invoicing platform, an API-based transaction clearance system, or an SOVD/digital stamping requirement. Suppliers must issue paper or electronic tax invoices containing prescribed particulars under the VAT Regulations (supplier name, TIN/VAT registration number, date, description of supply, taxable amount, and VAT charged) and retain records for seven years for inspection by Vanuatu Revenue and Customs.

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs may conduct retrospective VAT assessments within the seven-year record-keeping window. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims attract a default assessment of up to 200% of the VAT evaded, additional monetary penalties and interest under the VAT Act Cap 247, and criminal liability including fines and imprisonment on indictment. An International Company found to have traded with Vanuatu residents or to have claimed zero-rating without contemporaneous export documentation forfeits its statutory tax-exempt status, becomes retrospectively liable for VAT and penalties, and may be struck off by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission for non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is a managed float currency with full convertibility for current-account transactions and broad de facto liberalization of capital-account movements for non-resident-owned International Companies. Profit remittance and dividend distribution to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted and tax-free under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] and the zero-income-tax regime, provided the underlying balance is verified through standard bank source-of-funds and sanctions screening. Practical hurdles are operational rather than regulatory: VUV is not an internationally traded hard currency, so most cross-border settlement occurs through liquid channels in USD, AUD, NZD, or EUR. Multi-currency operating accounts and offshore USD clearing accounts are permitted and commonly used.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline exists under Vanuatu law. Export earnings from game art outsourcing services may be retained freely in any convertible currency, held in domestic or offshore bank accounts, and remitted abroad at will. On an ex gratia basis, a purely documentary purpose code advising the nature of the inward remittance is required by the receiving authorized dealer; ICs may maintain revenues in USD or AUD with zero mandatory conversion to VUV.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) acting jointly with the Minister of Finance under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [CAP 125] and the Exchange Control Act [CAP 61]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) supervises corporate registration, licensing, and financial services conduct.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low with legal constraints. PayPal is accepted and viable for low-value client invoices and micro-settlements in USD from North American and European game studios; however, PayPal's Vanuatu platform limits high-volume B2B flows and may impose aggregate account ceilings. Global digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Mercury) generally do not service Vanuatu-domiciled entities because VUV lacks international convertibility and the jurisdictions classify Vanuatu ICs as offshore low-tax entities requiring enhanced due diligence. It is legally permissible but operationally impractical for the IC to rely on EMIs as the primary clearing rail; SWIFT banking via a Tier 1 authorized dealer remains the contractual and liquidity backbone.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (zero-rate) - Vanuatu does not impose corporate income tax at the national level, and an International Company ('IC') registered under the International Companies Act Cap. 222 is expressly exempt from all Vanuatu taxes on international business income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax, no remittance tax, and no branch profits tax. The IC may distribute 100% of retained earnings to non-resident shareholders without domestic statutory withholding or deduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

IC Annual Return: no later than one calendar month after the anniversary of incorporation. ESR Annual Return: no later than 12 months after the end of the IC's financial year (financial year end commonly 31 December).

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None in VUV - Vanuatu has not enacted transfer pricing legislation, Local File/Master File thresholds, country-by-country reporting, or advance pricing agreements. The operative constraint is customer-side: if the client is a US public company or an EU/Japan/Korea taxpayer, that taxpayer must prepare contemporaneous CUP/TNMM documentation for the outsourcing fee. A missing foreign TP file triggers foreign penalties and adjustments irrespective of the zero Vanuatu rate. In practice, the IC should maintain an annual service agreement, cost build-up schedule, and margin benchmark for the 3D art outsourcing fee.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2023 and associated regulations apply to any IC conducting a relevant activity. A 3D art outsourcing studio falls within the ESR 'Service Business' category and must demonstrate in Vanuatu: substantive employees engaged in 3D pipeline roles, a physical office or racking location with production infrastructure, locally incurred operating expenditure, and board minutes documenting strategic and operational management in Vanuatu. Using the IC as a mailbox while all creative and administrative functions are performed through sub-contractors in high-tax jurisdictions breaches ESR, exposes the IC to strike-off, and triggers automatic information exchange with the beneficial owner's tax authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: VFSC penalty: ESR annual return rejection, daily administrative penalty accrual, compulsory strike-off upon issuance of a non-compliance certificate, and referral to the EU Code of Conduct Group; reputational listing on public blacklists and exclusion from correspondent banking.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false substance declarations face personal director disqualification, civil restoration orders from the VFSC, foreign tax authority information-exchange requests, and ancillary court orders including asset freezes and travel restrictions.
Tax Evasion / Abusive Cross-Border Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Domestic Vanuatu sanctions are minimal because no domestic transfer pricing statute exists, but customer-side consequences are severe: recharacterization under IRC Section 482 or equivalent foreign CFC/GAAR rules, tax adjustment penalties of 25-100% of the underpaid foreign tax, inclusion in the customer's country-by-country report, and potential permanent establishment findings against the foreign related studio.

Director personal liability: Foreign revenue authorities may pierce the IC to issue director-level notices and impose personal penalties for false documentation; in egregious cases, mutual legal assistance and extradition procedures can be initiated for criminal tax fraud.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act and VFSC AML rules: regulatory fines against the IC, suspension or revocation of the registered agent's license, freezing of the IC's bank account, and downgrade of the IC's AML risk classification from Low-Risk Standard to High-Risk, which usually results in forced account termination by correspondent banks.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for AML failures: criminal fines, imprisonment, civil restitution for assets laundered through the outsourcing invoices, and travel bans issued pending investigation. Nominated directors face immediate disqualification and potential immigration arrest.
Unregistered Vanuatu VAT on Domestic Supplies

Statutory sanction: VAT Act Cap. 246: VCIR assessment for unreported output VAT, default surcharge, interest accrual, monetary penalties of up to VUV 1,000,000 for corporations, and criminal prosecution of the managing officer for intentional evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in VAT suppression are jointly and severally liable for the assessed VAT debt; the liability survives corporate dissolution, and a strike-off order will not be issued by the VFSC until the VCIR tax debt is settled.
