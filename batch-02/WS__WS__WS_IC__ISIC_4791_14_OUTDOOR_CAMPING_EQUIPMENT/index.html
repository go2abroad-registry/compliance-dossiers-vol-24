Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Ministry of Revenue (MOR) – online portal: Samoa Tax Portal (SatisTax, https://revenue.gov.ws)

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) formed under the Samoa International Companies Act 1987 is not automatically registered for Samoan income tax. If the IC carries on a taxable activity in Samoa or derives Samoa-source income, it must apply to the Ministry of Revenue for a TIN using Form IRD-TIN-01 and register for direct taxes. Purely offshore ICs with no Samoa-source income and no local activity may obtain a tax exemption certificate from MOR without TIN registration. VAGST registration, where applicable, is made separately on Form VAGST-REG-01.

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a check-the-box pass-through election. An IC may, however, voluntarily register for VAGST if it conducts taxable activities, and may apply to MOR for a tax exemption certificate confirming its offshore income exemption. Hybrid mismatch elections are not recognised in Samoa's tax legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 60,000 of annual taxable supplies

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Samoa are chargeable to VAGST at 0% where the goods are physically exported out of Samoa within the prescribed period and evidence of export is retained. Offshore services supplied to a non-resident outside Samoa qualify for 0% if the services are not performed for the benefit of a person in Samoa and the supplier satisfies MOR documentary requirements.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Samoa Customs SED)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank evidence of foreign exchange receipt)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)
  • Supplier's formal declaration of export status under VAGST rules

No mandatory e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance system is in force. Tax invoices must meet VAGST requirements (supplier name, TIN, date, description, GST amount) but no government API clearance or digital stamping is required.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims attract immediate VAGST assessment at the standard 15% rate, together with late payment penalty of 20% per annum, a 20% shortfall penalty, and potential criminal liability under the Goods and Services Tax Act for deliberately evading tax or presenting false documentation. Retrospective clawback extends to all input credits wrongly claimed in connection with the non-qualifying export.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan tālā (WST) operates under a system of partial convertibility. Current account transactions are generally liberalized, subject to documentation and exchange control declaration. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment equity subscriptions in an international company, are subject to prior CBS approval and submission of Form BOP/FDI. Profit remittance is lawful but subject to withholding tax on dividends, production of audited financial statements, tax clearance certificate, and exchange control approval demonstrating that the underlying investment was properly admitted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export receipts from a Samoa-domiciled international company must be repatriated to Samoa within ninety (90) days of export and surrendered to a licensed commercial bank. The mandatory conversion requirement is 100% of foreign currency export proceeds converted into WST or retained in a designated foreign currency account only with prior CBS approval. Proceeds may be retained offshore only under a standing exemption granted to shipping or certain service-export entities; for ISIC 4791 e-commerce retail, no general exemption applies.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), acting as the Exchange Control Authority pursuant to the Exchange Control Act 1976 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1976 (as amended)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low viability. Samoa does not maintain a fintech EMI or electronic money institution licensing regime. Offshore digital EMI account providers cannot legally establish banking facilities for Samoa-domiciled international companies without obtaining a commercial bank licence under the Financial Institutions Act 1996. Payment aggregation via Stripe/PayPal is acceptable as a settlement conduit but cannot replace licensed banking relationships. International companies should therefore rely on tier-1 domestic banks with correspondent banking networks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the Samoa IC is routed via SWIFT MT103 from the ultimate beneficial owner's bank using purpose code under the IMF BPM6: 'FDI-equity injection' or 'Other Investment-Loan to related enterprise'. The transfer must name the exact IC legal name, registration number, and Samoa domestic bank account IBAN or CBC-number. A copy of the CBS pre-registration approval or Form BOP/FDI acceptance must accompany the transfer. Where the capital is deployed for raw inventory of camping equipment, the remitter should additionally reference 'ISIC 4791 online retail working capital'. The domestic beneficiary bank will require the source-of-funds certificate and a board resolution before crediting a Capital Escrow Account.Outbound trade settlements may be cleared through SWIFT MT103 for suppliers under USD 250,000 equivalent; for larger manufacturing purchase orders, a confirmed irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Samoan bank is the preferred rail. Payments to overseas outdoor gear manufacturers are made via correspondent bank clearance through ANZ or common Pacific correspondent networks. Settlement currencies permitted are WST, USD, AUD, NZD, and EUR at CBS-approved exchange rates. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% for resident domestic companies under the Income Tax Act 1974 (as amended), subject to temporary and sectoral variations.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory non-resident dividend withholding tax under the Income Tax Act 1974 where dividends are paid out of Samoa-source profits. For an exempt International Company, dividends distributed to non-resident shareholders are outside the Samoan tax net and suffer 0% Samoan withholding tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa applies transfer pricing rules under the Income Tax Act 1974 (s.34C as amended) only to resident taxpayers with cross-border connected person transactions; no statutory local-currency monetary threshold is promulgated. Exempt ICs are not within the transfer pricing net while the 0% exemption subsists. As a benchmarking proxy, practitioners commonly retain Master File/Local File documentation when consolidated turnover exceeds WST 5,000,000, although this amount is not a legal trigger.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2021 apply to ICs engaged in defined relevant activities, expressly including distribution and service centre business, which captures the internet retailing of outdoor camping equipment. The IC must demonstrate: board management and decision-taking performed in Samoa through the registered agent; adequate staff, premises, and annual operating expenditure; and core income-generating activities directed and controlled from Samoa. Passive reselling without local substance or booking arrangements in third countries triggers a gap in the exemption and exposes the IC to challenge before the SIFA Economic Substance Committee.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Income Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment of all undeclared profits at the 27% corporate rate, plus a civil penalty equal to 100% of the tax shortfall and interest at 6% per annum under the Income Tax Act 1974. Criminal fines of up to WST 1,000,000 apply on indictment.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign or knowingly permit a false certification are personally liable on a joint and several basis for the back taxes and penalties. Under the Crimes Act 2013, knowing provision of false information to a public authority attracts imprisonment of up to 7 years and disqualification of the director from acting as an IC director for 5 years.
Shell Company and Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under the International Companies Act 1988 without restoration; revocation of the registered agent's licence where the agent aided the sham; mandatory referral to the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit and disclosure on the beneficial ownership register; permanent ineligibility for a future IC licence.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability for all SIFA fees, restitution to third-party creditors, and a travel ban under the Frauds Act. Criminal conviction for furnishing false information to the Registrar carries imprisonment of up to 7 years and automatic disqualification from corporate management in Samoa.
AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and SIFA AML/CFT Rules, pecuniary penalties of up to WST 100,000 per breach, suspension or revocation of the registered agent's licence, and a directive to freeze the IC's bank accounts. The risk tier elevated to high risk where the low-risk standard category of ISIC 4791 is abused.

Director personal liability: Responsible officers are individually liable for imprisonment of up to 14 years and fines of up to WST 1,000,000 where money laundering is proved. Directors may be barred from holding any position in a financial institution or licensed agent for 10 years, and the court may order civil asset forfeiture of the total funds laundered.
