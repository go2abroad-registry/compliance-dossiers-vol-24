Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, CFC, hybrid mismatch, or territorial election mechanisms exist under Vanuatu law. The IC is statutorily exempt from all Vanuatu taxes on offshore income and capital gains (International Companies Act Cap 222, s. 40); the exemption is automatic, non-elective, indefinite, and cannot be waived by taxpayer action.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 in taxable turnover during any 12-month period (VAT Act No. 24 of 2022, s. 6); voluntary registration permitted after the first taxable supply

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for 0% VAT where the goods physically leave Vanuatu within 28 days of supply and the exporter retains a customs export declaration and transport document; offshore services qualify for 0% where supplied to a non-resident outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and the effective use and enjoyment of the service occurs outside Vanuatu. For the IC structure, offshore cloud infrastructure, database, and API monitoring services for foreign clients fall entirely outside the Vanuatu VAT net as non-taxable offshore supplies; where the IC voluntarily registers, such supplies are zero-rated rather than exempt, allowing input credit recovery.

  • Customs Export Declaration (VRCS Customs electronic CUSDEC entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Vanuatu
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 statement evidencing settlement from the non-resident buyer
  • Signed Offshore Service Contract containing place-of-supply, use-and-enjoyment, and non-resident status clauses

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or B2B clearance mandate is currently operative; VAT-registered sellers must issue serial-numbered VAT tax invoices within 7 days of supply, and VRCS accepts voluntary software-based e-invoicing (XML/Peppol-style UBL) without pre-clearance or digital fiscal stamp.

No exchange control or mandatory FX repatriation deadline exists in Vanuatu (Exchange Control Act repealed 1993). VRCS may scrutinize zero-rating substantiation for up to 5 years; fraudulent or reckless claims trigger refund clawback with interest at 25%, civil fines up to VT 10,000,000, imprisonment up to 10 years (VAT Act 2022, ss. 94-99), and registered-agent disqualification; deliberate misuse of an IC for tax fraud additionally exposes the entity to VFSC deregistration and a 20-year statutory bar on reincorporation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a liberalised capital and current account regime; there are no general exchange-control restrictions on payments or transfers. The VUV is not a globally deliverable hard currency, and market convertibility is supported by authorised foreign-exchange dealers. Profit remittances and capital repatriation may be conducted freely, subject only to standard anti-money laundering (AML), taxation clearance, and beneficial-ownership verification. No separate capital-control permit is required for dividend or capital repayment flows to foreign shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Vanuatu imposes no statutory obligation to surrender export proceeds to the central bank and no minimum mandatory conversion ratio into VUV. Exporters may hold foreign-currency receipts in licensed bank accounts. Periodic trade and balance-of-payments reporting to the Vanuatu Bureau of Statistics and the RBV is required for statistical aggregation, but repatriation timelines do not bind the International Company structure.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125]; acts as the central monetary authority, prudential regulator for licensed financial institutions, and the de facto foreign exchange administration in the Republic of Vanuatu.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-to-Low. Unlicensed foreign fintech electronic-money institutions (EMIs) and digital payment service providers cannot lawfully operate as deposit-taking institutions in Vanuatu. Such EMIs may only serve as auxiliary payment gateways or card-issuing agents for fiat disbursements, provided the entity maintains a qualifying local licensing or agency arrangement under RBV payment-system rules. Mercury, Stripe Billing, and similar external rails are acceptable as collection and disbursement processors but not as substitutes for a regulated Vanuatu bank account when preserving IBC tax-exempt status.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions must be structured as either equity or shareholder loan remittances via SWIFT MT103 through the IBC's Vanuatu operational bank account. The applicant shall use the harmonised purpose code for cross-border corporate investment (PURP: EQUI) and include a board resolution approving the capitalisation. Clearance of wires is accelerated when the sending parent entity's bank is a correspondent of the Vanuatu receiving bank and the reference line exactly matches the IBC's legal name. Capital escrow accounts will accept the injection and issue a certificate of deposit of share capital, preserving the IBC's exempt-tax status by avoiding any domestic trading through the registered agent.Outbound vendor payments, infrastructure subscriptions, and software royalties settle via (1) SWIFT MT103 Cover or SWIFT MT103 direct credit for USD, AUD, NZD and EUR-denominated invoices; (2) commercial letters of credit (L/C) advised through a top-tier correspondent bank when disbursements exceed USD 250,000 and require documentary assurance; and (3) local vatu-denominated RTGS settlement for domestic Vanuatu contractor payments. The platform must include automatic generation of the UNSPSC invoice code and export tariff code for each disbursement to satisfy both Vanuatu and receiving-jurisdiction customs filings.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) on VUV/USD entered with an eligible offshore bank because the vatu is non-deliverable outside Vanuatu.Deliverable spot and outright forwards on USD/EUR/AUD conducted with RBV-licensed authorised foreign-exchange dealers.Cross-currency basis swap on USD/AUD or USD/EUR for the conversion of subscription revenues to the IBC's functional treasury currency.Vanuatu-exempt forward contracts under ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a credit support annex and no speculative margin on the IBC balance sheet.Currency options on USD/AUD combined with collar structures for budgeted non-functional-currency disbursements.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Vanuatu has no corporate income tax law. An International Company (IC) incorporated under the International Companies Act 1992 (Cap. 222) is statutorily exempt from all income, profits, capital gains, and dividend taxation (ICA s.9).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Vanuatu levies no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders; the ICA s.9 exemption covenant guarantees freedom from dividend taxation.

Late filing: civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded plus default interest; knowingly false returns constitute tax evasion punishable by fine and imprisonment up to 5 years; VRC may also impose a late filing penalty of VUV 1,000 per month or a similar fixed surcharge.
Box 3 — Input Tax Credits VAT paid on local purchases and expenses attributable to taxable activities. Foreign-hosted cloud costs bear no VU VAT and are non-creditable, but do not attract output VAT either. Late filing: civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded plus default interest; knowingly false returns constitute tax evasion punishable by fine and imprisonment up to 5 years; VRC may also impose a late filing penalty of VUV 1,000 per month or a similar fixed surcharge.
Box 4 — Net VAT Payable / Refundable Output tax minus input tax; positive amount payable to VRC. Export-only ICs typically claim a refund or file nil VAT; supporting contracts and bank evidence (Mercury ACH) must evidence foreign receipt. Late filing: civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded plus default interest; knowingly false returns constitute tax evasion punishable by fine and imprisonment up to 5 years; VRC may also impose a late filing penalty of VUV 1,000 per month or a similar fixed surcharge.
Box 5 — Total Consideration Aggregate consideration for all supplies in the period. Report gross worldwide subscription revenue to enable zero-rating audit trail. Late filing: civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded plus default interest; knowingly false returns constitute tax evasion punishable by fine and imprisonment up to 5 years; VRC may also impose a late filing penalty of VUV 1,000 per month or a similar fixed surcharge.
Box 6 — Adjustments Corrections, credit notes, and bad debt relief. Foreign currency conversion to VUV using VRC exchange rates; chargeback reversals on Stripe billing should be recorded here. Late filing: civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded plus default interest; knowingly false returns constitute tax evasion punishable by fine and imprisonment up to 5 years; VRC may also impose a late filing penalty of VUV 1,000 per month or a similar fixed surcharge.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory TP documentation threshold is codified in Vanuatu law (no CIT/TP legislation). Adoption of OECD-aligned Master File/Local File documentation is strongly recommended where annual related-party transactions (licensing, intercompany services, cloud infrastructure) exceed VUV 50,000,000 (approximately USD 420,000), or where the related-party royalty/income ratio exceeds 30% of gross revenue, to support economic substance filings and defend against foreign CFC/treaty-shopping challenges.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 applies to ICs carrying on 'relevant activities,' which include an Intellectual Property Business and a Distribution and Service Centre business — both engaged by a DevTools software, database, and API monitoring group. For each applicable activity, the IC must: (1) be managed and directed from Vanuatu (quorum of board meetings physically in Vanuatu, minutes retained, strategic decisions made there); (2) maintain adequate physical premises, qualified full-time employees, and operating expenditure proportionate to the level of activity; (3) perform core income-generating activities (contract negotiation, pricing, pipeline management, monitoring dashboard support) in Vanuatu; (4) file an annual economic substance return with the VFSC. Failure to satisfy ESR exposes the IC to penalties and potential deregistration, undermining the viability of a pure 'invoice shell' model.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False VAT Declarations

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 150% of the evaded tax plus interest; criminal fine of up to VUV 5,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years under the Revenue (Value Added Tax) Act 1998; VRC may recompute the VAT assessment and enforce asset seizure.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise false returns are jointly and severally liable for civil penalties; conviction carries imprisonment, disqualification from managing companies in Vanuatu, and a travel ban pending criminal proceedings.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Default

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019: immediate penalty of VUV 2,000,000 for failure to file the ESR return, escalating VUV 1,000,000 per month for continuing non-compliance, up to VUV 10,000,000 for supplying false information; ultimate deregistration and striking-off of the IC.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined up to VUV 1,000,000, disqualified as officers, and subjected to a travel ban; third-party creditors can pierce the corporate veil where the IC is found to be a sham, rendering directors personally liable for company debts and contractual restitutions.
AML/CTF Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2017: corporate fines up to VUV 50,000,000, confiscation of funds, suspension of VFSC licence; involved individuals face imprisonment up to 15 years and fines up to VUV 25,000,000.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for AML offences committed with their knowledge or consent; sanctions include imprisonment, asset freezing, travel bans, and personal financial penalties, in addition to indefinite disqualification from holding position in a VFSC-regulated entity.
Misrepresentation to Foreign Tax Authorities / CRS Evasion

Statutory sanction: UK/US/Source-country anti-abuse rules and the VFSC CRS enforcement regime impose fines up to VUV 5,000,000 and administrative orders to correct disclosures; the foreign jurisdiction may treat the IC as tax-resident under CFC/management-and-control rules, back-taxing 20–30% of profits with interest and penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false residence declarations face prosecution for perjury in the source jurisdiction, personal assessment for unpaid corporate taxes under contractual indemnity, exclusion from cross-border banking partnerships, and criminal extradition in egregious fraud cases.
