Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of International Companies, Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC), Port Vila, Republic of Vanuatu

Governing statute

International Companies Act No. 32 of 1992, as amended (Vanuatu), Parts II and III governing incorporation, registered office and registered agent requirements; the domestic Companies Act [Cap. 191] does not apply to International Companies.

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (constitutional document) lodged by a VFSC-licensed registered agent with the Registrar, upon which the Certificate of Incorporation is issued under section 14 of the International Companies Act.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the Articles must authorize at least one share (with or without par value) and no escrow account or paid-in capital deposit is required for registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification by the Vanuatu Fire and Rescue Service covering means of egress, alarms, and extinguishing equipment; structural load inspection of the premises by the Public Works Department Building Inspectorate; workplace occupational health clearance under the Employment Act No. 1 of 2010, including first-aid, emergency evacuation, and operator work-station ergonomic checks.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the Articles must authorize at least one share (with or without par value) and no escrow account or paid-in capital deposit is required for registration.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (VIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official e-services portal at https://www.vird.gov.vu (supplemented by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) company registry for International Companies; customs declarations via the Vanuatu Customs Asycuda World electronic single window).

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax, so no income TIN is required. The VFSC Certificate of Incorporation bearing the International Company (IC) registration number serves as the statutory identifier. A separate VAT identification number is obtainable only where the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu; the non-resident offshore IC is not within the VAT net because the International Companies Act (Cap 222) prohibits domestic trading. If voluntary/exigible VAT registration were sought (e.g., after domestic conversion under the Companies Act), the entity must file Form VF 224 with VIRD, attaching the incorporation certificate, registered office certificate, directors/shareholders register, and projection of taxable turnover, with decision within 14 working days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone - A Vanuatu International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) is absolutely exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, stamp duties, and levies on income, profits, capital, assets, distributions, and share transfers. The exemption is written into the statute and is not dependent on a ruling. The IC is a non-resident offshore vehicle prohibited from acquiring assets in Vanuatu or trading with Vanuatu residents, thereby remaining outside the territorial VAT and customs regime. — Vanuatu levies no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax, so no income TIN is required. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - A Vanuatu International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act (Cap 222) is absolutely exempt from all Vanuatu taxes, duties, stamp duties, and levies on income, profits, capital, assets, distributions, and share transfers. The exemption is written into the statute and is not dependent on a ruling. The IC is a non-resident offshore vehicle prohibited from acquiring assets in Vanuatu or trading with Vanuatu residents, thereby remaining outside the territorial VAT and customs regime.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, flow-through, or hybrid mismatch election regime exists in Vanuatu. The only statutory elections are (i) voluntary VAT registration under the VAT Act (Cap 247) for a person making taxable supplies; and (ii) conversion of an IC into a domestic company under the Companies Act (Cap 191), which prospectively forfeits offshore tax exemption. Under Section 357 of the International Companies Act, a converting IC retains a 20-year tax exemption on pre-conversion operations. No GAAR-related hybrid election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 (four million vatu) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; no separate distance-selling threshold for intangible/offshore BPO services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Section 11 of the VAT Act to (a) goods exported from Vanuatu; and (b) services supplied to a recipient who is outside Vanuatu where the benefit of the supply is received outside Vanuatu (offshore/exported services). The remote BPO call center (multilingual inbound helpdesk and outbound tele-sales) qualifies where the contracting counterparty is a non-resident entity, the service is rendered from Vanuatu to foreign clients, and no recipient of the benefit is present or consuming the service within Vanuatu. Critical evidence: written master service agreement with the foreign principal, itemized invoices referencing the offshore contract, IP routing/telephony records showing foreign call destinations, and inbound foreign-currency payments from overseas.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Asycuda World format) - mandatory only for physical goods; for pure services, a detailed Service Export Schedule substitutes
  • Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill evidencing goods left Vanuatu (goods) or client delivery/usage records abroad (services)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/credit advice evidencing foreign currency receipt from the non-resident client
  • Zero-rated VAT tax invoice bearing supplier VAT registration number when registered, plus the dated Service Agreement
  • Packing List and Commercial Invoice for goods; for BPO services, a Detailed Service Statement with call traffic/log data

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, API stamping, or digital stamping mandate exists in Vanuatu. Hard-copy tax invoices are legally sufficient; electronic invoices are permitted but voluntary and are not transmitted to VIRD. Customs export declarations for physical goods are lodged through the Asycuda World portal, but services escape customs formalities.

VIRD may audit export zero-rating claims for up to five years from the return date. Where export status is disallowed - if services are in fact consumed by a Vanuatu resident or goods never departed - the supply is recharacterized as taxable at 12.5%, with interest accruing at 2% per month on the unpaid VAT and a civil penalty of up to 150% of the shortfall. Fraudulent zero-rating (false declarations, forged inward remittance certificates, sham offshore contracts) triggers criminal prosecution under the VAT Act with fines up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to seven years, plus retrospective clawback, deregistration for VAT, and joint and several liability of directors of the International Company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains a liberal exchange-control regime under its Exchange Control Act and the supervisory framework of the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu. The vatu is convertible for both current account and capital account transactions through authorised dealers. There are no general exchange-control approvals, conversion quotas, or profit-remittance restrictions applicable to a Vanuatu International Company. Dividends, service fees, royalties, and liquidation proceeds may be freely repatriated once the paying bank has completed standard KYC/AML verification of beneficial ownership, source of funds, and the underlying commercial purpose. The practical frictions are bank-level due diligence and correspondent-bank compliance requirements, not statutory exchange controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio for export or export-service receipts in Vanuatu. BPO call-centre service receipts may be retained 100% in foreign currency or converted into vatu through an authorised dealer. The only requirement is accurate transaction reporting to the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu for balance-of-payments statistics and to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for AML/CFT purposes.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium as a supplementary settlement rail, Low as a substitute for a licensed bank account. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed banks in Vanuatu and cannot provide regulatory capital escrow, VUV-denominated clearing, or local deposit protection. They are legally usable for USD/EUR B2B payables and receivables via virtual IBANs provided the entity maintains a downstream authorised-dealer bank account, records all flows for audit, and never treats the EMI as the sole repository for shareholder capital or client funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder funds must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own bank account to the International Company's account at an RBV-licensed authorised dealer. The payment narrative should state 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / ISSUANCE OF SHARES' and, where a purpose code is required, should be mapped to the receiving bank's balance-of-payments category for equity or direct-investment capital, commonly corresponding to SWIFT ISO purpose 'CFIB' or the bank's internal direct-investment code. The onboarding file must contain the board resolution authorising the share issue, updated share register, share certificates, bank deposit confirmation, statutory declaration of source of funds, and final UBO information. Cash deposits, crypto transfers, and third-party pass-through accounts are not acceptable for capital injection.Operational disbursements for the remote BPO call centre should be cleared through SWIFT MT103 from the licensed VUV/USD operating account for high-value payroll, telephony, software, and corporate payables. Low-value cross-border B2B payments may use Wise B2B or similar compliant EMI rails when the receiving counterparty has a verified business account. A commercial letter of credit is not normally applicable to pure call-centre services; if the corporate group also procures IT or telephony hardware for delivery to clients, a sight documentary credit issued or confirmed by an international bank may be used for the goods portion, while the service component remains a separate SWIFT MT103 remittance.Deliverable VUV/USD forward contracts with an RBV-licensed authorised dealer, limited to contracted BPO receivables and committed operating costs.Cross-currency swaps between VUV and USD with a licensed commercial bank, used only for matching long-term capital or financing cash flows of the International Company.Plain-vanilla FX options with a licensed bank for hedging confirmed portfolio-level exchange-rate exposure, if offered by the dealer.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not generally available onshore; where an offshore bank proposes an NDF, it must be documented as a derivative with no VUV settlement and must be explicitly approved under the company's treasury policy and confirmed as permissible by the onshore authorised dealer.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: 0 VUV — no statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies because Vanuatu does not impose corporate income tax and has no transfer pricing legislation. As a matter of prudent substance documentation, an International Company should nevertheless prepare a contemporaneous Local File and, if part of a multinational group exceeding EUR 750 million consolidated revenue, a Master File and country-by-country report may be required by the ultimate parent jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu does not operate a separate Economic Substance Regulations statute comparable to Caribbean OFC regimes. However, the VFSC and the International Companies Act require an International Company to establish and maintain a registered office, a licensed resident agent, and adequate corporate formality. For an offshore BPO call centre, economic substance should be demonstrated through locally held board meetings, a director or manager exercising real decision-making, invoicing and payment flows administered from Vanuatu, and written service contracts with the foreign BPO client. A bare shell with no office, no records, and no decision-making can be struck off and will create a foreign controlled-foreign-company or substance-shame risk for the ultimate beneficial owners.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Value Added Tax Act and the criminal code, the company may be assessed three times the evaded tax, fined up to VUV 2,000,000, and its directors subjected to imprisonment of up to seven years. VFSC will revoke the International Company's exempt status and may order judicial strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve false returns or fraudulent invoicing risk personal civil restitution for the evaded tax, travel bans preventing departure from Vanuatu, director disqualification for up to ten years, and custodial sentences.
Shell Company / Negligent Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The VFSC may impose striking-off, require liquidation, revoke incorporation, and the courts may declare the company's transactions void against creditors. The company may also be disqualified from banking with VFSC-authorised corporate service providers.

Director personal liability: Directors may be made personally liable for unconscionable distributions, required to restore misapplied company money, disqualified for up to ten years, and placed on a travel ban pending satisfaction of civil judgment.
AML/CFT Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Act, the company may be fined up to VUV 25,000,000, proceeds of crime may be confiscated, and the International Company may be ordered wound up. The VFSC may cancel the registered agent's licence and report the company to the FIU.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors who fail to report suspicious activity are subject to personal fines for each unreported transaction, civil claims for restitution by victims, travel bans, disqualification, and imprisonment.
Unlicensed Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: Carrying on a regulated financial activity without a licence is a criminal offence under the International Companies Act and the Financial Dealers Licensing Act. The court may order immediate cessation, void the offending contracts, disgorge profits, impose fines, and strike off the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors face personal civil liability for investor losses, confiscation of advisory and transaction fees, travel bans pending criminal trial, disqualification, and imprisonment for continued operation of the unlicensed business.
