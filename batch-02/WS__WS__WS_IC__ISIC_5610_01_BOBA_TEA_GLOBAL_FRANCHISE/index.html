Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: A qualifying Samoa International Company is a separate juridical entity that is not a pass-through and is statutorily treated as offshore tax-exempt. All foreign-source income, gains, distributions, and international business activities of the IC are exempt from Samoa income tax and withholding tax. The exemption is territorial and conditional upon the IC not carrying on domestic business in Samoa; any local-source business activity may trigger ordinary entity-level income taxation and forfeiture or restriction of the tax-exempt status.

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election and has no explicit hybrid-mismatch check-the-box regime. The only relevant statutory elections are: (i) the IC's application for and retention of the International Company Tax Exemption Certificate; (ii) voluntary conversion from International Company status to an ordinary domestic company, which ends the Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment; and (iii) voluntary registration for VAGST as an exporter in order to recover input tax while preserving income tax exemption. No group registration, fiscal unity, or consolidated filing election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 (Samoan tala) of taxable supplies in any continuous 12-month period; compulsory registration applies at this threshold, and voluntary registration is available for exporters and other persons carrying on taxable activities

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered IC may apply 0% VAGST only to direct exports of goods or to genuine offshore supplies of services. Goods must be physically exported from Samoa to a foreign destination within the prescribed period, with the risk and benefit in the goods passing to a non-resident purchaser. Offshore services, including international franchise licensing and royalty supplies, are zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident franchisee and the rights granted are used wholly outside Samoa. Supplies to Samoan domestic consumers, local franchisees, or local physical retail outlets remain subject to 15% VAGST. The exemption also does not authorize zero-rating if the IC remains outside the VAGST net; zero-rating is a VAGST-registered exporter mechanism.

  • Customs Export Declaration validated by the Samoa Customs Service
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / Sea Waybill showing the Samoa supplier as exporter and the foreign consignee
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Foreign currency sale contract, franchise agreement, or purchase order evidencing the cross-border supply
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 bank credit evidencing receipt of foreign currency export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin where required by Samoa Customs or the destination customs authority

Samoa does not currently impose mandatory real-time Government fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping for invoices. A registered VAGST person must issue a compliant tax invoice containing the supplier's name and VAGST number, recipient details, date, description of supply, taxable amount, and VAGST charged. Returns are filed through the Ministry of Revenue e-Services portal, while Customs export declarations are processed through the ASYCUDA customs system. No pre-approval or government clearance of individual commercial invoices is required.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to heightened audit scrutiny, particularly for related-party franchise royalty and syrup supply chains. The Ministry of Revenue may reverse a zero-rating determination where the documentation is incomplete, the goods did not physically leave Samoa, the services were consumed or enjoyed in Samoa, or the transaction lacks commercial substance. In such cases all output VAGST becomes payable at 15%, previously credited input VAGST is clawed back, and interest and statutory late-payment and shortfall penalties accrue from the original due date. Fraudulent export or refund claims may lead to criminal prosecution, monetary penalties, imprisonment of responsible directors, suspension or revocation of VAGST registration, forfeiture of customs bonds, and possible loss of the International Company tax-exempt status. Registered exporters must retain the full export evidence bundle for at least six years from the date of supply.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) operates under a managed-float regime with full current-account convertibility for documented trade and service transactions, while capital-account convertibility is restricted. Current-account settlements require underlying contracts/invoices routed through authorized dealers. Capital inflows, including equity injections and intra-group loans, require a Foreign Investment Certificate under the Foreign Investment Act 2020 and CBS exchange control authorization. Profit and dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders is permitted after final dividend withholding tax, audited financial statements, tax clearance, and dividend declaration are submitted; practical hurdles include documentary substantiation, limited WST market depth, and CBS approval of the foreign-currency payment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from the franchise syrup supply chain and royalty-related exports must be repatriated to a licensed Samoan bank within 3 months from the date of shipment under the Exchange Control Regulations 1975. The full FOB value of goods exported must be received in Samoa unless an approved deduction applies. Receipts must be surrendered and converted into WST through an authorized dealer upon credit, unless the CBS grants prior approval to maintain a foreign-currency account. Any shortfall or delayed remittance must be justified with documentary evidence, such as proof of defective goods, re-export, or commercial dispute settlements, to avoid exchange control penalties.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) — administering exchange control under the Exchange Control Act 1972 and the Exchange Control Regulations 1975 (as amended); all foreign exchange transactions require CBS general or specific authority, exercised through licensed authorized dealer banks.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Samoa International Companies (ICs) are statutorily exempt under the International Companies Act 1987 from all Samoan income tax on offshore business income. The general resident corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2012 is 27%, which does not attach to IC franchise royalty, syrup supply, or trademark licensing income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — dividends and other distributions paid by a Samoa International Company to non-resident shareholders are outside the Samoan tax net. For ordinary resident companies the Income Tax Act 2012 imposes a final non-resident dividend withholding tax of 15%, but this is not levied on IC distributions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory WST threshold is published in Samoa under the Income Tax Act 2012 transfer pricing provisions; the arm's length standard applies to resident CIT taxpayers without a mandatory Samoan Local File or Master File filing requirement. The exempt IC has no Samoan TP documentation obligation.
