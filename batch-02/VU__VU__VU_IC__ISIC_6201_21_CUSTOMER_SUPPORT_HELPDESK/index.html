Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital requirement; the International Company may elect authorized share capital in the Articles of Association with no mandatory escrow, capital deposit, or capital duty obligation under the IC Act (Cap. 222), s. 11.

Funding & unlock path: Where a physical office is maintained, obtain a building occupancy permit from the municipal council (Port Vila Municipal Council or Luganville Municipal Council) following a structural inspection; secure fire safety certification from the Vanuatu Police Force Fire and Rescue Services covering means of egress, extinguishers, and emergency lighting; high-tech electrical installations require a wiring certificate per Electricity Act licensing standards; workplaces with employees must register with the Department of Labour and display a Certificate of Compliance under the Employment Act and be covered by the Workmen's Compensation Act; no explosives or hazardous processes clearance is applicable.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Customs and Inland Revenue Department (CIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Government of Vanuatu; regulatory oversight of offshore International Companies is with the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Tax and customs administration is executed through the ASYCUDA World single-window portal for customs and the CIRD VAT registry for domestic and importation purposes.

E-government filing portal

An International Company (IC) is incorporated under the International Companies Act [CAP 222] and receives a unique VFSC Company Registration Number, which serves as its statutory tax identifier. The IC does not separately register for VAT or obtain an inland revenue TIN because it is statutorily exempt and prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents. To evidence tax-exempt status for banking and contracting counterparties, the IC requests from VFSC a Certificate of Good Standing and a Tax Exemption Certificate. If, contrary to the offshore profile, the IC or a related domestic vehicle makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu above the VAT threshold, it must submit Form VR-1 (VAT Registration Application) to the CIRD within 14 days, provide constitutional documents, evidence of place of business, and a resident tax representative, and will then be issued a VAT registration number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Vanuatu; exemption is mandatory and automatic for an IC under the International Companies Act, which provides that an IC and its operations, securities, and transactions are exempt from all taxes, duties, and charges. A foreign-owned entity may instead elect to incorporate as a domestic company, but Vanuatu currently imposes no income tax on domestic companies other than VAT and customs obligations. A non-resident supplier with Vanuatu taxable supplies may elect voluntary VAT registration on or after crossing the VUV 4,000,000 threshold; no hybrid mismatch or BEPS-related elections are operative in Vanuatu.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period (threshold in force as revised under the 2025 Budget Act; registration compulsory if exceeded; voluntary registration permitted below threshold).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) the export of goods physically leaving Vanuatu evidenced by a customs export declaration; and (ii) the supply of services to a non-resident recipient where the benefit of the services is received outside Vanuatu, including offshore helpdesk and customer-support SaaS supplied by an International Company to foreign clients. The IC’s cross-border omni-channel helpdesk and conversational-bot cloud services qualify as exported services under Section 7 of the VAT Act. The supplier must hold written contracts, itemised service invoices, delivery/usage evidence, and payment records showing foreign currency settlement to substantiate the offshore supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (lodged via ASYCUDA World, approved exit confirmation)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for goods; for pure services, digital service delivery log and offshore recipient confirmation)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/payment evidence proving receipt of export proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate exists in Vanuatu as of this stage. VAT-registered businesses may issue tax invoices in approved paper or PDF formats containing prescribed particulars (seller name and VAT number, buyer name, invoice date, description, VAT-exclusive amount, VAT rate, and VAT amount). Customs exports are processed exclusively through ASYCUDA World, which requires electronic lodgment of export declarations and supporting digital attachments. CIRD has publicly indicated a phased modernisation roadmap toward electronic reporting, but no mandatory clearance or e-invoice validation requirement is yet operational.

CIRD may scrutinise export zero-rating claims up to four years retrospectively. Where zero-rating is claimed fraudulently or without sufficient export evidence, the VAT Act imposes a dishonesty penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, plus interest, and criminal conviction may result in fines and imprisonment. Refunds already paid are clawed back with statutory interest, and repeated offending may trigger registration cancellation, disqualification of directors, and referral for tax evasion prosecution under the criminal code. For an International Company, loss of the statutory exemption or breach of the non-resident business prohibition can additionally trigger severe administrative penalties by VFSC, including strike-off and forfeiture of the offshore licence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has no exchange-control legislation in force and the Vatu (VUV) is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts, reflecting Vanuatu's acceptance of IMF Article VIII obligations; no approval, licence, or remittance restriction attaches to dividend distribution, profit repatriation, loan repayment, or capital disposal by International Companies. The RBV operates a managed-float arrangement that smooths the VUV against a trade-weighted basket of partner currencies. Practical convertibility is constrained only by thin offshore VUV liquidity, so settlement flows are executed through USD, AUD, NZD, or EUR correspondent channels. The FATF increased-monitoring designation raises bank-level due-diligence friction but creates no statutory convertibility or repatriation limits.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Vanuatu imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation obligation for export proceeds. SaaS and helpdesk service-export receipts may be retained offshore, swept into a Vanuatu licensed-bank account, or held in foreign currency indefinitely, subject only to ordinary AML/CFT record-keeping, sanctions screening, and FIU reporting thresholds under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 11 of 2018.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125]; regulates banks, foreign-exchange dealers, and money remitters under the Financial Institutions Act No. 6 of 1999, and houses the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) for AML/CFT supervision. International Companies are registered and supervised by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) under the International Companies Act [Cap 222].

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for offshore digital EMIs. Vanuatu law does not prohibit foreign EMI servicing and no domestic EMI licensing regime exists; the binding constraints are correspondent-bank risk appetite (FATF increased-monitoring status), thin VUV clearing, and EMI country matrices, which generally exclude Vanuatu-registered International Companies (Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, and Payoneer typically decline). Workable treasury architecture: Stripe Billing and PayPal serve as customer-facing collection gateways, with merchant settlement in USD into the Tier-1 Vanuatu bank account; where Stripe's supported-country matrix does not directly admit a VU International Company, interpose a US (Stripe Atlas) or EU merchant-of-record entity that re-invoices the VU IC for the helpdesk SaaS. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing law or mandatory Local File / Master File documentation threshold exists in Vanuatu.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has no local Economic Substance Regulations (ESR); International Companies must maintain a registered office and local agent, and should demonstrate commercial substance where subjected to foreign anti-avoidance scrutiny.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Foreign tax penalties, criminal prosecution, and civil fraud assessments in the taxpayer's home jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable for restitution, travel bans, and imprisonment under applicable foreign tax evasion statutes.
Shell Company Abusive Structures

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, recharacterization of income, and global anti-base-erosion penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil restitution orders and personal assessments when the corporate veil is pierced.
AML Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu AML/CFT fines up to VUV 1,000,000 per breach and suspension of any associated license.

Director personal liability: Directors may be subject to personal fines, travel bans, and imprisonment for wilful AML breaches.
