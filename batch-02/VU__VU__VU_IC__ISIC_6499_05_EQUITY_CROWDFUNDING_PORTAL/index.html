Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is required under the International Companies Act for an International Company; the constitution may state share capital without par value, and subscription moneys need not be escrowed at incorporation. If the proposed entity later holds an equity crowdfunding licence, the VFSC may impose a separate paid-up capital condition at licensing stage.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Vanuatu Fire and Emergency Service for the registered office floor; Certificate of Occupancy and structural load assessment from the Department of Public Works verifying wind and seismic load over server/record storage areas; and occupational health and safety clearance from the Department of Labour, including first-aid provision, emergency exit signage, worker registration and a workplace health and safety policy.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Customs and Inland Revenue Department (VCIRD), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: https://www.customs.gov.vu (Customs & Inland Revenue). Additional registration portal for International Companies: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu does not maintain a general national Tax Identification Number system because there is no income tax on non-resident-owned international business. A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is registered with VFSC and receives a VFSC registration number, which serves as the corporate identifier. If the IC becomes a VAT registrant (either mandatory upon taxable turnover exceeding VT 5,000,000 in any 12-month period or voluntarily), it must apply to VCIRD on Form VAT-REG-01, attaching the VFSC company extract, certificate of incorporation / registration, memorandum and articles of association, proof of registered office in Vanuatu, identity documents of directors/shareholders, bank account confirmation, and a description of the digital platform activity. After approval, VCIRD issues a VAT Registration Number (VRN) in a standard 8-digit format. For physical imports/exports, the IC must also obtain a Customs Identifier Code from VCIRD customs; no separate tax ID is otherwise required.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Vanuatu does not maintain a general national Tax Identification Number system because there is no income tax on non-resident-owned international business. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election mechanism. An International Company under the International Companies Act Cap. 222 is conclusively exempt from all national taxes, including income tax, capital gains tax, value added tax, and imposts on foreign-sourced transactions, unless it chooses to voluntarily register for VAT to recover input tax. No pass-through election exists. The IC cannot be disregarded for tax purposes; it is a taxable person only for the narrow purpose of an optional VAT registration. There are no statutory or administrative mechanisms to elect entity-level taxation or to trigger Cló version hybrid treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million Vanuatu Vatu) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is permitted. For an exempt International Company engaged solely in offshore equity crowdfunding intermediation, taxable supplies in Vanuatu are generally absent or are zero-rated export services, so the threshold is not practically triggered unless the IC voluntarily registers for VAT or carries on a domestic supply in Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under Section 21 of the VAT Act 2018 for: (a) goods exported or transferred from Vanuatu to a place outside Vanuatu; (b) services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply, where the direct benefit of the services is received outside Vanuatu; and (c) certain international transport and ancillary supplies. An equity crowdfunding portal operated by a Vanuatu International Company is eligible to treat the platform commissions and fees charged to non-resident issuers and investors as zero-rated export services, provided the services are not attributable to any taxable activity carried on inside Vanuatu and the IC maintains evidence that the customer is non-resident and the benefit of the service is enjoyed offshore. The exemption under the IC Act may also place the supplies wholly outside the VAT net; voluntary VAT registration allows zero-rated turnover to be reported and any attributable input VAT refunded.

  • VU Customs Export Declaration (Customs Form C1A) for physical goods, if applicable
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / express courier delivery evidence for exported goods
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List with full description, quantities, values, and export destination
  • For digital services: written service contract between the crowdfunding portal and the non-resident issuer/investor, evidencing offshore supply and offshore direct benefit
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit/transfer record evidencing receipt of the export consideration in foreign currency (for AML and record-keeping purposes; not a statutory tax requirement in Vanuatu but conventionally retained for a 180-day compliance window)

Vanuatu has no mandatory e-invoicing, real-time tax clearance, digital stamping, or fiscal API regime as of the current legislation. VAT-registered persons must issue sequential tax invoices containing the vendor's name and address, VRN, invoice date, supply description, amount excluding VAT, VAT amount, and the zero-rating basis if applicable. Returns are filed electronically through the VCIRD taxpayer portal on a monthly or quarterly basis as directed by VCIRD. No fiscal drilling or electronic administrative pre-clearance is required; however, all records must be retained for six years for audit.

Vanuatu's VAT administration can raise assessment for uncollected output VAT within five years from the relevant tax return; where fraud or deliberate suppression is shown, there is no limitation period. If zero-rating is claimed without satisfactory export/customer evidence, VCIRD will claw back the unpaid VAT at the standard 12.5% rate and impose default interest of 1% per month, plus a civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax shortfall. Willful evasion is a criminal offence carrying fines up to VT 5,000,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years. For physical export misdeclaration under the Customs Act Cap. 256, the goods are liable to forfeiture, and the exporter may be penalized in an amount up to triple the duty evaded, with cancellation of concessions. In addition, abuse of the International Company tax-exempt status through onshore or prohibited business triggers deregistration by VFSC, revocation of the international company licence, and penalties up to VT 10,000,000 or imprisonment for directors under the International Companies Act Cap. 222.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully liberalised de jure and de facto regime. The Vanuatu Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for residents and non-residents on both current and capital account. Vanuatu abolished exchange controls in the 1980s; no RBV approval, registration, license, or repatriation permit is required for shareholder capital subscriptions, crowdfunding investor transfers, dividend or profit remittances, liquidation proceeds, or any other cross-border payment involving an International Company (IC). The VUV operates on a managed-float basis linked to an undisclosed basket weighted toward USD, AUD, EUR, JPY, and NZD. The IC may hold unlimited multi-currency accounts (VUV, USD, AUD, EUR) and convert between currencies without restriction. Residual practical friction is confined to commercial KYC and correspondent-bank screening; the FATF grey-list status (2021-2024) has been delisted and does not constrain convertibility. No tax-clearance, pre-remittance approval, or beneficiary declaration is required by statute.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil statutory regime. Vanuatu imposes no surrender quota, no mandatory conversion ratio, no repatriation timeframe, and no designated account domiciling requirement for export proceeds, service revenues, or crowdfunding facilitation fees. All foreign currency receipts may be retained indefinitely in VUV, USD, AUD, or EUR accounts at the portal's discretion. The RBV maintains no exchange-control powers that would apply to International Companies, and the IC enjoys the same non-application of any would-be surrender obligation as domestic investors. No balance-of-payments reporting is triggered by the portal's fee income.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV), established under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap. 125], acts as the central bank, prudential regulator of all licensed domestic commercial banks, and operator of the national RTGS and payments infrastructure. Concurrent supervisory authority over the equity crowdfunding portal is exercised by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) under the Securities Dealers (Licensing) Act [Cap. 70], the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 9 of 2022 (AML/CTF Act). The RBV has no exchange-control department and does not administer capital movement restrictions. The VFSC additionally oversees the portal's client-money trust account regime and AML/CFT compliance, including fit-and-proper reporting obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Moderate-Low viability. Vanuatu does not operate a dedicated e-money institution (EMI) licensing category; the VFSC does not recognise offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Currencycloud, Airwallex, Revolut Business) as compliant custodians of crowdfunding client money. Using an EMI as the principal collection or escrow rail would breach the VFSC clients'-money trust rules and the deposit-taking prohibitions of the Banking Act. An offshore EMI may be used exclusively for the portal's own operating expenses (software, marketing, hosting) and must be fully segregated from investor capital. The compliant construct is a licensed Vanuatu commercial bank holding the Capital Escrow Account, with an EMI employed only as a supplementary low-value expense account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All crowdfunding subscriptions must be routed via SWIFT MT103 into the statutory Clients' Money Trust Account (Capital Escrow Account) held at ANZ Bank (Vanuatu) Limited, beneficiary '[Portal Name] Investor Client Trust Account', USD account, BIC ANZBVUVX. The subscribing investor's bank must include the unique investor reference code (format VU-IC-000123-CSF) in field 70 (Remittance Information); field 72 must contain only 'BEN' for beneficiary clearing instructions. USD payments from the United States may clear through the ANZ New York correspondent (ANZBUS33) or via a domestic US bank or fintech only if funds are pre-segregated and the onward transfer references the investor code. VUV-denominated domestic transfers settle through the RBV RTGS in near real-time. Verification protocol: dual signatory release (AML Compliance Officer plus one director) is required before any escrow-to-operating transfer; investor funds may never be co-mingled with operating balances; daily reconciliation against the trust deed and VFSC reporting calendar must be maintained. No RBV exchange-control approval or filing form is required for inbound capital.Outbound disbursements from escrow to investee companies settle via three clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 (default) for equity funding, milestone payments, and distribution of returns, with the escrow release instruction number quoted in field 70; (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP600, irrevocable, at-sight) where the investee requires documentary trade settlement; (iii) Vanuatu RTGS VUV transfers for on-island disbursements. USD payments clear via ANZ New York or ANZ Sydney USD clearing accounts; AUD via RITS; EUR via the BRED/BPCE Paris network. Each disbursement must be pre-authorised by an investment committee minute compliant with VFSC conditions. Standard bank-imposed limits: USD 1,000,000 per day per beneficiary without extended financial crime review; larger sums require 24-hour prior no-objection from the bank's financial crime unit. No exchange-control approval and no tax clearance certificate is required under Vanuatu law for International Company outward remittances.Outright deliverable foreign exchange forwards (30, 90, 180, and 360-day tenors) in VUV/USD, VUV/AUD, VUV/EUR, and VUV/JPY quoted by ANZ, BRED, and BSP for hedging operating expenses and escrow milestone obligations.Offshore Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) on the VUV executed through Singapore or Hong Kong desks of international banks, used when traditional deliverable VUV liquidity is insufficient for the portal's hedging requirements.Cross-currency swaps (VUV/USD and VUV/AUD) with Reserve Bank of Vanuatu-authorised commercial banks, suitable for long-dated exposure matching escrow liability duration.Strategic multi-currency sinking-fund deposits in USD, AUD, and VUV enabling the portal to avoid proprietary FX risk while maintaining budgeted VUV conversion corridors with stop-loss discipline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: N/A - Vanuatu has not enacted a transfer pricing documentation statute, and no Local File or Master File threshold in Vanuatu Vatu exists. The arm's length principle is nonetheless applied as a soft-law standard where the IC transacts with related parties in jurisdictions that enforce OECD transfer pricing rules.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations apply to a Vanuatu International Company earning income from relevant activities. Equity crowdfunding portal operation falls within financial intermediation/financing. The IC must demonstrate in Vanuatu: adequate physical premises, proportionate full-time local employees, real operating expenditure, and board management/control. A passive booking entity with no local substance will fail the ESR assessment and become subject to penalty and strike-off action initiated by the VFSC.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unlicensed Crowdfunding Activity

Statutory sanction: Criminal offence under the International Companies Act 1992 and Vanuatu licensing legislation: fine up to VUV 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 6 months; the VFSC may strike off the IC, freeze escrow accounts, and restrain bank wires.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for investor losses; the corporate veil is pierced for deliberate unlicensed trading. Directors face civil restitution orders, court-issued travel bans preventing departure from Vanuatu, and custodial sentences.
Tax Evasion and Offshore Fraud

Statutory sanction: Vanuatu applies mutual legal assistance and information-exchange instruments enabling foreign revenue authorities to obtain books, bank records, and UBO data; false declarations to the VFSC attract fines up to VUV 500,000 with escalation, and confiscation orders may attach to escrow balances.

Director personal liability: Fraud and false-pretence charges permit full veil piercing; directors may be ordered to make civil restitution of all misdirected funds, subjected to travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and imprisoned for up to 7 years under the Vanuatu Penal Code.
AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Financial Transactions Reporting Act 2018: corporate fines up to VUV 25,000,000; individual fines up to VUV 10,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering; VFSC licence revocation and targeted financial sanctions may follow.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally accountable for systemic AML failure; civil restitution to defrauded investors is ordered, travel bans are imposed while investigations are pending, and imprisonment applies upon conviction for laundering crowdfunding proceeds.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Evasion

Statutory sanction: Economic substance non-compliance triggers a fixed penalty of VUV 500,000 plus a daily compounding penalty of VUV 50,000; continuing default leads to strike-off, forfeiture of the registered agent's services, and deregistration of the IC.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for knowingly filing false ESR declarations; the corporate veil is pierced for unpaid regulatory penalties and creditor claims. Courts may issue travel bans to secure attendance, and directors face disqualification and imprisonment for false statements.
Investor Fraud and Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Theft and false-pretence offences under the Vanuatu Penal Code: imprisonment up to 7 years per offence; fraudulent trading and breach of the Financial Dealers Act expose the company to restitution orders, winding-up, and seizure of assets held in escrow accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or authorised fraudulent campaign materials become personally and criminally liable; civil restitution to every victim is mandatory, travel bans are issued upon charge, and imprisonment is imposed on conviction, with no possibility of corporate veil protection.
