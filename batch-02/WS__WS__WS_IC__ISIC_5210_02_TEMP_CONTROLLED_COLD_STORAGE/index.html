Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

The statutory registered office of an International Company must be a physical Samoan address, being the office of a licensed international company agent, where statutory records and service of process are maintained (International Companies Act 2014, Section 15); a purely virtual or mailbox office is not permissible as the statutory registered office. Virtual addresses remain acceptable only as non-statutory correspondence addresses. For carrying on a cold-storage trade in Samoa, the holder of the business license must also maintain operational premises (owned or leased), which may be satisfied through a properly documented third-party 3PL refrigerated warehouse tenancy agreement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), Government of Samoa; official taxpayer portal: https://www.revenue.gov.ws and self-service e-services at https://taxpayer.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign parent establishing a Samoa International Company (IC) must first incorporate through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL) and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation and an International Company Licence, then apply to the Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA) for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using Form TIN-01 supported by certified copies of the Certificate of Incorporation, International Company Licence, Memorandum and Articles of Association, directors' and shareholders' identity documents, and a statement of Samoa-registered office address. The IC is not placed on the ordinary resident company register for income tax where it is non-resident-owned and conducts no business with Samoa residents. Upon TIN issuance, the IC may be separately assessed for Goods and Services Tax (GST/VAGST) registration only if it makes taxable supplies in Samoa; an offshore-only IC is normally outside the GST registration net.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Samoa does not maintain a check-the-box or US-style classification election. The sole relevant mechanism is the statutory status of an International Company under the International Companies Act 1987, which electively exempts the entity from income tax, stamp duties, and exchange control on offshore income, and provides that GST (VAGST) does not apply to offshore supplies made by an IC. There is no hybrid mismatch election; ICs are instead required to comply with the substance and economic presence conditions of the Samoa International Companies regime and may voluntarily apply for GST registration only if engaging in taxable domestic supplies, but such application is commercially disfavoured because domestic GST registration would dilute the IC's offshore tax-exempt status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration is triggered when taxable turnover exceeds or is likely to exceed WST 60,000 in any consecutive 12-month period; registration is voluntary below the threshold, and an International Company conducting exclusively offshore or exempt supplies is outside the scope of the GST Act.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods supplied and genuinely exported from Samoa (including cold-chain pharmaceuticals), as evidenced by removal from Samoa within the statutory period, and to internationally supplied services rendered to non-resident recipients where the benefit is consumed outside Samoa and the supply does not relate to Samoan land or goods. For a Samoa International Company, offshore storage, logistics, and cold-chain services supplied wholly outside Samoa to non-resident pharmaceutical principals fall entirely outside the GST net rather than being merely zero-rated; claims for zero-rating refunds are reserved for GST-registered operators and require full contemporaneous evidence that the goods were exported, the recipient is a non-resident, and consideration was paid by a non-resident from offshore funds.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged with Samoa Revenue Authority Customs Division (duly certified by Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Samoa
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 credit advice proving receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Signed contract or purchase order identifying the non-resident principal and end-destination
  • Cold-chain temperature monitoring logs and certification for perishable pharmaceutical cargo integrity

Samoa does not operate a real-time fiscal e-invoicing clearance, API-based clearance, or digital stamp model (no equivalents of Peru-CPT, Chile-DTE, or Saudi-ZATCA). GST-registered persons must issue sequential tax invoices in paper or electronic format containing required statutory particulars: seller name and TIN, buyer name and TIN (where registered), invoice number, date, description, quantity, VAT-exclusive amounts, the 15% VAGST calculation, and total amount. SRA-prescribed formats apply, with record-keeping obligations for at least seven years; e-invoicing through SRA's e-Tax portal is accepted as an optional filing mechanism only.

SRA conducts post-clearance and tax audits of export zero-rating claims with emphasis on documentary authenticity, physical export verification, and repatriation of proceeds within 180 days. Failure to substantiate a zero-rated export or wrongful zero-rating gives rise to retrospective GST clawback at 15% plus penalty tax of 50% of the understated GST, escalating to 100% where the failure is deliberate, and monthly late-payment interest of 2% per month on the unpaid amount. Fraudulent or sham export arrangements attract criminal penalties under the Tax Administration Act 2012 of up to WST 100,000 fines and imprisonment up to three years, and the revenue authority may apply a six-year retrospective assessment period for evasion; directors and controlling partners are jointly liable in cases of connected-party export schemes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is pegged to an undisclosed basket comprising NZD, USD, AUD and EUR; there is no offshore deliverable WST market. Current-account payments are freely convertible through authorized dealers with documentary support, whereas capital-account transactions require prior Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) approval. WS_IC, as a non-resident International Company, may receive foreign-currency inflows and hold USD/EUR/AUD/NZD accounts without mandatory conversion, but any local-resident WST activity would trigger exchange-control classification and potentially alter non-resident status. Profit and capital remittances to non-resident shareholders require the Samoan Ministry of Revenue Tax Exemption Certificate, SIFA good-standing confirmation, and audited or reviewed accounts substantiating the distribution source.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must repatriate all export and invisible earnings within 30 days of receipt and surrender 100% of proceeds to an authorized dealer in WST unless a CBS retention-account exemption has been granted. WS_IC's cold-storage service fees constitute non-resident invisible export earnings; no mandatory surrender or conversion applies provided the authorized dealer holds signed storage-service contracts, invoiced debit notes, and an annual non-resident status declaration filed with SIFA and CBS. All supporting records, including waybills, temperature logs, and customer reconciliations, must be retained for at least five years for exchange-control audit.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutonu o Samoa) and its Exchange Control and Financial Institutions Supervision Department, operating under the Central Bank of Samoa Act 2016, Exchange Control Act 1973, Exchange Control Regulations 1971 (as amended), Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007, and Financial Institutions Act 1996.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Samoa's Financial Institutions Act 1996 reserves deposit-taking, remittance, and payment services to CBS-licensed institutions; offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer and Airwallex have no licensed branch presence in Samoa. Samoan authorized dealers will generally reject correspondent flows from unlicensed EMIs, and the CBS Exchange Control regime requires all IC treasury activity to flow through licensed authorized dealers. EMIs may be used at most as ancillary receivable-collection gateways if the EMI maintains a licensed correspondent bank relationship and all balances are swept daily to a tier-1 Samoa account, with prior CBS non-objection obtained.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital is routed via SWIFT MT103/MT202 to WS_IC's account at an authorized dealer, e.g., ANZ Bank (Samoa) Limited (ANZBWSSW) or BSP Samoa (BOSPSWSW), with purpose narrative INVCAP or LOAN and mandatory reference 'WS_IC [registration number] Capital Injection'. The authorized dealer must receive a board resolution authorizing the injection, shareholder identification and source-of-funds statements before crediting. No WST conversion applies to non-resident IC accounts; share capital and shareholder loans are reportable through the dealer's periodic CBS exchange-control returns, with loans exceeding WST 1,000,000 equivalent requiring dealer-level large-value notification to the CBS.Primary clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for customer transfers, MT202 for interbank cover, MT700/MT707 for commercial letters of credit, and the Samoa National Clearing System for domestic WST payments such as utilities, payroll and local warehouse fees. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Company (International Companies Act 1998, s.224 exemption from all Samoan income tax, stamp duty and exchange control); 25% standard corporate rate under the Income Tax Ordinance 2015 where residency or a Samoan business nexus arises

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% final withholding tax on dividends paid by a Samoan resident company to a non-resident; dividends declared by a Samoa International Company are outside the Samoa tax net (no WHT) by force of the ICA 1998 exemption.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 30 days after each anniversary of the date of incorporation. Failure to lodge within the filing window triggers late fees and strike-off exposure.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Samoan fiscal year ends 30 June; the return must be lodged within 3 months (by 30 September) after that date under self-assessment, with payment of any balance due at that time.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Samoa has no published statutory Local File or Master File monetary threshold. Compliance benchmark applied by the Ministry of Revenue: WST 1,000,000 aggregate annual related-party turnover (approximately USD 370,000) triggers the expectation of a benchmark analysis, intercompany agreements and substance evidence for cold-storage service flows.

Economic substance scope: Samoa aligns with OECD and Code of Conduct substance standards: ICs carrying on relevant activities (headquarters, distribution and service centers, financing, IP holding, insurance, banking, shipping, pure equity holding) must file a substance notification and demonstrate core income-generating activities in their jurisdiction of tax residence. Warehousing and logistics is not a listed relevant activity, but a pure equity-holding IC that owns the cold-storage operating subsidiary must file the reduced holding-company substance declaration and maintain the registered office, agent and records in Samoa. The exemption does not protect the IC from substance or CFC rules in the operating jurisdiction, and Samoa's automatic exchange of information will disclose ownership to the beneficial owner's home tax authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion - misuse of the International Company exemption

Statutory sanction: Withdrawal of exemption, reassessment at 25% corporate income tax on the undeclared Samoa-source income, penalty tax up to 100% of the shortfall, interest, forfeiture of tainted assets and criminal fines under the Income Tax Ordinance 2015 and the Tax Administration Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution and joint and several assessment of directors for unpaid tax where the exemption claim was fraudulent; departure prohibition orders for tax debtors; disqualification from directorships; imprisonment up to 5 years on indictment for evasion.
