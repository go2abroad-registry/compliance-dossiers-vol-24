Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), a directorate within the Department of Customs and Inland Revenue, Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official e-services portal: https://www.customsinlandrevenue.gov.vu and ASYCUDA World customs e-declaration portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Vanuatu International Company (IC) is incorporated under the International Companies Act 1992 through the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No domestic income tax TIN is required because the IC is a non-resident tax-exempt entity. If the IC makes taxable supplies in Vanuatu exceeding the VAT registration threshold, it must apply to VRC for VAT registration using Form VAT-01. A licensed resident agent and notarised corporate documents (certificate of incorporation, charter, memorandum, resolutions) are submitted; VRC issues a Tax Identification Number (VAT/TRN) upon approval. The IC must also obtain a Business Licence from the Vanuatu Investment Promotion Authority if carrying on any permitted local activity, and maintain a registered office and agent in Vanuatu.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Vanuatu International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and all domestic direct taxes. It is not treated as a pass-through or resident entity; its income is taxed in no jurisdiction unless the foreign owner is taxable locally in another jurisdiction. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Vanuatu International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and all domestic direct taxes. It is not treated as a pass-through or resident entity; its income is taxed in no jurisdiction unless the foreign owner is taxable locally in another jurisdiction. Scope is territorial: no Vanuatu-source taxation is levied; the IC may operate exclusively offshore.

Available election forms

Vanuatu does not maintain a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. An IC’s tax-exempt classification is automatic and mandatory under the International Companies Act. The only available election is to convert the entity into a domestic company under the Vanuatu Companies and Business Registration Act; such conversion subjects the entity to local business licensing and VAT rules where applicable but still does not impose income tax because Vanuatu does not levy a national profit tax. No fiscal transparency or pass-through election is available for ICs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VUV 4,000,000 (approximately USD 35,000) in taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months; a non-resident IC making taxable supplies in Vanuatu above this threshold is required to register for VAT and appoint a resident fiscal agent.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Vanuatu are zero-rated for VAT where the supplier obtains and retains acceptable proof of export, including customs clearance evidencing that the goods were shipped to a non-resident destination within applicable statutory time limits. Services supplied to non-resident persons are zero-rated where the services are performed outside Vanuatu, are effectively consumed offshore, and the recipient is not a resident of Vanuatu. An International Company engaged in offshore private-equity buyout advisory, syndication structuring, and loan facilitation may zero-rate its management and agency services to non-resident funds and holding companies provided those recipients derive no benefit from the supply inside Vanuatu. All zero-rated transactions must be declared in the VAT return with full documentation maintained for audit.

  • Customs Export Declaration (approved by Vanuatu Customs, ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 confirming repatriation of export proceeds
  • Original zero-rated VAT invoice issued by the supplier
  • Export contract, purchase order, or services agreement demonstrating the non-resident status of the recipient and offshore destination of the supply

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based digital stamping, or mandatory e-invoicing clearance regime is currently operational in Vanuatu. VAT-registered persons must issue compliant VAT invoices containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and TRN, recipient details, date, description, quantity, value per unit, total excluding VAT, VAT amount and rate). Export declarations are processed electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs system. The VRC has published a draft Standard Audit File for Tax (SAF-T) framework, but mandatory submission and cryptographic fiscal marking are not yet enacted.

The VRC conducts post-clearance and refund audits of zero-rating claims. Supporting documents are scrutinised for authenticity, weight of evidence, and consistency with shipping and customs records. Submission of forged, false or misleading export documentation or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the mandated period will result in: (i) reversal of the zero-rated treatment; (ii) assessment at the standard rate of 12.5% VAT; (iii) interest for late payment; (iv) civil penalties of 100% to 200% of the unpaid tax; (v) criminal prosecution for tax evasion under the Value Added Tax Act and Customs Act, punishable by fines and imprisonment; and (vi) revocation of the International Company's licence by the VFSC for serious non-compliance. Any refund or credit previously allowed is immediately clawed back with statutory interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations; the Vatu (VUV) is freely convertible for bona fide current-account transactions, and Vanuatu does not operate a restrictive capital-control schedule. In practice, the RBV manages the VUV against a trade-weighted basket and requires authorised dealers to file exchange-control notifications where any single cross-border capital flow exceeds VUV 50,000,000 (approx. USD 420,000), including equity injections, syndicated loan draw-downs, redemption proceeds and dividend repatriations. The tax-exempt, non-resident International Company faces no statutory prohibition on maintaining USD, EUR or AUD-denominated accounts, and profit remittance to offshore shareholders is free of Vanuatu withholding tax and requires no RBV permit; the operative hurdles are bank-level: source-of-funds evidence for the sponsor PE fund and each LP, UBO declarations, sanctions screening, and consistency of payment purpose across all wiring records. Capital-account flows therefore clear on a documented-transaction basis rather than a prior-approval basis, making the convertibility regime favourable but documentary in nature.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 60] and RBV exchange-control notices, a resident exporter of goods or services from Vanuatu must take all reasonable steps to obtain payment within 180 days of export, and any foreign currency received must be offered for sale to an authorised dealer within three months of receipt; the standard mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds is 100%, subject only to a written RBV retention allowance for approved import, debt-service or other current-account payments. These surrender obligations attach exclusively to 'residents'. The PE Buyout SPV is structured as a non-resident International Company and must preserve that status by (i) not contracting for Vanuatu-origin goods or services for its own account, (ii) keeping its bank-ledger activity in foreign-currency operating accounts with no VUV conversion of incidental receipts, and (iii) declaring non-resident status on every onboarding and transaction form. If the SPV were inadvertently treated as resident, 100% surrender of any Vanuatu-export-derived proceeds within three months would apply.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) under the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu Act [Cap 125]; the RBV is the sole monetary authority and operational administrator of exchange control under the Exchange Control Act [Cap 60], with day-to-day execution delegated to authorised dealer banks. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) separately licenses and supervises the International Company under the International Companies Act [Cap 222]; all treasury operations must be conducted through an RBV-licensed authorised dealer and in the exact legal name of the VFSC-registered International Company.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Viability: Low-to-Medium as a secondary rail; unsuitable as the primary treasury backbone. Vanuatu has no dedicated EMI or digital-bank licensing category; money-transmission activity requires registration with the RBV under the Financial Institutions Act (Cap 34), and the VFSC does not issue e-money licences. Offshore EMIs cannot clear VUV, cannot issue Vanuatu IBANs, and are not recognised by the RBV for exchange-control or prudential purposes. Their USD wires also trigger enhanced source-of-funds review at the receiving Vanuatu tier-1 bank because the SWIFT originator-information field may display the EMI rather than the originating LP, creating an FATF Recommendation 16 documentation gap. Legal constraints: an offshore EMI cannot hold the SPV's escrow funds, issue documentary credits, or provide the syndicated-loan settlement rails required for a leveraged buyout closing. Prescribed deployment: hold no more than 10% of SPV liquidity at any EMI, use it only for LP distributions below USD 50,000 per wire, and never route capital-call proceeds or acquisition consideration through it.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All investor equity and syndicated-loan draw-downs must enter through the SPV's USD or VUV account at an RBV-licensed authorised dealer (ANZ or BSP). Protocol: (i) issue a capital-call notice with a unique reference, e.g. 'CC-2025-001', stating the SPV's account particulars and contact desk; (ii) the LP or the syndicate facility agent remits by SWIFT MT103 with Field 50K showing the exact originator name as it appears in the subscription agreement, Field 59 the SPV's styled account, and Field 70 containing either 'INVESTOR EQUITY CONTRIBUTION - PRIVATE EQUITY BUYOUT SPV' or 'SYNDICATED LOAN DRAW-DOWN - LBO FINANCING', followed by the capital-call number; (iii) because Vanuatu does not operate national remittance purpose codes, the authorised dealer's AML engine reads Field 70, so purpose text must be uniform, dated and capable of reconciliation against the capital-call register; (iv) any single wire above VUV 50,000,000 (approx. USD 420,000) triggers an RBV capital-account notification and the bank may request a copy of the executed subscription or facility agreement; (v) funds are credited provisionally until escrow verification completes, consisting of OFAC/UN/EU sanctions screening, UBO re-match and source-of-funds verification against the subscription schedule. A statutory non-resident declaration is required; no Foreign Investment Certificate applies because the IC carries on no business in Vanuatu. The relationship manager must be pre-advised of the incoming amount, originator name and purpose to prevent temporary freezes.Clearing rails are segregated by instrument type: (1) Acquisition consideration: SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer to the seller's named beneficiary account with Field 70 citing 'DEBT/EQUITY CONSIDERATION FOR ACQUISITION OF [TARGET]', supported by executed board minutes and the notarised transaction document; amounts above USD 1,000,000 route via MT202 COVER to compress the correspondent-bank chain. (2) Acquisition of a Vanuatu-registered target: settlement must be made in VUV through the domestic electronic clearing and RBV settlement facility; the USD leg is converted by the authorised dealer at the same-day RBV reference rate. (3) Cross-border trade components or seller-financed working-capital advances: irrevocable sight letters of credit under UCP600 (SWIFT MT700) issued by the Vanuatu bank and advised through the beneficiary's bank; standby letters of credit (SWIFT MT760) available for take-out and completion guarantees. (4) LP distributions: monthly or quarterly SWIFT MT103 batches with purpose 'DISTRIBUTION TO LIMITED PARTNERS'; every disbursement requires a board resolution and the dual-signatory mandate (director plus VFSC-registered agent). All outbound wires must be pre-notified to the relationship manager with the executed transaction file attached; otherwise the bank may file a suspicious-transaction report under the Financial Transactions Reporting Act.Deliverable USD/VUV forward contracts with tenors up to 12 months, quoted by RBV-licensed authorised dealers, requiring a documented underlying acquisition or funding exposure; aggregated contract notional above VUV 50,000,000 triggers an exchange-control notification.Cross-currency swaps (USD/VUV, USD/AUD or USD/EUR) executed under an ISDA Master Agreement with a Vanuatu authorised dealer, subject to RBV prior approval where the swap notional forms part of the capital-account financing of the leveraged buyout and exceeds the VUV 50,000,000 reporting threshold.European-style FX options (calls on USD, puts on VUV, or reverse structures) with licensed dealers for protection against acquisition-currency cost overruns; option premiums qualify as current-account remittances and require no RBV approval below the notification threshold.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for tenors beyond 12 months are legally accessible but not recommended as a primary hedge because the USD-settlement structure creates a documentation gap with the Vanuatu authorised dealer; any NDF must be recorded in the SPV hedge register and supported by legal advice confirming it does not constitute unauthorised banking business in Vanuatu.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% – Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no branch profit tax, and no payroll tax at the national level.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Vanuatu imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by an International Company, whether paid to residents or non-residents.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No transfer-pricing documentation duty exists in Vanuatu. The VUV threshold is not applicable because no direct taxation statute creates an arm's length adjustment power and no Local File, Master File, or CbC reporting threshold is triggered locally.

Economic substance scope: Applicable as a regulatory, not tax, mandate. The VFSC Economic Substance Guidelines require an International Company carrying on 'relevant activities' to maintain substantial presence in Vanuatu. A private equity buyout SPV that is a pure equity-holding company qualifies for the reduced substance test: it must only satisfy its statutory filing duties and demonstrate adequate management of its equity interests. However, engaging in leveraged buyout financing, intra-group treasury functions, or investment advisory activity within the SPV triggers the full substance test, requiring local board meetings, decision-making, and qualified employees in Vanuatu.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Foreign-Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: In Vanuatu: fine up to VUV 1,000,000 and deregistration. In the residence jurisdiction: full back taxes, interest, penalties, and potential OECD/EU countermeasures including reinstatement on a harmful-tax blacklist and loss of correspondent banking access.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize false or incomplete disclosures face personal penal fines and imprisonment; forfeiture and travel restrictions may follow mutual legal assistance requests from foreign revenue authorities.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: VFSC may direct the agent to strike the company off the register, impound statutory records, block transactions, and impose a fine up to VUV 1,000,000 plus daily default penalties; local banks will terminate the company's accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for outstanding VFSC fees and penalties; the court may order disqualification from managing any Vanuatu company for up to 5 years and issue civil recovery orders against personal assets.
AML/CTF and Proceeds of Crime Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act: fines up to VUV 100,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 20 years. Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act: civil penalty orders up to VUV 10,000,000, licence revocation, and asset freezing orders.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally indictable, may suffer confiscation of personal assets, be placed on international watchlist systems, and receive custodial sentences up to 20 years for wilful money-laundering involvement.
False Statutory Declaration to the Regulator

Statutory sanction: Contravention of the International Companies Act: fine up to VUV 1,000,000 and a daily default fine up to VUV 10,000; deregistration and voidability of transactions entered after the offence.

Director personal liability: Concurrent personal fine and term of imprisonment; directors remain liable for restitution to defrauded investors and liquidators, and the corporate veil is pierced to enforce judgment against personal assets.
