Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), statutory agency established under the Revenue and Customs Authority Act No. 10 of 2016, operating within the Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; corporate registry for the International Company (IC) vehicle is the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); official VRCA portal: https://www.vrca.gov.vu; VFSC portal: https://www.vfsc.vu

E-government filing portal

Vanuatu imposes no national income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, estate duty or gift tax, so no income-tax TIN is issued. The primary statutory identifier for a foreign-owned IC is the VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and unique company registration number issued under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222]. A VAT identification number is obtained separately from VRCA by filing Form VAT-01 with the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered-office certificate and appointment of a local tax representative. Compulsory VAT registration arises only when taxable supplies to Vanuatu residents exceed VT 5,000,000 in any 12-month period. Because an IC is statutorily prohibited from carrying on business with Vanuatu residents or holding Vanuatu land, its syndicated-finance newswire API revenue constitutes exported supply and remains outside compulsory registration; voluntary registration may nevertheless be elected to recover input VAT on local operating costs.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No income-tax statute exists, so check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections are inapplicable. Statutory elections available: (i) conversion of an IC into an ordinary company under s. 8 of the International Companies Act [Cap. 222], thereby relinquishing tax-exempt status and becoming subject to VAT, stamp duty, customs duties and any future income-tax law; (ii) voluntary VAT registration under s. 10 of the VAT Act No. 10 of 1998 for below-threshold suppliers, subject to VRCA conditions and a minimum registration period; (iii) election of monthly VAT lodgment in lieu of quarterly filing by refund-active exporters with VRCA approval.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VT 5,000,000 (five million vatu) of taxable supplies in any 12-month period, tested at the end of each month (VAT Act s. 8(1)); registration becomes effective from the commencement of that 12-month period. No other indirect-tax nexus or threshold applies to the IC because no corporate or withholding income tax is levied in Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported services — including financial-market news aggregation, syndicated-finance newswire content and API data feeds — qualify for zero-rating under VAT Act s. 7(1) where supplied to a non-resident recipient who is not in Vanuatu at the time of supply, the services are not directly connected with land or goods situated in Vanuatu, and payment is received in foreign currency. Tangible goods are zero-rated only if physically removed from Vanuatu within 28 days of supply. The supplier must hold a valid VAT registration where required, issue a tax invoice marked 'Zero Rated', and maintain contemporaneous evidence of non-resident supply (executed subscriber agreement, API delivery logs, and bank/SWIFT/Stripe settlement records). Vanuatu has no exchange-control or forced-repatriation statute; the 180-day figure reflects standard documentary-credit and banking due-diligence convention rather than a statutory deadline.

  • ASYCUDA World electronic Customs Export Declaration, cleared by Vanuatu Customs
  • Bill of Lading, Master Airway Bill or courier shipment receipt evidencing removal from Vanuatu within 28 days (for physical exports)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list referencing the specific zero-rated supply
  • Signed subscriber agreement or API service contract identifying the overseas customer and delivery point
  • SWIFT MT103/MT950 inward remittance certificate, bank credit advice, or Stripe settlement statement evidencing foreign-currency payment for the exported newswire service

No real-time fiscal clearance, government API e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate is enacted as of this report. VRCA operates the ASYCUDA World platform for all electronic customs export/import declarations and reconciles export declarations against zero-rated VAT claims. Tax invoices must contain the prescribed particulars under VAT Act s. 22 and the Value Added Tax Regulations 1998 (supplier name and VAT number, invoice date, description, quantity, unit price, VAT amount or explicit zero-rated notation); registrants must retain all records for 7 years. Electronic invoices in PDF or structured XML are acceptable, but no clearance-before-issuance mandate applies to the syndicated-finance newswire API business.

VRCA conducts post-clearance and risk-based refund audits; unsupported zero-rating claims are reversed by amended assessment with statutory interest, and refunded amounts are clawed back as a debt due before any future credit or repayment is released. Knowingly false export declarations constitute an offence under VAT Act s. 31(1), attracting a fine of up to VT 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 2 years; the Revenue and Customs Authority Act No. 11 of 2016 empowers the Commissioner to assess, issue security demands, and distrain assets in recovery. Directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for the company's VAT debts under VAT Act s. 33. Fraudulent export-zero-rating schemes are additionally reportable under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act No. 8 of 2017 and may result in VFSC licence revocation, deregistration and refusal of future incentives.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu operates a fully liberalized and convertible foreign-exchange regime with no exchange-control legislation in force. The national currency, the Vanuatu Vatu (VUV), floats against a basket of major currencies, with the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu retaining an informal smoothing capacity rather than a fixed parity. Both current-account flows (financial newswire API subscription fees, content syndication royalties, market-data licensing income) and capital-account flows (equity capital injections, dividends, loan repayments, liquidation proceeds) are freely convertible and remittable without RBV approval, quantitative ceilings, or differentiated convertibility gateways. The principal operational hurdle is the absence of a deliverable offshore VUV market: all VUV/USD/EUR/AUD conversion must be executed onshore with a Tier-1 licensed commercial bank, and transactions above USD 250,000 generally require 1-2 business days of pricing notice. Profit and dividend remittances are entirely tax-free under the Vanuatu International Company exemption, but the remitting bank will require dividend minutes, an updated register of members, and CRS/FATCA certification as documentary evidence. No withholding taxes, exchange-control certificates, or central-bank permissions attach to outward remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Vanuatu imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory percentage conversion ratio, no inward-remittance filing window, no advance import deposit regime, and no RBV surrender certificate requirement. For ISIC 6391 digital service exports (syndicated finance news aggregation and newswire API subscriptions), the International Company may retain 100% of export proceeds in any licensed foreign-currency or multicurrency account at a Vanuatu bank, or hold such proceeds offshore at treasury discretion. The only compliance duties are record-keeping under the VFSC AML/CFT Rules 2021 and standard transactional verification (invoice, contract, and beneficial-owner identification) for outward wires and currency conversions.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) - the statutory central bank and monetary authority of Vanuatu, responsible for currency issuance, supervision of licensed deposit-taking institutions, and operation of the national payment and settlement systems. The Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) is the parallel regulator for International Companies, banking licensees, and AML/CFT compliance under the AML/CFT Act No. 44 of 2019.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Vanuatu law does not provide a dedicated EMI or electronic-money-institution licensing tier; e-money issuance is absorbed within the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu's licensing perimeter for deposit-taking institutions, and the VFSC offshore-banking route under the International Banking Act requires full licensed-bank capital in excess of USD 1 million, which is economically disproportionate for a newswire treasury vehicle. Established offshore EMIs (WISE, Payoneer, and similar SEPA-centric providers) may technically hold settlement accounts for the Vanuatu International Company, but they are not VFSC-authorised, they do not offer VUV-denominated rails, and global de-risking policies frequently terminate clients characterized as offshore-business-reference accounts. The legally sound architecture is a Tier-1 licensed Vanuatu bank as the primary liquidity depository and SWIFT entry point, with a licensed overseas EMI used only as an auxiliary downstream collection/disbursement layer for low-value USD/EUR subscription payments; VUV cross-border movements must not transit through unregulated EMIs.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: inbound SWIFT MT103 (or MT202/MT203 cover for large group-treasury transfers) must be addressed to the IC's USD capital-escrow or multicurrency operational account at ANZ or BSP Port Vila, with Field 70 narrative explicitly reading 'CAPITAL INJECTION / SUBSCRIPTION OF ORDINARY SHARES - VFSC CERTIFICATE NUMBER [IC-XXXX]'. Where the originator's system requires SWIFT category classification, Category 3 (Capital Account) is the correct mapping; for ordinary operating revenue from newswire/API subscriptions, the incoming MT103 or Stripe settlement file must carry Category 7 (Services) with the customer invoice number embedded. Verification required by the receiving Vanuatu bank: valid board resolution authorizing the subscription and bank-signatory mandate; updated register of members evidencing share allotment; source-of-funds statement of the subscribing entity; CRS/FATCA classification forms. No RBV license, exchange-control approval, or vatu conversion is required for the injection; however, the treasury must provide a 48-hour pre-alert for wires above USD 500,000 and expect standard RBV-corporate due-diligence queries. The targeted rails per the corporate finance blueprint are SWIFT Wire for B2B capital and operating flows and Stripe for low-value digital subscription collections.Primary clearing rail: outbound SWIFT MT103 with complete Field 70 narratives for content-technology license fees, market-data vendor payments, editorial consultant fees, dividends, and syndication partner remittances, executed directly from the ANZ/BSP Port Vila account through their international correspondent chains (ANZ Australia, BSP Papua New Guinea, or Westpac New Zealand). For procurement of research infrastructure and broadcast equipment, tier-1 banks issue and confirm Commercial Letters of Credit (documentary credits) under UCP 600, on sight or deferred basis, requiring underlying supplier invoices and pro-forma shipping documents. VUV-denominated domestic operating payments (payroll, rent, and local subcontractor fees) clear through RBV payment-settlement systems and are not routed via SWIFT. Cross-border disbursements require prior conversion of VUV into USD/EUR/AUD/B via the onshore spot market at the pricing bank; there is no offshore deliverable VUV market. Settlement in SWIFT and Stripe rails remains the mandated combination for this financial newswire treasury model.Onshore FX Spot (T+2 settlement in VUV/USD/EUR/AUD/NZD) available from ANZ, BSP, and BRED at commercial market rates without RBV approval.Onshore Deliverable FX Forwards (tenor 1-12 months) quoted by ANZ and BSP treasury desks for hedging known USD-denominated syndicated-finance receivables, collateralized by a line of credit or USD margin; no exchange-control clearance required.Onshore FX Swaps (spot-to-forward combinations, tenor up to 12 months) between commercial bank counterparties for managing short-term VUV liquidity positions.Offshore Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) referencing VUV/USD, executable through international treasury desks under ISDA/CSA, permissible because Vanuatu imposes no derivative licensing or exchange-control restriction on cross-border financial contracts, though liquidity is thin and bid-offer spreads are wide.Cross-Currency Interest Rate Swaps (VUV/USD or VUV/EUR) arranged through an offshore dealer bank, permissible without VFSC licensing for treasury hedging purposes, with physical settlement limited by the shallow onshore VUV market and therefore typically documented on a non-deliverable basis.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW. Vanuatu law does not provide a dedicated EMI or electronic-money-institution licensing tier; e-money issuance is absorbed within the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu's licensing perimeter for deposit-taking institutions, and the VFSC offshore-banking route under the International Banking Act requires full licensed-bank capital in excess of USD 1 million, which is economically disproportionate for a newswire treasury vehicle. Established offshore EMIs (WISE, Payoneer, and similar SEPA-centric providers) may technically hold settlement accounts for the Vanuatu International Company, but they are not VFSC-authorised, they do not offer VUV-denominated rails, and global de-risking policies frequently terminate clients characterized as offshore-business-reference accounts. The legally sound architecture is a Tier-1 licensed Vanuatu bank as the primary liquidity depository and SWIFT entry point, with a licensed overseas EMI used only as an auxiliary downstream collection/disbursement layer for low-value USD/EUR subscription payments; VUV cross-border movements must not transit through unregulated EMIs.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not Statutorily Applicable - Vanuatu does not impose corporate income tax and has not enacted transfer pricing documentation thresholds. For prudent risk management, entities are recommended to maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation whenever related-party revenue exceeds the equivalent of USD 1,000,000 annually.

Economic substance scope: The Vanuatu International Companies (Economic Substance) Act imposes a substance requirement for specific geographically mobile activities. Financial newswire aggregation and syndication is not a designated relevant activity under current Vanuatu law; nevertheless, the entity must maintain a registered agent, a registered office, and adequate records in Vanuatu. Any incidental financing or IP-holding function must preserve local board meetings and operational direction to avoid being characterized as a shell company under international exchange-of-information standards.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu International Companies Act and the Income Tax Act (as applicable to domestic entities), fines up to VUV 5,000,000 and confiscation of profits. Criminal prosecution may follow for willful evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for restitution of all evaded amounts. Courts may issue travel bans, asset freezing orders, and impose imprisonment up to seven years for fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the International Companies Register, penalty of VUV 10,000, and forced dissolution. The company may be referred to the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit for transparency violations.

Director personal liability: Directors can be disqualified from holding office for up to ten years. In cross-border actions, foreign courts may pierce the veil and impose civil liability for unpaid underlying taxes.
AML / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Vanuatu Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Act, penalties range from VUV 1,000,000 to VUV 20,000,000, plus forfeiture of the proceeds. The operator's license may be suspended or revoked.

Director personal liability: Individual directors face imprisonment up to ten years, personal fines, and confiscation orders. Non-resident directors remain subject to extradition under mutual legal assistance treaties.
False Statement to Regulator

Statutory sanction: Fine of VUV 2,000,000 for each false statement; the company is liable to deregistration without a winding-up procedure.

Director personal liability: Each director who signed the declaration is jointly and severally liable for the fine. A protection order may be issued limiting foreign travel. Imprisonment up to three years applies for deliberate falsification.
