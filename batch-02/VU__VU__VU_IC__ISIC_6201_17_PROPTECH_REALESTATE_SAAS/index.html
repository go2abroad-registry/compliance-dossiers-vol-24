Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) — International Companies Register, Bougainville House, Port Vila

Governing statute

International Companies Act No. 32 of 1992 (Cap. 222), as amended by the International Companies (Amendment) Act, particularly sections 5 to 18 regarding incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, and registration of an International Company.

Formation / constitutional filings

Certificate of Incorporation issued upon filing of the Memorandum and Articles of Association (Form IC-1) and statutory declarations through a licensed registered agent.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 15 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by the International Companies Act; no escrow or capital deposit is required. The authorized capital is set in the Memorandum and Articles, and at least one subscriber share must be issued before the Certificate of Incorporation is issued.

Funding & unlock path: No fire safety or structural clearance is required for a remote-offshore SaaS operation. In the event of a physical office, the Vanuatu Fire Service must certify fire prevention measures, Vanuatu Public Works Department must inspect structural load and occupancy limits, and the Department of Employment enforces workplace safety under the Occupational Health and Safety Act 2013 before the first employee occupies the premises.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by the International Companies Act; no escrow or capital deposit is required. The authorized capital is set in the Memorandum and Articles, and at least one subscriber share must be issued before the Certificate of Incorporation is issued.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs Authority (VRCA), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management; official portal: https://www.vrca.gov.vu (VRCA e-Services for VAT registration, returns, and payments).

E-government filing portal

A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is incorporated by a licensed registered agent under the International Companies Act Cap 222 and obtains a Certificate of Incorporation from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No income tax registration or TIN is required because the IC is statutorily exempt from all taxes, including income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, and VAT, in respect of its business operations. If the IC engages in local taxable activity above the VAT threshold or voluntarily opts into the VAT net, it must apply to the VRCA using Form VAT-01, attaching the VFSC incorporation certificate, registered agent confirmation, and evidence of business premises, whereupon the VRCA issues a VAT registration number. The VFSC corporate register reference serves as the sole statutory identifier for tax-exempt offshore operation; no separate TIN is issued.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone - Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC); statutorily exempt from all national taxes on offshore business activities, with no residency-based territorial taxation, no income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no wealth or inheritance tax. — A Vanuatu International Company (IC) is incorporated by a licensed registered agent under the International Companies Act Cap 222 and obtains a Certificate of Incorporation from the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). No income tax registration or TIN is required because the IC is statutorily exempt from all taxes, including income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, and VAT, in respect of its business operations. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - Vanuatu International Company (VU_IC); statutorily exempt from all national taxes on offshore business activities, with no residency-based territorial taxation, no income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no wealth or inheritance tax.

Available election forms

No income-tax check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election machinery exists in Vanuatu because the jurisdiction imposes no direct income tax. Available elections are limited to: (1) voluntary VAT registration under the Value Added Tax Act Cap 250 for local suppliers; (2) conversion of an International Company to a local company under the Companies Act Cap 191, which elects into ordinary local tax/VAT treatment and extinguishes the IC statutory exemption; and (3) application to the VFSC Registrar for a Certificate of Tax Exemption confirming the IC's exempt status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration applies to a person whose annual taxable turnover of goods and services exceeds VUV 5,000,000 in any twelve-month period; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold. A Vanuatu International Company engaged exclusively in offshore SaaS/PropTech subscriptions to non-residents is outside the local VAT net and is not obliged to register; local supply of property-management services to VUV-resident clients would be scrutinized against the VUV 5,000,000 nexus threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rated where the supplier holds a customs export declaration and documentary evidence that the goods were exported within the timeframe prescribed by the Commissioner of VRCA. Services: zero-rated where the services are supplied to a non-resident who is outside Vanuatu at the time of supply and the services are consumed wholly outside Vanuatu; software-as-a-service subscriptions, cloud-based PropTech screening, and real-estate rental analytics supplied to overseas clients qualify as exported services. The Vanuatu International Company is statutorily exempt from VAT altogether, so zero-rating is claimed only where the IC has voluntarily registered; in that case, the IC must zero-rate qualifying offshore supplies and may claim input credits for attributable local purchases. Failure to substantiate export status converts the zero-rated supply into an exempt or taxable supply with mandatory VAT output tax and clawback risk.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Vanuatu Customs ECS/declaration entry with export confirmation stamp)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export of goods from Vanuatu
  • Commercial invoice or tax invoice describing the exported goods or exported services
  • Signed contract or service agreement confirming non-resident customer and offshore consumption for SaaS/PropTech services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds

No national real-time e-invoicing, continuous transaction control (CTC), fiscal clearance API, or digital stamping regime is currently mandated in Vanuatu. VAT-registered entities must issue tax invoices and retain accounting records in accordance with the VAT Act Cap 250; there is no government API clearance or mandated third-party clearance infrastructure. VRCA has announced phased modernization of revenue administration, but e-invoicing remains voluntary/advisory in nature as of the current filing season.

VRCA is empowered to audit zero-rated export transactions, request underlying contractual and payment evidence, and retrospectively assess VAT on supplies lacking adequate export substantiation. Legal consequences under the VAT Act Cap 250 include additional tax at 25% per annum on unpaid VAT, penalty tax up to 200% of the evaded amount for fraudulent or negligent underpayments, fines of up to VUV 200,000, and imprisonment of up to two years for persons who make false declarations or knowingly produce forged export documentation. Refunds improperly obtained are subject to clawback with compound interest from the original date of refund. For the statutorily exempt Vanuatu International Company, no VAT return or refund claim is ordinarily required, but any voluntary VAT registration exposes the entity to the full audit, clawback, and criminal penalty framework; the 180-day figure reflects the standard banking/trade-finance expectation for export proceeds repatriation, as Vanuatu imposes no statutory FX control deadline for offshore international company revenue.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Vanuatu vatu (VUV) is a managed currency with restricted offshore deliverability; convertibility is freely available for current-account service receipts through authorized dealers, while capital-account transactions generally require RBV approval. However, International Companies registered under the International Companies Act [Cap. 222] are considered non-resident and are exempt from the Exchange Control Act in respect of foreign-currency accounts and overseas transactions, allowing the PropTech SaaS business to invoice in USD/AUD/EUR and maintain offshore liquidity with no VUV conversion burden. There are no income taxes, corporate profits taxes, withholding taxes, capital gains taxes, or dividend taxes imposed on International Companies, so profit remittance is not constrained by tax clearance requirements; remittance flows are governed solely by bank-level anti-money-laundering due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The statutory export-proceeds surrender regime applies to tangible merchandise exporters under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 65], which obliges tangible-goods exporters to repatriate proceeds within the RBV-published realization period (generally six months from shipment) and to offer the VUV equivalent to an authorized dealer. Electronic services, software-as-a-service subscriptions, and intangible-export receipts are not within the goods-surrender rules; accordingly, the ISIC 6201 PropTech SaaS International Company may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts in any permitted jurisdiction without mandatory conversion. No surrender form or VUV conversion ratio applies to this revenue model; remittance of dividends, royalties, or subcontractor payments follows contractual payment instructions through SWIFT with appropriate narrative.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu (RBV) under the Exchange Control Act [Cap. 65]; the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) administers financial licensing and AML/KYC matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MODERATE-to-HIGH through offshore-licensed digital e-money institutions, but LOW if the EMI requires a Vanuatu national payment license. A non-resident International Company may lawfully subscribe to foreign EMIs (e.g., UK- or Singapore-licensed electronic money institutions) for treasury rails, contracted outside Vanuatu; however, any money transmission service carried on in or from Vanuatu requires a VFSC Financial Dealers license under the Financial Dealers Licensing Act [Cap. 70]. Because the target payment rails include Stripe_Billing and ACH_Direct, the treasury blueprint should place merchant/deposit accounts in a supported foreign jurisdiction (United States, United Kingdom, or Singapore) and only use Vanuatu bank accounts for settlement sweeps, thereby avoiding unlicensed onshore EMI activity. Counsel must verify that the EMI has no Vanuatu-specific onboarding restriction and that its KYB process accepts the IC's registered-agent domicile and non-resident status.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no corporate income tax on any company, including International Companies (ICs); no CIT statute, no capital gains tax, and no payroll tax exist in Vanuatu.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an International Company to any shareholder, whether resident or non-resident.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

License fee and renewal application are due on each anniversary date of incorporation; renewal must be completed before the anniversary anniversary of the VFSC register, with the registered agent attesting within 30 days. Continuous non-renewal by year-end triggers administrative strike-off proceedings.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Notification must be filed within 12 months after the end of the financial year; the directorate reviews the substance declaration and may request supporting evidence within 30 days.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None applicable in Vanuatu - no domestic transfer pricing statute, no Local File or Master File threshold, and no CbC reporting obligation for ICs. Group-level thresholds of the parent jurisdiction govern: a US parent exceeding US$850 million consolidated revenue must include the VU entity in its CbC report, and OECD-compliant Local File preparation is market practice when intercompany service fees or royalty flows exceed US$10 million.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu enacted its economic substance framework under the International Companies Act to meet OECD BEPS minimum standards and EU Code of Conduct demands. ICs carrying on relevant activities - banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, finance and leasing, headquarters, holding company, IP business, distribution and service centre - must maintain substantial economic presence in Vanuatu: a physical office, locally present management, commensurate employees and expenditure, and documented CIGA performed in Vanuatu. For a PropTech Real Estate SaaS entity, owning the software IP shifts the company into the 'IP business' class; pure resale of licensed third-party software may fall outside ESR scope. Annual notification and documentary proof are mandatory for in-scope companies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Evasion / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Statutory penalties under the Vanuatu economic substance regime (fines denominated in millions of vatu per offence); compulsory VFSC strike-off; automatic reporting to the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices and exchange of information with the beneficial owner's residence country.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify false substance declarations are personally liable for penalties, may be disqualified from serving in any VFSC-regulated entity, and can be subjected to civil restitution proceedings and travel restrictions.
Unlicensed Financial Activity / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2018 with substantial fines and terms of imprisonment; VFSC may wind up the IC and confiscate all deposit balances as proceeds of crime.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal liability for undisclosed management of client funds, personal asset forfeiture, sequestration of property, and imposition of travel bans pending investigation.
Shell Company / Nominee Abuse

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off and dissolution; retrospective assessment by the foreign tax authority recharacterising the entire profit as taxable in the market country, typically with penalties of up to 200% of the evaded tax; the entity is also flagged in global beneficial ownership registers.

Director personal liability: Both the nominee director and the beneficial owner may be prosecuted for tax evasion; directors have personal liability for the company's outstanding regulatory charges and can be banned from obtaining any new Vanuatu registration.
Late Filing / Trading While Struck Off

Statutory sanction: Summary offence with fines and forfeiture of the right to enforce contracts concluded during the dissolved period; restoration to the register is possible only upon payment of all arrears and VFSC reinstatement fees.

Director personal liability: Directors become jointly and severally liable for all contractual and regulatory debts incurred while the company was struck off, and may be subject to director disqualification orders in Vanuatu and the entity's market jurisdictions.
