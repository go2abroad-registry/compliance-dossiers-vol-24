Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue of the Government of Samoa, Level 2, Fatu o Le Atua Building, Apia; taxpayer portal, online forms and eVAGST services via https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity electing Samoa International Company (IC) status under the International Companies Act 1987 is first incorporated by the Registrar of Companies and thereafter receives an International Company Certificate. Because the IC is structured as a tax-exempt, non-resident company, no income tax TIN is required for the IC's offshore operations. If the IC makes taxable supplies in Samoa or voluntarily registers for VAGST, it must submit Form VAGST-R to the Ministry of Revenue; upon approval and issuance of a VAGST registration number, that VAT ID becomes the IC's domestic indirect tax identifier.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity electing Samoa International Company (IC) status under the International Companies Act 1987 is first incorporated by the Registrar of Companies and thereafter receives an International Company Certificate. Because the IC is structured as a tax-exempt, non-resident company, no income tax TIN is required for the IC's offshore operations. If the IC makes taxable supplies in Samoa or voluntarily registers for VAGST, it must submit Form VAGST-R to the Ministry of Revenue; upon approval and issuance of a VAGST registration number, that VAT ID becomes the IC's domestic indirect tax identifier.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None available. A Samoa International Company is statutorily exempt from income tax under the International Companies Act 1987 and the Income Tax Ordinance 1959; Samoa does not operate a US-style check-the-box election, and no hybrid mismatch or deemed taxation election is available. The IC may, for administrative purposes, elect to be treated as a non-resident entity by the Ministry of Revenue, but such election has no effect on its underlying tax-exempt status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 80,000 (Samoa tala) of taxable supplies in any consecutive twelve-month period; voluntary registration is available for exporters such as a collectible trading card publisher that intends to zero-rate offshore sales.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Samoa International Company registered for VAGST may claim zero-rating on tangible goods such as collectible trading cards if the card supply is an export from Samoa, ownership of the goods passes to an overseas buyer, and the goods are physically delivered outside Samoa within 28 days (or within a longer period authorized by the Ministry of Revenue) of the supply. Services that are supplied directly in connection with land or goods located outside Samoa, or supplied to and enjoyed by a non-resident, may also be zero-rated. The exporter must retain contemporaneous documentary evidence of export and receipt of foreign sales consideration.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank Payment Justification / Foreign Currency Remittance Slip

Samoa does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-integrated e-invoicing, or digital stamping for VAGST invoices. A registered person must issue full VAGST tax invoices in sequential order using an approved invoice format that contains prescribed particulars. For export supplies, zero-rated commercial invoices must be issued with the export invoice wording and the IC's VAGST registration number; no government e-approval or digital stamping API is applied.

The Samoa Ministry of Revenue may retrospectively audit export zero-rating claims for six years. If the factual export and foreign payment conditions are not substantiated, the claimed zero rate is replaced by the 15% VAGST rate on the full invoice value, input credits originally attributed to the claimed export are clawed back, and late payment interest accrues from the original filing date. Fraudulent or negligent export VAT schemes on collectible trading cards expose the IC and its officers to civil penalties of up to 100% of tax evaded, monetary fines up to WST 100,000, deregistration of the IC, invalidation of the International Company certificate, and in aggravated cases criminal imprisonment under Samoa's Crimes Act and the Tax Administration Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is a managed-float currency pegged to a trade-weighted basket, with onshore convertibility available only through CBS-licensed authorized dealers. Current-account transactions are broadly liberalized, but capital-account movements, including equity injections, dividend repatriation, and derivative transactions, remain subject to exchange-control approval or express CBS delegation to authorized dealers. A Samoa International Company (IC) can achieve near-total de facto convertibility by maintaining a USD Foreign Currency Account (FCA), denominating all trading, disbursement, and settlement flows in USD, and avoiding WST conversion except for local statutory agent fees and compliance disbursements. Profit remittances funded entirely from USD FCA balances do not enter the WST conversion queue; dividend remittances require supporting board resolutions, source documentation, and, where applicable, a tax-clearance certificate from the Samoa Ministry for Revenue. The principal residual hurdle is the absence of a deep offshore deliverable WST market, making any hedging reliant on offshore non-deliverable contracts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBS Exchange Control notices, export proceeds in foreign currency must be repatriated to and lodged with an authorized dealer in Samoa within six months of the date of export, supported by shipping and customs documentation such as the air waybill, bill of lading, or electronic export entry. There is no mandatory percentage conversion into WST: 100 percent of documented export proceeds may be retained in an approved USD Foreign Currency Account provided the authorized dealer records settlement on the prescribed CBS foreign-exchange return. Where conversion into WST is elected, the full amount must be sold through the authorized dealer at the prevailing spot rate; private or offshore conversion through unlicensed intermediaries is prohibited.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), which administers the national exchange-control regime under the Exchange Control Act 1973 and issues delegated instructions to licensed authorized dealers

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Rating: LOW as a Samoa-domiciled settlement layer; MEDIUM as an offshore collection rail only. Samoa has no regulatory category for electronic money institutions, and payment-service provision is reserved for CBS-licensed banks and financial institutions. An offshore EMI account may be used to receive Stripe and Authorize.Net settlement flows before onward transfer to the Samoan USD FCA, but it must never be represented as a bank, hold Samoa-regulated treasury balances, or serve as a mechanism to bypass exchange-control recording. Any such misrepresentation would constitute an automatic KYC failure and regulatory red flag.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inward shareholder capital injections must be delivered via bank-to-bank SWIFT MT103 to the designated USD Foreign Currency Account at the Samoa authorized dealer, identifying the IC by its full registered name and account reference. The remittance information field should carry a CBS-compatible purpose descriptor pre-agreed with the receiving dealer, such as 'INVST-G1100 Equity injection of International Company,' with the beneficiary reference 'WS_IC / ISIC_3240' for reconciliation. Each injection requires the shareholder allotment resolution, the issued shares register, and the Ministry for Revenue tax-exemption certificate; the authorized dealer records the credit on the prescribed exchange-control return, and injections exceeding delegated dealer limits require prior CBS approval. Cash, cryptocurrency, or unlicensed third-party EMI pass-throughs are not acceptable channels for capital injection.Operational disbursements for raw materials, holographic cold-foil stock, inks, laminates, packaging, and logistics use two clearing rails. Open-account SWIFT MT103 payments with unique end-to-end transaction references are used for smaller vendor settlements below US$50,000, including sample pre-payments and routine material deposits. The standard disbursement structure is 30 percent telegraphic-transfer deposit and 70 percent at-sight under the Letter of Credit. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by an International Company to non-resident shareholders because IC distributions are exempt under the Samoan offshore regime. The standard statutory dividend withholding tax rate for an ordinary resident Samoan company is 15%, reduced to 5%–15% under an applicable double taxation agreement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No separate statutory Local File or Master File threshold is fixed in WST under Samoa domestic law. Transfer pricing documentation is required whenever a related-party transaction is material to the taxpayer’s position and is not priced on an arm’s length basis. Samoa follows the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines in applying the arm’s length standard through the Income Tax Act.

Economic substance scope: The Samoa International Companies regime is subject to economic substance oversight. An International Company engaged in ISIC 3240 collectible trading-card publishing must demonstrate genuine commercial substance: licensed registered agent in Samoa, recorded board and management decisions, physical place of business, employees or contractors with adequate qualifications, and real activities such as contract negotiation, IP ownership, licensing, printing supervision, and merchant settlement control. A letterbox company or shell IC with no substance may be struck off, have its exemption revoked, and be taxed as a resident Samoan company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under Samoa criminal and tax administration statutes; penalty tax of up to 200% of the evaded tax; confiscation of the IC’s bank accounts, card inventory, and printing receivables; corporate fine up to WST 1 million; SRA may recharacterize the IC as a resident Samoan company and assess tax at 27% on the entire undeclared profit stream.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where directors personally directed the evasion. Directors and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the company’s unpaid tax and penalties, and face imprisonment up to 14 years, civil restitution orders, passport surrender, and travel bans pending final tax clearance.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Registrar strike-off and dissolution; cancellation of international company status; retrospective loss of the tax-exempt zone; SRA may tax the shell company at 27% and deny deductions that cannot be substantiated; public strike-off notices disqualify the entity from banking, payment processor, and distributor agreements.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed registered agent who signed declarations knowing the entity lacked substance are personally liable for restoration costs, regulatory penalties, and unpaid annual government fees. A director may be barred from managing any Samoa company for up to 5 years and restrained from leaving Samoa without SRA or court authorization.
Money Laundering / AML-CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to WST 5 million under Samoa AML/CFT legislation; asset forfeiture and freezing of merchant accounts; revocation of the registered agent’s license; referral of directors and beneficial owners to foreign tax and law enforcement authorities through the Samoa Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors, AML compliance officers, and beneficial owners face personal criminal liability with imprisonment up to 10 years; regulatory travel bans, property restraint orders, and personal monetary penalties are available, and directors cannot rely on the company’s legal personality to shield proceeds obtained through deliberate AML breaches.
Withholding Tax Evasion and Exemption Overreach

Statutory sanction: SRA will assess non-resident withholding tax at 15% on the misclassified dividends, interest, or royalties, add penalties of up to 100% of the underpaid amount, and recover unpaid withholding tax from the company plus daily statutory interest. The IC exemption is treated as void from inception.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly caused the company to make exempt distributions without satisfying the International Company conditions are personally liable for the unpaid withholding tax and penalties. Courts may issue asset forfeiture orders, travel bans, and civil recovery judgments against directors to enforce collection.
