Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), successor to the Ministry for Revenue; administers the Tax Administration Act 2012, Income Tax Act 2012 and Value Added Goods and Services Tax Act 2002. Official portal and e-services: https://www.revenue.gov.ws

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Samoa International Company (IC) must obtain its TIN through a licensed Registered Agent in Apia: (1) the agent files SRA TIN-01 taxpayer registration accompanied by the IC's Certificate of Incorporation issued by the Registrar of International Companies (Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour), constitutional documents, director/shareholder register, registered agent address and AML/KYC due diligence; (2) SRA validates offshore status and issues a 10-digit TIN within 3-5 business days; (3) VAGST registration is required only if the IC makes taxable supplies exceeding WST 100,000 in any 12-month period, which does not apply to a pure offshore digital magazine exporter; (4) the TIN is mandatory to open a corporate bank account, receive Stripe/AdSense settlements through a Samoan licensed bank, and file annual compliance returns. No physical office in Samoa is required beyond the registered agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Samoa International Company is automatically exempt from Samoan income tax, corporate tax, dividend tax and royalty tax on foreign-source income, provided the IC does not trade with Samoan residents, own Samoan land, or carry on business in Samoa; exemption is statutory and requires no advance ruling.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or OECD hybrid-mismatch election exists in Samoa. Permitted elections include: (a) voluntary VAGST registration to recover input credits attributable to exported supplies; (b) an election under the International Companies Act to apply Companies Act governance provisions; and (c) an economic substance notification election evidencing that core income-generating activities are performed outside Samoa to preserve tax exemption. Samoa has not enacted domestic hybrid mismatch legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period (mandatory threshold; voluntary registration permitted below threshold). A pure offshore IC supplying digital magazine subscriptions exclusively to non-residents has no Samoan taxable nexus and is not required to register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to exported goods and exported services under the VAGST Act 2002. For the ISIC 5813 digital magazine portal: (i) the recipient must be a non-resident located outside Samoa at the time of supply; (ii) digital content is delivered and accessed outside Samoa; (iii) consideration is received in foreign currency via Stripe/AdSense payout rails; and (iv) no services are performed or enjoyed in Samoa and no Samoan resident subscribers are served. Supplies made to a Samoan resident are taxable at 15% and create a registration nexus.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic export entry via Samoa Customs Service using ASYCUDA World; for pure digital supplies a formal export register and exported-service declaration under the VAGST Act 2002 are substituted)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (required for physical goods; replaced by platform delivery logs and IP-geolocation evidence of access from outside Samoa for the digital magazine subscription service)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a licensed Samoa commercial bank evidencing foreign-currency inward remittance and FX repatriation within the 180-day deadline
  • Digital supply substantiation bundle: publisher export invoices, non-resident subscriber list, Stripe/AdSense payout settlement statements, and geolocation logs demonstrating off-Samoa consumption

None — Samoa does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, government API validation, e-invoicing clearance, or digital stamping. Electronic invoices (PDF/XML) are acceptable if they contain the statutory particulars under the VAGST Act 2002 (supplier name and TIN, issue date, sequential number, description, quantity, price, VAGST rate and amount, total). Records must be retained for at least 5 years; e-filing is available through the SRA portal without a clearance stamp.

The SRA cross-matches payment gateway settlement statements (Stripe/AdSense) against VAGST returns and bank IRCs during audit. Insufficient substantiation recharacterises the zero-rated supply as taxable at 15%, permitting retrospective reassessment within 5 years (10 years for fraud), penalty tax of up to 200% of the avoided tax for wilful false statements, late-payment interest at 2% per month compounding from the original due date, recovery of clawed-back input credits, criminal fines and imprisonment on conviction, deregistration of the IC, and potential personal liability for directors who certify false export documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoan Tala (WST) is fully convertible for bona fide current-account transactions (trade, services, royalties, licensing fees) through authorized dealers provided the underlying documentation (invoice, contract, revenue statement) is presented; capital-account transactions and outward transfers exceeding prescribed reporting thresholds require prior Central Bank approval, and dividend/profit remittances to non-residents are liberally approved after verification of source-of-funds and tax-clearance records. International Companies under the International Companies Act 1987 are statutorily deemed non-residents for exchange-control purposes; consequently, USD/EUR-denominated shareholder capital, subscription revenue from non-resident platforms, and outbound disbursements to non-resident counterparties flow freely through a licensed foreign-currency account without Central Bank pre-clearance or mandatory conversion into WST. Practical limitation: no deep onshore WST/USD forward market exists and any physical WST transaction involving resident counterparties (local payroll, rent, utilities) will trigger standard exchange-control documentation requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident commercial exporters, export proceeds must be collected within 30 days of the contractual due date (or, in the absence of a due date, within 90 days of export shipment), remitted via an authorized dealer, and surrendered into WST no later than 30 days after receipt. International Companies operating a digital magazine portal are classified as non-resident entities: subscription, advertising (AdSense), and sponsored-media revenues received from Stripe/Google in USD/EUR into a designated foreign-currency account are outside the resident surrender regime and carry no conversion obligation. If, however, the International Company earns any revenue from Samoan residents or conducts business within Samoa, the resident exporter surrender rules would apply and require either Central Bank derogation or cessation of in-country business to preserve the non-resident standing.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa), exercising statutory powers under the Exchange Control Act 1976, Exchange Control Regulations 1976, Financial Institutions Act 1996, and Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007; enforcement of exchange-control directives is delegated to authorized dealers (licensed commercial banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium feasibility under strict conditions. An offshore digital EMI (Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise Business) is legally acceptable as a payment-collection and settlement intermediary, but it cannot function as the sole treasury account because Samoan AML enforcement and the Central Bank's guidance on correspondent banking require that material funds settle into a bank account licensed in Samoa. Recommended architecture: Stripe/AdSense payouts → EMI USD account (for platform where Samoa is not a direct payout jurisdiction) → periodic SWIFT USD wire to the BSP Samoa operational account; direct EMI-to-EMI disintermediation of the Samoan licensed bank is not acceptable for Stage 4 compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial equity contribution shall be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's overseas account directly to the BSP Samoa USD Capital Escrow Account using purpose code '051 – Equity Injection / Capital Contribution' (or prevailing bank equivalent), with remittance information referencing the International Company registration number and the International Companies Act 1987. The bank must receive, prior to credit: signed board resolution, subscriber declaration form, UBO disclosure, and a source-of-wealth letter confirmed by the remitting bank. Enforcement note: because the entity is an International Company, the Samoa FIU does not require a CBS capital-import approval; however, funds arriving from a jurisdiction without credible AML standards are subject to a 5–10 business day hold and may be rejected absent the documentary bundle above. Alternative rail: Wise Business USD balance → local USD payout via SWIFT to BSP using the same purpose code; Stripe/AdSense-generated funds are not permitted to be the source of statutory capital.Settlement from the BSP Samoa operational account shall be executed by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for correspondent rebalancing) via BSP's New York and Sydney correspondent banks, clearing through CHIPS/Fedwire for USD and RITS/Fedwire-equivalent for AUD; typical purpose codes include 'Royalties or License Fees', 'Professional Fees and Consulting', 'Data Processing and Hosting', and 'Advertising and Marketing'. For print-media procurement (magazine distribution contracts, paper and printing services) exceeding USD 5,000 per shipment, an irrevocable confirmed commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700, at-sight basis) shall be opened through BSP's trade-finance desk with a 5-day negotiation window and standard ICC UCP600 shipping documents. No local RTGS/CLS constraint applies because all material treasury flows are USD-denominated outside the WST settlement system; the IC does not require access to Samoa's national payment switch for its core digital publishing operations.Outright deliverable USD/WST forward contracts, tenor up to 12 months, available through BSP Samoa Treasury for the limited WST exposure arising from local registered-agent fees, legal retainers, and office services; fully collateralized by the positive USD operating balance; requires one-time CBS notification via the authorized dealer.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) in WST transacted offshore through an international counterparty (e.g., a top-tier global bank with a Far East booking centre) when the IC's indirect WST liability is estimated above USD 100,000 equivalent per quarter; settlement is in USD with no WST delivery, and CBS notification is satisfied by the authorized dealer's monthly foreign-exchange report.Cross-currency swap (USD/EUR) for the periodic conversion of EUR-denominated subscription revenue (Stripe EUR settlements) into USD on a spot-plus-30-day sweep basis; permitted as a bespoke treasury product in Samoa, requires a formal bank credit approval and is subject to one-off CBS reporting, but is otherwise freely executable between a licensed Samoan bank and a non-resident IC.Natural-hedge policy supplement: invoicing exclusively in USD, maintaining WST cash balances below USD 5,000 equivalent converted only once monthly at the interbank mid-rate, and contractual pass-through of permanent FX risk to advertising clients, thereby rendering the WST NDF execution materially unnecessary for the 12-month treasury horizon.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% applicable to an International Company under the Samoa International Companies Act 1987; the standard domestic corporate income tax rate for resident companies is 25% under the Income Tax Act 2014

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for distributions made by an International Company; Samoa does not impose dividend withholding tax on International Company distributions, and the International Company is not a withholding agent for such payments.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: WST 100,000 (administrative de minimis threshold for cross-border related-party transactions when a Samoa tax return is required; a tax-exempt International Company has no mandatory transfer pricing documentation obligation unless it is assessed as resident or subject to Samoa-source income).

Economic substance scope: The Samoa International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations apply to International Companies carrying on relevant activities, including banking, insurance, fund management, financing or leasing, headquarters, shipping, and holding company activities. Digital magazine publishing and sponsored media placement do not constitute a relevant activity under the ESR. However, general anti-avoidance provisions and the sham transaction doctrine may disregard an International Company that has no commercial substance if it is interposed merely to divert advertising revenue away from the true economic operator.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion on undeclared Samoa-source revenue

Statutory sanction: Assessment on 100% of hidden taxable income plus a shortfall penalty of 200% of the evaded tax under the Samoa Income Tax Act 2014; daily penalties for failure to furnish a return; potential criminal fine up to WST 200,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; courts may issue travel bans, freezing orders, disqualification orders, and sequestration of personal assets.
Designation as a shell company without legitimate activity

Statutory sanction: The Samoa Revenue Division may apply general anti-avoidance rules to recharacterize revenue in the hands of the ultimate beneficial owner, impose back taxes with 200% penalties, and the Registrar may strike off the International Company for lacking economic substance.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly effect sham transactions may be personally liable for restitution, barred from serving as officers of any Samoa entity, and subject to criminal fines and imprisonment for false declarations.
Anti-money laundering / record-keeping breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018, penalties include a fine of up to WST 1,000,000 and up to 14 years imprisonment; the resident agent may be suspended and the International Company deregistered.

Director personal liability: Directors may face criminal prosecution, personal asset freezing, travel bans, and disqualification from managing companies; civil liability may be imposed for losses suffered by third parties through the money-laundering conduit.
Materially false annual return declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the International Companies Act 1987, knowingly making a false statement to the Registrar is an offence punishable by a fine of up to WST 10,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years; the company also faces strike-off and restoration penalties.

Director personal liability: The director or resident agent who signs the declaration bears personal criminal liability, including imprisonment, civil restitution to the Registrar and creditors, a travel ban pending trial, and deregistration of the entity.
