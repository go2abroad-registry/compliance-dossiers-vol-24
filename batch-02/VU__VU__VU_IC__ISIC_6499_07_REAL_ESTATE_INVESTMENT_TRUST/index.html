Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Vanuatu Revenue and Customs (VRC), Ministry of Finance and Economic Management, Port Vila; online services portal: https://www.vrc.gov.vu/. Formation and licensing of International Companies and Real Estate Investment Trusts: Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC); portal: https://www.vfsc.vu/.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned International Company (IC) obtains its statutory identifier from the VFSC rather than from VRC: (1) the beneficial owner appoints a VFSC-licensed registered agent; (2) the agent submits the proposed International Company name, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered address, and license application under the International Companies Act [Cap 222]; (3) the VFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation containing the company/trust registration number; (4) for REIT activity, the applicant applies for a financial services / international trust license and pays prescribed levy; (5) the IC does not receive a TIN because Vanuatu imposes no income tax on ICs; the VFSC registration number, business license number, and VRC customs importer/exporter code (if goods movement is needed) constitute the fiscal identity envelope.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the non-resident International Company is excluded from Vanuatu entity-level direct taxation; profits, dividends, interest, royalties, capital gains, and distributions are exempt under the International Companies Act so long as the IC does not carry on local (Vanuatu-resident) business outside permitted offshore activity. It is not eligible for or required to make a pass-through election; it is not a Vanuatu tax resident.

Available election forms

Vanuatu has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. The IC may elect in its constitutive instruments (and board resolutions) to operate as a single class share company, multi-class share company, segregated cell company, unit trust or bare trust for the REIT portfolio; it may also elect a VFSC-licensed trustee/management company. These elections determine the VFSC licence scope and the rights of investors, not domestic income-tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT (Value Added Tax) — Vanuatu levies a 12.5% VAT on domestic taxable supplies under the Value Added Tax Act; a non-resident International Company is outside the VAT net for offshore REIT services and for income sourced outside Vanuatu, resulting in an effective 0% indirect tax rate for the offshore exempt zone activity.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For a local taxable person, the VAT registration threshold is VUV 4,000,000 (annual taxable turnover). For the non-resident International Company REIT, no VAT registration threshold is engaged because the IC has no domestic taxable supply nexus and is not permitted to carry on local business in Vanuatu.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For Vanuatu VAT purposes, a taxable person must zero-rate exports of goods evidenced by removal from Vanuatu within the period required by VRC, and may zero-rate certain services supplied to non-residents if the direct benefit is received wholly outside Vanuatu. A non-resident International Company, however, is not a VAT taxable person for permitted offshore REIT activities, so its export character is established by the non-Vanuatu situs of the underlying real estate assets and the non-resident beneficiary base, with no input-VAT recovery required.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed via ASYCUDA World / VRC
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing external port of discharge
  • Commercial invoice and REIT distribution/redemption documentation
  • Inward remittance certificate, SWIFT MT103 or bank statement evidencing offshore payment
  • VFSC Certificate of Incorporation and REIT/financial services licence
  • Written mandate from the trustee/investment manager confirming offshore beneficial ownership

Vanuatu does not operate a national real-time e-invoicing or fiscal stamping platform. VAT-registered local suppliers must issue prescribed VAT invoices; imports and exports are cleared through VRC's ASYCUDA World electronic single-window system. A non-resident IC has no domestic electronic-invoicing obligation unless it registers for local VAT (which would exceed its permitted IC scope).

If an IC or its trustee makes VAT-taxable local supplies without registration, VRC can recover VAT, interest and penalties under the Value Added Tax Act, and customs duties plus penalties for undeclared imports. False export/inward-remittance claims are treated as revenue fraud: VRC may impose administrative penalties up to the amount of tax avoided, deny or claw back input VAT refunds, and VFSC may suspend or revoke the International Company REIT licence for breach of the International Companies Act or anti-money-laundering Rules. Investors claiming refunds on prohibited local operations are not protected by the tax-exempt zone.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Vanuatu maintains no exchange-control restrictions on current or capital account transactions. The vatu (VUV) is fully convertible in both directions for foreign direct investment, portfolio capital, dividend distributions, and loan repayments. Profit remittance is unrestricted and does not require prior central-bank approval, provided the underlying transactions are commercially documented and any required annual fees or licenses are current. Capital proceeds and investment returns may be repatriated freely in the same foreign currency as received or converted and remitted without conversion limits.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory surrender requirements for export receipts. Vanuatu does not mandate a timeline for repatriation or forcible conversion of export proceeds into VUV. Exporters may retain overseas earnings in freely convertible foreign-currency accounts with an authorised financial institution. Any FX transaction must be reported to the Reserve Bank of Vanuatu only through mandated statistical reporting by licensed banks; the transaction itself is not subject to prior approval or conversion ratios.

Central bank supervision: Reserve Bank of Vanuatu

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI providers have limited viability in Vanuatu. The Reserve Bank of Vanuatu supervises all deposit-taking and payment institutions under the Financial Institutions Act and the Payments and Settlements Systems Act. A non-licensed offshore EMI cannot operate in the Vanuatu payment corridor or open accounts with Vanuatu banks without a physical licensed local branch. The practical legal rating for using fintech EMIs is Medium for ancillary disbursement rails subject to full AMLCFT screening of the EMI, and Low for serving as the primary clearing counterparty for a REIT's capital escrow and dividend wires, due to lack of recognition as an authorised financial institution and absence of depositor protection.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - Vanuatu is a zero corporate income tax jurisdiction; an International Company (IC) under the International Companies Act Cap. 222 is statutorily exempt from income tax, capital gains tax, and dividend tax on its worldwide profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Vanuatu law imposes no withholding tax on dividends, no branch remittance tax, and no exchange control restrictions; distributions from an International Company to non-resident shareholders are paid free of any Vanuatu tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within two months (60 days) after each anniversary of incorporation; the annual government fee falls due on the same date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Deadline: · Frequency:

Default penalty of 25% of the unpaid VAT plus statutory interest; fraudulent evasion attracts a penal assessment of up to 200% of the evaded amount, daily late-payment surcharges, and imprisonment on conviction.
Box 10 - Input VAT Credit Deductible VAT paid on imported goods and local business expenses used for taxable supplies. Input credits require a valid VAT invoice; if the entity makes exempt financial services, input VAT must be apportioned and only the taxable-use portion claimed. Default penalty of 25% of the unpaid VAT plus statutory interest; fraudulent evasion attracts a penal assessment of up to 200% of the evaded amount, daily late-payment surcharges, and imprisonment on conviction.
Box 15 - Net VAT Payable or Refundable Output VAT less input VAT credits for the period. A registered person must file even a nil return; failure to submit a nil return attracts default penalties. Default penalty of 25% of the unpaid VAT plus statutory interest; fraudulent evasion attracts a penal assessment of up to 200% of the evaded amount, daily late-payment surcharges, and imprisonment on conviction.
Account Holder Block Identity, tax residence, and taxpayer identification number of each controlling person and reportable account holder resident in a CRS participating jurisdiction. Non-resident UBO data must reconcile with Part C of the IC Annual Return; certified passports and foreign TINs for each non-resident investor must be collected and retained. Monetary penalties per unreported account, daily default fines, potential revocation of the IC's tax-exempt status, and adverse rating by the OECD Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information.
Financial Account Block Account balance or value, income paid, and gross proceeds credited to each reportable account during the calendar year. For a non-resident REIT investor, passive rental income distributions and sale proceeds must be reported to the investor's country of tax residence. Monetary penalties per unreported account, daily default fines, potential revocation of the IC's tax-exempt status, and adverse rating by the OECD Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Vanuatu has no transfer-pricing legislation, no thin-capitalization rules, and no statutory documentation threshold expressed in Vatu (VUV). Prudent international standard for an exempt offshore REIT: prepare contemporaneous economic-substance and transfer-pricing files (Master File and Local File) whenever annual intercompany management, advisory, or financing charges reach or exceed VUV 10,000,000 (approximately USD 84,000), because Vanuatu banks and correspondent financial institutions apply OECD-aligned due diligence even absent a domestic TP statute.

Economic substance scope: Vanuatu has not enacted a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute applicable to ordinary International Companies; the ESR doctrine is enforced indirectly through the IC Act's offshore-conduct prohibition, VFSC licensing supervision of registered agents and intermediaries, and OECD/FHTP monitoring. An income-generating REIT should maintain: a physical registered office in Vanuatu; a licensed registered agent; books and records kept at the registered office (IC Act s.57); annual board meetings evidenced by signed minutes; and strategic control exercised from Vanuatu. Absence of these indicators re-characterizes the entity as a shell and exposes it to VFSC deregistration, blacklisting, and loss of the statutory tax exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Penal assessment of up to 200% of the evaded VAT plus statutory interest; criminal fine and imprisonment for fraudulent evasion; asset freezing orders and travel bans under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers are criminally liable on conviction; civil restitution orders compel repayment of evaded tax; courts may impose travel restrictions and disqualification from managing companies.
Shell Company / False Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: VFSC administrative strike-off and deregistration; forfeiture of the IC statutory tax exemption; mandatory re-registration as an ordinary domestic company subject to 12.5% VAT and all local charges; adverse listing on international blacklists.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for company debts contracted during non-compliance and for statutory penalties; nominee directors face VFSC license revocation and aggravated fines.
Unlicensed REIT / Unauthorized Collective Investment Scheme

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist orders; monetary fines per contravention; criminal conviction for unlicensed dealing; court-ordered restitution of investor funds; deregistration of the IC.

Director personal liability: Directors and fund managers are jointly and severally liable for investor losses; key personnel may be imprisoned and permanently banned by VFSC from carrying on financial services.
AML/CFT Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fines scaled to the severity of the breach; revocation of any financial services license; criminal money-laundering penalties including substantial fines and imprisonment; asset freezing and travel bans.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners face personal criminal liability, civil penalties, and asset confiscation; foreign travel may be restricted pending proceedings.
Domestic Trading While Holding IC Status

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of the tax exemption; retrospective application of all Vanuatu taxes on income and transactions; administrative penalties; compulsory conversion to an ordinary local company; deregistration.

Director personal liability: Statutory officers are personally liable for unremitted taxes and penalties and may be prosecuted for carrying on business without lawful registration.
