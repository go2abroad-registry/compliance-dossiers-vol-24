Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from Samoa to a non-resident recipient qualify for 0% VAGST. The goods must be physically exported within a documented period, and the seller must possess evidence of international transportation and foreign payment.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance. Tax invoices (paper or PDF) must contain seller and buyer VAGST ID numbers, description, value, and tax amount. VAGST returns are filed electronically through the SRA eTax portal. No digital stamping or continuous transaction control mechanism is applicable.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger retrospective clawback of the refunded VAGST amount plus a civil penalty of 50% of the tax sought, and interest at 1.5% per month. Criminal liability under the Tax Administration Act and Customs Act may include fines up to WST 20,000 and imprisonment for up to 2 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Samoa has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations (15 May 2006) and does not restrict payments and transfers for current international transactions. The WST is managed-float and deliverable, but the capital account is administered: non-resident capital injections, intercompany loans, share transfers and liquidation remittances require CBS approval through an authorized dealer, with documentary verification of commercial purpose. A WS_IC is exempt from the Exchange Control Act in respect of offshore foreign-currency business under the International Companies Act 1987, provided no transaction is conducted in WST or with a Samoan resident without CBS consent. Profit repatriation and dividend remittances are screened for substance, trade documentation and LME/CME copper-price benchmarking; practical hurdles include proof of physical scrap flows, invoice benchmarking, UBO disclosure thresholds and 3-10 business-day processing windows for CBS and AML verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exports of scrap copper wire from Samoa must be declared to Customs under the Customs Act 2014 and settled through an authorized dealer. The statutory rule requires export proceeds to be received within 6 months of the export date and, unless a foreign-currency account is approved by CBS, the exporter must offer the foreign currency to an authorized dealer for conversion into WST within one month of receipt; the mandatory conversion ratio for onshore settlement is 100% where the proceeds are not held in an approved foreign-currency account. For a WS_IC operating as a pure offshore consignment trader, the applicable effective ratio is 0% where all proceeds are held offshore or in a CBS-approved foreign-currency account and are applied to permissible offshore trade payments; any conversion to WST or onshore WST settlement triggers the 100% offer-to-sell obligation at the authorized dealer's buying rate.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS) acting as the exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act 1976, the Exchange Control Regulations 1981 and the Financial Institutions Act 1996; the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) licenses and supervises International Companies and any offshore banking license-holder. The Money Laundering Prevention Act 2007 and the Money Laundering Prevention Regulations 2009 complete the AML/CFT supervisory framework applicable to authorized dealers.

Equity and shareholder subordinated-debt injections must be routed by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the parent bank to the WS_IC's CBS-approved USD or AUD foreign-currency account, or to a capital-escrow account where escrow conditions are imposed. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% (general Samoa resident company rate under Income Tax Act 2012, as amended); effective rate 0% for a Samoa International Company (IC) because Section 202 of the International Companies Act 1987 grants a mandatory, absolute exemption from all Samoan taxes on income, profits, gains, capital, stamp duty, and withholding taxes.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by a Samoa International Company to non-resident shareholders. The general Samoan non-resident dividend withholding tax rate is 15% under the Income Tax Act 2012, but it never applies to an IC in good standing because Section 202 of the International Companies Act 1987 exempts all distributions by ICs from any Samoan tax. Repatriation is therefore free of Samoa withholding tax, provided the IC has not breached its 'international' restrictions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File/Master File, or CbC reporting threshold is imposed on a tax-exempt International Company under Samoa law. Samoa's Income Tax Act 2012 applies the arm's-length principle to associated persons but the IC's exemption removes it from the income tax net. Practical ESR substance benchmark applied by the Competent Authority for a distribution-and-service-centre IC trading recycled scrap copper: annual Samoan operating expenditure of at least WST 250,000 (approx. USD 90,000), a physical Samoan office, at least two full-time Samoan employees or an executed service agreement with the licensed registered agent, and board meetings physically held in Samoa at least twice per year. Related-party transaction evidence (independent pricing benchmark for the scrap purchase/resale margin) should be retained for 6 years even though no filing threshold exists.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2019, together with the Economic Substance Regulations 2020, requires every International Company to file an annual Economic Substance Declaration (ESD). The wholesale-of-waste-and-scrap activity (ISIC 4669) is classified as a 'distribution and service centre' - a relevant activity. A Samoa tax-resident IC must demonstrate that core income-generating activities occur in Samoa: contracting and negotiation in Samoa, Samoan management decisions, Samoan bank accounts for settlement, and proportionally adequate Samoan staff and expenditure. An IC that proves tax residence in an approved foreign jurisdiction (by foreign TRC and management evidence) is displaced from the Samoa substance test, but non-resident ICs without foreign TRC must satisfy it in Samoa. Deficiencies are reported through the automatic exchange of information to the ultimate-parent jurisdiction, which exposes the IC to immediate audit in the shareholder's tax country. The doctrine effectively forbids zero-substance shell trading: a scrap copper trading IC must be either genuinely managed in Samoa, or genuinely resident and taxable in another jurisdiction - never stateless paper.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: First-year fixed penalty of up to WST 5,000 plus WST 500 per month of continuing non-compliance; escalation to a second-year penalty of up to WST 25,000; mandatory information exchange with the beneficial owner's tax authority; deregistration and strike-off of the IC; the Competent Authority may refer the file to the Samoa Revenue Authority for income tax assessment at 27% on the IC's global trading profits as though the exemption were void.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed registered agent are jointly and severally liable for unpaid ESD penalties; directors face disqualification for up to 5 years under the International Companies Act 1987; the Samoa court may issue a departure prohibition order preventing directors and the principal beneficial owner from leaving Samoa until all penalties are settled.
Tax Evasion / Abusive Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: The exemption is voided retroactively to the date of the first prohibited transaction; the full 27% Samoan CIT is assessed on all Samoan-source trading profits; withholding tax at 15% is applied to every distribution made during the tainted period; late payment interest accrues at 2% per month, and a 50% shortfall penalty is added. The Minister of Customs and Revenue may also revoke the IC's registration certificates and freeze the Samoan settlement account.

Director personal liability: Officers who signed false annual returns or who knowingly directed the tainted transactions face criminal fines up to WST 50,000 and imprisonment for up to 3 years; courts pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution equal to the entire offshore profit trail, including fees paid to affiliates, and issue worldwide asset freezing orders against the directors.
AML/CFT Violation

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to WST 500,000 under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2018; confiscation of the full cargo value and vessel/container seizure; per-transaction penalties for unlicensed scrap dealing; customs penalties up to three times the value of the misdescribed goods under the Customs Act 2014; referral to the Solicitor General for civil forfeiture proceedings.

Director personal liability: Individual directors involved in the transactions face imprisonment up to 7 years; personal disgorgement of all fees and commissions earned in the tainted flow; travel bans and extradition exposure to the jurisdiction of the counterparty; the licensed registered agent may lose its license and be barred from acting as trustee or agent in Samoa.
GAAR / Transfer Pricing Adjustment

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may disregard the IC's exempt status or reallocate profits to the Samoa entity under the general anti-avoidance rule and transfer pricing provisions of the Income Tax Act 2012; re-adjusted profits are taxed at 27%; a 20% shortfall penalty applies plus daily late-interest; automatic exchange of information requires the reallocation to be notified to the parent and buyer jurisdictions, generating compensated assessments overseas.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the pricing policy are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax shortfall and penalties; where the pricing memoranda are fabricated or backdated, criminal liability attaches for forgery and tax evasion with imprisonment up to 5 years; the court may bar the directors from holding office in any Samoan or foreign company registered in Samoa.
