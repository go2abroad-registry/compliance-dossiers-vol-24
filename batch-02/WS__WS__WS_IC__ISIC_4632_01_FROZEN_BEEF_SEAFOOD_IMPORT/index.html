Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Revenue of Samoa, Taxpayer Services Division, Apia, Samoa; online and taxpayer portal at https://www.revenue.gov.ws, with import/customs declarations processed through the ASYCUDA World electronic single window under Samoa Customs Service.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Samoa International Company first files the Business/Taxpayer Registration Application (Form BTR-01) with the Ministry of Revenue together with the certificate of incorporation, the current Certificate of Exemption issued under the International Companies Act 2012, the memorandum and articles, certificate of registered office in Samoa, passport copies of directors/beneficial owners, and a statement of anticipated import/export turnover. The Ministry issues a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and, where the entity conducts import activities, a VAGST registration number; the same TIN is used as the Customs client identifier in ASYCUDA World. Processing is ordinarily completed within five to ten business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Samoa International Company first files the Business/Taxpayer Registration Application (Form BTR-01) with the Ministry of Revenue together with the certificate of incorporation, the current Certificate of Exemption issued under the International Companies Act 2012, the memorandum and articles, certificate of registered office in Samoa, passport copies of directors/beneficial owners, and a statement of anticipated import/export turnover. The Ministry issues a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and, where the entity conducts import activities, a VAGST registration number; the same TIN is used as the Customs client identifier in ASYCUDA World. Processing is ordinarily completed within five to ten business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. A Samoa International Company may elect to be treated as an International Company under the International Companies Act 2012, thereby securing exemption from Samoan income tax on non-Samoa-source income, subject to its Certificate of Exemption. For indirect tax purposes, the entity may voluntarily elect VAGST registration before reaching the statutory threshold, but may not elect to be treated as a transparent entity; hybrid mismatch elections are unavailable in Samoa.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 of annual taxable supplies; registration is compulsory from the date the threshold is reached or exceeded, and applies to importers of goods for business use where imports exceed the threshold in the relevant twelve-month period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAGST is zero-rated on export supplies of goods physically shipped out of Samoa, on services supplied to non-residents that are wholly consumed outside Samoa, and on international carriage of goods and passengers. A zero-rating claim requires the exporter to hold sufficient documentary evidence that the goods left Samoa or the services were supplied and consumed offshore, including transport evidence, customs clearance, and proof of foreign currency payment from a non-resident buyer.

  • Customs Export Declaration processed and authenticated through ASYCUDA World evidencing actual exportation from Samoa
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or equivalent ocean/air cargo transport document showing physical movement of frozen beef or seafood out of Samoan territory
  • Commercially certified export invoice and packing list describing the goods, quantity, and net weight, cross-referenced to the customs export declaration
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a licensed Samoan commercial bank evidencing receipt of the foreign currency sale proceeds from the non-resident buyer

Samoa does not mandate real-time fiscal e-invoicing or digital stamping of invoices. However, all import and export declarations, customs value calculations, payment of VAGST and duty, and supporting document uploads must be lodged electronically through the ASYCUDA World system. VAGST-registered persons must issue compliant VAGST invoices and retain electronic records, with clearance of imports conditional on the ASYCUDA assessment and payment of applicable VAGST at importation.

Export zero-rating claims are closely scrutinized by the Ministry of Revenue and Samoa Customs. Where goods are not in fact exported, where proceeds are not repatriated within the 180-day deadline, or where documentation is fabricated, the full 15% VAGST is immediately assessed, any refund is clawed back with interest, and penalties of up to 150% of the tax shortfall may be imposed under the Tax Administration Act 2012. Fraudulent claims may result in criminal prosecution and forfeiture of goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa Tala (WST) has no deep offshore deliverable market and remains subject to centralised exchange control. Current-account payments for genuine imports are generally permitted through an Authorized Dealer once commercial documentation is verified. Capital-account inflows into a Samoa International Company (IC) are treated as external capital and may be credited to a foreign-currency non-resident account without mandatory WST conversion; however, any subsequent conversion of WST into foreign currency, or remittance of local WST proceeds attributable to domestic activities, requires CBS Authorized Dealer approval. Profit remittance hurdles are moderate: the IC’s tax-exempt status must be evidenced by its Certificate of Incorporation/exemption letter and a board resolution declaring that no local Samoan business was conducted, otherwise the remittance may be re-characterised as local income or a prohibited IC activity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For physical exports from Samoa, the exporter must repatriate export proceeds through an Authorized Dealer within 30 days of receipt and convert 100% of the proceeds into WST unless the CBS grants a foreign-currency retention authorisation. For the WS_IC offshore trading platform, no customs export filing occurs in Samoa when the frozen beef and seafood shipments move directly from a non-Samoan seller to a non-Samoan buyer; incoming trade proceeds may remain in a designated USD non-resident account without mandatory surrender, provided the IC can evidence that the goods and underlying trade transaction did not pass through the Samoan customs territory.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (CBS), Exchange Control and Money Laundering Prevention Authority, Apia, Samoa; statutory mandate under the Exchange Control Act 1978 and the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and unsuitable as a primary clearing rail. Samoa has no digital EMI licensing regime, and CBS does not recognise offshore electronic money institutions as Authorized Dealers. An offshore fintech EMI cannot hold WST, issue Samoa-based import letters of credit, or discharge exchange-control reporting obligations. It may be used only as an ancillary USD collection mechanism for non-Samoan trade flows, subject to full customer due diligence and mirror-KYC; however, correspondent de-risking and the absence of regulatory supervision in Samoa make it unacceptable for capital escrow or trade settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Approved routing is a SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder’s bank in the shareholder’s jurisdiction to ANZ Bank Samoa Limited or BSP Samoa Limited for credit to the WS_IC USD non-resident account. Field 70 must clearly contain the composite reference 'CAP WS_IC FROZEN-BEEF-SEAFOOD-IMPORT' and use a CBS-recognised external purpose code such as 'equity participation / foreign direct investment'. The remitting bank shall pre-advise the Samoa receiving bank with the Certificate of Incorporation, board resolution approving the capital contribution, source-of-funds evidence, and a copy of the CBS authorised-dealer foreign-exchange declaration. Funds must be received in USD or AUD/NZD; crypto, third-party pass-through, or unregulated marketplace transfers are not compliant. If WST liquidity is later needed, conversion is executed at the Authorized Dealer’s onshore rate and only for bona fide local operating expenses, not for outward profit remittance without CBS approval.For open-account import settlements, the WS_IC instructs the Samoa bank to issue a SWIFT MT103 with Field 70 containing the invoice number, product tariff line, supplier name, and purpose code 'import payment for goods'. The Samoa bank must hold the proforma invoice, underlying signed contract, shipping document, and Samoa customs import declaration where goods cross Samoan customs. For documentary trade, the bank opens an irrevocable revolving Letter of Credit via SWIFT MT700 in favour of the global supplier; settlement under the LC is effected by MT103/MT202 against compliant presentation of bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, health certificates, and any required certificate of origin. Where the frozen beef and seafood are pure cross-border flows not landing in Samoa, payments can be made directly from the WS_IC USD account using the same documentary package and an 'offshore transit trade' purpose code; the Samoan bank records the transaction for CBS statistical reporting but no local import duty filing arises.Deliverable WST/USD FX forward from a CBS Authorized Dealer, tenor up to 12 months, supported by a genuine underlying import settlement or capital transaction.Non-deliverable WST/USD FX forward (NDF) with a licensed offshore bank, subject to CBS exchange-control approval, because WST is not fully deliverable outside Samoa.Cross-currency swap (WST/USD) for conversion of an approved external loan or trade facility, subject to CBS case-by-case approval and a maximum tenor aligned to the underlying real commercial exposure.FX options are not permitted as a standalone speculative instrument; they may be considered only where the CBS has issued an express hedging licence and the IC demonstrates no residual open position beyond the trade volume.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Samoa International Company under the International Companies Act 1987 (as amended); the onshore resident corporate income tax headline rate is 27% and is not applicable to an exempt International Company.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on any dividend or distribution made by a Samoa International Company to its non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Annual Return is due within the period prescribed under the International Companies Act 1987, normally aligned with the anniversary of incorporation or the calendar year for licensed companies. No Ministry for Revenue income tax return due date applies to the exempt entity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Samoa Tala (WST) filing threshold triggers mandatory Local File or Master File for an exempt International Company. Samoa tax law does not impose a substantive transfer-pricing documentation regime on International Companies. Nevertheless, for intercompany purchases of frozen beef and seafood through the cold-chain trading model, a prudent taxpayer should prepare OECD-aligned transfer pricing evidence if any entity in the chain has taxable operations in another jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: Samoa has not enacted an Economic Substance Regulations regime equivalent to BVI or Cayman. A Samoa International Company is not required to file an economic substance report solely because it carries on offshore trading. It must, however, maintain a registered office and licensed registered agent in Samoa and must not carry on prescribed domestic business activities in Samoa. For banking and AML counterparty purposes, substance is demonstrated by board minutes, contracts, purchase orders, bills of lading, and the actual commercial activity of importing frozen meat and seafood through the Samoa entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Under Samoa’s Tax Administration Act and Income Tax Act, the offending entity and principals may be liable for the full underpaid tax, penalty of up to 200% of the tax shortfall, monetary fines, and imprisonment for tax evasion offences.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in false filings or who allow the International Company to be used as a sham may be personally and jointly liable for unpaid taxes and penalties. The court may issue travel bans, asset freezing orders, and custodial sentences for fraud-related tax evasion.
Shell Company and False Substance Representation

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike off the International Company, dissolve the registration, and impose monetary penalties. Continued non-compliance may also prohibit the directors from being appointed in any Samoa-registered company.

Director personal liability: A director who allows the company to trade while insolvent or after strike-off proceedings may be personally liable for debts incurred. Civil restitution and director disqualification orders are available against those who abuse the corporate form.
Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Breach

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with Samoa’s AML/CFT statutory obligations exposes the International Company and its registered agent to substantial fines, licence suspension, mandatory remedial orders, and criminal prosecution in egregious cases.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers face criminal liability for money laundering, financing of terrorism, or AML compliance failures. Penalties include imprisonment, confiscation of proceeds, travel bans, and personal financial liability for laundering tainted funds.
Misrepresentation to Customs and Regulatory Authorities

Statutory sanction: Customs laws and the Ministry of Agriculture and Fisheries enforce seizure of the consignment, customs penalties, monetary fines, suspension or revocation of the import licence, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent importation.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or who direct unlawful importation may be held personally liable for customs penalties, restitution to the state, and imprisonment. Corporate veil-piercing is available where the International Company is used as a vehicle for deliberate regulatory evasion.
