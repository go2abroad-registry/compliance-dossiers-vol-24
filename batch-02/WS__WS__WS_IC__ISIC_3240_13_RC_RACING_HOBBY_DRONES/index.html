Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Samoa Revenue Authority (SRA), Ministry for Revenue, online portal at https://www.revenue.gov.ws/ (SRA Online Services). Incorporation and annual licensing of International Companies are administered jointly with the Samoa International Finance Authority (SIFA) under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour.

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned International Company (IC), Step 1 is incorporation through a licensed Samoan registered agent or SIFA, producing the Certificate of Incorporation and the IC registration number. Step 2 is application to the SRA for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) via the SRA registration form (IR-1) at the SRA One-Stop Shop, submitting the Certificate of Incorporation, the statutory tax exemption certificate issued under the International Companies Act 1987, and proof of the registered agent address. Step 3 is issuance by the SRA of the TIN and, where the IC undertakes domestic supply, a separate Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) registration certificate. Standard ICs are granted a 20-year tax exemption certificate and are excluded from the resident VAGST registration base for offshore business.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: International Companies enjoy a statutory exemption from Samoan income tax, VAGST, stamp duty on offshore transactions, and withholding tax on distributions to non-residents, provided the IC does not carry on domestic business in Samoa. The exemption is ring-fenced; any local Samoan-source trading activity converts the relevant income to a taxable resident status.

Available election forms

Samoa does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. An IC may file the statutory exemption certificate with SRA and, if it inadvertently engages in domestic trade, may elect to be treated as an ordinary resident company under the Income Tax Act 2012, which revokes the IC exemption for the domestic business segment. Hybrid mismatch elections are not legislated; however, SIFA administers elective exchange-of-information and treaty-compatible classification forms for ICs seeking to be recognized as non-resident tax-transparent vehicles in foreign jurisdictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

WST 100,000 annual taxable turnover (VAGST Act 1997) for compulsory registration; suppliers below the threshold may voluntarily register; the SRA may compel registration for any person making taxable supplies in the course of a taxable activity in Samoa.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under section 16(1)(a) of the VAGST Act 1997 to goods physically exported from Samoa, provided the supplier exports the goods within 28 days of sale or within any further period approved by the SRA, and to services supplied to a non-resident who is outside Samoa at the time of supply where the benefit of the services is not enjoyed in Samoa. The IC's FPV racing drone retail sales to overseas buyers qualify for zero-rating where the drones leave Samoa through Apia port or Faleolo International Airport and the supplier retains contemporaneous export evidence; input VAGST attributable to zero-rated supplies is refundable to the exporter.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic entry-outward lodged with the Samoa Customs Division of the SRA) with the unique export reference number and commercial invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Samoa, plus courier tracking logs (DHL/FedEx/UPS) showing foreign delivery
  • Bank Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 confirmation evidencing settlement in foreign currency, and payment gateway settlement reports from PayPal and Stripe

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate is in force in Samoa. VAGST-registered suppliers must issue a tax invoice containing prescribed particulars: supplier name and TIN, purchaser name and TIN, description of supply, taxable amount, and VAGST charged. Electronic returns and payments are lodged through SRA Online Services; the SRA applies risk-based audit selection and data-matching rather than mandatory pre-clearance of invoices. Digital record-keeping is mandatory for VAGST-registered persons.

Export zero-rating claims are reviewed by SRA audit with retrospective reassessment and clawback of output VAGST if the documented export, payment, or 180-day currency repatriation trail is defective. Penalties include a late payment penalty of 2% per month on the assessed amount, a civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax shortfall for false or misleading export declarations, and criminal sanctions of fines and imprisonment for fraudulent zero-rating claims. All export documentation and underlying FX evidence must be retained for at least seven years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Samoa Tala (WST) is a non-internationally-traded currency administered by the Central Bank of Samoa under a managed float referenced to a currency basket (USD, NZD, AUD, EUR, JPY, CNY). Current-account transactions (trade, services, remittances) are liberalized under the Exchange Control Regulations 2016 but must be routed through an authorized dealer bank with full supporting documentation at the point of execution. Capital-account transactions, including foreign direct investment, equity injections, and dividend remittances, remain subject to Central Bank of Samoa notification/approval thresholds and are policed by authorized dealers through prescribed exchange-control reporting forms. The WS_IC International Company is a non-resident entity under the International Companies Act 2018: it must not carry on business in Samoa, must transact exclusively in foreign currencies, and sits outside the domestic convertibility and surrender regime. Profit remittance analysis: dividends paid by the tax-exempt IC to non-resident shareholders are free of Samoa withholding tax and are remittable via SWIFT MT103 without Central Bank approval, provided (i) remittance is made from a foreign-currency account, (ii) the paying authorized dealer verifies the IC's Certificate of Good Standing and the registered agent's certification, and (iii) the payment narrative carries the BPM6 purpose code D210 (dividends). Practical hurdle: authorized dealers will not convert WST for non-resident ICs, and a domestic WST settlement by the IC would trigger residence reclassification risk; the treasury architecture must therefore remain 100% USD-denominated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident Samoan exporters, Regulation 7 of the Exchange Control Regulations 1982 (as amended in 2016) requires that all export proceeds be repatriated to Samoa through an authorized dealer and sold for WST within one month from the date of exportation unless a written exemption is obtained from the Central Bank. Traditional commodity exporters generally cannot retain foreign currency; non-traditional exporters may apply through their bank for approval to retain foreign-currency proceeds in an authorized-dealer account for working-capital purposes, subject to quarterly Central Bank reporting. For the WS_IC FPV drone treasury blueprint, the surrender rules are NOT triggered: the IC is a non-resident company, it does not export physical goods from Samoan customs territory (components are milled and integrated by contract manufacturers in China/Southeast Asia and shipped directly to end customers), and it holds no WST-denominated accounts. Critical compliance precondition: no Samoan customs export declaration may be made in the IC's name, retail proceeds (PayPal/Stripe) must never be converted into WST, and all settlement must remain in USD foreign-currency accounts at an authorized dealer so the Central Bank of Samoa continues to treat the entity as non-resident.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Samoa (Faletupe Tutotonu o Samoa)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a collections and disbursement adjunct; Low as a standalone core treasury bank. Legal constraints: offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Mercury, Airwallex, Payoneer, Currencycloud) are not licensed or supervised by the Central Bank of Samoa; balances held with them are unregulated custodial balances in Samoan regulatory terms, and the IC's registered agent and Samoan auditor must still reconcile all treasury movements to the authorized-dealer account. PayPal is available to Samoa-registered entities for receiving retail card payments and supports payouts to a Samoan bank USD account, but Stripe is NOT a supported acquiring market in Samoa; therefore the WS_IC must contract Stripe through a payment facilitator or a sister acquiring entity in a supported jurisdiction (US, Singapore, or Hong Kong), with net proceeds escalated to the tier-1 Samoan USD account via MT103. EMI architecture rule: use Airwallex (HK) or Mercury (US) only as a collection-side aggregator with a hard daily sweep policy to the ANZ/BSP Samoa USD account, so that the audit trail, Central Bank exchange-control filing trail, and registered-agent oversight remain intact and no more than a small operating float (under USD 25,000) is ever left offshore.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: the UBO holds a personal or corporate account at a well-rated bank in the UBO's home jurisdiction. Injection is executed by SWIFT MT103/202 to the WS_IC's USD account at ANZ or BSP Samoa (authorized dealer), with beneficiary exactly as 'WS_IC' and narrative 'Subscription for shares in Samoa International Company - direct investment equity capital (BPM6 code S101)'. Pre-clearance: the registered agent submits to the receiving bank the board minute allotting shares, the signed share subscription agreement, the updated register of members, and the share-capital ledger entry; the bank completes the Central Bank exchange-control notification and escalates for any approval where the transaction exceeds the per-transfer threshold for capital inflows. Enforcement protocol: (i) no cash deposits, bearer instruments, or crypto-to-fiat bridging; (ii) the remitter must be the named UBO or a holding entity documented in the UBO chain - third-party funding is rejected outright; (iii) the first credit is accepted only after account sign-off and a successful test wire; (iv) every capital credit must be mirrored in the IC's capital ledger and disclosed to the auditor to preserve the tax-exempt non-resident status.Retail settlement rails: (a) PayPal sweep - the PayPal USD balance is auto-swept at daily or weekly thresholds to the ANZ/BSP Samoa USD account through the platform's standard withdrawal rail; (b) Stripe (available only via the supported sister entity or payment facilitator) - payouts land in the sister entity's US bank account via US ACH, then are escalated via SWIFT MT103 to the Samoa tier-1 USD account. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as a collections and disbursement adjunct; Low as a standalone core treasury bank. Legal constraints: offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Mercury, Airwallex, Payoneer, Currencycloud) are not licensed or supervised by the Central Bank of Samoa; balances held with them are unregulated custodial balances in Samoan regulatory terms, and the IC's registered agent and Samoan auditor must still reconcile all treasury movements to the authorized-dealer account. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

27% (Income Tax Act 2012, operative rate for resident companies)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on distributions by a tax-exempt International Company: the IC's profits are exempt, so dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are neither Samoan-source income nor subject to withholding. The general statutory non-resident dividend WHT rate under the Income Tax Act 2012 is 15%, applied only where a company earns Samoa-source income.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory quantitative threshold is codified in Samoa's Income Tax Act 2012; the Commissioner may issue transfer pricing adjustments on any cross-border related-party transaction that does not satisfy the arm's length standard. Administrative best practice for an IC with annual foreign related-party turnover above WST 1,000,000 is to maintain a contemporaneous Local File, Master File and, where group turnover exceeds EUR 750 million, a Country-by-Country Report in line with OECD Chapter V; documentation must be produced within 30 days of an IRD request.

Economic substance scope: The International Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019/2020, enacted in response to EU Code of Conduct Group harmful tax practices work, require an IC carrying on a relevant activity to maintain in Samoa: (i) direction and management exercised locally, (ii) adequate employees in Samoa, (iii) adequate physical premises, and (iv) core income-generating activities performed in Samoa. The FPV racing drone retail chain (carbon-fiber milling, motor integration, and e-commerce distribution) falls within distribution and retail; if marketed as tax-exempt offshore, it must be supported by real substance. A pure post-box IC is a compliance nullity and risks reassessment, penalties and exchange-of-information disclosure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Certification

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 150% of the tax evaded, daily default interest, and criminal fine up to WST 10,000 per offence or imprisonment up to 2 years (Income Tax Act 2012 ss. 129-131, 140); the tax-exempt IC status is revoked with retroactive effect and a 27% CIT charge is applied on the full income stream.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax where evasion is attributable to their wilful act or default; personal bank accounts and offshore assets may be seized, exit clearance and travel bans imposed, and imprisonment applied against the exposing or beneficial owner.
Shell Company or Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation of the IC as a sham under anti-avoidance powers; strike-off under the International Companies Act; automatic exchange of beneficial ownership information to resident-tax authorities; penalty of up to WST 300,000 or 3 times the tax benefit, and closure of the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling shareholders lose limited-liability protection for debts of the shell; courts pierce the corporate veil to order civil restitution from personal assets, impose travel bans while investigations are pending, and may pursue extradition or prosecution in OECD jurisdictions cooperating with Samoa.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act 2007 and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2018, corporate fines up to WST 7,000,000 (or twice the benefit), natural-person fines up to WST 500,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; administrative deregistration and freezing orders on Samoan accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal prosecution, disqualification from acting as directors for up to 10 years, confiscation of unexplained wealth under proceeds-of-crime powers, and travel bans; the registered agent may be separately sanctioned for deficient AML procedures.
Late or Defective Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties of WST 150 (first month) plus WST 100 per subsequent month of default; automatic strike-off and dissolution after 6 months in default; forfeiture of assets to the Registrar available for collection; reinstatement requires a Supreme Court order, all arrears, and a new registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors who trade under a struck-off IC are personally liable for all debts contracted after dissolution, subject to damages for fraudulent trading, disqualification orders, and exclusion from future Samoan incorporations; travel bans may be imposed to secure the restitution of seized assets.
