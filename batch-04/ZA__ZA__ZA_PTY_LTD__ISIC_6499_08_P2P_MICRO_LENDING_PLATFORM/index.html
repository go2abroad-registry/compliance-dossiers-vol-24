Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

South African Revenue Service (SARS); electronic portal: SARS eFiling at www.sarsefiling.co.za (for corporate income tax, VAT vendor registration, and customs account administration).

E-government filing portal

A South African-incorporated (Pty) Ltd is automatically linked to SARS for Income Tax upon incorporation via Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) integration, producing an Income Tax Reference Number (ITR). VAT registration is a separate application: the entity must file a VAT101 form through eFiling once its taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold. Foreign-owned entities must also submit certified incorporation documents, Memorandum of Incorporation, proof of local registered address, director/public-officer identification, and a SARS resolution appointing a South African-resident public officer where required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A South African-incorporated (Pty) Ltd is automatically linked to SARS for Income Tax upon incorporation via Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) integration, producing an Income Tax Reference Number (ITR). VAT registration is a separate application: the entity must file a VAT101 form through eFiling once its taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold. Foreign-owned entities must also submit certified incorporation documents, Memorandum of Incorporation, proof of local registered address, director/public-officer identification, and a SARS resolution appointing a South African-resident public officer where required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

South Africa does not operate a US-style check-the-box or elective pass-through regime. A (Pty) Ltd is mandatorily a separate corporate taxpayer subject to 27% Corporate Income Tax for years of assessment ending on or after 31 March 2023 and to 20% Dividends Tax on distributions. Elections are limited to defined reliefs (e.g., section 11D R&D deduction, section 24C future-expenditure allowance, capital-gain rollover relief); hybrid mismatch rules under the Income Tax Act constrain tax-arbitrage outcomes and do not permit elective passthrough classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

R1,000,000 (ZAR one million) in total taxable supplies over the preceding 12 months triggers compulsory VAT registration; voluntary vendor registration is available once taxable supplies exceed R50,000. For the P2P micro-lending platform, interest and credit-related financial services are exempt under VAT Act section 12(a) and do not count toward the threshold, while separate platform, administration, or collection fees may constitute taxable supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated under section 11(1)(a) of the VAT Act where they are exported from South Africa within the prescribed period (90 days from date of supply) and documentary proof of export is retained. Services, including qualifying financial intermediation services supplied to non-residents, are zero-rated under section 11(2)(k) read with section 11(3) where the recipient is not a South African resident and the services are supplied for the purpose of an enterprise carried on outside South Africa. For the micro-lending platform, interest and lending transactions with non-resident borrowers are exempt financial services, but separately charged arrangement or platform fees may qualify for zero-rating if the offshore enterprise requirement is met.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD500) lodged through the Customs Clearing Environment
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by an authorised South African dealer bank
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and clearing agent confirmation of export across the SACU border

South Africa currently does not impose a real-time fiscal clearance or continuous-control API stamping mandate. Suppliers must issue tax invoices with the full statutory content prescribed by the VAT Act, retain records for at least five years, and file VAT201 returns electronically via SARS eFiling or an approved EDI. SARS is progressively aligning with OECD e-invoicing frameworks and uses risk-based digital audit analytics, including cross-referencing of customs and commercial data.

SARS may disallow export zero-rating and claw back output VAT, refunds, and input credits where the exporter fails the documentary-substantiation burden; interest runs at the prescribed SARS rate and understatement penalties apply under Tax Administration Act sections 222-223, ranging from 50% for substantial understatements up to 200% for intentional tax evasion or fraud. Criminal prosecution under section 234 of the Tax Administration Act can result in fines and imprisonment, and directors of a (Pty) Ltd may face personal liability for fraudulent VAT claims or reckless conduct, while the full documentation bundle must be retained for at least five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

South Africa operates a managed-float exchange-control regime. Current account transactions, including interest, platform fees, dividends declared out of after-tax profits, and bona fide service fees, may be remitted through an Authorized Dealer after verification and, where applicable, SARS tax-compliance clearance. Capital account flows, including inbound foreign equity, foreign shareholder loans, and repayments of non-resident borrowings, are subject to prior approval, registration, and reporting through the FinSurv BOP/BI system. Profit remittance hurdles include the need to demonstrate distributable reserves under the Companies Act, a valid SARS tax-clearance certificates for dividend remittances, a 20-percent dividends tax on certain distributions unless treaty relief applies, and the requirement that all inward capital injections be correctly registered on the 'BI' or 'BOP' forms used by the Authorized Dealer. ZAR is not freely convertible for residents; non-residents may externalize approved capital and returns via the exchange-control perimeter.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For any ancillary cross-border receipt or deemed export-of-services receipt generated by the platform, for example platform fees from non-resident borrowers or data-services revenue from foreign partners, the gross proceeds must be repatriated to South Africa through an Authorized Dealer and converted into ZAR within 180 days from the due date of the underlying transaction, unless SARB FinSurv has granted a foreign-currency-account retention dispensation. There is no fixed statutory conversion percentage under the South African FX Regulations; the default obligation is that all export and service-export proceeds be promptly surrendered to an Authorized Dealer in rand at the prevailing market rate. A foreign-currency-account holder must convert and repatriate any unutilized balance into rand when the authorized holding period expires. Physical merchandise exporters are likewise subject to the general 180-day receipt-and-surrender window. Non-resident P2P platform lending participants are not subject to these surrender rules, but the resident platform treasury must operate in ZAR for domestic clearing.

Central bank supervision: South African Reserve Bank (SARB), acting through the Financial Surveillance Department (FinSurv) as the delegated exchange-control authority under the Currency and Exchanges Act 9 of 1933, advised by National Treasury. Authorized Dealers, i.e., commercial banks approved by SARB, administer day-to-day foreign-exchange control, BOP reporting, and payment clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Payoneer Fit: High

Low as a primary banking substitute; Medium as an ancillary cross-border payment gateway. Offshore digital EMIs such as Payoneer, Nium, or Electronic Money Institutions are not authorized by SARB as Authorized Dealers and cannot provide a South African local clearing code, ZAR-denominated corporate account, or direct NPS clearing. They may only be used for foreign-currency collections/remittances in limited corridors when linked to a fully registered SARB bank account. Any EMI used for debtor collections or investor flows must be subject to the platform's own FICA/KYC program, BOP reconciliation, and exchange-control approval.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders or lenders send a clean-leg SWIFT MT103 from a regulated foreign bank to the platform's SARB Authorized Dealer ZAR account. The wire must include the investor's full legal name, country, corporate identifier, ultimate beneficial owner, economic purpose, and advance BOP/BI declaration. Under the South African Balance of Payments Manual, equity capital must be coded as direct investment and, if in the form of an intercompany loan, as other investment-foreign liability. Once received, the Authorized Dealer issues a BOP/BI registration reference, and the foreign loan must be recorded on the platform's BOP return; if the loan will earn interest, the platform must file an SA tax return and ensure the 15% withholding tax or applicable tax treaty rate is discharged on foreign-interest remittances. No inbound capital may be moved offshore again without the matching externalization registration or SARB approval.Domestic borrower loan disbursements must clear exclusively through the South African National Payment System: standard bank-credit transfers via the ACB (Automated Clearing Bureau) to the borrower's local bank, or instant PayShap transfers to a local wallet, depending on borrower preference. Cross-border trade payments and platform vendor payments outside the SACU should use SWIFT MT103 messages issued by the SARB Authorized Dealer; commercial letters of credit are legally permissible but operationally irrelevant to micro-loan disbursements, and would require documentary-trade evidence only if the platform imports equipment or effectuates physical trade settlements. Payments to non-resident lenders are settled through SWIFT MT103 using the registered BOP loan reference, with named beneficiary and creditor country, and are subject to withholding-tax confirmation for interest and principal-tracking against the initial inward loan registration.Forward exchange contracts with an SARB Authorized Dealer, permitted only where there is an underlying current-account or capital-account exposure from the platform's foreign funding or debtor repayments.Cross-currency interest-rate and principal swaps concluded through an Authorized Dealer or registered FSCA financial institution, used exclusively to hedge the currency mismatch between foreign-currency funding and ZAR borrower receivables.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in emerging-market currencies other than ZAR, permissible as a hedging overlay for non-ZAR debt and vendor receivables where there is documentary proof of exposure, but not for speculative treasury trading.Plain-vanilla foreign-exchange options purchased from an Authorized Dealer for firm projected conversion of cross-border principal interest flows, subject to SARB market conduct rules and Board-approved treasury risk policy.
