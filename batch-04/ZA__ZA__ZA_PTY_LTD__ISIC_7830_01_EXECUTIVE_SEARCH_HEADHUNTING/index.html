Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permitted with compliance conditions. Under section 23(1) of the Companies Act 71 of 2008, the registered office must be a physical street address in South Africa (a post-office box is insufficient) and must be the location where the company's statutory records are kept and accessible. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

South African Revenue Service (SARS); portal: SARS eFiling at https://www.sarsefiling.co.za

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity deriving South African-source income or carrying on a South African enterprise must apply to SARS for registration as a taxpayer and obtain a SARS income tax reference number. Applications are lodged electronically through the SARS eFiling portal for non-resident companies using Form IT100/Company Registration; proof of foreign incorporation, local public officer appointment, physical address, and financial year-end are required. VAT registration is obtained separately via SARS VAT 101 if taxable supplies exceed the threshold; the same tax reference number is used for income tax, VAT, PAYE, and customs purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (a South African resident private company is taxed as a separate entity on worldwide income under the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962; no pass-through treatment is available for a (Pty) Ltd; territorial exceptions apply only through specific reliefs such as the participation exemption and controlled foreign company rules) — A foreign entity deriving South African-source income or carrying on a South African enterprise must apply to SARS for registration as a taxpayer and obtain a SARS income tax reference number. Applications are lodged electronically through the SARS eFiling portal for non-resident companies using Form IT100/Company Registration; proof of foreign incorporation, local public officer appointment, physical address, and financial year-end are required. VAT registration is obtained separately via SARS VAT 101 if taxable supplies exceed the threshold; the same tax reference number is used for income tax, VAT, PAYE, and customs purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (a South African resident private company is taxed as a separate entity on worldwide income under the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962; no pass-through treatment is available for a (Pty) Ltd; territorial exceptions apply only through specific reliefs such as the participation exemption and controlled foreign company rules)

Available election forms

South Africa does not have a US-style check-the-box election. Elections available include: headquarter company status under section 9I for a foreign-owned South African company; asset-for-share rollover elections under section 42; hybrid mismatch ordering and corresponding elections under sections 25O-25U; and grouping/assessment elections for controlled foreign companies under section 9D. All elections are made through SARS eFiling or prescribed forms and must be supported by contemporaneous documentation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory VAT registration: ZAR 1,000,000 in taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary VAT registration: ZAR 50,000 in taxable supplies in the past 12 months; non-resident electronic service providers may have separate deemed-supply registration obligations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated under section 11(1)(a) of the VAT Act when they are physically exported from South Africa within the prescribed period and evidence of export is retained. Executive search/headhunting services supplied to a non-resident are zero-rated under section 11(2)(l) if the non-resident is not in South Africa at the time services are rendered and the services are not directly connected with immovable property or movable property situated in South Africa; the service supplier must retain the signed export service contract, proof of the foreign status and location of the client, and proof of payment in foreign currency. If the service is rendered to a South African resident or connected with a South African business, VAT must be charged at 15%.

  • Customs export declaration (SAD500 container/air manifest, or SARS Customs export declaration)
  • Commercial invoice and VAT zero-rated export invoice
  • Bill of lading / air waybill / road or rail consignment note
  • Inward Foreign Exchange Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/MT202 bank credit advice evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Signed export or headhunting services contract establishing the foreign-resident status and place of supply of the client

SARS does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping on every B2B invoice. VAT-registered vendors must issue full tax invoices, file VAT 201 returns electronically via eFiling, and submit payment linked to the SARS payslip reference. SARS operates data-driven e@sys validation, is progressively standardising e-invoicing through a SARS e-invoicing interoperability framework, and may require enhanced digital reporting for large taxpayers, but there is no per-invoice government clearance stamp.

SARS may deny zero-rating, raise additional VAT assessments, and impose interest for late payment. The Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011 applies understatement penalties: 10% for a substantial understatement, 25% for failure to take reasonable care, 50% for a tax position with no reasonable grounds, 100% for an impermissible avoidance arrangement, and 150% for intentional tax evasion. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims may also attract criminal prosecution, retrospective clawback of VAT refunds, and debarment from future registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The South African Rand (ZAR) is fully convertible for current-account transactions, including cross-border service fees, dividends, interest, and trade-related disbursements, subject to FIC Act reporting and supporting documentation; profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is permissible through an Authorised Dealer provided the 20% Dividends Tax (DT) is withheld and the paying bank confirms the source of funds. Capital-account transactions, such as foreign equity subscription and offshore shareholder loans, remain controlled and require Authorised Dealer reporting to SARB FinSurv, with no general restriction for a low-risk executive search entity but with mandatory FDI reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For service exports under ISIC 7830 (executive search and headhunting), foreign-currency invoiced fees must be received in South Africa within 30 days from date of invoice and surrendered to an Authorised Dealer, or credited to a SARB-approved Foreign Currency Account (FCA). There is no mandatory fixed conversion ratio; exporters may retain a portion in an FCA subject to Authorised Dealer limits and SARB directives, but onward repatriation, balances in excess of allowable thresholds, and any disposal require Authorised Dealer oversight. Statutory timeline: 30 days for both receipt and surrender/crediting to an FCA.

Central bank supervision: South African Reserve Bank (SARB), acting through its Financial Surveillance Department (FinSurv) under the Currency and Exchanges Act 9 of 1933, with delegated administration to Authorised Dealers licensed under the exchange control regulations.
