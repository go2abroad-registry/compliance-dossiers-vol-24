Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

South African Revenue Service (SARS) — eFiling portal at https://efiling.sars.gov.za

E-government filing portal

A company incorporated as a (Pty) Ltd is a South African tax resident and must obtain an income tax reference number by registering with SARS through eFiling or a SARS branch. For VAT, a vendor must register within 21 days of exceeding the R1,000,000 annual turnover threshold or earlier if compulsory registration applies. Non-resident foreign entities deriving South African-source income must register separately as a foreign company or foreign investment entity, appoint a public officer, and obtain a SARS income tax reference number; a separate VAT vendor number is required if taxable supplies exceed the registration threshold.

South Africa does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. A (Pty) Ltd is taxed at entity level on worldwide income, with distributions subject to dividends withholding tax. Binding private rulings may be obtained from SARS to clarify the classification of hybrid instruments and hybrid mismatch arrangements; anti-avoidance re-characterisation rules in sections 8E, 8EA, 8EB and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule may override contractual labels, but no elective entity pass-through regime is available.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from South Africa within the prescribed period qualify for the zero rate under section 11(1)(a) of the VAT Act if documentary proof of export is retained. Services supplied to non-residents and not used in South Africa may qualify under section 11(2). For airline inflight catering, zero rating may apply to supplies consumed outside South Africa on international flights where the goods are securely exported and supported by international carrier documentation; supplies for domestic flights remain taxable at 15%.

South Africa does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. SARS requires a valid VAT invoice containing the prescribed particulars under section 20(1) of the VAT Act, issued within 21 days of the supply. SARS eFiling provides an API-based channel for submitting VAT returns and declarations; electronic invoicing is permissible but no mandatory government-side clearance or e-invoicing database requirement is in force.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating claims attract an understatement penalty of up to 200% of the tax shortfall plus interest under the Tax Administration Act. False customs declarations and false VAT certificates may trigger criminal liability, fines of up to R1,000,000, and imprisonment under the Customs and Excise Act. SARS may retrospectively claw back the zero-rated VAT, impose additional tax, and deny refunds where export documentation, physical movement, or the 180-day foreign currency repatriation condition is not substantiated to SARS's satisfaction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The South African Rand (ZAR) is freely convertible for genuine current-account transactions, including trade settlement, operational expenditures, branch remittances, and dividends, provided the Authorised Dealer obtains the relevant supporting documentation and, where applicable, SARS tax clearance. Capital-account flows (equity subscriptions, shareholder loans, foreign direct investment, and outward investment) remain subject to regulated reporting and notification to SARB FinSurv through an Authorised Dealer. For a non-resident-owned ZA (Pty) Ltd, each inward foreign direct investment (FDI) transfer must be registered with the receiving bank so that the corresponding historic inward reference is recorded; this record creates (a) the ability to remit dividends, (b) the ability to repatriate capital on disinvestment, and (c) a compliant route for intercompany finance. Profit remittance hurdles: dividend payments require a valid SARS dividend withholding tax declaration or treaty-based withholding rate reduction; the remitting bank must observe Dividend Withholding Tax (DWT) compliance at 20% (or reduced rate under an applicable double-taxation agreement). No corporate-level exchange control approval is needed for an ordinary dividend remittance where the entity is in a tax-compliance position and the underlying capital was lawfully brought into South Africa through a registered inward route. The ZAR trades on a free float, so conversion risk is systematic rather than regulatory.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export earnings from South African goods and services, including inflight-catering meals and bonded kitchen supplies sold to foreign-domiciled airlines, must be paid into South Africa through an Authorised Dealer within 180 days from the date of export (extendable to 365 days with SARB FinSurv approval for capital items or long-dated trade contracts). The proceeds must be credited to a ZAR account of the exporting entity, surrendering foreign currency to the domestic market; there is no fixed-rate/conversion-free choice under a floating-exchange-rate regime, but the transaction must be reported via the balance-of-payments (BOP) reporting system. The exporter must retain the SARS-specific export declaration, customs entry (SAD500), airline pro-forma invoice/purchase order, and a certificate of export where border controls apply. In practice, the receivables must match the declared invoice values and no netting with intercompany payables may occur without prior Authorised Dealer approval.

Central bank supervision: South African Reserve Bank (SARB) acting through the Financial Surveillance Department (FinSurv), operating under the Currency and Exchanges Manual for Authorised Dealers, supported by the Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC) for AML/CFT supervision and SARS for tax clearance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility as the sole primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs, virtual payment service providers, and non-resident fintechs are not recognised as Authorised Dealers under SARB's Currency and Exchanges Manual and cannot legally process the inward FDI registration required for the repatriation of capital or for conversion of export proceeds. Such EMIs may only be used as ancillary receivables/payment facilitators for low-value, non-core transactions; they must never hold the main contractual account, the capital escrow account, or the SARB-notifiable international transfer channels. A fully locally-regulated niche EMI (e.g., a licensed forex subdivision or payment-service-oriented B2B provider) could play a limited role for small-ticket supplier payments in the SADC region, provided the principal treasury remains a South African banking counterparty. Alternative fintech bridge model: use the EMI solely to execute domestic ZAR ACH disbursements to local staff/suppliers and house the international remittance clearing through an authorised dealer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The preferred route is a direct SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the non-resident shareholder's bank to the South African corporate account at the chosen Authorised Dealer. Field 70 must explicitly contain the purpose code 'FDI Equity Contribution' or 'SA Share Subscription', reference the new beneficiary company name and share subscription agreement, and state 'CZ — Capital Transfer, No Inward Foreign Investment Certification Required - Authorised Dealer to file the inward formal notification'. The Authorised Dealer is obligated to obtain supporting documentation (share subscription resolution, allotment letter, international transaction report, and exchange-control controls) and will assign a unique 'inward foreign investment notification' reference. For funds coming as shareholder loans rather than equity, the same routing must state 'CS — Foreign Bank Credit Facility' or a specific 'import/working-capital' narrative that avoids misclassification of a loan as revenue; a formal loan agreement, commercial rate certificate, and a SARB record of outward financing capacity will be required for future interest and principal repatriation. Directors and shareholders must avoid using personal accounts for corporate capital injection; all inbound FDI must be wired directly into the corporate operational account, with no interposition of intermediaries, payment wallets, or digital foreign currency platforms.Trade disbursements from the South African catering kitchen will separate into (i) local domestic expenditure in ZAR cleared via the South African national payments system (EPG/ACH) and electronically transferred from the operational ZAR account, and (ii) international obligations (e.g., imported food-grade packaging, airline invoices, equipment leases, frozen goods imported from the EU or Asia) sent through the International Payments network by SWIFT MT103 using explicit underlying commercial invoice and customs reference details. For larger aviation contract disbursements, a documentary letter of credit via MT700/MT707 would, in rare cases, be used if the airline requires proof for supplies at hub stations; practically, monthly ACH/ZAR-wire settlement from the client's South African treasury to the operational account is the core rail for receivables, while the outbound to third-party international suppliers is typically structured using payment-on-delivery instructions with netting restrictions. All outward remittances require completion of the Authorised Dealer's BOP code, and invoices above the threshold must be supported by a SARS eFiling-generated Import/Export registration and customs declarations. The company should maintain a 'forex facilitation file' containing purchase orders, invoices, air waybills, customs clearances, and proof-of-payment to facilitate custom requests. PIS is computed under Companies Regulation 26(2): 1 point per employee; 1 point per R1 million of third-party liabilities; 1 point per R1 million of turnover; 1 point per shareholder.
